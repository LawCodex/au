<td rowspan="2">ELIZABETH THE SECOND, by the Grace of God Queen of Australia and Her other Realms and Territories, Head of the Commonwealth:<br>TO the Honourable Terence Rhoderic Hudson Cole RFD QC<br>WHEREAS it is desired to replace the Letters Patent issued to you in relation to an inquiry into certain matters relating to the building and construction industry:<br>BY these Letters Patent issued in Our name by Our Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia on the advice of the Federal Executive Council and pursuant to the Constitution of the Commonwealth of Australia, the <i>Royal Commissions Act 1902</i> and other enabling powers, We revoke the Letters Patent dated 8 August 2001 appointing you to be a Commissioner to inquire into certain matters relating to the building and construction industry.<br>WITNESS the Right Reverend Dr Peter John Hollingworth, Companion of the Order of Australia, Officer of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia.<br>Dated 29 August 2001<br>Governor‑General<br>By His Excellency’s Command<br>Minister for Employment, Workplace<br>Relations and Small Business<br>for the<br>Prime Minister</td>

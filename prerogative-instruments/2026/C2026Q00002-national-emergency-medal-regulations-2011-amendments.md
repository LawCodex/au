## Schedule—Amendments

##### National Emergency Medal Regulations 2011

**1 Paragraph 3(3)(b)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “6 years”.

**2 At the end of regulation 3**

Add:

(5) The Governor‑General may, on the recommendation of the Minister, vary a declaration under subregulation (2) to amend the statements mentioned in subregulation (4).

(6) The Minister must not make a recommendation under subregulation (5) unless:

(a) the emergency happened during the previous 6 years; and

(b) the Minister has received advice from the Committee about whether to amend the declaration.

**3 Before subregulation 10(2)**

Insert:

Advice about certain nationally-significant emergencies

**4 Before subregulation 10(3)**

Insert:

Advice about whether emergency should be declared nationally‑significant emergency

**5 After subregulation 10(4)**

Insert:

Advice about whether to amend nationally‑significant emergency declaration

(4A) A function of the Committee is to advise the Minister about whether to amend a declaration of an emergency to be a nationally‑significant emergency under subregulation 3(5).

(4B) If the Committee advises the Minister that a declaration should be amended under subregulation 3(5), or the Minister advises the Committee that the Minister will recommend to the Governor‑General that a declaration be so amended, a function of the Committee is to advise the Minister, where relevant, of the matters mentioned in paragraphs (4)(a) to (f) of this regulation in relation to the nationally‑significant emergency.

Advice about whether service requirements for sustained service may be waived

**6 Before subregulation 10(6)**

Insert:

Recommendation about significant service

**7 Paragraph 11(1)(a)**

Omit “2 members”, substitute “3 members”.

8 Application provisions

(1) The amendment of paragraph 3(3)(b) of the *National Emergency Medal Regulations 2011* made by this Schedule applies in relation to a declaration made on or after the commencement of this item, whether the emergency to which the declaration relates occurred before, on or after that commencement.

(2) Subregulations 3(5) and (6) and 10(4A) and (4B) of the *National Emergency Medal Regulations 2011*, as inserted by this Schedule, apply in relation to a variation made on or after the commencement of this item, whether the declaration to which the variation relates was made before, on or after that commencement.

(3) The amendment of regulation 11 of the *National Emergency Medal Regulations 2011* made by this Schedule applies in relation to the constitution of the Committee on and after the commencement of this item.

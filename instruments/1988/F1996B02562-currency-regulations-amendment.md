# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1988 No. 311**

**Currency Regulations2 (Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulation under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 3 March 1988.

N. M. STEPHEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

Paul Keating

Treasurer

**Designs of coins**

Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended:

(a) by omitting from subparagraph (d) (viiiF) “FINE” and substituting “9999”;

(b) by omitting from subparagraph (d) (viiiG) “FINE” and substituting “9999”;

(c) by omitting from subparagraph (d) (viiiH) “FINE” and substituting “9999”; and

(d) by omitting from subparagraph (d) (viiiJ) “FINE” and substituting “9999”.

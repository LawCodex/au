# World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983 - Proclamation under section 9 - Wet Tropical Rainforests of North-East Australia (19/01/1988)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>N. M. STEPHEN<br>Governor-General | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of<br>the Commonwealth of<br>Australia |
|---|---|

I, SIR NINIAN MARTIN STEPHEN, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and pursuant to subsection 6 (3) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983*, being satisfied that the property described in the Schedule is likely to be damaged, hereby declare that property to be property to which section 9 of that Act applies.

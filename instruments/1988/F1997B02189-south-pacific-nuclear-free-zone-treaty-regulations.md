# South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty Regulations

**enable**[image: Commonwealth Coat of Arms of Australia]

**Statutory Rules 1988 No. 3531**

**South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty**

**Regulations**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty Act 1986.*

15 December 1988

N. M. STEPHEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

Gareth Evans

Minister of State for Foreign Affairs and Trade

**Citation**

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty Regulations.

**Privileges and immunities of Treaty inspectors**

**2.** (**1**) The following privileges and immunities are conferred on a Treaty inspector in respect of acts done and words spoken and written by that inspector in the exercise of a power conferred by section 22 of the *South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty Act 1986*:

(a) immunity from arrest or detention;

(b) immunity from legal process;

(c) inviolability for all papers and documents.

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 21 December 1988.

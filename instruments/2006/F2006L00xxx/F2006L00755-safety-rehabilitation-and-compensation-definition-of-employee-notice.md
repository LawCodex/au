# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee) Notice 2006 (1)

**Notice No. 1 of 2006.**

I, KEVIN JAMES ANDREWS, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 7th March 2006

Kevin Andrews

KEVIN ANDREWS

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee) Notice 2006 (1)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on 11 March 2006.

###### 3 Declaration

For subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act* 1988, I declare that the class of persons mentioned in Column 2 of the Schedule, when on secondment to the organisation mentioned in Column 3 of the Schedule, are employees of that organisation for the purposes of the acts mentioned in Column 3 of the Schedule.

*Note* The persons are engaged in activities or perform acts at the request or direction, or for the benefit, of a Commonwealth authority.

## Schedule Employees

| Item | Class of persons | Class of Acts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sample Collection Officers, including Lead Drug Control Officers, Drug Control Officers, Chaperone Coordinators and Chaperones. | Acts performed at the request or direction of the Australian Sports Drug Agency, under the *Australian Sports Drug Agency Act 1990,* during the Melbourne 2006 Commonwealth Games. |
| 2 | Sample Collection Officers, including Lead Drug Control Officers, Drug Control Officers, Chaperone Coordinators and Chaperones. | Acts performed at the request or direction of the Australian Sports Anti-Doping Authority, under the *Australian Sports Anti-Doping Authority Act 2006,* during the Melbourne 2006 Commonwealth Games. |

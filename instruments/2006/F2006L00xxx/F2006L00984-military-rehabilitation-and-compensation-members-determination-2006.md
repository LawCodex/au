# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2006

[image]**Military Rehabilitation and Compensation**

**(Members) Determination 2006**

Dated 17 March 2006

BRUCE BILLSON

Minister for Veterans' Affairs

for Minister for Defence

**1**

**Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation*

*(Members) Determination 2006.*

I, BRUCE BILLSON, Minister for Veterans' Affairs, for the Minister for Defence, make this Determination under subsection 8(1) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

**2 Commencement and application**

(1) This Determination applies from 1 February 2006 until 30 April 2006.

(2) For paragraph 8(2)(a) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation*

*Act 2004,* this Determination applies from 1 February 2006 until 30 April

2006\.

**3 Person taken to be member**

For subsection 8(1) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004,* the person specified in Schedule 1 who performs the acts, or engages

in activities, specified in that Schedule is taken to be a member for the purposes of that Act.

[image]**Schedule 1 Person and activities or acts**

| **Item** | **person** | **Acts/activities** |
|---|---|---|

1 Mr Robert Lambert Nugent Acts performed, as war photographer at the

request or direction of the Defence Force.

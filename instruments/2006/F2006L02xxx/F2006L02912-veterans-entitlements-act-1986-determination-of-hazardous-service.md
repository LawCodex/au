# Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 - Determination of Hazardous Service - former Republic of Yugoslavia (19/07/2006)

## Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986

**Determination of Hazardous Service**

I, Bruce Billson, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, for the Minister for Defence:

revoke the Determination made under paragraph 120(7)(b) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* (the Act) on 16 December 1996 in relation to service in the former Republic of Yugoslavia on or after 24 January 1997; and

determine that:

(i). service rendered as a member of the Australian Defence Force while on exchange with the forces of other countries on or after 24 January 1997 in the specified area, is hazardous service for the purposes of the definition of “hazardous service” in paragraph 120(7) of the Act, and

(ii). the specified area comprises the former Republic of Yugoslavia.

c. This Determination is taken to have commenced on 24 January 1997.

Dated 19 July 2006

BRUCE BILLSON

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

for the Minister for Defence

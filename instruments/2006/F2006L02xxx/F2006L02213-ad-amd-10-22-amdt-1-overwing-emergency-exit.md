# AD/AMD 10/22 Amdt 1 - Overwing Emergency Exit

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/AMD 10/22 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

| AD/AMD 10/22 Amdt 1 | Overwing Emergency Exit | 8/2006 |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | All Falcon 10 aircraft for which the Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) Airworthiness Limitation Section, Chapter 5-40, is not at Revision 08 or above. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | Insert a copy of EASA AD 2006-0147 into Chapter 5-40 of the Aircraft Maintenance Manual.<br>Note: Updating Chapter 5-40 with Revision 08 terminates the requirement of this Directive, upon which the copy of this Directive may then be removed from the AMM. |
| Compliance: | Within 30 days after 3 August 2006. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 3 August 2006. |
| Background: | During an attempted operation of a Falcon 50 emergency exit, interference was reported between interior trim and the exit.<br>Amendment 1 is issued in response to a new EASA AD, which supersedes DGAC AD 1999-211-023(B) (the original issue of this Directive), and extends the repetitive overwing emergency exit opening test interval to 24 months. |

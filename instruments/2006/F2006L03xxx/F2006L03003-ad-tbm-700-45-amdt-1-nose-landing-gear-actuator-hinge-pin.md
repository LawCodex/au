# AD/TBM 700/45 Amdt 1 - Nose Landing Gear Actuator Hinge Pin

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/TBM 700/45 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 8 September 2006. |
| Background: | This AD results from one report of a Nose Landing Gear (NLG) hinge pin rupture that caused an uncommanded NLG retraction.<br>Investigations identified the unsafe condition as that resulting from an incomplete thermal treatment done on three hinge pin batches lowering their mechanical properties with a high risk of deformation under service loads.<br>These AD requirements are first to identify the concerned NLG and second to detect the defective hinge pins on aircraft and subsequently carry out replacement of those pins prior to pin rupture.<br>This amendment is issued to extend the landing gear applicability as listed in the original issue of this AD. |

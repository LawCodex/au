# Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979 - Declaration of eligible authority as agency - Northern Territory Police

Declaration of eligible authority as agency — Northern Territory Police

*Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*

I, PHILIP MAXWELL RUDDOCK, Attorney‑General:

(a) acting under section 34 of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979* (the ***Act***) and at the request of the Chief Minister of the Northern Territory; and

(b) being satisfied that:

(i) the law of the Northern Territory makes satisfactory provision in relation to the matters mentioned in subsection 35 (1) of the Act; and

(ii) the Northern Territory has entered into an agreement as required by subsection 35 (2) of the Act;

declare the Northern Territory Police to be an agency for the purposes of the Act.

Dated 24 October 2006

PHILIP RUDDOCK

Attorney‑General

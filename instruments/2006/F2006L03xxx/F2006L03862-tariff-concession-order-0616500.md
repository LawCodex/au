# Tariff Concession Order 0616500

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.09.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7608.20.00 | TUBING, ALUMINIUM ALLOY, round AND/OR square, having ALL of the<br>following:<br>(a) tensile strength NOT greater than 230 mPa;<br>(b) outer diameter NOT greater than 16 mm;<br>(c) wall thickness NOT less than 0.8 mm and NOT greater than<br>1.0 mm;<br>(d) length NOT less than 6 500 mm and NOT greater than 7 500<br>mm;<br>(e) weight per metre NOT less than 0.09 kg and NOT greater<br>than 0.18 kg<br>Op. 01.09.06 - TC 0616500 | 50 |

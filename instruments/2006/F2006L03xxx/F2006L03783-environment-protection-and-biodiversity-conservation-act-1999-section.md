# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Instrument repealing and adopting Recovery Plans (10/11/2006) (TAS)

Commonwealth of Australia

Instrument repealing and adopting Recovery Plans

I, IAN GORDON CAMPBELL, Minister for the Environment and Heritage:

1\. Pursuant to subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901,* hereby repeal the Recovery Plans for

**** Forty-Spotted Pardalote (*Pardalotus quadragintus)*;

Which are currently adopted under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*; and

2\. Under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as recovery plans for the listed threatened species specified below, made by the Tasmanian agency specified opposite those listed threatened species:

The repeal of the Recovery Plan for:

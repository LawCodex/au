# Tariff Concession Order 0607692

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Conor Annesley, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.05.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 5607.49.00 | ROPE AND/OR TWINE, POLYETHYLENE FIBRE, having ALL of the<br>following:<br>(a) 12 strands;<br>(b) braided;<br>(c) breaking strength NOT less than 130 kg and NOT<br>greater than 480 t;<br>(d) diameter being EITHER of the following:<br>(i) less than 3 mm; or<br>(ii) greater than 16 mm and NOT greater than 85 mm<br>Op. 01.05.06 - TC 0607692 | 50 |

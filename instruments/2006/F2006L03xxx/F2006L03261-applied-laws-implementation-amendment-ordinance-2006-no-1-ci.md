# Applied Laws (Implementation) Amendment Ordinance 2006 (No. 1) (CI)

**Territory of Christmas Island**

Applied Laws (Implementation) Amendment Ordinance 2006 (No. 1)1

I, PHILIP MICHAEL JEFFERY, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Ordinance under the *Christmas Island Act 1958*.

Dated 4 October 2006

P. M. JEFFERY

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

JAMES ERIC LLOYD

Minister for Local Government, Territories and Roads

**An Ordinance to amend the** ***Applied Laws (Implementation) Ordinance 1992*** **and for related purposes**

1 Name of Ordinance

This Ordinance is the *Applied Laws (Implementation) Amendment Ordinance 2006 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Ordinance commences on the day after it is registered.

3 Amendment of *Applied Laws (Implementation) Ordinance 1992*

Schedule 1 amends the *Applied Laws (Implementation) Ordinance 1992*.

4 Adoption of *Motor Vehicle Dealers Act 1973* (WA)(CI) as a law of the Territory

The *Motor Vehicle Dealers Act 1973* (WA)(CI) is adopted as a law of the Territory.

5 Transitional

A licence that was in force under section 15 of the *Motor Vehicle Dealers Act 1973* (WA)(CI) immediately before the commencement of this Ordinance, remains in force until the end of the period stated in the licence, as if this Ordinance had not been made.

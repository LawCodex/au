# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2006 (4)

**Notice No. 4 of 2006**

I, KEVIN JAMES ANDREWS, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 31st October 2006

Kevin Andrews

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2006 (4).*

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on registration on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (***the Act***), being satisfied that it would be desirable for the Act to apply to the employees of each of the corporations referred to in Schedule 1, I declare that each of the corporations is eligible to be granted a licence under Part VIII of the Act.

*Note* Each corporation is carrying on business in competition with a Commonwealth authority or with another corporation that was previously a Commonwealth authority.

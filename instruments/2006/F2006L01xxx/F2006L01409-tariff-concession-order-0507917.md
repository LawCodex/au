# Tariff Concession Order 0507917

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Jeannette Walsh, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.01.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 5407.20.00 | FABRIC, POLYPROPYLENE AND/OR POLYETHYLENE, not being knitted or<br>coated, comprising ALL of the following:<br>(a) width NOT less than 0.5 m and NOT greater than 5.0 m;<br>(b) weft tape width NOT less than 3.0 mm and NOT greater than<br>5.0 mm;<br>(c) warp tape width NOT less than 2.5 mm and NOT greater than<br>4.5 mm<br>(d) heat sealed selvedge NOT less than 5 mm and NOT greater<br>than 15 mm;<br>(e) selvedge edge warp tape width NOT less than 1.0 mm and<br>NOT greater than 3.0 mm for NOT less than 5 cm and NOT<br>greater than 20 cm on both edges of the fabric<br>Op. 01.01.06 - TC 0507917 | 50 |

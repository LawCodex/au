# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2006-2007 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2006

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2006–2007 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2006

**Select Legislative Instrument 2006 No.** 1

I, nicholas hugh minchin, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 19 JUNE 2006

Nick Minchin

Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2006–2007 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2006*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2006, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $2,710.77 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $2,710.77 | $243.97 |

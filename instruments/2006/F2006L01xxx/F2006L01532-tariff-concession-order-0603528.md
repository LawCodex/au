# Tariff Concession Order 0603528

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 07.02.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 7606.12.00 | SHEETS AND/OR COILS, ALUMINIUM ALLOY, BRIGHT COLD ROLLED,<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) thickness greater than 0.2 mm but NOT greater than 6 mm;<br>(b) width NOT less than 900 mm and NOT greater than 2 000 mm;<br>(c) bright finish gloss level NOT less than 320 Gloss Units<br>complying with DIN 67530;<br>(d) surface finish roughness NOT greater than 0.06 microns Ra<br>(roughness average);<br>(e) coated one side with polyethylene film<br>Op. 07.02.06 - TC 0603528 | 50 |

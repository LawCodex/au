# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Approved Funds) Declaration 2006

I, NICHOLAS HUGH MINCHIN, Minister for Finance and Administration, make this Declaration under subsection 4C (1) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 19 June 2006

Nick Minchin

Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Approved Funds) Declaration 2006*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 July 2006.

###### 3 Definition

In this Declaration:

***Act*** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

###### 4 Revocation

All previous declarations made under subsection 4C (1) of the Act are revoked.

###### 5 Approved funds

For subsection 4C (1) of the Act, each superannuation fund mentioned in Schedule 1 is declared to be an approved fund.

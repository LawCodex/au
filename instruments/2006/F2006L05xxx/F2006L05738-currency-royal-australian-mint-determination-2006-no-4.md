# Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2006 (No. 4)

*Currency Act 1965*

I, CHRIS PEARCE, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 11 December 2006

CHRIS PEARCE

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2006 (No. 4)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Definition

In this Determination:

***Act*** means the *Currency Act 1965*.

###### 4 Specification of coin

For subsection 13A (1) of the Act, item 1 of Schedule 1 specifies, for a coin having the denomination and standard composition specified in the item:

(a) the coin’s standard weight, and the allowable variation from that weight; and

(b) the coin’s dimensions; and

(c) the coin’s design.

###### 5 Application of Determination

The specification in Schedule 1 does not affect any other determination of the specification of a coin.

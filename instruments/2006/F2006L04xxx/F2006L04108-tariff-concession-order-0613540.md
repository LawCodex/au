# Tariff Concession Order 0613540

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 15.08.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8481.40.00 | VALVES, STEAM, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) inlet size NOT less than 3 cm (1 1/4 inches) and NOT<br>greater than 16 cm (6 inches);<br>(b) inlet pressure rating complying with American National<br>Standards Institute Class 1500 OR 2500 OR 3000 OR 4500;<br>(c) setting and testing in compliance with American Society<br>of Mechanical Engineers Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code<br>Sections I;<br>(d) operating temperature NOT less than minus 30 degrees<br>Celsius and NOT greater than 610 degrees Celsius;<br>(e) flanged OR welded connections;<br>(f) open bonnet construction with exposed spring and ifting<br>lever<br>Op. 15.08.06 - TC 0613540 | 50 |

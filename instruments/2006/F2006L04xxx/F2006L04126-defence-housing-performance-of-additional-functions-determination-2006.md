# Defence Housing (Performance of Additional Functions) Determination 2006

*Defence Housing Australia Act 1987*

I, BRENDAN NELSON, Minister for Defence, make this Determination under subsection 6 (4) of the *Defence Housing Australia Act 1987*.

Dated 9 November 2006

BRENDAN NELSON

Minister for Defence

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Defence Housing (Performance of Additional Functions) Determination 2006*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day on which Schedule 1 to the *Defence Housing Authority Amendment Act 2006* commences.

###### 3 Additional functions

For subsection 6 (4) of the *Defence Housing Australia Act 1987*, Defence Housing Australia may provide adequate and suitable housing for, and housing‑related services to:

(a) officers and employees of an Agency (within the meaning of the *Financial Management and Accountability Act 1997*) other than the Department, and their families; and

(b) persons contracted to provide goods or services to that Agency, and their families;

in order to meet the requirements of that Agency, and to the extent necessary to carry out this function adequately.

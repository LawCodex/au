# Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Regulation 2016

I, General the Honourable Sir Peter Cosgrove AK MC (Ret’d), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulation.

Dated 28 September 2016

Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Nigel Scullion

Minister for Indigenous Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This is the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Regulation 2016*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986.*

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986*.

***By‑Laws*** means the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) By‑Laws 2016*.

Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

Michael Keenan

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *International Transfer of Prisoners Act 1997.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *International Transfer of Prisoners Act 1997*.

###### 5 Peru is a transfer country to which the Act applies

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act:

(a) the Act applies to Peru; and

(b) Peru is declared to be a transfer country.

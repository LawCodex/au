# National Disability Insurance Scheme (Host Jurisdiction) Specification 2016

*National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013*

I, CHRISTIAN PORTER, Minister for Social Services, acting under section 10 of the *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013*, make this specification.

Dated: 16 February 2016

Christian Porter\
Minister for Social Services

**1 Name of specification**

This specification is the *National Disability Insurance Scheme (Host Jurisdiction) Specification 2016*.

**2 Commencement**

This specification commences on the day after registration.

**3 Revocation of previous instrument**

*National Disability Insurance Scheme (Host Jurisdiction) Specification 2013* – F2013L00803, registered on 20 May 2015 is revoked.

Note: paragraph 7(2)(b) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, as applied because of section 13 of the *Legislative Instruments Act 2003*, ensures that the previous operation of the *National Disability Insurance Scheme (Host Jurisdiction) Specification 2013* is not affected by its revocation under this provision.

**4 Host jurisdiction**

For the purposes of section 10 of the *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013,* each State and Territory mentioned in Schedule 1 is specified.

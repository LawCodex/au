# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument (No. 5) 2016

**Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument (No.5) 2016**

*Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*

I, Michelle Crocker, being the person occupying the position of GEMS Regulator, make this instrument under subsections 37(1) and (2) of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012.*

Dated 16 September 2016

MICHELLE CROCKER

\______________________________

Michelle Crocker

GEMS Regulator

###### 1 Name of Instrument

This Instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument (No.5) 2016*.

###### 2 Authority

This Instrument is made under subsections 37(1) and (2) of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

###### 3 Commencement

This Instrument commences the day after it is registered.

###### 4 Definitions

In this Instrument:

***Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

***Determination*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Air Conditioners and Heat Pumps) Determination 2013.*

***Exempt Models***: see section 5.

*Note:* Several other words and expressions used in this instrument have the meaning given by section 5 of the Act. For example:

GEMS

GEMS determination

GEMS labelling requirements

GEMS level requirements

model

model identifier

product classes

supply

###### 5 Exemption

The models specified in clause 1 of the Schedule (the ***Exempt Models***) are exempt from the requirements of the Determination as described at clause 2 of the Schedule with respect to the supplies of the exempt model specified at clause 3 of the Schedule.

###### 6 Conditions of exemption

The conditions at clause 4 of the Schedule are specified for the purposes of subsection 37(2) of the Act.

*Note:* Subsection 37(2) of the Act provides that the exemption may specify conditions to which the exemption is subject. Subsection 37(3) provides that a person must comply with a condition of an exemption to the extent that it applies to the person.

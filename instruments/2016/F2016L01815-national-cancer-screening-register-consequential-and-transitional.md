###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *National Cancer Screening Register (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2016*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Cancer Screening Register (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2016*.

###### 5 Prescribed registers or databases

Each of the registers or databases that contain information about individuals in connection with screening associated with cervical cancer mentioned in column 2, which exist under legislation of the State or Territory mentioned in column 1, is a prescribed register or database for the purposes of subitem 5(3) of Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Act:

|---|---|
| Australian Capital Territory | Cervical Cytology Register |
| New South Wales | Pap Test Register |
| Northern Territory | NT Cervical Screening Register |
| Queensland | Queensland Health Pap Smear Register |
| South Australia | South Australian Cervical Screening Register |
| Tasmania | Cervical Screening Register |
| Victoria | Cervical Register maintained by the Victorian Cytology Service Ltd |

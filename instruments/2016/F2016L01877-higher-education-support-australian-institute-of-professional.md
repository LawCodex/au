# Higher Education Support (Australian Institute of Professional Education) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2016

I, Katerina Lawler, delegate of the Minister for Education and Training, make this instrument under clauses 22-15 and 22-20 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 30 November 2016

Katerina Lawler

A/g Branch Manager

Funding Policy and Legislation Branch, Higher Education Group

Department of Education and Training\
Delegate of the Minister for Education and Training

###### 1 Name

This is the *Higher Education Support (Australian Institute of Professional Education) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2016*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under clauses 22-15 and 22-20 of the\
*Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

***body*** means Australian Institute of Professional Education ACN 126 628 215.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval As A Higher Education Provider

The body’s approval as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with clause 22-15 of the Act.

The *Higher Education Support Act 2003 – Higher Education Provider Approval* (No*.* 3 of 2014, F2014 L00442) is revoked.

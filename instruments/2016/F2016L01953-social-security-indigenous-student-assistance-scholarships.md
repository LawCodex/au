###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 23(24) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***disqualifying accommodation scholarship*** has the same meaning as in the Act.

***disqualifying education costs scholarship*** has the same meaning as in the Act.

###### 5 Disqualifying accommodation scholarship

For the purposes of subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition of *disqualifying accommodation scholarship* in subsection 23(1) of the Act, the Indigenous Commonwealth Accommodation Scholarship is specified as a disqualifying accommodation scholarship.

###### 6 Disqualifying education costs scholarship

For the purposes of subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition of *disqualifying education costs scholarship* in subsection 23(1) of the Act, the Indigenous Commonwealth Education Costs Scholarship is specified as a disqualifying education costs scholarship.

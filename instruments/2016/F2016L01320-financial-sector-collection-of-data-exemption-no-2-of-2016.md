# Financial Sector (Collection of Data) exemption No. 2 of 2016

Reporting Standard SRS 703.0 Fees Disclosed

Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001

I, Steve Davies, delegate of APRA, under paragraph 16(1B)(b) of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001* (the Act), EXEMPT those financial sector entities to which SRS 703.0 applies from the requirement specified in the Schedule attached to this instrument.

This instrument commences upon registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Dated: 23 August 2016

[Signed]

Steve Davies

General Manager

Statistics Division

**Interpretation**

In this Determination:

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***financial sector entity*** has the meaning given by section 5 of the Act.

***SRS 703.0*** means *Reporting Standard SRS 703.0 Fees Disclosed* determined by APRA on 5 December 2013 by Financial Sector (Collection of Data) (reporting standard) determination No. 100 of 2013.

Schedule

The requirement in paragraph 7(b) of SRS 703.0 to provide the information required by SRS 703.0 in respect of 30 June 2016.

*Note:* The reporting forms and the associated instruction guides form part of the reporting standards.

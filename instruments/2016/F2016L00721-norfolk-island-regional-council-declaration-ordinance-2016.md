###### 3 Authority

This Ordinance is made under section 19A of the *Norfolk Island Act 1979*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this Ordinance:

***LGA*** means the *Local Government Act 1993* (NSW) as in force in the Territory.

Note: The *Local Government Act 1993* (NSW) is in force in the Territory under section 18A of the *Norfolk Island Act 1979* and is amended by the *Norfolk Island Applied Laws Ordinance 2016*.

###### 5 Interpretation Act

The *Interpretation Act 1979* (Norfolk Island) does not apply to this Ordinance.

Note: The *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* applies instead because this Ordinance is a legislative instrument (see subsection 13(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003*).

### Part 2—Council and councillors

###### 6 Norfolk Island Regional Council

For the purposes of the definition of ***Norfolk Island Regional Council*** in subsection 4(1) of the Act, the council constituted by section 219 of the LGA for the area constituted by section 204A of the LGA is declared to be the Norfolk Island Regional Council.

###### 7 Councillors

(1) At the final transition time, the persons elected in the election provided for by the *Norfolk Island Regional Council Preparatory Election Ordinance 2016* become councillors of the Norfolk Island Regional Council.

(2) After the final transition time, the LGA applies in relation to the persons mentioned in subsection (1) as if they had been elected to civic office in relation to the Norfolk Island Regional Council under the LGA at the final transition time.

Note: For example, a civic office becomes vacant in circumstances set out in section 234 of the LGA.

# Specification of Arrangements for Maritime Crew Visa Applications 2016/115

[image]\
**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Migration Regulations 1994*

## ARRANGEMENTS FOR MARITIME CREW VISA APPLICATIONS 2016/115

*(Item 1227)*

I, *PETER DUTTON,* Minister for Immigration and Border Protection, acting under\
subregulations 2.07(5) and 2.07(6) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations) for Item 1227 of Schedule 1 to the Regulations:

REVOKE Instrument IMMI 15/126 (F2015L01765), Arrangements for Maritime Crew and Superyacht Applications 2015, signed 2 November 2015; and

SPECIFY for the purposes of subitem 1227(1) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, the approved forms listed in Column 3 in the Schedule to this Instrument;

SPECIFY for the purposes of paragraph 1227(3)(a) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, the place and manner for making an application listed in Column 4 in the Schedule to this Instrument.

This Instrument, Arrangements for Maritime Crew Visa Applications 2016/115, IMMI 16/115 commences immediately after the commencement of the *Migration Amendment (Temporary Activity Visas) Regulation 2016*.

Dated: 16 November 2016

Peter Dutton

THE HON PETER DUTTON MP

Minister for Immigration and Border Protection

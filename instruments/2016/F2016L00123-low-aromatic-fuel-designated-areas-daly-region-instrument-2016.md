**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 14(1) of the *Low Aromatic Fuel Act 2013*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Low Aromatic Fuel Act 2013.*

###### 5 Designated low aromatic fuel areas

(1) For subsection 14(1) of the Act, the areas described in Schedules 1, 2, 3 and 4 are designated as low aromatic fuel areas.

*All geographic coordinates are expressed in terms of the Geocentric Datum of Australia 1994 (GDA94) as described in the Commonwealth of Australia Gazette*

*GN35 of 6 September 1995, which can be considered equivalent to World Geodetic*

*System 1984 (WGS84) for this instrument.*

# Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2016—Nice

*Social Security Act 1991*

I, MALCOLM BLIGH TURNBULL, Prime Minister, make this declaration under subsection 35B(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

Dated 30 September 2016

MALCOLM BLIGH TURNBULL

Prime Minister

###### 1 Name of declaration

This declaration is the *Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2016—Nice.*

###### 2 Commencement

This declaration commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Definitions

In this declaration:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***terrorist act*** has the same meaning as in subsection 100.1(1) of the *Criminal Code.*

###### 4 Declared overseas terrorist act

For the purposes of subsection 35B(1) of the Act, the following terrorist act is a declared overseas terrorist act:

1\) the attack shortly after 10.30pm (local time) on the Promenade des Anglais, Nice, France, on 14 July 2016 between the following locations:

the corner of Rue Lenval and the Promenade des Anglais, 06200, Nice, and

Casino Du Palais De La Mèditerranèe, 13 Promenade des Anglais, 06000, Nice.

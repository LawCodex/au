**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Government compensation payment*** means a payment made by The Netherlands Government to people who were civil servants or military personnel who were in the service of the Dutch East Indies Government during the Japanese occupation of the Dutch East Indies during World War 2.

**5 Exempt Lump Sums**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the amount of a Government compensation payment is an exempt lump sum.

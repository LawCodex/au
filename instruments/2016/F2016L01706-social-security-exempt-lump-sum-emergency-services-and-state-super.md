**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***ESSSuper*** means Emergency Services and State Super.

***Centrelink pensioners*** means a group of pensioners in receipt of a Centrelink pension payment who were impacted by an incorrect deductible amount calculated by ESSSuper during changes to taxation laws effective from 1 July 2007 and reported to Centrelink by ESSSuper .

***ESSSuper compensation payment*** means a lump sum payment made to Centrelink pensioners by ESSSuper to compensate them for the impact of the incorrect deductible amount being reported to Centrelink.

**5 Exempt Lump Sums**

#### For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act an ESSSuper compensation payment is an exempt lump sum.

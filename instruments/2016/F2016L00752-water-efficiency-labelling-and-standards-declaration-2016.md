# Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Declaration 2016

*Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005*

I, Barnaby Joyce, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Agriculture and Water Resources, make this Declaration under paragraph 12 (1) (b) of the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005*.

Dated 2 May 2016

Barnaby Joyce

Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Agriculture and Water Resources

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Declaration 2016* (the Declaration).

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Revocation

This Declaration revokes the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Declaration 2005*.

###### 4 Laws to be corresponding State‑Territory laws

For paragraph 12 (1) (b) of the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005*, the following State and Territory laws correspond to the Act:

*(a) Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (New South Wales) Act 2005* (NSW);

(b) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005* (Vic);

(c) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Queensland) Act 2005* (Qld);

(d) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2006* (WA);

(e) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (South Australia) Act 2013* (SA);

(f) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2013* (Tas);

(g) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (ACT) Act 2015* (ACT);

(h) *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (National Uniform Legislation) Act 2014* (NT).

# Veterans' Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum – The Netherlands Government Compensation Payments) Determination 2016

*Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*

**Veterans’ Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum – The Netherlands Government Compensation Payments) Determination 2016**

Instrument 2016 No. R61

I, MARK HARRIGAN, delegate of the Repatriation Commission, make the following determination for paragraph 5H(12)(c) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

Dated this 2nd day of December 2016

M Harrigan

…………………………………………………………

MARK HARRIGAN

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum – The Netherlands Government Compensation Payments) Determination 2016.*

**2 Commencement**

This instrument is taken to have commenced on 1 March 2016.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 5H(12)(c) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* (VEA).

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***“income support supplement”*** means the payment called the income support supplement payable under Part IIIA of the VEA;

***“service pension”*** has the meaning given to *service pension* in subsection 5Q(1) of the VEA.

***“Netherlands compensation payment”*** means a payment made by the Netherlands Government to people who were civil servants or military personnel and who were in the service of the Dutch East Indies Government during the Japanese occupation of the Dutch East Indies during World

War 2.

**5 Exempt lump sum**

If a person or a person’s partner receives a *Netherlands compensation payment* and the person is eligible for, or in receipt of a *service pension* or *income support supplement,* then the amount of the *Netherlands compensation payment* received by the person or the person’s partner is an exempt lump sum.

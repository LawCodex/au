###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 202(2DA) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 5 Protection of personal information

(1) The following scholarships are specified for the purposes of subparagraph 202(1)(da)(ii) of the Act:

(a) the Indigenous Commonwealth Accommodation Scholarship; and

(b) the Indigenous Commonwealth Education Costs Scholarship.

(2) The following scholarships are specified for the purposes of subparagraph 202(2)(daaa)(ii) of the Act:

(a) the Indigenous Commonwealth Accommodation Scholarship; and

(b) the Indigenous Commonwealth Education Costs Scholarship.

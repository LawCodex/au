# CASA 242/02 - Approval under subregulation 207 (2)

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 242/02

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS**

**APPROVAL UNDER SUBREGULATION 207 (2)**

I, MALCOLM JOHN MUNRO WALKER, Acting General Manager, Flight Crew Licensing and Operational Standards, CASA, a delegate of CASA, under subregulation 207 (2) of the *Civil Aviation Regulations 1988* approve the operation of Eurocopter AS 332L helicopters by Lloyd Helicopters Pty Ltd, trading as CHC Helicopters (Australia), Aviation Reference Number 222688:

(a) with an approved flight data recorder system that retains its last 5 hours of recording; and

(b) with an approved cockpit voice recorder system that retains its last 30 minutes of recording and complies with the requirements of subsection 2 of section 103.20 of the Civil Aviation Orders except for recording simultaneously on only 3 channels.

This approval is subject to both recorder systems being operated in accordance with paragraphs 6.3, 6.4 and 6.6 of section 20.18 of the Civil Aviation Orders.

**[Signed Malcolm Walker]**

Malcolm J M Walker

Acting General Manager

Flight Crew Licensing and Operational Standards

12 April 2002

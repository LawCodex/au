# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2002-2003) Declaration

Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution\
(2002–2003) Declaration 2002

Statutory Rules 2002 No. 1311

I, SANDRA KAY WILSON, Branch Manager of the Superannuation Branch, Department of Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 6 June 2002

SANDRA WILSON

Delegate of the Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2002–2003) Declaration 2002.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Productivity contributions

For section 110D of the S*uperannuation Act 1976*, the table in subsection 110C (1) of that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 2002, as if the amounts set out in the following table were substituted for the amounts set out in that table.

Table

| Fortnightly rate of salary | Fortnightly productivity contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $1 278.00 | $38.34 |
| $1 278.00 or more, but less than $2 060.00 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $2 060.00 or more, but less than $3 090.00 | $61.80 |
| $3 090.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**Note**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 27 June 2002.

# Superannuation (CSS) (Surcharge Commutation Amount - Conversion Factors) Determination No. 1

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

**SUPERANNUATION ACT 1976**

**DETERMINATION**

The CSS Board, pursuant to subsections 146D(1) and (2) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* and in accordance with advice received from an actuary, DETERMINES as follows:

**Citation**

1\. This determination may be cited as the "Superannuation (CSS) (Surcharge Commutation Amount - Conversion Factors) Determination No. 1".

**Commencement**

2\. Pursuant to Item 9 of Schedule 2 to the *Superannuation Legislation Amendment*

*(Post-retirement Commutations) Act 2001*, this determination shall be deemed to have taken effect on 22 August 2001.

**Purpose**

3\. The purpose of this determination is to determine, in accordance with advice from an actuary, conversion factors that are applicable for the purpose of working out, in relation to a person, the yearly amount that would have to be paid to discharge a liability equal to the person’s surcharge commutation amount.

**Interpretation**

4.(1) In this determination, "Act" means the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

(2) Words and expressions defined in the Act have the same meaning in this determination.

**Schedule**

*Conversion factors in relation to indexed pensions and deferred benefits payable as indexed pensions*

5\. The conversion factors for the purposes of subsection 146D(1) of the Act are set out in Table 1 in the Schedule to this determination.

*Conversion factors in relation to non-indexed pensions and deferred benefits payable as*

*non-indexed pensions*

6\. The conversion factors for the purposes of subsection 146D(2) of the Act are set out in Table 2 in the Schedule to this determination.

# Determination that a distinct population of biological entities is a species for the purposes of the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (132) (27/04/2012)

Commonwealth of Australia

**Determination that a distinct population of biological entities is a species for the purposes of the** ***Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*** **(Cth) (132)**

I, TONY BURKE, Minister for Sustainability, Environment, Water, Population and Communities, pursuant to section 517 of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999* (Cth), hereby determine that the following distinct population of biological entities is a species for the purposes of that Act:

*Phascolarctos cinereus* (combined populations of Queensland, New South Wales and the Australian Capital Territory)

Dated this 27th day of April 2012

TONY BURKE

Minister for Sustainability, Environment, Water, Population and Communities

# Currency Legislation (Royal Australian Mint) Amendment Determination 2012 (No. 1)

*Currency Act 1965*

I, BERNIE RIPOLL, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 20 August 2012

BERNIE RIPOLL

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency Legislation (Royal Australian Mint) Amendment Determination 2012 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Determination is taken to have commenced as follows:

(a) on 13 August 2011 — sections 1 to 3 and Schedule 1;

(b) on 31 January 2012 — section 4 and Schedule 2.

3 Amendment of *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2011 (No. 3)*

Schedule 1 amends the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2011 (No. 3).*

4 Amendment of *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2012 (No. 1)*

Schedule 2 amends the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2012 (No. 1).*

# Tariff Concession Order 1135770

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 13.10.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8502.11.00 | GENERATORS, WELDING, AC AND/OR DC, with OR without compressor,<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) AC power output NOT greater than 13 500 W;<br>(b) weight NOT less than 286 kg and NOT greater than 990 kg;<br>(c) weld output NOT greater than 800 A<br>Op. 13.10.11 - TC 1135770 | 50 |

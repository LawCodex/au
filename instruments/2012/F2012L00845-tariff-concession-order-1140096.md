# Tariff Concession Order 1140096

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.12.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 6306.22.00 | SHELTERS OR TENTS, BEACH, clamshell shaped, free standing,<br>polyester, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) sewn in floor;<br>(b) mesh pockets;<br>(c) carry case;<br>(d) fibreglass poles;<br>(e) width NOT greater than 2.7 m;<br>(f) depth NOT greater than 1.4 m<br>Op. 01.12.11 - TC 1140096 | 50 |

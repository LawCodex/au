# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2012-2013) Declaration 2012

Superannuation (CSS)

Productivity Contribution

(2012-2013) Declaration 2012

I, STEPHEN PAUL WHITTON, Director, Australian Public Sector Payments and Entitlements, Position No. 44, and Delegate of the Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 12 June 2012

S WHITTON\
Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2012-2013) Declaration 2012*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration shall take effect from and including 1 July 2012.

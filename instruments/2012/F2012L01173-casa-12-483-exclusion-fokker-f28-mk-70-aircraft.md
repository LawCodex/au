# CASA 12/483 - Exclusion - Fokker F28 Mk 70 aircraft

Instrument Number: CASA 12/483

I, MIKE HIGGINS, Manager Continuing Airworthiness, a delegate of CASA, make this instrument under subparagraph 39.004(3)(b) of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

Mike Higgins

Manager, Continuing Airworthiness

Airworthiness and Engineering Branch

7 June 2012

Exclusion - Fokker F28 Mk 70 aircraft

1 Commencement

This instrument commences on 13 June 2012.

2 Application

This instrument applies to Fokker F28 Mk 70 aircraft (the **aircraft**) in relation to CASA airworthiness directives (ADs).

3 Exclusion

The aircraft is excluded from the operation of any of the *ADs* mentioned in schedule 1 current at the time of this instrument and as amended from time to time if it complies with State of Design Airworthiness directives issued for the aircraft.

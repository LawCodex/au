# Higher Education Provider Approval No. 1 of 2012

***NOTICE OF APPROVAL***

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Higher Education Support Act 2003***

**Higher Education Provider Approval No 1 of 2012**

**Approval under section 16-25, decision under paragraph 16-50(1)(a), and Notice of Approval under paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the** ***Higher Education Support Act 2003*****.**

I, Vanessa Graham, a delegate of the Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Science and Research for the purposes of sections 16-25 and 16-50 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003* (HESA):

(a) approve the application of Navitas Bundoora Pty Ltd ACN: 143 736 789 ABN: 92 143 736 789 as a Higher Education Provider in accordance with section 16-25 of HESA; and

(b) note that this approval decides the application of Navitas Bundoora Pty Ltd as required by paragraph 16-50(1)(a) of HESA; and

(c) hereby give Navitas Bundoora Pty Ltd written notice of my approval as required by paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of HESA.

In accordance with paragraph 16-55(2) of HESA this Notice of Approval commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

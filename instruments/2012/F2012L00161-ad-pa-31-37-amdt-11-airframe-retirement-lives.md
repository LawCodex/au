# AD/PA-31/37 Amdt 11 - Airframe Retirement Lives

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/PA-31/37 Amdt 10 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The retirement lives promulgated in this Directive are based on fatigue tests and analyses conducted by the manufacturer. According to these evaluations, the risk of airframe cracking increases to an unacceptable level beyond the specified life limitations.<br>Amendment 11 provides alleviation to the retirement requirement of paragraphs 1.a. and 2.a. by allowing a CASA Approved Damage Tolerance inspection program as an alternative.<br>Amendment 10 became effective on 25 February 1999 and removed the requirement to record average weight and flight times. This was a result of a sensitivity analysis carried out by CASA on the relative effects of different sortie profiles and their relative effect on true fatigue life.<br>Amendment 9 of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on<br>27 December 1990.<br>Amendment 8 of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 7 September 1975.<br>The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on<br>31 March 1975. |

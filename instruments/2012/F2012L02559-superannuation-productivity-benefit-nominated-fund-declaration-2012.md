# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Nominated Fund) Declaration 2012

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Nominated Fund) Declaration 20121

I, Penelope YING YEN WONG, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make the following Declaration under subsection 4B(1) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 14 December 2012

Penelope YING YEN WONG

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Nominated Fund) Declaration 2012.*

2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 January 2013.

###### 3 Definition

In this Declaration:

***Act*** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

###### 4 Revocation

The declaration titled ‘Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988 Declaration under subsection 4B(1)’ made under subsection 4B(1) of the Act on 27 June 1990 is revoked.

###### 5 Nominated Fund

For the purposes of the Act, AustralianSuper is declared to be the nominated fund.

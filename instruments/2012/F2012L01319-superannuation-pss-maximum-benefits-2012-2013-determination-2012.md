# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2012-2013) Determination 2012

Superannuation (PSS)

Maximum Benefits (2012-2013) Determination 2012

I, STEPHEN PAUL WHITTON, Director, Australian Public Sector Payments and Entitlements, Position No. 44, and Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 20 June 2012

S WHITTON

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2012-2013) Determination 2012*.

2 Commencement

This determination shall take effect from and including 1 July 2012.

3 Maximum Benefits – Lump Sums

For rule 5.6.5 of the PSS Rules, the table in rule 5.6.1 has effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 2012, as if the amounts specified in the following table were substituted for the amounts specified in that table:

# Tariff Concession Order 1136709

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 03.11.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7306.30.00 | TUBES, non-alloy steel, welded, circular cross section,<br>complying with American Society for Testing and Materials<br>standard ASTM A214/A214M-96(2005), having ALL of the following:<br>(a) carbon content NOT greater than 0.18%;<br>(b) manganese content NOT less than 0.27% and NOT greater<br>than 0.63%;<br>(c) outside diameter NOT less than 24.13 mm and NOT greater<br>than 26.7 mm;<br>(d) wall thickness NOT less than 2.05 mm and NOT greater<br>than 2.27 mm;<br>(e) length NOT less than 10 m;<br>(f) heat treated at NOT less than 900 degrees Celsius;<br>(g) straight lengths<br>Op. 03.11.11 - TC 1136709 | 50 |

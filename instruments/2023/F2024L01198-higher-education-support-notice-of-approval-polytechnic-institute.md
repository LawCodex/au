# Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Polytechnic Institute Australia Pty Ltd) Instrument 2023

I, Annette Cannell, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 29 September 2023

Annette Cannell

A/g Assistant Secretary\
Funding, Integrity and Students Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Polytechnic Institute Australia Pty Ltd) Instrument 2023*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** used in this instrument is defined in Schedule 1 to the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Approval of higher education provider

For the purposes of section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the Act, Polytechnic Institute Australia Pty Ltd (ACN 145 333 795; ABN 34 145 333 795) is approved as a higher education provider.

# Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) (Paragraph 15(1)(d)) Determination 2023

**Commonwealth of Australia**

**TELECOMMUNICATIONS (CARRIER LICENCE CHARGES) (PARAGRAPH 15(1)(d)) DETERMINATION 2023**

I, MICHELLE ROWLAND, Minister for Communications, under paragraph 15(1)(d) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997*, determine that the estimated total amount of grants likely to be made during the 2022‑2023 financial year under section 593 of the *Telecommunications Act 1997* is $2,435,000.

Dated 30 March 2023

MICHELLE ROWLAND

Minister for Communications

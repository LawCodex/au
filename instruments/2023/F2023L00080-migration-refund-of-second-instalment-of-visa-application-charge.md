# Migration (Refund of Second Instalment of Visa Application Charge) Instrument (LIN 23/022) 2023

LIN 23/022

Migration (Refund of Second Instalment of Visa Application Charge) Instrument (LIN 23/022) 2023

I, Karin Maier, delegate of the Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, under paragraph 2.12H(2)(f) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the ***Regulations***)*,* specify in the Schedule the provisions of Schedule 1 to the Regulations under which the second instalment of the visa application charge is payable.

[NOTE : Regulation 2.12H specifies that the Minister must refund the amount paid by way of the second instalment of the visa application charge in relation to an application for a visa, if any of certain specified circumstances exist and a written request is received from a specified person.]

This instrument is taken to have commenced on 2 October 2022.

Dated 2 February 2023

Karin Maier

Assistant Secretary, Skilled Visa Branch

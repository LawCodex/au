###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 223C of the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) APRA staff member;

(b) infringement officer.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993.*

###### 5 Determination

APRA staff members holding, occupying or performing the duties or an office or position titled General Manager or Executive Director are to be infringement officers for the purposes of exercising powers under Part 22 of the Act in relation to a contravention of a provision that is subject to an infringement notice under that Part.

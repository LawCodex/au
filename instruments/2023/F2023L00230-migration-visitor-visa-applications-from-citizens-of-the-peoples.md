# Migration (Visitor Visa Applications from Citizens of the People’s Republic of China) Instrument (LIN 23/020) 2023

LIN 23/020

Migration (Visitor Visa Applications from Citizens of the People’s Republic of China) Instrument (LIN 23/020) 2023

I, Brigitta Rudnicki, delegate of the Minister, acting under subclause 600.251(2) of Schedule 2 to the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the ***Regulations***):

(a) specify the areas in Schedule 1 as areas of the People’s Republic of China for subclause 600.251(2); and

(b) repeal *Migration Regulations 1994 –Visitor Visa Applications from Citizens of the People’s Republic of China – IMMI 13/008* (F2013L00513).

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 15 March 2023

**Brigitta Rudnicki**

A/g Assistant Secretary

Temporary Visas Branch

Department of Home Affairs

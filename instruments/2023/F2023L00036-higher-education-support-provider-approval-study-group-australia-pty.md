###### 3 Authority

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Notice of Revocation

(1) The approval of Study Group Australia Pty Limited (ACN 070 919 327) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act.

(2) The *Higher Education Provider Approval No. 1 of 2011* (F2011L00181) is repealed.

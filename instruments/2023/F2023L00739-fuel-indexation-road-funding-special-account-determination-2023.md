###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003* has the effect that expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015* as in force from time to time.

In this instrument:

***the Act*** means the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

### Part 2—Determination of amount to be credited to the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account for 2021-22

###### 5 Crediting of amounts to the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account

Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, an amount of $1,072,000,000 is specified to be credited to the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account on the day this instrument commences for the 2021-22 financial year.

# AusCheck (Major National Event—2024 Association of Southeast Asian Nations–Australia Special Commemorative Summit) Declaration 2023

LIN 23/085

AusCheck (Major National Event—2024 Association of Southeast Asian Nations–Australia Special Commemorative Summit) Declaration 2023

I, Clare O’Neil, Minister for Home Affairs, under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*, declare the 2024 Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN)–Australia Special Commemorative Summit to be a major national event.

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 6 December 2023

Clare O’Neil

Minister for Home Affairs

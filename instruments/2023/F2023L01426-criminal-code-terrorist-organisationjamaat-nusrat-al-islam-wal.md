###### 5 Terrorist organisation—Jama’at Nusrat al‑Islam wal‑Muslimin

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***terrorist organisation*** in subsection 102.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as Jama’at Nusrat al‑Islam wal‑Muslimin is specified.

(2) Jama’at Nusrat al‑Islam wal‑Muslimin is also known by the following names:

(a) al‑Mulathamun Battalion;

(b) al‑Murabitoun;

(c) al‑Murabitun;

(d) Ansar al‑Din;

(e) Ansar Dine;

(f) Force de Libération du Macina;

(g) GNIM;

(h) Group for Support of Islam and Muslims;

(i) Group for Supporting Islam and Muslims;

(j) Group of Support for Islam and All Muslims;

(k) Group to Support Islam and Muslims;

(l) GSIM;

(m) Islam and Muslims’ Support Group;

(n) JNIM;

(o) Jamaat Nosrat al‑Islam wal‑Mouslimin;

(p) Jama’a Nusrah al‑Islam wa al‑Muslimin;

(q) Jama’ah Nusrah al‑Islam wal‑Muslimin;

(r) Jama’at Nusrat al‑Islam wal Muslimeen;

(s) Jama’at Nusrat al‑Islam wal Muslimin;

(t) Katibat Macina;

(u) Macina Liberation Front;

(v) NIM;

(w) Nusrat al‑Islam wal Muslimeen;

(x) Nusrat al‑Islam wal Muslimin;

(y) the Sentinels;

(z) Sahara Emirate;

(za) Union for Supporting Islam and Muslims.

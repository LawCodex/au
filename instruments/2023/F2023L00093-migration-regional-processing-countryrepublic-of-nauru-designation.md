# Migration (Regional Processing Country—Republic of Nauru) Designation (LIN 23/017) 2023

LIN 23/017

Migration (Regional Processing Country—Republic of Nauru) Designation (LIN 23/017) 2023

I, Clare O’Neil, Minister for Home Affairs and Minister for Cyber Security, under subsection 198AB(1) of the *Migration Act 1958,* thinking that it is in the national interest to do so, designate that the Republic of Nauru is a regional processing country.

Dated 5 February 2023

The Hon. Clare O’Neil MP

Minister for Home Affairs

Minister for Cyber Security

# Migration (Designated APEC Economy) Instrument (LIN 23/008) 2023

LIN 23/008

Migration (Designated APEC Economy) Instrument (LIN 23/008) 2023

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, under the definition of ***designated APEC economy*** in regulation 1.03 of the *Migration Regulations 1994*, specify each economy mentioned in Schedule 1 as a designated APEC economy.

This instrument is taken to have commenced on 2 October 2022.

Dated 30 January 2023

ANDREW GILES

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

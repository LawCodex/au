# AusCheck (Major National Event—Indo-Pacific Quadrilateral Dialogue 2023) Declaration 2023

LIN 23/030

AusCheck (Major National Event—Indo-Pacific Quadrilateral Dialogue 2023) Declaration 2023

I, Clare O’Neil, Minister for Home Affairs, declare the Indo-Pacific Quadrilateral Dialogue 2023 to be a major national event under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*.

This instrument commences the day after registration.

Dated 13 April 2023

Clare O’Neil

Minister for Home Affairs

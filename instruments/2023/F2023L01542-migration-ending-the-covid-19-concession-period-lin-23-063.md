# Migration (Ending the COVID-19 Concession Period) (LIN 23/063) Specification 2023

LIN23/063

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, under subregulation 1.15N(2) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the ***Regulations***), specify 25 November 2023 for the purposes of paragraph 1.15N(1)(b) of the Regulations.

This instrument commences on the day after the instrument is registered.

Dated 23 November 2023

Andrew Giles

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

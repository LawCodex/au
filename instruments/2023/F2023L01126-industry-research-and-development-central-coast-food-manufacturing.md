## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Industry Research and Development (Central Coast Food Manufacturing Innovation Hub Program) Instrument 2022

**1 Section 4**

Insert:

***Central Coast Region*** means the region with the boundaries as shown on the map with the identifier PP4976, referred to in the Table to subclause 4(1) of the relevant Proclamation for the Central Coast area.

**2 Section 4 (definition of region)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***relevant Proclamation*** means the *Local Government (Council Amalgamations) Proclamation 2016* (NSW), as in force or existing at the time when this instrument commences.

**3 Subsection 5(2)**

Omit “at Lisarow”, substitute “in the Central Coast Region”.

**4 Paragraphs 5(3)(b) and (c)**

Omit “region”, substitute “Central Coast Region”.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Native Forest Workforce Transition Program (Program 2)*** means the Western Australian Government program under which payments are made to eligible native forestry workers in respect of their reskilling and retraining as they transition out of the industry.

###### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the amount of a payment made under the Native Forest Workforce Transition Program (Program 2) is an exempt lump sum.

###### 6 Application Provision

Section 5 applies to the calculation of the rate of social security payment payable to Native Forest Workforce Transition Program (Program 2) payment recipients for days on or after 30 June 2022.

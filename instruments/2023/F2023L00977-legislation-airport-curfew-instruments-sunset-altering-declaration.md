###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 51A(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

###### 4 Aligning of sunsetting

The following instruments are repealed by section 51A of the *Legislation Act 2003* on 1 April 2029:

(a) *Adelaide Airport Curfew Regulations 2018*;

(b) *Air Navigation (Essendon Fields Airport) Regulations 2018*;

(c) *Air Navigation (Gold Coast Airport Curfew) Regulations 2018*;

(d) *Sydney Airport Curfew (Curfew Aircraft) Instrument 2015*;

(e) *Sydney Airport Curfew (Dispensation) Guideline 2016*.

###### 5 Repeal of this instrument

This instrument is repealed at the start of 2 April 2029.

###### 5 Terrorist organisation—Boko Haram

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***terrorist organisation*** in subsection 102.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as Boko Haram is specified.

(2) Boko Haram is also known by the following names:

(a) Group of the Followers of the Prophet for Propagation and Holy Struggle;

(b) Jama’at Ahl al‑Sunnah lil‑Da’awa wal‑Jihad;

(c) Jama’atu Ahlis Sunna Lidda’awati Wal‑Jihad;

(d) Nigerian Mujahideen;

(e) Nigerian Taliban;

(f) People Committed to the Propagation of the Prophet’s Teachings and Jihad;

(g) Western Education is Forbidden;

(h) Yusuffiya Movement;

(i) Yusuffiya Sect.

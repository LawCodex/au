# ASIC Corporations (Product Intervention Order Extension—Short Term Credit) Instrument 2023/956

ASIC Corporations (Product Intervention Order Extension—Short Term Credit) Instrument 2023/956

I, Greg Yanco, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, acting in accordance with the written approval of the Minister under subsection 1023H(4) of the *Corporations Act 2001*, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 21 December 2023

Greg Yanco

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Product Intervention Order Extension—Short Term Credit) Instrument 2023/956.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1023H(1) of the *Corporations Act 2001.*

Part 2—Declaration

4 Terms of declaration

*ASIC Corporations (Product Intervention Order—Short Term Credit) Instrument 2022/647* remains in force until it is revoked.

# Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2023

I, PENNY WONG, Minister for Foreign Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 12 May 2023

PENNY WONG

Minister for Foreign Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2023*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***DPRK*** means the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.

***DPRK vessel class*** has the same meaning as in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017.*

***sanctioned vessel*** has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011.*

***vessel*** has the same meaning as in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017.*

###### 5 Designation to continue to have effect

For the purposes of subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, the designation of each vessel within the DPRK vessel class as a sanctioned vessel for the DPRK in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017* is declared to continue to have effect.

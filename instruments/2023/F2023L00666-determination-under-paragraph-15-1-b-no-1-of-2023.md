# Determination under paragraph 15(1)(b) No. 1 of 2023

AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION

Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997

###### DETERMINATION UNDER PARAGRAPH 15(1)(b) No. 1 of 2023

The AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION (ACCC) makes this determination under paragraph 15(1)(b) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997* and determines that the proportion of the ACCC’s costs for the 2020-21 financial year that is attributable to the ACCC’s telecommunications functions and powers is $11,200,750.

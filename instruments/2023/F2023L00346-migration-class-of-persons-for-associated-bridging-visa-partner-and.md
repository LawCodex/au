# Migration (Class of persons for associated bridging visa – Partner and Parent visas) Instrument (LIN 23/029) 2023

LIN 23/029

Migration (Class of persons for associated bridging visa – Partner and Parent visas) Instrument (LIN 23/029) 2023

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, make this instrument under paragraphs 010.611(1)(c) and 020.611(1)(b) of Schedule 2 to the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations):

(a) repeal *Migration Regulations 1994 – Class of Persons – IMMI 12/094* (F2012L02217); and

(b) specify as a class of persons for the purposes of paragraphs 010.611(1)(c) and 020.611(1)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, those persons who are granted a Subclass 010 (Bridging A) visa or a Subclass 020 (Bridging B) visa in association with an application for a substantive visa mentioned below that has not been finally determined:

(i) Subclass 801 (Partner) visa;

(ii) Subclass 804 (Aged Parent) visa;

(iii) Subclass 820 (Partner) visa;

(iv) Subclass 864 (Contributory Aged Parent) visa; and

(v) Subclass 884 (Contributory Aged Parent (Temporary)) visa.

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 21 March 2023

The Hon. Andrew Giles MP

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

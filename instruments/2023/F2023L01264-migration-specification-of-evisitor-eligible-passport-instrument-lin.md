# Migration (Specification of eVisitor eligible passport) Instrument (LIN 23/064) 2023

LIN 23/064

Migration (Specification of eVisitor eligible passport) Instrument (LIN 23/064) 2023

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, under regulation 1.11C of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (***the Regulations***):

(a) specify a valid passport of a kind mentioned in an item of the table in Schedule 1 as an eVisitor eligible passport; and

(b) specify the conditions mentioned in an item of the table in Schedule 1 as conditions that must be satisfied for the passport in mentioned in the same item; and

(c) repeal *Migration Regulations 1994 – Specification of an eVisitor – Eligible Passports – IMMI 13/078* (F2013L01415).

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 13 September 2023

The Hon. Andrew Giles MP

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

# National Health Security (SSBA Standards) Determination 2023

I, Mark Butler, Minister for Health and Aged Care, make the following Determination under subsection 35(1) of the *National Health Security Act 2023.*

Dated 4 September 2023

Mark Butler

Minister for Health and Aged Care

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *National Health Security (SSBA Standards) Determination 2023.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after the instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 35(1) of the *National Health Security Act.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

**Act** means the *National Health Security Act 2007*.

**SSBA Standards** means the Security Sensitive Biological Agent (SSBA) Standards, dated April 2013, published by the Commonwealth.

Note: The Security Sensitive Biological Agent (SSBA) Standards can be found on the Department’s website at http://www.health.gov.au/ssba.

###### 5 Determination

For subsection 35(1) of the Act, the SSBA Standards are determined.

###### 6 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### National Health Security (SSBA Standards) Determination 2013

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

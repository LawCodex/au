###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 11 of the *Disability Services and Inclusion Act 2023*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including eligible activity.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Disability Services and Inclusion Act 2023*.

###### 5 Regulated activities

For the purposes of section 11 of the Act, each of the following kinds of eligible activity is a regulated activity:

(a) an eligible activity that involves delivery of higher‑risk or complex supports to people with disability;

(b) an eligible activity that is delivered by persons undertaking the activity through substantial one‑on‑one contact with people with disability to whom the activity is provided (whether the contact is in person or by other means);

(c) an eligible activity that involves regular engagement over a prolonged period between persons undertaking the activity and people with disability to whom the activity is provided (whether the engagement is in person or by other means).

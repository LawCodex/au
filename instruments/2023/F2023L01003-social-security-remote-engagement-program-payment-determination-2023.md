###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Remote engagement program

For the purposes of paragraph 661A(2)(a) of the Act, the following arrangements are the remote engagement program:

(a) the arrangement set out in Part G of Annexure 1 to the Head Agreement for the Community Development Program 2019‑2024 between the Commonwealth and Paupiyala Tjarutja Aboriginal Corporation, as in force on 3 July 2023;

(b) the arrangement set out in Part G of Annexure 1 to the Head Agreement for the Community Development Program 2019‑2024 between the Commonwealth and Ngaanyatjarra Council Aboriginal Corporation, as in force on 3 July 2023.

###### 6 Remote engagement placement

For the purposes of paragraph 661A(2)(b) of the Act, the following parts of the remote engagement program are a remote engagement placement under the program:

(a) the REP Placement set out in Part G of Annexure 1 to the Head Agreement mentioned in paragraph 5(a) of this instrument;

(b) the REP Placement set out in Part G of Annexure 1 to the Head Agreement mentioned in paragraph 5(b) of this instrument.

###### 7 Remote engagement program payment

For the purposes of subsection 661E(2) of the Act, the rate of payment per fortnight of the remote engagement program payment is $190.

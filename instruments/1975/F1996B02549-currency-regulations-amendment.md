# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules**

**1975 No. 139**

REGULATIONS UNDER THE CURRENCY ACT 1965-1973.\*

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of Australia, acting with the advice of the Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act* 1965-1973.

Dated this fourteenth day of July, 1975.

JOHN R. KERR

Governor-General.

By His Excellency’s Command,

F. E. STEWART

Minister of State for Tourism and Recreation for and on behalf of the Treasurer.

[image]Amendments of the Currency Regulations†

**Standard weight of coins.**

**1.** Regulation 3 of the Currency Regulations is amended by omitting all words from and including the word “specified” and substituting the following word and paragraphs:—

“specified—

(a) in respect of a coin made on or before 31 December 1974— in the second column of Schedule 1 in relation to the denomination of the coin; and

(b) in respect of a coin made on or after 1 January 1975—in the second column of Schedule 2 in relation to the denomination of the coin.”.

**Dimensions of coins.**

**2.** Regulation 5 of the Currency Regulations is amended by omitting paragraphs (a) and (b) and substituting the following paragraphs:—

“(a) in respect of a coin made on or before 31 December 1974—

(i) for the shortest diameter of a coin of the denomination of Fifty cents or for the diameter of a coin of another denomination—a length not exceeding the length specified in the third column of Schedule 1 in relation to the denomination of the coin; and

[image](ii) for the thickness of the coin—a thickness not exceeding the thickness specified in the fourth column of Schedule 1 in relation to the denomination of the coin; and

“(b) in respect of a coin made on or after 1 January 1975—

(i) for the shortest diameter of a coin of the denomination of Fifty cents or for the diameter of a coin of another denomination—a length not exceeding the length specified in the third column of Schedule 2 in relation to the denomination of the coin; and

(ii) for the thickness of the coin—a thickness not exceeding the thickness specified in the fourth column of Schedule 2 in relation to the denomination of the coin.”.

**Remedy in respect of weight of coins.**

**3.** Regulation 6 of the Currency Regulations is amended by omitting all words from and including the word “specified” and substituting the following word and paragraphs:—

“specified—

(a) in respect of a coin made on or before 31 December 1974— in the fifth column of Schedule 1 in relation to the denomination of the coin; and

(b) in respect of a coin made on or after 1 January 1975—in the fifth column of Schedule 2 in relation to the denomination of the coin.

# South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Consultative Committee (Privileges and Immunities) Regulations

**Statutory Rules 1986 No. 3591**

**South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone**

**Consultative Committee (Privileges and**

**Immunities) Regulations**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *International Organizations (Privileges and Immunities) Act 1963.*

Dated 11 December 1986.

N. M. STEPHEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

Bill Hayden

Minister of State for Foreign Affairs

**Citation**

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Consultative Committee (Privileges and Immunities) Regulations.

**Interpretation**

**2.** In these Regulations, unless the contrary intention appears—

“Committee” means the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Consultative Committee;

“the Act” means the *International Organizations (Privileges and Immunities) Act 1963.*

**Act to apply to Committee**

**3.** The Committee is declared to be an international organization to which the Act applies.

**Committee to have juridical personality and legal capacities**

**4.** The Committee—

(a) is a body corporate with perpetual succession;

(b) has the capacity to contract; and

(S.R.202/86)—Cat. No. 12/10.7.1986

(c) is capable, in its corporate name, of acquiring, holding and disposing of real and personal property and of instituting legal proceedings.

**Privileges and immunities of persons working with the Committee**

**5.** (**1**) A person who is participating in the work of the Committee or is performing, whether alone or jointly with other persons, a mission on behalf of the Committee has the privileges and immunities specified in paragraphs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of Part I of the Fifth Schedule to the Act.

(**2**) A person who has participated in the work of the Committee or who has performed a mission on behalf of the Committee has the immunities specified in Part II of the Fifth Schedule to the Act.

**Waiver**

**6.** The Committee may waive any privileges and immunities to which a person upon whom privileges and immunities are conferred by regulation 5 is entitled by virtue of the Act or these Regulations.

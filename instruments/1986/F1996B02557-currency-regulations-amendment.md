# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1986 No. 831**

**Currency Regulations2 (Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 17 April 1986.

N. M. STEPHEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

C J. Hurford

Minister of State for Immigration and Ethnic Affairs for

and on behalf of the Treasurer

**Commencement**

**1.** These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 May 1986.

**Designs of coins**

**2.** Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended by inserting after sub-paragraph (d) (viiid) the following sub-paragraph:

“(viiie) in the case of a coin of the denomination of $10 that refers on its obverse side to the year 1986 as the year of the coin—the inscriptions ‘1836 • SOUTH AUSTRALIA • 1986’ and ‘10 DOLLARS’ and a representation of the coat of arms of the State of South Australia;”.

**NOTES**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 24 April 1986.

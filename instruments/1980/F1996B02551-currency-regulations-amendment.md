# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules**

**1980 No. 288**

REGULATIONS UNDER THE CURRENCY ACT 19651

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act* 1965.

Dated this nineteenth day of September 1980.

ZELMAN COWEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

JOHN HOWARD

Treasurer

\_______________

AMENDMENTS OF THE CURRENCY REGULATIONS2

###### Designs of coins

**1.** Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended—

(a) by omitting from sub-paragraph (vii) of paragraph (d) “and” (last occurring); and

(b) by adding at the end of paragraph (d) the following word and sub-paragraph:

“; and (ix) in the case of a coin of the denomination of Two hundred dollars-the inscription ‘TWO HUNDRED DOLLARS’ and a representation of a koala.”.

###### Schedule 2

**2.** Schedule 2 to the Currency Regulations is amended by inserting—

| “Two hundred dollars | 10.00 | 24.20 | 2.00 | .06” |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Before

| “Fifty cents | 15.55 | 31.65 | 2.80 | 40”. |

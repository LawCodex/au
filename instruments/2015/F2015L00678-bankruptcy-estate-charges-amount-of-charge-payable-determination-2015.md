# Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) (Amount of Charge Payable) Determination 2015

*Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) Act 1997*

I, GEORGE BRANDIS, Attorney-General, make this Determination under subsection 7(2) of the *Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) Act 1997*.

Dated 30 April 2015

GEORGE BRANDIS QC

Attorney-General

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) (Amount of Charge Payable) Determination 2015*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on 1 July 2015.

###### 3 Repeal

The *Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) (Amount of Charge Payable) Determination 2014* is repealed.

###### 4 Amount of charge payable

For subsection 7(2) of the *Bankruptcy (Estate Charges) Act 1997*, the percentage is 7.0%.

# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2015-2016) Determination 2015

**Superannuation (PSS)**

**Maximum Benefits (2015-2016)**

**Determination 2015**

I, STEPHEN PAUL WHITTON, Director, APS Technical Team, Position No. 44, and Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 22 May 2015

S WHITTON

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

**1 Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2015-2016) Determination 2015*.

**2 Commencement**

This determination takes effect on 1 July 2015.

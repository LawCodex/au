# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument 2015

*Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*

I, Michelle Croker, being the person occupying the position of GEMS Regulator, make this instrument under subsections 37(1) and (2) of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012.*

Dated 15 December 2015

MICHELLE CROKER

Michelle Croker

GEMS Regulator

###### 1 Name of Determination

This instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument 2015*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument comes into force on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

***exempt model***: see section 4.

*Note:* Several other words and expressions used in this instrument have the meaning given by section 5 of the Act. For example:

GEMS

GEMS determination

GEMS labelling requirements

GEMS level requirements

model

model identifier

product classes

supply

###### 4 Exemption of model of a GEMS product from requirements of GEMS determination

The model specified at clause 1 of the Schedule (the ***exempt model***) is exempt from the requirements of the GEMS determination as described at clause 2 of the Schedule with respect to the supplies or uses of the exempt model specified at clause 3 of the Schedule.

###### 5 Conditions of exemption

The conditions (if any) at clause 4 of the Schedule are specified for the purposes of subsection 37(2) of the Act.

*Note:* Subsection 37(2) of the Act provides that the exemption may specify conditions to which the exemption is subject. Subsection 37(3) provides that a person must comply with a condition of an exemption to the extent that it applies to the person.

###### Schedule Exemption and conditions of exemption

###### Part 1 Exemption

###### 1 Specification of exempt models

| **Model No.** | **Registration No.** |
|---|---|
| JM Aerofoil, 90JM25/4/9/30 13.2 kw (electric motor) | N/A |

###### 2 Requirements from which model is exempt

Requirements of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Three Phase Cage Induction Motors) Determination 2012* as follows:

| GEMS level requirements (section 6) | The model is exempted from the energy use and testing requirements mentioned in section 6 of the Determination. |
|---|---|
| GEMS labelling requirements (section 7) | The model is exempted from the labelling and testing requirements mentioned in section 7 of the Determination. |

###### 3 Supplies and uses to which the exemption applies

Supplies related to the replacement of motor fan combination units in the spiral freezing tunnel for the formed potato product line at Simplot Australia Pty Ltd’s Ulverstone Plant only.

###### 4 Specification of conditions of exemption

There are no conditions to which the exemption is subject.

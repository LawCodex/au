**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 14(1) of the *Low Aromatic Fuel Act 2013*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Low Aromatic Fuel Act 2013.*

###### 5 Designated low aromatic fuel area

For subsection 14(1) of the Act, the area of Great Palm Island, Queensland, being all that part of Great Palm Island above lowest astronomical tide, including associated infrastructure, within the area bounded by geodesic lines connecting the sequential points 1 to 9, then back to point 1, contained in the table below, is designated as a low aromatic fuel area:

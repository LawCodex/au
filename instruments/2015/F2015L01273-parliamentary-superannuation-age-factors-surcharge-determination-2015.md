# Parliamentary Superannuation Age Factors (Surcharge) Determination 2015 (No. 2)

Parliamentary Superannuation Age Factors (Surcharge) Determination 2015 (No. 2)1

*Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*

The Parliamentary Retiring Allowances Trust makes this Determination under subsection 22B(1) of the *Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*.

Signed for and on behalf of the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances Trust

Dated 12 August 2015

MICHAEL FRANCIS McCORMACK

Trustee of the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances Trust

PHILIP RUDDOCK

Trustee of the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances Trust

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Parliamentary Superannuation Age Factors (Surcharge) Determination 2015 (No. 2)*.

###### 2 Commencement and revocation

(a) This Determination commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

(b) This Determination revokes the *Superannuation Pension Conversion Factors Determination No. 2/2004* made by the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances Trust on 9 December 2004 under subsection 22B(1) of the *Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*.

###### 3 Age factors

For the purposes of section 22B of *Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*, the age factor for a person is determined under Tables A, B, C and D of the Schedule to this Determination, based on:

(a) whether the person is male or female;

(b) whether the benefit is a retirement pension or an invalidity pension; and

(c) the age of the person when the benefit becomes payable.

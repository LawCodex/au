###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 559(3B) of the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

###### 4 Purpose

This instrument sets out how to work out the amount of interest (if any) that the Fair Work Ombudsman, on behalf of the Commonwealth, must pay to a person under subsection 559(3A) of the Act.

Note: Interest is payable to a person under subsection 559(3A) of the Act if:

(a) the person is paid an amount under subsection 559(3) of the Act at a particular time; and

(b) the amount is at least $100; and

(c) the amount is attributable to an amount that was paid to the Commonwealth under subsection 559(1) of the Act more than 6 months before that time.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

***index number***, in relation to a quarter, means the All Groups Consumer Price Index number that is the weighted average of the 8 capital cities and is published by the Australian Statistician in respect of that quarter.

***interest period***: see section ^7.

***unclaimed amount*** means an amount that:

(a) is paid to the Commonwealth under subsection 559(1) of the Act; and

(b) the Fair Work Ombudsman subsequently authorises to be paid to a person under subsection 559(3) of that Act.

###### 6 Calculation of interest

The amount of interest (if any) that must be paid in respect of an unclaimed amount is to be worked out as follows:

(a) identify the interest period for the unclaimed amount (see section ^7);

(b) add together the amount of interest payable in respect of the unclaimed amount for each financial year in which the interest period for the unclaimed amount falls (see section ^8).

###### 7 Interest period for an unclaimed amount

The ***interest period*** for an unclaimed amount is the period that:

(a) starts on the day the unclaimed amount was paid to the Commonwealth under subsection 559(1) of the Act; and

(b) ends on the 14th day after the day the Fair Work Ombudsman authorises the unclaimed amount to be paid to a person under subsection 559(3) of that Act.

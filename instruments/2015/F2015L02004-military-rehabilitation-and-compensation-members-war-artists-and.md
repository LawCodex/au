# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members – War Artists and Entertainers - Service End Date) Amendment Determination 2015

Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members - War Artists and Entertainers - Service End Date) Amendment Determination 2015

Instrument 2015 No. MRCC 71

I, STUART ROBERT, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs and Minister also administering section 1 of the *Defence Act 1903* make this Determination under section 8 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

Dated this 6 day of December 2015

Stuart Robert

.........................................................................................................

STUART ROBERT

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members* – *War Artists and Entertainers* - *Service End Date) Amendment Determination 2015*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences when the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee – War Artists and Entertainers) Notice 2015* commences.

**3 Saving and application**

(1) A person who, immediately before the commencement of this instrument, is a member under:

(a) *the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2006 (No*.*2*); or

(b) the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2007*;

is, on the commencement of this instrument, a member under, respectively:

(c) *the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2006 (No*.*2*); or

(d) *the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2007;*

as amended by this instrument.

###### 4 Amendment

(1) The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2006 (No*.*2*) is amended in accordance with Part A of the Schedule.

(2) The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2007* is amended in accordance with Part B of the Schedule.

**Schedule**

**PART A**

Amendments to the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Members) Determination 2006 (No*.*2*)

1 Section 2

*after the section add:*

2A Definitions

*SRCA Notice 2015* means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee – War Artists and Entertainers) Notice 2015*.

2 Schedule 1

*substitute:*

| Item | Class of persons | Acts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Persons contracted by the Department of Defence to provide or support the provision of :<br>a) musical performances and entertainment; or<br>b) management and technical support for performers referred to in a) above.<br>at the request of the Defence Force. | Acts performed in the provision or support of musical performances and entertainment under the auspices of the Forces Advisory Committee on Entertainment or the Department of Defence — being acts performed at any time during the period<br>1 July 2004 to the day immediately before the *SRCA Notice 2015* commenced.<br>Note: under subsection 36(1) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* a period expressed to continue until a specified day – includes that day. |

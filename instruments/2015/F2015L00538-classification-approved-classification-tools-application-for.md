# Classification (Approved Classification Tools) (Application for Revocation of Classification) Determination 2015

**Classification (Approved Classification Tools) (Application for Revocation of Classification) Determination 2015**

*Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*

I, Michael Keenan, Minister for Justice, make the following determination under subsection 22CH(8) of the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

Dated 10 April 2015

Michael Keenan

Minister for Justice

1 Name

This determination is the Classification (Approved Classification Tools) (Application for Revocation of Classification) Determination 2015.

2 Commencement

This determination commences on the day it is registered.

3 Definitions

In this determination:

***Act*** means the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

***application for revocation*** means an application made under paragraph 22CH(2)(b) of the Act for the Classification Board to exercise its power to revoke a classification made by the operation of an approved classification tool.

***approved classification tool*** means a tool that has been approved under subsection 22CA(1) of the Act for the purposes of classifying one or more of the following:

publications;

films;

computer games.

***publisher*** means a person who has published or intends to publish classified material.

4 Class of persons who may make an application for revocation

For the purposes of paragraph 22CH(3)(c) of the Act, the following constitute a class of persons who may make an application for revocation:

the Minister;

a participating Minister;

the publisher of the classified material.

Note: For the definitions of ***participating Minister*** and ***publish***, see section 5 of the Act.

5 Time period to make an application for revocation

For the purposes of subparagraph 22CH(3)(b)(ii) of the Act, the specified period for making an application for revocation is 2 years after the day the decision made by the operation of the approved classification tool takes effect.

# Torres Strait Prawn Fishery Fishing Season Determination 2015

**DETERMINATION OF FISHING SEASON**

***Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984***

Section 15A

***Torres Strait Prawn Fishery Management Plan 2009***

Subsection 2.3(2)

**TORRES STRAIT PRAWN FISHERY FISHING SEASON DETERMINATION 2015**

The **PROTECTED ZONE JOINT AUTHORITY** makes the following Determination pursuant to section 15A of the *Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984* under subsection 2.3(2) of the *Torres Strait Prawn Fishery Management Plan 2009.*

**Dated:** 22 **November 2015**

**Senator the Hon. Anne Ruston**

Parliamentary Secretary to the Minister for Agriculture and Water Resources

on behalf of the Torres Strait Protected Zone Joint Authority

**Citation**

1\. This Determination may be cited as the Torres Strait Prawn Fishery Fishing Season Determination 2015.

**Commencement**

2\. This Determination commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

**Interpretation**

3\. Terms used in this Determination that are defined for the purposes of the *Torres Strait Prawn Fishery Management Plan 2009* have the same meanings in this Determination as they have in that Plan.

**Notes**: 1. Terms defined in the *Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984* have the same meanings in this Determination.

**Determination of the fishing season**

4\. The fishing season in the Torres Strait Prawn Fishery for a particular year is the period specified as follows:

a) for the 2016 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2016 to 6 am on 1 December 2016 local time;

b) for the 2017 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2017 to 6 am on 1 December 2017 local time;

c) for the 2018 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2018 to 6 am on 1 December 2018 local time;

d) for the 2019 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2019 to 6 am on 1 December 2019 local time;

e) for the 2020 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2020 to 6 am on 1 December 2020 local time;

f) for the 2021 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2021 to 6 am on 1 December 2021 local time;

g) for the 2022 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2022 to 6 am on 1 December 2022 local time;

h) for the 2023 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2023 to 6 am on 1 December 2023 local time;

i) for the 2024 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2024 to 6 am on 1 December 2024 local time; and

j) for the 2025 year, the period from 6 pm on 1 February 2025 to 6 am on 1 December 2025 local time.

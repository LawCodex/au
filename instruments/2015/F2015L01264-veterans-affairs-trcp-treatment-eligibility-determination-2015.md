**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under section 88A of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986.*

**4 Definitions:**

(1) In this Instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

***dependant***, in relation to a *member*, means a partner of a *member* or of a *former member* or a child or stepchild of a *member* or of a *former member*.

***entitled person*** has the meaning it has in the *Treatment Principles*.

***former member*** has the meaning it has in section 5 of the *MRCA*.

***member*** has the meaning it has in section 5 of the *MRCA*.

***MRCA Treatment Principles*** means the legislative instrument of that name made by the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Commission under paragraph 286(1)(a) of the *MRCA*.

***Treatment Principles*** means the document prepared by the Repatriation Commission under subsection 90(1) of the *Act*.

***veteran*** has the meaning it has in paragraph (a) of the definition of “veteran” in subsection 5C(1) of the *Act* and includes the extended meaning of “veteran” in subsection 81(2) of the *Act* (i.e. member of the Forces; member of a Peacekeeping Force).

Note: veteran is defined in the *Act* as including a deceased veteran

***White Card*** has the meaning it has in the *Treatment Principles* or the *MRCA Treatment Principles* as the case requires.

The following terms are defined in the *Act*:

child – s.5F(1)

dependant – s.11

MRCA – s.5Q(1)

partner – s.5E(1)

**5 Eligibility for TRCP treatment**

(1) A person in a class in Part A is eligible for the treatment in Part B as an *entitled person* under the *Treatment Principles* or as if the person is an *entitled person* under the *Treatment Principles*:

| PART A<br>A *veteran* eligible for treatment under Part V of the *Act.*<br>A dependant of a *veteran* – being a dependant eligible for treatment under Part V of the *Act.*<br>A *member* eligible for treatment under Chapter 6 of the *MRCA.*<br>A dependant of a *member* or of a *former member* – being a dependant eligible for treatment under Chapter 6 of the *MRCA.* | PART B<br>*TRCP treatment* |
|---|---|

Notes:

*TRCP treatment* may be applied to a non-service-related condition of a *veteran*, *member* or *former member* e.g. an otherwise “ineligible condition” of the holder of a *White Card*;

Because paragraph 88A(1)(d) of the *Act* enables eligibility for treatment to be determined for “a person”, the person need not be a veteran or dependant of a veteran (persons normally covered by the *Act*) and may be a person covered by the MRCA. Accordingly the *MRCA Treatment Principles* do not need a corresponding part dealing with TRCP Treatment.

Veterans and dependants of veterans are included in this instrument, albeit some veterans and dependants could be eligible for *TRCP treatment* without needing to be included in this instrument, so that *TRCP treatment* may be applied to any “ineligible condition” of a veteran i.e. a condition that is not service-related, and in relation to both veterans and dependants, so that *TRCP treatment* may be applied “whole-of-person” i.e. the person need not have any injury or disease. Thus *TRCP treatment* may be applied to the effects of frailty caused by the ageing process.

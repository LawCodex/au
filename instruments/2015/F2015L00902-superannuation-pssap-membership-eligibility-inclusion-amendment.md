# Superannuation (PSSAP) Membership Eligibility (Inclusion) Amendment Declaration 2015 (No. 1)

Superannuation (PSSAP) Membership Eligibility (Inclusion) Amendment Declaration 2015 (No. 1)1

I, MATHIAS HUBERT PAUL CORMANN, Minister for Finance, make this Declaration under subparagraph 13 (1) (a) (ii) of the *Superannuation Act 2005*.

Dated 19-06-2015

MATHIAS HUBERT PAUL CORMANN

Minister for Finance

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (PSSAP) Membership Eligibility (Inclusion) Amendment Declaration 2015 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 14 July 2005.

3 Application

The amendments made by items 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to this Declaration apply in relation to persons who become employees of Australian Hearing Services on or after 1 July 2005.

4 Amendment of *Superannuation (PSSAP) Membership Eligibility (Inclusion) Declaration 2005*

Schedule 1 amends the *Superannuation (PSSAP) Membership Eligibility (Inclusion) Declaration 2005.*

Schedule 1 Amendments

(section 4)

[1] Section 3, after the definition of ‘alternative superannuation scheme’

insert

***Australian Hearing Services*** means the body corporate established under Part 2 of the *Australian Hearing Services Act 1991.*

[2] Subparagraph 4 (2) (h) (iii)

omit

authority.

insert

authority; and

[3] After paragraph 4 (2) (h)

insert

(i) in item [13] of the Schedule, at the end of the earlier of:

(i) the day on which the person ceases to be an employee of **Australian Hearing Services**; and

(ii) the day immediately before the day on which the person becomes, in relation to the person’s employment with **Australian Hearing Services**, a member of an alternative superannuation scheme; or

(iii) if Australian Hearing Services ceases to be a corporate Commonwealth entity within the meaning of the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013* – the last day on which **Australian Hearing Services** is a corporate Commonwealth entity.

[4] Schedule 1, after item [12]

[13] A person who:

(a) is an employee of **Australian Hearing Services**; and

(b) in respect of whom contributions were accepted, purportedly under the Act, on or after 1 July 2005, in relation to the person’s employment with **Australian Hearing Services**; and

(c) is not, in relation to the person’s employment with **Australian Hearing Services**, a member of an alternative superannuation scheme.

|Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I Rod Wise a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer |

|declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs |

|Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from NOVEMBER 8, 2004 and |

|continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2. |

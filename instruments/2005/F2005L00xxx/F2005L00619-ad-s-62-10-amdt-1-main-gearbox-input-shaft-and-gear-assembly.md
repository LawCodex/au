# AD/S-62/10 Amdt 1 - Main Gearbox Input Shaft and Gear Assembly

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/S-62/10 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Sikorsky S-62 Series Helicopters

| AD/S-62/10 Amdt 1 | Main Gearbox Input Shaft and Gear Assembly | 4/2005 |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | All Model S62A and S62B Helicopters fitted with Main Gearboxes (MGB) part number (P/N) 6235-20000-3 through -10 inclusive. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | Inspect the input shaft helical gears, P/N S6235-20158 in accordance with Sikorsky Aircraft Service Bulletin No. 62B35-6.<br>Note: FAA AD 63-17-06 refers. |
| Compliance: | Within 250 flight hours from the previous inspection and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 250 flight hours. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 14 April 2005. |
| Background: | This Directive requires an inspection of the MGB input shaft and gear assembly for possible rotational play between the splines on the shaft and the splines on the gear, and loss of torque on the nut securing the gears to the shaft.<br>The amendment reissues the Directive with no change to the technical content.<br>The original issue of this Directive became 31 July 1987. |

# Telecommunications (Interception) Act 1979 - Declaration of eligible authority as agency - Tasmania Police

Declaration of eligible authority as agency — Tasmania Police

*Telecommunications (Interception) Act 1979*

I, PHILIP MAXWELL RUDDOCK, Attorney-General:

(a) acting under section 34 of the *Telecommunications (Interception) Act 1979* (***the Act***) and at the request of the Premier of the State of Tasmania; and

(b) being satisfied that:

(i) the law of Tasmania makes satisfactory provision in relation to the matters mentioned in subsection 35 (1) of the Act; and

(ii) the State of Tasmania has entered into an agreement as required by subsection 35 (2) of the Act;

declare Tasmania Police to be an agency for the purposes of the Act.

Dated 2 February 2005

PHILIP RUDDOCK

Attorney-General

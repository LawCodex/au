**|Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer |**

**| |**

**| (a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0401502 made on MARCH 3, 2004, in respect of the goods described in |**

**| TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from FEBRUARY 9, 2005; and |**

**| Revocation. |**

# AD/DHC-8/100 Amdt 2 - Fluorescent Lighting System

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/DHC-8/100 Amdt 1 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | Transport Canada has advised that numerous reports of fluorescent lamp holder damage due to overheating have been received. The heating results from arcing between the pins of the fluorescent tube and the lamp holder contacts, if the tube is not properly seated during installation. The resultant fumes and traces of visible smoke can be of concern to passengers and crew. |
|  | The original issue of this Directive required amendment of the Maintenance Program Manuals and a visual inspection of the fluorescent lights. |
|  | Amendment 1 did not change any of the requirements of the original issue of this Directive but was issued following advice from Office of Legislative Drafting, Attorney-General’s Department that the original issue of the Directive was incorrectly made and had no legal effect. |
|  | This Amendment amends the applicability to include -301aeroplances and to reduce the serial number range. The Amendment also adds the inspection of lavatory and optional sidelights with ballast, P/N BA08006-28-1, installed. |

# Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2005 (No. 4) Amendment Determination 2005 (No. 2)

I, CHRIS PEARCE, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 29 November 2005

CHRIS PEARCE

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2005 (No. 4) Amendment Determination 2005 (No. 2)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

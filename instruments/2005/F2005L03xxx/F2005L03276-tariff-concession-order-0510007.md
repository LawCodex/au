# Tariff Concession Order 0510007

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.08.05 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 9508.90.00 | PARTS, WATERSLIDE, being ANY of the following:<br>(a) fibreglass tube slide channels having a diameter<br>greater than 1 800 mm;<br>(b) fibreglass body slide channels;<br>(c) support columns and/or arms;<br>(d) weldments;<br>(e) structural frames;<br>(f) rails;<br>(g) platforms;<br>(h) electrical controls;<br>(i) water jets;<br>(j) support angles and/or cleats and/or brackets and/or<br>braces;<br>(k) run out and/or splash down channels<br>Op. 01.08.05 - TC 0510007 | 50 |

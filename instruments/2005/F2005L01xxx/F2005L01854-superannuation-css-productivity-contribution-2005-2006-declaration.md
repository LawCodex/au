# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2005-2006) Declaration 2005

Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2005‑2006) Declaration 20051

I, MICHAEL JOHN SIMPSON, Manager of the Civilian Defined Benefit Schemes Administration Section, Commonwealth Superannuation Administration, and delegate of the CSS Board, make this Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 27 June 2005

M SIMPSON

Delegate of the CSS Board

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2005‑2006) Declaration 2005*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 July 2005.

###### 3 Productivity contributions

For section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*, the table in subsection 110C (1) of that Act has effect, in relation to the financial year starting on 1 July 2005, as if the amounts specified in the following table were substituted for the amounts specified in the table in subsection 110C (1).

| Fortnightly rate of salary | Fortnightly productivity contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $1 473.33 | $44.20 |
| $1 473.33 or more, but less than $2 374.00 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $2 374.00 or more, but less than $3 561.00 | $71.22 |
| $3 561.00 or more | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2005-2006 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2005

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2005–2006 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 20051

I, nicholas hugh minchin, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 23 June 2005

NICK MINCHIN

Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2005–2006 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2005*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2005, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $2593.85 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $2593.85 or more | $233.45 |

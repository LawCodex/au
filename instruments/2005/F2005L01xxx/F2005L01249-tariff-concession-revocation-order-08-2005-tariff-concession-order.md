# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 08/2005 - Tariff Concession Order 0505625

**_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________**

**| |**

**| |**

**| TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER |**

**| |**

**| |**

**|Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer |**

**| |**

**| (a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9607509 made on JULY 4, 1996, in respect of the goods described in |**

**| TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from MAY 17, 2005; and |**

**| |**

**| (b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Order described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are |**

**| goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B |**

**| applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the |**

**| Revocation. |**

**5603.14.00 FABRIC,non-woven or not knitted, being spun bonded polyester,single, 50**

**cellular,honeycomb construction,having ALL of the following:- 16.05.05**

**(a) width NOT less than 440 cm with no seams or joints;**

**(b) pleat size 10 mm or 20 mm;**

**(c) solar heat rejection &gt; 70%;**

**(d) R values between 0.359 and 0.546;**

**(e) one side coloured, other side white or off-white;**

**(f) on a scale of 0.00 to 1.00, a shading co-efficient NOT greater**

**than 0.34;**

**(g) inherently resistant to bacterial growth;**

**(h) insulating properties to control heat flow.**

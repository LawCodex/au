# Tariff Concession Order 0513211

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 29.09.05 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8479.89.90 | SEPARATORS, WASTE PAPER FROM CARDBOARD, comprising ALL of the<br>following:<br>(a) feeder conveyors having a width NOT less than 1 800 mm<br>and NOT greater than 2 100 mm;<br>(b) feeder conveyors having a length NOT greater than 3 100 mm;<br>(c) spiked disc dischargers containing NOT less than 6 discs<br>and NOT greater than 12 discs<br>Op. 29.09.05 - TC 0513211 | 50 |

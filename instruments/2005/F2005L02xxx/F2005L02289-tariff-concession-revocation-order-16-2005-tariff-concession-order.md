**! !**

**! Under Section 269SC(1)&(4) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer !**

**! !**

**! (a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0303432 made on AUGUST 18, 2003, in respect of the goods described in !**

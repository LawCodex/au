# Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information - Woolworths Group Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2005

Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — Woolworths Group Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2005

*Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*

I, PHILIP RUDDOCK, Attorney-General, acting under subregulation 63 (6A) of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001* (the ***Regulations***), and having given my approval under regulation 38 of the Regulations, in relation to a superannuation interest in the Woolworths Group Superannuation Scheme (the ***Scheme***), established by Trust Deed dated 12 March 1970, as amended and in force on the commencement of this Determination (see Part 116 of Schedule 10 to the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2003*) (the ***Approval***)) determine that:

(a) the trustee of the Scheme is not required to provide the information about the interest mentioned in any of paragraphs 63 (4A) (aa) to (g) and (i) of the Regulations; and

(b) the trustee of the Scheme must provide the information about the interest that is mentioned in the definitions of the factors A and V in item 1 of the table in clause 2 of Part 116 of Schedule 10 to the Approval.

Dated 16 September 2005

PHILIP RUDDOCK

Attorney-General

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee) Notice 2005 (2)

**Notice No. 2 of 2005.**

I, KEVIN JAMES ANDREWS, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated: 16 August 2005

Kevin Andrews

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee) Notice 2005 (2)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on 17 August 2005.

###### 3 Declaration

For subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* I declare that:

(a) the class of persons specified in Column 1 of the Schedule, being persons who engage in the activities or perform acts specified in Column 2 of the Schedule, at the request or direction of the Attorney General, shall be taken to be employed by the Commonwealth, and

(b) the employment of such persons shall, for those purposes, be taken to be constituted by the performance by such persons of the acts or activities referred to in the Schedule.

## Schedule Definition of Employee

| Item | Class of Persons | Acts or Activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Members of the Wild Fire Assessment Team, appointed under the authority of the Attorney General, who are operating at the direction of Emergency Management Australia, a division of the Attorney General’s Department, in response to major forest fires burning in Indonesia. | All acts performed in connection with responding to major forest fires burning in Indonesia. |

# AD/PA-44/18 Amdt 1 - Combustion Heater Fuel Pumps

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/PA-44/18 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The original issue of this Directive required a one- time inspection of the combustion heater fuel pumps for fuel leakage. If leakage was found, repair or replacement of the fuel pump was undertaken. This Directive retains all those actions and includes additional serial numbers for the Models PA-44-180 aeroplanes in the applicability section. This Directive results from further investigation that concluded that additional fuel pumps that did not meet the quality control (inspection or design) requirements of the Directive had been installed in Models PA-44-180 aeroplanes. Quality control problems with the heater fuel pump could result in failure of the heater fuel pump. Such failure could lead to fire or explosion in the cockpit. |

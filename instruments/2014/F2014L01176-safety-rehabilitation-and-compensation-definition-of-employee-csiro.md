# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Employee – CSIRO Volunteers) Notice 2014

I, ERIC ABETZ, Minister for Employment, make this Notice under subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 22 August 2014

ERIC ABETZ

Minister for Employment

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of *Employee* – CSIRO Volunteers) Notice 2014.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of subsection 5(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*, I declare that:

(a) the class of persons specified in Column 1 of the Schedule, being persons who engage in the activities or perform acts specified in Column 2 of the Schedule, at the request or direction of the Commonwealth, shall be taken to be employed by the Commonwealth; and

(b) the employment of such persons shall, for those purposes, be taken to be constituted by the performance by such persons of the acts or activities specified in Column 2 of the Schedule.

###### Schedule

| **Column 1: Class of persons** | **Column 2: Class of acts** |
|---|---|
| Persons who, under the control or direction of a Commonwealth officer assist, without receiving remuneration (other than payment of expenses incurred) the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) at:<br>the Visitor Centre (Canberra Space Centre) at the Canberra Deep Space Communication Complex<br>Visitor Centres within the Australia Telescope National Facility<br>the CSIRO Discovery Centre<br>the Australian National Herbarium<br>the Australian National Insect Collection<br>the Australian National Wildlife Collection<br>the Australian National Fish Collection<br>the Australian Tree Seed Centre<br>the National Soil Archive | Acts performed in connection with the rendering of assistance. |

# High Court of Australia Rule of Court (09/09/2014)

**HIGH COURT OF AUSTRALIA**

**RULE OF COURT**

(as of 9 September 2014)

IT is ordered as follows:-

1\. Sittings of the Court for the transaction of all such business as may be brought before it shall be held during the year 2015 at Canberra and other places as required throughout the year and commencing on the days hereunder mentioned, that is to say:

Monday, 2 February 2015

Monday, 2 March 2015

Tuesday, 7 April 2015

Monday, 4 May 2015

Tuesday, 9 June 2015

Monday, 3 August 2015

Monday, 31 August 2015

Tuesday, 6 October 2015

Monday, 2 November 2015

Monday, 30 November 2015

2\. Sittings to hear applications for special leave to appeal will also be held on the days hereunder mentioned, that is to say:

Friday, 13 February 2015

Friday, 13 March 2015

Friday, 17 April 2015

Friday, 15 May 2015\
Friday, 19 June 2015\
Friday, 14 August 2015

Friday, 11 September 2015

Friday, 16 October 2015

Friday, 13 November 2015

Friday, 11 December 2015

3\. The winter recess shall commence on Saturday, 20 June 2015. The summer recess shall commence on Saturday, 12 December 2015.

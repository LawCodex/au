# Instrument under Section 37 of the Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012 - Stolway Pty Ltd

**Instrument under Section 37 of the GEMS Act**

**1 Power**

This legislative instrument is made under section 37 of the GEMS Act.

**2 Commencement**

This instrument comes into force on the day after it is registered.

*Note 1* The GEMS Regulator may repeal, rescind, revoke, amend, or vary this instrument ― see s 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

*Note 2* For sunsetting of legislative instruments see Part 6 of the Legislative Instruments Act 2003.

**3 Exemption**

I, **David Walker**, the GEMS Regulator, exempt the Models from the requirements of section 6 of the Determination, subject to the condition specified in section 4.

**4 Condition**

The Models are supplied only for use in:

(a) the Australia Pacific Liquefied Natural Gas Project; or

(b) the Gladstone Liquefied Natural Gas Project.

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Australia Pacific Liquefied Natural Gas Project*** means the incorporated joint venture of that name between Origin Energy, ConocoPhillips and Sinopec.

***Determination*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Air Conditioners and Heat Pumps) Determination 2013;*

***GEMS Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012* (Cth);

***GEMS Regulator*** has the meaning given in section 5 of the GEMS Act.

***Gladstone Liquefied Natural Gas Project*** means the unincorporated joint venture of that name between Santos, Petroliam Nasional Berhad (PETRONAS), Total and the Korean Gas Corporation (KOGAS).

***Manufacture****r* means the manufacturer specified in the Schedule.

***Models*** means the models of air conditioners produced by the Manufacturer specified in the Schedule.

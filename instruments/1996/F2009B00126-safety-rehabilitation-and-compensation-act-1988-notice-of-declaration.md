# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 - Notice of Declaration (Notice No. CA2 of 1996)

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

*SAFETY, REHABILITATION AND COMPENSATION ACT 1988*

NOTICE OF DECLARATION

Australia Foundation for Culture and the Humanities Ltd

Notice No. CA2 of 1996

I, PETER KEASTON REITH, Minister for Industrial Relations, hereby declare, for the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition of “Commonwealth authority” in subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988,* that on and from the date of publication of this Notice in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette*, the following body corporate is a body corporate to which the Act applies:

Australia Foundation for Culture and the Humanities Ltd

(ACN 072 479 835)

Peter Reith

Minister for Industrial Relations

Dated 09 JUL 1996 1996

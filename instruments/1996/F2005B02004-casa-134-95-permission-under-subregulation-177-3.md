# CASA 134/95 - Permission under subregulation 177(3)

CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY AUSTRALIA

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 134/95

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS**

**PERMISSION UNDER SUBREGULATION 177 (3)**

I, PAUL LYNTON EYRE, Senior Airworthiness Inspector, a delegate of CASA, under subregulation 177 (3) of the Civil Aviation Regulations give permission for each aircraft listed in the Schedule to be flown under the I.F.R. without being equipped with an outside air temperature indicator as required by CASA under subregulation 177 (1A) and specified in subparagraph 1 (f) of Appendix IV to section 20.18 of the Civil Aviation Orders.

The permission for each aircraft is subject to the condition that the aircraft is only used to carry out private operations.

**SCHEDULE**

***Aircraft List***

The aircraft bearing the nationality mark VH and the registration mark LLD. The aircraft bearing the nationality mark VH and the registration mark AKY. The aircraft bearing the nationality mark VH and the registration mark AGI. The aircraft bearing the nationality mark VH and the registration mark ONP.

**[Signed Paul L. Eyre]**

PAUL EYRE

Senior Airworthiness Inspector

2nd January 1996

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1996-97 Continuing Contributions Declaration

**Statutory Rules 1996 No. 1161**

————————

**Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1996-97 Continuing Contributions Declaration**

I, JOHN FAHEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988.*

Dated 17 June 1996.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance

————

**Citation**

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1996-97 Continuing Contributions Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

**Substitution of amounts**

**2.** For the purposes of section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, in relation to the financial year commencing on 1 July 1996, as if the amounts set out in the following Table were substituted for the amounts set out in the Table in that Schedule:

**TABLE**

| *Pay period rate of salary* | *Pay period contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $225.50 | $13.53 |
| $225.50 or more but less than | The amount that is 6% of the |
| $1,737.69 | employee's pay period rate of salary |
| $1,737.69 or more | $104.26 |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 28 June 1996.

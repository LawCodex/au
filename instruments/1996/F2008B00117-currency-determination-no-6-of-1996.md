# Currency Determination No. 6 of 1996

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 6 of 1996**

I, CHARLES RODERICK KEMP, Assistant Treasurer, acting for the Treasurer, make the following Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 9th December 1996.

Assistant Treasurer

**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 6 of 1996.

**Commencement**

**2.** This Determination commences on 13 December 1996.

**Specifications of certain coins**

**3.** This Determination specifies, in relation to certain coins having the denominations and standard compositions specified in columns 1 and 2 of Part 1 of the Schedule, the standard weights, the allowable variations from those standard weights, the dimensions and the designs respectively set out in columns 3, 4 and 5 of that Part.

**Application of Determination**

**4.** This Determination does not affect any other determination of the specifications of a coin.

**PART 2—EXPLANATION OF SYMBOLS**

**1. Shape:**

**S1** = circular.

**2. Edge:**

**E1** = milled.

**E2** = 8 segments, of which 4 are milled and 4 are plain.

96M193A, 15/11/96, 12:29 PM 96101205

**3. Obverse:**

**O1** = a circle of raised beads, immediately inside the rim, enclosing:

(a) the inscriptions:

(i) "ELIZABETH II"; and

(ii) "AUSTRALIA"; and

(b) an inscription stating, in arabic numerals, the amount, in dollars of the denomination of the coin, followed by the word "DOLLAR" or "DOLLARS", as the case requires;

that, in turn, surround an effigy of the Queen.

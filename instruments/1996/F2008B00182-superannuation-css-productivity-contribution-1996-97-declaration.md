# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1996-97) Declaration

Statutory Rules 1996 No. 1151

\__________________

I, JOHN FAHEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 10 June 1996.

john fahey

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1996-97) Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Productivity contributions

**2.** In relation to the financial year commencing on 1 July 1996, the Table in subsection 110C (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in that Table.

**TABLE**

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $1,014.67 | $30.44 |
| $1,014.67 or more, but less than $1,634.67 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,634.67 or more, but less than $2,452.00 | $49.04 |
| $2,452.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
|  |  |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 28 June 1996.

# Family Law Act 1975 s 69ZF(1) - Proclamation (04/12/1996)

*Family Law Act 1975*

**PROCLAMATION**

I, WILLIAM PATRICK DEANE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of

Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under section

subsection 69ZF (1) of the *Family Law Act 1975* declare that all the child welfare law

provisions of Part VII of that Act extend to New South Wales on and from 1 January

1997\.

Signed and sealed with the

Great Seal of Australia

on 4 December 1996

WILLIAM DEANE

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

DARYL WILLIAMS

Attorney-General and Minister for Justice

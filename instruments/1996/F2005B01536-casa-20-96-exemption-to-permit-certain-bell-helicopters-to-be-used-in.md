# CASA 20/96 - Exemption to Permit Certain Bell Helicopters to be used in Regular Public Transport Operations at the Parramatta Heliport in NSW

EXEMPTION NUMBER: CASA 20/96

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS**

**EXEMPTION TO PERMIT CERTAIN BELL HELICOPTERS TO**

**BE USED IN REGULAR PUBLIC TRANSPORT OPERATIONS**

**AT THE PARRAMATTA HELIPORT IN NSW**

I, JOHN EDWIN INNES PIKE, a delegate of CASA, under regulation 308 of the Civil Aviation Regulations (the Regulations), having taken into account all relevant considerations relating to the interests of safety, exempt Bell 206 helicopters operated by Sydney Helicopters Pty Ltd and engaged in regular public transport operations from compliance with subregulation 92A (3) of the Regulations when taking off from, or landing at, the Parramatta Heliport, NSW.

**[Signed John Pike]**

JOHN PIKE

Deputy Director

1 November 1996

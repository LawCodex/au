# Superannuation Act 1976 - Declaration (13/05/1984)

SUPERANNUATION ACT 1976

DECLARATION

I, John Sydney Dawkins, Minister of State for Finance:-

(a) in pursuance of section 134 of the Superannuation

Act 1976 declare the Superannuation Scheme for

Australian Universities to be an eligible

superannuation scheme for the purposes of

Division 3 of Part IX of that Act;

(b) in pursuance of sub-section 145(11) of the

Superannuation Act 1976, declare the

Superannuation Scheme for Australian Universities

to be an approved superannuation scheme for the

purposes of section 145 of that Act; and

(c) specify the first day of January, one thousand

nine hundred and eighty-three, as the day on

which these declarations are to be deemed to have

come into force.

Dated this thirteenth day of May 1984.

J S Dawkins

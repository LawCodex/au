Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Health Insurance (Section 3C Diagnostic Imaging Services – National Lung Cancer Screening Program) Determination 2025.*

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 3C(1) of the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

***bulk‑billed*** has the meaning given by the *Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Services Table) Regulations (No. 2) 2020.*

***computed tomography*** or ***CT*** has the meaning given by the *Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Services Table) Regulations (No. 2) 2020.*

***pack-years*** means the number of cigarettes a person smoked per day multiplied by the number of years the person has smoked.

***relevant provisions*** means all provisions, of the Act and regulations made under the Act, and the *National Health Act 1953* and regulations made under the *National Health Act 1953*, relating to medical services, professional services or items.

***relevant service*** means a health service, as defined in subsection 3C(8) of the Act, that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument.

***remote location*** has the meaning given by the *Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Services Table) Regulations (No. 2) 2020.*

***smoke or smoking*** means the action of inhaling and exhaling cigarette tobacco and includes packaged cigarettes and roll-your-own cigarettes (rollies). It does not include other forms of tobacco or nicotine smoking or consumption, such as vaping.

(2) Unless the contrary intention appears, a reference in this instrument to a provision of the Act or the *National Health Act 1953* or regulations made under the Act or under the *National Health Act 1953* as applied, adopted or incorporated in relation to specifying a matter is a reference to those provisions as in force from time to time and any other reference to provisions of an Act or regulations is a reference to those provisions as in force from time to time.

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

 clinically relevant service;

 diagnostic imaging services table;

 item;

 professional service.

###### 5 Treatment of relevant services

For subsection 3C(1) of the Act, a relevant service, provided in accordance with this instrument and as a clinically relevant service, is to be treated, for the relevant provisions, as if:

(a) it were both a professional service and a general medical service; and

(b) there were an item in the diagnostic imaging services table that:

(i) related to the service; and

(ii) specified for the service a fee in relation to each State, being the fee specified in the Schedule 1 in relation to the service.

###### 6 Eligibility for services

(1) A patient is eligible to receive a service specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument if they:

(a) receive a request for the service as part of the National Lung Cancer Screening Program when they are aged between 50 years and 70 years and 364 days; and

(b) are asymptomatic (show no signs or symptoms of lung cancer); and

(c) currently smoke or have quit smoking in the past ten years; and

(d) have a history of cigarette tobacco smoking of at least 30 pack-years.

(2) If a patient has been assessed as eligible to receive a service described in items 57410 and 57413, the patient remains eligible to receive these services until they turn 71 years and 364 days.

###### 7 Limitation on services

For the purposes of MBS items 57410 and 57413, clause 2.2.1(1)(a) of the diagnostic imaging services table does not apply if the services are provided as part of a mobile screening service provided more than 30 kilometres from a hospital that provides computed tomography radiology service or fixed radiology infrastructure.

(1) At the start of 1 July 2026 (the ***indexation time***), each amount covered by subsection (2) is replaced by the amount worked out using the following formula:

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

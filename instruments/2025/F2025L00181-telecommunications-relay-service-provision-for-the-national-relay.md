# Telecommunications (Relay Service Provision for the National Relay Service – Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2025

The AUSTRALIAN COMMUNICATIONS AND MEDIA AUTHORITY makes this Determination under subsection 110(3) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

Dated: 21 February 2025

Samantha Yorke

[signed]

Member

Cathy Rainsford

[signed]

General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Telecommunications (Relay Service Provision for the National Relay Service* – *Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences at the start of the day after the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed free of charge at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This Determination is made under subsection 110(3) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Repeal of the Telecommunications (Relay Service Provision for the National Relay Service – Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2015

The *Telecommunications (Relay Service Provision for the National Relay Service* – *Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2015* [F2015L00366] is repealed.

###### 5 Object

The object of this Determination is to determine the NRSP to be a section of the telecommunications industry for the purposes of Part 6 of the Telecommunications Act.

###### 6 Definitions

In this Determination:

***National Relay Service*** has the meaning given in section 5 of the TCPSS Act.

***NRS*** means the National Relay Service.

***NRSP*** means a person who provides a relay service for the NRS pursuant to a contract entered into, or a grant made, under section 14 of the TCPSS Act.

***relay service*** means a service that allows a user of the service to communicate with other persons by means of an intermediary that relays communications between the user and the other person or persons.

***TCPSS Act*** means the *Telecommunications (Consumer Protection and Service Standards) Act 1999*.

***Telecommunications Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 7 Application

This Determination applies to a person who is a NRSP.

###### 8 Specification of telecommunications activity

For the purposes of this Determination, the specified telecommunications activity is the provision of a relay service for the NRS.

###### 9 Section of telecommunications industry

Under subsection 110(3) of the Telecommunications Act, it is determined that,\
persons:

to whom this Determination applies; and

who carry on, or propose to carry on, the telecommunications activity specified in section 8;

constitute a section of the telecommunications industry for the purposes of Part 6 of the Telecommunications Act, and in accordance with subsection 110(4) of the Telecommunications Act, the section is to be identified as the “National Relay Service Provider”.

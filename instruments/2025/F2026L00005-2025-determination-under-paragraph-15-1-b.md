# 2025 Determination under Paragraph 15(1)(B)

AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION

Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997

**2025 DETERMINATION UNDER PARAGRAPH 15(1)(b)**

The AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION (ACCC) makes this determination under paragraph 15(1)(b) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997* and determines that the proportion of the ACCC’s costs for the 2023-24 financial year that is attributable to the ACCC’s telecommunications functions and powers is $13,319,393.

###### 3 Authority

These Rules are made under the *Judiciary Act 1903*.

###### 4 Sittings of the High Court in 2026

(1) Sittings of a Full Court of the High Court are to be held at Canberra and other places as required during the following periods:

Monday 2 February 2026 to Friday 13 February 2026

Tuesday 10 March 2026 to Friday 20 March 2026

Tuesday 7 April 2026 to Friday 17 April 2026

Monday 4 May 2026 to Friday 15 May 2026

Tuesday 9 June 2026 to Friday 19 June 2026

Monday 3 August 2026 to Friday 14 August 2026

Monday 7 September 2026 to Friday 18 September 2026

Tuesday 6 October 2026 to Friday 16 October 2026

Monday 2 November 2026 to Friday 13 November 2026

Monday 30 November 2026 to Friday 11 December 2026

(2) The winter recess begins on Saturday 20 June 2026.

(3) The summer recess begins on Saturday 12 December 2026.

###### 5 Repeal of these Rules

These Rules are repealed at the start of 1 January 2027.

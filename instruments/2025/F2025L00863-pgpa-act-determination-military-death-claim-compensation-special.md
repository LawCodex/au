# PGPA Act Determination (Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2025)

I, KATY GALLAGHER, Minister for Finance, make the following determination.

Dated 19 July 2025

KATY GALLAGHER

Minister for Finance

Contents

### Part 1—Establishment

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *PGPA Act Determination (Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2025)*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 78(1) and 78(3) of the PGPA Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Defence Force*** has the meaning given by the MRC Act.

***DRC Act*** means the *Safety Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988.*

***MDCC Instrument*** means the *PGPA Act (Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2015 – Establishment) Determination 2015/08.*

***MDCC Special Account 2015*** means the Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2015 established by the MDCC Instrument.

***MRC Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***PGPA Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***the special account*** means the Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2025 established by this instrument.

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(a) of the PGPA Act, the Military Death Claim Compensation Special Account 2025 is established as a special account.

###### 6 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(d) of the PGPA Act, the accountable authority responsible for the special account is the Secretary of the Department of Veterans’ Affairs.

### Part 2—Special account credits and debits

###### 7 Amounts that may be credited to the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the following amounts may be credited to the special account:

amounts received in accordance with a determination of a claim of the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Commission in relation to the death of a member of the Defence Force; and

amounts received for the benefit of a person resulting from a claim made under the DRC Act or the MRC Act in relation to the death of a member of the Defence Force.

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the following amounts must be credited to the special account:

the proceeds of amounts invested for the benefit of a person resulting from a claim made under the DRC Act or the MRC Act in relation to the death of a member of the Defence Force.

###### 8 Purposes of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(c) of the PGPA Act, the purposes of the special account, in relation to which amounts may be debited from the special account, are the following:

payment of compensation made under the DRC Act or the MRC Act in relation to the death of a member of the Defence Force;

to invest amounts for the benefit of a person under the DRC Act or the MRC Act in relation to the death of a member of the Defence Force;

to repay amounts where a court order, an Act or other law requires or permits the repayment of an amount received.

### Part 3—Transitional, revocation and repeal provisions

###### 9 Opening balance of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the amount in subsection 9(2) (***opening balance***) is required to be credited to the special account immediately after its establishment.

The opening balance is equal to the amount standing to the credit of the MDCC Special Account 2015 immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

###### 10 Revocation of MDCC Instrument

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the MDCC Instrument is revoked.

###### 11 Additional repeal provisions

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, Part 3 of this instrument is repealed at the end of the seventh day after the revocation of the MDCC Instrument under section 10 (***date of repeal***).

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the following definitions in section 4, are repealed on the date of repeal:

definition of MDCC Instrument; and

definition of MDCC Special Account 2015.

Despite subsection 11(1), the effects of Part 3 of this instrument remain in force after the date of repeal.

# Dental Benefits Amendment Rules (No.2) 2025

I, Mark Roddam, as delegate of the Minister for the Health and Ageing, make the following Rules.

Dated 18 September 2025

Mark Roddam

First Assistant Secretary,

Primary Care Division,

Department of Health, Disability and Ageing

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Dental Benefits Amendment Rules (No.2) 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences 1 January 2026.

(1) Each provision of this instrument specified in column 1 of the table commences, or is taken to have commenced, in accordance with column 2 of the table. Any other statement in column 2 has effect according to its terms.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 60(1) of the *Dental Benefits Act 2008.*

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

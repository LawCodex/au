# Census and Statistics (2026 Census Day) Proclamation 2025

I, the Honourable Sam Mostyn AC, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Proclamation.

Signed and Sealed with the

Great Seal of Australia on

10 July 2025

Sam Mostyn AC

Governor‑General

By Her Excellency’s Command

Dr Andrew Leigh

Assistant Minister for Productivity, Competition, Charities and Treasury\
Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

This instrument is made under subsection 8(2) of the *Census and Statistics Act 1905*.

###### 4 Census day 2026

The Census day for 2026 is appointed as 11 August 2026.

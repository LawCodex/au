###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 85(1) of Schedule 1 to the *Universities Accord (Cutting Student Debt by 20 Per Cent) Act 2025*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Universities Accord (Cutting Student Debt by 20 Per Cent) Act 2025*.

###### 5 Modification rules

(1) For the purposes of subitem 85(1) of Schedule 1 to the Act:

(a) provisions of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*, as amended by Schedule 1 of the Act, that refer to 1 June 2025, are modified and apply as if a reference to 1 June 2025 were a reference to 3 June 2025; and

(b) provisions of the *Australian Apprenticeship Support Loans Act 2014*, as amended by Schedule 1 of the Act, that refer to 1 June 2025, are modified and apply as if a reference to 1 June 2025 were a reference to 2 June 2025.

(c) provisions of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016*, as amended by Schedule 1 of the Act, that refer to 1 June 2025, are modified and apply as if a reference to 1 June 2025 were a reference to 2 June 2025.

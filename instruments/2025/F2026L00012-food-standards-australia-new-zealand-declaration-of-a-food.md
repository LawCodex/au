# Food Standards Australia New Zealand (Declaration of a Food) Declaration 2025

I, Rebecca White, Assistant Minister for Health, Disability and Aged Care, make the following declaration.

Dated 17 November 2025

Rebecca White

Assistant Minister for Health, Disability and Aged Care

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Food Standards Australia New Zealand (Declaration of a Food) Declaration 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument takes effect on the day after the later of the following:

the day the instrument is registered;

the day a copy of the instrument is published in the New Zealand Gazette.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 6(1) of the *Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991*.

***cell biomass*** has the same meaning as in Standard 3.4.1.

***cell line*** has the same meaning as in Standard 3.4.1.

***Standard 3.4.1*** means the *Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code – Standard 3.4.1 – Food safety requirements for processing of cell-cultured food* as in force on the day this instrument commences.

Note: Standard 3.4.1 can be found on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 5 Declaration of cell lines as a food

For the purposes of subsection 6(1) of the Act, cell lines are declared a food.

###### 6 Declaration of cell biomass as a food

For the purposes of subsection 6(1) of the Act, cell biomass is declared a food.

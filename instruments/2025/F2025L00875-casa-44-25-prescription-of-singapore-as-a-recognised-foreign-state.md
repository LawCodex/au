CASA 44/25 — Prescription of Singapore as a Recognised Foreign State Instrument 2025

1 Name

This instrument is the *CASA 44/25* — *Prescription of Singapore as a Recognised Foreign State Instrument 2025*.

2 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on 1 August 2025; and

(b) is repealed at the end of 31 July 2028.

3 Prescription

Singapore is prescribed as a recognised foreign State under regulation 61.047 of CASR.

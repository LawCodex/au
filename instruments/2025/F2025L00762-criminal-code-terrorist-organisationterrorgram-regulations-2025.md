###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Criminal Code Act 1995*.

###### 4 Terrorist organisation—Terrorgram

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***terrorist organisation*** in subsection 102.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as Terrorgram is specified.

(2) Terrorgram is also known by the name The Terrorgram Collective.

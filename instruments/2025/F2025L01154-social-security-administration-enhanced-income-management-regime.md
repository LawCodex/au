###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 123SLA(2), 123SLA(5), 123SLD(2), 123SLD(5), 123SLG(2), 123SLG(5), 123SLJ(2), 123SLJ(5), 123SM(1A), 123SM(2A), 123SP(1A) and 123SP(2A) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

***Declared Area Table*** means the Declared Area Table relating to the enhanced income management regime published by the Department of Social Services at the time this instrument is made.

Note: At the time this instrument is made, the Declared Area Table could be viewed on the Australian Government’s Guides to Social Policy Law website (https://guides.dss.gov.au).

***Human Services Department*** has the same meaning as in subsection 23(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***qualified portion*** has the same meaning as in section 123SB of the Act.

***subject to the enhanced income management regime*** has the same meaning as in section 123SB of the Act.

***subject to the income management regime*** has the same meaning as in section 123TC of the Act.

***test time*** has the same meaning as in sections 123SCA, 123SCB, 123SCC, 123SCJ, 123SCL, 123SD and 123SDA of the Act.

***voluntary enhanced income management agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 123SB of the Act.

###### 5 Qualified portion of payment

(1) For the purposes of subparagraphs 123SLA(1)(a)(ii), 123SLA(4)(a)(ii), 123SLD(1)(a)(ii), 123SLD(4)(a)(ii), 123SLG(1)(a)(ii), 123SLG(4)(a)(ii), 123SLJ(1)(a)(ii), 123SLJ(4)(a)(ii), 123SM(1)(a)(ii) and 123SM(2)(a)(ii) of the Act, if:

(a) a person is subject to the enhanced income management regime under section 123SCA, 123SCB, 123SCC, 123SCJ, 123SCL, 123SD or 123SDA of the Act; and

(b) immediately before the person’s test time, the person was not:

(i) subject to the enhanced income management regime under Part 3AA of the Act; or

(ii) subject to the income management regime under Part 3B of the Act;

the qualified portion of the person’s payment is 0% for the period worked out under subsection 6(1).

(2) For the purposes of subparagraphs 123SP(1)(a)(ii) and 123SP(2)(a)(ii) of the Act, if:

(a) a person is subject to the enhanced income management regime under section 123SE of the Act; and

(b) immediately before the time the person’s voluntary enhanced income management agreement comes into force, the person was not:

(i) subject to the enhanced income management regime under Part 3AA of the Act; or

(ii) subject to the income management regime under Part 3B of the Act;

the qualified portion of the person’s payment is 0% for the period worked out under subsection 6(2).

###### 6 Qualified portion period

(1) Subject to subsection (3), the period specified in subsection 5(1) begins on the day of the person’s test time and ends after:

(a) if the person is subject to the enhanced income management regime under section 123SCA, 123SCB, 123SCC or 123SCJ of the Act – 28 days; or

(b) if the person is subject to the enhanced income management regime under section 123SCL, 123SD or 123SDA of the Act – either 28 days or 56 days, depending on the number of days applicable to the person, as worked out by reference to the Declared Area Table.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the period specified in subsection 5(2) begins on the day the person’s voluntary enhanced income management agreement comes into force and ends after either 28 days or 56 days, depending on the number of days applicable to the person, as worked out by reference to the Declared Area Table.

(3) If:

(a) the period worked out under subsection (1) or (2) in relation to a person has not ended; and

(b) the person requests the Human Services Department to end the period,

the period ends on the day the request was made.

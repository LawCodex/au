# Migration (Approval of Primary and Fall-back Reporting Systems—International Passenger Cruise Ships) Instrument 2025

LIN 25/060

I, Stephanie Foster, Secretary of the Department of Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 15 September 2025

Stephanie Foster PSM

Secretary

Department of Home Affairs

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Approval of Primary and Fall-back Reporting Systems—International Passenger Cruise Ships) Instrument 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the following provisions of the *Migration Act 1958*:

(a) section 245J;

(b) section 245K

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***ABOC*** means the Australian Border Operations Centre.

***Act*** means the *Migration Act 1958*.

***Department*** means the Department of Home Affairs including the Australian Border Force.

***international passenger cruise ship*** has the meaning given by regulation 3.13 of the Regulations.

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

### Part 2—Primary reporting systems for crew

###### 6 Approval of primary reporting system—crew

For the purposes of section 245J of the Act, the following system is the approved primary reporting system for each member of the crew of an international passenger cruise ship.

(a) the interactive Advance Passenger Information system called the Advance Passenger Processing System, as provided by C P S Systems Pty Ltd (ABN 64 000 965 551, ACN 000 965 551), part of the Société Internationale de Télécommunications Aéronautiques NaamlozeVennootschap.

### Part 4—Fall-back reporting systems for passengers and crew

###### 10 Approval of fall-back reporting systems

(1) For the purposes of section 245K of the Act, the following systems are approved as fall-back reporting systems for each passenger and crew of an international passenger cruise ship:

(a) the ABOC, by telephone to either of the following telephone numbers in Australia:

(i) 1300 368 126; or

(ii) (02) 6264 1301; or

(b) the ABOC, by email: ABOC@abf.gov.au; or

(c) the ABOC, by SITATEX to the address CBRIXCR; or

(d) APPchat.

###### 11 Information to be reported under fall-back reporting systems

For the purposes of subsection 245K(2) of the Act, the information in the following table is specified to be reported about each passenger and member of the crew of an international passenger cruise ship arriving in Australia.

| Item | Information |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Lloyd’s Shipping Number (the unique seven digit number assigned by the Lloyd’s Register of Ships), port of arrival of the ship and the expected date of arrival of the ship. |
| 2 | Where the operator has confirmed that each passenger and each crew member who has boarded the ship, each person holds:<br>(a) a travel document, other than an Australian or New Zealand passport, and a visa which is in effect that permits the person to travel to and enter Australia; or<br>(b) a current Australian or New Zealand passport. |
| 3 | For each passenger and each crew member whom the operator has not confirmed as falling within Item 2, whether the operator has obtained up-lift approval from the ABOC. |

## Schedule 1—Amendment

**Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Emergency Service Facilities – Queensland) Instrument 2015**

**1 Section 5, table item 2**

Omit “Queensland Fire and Rescue Service”, substitute “Queensland Fire and Rescue”.

**2 Section 5, table item 2**

Omit “Fortitude Valley,”, “Ipswich,” and “Robina,”.

**3 Section 5, table item 2**

After “South Townsville,”, insert “Toowoomba,”.

**4 Section 5, table item 3**

Omit “Queensland Ambulance Service and Queensland Fire and Rescue Service”, substitute “Queensland Ambulance Service and Queensland Fire and Rescue”.

**5 Section 5, table item 3**

Omit “, Toowoomba”.

**6 Section 5, table item 4**

After “Buderim,”, insert “Coomera,”.

**7 Section 5, table item 4**

After “Lutwyche,”, insert “Parkhurst,”.

**8 Section 5, table item 4**

After “Southport”, insert “, Toowoomba”.

**9 Section 5, table item 6**

After “Garbutt (Townsville),”, insert “Gunyarra (Whitsunday),”.

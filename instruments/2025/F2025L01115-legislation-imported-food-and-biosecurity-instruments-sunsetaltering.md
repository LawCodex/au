###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 51A(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

###### 4 Aligning of sunsetting

The following instruments are repealed by section 51A of the *Legislation Act 2003* on 1 April 2031:

(a) *Imported Food Charges (Collection) Regulation 2015*;

(b) *Imported Food Charges (Imposition—Customs) Regulation 2015*;

(c) *Imported Food Charges (Imposition—General) Regulation 2015*;

(d) *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (Customs) Regulation 2016*;

(e) *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Regulation 2016*.

###### 5 Repeal of this instrument

This instrument is repealed at the start of 2 April 2031.

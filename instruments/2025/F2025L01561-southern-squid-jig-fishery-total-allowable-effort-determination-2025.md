###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Southern Squid Jig Fishery (Total Allowable Effort) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 January 2026.

###### 3 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 31 December 2026.

###### 4 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 11(1) of the Management Plan for the purpose of subsection 17(6)(aa) of the Act.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Management Plan*** means *Southern Squid Jig Fishery Management Plan 2005.*

***Act*** means *the Fisheries Management Act 1991.*

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3 of the Management Plan, including the following:

***fishery;***

***fishing year;***

***Southern Squid Jig Fishery;***

***squid jigging;***

***squid jigging machine;***

***standard squid jigging machine;*** and

***total allowable effort or TAE***.

###### 6 Determination of total allowable effort

The total allowable effort for the Southern Squid Jig Fishery for the 2026 fishing year, commencing on 1 January 2026 and ending on 31 December 2026 (inclusive), is 550 standard squid jigging machines.

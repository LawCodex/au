###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *International Monetary Agreements Act 1947*.

Note: Under subsection 8B(3) of the *International Monetary Agreements Act 1947*, the Treasurer may give notice of an amendment or renewal of the New Arrangements to Borrow by a decision of the Executive Board of the International Monetary Fund. Upon commencement of this instrument, the Treasurer may direct that an amount be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund to enable Australia to carry out its obligations under the amended or renewed New Arrangements to Borrow.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *International Monetary Agreements Act 1947* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

***the Act*** means the *International Monetary Agreements Act 1947*.

### Part 2—Notice of a decision of the International Monetary Fund

###### 5 Giving of notice of a decision

Under subsection 8B(3) of the Act, Decision No. 17794‑(24/78) of the Executive Board of the Fund, dated 19 July 2024, relating to the amendment or renewal of the New Arrangements to Borrow, is notified.

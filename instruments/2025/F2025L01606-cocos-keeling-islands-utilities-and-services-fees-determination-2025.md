###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 7 of the *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services Ordinance 2016*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act or section 5 of the Ordinance, including the following:

(a) fee;

(b) utility or service.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Act 1955*.

***aircraft*** has the same meaning as in section 3 of the *Civil Aviation Act 1988*.

***daylight operations*** means 7:01 am to 6 pm.

***deputy harbour master*** means a person appointed as a deputy harbour master of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands under section 7A of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CKI).

***function***, when used in Part 3, has the same meaning as in section 5 of the *Interpretation Act 1984* (WA)(CKI).

***GRT*** means gross registered tonne.

***harbour master*** means:

(a) the person appointed as the harbour master of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands under section 4 of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CKI); and

(b) a person appointed as an acting harbour master of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands under paragraph 52(1)(b) of the *Interpretation Act 1984* (WA)(CKI).

***inward cargo*** means goods that have been unloaded from a vessel.

***MTOW*** means maximum take-off weight.

***night operations*** means 6:01 pm to 7 am.

***Ordinance*** means the *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services Ordinance 2016*.

***outward cargo*** means goods that are intended to be loaded onto a vessel.

***Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands*** means the area declared to be the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands under section 10 of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CKI).

***TEU*** means twenty-foot equivalent unit.

***vessel*** has the same meaning as in section 14 of the *Navigation Act 2012*.

***wharf area*** means the Rumah Baru Wharf on West Island and the limits of that wharf used for the movement or storage of goods.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 7(3) of the Ordinance, the fees determined in subsection (1) apply on and from the date on which this instrument commences.

Payment of fees

(3) For the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(a) of the Ordinance, a fee determined in subsection (1) must be paid by electronic funds transfer to a bank account nominated by the Administrator or a person acting on behalf of the Administrator.

(4) For the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(b) of the Ordinance, a fee determined in subsection (1) is payable by the operator, owner or hirer of the aircraft to which the fee relates within 30 days of the date of the invoice issued in relation to the fee.

(5) An invoice mentioned in subsection (4) may be issued by the Administrator or a person acting on behalf of the Administrator.

###### 6 Waiver of airport facilities fees

(1) The Administrator may waive a fee determined in subsection 5(1), in relation to a passenger air service, on written application by:

(a) the operator, owner or hirer of the aircraft to which the fee relates; or

(b) the manager of Cocos (Keeling) Islands Airport.

(2) An application to waive a fee under this section must include the following:

(a) the period for which the waiver is requested;

(b) an explanation of how the waiver will benefit the Cocos (Keeling) Islands community.

(3) Before deciding to waive a fee under this section, the Administrator must:

(a) consider whether the waiver will benefit the Cocos (Keeling) Islands community; and

(b) be satisfied that the benefit of the waiver will outweigh any loss of revenue; and

(c) be satisfied that the waiver will not have a detrimental impact on any contracted air services for Cocos (Keeling) Islands Airport.

(4) If the Administrator waives a fee under this section, they must provide written notice of the waiver to:

(a) the operator, owner or hirer of the aircraft to which the fee relates; and

(b) the manager of Cocos (Keeling) Islands Airport.

(5) The written notice must specify the period for which the waiver is in force.

###### 8 Inward cargo storage

(1) The harbour master may, on their own initiative or on written application by the owner of the inward cargo, vary the period determined in column 2 of item 4 in the table in subsection 7(1) (the ***determined period***) in relation to inward cargo by publishing a written notice specifying a longer period.

(2) Before deciding to vary the determined period, the harbour master must consider whether any of the following circumstances exist in relation to inward cargo that is, or may be, subject to a fee under subsection 7(1) to which the determined period relates:

(a) weather events that cause unsafe conditions for the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(b) failures of equipment at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands that prevents or delays the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(c) vessel congestion at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands that prevents or delays the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(d) any other exceptional circumstances that, in the interests of fairness or safety, require a longer period to remove the inward cargo from the wharf area.

(3) A notice published under subsection (1) of this section:

(a) must be provided to the owner of the inward cargo or their shipping agent; and

(b) may be published in a manner that informs users of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands and members of the public of the longer period.

(4) A deputy harbour master may perform the functions of the harbour master under this section.

###### 9 Outward cargo storage

(1) The harbour master may, on their own initiative or on written application by the owner of the outward cargo, vary the period determined in column 2 of item 4 in the table in subsection 7(1) (the ***determined period***) in relation to outward cargo by publishing a written notice specifying a longer period.

(2) Before deciding to vary the determined period, the harbour master must consider whether any of the following circumstances exist in relation to outward cargo that is, or may be, subject to a fee under subsection 7(1) to which the determined period relates:

(a) weather events that cause unsafe conditions for vessel berthing at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands, or the handling of outward cargo in the wharf area, within the determined period;

(b) failures of equipment at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands that prevents or delays the removal of outward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(c) vessel congestion at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands that prevents or delays the removal of outward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(d) delayed arrival, beyond the determined period for the storage of the outward cargo in the wharf area, of the vessel at the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands that is to collect the outward cargo;

(e) any other exceptional circumstances that, in the interests of fairness or safety, require a longer period to remove the outward cargo from the wharf area.

(3) A notice published under subsection (1) of this section:

(a) must be provided to the owner of the outward cargo or their shipping agent; and

(b) may be published in a manner that informs users of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands and members of the public of the longer period.

(4) A deputy harbour master may perform the functions of the harbour master under this section.

###### 10 Waiver of marine traffic and harbour facilities fees

(1) The harbour master may, on their own initiative or on written application by the person to whom the fee applies, waive a fee determined in subsection 7(1).

(2) Before deciding to waive a fee determined in subsection 7(1), the harbour master must consider whether any of the following circumstances exist in relation to the utility or service to which the fee relates:

(a) if the fee relates to storage of inward cargo or outward cargo in the wharf area—any of the circumstances mentioned in subsection 8(2) or 9(2);

(b) if the fee relates to a utility or service set out in column 1 of any item in the table in subsection 7(1) that is provided to a member of the Cocos (Keeling) Islands community—the benefit to the community in waiving the fee;

(c) in any case—any other circumstances in which the harbour master is satisfied it is appropriate to waive the fee.

(3) If the harbour master waives a fee under subsection (1) of this section, written notice of the waiver:

(a) must be provided to the person who would otherwise be required to pay the fee or, if the fee relates to cargo, the person’s shipping agent; and

(b) may be published in a manner that informs users of the Port of Cocos (Keeling) Islands and members of the public of the waiver.

(4) The written notice must specify the period for which the waiver is in force.

(5) A deputy harbour master may perform the functions of the harbour master under this section.

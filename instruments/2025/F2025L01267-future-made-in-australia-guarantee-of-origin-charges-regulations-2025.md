### Part 2—Charges

###### 5 Charge payable in relation to production profile

(1) For the purposes of subsection 9(2) of the Act, the prescribed amount of charge on a production profile for a financial year ending on or after 30 June 2026 is:

(a) if the production profile is a registered profile on 1 July of the financial year—$8,166; or

(b) if the production profile becomes a registered profile after 1 July of the financial year—the amount calculated using the formula in subsection (2) of this section.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the formula is:

[image: Start formula start fraction Period remaining in the financial year over Number of days in the financial year times $8,166 end formula]

where:

***period remaining in the financial year*** means the number of days in the period:

(a) beginning on the day in the financial year that the production profile becomes a registered profile; and

(b) ending on 30 June of the financial year.

(3) The amount calculated under subsection (2) must be rounded to the nearest cent (rounding 0.5 cents upwards).

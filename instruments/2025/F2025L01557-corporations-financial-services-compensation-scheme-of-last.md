# Corporations (Financial Services Compensation Scheme of Last Resort—Special Levy) Determination 2025

I, Daniel Mulino, Assistant Treasury and Minister for Financial Services, having been notified under subsection 1069F(3) of the *Corporations Act 2001* (the ***Act***) that a revised claims, fees and costs estimate for the 2025-26 levy period for the licensed personal advice sub‑sector could cause the sub-sector levy cap for the levy period and the sub-sector to be exceeded, and being satisfied of the matters set out in subsection 1069H(5) of the Act,

make the following determination.

Dated 11 December 2025

Dr Daniel Mulino

Assistant Treasurer\
Minister for Financial Services

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Corporations Act 2001* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

***2025-26 levy period*** means the 12-month period starting from 1 July 2025.

***ASIC Levy Regulations*** means the *ASIC Supervisory Cost Recovery Levy Regulations 2017*.

***claims handling and settling services providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 72AA(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations*.*

***credit intermediaries sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 25(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***credit providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 26(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***custodians sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by section 31 of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***deposit product providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 27(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***insurance product distributors sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by section 70 of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***insurance product providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025‑26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 72(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***large futures exchange participants sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 64(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***large securities exchange participants sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 65(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***licensees that provide only general advice to retail or wholesale clients sub‑sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by section 40 of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***licensees that provide personal advice on relevant financial products to retail clients sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 43(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***licensees that provide personal advice to retail clients on only products that are not relevant financial products sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 42(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***managed discretionary account providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 32(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***margin lenders sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 23(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***operators of investor directed portfolio services sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 34(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***payment product providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 28(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***responsible entities sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 35(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***retail over‑the‑counter derivatives issuers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 61(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***risk management product providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 71(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***securities dealers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 67(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***small and medium amount credit providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 24(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***sub-sector*** has the same meaning as in the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***superannuation trustees sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 36(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

***the Act*** means the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***traditional trustee company service providers sub-sector*** means the sub-sector that, for the 2025-26 levy period, has the meaning given by subsection 33(1) of the ASIC Levy Regulations.

### Part 2—Special levy for 2025-26 levy period for several sub‑sectors

###### 5 Special levy imposition

Under subsection 1069H(2) of the Act, for the purposes of paragraph 1069H(5)(a) of the Act and paragraph 9(b) of the *Financial Services Compensation Scheme of Last Resort Levy Act 2023*, a special levy has been specified as needing to be imposed by section 9 of that Act for the 2025-26 levy period in accordance with this instrument.

###### 7 Total amount of special levy

For the purposes of paragraph 1069H(5)(c) of the Act, the sum of the total amounts of special levy specified under section 6 of this instrument is $47,289,000.03.

Note: The sum specified in this section does *not* exceed the difference between:

the revised claims, fees and costs estimate for the levy period and the licensed personal advice sub-sector; and

the total amount of levy already paid as worked out under subsection 1069H(6) of the Act.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 6 of the *Private Health Insurance (Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Levy) Act 2007*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Private Health Insurance (Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Levy) Act 2007*.

###### 5 Levy imposition days

(1) For subsection 4(2) of the Act, each of the following days is a levy imposition day:

(a) for the financial year beginning on 1 July 2024 – 15 March 2025;

(b) for the financial year beginning on 1 July 2025 and for each subsequent financial year – 15 September in that financial year.

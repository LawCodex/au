# Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance (Declared Terrorist Incident—Bondi Beach) Declaration 2025

I, Jim Chalmers, Treasurer, being satisfied of the matters set out in subsection 6(1) of the *Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003*, make the following declaration.

Dated 16 December 2025

Dr Jim Chalmers

Treasurer

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

### Part 2—Declaration of terrorist incident

###### 5 Declaration of terrorist incident

(1) Under subsection 6(1) of the Act, the terrorist act covered by subsection (2) constitutes a declared terrorist incident for the purposes of the Act.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), this subsection covers the mass shooting event that occurred at Bondi Beach, New South Wales, on 14 December 2025.

# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Clothes Washing Machines Determination—Changes to Incorporated Instrument) Declaration (No. 1) 2025

I, Daniel Croucher, GEMS Regulator, make the following instrument.

Dated 19 September 2025

Daniel Croucher

GEMS Regulator

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Clothes Washing Machines Determination—Changes to Incorporated Instrument) Declaration (No. 1) 2025.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered*.*

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 34A(2) of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012.*

***AS/NZS 2040.1:2021*** means *Australian/New Zealand Standard 2040.1:2021 Performance of household electrical appliances – Clothes washing machines – Part 1: Methods for measuring performance, energy and water consumption*, as in force or existing on the day the Determination was made.

Note: AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 is available from Standards Australia Limited.

***AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 Amd 1:2025*** means *Australian/New Zealand Standard 2040.1:2021 Performance of household electrical appliances – Clothes washing machines – Part 1: Methods for measuring performance, energy and water consumption Amendment No.1*, made on 25 July 2025.

Note: AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 Amd 1:2025 is available from Standards Australia Limited.

***Determination*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Clothes Washing Machines) Determination 2024.*

###### 5 Application to incorporated instrument

This instrument applies to AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 as incorporated in the Determination.

###### 6 Specified changes

For the purposes of subsection 34A(2) of the Act, AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 has effect in relation to the Determination as if AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 contains the changes specified in AS/NZS 2040.1:2021 Amd 1:2025.

###### 7 Repeal

This instrument is repealed at the same time the Determination is revoked.

Note 1: This instrument will be repealed under subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901.*

Note 2: Section 35 of the Act provides for the revocation of GEMS determinations.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 11(2) of the *Nature Repair Act 2023.*

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) biodiversity;

(b) biodiversity project;

(c) Register.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Nature Repair Act 2023*.

###### 5 Day on or after which applications may be made

For the purposes of subsection 11(2) of the Act, applications to approve the registration of a biodiversity project on the Register may be made on or after 1 March 2025.

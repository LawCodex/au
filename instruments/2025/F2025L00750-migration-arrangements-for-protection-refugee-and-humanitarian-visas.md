# Migration (Arrangements for Protection, Refugee and Humanitarian Visas) Amendment (Internet Lodgement) Instrument 2025

LIN 25/050

I, Lavinia Mitchell, delegate of the Minister, make the following instrument.

Dated 25 June 2025

Lavinia Mitchell

Assistant Secretary (Senior Executive Band 1)

Humanitarian Policy and Child Wellbeing Branch

Refugee, Humanitarian and Settlement Division

Department of Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Arrangements for Protection, Refugee and Humanitarian Visas) Amendment (Internet Lodgement) Instrument 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) 1 July 2025; and

(b) the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument made under the following provisions of the *Migration Regulations 1994*:

(a) subregulation 2.07(5);

(b) item 1402 of Schedule 1.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

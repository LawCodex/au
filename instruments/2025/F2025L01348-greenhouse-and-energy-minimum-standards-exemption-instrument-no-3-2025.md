# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument (No. 3) 2025

I, Melanie Ford, being the person occupying the position of Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) Regulator, make the following instrument.

Dated 24 October 2025

Melanie Ford

GEMS Regulator

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Exemption) Instrument (No. 3) 2025.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 37(1) of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012.*

***Determination*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Incandescent Lamps for General Lighting Services) Determination 2024*.

***exempt models***: see section 5.

###### 5 Exemption

The models specified in clause 1 of Schedule 1 (the ***exempt models***) are exempt from the requirements of the Determination as described at clause 2 of Schedule 1 with respect to the supplies of the exempt models specified at clause 3 of Schedule 1.

###### 6 Conditions of exemption

The conditions at clause 4 of the Schedule are specified for the purposes of subsection 37(2) of the Act.

###### 7 Repeal

This instrument is repealed five years after the date of commencement.

## Schedule 1—Exemption and conditions

**1 Specification of exempt models**

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Item | Model Number | Description |
| 1 | B517UK | Mini GU10 35W Narrow Beam Halogen |
| 2 | B516UK | Mini GU10 28W Narrow Beam Halogen |
| 3 | B505 | Halogen 35W SES |
| 4 | B510 | Halogen 12V 20W GY6.35 |

**2 Requirements from which models are exempt**

The requirements of the Determination are described in column 2 in the table below and are located in the part of the Determination specified in column 3 for that item.

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Item | Type of Requirements | Relevant Part of Determination |
| 1 | GEMS level requirements | Part 3 of the Determination |
| 2 | GEMS labelling requirements | Part 4 of the Determination |
| 3 | GEMS other requirements | Part 5 of the Determination |

**3 Supplies to which the exemption applies**

This exemption applies in relation to any supplies of the exempt models.

**4 Specification of conditions of exemption**

**This exemption is subject to the following conditions:**

Products of exempt models that are offered for sale or sold must clearly display on the exterior of the packaging of those products that the product is for use in lava lamps only;

**Where products of exempt models are offered for sale or sold online, the web page where the product(s) is offered for sale or sold must clearly display that the product(s) is for use in lava lamps only.**

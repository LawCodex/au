# Higher Education Support Provider Approval (Carnegie Mellon University) Revocation 2025

I, Annette Cannell, delegate of the Minister for Education, make this instrument under section 22‑2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Dated 25 November 2025

Annette Cannell

A/g Assistant Secretary

HELP and Student Experience Branch

Department of Education

Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support Provider Approval (Carnegie Mellon University) Revocation 2025.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Notice of Revocation

The approval of Carnegie Mellon University (ACN 116 769 423) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act.

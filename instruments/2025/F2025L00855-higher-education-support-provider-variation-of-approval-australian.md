# Higher Education Support (Provider Variation of Approval — Australian University College of Divinity Ltd) Instrument 2025

Higher Education Support (Provider Variation of Approval ­­— Australian University College of Divinity Ltd) Instrument 2025

I, Larissa Hinds, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 26 June 2025

Larissa Hinds

Assistant Secretary\
HELP, Student and Provider Integrity Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Provider Variation of Approval — Australian University College of Divinity Ltd) Instrument 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-70 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Notice of variation of approval

For the purposes of section 16-70 of the Act, the approval of the higher education provider previously known as Sydney College of Divinity Ltd (ABN 39 002 653 036) is varied to reflect that the higher education provider is now named Australian University College of Divinity Ltd.

# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 1 of 2025

## Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 1 of 2025

Provision of statistical information about financial sector entities to the Reserve Bank of Australia and the Australian Bureau of Statistics during the 2024-25 financial year

### Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Clare Gibney, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 51(1)(a) of the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998, fix the charges specified in the attached Schedule of charges in respect of the specified services and facilities provided by APRA.

The instrument commences on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Dated: 23 June 2025

Clare Gibney\
Executive Director\
Chief of Staff and Enterprise Services

**Interpretation**\
\
In this Notice:

## ABS means the Australian Bureau of Statistics. Act means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998. API means Application Programming Interface. APRA means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority. charge means a charge that reasonably relates to the costs and expenses incurred or to be incurred by APRA in relation to the matters to which the charge relates. EFS means Economic and Financial Statistics. financial sector entity has the meaning in section 5 of the Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001. RBA means the Reserve Bank of Australia.

## Schedule of charges

| **Column 1**<br>**Services for which the charge is imposed** | **Column 2**<br>**Amount of the charge** | **Column 3**<br>**Person required to pay the charge** | **Column 4**<br>**When the charge is to be paid** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provision to the ABS during the 2024-25 financial year of the statistical information specified in Attachment 1 concerning financial sector entities. | $243,492 | ABS | 28 days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |
| Provision to the RBA during the 2024-25 financial year of the statistical information specified in Attachment 2 concerning financial sector entities. | $420,486 | RBA | 28 days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |
| The development cost of the EFS collection system to be recovered for the 2024-25 financial year from the ABS. | $14,291 | ABS | 28 days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |
| The development cost of the EFS collection system to be recovered for the 2024-25 financial year from the RBA. | $18,189 | RBA | 28 days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |
| The maintenance and support costs of the data sharing APIs used by the RBA during the 2024-25 financial year. | $97,426 | RBA | 28 days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |

In relation to the Schedule of charges above:

(a) Each of the charges is not subject to GST under the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*.\
(b) APRA may waive or refund, in whole or part, any amount of charge specified in the Schedule of charges if APRA is satisfied that it is appropriate in the circumstances. A waiver is only effective if it is given by APRA in writing.

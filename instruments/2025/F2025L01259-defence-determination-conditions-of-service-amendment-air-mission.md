###### 13.2.5C Class of air travel for members on air mission travel

<table>
<tr>
<td>1.</td>
<td colspan="3">A member is eligible for travel in economy class if they are crew or mission related personnel who are required to travel for the purpose of an Air Force air mission.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2.</td>
<td colspan="3">Despite subsection 1, a member is eligible to travel at a higher class if the decision maker is satisfied that it is reasonable having regard to all of the following.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td colspan="2">The reasons for the travel.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>b.</td>
<td colspan="2">The costs and benefits of the travel at the higher class.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>c.</td>
<td colspan="2">The nature of the duty overseas.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3.</td>
<td colspan="3">To avoid doubt, section 13.2.5 applies if the member is pregnant.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>4.</td>
<td colspan="3">In this section, <b>decision maker</b> means a member who meets one of the following.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td colspan="2">They occupy one of the following positions in an Air Force Force Element Group and hold the rank of Group Captain, or the APS classification of EL2, or higher.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td></td>
<td>i.</td>
<td>Commander.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td></td>
<td>ii.</td>
<td>Deputy Commander.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td></td>
<td>iii.</td>
<td>Officer Commanding.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>b.</td>
<td colspan="2">They occupy one of the following positions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td></td>
<td>i.</td>
<td>Director Air Mobility Division.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td></td>
<td>ii.</td>
<td>Director Special Purpose Aircraft.</td>
</tr>
</table>

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***F-111 deseal/reseal payment*** means a one-off ex-gratia lump sum payment made by the Department of Veterans’ Affairs to a participant in the F-111 deseal/reseal program.

***F-111 deseal/reseal program*** means the deseal/reseal program conducted by the Royal Australian Air Force and their contractors to undertake maintenance on F-111 aircraft in Amberley, Queensland, between 1973 and 2000.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Exempt lump sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, a F-111 deseal/reseal payment is an exempt lump sum.

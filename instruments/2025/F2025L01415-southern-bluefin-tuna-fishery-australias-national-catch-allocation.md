Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery (Australia’s National Catch Allocation) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 December 2025.

###### 3 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 30 November 2026.

###### 4 Authority

This instrument is made pursuant to paragraph 17(6)(aa) of the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* and under subclause 17.2 of the *Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery Management Plan 1995.*

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in clause 3 of the Management Plan, including the following:

***SBT Fishery****; and*

***season****.*

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991.*

***Management Plan*** means the *Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery Management Plan 1995.*

###### 6 Determination of Australia’s national catch allocation

Australia’s national catch allocation for the fishing season in the Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery that commences on 1 December 2025 and ends on 30 November 2026 is 6,930,250 kilograms.

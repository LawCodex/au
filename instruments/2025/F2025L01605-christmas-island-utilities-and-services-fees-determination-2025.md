###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 7 of the *Christmas Island Utilities and Services Ordinance 2016*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act or section 5 of the Ordinance, including the following:

(a) Christmas Island;

(b) fee;

(c) utility or service.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Christmas Island Act 1958*.

***aircraft*** has the same meaning as in section 3 of the *Civil Aviation Act 1988*.

***bagged phosphate*** means phosphate contained in bulker bags but does not include bulk phosphate.

***daylight operations*** means 7:01 am to 6 pm.

***deputy harbour master*** means a person appointed as a deputy harbour master of the Port of Christmas Island under section 7A of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CI).

***function***, when used in Part 3, has the same meaning as in section 5 of the *Interpretation Act 1984* (WA)(CI).

***GRT*** means gross registered tonne.

***harbour master*** means:

(a) the person appointed as the harbour master of the Port of Christmas Island under section 4 of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CI); and

(b) a person appointed as an acting harbour master of the Port of Christmas Island under paragraph 52(1)(b) of the *Interpretation Act 1984* (WA)(CI).

***inward cargo*** means goods that have been unloaded from a vessel.

***MTOW*** means maximum take-off weight.

***night operations*** means 6:01 pm to 7 am.

***Ordinance*** means the *Christmas Island Utilities and Services Ordinance 2016*.

***outward cargo*** means goods that are intended to be loaded onto a vessel.

***Port of Christmas Island*** means the area declared to be the Port of Christmas Island under section 10 of the *Shipping and Pilotage Act 1967* (WA)(CI).

***recreational vessel*** means a vessel that is:

(a) not for use in connection with a commercial or governmental activity; and

(b) not more than 17.5 metres long.

***TEU*** means twenty-foot equivalent unit.

***vessel*** has the same meaning as in section 14 of the *Navigation Act 2012*.

***wharf area*** means the wharves appointed, and the limits of those wharves fixed, under section 15 of the *Customs Act 1901* by the notice published in Gazette No. GN 36 of 11 September 2002 on page 2415.

Payment of fees

(3) For the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(a) of the Ordinance, a fee determined in subsection (1) must be paid by electronic funds transfer to a bank account nominated by the Administrator or a person acting on behalf of the Administrator.

(5) An invoice mentioned in subsection (4) may be issued by the Administrator or a person acting on behalf of the Administrator.

###### 6 Waiver of airport facilities fees

(1) The Administrator may waive a fee determined in subsection 5(1), in relation to a passenger air service, on written application by:

(a) the operator, owner or hirer of the aircraft to which the fee relates; or

(b) the manager of Christmas Island Airport.

(2) An application to waive a fee under this section must include the following:

(a) the period for which the waiver is requested;

(b) an explanation of how the waiver will benefit the Christmas Island community.

(3) Before deciding to waive a fee under this section, the Administrator must:

(a) consider whether the waiver will benefit the Christmas Island community; and

(b) be satisfied that the benefit of the waiver will outweigh any loss of revenue; and

(c) be satisfied that the waiver will not have a detrimental impact on any contracted air services for Christmas Island Airport.

(4) If the Administrator waives a fee under this section, they must provide written notice of the waiver to:

(a) the operator, owner or hirer of the aircraft to which the fee relates; and

(b) the manager of Christmas Island Airport.

(5) The written notice must specify the period for which the waiver is in force.

###### 8 Inward cargo storage

(1) The harbour master may, on their own initiative or on written application by the owner of the inward cargo, vary the period determined in column 2 of item 5 in the table in subsection 7(1) (the ***determined period***) in relation to inward cargo by publishing a written notice specifying a longer period.

(2) Before deciding to vary the determined period, the harbour master must consider whether any of the following circumstances exist in relation to inward cargo that is, or may be, subject to a fee determined in the table in subsection 7(1):

(a) weather events that cause unsafe conditions for the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(b) failures of equipment at the Port of Christmas Island that prevents or delays the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(c) vessel congestion at the Port of Christmas Island that prevents or delays the removal of inward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(d) any other exceptional circumstances that, in the interests of fairness or safety, require a longer period to remove the inward cargo from the wharf area.

(3) A notice published under subsection (1) of this section:

(a) must be provided to the owner of the inward cargo or their shipping agent; and

(b) may be published in a manner that informs users of the Port of Christmas Island and members of the public of the longer period.

(4) A deputy harbour master may perform the functions of the harbour master under this section.

###### 9 Outward cargo storage

(1) The harbour master may, on their own initiative or on written application by the owner of the outward cargo, vary the period determined in column 2 of item 5 in the table in subsection 7(1) (the ***determined period***) in relation to outward cargo by publishing a written notice specifying a longer period.

(2) Before deciding to vary the determined period, the harbour master must consider whether any of the following circumstances exist in relation to outward cargo that is, or may be, subject to a fee determined in the table in subsection 7(1):

(a) weather events that cause unsafe conditions for vessel berthing at the Port of Christmas Island, or the handling of outward cargo in the wharf area, within the determined period;

(b) failures of equipment at the Port of Christmas Island that prevents or delays the removal of outward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(c) vessel congestion at the Port of Christmas Island that prevents or delays the removal of outward cargo from the wharf area within the determined period;

(d) delayed arrival, beyond the determined period for the storage of the outward cargo in the wharf area, of the vessel at the Port of Christmas Island that is to collect the outward cargo;

(e) any other exceptional circumstances that, in the interests of fairness or safety, require a longer period to remove the outward cargo from the wharf area.

(3) A notice published under subsection (1) of this section:

(a) must be provided to the owner of the outward cargo or their shipping agent; and

(b) may be published in a manner that informs users of the Port of Christmas Island and members of the public of the longer period.

(4) A deputy harbour master may perform the functions of the harbour master under this section.

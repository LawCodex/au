# Banking (restricted word or expression) determination No. 1 of 2025 – Credit Co-operative

**Banking (restricted word or expression) determination No. 1 of 2025 – Credit Co-operative**

Banking Act 1959

I, Ian Beckett, a delegate of APRA:

under subsection 66(5) of the *Banking Act 1959* (the Act), and subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, REVOKE *Banking (restricted word or expression) determination No. 1 of 2015* made on 6 August 2015; and

under subsection 66(5) of the Act, DETERMINE that the expression ‘credit co-operative’ is a restricted expression for the purposes of section 66 of the Act.

This instrument commences on the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Dated: 12 September 2025

Ian Beckett

Acting Executive Director

Policy and Advice Division

**Interpretation**

In this instrument:

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***restricted expression*** has the meaning given in subsection 66(4) of the Act.

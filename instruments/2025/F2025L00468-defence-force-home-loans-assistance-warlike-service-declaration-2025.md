# Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) (Warlike Service) Declaration 2025

I, MATT KEOGH, Minister for Defence Personnel, make the following Declaration under section 3C of the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Act 1990*.

Dated 27 March 2025

The Hon Matt Keogh MP

Minister for Defence Personnel

Contents

This instrument is the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) (Warlike Service) Declaration 2025*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 3C of the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Act 1990*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Act 1990*.

***ADF*** means the Australian Defence Force (within the meaning of the *Defence Act 1903*).

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Warlike service

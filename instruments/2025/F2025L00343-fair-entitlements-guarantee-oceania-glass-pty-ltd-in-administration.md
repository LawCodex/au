# Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Oceania Glass Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2025

I, MURRAY WATT, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated

10 March 2025

Murray Watt

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Oceania Glass Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2025.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 49 of the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012*.

###### 4 Declaration

The *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012* applies in relation to persons who were employed, but are no longer employed, by Oceania Glass Pty Ltd (Administrators Appointed) A.C.N. 630 152 206 which is an entity under administration under Part 5.3A of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

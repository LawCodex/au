# Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Communications Access Coordinator) Instrument (No. 2) 2025

made under section 6R of the

Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979

**Compilation No. 1**

**Compilation date:** 17 February 2026

**Includes amendments:** F2026L00106

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Communications Access Coordinator) Instrument (No. 2) 2025* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 17/02/2026 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au).

**Application, saving and transitional provisions**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. Any modifications affecting the law are accessible on the Register.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Communications Access Coordinator) Instrument (No. 2) 2025*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 6R of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*.

***APS1-6*** means, in relation to a position, a position that is at an approved classification mentioned in any of table items 1 to 6 under subsection (2).

***Criminal Justice Division*** means the work unit in the Department known as the Criminal Justice Division.

***Department*** means the Department of Home Affairs.

***Head of National Security*** means the SES3 position known by the title Head of National Security.

***ICP Determination*** means the *Interception Capability Plan Determination 2024,* made under subsection 195(4) of the Act.

***Identity Checks Determination*** means the *Telecommunications (Service Provider — Identity Checks for Prepaid Mobile Carriage Services) Determination 2017*, made under subsection 99(1) of the Telecommunications Act.

***Law Enforcement and Domestic Security Group*** means the work unit in the Department known as the Law Enforcement and Domestic Security Group.

***Office of the Communications Access Coordinator*** means the work unit in the Department known as the Office of the Communications Access Coordinator.

***position*** means a position in the Department.

***Telecommunications Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997.*

### Part 2—Communications Access Coordinator

###### 6 Specification of classes of persons

For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***Communications Access Coordinator*** in subsection 6R(1) of the Act, a person who from time to time holds, occupies or performs the duties of any of the following positions is specified:

(a) Head of National Security;

(b) an SES3 position in the Law Enforcement and Domestic Security Group;

(c) an SES1 or SES2 position in the Criminal Justice Division;

(d) an EL2 position in the Office of the Communications Access Coordinator;

(e) an EL1 position in the Office of the Communications Access Coordinator;

(f) an APS1-APS6 position in the Office of the Communications Access Coordinator.

Amending laws are annotated in the legislation history and amendment history.

The amendment history in endnote 4 provides information about amendments at the provision (generally section or equivalent) level. It also includes information about any provision of the compiled law that has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

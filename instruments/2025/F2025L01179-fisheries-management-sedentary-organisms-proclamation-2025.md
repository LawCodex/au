I, the Honourable Sam Mostyn AC, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Proclamation.

Great Seal of Australia on

18 September 2025

Sam Mostyn AC

Governor‑General

By Her Excellency’s Command

Julie Collins

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

###### 6 Sedentary organisms to which the Act applies—animals

(1) For the purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, an organism is a sedentary organism to which the Act applies if the organism:

(a) belongs to a phylum specified in column 1 of an item of the following table; and

(b) if one or more sub‑categories of the phylum is specified in column 2 of that item—belongs to one of the specified sub‑categories; and

(c) if a life stage is specified in column 3 of that item for the phylum or the sub‑category to which the phylum belongs—is in the specified life stage.

###### 7 Sedentary organisms to which the Act applies—plants

For the purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, an organism is a sedentary organism to which the Act applies if the organism belongs to:

(a) a division specified in column 1 of an item of the following table; and

(b) if one or more sub‑categories of the division is specified in column 2 of that item—one of the specified sub‑categories.

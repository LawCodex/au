This instrument is made under the *Export Finance and Insurance Corporation Act 1991*.

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Export Finance and Insurance Corporation Act 1991*.

### Part 2—Limitations

###### 6 Maximum contingent liability under Part 4 of the Act

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 68(1)(a) of the Act, the amount prescribed for contracts entered into, and guarantees given under, Part 4 of the Act (other than contracts of insurance entered into under section 22 of the Act) is $5 billion.

Note: Section 8 of this instrument provides for the conversion of amounts in foreign currency.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 68(1)(b) of the Act, the amount prescribed for contracts of insurance entered into under section 22 of the Act is $2 billion.

Note: Section 8 of this instrument provides for the conversion of amounts in foreign currency.

###### 7 Limit of total amount of loans under Part 4 of the Act

For the purposes of subsection 69(1) of the Act, the amount prescribed for money lent by EFIC under Part 4 of the Act and not repaid or written off is $6 billion.

Note: Section 8 of this instrument provides for the conversion of amounts in foreign currency.

###### 8 Conversion of amounts in foreign currency

In calculations for the purposes of paragraph 68(1)(a) or (b) or subsection 69(1) of the Act, an amount in foreign currency is to be converted into Australian currency using:

(a) the most recent exchange rate published by the Reserve Bank of Australia; or

(b) if the amount is in a currency for which the Reserve Bank of Australia does not publish an exchange rate—the current market exchange rate.

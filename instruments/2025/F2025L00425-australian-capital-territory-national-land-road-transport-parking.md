# Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) (Parking Authority) Declaration 2025

I, Karen Doran, Chief Executive of the National Capital Authority, make the following declaration.

Dated 24 March 2025

Karen Doran

Chief Executive of the National Capital Authority

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) (Parking Authority) Declaration 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences immediately after the commencement of *Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) Rules 2025*.

Note: The Rules commenced immediately after the Ordinance, which commenced on 1 April 2025.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 33(2) of the *Road Transport (Safety and Traffic Management) Regulation 2017* (ACT)(Cth), as applied by the *Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) Ordinance 2025* and the *Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) Rules 2025*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Ordinance or the Rules, including ***Ordinance***.

In this instrument:

***Rules*** means the *Australian Capital Territory National Land (Road Transport) Rules 2025*.

###### 5 Parking authority

The High Court of Australia is declared to be a parking authority for the area of Block 15, Section 28 of Parkes.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 24(3) of the *Public Service Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***APS Award*** means the *Australian Public Service Enterprise Award 2015.*

***APS employee*** means:

(a) a person engaged under section 22 of the Public Service Act; or

(b) a person who is engaged as an APS employee under section 72 of the Public Service Act.

***Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Act 2024*.

***Defence Agreement*** means the *Defence Enterprise Collective Agreement 2024*, as in operation immediately before the commencement of this Instrument.

***Director-General*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Act 2024*.

***Employee*** means an APS employee to whom this Instrument applies in accordance with section 5.

***Fair Work Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009.*

***Instrument*** means the *Public Service (Terms and Conditions of Employment) (Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator) Determination 2025.*

***NES*** means the National Employment Standards in Part 2-2 of the Fair Work Act.

***Public Service Act*** means the *Public Service Act 1999*.

###### 5 Application of this Instrument

This Instrument applies to all APS employees of the Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator other than a Senior Executive Service employee and equivalent levels.

###### 6 When this Instrument ceases to apply to employees

This Instrument ceases to apply to an employee if:

(a) an enterprise agreement applies to the employee (for the purposes of the Fair Work Act); or

(b) a workplace determination is made that covers the employee (for the purposes of the Fair Work Act).

Note: A workplace determination operates from the day on which it is made (see section 276 of the Fair Work Act).

###### 7 Terms and conditions of employment

(1) The terms and conditions of employment applying to an employee:

(a) are the terms and conditions of employment set out in the Defence Agreement, as amended by this Instrument; and

(b) are not any of the terms and conditions of employment set out in the *Australian Public Service Enterprise Award 2015*.

*Defence Agreement—modifications*

(2) The terms and conditions set out in the Defence Agreement (as applied by operation of subsection 7(1)) are modified as follows:

(a) a reference in the Defence Agreement to “agency” is a reference to the Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator (unless context provides otherwise);

(b) a reference in the Defence Agreement to the term “Agreement” is a reference to the Defence Agreement as applied by this Instrument (unless context provides otherwise);

(c) A reference in the Defence Agreement to the “commencement of the Agreement” or “commencement of this Agreement” is a reference to the commencement of this Instrument;

(d) a reference in the Defence Agreement to “Defence” is a reference to the Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator (unless context provides otherwise);

(e) a reference in the Defence Agreement to “the Department” is a reference to the Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator (unless context provides otherwise);

(f) a reference in the Defence Agreement to an employee covered by the Defence Agreement is a reference to an employee to whom this Instrument applies;

(g) references in the Defence Agreement to the National Workplace Relations Committee in clause K1 of the Defence Agreement are omitted and substituted with “Australian Naval Nuclear Power Safety Regulator Workforce Consultative Committee where one is in place”;

(h) clause G4 of the Defence Agreement, which sets out procedures for Individual Flexibility Arrangements, is omitted;

(i) clause K2 of the Defence Agreement, which relates to Group Consultative Committees, is omitted;

(j) a reference in the Defence Agreement to “the Secretary of the Department of Defence” is a reference to the Director-General;

(k) clause A3.2 of the Defence Agreement, which specifies the nominal expiry date of that Agreement, is omitted.

###### 8 Procedure for dealing with disputes

To avoid doubt, clause K11 of the Defence Agreement, which provides for the dispute resolution procedure, as modified by operation of subsection 7(1) is:

(a) a term or condition of employment applying to an employee to whom this Instrument applies; and

(b) for the purposes of paragraph 738(d) of the Fair Work Act, a term that provides for a procedure for dealing with disputes arising under this Instrument.

###### 9 Interaction with other determinations made under the Public Service Act

To the extent possible, this Instrument is to operate concurrently with any determination made under subsection 24(1) of the Public Service Act.

Without limiting subsection (1), this Instrument does not prevent a determination made under subsection 24(1) of the Public Service Act increasing the amount of salaries, allowances or other monetary entitlements provided under this Instrument.

###### 10 Interaction with the NES and the APS Award

This Instrument has no effect to the extent that it would exclude the NES or any provision of the NES.

While this Instrument is in force, the APS Award does not apply to employees covered by this Instrument.

**Note:** The effect of this subsection is that this instrument will interact with the APS Award in the same way as an enterprise agreement. See section 57 of the Fair Work Act.

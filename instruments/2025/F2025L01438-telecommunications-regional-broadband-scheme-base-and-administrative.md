###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraphs 12(4)(b) and 16(8)(f) of the *Telecommunications (Regional Broadband Scheme) Charge Act 2020*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3 of the Act, including the following:

(a) administrative cost component;

(b) base component;

(c) eligible financial year; and

(d) month.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications (Regional Broadband Scheme) Charge Act 2020*.

###### 5 Determination of base component for each month in the sixth eligible financial year

For the purposes of paragraph 12(2)(d) of the Act, the amount of $2.1706 is determined for each of the months in the sixth eligible financial year.

###### 6 Determination of administrative cost component for each month in the sixth eligible financial year

For the purposes of paragraph 16(6)(d) of the Act, the amount of $0.00665 is determined for each of the months in the sixth eligible financial year.

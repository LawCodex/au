# Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) (Paragraph 15(1)(d) Estimated Total Grants) Determination 2025

**Commonwealth of Australia**

**TELECOMMUNICATIONS (CARRIER LICENCE CHARGES)**\
**(PARAGRAPH 15(1)(d) ESTIMATED TOTAL GRANTS) DETERMINATION 2025**

I, MICHELLE ROWLAND, Minister for Communications, under paragraph 15(1)(d) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997,* determine that the estimated total amount of grants likely to be made during the 2024-25 financial year under section 593 of the Telecommunications Act 1997 is $2,721,000.

Dated 28 January 2025

MICHELLE ROWLAND

Minister for Communications

# Migration (English Language Requirements for Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) Visas) Instrument 2025

LIN 25/089

I, Matt Thistlethwaite, Assistant Minister for Immigration, make the following instrument.

Dated 5 August 2025

Matt Thistlethwaite

Assistant Minister for Immigration

Contents

## Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (English Language Requirements for Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) Visas) Instrument 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 7 August 2025.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under clause 485.212 of Schedule 2 to the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

average band score means a test score calculated from the makeup of the scores received for each of the English test components.

English test component means each of the following in relation to an English language test:

(a) listening;

(b) reading;

(c) writing; and

(d) speaking.

***overall band score*** means a test score calculated from the makeup of the scores received for each of the English test components.

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

***total band score*** means a test score calculated form the makeup of the scores received for each of the English test components.

***visa applicant*** means an applicant for a Skilled (Provisional) (Class VC) visa in one of the following streams within the Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) visa:

(a) Post-Vocational Education Work stream; or

(b) Post-Higher Education Work stream.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Part 2—Specified English language requirements

###### 6 Specified English language tests

For subparagraph 485.212(1)(a)(i) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the following English language tests are specified:

(a) Cambridge Advanced test (C1 Advanced);

(b) Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program General (CELPIP General);

(c) International English Language Testing System Academic (IELTS Academic);

(d) International English Language Testing System General Training (IELTS General Training);

(e) LANGUAGECERT Academic Test (LANGUAGECERT Academic);

(f) Michigan English Test (MET);

(g) Occupational English Test (OET);

(h) Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic);

(i) Test of English as a Foreign Language internet based test (TOEFL iBT).

###### 7 Specified required test scores to be achieved

For subparagraph 485.212(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the ***required test scores*** to be achieved, in one of the English language tests, specified in section 6, unless the test was a C1 Advanced, are specified:

(a) in Schedule 1; or

(b) if the visa applicant is a Hong Kong passport holder or British National (Overseas) passport holder—in Schedule 2.

###### 8 Specified period and requirements

For subparagraph 485.212(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the following period and requirements are specified:

(a) the visa applicant achieved the required test score specified under section 7 within the period of 1 year before the day on which the applicant made the application for the relevant visa.

###### 9 Specified passports in relation to which English language proficiency requirements do not apply

For paragraph 485.212(1)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, a valid passport issued by one of the following countries to a citizen of that country is specified:

(a) Canada;

(b) New Zealand;

(c) the Republic of Ireland;

(d) the United Kingdom;

(e) the United States of America.

###### 10 Specified evidence of English language proficiency—tests undertaken before commencement of this instrument

(1) For the purposes of subparagraphs 485.212(1)(a)(i) and (ii) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the requirements under subsection (2) are specified:

(a) for a person who makes a valid application for a Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) visa in either the Post-Vocational Education Work stream or the Post-Higher Education Work stream (the ***visa applicant***) on or after the day this instrument commences; and

(b) where the visa application made by the visa applicant is accompanied by evidence that:

(i) the visa applicant has undertaken a ***language test*** specified in paragraph (2)(a) or (b); and

(ii) the visa applicant has achieved the ***required language test scores*** specified in paragraph (2)(a) or (b) for the relevant specified language test; and

(iii) the specified required language test score was:

(A) achieved within the period of 1 year immediately before the day on which the relevant visa application was made; and

(B) in a test that was undertaken before the day on which this instrument commences;

unless the specified language test was an OET, the specified required language test score was achieved in a single sitting.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the following language tests and required language test scores are specified:

(a) in Schedule 3; or

(b) if the visa applicant is a Hong Kong passport holder or British National (Overseas) passport holder—in Schedule 4.

## Part 3—Application provisions

###### 11 Application of this instrument

This instrument applies in relation to a visa application that is made on or after the commencement of this instrument.

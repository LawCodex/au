## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Animal Effluent Management) Methodology Determination 2019

**1 Section 17**

Repeal the section, substitute:

**17 Crediting period for non-biomethane projects**

For paragraph 69(3)(b) and subparagraph 70(3)(d)(ii) of the Act, if an animal effluent management project is a non-biomethane project that, during its crediting period or periods:

(a) uses biogas to generate electricity—the period of 15 years is specified; or

(b) uses a flare as a combustion device—the period of 15 years is specified; or

(c) is any other project to which paragraphs (a) or (b) do not apply—the period of 12 years is specified.

**2 Paragraph 34(d)**

Omit “non-biomethane project or a”.

**3 Paragraph 34(d) (note)**

Omit “subsections 17(3) and 17A(3)”, substitute “subsection 17A(3)”.

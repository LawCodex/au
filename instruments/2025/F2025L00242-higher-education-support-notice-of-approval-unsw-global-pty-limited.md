# Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – UNSW Global Pty Limited) Instrument 2025

I, Larissa Hinds, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 19 February 2025

Larissa Hinds

Assistant Secretary\
HELP, Students and Provider Integrity Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – UNSW Global Pty Limited) Instrument 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** used in this instrument is defined in Schedule 1 to the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Approval of higher education provider

For the purposes of section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the Act, UNSW Global Pty Limited (ACN 086 418 582; ABN 62 086 418 582) is approved as a higher education provider.

# Migration Amendment (Status of Forces Agreement—United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025

I, the Honourable Sam Mostyn AC, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Dated 20 March 2025

Sam Mostyn AC

Governor‑General

By Her Excellency’s Command

Matt Thistlethwaite

Assistant Minister for Immigration\
Parliamentary Secretary to the Minister for Immigration and Multicultural Affairs

This instrument is made under the *Migration Act 1958*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Migration Regulations 1994

**1 Regulation 1.03 (paragraph (a) of the definition of SOFA forces civilian component member)**

After “Turkey”, insert “, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland”.

**2 Regulation 1.03 (paragraph (a) of the definition of SOFA forces member)**

After “Turkey”, insert “, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland”.

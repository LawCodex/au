Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Heard Island and McDonald Islands Fishery (Trawl Fishing Capacity) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 December 2025.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made pursuant to paragraph 17(6)(aa) of the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* and under section 11A of the *Heard Island and McDonald Islands Fishery Management Plan 2002* (the Plan).

###### 4 Definitions

A term used in this instrument that is defined for the purposes of the Plan has the same meaning in this instrument as it has in the Plan.

Note: Terms defined in the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* have the same meanings in this instrument

###### 5 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 30 November 2030.

###### 6 Determination of fishing capacity

For the purposes of Section 11A of the Plan, AFMA determines the minimum quota for trawl methods expressed as a percentage of Statutory Fishing Rights for the fishing years which commence on 1 December 2025 and ends on 30 November of the following year until 2030 to be 25.5% of the Statutory Fishing Rights held in the fishery for each trawler.

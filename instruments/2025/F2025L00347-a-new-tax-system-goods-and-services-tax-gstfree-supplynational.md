# A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) (GST‑free Supply—National Disability Insurance Scheme Supports) Amendment (Application Period) Determination 2025

I, Amanda Rishworth, Minister for the National Disability Insurance Scheme, make the following determination.

Dated 11.03.2025

Amanda Rishworth

Minister for the National Disability Insurance Scheme

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) (GST‑free Supply—National Disability Insurance Scheme Supports) Amendment (Application Period) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 177‑10(5) of the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) (GST‑free Supply—National Disability Insurance Scheme Supports) Determination 2021

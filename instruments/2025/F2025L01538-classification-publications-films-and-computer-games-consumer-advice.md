# Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) (Consumer Advice Terms) Determination 2025

The Classification Board makes the following determination under section 12A of the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*, pursuant to the resolution on 10 December 2025.

Signed on behalf of the Classification Board by\
Steven Thomson

Director, Classification Board – 10 December 2025

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) (Consumer Advice Terms) Determination 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after the end of the period of 3 months beginning on the day this instrument is registered (the ***commencement date***).

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 12A of the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

###### 4 Repeal

The *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) (Consumer Advice Terms) Determination 2024* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

Note 1: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

classifiable element;

classified;

Code;

film.

Note 2: The expression ***computer game*** is defined in section 5A of the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

***classification guidelines*** means the guidelines determined under section 12 of the Act.

###### 6 Consumer advice terms for films—Schedule 1

For the purposes of paragraph 12A(1)(a) of the Act, the list of consumer advice terms to be used in determining consumer advice for a film is set out in Schedule 1.

###### 7 Consumer advice terms for computer games—Schedule 2

For the purposes of paragraph 12A(1)(a) of the Act, the list of consumer advice terms to be used in determining consumer advice for a computer game is set out in Schedule 2.

###### 8 Application

This instrument determines the list of consumer advice terms to be used for the purposes of determining the consumer advice:

for a film or computer game that has been classified under the Act on and from the commencement date;

in relation to applications for classification for a film or computer made on and from the commencement date.

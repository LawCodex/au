CASA 11/25 — Prescription of Japan as a Recognised Foreign State Instrument 2025

1 Name

This instrument is the *CASA 11/25* — *Prescription of Japan as a Recognised Foreign State Instrument 2025*.

2 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on 1 February 2025; and

(b) is repealed at the end of 31 January 2027.

3 Prescription

Japan is prescribed as a recognised foreign State under regulation 61.047 of CASR.

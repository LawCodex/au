# Australian Maritime Safety Authority Fees Determination 2025

Australian Maritime Safety Authority Fees Determination 2025

made under the *Australian Maritime Safety Authority Act 1990*

I, Kaylene Dale, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Maritime Safety Authority, make this determination under subsection 47(1) of the *Maritime Safety Authority Act 1990*.

Dated 11 September 2025

**Kaylene Dale**\
Chief Executive Officer

1 Name of instrument

This instrument is the *Australian Maritime Safety Authority Fees Determination 2025*.

1A Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 October 2025.

2 Definitions

In this instrument:

***AMSA*** means the Australian Maritime Safety Authority.

***AMSA office*** means the nearest office of AMSA at which an AMSA inspector is stationed.

***GMDSS*** means Global Marine Distress and Safety System.

***inspector*** means a person appointed as an inspector under subsection 254(1) of the *Navigation Act 2012*.

***officer*** means an employee of AMSA or a person engaged by AMSA to perform a service, including an inspector.

***regulations or Marine Orders*** means regulations or orders made under the *Navigation Act 2012* or the *Protection of the Sea (Prevention of Pollution from Ships) Act 1983.*

3 Liability for charge

(1) A person is liable to pay a charge to AMSA if:

(a) the person asks AMSA to perform a service; or

(b) AMSA performs a service for the person’s benefit.

(2) The master, owner or agent of a vessel is taken to be the person for whose benefit an inspection or survey is performed to determine whether:

(a) the vessel is seaworthy or substandard; or

(b) equipment or cargo complies with the regulations or Marine Orders.

4 Amount of charge

(1) The amount of charge payable for a service is:

(a) either:

(i) the amount mentioned for the service in an item in Schedule 1; or

(ii) if the service is not mentioned in Schedule 1 — the hourly rate mentioned in section 5; and

(b) any of the following that apply to the service:

(i) the charge for waiting time mentioned in section 7;

(ii) the charge for travel to and from the location of service mentioned in section 8;

(iii) any other reasonable expense incurred in the provision of the service, including transport, use of vehicle and accommodation away from an AMSA office.

(2) If a request for a service is cancelled, the charge is payable in accordance with subsection (1) for work done up to the time of cancellation.

5 Hourly rate

The hourly rate for services provided by an inspector or equivalent AMSA officer is:

(a) $272 for each hour, calculated for each half hour or remaining part of a half hour; or

(b) for a service that takes less than 30 minutes — the fixed amount of $136.

6 Estimates

(1) If the hourly rate is payable, the person requesting the service may, before the service starts, ask for an estimate of the total charge for the service.

(2) AMSA may, with the person’s agreement, charge the hourly rate to assess the complexity of the service if AMSA considers that is necessary for AMSA to give a reasonably accurate estimate of charge for the service because of its complexity.

7 Charge for waiting time

The charge for waiting time is the hourly rate calculated for each 6 minutes of the total time, or remaining part of 6 minutes, that an officer has to wait in any of the following circumstances:

(a) the officer is available where and when the service is to be performed, but has to wait before performing the service for more than half an hour for a reason outside the control of the officer;

(b) the service is interrupted, 1 or more times, for a reason outside the control of the officer and the officer is unable to perform the service for a total period of at least half an hour;

(c) the officer has performed the service and has to wait for at least half an hour at the place where the service was performed.

8 Charge for travel to location of service

(1) The charge payable for an officer who travels to perform a service at a location other than where the officer is normally stationed is the sum of:

(a) the hourly rate for the time taken for travelling, including any travel to and from a vessel that is not alongside a berth; and

(b) the costs AMSA reasonably incurs for the travel; and

(c) for travel more than 25 km from an AMSA office — the charge per kilometre specified for the financial year by the Australian Taxation Office for business use of the vehicle used.

(2) If more than 1 service is performed during a visit, the charge mentioned in subsection (1) is payable only once for all the services.

9 When charges payable

(1) AMSA may issue an invoice to, or request payment from, a person who is liable to pay a charge.

(2) A charge is payable:

(a) when AMSA makes the request; or

(b) within 14 days, or another period specified by AMSA, after the invoice is issued.

10 Payment in advance

(1) If the charge for the service is not the hourly rate, AMSA may request payment before the service is performed.

(2) If the charge for the service is the hourly rate, AMSA may request payment, on agreement with the person requesting the service, on completion of agreed proportions of the estimated total hours needed to complete the service.

11 Penalty interest on overdue charges

(1) Interest is payable for any balance of charge not paid in accordance with section 9.

(2) The interest rate is compounded at 1.5% for each month or remaining part of a month for which the amount is unpaid.

*Note* The Treasurer has exempted the following from the goods and services tax (GST):

(a) charges for approvals and inspections of cargoes, containers, vessels and related equipment, as required by safety standards;

(b) charges for examinations, licences, certificates and approvals relating to seafarers, as required by safety standards;

(c) charges for other approvals, licences, certificates, exemptions and declarations required by safety standards;

(d) charges for registration of ships.

Accordingly, the charges for specific services mentioned in this Determination exclude GST. Some miscellaneous services will fall outside the exemption and will therefore be subject to GST. If so, GST will be added to any charges calculated in accordance with this Determination, taking the GST-inclusive hourly rate of charge to $299.20.

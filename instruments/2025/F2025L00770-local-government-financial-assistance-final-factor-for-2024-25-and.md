### Part 2—Final factor for the 2024-25 year

###### 5 Determination of final factor for 2024-25

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2024‑25 year is 0.7945.

###### 6 How the final factor was worked out

(1) Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2024-25 year is to be worked out by applying the formula set out in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act and then adjusting the result under whichever of paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are applicable.

Paragraph 8(1)(a) formula

(2) The factor calculated under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act is as follows:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 26,949,102 over 26,310,635 end fraction close bracket multiplied by open bracket start fraction 140.7 over 137.4 end fraction close bracket equals 1.04887 end formula.]

Note 1: For the purposes of section 4A of the Act, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2023 on 19 June 2025 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 2: For the purposes of section 4A of the Act, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2022 on 13 June 2024 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 3: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 30 April 2025 by the Statistician in respect of the 2025 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 4: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 24 April 2024 by the Statistician in respect of the 2024 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Paragraph 8(1)(b) modification

(3) The result of subsection (2) was modified by increasing the factor by 0.0001 under paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Act as the fifth decimal place was greater than 4.

Paragraph 8(1)(c) modification

(4) The result of subsection (3) was modified under paragraph 8(1)(c) of the Act as a result of special circumstances, being the need to account for the Commonwealth’s decision to bring forward 85 per cent of the payments in the 2024-25 year into 2023-24 and 50 per cent of the payments in the 2025-26 year into the 2024-25 year. The modification was worked out by multiplying the result of subsection (3) by the following formula:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 2024-25 amount minus 2023-2024 amount plus 2025-26 amount over 2023-24 final entitlement end fraction close bracket multiplied by open bracket start fraction 1 over sub (3) factor end fraction close bracket end formula.]

Where:

***2024-25 amount*** means the amount that would have been the base figure for the year 2024-25 if the Commonwealth had not decided to bring forward any payments.

***2023-24 amount*** means the 85 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2024-25 to be paid in the year 2023-24.

***2025-26 amount*** means the 50 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2025-26 to be paid in the year 2024-25.

***2023-24 final entitlement*** means the base figure for the year 2023-24.

***sub (3) factor*** means the result of subsection (3).

### Part 3—Estimated factor for the 2025-26 year

###### 7 Determination of estimated factor for 2025-26

For the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(b) of the Act, the estimated factor in relation to the 2025-26 year is 0.7739.

###### 8 How the estimated factor has been worked out

(1) Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2025-26 year is to be worked out by applying the formula set out in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act and then adjusting the result under whichever of paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are applicable.

Paragraph 8(1)(a) formula

(2) The factor calculated under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act is as follows:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 27,395,019 over 26,949,102 end fraction close bracket multiplied by open bracket start fraction 145.6 over 140.7 end fraction close bracket equals 1.05195 end formula.]

Note 1: For the purposes of section 4A, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2024 on 19 June 2025 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 2: For the purposes of section 4A, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2023 on 19 June 2025 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 3: The formula uses the 2025-26 Budget forecast of the Department of the Treasury of the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, in respect of the 2026 March quarter.

Note 4: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 30 April 2025 by the Statistician in respect of the 2025 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Paragraph 8(1)(b) modification

(3) The result of subsection (2) was modified by increasing the factor by 0.0001 under paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Act as the fifth decimal place was greater than 4.

Paragraph 8(1)(c) modification

(4) The result of subsection (3) was modified under paragraph 8(1)(c) of the Act as a result of special circumstances, being the need to account for the Commonwealth’s decision to bring forward 50 per cent of the payments in the 2025-26 year into the 2024-25 year. The modification was worked out by multiplying the result of subsection (3) by the following formula:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 2025-26 amount minus 2024-25 amount over 2024-25 final entitlement end fraction close bracket multiplied by open bracket start fraction 1 over sub (3) factor end fraction close bracket end formula.]

Where:

***2025-26 amount*** means the amount that would have been the base figure for the year 2025-26 if the Commonwealth had not decided to bring forward any payments.

***2024-25 amount*** means the 50 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2025-26 to be paid in the year 2024-25.

***2024-25 final entitlement*** means the base figure for the year 2024-25.

***sub (3) factor*** means the result of subsection (3).

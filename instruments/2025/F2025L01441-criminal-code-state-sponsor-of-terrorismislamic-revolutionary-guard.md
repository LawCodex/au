###### 4 State sponsor of terrorism—Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps

(1) For the purposes of the definition of ***state sponsor of terrorism*** in subsection 110.3(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the entity known as the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps is specified.

(2) The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps is also known by the following names:

(a) IRG;

(b) IRGC;

(c) Islamic Revolutionary Corps;

(d) Islamic Revolutionary Guard;

(e) Pasdaran;

(f) Pasdaran‑e Enghelab;

(g) Pasdaran‑e Enghelab‑e Islami;

(h) Pasdaran‑e Enghelab‑e Islami (Pasdaran);

(i) Revolutionary Guards;

(j) Sepah;

(k) Sepah‑e Pasdaran‑e Enghelab‑e Islami;

(l) Sepah Pasdaran;

(m) the Army of the Guardians of the Islamic Revolution;

(n) the Iranian Revolutionary Guards.

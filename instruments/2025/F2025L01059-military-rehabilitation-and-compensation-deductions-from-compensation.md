# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Deductions from Compensation) Approval 2025

Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Deductions from Compensation) Approval 2025

I, Matthew James Keogh, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs make the following instrument.

Dated 1 September 2025

Matthew James Keogh

MINISTER FOR VETERANS' AFFAIRS

**Contents**

### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Deductions from Compensation) Approval 2025*.

### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the day it is registered.

### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 431(3) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004* (the Act).

### 4 Repeals

The *MRCA Approved Classes of Payments Determination 2015* is repealed.

### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

*Act* means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

*State Housing Authority* means an authority listed in the definition of *Government rent* in subsection 5N(1) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

### 6 Approved classes of payments for deductions from compensation

For paragraph 431(1)(b) of the Act, the following are approved classes of payments:

payments to a *State Housing Authority* that are —

payments of rent;

payments of principal or interest of loans; or

payments of debts;

payments to Westpac Banking Corporation (ABN 33 007 457 141) that are —

payments of principal or interest of loans; or

payments of insurance premiums.

###### 6 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in, or have the meaning given by, the Act, including the following:

applicable methodology determination;

Australian carbon credit unit;

carbon abatement;

carbon dioxide equivalent;

crediting period;

eligible carbon abatement;

eligible offsets project;

emission;

greenhouse gas;

offsets project;

offsets report;

project;

Regulator;

reporting period.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Act 2011*.

***anaerobic digestion*** means a biological process in which organic matter is broken down by microorganisms in the absence of oxygen.

***biogas*** means a mixture of gases, including methane, that is generated as a result of anaerobic digestion and includes landfill gas.

***biogas source facility***, in relation to a project, means a facility that supplies biogas to be treated as part of the project so as to produce biomethane, and includes:

a facility producing biogas for a project biomethane facility; and

if biogas is produced at a project biomethane facility—that project biomethane facility.

Note: A biomethane facility can be its own biogas source facility if the biomethane facility also produces biogas to be treated by carrying out biomethane production.

***biogas upgrading*** means the process by which biogas is refined and stripped of impurities to produce biomethane, which may include (but is not limited to) one or more of the following:

pre-treatment processing of biogas; or

the drying or scrubbing of biogas; or

post-treatment compression of the refined biomethane produced by the process.

***biogas upgrading system*** means a system of equipment that is capable of undertaking biogas upgrading.

***biogas waste*** means putrescible organic waste material that can be treated through anaerobic digestion to produce biogas.

***biomethane*** means a high-methane content gas that is:

produced by biogas upgrading; and

suitable for use as a natural gas substitute.

***biomethane conversion and displacement project*** means a project that:

involves the installation of one or more biogas upgrading systems at a project biomethane facility; and

if involving landfill gas capture for biomethane, also involves biomethane production.

***biomethane displacement only project*** means a project that:

involves the installation of one or more biogas upgrading systems at a project biomethane facility; and

involves biomethane production; and

does not involve landfill gas capture for biomethane or emissions destruction; and

is not a restarting biomethane displacement only project.

***biomethane facility*** means a facility:

at which biomethane production is undertaken or intended to be undertaken; and

from which the resulting biomethane is sent, or is proposed to be sent, to an end use where it can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

Note 1: If biogas upgrading occurs at the project landfill, the project landfill may also be a biomethane facility.

Note 2: Biomethane facilities that are used in a landfill gas capture and destruction project that involve landfill gas capture for biomethane become known as “project biomethane facilities”.

***biomethane production*** means:

treating biogas, some of which has a landfill gas component, by biogas upgrading at a project biomethane facility to produce biomethane, with or without biogas produced from other biogas waste; and

sending the biomethane to an end use where it can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

Note 1: Acceptable end uses may include sale and transport to a gas retailer or gas consumer, or onsite combustion for heat or power.

Note 2: Combustion of biomethane may occur onsite at the biomethane facility, or offsite if the biomethane is transported from the biomethane facility to an end user. This may be through injection into a gas distribution or transmission network, transport via road, or another gas transport mechanism.

***biomethane project*** means a biomethane conversion and displacement project, a biomethane displacement only project or a restarting biomethane displacement only project.

***carbon tax waste***, in relation to a landfill site, means waste that was deposited in the landfill site between 1 July 2012 and 30 June 2014.

***CEO statement*** means a written statement by a person occupying the role of, or that is equivalent to, the chief executive officer or chief financial officer of the project proponent for a project.

***classification***, of a project as a project type or project sub-type—see sections 14 to 16.

***closed landfill*** means a landfill site that has stopped receiving waste.

***collection efficiency***, of a landfill gas collection system used for a project landfill, means the proportion of landfill gas generated by the project landfill that is collected and combusted by the landfill gas collection system, expressed as a percentage.

***combustion device*** means a flare, boiler or internal combustion engine that, when operated in accordance with the manufacturer’s specifications:

achieves the complete combustion of methane; and

has a functional monitoring and control system.

***complete***: the combustion of landfill gas or biomethane is ***complete*** if the combustion has a destruction efficiency of 98% or more of the methane in the landfill gas or biomethane.

***conversion abatement***, in relation to a project, means the carbon dioxide equivalent net abatement amount for the project for a reporting period (worked out in accordance with Division 2 of Part 4) attributable to the conversion of methane (CH4) to carbon dioxide (CO2) by carrying out:

landfill gas capture for biomethane; or

emissions destruction.

Note: Landfill gas capture for biomethane involves sending landfill gas to a biogas upgrading system to be turned into biomethane. This landfill gas is considered to have its methane component converted to carbon dioxide, as if it had been sent to a combustion device, because subsequent usage of the biomethane results in its combustion.

***daily/operational cover***, in relation to a project landfill, means the area of the landfill normally covered by material used to cover a day’s deposition of waste in active landfill waste disposal areas of the landfill.

***designated large facility*** has the meaning given by the *National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Act 2007*.

***displacement abatement****,* in relation to a project, means the carbon dioxide equivalent net abatement amount for the project for a reporting period (worked out in accordance with Division 2 of Part 5) attributable to biomethane production.

***earlier determination*****,** in relation to a project, means:

the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Landfill Gas) Methodology Determination 2015*; or

the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Electricity Generation from Landfill Gas) Methodology Determination 2021*;

as in effect when that methodology determination became the applicable methodology determination for the project.

***electricity generation and flaring project*** means a project that:

collects landfill gas and combusts it in an electricity generator; and

may combust landfill gas in a flare at any time that electricity generation is not viable (including for commercial reasons).

***eligible biogas*** means:

biogas produced from eligible biogas waste; or

landfill gas.

***eligible biogas waste*** means biogas waste that is:

domestic or commercial wastewater or industrial wastewater within the meaning of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Domestic, Commercial and Industrial Wastewater) Methodology Determination 2015*; or

mixed solid waste within the meaning of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Alternative Waste Treatment) Methodology Determination 2015*; or

eligible organic material within the meaning of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Source Separated Organic Waste) Methodology Determination 2016*; or

eligible animal effluent biogas waste within the meaning of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative—Animal Effluent Management) Methodology Determination 2019*.

Note: The proportion of eligible biogas waste used to generate biogas to be treated by biomethane production as part of the project must be able to be determined.

***emissions destruction*** means:

collecting landfill gas; and

destroying the proportion of the biogas that is methane using a combustion device.

***existing enhanced project***—see section 8.

***existing upgrade project***—see section 9.

***final cover***, in relation to a project landfill, means the area of the landfill normally covered by material used to cover previously active landfill waste disposal areas of the landfill that have reached final height and are unlikely to be used for waste disposal again.

***flaring only project*** means a project that:

involves emissions destruction; and

combusts landfill gas only with a flare; and

does not generate electricity after its classification as a flaring only project.

***flaring only project (small and regional landfill)*** means a project:

that involves emissions destruction; and

that combusts landfill gas only with a flare; and

that does not generate electricity after its classification as a flaring only project (small and regional landfill); and

the project landfill for which is in an outer regional, remote or very remote area (within the meaning of the Remoteness Areas ASGS).

***forerunner project***, in relation to a restarting biomethane displacement only project—see the definition of ***restarting biomethane displacement only project***.

***intermediate cover***, in relation to a project landfill, means the area of the landfill normally covered by material used to cover previously active landfill working areas that will not be used for waste disposal for an extended period of time (typically a few weeks to a few months).

***landfill gas*** means gas generated from anaerobic decomposition of biological material from a project landfill.

***landfill gas capture and destruction project***—see section 11.

***landfill gas capture for biomethane*** means:

collecting landfill gas; and

sending that landfill gas to a biogas upgrading system that is part of a project biomethane facility.

Note: Conversion abatement from landfill gas capture for biomethane only occurs when biomethane produced from landfill gas as part of the project can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

***landfill gas collection system*** means a system to collect and combust landfill gas.

***landfill site*** means a site where waste is or was buried in accordance with a State or Territory licence.

***legacy waste*** means waste accepted by a landfill site before 1 July 2016.

***monitoring and control system***—see section 7.

***monitoring requirements*** means the requirements set out in Division 2 of Part 6.

***natural gas*** means a substance that:

is in a gaseous state at standard temperature and pressure; and

consists of naturally occurring hydrocarbons, or a naturally occurring mixture of hydrocarbons and non-hydrocarbons, the principal constituent of which is methane; and

is suitable for consumption.

***new project*** means a project that involves:

the installation of a landfill gas collection system for a project landfill, if no such system has previously been installed for that landfill; or

the combustion, in an electricity generator, of landfill gas from a project landfill, if that landfill has not previously been used to produce electricity.

***NGA Factors document*** means the document entitled “National Greenhouse Accounts Factors” published by the Department, as existing from time to time.

Note: The NGA Factors document could in 2025 be viewed on the Department’s website (https://www.dcceew.gov.au).

***NGER (Measurement) Determination*** means the *National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting (Measurement) Determination 2008*.

***non-biomethane project*** means a landfill gas capture and destruction project that involves the carrying out of only the emissions destruction type of project activities.

***non-legacy waste*** means waste that is not legacy waste.

***non-monitored period***—see section 79.

***non-safeguard LFG project*** means a landfill gas capture and destruction project that is not a safeguard LFG project.

***open landfill*** means a landfill site that continues to receive waste.

***project activity*** means an activity of a type mentioned in section 13.

***project biomethane facility***, in relation to a project that involves biomethane production, means a biomethane facility used in carrying out the project.

Note: The section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to a biomethane conversion and displacement project or a biomethane displacement only project, and the section 22 application in relation to a restarting biomethane displacement only project, must include details of at least one project biomethane facility (see paragraph 18(2)(c)).

***project emissions*** means project emissions for the purposes of sections 53 and 67.

***project landfill***—see section 11.

***project sub-type***—see section 15.

***project type***—see section 14.

***Remoteness Areas ASGS*** means the document entitled “Remoteness Areas: Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 3” for the reference period of July 2021—June 2026, published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics on 21 March 2023, as existing from time to time.

Note: The Remoteness Areas ASGS could in September 2025 be viewed on the Australian Bureau of Statistics website (https://www.abs.gov.au).

***restarting biomethane displacement only project*** means a project that:

uses a biomethane facility that was part of an eligible offsets project (the ***forerunner project***):

that involved biomethane production; and

for which the applicable methodology determination was this instrument, as in effect when it became so applicable, or the earlier determination in relation to the forerunner project; and

for which the crediting period has ended; and

involves biomethane production; and

has a crediting period greater than zero (see section 30).

***restarting flaring only project***: a project is a ***restarting flaring only project*** if:

the project landfill for the project was previously the project landfill for an eligible offsets project: that involved emissions destruction; and for which the applicable methodology determination was this instrument, as in effect when it became so applicable; and

the project combusts landfill gas only with a flare; and

the project does not generate electricity after its classification as a restarting flaring only project; and

the crediting period for the project is greater than zero (see section 30).

***safeguard LFG project*** means a landfill gas capture and destruction project:

for which the project landfill, at and after the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project, is a designated large facility; or

if the project landfill becomes such a facility after the making of the section 22 or section 128 application—that the project proponent elects to classify as a safeguard LFG project (in accordance with section 16).

Note: The classification of such projects under this instrument has no effect on requirements in relation to designated large facilities in the *National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Act* 2007, instruments made under that Act, or the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Rule 2015*.

***section 22 application***, in relation to an offsets project, means an application under section 22 of the Act to declare the project as an eligible offsets project covered by this instrument.

***section 128 application***, in relation to an eligible offsets project, means an application under section 128 of the Act to apply this instrument to the project from the start of a specified reporting period under the earlier determination in relation to the project.

***State or Territory licence***, in relation to a project landfill in a State or Territory, means a licence or other permission (however described) issued in respect of the landfill by the relevant environmental authority (the ***State or Territory environmental regulator***) of that State or Territory.

***sub-facility zone*** has the same meaning as in the NGER (Measurement) Determination.

***upgrade project*** means a project that:

involves a project landfill or project biomethane facility in relation to which no Australian carbon credit units were issued under section 11 of the Act for the 4-year period ending immediately before the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project; and

treats collected gas by emissions destruction or landfill gas capture for biomethane.

###### 7 Meaning of monitoring and control system

A ***monitoring and control system***, for a flare, is a system that consists of:

a monitoring system that detects the flame of the flare and monitors whether the flare is operating to the manufacturer’s specifications for the complete combustion of methane; and

an associated control system that shuts down landfill gas flow to the flare either when no flame is detected or when the flare is not operating to those specifications.

Note: An example of a monitoring and control system for a flare is a flare management system that incorporates a UV detection sensor.

A ***monitoring and control system***, for a biogas upgrading system, is a system that:

monitors the flow rate of landfill gas and biomethane; and

monitors the operation and output of biogas upgrading systems; and

is capable of automatically stopping biogas flow to the biogas upgrading system if the system is not operating to the manufacturer’s specifications to enable biogas upgrading.

A ***monitoring and control system***, for an electricity generator, is a system that consists of:

a monitoring system that monitors whether the generator is operating to the manufacturer’s specifications for the complete combustion of methane; and

an associated control system that shuts down landfill gas flow to the generator when it is not operating to those specifications.

###### 8 Meaning of existing enhanced project

A project is an ***existing enhanced project***, from the relevant time, if:

before the relevant time, the project was a project, other than an upgrade project or a transitioning (upgrade) project, covered by the earlier determination in relation to that former project; and

the application of this instrument to the project is approved, under section 130 of the Act, from the start of a particular reporting period under that earlier determination (the ***relevant time***); and

the project is a biomethane conversion and displacement project, flaring only project or electricity generation and flaring project.

The reporting period mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) is taken to be the first reporting period for the project under this instrument.

###### 9 Meaning of existing upgrade project

A project is an ***existing upgrade project***, from the relevant time, if:

before the relevant time, the project was an upgrade project or a transitioning (upgrade) project covered by the earlier determination in relation to that former project; and

the application of this instrument to the project is approved, under section 130 of the Act, from the start of a particular reporting period under that earlier determination (the ***relevant time***).

The reporting period mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) is taken to be the first reporting period for the project under this instrument.

###### 10 References to factors and parameters from external sources

If:

(a) a calculation required by this instrument, for a project for a reporting period, includes a factor or parameter; and

(b) the factor or parameter is defined in, or worked out by reference to a method in, another instrument or writing;

the project proponent for the project must, in undertaking the calculation for the reporting period, ascertain the factor or parameter by reference to the version of the instrument or writing in force at the end of the reporting period.

Subsection (1) does not apply if:

this instrument specifies otherwise; or

it is not possible to ascertain the factor or parameter by reference to that version of the instrument or writing.

If paragraph (2)(b) applies, the proponent must:

ascertain the factor or parameter by reference to an earlier version of the instrument or writing; or

make a conservative estimate of the factor or parameter by reference to the following, as applicable: any applicable measurement or estimation approaches or requirements in the NGER (Measurement) Determination; any relevant historical data for the project, or other project data that the proponent considers relevant; any other matters that the proponent considers relevant.

### Part 2—Landfill gas capture and destruction projects

###### 11 Landfill gas capture and destruction projects

For the purposes of paragraph 106(1)(a) of the Act, this instrument applies to an offsets project in which either or both of the following occur, in a way that can reasonably be expected to result in eligible carbon abatement:

landfill gas from a landfill site is collected and treated in a way that destroys methane;

landfill gas from a landfill site, with or without biogas produced from other biogas waste, is collected and treated by biogas upgrading at a project biomethane facility to produce biomethane which is then sent to an end use where it can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

A project covered by subsection (1) is a ***landfill gas capture and destruction project***.

A landfill site mentioned in subsection (1) is the ***project landfill*** for the landfill gas capture and destruction project.

Unless otherwise stated, a reference in this instrument to an eligible offsets project is a reference to a landfill gas capture and destruction project that is an eligible offsets project.

### Part 3—Project requirements

#### Division 1—Preliminary

###### 12 Operation of this Part

For the purposes of paragraph 106(1)(b) of the Act, Divisions 2 and 3 of this Part set out the requirements that must be met for landfill gas capture and destruction projects to be eligible offsets projects. These requirements include specifications of project types and sub-types to which landfill gas capture and destruction projects must belong, and particular requirements relating to some project types and sub-types.

Note: The section 22 application in relation to a project must include details of how the project meets all relevant requirements set out in this instrument, including details of any sub-methods applicable to the project: see paragraphs 13(1)(d) and (n) of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Rule 2015*. The section 128 application in relation to a project must include a statement that the project meets the requirements of the applicable methodology determination: see paragraph 90(e) of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Rule 2015*.

Division 4 of this Part specifies:

for the purposes of subparagraph 27(4A)(a)(ii) of the Act, requirements in lieu of the newness requirement for certain projects; and

for the purposes of subparagraph 27(4A)(b)(ii) of the Act, a requirement in lieu of the regulatory additionality requirement.

For the purposes of paragraph 69(3)(b) of the Act, Division 5 of this Part specifies crediting periods for certain project types and project sub-types.

###### 16 Classification of a project as project type and sub-type

A landfill gas capture and destruction project must be classified as a project type or project sub-type on each applicable day mentioned in subsection (2). The classification must be documented in the manner mentioned in respect of that day.

For the purposes of subsection (1), the applicable days are:

###### 17 Information required for project applications

The section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to a landfill gas capture and destruction project must:

be accompanied by a copy of the State or Territory licence in relation to any project landfill for the project, as in force at the time of making the application; and

include written evidence that the project proponent for the project intends:

to combust landfill gas to generate electricity for use on-site, for supply to the grid, or both; or

to treat landfill gas, with or without biogas produced from other biogas waste, by biogas upgrading at a project biomethane facility to produce biomethane which will then be sent to an end use where it can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute; or

to combust landfill gas in a flare; and

specify the project type and project sub-type (as the case requires) to which the project belongs.

###### 18 Requirements for projects involving landfill gas capture for biomethane or emissions destruction

There must be only one project landfill for a landfill gas capture and destruction project that involves:

landfill gas capture for biomethane; or

emissions destruction.

#### Division 3—Requirements for project types and sub-types

###### 19 Requirements for non-biomethane projects

Substantive requirements

(1) The project landfill for a non-biomethane project must not also be the project landfill for another project that is:

(a) a non-biomethane project or a biomethane conversion and displacement project under this instrument; or

(b) covered by another methodology determination, but the same in substance as a project mentioned in paragraph (a).

Application requirements

(2) The section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to a non-biomethane project must include a description of how inputs used for calculating net conversion abatement under this instrument will cover the whole of the project landfill.

###### 20 Application requirements for biomethane conversion and displacement projects, biomethane displacement only projects and restarting biomethane displacement only projects

This section applies to the following:

the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to a biomethane conversion and displacement project or biomethane displacement only project;

the section 22 application in relation to a restarting biomethane displacement only project.

The application must include the following information:

which project activities will be carried out as part of the project;

a description of at least one project biomethane facility in relation to the project, and the following details about each such project biomethane facility:

the location of the facility;

the capacity of the facility, in m3 of biomethane produced by the facility per calendar year;

any known proposal for the expansion of the facility over the course of the project;

the intended recipients of biomethane produced by the facility;

the basis on which the project proponent is expected to comply with the requirements of this Part in respect of the facility;

a description of at least one biogas upgrading system that will be used for the project, and details of its location;

a declaration by the project proponent for the project that biomethane produced by project biomethane facilities can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute;

a description as to how the project can reasonably be expected to result in eligible carbon abatement.

Note: In relation to paragraph (2)(b), note that biomethane facilities may be added to the project after its declaration as an eligible offsets project, provided that they are detailed in the relevant offsets report about the project: see section 73.

###### 21 Requirements for existing enhanced projects

Substantive requirements

The project proponent for an existing enhanced project must have invested in new infrastructure for the landfill gas collection system or biogas upgrading system for the project during the period that:

started immediately after the project was declared as an eligible offsets project under the earlier determination in relation to the project; and

ended immediately before the making of the section 128 application in relation to the project.

Application requirements

The section 128 application in relation to the project must include a CEO statement certifying the following:

that the project proponent intends to invest in further infrastructure for the landfill gas collection system or biogas upgrading system for the project, as the case requires;

that such investment will contribute to the project’s carbon dioxide equivalent net abatement amount;

that consultation undertaken by the project proponent with the owner and operator of the project landfill or project biomethane facility (as the case requires) and the State or Territory environmental regulator supports paragraphs (a) and (b);

if the project landfill is, or is reasonably likely to become during the crediting period for the project, a designated large facility—that the operator of the landfill is aware of the effect of the safeguard provisions of the *National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Act 2007* in relation to carbon abatement of any covered emissions from the project landfill, and consents to the project on that basis.

###### 22 Application requirements for upgrade projects and existing upgrade projects

The section 22 or section 128 application in relation to an upgrade project must include a CEO statement certifying the following:

that the proponent intends to invest in infrastructure for the landfill gas collection system or biogas upgrading system for the project, as the case requires;

that such investment will contribute to the project’s eligible abatement;

that consultation undertaken by the project proponent with the owner and operator of the project landfill or project biomethane facility (as the case requires) endorses the statements in paragraphs (a) and (b).

If the project landfill for an existing upgrade project is, or is reasonably likely to become during the crediting period for the project, a designated large facility, the section 128 application in relation to the project must include a CEO statement certifying that the operator of the landfill is aware of the effect of the safeguard provisions of the *National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Act 2007* in relation to carbon abatement of any covered emissions from the project landfill, and consents to the project on that basis.

###### 23 Requirements for flaring only projects

A flaring only project must be a project to which the following apply:

the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project includes a CEO statement certifying that, on the basis of modelling undertaken by the project proponent for the project within the period of 12 months immediately before the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application, the generation of electricity as part of the project is unlikely to provide a reasonable return on investment;

there is no regulatory or contractual obligation which requires combustion of landfill gas from the project landfill beyond that which would have been combusted without the project;

consultation undertaken by the project proponent with the owner and operator of the project landfill endorses the statements in paragraphs (a) and (b).

###### 24 Requirements for flaring only projects (small and regional landfill)

A flaring only project (small and regional landfill) must be a project to which the following apply:

the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project includes a CEO statement certifying that, on the basis of modelling undertaken by the project proponent for the project within the period of 12 months immediately preceding the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application, the generation of electricity as part of the project is unlikely to provide a reasonable return on investment;

in the 3-year period immediately preceding the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application:

the average annual amount of waste received by the project landfill was less than 50,000 tonnes; and

the project landfill was not previously the project landfill for any other eligible offsets project;

consultation undertaken by the project proponent with the owner and operator of the project landfill endorses the statements in paragraphs (a) and (b).

###### 25 Requirements for restarting flaring only projects

A restarting flaring only project must be a project to which the following apply:

neither electricity generation nor biomethane production can or does occur in respect of emissions from the project landfill on and after the day that is: immediately before the day the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project is made; or the start of the first reporting period for which the project is classified as a restarting flaring only project;

consultation undertaken by the project proponent with the owner and operator of the project landfill supports paragraph (a).

##### Subdivision 2—Method for calculating net conversion abatement amount

###### 36 Simplified outline of this Subdivision

Net conversion abatement is calculated as project abatement minus baseline abatement.

If a project is a biomethane conversion and displacement project and involves upgrading biogas that is solely sourced from the project landfill, an alternative method of calculating net abatement is to determine the amount of methane that is destroyed after the biogas is sent to a biomethane facility for biogas upgrading, based on the quantity and composition of the biomethane produced by this means.

Any emissions generated by the project from undertaking landfill gas capture for biomethane (for example, fuel use) are deducted from the net conversion abatement amount.

For each project biomethane facility, the gross abatement amount is calculated as the natural gas combustion emissions displaced by the quantity of biomethane produced from biogas upgrading as part of the project. As biomethane and natural gas are identical from a fuel combustion standpoint, it is assumed that displacement occurs on a one-to-one basis.

The gross abatement amount is multiplied by the proportion of biogas that is eligible biogas, to prevent crediting of biomethane created from ineligible waste sources. The fraction of eligible biogas is worked out for each biogas source facility that supplies biogas during a reporting period. If a biogas source facility supplies a mixture of eligible and non-eligible biogas to a project during a reporting period, the fraction of eligible biogas from the biogas must be worked out in accordance with equation 27.

From this are deducted any emissions generated by the operation of the project biomethane facility or associated with the production of biomethane (for example, fuel and electricity used at a project biomethane facility, or fuel used in transporting biomethane).

#### Division 4—Eligible abatement fraction

###### 63 Simplified outline of this Division

The eligible abatement fraction for a project for a reporting period is the proportion of gross abatement for the project that is associated with biomethane produced from eligible biogas during the reporting period. This excludes abatement created from biogas upgrading of ineligible biogas. Only the eligible abatement fraction of gross abatement is counted towards the net abatement to ensure that biomethane produced from ineligible sources is not credited.

The eligible abatement is expressed as a fraction, representing the proportion of biogas sent to a biogas upgrading system for the project that is eligible biogas. If 100% of biogas that undergoes biogas upgrading is eligible biogas, there is no deduction for ineligible abatement.

The eligible abatement fraction is worked out as the proportion of the quantity of eligible biogas supplied by all biogas source facilities during a reporting period to the total quantity of biogas treated during a reporting period. The quantity of eligible biogas sent from each biogas source facility is worked out by:

(a) direct measurement of the quantity of eligible biogas sent for biogas upgrading; or

(b) estimation of the proportion of biogas sent for biogas upgrading that is eligible biogas.

### Part 6—Monitoring, record-keeping and reporting requirements

#### Division 1—Offsets report requirements

###### 70 Operation of this Division

For the purposes of paragraph 106(3)(a) of the Act, this Division sets out information that must be included in an offsets report about an eligible offsets project for a reporting period.

###### 71 Required information for offsets reports—general information

An offsets report about a project for a reporting period must include the following information:

(a) a copy of any State or Territory licence in relation to the project landfill, as in force at the end of the reporting period;

(b) a list of the project activities that were carried out during the reporting period, including:

(i) details of any additional project activity that began to be carried out after the section 22 application or section 128 application was made, or since the previous offsets report was given to the Regulator, and the date on which that activity commenced; and

(ii) details of any project activity that was previously carried out, that has stopped being carried out since the previous offsets report was given to the Regulator, and the date on which that activity stopped;

(c) a description of all sources of project emissions in respect of the project;

(d) if landfill gas capture for biomethane has been carried out as part of the project—evidence that biogas sent to biogas upgrading systems is used to produce biomethane that can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute;

(e) if biomethane production has been carried out as part of the project:

(i) details of the source of any biogas treated during the reporting period; and

(ii) details of the biogas upgrading systems used for the project; and

(iii) details about the end use, or anticipated end use, of the biomethane produced during the reporting period; and

(iv) details about the measurement of the volume of biomethane sent out by biogas upgrading systems (*QBM, K*) in accordance with the monitoring requirements, and the point at which biomethane flow is measured; and

(v) a declaration by the project proponent for the project that biomethane produced by all project biomethane facilities involved in the project during the reporting period can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

Note: For the purposes of paragraph (d), evidence that biomethane produced by the project can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute may include invoices or other records of commercial transactions in which biomethane is traded as a substitute for natural gas for a use that results in its combustion.

###### 72 Required information for offsets reports—net abatement calculations

An offsets report about a project for a reporting period must include details of the net abatement calculations for the reporting period, including the following:

(a) the output of each equation in this instrument used to calculate the net abatement amount for the reporting period;

(b) if landfill gas capture for biomethane is carried out as part of the project—details about the biomethane production loss factor used to calculate net abatement;

(c) if biomethane production is carried out as part of the project—details of the displacement abatement calculations (that is, calculations made under Division 2 of Part 5), including the following:

(i) information on volumes and methane concentrations of biomethane produced during the reporting period;

(ii) volumes and eligible abatement fractions of biogas treated by each project biomethane facility during the reporting period;

(iii) information on the sources and volumes of project emissions from treatment of biogas by biogas upgrading;

(iv) if *QBG, EI, g* is determined in accordance with equation 28 for a reporting period:

an explanation of how the proportion of biogas that is eligible biogas (*EBg*) was determined; and

evidence or data supporting how *EBg* was calculated; and

a signed declaration from the person who estimated *EBg* that the value for that factor is accurate and conservative.

###### 73 Required information for offsets reports—details of certain project changes

An offsets report about a project for a reporting period must include details of any of the following changes made to the project since the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to the project, or the giving of the previous offsets report to the Regulator:

a project biomethane facility being added to the project;

a biogas upgrading system being added to the project;

whether the project, at the start of the reporting period, was classified as a different project type or sub-type from the previous reporting period, and details of how the project meets all requirements of the different type or sub-type;

any other changes to the project resulting in the information given in the section 22 application, section 128 application or previous offsets report being out of date.

If paragraph (1)(a) applies, an offsets report must also include:

(a) the intended recipients of biomethane produced by the new project biomethane facility; and

(b) a declaration by the project proponent for the project that biomethane produced by the new project biomethane facility can reasonably be expected to be combusted within Australia as a natural gas substitute.

For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c):

(a) the relevant requirements include requirements relating to a section 22 application or section 128 application in relation to that project type or sub-type; and

(b) any references, in those requirements, to information to be included in the section 22 application or section 128 application are taken to be references to information to be included in the offsets report; and

(c) any references, in those requirements, to time periods ending immediately before the making of the section 22 application or section 128 application are taken to be references to time periods ending immediately before the start of the reporting period to which the offsets report relates.

###### 74 Required information for offsets reports—determination of certain factors and parameters

(1) If subsection 10(3) applies to a project proponent for a project for a reporting period in relation to a factor or parameter, the offsets report about the project for the reporting period must include the following information about the factor or parameter:

(a) the reasons why the proponent did not ascertain the factor or parameter by reference to the version of the instrument or writing in force at the end of the reporting period, in accordance with subsection 10(1);

(b) if the proponent ascertained the factor or parameter by reference to an earlier version of the instrument or writing:

(i) the date when that version came into effect; and

(ii) the start and end dates of each use of that version to ascertain the factor or parameter;

(c) if the proponent made an estimate of the factor or parameter in accordance with paragraph 10(3)(b)—details of any matters in that paragraph to which the proponent had reference.

(2) If a parameter is determined under section 79 for the purpose of working out the carbon dioxide equivalent net abatement amount for a landfill gas capture and destruction project for a reporting period, the offsets report about the project for the reporting period must include the following information about the parameter:

(a) the name of the parameter;

## Schedule 1—Determining regulatory proportion

### Part 1—Operation of this Schedule

###### 1 Operation of this Schedule

(1) For the purposes of sections 49, 50 and 51, the regulatory proportion of the methane combusted or taken to be combusted for an eligible offsets project for a reporting period that would have been combusted without the project (*WB, Reg*) is determined, at the project proponent’s election, in accordance with any of Parts 2 to 4 of this Schedule.

No obligation on State or Territory environmental regulators

(2) To avoid doubt, this Schedule does not require a State or Territory environmental regulator to do anything.

### Part 2—Regulatory proportion determined on basis of State or Territory licence conditions

###### 2 References to State or Territory licence

In this Part, a reference to a State or Territory licence is a reference to the State or Territory licence in relation to the project landfill for the project, as in force at the end of the reporting period for which *WB, Reg* is worked out in accordance with this Part.

###### 3 Operation of this Part

(1) This Part sets out methods for working out *WB, Reg* on the basis of certain conditions (the ***relevant conditions***) included in the State or Territory licence.

(2) If the State or Territory licence includes only one relevant condition, *WB,Reg* is worked out by using the method that relates to that condition.

(3) If the State or Territory licence includes more than one relevant condition, *WB,Reg* is worked out by:

(a) using the method that relates to each of those conditions to work out a value for *WB,Reg*; and

(b) selecting the highest of those values.

(4) However, if the operation of subclause (2) or (3) results in *WB,Reg* being lower than *WB, Def* (worked out using whichever of equation 18, 19 or 20 is applicable), then *WB,Reg* is taken to be *WB, Def.*

(5) *WB,Reg* is also taken to be *WB, Def* if the State or Territory licence does not include any relevant conditions.

###### 4 Reporting periods for which regulatory proportion must be worked out

(1) If this Part applies, *WB, Reg* must be worked out in accordance with clause 3 for the following reporting periods:

(a) the first reporting period for the project under this instrument;

(b) each reporting period in respect of which a change to a relevant condition included in the State or Territory licence would result in a different value for *WB, Reg*;

(c) each reporting period that starts 5 years after the start of the previous reporting period for which *WB, Reg* was worked out in accordance with clause 3.

(2) If *WB, Reg* is not required to be worked out for a reporting period under subclause (1), it is taken to have the same value for that reporting period as it had in the immediately previous reporting period.

###### 11 Regulatory proportion determined by independent expert

(c) have more than 3 years’ experience in:

(ii) the design and operation of landfill gas collection systems; and

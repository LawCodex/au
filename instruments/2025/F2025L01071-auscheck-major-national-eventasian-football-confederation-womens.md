# AusCheck (Major National Event—Asian Football Confederation Women’s Asian Cup 2026) Declaration 2025

LIN 25/085

(1) I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*, declare the Asian Football Confederation Women’s Asian Cup 2026 to be a major national event for the purposes of the Act.

(2) This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 04 September 2025

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

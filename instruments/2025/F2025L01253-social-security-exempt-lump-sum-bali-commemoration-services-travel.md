###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Bali Commemoration Services Travel Assistance Payment*** means a lump sum payment made to an eligible claimant in accordance with the *‘2025 Bali Commemoration Services – Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines’* for attendance at a Commemoration Service.

***Commemoration Service*** means an official memorial service for the 2005 Bali bombings held on 1 October 2025 at an eligible location identified in the 2025 Bali Commemoration Services – Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines.

***2025 Bali Commemoration Services – Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines*** means the program guidelines dated 16 September 2025, and published on the GrantConnect website (www.grants.gov.au – GO7907), for a grant program operated by the Australian Government to support the participation of eligible claimants that have travelled and participated in a Commemoration Service.

###### 5 Exempt lump sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, a Bali Commemoration Services Travel Assistance Payment is an exempt lump sum.

# Telecommunications (Customer Communications for Outages Industry Standard Amendment) Direction 2025

I, Anika Wells, Minister for Communications, make the following direction.

Dated 8 December 2025

Anika Wells

Minister for Communications

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Telecommunications (Customer Communications for Outages Industry Standard Amendment) Direction 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 125AA(4) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) ACMA;

(b) carrier;

(c) carriage service;

(d) carriage service provider;

(e) emergency call service.

In this instrument:

***2024 standard*** means the *Telecommunications (Customer Communications for Outages) Industry Standard 2024*.

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***Custodian*** has the same meaning as in the *Telecommunications (Consumer Protection and Service Standards) Act 1999*.

***emergency service organisation*** has the same meaning as in section 147 of the *Telecommunications (Consumer Protection and Service Standards) Act 1999*.

***outage*** means a major outage or significant local outage as defined in the 2024 standard.

***outage register***: see paragraph 6(1)(a).

***relevant carriage service*** has the same meaning as in the 2024 standard.

###### 5 Direction to the ACMA

(1) I direct the ACMA to amend the 2024 standard so that it gives effect to the objectives, and includes the content, set out in section 6.

(2) The amendments required by subsection (1) are to:

(a) be made and commence in full no later than 31 March 2026; and

(b) require the outage registers to be operational by 30 June 2026.

(3) The amendments required by subsection (1) may:

(a) deal with matters differently for different classes of carriers or carriage service providers; and

(b) exempt certain classes of carriers or carriage service providers from particular requirements.

(4) I direct that the ACMA may further amend the 2024 standard as it considers necessary from time to time, provided that the standards in force under subsection 125AA(1) of the Act continue to comply with any directions in force under subsection 125AA(4) of the Act.

(5) In amending the 2024 standard as required by subsection (1), the ACMA may define any terms that it considers appropriate or necessary, including terms used in this instrument but not defined in section 4.

(6) In amending the 2024 standard as required by subsection (1), the ACMA must have regard to other obligations and processes relating to the emergency call service.

###### 6 Additional objectives and content of the standard

*Additional objectives of the standard*

(1) The amendments to the 2024 standard required by subsection 5(1) are to give effect to the following additional objectives, as they relate to the telecommunications activities of carriers, and as appropriate, carriage service providers:

(a) that a register is caused, by each carrier, to be established and published on a publicly available website (an ***outage register***) that includes information about outages that have occurred, or have continued, on or after 31 March 2026;

(b) that the outage register is maintained to ensure information is included in the outage register about an outage as soon as practicable, and is kept up-to-date and accurate;

(c) that information remains in the outage register for a reasonable period of time;

(d) that, to the extent technically practicable and appropriate, the objectives in paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) are given effect to in a manner that reflects the following criteria:

standardised presentation of outage information;

presentation of information in a plain English and accessible format, including compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 and any replacement guidelines or other best practice regarding accessibility;

publication in a manner that allows the information to be extracted by external entities (such as the Custodian or emergency service organisations) for analytical purposes;

presentation of accurate and up-to-date information about outages to the public;

(e) that each carriage service provider who is not a carrier but who supplies a relevant carriage service ensures that, at all times on and after 30 June 2026, there is displayed on its publicly available website a link to the outage register published by the carrier for their service.

Note: The objectives mentioned in this subsection are to be additional to, and not in substitution for, the objectives referred to in subsection 6(1) of the *Telecommunications (Customer Communications for Outages Industry Standards) Direction 2024*.

*Additional content of the standard*

(2) The 2024 standard, as amended to give effect to the objectives in subsection 6(1), must include a requirement for the outage register to display, at a minimum, the following information about each outage, insofar as it is known by the carrier or carriage service provider:

(a) the date the outage commenced;

(b) the duration of the outage;

(c) the geographical areas impacted or likely to be impacted by the outage;

(d) to the extent technically practicable and appropriate, the types and respective numbers of carriage services impacted by the outage;

(e) to the extent technically practicable and appropriate, the number of premises impacted by the outage;

(f) if appropriate, the carrier or carriage service provider responsible for resolving the outage.

Note: The content mentioned in this subsection is to be additional to, and not in substitution for, the content referred to in subsection 6(2) of the *Telecommunications (Customer Communications for Outages Industry Standards) Direction 2024*.

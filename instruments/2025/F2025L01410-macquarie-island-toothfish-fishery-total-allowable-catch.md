Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Macquarie Island Toothfish Fishery (Total Allowable Catch) Determination 2025.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 15 April 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made pursuant to paragraph 17(6)(aa) of the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* and under section 11 of the *Macquarie Island Toothfish Fishery Management Plan 2006* (the Plan).

###### 4 Definitions

A term used in this instrument that is defined for the purposes of the Plan has the same meaning in this instrument as it has in the Plan.

Note: Terms defined in the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* have the same meanings in this instrument.

###### 5 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 14 April 2028.

###### 6 Total Allowable Catch

The total allowable catch for target species and the limits to the amounts that may be taken of other species for the 2026-27 fishing year commencing 15 April 2026 and ending 14 April 2027, and for the fishing year 2027-28, commencing on 15 April 2027 and ending on 14 April 2028 are:

404 tonnes for each fishing year for Patagonian toothfish (*Dissostichus eleginoides)*; and

50 tonnes for each fishing year for each of the other species in the Macquarie Island Toothfish Fishery.

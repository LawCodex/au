## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Rule 2015

**1 Paragraph 16(2)(b)**

Omit “entity; and”, substitute “entity.”

**2 Paragraph 16(2)(c)**

Repeal the paragraph.

**3 Subsections 16(3) and (4)**

Repeal the subsections, substitute:

(3) An application is not required to be accompanied by identity evidence for a person if that person’s identity has previously been verified by the Regulator based on identity evidence provided under:

a biodiversity law (within the meaning of the *Clean Energy Regulator Act 2011*), or

a climate change law (within the meaning of that Act).

**4 Subsection 17(1)**

Omit “that is current at the time the application is made”.

**5 Subsection 18(2)**

**Repeal the subsection, substitute:**

**(2) An application accompanied by a reference from an authorised referee that verifies the individual’s identity is taken to satisfy the requirement that the application be accompanied by identity evidence.**

**6 At the end of Part 29**

Add:

#### Division 4— Application provisions relating to the Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (Identity Evidence) Rule 2025

###### 128 Amendments made by the Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (Identity Evidence) Rule 2025

The amendments of this Rule made by Schedule 1 to the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (Identity Evidence) Rule 2025* (the ***amending rule***) apply in relation to applications made on or after the commencement of the amending rule.

**7 Paragraph 1(b) of Schedule 1**

**Omit “current”.**

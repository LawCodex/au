# Telecommunications (Mobile Network Coverage Maps) Direction 2025

I, Anika Wells, Minister for Communications, make the following direction.

Dated 8 December 2025

Anika Wells

Minister for Communications

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Telecommunications (Mobile Network Coverage Maps) Direction 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 125AA(4) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) ACMA;

(b) carrier;

(c) carriage service;

(d) carriage service provider;

(e) customer equipment; and

(f) telecommunications network.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***National Audit of Mobile Coverage*** means the Australian Government initiative, conducted by Accenture Australia, which is independently measuring and mapping the real-world, actual experience of mobile phone coverage and performance across regional and rural Australia.

***Relevant Mobile Telecommunications Service*** means a service (other than a wi-fi service):

where an end-user can use a carriage service while moving continuously

between places; and

where the customer equipment used for or in relation to the supply of the

service:

is not in physical contact with any part of the telecommunications network by means of which the service is supplied; and

is not a smart device (for example, smart watch);

that enables an end-user to either:

(i) communicate with other end-users either by means of voice calls or short message service; or

(ii) send and receive data and accessing the internet;

that is supplied to the public.

###### 5 Direction to the ACMA

I direct the ACMA to determine a standard under subsection 125AA(1) of the Act that gives effect to the objectives set out in section 7 of this instrument.

The standard is to:

be determined no later than 31 March 2026; and

commence in full at the earliest opportunity and by no later than 30 June 2026.

I direct that the ACMA may vary the standard as it considers necessary from time to time, provided that the standard as varied complies with subsection 5(1) and section 6 of this instrument.

In determining the standard, the ACMA may define any terms that it considers appropriate or necessary, including terms used in this instrument but not defined in section 4 of this instrument or in the Act.

###### 6 Application of the standard and other matters

The standard is to apply, as relevant, to carriers and carriage service providers who supply Relevant Mobile Telecommunications Services.

The standard may:

deal with matters differently for different classes of carriers and carriage service providers; or

exempt certain classes of carriers or carriage service providers from particular provisions of the standard.

###### 7 Objectives of the standard

The standard is to be drafted to give effect to the following objectives, as they relate to how carriers and carriage service providers represent to the public, their customers and prospective customers, network coverage information of the Relevant Mobile Telecommunications Services which they supply (or offer to supply):

that mobile coverage maps are prepared and published in a manner that is comparable, including standardisation of approaches to the extent necessary to ensure meaningful comparability;

that mobile coverage maps are prepared and published in a manner that provides clear, up-to-date and useful information about the service that can reasonably be expected in a given geographical area, and which, to the extent technically and practicably appropriate, incorporates or reflects any or all of the following matters:

appropriate modelling methodologies and assumptions so there is a reasonable likelihood they represent on-ground experience;

a standardised approach to service metrics (which may include for example, representing signal strength, reliability, and quality);

the visual representation of reasonably expected service levels accompanied by plain language descriptions of indicative end-user activities at each level in a given geographical location;

compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, any replacement guidelines or other best practice regarding accessibility;

the provision of information that explains methodologies, assumptions and any limitations around the accuracy of the maps including coverage depiction (for example, regarding indoor coverage, expected coverage for users inside moving vehicles, geographical areas prone to congestion, or known blackspots that conflict with predicted coverage displayed);

capability for enabling data contained in published mobile coverage maps to be extracted by other organisations (such as emergency services organisations) for analytical purposes; and

any other matter that improves the comparability, clarity, and utility of mobile coverage maps; and

mobile coverage maps capture information required under subparagraph 7(1)(b)(iii) for different network technologies or technology platforms (for example, Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite Direct to Device) used by the carrier or carriage service provider to supply the Relevant Mobile Telecommunications Services.

In developing the standard, the ACMA must have regard to all available findings of the National Audit of Mobile Coverage.

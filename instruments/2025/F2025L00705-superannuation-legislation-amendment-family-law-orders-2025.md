###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the following:

(a) section 22CK of the *Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*;

(b) section 93DE of the *Superannuation Act 1922*;

(c) section 146MH of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Superannuation (Family Law — Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948) Orders 2004

**1 Subsection 1.03(1) (definition of FLS Regulations)**

Repeal the definition.

**2 Subsection 1.03(1) (definition of Secretary)**

Omit “Department of Finance and Administration”, substitute “Finance Department”.

**3 Clause 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 (table item 2, column headed “Method or factor”)**

Omit:

(ABP × Parl. A + AARA – PC) × DPFy+m,s

substitute:

(ABP × Parl. A + AARA – PC) × DPFy+m

##### Superannuation (Family Law — Superannuation Act 1922) Orders 2004

**4 Subsection 3(1) (definition of FLS Regulations)**

Omit “*Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*”, substitute “*Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2025*”.

**5 Section 4 (step 2)**

Omit “subclause 2 (2) of Schedule 4”, substitute “clause 3 of Schedule 5”.

**6 Section 6 (steps 3A and 3B)**

Omit “subclause 2 (2) of Schedule 4”, substitute “clause 3 of Schedule 5”.

##### Superannuation (Family Law — Superannuation Act 1976) Orders 2004

**7 Subsection 1.03(1) (definition of FLS Regulations)**

Repeal the definition.

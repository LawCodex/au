## Part 1—Preliminary

### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Continued Fringe Benefits) Determination 2025*.

### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the day it is registered.

### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 53B(1) and (2) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

### 4 Repeals

*Veterans' Entitlements (Continued Fringe Benefits) Determination 2015* (Instrument 2015 No. R24) is repealed.

### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

## Part 2—Continued fringe benefits eligibility circumstances

### 6 Continued fringe benefits eligibility circumstances

For subsection 53A(2) of the Act, a continued fringe benefits eligibility circumstance is that the Commission determines that the person:

has capacity to work for more than 8 hours per week; and

is likely to have that capacity for the foreseeable future.

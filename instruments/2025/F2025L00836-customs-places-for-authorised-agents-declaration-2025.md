# Customs (Places for Authorised Agents) Declaration 2025

LIN25/074

I, Gavan Reynolds AO, Comptroller‑General of Customs, make the following declaration.

Dated 7 July 2025

Gavan Reynolds AO

Comptroller‑General of Customs

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Customs (Places for Authorised Agents) Declaration 2025*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 181(2) of the *Customs Act 1901*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in subsection 4(1) of the Act, including the following:

(a) Australia;

(b) Place.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Customs Act 1901*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Declared places for authorised agents

All places in Australia are declared to be places to which subsection 181(2) of the Act applies.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Comptroller-General of Customs Declaration of Places No. 1 of 2015

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Export Control (Transition Assistance—Phase Out of Live Sheep Exports by Sea Programs) Instrument 2025*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 424E of the *Export Control Act 2020*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Export Control Act 2020*.

***livestock transport business*** means a business that:

(a) on or after the commencement of this instrument is engaged in the transport of live sheep for export by sea; and

(b) was engaged in the transport of live sheep for export by sea for any period of time between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2025.

***livestock transport related business*** means a business that:

(a) on or after the commencement of this instrument is engaged in the transport of goods related to, or for use in, the export of live sheep by sea; and

(b) was engaged in the transport of goods related to, or for use in, the export of live sheep by sea for any period of time between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2025.

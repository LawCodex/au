# Superannuation (CSS) Former Contributors for Units of Pension Regulations

Statutory Rules 1978 No. 281 as amended

made under the

Superannuation Act 1976

This compilation was prepared on 12 June 2003\
taking into account amendments up to SR 1996 No. 102

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting,\
Attorney-General’s Department, Canberra

###### 1 Citation [see Note 1]

These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Former Contributors for Units of Pension Regulations.

###### 2 Commencement

These Regulations shall be deemed to have taken effect from and including 1 July 1976.

###### 3 Interpretation

In these Regulations, ***the Act*** means the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

###### 4 Application of the Act etc to certain persons

The Act and the provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule apply subject to the modifications specified in the Schedule to and in relation to those persons to whom section 183 of the Act applies:

(a) who have ceased to be eligible employees, being eligible employees referred to in paragraph (a) of the definition of ***eligible employee*** in subsection 3 (1) of the Act;

(b) who have not, after so ceasing, again become eligible employees; and

(c) who:

(i) immediately before the commencing day, were, or would, but for subsection 100J (2), 107K (2), 110 (2) or 119K (2) of the superseded Act, have been, contributors to the Fund (within the meaning of the superseded Act) under Part III of that Act; or

(ii) would, but for section 19A of the superseded Act, have become such contributors on or after the commencing day.

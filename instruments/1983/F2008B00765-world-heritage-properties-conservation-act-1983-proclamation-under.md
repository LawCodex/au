# World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983 - Proclamation under section 11 - Franklin River (26/05/1983)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>N. M. STEPHEN<br>Governor General | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of<br>the Commonwealth of<br>Australia |
|---|---|

I, SIR NINIAN MARTIN STEPHEN, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and in pursuance of sub-section 8 (3) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983*, being satisfied that the Aboriginal site, being the open site of ice-age occupation at the base of the flying-fox pylon on the west bank of the Franklin River at approximately Latitude 42° 28’ 02” S, Longitude 145° 45’ 51” E, is likely to be damaged or destroyed, hereby declare that site to be a site to which section 11 of that Act applies.

(L. S.) GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 26 May 1983.

By His Excellency’s Command,

BARRY COHEN

Minister of State for

Home Affairs and Environment

GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!

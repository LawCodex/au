# World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983 - Proclamation under section 9 - Franklin River (26/05/1983)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>N. M. STEPHEN<br>Governor General | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of<br>the Commonwealth of<br>Australia |
|---|---|

I, SIR NINIAN MARTIN STEPHEN, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and in pursuance of sub-section 6 (3) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983*, being satisfied that the property described in the Schedule is likely to be damaged or destroyed, hereby declare that property to be property to which section 9 of that Act applies.

\__________

SCHEDULE

The open archaeological site of ice-age occupation at the base of the flying-fox pylon on the west bank of the Franklin River, in the State of Tasmania, at approximately Latitude 42° 28’ 02” South, Longitude 145° 45’ 51” East.

(L. S.) GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 26 May 1983.

By His Excellency’s Command,

BARRY COHEN

Minister of State for

Home Affairs and Environment

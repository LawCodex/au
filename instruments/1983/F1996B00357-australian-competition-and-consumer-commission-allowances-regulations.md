# Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (Allowances) Regulations

Statutory Rules 1983 No. 241 as amended

made under the

Trade Practices Act 1974

This compilation was prepared on 23 October 2000\
taking into account amendments up to SR 1995 No. 333

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting,\
Attorney-General’s Department, Canberra

###### 1 Citation [see Note 1]

These Regulations may be cited as the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (Allowances) Regulations.

###### 2 Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the contrary intention appears, ***the Act*** means the *Trade Practices Act 1974*.

###### 3 Allowances payable to members of the Commission

For the purposes of subsection 9 (2) of the Act, the following allowances are prescribed, namely, the allowances payable to an officer of the Australian Public Service.

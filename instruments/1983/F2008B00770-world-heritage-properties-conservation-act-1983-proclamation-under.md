# World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983 - Proclamation under section 9 - para 3(b) of the World Heritage Properties Conservation Regulations (26/05/1983)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>N. M. STEPHEN<br>Governor General | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of<br>the Commonwealth of<br>Australia |
|---|---|

I, SIR NINIAN MARTIN STEPHEN, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and in pursuance of sub-section 6 (3) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983*, being satisfied that the property described in paragraph 3 (b) of the World Heritage Properties Conservation Regulations is likely to be damaged or destroyed, hereby declare that property to be property to which section 9 of that Act applies.

(L. S.) GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 26 May 1983.

By His Excellency’s Command,

BARRY COHEN

Minister of State for

Home Affairs and Environment

GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!

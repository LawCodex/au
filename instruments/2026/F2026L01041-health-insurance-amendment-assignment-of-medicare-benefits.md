## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance Regulations 2018

**1 At the end of section 65C**

Add:

Transitional arrangements—episodic agreements entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027

(7) Despite paragraph (6)(c), an episodic agreement entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027 is not required to be signed and dated by the assignor if the assignor provides the assignor’s verbal agreement to enter the agreement to the other party.

(8) If the assignor verbally agrees to enter the agreement in accordance with subsection (7), the agreement must specify that the verbal agreement has been provided and the date on which the verbal agreement was provided.

**2 After subsection 65CA(4)**

Insert:

(4A) Despite subparagraph (2)(b)(ii), a declaration in respect of an enduring agreement entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027 is not required to be in writing.

**3 At the end of subsection 65CA(8)**

Add:

; (f) if the agreement is entered into on the verbal agreement of the assignor—at the start of 1 July 2027.

**4 At the end of section 65CB**

Add:

Transitional arrangements—enduring agreements entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027

(6) Despite paragraph (5)(e), if a declaration in respect of an enduring agreement entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027 is not in writing, the agreement:

(a) is not required to include a written declaration; and

(b) must specify that the declaration has been provided verbally.

(7) Despite paragraph (5)(g), an enduring agreement entered into between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027 is not required to be signed and dated by the assignor if the assignor provides the assignor’s verbal agreement to enter the agreement to the other party.

(8) If the assignor verbally agrees to enter the agreement in accordance with subsection (7), the agreement must specify that the verbal agreement has been provided and the date on which the verbal agreement was provided.

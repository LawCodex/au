###### 6.5.12 Additional removal – urgently required items

<table>
<tr>
<td>1.</td>
<td colspan="2">This section applies to a member who is eligible for a removal of their furniture and effects under this Part.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2.</td>
<td colspan="2">A member is eligible for an additional removal of essential household items if the CDF is satisfied of all of the following.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td>They are required to be removed before the majority of a member’s furniture and effects.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>b.</td>
<td>They will be urgently required at the member’s new location.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3.</td>
<td colspan="2">An <b>additional removal</b> is a removal by one of the following means.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td>A removal by a Commonwealth removalist.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>b.</td>
<td>If the CDF is also satisfied that a privately arranged removal is the most cost‑effective within the required time frame — a privately arranged removal.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>4.</td>
<td colspan="2">If subsection 3.b applies, the member is reimbursed the cost of the privately arranged removal.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td colspan="2">Sections 6.5.15 and 6.5.16</td>

</tr>

<table>
<tr>
<td>1.</td>
<td colspan="3">Subsections 2 and 3 apply if all of the following are met.</td>

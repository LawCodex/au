# Murray-Darling Basin Agreement (Schedule D – Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade) Protocol 2026

The Murray‑Darling Basin Authority, in consultation with the Basin Officials Committee and having considered the advice, if any, given by each person nominated by a Contracting Government under subclause 6(4) of Schedule D to the Murray-Darling Basin Agreement, makes the following Protocol under clause 6 of Schedule D to the Murray-Darling Basin Agreement and section 18E of the *Water Act 2007*.

## Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Murray-Darling Basin Agreement (Schedule D – Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade) Protocol 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under clause 6 of Schedule D to the Murray‑Darling Basin Agreement and section 18E of the *Water Act 2007*.

###### 4 Purpose

The purpose of this protocol is to contribute, in part, to the implementation of the principles in the Annexure to this protocol, which have been agreed by the Contracting Governments.

Note: The operation of the following also contribute to the implementation of the principles in the Annexure:

(a) the Agreement, in particular Schedule D;

(b) procedures;

(c) the Basin Plan;

(d) State water transfer law;

(e) policies made by Contracting Governments.

###### 5 Application

This protocol applies to interstate transfers and intervalley transfers.

###### 6 Definitions

In this protocol:

***Act*** means the *Water Act 2007*.

***Barmah Choke*** means the Barmah Choke as determined by the Ministerial Council under subclause 3(3) of Schedule D to the Agreement.

***exceptional circumstances*** has the meaning given by subsection 12(2).

***process*** in relation to a transfer of tradeable water rights includes all steps related to the processing of the transfer, including recording and reconciling information relating to the transfer.

***State water transfer law*** means a law of a State Contracting Government that regulates transfers to which Schedule D of the Agreement applies and includes any regulations, or other instruments, made under those laws.

***trading zone*** means an area of the Murray-Darling Basin from or to which interstate transfers or intervalley transfers of tradeable water rights may be permitted.

Note 1: A number of expressions used in this protocol are defined in the Act including the following:

(a) Agreement;

(b) Authority;

(c) Basin Plan;

(d) tradeable water rights;

(e) water access right;

(f) water allocation.

Note 2: A number of expressions used in this protocol are defined in the Agreement, including the following:

(a) approval authority;

(b) Barmah Choke allocation account;

(c) Barmah Choke entitlement record;

(d) interstate transfer;

(e) intervalley transfer;

(f) procedures;

(i) State water entitlement;

(k) valley;

(l) financial year.

###### 7 Schedule

Each instrument that is specified in the Schedule to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule.

## Part 2—Processing transfers

###### 8 Processing transfers

(1) The Authority, each State Contracting Government and each approval authority must process interstate transfers and intervalley transfers of tradeable water rights for the purpose of this protocol.

Note: There are other requirements that may apply to the processing of interstate transfers and intervalley transfers of tradeable water rights. These potentially include those under:

(a) the Act; and

(b) the Agreement, in particular Schedule D; and

(c) procedures; and

(d) State water transfer law; and

(e) the Basin Plan, including Water Trading Rules in Chapter 12 and instruments made under it (such as declarations made by the Inspector‑General of Water Compliance made under s 12.22 of the Basin Plan applying to exchange rate trade).

(2) When processing transfers under this section relating to the balance of the Barmah Choke, approval authorities must:

(a) ensure any information published in relation to the balance, or other announcement that may affect the market for transfers across the Barmah Choke, accurately reflects any decision made by the Authority that relates to the balance or such transfers; and

(b) take reasonable steps to ensure the accuracy of transfers recorded that affect the balance; and

(c) promptly rectify any errors relating to the balance.

###### 9 Notification of changes to State water transfer law

Each State Contracting Government must notify the Authority and each other State Contracting Government of any changes made to its State water transfer law that affect the processing of interstate transfers and intervalley transfers under this protocol.

## Part 3—Transfers across the Barmah Choke

###### 10 Barmah Choke allocation account

(1) The Authority must record relevant information against the Barmah Choke allocation account for the purposes of sub-clause 11(3) of Schedule D to the Agreement whenever it is notified that an approval authority or State Contracting Government approves a water allocation transfer across the Barmah Choke.

(2) An approval authority or State Contracting Government must not approve a transfer if that transfer would result in a negative balance in the Barmah Choke allocation account.

(3) In maintaining the Barmah Choke allocation account the Authority must:

(a) establish systems and processes for the purpose of enabling updates to the balance of the Barmah Choke allocation account in real time, or as close as reasonably possible to real time; and

(b) publish information on the balance of the Barmah Choke allocation account in a manner that makes it generally available.

###### 11 Restrictions on transfer of water access entitlements

A water access entitlement may not be transferred from above to below the Barmah Choke, except where the entitlement has previously been entered in the Barmah Choke entitlement record as having been transferred from below to above the Barmah Choke.

###### 12 Exceptional circumstances advice

(1) For the purposes of clause 12 of Schedule D to the Agreement, the Authority may advise in relation to water allocation transfers across the Barmah Choke:

(a) that the Authority is satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist, or are likely to exist, in respect of net transfers of water allocations from above to below the Barmah Choke for the period; and

(b) the maximum volume of transfers that would be justified for the period by the exceptional circumstances; and

(c) any conditions relating to such transfers that the Authority considers would be appropriate having regard to the exceptional circumstances.

Note: Section 101B of the Act prescribes requirements for a how a person who makes a water market decision first announces the decision.

(2) ***Exceptional circumstances*** exist if the usual challenges in delivering water within the channel capacity at the Barmah Choke are significantly reduced.

(3) The Authority may only advise that exceptional circumstances exist or are likely to exist if it is satisfied that hydrologic connections and water supply considerations can be managed such that the advice, if acted on by State Contracting Governments, would not lead to material negative impacts on:

(a) the environmental condition of the Barmah-Millewa Forest; or

(b) the availability of water to fulfil the water access rights of third parties.

Note: See items 17 and 20 in clause 1 of the Annexure to this protocol for the potential interaction between a determination of exceptional circumstances with the law of a State.

(4) In deciding whether exceptional circumstances exist, the Authority must have regard to the following:

(a) current water allocations against entitlements in trading zones above and below the Barmah Choke;

(b) projected water availability for the specified financial year and future financial years;

(c) current and projected demand for water allocations below the Barmah Choke;

(d) available channel capacity for the specified financial year;

(e) potential impacts of transfers that may be permitted for the specified financial year on the delivery of State water entitlements if State Contracting Governments were to act in accordance with the advice;

(f) any advice provided by the Basin Officials Committee following consultation;

(g) any other factor the Authority considers relevant.

(5) If the Authority provides advice under subsection (1), the Authority must publish reasons explaining the basis on which it is satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist in a manner that makes those reasons generally available.

###### 13 Announcement of matters relevant to exceptional circumstances advice

(1) The Authority must announce for each financial year whether or not it will consider providing advice under clause 12 of Schedule D to the Agreement for a period or periods within that financial year.

Note: Section 101B of the Act prescribes requirements for a how a person who makes a water market decision first announces the decision.

(2) An announcement must be made by no later than the 31st of May before the beginning of the relevant financial year.

(3) The Authority may make an announcement for the purposes of subsection (1) based on any information it considers relevant.

(4) The Authority may revoke an announcement and make a new announcement to replace it at any time before the start of the specified financial year.

(5) For the avoidance of doubt, an announcement made under this section that the Authority will consider providing advice does not require the Authority to provide advice under clause 12 of Schedule D.

###### 14 Arrangements to support trade opportunities

The Authority and the approval authorities for New South Wales and Victoria are to agree arrangements for processing interstate transfers and intervalley transfers across the Barmah Choke.

Note: Section 101B of the Act prescribes requirements for a how a person who makes a water market decision first announces the decision.

## Part 4—Miscellaneous

###### 15 Review

The Authority may review and, if appropriate, consider amending this protocol if:

(a) the Agreement is amended in a way that may be relevant to interstate transfers or intervalley transfers; or

(b) any report prepared under clause 15 of Schedule D to the Agreement indicates that a review of this protocol is appropriate; or

(c) an independent auditor appointed under Schedule D to the Agreement recommends, in connection with his or her appointment as an independent auditor, that a review is appropriate.

**Annexure**

(1) The Contracting Governments have agreed the following principles in seeking to further the purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement.

| **Item** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| 1 | Interstate transfers and intervalley transfers of tradeable water rights may occur in accordance with rules for interstate transfers and intervalley transfers, including those specifying trading zones, types of tradeable water rights, and types of transfer permitted between particular sources as reported by the State Contracting Governments, for the purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement. |
| 2 | Interstate and intervalley trading zones will be specified in procedures. The Authority will work with Contracting State Governments and the Basin Officials Committee to support the Basin Officials Committee to define trading zones in the procedures. |
| 3 | Interstate transfers and intervalley transfers, including water allocation transfers and tagged trade, will be permitted subject to conditions imposed by the laws of the relevant State Contracting Governments. Conditions applying to each trading zone will be specified in procedures. |
| 4 | State Contracting Governments and the Authority will process transfers, including recording and reconciling information relating to transfers, in accordance with the protocols and procedures. |
| 5 | State Contracting Governments should not approve transfers that are not permitted for the purposes of the matters agreed to further the purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement. |
| 6 | When processing transfers, the Authority, approval authorities and State Contracting Governments will comply with the procedures. |
| 7 | The Contracting Governments affirm that exchange rates will not be applied as a condition of exchange rate trade, and transfers of this kind will not be approved, unless the Inspector‑General of Water Compliance has made an applicable declaration under section 12.22 of the Basin Plan. |
| 8 | A retailer in a State of Destination may impose and recover such charges as it is entitled to recover from the person entitled to receive a tagged allocation under the law of the State of Destination. |
| 9 | Bulk water suppliers in a State of Origin should adopt the principle that bulk water charges be billed in the same way as State water entitlement holders are charged, that is, directly to the interstate owner of the share as at the date of billing. |
| 10 | The Authority, State Contracting Governments and approval authorities will process transfers relating to the Barmah Choke in accordance with the protocols and procedures. |
| 11 | The Authority and the approval authorities for New South Wales and Victoria will agree on arrangements for processing transfers across the Barmah Choke. This subsequent agreement will be reflected in the procedures. |
| 12 | There will be no transfer of water access entitlements from above to below the Barmah Choke, except where the entitlement has previously been entered in the Barmah Choke entitlement record as having been transferred from below to above the Barmah Choke. |
| 13 | The Authority is responsible for establishing and maintaining the Barmah Choke allocation account. (2) In this Annexure:

***bulk water supplier*** means an authority responsible for recovering costs associated with operating headworks from which allocations are supplied within a State of Origin.

***retailer*** means the authority responsible for accounting for transfers of allocations, administering tagging arrangements and accounting for water used pursuant to allocations within a State.

***State of Destination*** means the State in which an allocation made under a tagged entitlement will be extracted.

***State of Origin*** means the State in which an allocation under a tagged entitlement will be made.

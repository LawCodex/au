### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Multiple Entitlement Exclusion) Determination 2026*.

### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 424L(1) of the Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901.*

**4 Repeals**

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Multiple Entitlement Exclusion) Determination 2024* is repealed.

5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

energy supplement.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

### 6 Person not entitled to energy supplement

A person is not entitled to an energy supplement under section 83A of the Act for a day if the person is entitled to an energy supplement for the day under:

section 209A of the Act;

section 220D of the Act; or

section 62A of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

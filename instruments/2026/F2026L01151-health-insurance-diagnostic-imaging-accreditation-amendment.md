## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Accreditation) Instrument 2020

**1 Section 5**

Repeal the section.

**2 Subsection 6(1) (paragraph (b) of the definition of Medical Imaging Accreditation Program)**

Omit “Version 11-2019 RANZCR standards as at 30 June 2019”, substitute “Version 12-2025 RANZCR standards as at 7 November 2025”.

**3 Subsection 6(1) (definition of previous instrument)**

Repeal the definition.

**4 Subsection 7(2)**

Repeal the subsection.

**5 Paragraph 9(1)(b)**

Omit “previous instrument”, substitute “*Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Accreditation) Instrument 2010*”.

**6 Subsection 17(1)**

After “the day specified in the decision”, insert “(which must be on or after the day on which the decision is made)”.

**7 Paragraph 17(2)(d)**

After “procedures”, insert “(other than a decision made on the ground that there is a potential danger to public health or safety if the accreditation is not varied)”.

**8 Paragraph 17(2)(e)**

After “part”, insert “(other than a decision made on the ground that there is a potential danger to public health or safety if the accreditation is not suspended)”.

**9 Section 19**

Repeal the section (not including the heading), substitute:

Application for accreditation made under section 11 before 1 October 2026

The amendments made by the *Health Insurance (Diagnostic Imaging Accreditation) Amendment Instrument 2026* to the definition of ***Medical Imaging Accreditation Program*** in subsection 6(1) of this instrument do not apply in relation to an application for accreditation made before 1 October 2026 but not decided before 1 October 2026.

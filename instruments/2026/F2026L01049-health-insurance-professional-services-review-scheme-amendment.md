## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Professional Services Review Scheme) Regulations 2019

**1 Section 5**

Insert:

***relevant DVA law*** has the same meaning as in Part VAA of the Act.

**2 Section 5 (definitions of relevant phone service and relevant service)**

Repeal the definitions, substitute:

***relevant phone service***: see section 5B.

***relevant service***: see section 5A.

**3 At the end of Part 1**

Add:

###### 5A Meaning of relevant service

(1) A ***relevant service*** is:

(a) a service specified in any of the items of the general medical services table covered by subsection (2); or

(b) a service:

(i) to which paragraph (c) or (d) of the definition of ***service*** in subsection 81(1) of the Act applies; and

(ii) that is of a kind that, if the service had not been rendered in connection with the provision of treatment under a relevant DVA law, medicare benefit would have been payable in respect of the service for a service specified in any of the items of the general medical services table covered by subsection (2).

(2) This subsection covers the following items of the general medical services table:

(a) an item in Group A1, A2, A5, A6, A7, A9, A11, A13, A14, A15, A17, A20, A21, A22, A23, A27, A35 or A48;

(b) an item in Subgroup 1 or 4 of Group A36;

(i) Subgroup 1;

(ii) Subgroup 2;

(iii) Subgroup 3;

(iv) Subgroup 10;

(v) Subgroup 11;

(vi) Subgroup 13;

(vii) Subgroup 15;

(viii) Subgroup 16;

(ix) Subgroup 19;

(x) Subgroup 21;

(xi) Subgroup 27;

(xii) Subgroup 28;

(xiii) Subgroup 29;

(xiv) Subgroup 39;

(xv) Subgroup 40;

(d) an item listed in the following table.

###### 5B Meaning of relevant phone service

(1) A ***relevant phone service*** is:

(a) a service specified in any of the items of the general medical services table covered by subsection (2); or

(b) a service:

(i) to which paragraph (c) or (d) of the definition of ***service*** in subsection 81(1) of the Act applies; and

(ii) that is of a kind that, if the service had not been rendered in connection with the provision of treatment under a relevant DVA law, medicare benefit would have been payable in respect of the service for a service specified in any of the items of the general medical services table covered by subsection (2).

(2) This subsection covers the following items of the general medical services table:

(a) an item in any of the following Subgroups of Group A40:

(i) Subgroup 2;

(ii) Subgroup 8;

(iii) Subgroup 10;

(iv) Subgroup 16;

(v) Subgroup 28;

(vi) Subgroup 40;

(b) item 92176 or 92177 in Subgroup 26 of Group A40.

Note: Some services are specified in items set out in determinations under subsection 3C(1) of the Act.

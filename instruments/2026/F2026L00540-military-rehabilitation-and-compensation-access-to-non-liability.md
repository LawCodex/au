## Part 1—Preliminary

### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Access to Non-Liability Health Care for Malignant Neoplasia and Pulmonary Tuberculosis Treatment) Determination 2026.*

### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraphs 284A(1)(a) and (d) of the Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901.*

4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

member of a Peacekeeping Force;

VEA.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***Australia*** includes an external territory.

*specified condition* means one or more of the following conditions:

malignant neoplasia; or

pulmonary tuberculosis.

***specified treatment*** means the treatment specified in section 7.

Part 2—Specified class of person

### 5 Eligibility

A person is eligible for the specified treatment if the person meets either of the following criteria:

the person is taken to have rendered eligible war service due to the applicability of section 7 of the VEA to the person;

the person is eligible for a pension due to the applicability of subsection 13(6) of the VEA to the person.

Despite subsection (1), a person is not eligible for the specified treatment if:

the person meets the criteria specified in subsection (1) only because of operational service rendered as a member of the naval, military or air forces of a Commonwealth country or an allied country; and

the Commission is not satisfied that the person was domiciled in Australia immediately before their appointment or enlistment in the naval, military or air forces of a Commonwealth country or an allied country.

A person is eligible for the specified treatment if the person was, or would have been, eligible for a pension under sections 69, 69A or 69B of the VEA insofar as those sections applied, or would have applied, to the person immediately before 1 July 2026.

A person is eligible for the specified treatment if the person is a member of a Peacekeeping Force.

### 6 Making a request for treatment

To be eligible for the specified treatment, a person who meets the criteria in section 5 must make a request for the specified treatment.

A request for treatment under this section must be made in writing in the form approved by the Commission for the purposes of this instrument.

**Part 3—Specified kind of treatment**

7 Treatment for malignant neoplasia or pulmonary tuberculosis

A person who is eligible in accordance with section 5 may be provided with treatment that can be:

applied to the specified condition; and

provided under subsection 287(1) of the Act or Part V of the VEA.

**Part 4—Transitional and other matters**

8 Transitional matters

A request for treatment for the specified condition that was received by the Department prior to the commencement of this instrument may be dealt with as if it were made under this instrument where, at the time of commencement:

the Department has not yet processed the request; or

the treatment has not been completed.

Note: This section applies to treatment for the specified condition that was underway under subsection 85(2) of the VEA immediately prior to commencement of this instrument.

9 Other matters—backdating treatment

A person who is eligible to be provided with the specified treatment is eligible from and including the date that is 3 months before the request under section 6 is received by the Department.

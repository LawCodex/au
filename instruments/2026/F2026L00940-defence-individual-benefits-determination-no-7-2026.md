###### 4 Interpretation

1\. The following definitions apply in this Determination:

**Annual allowance** has the same meaning given in section 5.4.42 of the Defence Determination.

**Defence Determination** means Defence Determination 2016/19, Conditions of service, as in force from time to time.

**Reference salary** has the same means as given in Remuneration Tribunal (Compensation for Loss of Office for Holders of Certain Public Offices) Determination 2018, as in force from time to time.

**Salary** means the amount payable to the member under section B.1.6 of the Salaries Determination.

**Salaries Determination** means Defence Force Remuneration Tribunal Determination No. 2 of 2017, as in force from time to time.

**Transition period** has the same meaning given in section 5.1.1A of the Defence Determination.

2\. A reference to the **daily rate** of an annual rate of an allowance or salary, including the reference salary, means one 365th of the annual rate.

###### 5 Application

This Determination applies to the member with the employee identification number 8080430 from the day they commence their transition period.

###### 8 Payment on taking recreation leave

Despite subsection 5.4.36.1 of the Defence Determination, the member is to be paid the daily rate of salary on a day the member is on recreation leave.

**Note:** The allowance payable under section 6 of this Determination continues to be payable on a day the member takes recreation leave.

Subsection 5.4.36.4 of the Defence Determination does not apply to the member.

###### 9 Payout of recreation leave

For the purpose of the formula in subsection 5.4.44A of the Defence Determination, the following apply.

a. **A** is the sum of the following:

i. the daily rate of the member’s reference salary on their last day of their appointment as a statutory office holder.

ii. the daily rate of annual allowances the member is eligible for.

b. **D** is the sum of the following:

i. the daily rate of the member’s reference salary on their last day of their appointment as a statutory office holder.

ii. the daily rate of annual allowances the member is eligible for.

Despite subsection 5.5.26.4 and subsection 5.5.26.6 of the Defence Determination, the payment for long service leave credits is calculated in accordance with the following formula.

[image: Start formula payment of long service leave credits equals A multiplied by open bracket the sum of B and C divided by 12 close bracket end formula]

Where:

**A** is the number of long service leave credits in months the member holds on their last day of service.

**B** is the member’s reference salary on their last day of their appointment as a statutory office holder.

**C** is the sum of the rate of any annual allowances payable to the member on their last day of service in the ADF.

###### 11 Cessation of benefits

This determination ceases to apply on the day after any of the following occur.

The member ceases to be a member of the Permanent Forces.

The member is appointed, or posted, to a position in which they cease to undertake activities that support their transition from the Permanent Forces.

###### 12 Repeal

This Determination is repealed on 31 December 2027.

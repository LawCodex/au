##### Subdivision E—Secondary person‑centred supplements—New home supplement and significant expansion supplement

###### 231‑55 Purpose of this Subdivision

For the purposes of section 231 of the Act, this Subdivision prescribes:

(a) the following secondary person‑centred supplements for an individual for a day for the classification types ongoing and short‑term for the service group residential care:

(i) the new home supplement;

(ii) the significant expansion supplement; and

(b) the circumstances in which those supplements will apply to an individual for a day; and

(c) the amounts of those supplements for an individual for a day; and

(d) the requirements for making and revoking determinations for those supplements.

Note: Subdivision D of this Division prescribes the following secondary person‑centred supplements for the purposes of subsection 231(1) of the Act:

(a) the fee reduction supplement;

(b) the initial entry adjustment supplement;

(c) the veterans’ supplement;

(d) respite supplement.

###### 231‑60 New home supplement—circumstances for applicability and amount

Circumstances for applicability of new home supplement

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 231(2)(a) of the Act, the circumstances in which the new home supplement will apply to an individual for a day are that:

(a) the funded aged care service delivered to the individual is for the classification type ongoing or short‑term for the service group residential care in an approved residential care home; and

(b) the funded aged care service is delivered to the individual in a post‑2025 newly constructed home (see section 231‑65); and

(c) either or both of the following apply:

(i) the individual is a low means resident;

(ii) the individual is an individual to whom the respite supplement applies (see section 231‑30); and

(d) a new home supplement determination is in effect under subsection 231‑85(1) in relation to the home for the day; and

(e) a registered provider did not receive Commonwealth funding under the Aged Care Capital Assistance Program for the construction of the home.

Amount

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 231(2)(b) of the Act, the following amount of the new home supplement is prescribed:

(a) for a day before 1 January 2027—nil;

(b) for a day in the period during which a new home supplement determination is in effect between 1 January 2027 and 31 December 2051—$30.00 for an individual for a day;

(c) for a day on or after 1 January 2052—nil.

Note: For the purposes of paragraph (b), the new home supplement determination is in effect for a maximum period of 25 years between 1 January 2027 and 31 December 2051 (see subsection 231‑85(3)).

###### 231‑65 Meaning of post‑2025 newly constructed home

An approved residential care home is a ***post‑2025 newly constructed home*** if:

(a) each building in which funded aged care services are delivered to individuals in the home was constructed on or after 1 November 2025; or

(b) each building in which funded aged care services are delivered to individuals was converted, on or after 1 November 2025, from one or more buildings that, before that date, were used for a purpose other than delivering any of the following:

(i) funded aged care services to individuals in an approved residential care home;

(ii) residential care in a residential care service (within the meaning of the *Aged Care Act 1997*, as in force immediately before 1 November 2025);

(iii) flexible care provided in a residential setting in a flexible care service (within the meaning of the *Aged Care Act 1997*, as in force immediately before 1 November 2025).

###### 231‑70 Significant expansion supplement—circumstances for applicability and amount

Circumstances for applicability of significant expansion supplement

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 231(2)(a) of the Act, the circumstances in which the significant expansion supplement will apply to an individual for a day are that:

(a) the funded aged care service delivered to the individual is for the classification type ongoing or short‑term for the service group residential care in an approved residential care home; and

(b) the funded aged care service is delivered to the individual in a significantly expanded home (see section 231‑75); and

(c) either or both of the following apply:

(i) the individual is a low means resident;

(ii) the individual is an individual to whom the respite supplement applies (see section 231‑30); and

(d) a significant expansion supplement determination is in effect under subsection 231‑90(1) in relation to the home for the day; and

(e) the new home supplement does not apply in relation to the home for the individual for the day; and

(f) a registered provider did not receive Commonwealth funding under the Aged Care Capital Assistance Program for the expansion of the home.

Amount

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 231(2)(b) of the Act, the following amount of the significant expansion supplement is prescribed:

(a) for a day before 1 January 2027—nil;

(b) for a day in the period during which a significant expansion supplement determination is in effect between 1 January 2027 and 31 December 2051—$15.00 for an individual for a day;

(c) for a day on or after 1 January 2052—nil.

Note: For the purposes of paragraph (b), the significant expansion supplement determination is in effect for a maximum period of 15 years between 1 January 2027 and 31 December 2051 (see subsection 231‑90(3)).

###### 231‑75 Meaning of significantly expanded home

An approved residential care home is a ***significantly expanded home*** if the number of operational beds for the home increases by 40% or more after 1 November 2025, including because of an extension to the home or the conversion of offline beds into operational beds.

###### 231‑80 Application for new home supplement or significant expansion supplement determination

Application for new home supplement determination

(1) From 1 December 2026, a registered provider may apply, in the approved form, to the System Governor for a new home supplement determination under subsection 231‑85(1) in relation to an approved residential care home of the provider.

Note: For penalties for knowingly providing false or misleading information or documents in applications, see section 591 of the Act and Part 7.4 of the *Criminal Code*.

Application for significant expansion supplement determination

(2) From 1 December 2026, a registered provider may apply, in the approved form, to the System Governor for a significant expansion supplement determination under subsection 231‑90(1) in relation to an approved residential care home of the provider.

Note: For penalties for knowingly providing false or misleading information or documents in applications, see section 591 of the Act and Part 7.4 of the *Criminal Code*.

###### 231‑85 New home supplement determination

System Governor must decide application for determination

(1) The System Governor must, within 28 days after receiving an application in accordance with subsection 231‑80(1), determine:

(a) whether to make a new home supplement determination in relation to the approved residential care home; and

(b) if the System Governor decides to make the determination under paragraph (a)—the period specified in accordance with subsection (3) during which the determination is in effect.

Note 1: A decision by the System Governor under paragraph (a) of this subsection to refuse to make a new home supplement determination is a reviewable decision under section 557 of the Act (see section 557‑5 of this instrument).

Note 2: See also section 588 of the Act (which allows the System Governor to request further information, and provides for the consequence of not giving any requested further information in the requested time, and the extension of the decision‑making period if requested information is given in the requested time).

(2) In deciding whether to make the determination, the System Governor must consider whether the circumstances for the new home supplement set out in paragraphs 231‑60(1)(a), (b) and (e) apply in relation to the approved residential care home of the registered provider.

Maximum period for determination

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the maximum period that may be specified is a period of 25 years that:

(a) begins on the later of:

(i) 1 January 2027; and

(ii) the day the new home supplement determination takes effect under paragraph 231‑95(3)(a); and

(b) ends on the earlier of:

(i) the day the new home supplement determination is revoked under subsection 231‑105(1); and

(ii) 31 December 2051.

Example 1: If the new home supplement determination takes effect on 1 January 2027, the determination will be in effect for 25 years (ending on 31 December 2051), unless the determination is revoked earlier (see subsection 231‑105(1)).

Example 2: If the new home supplement determination takes effect on 1 January 2032, the determination will be in effect for 20 years (ending on 31 December 2051), unless the determination is revoked earlier (see subsection 231‑105(1)).

###### 231‑90 Significant expansion supplement determination

System Governor must decide application for determination

(1) The System Governor must, within 28 days after receiving an application in accordance with subsection 231‑80(2), determine:

(a) whether to make a significant expansion supplement determination in relation to the approved residential care home; and

(b) if the System Governor decides to make the determination under paragraph (a)—the period specified in accordance with subsection (3) during which the determination is in effect.

Note 1: A decision by the System Governor under paragraph (a) of this subsection to refuse to make a significant expansion supplement determination is a reviewable decision under section 557 of the Act (see section 557‑5 of this instrument).

Note 2: See also section 588 of the Act (which allows the System Governor to request further information, and provides for the consequence of not giving any requested further information in the requested time, and the extension of the decision‑making period if requested information is given in the requested time).

(2) In deciding whether to make the determination, the System Governor must consider whether the circumstances for the significant expansion supplement set out in paragraphs 231‑60(1)(a), (b), (e) and (f) apply in relation to the approved residential care home of the registered provider.

Maximum period for determination

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the maximum period that may be specified is a period of 15 years that:

(a) begins on the later of:

(i) 1 January 2027; and

(ii) the day the significant expansion supplement determination takes effect under paragraph 231‑95(3)(b); and

(b) ends on the earlier of:

(i) the day the significant expansion supplement determination is revoked under subsection 231‑105(2); and

(ii) 31 December 2051.

Example 1: If the significant expansion supplement determination takes effect on 1 January 2027, the determination will be in effect for 15 years (ending on 31 December 2041), unless the determination is revoked earlier (see subsection 231‑105(2)).

Example 2: If the significant expansion supplement determination takes effect on 1 January 2040, the determination will be in effect for 12 years (ending on 31 December 2051), unless the determination is revoked earlier (see subsection 231‑105(2)).

###### 231‑95 Notification of System Governor’s decision on application for new home supplement determination or significant expansion supplement determination

System Governor must give notice of determination decision

(1) The System Governor must, within 14 days after making either of the following decisions, give written notice to the applicant of:

(a) a decision about whether to make a new home supplement determination under subsection 231‑85(1);

(b) a decision about whether to make a significant expansion supplement determination under subsection 231‑90(1).

Content of notice

(2) The notice must state the following (as applicable):

(a) the System Governor’s decision about whether to make the determination;

(b) if the System Governor’s decision is to make the determination—both of the following:

(i) the day on which the determination takes effect;

(ii) the day on which the determination will cease;

(c) if the System Governor’s decision is to refuse to make the determination—the reasons for the decision.

Note: For the purposes of subparagraph (b)(ii):

(a) the new home supplement determination will cease at the end of the period specified in subsection 231‑85(3); and

(b) the significant expansion supplement determination will cease at the end of the period specified in subsection 231‑90(3).

Day determination takes effect

(3) For the purposes of subparagraph (2)(b)(i), the determination takes effect on the day:

(a) for a new home supplement determination—the application for a determination was made under subsection 231‑80(1); or

(b) for a significant expansion supplement determination—the application for a determination was made under subsection 231‑80(2).

###### 231‑100 Transfer of entitlement to new home supplement or significant expansion supplement to another registered provider

(1) This section applies if:

(a) either of the following is in effect in relation to the approved residential care home of a registered provider (the ***outgoing provider***) in which funded aged care services are delivered to an individual:

(i) a new home supplement determination under subsection 231‑85(1);

(ii) a significant expansion supplement determination under subsection 231‑90(1); and

(b) the outgoing provider ceases to deliver the ongoing or short term funded aged care services through the service group residential care to the individual in the approved residential care home; and

(c) there is continuity of the delivery of those services to that individual through that service group in the same approved residential care home by another registered provider (the ***incoming provider***).

(2) The outgoing provider ceases to be entitled to be paid an amount of supplement for an individual, and the incoming provider becomes entitled to be paid that amount, on the day on which the incoming provider commences delivering the funded aged care service to the individual in the approved residential care home (whether that entitlement arose before, on or after that day).

Note: If this section applies, the incoming provider is not required to apply for a determination in relation to the home in the period during which that determination is in effect. However, that determination may be revoked, see section 231‑105.

###### 231‑105 Revoking a determination

New home supplement determination

(1) The System Governor may revoke a new home supplement determination that is in effect in relation to an approved residential care home if:

(a) the System Governor reasonably believes that the circumstances for making that determination were not met in relation to the home, at the time the determination was made; or

(b) all of the following apply:

(i) the System Governor is notified of the occurrence of an event or a change in relation to the home;

(ii) the requirements for making the determination are no longer met in relation to the home;

(iii) the System Governor considers it reasonable to do so.

Note: A decision under this subsection by the System Governor to revoke the new home supplement determination is a reviewable decision under section 557 of the Act (see section 557‑5 of this instrument).

Significant expansion supplement determination

(2) The System Governor may revoke a significant expansion supplement determination that is in effect in relation to an approved residential care home if:

(a) the System Governor reasonably believes that the circumstances for making that determination were not met in relation to the home, at the time the determination was made; or

(b) all of the following apply:

(i) the System Governor is notified of the occurrence of an event or a change in relation to the home;

(ii) the requirements for making that determination are no longer met in relation to the home;

(iii) the System Governor considers it reasonable to do so.

Note: A decision under this subsection by the System Governor to revoke the significant expansion supplement determination is a reviewable decision under section 557 of the Act (see section 557‑5 of this instrument).

Effect of revocation

(3) If a new home supplement determination or significant expansion supplement determination is revoked under subsection (1) or (2), the revocation takes effect on the day the System Governor revokes that determination.

###### 231‑110 Revoking a determination—notice before revocation

Notice must be given before revoking determination

(1) Before the System Governor revokes either of the following determinations in relation to an approved residential care home, the System Governor must give the registered provider of the home written notice that the System Governor is considering revoking the determination:

(a) a new home supplement determination made under subsection 231‑85(1) in relation to the home;

(b) a significant expansion supplement determination made under subsection 231‑90(1) in relation to the home.

Content of notice

(2) The notice must:

(a) state the determination the System Governor is considering revoking; and

(b) set out the reasons why the System Governor is considering revoking the determination; and

(c) invite the registered provider to make a submission, in writing, to the System Governor in relation to the matter within the period specified in the notice (which must not be less than 28 days after giving the notice); and

(d) inform the registered provider that the System Governor may decide to revoke the determination:

(i) if no submission is made within the specified period; or

(ii) after considering any submission made by the provider within the specified period.

System Governor may request further information

(3) The System Governor may also request, in the notice given under subsection (1), that the registered provider give the System Governor the information specified in the notice, within the period specified in the notice for the purposes of paragraph (2)(c), to assist the System Governor to decide whether to revoke the determination.

Note: The registered provider is not obliged to give the requested information.

(4) If the registered provider makes a submission in accordance with the invitation under paragraph (2)(c), the System Governor may, by written notice, request the individual to give the System Governor further information, within the period specified in the notice (which must not be less than 28 days after giving the notice), to assist the System Governor to decide whether to revoke the determination.

Note 1: The System Governor may request further information under this subsection regardless of whether information was requested or given under subsection (3).

Note 2: The registered provider is not obliged to give the requested information.

Extended period for making submission or giving further information

(5) The System Governor may, at the request of the individual, extend the period specified for the purposes of paragraph (2)(c) or subsection (4).

System Governor must consider submissions and further information

(6) The System Governor must consider any submission made in accordance with the invitation under paragraph (2)(c) and any further information given in response to a request under subsection (3) or (4).

Timeframe for deciding whether to revoke determination

(7) The System Governor must make a decision whether to revoke the determination within 28 days of the day that begins on:

(a) if no submission is made in accordance with the invitation in the notice under paragraph (2)(c)—the day after the end of:

(i) the period specified in the notice under paragraph (2)(c); or

(ii) if the period specified in the notice has been extended under subsection (5)—the extended period; or

(b) the later of:

(i) if a submission is made in accordance with the invitation in the notice under paragraph (2)(c) and no further information is requested under subsection (4)—the day the submission is made; and

(ii) if a submission is made in accordance with the invitation in the notice under paragraph (2)(c) and further information is requested under subsection (4)—the day the registered provider gives the System Governor the further information.

###### 231‑115 Notice of decision to revoke supplement determination

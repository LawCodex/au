## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Aged Care Rules 2025

**1 Subsection 87‑7(1) (table item 1)**

Repeal the item, substitute:

| 1 | Immediate | Either of the following circumstances applies to the individual:<br>(a) an approved needs assessor considers the classification type short‑term for the service group home support should be approved for the individual;<br>(b) the individual had motor neurone disease at the time the individual’s most recent aged care needs assessment was undertaken |
|---|---|---|

**2 Subsection 87‑7(2) (table item 1)**

Repeal the item, substitute:

| 1 | Immediate | Either of the following circumstances applies to the individual:<br>(a) an approved needs assessor considers the classification type short‑term for the service group home support should be approved for the individual;<br>(b) the individual had motor neurone disease at the time the individual’s most recent aged care needs assessment was undertaken |
|---|---|---|

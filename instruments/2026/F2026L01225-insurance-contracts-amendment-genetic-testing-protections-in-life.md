## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Insurance Contracts Regulations 2017

**1 Part 2 (heading)**

Repeal the heading, substitute:

### Part 2—Disclosures, misrepresentations and use of certain information

**2 At the end of Part 2**

Add:

#### Division 4—Use of protected genetic information in relation to contracts of life insurance

###### 13A Information taken to be protected genetic information

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33F(5) of the Act, information mentioned in paragraphs 33F(1)(a) and (b) of the Act about an individual is ***protected genetic information*** if the information relates to genetic testing of the individual (or a genetic relative of the individual) for any of the following:

(a) hereditary cancer predispositions, including (without limitation) Lynch Syndrome, Gorlin Syndrome, BRCA1‑ and BRCA2‑associated hereditary cancer syndromes and Li‑Fraumeni Syndrome;

(b) hereditary cardiovascular predispositions, including (without limitation) hypertrophic cardiomyopathy;

(c) hereditary haematological predispositions, including (without limitation) Factor V Leiden thrombophilia;

(d) hereditary neurological, neuromuscular and muscular predispositions, including (without limitation) Huntington disease and Friedreich ataxia;

(e) hereditary renal predispositions, including (without limitation) autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease and Alport syndrome;

(f) hereditary respiratory predispositions, including (without limitation) alpha‑1 antitrypsin deficiency;

(g) hereditary metabolic predispositions, including (without limitation) Fabry disease and familial hypercholesterolaemia.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 33F(5) of the Act, information about an individual is ***protected genetic information*** if:

(a) the information relates to a disease for which the individual, or a genetic relative of the individual, has received a clinical diagnosis; but

(b) no signs or symptoms of the disease have manifested in the person who received the diagnosis.

Note: This subsection has effect despite subsection 33F(2) of the Act (see subsection 33F(5) of the Act).

(3) Subsection (2) does not limit subsection (1).

(4) This section does not limit the things covered by subsection 33F(1) of the Act.

**4 In the appropriate position in Part 5**

Insert:

###### 43 Application of amendments made by the Insurance Contracts Amendment (Genetic Testing Protections in Life Insurance) Regulations 2026

(1) Despite the repeal and substitution of section 39A by Schedule 1 to the *Insurance Contracts Amendment (Genetic Testing Protections in Life Insurance) Regulations 2026*, an offence against subsection 33C(5) of the Act is a prescribed offence for the purpose of paragraph 75X(a) of the Act regardless of whether the conduct constituting the offence occurred before, on or after the commencement of that Schedule.

(2) Nothing in Schedule 1 to the *Insurance Contracts Amendment (Genetic Testing Protections in Life Insurance) Regulations 2026* affects the validity of an infringement notice issued before the commencement of that Schedule.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services – Allied Health and other Primary Health Care Services) Determination 2024

**1 Schedule 2 (items 10955, 10957 and 10959, column 2, paragraph (b))**

Omit “aged care facility”, substitute “care home”.

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services ‑ Optometric Services) Determination 2020

**2 Subsection 5(1) (definition of residential aged care facility)**

Omit “aged care facility”, substitute “care home”.

**3 Schedule (item 10931, column 2, subparagraph (a)(ii))**

Omit “aged care facility”, substitute “care home”.

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services – Other Medical Practitioner) Determination 2018

**4 Schedule 1 (items 761, 763, 766, 769, 772, 776, 788, 789, 2198 and 2200, column 2)**

Omit “aged care facility” (wherever occurring), substitute “care home”.

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services – Telehealth Attendances) Determination 2021

**5 Subsection 5(1) (definition of residential aged care facility)**

Omit “aged care facility”, substitute “care home”.

**6 Paragraph 1.1.04(4)(a)**

Omit “aged care facility”, substitute “care home”.

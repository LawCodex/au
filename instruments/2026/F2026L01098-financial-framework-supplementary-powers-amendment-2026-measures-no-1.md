#### Division 10—Amendments made by the Financial Framework (Supplementary Powers) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026

###### 35 Power to vary or administer grants or arrangements

Despite the repeal of table items in Schedule 1AA by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Financial Framework (Supplementary Powers) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026*, those items continue in force for the purposes of sections 32B and 32C of the Act in relation to the administration or variation of grants or arrangements made before the repeal, but not so as to authorise any new commitment by the Commonwealth to spend money.

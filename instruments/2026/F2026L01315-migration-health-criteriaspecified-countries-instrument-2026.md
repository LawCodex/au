# Migration (Health Criteria—Specified Countries) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/090

I, Paolo Mezzatesta, delegate of the Minister, make the following instrument.

Dated 24 September 2026

Paolo Mezzatesta

Director

Detention and Migration Health Policy Section

Health Policy and Assurance Branch

Department of Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Health Criteria—Specified Countries) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subparagraph 2.25A(1)(b) of the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***foreign country*** has the same meaning as the definition in section 2B of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 6 Specified countries

For the purposes of subparagraph 2.25A(1)(b) of the Regulations, a specified country (whether Australia or a foreign country) is a country listed in column 1 of the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument.

###### 7 Application of this instrument

This instrument applies to an application for a visa:

made on or after the commencement of this instrument; or

made but not finally determined before the commencement of this instrument.

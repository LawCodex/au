###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Electoral Legislation Amendment (Electoral Reform) Act 2025*.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this instrument:

***amending Act*** means the *Electoral Legislation Amendment (Electoral Reform) Act 2025*.

***Electoral Act*** means the *Commonwealth Electoral Act 1918*.

***operative day*** means 1 January 2027.

***Referendum Act*** means the *Referendum (Machinery Provisions) Act 1984*.

***transition time gift*** means a gift to a candidate in an election or by‑election if:

(a) the gift is made or received (as the case may be) on or after the operative day; and

(b) the writ for the election or by‑election was issued before the operative day.

(2) If a provision of this instrument:

(a) relates to an amendment of the Electoral Act; and

(b) uses an expression that has a particular meaning in the Electoral Act, or in the provision of the Electoral Act that the amendment relates to;

then the expression has that particular meaning in the provision of this instrument.

(3) If a provision of this instrument:

(a) relates to an amendment of the Referendum Act; and

(b) uses an expression that has a particular meaning in the Referendum Act, or in the provision of the Referendum Act that the amendment relates to;

then the expression has that particular meaning in the provision of this instrument.

### Part 2—Deferred operation of Electoral Act amendments

###### 5 Deferred operation of Electoral Act amendments

(1) If:

(a) in accordance with section 2 of the amending Act, an item of the amending Act commences on 1 July 2026; and

(b) the item amends, inserts, substitutes or repeals a provision of the Electoral Act;

then the amendment, insertion, substitute or repeal does not apply until the start of the operative day.

(2) The Electoral Act as in force immediately before 1 July 2026 continues to apply until the end of the day before the operative day.

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) have effect subject to any other provision of this instrument.

### Part 3—Schedule 1: key definitions

###### 6 Subsection 287(9) of the Electoral Act

Subsection 287(9) of the Electoral Act, as amended by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies on and after the operative day in relation to an election, other than an election the writ for which was issued before the operative day.

###### 7 Requirement in section 287F of the Electoral Act to register as a significant third party

(1) This section applies in relation to section 287F of the Electoral Act as amended by Schedule 1 to the amending Act.

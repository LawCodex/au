## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health (Transitional Electronic National Residential Medication Chart) Special Arrangement 2025

**1 Section 4 (definition of approved supplier)**

After the definition of approved supplier, insert:

ADHA Electronic Prescribing Conformance Register means the register, however described, maintained by the Australian Digital Health Agency that identifies dispensing software products that are conformant to a conformance profile for dispensing software, as existing 5 August 2026.

Note: The Electronic Prescribing Conformance Register is accessible through the Australian Digital Health Agency’s website (http://www.digitalhealth.gov.au).

conformant dispensing software means:

(a) CPv2.3 dispensing software; or

(b) CPv3.0 dispensing software.

CPv2.3 dispensing software means dispensing software that is recorded on the ADHA Electronic Prescribing Conformance Register as conformant to conformance profile version 2.3.

CPv3.0 dispensing software means dispensing software that is recorded on the ADHA Electronic Prescribing Conformance Register as conformant to conformance profile version 3.0.

**2 Section 4 (definition of electronic medication chart system)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***electronic medication chart system*** means a software system, owned and operated by a software vendor, that:

(a) is used for prescribing and recording the administration of pharmaceutical benefits to persons receiving residential care at an approved residential care home; and

(b) is accessible by approved suppliers for the purpose of viewing and annotating electronic medication charts created using the system.

**3 Subsection 8(2)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(2) A prescription for the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit under this Special Arrangement may only be made:

(a) for a person receiving residential care at an approved residential care home; and

(b) using:

(i) a transitional conformant electronic medication chart system that generates an electronic medication chart prescription order; or

(ii) an electronic medication chart system that allows for electronic medication chart prescriptions, created using the system to be sent to the national prescription delivery service.

**4 Subsection 9(1)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(1) An approved supplier may supply a pharmaceutical benefit under this Special Arrangement to a person receiving residential care in an approved residential care home if the prescription for the pharmaceutical benefit:

(a) either:

(i) was created using a transitional conformant electronic medication chart system and is dispensed using any conformant dispensing software; or

(ii) was created using an electronic medication chart system that allows for electronic medication prescriptions, created using the system to be sent to the national prescription delivery service and is dispensed using CPv2.3 dispensing software; and

(b) was made available to the approved supplier before the pharmaceutical benefit is supplied.

**5 Subsection 10(1)**

After subsection 10(1) Insert:

(1A) An approved supplier must not make a claim for payment for a supply mentioned in paragraph 9(1)(a)(i) unless:

(a) the pharmaceutical benefit was dispensed using conformant dispensing software; and

(b) the claim is made using conformant dispensing software.

(1B) An approved supplier must not make a claim for payment for a supply mentioned in paragraph 9(1)(a)(ii) unless:

(a) the pharmaceutical benefit was dispensed using CPv2.3 dispensing software; and

(b) the claim is made using CPv2.3 dispensing software.

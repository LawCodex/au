### Part 2—Final factor for the 2025-26 year

###### 5 Determination of final factor for 2025-26

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2025‑26 year is 2.0865.

###### 6 How the final factor was worked out

(1) Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2025-26 year is to be worked out by applying the formula set out in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act and then adjusting the result under whichever of paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are applicable.

*Paragraph 8(1)(a) formula*

(2) The factor calculated under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act is as follows:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 27,383,531 over 26,949,102 end fraction close bracket times open bracket start fraction 101.7 over 97.7 end fraction close bracket equals 1.05772 end formula]

Note 1: For the purposes of section 4A of the Act, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2024 on 18 June 2026 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 2: For the purposes of section 4A of the Act, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2023 on 19 June 2025 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 3: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 29 April 2026 by the Statistician in respect of the 2026 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 4: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 28 January 2026 by the Statistician in respect of the 2025 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

*Paragraph 8(1)(b) modification*

(3) The result of subsection (2) was *not* modified under paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Act as the fifth decimal place was *not* greater than 4.

*Paragraph 8(1)(c) modification*

(4) The result of subsection (2) was modified under paragraph 8(1)(c) of the Act as a result of special circumstances, being the need to account for the Commonwealth’s decision to bring forward 50 per cent of the payments in the 2025-26 year into the 2024-25 year and 80 per cent of the payments in the 2026‑27 year into the 2025-26 year. The modification was worked out by multiplying the result of subsection (2) by the following formula:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 2025-26 amount minus 2024-25 amount plus 2026-27 amount over 2024-25 final entitlement end fraction close bracket times open bracket start fraction 1 over subsection (2) factor end fraction close bracket end formula]

Where:

***2025-26 amount*** means the amount that would have been the base figure for the year 2025-26 if the Commonwealth had not decided to bring forward any payments.

***2024-25 amount*** means the 50 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2025-26 to be paid in the year 2024-25.

***2026-27 amount*** means the 80 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2026-27 to be paid in the year 2025-26.

***2024-25 final entitlement*** means the base figure for the year 2024-25.

***sub (2) factor*** means the result of subsection (2).

### Part 3—Estimated factor for the 2026-27 year

###### 7 Determination of estimated factor for 2026-27

For the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(b) of the Act, the estimated factor in relation to the 2026-27 year is 0.1559.

###### 8 How the estimated factor was worked out

(1) Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2026-27 year is to be worked out by applying the formula set out in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act and then adjusting the result under whichever of paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are applicable.

*Paragraph 8(1)(a) formula*

(2) The factor calculated under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act is as follows:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 27,795,993 over 27,383,531 end fraction close bracket times open bracket start fraction 104.7 over 101.7 end fraction close bracket equals 1.04501 end formula]

Note 1: For the purposes of section 4A, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2025 on 18 June 2026 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 2: For the purposes of section 4A, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2024 on 18 June 2026 (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

Note 3: The formula uses the 2026-27 Budget forecast of the Department of the Treasury of the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, in respect of the 2027 March quarter.

Note 4: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 29 April 2026 by the Statistician in respect of the 2026 March quarter (published and available at www.abs.gov.au).

*Paragraph 8(1)(b) modification*

(3) The result of subsection (2) was *not* modified under paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Act as the fifth decimal place was *not* greater than 4.

*Paragraph 8(1)(c) modification*

(4) The result of subsection (2) was modified under paragraph 8(1)(c) of the Act as a result of special circumstances, being the need to account for the Commonwealth’s decision to bring forward 80 per cent of the payments in the 2026-27 year into the 2025-26 year. The modification was worked out by multiplying the result of subsection (2) by the following formula:

[image: Start formula open bracket start fraction 2026-27 amount minus 2025-26 amount over 2025-26 final entitlement end fraction close bracket times open bracket start fraction 1 over subsection (2) factor end fraction close bracket end formula]

Where:

***2026-27 amount*** means the amount that would have been the base figure for the year 2026-27 if the Commonwealth had not decided to bring forward any payments.

***2025-26 amount*** means the 80 per cent of payments that were brought forward from the year 2026-27 to be paid in the year 2025-26.

***2025-26 final entitlement*** means the base figure for the year 2025-26.

***sub (2) factor*** means the result of subsection (2).

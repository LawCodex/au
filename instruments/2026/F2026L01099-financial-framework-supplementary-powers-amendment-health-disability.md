## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Financial Framework (Supplementary Powers) Regulations 1997

**1 Part 3 of Schedule 1AB (table item 75, column headed “Name”)**

Omit “Grant”, substitute “Grants”.

**2 Part 3 of Schedule 1AB (cell at table item 75, column headed “Purpose”)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

To provide funding to the Royal Children’s Hospital Good Friday Appeal to support the Royal Children’s Hospital to implement the following initiatives:\
(a) the Melbourne Children’s Campus Mental Health Strategy;\
(b) the Enhancing Regional Paediatric Healthcare Access Project to enhance the capacity and capability of regional and rural paediatric health services for children under the age of 18 years by improving access to, and the quality and coordination of, those services, including by doing any of the following:\
(i) supplying medical or allied health equipment, including equipment for the purposes of training;\
(ii) providing education and training for medical, allied health and other staff who support or enable the delivery of medical or allied health services;\
(iii) undertaking research programs;\
(iv) providing adequate medical, nursing or allied health staffing;\
(v) building works to deliver health services to paediatric patients;\
(vi) developing new models of health care;\
(vii) delivering health care closer to patients’ homes;\
(viii) improving access to mental health care for priority population groups;\
(c) the National Paediatric Health Literacy Platform, involving the development, enhancement or operation of an online health platform for children under the age of 18 years and their parents or carers that supports paediatric care or sharing of evidence‑based and age‑appropriate health information, including any of the following:\
(i) resources, advice and tools for parents or carers to support children’s health;\
(ii) surveys of paediatric patients, their parents or carers;\
(iii) resources on health issues facing children, on mental wellbeing and on triaging access to online health resources;\
(iv) social media content to combat health misinformation and to engage with children on health and mental health issues.\
This purpose has the objective it would have if it were limited to measures:\
(a) with respect to postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution); or\
(b) to give effect to Australia’s obligations under either or both of the following:\
(i) the Convention on the Rights of the Child, particularly Articles 4 and 24;\
(ii) the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, particularly Articles 2 and 12.

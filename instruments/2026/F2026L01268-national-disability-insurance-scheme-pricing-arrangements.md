###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 45C of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in definitions section of the Act, including the following:

(a) NDIS support;

(b) Participant.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013.*

***NDIS pricing document*** means either of the following documents published on the National Disability Insurance Agency’s website, as in force or existing from time to time:

(a) NDIS Pricing Schedule 2026-27;

(b) NDIS Pricing Schedule for Specialist Disability Accommodation 2026-27

Note 1: These documents could in 2026 be viewed on the Agency’s website (www.ndis.gov.au).

Note 2: Documents may be incorporated into a legislative instrument made under subsection 45C(1) of the Act as in force from time to time: subsection 45C(11).

###### 5 Maximum amounts for NDIS supports

(1) For the purposes of subsection 45C(1) of the Act, the maximum amount for the acquisition or provision of an NDIS support, or a class of NDIS supports, is:

(a) if a maximum amount is specified for the NDIS support or class of NDIS supports in an NDIS pricing document – that amount; or

(b) if a method for determining a maximum amount is specified for an NDIS support or class of NDIS supports in an NDIS pricing document – the maximum amount worked out using that method.

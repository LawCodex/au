# Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Edvantage Institute Australia Pty Ltd) Instrument 2026

I, Annette Cannell, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 31 August 2026

Annette Cannell

Acting Assistant Secretary\
HELP and Student Experience Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Edvantage Institute Australia Pty Ltd) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** used in this instrument is defined in Schedule 1 to the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Approval of higher education provider

For the purposes of section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the Act, Edvantage Institute Australia Pty Ltd (ACN 617 256 818; ABN 32 617 256 818) is approved as a higher education provider.

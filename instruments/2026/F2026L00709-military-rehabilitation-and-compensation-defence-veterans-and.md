## Part 1— Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence, Veterans’ and Families’ Acute Support Package) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 268B of the Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901.*

###### 4 Repeals

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence, Veterans' and Families' Acute Support Package) Instrument 2022* is repealed.

In this instrument:

***acute support package***—see subsection 7(1).

***support plan***—see section 8.

In this instrument, a reference to a child of a member, former member or deceased member includes a reference to: a stepchild of the member, former member or deceased member; and a person in respect of whom the member or former member stands, or the deceased member stood, in the position of a parent.

## Part 2­—Eligibility for acute support package

###### 7 Commission may grant acute support package

The Commission may decide to grant a package (an ***acute support package***) of assistance or benefits to a person if:

subsections (2), (3), (4), (5), (6) or (7) apply to the person at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and

if the person has previously been granted an acute support package—either:

the period during which assistance or benefits for that acute support package may be provided has ended by operation of subsection 11(2); or

the acute support package has been revoked; and

the Commission is satisfied that it is appropriate to grant an acute support package to the person to assist the person to adjust to new and challenging life circumstances.

*Members or former members*

The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person who is a member or former member if: the person is under 65 years of age at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and the person is receiving, or is eligible to receive: compensation for incapacity under Part 3 or 4 of Chapter 4 of the Act; or a Special Rate Disability Pension; or a pension under Part II of the VEA as a person to whom section 23, 24 or 25 of that Act applies; or a veteran payment made under an instrument made under section 45SB of the VEA; and the Commission is satisfied that the person, or a related person, is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis.

*Related persons*

The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person who is a related person of a member or former member if: the member or former member is a person to whom paragraphs 7(2)(a) and 7(2)(b) apply; and the Commission is satisfied that the person is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis; and the Commission is satisfied that the crisis, or the risk of experiencing crisis, is: impacting or involving the member or former member; or connected to the member or former member’s wellbeing.

*Wholly dependent partners and war widows or war widowers*

The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person who is a wholly dependent partner of a deceased member or a war widow or a war widower if: the person is under 65 years of age at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and the deceased member’s death occurred no more than 2 years before the day the person’s eligibility for the package is determined; and any of the following apply: the deceased member’s death was related to service rendered by the member; or the Commission is satisfied that the deceased member’s death was a suicide related to service rendered by the member; or the deceased member’s death resulted from an injury (within the meaning of the DRCA).

*Partner of a deceased member*

(5) The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person if:

the person is under 65 years of age at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and

the person was the partner of a deceased member immediately before the deceased member’s death; and

the person was partly dependent on the deceased member at the date of the deceased member’s death; and

the deceased member’s death occurred no more than 2 years before the day the person’s eligibility for the package is determined; and

the deceased member’s death resulted from an injury (within the meaning of the DRCA).

*Parent or Step-Parent*

(6) The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person if:

the person is a parent or step-parent of:

a deceased member; or

a wholly dependent partner of a deceased member; or

a war widow or war widower; or

another person who was the partner of a deceased member; and

the deceased member’s death occurred no more than 2 years before the day the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and

either:

the deceased member’s death was related to service rendered by the member; or

the Commission is satisfied that the deceased member’s death was a suicide related to service rendered by the member; and

the Commission is satisfied that the person is parenting a child of the deceased member; and

at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined, the child is under 18 years of age; and

the Commission is satisfied that the person, or the child, is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis.

*Former partner of a member or former member*

(7) The Commission may decide to grant an acute support package to a person if:

the person is under 65 years of age at the time the person’s eligibility for an acute support package is determined; and

the person was the partner of a member or former member; and

the member or former member is receiving, or is eligible to receive: compensation for incapacity under Part 3 or 4 of Chapter 4 of the Act; or a Special Rate Disability Pension; or a pension under Part II of the VEA as a person to whom section 23, 24 or 25 of that Act applies; or a veteran payment made under an instrument made under section 45SB of the VEA; and

at the time the person’s eligibility for the acute support package is determined, either or both of the following apply:

the person ceased being the member’s or former member’s partner within the previous 12 months;

a child of the member or former member who is under 18 years of age lives with the person; and

the Commission is satisfied that the person is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis.

## Part 3—Support plan for acute support package

###### 8 Commission must prepare support plan

If the Commission grants an acute support package to a person, the Commission must prepare a plan (a ***support plan***) for the person.

*Contents of support plan*

The support plan must include all of the following: the names of any people who are to be provided with assistance or benefits under the support plan; the kinds of assistance or benefits that the Commission is satisfied are appropriate to be provided to the people; the period or periods during which those assistance or benefits are to be provided; the providers that are to provide the assistance or benefits; the payment arrangements relating to the provision of the assistance or benefits; the day on which the support plan comes into effect.

*People included in support plan*

A related person may be included in the support plan for an acute support package for a member or former member with approval from the Commission if the Commission is satisfied that the related person is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis.

A child under 18 years of age may be included in the support plan for an acute support package granted to a:

wholly dependent partner of a deceased member;

war widow;

war widower;

partner of a deceased member;

parent or step-parent of a deceased member;

parent or step-parent of a wholly dependent partner of a deceased member;

parent or step-parent of a war widow or war widower;

parent or step-parent of another person who was the partner of a deceased member; or

former partner of a member or former member;

with approval from the Commission if the person who has been granted the acute support package is parenting the child and the Commission is satisfied that the child is experiencing, or is at risk of experiencing, crisis.

*Kinds of assistance or benefits included in support plan*

The kinds of assistance or benefits that may be included in a support plan for the purposes of paragraph 8(2)(b) are one or more of the following:

(a) child care services for a child who is attending, or not yet attending, primary school;

(b) counselling;

(c) household assistance;

(d) services to build capacity;

(e) academic and extra‑curricular support services for a child who is under 18 years of age;

(f) transport services in relation to assistance or benefits being provided under the support plan;

(g) equipment and other goods in relation to assistance or benefits being provided under the support plan;

(h) any other kind of assistance or benefits that the Commission is satisfied will assist the person being provided with the assistance or benefits to adjust to new and challenging life circumstances.

*Child care service providers*

For the purposes of paragraph 8(2)(d), a provider of child care services must not be included in a support plan unless the provider is an approved child care service (as defined in the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999*).

###### 9 Varying support plan

(1) The Commission may vary a support plan on request by:

(a) the person for whom the support plan was prepared; or

any person who is included in the support plan

if the Commission is satisfied that it is appropriate to do so.

The Commission may vary a support plan on the Commission’s own initiative if the Commission is satisfied that it is appropriate to do so.

###### 10 Revoking an acute support package

(1) The Commission must revoke an acute support package for a person to whom subsections 7(2), 7(3) or 7(7) apply if the relevant member or former member:

is no longer receiving, or is no longer eligible to receive:

compensation for incapacity under Part 3 or 4 of Chapter 4 of the Act; or

a Special Rate Disability Pension; or

is no longer receiving:

a pension under Part II of the VEA as a person to whom section 23, 24 or 25 of that Act applies; or

a veteran payment made under an instrument made under section 45B of the VEA.

(2) The Commission may revoke an acute support package if:

(a) if the acute support package was prepared for a member or former member—the Commission is satisfied that the member or former member is no longer experiencing, or is no longer at risk of experiencing, crisis; or

(b) if the acute support package was prepared for a person to whom any of subsections 7(3) to 7(7) apply—the Commission is satisfied that the person is no longer experiencing, or is no longer at risk of experiencing, crisis; or

(c) a person who is included in the relevant support plan does not comply with a request made to the person under section 14; or

(d) the Commission is satisfied that, as a result of new and challenging life circumstances for the person for whom the acute support package was prepared, it is appropriate for the acute support package to be revoked in order for the Commission to grant a new, revised acute support package to the person.

*Effect of revocation*

(3) If an acute support package is revoked, assistance or benefits must no longer be provided under a support plan prepared in respect of that acute support package.

## Part 4—Conditions and limits on assistance or benefits

###### 11 Assistance or benefits generally

(1) Subject to sections 12 and 13, the total value of assistance or benefits that may be included in a support plan is:

(a) for a support plan prepared for an acute support package granted to a member, former member, former partner of a member or former member, or for a related person of a member or former member:

(i) a maximum of $7,500 in relation to one or more periods during which assistance or benefits are to be provided that add up to 12 months; and

(ii) if the 12-month period referred to in subparagraph (i) has been accumulated—a maximum of $5,000 in relation to one or more periods during which assistance or benefits are to be provided that add up to a further 12 months; and

(b) for a support plan prepared for an acute support package granted to a person to whom subsections 7(4), 7(5) or 7(6) apply—a maximum of $27,835 every 12 months for a continuous 24‑month period starting on the day the support plan comes into effect.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the period during which assistance or benefits included in a support plan for the purposes of this section may be provided ends immediately after:

(a) for a support plan prepared for an acute support package granted to a member, former member, former partner of a member or former member, or for a related person of a member or former member—the earlier of:

(i) the end of the further 12-month period referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(ii); or

(ii) the end of a 48‑month period starting on the day the support plan comes into effect; or

(b) for a support plan prepared for an acute support package granted to a person to whom subsections 7(4), 7(5) or 7(6) apply—the end of the 24‑month period mentioned in paragraph (1)(b).

###### 12 Additional counselling

In addition to the general assistance or benefits in section 11, assistance or benefits may be included in a support plan prepared for an acute support package granted to a member, former member, former partner of a member or former member or for a related person of a member or former member in the form of a maximum of 4 courses of counselling every 12 months for a continuous 36-month period.

(2) The 36-month period starts on the day after the end of the period referred to in paragraph 11(2)(a) in relation to the support plan.

###### 13 Additional assistance or benefits in relation to children

This section applies if a person who is named in a support plan for the purposes of paragraph 8(2)(a) is a child who is attending, or not yet attending, primary school.

In addition to the assistance or benefits in section 11, assistance or benefits may be included in the support plan in relation to the child for a continuous period starting on the later of:

(a) the day the support plan comes into effect; or

(b) the day the child is included in the support plan for the purposes of paragraph 8(2)(a);

and ending on the first anniversary of the start of the period that occurs after the child has completed primary school.

(3) The value of assistance or benefits included in the support plan in relation to the child must not exceed:

(a) until the first‑occurring anniversary of the start of the period for which the child is attending primary school—$10,000 every 12 months; and

(b) thereafter until the period ends—$5,000 every 12 months.

(4) Assistance or benefits provided under this section do not count towards the maximum amounts or periods referred to in section 11.

## Part 5—Other matters

###### 14 Commission may request information

The Commission may request information from any person named in a support plan in relation to the provision of assistance or benefits included in that support plan.

Note: Failure to comply with a request under this section may result in the revocation of the acute support package (see paragraph 10(2)(c)).

###### 15 Transitional matters

This section applies to acute support packages which were granted under the: *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence, Veterans’ and Families’ Acute Support Package) Instrument 2022;* or *Veterans’ Entitlements (Defence, Veterans’ and Families’ Acute Support Package) Instrument 2022.*

An acute support package which remains in effect immediately before 1 July 2026 and to which subsection (1) applies is an acute support package for the purposes of this instrument.

If a person has been granted an acute support package which remains in effect immediately before 1 July 2026, the person is not eligible for a new acute support package until that acute support package has ended.

Nothing in this instrument: affects a person’s rights at the commencement day so as to disadvantage the person; or imposes liabilities on a person in respect of anything done or omitted to be done before the commencement day.

In this section:

***commencement day*** means the day the instrument commences.

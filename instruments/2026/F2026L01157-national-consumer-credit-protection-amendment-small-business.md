## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Consumer Credit Protection Regulations 2010

**1 Subregulation 28RB(7) (paragraph (b) of the definition of exemption period)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(b) ends at the start of 3 October 2036.

# Charter of the United Nations (Listed Persons and Entities) Amendment (No. 3) Instrument 2026

I, Penny Wong, Minister for Foreign Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 15 September 2026

Penny Wong

Minister for Foreign Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Charter of the United Nations (Listed Persons and Entities) Amendment (No. 3) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 15 of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

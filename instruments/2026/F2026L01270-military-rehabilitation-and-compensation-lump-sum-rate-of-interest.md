# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Lump Sum Rate of Interest) Determination 2026

I, Matthew James Keogh, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following determination under subsection 79(3) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

Dated 20 September 2026

Matthew James Keogh

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Lump Sum Rate of Interest) Determination 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 79(3) of the Act.

###### 4 Repeals

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Lump Sum Rate of Interest) Determination 2016* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

###### 6 Rate of interest

For the purposes of subsection 79(3) of the Act, the rate of interest is the weighted average yield of 90‑day bank‑accepted bills published by the Reserve Bank of Australia, as settled immediately before the last day of the 30‑day settlement period.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Student Identifiers (VET Exemptions) Instrument 2021

**1 Section 4**

Insert:

**VET Data Provision Requirements has the meaning given by section 187 of the National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011.**

**2 Section 4 (note to the definition of National VET Data Policy)**

Omit “2021 be viewed on the Department of Education, Skills and Employment’s”, substitute “2026 be viewed on the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations’”.

**3 Section 4**

Insert:

***VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy*** has the same meaning as in the *Data Provision Requirements*.

**4 After paragraph 7(a)**

Insert:

(ab) a registered training organisation that has an exemption from the requirement to report data under section 6A of the Data Provision Requirements if:

the ‘national security, border protection and policing exemptions’ contained in section 4 of the Data Provision Exemptions Policy apply; or

the ‘delivery of emergency or safety community services exemption’ contained in section 5 of the Data Provision Exemptions Policy apply; or

**5 After subsection 10(2)**

**Insert:**

The amendments to this instrument made by the *Student Identifiers (VET Exemptions) Amendment (Data Streamlining) Instrument 2026* apply to a registered training organisation on and from the day that the amendments made by the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Data Provision Requirements) Amendment (Data Streamlining) Instrument 2026* (the ***DPR Amending Instrument*****)** apply to that registered training organisation.

Note: Part 5 of the Data Provision Requirements will, from the day the DPR Amending Instrument commences, set out when the amendments made by the DPR Amending Instrument apply to a registered training organisation.

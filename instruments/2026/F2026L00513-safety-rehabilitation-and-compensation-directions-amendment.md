###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Directions Amendment Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under sections 89D and 101 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

*Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Directions 2019*

**Section 7A**

Repeal the section, substitute:

**7A Matters to be considered in deciding whether to grant a licence – corporate group requirement for eligible corporations**

**This section applies where:**

**a corporation has been declared to be eligible to apply to be granted a licence under section 100 of the Act after the commencement of this section; and**

that corporation has made an application in accordance with section 102 of the Act to be granted a licence.

**In determining an application for a licence under section 104 of the Act, a primary criterion to be considered by the Commission is whether it is satisfied, on reasonable grounds, that the applicant is a member of a corporate group in which a majority of employees in the corporate group are, at the time of the application, covered by the Act.**

**7B Matters to be considered in deciding whether to grant a licence – employee entitlements and support arrangements requirement for eligible corporations**

In determining an application for a licence under section 104 of the Act, a primary criterion to be considered by the Commission is whether it is satisfied, on reasonable grounds, that the licence:

would not result in an overall reduction in workers’ compensation entitlements; and

would not result in an overall reduction in supporting arrangements for claims management, including timeliness of decision making, rehabilitation and return to work

for the employees of the applicant to be covered by the Act.

**7C Matters to be considered in deciding whether to grant a licence – employee consultation requirement for eligible corporations**

**In determining an application for a licence under section 104 of the Act, the Commission must take into account, consider and respond to the views and concerns of employees of the applicant to be covered by the Act and the relevant registered unions with respect to the criteria for a grant of licence, as it applies to the applicant.**

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 500E(2), 546(2) and 600(2) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***social security law*** has the same meaning as in subsection 23(17) of the Act.

###### 6 Circumstances relating to criteria

The Secretary may determine that a person does not satisfy the criteria set out in paragraphs 500E(1)(b), 546(1)(b) and 600(1)(b) of the Act if the Secretary is satisfied that:

(a) the person is required, under a provision of the social security law, to provide additional evidence that the person satisfies paragraph 500(2A)(a), 500(2B)(a), 540(2)(a) or 593(1AC)(a) of the Act (as the case requires); or

(b) the person has incurred an overpayment arising from the person’s failure to comply with the provisions of the social security law; or

(c) the person has contravened a provision of the social security law; or

(d) an event or change of circumstances has occurred, or is likely to occur, that might affect:

(i) the person’s qualification for parenting payment, youth allowance or jobseeker payment (as the case requires); or

(ii) parenting payment, youth allowance or jobseeker payment (as the case requires) being payable to the person.

###### 7 Maximum prospective period

For paragraphs 500E(1)(b), 546(1)(b) and 600(1)(b) of the Act, the maximum length of a period is 12 weeks.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) leviable provider;

(b) risk rated premium component;

(c) special tuition protection component;

(d) year.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *VET Student Loans (VSL Tuition Protection Levy) Act 2020.*

***approved course provider charge*** has the same meaning as in the *VET Student Loans (Charges) Act 2016.*

***financial statement*** means the statement provided by a leviable provider to the Secretary in accordance with section 52 of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016* and section 113 of the *VET Student Loans Rules 2016* for the financial year ending on a date in 2025*.*

***risk management purposes*** means purposes relating to a leviable provider’s ability to comply with, or continue to comply with, the requirements of their registration, where the regulator takes a risk management approach and determines a shorter renewal period is warranted.

| Note: | A leviable provider’s registration may be renewed for a period less than the maximum period allowed for reasons that are not due to risk management. For example, where an entity changes its name and so must be re-registered and the existing registration end date is retained. |

***statement of general information*** means information or documents given by a leviable provider to the Secretary in accordance with a notice given under section 53 of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016*.

An expression used in this instrument that is also used in the *VET Student Loans Act 2016* has the same meaning as in that Act.

(1) The amount for the purposes of step 1 of the method statement in subsection 11(2) of the Act is $6.00.

(2) The percentage for the purposes of step 2 of the method statement in subsection 11(2) of the Act is 0.13%.

(a) financial strength risk factor;

(b) completion rate risk factor;

*Determining the completion rate percentage*

A leviable provider’s completion rate percentage is worked out using the formula:

[image: Start formula start fraction Passed EFTSL over Passed EFTSL plus Failed EFTSL plus Ongoing EFTSL plus Date Missing EFTSL end fraction times 100]

where:

***Data missing EFTSL*** means the total EFTSL value for units of study with a census date in the previous calendar year for which no unit of study completion status has been reported by the provider in the provider’s statement of general information.

***EFTSL*** has the same meaning given by subsections (3) and (4).

***EFTSL value***, of a unit of study, has the meaning given by subsections (5), (6) and (7).

***Failed EFTSL*** means the total EFTSL value for units of study with a census date in the previous calendar year reported by the provider as having a unit of study completion status of ‘failed’ in the provider’s statement of general information.

***Ongoing EFTSL*** means the total EFTSL value for units of study with a census date in the previous calendar year reported by the provider as having a unit of study completion status of ‘unit of study to be commenced later in the year or still in process of completing or completion status not yet determined’ in the provider’s statement of general information.

***Passed EFTSL*** means the total EFTSL value for units of study with a census date in the previous calendar year reported by the provider as having a unit of study completion status of ‘successfully completed all the requirements’ in the provider’s statement of general information.

Note: For the definition of statement of general information, see section 4.

*Meaning of* ***EFTSL***

(3) An ***EFTSL*** is an equivalent full‑time student load for a year. It is a measure, in respect of a course, of the study load for a year of a student undertaking that course of study on a full‑time basis.

(4) A particular amount of EFTSL is an amount of study, undertaken with an approved course provider as part of a course, represented by units of study with EFTSL values the sum of which equals that amount.

*Meaning of* ***EFTSL value***

(5) The ***EFTSL value*** of a unit of study is the value that the approved course provider with which the unit may be undertaken determines in writing to be the EFTSL value of the unit, expressed as a fraction of one EFTSL.

(6) If the unit can form part of more than one course, the provider may determine an EFTSL value of the unit for each such course.

(7) If a unit of study is subject to separate determinations in relation to different courses, a reference in this instrument to the EFTSL value of the unit is, when the unit forms part of such a course, a reference to the EFTSL value of the unit determined under subsection (6) for the course.

*Working out the weighted late payment measure*

leviable provider’s weighted late payment measure is worked out by adding the results of the calculations in the following paragraphs:

(a) for 2025, work out [image: start formula 0.7 times open bracket VSLPC plus VSLTPL close bracket end formula]

(b) for 2024, work out [image: start formula 0.2 times open bracket VSLPC plus VSLTPL close bracket end formula]

(c) for 2023, work out [image: start formula 0.1 times open bracket VSLPC plus VSLTPL close bracket end formula]

where:

***VSLPC***, for a year, means:

the number of days after the due date on which the approved course provider

charge for that year was received; or

if payment of the approved course provider charge was received on or before the

due date—zero; or

(c) if the approved course provider charge was not payable—zero.

***VSLTPL***, for a year, means:

the number of days after the due date on which the VSL tuition protection levy for

that year was received; or

if payment of the VSL tuition protection levy was received on or before the due

date—zero; or

(c) if the VSL tuition protection levy was not payable—zero.

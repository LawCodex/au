###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Multiple Entitlement Exclusion) Determination 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 65A(1) of the Act.

###### 4 Repeals

The *Veterans’ Entitlements (Multiple Entitlement Exclusion) Determination 2024* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5Q of the Act, including the following:

(a) energy supplement;

(b) general rate.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986*.

***MRCA*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

###### 6 Person not entitled to energy supplement under section 62A of the Act

A person is not entitled to an energy supplement under section 62A of the Act for a day if, for the day, the person is entitled to:

(a) both:

(i) a pension under Part II or IV of the Act at the general rate; and

(ii) an energy supplement under section 83A of the MRCA; or

(b) both:

(i) a pension under Part II or IV of the Act at a rate other than the

general rate; and

(ii) an energy supplement under section 209A of the MRCA; or

(c) an energy supplement under section 220D of the MRCA.

###### 7 Person not entitled to energy supplement under section 62B of the Act

A person is not entitled to an energy supplement under section 62B of the Act for a day if the person is entitled to an energy supplement for the day under section 238A of the MRCA.

###### 8 Person not entitled to energy supplement under section 118PA of the Act

A person is not entitled to an energy supplement under section 118PA of the Act for a day if the person is entitled to an energy supplement for the day under:

section 62B of the Act; or

section 238A of the MRCA.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Determination 2013

**1 Section 4**

Omit “***corresponding State‑Territory law*** has the same meaning as in the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005*.”

**2 Paragraph 5(2)(a)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(a) the fee is the fee set out in Schedule 1 for the relevant number of products for the specified charging year;

**3 Paragraph 5(2)(b)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(b) on and after 15 September 2030, the fee for the relevant number of products set out in Schedule 1 is indexed in accordance with section 6 of this instrument.

**4 Paragraph 5(2)(c)**

Repeal the paragraph.

**5 Paragraph 5(3)(a)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

on and after 15 September 2026 until 14 September 2027, divide the number of minor products by 10 and round the result up to the nearest whole number;

**6 Paragraph 5(3)(b)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

on and after 15 September 2027, divide the number of minor products by 5 and round the result up to the nearest whole number;

**7 After paragraph 5(3)(b)**

Insert:

add the result of either paragraph (a) or (b) (as applicable) to the number of products that are not minor products (if any).

**8 Subsection 5(5)**

Repeal the subsection.

**9 After section 5**

Insert:

**6 Indexation**

If a provision of this instrument states that the fee is to be indexed in accordance with this section, then at the start of each charging year beginning on or after 15 September 2030, the fee for the relevant number of products set out in Schedule 1 is worked out using the following formula:

| Indexation factor for a charging year | x | Fee for the previous charging year for the relevant number of products |
|---|---|---|

The ***indexation factor*** for a charging year is the number worked out using the following formula:

| Consumer price index number for the reference month | x | 0.15 | + | Wage price index number for the reference quarter | x | 0.85 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer price index number for the base month |  |  |  | Wage price index number for the base quarter |  |  |

where:

***base month*** means the December before the reference month.

***base quarter*** means the December quarter before the reference quarter.

***consumer price index number*** means the All Groups Consumer Price Index number for Australia published by the Australian Statistician for that month.

***reference month*** means the December immediately before the charging year.

***reference quarter*** means the December quarter immediately before the charging year.

***wage price index number*** means all sectors Australia Wage Price Index number published by the Australian Statistician for that quarter.

If the fee worked out under subsection (1) is not a multiple of $10, the amount should be rounded to the nearest multiple of $10.

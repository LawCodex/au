**4 Schedules**

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments commencing the day after registration of the instrument

**Industrial Chemicals (Fees and Charges) Rules 2020**

**Subsection 5(1) (table item 1, column headed “Amount ($)”)**

Omit “80”, substitute “85”.

## Schedule 2—Amendments commencing 1 September 2026

**Industrial Chemicals (Fees and Charges) Rules 2020**

**Subsection 5(1) (cell at table item 20, column headed “Amount ($)”)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

if the export is to a category A country for the industrial chemical—2,555; or

if the export is to a category B country for the industrial chemical—5,095; or

if the export is to a category C country—2,555

**Paragraph 5(2)(b)**

Omit “$18,060”, substitute “20,385”.

**Paragraph 5(3)(e)**

Omit “$7,235”, substitute “$7,475”.

**Paragraph 5(4)(e)**

Omit “$2,735”, substitute “$2,825”.

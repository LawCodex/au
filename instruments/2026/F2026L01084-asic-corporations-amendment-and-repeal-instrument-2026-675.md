# ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/675

ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/675

I, Kate Metz, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 19 August 2026

Kate Metz

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/675.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under sections 341, 601QA, 741, 926A and 1020F of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

Schedule 1—Amendments

*ASIC Corporations (Disregarding Technical Relief) Instrument 2026/180*

**1 Paragraph 7(a) (paragraph (g) of the notional definition of** ***technical relief instrument*****)**

After “*ASIC Corporations (Uncontactable Members) Instrument 2016/187*”, insert “(as in force at any time before its repeal)”.

*ASIC Corporations (Platforms—IDPSs and IDPS-like Schemes) Instrument 2026/395*

**2 Section 10 (notional subparagraph 912ADG(4)(a)(iii) of the** ***Corporations Act 2001*****)**

After “by a nominated electronic means”, insert “(within the meaning of section 9 as notionally modified or varied for the purposes of Parts 7.7 and 7.9 by *ASIC Corporations (Electronic Disclosure) Instrument 2025/447*)”.

**3 Section 10 (note to notional subparagraph 912ADG(4)(a)(iii) of the** ***Corporations Act 2001*****)**

Omit the note.

**4 Subsection 18(2) (notional subparagraph 601FBE(3)(a)(iii) of the** ***Corporations Act 2001*****)**

After “by a nominated electronic means”, insert “(within the meaning of section 9 as notionally modified or varied for the purposes of Parts 7.7 and 7.9 by *ASIC Corporations (Electronic Disclosure) Instrument 2025/447*)”.

**5 Subsection 18(2) (note to notional subparagraph 601FBE(3)(a)(iii) of the** ***Corporations Act 2001*****)**

Omit the note.

Schedule 2—Repeals

*ASIC Corporations (Uncontactable Members) Instrument 2016/187*

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

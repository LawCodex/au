###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Weekly Payments—Class of Persons) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 58A(3C) and 121(5C) of the Act.

###### 4 Repeals

The *Veterans’ Entitlements (Weekly Payments­—Class of Persons) Instrument 2021* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

***approved payment*** means any of the following:

attendant allowance;

clothing allowance;

pension payable under Part II or IV of the Act at a rate determined under or by reference to Division 4 of Part II of the Act;

energy supplement;

income support supplement;

loss of earnings allowance;

orphan’s pension;

pension supplement;

recreation transport allowance;

service pension;

veteran payment;

veterans supplement;

war widows pension.

***attendant allowance*** means a payment under section 98 of the Act.

***clothing allowance*** means a payment under section 97 of the Act.

***decoration allowance*** means a payment under section 102 of the Act as in force immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

***energy supplement*** means a payment made under Part VIAAD of the Act.

***income support supplement*** means a payment under Part IIIA of the Act.

***loss of earnings allowance*** means a payment under section 108 of the Act.

***orphan’s pension*** means a payment under subsection 30(2) of the Act.

***pension*** has the same meaning as in subsection 5Q(1) of the Act.

***pension period*** has the same meaning as in subsection 5Q(1) of the Act.

***pension supplement*** means a payment under Part IIID of the Act.

***prisoner of war recognition supplement*** means a payment under Part VIB of the Act as in force immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

***recreation transport allowance*** means a payment under section 104 of the Act.

***veteran payment*** means a payment under Part IIIAA of the Act.

***veterans supplement*** means a payment under Part VIIA of the Act.

***war widows pension*** means a payment under subsection 30(1) of the Act (whether to a person as a widow or widower).

###### 6 Approved class of persons

For subsections 58A(3A) and 121(5A) of the Act, a person is in an approved class if all of the following applies to the person:

(a) the person is an Australian resident living in Australia;

(b) the person is receiving an approved payment;

(c) the person has identified themselves as:

(i) not having access to safe and secure housing;

(ii) using emergency accommodation;

(iii) a refugee; or

(iv) financially vulnerable and significantly disadvantaged.

(d) the person:

(i) has voluntarily agreed to be paid their payment weekly as described in subsection 58A(3A) or subsection 121(5A) of the Act; and

(ii) has not revoked or withdrawn that agreement.

###### 7 Transitional provision­—decoration allowance

This section applies in relation to a payment of decoration allowance for which the pension period:

commenced before 1 July 2026; and

ends on or after that date.

Note: See section 108 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004*.

A payment of decoration allowance to which subsection (1) applies is an approved payment for the purposes of this instrument.

###### 8 Transitional provision­—prisoner of war recognition supplement

This section applies in relation to a payment of prisoner of war recognition supplement if immediately before the commencement of this instrument:

the supplement was being paid to a person under Part VIB of the Act; and

payment is within a fortnight ending before the transfer fortnight, within the transfer fortnight or a later fortnight.

Note: See section 109 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004*.

A payment of a prisoner of war recognition supplement to which subsection (1) applies is taken to be an approved payment for the purposes of this instrument.

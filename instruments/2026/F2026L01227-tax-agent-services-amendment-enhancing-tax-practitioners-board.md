###### 25EA Suspensions and terminations by Board under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act

(1) If the Board has:

(a) suspended an entity’s registration under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act; or

(b) terminated an entity’s registration under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act;

then:

(c) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(d) the information specified under subsection (3) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (4) of this section.

(2) However, paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in relation to the termination of an individual’s registration if the registration was terminated because the individual died.

Specified information

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(d), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement to the effect that the entity’s registration has been suspended or the entity’s registration has been terminated (whichever applies);

(b) the date on which the suspension or termination takes effect;

(c) if the entity’s registration has been suspended—the action that must be taken for the Board to lift the suspension;

(d) if the entity’s registration has been terminated and the Board determines, under section 40‑25 of the Act, a period during which the entity must not apply for registration—a statement to the effect that the entity must not apply for registration during that period;

(e) the reasons for the suspension or termination;

(f) without limiting paragraph (e) of this subsection, if the reasons for the suspension or termination relate to particular conduct of the entity—details of that conduct;

(g) if the Board, under section 40‑40 of the Act, lifts the entity’s suspension of registration—a statement to the effect that the suspension has been lifted.

Note 1: For when a suspension under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act takes effect, see subsection 40‑30(2) of the Act.

Note 2: For when a termination under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act takes effect, see subsection 40‑20(2) of the Act.

Prescribed period

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1), the following period is prescribed:

(a) if the entity’s registration has been suspended as mentioned in paragraph (1)(a)—the period:

(i) beginning on the day when the suspension takes effect; and

(ii) ending on the day that is 5 years after the day on which the suspension is no longer in force;

(b) if the entity’s registration has been terminated as mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) and paragraph (c) of this subsection does not apply—the period of 5 years beginning on the day on which the termination takes effect;

(c) if the entity’s registration has been terminated as mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) and, under subsection 40‑25(1) of the Act, the Board has determined a period during which the entity must not apply for registration—the period:

(i) beginning on the day that the termination takes effect; and

(ii) ending on the day that is 5 years after the day on which the period determined under subsection 40‑25(1) of the Act ends.

###### 25EB Suspensions by Board under Subdivision 40‑D of the Act

(1) If the Board has suspended an entity’s registration under Subdivision 40‑D of the Act, then:

(a) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (2) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (3) of this section.

Specified information

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement to the effect that the entity’s registration has been suspended;

(b) the date on which the suspension takes effect;

(c) the period for which the suspension is in force;

(d) the reasons for the suspension;

(e) without limiting paragraph (d) of this subsection, if the reasons for the suspension relate to particular conduct of the entity—details of that conduct.

Note: For when a suspension under Subdivision 40‑D of the Act takes effect, see subsection 40‑50(3) of the Act.

Prescribed period

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the prescribed period is the period:

(a) beginning on the day on which the suspension takes effect; and

(b) ending on the day on which the suspension is no longer in force.

###### 25JA Institution of proceedings in respect of criminal offences

(1) If a person institutes proceedings against an entity in a court in respect of an offence against a provision in Division 51 of the Act, then:

(a) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (2) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (3) of this section.

Specified information

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement to the effect that proceedings in respect of the offence have been instituted against the entity;

(b) details of the offence provision;

(c) details of the conduct alleged to constitute the offence.

Prescribed period

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the prescribed period is the period:

(a) beginning on the day on which the proceedings were instituted; and

(b) ending on the day on which any of the following occurs:

(i) the proceedings are dismissed;

(ii) the court finds the entity guilty of the offence;

(iii) the court finds the entity not guilty of the offence;

(iv) the proceedings are otherwise concluded or completed.

###### 25JB Conviction of criminal offences

(1) If a court convicts an entity of an offence against a provision in Division 51 of the Act, then:

(a) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (2) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (3) of this section.

Specified information

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement that the court has convicted the entity of the offence;

(b) details of the offence provision;

(c) details of the conduct constituting the offence;

(d) details of any sentence or penalty that was imposed;

(e) the date on which the entity was convicted of the offence;

(f) information about how a member of the public may gain access to written documentation of the conviction.

Prescribed period

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the prescribed period is the period:

(a) beginning on the day on which the entity is convicted; and

(b) ending on:

(i) if the entity is a natural person and the court imposes a term of imprisonment—the day that is 5 years after the day on which the term of imprisonment ends; or

(ii) in any other case—the day that is 5 years after the day on which the entity was convicted.

###### 25JC Infringement notices issued to and paid by unregistered entities

(1) If an entity is given an infringement notice under Part 5 of the Regulatory Powers Act, as that Part applies in relation to the provisions of the Act specified under subsection (2), and the entity pays the amount that is payable under the notice, then:

(a) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (3) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (4) of this section.

Specified provisions

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the following provisions of the Act are specified:

(a) subsections 50‑5(1) and (2) (providing tax agent services if unregistered);

(b) subsections 50‑10(1) and (2) (advertising tax agent services if unregistered);

(c) section 50‑15 (representing that you are a registered tax agent or BAS agent if unregistered);

(d) section 50‑17 (providing tax (financial) advice services if not registered);

(e) section 50‑18 (advertising tax (financial) advice services if not registered or qualified).

Specified information

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement that the entity has paid the amount that is payable under the infringement notice;

(b) a statement that payment of the amount is not an admission of guilt or liability;

(c) the provision of the Act to which the infringement notice relates;

(d) the date on which the amount payable under the infringement notice was paid;

(e) a copy of the infringement notice.

Prescribed period

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1), the prescribed period is the period of 5 years beginning on the day on which the amount payable under the infringement notice was paid.

Withdrawal of infringement notice

(5) If the infringement notice is withdrawn:

(a) the entity must be removed from the register, unless the entity is otherwise required to be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (3) must be removed from the register in respect of the entity.

###### 25JD Enforceable undertakings

(1) If an authorised person mentioned in subsection 52‑10(2) of the Act accepts, under Part 6 of the Regulatory Powers Act as that Part applies in relation to the provisions of the Act, an enforceable undertaking from an entity in relation to complying with a provision of the Act, then:

(a) the entity must be entered on the register; and

(b) the information specified under subsection (2) must be entered on the register in respect of the entity;

for the period prescribed under subsection (3) of this section.

Specified information

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the following information is specified:

(a) a statement that an authorised person has accepted an enforceable undertaking from the entity;

(b) the provision of the Act to which the enforceable undertaking relates;

(c) details of the conduct that resulted in the entity giving the enforceable undertaking;

(d) details of the enforceable undertaking;

(e) the date on which the authorised person accepted the enforceable undertaking.

Prescribed period

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the period prescribed is the period:

(a) beginning on the day on which the authorised person accepted the enforceable undertaking; and

(b) ending on the day that is 5 years after the day on which the enforceable undertaking is withdrawn, cancelled or expires.

#### Division 2—Amendments made by the Tax Agent Services Amendment (Enhancing Tax Practitioners Board Sanctions) Regulations 2026

###### 39 Definitions

In this Division:

***amending regulations*** means the *Tax Agent Services Amendment (Enhancing Tax Practitioners Board Sanctions) Regulations 2026*.

***commencement time*** means the time when the amending regulations commence.

###### 40 Application—general

Subject to this Division, the amendments made by the amending regulations apply in relation to conduct engaged in by an entity on or after the commencement time.

Note: Item 70 of Schedule 1 to the *Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Accountability for Tax Adviser Misconduct and Other Measures) Act 2026* provides that the amendments to the Act made by that Schedule apply in relation to conduct engaged in on or after the commencement of that Schedule.

###### 41 Orders, suspensions and terminations by the Board

The amendments of section 25E of this instrument made by the amending regulations apply in relation to an order made, or a suspension or termination (other than a termination under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act) that occurs, on or after the commencement time in respect of conduct that occurs on or after the commencement time.

###### 42 Suspensions and terminations by Board under Subdivision 40‑A of the Act

Section 25EA of this instrument, as inserted by the amending regulations, applies in relation to a suspension or termination that occurs on or after the commencement time in respect of conduct that occurs on or after the commencement time.

###### 43 Review by the Administrative Review Tribunal

The amendments of section 25L of this instrument made by the amending regulations apply in relation to an application made to the ART on or after the commencement time.

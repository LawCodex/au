# Marine Order 54 (Coastal pilotage) Amendment Order 2026

AMSA MO 2026/10

Marine Order 54 (Coastal pilotage) Amendment Order 2026

I, Kaylene Dale, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Maritime Safety Authority, make this Order under subsection 342(1) of the *Navigation Act 2012*.

Dated 17 September 2026

Kaylene Dale\
Chief Executive Officer

1 Name of Marine Order

This Marine Order is *Marine Order 54 (Coastal pilotage) Amendment Order 2026*.

2 Commencement

This Marine Order commences on the day after registration.

3 Amendment of *Marine Order 54 (Coastal pilotage) 2026*

Schedule 1 amends *Marine Order 54 (Coastal pilotage) 2026*.

Schedule 1 Amendment

[1] Section 4(1), definition of *draught*

substitute

***draught***, for a vessel, means the vertical distance between the waterline and the lowest part of the vessel, as measured when the vessel is stopped in the water and not making way.

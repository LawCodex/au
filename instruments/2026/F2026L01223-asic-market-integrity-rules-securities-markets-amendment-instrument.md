**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This instrument is the *ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) Amendment Instrument 2026/574*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day that is 18 months after the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 798G(1) of *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

5.6.3 Trading System controls and arrangements

(1) Without limiting any other rule, in relation to a Trading System, a Trading Participant must have in place appropriate controls and arrangements, including the following:

(a) controls to prevent Trading Messages being submitted into a Trading Platform that interfere with the efficiency and integrity of the relevant Market or the proper functioning of that Trading Platform, including but not limited to having automated filters, filter parameters and processes to record any changes to the filters or filter parameters;

(b) security arrangements to monitor for and prevent unauthorised persons having access to a Trading System;

(c) controls, including automated controls, that enable immediate suspension, limitation or prohibition of trading in respect of:

(i) one or more Authorised Persons or clients;

(ii) the operation of one or more Trading Algorithms used by the Trading Participant;

(iii) one or more financial products;

(iv) one or more Markets;

(v) one or more Trading Systems;

(d) controls that enable immediate:

(i) suspension of, limitation of, or prohibition on, the entry into a Market of Trading Messages in a series of related Trading Messages where the Trading Participant has identified that Trading Messages in the series have entered a Market and have interfered with or are likely to interfere with the efficiency or integrity of the relevant Market;

(ii) cancellation of Trading Messages in a series that have already entered a Market where the entry of further messages in the series has been suspended, limited or prohibited under subparagraph (i);

(iii) suspension of, limitation of, or prohibition on, the entry into any Crossing System operated by the Trading Participant of Orders in a series of related Orders where the Trading Participant has identified that Orders in the series have entered the Crossing System operated by the Trading Participant and have interfered with or are likely to interfere with the efficiency or integrity of the Crossing System; and

(iv) cancellation of Orders in a series that have already entered a Crossing System operated by the Trading Participant where the entry of further Orders in the series has been suspended, limited or prohibited under subparagraph (iii).

(2) A Trading Participant must at all times have direct control over all its Trading System controls required under subrule (1)(a).

5.6.3A Monitoring and surveillance of trading activity

(1) A Trading Participant must have in place adequate monitoring systems that enable the Trading Participant to immediately identify a Trading Message or series of Trading Messages that are likely to interfere or have interfered with the efficiency or integrity of a Market, or the proper functioning of a Trading Platform.

(2) A Trading Participant must have in place adequate post-trade surveillance arrangements that enable the Trading Participant to identify as soon as possible a Trading Message or series of Trading Messages that may have created, or may have been intended to create, the appearance described in Rule 5.7.1, having regard to the matters set out in Rule 5.7.2.

(3) A Trading Participant must maintain records of Trading Messages identified under subrule (1) or (2) and any measures taken in respect of those Trading Messages, for a period of seven years from the date of the relevant Trading Message.

5.6.3B Responsibility and testing of Trading Algorithms

(1) Without limiting any other rule, a Trading Participant must have appropriate controls and governance arrangements (including written procedures) for the development, testing, approval, deployment and monitoring of all Trading Algorithms used by the Trading Participant or that the Trading Participant makes available to a client.

(2) For the purposes of subrule (1), a Trading Participant must ensure that before it:

(a) uses a Trading Algorithm for the first time; or

(b) first makes a Trading Algorithm available to any client; or

(c) makes a material change to a Trading Algorithm,

the Trading Participant takes reasonable steps to test that the Trading Algorithm will not interfere with the efficiency and integrity of a Market, or any Crossing System operated by the Trading Participant or the proper functioning of a Trading Platform.

(3) For the purposes of subrule (1) and (2), the development and testing of the Trading Algorithm should be conducted by persons who are suitably qualified to do so.

(4) A Trading Participant must maintain records that demonstrate compliance with subrules (1) and (2) for a period of seven years.

**80 Rule 5.6.8A**

Repeal the rule, substitute:

5.6.8A Annual Review of Trading System

(1) After a Trading Participant begins using a Trading System in relation to a Market, the Trading Participant must review (***Annual Review***) the Trading System at least once every 12 months unless the Trading Participant has performed a Material Change Review in that period.

(2) For the purposes of subrule (1), the Annual Review must include an assessment of whether the Trading Participant’s controls, arrangements and resources in relation to the Trading System enable the Trading Participant to comply with Rule 5.5.2 and Part 5.6 of these Rules and as necessary involve:

(a) review of the Trading Participant’s policies, procedures, system design documentation and other relevant documentation in relation to the Trading System;

(b) review of the Trading Participant’s policies, procedures and other relevant documentation for implementation of changes to the Trading System; and

(c) testing of the Trading Participant’s controls, arrangements and resources in relation to the Trading System.

(3) A Trading Participant must ensure the Annual Review is conducted by a person who is suitably qualified and experienced to review the Trading Participant’s controls, arrangements and resources relating to the Trading System.

(4) Where a Trading Participant performs an Annual Review of a Trading System, the Trading Participant must make a written statement within 20 business days upon completion of the Annual Review from at least one officer who has responsibility for a function of the Trading Participant’s business that uses the Trading System (***responsible officer***) about whether the Trading Participant has complied with the Trading Participant’s obligations in this rule and the name and position of each responsible officer providing the statement, and the date the responsible officer made the statement.

(5) A Trading Participant must document and retain records for a period of at least seven years relating to:

(a) the methodology, scope and results of the Annual Review; and

(b) any recommended changes in relation to the Annual Review applied to the Trading Participant’s controls, arrangements and resources as a result of the Annual Review.

(1) A Market Participant must not, as Principal, make a Bid or Offer for, or deal in, any financial product:

(a) with the intention; or

(b) if that Bid, Offer or dealing has the effect, or is likely to have the effect;

(c) of active trading in any financial product; or

(d) with respect to the market for, or the price of, any financial product.

(2) A Market Participant must not make a Bid or Offer for, or deal in, any financial product on account of any other person where:

(a) the Market Participant intends to create; or

(c) the Market Participant ought reasonably suspect that:

(i) taking into account the matters specified in Rule 5.7.2, the person (or if the person is acting on behalf of another person, that other person) has placed the Order with the intention of creating; or

(ii) taking into account the matters specified in Rule 5.7.2, the Order placed will have the effect of creating;

a false or misleading appearance:

(d) of active trading in any financial product; or

(e) with respect to the market for, or the price of, any financial product.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Disability Insurance Scheme (Old Framework Plans) Determination 2024

**1 Section 4**

Repeal the definition of ***price-limited***, substitute:

***priced***: a support item is ***priced*** if a pricing document that specifies the support item states an amount for that support item.

**2 Section 4**

Repeal paragraph (a) of the definition of ***pricing document***, substitute:

(a) the document titled *Pricing Schedule*

**3 Section 4, definition of pricing document (note)**

Omit “2024”, substitute “2026”.

**4 Subsection 6(1), step 3, paragraph (a)**

Omit “price-limited”, substitute “priced”.

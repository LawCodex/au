# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 1 of 2026

## Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 1 of 2026

Information provided by general insurers and Lloyd’s underwriters for the purposes of the National Claims and Policies Database under Reporting Standard GRS 800.1, GRS 800.2, GRS 800.3, LOLRS 800.1, LOLRS 800.2 and LOLRS 800.3

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Declan Latimer, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 57(2)(b) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998* (the Act) and subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*:

revoke the determinations listed in Schedule 1; and

determine that all or a specified part of the reporting documents of a kind specified in the Schedule 2 do not contain confidential information.

This instrument commences at the start of the day after the day the instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The effect of this instrument is that the non-confidential information may be published under subsection 56(5C) of the Act.

Dated: 30 June 2026

Declan Latimer

General Manager

General Insurance and Banking

**Interpretation**

In this determination:

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***reporting document*** and ***reporting standard*** have their respective meanings given in the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001*.

### Part 1—General insurer policy data

1\. The following parts of reporting documents given to APRA in accordance with *Reporting Standard GRS 800.1 Policy Data: Public and Product Liability and Professional Indemnity Insurance* where a period of 14 days has elapsed from the date the reporting documents were required to be given to APRA:

a. the number of reportable policies (count of unique entries in GRF 800.1.7);

b. the number of risks written (count of unique entries in GRF 800.1.8 where those unique entries are absent from reporting documents in respect of the previous year’s reporting periods);

c. the sum of the gross earned premium (sum of GRF 800.1.15b); and

d. the sum of the gross written premium (sum of GRF 800.1.16),

for all policies corresponding to a combination of attributes in the table below, such that for each combination, only one attribute per data item applies:

| **Data item** | **Attribute** |
|---|---|

| Date of commencement / inception (Term Inception Date) (GRF 800.1.11) | The calendar year |
| Product type (GRF 800.1.9) | Either PUB; PRO; CON; CYB; any other Public & Products; D&O or EPL; MAL; MAN; or any other Professional Risk |
| Class of Business (GRF 800.1.2) | The class of business |
| State (GRF 800.1.18) | Either ACT, NSW, NT, QLD, SA, TAS, VIC, WA or none |
| Excess/Deductible/Attachment point (GRF 800.1.20) | Either $0-$499.99, $500-$999.99 or ≥$1000 |

Note: For example, this item determines non-confidential, among other things, the number of reportable policies, number of risks, number of in-force risks, the sum of the gross earned premium across those policies and the sum of the gross written premium across those policies, for policies reported by entities in 2025, commenced in 2025, for medical indemnity and malpractice professional indemnity insurance, for work done in NSW, where the deductible is less than or equal to $499.99.

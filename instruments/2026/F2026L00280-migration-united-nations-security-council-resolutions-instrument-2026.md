# Migration (United Nations Security Council Resolutions) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/008

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 5 March 2026

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (United Nations Security Council Resolutions) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 21 March 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Migration (United Nations Security Council Resolutions) Regulations 2007*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Regulations, including the following:

(a) ***UNSC-designated person***;

(b) ***resolution***.

In this instrument:

***Regulations*** means the *Migration (United Nations Security Council Resolutions) Regulations 2007*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Resolutions

For the definition of ***resolution*** in regulation 4 of the Regulations, each resolution mentioned in column 1 of an item in the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument is specified.

Note: Regulation 5 of the Regulations provides that a person is a ***UNSC-designated person*** if, under a resolution, Australia is required to prevent the person entering or transiting through Australian territory.

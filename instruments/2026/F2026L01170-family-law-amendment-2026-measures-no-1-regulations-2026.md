Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Surrogacy parentage orders—Australian Capital Territory

##### Family Law Regulations 2024

**1 Section 49 (table item 8)**

Omit “Section 26”, substitute “Section 28H”.

**2 Before section 140**

Insert:

#### Division 1—Application and transitional arrangements in relation to the commencement of this instrument

**3 At the end of Part 15**

Add:

#### Division 2—Amendments made by the Family Law Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026

###### 150 Surrogacy arrangements—Australian Capital Territory

(1) The amendment of section 49 of this instrument made by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Family Law Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026* applies in relation to the following proceedings:

(a) proceedings instituted on or after the day that Part commences;

(b) proceedings instituted before, and not finally determined before, the day that Part commences, other than proceedings in respect of which a final hearing has commenced before the day that Part commences.

(2) Despite the amendment of section 49 of this instrument made by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Family Law Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026*, section 26 of the *Parentage Act 2004* (ACT), as in force immediately before the repeal of that section by the *Parentage (Surrogacy) Amendment Act 2024* (ACT), is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 60HB(1) of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

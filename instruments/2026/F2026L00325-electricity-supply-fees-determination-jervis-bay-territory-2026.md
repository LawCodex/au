###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***11kV time‑of‑use demand supply*** means an 11kV electricity supply, on a time‑of‑use demand basis, to a customer in relation to whom a minimum demand of 200 kVA of electricity consumption per month is anticipated.

Note: Fees for 11kV time‑of‑use demand supply are set out in clause 8 of Schedule 2.

***12-month period*** means the period of time from 1 April to 31 March each year.

***billing period*** means each successive period during which a customer is billed for electricity consumed.

***controlled off‑peak supply*** means a supply of electricity to a customer’s residence or premises that is periodically switched on and off for load control reasons.

***customer***, means

(a) a person for whom the service is provided under a customer contract; or

(b) a person who has applied, orally or in writing, for the service to be provided under a customer contract.

Note: A person may be an individual, a body corporate or a body politic (a government body): see section 2C of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

***domestic supply*** means the supply in a controlled manner to a residence.

***domestic supply controlled off-peak***, for a domestic supply customer, means a supply that is periodically switched on and off for load control reasons by time switch controls located in the customer’s residence switchboard.

***disconnection fee*** means

***fee*** means an amount payable by a customer:

for an electricity supply,

for use by the customer of a service related to the electricity supply, or

in connection with the electricity supply, or a service related to the electricity supply,

at the rate mentioned in this instrument for the supply or service or in connection with the supply or service. For the avoidance of doubt, a fee in this instrument includes an amount charged in relation to disconnection or reconnection of the electricity supply.

***general supply*** means a supply of electricity to a customer that is not:

a domestic supply; or

an 11kV time-of-use demand supply; or

a LV industrial supply.

***general supply controlled off-peak***, for a general supply customer, means a supply that is periodically switched on and off for load control reasons by time switch controls located in the customer’s premises switchboard.

***GST*** has the meaning given by section 195-1 of the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*.

***LV industrial supply*** means a supply of low voltage electricity to a customer who has consumed more than 160,000 kWh of electricity in a previous 12‑month period.

Note: Fees for LV industrial supply of electricity are set out in clause 7 of Schedule 2.

***off-peak period*** means a period that is not a peak period or a shoulder period.

***old instrument*** has the meaning given by section 13.

***peak period*** means:

(a) 7 am to 9 am on a week day; or

(b) 5 pm to 8 pm on a week day.

***shoulder period*** means:

(a) 9 am to 5 pm on a week day; or

(b) 8 pm to 10 pm on a week day.

***supply*** of electricity includes the provision of services related to the supply of electricity.

***week day*** means a day that is not a Saturday or a Sunday.

###### 6 Fees for supply of electricity

(1) The fees for supply of electricity are set out in:

(a) for domestic supply in a billing period — Part 2 of Schedule 2; and

(b) for domestic supply controlled off-peak in a billing period — Part 3 of Schedule 2; and

(c) for general supply in a billing period — Part 5 of Schedule 2; and

(d) for general supply controlled off-peak in a billing period — Part 6 of Schedule 2; and

(e) for LV industrial supply in a billing period — Part 7 of Schedule 2; and

(f) for an 11kV time-of-use demand supply — Part 8 of Schedule 2.

Note: A general supply customer is normally a business customer who is not a domestic customer or a large industrial customer.

(2) If a customer is a pensioner, the fees for domestic supply are subject to a rebate of $0.82 for each day, to be applied before the application of GST to the customer’s account.

(3) If a customer is dependent on life support or constant positive airway pressure respirators, the fees for domestic supply are subject to a rebate of $0.95 for each day, to be applied before the application of GST to the customer’s account.

###### 7 Fees – miscellaneous

A fee or charge for an item mentioned in Schedule 3 is the amount mentioned in Schedule 3for the item.

###### 8 11kV time-of-use demand supply

If a customer has a minimum use of 200 kVA of electricity per month, the customer may be connected to 11kV time-of-use demand supply.

###### 9 LV industrial supply

A customer may be connected to a LV industrial supply of electricity if:

(a) the customer has consumed more than 160,000 kWh of electricity in a previous 12‑month period; and

(b) the customer requests low voltage industrial supply.

###### 10 Period for payment

A fee or charge for the supply of an item mentioned in Schedule 2 or 3, must be paid within 21 days of the day a bill is issued for the amount of the fee or charge.

###### 11 Non-payment of fees

(1) The notice of disconnection fee mentioned in item 6 of Part 3 of Schedule 3 is payable if a fee for supply of electricity is not paid by a customer within 18 days after the expiry of the period mentioned in section 10.

(2) If a fee is not paid by a customer within 10 days after the delivery of a notice of disconnection, the supply of electricity to the customer may be disconnected and the combined disconnection/reconnection fee mentioned in item 1 or 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 3 is payable.

(3) If a supply of electricity is disconnected under subsection (2), the customer must not be reconnected until the customer pays the outstanding amount of the electricity fees and any fees payable under subsections (1) and (2).

###### 12 Refund of security deposit

(1) This section applies if:

(a) a customer has paid a security deposit for a supply of electricity; and

(b) the customer requests that the supply of electricity be disconnected; and

(b) the customer receives a final account for the supply of electricity.

(2) If the customer pays the final account in full, the Department will refund the security deposit in full to the customer.

(3) If the customer does not pay the final account in full, the Department will retain the amount owing on the final account and refund the balance of the security deposit to the customer.

###### 13 Transitional provision

(1) If:

(a) a thing was done for a particular purpose under the old instrument as in force immediately before the instrument was repealed; and

(b) the thing could be done for that purpose under this instrument;

the thing has effect for the purposes of this instrument as if it had been done for that purpose under this instrument.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), a reference in that subsection to a thing being done includes a reference to a notice, application or other instrument being given or made.

(3) In this section:

***old instrument*** means *Electricity Supply Fees Determination 2019 (Jervis Bay Territory)*

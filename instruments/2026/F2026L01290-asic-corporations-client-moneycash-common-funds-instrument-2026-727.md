# ASIC Corporations (Client Money—Cash Common Funds) Instrument 2026/727

ASIC Corporations (Client Money—Cash Common Funds) Instrument 2026/727

I, Nathan Bourne, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 23 September 2026

Nathan Bourne

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Client Money—Cash Common Funds) Instrument 2026/727.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 992B(1) of the *Corporations Act 2001.*

4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

5 Simplified outline of this instrument

This instrument modifies the Act to allow financial services licensees to pay client money into an account that relates to a cash common fund that is a registered scheme and is operated by a licensed trustee company.

6 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Corporations Act 2001*.

Part 2—Declaration

7 Section 981B modification—common funds that are registered schemes

Part 7.8 of the Act applies in relation to a financial services licensee as if section 981B were modified or varied by, after subparagraph 981B(1)(a)(i), inserting:

“(ia) with a licensed trustee company and relates to a common fund (within the meaning of subsection 601SCA(2)) that is also a registered scheme and only invests in one or more of the following and only to the extent authorised by the constitution of the scheme:

(A) fixed interest securities;

(B) negotiable instruments; or”.

Part 3—Repeal

8 Repeal

This instrument is repealed at the start of 1 October 2031.

Schedule 1—Repeals

*ASIC Corporations (Client money - Cash common funds) Instrument 2016/671*

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

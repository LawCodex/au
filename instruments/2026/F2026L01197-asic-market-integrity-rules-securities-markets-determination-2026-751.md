# ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) Determination 2026/751

ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) Determination 2026/751

I, Jacqueline Cochrane, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 7 September 2026

Jacqueline Cochrane

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) Determination 2026/751.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) 13 October 2026; and

(b) 20 business days following the day this instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subrule 6.2.1(4) of the *ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) 2017* (***Rules***).

4 Simplified outline of this instrument

This instrument determines which Equity Market Products are Tier 1 and Tier 2 for the purposes of subrule 6.2.1(1) of the Rules.

5 Interpretation

(1) In this instrument, unless the contrary intention appears, capitalised terms have the same meaning as in the Rules.

(2) In this instrument, ***stock code***, in relation to an Equity Market Product, means the unique symbol assigned to the Equity Market Product under Rule 9.2.3 of the Rules.

Part 2—Determination

6 Tier 1 Equity Market Products and Tier 2 Equity Market Products

(1) Each Equity Market Product whose stock code is set out in the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument is a Tier 1 Equity Market Product.

(2) Each Equity Market Product whose stock code is set out in the table in Schedule 2 to this instrument is a Tier 2 Equity Market Product.

Part 3—Repeals

7 Repeal

Each instrument that is specified in Schedule 3 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in Schedule 3, and any other item in Schedule 3 has effect according to its terms.

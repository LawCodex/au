###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Independent Parliamentary Expenses Authority Act 2017*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Authority;

(b) Authority‑administered MP resource;

(c) designated MP resource;

(d) member of parliament.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Independent Parliamentary Expenses Authority Act 2017*.

***COMCAR*** means the car‑with‑driver transport service, known by that name, that is operated by the Commonwealth.

***Commonwealth transport*** has the same meaning as in the PBR Regulations.

***Commonwealth transport determination*** has the same meaning as in the PBR Regulations.

***PBR Act*** means the *Parliamentary Business Resources Act 2017*.

***PBR Regulations*** means the *Parliamentary Business Resources Regulations 2017*.

***transport costs*** has the same meaning as in the PBR Regulations.

### Part 2—Scope of functions of the Authority

###### 5 Scope of functions of the Authority—Authority‑administered MP resources

Resources that are not Authority‑administered MP resources—members of parliament

(1) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(1A)(a)(v) of the Act, the following resources that have been, or would be, paid or provided by the Commonwealth to, or in relation to, members of parliament are prescribed:

(a) private plated vehicles, and any costs associated with operating or maintaining private plated vehicles, that are provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(a) of that Act);

(b) allowances payable instead of providing private plated vehicles, being allowances provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(b) of that Act);

(c) allowances or expenses payable in relation to internet or telephone services provided to private residences, being allowances or expenses provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(c) of that Act);

(d) photographic services prescribed by section 69 of the PBR Regulations;

(e) flags and certificates prescribed by section 70 of the PBR Regulations;

(f) Commonwealth transport provided in accordance with a Commonwealth transport determination made under subsection 33(1) of the PBR Act;

(g) offices, and temporary office accommodation and facilities, that are prescribed by section 72 or 73 of the PBR Regulations and provided in accordance with a determination made under subsection 33(1) of the PBR Act;

(h) public resources that are prescribed by section 74 of the PBR Regulations (which deals with resources for offices) and provided in accordance with a determination made under subsection 33(1) of the PBR Act;

(i) resources relating to information and communications technology, or photography, if the resources are prescribed by section 75 of the PBR Regulations (which deals with resources during election periods) and provided in accordance with a determination made under subsection 33(1) of the PBR Act;

(j) offices that are provided in accordance with a determination made under subsection 33(2) of the PBR Act;

(k) insurance provided under section 78 of the PBR Regulations;

(l) resources provided under section 79 of the PBR Regulations in relation to insuring personal effects at official residences for the Prime Minister;

(m) Commonwealth transport provided under section 94A of the PBR Regulations.

Resources that are not Authority‑administered MP resources—former members of parliament and estates

(2) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(1A)(a)(v) of the Act, the following resources are prescribed:

(a) allowances that have been, or would be, paid by the Commonwealth under subsection 15(1) of the PBR Act to former members of parliament;

(b) allowances that have been, or would be, paid by the Commonwealth under subsection 15(2) of the PBR Act to the estates of persons who die while a member of parliament.

Resources that are not Authority‑administered MP resources—former Prime Ministers

(3) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(1A)(a)(v) of the Act, the following resources that have been, or would be, paid or provided by the Commonwealth to, or in relation to, former Prime Ministers are prescribed (subject to subsection (4) of this section):

(a) private plated vehicles, and any costs associated with operating or maintaining private plated vehicles;

(b) allowances payable instead of being provided with private plated vehicles;

(c) goods, services, equipment and expenses relating to telephones, the internet or other means of communication;

(d) offices provided to former Prime Ministers, and other resources provided for the conduct in those offices of a former Prime Minister’s business as a former Prime Minister;

(e) goods, services, equipment and expenses relating to information and communications technology;

(f) access to Commonwealth Parliament Offices, and resources connected with the provision of such access;

(g) close protection, and guarding of residences of former Prime Ministers, by the Australian Federal Police;

(h) security assessments, enhancements and arrangements at residences or offices of former Prime Ministers.

(4) Subsection (3) does not apply in relation to the any of the following resources, or in relation to the costs of any of the following resources:

(a) office stationery and supplies (other than supplies specified by subsection (5));

(b) printing;

(c) postage;

(d) minor office equipment (other than equipment specified by subsection (5)), along with accessories, consumables, repairs and maintenance relating to that minor office equipment;

(e) software other than software provided by the Department of Parliamentary Services;

(f) publications (whether printed or electronic);

(g) incidental fees and charges (such as transaction, administration and delivery fees or charges) associated with the provision of, or cost of, a resource covered by another paragraph of this subsection.

Note: If a resource covered by a paragraph of this subsection, or the cost of a resource covered by a paragraph of this subsection, has been, or would be, paid or provided by the Commonwealth to, or in relation to, a former Prime Minister under Part 2 of the PBR Act, then that resource or cost remains an Authority‑administered MP resource.

(5) For the purposes of paragraphs (4)(a) and (d), the following supplies and equipment are specified:

(a) first aid kits and supplies;

(b) work health and safety equipment and supplies;

(c) bathroom supplies;

(d) cleaning supplies;

(e) equipment relating to information and communications technology.

Authority’s functions do not include incurring expenses relating to, or paying or providing, certain Authority‑administered MP resources

(6) For the purposes of subsection 12(2A) of the Act, the following Authority‑administered MP resources are prescribed:

(a) travel expenses of a member of parliament, payable under section 30 of the PBR Act, that are transport costs for travel (whether by a member of parliament or by another person) by COMCAR;

(b) transport costs for travel by COMCAR that are payable in accordance with a determination made under section 16 of the PBR Act (which deals with resources for former Prime Ministers and certain others);

(c) expenses prescribed by section 65 of the PBR Regulations for cancelling travel arrangements that relate to travel by COMCAR.

Note: The Authority still has other functions under the Act in relation to Authority‑administered MP resources of the kinds prescribed this subsection.

###### 6 Scope of functions of the Authority—designated MP resources

Resources that are not designated MP resources

(1) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(1B)(a)(ii) of the Act, the following resources that have been paid or provided to members of parliament are prescribed:

(a) allowances paid instead of providing private plated vehicles, being allowances provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(b) of that Act);

(b) Commonwealth transport provided in accordance with a Commonwealth transport determination made under subsection 33(1) of the PBR Act.

Designated MP resources to which the Authority’s audit function does not apply

(2) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(1)(i)(i) of the Act, the following designated MP resources are prescribed:

(a) private plated vehicles provided to members of parliament, and any costs associated with operating or maintaining those vehicles, being vehicles and costs provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(a) of that Act);

(b) allowances or expenses paid in relation to internet or telephone services provided to private residences of members of parliament, being allowances or expenses provided as remuneration under section 14 of the PBR Act (see paragraph 14(4)(c) of that Act).

Note: The Authority still has other functions under the Act in relation to designated MP resources of the kinds prescribed this subsection.

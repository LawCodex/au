### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Small Amounts of Compensation—Definition of Specified Number) Instrument 2026*.

### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the day it is registered.

### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 138(3) of the Act.

### 4 Repeals

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specification in Relation to Definition of Specified Number) Instrument 2015* is repealed.

5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Commission.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

### 6 Specified number

For the purposes of the definition of ***specified number*** in subsection 138(3) of the Act, the number specified by the Commission is 0.03.

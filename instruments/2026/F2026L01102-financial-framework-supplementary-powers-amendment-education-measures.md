## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Financial Framework (Supplementary Powers) Regulations 1997

**1 Part 4 of Schedule 1AB (in the appropriate position in the table)**

Insert:

| 807 | Inclusion Support Program | To support either or both of the following:<br>(a) all children having the opportunity to participate in a quality approved early learning program;<br>(b) parents or carers of children with additional needs having access to appropriate and affordable child care services that allow them to participate in the workforce, including parents or carers of:<br>(i) children with disability; and<br>(ii) children who are undergoing disability assessment; and<br>(iii) Indigenous children; and<br>(iv) children from culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds; and<br>(v) children from a background that involves refugee status or humanitarian intervention;<br>as a measure to give effect to Australia’s obligations under the Convention on the Rights of the Child, particularly Articles 4, 18 and 23. |

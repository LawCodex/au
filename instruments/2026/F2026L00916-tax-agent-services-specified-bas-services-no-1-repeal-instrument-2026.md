# Tax Agent Services (Specified BAS Services No. 1) Repeal Instrument 2026

I, Peter de Cure AM, Chair of the Tax Practitioners Board, make the following instrument under the *Tax Agent Services Act 2009*.

Dated 15 April 2026

Peter de Cure AM

Chair on behalf of the Tax Practitioners Board

Contents

###### 1 Name of instrument

This instrument is the *Tax Agent Services (Specified BAS Services No. 1) Repeal Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 90-10(1A) of the *Tax Agent Services Act 2009*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Tax Agent Services (Specified BAS Services No. 2) Instrument 2020 [F2020L01406] registered 5 November 2020.

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

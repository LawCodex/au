## Schedule 1—Description of quality assurance activity

###### 1 Name of activity

Australian and New Zealand Society of Cardiac and Thoracic Surgeons (ANZSCTS) Cardiac Surgery Monitoring Program.

###### 2 Description of activity

The ANZSCTS Cardiac Surgery Monitoring Program (the Program) collates data using a standardised dataset and an agreed data dictionary. Participating institutions use these tools to support consistent data collection for adult cardiac surgical patients.

The Program statistically evaluates outcomes of adult cardiac surgical procedures. It assesses the performance of individual surgeons and hospital units against an agreed set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). This analysis considers a patient’s risk of complications and the surgical procedures and methods used to determine the most successful patient outcomes. The data set and definitions are reviewed regularly to allow integration with evolving databases.

This declaration applies to the Program’s peer review and quality assurance analysis process, the outcomes of specific quality assurance investigations, any identifying data received and any follow up actions with individual surgeons.

The activity is undertaken by the Australia and New Zealand Cardiac and Thoracic Surgery Research Institution (ANZCTSRI). ANZCTSRI is a not-for-profit organisation established by the ANZSCTS to conduct the Program.

ANZCTSRI provides regular disclosures of de-identified information using data from the Activity in annual reports which show detailed unit comparisons for cardiac procedures performed in the calendar year. The published data compares results against the KPIs. Results are also published in academic journals. Extracts of reports are provided to the Departments of Health and hospitals in participating jurisdictions.

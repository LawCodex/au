## Schedule 1—Description of quality assurance activity

**1 Name of activity**

The name of the quality assurance activity is the I-MED Radiology Network Review of Radiologist Reports.

**2 Description of activity**

The activity involves structured peer review of radiology reports by Radiologists engaged by I‑MED to assess report accuracy, clarity and clinical appropriateness.

The activity provides feedback and recommendations to practitioners and monitors the implementation of these recommendations. The activity also identifies and manages quality issues, supports escalation to further review where required, and informs broader quality improvements across I-MED’s services.

The Activity aims to improve clinical performance, clinical decision making, communication and patient safety on a nationally consistent basis.

The Activity is overseen by I‑MED’s clinical governance committee and supported by designated clinical leaders.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 84AAM(3) and 84AAN(3) of the *National Health Act 1953*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***authorised nurse prescriber*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

***eligible nurse prescriber*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

***Health Practitioner Regulation National Law*** has the same meaning as in section 132A of the Act.

***nurse prescriber agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 84AAN of the Act.

***scheduled 2, 3, 4 and 8 medicines*** means medicines that are included in any of Schedules 2, 3, 4 and 8 of the current Poisons Standard (within the meaning of the *Therapeutic Goods Act 1989*).

###### 5 Requirements to be an eligible nurse prescriber

For the purposes of paragraph 84AAM(1)(b) of the Act, a person must meet the requirements in subsection (2) to be an eligible nurse prescriber.

The registration of the person in the health profession of nursing must be endorsed by the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia under section 94 of the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law as qualified to administer, obtain, possess, prescribe, supply or use scheduled 2, 3, 4 and 8 medicines.

###### 6 Criteria for approval as an authorised nurse prescriber

For the purposes of subsection 84AAN(2) of the Act, the Secretary may approve the application of an eligible nurse prescriber to be an authorised nurse prescriber if the Secretary is satisfied the eligible nurse prescriber meets the criteria specified in this section.

The eligible nurse prescriber must have a nurse prescriber agreement in place with one or more PBS prescribers, none of whom are an authorised nurse prescriber.

###### 7 Conditions on approval as an authorised nurse prescriber

For the purposes of subsection 84AAN(2) of the Act, the approval of a person as an authorised nurse prescriber is subject to the condition that the authorised nurse prescriber does not, under a nurse prescriber agreement, prescribe a pharmaceutical benefit that could not be prescribed by a PBS prescriber with whom the agreement is made.

Example: An authorised nurse prescriber is in a prescriber agreement with an authorised nurse practitioner. The approval of the authorised nurse prescriber under section 84AAN of the Act is subject to the condition that the authorised nurse prescriber does not, under that prescriber agreement, prescribe any pharmaceutical benefits that the authorised nurse practitioner could not also prescribe.

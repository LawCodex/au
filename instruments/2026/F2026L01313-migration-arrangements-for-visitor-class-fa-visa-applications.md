# Migration (Arrangements for Visitor (Class FA) visa applications) Amendment Instrument 2026

LIN 26/060

I, Jill Ogden, delegate of the Minister, make the following instrument.

Dated 24 September 2026

Jill Ogden

Assistant Secretary

Skilled and Temporary Visa Policy Branch

Department of Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Arrangements for Visitor (Class FA) visa applications) Amendment Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subregulation 2.07(5) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* for the purposes of table item 3 under subitem 1236(6) of Scheule 1 to those regulations.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

# Defence Trade Controls (Australian Military Sales Program item) Amendment Determination 2026

I, Richard Marles, Minister for Defence, make the following determination.

Dated 9 July 2026

Richard Marles

Minister for Defence

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Defence Trade Controls (Australian Military Sales Program item) Amendment Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 5AA of the *Defence Trade Controls Act 2012*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Defence Trade Controls (Australian Military Sales Program item) Determination 2024

**1 Section 4 (definition of AEW&C)**

Repeal the definition.

**2 Section 4 (definition of aircraft)**

Repeal the definition.

**3 Section 4 (definition of UAV)**

Repeal the definition.

**4 Section 5**

Substitute:

(1) This section specifies DSGL goods or DSGL technology that is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(2) DSGL goods or DSGL technology specified in column 1 of an item in the following table that is covered by the DSGL reference number specified in column 2 is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

|  | **Column 1** | **Column 2** |
|---|---|---|
| **Item** | **DSGL goods or DSGL technology** | **DSGL reference number** |
| 1 | Ghost Shark Extra-Large Autonomous Undersea Vehicle | ML9. |
| 2 | Active Electronically Scanned Array | ML5. |
| 3 | Jindalee Operational Radar Network high-frequency over-the-horizon radar | ML5. |

(3) A component specially designed for DSGL goods mentioned in subsection (2) is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(4) Software covered by DSGL reference number ML21. specially designed or modified for the development, production, operation or maintenance of:

(a) DSGL goods or DSGL technology mentioned in subsection (2); or

(b) a component covered by subsection (3);

is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(5) Technology covered by DSGL reference number ML22. specially required for the development, production, operation, installation, maintenance (checking), repair, overhaul or refurbishing of:

(a) DSGL goods or DSGL technology mentioned in subsection (2); or

(b) a component covered by subsection (3);

is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

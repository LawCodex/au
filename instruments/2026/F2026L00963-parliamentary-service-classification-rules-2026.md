# Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2026

We, Senator the Hon Sue Lines, President of the Senate, and the Hon Milton Dick MP, Speaker of the House of Representatives, make the following rules.

Dated 10/07/2026

Senator the Hon Sue Lines The Hon Milton Dick MP

President of the Senate Speaker of the House of Representatives

### Part 2—Classification of Parliamentary Service employees

###### 6 Approved classifications

A classification is an ***approved classification*** if the classification is:

(a) a classification under a modern award, as in force at a particular time or as in force from time to time; or

(b) a classification mentioned in column 2 of the table in Schedule 1; or

(c) a training classification mentioned in column 1 of the table in Schedule 2.

Note: Subsection 23(2) of the Act provides that the Classification Rules may apply, adopt or incorporate, with or without modification, any of the provisions of a modern award as in force at a particular time or as in force from time to time.

###### 7 Classification of Parliamentary Service employees—general

(1) A Secretary of a Department must allocate an approved classification to each Parliamentary Service employee in the Department, subject to subsection (3).

(2) The approved classification allocated to the employee must be based on the group of duties that are determined by the Secretary to be the duties to be performed by the employee in the Department (other than duties temporarily assigned to the employee).

(3) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a Parliamentary Service employee who is covered by both paragraphs 8(1)(a) and (b).

Note: Paragraphs 8(1)(a) and (b) together cover employees who move, on a temporary basis, between Departments other than for a promotion. The Secretary must allocate to such employees an approved classification under subsection 8(2).

###### 8 Classification of Parliamentary Service employees—temporary moves between Departments not associated with promotion

(1) This section applies to a Secretary of a Department if:

(a) an ongoing Parliamentary Service employee moves to the Department in accordance with an agreement to which section 39 of the *Parliamentary Service Determination 2024* applies; and

(b) the agreed movement is a temporary move.

(2) The Secretary must allocate to the employee:

(a) the approved classification (the ***existing classification***) that was allocated to the employee immediately before moving to the Department; or

(b) another approved classification that is listed in the same cell of the table in Schedule 1 as the existing classification.

(3) The allocation of an approved classification in accordance with subsection (2) does not prevent the employee from:

(a) performing the duties that are to be performed by the employee following the move to the Department; and

(b) receiving the pay and other entitlements that are to be paid to the employee following the move to the Department.

###### 9 Classification of SES employees

For the purposes of section 34 of the Act, a Parliamentary Service employee is classified as an SES employee if the employee is allocated any of the following approved classifications:

(a) Senior Executive Service Band 1;

(b) Senior Executive Service Band 2;

(c) Senior Executive Service Band 3.

###### 10 Classification of duties

(1) A Secretary of a Department must allocate an approved classification to each group of duties to be performed in the Department.

(2) The allocation of an approved classification to a group of duties must be based on the work value of the group of duties.

(3) If a training classification is allocated to a group of duties, the Secretary must ensure that the duties include a requirement to undergo training.

(4) If a group of duties to be performed in the Department involves work value to which more than one approved classification could be allocated, the Secretary may allocate more than one approved classification to the group of duties, subject to subsection (6).

(5) If more than one approved classification is allocated to a group of duties under subsection (4), the approved classifications are collectively to be known as a ***broadband***.

(6) Subsection (4) does not apply to a group of duties to be performed by an SES employee.

###### 11 Work level standards

(1) A Secretary of a Department must, in writing, determine work level standards describing the work requirements for each approved classification that has been allocated to a group of duties to be performed in the Department.

(2) The work level standards for an approved classification must reflect the work value requirements for the classification.

(3) A determination under subsection (1) is not a legislative instrument.

###### 12 Employees in training classifications

(1) If a Parliamentary Service employee in a Department:

(a) is engaged at a training classification in column 1 of an item in the table in Schedule 2; and

(b) satisfactorily finishes each training requirement for the training classification;

the Secretary of the Department must allocate to the employee the approved classification mentioned in column 2 of that item.

(2) This section does not apply to the employee if the employee is engaged in accordance with subsection 19(4) of the *Parliamentary Service Determination 2024* for the purpose mentioned in paragraph (c) of that subsection.

Note: Paragraph 19(4)(c) of the *Parliamentary Service Determination 2024* provides that a non‑ongoing non‑SES employee can be engaged under that subsection for the purpose of the employee gaining skills and experience.

###### 13 Reduction of classification

(1) This section applies if a Secretary reduces the classification of a Parliamentary Service employee without the employee’s consent.

(2) The Secretary must allocate to the employee a classification that is in a lower classification group than the employee’s classification before the reduction.

###### 14 Delegations

Delegations by Secretary

(1) A Secretary may, in writing, delegate to a Parliamentary Service employee any of the Secretary’s powers or functions under this instrument (other than this section).

(2) A Secretary may, in writing and with the prior written consent of the Parliamentary Service Commissioner, delegate to a person who is not a Parliamentary Service employee any of the Secretary’s powers or functions under this instrument (other than this section).

Subdelegations

(3) If a Parliamentary Service employee (the ***first delegate***) to whom powers or functions are delegated under subsection (1) is an SES employee or acting SES employee, the first delegate may, in writing, delegate any of those powers or functions to another Parliamentary Service employee (the ***second delegate***).

(4) However, if the first delegate is subject to directions in relation to the exercise of a power or function delegated under subsection (1), the first delegate must give corresponding directions to the second delegate.

(5) A power or function that is exercised or performed by a person under a delegation under subsection (3) is taken, for the purposes of this instrument, to have been exercised or performed by the person who originally delegated the corresponding power or function under subsection (1).

Appropriate work‑related qualities

(6) A person (the ***delegator***) must not delegate under this section a power or function to another person unless the delegator is reasonably satisfied that the other person has the appropriate work‑related qualities to exercise the power or perform the function (as the case requires).

Directions

(7) A person exercising powers or functions under a delegation under this section must comply with any directions of the person who delegated the power or function.

### Part 3—Application and transitional provisions

###### 15 Classifications allocated to employees before commencement

(1) This section applies if:

(a) immediately before the commencement of this section:

(i) a person is a Parliamentary Service employee in a Department; and

(ii) the person is allocated an approved classification (the ***existing classification***) under the *Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2010* (as in force before that commencement); and

(b) at that commencement, there is an approved classification (the ***new classification***) in this instrument that is the same as the existing classification.

(2) At that commencement, the Secretary of the Department is taken to have allocated the new classification to the person under section 7 of this instrument.

###### 16 Classifications allocated to duties before commencement

(1) This section applies if:

(a) immediately before the commencement of this section, a group of duties to be performed in a Department is allocated one or more approved classifications (the ***existing classifications***) under the *Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2010* (as in force before that commencement); and

(b) at that commencement, there are approved classifications (the ***new classifications***) in this instrument that are the same as the existing classifications.

(2) At that commencement, the Secretary of the Department is taken to have allocated the new classifications to the group of duties under section 10 of this instrument.

###### 17 Work level standards issued before commencement

(1) This section applies if:

(a) immediately before the commencement of this section, work level standards applied in a Department for a classification (the ***existing classification***) under the *Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2010* (as in force before that commencement); and

(b) at that commencement, there is an approved classification (the ***new classification***) in this instrument that is the same as the existing classification.

(2) At and after the commencement of this section, the work level standards for the existing classification continue in force, and may be dealt with, as if they were determined under subsection 11(1) of this instrument for the new classification.

###### 18 Delegations in force before commencement

Delegations by Secretary

(1) Subsection (2) applies if a delegation made under subrule 13(1) of the *Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2010* was in force immediately before the commencement of this section.

(2) At and after the commencement of this section, the delegation continues in force, and may be dealt with, as if it were made under subsection 14(1) of this instrument.

Delegations by person other than Secretary

(3) Subsection (4) applies if a delegation made under subrule 13(3) of the *Parliamentary Service Classification Rules 2010* was in force immediately before the commencement of this section.

(4) At and after the commencement of this section, the delegation continues in force, and may be dealt with, as if it were made under subsection 14(3) of this instrument.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Electricity Retail) Regulations 2019

**1 Section 4 (paragraph (d) of paragraph beginning “Under the code:”)**

Omit “, or the relevant tariff cap, determined by the AER for the supply”.

**2 Section 5 (definition of free usage period) (note)**

Omit “paragraph 18(3)(b)”, substitute “subsections 11A(3) and 18(4)”.

**3 Section 5 (definition of reasonable use cap)**

Omit “paragraph 18(3)(a)”, substitute “subsection 11A(2)”.

**4 Section 11 (heading)**

After “**make**”, insert “**available**”.

**5 Section 11 (heading)**

Omit “**customer**”, substitute “**customers**”.

**6 Subsection 11(1)**

After “make”, insert “available”.

**7 At the end of subsection 11(1)**

Add:

Civil penalty: 300 penalty units.

**8 At the end of subsection 11(2)**

Add:

Civil penalty: 300 penalty units.

**9 At the end of Subdivision AD of Division 2 of Part 2**

Add:

###### 11A Free usage periods for SSO regulated tariffs

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) apply to an electricity retailer supplying electricity to a residential customer under an SSO regulated tariff.

Electricity retailers must not charge for usage up to reasonable use cap during free usage period

(2) The electricity retailer must not charge for electricity supplied to the customer in a free usage period on a day if the electricity used by the customer in that period on the day does not exceed 24 kilowatt hours (the ***reasonable use cap***).

Civil penalty: 300 penalty units.

Electricity retailers must not charge more than reasonable use tariff cap for usage over reasonable use cap

(3) The electricity retailer must not charge more than the reasonable use tariff cap determined under subsection 18(4) for any electricity exceeding the reasonable use cap used by the customer in a free usage period on a day.

Civil penalty: 300 penalty units.

**10 Subsection 16(1B)**

Omit “paragraph 18(3)(b)”, substitute “subsection 18(4)”.

**11 At the end of subsection 18(1)**

Add:

Note: A residential customer may use up to 24 kilowatt hours of electricity in a free usage period on a day without being charged, see subsection 11A(2).

**12 Subsection 18(3)**

Repeal the subsection.

**13 Subsection 18(4)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(4) For the purposes of subsection 11A(3), the AER must, by legislative instrument, determine the maximum amount (the ***reasonable use tariff cap***) an electricity retailer may charge a residential customer for any electricity exceeding the reasonable use cap used by the customer in a free usage period on a day.

#### Division 3—Amendments made by the Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Electricity Retail) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2026

###### 23 Reasonable use tariff cap determinations

A determination made under subsection 18(4) that was in force immediately before the commencement of this section continues in force (and may be dealt with) on and after that commencement as if it had been made under subsection 18(4) as amended by the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Electricity Retail) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2026*.

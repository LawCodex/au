Contents

1\. National Competitive Grants Program: Explore processes

The National Competitive Grants Program (NCGP) is designed to achieve the Australian Government objectives for research and innovation

This grant opportunity is part of the NCGP which contributes to the ARC’s Outcome 1: growth of knowledge and innovation through managing research funding schemes, measuring research excellence and providing advice.

The ARC works with stakeholders to plan and design the grant program according to the *Commonwealth Grants Rules and Principles 2024 (CGRPs).*



The grant opportunity opens

We (the ARC) publish the grant opportunity guidelines and advertise on GrantConnect.



We enter into a grant agreement with You

To apply, We will enter into a Head Agreement with You through RMS.



You (the Administering Organisation) complete and submit a grant application

You will complete an application form on the ARC’s Research Management System (RMS) and address all of the eligibility and assessment criteria to be considered for a grant.



We manage the assessment of all applications

We manage the assessment of applications against eligibility criteria and assessment criteria.

Your application will be assigned to Expert Assessors to undertake in-depth assessments, which will be moderated and assigned a final score. Applications will be deemed highly competitive, competitive, or not competitive.

The ARC may consider additional information from internal sources and/or third parties, including other Commonwealth agencies, on a range of matters relevant to the assessment of Your application. Such information may include, but is not limited to, matters relating to research integrity and research security, as well as any other considerations relevant to the application, for the purpose of advising the decision maker.



We make grant recommendations

The ARC will create a list of applications recommended as suitable for funding. Highly competitive applications will be prioritised for funding. Where sufficient funding is available, competitive applications will be recommended for funding through a weighted randomisation process overseen by an independent probity advisor to ensure equitable outcomes across disciplines and alignment with ARC strategic goals and Government priorities.

The ARC Board reviews the final funding recommendations, taking into account the assessment criteria, compliance with eligibility requirements and the grant application process, alignment with Australian Government priorities, and other advice relevant to the application. The ARC Board may also consider advice from other Commonwealth agencies and make its own independent enquiries.

If the Minister directs the ARC Board to not fund a project for reasons relevant to security, defence or international relations of Australia, the Minister must notify the ARC Board, advise the Parliamentary Joint Committee on Intelligence and Security and table a statement in Parliament as required under the ARC Act. The ARC Board must comply with the Minister’s notice.



Grant decisions are made

The ARC Board decides which applications are to receive funding and duration of funding.



We notify You of the outcome

We advise You if Your application was successful or unsuccessful through RMS.



Assurance and compliance checks are completed before entering into a grant agreement

If You are successful, You will be required to complete additional assurance and compliance as per the grant opportunity guidelines before accepting the grant schedule.



Delivery of the grant

You undertake the grant activity as set out in Your grant agreement. We manage the grant, by working with you, monitoring Your progress and making payments.



Evaluation of the grant opportunity

We evaluate Your specific grant activity and the individual grant opportunity as a whole. We base this on information You provide to Us and additional information that we collect from various sources.

2\. About the National Competitive Grants Program

2.1 The ARC administers funding of excellent pure basic, strategic basic and applied research in all disciplines under the National Competitive Grants Program (NCGP), excluding medical research. The ARC does not fund experimental development.

2.2 The ARC considers the alignment of projects funded under the NCGP with Australian Government priorities, including the National Science and Research Priorities:

Priority 1: Transitioning to a net zero future - to develop and adopt next generation technologies, particularly in clean energy and storage, advanced materials, artificial intelligence, quantum and robotics

Priority 2: Supporting healthy and thriving communities - so more Australians can enjoy healthier lives from birth well into old age

Priority 3: Elevating Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander knowledge systems - to empower Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people particularly to have more of a say in advancing research that affects them

Priority 4: Protecting and restoring Australia’s environment - to mitigate the impacts of climate change and protect our biodiversity, diverse landscapes and ecosystems

Priority 5: Building a secure and resilient nation - to strengthen our democratic institutions while addressing economic, social, geopolitical, defence and national security challenges

2.3 ARC-funded research activities should advance, and not compromise, Australia's security, defence and international relations.

2.4 ARC-funded research activities should support our inclusive national identity built around our shared values including democracy, the rule of law, basic freedoms and the fair go.

About the Explore grant opportunity

The Explore grant opportunity is funded under the NCGP.

The Explore grant opportunity provides grant funding to support short-term, highly novel and potentially transformative research projects with the potential to move beyond existing paradigms of research – whether by creating new knowledge; addressing a significant problem; trialling innovative methodologies, interdisciplinary collaborations, or partnerships; or exploring a radically new approach or area of inquiry.

The intended outcome of the grant opportunity is to expand research capacity in Australia by supporting excellent researchers and teams to take greater risks in pursuit of knowledge.

Explore grants include salary funding specifically for eligible early-career Chief Investigators (CI) referred to as Salaried Chief Investigators (SCIs).

The Explore objectives are: Research Excellence: Identify and enable high-quality, innovative research spanning pure basic, strategic basic and applied research domains across all disciplines (excluding medical research and experimental development), with a focus on fostering ambitious projects that exhibit potential for high-risk, high-reward outcomes. Benefit: Support research that serves the national interest in diverse ways, including fundamental knowledge creation; commercial, cultural, economic, environmental, or social benefits; and/or alignment with ARC strategic goals and Australian government priority areas. Capacity: Strengthen Australia’s research capacity by fostering a skilled, inclusive and adaptable research workforce, supported by flexible career pathways that enable world-class research and impact for Australia. Collaboration: Support and promote mutually beneficial research collaboration between Australian higher education institutions and local or international partners, such as community organisations, government, industry, or international research institutions.

2.10 The grant commencement date and active project assessment date for each Explore grant opportunity will be available on the ARC website.

4\. Eligibility criteria

Who is eligible to apply for a grant?

4.1 We will only accept applications from Eligible Organisations listed in Appendix B, consistent with the objects under section 3 of the ARC Act. The Eligible Organisation that applies will be the ‘Administering Organisation’ (henceforth ‘You’).

4.2 To be eligible to apply for funding, You must have entered into a Head Agreement with the ARC.

What are the eligibility requirements for applications?

4.3 Organisation roles available under the Explore grant opportunity are:

Administering Organisation

Eligible Organisation

Partner Organisation

Other Organisation.

4.4 An application must include an Administering Organisation and may include Eligible Organisation(s), Partner Organisation(s) and/or Other Organisation(s).

4.5 You can only submit one application for the same project in any grant opportunity. This includes applications which fundamentally have the same research intent, approach, and outcomes, regardless of any variations in the research, the named participants and/or organisations.

4.6 Your application must:

nominate at least one CI or SCI candidate. The first named CI on the application will be the Project Leader.

request a minimum level of funding of $50,000 per year up to a maximum of $400,000 per year in project costs in Your application.

4.7 All participating organisations on an application must not be included on the National Redress Scheme’s website on list of ‘Institutions that have ’declined to join’ or have been ‘unresponsive to the Scheme’ (www.nationalredress.gov.au).

4.8 You and each Eligible Organisation on an application must contribute cash and/or in-kind and/or other material resources to the application having regard to the total cost of the proposed project and the relative contribution of any CIs or Partner Investigator(s) (PIs) at the organisation.

4.9 Your application must include a certification by Your Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Research) or their equivalent or delegate that You have obtained a commitment from any Partner Organisation(s) of their involvement and cash and/or in-kind contribution amounts as outlined in the application. Written confirmation of the contributions will need to be available at award and must at least match the amount included in the application form.

4.10 Your application must ensure that where an ICES is requested, supporting evidence of genuine engagement is uploaded for each applicable Partner Organisation or Other Organisation. Guidance on supporting evidence for Indigenous Community Engagement Supplement requests is outlined on the ARC website.

4.11 An individual must not be nominated for more than one salary in any grant opportunity.

4.12 You must ensure that any additional certification requirements applied by the ARC as a result of findings of breaches of *the Australian Code for the Responsible Conduct of Research* (2018 version), are provided by the grant opportunity closing date for any named participant on an application.

4.13 Your application must include a certification by Your Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Research) or equivalent in the Application that You have in accordance with the University responsibilities defined in the *ARC Research Security Framework* (2026 version) assessed research security risks in relation to the application, including in relation to participation of all named participants, and where risks are identified, have ensured appropriate mitigations are in place for the duration of the project.

4.14 Your application must not include medical research as detailed in the *ARC Medical Research Policy* (2025 version) on the ARC website.

Who is eligible to be a Partner Organisation?

4.15 Organisations that are not Eligible Organisations but that are named as participating organisations on an application and provide cash and/or in-kind contributions will be Partner Organisations.

4.16 Each Partner Organisation must:

participate in the project for the project activity period unless otherwise approved by Us; and

make a contribution of cash and/or in-kind and/or other material resources that is specific to the project, and having regard to the total cost of the project and not be part of a broader contribution to Your organisation or an Eligible Organisation.

4.17 A Partner Organisation cannot be:

an Eligible Organisation;

a controlled entity of any Eligible Organisation; or

an entity (for example a joint venture) where more than 50% is owned by one or more Eligible Organisations.

4.18 Partner Organisation cash contributions cannot be sourced from funds awarded or appropriated by the Commonwealth or an Australian State or Territory Governments for the purposes of research, nor from funds previously used to leverage government research or research infrastructure funding.

Who is eligible to be an Other Organisation?

4.19 Organisations that are not Eligible Organisations and not Partner Organisations but are named as participating organisation on an application will be Other Organisations. Other Organisations are not required to make a cash or in-kind contribution to the project but must be relevant to and involved with the project.

Who is eligible to be a named participant?

4.20 Roles that named participants may be nominated for under these guidelines are:

CIs;

SCI candidates; and

PIs.

4.21 A Project cannot commence until all named participants meet the eligibility criteria in these grant opportunity guidelines for the selected role they are to perform.

4.22 We will exclude an application as being ineligible if a named participant is the subject of a decision by the ARC’s CEO or delegate, that any application they make to the ARC will be excluded from consideration for a period of time, whether or not they otherwise meet the eligibility requirements, for research integrity reasons. Such decisions will generally reflect consequential action taken by the ARC in response to findings of a serious breach of the *Australian Code for the Responsible Conduct of Research* (2018 version) (including a finding of research misconduct, where this term is used). This is an ongoing criterion, including findings made during the application and assessment process.

4.23 All named participants in an application must have met their obligations regarding previously funded projects, including submission of satisfactory final reports to the ARC at the grant opportunity closing date and any relevant consequential actions that may have been applied under the *ARC Research Integrity and Other Misconduct Policy (2026 version)*.

4.24 All named participants must have an up‑to‑date RMS profile that includes an ORCID, and declarations relating to foreign affiliations, relationships and financial interests as outlined in the *ARC Research Security Framework* (2026 version). This ongoing obligation applies at each stage of the assessment process and throughout the duration of the project. If during the assessment process, We find that any named participant’s RMS profile does not include all required declarations relating to foreign affiliations, relationships and financial interests, We may recommend to the ARC Board that Your application not be considered further.

All CIs and SCIs in an application must not currently hold an Explore grant nor have been awarded an Explore grant within the past 3 years.

Chief Investigators

4.26 CIs must satisfy the following criteria:

as at the active project assessment date and for the duration of the project activity period the CI must stay within the ARC Research Points limit as set out in clauses 4.43 to 4.45; and

as at the grant commencement date, and, if successful, for the project activity period be either:

i. an employee for at least 0.2 FTE at an Eligible Organisation (unless they are also SCI candidates, see clause 4.32); or

ii. be a holder of an honorary academic appointment (as defined in the Glossary) at an Eligible Organisation.

4.27 The first-named CI will be the Project Leader.

4.28 CIs who hold an honorary appointment (as defined in the Glossary) at an Eligible Organisation are not eligible to be the Project Leader.

4.29 CIs must reside in Australia for more than 50% of the project activity period. Any significant absences including fieldwork or study leave directly related to the project must have approval from You and must not total more than half the project activity period. In extraordinary circumstances, a change may be approved by Us via a formal Variation request.

4.30 CIs must not undertake a Higher Degree by Research (HDR) during the project activity period.

SCI candidates

To be eligible for an SCI salary, the nominated individual must meet the eligibility criteria to apply as a CI (including clauses 4.26 to 4.30) and as at the grant opportunity closing date be an early career researcher (ECR) (between 0 and 5 years since PhD) by having: an award of PhD date on, or after the grant opportunity eligibility dates listed on the ARC website; or an award of PhD date together with an allowable period of career interruption that would be commensurate with an award of PhD date on or after the grant opportunity eligibility dates listed on the ARC website. More information on allowable career interruptions, and the period allowed for each, is available in the Instructions to Applicants and the ‘Eligibility and Career Interruptions Statement’ on the ARC website.

SCI candidates are not required to be employed at the grant opportunity closing date but must be employed at an Eligible Organisation (as a minimum) for the requested ARC Salary support component by the project start date.

SCI candidates must work a minimum of 80% of their allocated ARC Salary supported time on research activities related to the Project.

SCI candidates must not spend more than 20% of their allocated ARC Salary supported time on activities not related to the Project, such as teaching.

An individual who has previously been awarded an SCI salary may apply for another SCI role but can only hold one salary at a time.

An individual can only apply for one SCI salary in any Explore round.

SCI salaries cannot be held independently of an Explore Project.

Partner Investigators

As at the grant commencement date, or if successful, at any time during the project activity\
period, named participants who: meet the eligibility criteria to be a CI, cannot elect to be a PI; and do not meet the eligibility criteria to be a CI, must be a PI.

What are the limits on the number of applications and projects per named participant?

4.39 Individuals nominated in an application may be named on other ARC grant opportunity applications or active projects.

The limits in clauses 4.41 to 4.45 only apply to CIs and SCI candidates.

A CI and SCI can be concurrently funded through the NCGP for up to the ARC Research Points limit. ARC Research Points for CI and SCI participants on Explore projects are outlined on the ARC website.

NCGP legacy schemes will not be counted in this limit.

A CI and SCI can only be named on a maximum of 2 applications per Explore grant opportunity round as a CI. Of these applications, a named participant can only be named on one application as a Project Leader. See also clause 4.35.

At the grant opportunity closing date for Explore applications, We will count a CI’s and SCI’s ARC Research Points including: their ARC Research Points on active projects across the NCGP on the grant opportunity’s active project assessment date; their ARC Research Points on completed projects with outstanding Final Reports where the Final Report is overdue and has not been lodged by the application submission deadline; and their proposed ARC Research Points on applications submitted, under assessment or awaiting acceptance of grant schedule on the grant opportunity’s active project assessment date.

Roles on projects cannot be relinquished to meet application ARC Research Points limits. If any named participant on a project exceeds the ARC Research Points, the project will be ineligible.

It is Your responsibility to determine if applying for, or holding, a project under these grant opportunity guidelines will affect an individual researcher’s eligibility for other ARC grant opportunities as other ARC grant opportunities may have different ARC Research Points limits. We reserve the right to change ARC Research Points, project and application limits in future grant opportunities.

Security, Defence or International Relations of Australia

4.47 Pursuant to the ARC Act, the Minister may at any time give a notice stating that, for reasons relevant to the security, defence or international relations of Australia, the ARC Board should not approve a grant. The ARC Board must comply with any such notice from the Minister. Accordingly, if the Minister issues such a notice in respect of an application, that application will be ineligible to receive funding.

5\. What the grant money can be used for

Eligible grant activities

5.1 The National Competitive Grants Program supports research activities that meet the definition of ‘research’ as defined in the Glossary.

5.2 You can only request and spend the grant on eligible expenditure items that directly support the project and in accordance with any additional conditions or special conditions in the grant agreement.

5.3 Eligible expenditure items may include:

salary and 30% on-costs for the SCI recipient;

salary support for other personnel, for example, research associates and assistants, technicians and laboratory attendants at an appropriate salary level, including 30% on-costs, at the employing organisation;

teaching relief for CIs up to a total of $50,000 per CI per year per project (except where a CI has been awarded an SCI);

equipment (and its maintenance) and consumables, including specialised computer equipment and software essential to the project. Funding will not be provided for equipment or consumables that are considered to be for broad general use;

travel costs essential to the project up to $50,000 over the project activity period. The following travel costs are not included in this $50,000 limit: expenditure on field research essential to the project, including technical and logistical support, travel expenses (including travel visas for the purpose of field research in foreign countries, accommodation, meals and incidental costs); and reasonable essential costs to allow a participant who is a carer, or who personally requires care or assistance, to undertake travel essential to the project.

Indigenous Community Engagement Supplement (ICES) up to a total of $25,000. This includes the following costs: travel relevant expertise repatriation costs equipment and consumables.

5.4 You must incur the expenditure on your project during the project activity period for it to be eligible.

Ineligible grant activities

5.5 You cannot request or use grant funds for:

research activities, infrastructure or projects previously funded or currently being funded through any other Commonwealth grant;

medical research as detailed in the ARC *Medical Research Policy* (2025 version) on the ARC website;

experimental development, as defined in the Glossary;

activities leading solely to the creation or performance of a work of art, including visual art, musical compositions, drama, dance, film, broadcasts, podcasts, designs and literary works, unless those works are directly related to the project’s research activities and demonstrably research based;

contracted research or consultancy arrangements where one or more Organisation(s) is seeking expert external assistance, not available within their own organisation, to develop specific applications or outputs such as grey literature that involve little innovation or are low risk;

production of computer programs, research aids and tools; data warehouses, catalogues or bibliographies, or teaching materials, unless these meet the definition of ‘research’;

basic facilities that must be provided (where relevant) and funded by You and are not funded by the grant: bench fees or similar laboratory access fees; access to a basic library collection; access to film or music editing facilities; work accommodation (for example, laboratory and office space, suitably equipped and furnished); basic computer facilities such as desktop computers, portable computer devices, printers, word processing, and other standard software; and standard reference materials or funds for abstracting services;

capital works and general infrastructure costs;

costs not directly related to the project, including but not limited to professional membership fees, professional development courses, fees for patent application and maintenance, equipment for live music or drama performances, equipment for gallery and museum exhibitions, visas to work in Australia, relocation costs, entertainment costs, purchase of alcohol, insurance, mobile phones (purchase or call charges) and other indirect costs;

fees for international students or the Higher Education Contribution Scheme (HECS) and Higher Education Loan Program (HELP) liabilities for students;

salaries and/or on-costs, in whole or in part, for CIs (except for those applying for SCI salaries) or PIs;

HDR stipends for CIs, SCI recipients and PIs, in whole or in part; and

salary top ups for personnel above the salary level specified in these grant opportunity guidelines or published on the ‘Salaries and Stipends’ page of the ARC website (any top-up salary or stipend support required in order to comply with institutional levels may be provided by funds other than ARC funds).

Risk tolerance statement

6.3 The ARC has a high-risk appetite for projects funded through the Explore grant opportunity. Projects where risks arise from the untested or paradigm-shifting nature of the proposed research, which create uncertainty about the potential ‘success’ of the project are welcomed. The Explore grant opportunity has moderate tolerance for risks related to investigators exploring new fields, early career researchers (ECR) leading projects, or trialling new/risky methodologies. However, there is low tolerance in the grant opportunity for risks relating to poor research design or execution, or personnel-related risks such as unethical conduct or failure to protect health/safety.

All NCGP schemes have no appetite for compliance-related risks, such as non-compliance with financial, integrity, security and reporting obligations. For more information about Explore risk tolerance see Appendix C.

7\. How to apply

Application process

7.1 Instructions To Applicants will be made available on GrantConnect on the opening of the grant opportunity and applicants must follow the processes described in those Instructions, including regarding attachments to the application.

7.2 Your application must be completed in RMS. We will not accept applications received after the closing time, other than in exceptional circumstances (such as due to natural disasters) in which case We will discuss this with You, and if We agree, invite You to make a late application.

7.3 You cannot change Your application after the closing date and time, unless invited to by the ARC.

7.4 We reserve the right to seek evidence to support the certification of applications at any point.

7.5 Questions during the application period should be directed to the Administering Organisation’s Research Office. Answers to Frequently Asked Questions will be posted on GrantConnect.

7.6 If You wish to withdraw Your application, You must inform Us in writing.

Your responsibilities

7.7 You must ensure that any SCI is employed by You or at an Eligible Organisation for the project activity period.

7.8 You or if not employed by You, the employing organisation are responsible for managing changes to the SCI’s working hours.

7.9 You must ensure the SCI recipient has access to the following leave entitlements in line with Your normal practice:

parental leave;

recreation leave;

sick leave; and

additional leave of up to twelve months using accrued leave or leave without pay.

7.10 You are responsible for ensuring that Your application is complete and accurate, including the content of declarations by named participants. Giving false or misleading information is a serious offence under the *Criminal Code Act 1995* (Cth). We will investigate any incomplete, inaccurate, false or misleading information and may recommend to the ARC Board that Your application not be considered further.

7.11 For the avoidance of doubt, this includes failure to disclose:

any conflict of interest of any named participant, including but not limited to matters described in sections 3.1 and 3.3 of the *ARC Conflict of Interest and Confidentiality Policy* (2025 version) on the ARC website;

8\. The grant selection process

Assessment of grant applications

8.1 We will review Your application against the eligibility criteria. If ineligible, We must not recommend the application for funding.

8.2 All applications will be considered through a competitive peer review process, based on:

how well it meets the weighted assessment criteria;

how it is ranked against other applications; and

whether it provides value with money (as defined in the Glossary).

8.3 Based on the peer review assessments, applications will be categorised as highly competitive, competitive and not competitive. Applications assessed as highly competitive will be prioritised for funding. Applications assessed as competitive may be recommended for funding where sufficient funding is available. Competitive applications recommended for funding will be selected through a weighted randomisation process to ensure equitable outcomes across disciplines, aligned with ARC strategic goals and Government priorities. This process will be overseen by an independent probity advisor.

8.4 We may seek advice and information on security or other matters from Commonwealth agencies and third-party sources at any time during the process. We may consult with You about this information.

8.5 During the assessment, We may request additional information, which does not change the nature of Your application.

Due diligence

8.6 Due diligence activity may be undertaken at eligibility, assessment or decision-making stages. The ARC may consider the outcomes of due diligence activity and provide advice to the ARC Board and/or Minister.

8.7 We may seek additional information about you, project partners, related entities and associated entities, and related personnel from third party sources, including other Commonwealth agencies, for due diligence purposes. The ARC may also consider information that is discovered through the normal course of business. See clauses 13.14 to 13.22 (Privacy) for information on how we use the information provided to us.

8.8 If the ARC identifies risks which would affect the assessment, it may ask You to respond to these, subject to security considerations. If the ARC identifies risks that cannot be adequately mitigated, it may recommend that the ARC Board exclude Your application from further consideration or the Minister notify the Board that it should refuse your application under section 47(8) of the ARC Act.

8.9 The ARC Board and Minister may approve applications pending specific conditions based on the advice from the ARC.

Research integrity

8.10 The ARC may consider the relevance of any Research Integrity Event to the proposed research outlined in the application.

8.11 We may undertake actions, and exercise any rights, expressed as being able to be undertaken or exercised by the ARC under the *ARC Research Integrity and Other Misconduct Policy* (2026 version) (including precautionary and consequential actions) to address or mitigate any potential or proven risks regarding any named participant on Your application.

Research security

8.12 The ARC may provide advice to the Minister for Education on whether the applicant or any named participant has affiliations, relationships or financial interests with any foreign entities which may be contrary to Australia’s sovereignty, values and national interests.

8.13 In order to protect against risks of foreign interference and other issues relevant to the security, defence or international relations of Australia, We may share relevant outcomes of due diligence with other Commonwealth agencies for the purpose of the other agencies’ grant-related purposes or countering foreign interference purposes in relation to specified individuals, entities or projects. This may include information in Your application, and personal information of named participants in RMS.

Who will approve grants

8.14 The ARC Board will decide which applications to fund, after considering the advice from peer review, any advice of the ARC, and alignment with ARC strategic goals and Australian Government priorities. The ARC Board’s decision is final in all matters.

8.15 The Minister may at any time decide that, for reasons relevant to the security, defence or international relations of Australia, a grant should not be approved. The ARC Board must comply with the Minister’s decision where the ARC Board decides which grants to fund.

## Schedule 1—General Funding Strategy 2026/27 – 2028/29

###### 1 INTRODUCTION

###### 1.1 About ARENA

The Australian Renewable Energy Agency (ARENA) was established on 1 July 2012 by the *Australian Renewable Energy Agency Act 2011* (Cth) (ARENA Act). ARENA is a corporate Commonwealth entity for the purposes of the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013* (Cth) (PGPA Act).

The ARENA Act is supported by the *Australian Renewable Energy Agency Regulations 2025*, which provide that ARENA must additionally assist the Clean Energy Finance Corporation in the administration of its Clean Energy Innovation Fund, and which specify additional amounts payable to ARENA under subsection 64(2) of the ARENA Act.

Our **vision** is a prosperous Australia thriving in a net zero world.

Our **mission** is to support global emissions reduction by accelerating innovation and the deployment of renewable energy, electrification and energy efficiency technologies, to the benefit of all Australians.

Our **purpose** is to support improvements in the competitiveness and supply of renewable energy and the uptake of energy efficiency and electrification by providing financial assistance and sharing knowledge, to accelerate innovation that benefits all Australians, and facilitates the achievement of Australia’s greenhouse gas emissions targets.

Through our role in improving the competitiveness and increasing the supply of renewable energy in Australia, ARENA is helping to achieve the Government’s climate change and energy objectives, anchored by emissions reduction goals of 43% by 2030, 62–70% by 2035 and net zero by 2050.

We acknowledge that Australia’s path towards net zero traverses the lands and waters of First Nations peoples. In carrying out our work, ARENA supports the Government’s commitment to meaningful engagement by:

incorporating engagement and benefit-sharing requirements into our program guidelines and funding agreements

engaging First Nations members and advisers to our Advisory Panel, and First Nations advisers to continually enhance our internal cultural capability

implementing our Reflect Reconciliation Action Plan.

###### 1.2 About the General Funding Strategy

The General Funding Strategy (GFS) guides how ARENA provides financial assistance in accordance with the ARENA Act, associated regulations and the PGPA Act. ARENA is required to develop a GFS each financial year that sets out our principal objectives and priorities for the provision of financial assistance over the current and upcoming two financial years.

When developing the GFS, ARENA must have regard to the most recent Statement of Expectations (SoE) issued jointly by the Minister for Energy and Climate Change and the Minister for Finance. In response to the most recent SoE, issued in 2025, ARENA has committed to align with government expectations to support renewable energy development, industry growth, electrification (including community electrification), and First Nations and community benefits through programs and collaborations, as well as to undertake work to understand the needs of the emerging offshore wind industry.

The GFS is provided to the Minister for Climate Change and Energy for approval and ceases to be in force when the GFS for the next financial year comes into force. ARENA may not enter into an agreement to provide financial assistance unless the assistance is in accordance with the GFS in force at the relevant time. It is the ARENA Board’s responsibility to ensure the GFS is published on ARENA’s website.

###### 2 PRINCIPAL OBJECTIVES AND PRIORITIES FOR ARENA FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

ARENA provides financial assistance to Australian researchers, innovators and businesses for projects that align with our principal objectives – described as our strategic priorities – or the objectives of the budget measure programs that ARENA is responsible for delivering.

***ARENA’s strategic priorities***

For the period of this GFS (2026–27 to 2028–29), ARENA’s strategic priorities are to:

Unlock ultra low-cost solar

Optimise the transition to renewable electricity

Advance hydrogen for industrial decarbonisation

Support the transition to low emissions metals

Decarbonise transport.

These priorities reflect our mission to support global emissions reduction by:

Lowering the cost and increasing the availability of renewable energy

Growing the share of renewables in the electricity mix

Supporting fuel switching, energy efficiency and electrification

Increasing flexibility in how and when we use electricity

Driving the development of technology solutions for hard-to-abate sectors, where reducing emissions is costly or currently not feasible.

###### 2.1 Guiding principles

Our strategic priorities address the key challenges we aim to solve through the provision of financial assistance and knowledge sharing. We use the following guiding principles to set our strategic priorities.

***Maximum impact***

ARENA strives to achieve maximum impact and value in its strategic priorities (via the projects we fund) through:

1\. **High impact**: each priority individually contributes to a significant part of ARENA’s mission.

2\. **Current relevance and additionality**: market pull or need for ARENA support, minimal overlap with other grant-funding efforts in the ecosystem, and potential for acceleration in Australia.

3\. **Long-term viability**: technology pathways have the potential to lead to long-term decarbonisation and have a route to commercial viability.

***Responsive***

ARENA is responsive to market conditions, technology developments and industry needs. We regularly review our strategic priorities to ensure our financial assistance will have the highest impact. We aim to be agile, responding swiftly to take advantage of opportunities that could benefit Australia.

***Diversity***

Given the uncertainty and risk inherent in researching, developing and commercialising new solutions, ARENA seeks diversity in its portfolio when providing financial assistance to ensure it is future-proofed and resilient in different policy and economic scenarios.

Accordingly, we strive to ensure our overall portfolio includes activities at different stages of development on the commercialisation pathway including:

research into, and development of, early-stage technologies that are world-leading or address specific Australian requirements

demonstration of the feasibility of new technologies or supporting business models that are novel in application, including novel ownership structures for projects with clear benefits for First Nations peoples

pre-commercial deployment of technologies where this is expected to improve the competitiveness of technology pathways.

***Commercial focus***

ARENA’s financial assistance is commercially focused and provided to advance technologies or business models towards commercial readiness. We seek to provide the minimum financial assistance needed to achieve an outcome, and in the most appropriate form, maximising efficiency and value for public investment.

ARENA provides financial assistance in the form of grants. Where there is the possibility of upside from a project in the future, ARENA may negotiate a recoupment mechanism that sees some or all grant funding returned should the upside scenario eventuate. In so doing, ARENA ensures that funding continues to represent value for the Australian taxpayer.

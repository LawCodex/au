## Schedule 6—Amendments to Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 113/00 – Acoustic Vehicle Alerting Systems for Quiet Road Transport Vehicles) 2024

**Section 3 (Applicability)**

Clause 3.2:

*Omit* “3.2.1”, *substitute* “3.1.2”.

**Section 6 (Exemptions and Alternative Procedures**

Subclause 6.5.2:

After requirements, *insert* “of”.

After clause 6.5.2, *insert*

“6.6. For the measurement of sound emitted by vehicles in motion, testing may be performed on a test site that meets the specifications of clause 6.6.1 in lieu of the specifications in paragraph 2.1.2 of Annex 3 to Appendix A:

6.6.1 The test site shall take the form of an open space of 25m radius having a central part of at least 10m radius which is practically level, consisting of concrete, asphalt or similar material and not covered with powdery snow, tall grass, loose soil, ashes or the like. The test site surface shall be dry and such as not to cause excessive tyre noise. Whilst the test is in progress no person shall be in the measurement area except the observer and the driver. Their presence must have no influence on the meter reading.”

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Reserve Bank Act 1959*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Reserve Bank Act 1959*.

### Part 2—Secrecy

###### 6 Declarations of secrecy

For the purposes of paragraphs 25AJ(3)(a), 25E(3)(a) and 25NJ(3)(a) of the Act, the prescribed form for a declaration of secrecy by a member of the Monetary Policy Board, Payments System Board or Governance Board is the form in Schedule 1 to this instrument.

###### 7 Prescribed bodies—disclosure of protected information or production of protected documents

For the purposes of paragraph 79A(4)(c) of the Act, the following bodies are prescribed:

(a) the Department;

(b) the Department administered by the Minister who administers the *Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018*;

(c) the Australian Bureau of Statistics;

(d) the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission;

(e) the Australian Office of Financial Management;

(f) the Australian Signals Directorate;

(g) the Bank for International Settlements;

(h) the following bodies operating under the Bank for International Settlements:

(i) the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision;

(ii) the Committee on the Global Financial System;

(iii) the Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures;

(iv) the Markets Committee;

(i) any body (however described) operating under a body mentioned in subparagraph (h)(i), (ii), (iii) or (iv);

(j) the Financial Stability Board and any body (however described) operating under the Board’s Charter;

(k) the International Monetary Fund;

(l) The Treasury (New Zealand).

### Part 3—Application and transitional provisions

###### 8 Application—disclosure of protected information or production of protected documents

Section 7 applies in relation to information disclosed or obtained or a document given or produced before, on or after the commencement of this instrument.

## Schedule 1—Form for declaration of secrecy by Board members

Note: See section 6.

*Reserve Bank Act 1959*

I, [*name*] of [*address*], a member of one or more of the Bank’s Boards, solemnly and sincerely declare that I will at all times maintain secrecy in relation to the affairs of the Bank’s Boards and of the Bank and, in particular, that I will not directly or indirectly communicate or divulge any information that comes to my knowledge in the performance of my functions or duties, or the exercise of my powers, as a member of a Board of the Bank, except by authority of that Board or under compulsion or obligation of law.

[*Signature of declarant*]

Declared at [*place*] on [*date*].

Before me,

Justice of the Peace.

[*or* Commissioner for taking Affidavits.]

# Customs (Place for the Examination of International Mail—Norfolk Island) Approval 2026

LIN 26/076

I, Gavan Reynolds AO, Comptroller-General of Customs, make the following approval.

Dated 21 September 2026

Gavan Reynolds AO

Comptroller-General of Customs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Customs (Place for the Examination of International Mail—Norfolk Island) Approval 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 183UA(2) of the *Customs Act 1901 (N.I.).*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Customs Act 1901* (NI) as applied in Norfolk Island.

***Customs place*** has the same meaning as in subsection 183UA(1) of the Act.

Note: The *Customs Act 1901* (NI) is the *Customs Act 1901* as modified by the *Norfolk Island Customs Ordinance 2016*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Approval of Customs Place

For the purposes of paragraph 183UA(1)(f) of the Act, the Norfolk Island Delivery Annex located on 47 Taylors Rd, Norfolk Island, New South Wales, with the postcode of 2899, is an approved customs place for the examination of international mail.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Approval of Place for the Examination of International Mail No.1 of 2016

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

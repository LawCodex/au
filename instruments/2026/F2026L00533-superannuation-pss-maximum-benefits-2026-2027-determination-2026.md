# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2026-2027) Determination 2026

**Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2026-2027) Determination 2026**

I, ADAM NETTHEIM, Chief Customer Officer, and Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 07 May 2026

A NETTHEIM

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

1 **Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2026-2027) Determination 2026*.

2 **Commencement**

This determination takes effect on 1 July 2026.

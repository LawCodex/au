# Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Rules 2026

I, Matthew James Keogh, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following rules under section 63 of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019*.

Dated 2 June 2026

Matthew James Keogh

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Rules 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 63 of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Commission;

(b) eligible person;

(c) treatment.

***Act*** means the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019.*

***attendant*** means a person who is authorised by the Commission under subsection 22(2) of the *Act* to accompany an eligible person as the eligible person’s attendant.

***residence***, for a person at a time, means the permanent or temporary place of residence of that person at that time.

***travel***, for an eligible person, means travel, referred to in section 22 of the Act, from the person’s residence to the person’s destination and return.

###### 5 Prescribed travelling expenses for eligible persons

For the purposes of subsection 22(1) of the Act, the following are prescribed travelling expenses in relation to an eligible person:

(a) costs in connection with travel by the eligible person for the purpose of the eligible person obtaining treatment;

(b) costs of accommodation in connection with travel by the eligible person for the purpose of the eligible person obtaining treatment.

###### 6 Conditions for payment of an eligible person’s travelling expenses

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 22(1)(c) of the Act, this section prescribes conditions that must be satisfied for an eligible person to be entitled to be paid travelling expenses in connection with travel for the purpose of obtaining treatment.

Costs of an eligible person’s travel

(2) The Commonwealth is liable to pay travelling expenses to the eligible person for any costs reasonably incurred for travel if:

(a) the costs are incurred in respect of travel undertaken by the person that is necessary for the person to obtain treatment for an injury or disease of the person; and

(b) any one or more of the following applies:

(i) if the travel is by ambulance services—the person’s injury or disease reasonably requires using those services;

(ii) the travel is by public transport;

(iii) if the travel is by means other than ambulance services or public transport—it is unreasonable for the person to use public transport having regard to the nature of the person’s injury or disease, or public transport is unavailable.

Costs of an eligible person’s accommodation

(3) The Commonwealth is liable to pay travelling expenses for accommodation to an eligible person for any costs reasonably incurred if:

(a) the person undertakes travel that is necessary for the person to obtain treatment for an injury or disease of the person; and

(b) it is necessary for the person to remain at the place to which the travel was undertaken to obtain the treatment; and

(c) the costs are incurred in respect of the person’s accommodation at that place.

###### 7 Prescribed travelling expenses for an eligible person’s attendant

For the purposes of subsection 22(2) of the Act, the following costs are prescribed travelling expenses in relation to an eligible person attendant:

(a) costs in connection with travel by the attendant for the purpose of the eligible person obtaining treatment;

(b) costs of accommodation in connection with travel by the attendant for the purpose of the eligible person obtaining treatment.

###### 8 Conditions for payment of an attendant’s travelling expenses

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 22(2)(c) of the Act, this section prescribes conditions that must be satisfied for an attendant to be entitled to be paid travelling expenses in connection with travel for the purpose of accompanying an eligible person.

Costs of an attendant’s travel

(2) The Commonwealth is liable to pay travelling expenses to the attendant for any costs reasonably incurred if:

(b) the costs are incurred in respect of the attendant’s travel for the purpose of accompanying an eligible person; and

(c) any one or more of the following applies:

(i) if the travel is by ambulance services—the eligible person’s injury or disease reasonably requires using those services;

(ii) the travel is by public transport;

(iii) if the travel is by means other than ambulance services or public transport—it is unreasonable for the eligible person to use public transport having regard to the nature of the eligible person’s injury or disease, or public transport is unavailable.

Costs of an attendant’s accommodation

(3) The Commonwealth is liable to pay travelling expenses for accommodation to an attendant for any costs reasonably incurred if:

(a) the Commission approves the attendant to accompany an eligible person for travel that is necessary for the eligible person to obtain treatment for an injury or disease; and

(b) it is necessary for the eligible person and the attendant to remain at the place to which the travel was undertaken to obtain that treatment; and

(c) the costs are incurred in respect of the attendant’s accommodation at that place.

###### 9 Amount of travelling expenses for travel

(1) The amount of travelling expenses that the Commonwealth is liable to pay under subsections 6(2) and 8(2) for travel is the amount determined by the Commission to be the amount reasonably incurred in respect of the travel.

(2) In determining an amount under subsection (1), the Commission may determine the amount of travelling expenses for the travel (the ***journey***) using the following formula:

The length of the journey in kilometres multiplied by the specified rate per kilometre,

where:

the ***length of the journey in kilometres*** is the number of whole kilometres in the journey; and

the ***specified rate per kilometre*** is the rate per kilometre specified in an instrument under subsection 16(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (as that instrument is in force from time to time).

Note: For example, the Commission may use this formula if a person used a private vehicle to make a journey, but not if a person flew on a commercial airline.

###### 10 Amount of payment for accommodation

The amount of travelling expenses that the Commonwealth is liable to pay under subsections 6(3) and 8(3) for accommodation is the amount reasonably incurred in respect of the accommodation.

###### 11 Matters to be considered in determining claims for travel and accommodation

In determining claims made under section 24 of the Act, the Commission must have regard to the following matters:

(a) the places where appropriate treatment was available to the eligible person;

(b) the means of transport available to the eligible person for travel to those places for the treatment;

(c) the means of transport appropriate for the eligible person to take for the travel;

(d) the routes by which the eligible person could have travelled;

(e) the accommodation available to the eligible person at the place to which the travel was undertaken;

(f) any other relevant matters.

###### 12 Transitional arrangements in relation to the commencement of this instrument

(1) This instrument applies to travelling expenses claimed in relation to travel undertaken on or after 1 July 2026.

(2) Despite the repeal of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Rules 2019* by section 13 of this instrument, those Rules, as in force immediately before 1 July 2026, continue to apply on and after that date in relation to travelling expenses for travel that starts before that date, whether or not a claim for those travelling expenses is made before or after 1 July 2026.

**13 Repeals**

The *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Rules 2019* are repealed.

### Part 2—Payment dates

###### 6 Prescribed Dates for Payment – paragraph 6(1)(c) of the Act

For the purpose of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***prescribed date for payment*** in relation to levy payable for leviable disposals taking place in a financial year specified in column 1 of an item in the Table, a reference to 31 December following the end of that financial year is instead taken to be a reference to the date specified in column 2 of that item.

###### 7 Payment by Instalments – paragraph 6(1)(d) of the Act

This section applies to all amounts of levy payable for leviable disposals taking place in a financial year specified in column 1 of an item in the Table.

For the purposes of the Act, any amount of levy that, pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of the ***prescribed date for payment*** and section 6 of this instrument, would be required to be paid by the date specified in column 2 of an item in the Table is taken to have been paid by that date if the levy is paid by instalments worked out as follows: 50% of the levy payable is paid on or before the date specified in column 2 of that item; and the balance of the levy payable is paid on or before the date specified in column 3 of that item.

### Part 3—Application, saving and transitional provisions

###### 9 Saving provision

(1) The *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemical Products (Collection of Levy)(Payment Dates) Direction 2025*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this instrument, continues to apply on and after that commencement in relation to amounts of levy payable for leviable disposals that took place in the 2024/2025 financial year.

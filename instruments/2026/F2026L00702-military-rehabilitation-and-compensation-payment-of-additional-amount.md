**Part 1—Preliminary**

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Payment of Additional Amount) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 80A(3) of the Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901.*

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in subsection 5(1) of the Act, including the following:

child;

Commission;

eligible young person.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***care***, in respect of an eligible young person or child on the date determined under subsection 80(2) of the Act, means either of the following:

unpaid physical care that is provided by a person on an ongoing regular basis; or

Note: Any form of paid physical care, or unpaid physical care that is irregular and occasional, may not be regarded as constituting care for the purposes of this definition.

economic support provided under sole or shared care arrangements.

Note: Economic support includes payments made under family law or court orders.

***impaired person*** means the impaired person who is referenced in subsection 80(1) of the Act.

### Part 2—Criteria for payment of additional amount

###### 5 Persons to whom additional amount is to be paid

The additional amount payable under section 80 of the Act in respect of an eligible young person or child of the impaired person may be paid to any of the following persons:

the impaired person; or

another person who provides care in respect of the eligible young person or child.

**6 Sole carer**

Where the eligible young person or child does not have another person providing care to them, the amount payable under section 80 of the Act is to be paid to the impaired person in full.

**7 Shared care arrangements**

Where care in respect of the eligible young person or child is shared between the impaired person and another person or persons, each person is to be paid a percentage of the amount payable under section 80 of the Act as follows:

50 percent to the impaired person; and

50 percent to the other person or persons.

Where there are two or more other persons who provide care to the eligible young person or child, the 50 percent payment payable under paragraph 7(1)(b) is to be divided equally among those persons.

Despite subsection (1), where shared care arrangements are specified or recorded in writing, including pursuant to a family law agreement or court order, the payment is to be apportioned in accordance with those written care arrangements.

Note: If a written care arrangement is in place, the terms of that arrangement are intended to override the standard position that there be an even split of the amount between the impaired person and any other persons who provide care to an eligible young person or child.

**8 Payment upon death of person**

A person to whom an additional amount is payable under section 80 of the Act is to receive the payment despite the death of the person.

Where:

an additional amount is payable under section 80 of the Act to a person; and

the person has died,

the amount payable is to be paid to the person’s estate.

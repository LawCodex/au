###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 47H(5) of the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000.*

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

default;

registered provider.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***PRISMS*** means the computer system established by the Secretary under section 109 of the Act.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Requirements for a notice on student default

For the purposes of section 47H of the Act, the provider must enter the following information into PRISMS, for each overseas student or intending overseas student that defaults in relation to a course provided by the provider at a location and for whom the provider is required to provide a refund under section 47E:

(a) if the provider has not provided a refund in accordance with section 47E of the Act — the reason why it has not done so;

(b) if the provider has provided a refund in accordance with section 47E of the Act:

(i) the date that the refund was paid by the provider to the student; and

(ii) confirmation that the refund amount was calculated in accordance with subsection 47E(4) of the Act; and

(iii) the date when the student default occurred.

Note: The notice must also include the information required by subsection 47H(3) of the Act.

# ASIC Corporations (Repeal) Instrument 2026/329

ASIC Corporations (Repeal) Instrument 2026/329

I, Leah Sciacca, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 11 September 2026

Leah Sciacca

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Repeal) Instrument 2026/329*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under:

(a) subsection 11(4) of the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001* for the purposes of subparagraphs 912A(2)(a)(i) and 1017G(2)(a)(i) of the *Corporations Act 2001*; and

(b) subsection 12A(6) of the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001* for the purposes of:

(i) paragraph 47(1)(b) of the *Retirement Savings Accounts Act 1997*; and

(ii) paragraph 101(1)(b) of the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*; and

(iii) subparagraph 47(1)(h)(i) of the *National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009*; and

(iv) subparagraph 47(1)(e)(i) of the *National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009* as notionally inserted by item 2.17 of Schedule 2 to the *National Consumer Credit Protection Regulations 2010*; and

(c) subsections 341(1), 601QA(1), 926A(2), 992B(1) and 1280A(4) of the *Corporations Act 2001*; and

(d) paragraphs 283GA(1)(a), 601QA(1)(a) and (b), 741(1)(a), 911A(2)(l), 926A(2)(a) and (c), 992B(1)(a) and (c) and 1020F(1)(a) of the *Corporations Act 2001*; and

(e) subsection 109(3) of the *National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009*; and

(f) item 41 of Schedule 2 to the *National Consumer Credit Protection (Transitional and Consequential Provisions) Act 2009*; and

(g) subrule 1.2.1(3) of the *ASIC Market Integrity Rules (Securities Markets) 2017*.

4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

5 Repeal of amending and repealing instruments

(1) The repeal of an instrument by section 4 does not affect any amendment to or repeal of another instrument (however described) made by the instrument.

(2) Subsection (1) does not limit the effect of section 7 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* as it applies to the repeal of an instrument by section 4 of this instrument.

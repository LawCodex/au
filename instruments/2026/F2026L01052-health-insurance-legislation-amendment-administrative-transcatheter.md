## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services –Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation) Determination 2018

**1 Subsection 5(1) (definition of TAVI Practitioner)**

Omit “Cardiac Accreditation Services Limited”, substitute “Australian Transcatheter Valve Therapies Accreditation Committee”.

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services—Transcatheter Mitral Valve Repair) Determination 2021

**2 Section 4 (definition of TMVr accreditation committee)**

Omit “committee of that name administered by Cardiac Accredited Services Limited”, substitute “Australian Transcatheter Valve Therapies Accreditation Committee”.

# Migration (Arrangements for Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) Visa) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/048

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 29 June 2026

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Arrangements for Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) Visa) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences immediately after the commencement of the *Migration Amendment (Working Holiday Maker Age Criteria) Regulations 2026*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***AEDST*** means Australian Eastern Daylight Saving Time.

***AEST*** means Australian Eastern Standard Time.

***Internet application*** has the meaning given by regulation 1.03 of the Regulations.

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994.*

***written notice*** means an email from the Department:

(a) notifying a person that they can make a visa application using an approved form that is specified in subsection 6(1) in a manner other than as an internet application; and

(b) including instructions about how to provide the form to the Department.

###### 6 Form, place and manner for making an application

(1) For the purposes of subitem 1225(1) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, the following approved forms are specified:

(a) Form 1150 (Internet);

(b) Form 1150.

(2) For the purposes of subitem 1225(3) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, in relation to the class of persons specified under section 7, the manner in which an application must be made is:

(a) using Form 1150 (Internet) as an internet application; or

(b) if a written notice has been given to the applicant:

(i) using Form 1150; and

(ii) attaching a copy of the written notice to the application; and

(iii) submitting the application and written notice by email to WHM.invited.applications@homeaffairs.gov.au within 7 calendar days after the day on which the written notice was sent, calculated using AEST or AEDST as applicable on that day.

###### 7 Class of persons for Working Holiday visa applications

For the purposes of subitem 1225(3) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, the specified class of persons is:

(a) an applicant who holds a valid passport that:

(i) was issued by a country or region mentioned in column 1 of an item of the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument; and

(ii) satisfies the conditions (if any) mentioned in column 2 of the same table item; or

(b) an applicant who:

(i) is in Australia; and

(ii) when the applicant most recently entered Australia—held a passport that:

(A) was issued by a country or region mentioned in column 1 of an item of the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument; and

(B) satisfied the conditions (if any) mentioned in column 2 of the same table item; and

(iii) since the most recent entry, no longer holds that passport as a valid passport.

###### 8 Specified kinds of working holiday eligible passport

For the purposes of subclause 417.211(2) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the specified kind of working holiday eligible passport is a passport that:

(a) was issued by a country or region specified in column 1 of an item of the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument; and

(b) is held by an applicant who is in a class of persons specified in section 7.

Note 1: A passport is not a working holiday eligible passport if it is not issued by a country or region mentioned in an item of Schedule 1 to the instrument.

Note 2: Subclause 417.211(1A) provides for certain circumstances in which an applicant who:

(a) is in Australia; and

(b) entered Australia as the holder of a working holiday eligible passport that has since expired;

is not required to hold a working holiday eligible passport of a kind specified for subclause 417.211(2) at time of application under Schedule 2 to the Regulations.

Note 3: For Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the definition of ***working holiday eligible passport*** is provided in clause 417.111.

###### 9 Age

For the purposes of paragraph 1225(3AA)(b) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations, the younger age (if any) is the age, expressed in years, specified in column 3 of an item of the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument, in relation to the country or region in column 1 of the same table item.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Export Control (Transition Assistance–Phase Out of Live Sheep Exports by Sea Programs) Instrument 2025

**1 Section 5 (at the end of the table)**

Add:

| 7 | WA Roadmap Implementation Fund: Farm System Productivity Program | To provide funding to support Western Australian-specific system‑level initiatives that enable sheep producers and production and farming system participants who are, are likely to be, or will be negatively affected by the phasing out of the export of live sheep by sea to improve their efficiency and profitability and adapt to changing markets. | To support sheep producers and production and farming system participants to prepare for, adapt and respond to the phasing out of the export of live sheep by sea by promoting industry-wide collaboration and knowledge-sharing, enhanced decision-making capabilities, and innovation and technology in the sheep supply chain. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | WA Roadmap Implementation Fund: Training, Capability and Farm Systems Program | To provide funding to industry organisations to:<br>deliver Western Australian-specific capability-building, productivity and sustainability initiatives; and<br>procure agricultural assets for use in training at Western Australian sheep demonstration sites,<br>to assist sheep producers and other sheep supply chain participants who are, are likely to be, or will be negatively affected by the phasing out of the export of live sheep by sea. | To support sheep producers and other sheep supply chain participants to prepare for, adapt and respond to the phasing out of live sheep exports by sea by improving the capability, productivity and sustainability of their business. |

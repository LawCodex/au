###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 16(1) of the *Air Services Act 1995*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The expression AA (short for Airservices Australia) is used in this instrument and is defined in the *Air Services Act 1995*.

In this instrument:

***EMP*** means the environmental monitoring program implemented by WSA.

Note: The details of the EMP could in 2026 be viewed on https://wsiairport.com.au/.

***EMP monitoring data*** means the monitoring detail published by WSA on its website as required as part of the EMP.

***RRO mode*** means:

(a) the set of flight paths described as RRO-Night in Table 7.13 of the WSI EIS 2024; or

(b) an alternative set of flight paths that operate during a WSI night period and utilise Runway 05 for arrivals, or Runway 23 for departures as part of the same runway mode.

***RRO‑NAP*** means the set of procedures described as Reciprocal Runway Operations – Noise Abatement Procedures in Table 7.14 of the WSI EIS 2024.

***WSA*** means the Western Sydney Airport Co Limited.

***WSI*** means the Western Sydney International (Nancy‑Bird Walton) Airport.

***WSI EIS 2024*** means the *Western Sydney International (Nancy‑Bird Walton) Airport – Airspace and flight path design Environmental Impact Statement 2024*, as in force or existing at the time this instrument commences.

Note: The WSI EIS 2024 could in 2026 be viewed on https://www.wsiflightpaths.gov.au.

***WSI night period*** means a period that starts at 11 pm on a day and ends at 5.30 am on the next day.

###### 5 AA must preference RRO mode

(1) AA must preference the use of RRO mode for WSI during a WSI night period if AA is satisfied it is safe to do so, taking into consideration operating conditions.

Note: Under Part 172 of the *Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998*, any air traffic service provided by AA as an ATS provider must be in accordance with the requirements of that Part and the Manual of Standards for that Part.

(2) If AA is satisfied it is safe to preference the use of RRO mode for WSI during a WSI night period, AA must preference RRO‑NAP unless AA is satisfied it is not safe to do so, taking into consideration operating conditions.

###### 6 AA must provide reports about the use of RRO mode

(1) AA must publish, as soon as practicable after the end of a reporting month, a report on its website setting out:

(a) the utilisation rates of RRO mode and RRO‑NAP in the reporting month; and

(b) the operating conditions and any other matters that prevented RRO mode and RRO‑NAP from being used in the reporting month.

(2) A ***reporting month*** is:

(a) the month starting on the day the first commercial passenger flight departs from or arrives at WSI; or

(b) each subsequent month.

Note: For the meaning of month, see section 2G of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

###### 7 Reports to the Minister about performance of section 5 direction

(1) As soon as practicable after the end of a 5‑year period, AA must provide to the Minister a report setting out AA’s views about the performance of the direction given under section 5 during that period.

(2) A ***5‑year period*** is:

(a) the period of 5 years beginning on the day the first commercial passenger flight departs from or arrives at WSI; or

(b) each subsequent period of 5 years.

###### 8 AA must consider EMP in future reviews of noise abatement procedures and flight paths

AA must consider, as part of their flight paths change processes, the EMP and EMP monitoring data when undertaking future reviews of noise abatement procedures and flight path modifications for WSI.

###### 9 This instrument ceases after 2056

This instrument ceases to be in force at the start of 1 January 2057.

# Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Sydney Metropolitan Institute of Technology Pty Ltd) Instrument 2026

I, Annette Cannell, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 19 June 2026

Annette Cannell

Acting Assistant Secretary\
HELP & Student Experience Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Notice of Approval – Sydney Metropolitan Institute of Technology Pty Ltd) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** used in this instrument is defined in Schedule 1 to the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Approval of higher education provider

For the purposes of section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the Act, Sydney Metropolitan Institute of Technology Pty Ltd (ACN 607 943 500; ABN 60 607 943 500) is approved as a higher education provider.

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Special Assistance) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 424 of the Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

###### 4 Repeals

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Special Assistance) Instrument 2023* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

dependant;

member;

former member;

deceased member.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

***specified condition*** means a mental ailment, disorder, defect or morbid condition (whether it began suddenly or developed over time) or a mental injury.

***VEA*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986.*

###### 6 Special assistance—eligible young persons

For the purposes of section 424 of the Act, special assistance may be provided to a person to whom all of the following applies:

(a) the person is or was a dependant of a member, former member or deceased member; and

(b) the person has been diagnosed with a specified condition by a medical practitioner; and

(c) the person meets subparagraphs (b)(i) and (iii) of the definition of ***eligible young person*** in subsection 5(1) of the Act; and

(d) the person does not meet subparagraph (b)(ii) of that definition solely because the specified condition prevents the person from undertaking full‑time education at a school, college, university or other educational institution; and

(e) apart from the circumstance mentioned in paragraph (d), the person would be entitled to compensation or another benefit under the Act or would be entitled to such compensation or benefit if a claim were made in respect of the person.

###### 7 Special assistance—children

For the purposes of section 424 of the Act, special assistance may be provided to a person to whom all of the following applies:

(a) the person is or was a dependant of a member, former member or deceased member; and

(b) the person has been diagnosed with a specified condition by a medical practitioner; and

(c) the person meets subparagraph (b)(i) of the definition of ***child*** in subsection 5F(1) of the VEA; and

(d) the person does not meet subparagraph (b)(ii) of that definition solely because the specified condition prevents the person from undertaking full‑time education at a school, college or university; and

(e) apart from the circumstance mentioned in paragraph (d), the person would be entitled to assistance, an allowance or a benefit under the VEA, or would be entitled to such assistance, allowance or benefit if a claim were made in respect of the person.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Financial Framework (Supplementary Powers) Regulations 1997

**1 Part 4 of Schedule 1AB (table item 617, column headed “Objective(s)”)**

After “regional Australia”, insert “, including capital works for Indigenous community infrastructure across regional Australia”.

**2 Part 4 of Schedule 1AB (table item 619, column headed “Objective(s)”)**

After “remote areas”, insert “, including for the benefit of Indigenous Australians”.

**3 Part 4 of Schedule 1AB (in the appropriate position in the table)**

Insert:

| 801 | Thriving Suburbs Program | To provide funding to Territory and local governments, or to entities collaborating with local governments, to plan and construct or upgrade community infrastructure to enhance amenity, liveability and social cohesion in urban, suburban and peri‑urban areas of Australia.<br>This objective has the effect it would have if it were limited to measures:<br>(a) involving the granting of financial assistance to a State or Territory; or<br>(b) undertaken in, or in relation to, a Territory. |
|---|---|---|
| 802 | Urban Precincts and Partnerships Program | To provide funding to State, Territory and local governments, universities and not‑for‑profit organisations to support the planning and delivery of precincts in urban areas, including for the benefit of Indigenous Australians.<br>This objective has the effect it would have if it were limited to measures:<br>(a) in connection with trade and commerce with other countries, or among the States and Territories; or<br>(b) with respect to postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution); or<br>(c) with respect to Indigenous Australians and particular groups of Indigenous Australians; or<br>(d) to give effect to Australia’s obligations under one or more of the following:<br>(i) the Kyoto Protocol to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, particularly Article 10;<br>(ii) the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, particularly Articles 2 and 6;<br>(iii) the International Labour Organization’s Convention concerning Employment Policy, particularly Articles 1 and 2;<br>(iv) the Paris Agreement, particularly Articles 4 and 7;<br>(v) the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, particularly Article 4; or<br>(e) that are peculiarly adapted to the government of a nation and cannot otherwise be carried on for the benefit of the nation; or<br>(f) involving the granting of financial assistance to a State or Territory; or<br>(g) undertaken in, or in relation to, a Territory. |

###### Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act including the following:

AFMA

boat;

e-monitoring equipment.

fishing concession;

holder;

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

***e-monitoring system*** means e-monitoring equipment, together with any associated peripheral devices.

***nominated boat*** means a boat that a fishing concession holder has been authorised by AFMA to use for a fishing concession.

***operate*** or ***operational,*** in relation to an e-monitoring system, means the system is:

powered on; and

all components of the e-monitoring system are active and functioning as intended, including all cameras, sensors, data‑capture mechanisms, and associated hardware or software required for the system to perform its functions, and

the system records all video and sensor data onto the data drive; and

the system sends signals (in an AFMA approved format and timeframe) to AFMA, or the e-monitoring service provider, that accurately show function test completion, sensor activity and boat positional information.

***port*** means (a) port boundaries set by State or territory port authorities, or (b) a place intended or used for the berthing, loading, unloading, and servicing of boats.

###### E-monitoring system types for the Eastern Tuna and Billfish Fishery

A person who is required under the *Fisheries Management (E-monitoring Eastern Tuna and Billfish Fishery) Direction 2026,* as in force from time to time, to install and operate an e-monitoring system on a nominated boat, must use one of the following e-monitoring system types for the fishery: Archipelago FishVue Vantage; Anchor Lab BlackBox VX52; Saltwater Aaeon VPC-3350S-IS-A12-CS-E0002; Saltwater Sintrone ABOX-5210P-i3-SWC; Satlink SeaTube Nano 20600SEM099; Satlink SeaTube Nano+ 20600SEM100; Teem Fish AIHUB 11.

Notwithstanding clause 5(1), a fishing concession holder (or a person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) who had installed an Archipelago Marine Research EM Observe v5.0 system on a nominated boat prior to the commencement of this instrument is not required to comply with clause 5(1) until (a) that system becomes non-operational, and (b) if the nominated boat was not in port when the system became non-operational, it has returned to a port., and (c) a further fishing related activity occurs.

###### E-monitoring system types for the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery

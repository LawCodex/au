**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Migration (Nil VAC Arrangements and Other Matters) Instrument 2026* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 15 July 2026 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au). The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. For more information on any uncommenced amendments, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Application, saving and transitional provisions for provisions and amendments**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. For more information on any modifications, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

### Part 2—Nil VAC arrangements

#### Division 1—Commission for the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources

###### 6 Definitions

In this Part:

***CCAMLR*** means the Commission for the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources.

***CCAMLR applicant*** means an applicant for a visa who has official business with CCAMLR, including subsidiary bodies established by CCAMLR and the Scientific Committee, as:

(a) a representative of a Party; or

(b) a staff member of CCAMLR; or

(c) an expert or consultant on missions for CCAMLR; or

(d) an observer to CCAMLR.

***Convention*** means the Convention on the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources drawn up at Canberra on 20 May 1980, which entered into force on 7 April 1982.

***Expert*** means a person performing short term or temporary projects on behalf of CCAMLR and includes a person serving on the Scientific Committee or on another committee of CCAMLR or participating in the work of or performing a mission on behalf of CCAMLR or the Scientific Committee, without necessarily receiving remuneration from CCAMLR or the Scientific Committee, but does not include staff members.

***family member*** means a person who is a member of the family unit of a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of ***CCAMLR applicant***.

***Observer to CCAMLR*** means a person who:

(a) has been invited by CCAMLR or the Scientific Committee to attend their meetings or the meetings of their subsidiary bodies; and

(b) is so invited as a representative of an organisation, including an intergovernmental or non-governmental organisation.

***Party*** means a Party to the Convention.

***Representative of a Party*** means a representative of a Party to the Convention in attendance at Conferences or meetings convened by CCAMLR or subsidiary bodies established by CCAMLR or the Scientific Committee and includes delegates, alternates, advisers, technical experts, and secretaries of delegations.

***Scientific Committee*** means the Scientific Committee of CCAMLR and subsidiary bodies established by the Scientific Committee.

***staff member*** means the Executive Secretary and all persons appointed or recruited for full-time employment with CCAMLR and subject to its staff regulations, but does not include:

(a) experts; or

(b) persons recruited locally and assigned to hourly rates of pay.

###### 7 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 400 visa—all streams

For sub-subparagraph 1231(2)(a)(iv)(A) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, a CCAMLR applicant or family member is specified.

###### 8 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 600 visa—Business Visitor and Tourist streams

For subparagraph 1236(2)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, a CCAMLR applicant or family member who is seeking to satisfy the primary criteria for a subclass 600 visa in one of the following streams is specified:

(a) the Business Visitor stream; or

(b) the Tourist stream.

###### 9 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 408 visa—Invited Participant in an event

For subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations:

(a) a CCAMLR applicant or family member who is seeking to satisfy the criterion in clause 408.219A of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations on the basis of clause 408.221 of that Schedule is specified; or

(b) an applicant who is seeking to satisfy the secondary criteria for the grant of a subclass 408 visa on the basis that the applicant is a family member of a person who:

(i) holds a subclass 408 visa; and

(ii) at the time of applying for the visa, the person was in the class of persons covered by paragraph (a) as a CCAMLR applicant.

#### Division 2—Workplace Justice visa

###### 10 Class of persons—Workplace Justice visa applicants

For the purposes of subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, an applicant who is seeking to satisfy the criterion in clause 408.219A of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations on the basis of clause 408.228A (Workplace Justice) of that Schedule is specified.

#### Division 3—2027 Men’s Rugby World Cup

###### 11 Definitions

In this Division:

***2027 Men's Rugby World Cup*** means the 2027 Men’s Rugby World Cup hosted by Australia from 1 October 2027 to 13 November 2027.

***local organising committee*** means Rugby World Cup (Australia) Pty Ltd (ABN: 33 666 859 641), which is the entity responsible for the operational delivery of the 2027 Men’s Rugby World Cup.

***Rugby World Cup Limited*** means the wholly-owned subsidiary of World Rugby that is responsible for the organisation of the Rugby World Cup.

***World Rugby*** means the global governing body for the sport of rugby union, whose headquarters is located in Dublin, Ireland.

###### 12 Specified event for paragraph 408.229(b)

For paragraph 408.229(b) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the 2027 Men’s Rugby World Cup is a specified event.

###### 13 Specified class of persons for paragraph 408.229(c)

For paragraph 408.229(c) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the class of persons specified, in relation to the event specified in section 12, is a person who is:

(a) approved for accreditation for the 2027 Men’s Rugby World Cup by the local organising committee; and

(b) any of the following persons:

(i) Rugby World Cup Limited personnel;

(ii) 2027 Rugby World Cup personnel;

(iii) a member of a competing team;

(iv) a tournament official;

(v) other accredited persons required to perform certain tournament functions in Australia.

###### 14 Visa application charge

For subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the specified class of persons is a person who is:

(a) seeking to satisfy clause 408.229 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; and

(b) in the class of persons specified in section 13.

###### 15 Repeal

This Division is repealed on 14 November 2027.

#### Division 3A—Rugby League World Cup 2026

###### 15A Definitions

In this Division:

***Australian Rugby League Commission*** means the Australian Rugby League Commission Limited (ABN: 94 003 107 293), which is the controlling body and administrator of Rugby League in Australia and responsible for the organisation of the Rugby League World Cup 2026.

***Rugby League World Cup 2026*** means the Rugby League World Cup 2026 hosted by Australia from 15 October 2026 to 15 November 2026.

###### 15B Specified event for paragraph 408.229(b)

For paragraph 408.229(b) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the Rugby League World Cup 2026 is a specified event.

###### 15C Specified class of persons for paragraph 408.229(c)

For paragraph 408.229(c) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the class of persons specified, in relation to the event specified in section 15B, is a person who is:

(a) approved for accreditation for the Rugby League World Cup 2026 by the Australian Rugby League Commission; and

(b) any of the following persons:

(i) a member of a competing team;

(ii) official coaching staff;

(iii) a match official;

(iv) official support staff;

(v) personnel of broadcast rights‑holders;

(vi) personnel of non‑rights‑holder media;

(vii) VIPs;

(viii) other persons participating in the Rugby League World Cup 2026 in an official capacity.

###### 15D Visa application charge

For subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the specified class of persons is a person who is:

(a) an applicant for a Temporary Activity (Class GG) visa who seeks to satisfy clause 408.229 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; and

(b) in the class of persons specified in section 15C.

###### 15E Repeal

This Division is repealed on 16 November 2026.

#### Division 4—Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games

##### Subdivision 1—Definitions

###### 16 Definitions

In this Division:

***Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games*** means the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games being hosted by Brisbane, Australia from 23 July 2032 to 5 September 2032.

***Organising Committee*** means the Brisbane Organising Committee for the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games (ABN: 49 672 341 604), which is the entity responsible for the organisation and operational delivery of the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

##### Subdivision 2—Subclass 600 visa

###### 17 Class of persons for nil VAC—Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games

For subparagraph 1236(2)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, a person is specified to be in a class of persons if:

(a) the person seeks to satisfy the primary criteria for the grant of a subclass 600 visa:

(i) in the Tourist stream as set out in Subdivision 600.22 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; or

(ii) in the Business Visitor stream as set out in Subdivision 600.24 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; and

(b) the person is invited by the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games Organising Committee to attend meetings and for purposes relating to the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

##### Subdivision 3—Subclass 408 visa

###### 18 Specified event for paragraph 408.229(b)

For paragraph 408.229(b) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games is a specified event.

###### 19 Specified class of persons for paragraph 408.229(c)

For paragraph 408.229(c) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations, the class of persons specified in relation to the event specified in section 18 is a person who is:

(a) approved for accreditation for the event by the Organising Committee; and

(b) a member or personnel of any of the following:

(i) the International Olympic Committee (IOC);

(ii) IOC Controlled entities;

(iii) National Olympic Committees;

(iv) International Federations;

(v) Rights-Holding Broadcasters;

(vi) the Official Timekeeper;

(vii) IOC Marketing Partners;

(viii) Media.

###### 20 Visa application charge

For subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the specified class of persons is a person who is:

(a) seeking to satisfy clause 408.229 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; and

(b) in the class of persons specified in section 19.

###### 21 Repeal

This Subdivision is repealed on 6 September 2032.

#### Division 5—Other nil VAC arrangements

##### Subdivision 1—Pacific Australia Card

###### 22 Definitions

In this Subdivision:

***Pacific Australia Card*** means a card by that name issued to a person by the Government of Australia.

###### 23 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 600 visa—Business Visitor and Tourist streams

For subparagraph 1236(2)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, a person (the applicant) is specified to be in a class of persons if:

(a) the person holds a valid Pacific Australia Card; and

(b) the person seeks to satisfy the primary criteria for the grant of a subclass 600 visa:

(i) in the Tourist stream as set out in Subdivision 600.22 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; or

(ii) in the Business Visitor stream as set out in Subdivision 600.24 of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations; and

(c) the person is outside Australia at the time of application.

##### Subdivision 2—Family of passengers or crew of MH370

###### 24 Definitions

In this Subdivision:

***member of the family unit*** has the same meaning as in subregulation 1.12(2) of the Migration Regulations.

***MH370*** means Malaysia Airlines flight MH370 (aircraft registration 9M‑MRO).

###### 25 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 600 visa—Tourist stream

An applicant for a subclass 600 visa in the Tourist stream who is:

(a) a member of the family unit of a passenger or member of the crew on board MH370; or

(b) a representative of the family of a passenger or member of the crew on board MH370, who is:

(i) approved by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People’s Republic of China; or

(ii) approved by the Department of Home Affairs and Malaysia Airlines;

is in a class of persons specified for subparagraph 1236(2)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations.

##### Subdivision 3—Eligible sponsor and supporter

###### 26 Definitions

In this Subdivision:

***eligible sponsor*** has the same meaning as defined in subclause 408.229A(9) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations.

***eligible supporter*** has the same meaning as defined in subclause 408.229A(10) of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations.

###### 27 Class of persons for nil VAC—subclass 408 visa—Entertainment stream

For the purposes of subparagraph 1237(2)(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the specified class of persons is:

(a) a person (the ***applicant***) who is seeking to satisfy clause 408.219A of Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations on the basis of clause 408.229A of Schedule 2 to the Regulations; and

(b) a person in relation to whom there is an eligible sponsor or eligible supporter who is one of the following:

(i) a charity registered by the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission;

(ii) an agency of the Commonwealth;

(iii) an agency of a State;

(iv) an agency of a Territory;

(v) an agency, Embassy or Consulate of a foreign government;

(vi) the Queensland Art Gallery and Gallery of Modern Art;

(vii) the Australian Grand Prix, where the sponsorship or support relates to the Formula One Australian Grand Prix or the Australian Motorcycle Grand Prix;

(viii) PI SBK Pty Ltd, where the sponsorship or support relates to Superbike events;

(ix) Major Brisbane Festivals Pty Ltd;

(x) DarkLab Pty Ltd, where the sponsorship or support relates to the Dark Mofo Festival;

(xi) Moorilla Estate Pty Ltd, where the sponsorship or support relates to the Mona Foma; and

(c) a person who will only engage in entertainment activities that are organised by or on behalf of an eligible sponsor or eligible supporter listed in paragraph (b).

##### Subdivision 4—Emergency services

###### 28 Class of persons for nil VAC – subclass 400 visa—all streams

For sub-subparagraph 1231(2)(a)(iv)(A) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the specified class of persons is a person who has been invited to Australia by the Australian Government, or by an Australian State or Territory government, to assist in providing emergency services in connection with a disaster or emergency.

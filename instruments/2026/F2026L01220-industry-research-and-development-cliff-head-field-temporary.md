###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***Cliff Head oil field area*** means the area covered by the petroleum production licence WA-31-L.

***Cliff Head project*** means the wells and infrastructure in the Cliff Head oil field area, including the wellhead platform, the pipeline authorised by pipeline licence WA-12-PL and other subsea infrastructure.

***program***: see subsection 5(1).

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the Cliff Head Field Temporary Operations Program (the ***program***) is prescribed.

(2) The program provides funding to support:

(a) the operation, monitoring, inspection, maintenance and repair of the Cliff Head project; and

(b) responding to emergencies relating to the Cliff Head project, including activities to:

(i) minimise harm to human life or the environment; and

(ii) secure the safety and structural integrity of the Cliff Head project; and

(c) obtaining insurance in relation to the matters at paragraphs (a) and (b); and

(d) membership of organisations that provide oil spill response services, training and equipment.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the power of the Parliament to make laws with respect to external affairs (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xxix) of the Constitution) is specified.

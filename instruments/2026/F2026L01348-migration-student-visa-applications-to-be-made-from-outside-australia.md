# Migration (Student Visa Applications to Be Made from outside Australia) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/102

I, Julian Hill, Assistant Minister for Citizenship, Customs and Multicultural Affairs make the following instrument.

Dated 1 October 2026

Julian Hill

Assistant Minister for Citizenship, Customs and Multicultural Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Student Visa Applications to Be Made from outside Australia) Instrument 2026*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 1222(5)(e) of Schedule 1 to the *Migration Regulations 1994* for the purposes of subparagraph 1222(4)(a)(ii) of that Schedule.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Migration Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

***stream*** has the meaning given by subsection 2.03(1B) of the Migration Regulations.

***subclass*** in relation to a visa, has the meaning given by subsection 1.07(1) of the Migration Regulations.

###### 6 Class of persons

(1) For the purposes of subparagraph 1222(4)(a)(ii) of the Migration Regulations, the class of persons to which subsection (2) applies is specified.

(2) This subsection applies in relation to a class of persons where the person:

(a) holds a visa of a subclass mentioned in Column 1 of an item in the following table; and

(b) was granted that visa on the basis of criteria set out in a stream mentioned in Column 2 of the same item; and

(c) satisfies the requirements, if any, mentioned in Column 3 of the same item.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 78(1) and 78(3) of the PGPA Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Donations Instrument*** means the *PGPA Act Determination (Innovation, Science and Technology – Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2016) — Establishment*.

***Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2016*** means the Innovation, Science and Technology – Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2016 established by the Donations Instrument.

***PGPA Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***the special account*** means the Innovation, Science and Technology – Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2026 established by this instrument.

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(a) of the PGPA Act, the *Innovation, Science and Technology – Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2026* is established as a special account.

### Part 2—Special account

###### 6 Amounts that may be credited to the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the following amounts may be credited to the special account:

(a) amounts received in the course of the performance of functions that relate to the purposes of the special account; and

(b) amounts received from any person for the purposes of the special account.

###### 7 Purposes of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(c) of the PGPA Act, the purposes of the special account, in relation to which amounts may be debited from the special account, are:

(a) to make payments consistent with the terms of donations, bequests, sponsorships and any other contributions for activities related to:

(i) promoting greater understanding and awareness of science, technology, engineering, mathematics and innovation within the community; and

(ii) supporting science education and research, including promoting science, technology, engineering, mathematics and innovation capacity in students, teachers and the community; and

(b) to reduce the balance of the special account (and, therefore, the available appropriation for the special account) without making a real or notional payment; and

(c) to repay amounts that have been credited to the special account where a court order, Act or other law requires or permits the repayment of an amount received.

###### 8 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(d) of the PGPA Act, the accountable authority responsible for the special account is the Secretary of the Department of Industry, Science and Resources.

### Part 3—Transitional, revocation and repeal provisions

###### 9 Opening balance of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the amount in subsection 9(2) (***opening balance***) is required to be credited to the special account immediately after its establishment.

The opening balance is equal to the amount standing to the credit of the Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2016 immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

###### 10 Revocation of the Donations Instrument

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the Donations Instrument is revoked.

###### 11 Additional repeal provisions

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, Part 3 of this instrument is repealed at the end of the seventh day after the revocation of the Donations Instrument under section 10 (***date of repeal***).

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the following definitions in section 4 are repealed on the date of repeal:

definition of Donations Instrument; and

definition of Donations, Bequests and Sponsorship Special Account 2016.

Despite subsection 11(1), the effects of Part 3 of this instrument remain in force after the date of repeal.

# Family Law (Superannuation) (Interest Rate for Adjustment Period) Determination 2026

I, Guy Francis Thorburn, Australian Government Actuary, make the following determination under section 76 of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2025*.

Dated: 29 May 2026

Guy Francis Thorburn

Australian Government Actuary

Contents

###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Interest Rate for Adjustment Period) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under section 76 of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2025*.

###### 4 Definition

In this determination:

***regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2025*.

###### 5 Adjustment period—financial year

For paragraph 76(4)(a) of the regulations, the interest rate for the adjustment period, being the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026, is 0.061.

###### 6 Adjustment period—less than 12 months within financial year

(1) This section applies to an adjustment period of less than 12 months that begins and ends in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026.

(2) For paragraph 76(4)(b) of the regulations, the method for working out the interest rate for the adjustment period is by using the formula:

[image: Start formula 1.061 superscript start fraction d over 365 end fraction end superscript minus 1 end formula]

where:\
***d*** is the number of days in the adjustment period.

###### 7 Adjustment period—12 months not within financial year

(1) This section applies to an adjustment period of 12 months that begins in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2025 and ends in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026.

(2) For paragraph 76(4)(c) of the regulations, the method for working out the interest rate for the adjustment period is by using the formula:

[image: Start formula 1.069 superscript start fraction d1 over d1 plus d2 end fraction end superscript times 1.061 superscript start fraction d2 over d1 plus d2 end fraction end superscript minus 1 end formula]

where:

***d1*** is the number of days in the adjustment period in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2025.

***d2*** is the number of days in the adjustment period in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026.

###### 8 Adjustment period—less than 12 months not within financial year

(1) This section applies to an adjustment period of less than 12 months that begins in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2025 and ends in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026.

(2) For paragraph 76(4)(c) of the regulations, the method for working out the interest rate for the adjustment period is by using the formula:

[image: Start formula 1.069 superscript start fraction d1 over 365 end fraction end superscript times 1.061 superscript start fraction d2 over 365 end fraction end superscript minus 1 end formula]

where:

***d1*** is the number of days in the adjustment period in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2025.

***d2*** is the number of days in the adjustment period in the financial year beginning on 1 July 2026.

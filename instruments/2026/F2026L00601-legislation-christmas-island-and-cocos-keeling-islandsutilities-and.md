###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 51A(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

###### 4 Aligning of sunsetting

The following instruments are repealed by section 51A of the *Legislation Act 2003* on 1 October 2031:

(a) *Christmas Island Utilities and Services (Electricity Supply and Services Fees) Determination 2016*;

(b) *Christmas Island Utilities and Services (Vehicle Examination Fees) Determination 2019*;

(c) *Christmas Island Utilities and Services (Water, Sewerage and Building Application Services Fees) Determination 2016*;

(d) *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services (Electricity Supply and Services Fees) Determination 2016*;

(e) *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services (Vehicle Examination Fees) Determination 2019*;

(f) *Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services (Water, Sewerage and Building Application Services Fees) Determination 2016*.

###### 5 Repeal of this instrument

This instrument is repealed at the start of 2 October 2031.

### Part 2—Reducing funding for groups of supports

#### Division 1—The percentage for reducing funding component amounts

###### 5 Reduced funding for specified groups of supports

For the purposes of paragraph 34A(1)(a) of the Act:

(a) each of the following groups of supports is specified:

(i) assistance with social, economic and community participation (the ***SECP group of supports***);

(ii) improved daily living skills (the ***IDLS group of supports***); and

(b) the percentage for reducing funding component amounts for each group of supports is determined to be as follows:

(i) for the SECP group of supports—50%;

(ii) for the IDLS group of supports—10%.

###### 6 Class of participants’ plans to which percentage applies

For the purposes of paragraph 34A(1)(b) of the Act, the percentage determined under paragraph 5(b) of this instrument for a group of supports applies to all old framework plans that come into effect on or after 1 October 2026.

#### Division 2—Excluded subgroups

###### 7 Excluded subgroups within the SECP group of supports

(1) For the purposes of subsection 34A(1C) of the Act, the following subgroups within the SECP group of supports are specified as ***excluded subgroups*** for the purposes of subsection 34A(2A) of the Act:

(a) supports in employment;

(b) high intensity supports;

(c) intensive and complex behaviour supports.

(2) For the purposes of this instrument, an excluded subgroup specified in subsection (1) consists of the supports specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1 for the subgroup.

###### 8 Excluded subgroups within the IDLS group of supports

(1) For the purposes of subsection 34A(1C) of the Act, the following subgroups within the IDLS group of supports are specified as ***excluded subgroups*** for the purposes of subsection 34A(2A) of the Act:

(a) disability related health supports;

(b) customised and wearable technology;

(c) hearing services.

(2) For the purposes of this instrument, an excluded subgroup specified in subsection (1) consists of the supports specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1 for the subgroup.

#### Division 3—Subclasses of plans for excluded groups

###### 9 Subclasses of plans for excluded groups

(1) For the purposes of subsection 34A(1CA) of the Act, each of the following is, for the purposes of subsection 34A(2A) of the Act, a subclass of participants’ plans in relation to an excluded subgroup:

(a) subclass A;

(b) subclass B;

(c) subclass C;

(d) subclass D.

Subclass A plans

(2) A plan is in ***subclass A*** in relation to an excluded subgroup if:

(a) the plan was made otherwise than by renewal by force of section 50A of the Act; and

(b) the plan comes into effect on or after 1 October 2026; and

(c) the Agency’s records show that, in preparing the plan, an amount was allocated for supports in the excluded subgroup as part of determining the funding component amount for the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup.

Note: An amount may be allocated for supports in an excluded subgroup in preparing a plan even though the amount is not separately specified in the plan and funding may be used flexibly within the relevant group of supports.

Subclass B plans

(3) A renewed plan is in ***subclass B*** in relation to an excluded subgroup if:

(a) the renewed plan comes into effect on or after 1 February 2027; and

(b) the source plan for the renewed plan came into effect on or after 1 October 2026; and

(c) the Agency’s records show that, in preparing the source plan, an amount was allocated for supports in the excluded subgroup as part of determining the funding component amount for the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup.

Subclass C plans

(4) A renewed plan is in ***subclass C*** in relation to an excluded subgroup if:

(a) the renewed plan comes into effect on or after 1 February 2027; and

(b) the source plan for the renewed plan came into effect before 1 October 2026; and

(c) the funding for supports in the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup under the plan then in effect for the participant was not, at any time during 2026, managed wholly or to a specified extent by either of the following:

(i) the participant;

(ii) a nominee of the participant in accordance with subsection 43(5) or (6) of the Act; and

(d) the Agency’s records show that:

(i) at any time during 2026, a payment of an NDIS amount was made in accordance with the plan then in effect for the participant for supports in the excluded subgroup; and

(ii) in preparing the source plan, an amount was allocated for supports in the excluded subgroup as part of determining funding for the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup.

Subclass D plans

(5) A renewed plan is in ***subclass D*** in relation to an excluded subgroup if:

(a) the renewed plan comes into effect on or after 1 February 2027; and

(b) the source plan for the renewed plan came into effect before 1 October 2026; and

(c) the funding for supports in the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup under the plan then in effect for the participant was, at any time during 2026, managed wholly or to a specified extent by either of the following:

(i) the participant;

(ii) a nominee of the participant in accordance with subsection 43(5) or (6) of the Act; and

(d) the Agency’s records show that:

(i) at any time during 2026, a payment of an NDIS amount was made in accordance with the plan then in effect for the participant for supports in the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup; and

(ii) in preparing the source plan, an amount was allocated for supports in the excluded subgroup as part of determining funding for the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup.

Definitions

(6) In this instrument:

***renewed plan*** means a plan that, by force of section 50A of the Act, is renewed as a new plan.

***source plan***, for a renewed plan, means the most recent plan for a participant that:

(a) was made otherwise than by renewal by force of section 50A of the Act; and

(b) was replaced, directly or through one or more renewed plans, by the renewed plan.

#### Division 4—Determining the portion of the funding component amount attributable to an excluded subgroup

###### 10 How to determine the portion attributable to an excluded subgroup

(1) For the purposes of subsection 34A(2B) of the Act, this section specifies how to determine the portion of the funding component amount stated in a plan for a group of supports that is attributable to funding for the supports in an excluded subgroup within that group.

(2) The portion is to be determined by identifying the amount allocated, in preparing the relevant plan, for supports in the excluded subgroup as part of determining funding for the group of supports containing the excluded subgroup.

Note: The amount referred to in subsection (2) may have been taken into account in determining funding for a group of supports without being separately specified in the plan.

(3) The amount referred to in subsection (2) is to be identified:

(a) if the amount is separately specified in the relevant plan—from that plan; or

(b) otherwise—from the Agency’s records.

(4) For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), the ***relevant plan*** is:

(a) for a plan in subclass A—that plan; or

(b) for a plan in subclass B, C or D—the source plan for that plan.

#### Division 5—Application and transitional provisions

###### 11 Application

(1) Section 6 applies in relation to an old framework plan that comes into effect on or after 1 October 2026 whether or not a plan reassessment resulting in the plan began before that day.

(2) In applying section 9 or 10, regard may be had to information in the Agency’s records about a payment made, or an amount allocated, before 1 October 2026.

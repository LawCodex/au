# Immigration (Education) (Classes of Temporary Visas Eligible for English Courses) Determination 2026

LIN 25/087

I, Anne Aly, Minister for Multicultural Affairs, make the following determination.

Dated 27 February 2026

Anne Aly

Minister for Multicultural Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Immigration (Education) (Classes of Temporary Visas Eligible for English Courses) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 4(2) and 4A(2) of the *Immigration (Education) Act 1971*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Immigration (Education) Act 1971*.

***class***, for the purposes of the table in section 6, has the meaning given by regulation 2.01 of the Migration Regulations.

***Migration Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

***temporary visa*** has the meaning given by subsection 30(2) of the *Migration Act 1958*.

***subclass***, in relation to a visa, has the meaning given by regulation 2.02 of the Migration Regulations.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Specified temporary visas

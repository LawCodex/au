# Fuel Quality Standards (Petrol) Amendment Determination (No. 3) 2026

I, Chris Bowen, Minister for Climate Change and Energy, make the following instrument.

Dated 30 September 2026

Chris Bowen

Minister for Climate Change and Energy

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fuel Quality Standards (Petrol) Amendment Determination (No. 3) 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 21 of the *Fuel Quality Standards Act 2000*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

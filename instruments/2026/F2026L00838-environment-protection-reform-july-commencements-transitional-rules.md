### Part 2—Modifications of Division 3 of Part 3 of Schedule 2 to the Environment Protection Reform Act 2025

###### 4 Modifications of Division 3 of Part 3 of Schedule 2 to the Environment Protection Reform Act 2025

(1) Item 604 of Schedule 2 to the *Environment Protection Reform Act 2025* is modified as follows:

(a) the reference in paragraph 604(1)(a) to an environmental law is taken to include a reference to a legislative instrument made under an environmental law;

(b) the reference in paragraph 604(1)(b) to the environmental law is taken to include a reference to the legislative instrument made under the environmental law;

(c) the reference in paragraph 604(1)(b) to the amendments made by Parts 1 and 2 of this Schedule is taken to include a reference to amendments made by the *Environment Protection Reform Legislation Amendment (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026* or the *Environment Protection Reform Legislation Amendment (Consequential Amendments) Rules 2026*;

(d) the reference in subitem 604(2) to the environmental law is taken to include a reference to the legislative instrument made under the environmental law.

(2) Item 607 of Schedule 2 to the *Environment Protection Reform Act 2025* is modified as follows:

(a) the reference in paragraph 607(1)(a) to an environmental law is taken to include a reference to a legislative instrument made under an environmental law;

(b) the reference in paragraph 607(1)(b) to the environmental law is taken to include a reference to the legislative instrument made under the environmental law;

(c) the reference in paragraph 607(1)(b) to the amendments made by Part 1 or 2 of this Schedule is taken to include a reference to amendments made by the *Environment Protection Reform Legislation Amendment (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026* or the *Environment Protection Reform Legislation Amendment (Consequential Amendments) Rules 2026*.

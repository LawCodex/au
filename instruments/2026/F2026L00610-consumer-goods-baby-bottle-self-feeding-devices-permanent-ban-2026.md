###### 4 Permanent ban—baby bottle self‑feeding devices

(1) A permanent ban is imposed on baby bottle self‑feeding devices.

Baby bottle self‑feeding devices

(2) A ***baby bottle self‑feeding device*** is a product that:

(a) is designed to allow a baby to feed from a bottle without another person holding the bottle; and

(b) is designed to do so:

(i) by supporting the position of the bottle; or

(ii) by means of a straw, tube or similar device (or something attached to a straw, tube or similar device).

(3) However, a product is not a ***baby bottle self‑feeding device*** if:

(a) the product has a tube; and

(b) the baby cannot be fed using the product without the baby being held by another person.

# ASIC Corporations (Approval of Auditing Competency Standard) Instrument 2026/733

ASIC Corporations (Approval of Auditing Competency Standard) Instrument 2026/733

I, Claire LaBouchardiere, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 23 September 2026

Claire LaBouchardiere

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Approval of Auditing Competency Standard) Instrument 2026/733*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation; and

(b) 1 October 2026.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1280A(1) of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Simplified outline of this instrument

This instrument approves the auditing competency standard that an applicant for registration as an auditor must satisfy if they do not have the practical experience in auditing that is prescribed in the *Corporations Regulations 2001*.

5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***2026 auditing competency standard*** means the *Auditing Competency Standard for Registered Company Auditors* dated October 2026 and issued by CPA Australia, Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand and the Institute of Public Accountants.

***Act*** means the *Corporations Act 2001*.

Part 2—Approval

6 2026 auditing competency standard

The 2026 auditing competency standard is approved for the purposes of paragraph 1280(2)(b) of the Act.

# Higher Education Support (Provider Variation of Approval — Excelsia University College Limited) Instrument 2026

I, Annette Cannell, delegate of the Minister for Education, make the following instrument.

Dated 15 May 2026

Annette Cannell

Acting Assistant Secretary\
HELP and Student Experience Branch\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support (Provider Variation of Approval — Excelsia University College Limited) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-70 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Notice of variation of approval

For the purposes of section 16-70 of the Act, the approval of the higher education provider previously known as Wesley Institute (ABN 50 360 319 774) is varied to reflect that the higher education provider is now named Excelsia University College Limited.

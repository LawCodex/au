This instrument is made under the *Criminal Code Act 1995*.

###### 4 Prohibited hate group—White Australia

(1) For the purposes of the definition of ***prohibited hate group*** in subsection 114A.2(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as White Australia is specified.

(2) White Australia is also known by the following names:

(a) WA;

(b) WAP;

(c) White Australia Party.

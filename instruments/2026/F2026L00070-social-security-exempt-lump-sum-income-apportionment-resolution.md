###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Income Apportionment Resolution Scheme*** is the Commonwealth scheme established by force of Schedule 3 to the *Social Security and Other Legislation Amendment (Technical Changes No. 2) Act 2025*.

***resolution payment*** means an amount of reparation paid under the Income Apportionment Resolution Scheme as determined by the Minister by legislative instrument under subitem 3(1) of Schedule 3 to the *Social Security and Other Legislation Amendment (Technical Changes No. 2) Act 2025*.

###### 5 Exempt lump sum

A resolution payment is an exempt lump sum for the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act.

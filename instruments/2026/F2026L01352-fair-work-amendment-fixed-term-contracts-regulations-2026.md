## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Fair Work Regulations 2009

**1 Paragraph 2.15(7)(b)**

Omit “work that is funded in whole or in part by”, substitute “work that is more than 50% funded by any of the following (or any combination of the following)”.

**2 Subparagraphs 2.15(7)(b)(i) and (ii)**

Omit “or” (last occurring).

**3 Paragraph 2.15(7)(i)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(i) the contract is entered into on or after 1 November 2024.

**4 Paragraph 2.15(9)(b)**

Omit “work that is funded in whole or part by”, substitute “work that is more than 50% funded by any of the following (or any combination of the following)”.

**5 Subparagraphs 2.15(9)(b)(i) and (ii)**

Omit “or”.

**6 Paragraph 2.15(9)(j)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(j) the contract is entered into on or after 1 November 2024.

**7 In the appropriate position in Chapter 7**

Insert:

### Part 7‑8—Amendments made by the Fair Work Amendment (Fixed Term Contracts) Regulations 2026

###### 7.13 Application of amendments

The amendments of paragraphs 2.15(7)(b) and (9)(b) of these Regulations made by the *Fair Work Amendment (Fixed Term Contracts) Regulations 2026* apply in relation to contracts entered into on or after 1 November 2026.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Rule 2015

**1 Section 7 (heading)**

After “information”, insert “and documents”.

**2 Section 7**

Before “For”, insert “(1)”.

**3 Section 7**

Omit “paragraph 13(1)(d) of the Act, the following information”, substitute “the purposes of paragraphs 13(1)(d) and (g) of the Act, the following information and documents”.

**4 After paragraph 7(e)**

Insert:

(ea) if:

(i) a declaration statement about the project was given to the Regulator under subsection 13(5); or

(ii) before the declaration of the project as an eligible offsets project, a signed statement containing all the matters mentioned in subsection 13(5) was given to the Regulator;

each of the following:

(iii) information about the steps the project proponent will take to prevent the direct or indirect transfer to the class of entity specified in the statement of any Australian carbon credit units issued pursuant to the certificate of entitlement;

(iv) a signed statement by the project proponent certifying the matter mentioned in subsection 9(12);

**5 At the end of section 7**

Add:

(2) To avoid doubt, subparagraphs (1)(ea)(iii) and (iv) apply in relation to a project proponent for an eligible offsets project if the previous proponent for the project gave the statement mentioned in subparagraph (1)(ea)(i) or (ii).

**6 At the end of section 9**

Add:

(11) Subsections (12) to (15) apply in relation to a project to which paragraph 7(1)(ea) applies.

(12) It is an eligibility requirement that, if any Australian carbon credit units were issued during the period mentioned in subsection (13), the project proponent for the project did not transfer any of those units, and is not aware of any of those units having been transferred, to the class of entity specified in the statement mentioned in subparagraph 7(1)(ea)(i) or (ii), as the case requires (the ***relevant statement***).

(13) For the purposes of subsection (12), the period is one of the following, ending on the day the application is made:

(a) the period of 2 years;

(b) if paragraph (a) does not apply, and the reporting period to which the application relates was longer than 2 years—the period of the same length as the reporting period.

(14) It is an eligibility requirement that the information mentioned in subparagraph 7(1)(ea)(iii) demonstrates that the project proponent for the project has arrangements in place to take the relevant steps.

(15) It is an eligibility requirement that, during the 2-year period ending on the day the application is made, no Australian carbon credit units issued in relation to the project were surrendered in accordance with subsection 22XN(1) of the NGER Act by the class of entity specified in the relevant statement.

**7 Subsection 13(1)**

After “following information”, insert “and documents”.

**8 Subsection 13(1)**

**After “that the information”, insert “or a document”.**

**9 At the end of section 13**

Add:

(5) If:

(a) the applicable methodology determination for the project is the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative*—*Improved Native Forest Management in Multiple-use Public Native Forests) Methodology Determination 2026*; and

(b) the project proponent for the project intends to prevent the direct or indirect transfer of Australian carbon credit units issued in relation to the project to a particular class of entity; and

(c) the project proponent consents to the issue of Australian carbon credit units in relation to the project being conditional on meeting the eligibility requirements mentioned in subsections 9(12) to (15);

the application must be accompanied by a signed statement (the ***declaration statement***) setting out:

(d) the intention mentioned in paragraph (b), including the class of entity to which the intention relates; and

(e) the steps that the project proponent intends to take to fulfil that intention; and

(f) the consent mentioned in paragraph (c).

Note: For the purposes of paragraph (d), the class of entity may be specified by reference to defined terms in other legislation, such as a “designated large facility” or “controlling corporation” within the meaning of the NGER Act. It may also be specified by reference to sectors of the economy in which the relevant entities are engaged, including by means of the Australian and New Zealand Standard Industrial Classifications, known as “ANZSIC codes”. The ANZSIC codes could, in 2026, be viewed on the Australian Bureau of Statistics website (https://www.abs.gov.au).

**10 At the end of subsection 93A(1)**

Add:

; (k) if a declaration statement about the project within the meaning of subsection 13(5), or the statement mentioned in subparagraph 7(1)(ea)(ii), was given to the Regulator—detailed information about that statement.

**11 At the end of Part 29**

Add:

#### Division 6—Application provisions relating to the Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (INFM Projects) Rules 2026

###### 130 Amendments made by the Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (INFM Projects) Rules 2026

The amendments of this instrument made by items 1-8 and 10 of the *Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Amendment (INFM Projects) Rules 2026* (the ***amending instrument***) apply in relation to an offsets project if, when the amending instrument commences, the Regulator has not yet declared the project as an eligible offsets project.

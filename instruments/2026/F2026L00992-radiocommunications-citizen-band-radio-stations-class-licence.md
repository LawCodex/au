# Radiocommunications (Citizen Band Radio Stations) Class Licence Variation 2026 (No.1)

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following instrument under section 132 of the *Radiocommunications Act 1992*.

Dated: 23 July 2026

Adam Suckling

[signed]

Member

Michael Brealey

[signed]

General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### 1 Name

This is the *Radiocommunications (Citizen Band Radio Stations) Class Licence Variation 2026 (No.1).*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the start of the day after the day it is registered.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed free of charge at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 132 of the *Radiocommunications Act 1992.*

**4 Variations**

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 is varied as set out in the applicable items in that Schedule.

###### Schedule 1—Variations

(section 4)

*Radiocommunications (Citizen Band Radio Stations) Class Licence 2025* [F2025L01088]

**1 Subsection 7(1) (note 3)**

Repeal the note.

**2 Schedule 1 (cell at table item 1, column 3)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

Either:\
(a) the CB station must:\
(i) use AM, or an emission mode that begins with F3E or G3E; and\
(ii) be operated with a maximum transmitter power of 4 W pZ; and\
(iii) be operated with a necessary bandwidth that is not greater than 6 kHz; or\
(b) the CB station must:\
(i) use SSB modulation; and\
(ii) use LSB or USB; and\
(iii) be operated with a maximum transmitter power of 12 W pX; and\
(iv) be operated with a necessary bandwidth that is not greater than 3 kHz.

**3 Schedule 1 (cell at table item 2, column 3)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

Either:\
(a) the CB station must:\
(i) use AM, or an emission mode that begins with F3E or G3E; and\
(ii) be operated with a maximum transmitter power of 4 W pZ; and\
(iii) be operated with a necessary bandwidth that is not greater than 6 kHz; or\
(b) the CB station must:\
(i) use SSB modulation; and\
(ii) use LSB or USB; and\
(iii) be operated with a maximum transmitter power of 12 W pX; and\
(iv) be operated with a necessary bandwidth that is not greater than 3 kHz.

**4 Schedule 1 (cell at table item 6, column 3)**

Omit ‘sub-items 29 to 71’, substitute ‘sub-items 56 to 71’.

Dated 20 August 2026

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *National Environmental Protection Agency Rules 2026*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *National Environmental Protection Agency Act 2025*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) CEO;

(b) registrable decision;

(c) Regulatory Powers Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Environmental Protection Agency Act 2025*.

***EPBC Act*** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

#### Division 3—Registrable decisions and prescribed matters under the Hazardous Waste (Regulation of Exports and Imports) Act 1989

###### 9 Registrable decisions under the Hazardous Waste (Regulation of Exports and Imports) Act 1989

Decisions prescribed as registrable decisions

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 19(a) of the Act, a prescribed decision is a decision by the CEO (as an authorised person) to accept an undertaking under section 114 of the Regulatory Powers Act in relation to a provision of the *Hazardous Waste (Regulation of Exports and Imports) Act 1989*.

Information about registrable decisions that must be published on register

(2) For the purposes of section 20 of the Act, the following information must be published on the register in relation to the registrable decision prescribed by subsection (1):

(a) the name of the person who has given the undertaking;

(b) the content of the undertaking;

(c) the day the undertaking was accepted by the CEO (as an authorised person).

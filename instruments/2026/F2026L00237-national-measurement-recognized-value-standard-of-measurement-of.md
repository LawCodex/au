###### 6 Recognized-Value Standard of Measurement of Acceleration due to Gravity

The magnitude of the acceleration in a vacuum of a body due to the gravitational attraction of the earth at the benchmark inscribed “*Absolute Gravity Station 200498.9905, Sydney, Commonwealth of Australia*” installed in room C269 of the National Measurement Institute, 36 Bradfield Road, West Lindfield, NSW, is a Recognized-Value Standard of Measurement having a value of 9.7963779533 m·s-2 with an expanded uncertainty of 106 ×10-9 m·s-2.

Note: The expanded uncertainty is based on a standard uncertainty multiplied by a coverage factor k=2, providing a level of confidence of 95%.

# Fisheries Management (South Australia Closure) Direction 2026

I, Wez Norris, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, make the following direction.

Dated 31 August 2026

Wez Norris

Chief Executive Officer

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fisheries Management (South Australia Closure) Direction 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 41A(2) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991.*

***Management Plan*** means *Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.*

***South Australian Coastal Waters Sector*** means the area described in clause 7 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Management Plan

***Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery*** means commercial fishing (other than recreational fishing or fishing using a charter boat), for scalefish, shark and other fish (other than fish mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the definition of scalefish), by the fishing methods allowed by fishing concessions granted under this Management Plan and the Act, in the area of the fishery

***World Geodetic System 1984 (WGS84)*** means the World Geodetic System 1984 datum that is used for cartography, geodesy, and satellite navigation purposes and is defined and maintained by the United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency.

Terms used in this Direction have the same meanings in this instrument as they have in the Management Plan.

Note: Terms defined in the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* have the same meaning in this instrument (see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*).

###### 5 To whom this applies

This Direction applies to the holder of a fishing concession (or a person acting on behalf of that holder) in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery.

###### 6 Direction not to engage in fishing

A holder of a fishing concession, (or a person acting on behalf of that holder of aa fishing concession), is directed not to engage in fishing in the area described in Schedule 1, in the period between of commencement of this instrument to 30 April 2027.

**7 Coordinates**

The geographic coordinates in this instrument are expressed in terms of the World Geodetic System 1984 (WGS84) means the datum that is used for cartography, geodesy, and satellite navigation purposes and is defined and maintained by the United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency

In this instrument, WGS84 applies to all coordinates prescribed in Schedule 1 of this instrument.

**8 Repeal of this Instrument**

This instrument is repealed at 11:59pm on 1 May 2027

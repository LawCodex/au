###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 51(1)(c) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

###### 4 Deferral of sunsetting

The *Inspector‑General of the Australian Defence Force Regulation 2016*, for which the sunsetting day is 1 October 2026, is repealed by section 51 of the *Legislation Act 2003* on 1 October 2028.

###### 5 Repeal of this instrument

This instrument is repealed at the start of 2 October 2028.

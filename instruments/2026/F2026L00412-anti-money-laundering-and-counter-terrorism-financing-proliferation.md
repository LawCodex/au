### Part 2—Proliferation financing

###### 5 Meaning of proliferation financing—offences against the Charter of United Nations Act 1945

Offences involving the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, an offence against section 27 of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945* is prescribed to the extent that the conduct constituting the offence is a contravention of a provision of the *Charter of the United Nations (Sanctions—Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Regulations 2008* that is a UN sanction enforcement law (within the meaning of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945*).

Note: For the provisions of the above regulations that constitute a UN sanction enforcement law within the meaning of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945*, see table item 4 of Schedule 1 to the *Charter of the United Nations (UN Sanction Enforcement Law) Declaration 2008*.

Offences involving Iran

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, an offence against section 27 of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945* is prescribed to the extent that the conduct constituting the offence is a contravention of a provision of the *Charter of the United Nations (Sanctions—Iran) Regulations 2025* that is a UN sanction enforcement law (within the meaning of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945*).

Note: For the provisions of the above regulations that constitute a UN sanction enforcement law within the meaning of the *Charter of the United Nations Act 1945*, see table item 7 of Schedule 1 to the *Charter of the United Nations (UN Sanction Enforcement Law) Declaration 2008*.

###### 6 Meaning of proliferation financing—offences against the Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, an offence against section 16 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Act 2011* is prescribed to the extent that the conduct constituting the offence is a contravention:

(a) of one or more of the sanction laws (within the meaning of that Act) covered by subsection (2) or (3) of this section; or

(b) of a condition of an authorisation under such a sanction law.

Offences involving the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea

(2) This subsection covers the following sanction laws:

(a) regulation 13 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves providing a sanctioned service of a kind mentioned in any one or more of the following provisions of those regulations:

(i) subregulation 5(8);

(ii) subregulation 5(9);

(iii) subregulation 5(10);

(iv) subregulation 5(11);

(b) regulation 13A of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves engaging in a sanctioned commercial activity within the meaning of that term in regulation 5CA of those regulations;

(c) regulation 14 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves assets made available to, or for the benefit of, a designated person or entity for a country mentioned in item 1 of the table in regulation 6 of those regulations;

(d) regulation 15 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the controlled asset is owned or controlled by a designated person or entity for a country mentioned in item 1 of the table in regulation 6 of those regulations.

Offences involving Iran

(3) This subsection covers the following sanction laws:

(a) regulation 12 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves making a sanctioned supply within the meaning of subregulation 4(1) of those regulations involving export sanctioned goods described in item 2 of the table in subregulation 4(2) of those regulations;

(b) regulation 13 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves providing a sanctioned service within the meaning of subregulation 5(4) of those regulations, of a kind mentioned in item 2 of the table in that subregulation;

(c) regulation 14 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the contravention involves assets made available to, or for the benefit of, a designated person or entity for a country mentioned in item 4 of the table in regulation 6 of those regulations;

(d) regulation 15 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, if the controlled asset is owned or controlled by a designated person or entity for a country mentioned in item 4 of the table in regulation 6 of those regulations.

###### 7 Meaning of proliferation financing—prescribed laws of the Commonwealth

For the purposes of subparagraph (d)(ii) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, the following laws of the Commonwealth are prescribed:

(a) the *Chemical Weapons (Prohibition) Act 1994*;

(b) the *Comprehensive Nuclear‑Test‑Ban Treaty Act 1998*;

(c) the *Crimes (Biological Weapons) Act 1976*;

(d) the *Nuclear Non‑Proliferation (Safeguards) Act 1987*;

(e) the *Weapons of Mass Destruction (Prevention of Proliferation) Act 1995*.

###### 8 Meaning of proliferation financing—prescribed Commonwealth, State and Territory offences

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (g) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, offences in Part XIII of the *Customs Act 1901* that involve export of prohibited exports are prescribed, if the export is prohibited under one or more of the following provisions of the *Customs (Prohibited Exports) Regulations 1958*:

(a) Division 2A of Part 3 of those regulations (exportation of goods in relation to autonomous sanctions);

(b) regulation 13CO (exportation of goods to Democratic People’s Republic of Korea);

(c) regulation 13CQ (exportation of certain goods to Iran);

(d) provisions in Division 4A of Part 3 of those regulations to the extent that the contraventions relate to the exportation of items listed in Part 2 of the *Defence and Strategic Goods List 2024* (as incorporated for the purposes of identifying prohibited exports by paragraph 112(2A)(aa) of the *Customs Act 1901)*.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (g) of the definition of ***proliferation financing*** in section 5 of the Act, offences in Part XIII of the *Customs Act 1901* that involve import of prohibited imports are prescribed, if the import is prohibited under one or more of the following provisions of the *Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 1956*:

(a) regulation 4Y;

(b) regulation 4Z.

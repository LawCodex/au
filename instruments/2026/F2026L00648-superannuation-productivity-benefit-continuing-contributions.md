**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

**4 Schedules**

Schedule 1 amends the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2013* as set out in the application items in the Schedule.

**5 Application of amendments made by this instrument**

(1) The amendments made by Schedule 1 to this instrument apply in relation to a pay period that ends on 1 July 2026 or a later day.

(2) To avoid doubt, the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2013*, as in force immediately before 1 July 2026, applies in relation to pay periods ending before 1 July 2026.

**Schedule 1—Amendments**

***Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2013***

**1 Subsection 3(1)**

Omit “1 July 2014”, substitute “1 July 2026”.

**2 Table in subsection 3(1)**

Omit the table in subsection 3(1), substitute:

| **Pay period (weekly) rate of salary** | **Pay period (weekly) contribution** |
|---|---|
| Less than $112.75 | $13.53 |
| $112.75 or more | An amount equal to the employer’s *individual superannuation guarantee amount* on the relevant *QE day* for the person as defined under the *Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992*. |

Note: Section 3 of this instrument, as in force immediately before 1 July 2026, applied in relation to pay periods ending between 1 July 2013 and 30 June 2026.

**3 Subsection 3(2)**

Repeal the subsection.

# Work Health and Safety (Construction Induction Training — Defence Technical Specialists) Exemption 2026

*Work Health and Safety Regulations 2011*

Regulation 684

**Work Health and Safety (Construction Induction Training — Defence Technical Specialists) Exemption 2026**

Comcare, acting under regulation 684 of the *Work Health and Safety Regulations 2011* (WHS Regulations) and having had regard to all relevant matters, grants exemption from compliance with regulation 316, and subregulations 317(1), 317(2) and 326(1), of the WHS Regulations on the terms prescribed in the attached Exemption Instrument.

Date: 7 May 2026

Justin Napier

General Manager, Regulatory Operations Group, Comcare

[image: Australian Government /Comcare]

**EXEMPTION INSTRUMENT**

### 1. Name

This exemption Instrument may be cited as the *Work Health and Safety (Construction Induction Training — Defence Technical Specialists) Exemption 2026.*

### 2. Applicant

The exemption has been granted on the written application of the Department of Defence (**Defence**).

### 3. Definitions

In this Instrument:

***DCE Program*** *means* work within the business or undertaking of Defence whereby each of Australia’s 3 Hobart-class Destroyers will be rotated through the Osborne Naval Shipyard in South Australia for maintenance and enhancement by the end of 2034, or such further time as required.

***Technical Specialists*** *means* workers recruited from overseas to carry out construction work, as part of the DCE Program, that involves the provision of technical support for the installation, set-to-work, integration and testing of platform and combat system hardware equipment.

***GCIT*** *means* general construction induction training as defined in regulation 5 of the WHS Regulations.

### 4. Persons to whom the exemption applies

The exemption applies to:

Technical Specialists carrying out construction work on the DCE Program; and

persons conducting a business or undertaking (**PCBUs**) who have a Technical Specialist carrying out, or about to carry out, construction work that is: for the business and undertaking of that PCBU; and part of the DCE Program.

### 5. The work or thing to which the exemption relates

The exemption applies in relation to:

construction work carried out, or about to be carried out, as part of the DCE Program; and

the requirement for workers who are Technical Specialists to have:

successfully completed GCIT; and

available evidence of having successfully completed GCIT.

### 6. Circumstances in which the exemption applies

This exemption applies so that:

PCBUs:

(i) are not required to ensure that GCIT is provided to a Technical Specialist before the Technical Specialist carries out construction work - regulation 316;

(ii) may direct or allow a Technical Specialist to undertake construction work notwithstanding that the Technical Specialist has not successfully completed GCIT - subregulation 317(1); and

(iii) are not required to ensure that a Technical Specialist holds a GCIT card or GCIT certification - subregulation 317(2); and

Technical Specialists, as workers, do not have to keep available for inspection a GCIT card or GCIT certification - subregulation 326(1).

### 7. Regulations to which the exemption applies

This exemption applies in relation to:

regulation 316, and subregulations 317(1) and (2), which imposes duties on PCBUs who engage, direct or allow workers to carry out construction work; and

subregulation 326(1) which imposes duties on workers who carry out construction work.

### 8. Conditions placed on the exemption

This exemption has the following conditions requiring Defence, as the Applicant, to:

ensure that before a Technical Specialist carries out any construction work as part of the DCE Program: work health and safety induction training (**WHS IT**) is provided to the Technical Specialist; and the Technical Specialist undertakes a suitable written WHS IT competency assessment.

(b) ensure that the WHS IT:

(i) is provided by trainers who hold at least a Certificate IV in training and assessment (or equivalent);

(ii) includes the following subject areas:

(A) the roles, responsibilities and rights of duty‑holders under the *Work Health and Safety Act 2011* and WHS Regulations;

(B) health and safety consultation, and reporting, processes;

(C) the principles of risk management;

(D) common construction hazards and control measures;

(E) safety information and documentation relevant to the work and workplace; and

(F) emergency and incident management procedures;

(iii) includes a physical WHS tour of the workplace at Osborne Naval Shipyard; and

(iv) caters for Technical Specialists who are not fully conversant in English by providing them with an interpreter for WHS IT.

maintain a register of the date of completion of: WHS IT of each Technical Specialist; and written WHS IT competency assessment of each Technical Specialist.

keep a record of: the WHS IT course content; and the written answers of each WHS IT competency assessment.

(e) ensure Technical Specialists carrying out construction work are supervised at all times by a person who:

(i) has successfully completed GCIT training; and

(ii) who holds a Naval Shipbuilding and Sustainment Identification Card.

### 9. Commencement date

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

### 10. Expiration date

Unless this instrument is cancelled or its expiration is amended under regulation 697 of the WHS Regulations, this instrument expires on the earliest of the:

completion of the DCE Program; or

close of 31 December 2034.

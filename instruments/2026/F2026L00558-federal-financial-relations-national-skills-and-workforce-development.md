Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Federal Financial Relations Act 2009* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

### Part 2—Determination of national skills and workforce development payments for 2024-25

###### 5 National skills and workforce development payments for 2024-25

For the purposes of section 12A of the Act, each item of the following table specifies an amount that is to be paid to the State specified in the item, for the 2024-25 financial year, for the purpose of making a grant of financial assistance for the purpose of expenditure in accordance with the skills and workforce development agreement:

| Item | For this State: | The amount of financial assistance for the 2024‑25 financial year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | $684,722,498.35 |
| 2 | Victoria | $561,801,638.92 |
| 3 | Queensland | $450,231,922.29 |
| 4 | Western Australia | $241,087,042.06 |
| 5 | South Australia | $151,579,612.32 |
| 6 | Tasmania | $46,135,357.26 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | $38,596,802.26 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | $21,009,378.03 |
|  | **Total** | **$2,195,164,251.49** |

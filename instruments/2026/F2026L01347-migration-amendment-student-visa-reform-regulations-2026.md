Omit “applicant; or”, substitute “applicant;”.

**6 Sub‑subparagraph 1222(2)(a)(ic)(C) of Schedule 1**

Repeal the sub‑subparagraph.

**7 After paragraph 1222(3)(a) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

(aa) An applicant who:

(i) seeks to satisfy the primary criteria for the grant of a Subclass 500 (Student) visa; and

(ii) holds a Subclass 500 (Student) visa (whether on the basis of satisfying the primary criteria or the secondary criteria for the grant of that visa); and

(iii) in the case that the applicant holds that visa on the basis of satisfying the primary criteria for the grant of that visa—is not included in a class of persons specified in subitem (3A);

must be outside Australia.

**8 Paragraph 1222(3)(b) of Schedule 1**

Omit “An”, substitute “Subject to paragraph (e), any other”.

**9 After paragraph 1222(3)(d) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

(da) An application may be made by a person (the ***secondary applicant***) who is seeking to satisfy the secondary criteria for the grant of a Subclass 500 (Student) visa only if:

(i) the applicant (the ***primary applicant***) who is seeking to satisfy the primary criteria for the grant of that visa is a Defence student, a Foreign Affairs student or holds a passport from a Pacific‑regional country or a country specified in subregulation (5A); or

(ii) the secondary applicant is in Australia and, at the commencement of this subparagraph, held a Subclass 500 (Student) visa (the ***earlier visa***) on the basis of satisfying the secondary criteria for the grant of the earlier visa, and in respect of which the primary applicant satisfied the primary criteria; or

(iii) the secondary applicant is in Australia and, before the commencement of this subparagraph, applied for a Subclass 500 (Student) visa (the ***earlier visa***) which was granted, on or after that commencement, on the basis of satisfying the secondary criteria for the grant of the earlier visa, and in respect of which the primary applicant satisfied the primary criteria; or

(iv) the secondary applicant is in Australia, was born in Australia on or after the commencement of this subparagraph, is a child of the primary applicant and, at that commencement, the primary applicant held a Subclass 500 (Student) visa (the ***earlier visa***) on the basis of satisfying the primary criteria for the grant of the earlier visa; or

(v) the secondary applicant is in Australia, was born in Australia on or after the commencement of this subparagraph, is a child of the primary applicant and, before that commencement, the primary applicant applied for a Subclass 500 (Student) visa (the ***earlier visa***) which was granted, on or after that commencement, on the basis of satisfying the primary criteria for the grant of the earlier visa; or

(vi) the secondary applicant is included in a class of persons specified in an instrument under paragraph (5)(c).

**10 Paragraph 1222(3)(e) of Schedule 1**

Omit “may”, substitute “must”.

**11 After subitem 1222(3) of Schedule 1**

(a) Defence students;

(d) classes of persons specified in an instrument under paragraph (5)(d).

(4A) This subitem applies to an applicant if the applicant satisfies each of the following paragraphs:

**13 Subparagraph 1222(4)(b)(i) of Schedule 1**

Repeal the subparagraph.

**14 After subitem 1222(4) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

(4B) This subitem apples to an applicant if the applicant satisfies each of the following paragraphs:

(a) the applicant is not the holder of a substantive visa;

(b) the last substantive visa held by the applicant was a student visa (the ***earlier visa***);

(c) the following circumstances exist in relation to the earlier visa:

(i) the earlier visa was cancelled;

(ii) the ART made the decision to set aside and substitute the cancellation decision or the Minister’s decision not to revoke the cancellation;

(iii) the ART’s decision was made after the day when the earlier visa ceased to be in effect;

(d) the application is made within 28 days after:

(i) if the ART’s decision is given to the applicant orally—the applicant is taken, under subsection 368(7) of the Act, to have been notified of the decision; or

(ii) otherwise—the applicant is taken, under section 379C of the Act, to have received the notification of the ART’s decision;

(e) the applicant has not previously been granted a visa based on an application made when the applicant did not hold a substantive visa.

**19 Clause 500.411 of Schedule 2**

###### 500.411

### Part 166—Amendments made by the Migration Amendment (Student Visa Reform) Regulations 2026

###### 16601 Operation of amendments

The amendments made by Schedule 1 to the *Migration Amendment (Student Visa Reform) Regulations 2026* apply in relation to an application for a visa made on or after the commencement of that Schedule.

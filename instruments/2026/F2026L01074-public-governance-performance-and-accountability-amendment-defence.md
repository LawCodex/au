## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Rule 2014

**1 After clause 7 of Schedule 1**

Insert:

###### 8 Defence Delivery Agency

(1) For the purposes of the finance law:

(a) the Defence Delivery Agency is a listed entity; and

(b) the National Armaments Director is the accountable authority of the listed entity; and

(c) the following persons are officials of the listed entity:

(i) the National Armaments Director;

(ii) persons engaged under the *Public Service Act 1999* to assist the National Armaments Director;

(iii) members of the Australian Defence Force whose services are made available to the National Armaments Director;

(iv) officials of other Commonwealth entities, and employees of Commonwealth companies, whose services are made available to the National Armaments Director; and

(d) the purposes of the listed entity include the following:

(i) to deliver materiel acquisition, sustainment and disposal for air, land, maritime (excluding nuclear‑powered submarines) and space capabilities in line with the National Defence Strategy;

(ii) to provide independent advice to the Defence Minister and the Department of Defence across the Defence capability lifecycle including, at project entry to the Integrated Investment Program, advice on project delivery (including optimal delivery options), cost and feasibility, implications for the defence industry and other matters as required;

(iii) to build an integrated and professional delivery workforce;

(iv) to steward the domestic defence industry, including by serving as the primary Defence point for government engagement and international engagement to support industry opportunities;

(v) to provide policy and specialist advice and services, including cost estimation, in support of defence capability;

(vi) to undertake such other relevant tasks as the Defence Minister may require from time to time.

(2) In this clause:

***defence capability*** includes materiel, technology, a platform, a network, a system or a service that is required in connection with the defence of Australia.

***Defence Minister*** means the Minister administering the *Defence Force Discipline Act 1982*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 182(1) of the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Agriculture Department;

(b) ALOP;

(c) Australian territory;

(d) goods.

In this instrument:

***ACP*** means Asian citrus psyllid (*Diaphorina citri*).

***Act*** means the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

***citrus canker*** means disease caused by *Xanthomonas citri subsp. citri*.

***fresh pomelo*** means unprocessed fresh whole fruit of *Citrus maxima*.

***import permit*** means a permit granted under section 179 of the Act that authorises a person to bring or import particular goods into Australian territory.

###### 5 Purpose of this instrument

For the purposes of subsection 182(1) of the Act, this instrument provides that a specified class of goods must not be brought or imported into Australian territory for a specified period.

Note: Goods included in a class of goods specified in Part 2 are ***suspended goods*** for the purposes of the Act.

###### 6 Reasons for making this instrument

(1) For the purposes of subsection 182(5) of the Act, this section sets out my reasons for making this instrument.

(2) I am satisfied that the level of biosecurity risk associated with the bringing or importation into Australian territory of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam is unacceptable, for the following reasons:

(a) I have considered a risk assessment conducted by a biosecurity worker in the Agriculture Department in relation to the bringing or importation into Australian territory of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam;

(b) the risk assessment identified a number of biosecurity risks associated with the bringing or importation of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam, including the risks of pests and diseases (such as ACP and citrus canker) entering into, establishing or spreading in Australian territory;

(c) if ACP or citrus canker enter Australian territory with fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam, there is potential for ACP and citrus canker to cause harm to plant health in Australia. ACP is also a vector of serious citrus diseases. The establishment or spread of ACP or citrus canker in Australian territory would result in costs associated with surveillance, containment, eradication and ongoing management, and would have economic consequences for domestic citrus production and trade;

(d) phytosanitary measures had been agreed to manage the level of biosecurity risk associated with these pests and diseases to a level that is consistent with the ALOP for Australia;

(e) however, the Agriculture Department has identified that a substantial proportion of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam that have been brought or imported into Australian territory have exhibited signs that are consistent with pest or disease issues, which are intended to be identified and addressed through the agreed phytosanitary measures;

(f) having regard to the above, the identified issues indicate that the level of biosecurity risk associated with the bringing or importation of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam has not been managed to a level that is consistent with the ALOP for Australia.

(3) A suspension of the bringing or importation of fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam for 6 months is necessary to allow for further discussions by the Agriculture Department to progress any improvements to manage the biosecurity risk associated with the goods to an acceptable level.

###### 7 Repeal

This instrument is repealed at the end of the period of 6 months beginning on the day this instrument commences.

### Part 2—Suspended goods

###### 8 Class of goods to which this Part applies

This Part applies to the following class of goods—fresh pomelos grown or produced in Vietnam.

###### 9 Suspension of goods to which this Part applies

Goods included in a class of goods to which this Part applies must not be brought or imported into Australian territory for a period of 6 months commencing on the commencement of this instrument.

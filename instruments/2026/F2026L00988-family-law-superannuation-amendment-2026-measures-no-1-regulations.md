### Part 7A—Ministerial directions relating to approved methods or factors

###### 97A Scope of this Part

(1) This Part applies in relation to a trustee of an eligible superannuation plan if there is an approval instrument in force that approves a method or factor (the ***target method or factor***) for, or in relation to, a superannuation interest (the ***target superannuation interest***) in the plan.

Note: Subsections 90XT(3A) and 90YY(4A) of the Act allow the Minister to give a trustee of this kind a direction in relation to the target method or factor.

(2) An ***approval instrument*** is an instrument made by the Minister under subsection 62(1), 70(1) or 79(1) of this instrument.

Note: Approval instruments made under subsection 62(1), 70(1) or 79(1) of this instrument are made for the purposes of certain provisions in section 90XT or 90YY of the Act: see subsections 62(3), 70(2) and 79(2) of this instrument.

###### 97B Circumstances in which a direction may be given

(1) This section prescribes, for the purposes of subsections 90XT(3A) and 90YY(4A) of the Act, circumstances in which the Minister may give a direction to the trustee under either of those subsections in relation to the target method or factor.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the Minister may give such a direction at any time when the Minister is satisfied that it would be appropriate for the target method or factor to be reviewed.

(3) Before the Minister decides whether the Minister is satisfied as mentioned in subsection (2), the Minister must consult with the Australian Government Actuary about the matter.

###### 97C What must be included in a direction

(1) This section prescribes, for the purposes of subsections 90XT(3B) and 90YY(4B) of the Act, matters that must be included in a direction given by the Minister to the trustee under subsection 90XT(3A) or 90YY(4A) of the Act in relation to the target method or factor.

(2) The direction must include all of the following:

(a) the date on which the direction is given;

(b) the name and (if applicable) the ABN of the trustee;

(c) the name of the eligible superannuation plan;

(d) the name of the approval instrument that approves the target method or factor;

(e) information sufficient to identify the target method or factor;

(f) for each thing that the direction requires the trustee to do—the period within which the trustee must do the thing.

(3) The direction must be signed by the Minister and must give the Minister’s name and Ministerial title.

###### 97D What a direction may require the trustee to do

(1) This section prescribes, for the purposes of subsections 90XT(3A) and 90YY(4A) of the Act, things that the trustee may be required to do by a direction given by the Minister to the trustee under either of those subsections in relation to the target method or factor.

Review and recommendations

(2) The direction may require the trustee to do the following:

(a) arrange for a superannuation actuary to conduct a review of the target method or factor, for the purpose of informing the provision of recommendations in accordance with paragraph (b) of this subsection;

(b) after the review is completed, provide to the Department one or more of the following recommendations in relation to the target superannuation interest:

(i) that the Minister continue to approve the target method or factor for, or in relation to, the target superannuation interest, either in its current form or with modifications specified in the recommendation;

(ii) that the Minister no longer approve the target method or factor for, or in relation to, the target superannuation interest, either in its current form or with modifications;

(iii) that the Minister approve a different method or factor (as specified in the recommendation) for, or in relation to, the target superannuation interest, either in addition to, or instead of, approving the target method or factor;

(iv) that the Minister no longer approve any method or factor for, or in relation to, the target superannuation interest;

(c) provide to the Department the reasons for the recommendation or recommendations provided in accordance with paragraph (b) of this subsection.

Note: The Minister approves a method or factor for, or in relation to, a superannuation interest in an eligible superannuation plan by making an approval instrument under subsection 62(1), 70(1) or 79(1).

(3) If the direction requires the trustee to arrange for a review of the target method or factor as mentioned in subsection (2), then the direction may require the trustee to provide some or all of the following to the Department after the review is completed:

(a) a statement that a superannuation actuary has completed the review, and the date on which the review was completed;

(b) the name, address and contact details of the superannuation actuary who conducted the review.

Other relevant information etc.

(4) The direction may require the trustee to provide some or all of the following to the Department:

(a) a statement setting out:

(i) the number of members of the plan who have the target superannuation interest; and

(ii) the age and gender profiles of those members, and any other characteristics of those members that may be relevant to a decision by the Minister about which method or factor (if any) to approve for, or in relation to, the target superannuation interest;

(b) information, as specified in the direction, about actuarial investigations relating to the target superannuation interest that have been, or are to be, carried out;

(c) any actuarial report, or reports, relating to the target superannuation interest that are specified in the direction;

(d) a copy of the governing rules of the eligible superannuation plan.

Note: The direction may require the trustee to provide something mentioned in this subsection whether or not the direction (or any other direction) requires the trustee to arrange for a review of the target method or factor, and to provide recommendations, as mentioned in subsection (2).

Administrative information

(5) The direction may require the trustee to provide some or all of the following to the Department:

(a) the name, address and (if applicable) ABN of the trustee;

(b) a telephone number and email address for the trustee;

(c) the name and (if applicable) ABN of the eligible superannuation plan;

(d) if the eligible superannuation plan has at any time had a different name or ABN—all previous names or ABNs (as appropriate) of the plan, the dates on which each change to the name or ABN of the plan occurred, and the mechanism by which each such change was made.

# Veterans’ Entitlements (Eligibility for Treatment—Improved Access to Treatment for Tinnitus) Determination 2026

Veterans’ Entitlements (Eligibility for Treatment—Improved Access to Treatment for Tinnitus) Determination 2026

I, Luke Brown, delegate of the Repatriation Commission, make the following instrument.

Dated 23 March 2026

Luke Brown, First Assistant Secretary Policy and Research Division

Department of Veterans’ Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Eligibility for Treatment—Improved Access to Treatment for Tinnitus) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 17 March 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 88A of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

***Commission*** means the body corporate known as the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Commission established under section 361 of the MRCA.

***MRCA*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

***specified condition*** means tinnitus, where that condition is defined in a Statement of Principles.

***Statement of Principles*** means a determination made by the Repatriation Medical Authority, for the purposes of the Act and the MRCA, under subsection 196B(3) of the *Act*.

**5 Specified class of persons**

For paragraphs 88A(1)(a) and (d) of the Act, a person is in a specified class if both of the following apply to that person:

the person has made a claim under paragraph 319(1)(a) of the MRCA, which:

relates to the specified condition; and

has been accepted by the Commission under section 23 of the MRCA;

in addition to making the claim referred to in paragraph (a), the person has made a claim for compensation under paragraph 319(1)(d) of the MRCA, which:

relates to the specified condition; and

has not yet been determined by the Commission.

**Specified kind of treatment**

A person in a class specified in section 5 of this instrument is eligible for treatment that is provided by Australian Hearing Services under the Improved Access to Treatment for Tinnitus pilot.

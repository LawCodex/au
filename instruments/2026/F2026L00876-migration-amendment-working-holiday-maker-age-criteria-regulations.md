## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Migration Regulations 1994

**1 Paragraph 1224A(3)(a) of Schedule 1**

Omit “for this paragraph”, substitute “made for this item under subregulation 2.07(5)”.

**2 Subparagraph 1224A(3)(aaa)(ii) of Schedule 1**

Omit “made under”, substitute “mentioned in”.

**3 After paragraph 1224A(3)(aaa) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

(aab) The applicant must be aged at least 18 and no more than:

(i) unless subparagraph (ii) applies—35; or

(ii) if the instrument mentioned in paragraph (a) specifies a younger age for the foreign country that issued the passport the applicant holds (or, if paragraph (aaa) applies, held)—that age.

**4 After subitem 1225(3) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

(3AA) The applicant is aged at least 18 and no more than:

(a) unless paragraph (b) applies—35; or

(b) if the instrument mentioned in subitem (3) specifies a younger age for the foreign country that issued the passport the applicant holds (or, if paragraph (3BC)(b) applies, held)—that younger age.

**5 Subitem 1225(5) of Schedule 1 (note)**

Repeal the note.

**6 Paragraph 417.211(1)(a) of Schedule 2**

Omit “paragraph (2)(a)”, substitute “subclause (2)”.

**7 Subclause 417.211(2) of Schedule 2**

Repeal the subclause, substitute:

(2) The applicant holds a working holiday eligible passport of a kind specified in a legislative instrument made by the Minister for the purposes of this subclause.

**8 Clause 462.211 of Schedule 2**

Omit “462.212,”.

**9 Clause 462.211A of Schedule 2**

Omit “clause 462.212 and”.

**10 Paragraph 462.211B(1)(a) of Schedule 2**

Omit “462.212,”.

**11 Clause 462.212 of Schedule 2**

Repeal the clause.

**12 In the appropriate position in Schedule 13**

Insert:

### Part 163—Amendments made by the Migration Amendment (Working Holiday Maker Age Criteria) Regulations 2026

###### 16301 Operation of amendments

The amendments of these Regulations made by Schedule 1 to the *Migration Amendment (Working Holiday Maker Age Criteria) Regulations 2026* apply in relation to an application for a visa made on or after the commencement of that Schedule.

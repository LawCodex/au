###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 78(1) and 78(3) of the PGPA Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Australian Immunisation Register*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Immunisation Register Act 2015*.

***Australian Immunisation Register Instrument*** means the *PGPA Act Determination (Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2016) — Establishment.*

***Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2016*** means the special account established by the Australian Immunisation Register Instrument.

***PGPA Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***the special account*** means the Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2026 established by this instrument.

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(a) of the PGPA Act, the Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2026 is established as a special account.

## Part 2 —Special account

###### 6 Amounts that may be credited to the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the following amounts may be credited to the special account:

(a) amounts received in the course of the performance of functions that relate to the purposes of the special account; and

(b) amounts received from any person by, or on behalf of the Commonwealth for the purposes of the special account.

###### 7 Purposes of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(c) of the PGPA Act, the purposes of the special account, in relation to which amounts may be debited from the special account, are:

(a) to make payments in relation to incentive payments by or on behalf of the Commonwealth to recognised vaccination providers who notify the Australian Immunisation Register that they have completed immunisations for children up to seven years of age, through the National Immunisation Program;

(b) to reduce the balance of the special account (and, therefore, the available appropriation) without making a real or notional payment; and

(c) to repay amounts that have been credited to the special account where a court order, Act or other law requires or permits the repayment of an amount received.

###### 8 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(d) of the PGPA Act, the accountable authority responsible for the special account is the Secretary of the Department of Health, Disability and Ageing.

## Part 3—Transitional, revocations and repeal provisions

###### 9 Opening balance of the special account

(1) For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the amount in subsection 9(2) (***opening balance***) is required to be credited to the special account immediately after its establishment.

(2) The opening balance is equal to the amount standing to the credit of the Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2016 immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

###### 10 Revocation of the Australian Immunisation Register Instrument

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the Australian Immunisation Register Instrument is revoked.

###### 11 Additional repeal provisions

(1) For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, Part 3 of this instrument is repealed at the end of the seventh day after the revocation of the Australian Immunisation Register Instrument under section 10 (**date of repeal**).

(2) For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the following definitions in section 4 are repealed on the date of repeal:

(a) definition of Australian Immunisation Register Instrument;

(b) definition of Australian Immunisation Register Special Account 2016.

(3) Despite subsection 11(1), the effects of Part 3 of this instrument remain in force after the date of repeal.

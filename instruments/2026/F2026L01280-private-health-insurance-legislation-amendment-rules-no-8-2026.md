## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Private Health Insurance (Complying Product) Rules 2015

**1 Clause 4 Definitions of Part 1 Preliminary**

Repeal the definition of National Law, substitute:

***National Law***, for a State or Territory, means the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law set out in the Schedule to the *Health Practitioner Regulation National Law Act 2009* (Qld), as it applies (with or without modification) as a law of the State or Territory.

*Note* The Intergovernmental Agreement for a National Registration and Accreditation Scheme for the Health Professions that was made on 26 March 2008 provides for the enactment of the State and Territory legislation mentioned in this definition.

**2 Clause 4 Definitions of Part 1 Preliminary**

Repeal the definition of ***registered podiatric surgeon***, substitute:

***registered surgical podiatrist*** means a surgical podiatrist who holds specialist registration in the specialty of surgical podiatry under the National Law.

Note: The registration requirements for a registered surgical podiatrist for the purpose of these Rules are the same registration requirements for surgical podiatrist as set out in rule 8 of the Private Health Insurance (Accreditation) Rules as made from time to time.

**6 Note 2 of Schedule 5—Clinical categories**

Repeal Note 2; substitute:

Note 2: The treatments that must be covered are any hospital treatments that are in the scope of cover of a clinical category in relation to which the policy provides cover. The scope of cover includes, without limitation:

any hospital treatment involving the provision of an MBS item number listed in column 3 below; and

except for the clinical category “Podiatric surgery (provided by a registered surgical podiatrist)”—any hospital treatment:

that is provided in relation to a treatment within the scope of cover of a particular clinical category or that involves the provision of an MBS item number listed in column 3 below; and

that involves the provision of an MBS item number listed in Schedule 6 (common treatments) or Schedule 7 (support treatments).

## Schedule 2—Amendments

##### Private Health Insurance (Accreditation) Rules 2011

**1 Clause 4 Definitions of Part 1 Preliminary**

Repeal the definition of National Law, substitute:

***National Law***, for a State or Territory, means the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law set out in the Schedule to the *Health Practitioner Regulation National Law Act 2009* (Qld), as it applies (with or without modification) as a law of the State or Territory.

*Note* The Intergovernmental Agreement for a National Registration and Accreditation Scheme for the Health Professions that was made on 26 March 2008 provides for the enactment of the State and Territory legislation mentioned in this definition.

**2 Clause 8 of Part 2**

Repeal the entire clause, substitute:

**Treatments provided by surgical podiatrists**

If the treatment is of a kind practised by a surgical podiatrist, the standard for that kind of treatment is that it must be provided by a surgical podiatrist who holds specialist registration in the specialty of surgical podiatry under the National Law.

CASA EX43/26 — Repeal of Certain Flight Crew Licensing Exemptions Instrument 2026

1 Name

This instrument is *CASA EX43/26 — Repeal of Certain Flight Crew Licensing Exemptions Instrument 2026*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the time that it is registered.

*Note* This instrument will be repealed in accordance with section 48A of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

3 Repeal of provisions and instrument

The following provisions and instrument are repealed:

(a) Parts 4, 5, 15 and 17 of *CASA EX32/24 — Flight Crew Licensing and Other Matters (Miscellaneous Exemptions) Instrument 2024*;

(b) *CASA EX76/24 — CAR Part 5 – Exemption Instrument 2024*;

(c) subsection 9 of *Civil Aviation Order 95.54 (Part 131 Recreational Activity and Specialised Balloon Operations) Instrument 2024*.

# Radiocommunications (Spectrum Licence Renewal – Information and Documents) Instrument 2026

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following instrument under subsections 77A(6) and (7) of the *Radiocommunications Act 1992*.

Dated: 21 May 2026

Adam Suckling

[signed]

Member

Michael Brealey

[signed]

General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### Part 1 Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This is the *Radiocommunications (Spectrum Licence Renewal – Information and Documents) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the start of the day after the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation is available, free of charge, at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 77A(6) and (7) of the Act.

###### 4 Interpretation

(1) In this instrument, unless the contrary intention appears:

***1800 MHz band*** means the following frequency bands:

(a) 1710 MHz to 1785 MHz;

(b) 1805 MHz to 1880 MHz.

***2 GHz band*** means the following frequency bands:

(a) 1920 MHz to 1980 MHz;

(b) 2110 MHz to 2170 MHz.

***2.3 GHz band*** means the frequency band 2302 MHz to 2400 MHz.

***2.5 GHz band*** means the following frequency bands:

(a) 2500 MHz to 2570 MHz;

(b) 2620 MHz to 2690 MHz.

***2.5 GHz mid-band gap*** means the frequency band 2570 MHz to 2620 MHz.

***3.4 GHz band*** means the frequency band 3400 MHz to 3700 MHz.

***700 MHz band*** means the following frequency bands:

(a) 703 MHz to 748 MHz;

(b) 758 MHz to 803 MHz.

***850/900 MHz band*** means the following frequency bands:

(a) 824 MHz to 845 MHz;

(b) 869 MHz to 915 MHz.

***Act*** means the *Radiocommunications Act 1992*.

***authorised person***, in relation to a spectrum licence, means a person authorised under subsection 68(1) of the Act by the licensee of the licence to operate radiocommunications devices under the licence.

***fixed station*** means a station that is not a mobile station.

***frequency range***, for a spectrum licence, means a part of the spectrum in which operation of radiocommunications devices is authorised under the licence.

Note: The frequency ranges for a spectrum licence are specified in the condition required to be included in the licence by paragraph 66(1)(a) of the Act.

***GDA94*** means the geodetic datum designated as the “Geocentric Datum of Australia (GDA94)”, gazetted in the Commonwealth of Australia *Gazette* No. GN 35 on 6 September 1995, as existing on that date.

Note 1: Commonwealth of Australia *Gazette* No. GN 35 is available, free of charge, from the Federal Register of Legislation at www.legislation.gov.au.

Note 2: More information on GDA94 is available from the Geoscience Australia website at www.ga.gov.au.

***geographic area***, for a frequency range, means the area within which operation of a radiocommunications device is authorised under a spectrum licence in relation to the frequency range.

Note: Each frequency range for a spectrum licence has a geographic area. If a spectrum licence has two or more frequency ranges, the geographic areas for those ranges may be the same, or they may be different, or some may be the same and some may be different. The geographic area for a frequency range is specified in the condition required to be included in the licence by paragraph 66(1)(c) of the Act.

***mobile station*** has the meaning given by:

(a) the *Radiocommunications (Interpretation) Determination 2025*; or

(b) if a later instrument replaces that determination and defines the expression – the later instrument.

Note: The *Radiocommunications (Interpretation) Determination 2025* is available, free of charge, from the Federal Register of Legislation at www.legislation.gov.au.

***NBN Co*** means NBN Co Limited (ACN 136 533 741).

***rail entity*** means each of the following:

(a) the Public Transport Authority of Western Australia;

(b) Queensland Rail Limited (ACN 132 181 090);

(c) the South Australian Department of Infrastructure and Transport, regardless of whether its name changes after the commencement of this instrument;

(d) Sydney Trains;

(e) Victorian Rail Track.

***record keeping rules*** means the *Audit of Telecommunications Infrastructure Assets – Record Keeping Rules*, made by the ACCC under subsection 151BU(1) of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*, as those rules were in force on 15 December 2023.

Note: The record keeping rules are available, free of charge, from the ACCC’s website at www.accc.gov.au.

***reference date***, for an application for renewal of a spectrum licence, means the 31 January preceding the day the application was made.

***reference period***, for an application for renewal of a spectrum licence, means the period:

(a) commencing on the reference date for the application; and

(b) ending on the third anniversary of the day on which the spectrum licence expires.

***registered***: see subsection (2).

***related body corporate*** has the meaning given by the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***related spectrum licence***: see subsection (3).

***spectrum licensed band*** means each of the following:

(a) the 700 MHz band;

(b) the 850/900 MHz band;

(c) the 1800 MHz band;

(d) the 2 GHz band;

(e) the 2.3 GHz band;

(f) the 2.5 GHz band;

(g) the 2.5 GHz mid-band gap;

(h) the 3.4 GHz band.

***station*** has the meaning given by:

(a) the *Radiocommunications (Interpretation) Determination 2025*; or

(b) if a later instrument replaces that determination and defines the expression – the later instrument.

Note: The *Radiocommunications (Interpretation) Determination 2025* is available, free of charge, from the Federal Register of Legislation at www.legislation.gov.au.

***telecommunications network*** has the meaning given by the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***television entity*** means each of the following:

(a) the Australian Broadcasting Corporation;

(b) Channel Seven Sydney Pty Ltd (ACN 000 145 246);

(c) Nine Network Australia Pty Ltd (ACN 008 685 407);

(d) Network Ten (Sydney) Pty Ltd (ACN 008 664 962).

Note: A number of other expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) ACCC;

(b) ACMA;

(c) apparatus licence;

(d) class licence;

(e) frequency band;

(f) licensee;

(g) radiocommunications device;

(h) radiocommunications transmitter;

(i) Register;

(j) spectrum licence.

(2) In this instrument, a radiocommunications device is ***registered*** if the Register contains the information about the device determined by the ACMA under paragraph 144(1)(e) of the Act.

Note: A spectrum licence is subject to a condition that a radiocommunications transmitter must not be operated unless it is registered, or exempt from registration (see section 69 of the Act). The ACMA may refuse to include in the Register details of a radiocommunications transmitter proposed to be operated under a spectrum licence if the ACMA is satisfied that operation of the transmitter could cause an unacceptable level of interference to the operation of other radiocommunications devices under any licence (see section 145 of the Act).

(3) In this instrument, a spectrum licence is a ***related spectrum licence*** for another spectrum licence if:

(a) both licences authorise the operation of radiocommunications devices in frequency ranges that are in the same spectrum licensed band; and

(b) either:

(i) both licences are held by the same person; or

(ii) one licence is held by a body corporate, and the other licence is held by a related body corporate of that body corporate.

(4) If:

(a) a person applies for renewal of a spectrum licence (the ***subject licence***); and

(b) the person, or a related body corporate of the person, also applies for renewal of another spectrum licence (the ***other licence***); and

(c) the other licence is a related spectrum licence for the subject licence; and

(d) this instrument requires:

(i) the application for renewal of the subject licence to be accompanied by information or a document; and

(ii) the application for renewal of the other licence to be accompanied by information or a document; and

(e) the information or document for the subject licence and for the other licence would be identical, other than the identity of the person making the application; and

(f) the application for renewal of the subject licence is accompanied by:

(i) the information or document; and

(ii) a statement that identifies the other licence and is to the effect that this subsection applies in relation to the information or document and the other licence;

then, despite anything else in this instrument, the application for renewal of the other licence does not need to be accompanied by the information or document.

(5) In this instrument, the following expressions have the same meaning as in the record keeping rules (whether or not they are expressly defined by the record keeping rules):

(a) ***fixed wireless site***;

(b) ***geographic coverage***;

(c) ***mobile site***;

(d) ***radio (fixed)***;

(e) ***radio (mobile)***;

(f) ***RFNSA ID***;

(g) ***technology type***.

Note: The record keeping rules do not expressly define the expressions ***geographic coverage*** and ***technology type***.

(6) In this instrument, unless the contrary intention appears, a reference to a part of the spectrum, frequency band or frequency range includes all frequencies that are greater than but not including the lower frequency, up to and including the higher frequency.

###### 5 References to other instruments

In this instrument, unless the contrary intention appears:

(a) a reference to any other legislative instrument is a reference to that other legislative instrument as in force from time to time; and

(b) a reference to any other kind of instrument or writing is a reference to that other instrument or writing as in force, or existing, from time to time.

Note 1: For references to Commonwealth Acts, see section 10 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*; and see also subsection 13(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003* for the application of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* to legislative instruments.

Note 2: All Commonwealth Acts and legislative instruments are registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note 3: See section 314A of the Act.

###### Part 2 Spectrum licences used in mobile networks

###### 6 Application and interpretation

(1) This Part applies in relation to an application for renewal of a spectrum licence (the ***subject licence***), other than a spectrum licence held:

(a) by, or for the benefit or use of, a rail entity;

(b) by a television entity;

(c) by NBN Co.

(2) In this Part, ***subject licence*** has the meaning given by subsection (1).

###### 7 Information and documents – existing mobile sites and coverage

*Information and documents – existing sites*

(1) In relation to each mobile site where a radiocommunications transmitter is:

(a) operated under any of the following:

(i) the subject licence;

(ii) a related spectrum licence for the subject licence; and

(b) is used for radio (mobile);

an application must be accompanied by the following information:

(c) the site’s RFNSA ID and location;

(d) the technology type;

(e) the frequency band used by each transmitter at the site.

Note 1: This information is to be provided in the document specified in subsection (2).

Note 2: This information is substantially the same as the information specified in paragraph 5(2)(b)(i) of the record keeping rules, but confined to the subject licence and the related spectrum licences for the subject licence.

Example for paragraph (d): 5G mobile technology.

(2) An application must be accompanied by a document that:

(a) is an electronic file; and

(b) is in a format that is supported, and can be read, by Microsoft Excel; and

(c) contains the information specified in subsection (1).

*Information and documents – existing coverage*

(3) In relation to each mobile site where a radiocommunications transmitter is:

(a) operated under any of the following:

(i) the subject licence; and

(ii) a related spectrum licence for the subject licence; and

(b) used for radio (mobile);

an application must be accompanied by the outdoor coverage for the mobile site, by frequency band for each technology type.

Note 1: This information is to be provided in the document specified in subsection (5).

Note 2: This information is substantially the same as the information specified in paragraph 5(2)(b)(iii) of the record keeping rules, but confined to the subject licence and the related spectrum licences for the subject licence.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3), the information must be information about radiocommunications transmitters operated by the licensee or by any related body corporate of the licensee.

(5) An application must be accompanied by a document that:

(a) is an electronic file; and

(b) either:

(i) is in a keyhole markup language format; or

(ii) is in an ESRI Shapefile format; and

(c) is not in the MapInfo TAB format; and

(d) contains the information specified in subsection (3).

###### 8 Information and documents – future use of the licence

*Information and documents – generally*

(1) An application must be accompanied by either (but not both):

(a) the document described in subsection (2); or

(b) the information described in subsections (4) and (7), and the document described in subsection (8).

*Document – no change to use*

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), the document must:

(a) be signed by the chief executive officer or a director of the licensee (the ***signatory***); and

(b) contain a statement to the effect of the statement in subsection (3); and

(c) contain a statement to the effect that the signatory acknowledges that it is a serious offence to give false or misleading information.

Note 1: The effect of the document is that the signatory is declaring that the general use to be made of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence is not intended to change during the reference period for the licence.

Note 2: The ACMA may provide a sample of such a document on its website, at www.acma.gov.au.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), the statement must be to the following effect:

To the best of my knowledge, and on the basis of the information I possess as part of my position at [name of licensee], I, [name of signatory], declare that [name of licensee] intends to continue making the same general use of radiocommunications devices operated under [subject licence number or identifier] (if renewed) in the period commencing on [the 31 January immediately preceding the application] and ending on [the third anniversary of the day on which the licence expires], as it has done in the year before [the 31 January immediately preceding the application], and that there are no plans to change that use in relation to all or any part of the spectrum or area covered by that licence during that period.

*Information and documents – changed future use*

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), and subject to subsections (5) and (6), the information must include a description of each general use (***future general use***) intended or proposed to be made, by the licensee, of one or more radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence during the reference period for the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note: This subsection only applies where the application is not accompanied by the document described in subsection (2). This means there is likely to be a change in general use made by a licensee of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated, or to be operated, under the subject licence during the reference period of the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Examples of general use: The general use made, or to be made, by a licensee of a radiocommunications device may include:

(a) the provision of a public mobile telephone service, within the meaning of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*;

(b) the provision of a service for communications between hand-held devices and satellites;

(c) the provision of an internet service;

(d) the provision of a communications service, but not to the public;

(e) the internal communications of the licensee;

(f) ‘backhaul’, or the transfer of communications for the purposes of a public mobile telephone service.

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4), a description of a future general use must include the following:

(a) the technology type of the radiocommunications devices relevant to the use;

(b) the nature of the proposed service (if any) to be provided as part of the use;

(c) the end-users (if any) of any such proposed service.

(6) The information does not need to include a description of a future general use if the use is the same as the general use of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence in the year before the reference period for the licence.

(7) If, for any:

(a) part of the frequency range for the subject licence; or

(b) part of a geographic area for a frequency range for the licence;

a general use is described for the purposes of subsection (4), for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b) the information must include a description of:

(c) the part of the frequency range where that future general use is to be made; and

(d) the part of the geographic area where that future general use is to be made; and

(e) the month and year from when that future general use is to be made.

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the document must:

(a) be signed by the chief executive officer or a director of the licensee (the ***signatory***); and

(b) refer to the information specified in subsections (4) and (7); and

(c) contain a statement to the effect that the signatory acknowledges that it is a serious offence to give false or misleading information.

Note 1: The effect of the document is that the signatory is declaring that the general use to be made of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated or to be operated under the subject licence is intended to change during the reference period for the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note 2: The ACMA may provide a sample of such a document on its website, at www.acma.gov.au.

###### 9 Information and documents – authorised persons

(1) Subject to subsection (2), an application must be accompanied by the following information for each geographic area that relates to the subject licence:

(a) the parts of that geographic area where an authorised person is authorised to operate radiocommunications devices under the licence;

(b) for each such part – the name of each such authorised person;

(c) for each such part – the frequency range, or part of the frequency range, within which the authorised person is authorised to operate radiocommunications devices under the licence;

(d) such information as is necessary to allow a person to identify how the geographic information contained in the document specified in subsection (3) relates to the other information specified in paragraph (a), (b) or (c).

Note: To the extent this information consists of geographic information, the geographic information is to be provided in the document specified in subsection (3).

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to an authorised person that:

(a) only operates a radiocommunications device that is not required to be registered under section 69 of the Act; or

(b) is a related body corporate of the licensee who made the application.

(3) An application must be accompanied by a document that:

(a) is an electronic file; and

(b) either:

(i) is in a keyhole markup language format; or

(ii) is in an ESRI Shapefile format; and

(c) is not in the MapInfo TAB format; and

(d) contains the geographic information specified in subsection (1).

Note: The document does not need to contain the other information specified in subsection (1). That other information must be provided, but may be provided in another document.

###### 10 Requirements to be met by information provided

(1) Information provided in accordance with this Part:

(a) must be true and correct on the reference date for the application; and

(b) to the extent the information is geographic information, and subject to subsections (2) and (3) – must be described using GDA94.

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in relation to geographic information that is provided in a document that is in a keyhole markup language format.

(3) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in relation to geographic information that is provided for the purposes of subsection 8(7).

(4) Information provided in accordance with section 7:

(a) only needs to accompany the application to the extent it relates to the operation of radiocommunications transmitters under:

(i) the subject licence; or

(ii) a related spectrum licence for the subject licence; and

(b) must not include information about the operation of radiocommunications devices under any other spectrum licence, an apparatus licence or a class licence.

(5) Information provided in accordance with section 8:

(a) only needs to accompany the application to the extent it relates to the operation of radiocommunications devices under the subject licence; and:

(b) may include information about the operation of radiocommunications devices under any other spectrum licence, an apparatus licence or a class licence.

###### 17 Information and documents – existing sites

(a) operated under any of the following:

(i) the subject licence;

(ii) a related spectrum licence for the subject licence; and

(b) is used for a telecommunications network primarily for communications by a rail entity or a television entity;

an application must be accompanied by the following information:

(c) the site’s location;

(d) the technology type;

(e) the frequency band used by each transmitter at the site.

Note 1: This information is to be provided in the document specified in subsection (2).

Note 2: This information is similar to the information specified in paragraph 5(2)(b)(i) of the record keeping rules. However, rail entities and television entities are not subject to the record keeping rules, and operate radiocommunications devices for different purposes to the entities subject to the record keeping rules.

(2) An application must be accompanied by a document that:

(a) is an electronic file; and

(b) is in a format that is supported, and can be read, by Microsoft Excel; and

(c) contains the information specified in subsection (1).

###### 18 Information and documents – future use of the licence – television entities

*Application*

(1) This section applies in relation to a subject licence held by a television entity.

*Information and documents – generally*

(2) An application must be accompanied by either (but not both):

(a) the document described in subsection (3); or

(b) the information described in subsections (5) and (8), and the document described in subsection (9).

*Document – no change to use*

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(a), the document must:

(a) be signed by the chief executive officer or a director of the licensee (the ***signatory***); and

(b) contain a statement to the effect of the statement in subsection (4); and

(c) contain a statement to the effect that the signatory acknowledges that it is a serious offence to give false or misleading information.

Note 1: The effect of the document is that the signatory is declaring that the general use to be made of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence is not intended to change during the reference period for the licence.

Note 2: The ACMA may provide a sample of such a document on its website, at www.acma.gov.au.

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3)(b), the statement must be to the following effect:

To the best of my knowledge, and on the basis of the information I possess as part of my position at [name of licensee], I, [name of signatory], declare that [name of licensee] intends to continue making the same general use of radiocommunications devices operated under [subject licence number or identifier] (if renewed) in the period commencing on [the 31 January immediately preceding the application] and ending on [the third anniversary of the day on which the licence expires], as it has done in the year before [the 31 January immediately preceding the application], and that there are no plans to change that use in relation to all or any part of the spectrum or area covered by that licence during that period.

*Information and documents – changed future use*

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), and subject to subsections (6) and (7), the information must include a description of each general use (***future general use***) intended or proposed to be made, by the licensee, of one or more radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence during the reference period for the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note 1: This subsection only applies where the application is not accompanied by the document described in subsection (3). This means there is likely to be a change in general use made by a licensee of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated, or to be operated, under the subject licence during the reference period of the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note 2: The subject licence may be subject to a condition that limits the general use to which radiocommunications devices operated under the licence may be made. Unless that condition is varied or revoked by the ACMA, or not included on any renewed licence, the licensee may not be permitted to change the general use made, or to be made, of radiocommunications devices under the licence.

Examples of general use: The general use made, or to be made, by a licensee of a radiocommunications device may include:

(a) the provision of a public mobile telephone service, within the meaning of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*;

(b) the provision of a service for communications between hand-held devices and satellites;

(c) the provision of an internet service;

(d) the provision of a communications service, but not to the public;

(e) the internal communications of the licensee;

(f) ‘backhaul’, or the transfer of communications for the purposes of a public mobile telephone service.

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5), a description of a future general use must include the following:

(a) the technology type of the radiocommunications devices relevant to the use;

(b) the nature of the proposed service (if any) to be provided as part of the use;

(c) the end-users (if any) of any such proposed service.

(7) The information does not need to include a description of a future general use if the use is the same as the general use of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence in the year before the reference period for the licence.

(8) If, for any:

(a) part of the frequency range for the subject licence; or

(b) part of a geographic area for a frequency range for the licence;

a general use is described for the purposes of subsection (5), for the purposes of paragraph (2)(b) the information must include a description of:

(c) the part of the frequency range where that future general use is to be made; and

(d) the part of the geographic area where that future general use is to be made; and

(e) the month and year from when that future general use is to be made.

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), the document must:

(a) be signed by the chief executive officer or a director of the licensee (the ***signatory***); and

(b) refer to the information specified in subsections (5) and (8); and

(c) contain a statement to the effect that the signatory acknowledges that it is a serious offence to give false or misleading information.

Note 1: The effect of the document is that the signatory is declaring that the general use to be made of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated or to be operated under the subject licence is intended to change during the reference period for the licence, if renewed.

Note 2: The ACMA may provide a sample of such a document on its website, at www.acma.gov.au.

Note 1: This subsection only applies where the application is not accompanied by the document described in subsection (3). This means there is likely to be a change in general use made by a licensee of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated, or to be operated, under the subject licence during the reference period of the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note 2: In this section, a change in technology may be a different future general use: see subsection (11).

Examples of general use: The general use made, or to be made, by a licensee of a radiocommunications device may include:

(a) the provision of a public mobile telephone service, within the meaning of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*;

(b) the provision of a service for communications between hand-held devices and satellites;

(c) the provision of an internet service;

(d) the provision of a communications service, but not to the public;

(e) the internal communications of the licensee;

(f) ‘backhaul’, or the transfer of communications for the purposes of a public mobile telephone service.

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5), a description of a future general use must include the following:

(a) the technology type of the radiocommunications devices relevant to the use;

(b) the nature of the proposed service (if any) to be provided as part of the use;

(c) the end-users (if any) of any such proposed service.

(7) The information does not need to include a description of a future general use if the use is the same as the general use of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence in the year before the reference period for the licence.

Note: See subsection (11).

(8) If, for any:

(a) part of the frequency range for the subject licence; or

(b) part of a geographic area for a frequency range for the licence;

a general use is described for the purposes of subsection (5), for the purposes of paragraph (2)(b) the information must include a description of:

(c) the part of the frequency range where that future general use is to be made; and

(d) the part of the geographic area where that future general use is to be made; and

(e) the month and year from when that future general use is to be made.

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), the document must:

(a) be signed by one of the following persons (the ***signatory***):

(i) if the licensee is a body corporate – the chief executive officer or a director of the licensee;

(ii) if the licensee is not a body corporate – the licensee;

(iii) if the licensee is not a body corporate – a person authorised to act on behalf of the licensee in relation to the licence; and

(b) refer to the information specified in subsections (5) and (8); and

(c) contain a statement to the effect that the signatory acknowledges that it is a serious offence to give false or misleading information.

Note 1: The effect of the document is that the signatory is declaring that the general use to be made of some or all of the radiocommunications devices operated or to be operated under the subject licence is intended to change during the reference period for the licence, if the licence were renewed.

Note 2: The ACMA may provide a sample of such a document on its website, at www.acma.gov.au.

Note 3: The licensee for some licences held by, or for the benefit or use of, rail entities may not be a body corporate. For example, the licensee may be a State or Territory, a Minister in the government of a State or Territory, or the Secretary of a Department of a State or Territory.

*Interpretation*

(10) In this section, for a subject licence held by, or for the benefit or use of, a rail entity, each of the following is a ***related party*** of the licensee:

(a) unless a State or Territory is the licensee – the State or Territory that governs, controls or established the licensee;

(b) a Minister of the government of that State or Territory;

(c) a body corporate created or established for a public purpose by a law of that State or Territory;

(d) an authority of that State or Territory;

(e) a Department of that State or Territory.

(11) For the purposes of this section, if:

(a) in the year before the reference period for the licence, a person operated radiocommunications devices under the subject licence; and

(b) each such device was based on, or used, GSM-R technology; and

(c) at the reference date for the licence, one or more of the following intends or proposes to operate one or more radiocommunications devices under the licence in the reference period (if renewed):

(i) the person;

(ii) the licensee;

(iii) a related party of the licensee; and

(d) one or more of those devices is based on, or uses, a technology other than GSM-R technology;

then:

(e) the operation of one or more radiocommunications devices using a technology other than GSM-R technology, by one of the persons mentioned in paragraph (c), is a ***future general use***; and

(f) that future general use is not the same as the general use of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence in the year before the reference period for the licence.

Example for paragraph (d): The Future Railway Mobile Communication System (FRMCS) is a technology other than GSM-R technology.

Note: More information about GSM-R and the Future Railway Mobile Communication System (FRMCS) is available, free of charge, from the International Union of Railways at www.uic.org.

(12) Subsection (11) does not otherwise limit or affect whether a future general use is the same as a general use of radiocommunications devices operated under the subject licence in the year before the reference period.

###### 20 Requirements to be met by information provided

(1) Information provided in accordance with this Part:

(a) must be true and correct on the reference date for the application; and

(b) to the extent the information is geographic information, and subject to subsection (2) – must be described using GDA94.

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in relation to geographic information that is provided for the purposes of subsection 18(8) or subsection 19(8).

(3) Information provided in accordance with section 17:

(a) only needs to accompany the application to the extent it relates to the operation of radiocommunications devices under:

(i) the subject licence; or

(ii) a related spectrum licence of the subject licence; and

(b) must not include information about the operation of radiocommunications devices under any other spectrum licence, an apparatus licence or a class licence.

(4) Information provided in accordance with section 18 and section 19:

(a) only needs to accompany the application to the extent it relates to the operation of radiocommunications devices under the subject licence; and:

(b) may include information about the operation of radiocommunications devices under any other spectrum licence, an apparatus licence or a class licence.

###### 2.2A Non‑invasive medical devices intended to maintain patency, or flush the lumen, of another medical device

###### 3 When master file may be approved

(1) The Secretary may approve a technical master file or an update of such a file only if the Secretary is satisfied that the technical master file demonstrates that manufacture of the blood or blood components conforms with each standard that is applicable to the blood or blood components (as in force at the time the Secretary is so satisfied).

(2) The Secretary may approve a plasma master file or an update of such a file only if the Secretary is satisfied that the plasma master file demonstrates that manufacture of the plasma conforms with each standard that is applicable to the plasma (as in force at the time the Secretary is so satisfied).

Note: Guidance on the preparation of plasma master files can be found in the *Guideline on the Scientific Data Requirements for a Plasma Master File (PMF)* published by the European Medicines Agency. The current revision of the Guideline could in 2026 be viewed on the Therapeutic Goods Administration website (www.tga.gov.au).

###### 4 When an approval is in effect

(1) An approval of a master file under clause 3 comes into effect on the day the approval is granted and remains in effect until the latest of the following:

(a) 15 months after the day the approval of the master file is granted;

(b) if the Secretary approves an annual update of the master file—15 months after the day the approval of the annual update of the master file is granted;

(c) if the sponsor makes a request under subclause (2) for an extension of the period for which the approval is in effect, either:

(i) if the Secretary agrees to the request—the day specified under paragraph (2)(b); or

(ii) if the Secretary refuses to agree to the request—the day the sponsor is notified of the refusal.

Requests for extension of the period an approval is in effect

(2) A sponsor may request the Secretary to extend the period an approval is in effect. Such a request must:

(a) be in writing; and

(b) specify the day on which the period, as extended, is to end (subject to subclause (1)); and

(c) be made at least 28 days before the day the approval would otherwise cease to be in effect under paragraph (1)(a) or (b).

(3) On receiving a request under subclause (2), the Secretary must within 28 days either:

(a) agree to the request; or

(b) refuse to agree to the request.

(4) In making a decision under subclause (3), the Secretary may, without limitation, have regard to:

(a) whether the sponsor has made the request because of delays in receiving data from a third party; and

(b) any effect the decision would have on the safety or quality of the therapeutic goods covered by the approval.

Notification of decision

(5) The Secretary must, in writing, notify a sponsor of a decision under subclause (3) as soon as practicable after the decision is made.

###### 5 Effect of approval of a significant update

If the Secretary approves a significant update of a master file, the approval of the master file is taken to apply to the master file as amended.

###### 6 Minor amendments of master file

(1) A sponsor of therapeutic goods mentioned in items 18 or 19 of the table in clause 1 may make minor amendments to a master file after the master file is approved under clause 3.

(2) The approval of the master file is taken to apply to the master file as amended.

Meaning of **minor amendment**

(3) For the purpose of this section an amendment is a ***minor amendment*** if the amendment is not likely to have a significant effect on the quality or safety of the therapeutic goods to which the master file relates.

Note: If a master file as amended includes amendments other than minor amendments, the approval of the master file will not apply to the master file as amended unless the Secretary approves a significant update or an annual update of the master file.

# Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance (Declared Terrorist Incident—Perth CBD) Declaration 2026

I, Daniel Mulino, Assistant Treasurer and Minister for Financial Services, being satisfied of the matters set out in subsection 6(1) of the *Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003*, make the following declaration.

Dated 18 March 2026

Dr Daniel Mulino

Assistant Treasurer\
Minister for Financial Services

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

***the Act*** means the *Terrorism and Cyclone Insurance Act 2003*.

### Part 2—Declaration of terrorist incident

###### 5 Declaration of terrorist incident

(1) Under subsection 6(1) of the Act, the terrorist act covered by subsection (2) constitutes a declared terrorist incident for the purposes of the Act.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), this subsection covers the attempted bombing of a crowd of people which occurred in the suburb of Perth, Western Australia, on 26 January 2026.

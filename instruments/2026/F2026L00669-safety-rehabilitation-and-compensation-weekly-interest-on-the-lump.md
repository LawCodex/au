# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Weekly Interest on the Lump Sum) Instrument 2026

I, AMANDA RISHWORTH, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following instrument.

Dated 29 May 2026

Amanda Rishworth

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Weekly Interest on the Lump Sum) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 21(5) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 6 Specified rate

For the purposes of the definition of ***weekly interest on the lump sum*** in subsections 21(3) and 21A(3) of the Act, the rate of 4.46 per cent per annum is specified for the period from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 26C(2) of the *Native Title Act 1993*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Native Title Act 1993*.

***Geocentric Datum of Australia 2020*** or ***GDA2020*** has the same meaning that Reference Frame has in the *National Measurement (Recognized‑Value Standard of Measurement of Position) Determination 2026* as in force on 12 March 2026.

***White Cliffs opal or gem mining area*** has the meaning given by section 8.

###### 5 Datum and projection for ascertaining the position of points

(1) For the purposes of this instrument, the position on the surface of the Earth of the areas described in this instrument is to be determined by reference to the Geocentric Datum of Australia 2020 (GDA2020).

(2) All coordinates in this instrument are expressed in eastings and northings of the Map Grid of Australia 2020 (MGA2020) Zone 54.

###### 6 Deposited Plan

A reference in this instrument to a Deposited Plan is a reference to a Deposited Plan lodged under the relevant law of New South Wales as the Deposited Plan exists immediately before the commencement of this instrument.

### Part 2—Approved opal or gem mining area

###### 7 Approved opal or gem mining area

For the purposes of section 26C of the Act, the area in New South Wales that consists of the White Cliffs opal or gem mining area is an approved opal or gem mining area.

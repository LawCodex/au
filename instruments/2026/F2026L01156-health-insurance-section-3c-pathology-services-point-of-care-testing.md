## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Section 3C Pathology Services – Point-of-Care Testing Services) Determination 2024

**1 Section 4 (definition of eligible Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health worker)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***eligible Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health worker***:

has the meaning given by the *Health Insurance (Section 3C General Medical Services – Allied Health and other Primary Health Care Services)*; or

means an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander health worker who has attained a Certificate II or higher in Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Primary Health Care or equivalent experience.

**2 Section 4 (definition of health professional)**

Omit “worker.”, substitute “worker, or another staff member designated by the health service who holds current certification as a competent point-of-care testing operator through the First Nations Molecular Point-of-Care Testing Program.”.

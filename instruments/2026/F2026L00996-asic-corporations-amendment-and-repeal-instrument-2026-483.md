# ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/483

ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/483

I, Amanda Zeller, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 27 July 2026

Amanda Zeller

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Amendment and Repeal) Instrument 2026/483*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation; and

(b) commencement of *Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Financial Systems and Other Measures) Act 2025*.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 673(1) of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

Schedule 1—Amendment

*ASIC Corporations (Relief to Facilitate Admission of Exchange Traded Funds) Instrument 2024/147*

**1 Section 4 (paragraph (e) of the definition of** ***ETF*****)**

Renumber as paragraph (d).

**2 Section 4 (paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition of** ***withdrawal fee per security*****)**

Omit “relation an ETF”, substitute “relation to an ETF”.

**3 Paragraph 9(a)**

Omit “671A”, substitute “671D”.

**4 Paragraph 9(a) (notional section 671AA of the** ***Corporations Act 2001*****)**

Omit “671AA”, substitute “671DA”.

**5 Paragraph 9(b)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(b) by, after “671D” in the note to subsection 671B(1), inserting “, and section 671DA, which expands the normal concept of substantial holding and relevant interest in relation to exchange traded funds.”;

**6 Paragraph 9(c)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(c) by inserting at the end of subsection 672AD(1):

“Note: Section 671DA expands the normal concept of relevant interest in relation to exchange traded funds.”;

(d) by inserting at the end of subsection 672BC(1):

“Note: Section 671DA expands the normal concept of relevant interest in relation to exchange traded funds.”.

Schedule 2 – Repeal

*ASIC Corporations (Bidder Giving Substantial Holding Notice) Instrument 2023/685*

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

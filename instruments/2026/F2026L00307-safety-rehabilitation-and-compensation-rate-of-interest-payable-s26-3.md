# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rate of Interest Payable – s26(3)) Notice 2026

I, Amanda Rishworth, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following notice.

Dated 11 March 2026

Amanda Rishworth

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rate of Interest Payable – s26(3)) Notice 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 April 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 26(3) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1998*.

###### 4 Rate of Interest payable under subsection 26(3)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 26(3) of the Act is specified to be the weighted average yield of 90 day bank-accepted bills, as published by the Reserve Bank of Australia, settled immediately prior to the last day of the thirty day settlement period.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

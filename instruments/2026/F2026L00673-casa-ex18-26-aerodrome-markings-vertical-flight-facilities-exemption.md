CASA EX18/26 — Aerodrome Markings (Vertical Flight Facilities) Exemption 2026

1 Name

This instrument is *CASA EX18/26 — Aerodrome Markings (Vertical Flight Facilities) Exemption 2026*.

2 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on the day after it is registered; and

(b) is repealed at the end of 30 April 2029.

3 Definitions

*Note* In this instrument, certain terms and expressions have the same meaning as they have in the *Civil Aviation Act 1988* and the regulations. These include ***aerodrome operator***, ***aeroplane***, ***aircraft***, ***certified aerodrome*** and ***helicopter***.

In this instrument:

***AC 139-10*** means CASA Advisory Circular 139-10, as issued by CASA from time to time.

*Note* As at the commencement of this instrument, the latest version of this Advisory Circular is AC 139-10, v1.0, Guidelines for vertical flight facilities at aerodromes designed for aeroplanes, dated March 2026.

***helicopter area*** has the same meaning as in Division 12 of Chapter 8 of the Part 139 MOS.

***Part 139 MOS*** means the *Part 139 (Aerodromes) Manual of Standards 2019* as in force from time to time.

***vertical flight aircraft*** means a helicopter or a VTOL capable aircraft and does not include a remotely piloted aircraft.

***vertical flight facilities*** means aerodrome facilities provided for the movements of vertical flight aircraft.

*Note* Vertical flight facilities may be provided exclusively for the movements of vertical flight aircraft (such as a final approach and touch down area (FATO) for vertical flight aircraft only), or for the movement of both vertical flight aircraft and aeroplanes.

***VTOL capable aircraft*** means a heavier-than-air aircraft, other than an aeroplane or helicopter, capable of performing vertical procedures by means of more than two lift/thrust units.

4 Application

This instrument applies to an aerodrome operator of a certified aerodrome if the certified aerodrome has vertical flight facilities with markings or markers as recommended and specified in AC 139-10.

5 Exemption — aerodrome operator

The operator of the certified aerodrome is exempt from compliance with subregulation 139.065(3) of CASR to the extent that the subregulation requires the operator to comply with the requirements of Division 12 of Chapter 8 of the Part 139 MOS in relation to the markers and markings of helicopter areas on aerodromes that are inconsistent with the recommendations and specifications mentioned in AC 139‑10 for markers and markings for vertical flight facilities.

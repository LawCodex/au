## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Telecommunications Universal Service Obligation (Standard Telephone Service – Requirements and Circumstances) Determination 2023

**1 Subsection 4(1) (definition of CSG Standard)**

Repeal the definition, substitute

***CSG Standard*** means the *Telecommunications (Customer Service Guarantee) Standard 2023* as amended from time to time.

**2 Subsection 4(1) (definition of request)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***request***, in relation to a standard telephone service, means:

a request made by a person for the supply of a standard telephone service; or

a request that is taken to be made where a primary universal service provider withdraws, replaces or ceases to supply a technology used to provide a standard telephone service to the person by a specified date, and the person is required to obtain the standard telephone service using an alternative technology on or before that date.

**3 Sub-subparagraph 7(1)(a)(iv)(B)**

Omit, ‘section 20’, substitute ‘Part 3’.

**4 Paragraph 7(4)(a)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

the person making the request does not make available, at the expense of the person making the request, an electricity supply sufficient for the technology used (or proposed to be used) to provide the standard telephone service at the premises, and the primary universal service provider has, before refusing to supply the service: given regard to any reasonable steps taken by the person to arrange for a power supply and have the power supply activated; and given the person a written notice that clearly sets out:

(A) the steps the person must take to obtain such an electricity supply; and

(B) the technical or other requirements for that electricity supply including, if relevant, any requirements that must be met by a licensed electrician;

the written notice referred to in subparagraph (ii) above must have been provided by the provider to the person making the request:

(A) if the request is a ‘request’ within the meaning of paragraph 4(1)(a)—within 10 calendar days; or

(B) if the request is a ‘request’ within the meaning of paragraph 4(1)(b)—no less than 90 calendar days before any change of technology.

**5 Subparagraph 7(4)(h)(ii)**

Omit ‘section 21 or section 22’, substitute ‘section 24 or section 25’.

**6 Subparagraph 7(4)(h)(iii)**

Omit ‘sections 23, 24 and 27’, substitute ‘sections 26, 27 and 30’.

**7 Subsection 7(5)**

Omit ‘subsection 21(3)’, substitute ‘subsection 24(3)’.

**8 Paragraph 7(5)(a)**

Omit ‘subsection 21(3)’, substitute ‘subsection 24(3)’.

**9 Paragraph 7(5)(b)**

Omit ‘subsection 21(2)’, substitute ‘subsection 24(2)’.

**10 Section 9 (Repeal)**

Repeal the section, substitute:

This instrument is repealed when the CSG Standard is no longer in force.

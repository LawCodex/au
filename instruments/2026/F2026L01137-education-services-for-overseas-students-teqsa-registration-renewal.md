###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

ESOS agency;

registered provider; and

TEQSA.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

###### 6 Period within which applications for renewal of registration must be made to TEQSA

(1) Applications made by a registered provider to the Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency (TEQSA) for renewal of registration under subsection 10D(1) of the Act, where TEQSA is the ESOS agency for the registered provider, must be made:

(a) at least 90 days prior to the date on which a provider’s registration is due to expire; or

(b) within such shorter period as TEQSA allows.

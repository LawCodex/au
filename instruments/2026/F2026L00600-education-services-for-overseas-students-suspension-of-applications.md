# Education Services for Overseas Students (Suspension of Applications for Registration to the National VET Regulator) Determination 2026

I, The Hon Julian Hill MP, Assistant Minister for International Education, make the following determination.

Dated 18 May 2026

The Hon Julian Hill MP

Assistant Minister for International Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Education Services for Overseas Students (Suspension of Applications for Registration to the National VET Regulator) Determination 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 14E(1) and 14F(1) of the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) course;

(b) National VET Regulator;

(c) provider;

(d) registered;

(e) registered VET provider.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000.*

***National Register*** has the same meaning as in the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011.*

###### 5 Suspension of making applications

(1) For the purposes of subsection 14E(1) of the Act, no applications may be made to the National VET Regulator under section 9 of the Act until after the day 12 months after the day this instrument commences.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 14F(1) of the Act, no applications may be made to the National VET Regulator under section 10H of the Act until after the day 12 months after the day this instrument commences.

###### 6 Applications to which this instrument applies

(1) Subject to subsection (2), for the purposes of subsections 14E(4) and 14F(4), this instrument applies to all applications made to the National VET Regulator under sections 9 and 10H of the Act, unless the application was made by:

(a) a government school; or

(b) a registered VET provider that is owned or controlled (whether directly or indirectly) by a State or Territory; or

(c) a Table A provider (within the meaning of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*).

(2) For the purposes of subsection 14F(4) of the Act, this instrument applies to all applications made to the National VET Regulator under section 10H, unless:

(a) the application relates to adding a location for a course the relevant provider is registered to deliver; or

(b) the application:

(i) relates to a course identified on the National Register as superseding another course listed on the National Register (the ***superseded course***); and

(ii) is made by a registered VET provider that, at the time the application was made, was registered under the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011* to deliver the superseded course.

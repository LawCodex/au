###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1061ZAAK(8) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Fares allowance for travel by taxi

The amount referred to in subsection 1061ZAAK(3) of the *Social Security Act 1991* is varied to the rate of cents per kilometre for cars for an income year determined, from time to time, by legislative instrument under subsection 28-25(4) of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997*.

###### 5 Fares allowance for travel by another vehicle

In subsection 1061ZAAK(4) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, omit the table and substitute:

| **Vehicle** | **Rate of fares allowance per kilometre** |
|---|---|
| All private vehicles (excluding taxis) | The rate of cents per kilometre for cars for an income year determined, from time to time, by legislative instrument under subsection 28-25(4) of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* |

# List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Australia’s High Seas Permits) Instrument, October 2026

I, BELINDA JAGO, Branch Head, Ocean and Wildlife Branch, as Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following instrument.

Date 7 October 2026

BELINDA JAGO

Belinda Jago

Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Australia’s High Seas Permits) Instrument, October 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 303DC(1)(a) of the EPBC Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

**EPBC Act** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

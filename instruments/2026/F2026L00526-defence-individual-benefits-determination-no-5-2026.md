###### 4 Purpose

The purpose of this Determination is to re-credit a member’s leave credits when they are required to take leave in connection with travel between Kyiv, Ukraine and Warsaw, Poland due to circumstances affecting the ability to travel by air.

###### 5 Application

This Determination applies to the member who holds employee identification number 8265858.

###### 6 Definitions

**Defence Determination** means Defence Determination 2016/19, Conditions of service, as in force from time to time.

**Posting location** means Kyiv, Ukraine.

**Decision maker** means Director Attaché and Overseas Management.

**Posting period** means the period starting the on commencement day of this Determination commences and ending on 31 October 2026.

**Travel** means any travel undertaken by the member that is in connection with a benefit provided under the Defence Determination.

**Note**: To avoid any doubt, benefits provided include a reimbursement for the cost of travel.

###### 7 Re-credit of leave credits when undertaking travel

This section applies if all of the following have been met.

The member is undertaking travel to or from the posting location and Warsaw, Poland.

The Defence Determination requires the member to take a type of leave while undertaking the travel.

The decision maker is satisfied of one of the following.

The member is unable to travel by air from or to the posting location due to an air space closure between the posting location and Warsaw, Poland.

That air travel between the posting location and Warsaw, Poland is unsafe.

The member is to be re-credited any leave credits that are taken on a day they are undertaking travel to or from the posting location and Warsaw, Poland during the posting period.

**Note:** If there are no air space closures affecting air travel from or to the posting location and the decision maker is satisfied that air travel is not unsafe, this section has no effect and rules that would otherwise apply under the Defence Determination continue to apply.

###### 8 Revocation

This Determination is revoked on 31 October 2026.

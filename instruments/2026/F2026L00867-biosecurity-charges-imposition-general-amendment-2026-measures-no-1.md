## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Amendments

##### Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Regulation 2016

Insert:

***biocontainment unit*** means an isolation unit in a post‑entry quarantine facility in which a consignment, or part of a consignment, of live birds, or birds’ eggs for hatching, is kept to minimise the risk that the live birds or eggs spread, or become infected with, a disease or pest while in the facility.

### Part 2—Application provisions

##### Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Regulation 2016

**16 In the appropriate position in Part 3**

Insert:

###### 15 Amendments made by the Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026

(1) The amendments of items 5, 5A and 7 to 10 of the table in subsection 9(1) of this instrument made by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026* apply in relation to:

(a) a reservation or confirmation of a reservation of a place in a post‑entry quarantine facility that is made on or after 1 July 2026; and

(b) a reservation or confirmation of a reservation of a place in a post‑entry quarantine facility that is made before 1 July 2026 if:

(i) the reservation or confirmation of the reservation was made in relation to an animal or consignment that is to enter the facility on or after 1 July 2026; and

(ii) the amount (the ***relevant amount***) that applied in relation to the animal or consignment under item 5, 5A, 7, 8, 9 or 10 of the table in subsection 9(1) of this instrument, as in force immediately before 1 July 2026, had not been paid before 1 July 2026; and

(iii) no invoice had been issued by the Agriculture Department in relation to the relevant amount before 1 July 2026; and

(iv) no demand for payment of the relevant amount had been made before 1 July 2026.

(2) Items 10A and 10B of the table in subsection 9(1) of this instrument, as inserted by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026*, apply in relation to a reservation or confirmation of a reservation of a place in a post‑entry quarantine facility that is made on or after 1 July 2026.

(3) The amendment of item 14 of the table in subsection 9(1) of this instrument made by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026* applies in relation to the first mooring of a vessel at a port in Australian territory on or after 1 July 2026.

(4) Item 15 of the table in subsection 9(1) of this instrument, as added by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the *Biosecurity Charges Imposition (General) Amendment (2026 Measures No. 1) Regulations 2026*, applies in relation to the carrying out of diagnostic activities on or after 1 July 2026 in relation to goods on board a vessel.

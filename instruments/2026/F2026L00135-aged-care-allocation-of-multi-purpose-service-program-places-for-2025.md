###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 94(1) of the *Aged Care Act 2024*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) entity

(b) Multi-Purpose Service Program

(c) residential care home

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aged Care Act 2024.*

***financial year*** means the 2025-2026 financial year commencing on 1 July 2025 and ending on 30 June 2026.

###### 5 Number of places available for allocation in 2025-2026

For the purposes of subparagraph 94(1)(b)(i), of the Act, the number of places available to be allocated to entities for delivering funded aged care services through the service group residential care under the Multi-Purpose Service Program in the 2025-2026 financial year is 100 places.

For the purposes of subparagraph 94(1)(b)(ii) of the Act, all 100 places under subsection (1) are for a residential care home.

# Comptroller-General of Customs (International Mail Centres) Approval 2026

LIN 25/131

I, Gavan Reynolds AO, Comptroller-General of Custom, make the following instrument.

Dated 19 March 2026

Signed.

Gavan Reynolds AO

Comptroller-General of Customs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Comptroller-General of Customs (International Mail Centres) Approval 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 13 April 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 183UA(2) of the *Customs Act 1901*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in subsection 183UA(1) of the Act, including Customs place.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Customs Act 1901*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Approval

For the purposes of paragraph (f) of the definition of ***Customs place*** in subsection 183UA(1) of the Act, a place specified in column 1 of an item in the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument, and located at the street address in column 2 of the same table item, is approved as a place for the examination of international mail.

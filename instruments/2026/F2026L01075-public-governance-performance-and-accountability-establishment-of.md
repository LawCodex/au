###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 78(1) of the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) accountable authority;

(b) listed entity.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***defence capability*** includes materiel, technology, a platform, a network, a system or a service that is required in connection with the defence of Australia.

***Defence Delivery Agency*** means the Executive Agency that is known as the Defence Delivery Agency and is a listed entity.

***Defence Department*** means the Department administered by the Minister administering the *Defence Force Discipline Act 1982*.

***Executive Agency*** has the same meaning as in the *Public Service Act 1999*.

### Part 2—Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For the purposes of paragraph 78(1)(a) of the Act, the Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026 is established as a special account.

###### 6 Credits to the special account

For the purposes of paragraph 78(1)(b) of the Act, there must be credited to the Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026 amounts equal to the following:

(a) amounts received by the Defence Delivery Agency from the Defence Department, or from another Commonwealth entity, for the performance of functions that relate to the acquisition, sustainment or disposal of defence capability for the Commonwealth;

(b) amounts received by, or on behalf of, the Commonwealth in connection with the performance of functions that relate to the acquisition, sustainment or disposal of defence capability for the Commonwealth.

Note: An Appropriation Act may contain a provision to the effect that, if any of the purposes of a special account is a purpose that is covered by an item in the Appropriation Act (whether or not the item expressly refers to the special account), then amounts may be debited against the appropriation for that item and credited to that special account.

###### 7 Debits from the special account

For the purposes of paragraph 78(1)(c) of the Act, amounts may be debited from the Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026 for the purposes of the special account.

###### 8 Purposes of the special account

The purposes of the Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026 are as follows:

(a) to pay or discharge the costs, expenses and other obligations incurred by, or on behalf of, the Commonwealth for the purposes of the acquisition, sustainment or disposal of defence capability for the Commonwealth;

(b) to make a repayment to the Defence Department of an amount that was previously paid by the Defence Department to the Defence Delivery Agency and credited to the special account under paragraph 6(a);

(c) to reduce the balance of the special account (and therefore the available appropriation for the special account) without making a real or notional payment.

###### 9 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

The accountable authority of the Defence Delivery Agency is responsible for the Defence Delivery Agency Special Account 2026.

Note: Schedule 1 to the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Rule 2014* prescribes a person or group of persons to be the accountable authority of the Defence Delivery Agency.

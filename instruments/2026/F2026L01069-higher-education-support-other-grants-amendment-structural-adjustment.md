###### 2 Commencement

#### Division 2—Structural Adjustment Fund

###### 59F Program objectives

(1) The Structural Adjustment Fund is specified as a program for the purpose of supporting structural adjustment under item 9B of the table in subsection 41‑10(1) of the Act.

(2) The objectives of the program are to:

(a) provide financial assistance to Table A providers to support structural adjustments required as a result of the transition to new funding arrangements under Part 2-2 of the Act (referred to as the managed growth funding system) from 2027; and

(b) ensure that Table A providers can develop and implement new measures to:

(i) grow enrolments, including for students from under-represented backgrounds;

(ii) make structural adjustments to courses to best meet student needs and create more opportunities for Australians from under-represented backgrounds to go to university; and

(iii) improve the quality, accessibility and sustainability of higher education for the communities they serve.

###### 59G Extra conditions of eligibility

(1) A Table A provider must have entered into a funding agreement under section 30-25 of Part 2-2 of the Act for 2026.

(2) A Table A provider must have, and must provide to the Department, a Sustainability Plan, in the format required by the Department, which includes details of the Table A provider’s proposed activities to support its transition to the managed growth funding system.

###### 59H Grants to be made in respect of a year

Grants under this program are made in respect of a year.

###### 59I Method by which the amount of grants under the program are determined

The Minister will determine, in writing, the grant amount for a Table A provider for 2026 under paragraph 41-30(b) of the Act.

###### 59J Conditions that apply to grants

Use of grant

(1) A Table A provider must only use a grant provided under this program for the program objectives set out in section 59F.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), activities that support the program objectives include, but are not limited to, those that:

(a) attract and provide support for the following Australians to participate and succeed in higher education:

(i) students from low socioeconomic backgrounds; and

(ii) students from regional and remote areas of Australia; and

(iv) Indigenous persons; and

(b) support the long-term growth of the higher education sector to meet future skills needs; and

(c) improve the quality, accessibility and sustainability of higher education for the communities they serve; and

(d) increase access to higher education in as many geographic locations as possible, including regional and remote areas; and

(e) promote collaboration between Table A providers and vocational education and training providers to create and support pathways to higher education.

(3) The Table A provider must only use the grant:

(a) in accordance with the Table A provider’s Sustainability Plan provided under subsection 59G(2); and

(b) for activities that are new or additional to the Table A provider’s existing activities.

(4) The Table A provider must spend the grant by 31 December 2030.

Reporting

(5) The Table A provider must submit a progress report, in the format required by the Department, by 31 December 2027.

(6) If the Table A provider has not fully expended the grant by 31 December 2027, the provider must submit a final report in the format required by the Department by the earlier of:

(a) the day that is 30 days after the grant is fully expended by the provider; and

(b) 30 January 2031.

Note: The Minister may impose other conditions that apply to grants under section 41‑25 of the Act.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

Chief Executive Medicare;

hospital treatment;

Secretary.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Health Act 1953*.

***authority approval number*** has the same meaning as in the Regulations.

***authority prescription*** has the same meaning as in the Regulations.

***authority required procedures*** has the same meaning as in the Regulations.

***CTS claim*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

***eligible approved supplier*** means an approved supplier mentioned in column 3 of an item in the table in Schedule 1.

***eligible hospital*** means a hospital mentioned in column 2 of an item in the table in Schedule 1.

***patient of an eligible hospital*** means an in-patient who occupies a bed in an eligible hospital for the purpose of receiving hospital treatment.

***PBS prescriber*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

***pharmaceutical benefit*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

***electronic medication charting system*** means a system which facilitates the electronic management of medications and associated electronic medication chart orders for patients of an eligible hospital.

***electronic medication chart order*** means an order for the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit, prepared and approved electronically and which forms part of the eligible hospital’s electronic medication charting system for the purpose of treating a patient of the hospital.

***paper-based medication chart order*** means a paper-based order for the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit for the purpose of treating a patient of an eligible hospital.

***Regulations*** means the *National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 2017*.

***relevant streamlined authority code*** has the meaning given by the Regulations.

***Rules*** means the *National Health (Supply of Pharmaceutical Benefits—Under Co‑payment Data and Claims for Payment) Rules 2022*.

***Schedule 1*** means Schedule 1 to this instrument.

***special arrangement supply:*** see section 6.

***streamlined authority prescription*** means an authority prescription where authority required procedures are met by inclusion of the relevant streamlined authority code in the prescription.

###### Part 2 – Special arrangements for prescribing, supply and claiming of pharmaceutical benefits

###### 6 Meaning of special arrangement supply

A supply of a pharmaceutical benefit to a person is a ***special arrangement supply*** of the benefit if:

the person is a patient of an eligible hospital;

the benefit is supplied by the eligible approved supplier mentioned in column 3 of the table in Schedule 1 for that eligible hospital;

the benefit is supplied on the basis of a prescription written by a PBS prescriber:

by creating an electronic medication chart order within an electronic medication charting system; or

using a paper-based medication chart order.

###### 7 Pharmaceutical benefits covered by this instrument

This instrument applies to each pharmaceutical benefit that is generally available for supply under Part VII of the Act.

A pharmaceutical benefit is ***generally available for supply under Part VII of the Act*** if:

(a) it does not contain a drug that is subject to a declaration under subsection 85(2A) of the Act; and

(b) it is not subject to a determination under paragraph 85(8)(a) of the Act; and

(c) for a pharmaceutical benefit in relation to which a determination under paragraph 85(8)(b) has been made about the circumstances in which the benefit can only be supplied under a special arrangement under section 100 - the supply is not in those circumstances.

###### 8 Prescribing a special arrangement supply of a pharmaceutical benefit - general

A PBS prescriber may only write a prescription for a special arrangement supply for the purpose of treating a patient of an eligible hospital.

A PBS prescriber may only write an authority prescription for a special arrangement supply where the prescription is a streamlined authority prescription.

Note: Other kinds of authority prescriptions require an authority approval number to be allotted to the prescription by the Chief Executive Medicare or the Minister.

###### 9 Prescribing a special arrangement supply of a pharmaceutical benefit – modification of section 41

Section 41 of the Regulations applies to a prescription for a special arrangement supply with the modifications set out in this section.

A reference to a medication chart is taken to include a reference to an electronic medication chart order or a paper-based medication chart order.

The following provisions do not apply:

paragraphs 41(2)(c), 41(2)(f) and 41(2)(h);

subparagraph 41(1)(b)(ii);

subsection 41(3).

For the purposes of subparagraph 41(2)(a)(i) of the Regulations, in identifying the pharmaceutical benefit the PBS prescriber is not required to comply with subsection 41(2A).

A PBS prescriber must: for an electronic medication chart order - electronically authorise the order in the electronic medication charting system; for a paper-based medication chart order – sign the order.

*Completing electronic and paper-based medication chart orders ‑ authority prescriptions*

If the prescription is a streamlined authority prescription, the electronic medication chart order or paper-based medication chart order must contain the relevant streamlined authority code (if any) for the pharmaceutical benefit.

###### 10 Supplies by eligible approved suppliers

Sections 45, 47 and 51 of the Regulations apply to the special arrangement supply of a pharmaceutical benefit, with the modifications set out in this section.

A reference in sections 45, 47 and 51 of the Regulation to a medication chart prescription is taken to include a reference to a prescription written using an electronic medication chart order or a paper-based medication chart order in accordance with section 41 of the Regulations, as modified by this Special Arrangement.

A reference in section 45 of the Regulations to an electronic medication chart is taken to be a reference to an electronic medication chart order.

The following provisions of the Regulations do not apply: subparagraph 45(2)(b)(i); paragraph 45(2)(c); paragraph 45(2)(d); subsection 45(2B); subsection 45(7).

Section 45 of the Regulation applies as if it also requires an eligible approved supplier, or a person authorised by the eligible approved supplier, to verify in the electronic medication chart order or write in the paper-based medication chart order:

that the pharmaceutical benefit has been supplied; and

the date on which the pharmaceutical benefit was supplied.

A reference in section 51 of the Regulations to writing the words “immediate supply necessary” on a prescription is taken to include a reference to including those words: in the electronic medication charting system, or the part of the copy of the electronic medication chart order within the system, that contains the completed section by which the prescription was written; or in a paper-based medication chart order, or the part of the copy of that chart, that contains the completed section by which the prescription was written.

###### 11 Claims for payment

The Rules apply to a claim for payment for a special arrangement supply with the following modifications:

A reference in the Rules to a medication chart prescription is taken to include a reference to a prescription written using an electronic medication chart order or a paper-based medication chart order in accordance with section 41 of the Regulations, as modified by this Special Arrangement.

Items 20 and 21 of the table to Schedule 1 of the Rules do not apply.

###### 12 Pharmacy records

If the Chief Executive Medicare notifies an eligible hospital in writing that a copy of an electronic medication chart order or a paper-based medication chart order written for a special arrangement supply is required to be submitted, the eligible hospital must submit a copy of the electronic medication chart order or paper-based medication chart order to Services Australia.

*Electronic pharmacy records*

For each special arrangement supply of a pharmaceutical benefit in respect of which a claim is made under section 99AAA of the Act by an eligible approved supplier, the approved supplier must: prepare an electronic pharmacy record; and retain the electronic pharmacy record for at least 2 years from the date the pharmaceutical benefit was supplied.

Note: This subsection establishes record keeping requirements that correspond to the requirements contained in section 61 of the Regulations.

The electronic pharmacy record must contain all information required by the Rules (other than information mentioned in items 20 and 21 in the table in Schedule 1 of the Rules) to be given to the Secretary by an approved supplier for a CTS claim in relation to the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit for the purpose of treating a patient of the eligible hospital.

If the Chief Executive Medicare notifies an eligible approved pharmacy in writing that a copy of an electronic pharmacy record is required to be submitted, the approved pharmacy must submit a copy of the record to Services Australia.

A notification given under subsection (1) or (4) may apply to one or more electronic medication chart orders, paper-based medication chart orders or electronic pharmacy records, as appropriate.

**Part 3 - Application and transitional arrangements**

**13 Definitions**

In this Part:

(a) ***former Special Arrangement*** means the *National Health (Epworth and Cabrini Private Hospitals Paperless Prescribing and Claiming Trial) Special Arrangement 2016* (PB 28 of 2016), as in force immediately before the transition time;

(b) ***transition time*** means 30 September 2026;

(c) ***transitional prescription*** means a prescription for the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit under the former Special Arrangement that was made:

(i) before the transition time; and

(ii) in accordance with the former Special Arrangement.

**14 Supplies made on transitional prescriptions**

Despite the repeal of the former Special Arrangement, section 8 of the former Special Arrangement continues to apply to the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit based on a transitional prescription, as if the repeal had not happened, whether the supply was made before, on or after the transition time.

**15 Claims for supplies made on transitional prescriptions**

(1) This section applies where a claim for payment is made after the transition time for the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit made on the basis of a transitional prescription, whether the supply was made before, on or after the transition time.

(2) Despite the repeal of the former Special Arrangement, section 8 of the former Special Arrangement continues to apply to the claim, as if the repeal had not happened.

**16 Retention of records and provision of documents**

If an eligible approved supplier was required to prepare an electronic pharmacy record under subsection 8(3) of the former Special Arrangement in relation to the supply of a pharmaceutical benefit and retain a copy of that record for 2 years from the date the pharmaceutical benefit was supplied, and that 2 year period has not yet passed:

(a) that requirement continues to apply despite the repeal of the former Special Arrangement; and

(b) subsection 12(4) of this Special Arrangement applies to a notification for a copy of that pharmacy record made after the transition time.

(2) Subsection 12(1) of this Special Arrangement applies to a request for a copy of a medication chart (as defined in the previous Special Arrangement) made after the transition time as if that medication chart were an electronic medication chart order or paper-based medication chart order, as appropriate.

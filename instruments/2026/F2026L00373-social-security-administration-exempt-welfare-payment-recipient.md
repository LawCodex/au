###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

***approved child care service*** has the same meaning as in subsection 3(1) of the Family Assistance Administration Act.

***Family Assistance Act*** means the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999*.

***Family Assistance Administration Act*** means the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999*.

***pre-school*** includes kindergarten.

***program of regular health and development assessments*** means, in relation to a child of a particular age, a series of health and development assessments that are provided to that child:

by one or more treating health professionals; and

in accordance with the schedule of health and development assessments for a child of that age, as set out in the record published by the Northern Territory Government and known as the Personal Health and Immunisation Record.

***school age child*** has the same meaning as in section 123TC of the Act.

***severe disability or severe medical condition*** has the meaning given by Part 2.5 of the Social Security Act.

***Social Security Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***terminal condition*** has the meaning given by Part 2.5 of the Social Security Act.

***treating health professional*** has the same meaning as in subsection 197(1) of the Social Security Act.

***treatment*** includes care.

###### 6 Specified activities—school age child (with terminal condition)

For subparagraph 123UGD(1)(b)(iii) of the Act, the specified activity, for a school age child with a terminal condition, is a program of treatment for the terminal condition, provided by one or more treating health professionals.

###### 7 Specified activities—child (other than a school age child)

Specified number and kind of activities

(1) For paragraph 123UGD(1)(c) of the Act, the specified number and kind of activities for a child (other than a school age child) who has a terminal condition is 1 activity of a kind mentioned in subsection (4).

(2) For paragraph 123UGD(1)(c) of the Act, the specified number and kind of activities for a child (other than a school age child) who does not have a terminal condition are:

(a) for a child who is aged under 3–2 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4);

(b) for a child who is aged 3 or over but under 4:

(i) if the child is unable to participate in an engagement related activity–3 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4);

(ii) otherwise–2 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4) and 1 activity of a kind mentioned in subsection (5);

(c) for a child who is aged 4 or over and attending pre-school–2 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4);

for a child who is aged 4 or over and not attending pre-school:

(i) if the child is unable to participate in an engagement related activity–3 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4); or

(ii) otherwise–2 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (4) and 2 activities of a kind mentioned in subsection (5).

(3) For subsection (2), a child is ***unable to participate in an engagement related activity*** if:

(a) the child has a severe disability or severe medical condition; and

(b) because of that disability or condition, the child is not able to participate in any of the kinds of activities mentioned in subsection (5).

Health related activities

(4) The kind of activities for a child are:

(a) an activity to meet the immunisation requirements (within the meaning of the Family Assistance Act) for the child;

(b) a program of regular health and development assessments for the child;

(c) a program of treatment for the child (however described), provided by one or more treating health professionals.

Engagement related activities

(5) The kind of activities for a child are:

(a) an activity of attending, for at least 8 hours per week, an approved child care service;

(b) an activity of attending playgroup (whether formally facilitated or otherwise);

(c) an activity of attending creche;

(d) an activity of attending Kindergym;

(e) an activity of attending primary school;

(f) any other age appropriate activity that is designed to promote the child’s social, emotional, mental or physical development.

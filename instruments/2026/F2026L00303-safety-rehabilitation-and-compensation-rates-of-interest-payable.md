# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rates of Interest Payable) Notice 2026

I, Amanda Rishworth, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following notice.

Dated 11 March 2026

Amanda Rishworth

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rates of Interest Payable) Notice 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 April 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 97L(3), 97M(7), 97P(1), 97P(2) and 97P(4) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument ***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 6 Rate of interest payable under subsection 97L(3)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 97L(3) of the Act is the cash rate target published by the Reserve Bank of Australia.

###### 7 Rate of interest payable under subsection 97M(7)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 97M(7) of the Act is the cash rate target published by the Reserve Bank of Australia.

###### 8 Rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(1)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(1) of the Act is the monthly average yield for 90-day Bank Accepted Bills for the May preceding the date when the premium or regulatory contribution is payable, plus 7% per annum.

###### 9 Rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(2)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(2) of the Act is the monthly average yield for 90-day Bank Accepted Bills for the May preceding the date when the exit contribution or regulatory contribution is payable, plus 7% per annum.

###### 10 Rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(4)

The rate of interest payable under subsection 97P(4) of the Act is the monthly average yield for 90-day Bank Accepted Bills for the May preceding the date when the exit contribution is payable, plus 7% per annum.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 58B of the *Defence Act 1903*.

###### 4 Definitions

**Defence Determination** means Defence Determination 2016/19, Conditions of service, as in force from time to time.

###### 5 Extension of benefits

Despite paragraph 4.7.3 of the Defence Determination, Part 7 of Chapter 4 of the Defence Determination applies to the family of the deceased member who held the employee identification number 8231781.

###### 6 Cessation of Benefits

Benefits provided under this Determination cease on 30 June 2027.

###### 7 Schedule

The Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 8 Repeal

This Determination is repealed on 1 October 2027.

## Schedule 1—Transitional provisions

###### 1 Application

This Schedule applies to the family of the deceased member who held the employee identification number 8231781.

###### 2 Authorisation of benefits

Any benefit received by the family of the deceased member who held the employee identification number 8231781 for the purpose of Part 7 of Chapter 4 of the Defence Determination between the following days is taken to be authorised by this Determination as though it applied at the time.

a. The day after Part 7 of Chapter 4 of the Defence Determination ceased to apply.

b. The day before the commencement of this Determination.

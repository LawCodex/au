###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 58B of the *Defence Act 1903*.

###### 4 Purpose

This Determination provides a payment in lieu of rent allowance in Australia to a member in specific circumstances.

###### 5 Application

This Determination applies to the member who holds employee identification number 8552302.

###### 6 Definitions

**Decision maker** means one of the following.

Executive Director Relocations and Housing.

Director Relocations and Housing.

**Defence Determination** means Defence Determination 2016/19, Conditions of service, as in force from time to time.

**Rental property** means the property the member held a lease over in Teneriffe, Queensland at the commencement of this Determination.

###### 7 Payment

The member is eligible for a payment of $ 970.00 per fortnight from 2 January 2026 until the earlier of the following dates.

2 January 2027.

A date set by the decision maker.

For the purpose of subsection 1, if the member is eligible for the payment for a part of a fortnight, the amount the member is paid for that fortnight is calculated using the following formula.

[image: Start formula payment open bracket dollars close bracket equals 970 times A divided by 14 end formula]

Where:

**A** is the number of days the member is eligible for the payment in the fortnight.

###### 8 Obligation to notify

The member must notify Defence Housing Australia if any of the following occurs.

The member returns to Australia permanently before 2 January 2027.

The member’s lease agreement ends before 2 January 2027.

The member is evicted from the rental property.

The member sublets the rental property.

A person other than the member occupies the rental property, whether for a fee or not.

###### 9 Overseas conditions of service benefits

Chapter 14, 15 and 16 of the Defence Determination do not apply to the member.

###### 10 Revocation

This Determination is revoked on 3 March 2027.

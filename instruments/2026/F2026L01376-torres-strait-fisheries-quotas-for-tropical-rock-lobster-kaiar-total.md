###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 15A(4) of the *Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984* and subsection 13(1) of the *Torres Strait Fisheries (Quotas for Tropical Rock Lobster (Kaiar)) Management Plan 2018*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Management Plan, including the following:

(a) fishing season;

(b) total allowable catch;

(c) TRL fishery; and

(d) tropical rock lobster.

In this instrument:

***Management Plan*** means the *Torres Strait Fisheries (Quotas for Tropical Rock Lobster (Kaiar)) Management Plan 2018*.

###### 5 Determination of total allowable catch

For the purposes of subsection 15A(4) of the Act and subsection 13(1) of the Management Plan, the total allowable catch of tropical rock lobster in the TRL fishery for the fishing season commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 30 September 2027, is 200,000 kilograms (unprocessed weight).

For the purposes of subsection 15A(4) of the Act and subsection 13(1) of the Management Plan, the total allowable catch of tropical rock lobster in the TRL fishery for the fishing season commencing on 1 December 2027 and ending on 30 September 2028, is 200,000 kilograms (unprocessed weight).

For the purposes of subsection 15A(4) of the Act and subsection 13(1) of the Management Plan, the total allowable catch of tropical rock lobster in the TRL fishery for the fishing season commencing on 1 December 2028 and ending on 30 September 2029, is 200,000 kilograms (unprocessed weight).

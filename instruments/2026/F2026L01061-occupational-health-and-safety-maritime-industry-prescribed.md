###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 4A of the *Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) Act 1993*.

**4 Schedules**

Each instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in that Schedule, and any other item in that Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) Act 1993* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

***the Act*** means the *Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) Act 1993.*

***Norfolk Island*** means the Territory of Norfolk Island, as described in Schedule 1 to the *Norfolk Island Act 1979.*

###### 6 Declaration

A ship: which is not a ship to which paragraphs 10(a) or (b) of the *Navigation Act 1912* would apply if that Act had not been repealed; and which is not a ship to which subsections 6(3) or (3A) of the Act applies; and which is a ship to which paragraph 10(c) of the *Navigation Act 1912* would apply if that Act had not been repealed; and either: of which the majority of the crew are residents of Norfolk Island; or which is operated by any of the following (whether or not in association with any other person, firm or company, being a person, firm or company of any description), namely: a person who is a resident of, or has his or her principal place of business in, Norfolk Island; or a firm that has its principal place of business in Norfolk Island; or a company that is incorporated, or has its principal place of business, in Norfolk Island; or to which both (i) and (ii) apply;

is not a prescribed ship.

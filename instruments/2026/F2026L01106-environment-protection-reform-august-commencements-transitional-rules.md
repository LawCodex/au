###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under item 715 of Schedule 1 to the *Environment Protection Reform Act 2025*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***amending Act*** means the *Environment Protection Reform Act 2025*.

***commencement day*** means 24 August 2026.

***principal Act*** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

### Part 2—Transitional provisions

###### 5 Ministerial declarations and accredited management or authorisation frameworks—Schedule 1, items 83 to 102, 104, 112 and 113

(1) The amendments of section 32 of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a declaration under section 33 of the principal Act made:

(a) before the commencement day and continued in force on and after the commencement day by subsection (2); or

(b) on or after the commencement day.

(2) A declaration (the ***existing declaration***) under section 33 of the principal Act that:

(a) was in force immediately before the commencement day; and

(b) refers to a management arrangement or authorisation process that is an accredited management arrangement or an accredited authorisation process for the purposes of the existing declaration;

is taken, on and after the commencement day, to be a declaration (the ***new declaration***) that refers to a management or authorisation framework that is accredited for the purposes of the new declaration under section 33 of the principal Act, as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act.

(3) Section 34AA of the principal Act, as added by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies in relation to a declaration under section 33 of the principal Act made:

(a) before the commencement day and continued in force on and after the commencement day by subsection (2); or

(b) on or after the commencement day;

such that the declaration may, on and after the commencement day, apply, adopt or incorporate (with or without modifications) any matter contained in an instrument or other writing:

(c) as in force at a particular time; or

(d) as is in force or existing from time to time.

(4) The amendments of sections 34B to 34F of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a declaration under section 33 of the principal Act made on or after the commencement day.

(5) Subject to subsection (6) of this section, sections 35 to 35E and 36A to 36F of the principal Act, as added, amended or substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, apply in relation to a declaration under section 33 of the principal Act made:

(a) before the commencement day and continued in force on and after the commencement day by subsection (2); or

(b) on or after the commencement day.

(6) A declaration under section 33 of the principal Act cannot be suspended, varied or revoked under subsection 35(1) of the principal Act, as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, on or after the commencement day if:

(a) the declaration was in force immediately before the commencement day; and

(b) the suspension, variation or revocation is because of a situation mentioned in paragraph 35(2)(c), (d) or (e), as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act;

unless that declaration has been varied (other than because of a situation mentioned in paragraph (b) of this subsection) on or after the commencement day.

###### 6 Bilateral agreements—Schedule 1, items 116 and 118A

(1) Subsection 45(3) of the principal Act, as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act and to the extent that paragraph (a) or (b) of that subsection applies, applies in relation to a bilateral agreement entered into on or after the commencement day with a State or a self‑governing Territory that includes a declaration under section 46 of the principal Act, whether:

(a) the bilateral agreement with that State or Territory that is mentioned in paragraph 45(3)(a) of the principal Act as including a declaration under section 47 of the principal Act was entered into before, on or after the commencement day; or

(b) the bilateral agreement that is mentioned in paragraph 45(3)(b) of the principal Act as including a declaration under section 46 or 47 of the principal Act was entered into before, on or after the commencement day.

(2) Section 47A of the principal Act, as inserted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies in relation to an amendment made, on or after the commencement day, to the specified manner in which the actions are assessed in a bilateral agreement that includes the declaration described in subsection 47(1) of the principal Act, whether the bilateral agreement was entered into before, on or after the commencement day.

###### 7 Reconsideration of decisions—Schedule 1, items 182 to 186

The amendments of section 78B of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a request made on or after the commencement day under section 78A of the principal Act for reconsideration of a decision under subsection 75(1) of the principal Act, whether the decision under subsection 75(1) of the principal Act was made before, on or after the commencement day.

###### 8 Recovery plans—Schedule 1, items 357 to 378, 380, 381 and 404 to 430

(1) The amendments of section 269AA of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a decision made on or after the commencement day about whether to have a recovery plan for a listed threatened species or a listed threatened ecological community (or part of such a species or community).

(2) The amendments of section 269A of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a recovery plan that:

(a) is made or adopted on or after the commencement day; and

(b) is the result of a decision made on or after the commencement day, under subsection 269AA(1) or (1B) of the principal Act (as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act), to have a recovery plan for the listed threatened species or listed threatened ecological community (or part of such a species or community).

(3) The amendments of sections 270, 273 to 275, 278, 279, 282 and 283 of the principal Act (to the extent those sections relate to recovery plans) made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a recovery plan made, adopted or varied on or after the commencement day.

(4) Subsection 283A(1) of the principal Act (to the extent that subsection relates to recovery plans), as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies in relation to a recovery plan made or adopted before, on or after the commencement day.

###### 9 Threat abatement plans—Schedule 1, items 382 to 430

(1) The amendments of section 270A of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a decision made on or after the commencement day about whether to have a threat abatement plan for a key threatening process (or part of a key threatening process).

(2) The amendments of section 270B of the principal Act made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a threat abatement plan that:

(a) is made or adopted on or after the commencement day; and

(b) is the result of a decision made on or after the commencement day, under subsection 270A(1) of the principal Act (as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act), to have a threat abatement plan for a key threatening process (or part of a key threatening process).

(3) The amendments of sections 271, 273 to 275, 278, 279, 282 and 283 of the principal Act (to the extent those sections relate to threat abatement plans) made by Schedule 1 to the amending Act apply in relation to a threat abatement plan made, adopted or varied on or after the commencement day.

(4) Subsection 283A(1) of the principal Act (to the extent that subsection relates to threat abatement plans), as substituted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies in relation to a threat abatement plan made or adopted before, on or after the commencement day.

###### 10 Rulings—Schedule 1, item 572

Section 514YT of the principal Act, as inserted by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, applies in relation to the performance of a function or the exercise of a power on or after the commencement day, including to the performance of a function or the exercise of a power that relates to an action referred under section 68, 69 or 71 of the principal Act before, on or after the commencement day.

###### 11 Unacceptable impacts—Schedule 1, item 588

Sections 527F and 527H of the principal Act (to the extent those sections apply to Division 1A of Part 7 of the principal Act), as added by Schedule 1 to the amending Act, apply in relation to a decision under Division 1A of Part 7 of the principal Act made on or after the commencement day if the referral to which the decision relates is made:

(a) under section 68, 69 or 71 of the principal Act; and

(b) on or after the day section 136B of the principal Act commences.

### Part 3—Modifications of Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the amending Act

###### 12 Modifications of Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the amending Act

(1) Subitem 677(2) of Schedule 1 to the amending Act is modified by omitting “a bilateral agreement entered into on or after the commencement day” and substituting “a bilateral agreement entered into before, on or after the commencement day”.

(2) Item 693 of Schedule 1 to the amending Act is modified by omitting “Section 56C” and substituting “Despite subitem 677(1), section 56C”.

(3) Item 695 of Schedule 1 to the amending Act is modified by omitting “Section 65D” and substituting “Despite subitem 677(1), section 65D”.

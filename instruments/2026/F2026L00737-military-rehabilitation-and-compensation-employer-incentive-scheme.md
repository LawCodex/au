Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Employer Incentive Scheme) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 62A(1) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Some expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including but not limited to the following:

(a) approved program provider (section 41);

(b) approved rehabilitation program (section 41);

(c) civilian work (section 5);

(d) vocational assessment and rehabilitation (section 41).

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***EIS participant***—see section 6.

***employment day***—see section 8.

***gross wages*** means amounts paid as wages or salary:

(a) before income tax deductions; and

(b) excluding an amount or part of an amount paid as allowance, overtime, commission, bonus, superannuation, reimbursement, or compensation.

***incentive payment*** means a payment calculated under section 10.

***qualifying employment***—see section 7.

***relevant employment laws*** means laws relating to the provision of employment that apply in the State or Territory where the employment is located including, but not limited to, laws regarding conditions of employment, payment of superannuation, and workers’ compensation insurance.

***Scheme*** means the scheme established by this instrument.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Meaning of EIS participant

(1) A person is an EIS participant if all of the following apply:

(a) the Commission is required to assist the person to find suitable civilian work under paragraph 61(3)(c) or subsection 62(3) of the Act;

(b) the Commission has determined that the person is to undertake an approved rehabilitation program that includes vocational assessment and rehabilitation;

(c) immediately before the employment day, the person is receiving compensation for incapacity under Part 3 or 4 of Chapter 4 of the Act;

(d) the person is unemployed and is unable to return to their previous employment;

(e) the Commission has approved the person’s participation in the Scheme under subsection (2).

(2) The Commission may approve participation in the Scheme under this subsection if:

(a) the person proposes to undertake employment that, in the opinion of the Commission, is qualifying employment; and

(b) the Commission is satisfied that participation in the Scheme is likely to lead to ongoing employment for the person.

###### 7 Meaning of qualifying employment

(1) Employment is ***qualifying employment*** if, in relation to a person, it meets each requirement in subsection (2) and is provided by an employer who satisfies subsection (3).

(2) For subsection (1), the requirements are:

(a) the employment is suitable civilian work provided in Australia;

(b) the employment is ongoing (whether full-time or part-time), or an apprenticeship or a traineeship;

(c) an approved program provider has assessed the employment as suitable work for the person (after reasonable adjustments are made, if any);

(d) the place of employment meets applicable health and safety standards;

(e) the provision of the employment is compliant with relevant employment laws;

(f) the employment is not self-employment, a subcontracted position, or a government-funded position.

(3) An employer satisfies this subsection if it:

(a) has not previously employed the person; and

(b) is not an immediate relative of the person or controlled by an immediate relative of the person; and

(c) before the employment date, has provided the Commission with all of the following information:

(i) the amount of wages for the employment; and

(ii) the usual weekly hours of the employment; and

(iii) the usual duties of the employment; and

(iv) the conditions (if any) of the employment.

(4) In this section:

***government-funded position*** means employment by an agency or entity of the Commonwealth or a State or Territory, but does not include employment by a local government unless the employment is directly funded by a State or Territory government.

***immediate relative*** has the meaning that ***related person*** has in subsection 15(2) of the Act.

###### 8 Meaning of employment day

(1) The ***employment day***, in relation to qualifying employment, means the first day on which the EIS participant is required to perform duties under the employment.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the Commission may nominate a day as the employment day if:

(a) the Commission is satisfied that the EIS participant was, or will be, unable to perform duties on the day mentioned in subsection (1) because of factors beyond their control; or

(b) it is otherwise appropriate, in the opinion of the Commission, to adjust the employment day.

### Part 2—Payments to employers

###### 9 Commission may make payments to employers

(1) The Commission may make payments under this Part in respect of the provision of qualifying employment to an EIS participant.

(2) In this Part:

***employer*** means the employer providing qualifying employment to the EIS participant.

***first employment stage*** means the period of 3 months beginning on the employment day.

***second employment stage*** means the period of 3 months beginning on the day after the end of the first employment stage.

###### 10 Calculation of incentive payments

(1) An incentive payment (an ***initial payment***) is an amount equal to 75% of the gross wages paid by the employer to the EIS participant during the first employment stage.

(2) An incentive payment (a ***secondary payment***) is an amount equal to 40% of the gross wages paid by the employer to the EIS participant during the second employment stage.

(3) An incentive payment (a ***tertiary payment***) is the lesser of the following amounts:

(a) an amount equal to 10% of the gross wages paid by the employer to the EIS participant within the period of 12 months beginning on the employment day;

(b) $2,000.

###### 11 Employer must claim for payment

(1) An employer must submit a claim for an incentive payment in the approved form.

(2) A claim for an incentive payment is invalid if it is submitted:

(a) before the end of the employment period to which the payment relates; or

(b) more than 15 months after the employment day.

###### 12 Commission may pay claim

(1) The Commission may pay the amount of a claim if it is satisfied that:

(a) the amount claimed has been properly calculated under section 10; and

(b) the claim has been validly submitted under section 11.

Note: Section 13 also affects the Commission’s power to pay incentive payments.

(2) An amount payable under this Part is only to be paid to the employer.

###### 13 Conditions relating to employers and payments

(1) Despite anything else in this instrument, the Commission may refuse to make a payment to an employer if, in the Commission’s opinion, the employer has failed to satisfy subsection (2) at any point:

(a) within the period of 2 months ending on the day before the employment day; or

(b) within the period of 15 months beginning on the employment day.

(2) An employer satisfies this subsection if:

(a) it has not, without a legitimate business reason, dismissed an employee for the purpose of creating a vacancy for the EIS participant or retaining the EIS participant; and

(b) the wages and conditions provided to the EIS participant are comparable to other employees doing similar work for that employer; and

(c) it is compliant with relevant employment laws relating to the qualifying employment.

###### 14 Commission may request information

(1) For the purposes of the Scheme, the Commission may request information from the EIS participant or the employer.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Commission may request:

(a) documents that record wages paid to an EIS participant;

(b) business records that demonstrate or otherwise relate to the employer’s compliance with the conditions of the Scheme.

(3) If the Commission requests information under subsection (1), the person must provide the information within 10 business days or such further time as the Commission allows.

(4) If a person fails or refuses to provide information or documents requested by the Commission under subsection (1), the Commission may refuse to make a payment under this Part.

## Schedule 1—Transitional provisions

### Part 1—Preliminary

**1 Definitions**

In this Schedule:

***commencement day*** means the day that section 1 of this instrument commenced.

***DRCA EIS*** means the scheme known as the Veterans’ Employer Incentive Scheme as it was administered by the MRCC under the old DRCA immediately before the commencement day.

***existing employment***, in relation to a person, means employment that:

(a) commenced within the period of 12 months ending on the day before the commencement day; and

(b) continued, or was reasonably expected to continue, on and from the commencement day.

***former EIS*** means the scheme known as the Veterans’ Employer Incentive Scheme as it was administered by the Commission under the Act immediately before the commencement day.

***MRCA CTPA*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* as in force on and after 1 July 2026.

***MRCC*** has the same meaning that it has in the old DRCA.

***old DRCA*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988* as in force immediately before 1 July 2026.

**2 No double payment**

Nothing in this Schedule permits an employer to receive a payment under the Scheme that is calculated in the same or substantially the same manner as a payment the employer already received in respect of the same EIS participant.

### Part 2—Transitional matters relating to former EIS

**3 Application provision—employment provided under former EIS**

(1) The Scheme applies to existing employment provided to a person under the former EIS.

(2) Where the Scheme applies because of subitem (1), the existing employment is taken to be qualifying employment.

(3) Subject to item 2, an employer is not prevented from claiming an incentive payment under the Scheme only because the employment commenced under the former EIS.

**4 Transitional provision—process etc. not determined under former EIS**

(1) A process commenced but not determined under the former EIS is taken to have been commenced under the Scheme.

(2) A claim for payment made but not determined under the former EIS is taken to have been made under the Scheme.

(3) Subitems (1) and (2) do not apply to a process or claim unless it was commenced or made within the period of 15 months ending on the day before the commencement day.

### Part 3—Transitional matters relating to DRCA EIS

**5 Application provision—employment provided under DRCA EIS**

(1) The Scheme applies to existing employment provided to a person if:

(a) immediately before 1 July 2026, that employment was provided under the DRCA EIS; and

(b) from 1 July 2026, Part 2 of Chapter 3 of the Act applies to the person because of section 104 of the MRCA CTPA.

(2) Where the Scheme applies because of subitem (1), the existing employment is taken to be qualifying employment.

(3) Subject to item 2, an employer is not prevented from claiming an incentive payment under the Scheme only because the employment commenced under the DRCA EIS.

**6 Transitional provision—process etc. not determined under DRCA EIS**

(1) A process commenced but not determined under the DRCA EIS is taken to have been commenced under the Scheme.

(2) A claim for payment made but not determined under the DRCA EIS is taken to have been made under the Scheme.

(3) Subitems (1) and (2) do not apply to a process or claim unless it was commenced or made within the period of 15 months ending on the day before the commencement day.

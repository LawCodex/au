***Act*** means the *National Health and Medical Research Council Act 1992*.

### Part 2—Regulatory recommendations and guidelines

###### 6 Consultation about regulatory recommendations—information required in notices

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 12(2)(c) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under subsection 12(2) of the Act is the following:

(a) the subject matter of the proposed regulatory recommendation to which the notice relates;

(b) a summary of the proposed regulatory recommendation.

Note: A notice published under subsection 12(2) of the Act must also include the matters mentioned in that subsection, including specifying the period within which submissions relating to the proposed recommendation may be made.

(2) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(3)(a)(iv) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under paragraph 12(3)(a) of the Act is the subject matter of the draft regulatory recommendation to which the notice relates.

Note: A notice published under paragraph 12(3)(a) of the Act must also include the matters mentioned in that paragraph, including specifying the period within which submissions relating to the draft recommendation may be made.

(3) For the purposes of subparagraph 12(3)(b)(ii) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under paragraph 12(3)(b) of the Act is the following:

(a) the subject matter of the recommendation to which the notice relates;

(b) the reasons why the Council no longer proposes to provide the recommendation to the CEO.

Note: A notice published under paragraph 12(3)(b) of the Act must also include the matters mentioned in that paragraph.

###### 7 Consultation about guidelines—information required in notice

For the purposes of subparagraph 13(d)(iv) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under paragraph 13(d) of the Act is the subject matter of the draft guidelines to which the notice relates*.*

Note: A notice published under paragraph 13(d) of the Act must also include the matters mentioned in that paragraph, including specifying the period within which submissions relating to the draft guidelines may be made.

###### 8 Interim regulatory recommendations and guidelines—information required in notices

For the purposes of paragraphs 14(2)(d), (4)(d) and (6)(d) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under subsection 14(2), (4) or (6) of the Act (as the case may be) is the following:

(a) the subject matter of the interim regulatory recommendation, or the interim guidelines, to which the notice relates;

(b) a statement about where copies of the interim regulatory recommendation or interim guidelines can be obtained*.*

Note: A notice published under subsection 14(2), (4) or (6) of the Act (as the case may be) must also include the matters mentioned in that subsection, including specifying the period within which submissions relating to the interim regulatory recommendation or interim guidelines may be made.

###### 9 Proposal to dispense with consultation requirements—notice requirements

(1) For the purposes of subsection 14B(2) of the Act, the period within which a notice under that subsection must be published is the period of 30 days starting on the day after the Council or the Australian Health Ethics Committee (as the case may be) decides to propose to dispense with the requirement (the ***consultation requirement***) for all or any of the steps set out in section 12 or 13 or subsection 14A(2) of the Act.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 14B(2)(b) of the Act, the information to be included in a notice published under subsection 14B(2) of the Act is the following:

(a) the subject matter of the proposed regulatory recommendation or the proposed guidelines in relation to which the Council or the Australian Health Ethics Committee proposes to dispense with the consultation requirement;

(b) a summary of the proposed regulatory recommendation or the proposed guidelines.

Note: A notice published under subsection 14B(2) of the Act must also include the matters mentioned in that subsection.

### Part 3—Application, savings and transitional provisions

###### 10 Continuing application of National Health and Medical Research Council Regulation 2016

The *National Health and Medical Research Council Regulation 2016*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this instrument, continues to apply on and after that commencement in relation to a notice published before that commencement.

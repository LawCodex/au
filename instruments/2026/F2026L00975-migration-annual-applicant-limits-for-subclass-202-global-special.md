# Migration (Annual Applicant Limits for Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian) Visas) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/035

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 16 July 2026

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

**Contents**

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Annual Applicant Limits for Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian) Visas) Instrument 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 1402(3D) of Schedule 1 to the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

Note: Subitem 1402(3E) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations 1994 provides that without limiting subitem 1402(3D), an instrument made under that subitem may make provision for working out which applicants are to be counted, and which applicants are to be disregarded, in determining whether the specified limit would be exceeded.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Migration Act 1958*.

***approved proposing organisation*** has the meaning given by clause 202.111 of the Migration Regulations.

***Community Support Program*** has the meaning given by clause 202.223 of the Migration Regulations.

***financial year*** has the meaning given by section 2B of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

***Migration Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

### Part 2—Limit on the number of applicants

###### 6 Limit on the number of applicants

For the purposes of paragraph 1402(3A)(d) of the Migration Regulations, the limit (if any) on the number of applicants that an approved proposing organisation may propose in a financial year is specified in the table in Schedule 1 to this instrument.

###### 7 Determining whether a specified limit would be exceeded

(1) In determining whether the specified limit under section 6 would be exceeded, applicants are to be counted:

(a) in relation to the limit specified for the approved proposing organisation that has proposed the applicant at the time of making the application;

(b) in relation to the limit specified for the approved proposing organisation for the financial year in which the applicant applies for the visa; and

(c) regardless of whether the applicant is seeking to satisfy the primary criteria or secondary criteria for the visa in Schedule 2 to the Migration Regulations.

(2) In determining whether the limit under section 6 would be exceeded, applicants are to be disregarded in relation to the limit specified for an approved proposing organisation that proposes the applicant after the application is made.

Note: In determining whether the specified limit under section 6 would be exceeded, an applicant who is proposed by an APO at the time of making the application, and is counted towards the limit specified for the APO under paragraph 7(1)(a), continues to be counted towards the limit for that APO, despite the applicant being disregarded in counting towards the limit specified for any APO that may subsequently propose the applicant under subsection 7(2).

### Part 3—Application and transitional provisions

###### 8 Application

(1) Section 6 applies, for the purposes of paragraph 1402(3A)(d) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, in relation to an application for a visa that is made on or after the commencement of this instrument.

(2) For the purposes of working out whether a limit specified under section 6 would be exceeded in relation to an application mentioned in subsection (1), a person may be counted or disregarded as an applicant under this instrument whether the person’s visa application is made before, on or after the commencement of this instrument.

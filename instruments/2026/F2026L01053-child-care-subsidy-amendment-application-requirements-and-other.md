## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Application for approval

##### Child Care Subsidy Secretary’s Rules 2017

**1 Section 4**

Insert:

***national police check*** means a nationally coordinated criminal history check provided by the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission established by the *Australian Crime Commission Act 2002* or an accredited body (within the meaning of the *Australian Crime Commission Act 2002).*

**2 Paragraph 6(1)(k)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(k) all of the following in relation to each person with management or control of the provider:

(i) if the person is an individual—a National Personal Insolvency Index check using the Bankruptcy Register Search service provided by the Australian Financial Security Authority, carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

(ii) if the person is an individual—a digitally verifiable copy of a national police check, carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

(iii) if the person is an individual—a current and historical personal name extract search of the records of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

(iv) if the person is a company—a current and historical company extract search of the records of the Australian Security and Investments Commission, carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

(v) evidence of whether the person appears on the banned and disqualified register held by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (in the form of a computer printout of the results of the search), dated no more than 60 days prior to the date of application;

(vi) if the person is an individual—details of the person’s working with children check (within the meaning of subsection 195D(2) of the Family Assistance Administration Act);

**3 Subparagraph 6(1)(n)(vi)**

Repeal the subparagraph, substitute:

(vi) in relation to each person responsible for the day-to-day operation of the service, where the person is an individual— digitally verifiable evidence that a national police check has been carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

(via) in relation to each person responsible for the day-to-day operation of the service, where the person is an individual— details of the person’s working with children check (within the meaning of subsection 195D(2) of the Family Assistance Administration Act);

**4 At the end of paragraph 6(1)(o)**

Insert:

; (p) information, in a form and manner approved by the Secretary, about how the provider will comply with paragraph 194C(da) of the Family Assistance Administration Act.

**5 Subparagraph 7(c)(vi)**

Repeal subparagraph, substitute:

(vi) in relation to each person responsible for the day-to-day operation of the service, where the person is an individual— digitally verifiable evidence that a national police check has been carried out no more than 60 days prior to the date of the application;

**6 At the end of paragraph 7(c)**

Insert:

(viii) information, in a form and manner approved by the Secretary, about how the provider will comply with paragraph 194D(da) of the Family Assistance Administration Act in respect of the service;

**7 Paragraph 11(d)**

Before “police checks” (wherever occurring), insert “national”.

### Part 2—Provider requirements

##### Child Care Subsidy Secretary’s Rules 2017

**8 Section 4 (definition of mandatory child safety training)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***mandatory child safety training*** has the same meaning as in section 4 of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017.*

**9 Section 4**

Insert:

***advanced child safety training*** has the same meaning as in section 4 of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017.*

***child protection training*** has the same meaning as in section 4 of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017.*

***foundation child safety training*** has the same meaning as in section 4 of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017.*

**10 At the end of section 11**

Add:

; (i) for in home care providers—records relevant to the provider’s compliance with subsection 48A(10A) of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017* (about foundation child safety training, advanced child safety training and child protection training) including, for each person covered by subsection 48A(10A) of the Minister’s Rules:

(i) the name of the person; and

(ii) the date on which the person commenced to hold the position and the date on which the person commenced working directly with children at the service; and

(iii) the date on which the person completed foundation child safety training, advanced child safety training and child protection training and expiry date of the training (if applicable);

(j) for providers of a service funded under the Community Child Care Fund Restricted grant program, that is not an education and care service under the Education and Care Services National Law—records relevant to the provider’s compliance with subsection 48E(2) of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017* (about foundation child safety training, advanced child safety training and child protection training) including, for each person covered by subsection 48E(2) of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017*:

(i) the name of the person; and

(ii) the date on which the person commenced to hold the position and the date on which the person commenced working directly with children at the service; and

(iii) the date on which the person completed foundation child safety training, advanced child safety training and child protection training and expiry date of the training (if applicable).

**11 At the end of section 12**

Add:

; (m) records about compliance with subsections 48A(10A) of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules 2017* as required under paragraph 11(i) of these Rules;

(n) records about compliance with subsection 48E(2) of the *Child Care Subsidy Minister’s Rules* as required under paragraph 11(j) of these Rules.

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Bass Strait Central Zone Scallop Fishery (Fishing Season) Determination 2026.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 June 2026.

###### 3 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 1 January 2027.

###### 4 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 17(6)(aa) of the Act for the purpose of section 3 of the Management Plan.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991.*

***Management Plan*** means *Bass Strait Central Zone Scallop Fishery Management Plan 2002.*

Any term used in this instrument that is defined for the purpose of the Management Plan has the same meaning in this instrument as it has in the Management Plan.

Note: Terms defined in the *Fisheries Management Act 1991* have the same meaning in this instrument (see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*).

###### 6 Determination of fishing season

The fishing season for the Bass Strait Central Zone Scallop Fishery is determined to be the period commencing 1 June 2026 and ending 31 December 2026.

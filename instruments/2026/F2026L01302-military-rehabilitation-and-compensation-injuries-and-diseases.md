###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Injuries and Diseases Attributable to Defence Service—Presumptive Liability) Amendment Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 27A(6) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

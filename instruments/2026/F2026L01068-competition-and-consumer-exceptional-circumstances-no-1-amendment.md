# Competition and Consumer (Exceptional Circumstances) (No. 1) Amendment Declaration 2026

I, Andrew Leigh, Assistant Minister for Productivity, Competition, Charities and Treasury, being satisfied of the matters set out in paragraphs 95AE(1)(a) and (b) of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*, make the following declaration.

Dated 17 August 2026

Dr Andrew Leigh

Assistant Minister for Productivity, Competition, Charities and Treasury

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

This instrument is made under:

(a) the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*; and

(b) an application law (within the meaning of section 150A of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*).

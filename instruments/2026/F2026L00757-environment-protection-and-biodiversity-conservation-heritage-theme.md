# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation (Heritage Theme for 2026-2027) Determination 2026

I, Murray Watt, Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following determination.

Dated 17 June 2026 at 12:25 pm

Murray Watt

Minister for the Environment and Water

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation (Heritage Theme for 2026-2027) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences immediately after it is made.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 324H of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

***assessment period*** has the meaning given by subsection 324G(1) of the Act.

###### 5 Heritage theme—2026-2027

For the purposes of subsection 324H(1) of the Act, the heritage theme for the assessment period beginning on 1 July 2026 is:

*Places and related stories that have made a fairer, more inclusive Australia.*

###### 6 Repeals

The *Priority Heritage Theme for Nominations for Assessment for the National Heritage List (Assessment Period commencing 1 July 2021) Determination 2020* is repealed.

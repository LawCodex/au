# CASA ADCX 002/26 - Repeal of Airworthiness Directive AD/AMD 50/30 Amdt 1 Instrument 2026

Instrument number CASA ADCX 002/26

I, DAVID PUNSHON, Manager, Continued Operational Safety, a delegate of the Civil Aviation Safety Authority, make this instrument under subregulation 39.001 (1) of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

**David Punshon**\
Manager, Continued Operational Safety

**19 August 2026**

## 1. Name of Instrument

This instrument is *CASA ADCX 002/26 - Repeal of Airworthiness Directive AD/AMD 50/30 Amdt 1 Instrument 2026*.

## 2. Commencement

This instrument commences on **26 August 2026.**

## 3. Repeal

The Airworthiness Directive (**AD**) mentioned in Schedule 1 is repealed on the date specified for it in the Schedule.

### Schedule 1 Repeal of AD

| AD | Repeal date |
|---|---|
| AD/AMD 50/30 Amdt 1 | **26 August 2026** |

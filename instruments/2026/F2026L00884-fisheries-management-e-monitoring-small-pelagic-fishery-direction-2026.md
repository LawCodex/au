###### Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act including the following:

AFMA

boat;

e-monitoring;

e-monitoring data;

fishing concession;

holder;

fishing-related activity.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

***catch*** means any fish, aquatic animal, or other marine organism that is taken, captured, or interacts with fishing gear, whether intentionally or unintentionally, and includes:

anything retained on board;

anything discarded or returned to the water;

any incidental or unintended take; and

any fish, aquatic animal, or other marine organism that interacts with fishing gear, whether or not it is brought on board.

***data drive*** means a component of an e-monitoring system that can store e‑monitoring data and can be removed by the operator of the e-monitoring system but does not include removable Random Access Memory.

***e-monitoring service provider*** means the party a fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) has chosen to supply the e-monitoring system and associated services

***e-monitoring system*** means electronic monitoring equipment, together with any associated peripheral devices, approved for use in the Small Pelagic Fishery as specified in clause 7 of the *Fisheries Management (Type Approved E-monitoring Systems) Direction 2026*, as in force from time to time

***fishing event*** means the setting of a net, hauling of a net, pumping of catch and handling of catch.

***operating*** or ***operational,*** in relation to an e-monitoring system, means:

the system is powered on; and

all components of the e-monitoring system are active and functioning as intended, including all cameras, sensors, data‑capture mechanisms, and associated hardware or software required for the system to perform its functions, and

the system records all video and sensor data onto the data drive; and

the system sends signals (in an AFMA approved format and timeframe) to AFMA, or the e-monitoring service provider, that accurately show function test completion, sensor activity and boat positional information.

***port*** means boundaries set by state port authorities and a place intended for the berthing, loading, unloading, and servicing of boats.

###### Repeal

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is repealed.

###### Requirement to install and operate an e-monitoring system

A fishing concession holder (or a person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must ensure that an e‑monitoring system is installed and operating on its nominated boats at all times if the boat:

is used to fish in the midwater trawl sector of the Small Pelagic Fishery; or

is equipped or authorised to fish using both midwater trawl gear and purse seine gear in the Small Pelagic Fishery.

Note: A fishing concession authorising fishing in the Small Pelagic Fishery is the class of concession that this direction applies to.

At all times, the fishing concession holder (or a person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must have the e-monitoring system installed and configured on the nominated boats in a manner that ensures: each camera and sensor provides clear coverage of fishing events that allows AFMA to identify catch species and fishing-related activities carried out by the boat; and e-monitoring data can be captured reliably, securely and accurately.

Before undertaking any fishing related activity, the fishing concession holder must submit to AFMA a completed AFMA-approved form demonstrating that a e-monitoring system has been installed on the boat and is configured to meet the requirements in clause 6(2). The fishing concession holder must ensure that the details contained in this AFMA-approved document are accurate and always kept up to date.

Note: Compliance with keeping the details contained in the AFMA-approved document accurate and up to date may include lodging a further form with AFMA or provide particulars of the change to AFMA in writing.

For the purpose of subsection 40A(3) of the Act, the obligation prescribed in this clause commences on the day the e-monitoring system is first installed.

Note: Clause 7(2) imposes certain obligations on a person subject to the requirement to arrange repair of an e-monitoring system if the e-monitoring system ceases to function correctly.

An e‑monitoring system is not required to be operational while a nominated boat is in port.

###### Requirement to monitor function of an e-monitoring system, arrange repair and provide e-monitoring data

A fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must at all times maintain their e-monitoring system in good working order by: undertaking system function tests:

immediately after a data drive exchange;

prior to starting a new trip; and

when there is a reasonable suspicion of issues or fault with the system. reporting suspected issues with the e-monitoring system to their chosen e-monitoring service provider as soon as practicable; cleaning camera lenses to ensure that a clear field of view is maintained at all times; and ensuring camera views of fishing events remain unobstructed.

If, due to a system fault, malfunction or other issue, the concession holder (or person acting on behalf of a concession holder) is unable to make the e‑monitoring system operational, they must: notify AFMA as soon as the concession holder (or person acting on behalf of a concession holder) becomes aware the e-monitoring system is not functioning as intended and comply with any instructions provided by AFMA; and take all reasonable steps to have the e-monitoring system repaired as soon as practical.

A fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must ensure that they have sufficient data drives for their upcoming trip and give AFMA adequate notice if they require additional data drive(s).

A fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must exchange the data drive when any of the following occurs:

the data drive's storage capacity is reached; or

the data drive has failed a systems test; or

the boat has made the first landing of the month; or

as instructed by AFMA.

When any of the events in clause 7(4) occurs, the fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must return the data drive to AFMA within one business day of the boat being in port by:

packaging the data drive into a AFMA pre-addressed package and sending it via registered post; and

emailing the postal tracking number to AFMA.

A fishing concession holder (or person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) must not damage, alter, or otherwise tamper with the data drive.

For the purpose of s40A(3) of the Act, the obligations prescribed in clause 7 commence on: 1 July 2026 in the case where an e-monitoring system has already been installed before the commencement of this instrument; or the day the e-monitoring system is first installed in the case where the e-monitoring system is installed after the commencement of this instrument.

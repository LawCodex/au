###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 618(2) of the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) affected provisions;

(b) Australian territory;

(c) conveyance;

(d) goods;

(e) protected zone area.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

***Torres Strait permanent biosecurity monitoring zone*** has the same meaning as in the *Biosecurity Regulations 2026*.

### Part 2—Regulated movements

###### 5 Regulated movements between parts of Australian territory

Subdivision B of Division 2 of Part 4 of Chapter 11 of the Act applies to movements that:

(a) are of a thing specified in column 1 of an item in the following table; and

(b) are from a part (the ***origin part***) of Australian territory specified in column 2 of that item; and

(c) are to another part (the ***destination part***) of Australian territory specified in column 3 of that item.

The affected provisions are subject to any exemptions that are applicable under the Act (see, for example, regulations made for the purposes of sections 120 (notice must be given of goods to be unloaded in Australian territory) and 193 (pre‑arrival reporting) of the Act).

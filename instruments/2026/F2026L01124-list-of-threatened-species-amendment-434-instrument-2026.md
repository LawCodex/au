# List of Threatened Species Amendment (434) Instrument 2026

I, Murray Watt, Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following instrument.

Dated 20 August 2026

Murray Watt

Minister for the Environment and Water

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *List of Threatened Species Amendment (434) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 184 of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Declaration under s178, s181, and s183 of the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - List of threatened species, List of threatened ecological communities and List of threatening processes

**1 In the appropriate position under the heading “SPECIES THAT ARE CRITICALLY ENDANGERED”**

Insert each of the following species:

*Budginmaya eulae*

*Gossia hillii*

*Nurus atlas*

*Persoonia flexifolia*

*Zieria odorifera* subsp. *copelandii*

**2 In the appropriate position under the heading “SPECIES THAT ARE ENDANGERED”**

Insert each of the following species:

*Auranticarpa edentata*

*Brachyscome mittagongensis*

*Mogurnda clivicola*

*Neochanna cleaveri*

*Olearia suffruticosa*

*Probosciger aterrimus macgillivrayi*

*Synostemon hamersleyensis*

*Thelymitra lucida*

**3 In the appropriate position under the heading “SPECIES THAT ARE VULNERABLE”**

Insert the following species:

*Galaxiella toourtkoourt*

**4 In the appropriate position under the heading “SPECIES THAT ARE VULNERABLE”**

Omit each of the following species:

*Mogurnda clivicola*

*Probosciger aterrimus macgillivrayi*

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Direct Deductions Arrangements) Approval 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 122B of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986.*

###### 4 Repeals

The *Veterans’ Entitlements (Direct Deductions Arrangements) Approval 2025* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

child;

Commission;

dependant;

service pension;

veteran payment.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

***attendant allowance*** means an allowance under section 98 of the Act.

***clothing allowance*** means an allowance under section 97 of the Act.

***disability compensation payment*** means any of the following:

(a) a pension under Part II or IV of the Act (other than a pension payable at a rate determined in accordance with section 30 of the Act to a dependant of a deceased veteran);

(b) a pension payable because of subsection 4(6) or (8B) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Transitional Provisions and Consequential Amendments) Act 1986* (other than a pension payable in respect of a child).

***income support supplement*** means a payment under Part IIIA of the Act.

***pensions payable to dependants of deceased veterans*** means pensions payable at a rate determined in accordance with subsection 30(1) or paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act.

***recreation transport allowance*** means an allowance under section 104 of the Act.

***State Housing Authority*** means an authority listed in the definition of ***Government rent*** in subsection 5N(1) of the Act.

###### 6 Approved classes of pensions etc. from which deductions may be made

For section 122B of the Act, the following are approved classes of pensions, allowances or pecuniary benefits:

(a) attendant allowances;

(b) clothing allowances;

(c) disability compensation payments;

(d) income support supplements;

(e) pensions payable to dependants of deceased veterans;

(f) recreation transport allowances;

(g) service pensions;

(h) veteran payments.

###### 7 Approved classes of payments for which deductions may be made

For section 122B of the Act, the following are approved classes of payments:

(a) payments to a State Housing Authority that are—

(i) payments of rent;

(ii) payments for principal or interest of a loan; or

(iii) payments for a debt;

(b) payments to Westpac Banking Corporation (ABN 33 007 457 141) that are—

(i) payments for principal or interest of a loan; or

(ii) payments of insurance premiums.

###### 8 Transitional provision

This section applies in relation to a decoration allowance (within the meaning of the Act as in force immediately before 1 July 2026) for which the pension period:

commenced before 1 July 2026; and

ends on or after that date.

Note: See section 108 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional*

*Provisions) Act 2004*.

A decoration allowance to which subsection (1) applies is an approved class of allowance.

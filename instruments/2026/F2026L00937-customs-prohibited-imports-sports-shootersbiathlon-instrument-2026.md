###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the following provisions of the *Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 1956*:

(a) subitem 1.7 of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations;

(b) subitem 1A.7 of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 1956*, including the following:

(a) certified international sports shooter;

(b) certified sports shooter;

(c) restricted category C article.

In this instrument:

***Biathlon Australia*** means Biathlon Australia Ltd (ABN 18 883 464 584).

***biathlon event*** means:

(a) a biathlon competition conducted or authorised by Biathlon Australia; or

(b) an event or activity directly related to training for, preparing for, or participating in, a competition covered by paragraph (a).

***Regulations*** means the *Customs (Prohibited Imports) Regulations 1956*.

###### 6 Certified sports shooter—sporting association or body

For the purposes of subparagraph 1.1A(a)(i) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, Biathlon Australia is specified.

###### 7 Certified sports shooter—specified sporting event

For the purposes of paragraph 1.1A(c) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, the following sporting events are specified in relation to Biathlon Australia:

(a) biathlon events;

(b) the Commonwealth Games or an associated event;

(c) the Olympic Games or an associated event;

(d) the Paralympic Games or an associated event.

###### 8 Certified sports shooter—additional criteria

For the purposes of paragraph 1.1A(e) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, the following criteria are specified:

(a) the use, by the person, of the restricted category C article may only be for:

(i) participation in the event; or

(ii) training or preparation directly connected with the event; and

(b) the use, by the person, of the restricted category C article must not be for any purpose that is not covered by paragraph (a); and

(c) the person must hold a membership of Biathlon Australia that is:

(i) in effect; and

(ii) of a kind that permits participation in a biathlon event; and

(iii) not suspended.

###### 9 Certified international sports shooter—specified sporting event

For the purposes of paragraph 1A.1A(a) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, the following sporting events are specified:

(a) biathlon events;

(b) the Commonwealth Games or an associated event;

(c) the Olympic Games or an associated event;

(d) the Paralympic Games or an associated event.

###### 10 Certified international sports shooter—sporting association or body

For subparagraph 1A.1A(b)(iv) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, Biathlon Australia is specified as a sporting association or body in relation to biathlon events.

###### 11 Certified international sports shooter—additional criteria

For the purposes of paragraph 1A.1A(e) of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Regulations, the following criteria are specified:

(a) the person is a participant in a sporting event specified in section 9; and

(b) the use, by the person, of the restricted category C article may only be for:

(i) participation in the event; or

(ii) training or preparation directly connected with the event; and

(c) the use, by the person, of the restricted category C article must not be for any purpose that is not covered by paragraph (b).

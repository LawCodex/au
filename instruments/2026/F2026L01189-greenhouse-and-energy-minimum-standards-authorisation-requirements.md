# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Authorisation Requirements for Testing GEMS Products) Instrument 2026

I, Melanie Ford, GEMS Regulator, make the following instrument.

Dated 4 September 2026

Melanie Ford

GEMS Regulator

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Authorisation Requirements for Testing GEMS Products) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the day it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 125 of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) GEMS determination;

(b) GEMS product;

(c) product class.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*.

###### 6 Requirements for all product classes

(1) For the purposes of section 125 of the Act, a person is authorised to test GEMS products in a product class if the person is accredited to carry out the test by:

(a) the National Association of Testing Authorities, Australia (ACN 004 379 748); or

(b) another signatory to:

(i) the Mutual Recognition Arrangement operated by the Asia Pacific Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated (New Zealand registration number 1877392); or

(ii) the Multilateral Recognition Arrangement operated by the Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated (New Zealand registration number 50223540).

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), a person is accredited to test GEMS products in a product class if:

(a) the scope (however described) of the accreditation includes the testing as required under the GEMS determination covering the product class; and

(b) the accreditation is not suspended or cancelled before or while testing the GEMS products; and

(c) the person complies with any condition to which the accreditation is subject, to the extent the condition relates to the testing.

(3) In this section:

***accredited*** includes certified, endorsed and recognised.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

### National Health (Commonwealth Price and Conditions for Commonwealth Payments for Supply of Pharmaceutical Benefits) Determination 2019 (PB 114 of 2019)

[1] Schedule 1, omit entry for Cefuroxime

[2] Schedule 4, entries for Beclometasone

omit:

| Beclometasone | Pressurised inhalation in breath actuated device containing beclometasone dipropionate 50 micrograms per dose, 200 doses (CFC-free formulation) |
|---|---|

[3] Schedule 4, omit entry for Cefuroxime

[4] Schedule 4, after entry for Ciclesonide in the form Pressurised inhalation 160 micrograms per dose, 120 doses (CFC‑free formulation)

insert:

| Ciclosporin | Eye drops 1 mg per mL, 2 mL |
|---|---|

[5] Schedule 4, after entry for Glyceryl trinitrate

insert:

| Glycomacropeptide formula with long chain polyunsaturated fatty acids and docosahexaenoic acid and low in phenylalanine | Sachets containing oral powder 40 g, pack of 30 (PKU GMPro Delight) |
|---|---|

[6] Schedule 4, after entry for Sirolimus

insert:

| Tacrolimus | Ointment 1 mg per g, 30 g |
|---|---|

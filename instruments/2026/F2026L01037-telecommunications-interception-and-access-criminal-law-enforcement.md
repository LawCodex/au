# Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Criminal Law-Enforcement Agency—ACT Integrity Commission) Declaration (No. 2) 2026

LIN 26/086

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 4 August 2026

Signed

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Criminal Law-Enforcement Agency—ACT Integrity Commission) Declaration (No. 2) 2026.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 12 August 2026.

Note: See paragraph 110A(10)(b) of the Act for cessation of this instrument.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 110A of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*.

***ACT Integrity Commission*** has the same meaning given by Chapter 2 of the *Integrity Commission Act 2018* (ACT).

***criminal law-enforcement agency*** has the same meaning given by section 110A of the Act.

###### 5 Declaration

(1) The declaration under this section is subject to the condition set out in section 6.

(2) Under paragraph 110A(3)(a) of the Act, the ACT Integrity Commission is declared to be a criminal law-enforcement agency.

(3) Under paragraph 110A(3)(b) of the Act, each staff member of the criminal law-enforcement agency declared under subsection 5(2) of this instrument is declared to be an officer of that criminal law-enforcement agency for the purposes of the Act.

###### 6 Conditions

Under subsection 110A(6) of the Act, the declaration in section 5 of this instrument is subject to the condition that officers covered by subsection 5(3) of this instrument must not exercise powers under the Act with respect to any preliminary inquiries conducted by the ACT Integrity Commission under section 86 of the *Integrity Commission Act 2018* (ACT).

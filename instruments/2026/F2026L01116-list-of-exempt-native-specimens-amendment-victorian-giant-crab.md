# List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Victorian Giant Crab Fishery) Instrument, August 2026

I, BELINDA JAGO, Branch Head, Ocean and Wildlife Branch, as Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following instrument.

24 August 2026

BELINDA JAGO

Belinda Jago

Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Victorian Giant Crab Fishery) Instrument, August 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 303DC(1)(a) of the EPBC Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

**EPBC Act** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

**2 Under the heading Freshwater and Marine Animals**

Insert the following rows in the appropriate place in the table

| **Taxon/Item** | **Common Name** | **Notation** |
|---|---|---|
| Specimens that are or are derived from invertebrates taken in the Victorian Giant Crab Fishery as defined in the management regime in force under the *Fisheries Act 1995* (Vic), Fisheries Regulations 2019 (Vic) and the Fisheries (Fees, Royalties and Levies) Regulations 2017 (Vic), but not including:<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 209 of the EPBC Act (Australia’s List of Migratory Species), or<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 248 of the EPBC Act (Australia’s List of Marine Species), or<br>specimens that belong to eligible listed threatened species, as defined under section 303BC of the EPBC Act, or<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 303CA of the EPBC Act (Taxa listed in the CITES Appendices). | Victorian Giant Crab Fishery | The specimen, or the invertebrate from which it is derived, was taken lawfully;<br>Approval for specimens of invertebrates taken in the Victorian Giant Crab Fishery (the fishery) to be included in the List of Exempt Native Specimens has been provided on the basis the fishery will be managed in accordance with the management regime in force under the *Fisheries Act 1995* (Vic), Fisheries Regulations 2019 (Vic) and the Fisheries (Fees, Royalties and Levies) Regulations 2017 (Vic). During the term of the approval, the Victorian Fisheries Authority must:<br>(a) Consistent with Appendix B of the *Guidelines for the ecologically sustainable management of fisheries – 2nd edition*, provide the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (the department) with an annual report by 30 September each year.<br>(b) In the interim, if there are any intended changes to legislative instruments or material changes to management arrangements that may affect the basis on which the approval was made for this fishery, the department must be notified;<br>The specimens are included in the list until 24 August 2031. |

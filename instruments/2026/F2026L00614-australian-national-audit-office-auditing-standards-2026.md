Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in definitions section of the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Auditor-General Act 1997*.

***attestation engagement*** has the same meaning as in ASAE 3000 *Assurance Engagements Other than Audits or Reviews of Historical Financial Information*, issued on 1 January 2025 by the AUASB.

***AUASB*** means the Australian Auditing and Assurance Standards Board established under section 227A of the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001*.

***Audit Office*** means the Australian National Audit Office.

***compliance engagement*** has the same meaning as in ASAE 3100 *Compliance Engagements*, issued on 6 September 2022 by the AUASB.

***direct engagement*** has the same meaning as in ASAE 3500 *Performance Engagements*, issued on 16 December 2024 by the AUASB.

***historical financial information*** has the same meaning as in ASAE 3000 *Assurance Engagements Other than Audits or Reviews of Historical Financial Information*.

***INTOSAI*** means the International Organization of Supreme Audit Institutions.

***materiality*** has the same meaning as ‘significance’ as defined in ASAE 3500 *Performance Engagements*

***operative date*** means the date from which the AUASB Standard is to be applied as set out in the relevant standard.

### Part 2—Auditing standards

###### 6 Australian National Audit Office Auditing Standards

(1) The *Australian National Audit Office Auditing Standards 2026* apply to the extent that they:

(a) are not inconsistent with the requirements of the Act or other legislation; and

(b) apply to the relevant audit or review.

###### 7 Annual financial statements audits

(1) Annual financial statements audits conducted under Division 1 of Part 4 of the Act must be conducted in accordance with standards as in force from time to time as made by the AUASB pursuant to:

(a) paragraph 227B(1)(a) of the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001*, and

(b) section 336 of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

###### 8 Audit of annual consolidated financial statements

The audit of the annual consolidated financial statements conducted under Division 1 of Part 4 of the Act, and section 49 of the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013* must be conducted in accordance with the standards specified in section 7.

###### 9 Annual performance statements audits

Annual performance statements audits conducted under Division 1A of Part 4 of the Act must be conducted in accordance with ASAE 3000 *Assurance Engagements Other than Audits or Review of Historical Financial Information*, as in force at its operative date.

###### 10 Performance audits

(1) Performance audits conducted under Division 2 of Part 4 of the Act must be conducted in accordance with the following, as in force at their operative date:

(a) ASAE 3500 *Performance Engagements*:

(i) except for paragraphs 37-38 and 55; and

(ii) except that ‘material’ and ‘materiality’ are to be used instead of ‘significant’ and ‘significance’ respectively;

(b) paragraphs 116, 122, 124 and 126, of ISSAI 3000 *Performance Audit Standard*, issued in 2019 by INTOSAI; and

(c) if the performance audit is conducted wholly or in part as a compliance engagement – to the extent that it is a compliance engagement, additionally to paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), ASAE 3100 *Compliance Engagements*, except for paragraph 56.

Note: All performance audits are reasonable assurance engagements.

###### 11 Assurance reviews

(1) Assurance reviews conducted under Division 2A of Part 4 of the Act must be conducted in accordance with the following, as in force at their operative date:

(a) if the assurance review is a review of historical financial information:

(i) ASRE 2400 *Review of a Financial Report Performed by an Assurance Practitioner Who is Not the Auditor of the Entity*, issued on 6 September 2022 by the AUASB; or

(ii) ASRE 2405 *Review of Historical Financial Information Other than a Financial Report*, issued on 6 September 2022 by the AUASB;

(b) if the assurance review is an attestation engagement not relating to historical financial information:

(i) ASAE 3000 *Assurance Engagements Other than Audits or Review of Historical Financial Information*; and

(ii) additionally, if the assurance review is conducted wholly or in part as a compliance engagement – to the extent that it is a compliance engagement, additionally to subparagraph 1(b)(i), ASAE 3100 *Compliance Engagements*; or

(c) if the assurance review is a direct engagement:

(i) ASAE 3500 *Performance Engagements*, except for paragraph 55, and except that ‘material’ and ‘materiality’ are to be substituted for ‘significant’ and ‘significance’ respectively;

(ii) paragraphs 116, 122, 124 and 126, of ISSAI 3000 *Performance Audit Standard*; and

(iii) additionally, if the assurance review is conducted wholly or in part as a compliance engagement – to the extent that it is a compliance engagement, additionally to subparagraphs 1(c)(i) and (ii), ASAE 3100 *Compliance Engagements*, except for paragraph 56.

Note: For avoidance of ambiguity, audits etc. by arrangement conducted under Division 3 of Part 4 of the Act, and Auditor-General functions under other Acts conducted under Division 4 of Part 4 of the Act, may be conducted in accordance with any of the following to the extent that they apply to the relevant audit or review:

(a) the standards specified in section 7;

(b) the standards specified in sections 9 to 11;

(c) the standards listed in the following table that are issued by the AUASB under section 227B(1)(b) of the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001* Act, as in force on their operative date.

| **Item** | **Standard Number** | **Title** | **Issue Date** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ASA 805 | *Special Considerations – Audits of Single Financial Statements and Specific Elements, Accounts or Items of a Financial Statement* | May 2017 |
| 2 | ASRE 2400 | *Review of a Financial Report Performed by an Assurance Practitioner Who is Not the Auditor of the Entity* | September 2022 |
| 3 | ASRE 2405 | *Review of Historical Financial Information Other than a Financial Report* | September 2022 |
| 4 | ASAE 3150 | *Assurance Engagements on Controls* | September 2022 |
| 5 | ASRS 4400 | *Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements* | September 2022 |

###### 12 Audit of the Australian National Audit Office conducted by the Independent Auditor

An audit of the Audit Office conducted by the Independent Auditor under Division 2 of Part 7 of the Act must be conducted in accordance with the standards which would apply if the audit was conducted by the Auditor-General.

### Part 3—Transitional provisions

###### 13 Transitional provision

(1) Despite Schedule 1, the auditing standards which apply in respect of an audit or review are:

(a) the standards set by the Auditor-General in force at the commencement of the reporting period to which the subject matter of the audit relates, if the audit or review is conducted under:

(i) Division 1 of Part 4 of the Act;

(ii) section 49 of the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*;

(iii) Division 1A of Part 4 of the Act; or

(iv) Division 2A of Part 4 of the Act (if the engagement is an attestation engagement); or

(v) section 44 of the Act; or

(b) the standards set by the Auditor-General in force at the commencement of the audit or review, if the audit or review is conducted under:

(i) Division 2 of Part 4 of the Act;

(ii) Division 2A of Part 4 of the Act (if the engagement is a direct engagement); or

(iii) section 45 of the Act.

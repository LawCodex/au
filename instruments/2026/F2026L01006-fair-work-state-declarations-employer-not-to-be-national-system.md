###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 14(4)(a) of the *Fair Work Act 2009.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009.*

###### 5 Endorsement of State Declaration

For paragraph 14(4)(a) of the Act, a declaration as in force on 1 July 2026 under Part 3 of Schedule 5 to the *Industrial Relations Regulation 2018* (Qld) that an employer mentioned in Schedule 1 is not to be a national system employer for the purposes of the Act, is endorsed.

## Schedule 1—Employer declared by or under a law of Queensland not to be a national system employer

**Employer**

Sunshine Coast Waterways Authority

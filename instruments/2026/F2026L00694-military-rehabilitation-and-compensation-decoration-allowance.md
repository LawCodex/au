# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Decoration Allowance) Determination 2026

I, Matthew James Keogh, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 26 May 2026

Matthew James Keogh

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Decoration Allowance) Determination 2026*.

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 230C(1) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

Note: See section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* for rules in relation to the exercise of powers between enactment and commencement.

**4 Definitions**

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Commission;

(b) VEA;

(c) warlike service.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***eligible decoration*** means:

(a) any of the following decorations awarded for gallantry during eligible service:

(i) the Victoria Cross;

(ii) the Cross of Valour;

(iii) the Star of Courage;

(iv) the Distinguished Service Order;

(v) the Distinguished Service Cross;

(vi) the Military Cross;

(vii) the Distinguished Flying Cross;

(viii) the Distinguished Conduct Medal;

(ix) the Conspicuous Gallantry Medal;

(x) the Distinguished Service Medal;

(xi) the Military Medal;

(xii) the Distinguished Flying Medal;

(xiii) the member of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire (Military Division);

(xiv) the Medal of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire (Military Division) (1919–1958);

(xv) the Medal of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire (Military Division) with Gallantry Emblem (1958–1974);

(xvi) the Victoria Cross for Australia;

(xvii) the Star of Gallantry;

(xviii) the Medal for Gallantry; and

(b) the George Cross; and

(c) the George Medal.

***eligible service*** means:

(a) World War 1;

(b) World War 2;

(c) warlike service.

***World War 1*** has the same meaning as in subsection 442(1) of the Act.

***World War 2*** has the same meaning as in subsection 442(1) of the Act.

**5 Decoration allowance**

(1) The Commonwealth is liable to pay a weekly allowance (***decoration allowance***) to a person who has been awarded an eligible decoration if the person:

(a) is in receipt of a pension under Part II of the VEA; or

(b) would be in receipt of a pension under Part II of the VEA but for:

(i) the cancellation, under subsection 124(1) of the VEA, of a pension that had been granted to the person under Part II; or

(ii) section 26, 30C or 30D of the VEA.

(2) The amount of decoration allowance to be paid to a person under subsection (1) is $1.05 per week.

(3) Decoration allowance may be paid on a fortnightly basis.

**6 Application**

This instrument applies subject to section 108 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004*.

Note 1: Section 108 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* provides that, despite the repeal of section 102 of the old VEA by Division 4 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Simplification Act, that section continues to apply, on and after the date of commencement, in relation to a pension period (within the meaning of the old VEA) that starts before that date and ends on or after that date, as if that repeal had not happened.

Note 2: The following definitions in the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* apply for the purposes of section 108:

***date of commencement*** means 1 July 2026.

***old VEA*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*, as in force immediately before 21 April 2025.

***Simplification Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements, Treatment and Support (Simplification and Harmonisation) Act 2025.*

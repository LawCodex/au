###### 5 Use of computer programs

Relevant provisions under which decisions may be made by operation of a computer program

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 541A(2)(a) of the Act, a decision under the following relevant provisions of the Act may be made by the operation of a computer program (an ***authorised computer program***) under an arrangement made under subsection 541A(1) of the Act:

(a) subsections 122(2) and (3);

(b) section 124;

(c) paragraph 125(2)(b);

(d) sections 126, 127 and 128.

Classes of persons who may use computer programs

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 541A(2)(b) of the Act, each of the following is a class of persons that may use an authorised computer program for any purposes referred to in subsection 541A(1) of the Act in relation to a decision referred to in subsection (1):

(a) biosecurity officers who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables them to access the authorised computer program;

(b) biosecurity enforcement officers who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables them to access the authorised computer program;

(c) APS employees of the Agriculture Department who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables them to access the authorised computer program;

(d) persons:

(i) who are performing services for the Agriculture Department under a contract; and

(ii) who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables the person to access the authorised computer program;

(e) any other persons:

(i) who are employed or engaged by a Commonwealth body to provide services to the Commonwealth in connection with the Agriculture Department; and

(ii) who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables the person to access the authorised computer program;

(f) biosecurity industry participants who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables them to access the authorised computer program;

(g) persons:

(i) who are a person in charge of goods in relation to which the authorised computer program is to be used; and

(ii) who have a unique identifier issued by a Commonwealth body that enables the person to access the computer program.

Conditions of use of computer program

(3) For the purposes of paragraph 541A(2)(c) of the Act, a person in a class of persons who may use an authorised computer program under subsection (2) must:

(a) be satisfied on reasonable grounds that information entered into the authorised computer program by the person for the purpose of enabling decisions to be made by operation of the computer program is true and correct; and

(b) ensure that the information is accurately entered into the authorised computer program.

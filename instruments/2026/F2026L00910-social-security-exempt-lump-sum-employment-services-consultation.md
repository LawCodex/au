###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***employment services consultation, research and trial activities*** means the consultation, research and trial activities, in relation to employment services, undertaken by or for the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations.

***participant incentive payment*** means a payment made by, or on behalf of, the Commonwealth to a person for their participation in employment services consultation, research and trial activities.

###### 5 Exempt lump sum

For the purposes of the Act, a participant incentive payment is an exempt lump sum under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act.

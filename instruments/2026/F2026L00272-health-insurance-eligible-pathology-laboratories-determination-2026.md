###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 23DBA(1) of the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

###### 4. Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** *means the Health Insurance Act 1973.*

***Category GX (General)*** means the category of that name set out in section 17 of in the *Health Insurance (Accredited Pathology Laboratories – Approval) Principles 2017,* as in force from time to time.

***Category GY (General)*** means the category of that name set out in section 17 of the *Health Insurance (Accredited Pathology Laboratories – Approval) Principles 2017,* as in force from time to time.

***Category S (Specialised)*** means the category of that name set out in section 17 of the *Health Insurance (Accredited Pathology Laboratories – Approval) Principles 2017,* as in force from time to time.

Note 1 Unless the contrary intention appears, expressions used in this determination have the same meanings as in the Act—see section 13 of the Legislation Act 2003. The term accredited pathology laboratory is defined in subsection 3(1) of the Act.

###### 6. Categories of eligible pathology laboratories

(1) For the purpose of the definition of eligible pathology laboratory in subsection 23DA(1) of the Act, the following categories are prescribed:

accredited pathology laboratories allocated Category GX (General) in the approval given under section 23DN of the Act relating to the laboratory;

accredited pathology laboratories allocated Category GY (General) in the approval given under section 23DN of the Act relating to the laboratory;

accredited pathology laboratories allocated Category S (Specialised) in the approval given under section 23DN of the Act relating to the laboratory.

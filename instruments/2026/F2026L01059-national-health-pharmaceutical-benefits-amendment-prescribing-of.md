Mark Butler

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 2017

**1 Subsection 5(1)**

Insert:

***authorised nurse prescriber*** has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Act.

**2 Section 15 (heading)**

Omit “**or authorised nurse practitioner**”, substitute “**, authorised nurse practitioner or authorised nurse prescriber**”.

**3 At the end of section 15**

Add:

; (d) an application for approval as an authorised nurse prescriber under subsection 84AAN(1) of the Act.

**4 After paragraph 16(1)(d)**

Insert:

(da) an eligible nurse prescriber under section 84AAN of the Act; or

**5 At the end of section 16**

Add:

(5) If the Secretary allots a number to an approval mentioned in any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (da) in respect of a person, the Secretary may allot the same number to another approval mentioned in those paragraphs in respect of the person.

**6 Section 29 (at the end of the definition of practitioner)**

Add:

; (e) an authorised nurse prescriber.

**7 Paragraph 40(1)(a)**

Omit “or an authorised nurse practitioner”, substitute “, an authorised nurse practitioner or an authorised nurse prescriber”.

**8 Paragraph 40(1)(j)**

Omit “or authorised nurse practitioner”, substitute “, authorised nurse practitioner or authorised nurse prescriber”.

**9 Subsection 49(3)**

Omit “or authorised nurse practitioner” (first occurring), substitute “, an authorised nurse practitioner or an authorised nurse prescriber”.

**10 Subsection 49(3) (Note 1)**

Omit “or authorised nurse practitioner”, substitute “, authorised nurse practitioner or authorised nurse prescriber”.

**[Signed S. Campbell]**

Steven Campbell\

13 May 2026

CASA EX06/26 — Variation of Approved Maintenance Program Instrument 2026

1 Name

This instrument is *CASA EX06/26 — Variation of Approved Maintenance Program Instrument 2026*.

2 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on the day after it is registered; and

(b) is repealed at the end of 30 April 2029.

*Note* For regulation 11.250 of CASR, the directions under section 6 cease to be in force at the end of 30 April 2029.

3 Definitions

*Note* In this instrument, certain terms and expressions have the same meaning as they have in the *Civil Aviation Act 1988* and the regulations. These include ***instructions for continuing airworthiness*** and ***Part 42 Manual of Standards***.

In this instrument:

***approved maintenance program***, for an aircraft, has the meaning given by subregulation 42.015(1) of CASR.

***person responsible for continuing airworthiness***, for an aircraft, has the meaning given by regulation 42.105 of CASR.

***relevant change***: see paragraph 4(b).

***relevant requirements***: see paragraph 4(b).

***required actions***: see subsection 5(1).

4 Application

This instrument applies if:

(a) there is an approved maintenance program for an aircraft; and

(b) there is a change (the ***relevant change***) to the requirements (the ***relevant requirements***) in the instructions for continuing airworthiness for the aircraft, or an aeronautical product fitted to the aircraft, that relate to maintenance that is required by the instructions to be carried out on a regular basis; and

(c) as a result of the change, the program no longer complies with the requirements.

*Note* Subsection 2.9.1 of the Part 42 Manual of Standards states the requirements to be met for the approved maintenance program for an aircraft to comply with the requirements in the instructions for continuing airworthiness for the aircraft, or an aeronautical product fitted to the aircraft, that relate to maintenance that is required by the instructions to be carried out on a regular basis.

5 Exemptions — time limit mentioned in subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR

(1) The person responsible for continuing airworthiness for the aircraft (the ***relevant person***) is exempt from compliance with regulation 42.110 of CASR to the extent that it requires the person to comply with the 90-day time limit, mentioned in subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR, to carry out the actions (the ***required actions***) mentioned in the subregulation in relation to the relevant change.

(2) The registered operator of the aircraft is exempt from compliance with subregulation 42.030(1), and paragraph 42.030(2)(a), of CASR to the extent that the provisions require the operator to ensure that the relevant person complies with the 90-day time limit, mentioned in subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR, to carry out the required actions in relation to the relevant change.

(3) The exemption under subsection (1) is subject to the condition stated in subsection (4).

(4) The relevant person must carry out the required actions within 1 year after the occurrence of the relevant change.

(5) The exemption under subsection (2) is subject to the condition stated in subsection (6).

(6) The registered operator of the aircraft must ensure that the relevant person carries out the required actions within 1 year after the occurrence of the relevant change.

6 Directions — maintenance before required actions are carried out

(1) This section applies if:

(a) the person responsible for continuing airworthiness for the aircraft (the ***relevant person***) makes use of the exemption under subsection 5(1); and

(b) the person has not carried out the required actions in relation to the relevant change; and

(c) the period mentioned in subsection 5(4), in which the person must carry out the required actions, has not ended; and

(d) maintenance relating to the change is required, because of the relevant requirements, to be carried out on the aircraft or an aeronautical product fitted to the aircraft.

(2) The relevant person and registered operator of the aircraft are each directed to ensure that the maintenance is carried out, in accordance with the relevant change.

*Note* Under regulation 42.145 of CASR, the person responsible for continuing airworthiness for an aircraft must ensure that maintenance is carried out on the aircraft as required by the maintenance program for the aircraft. The effect of the directions under this section is that the relevant person and registered operator of the aircraft must each ensure that the maintenance relating to the relevant change is carried out on the aircraft or an aeronautical product fitted to the aircraft, even though the relevant person has not yet carried out the required actions in relation to the change.

7 Exemptions — subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR

(1) This section applies if:

(a) the non-compliance by the approved maintenance program with the relevant requirements, as a result of the relevant change, is supported by technical justification given by the person responsible for continuing airworthiness for the aircraft (the ***relevant person***) to CASA; and

(b) CASA gives written notice to the person that it is satisfied the non‑compliance will have no adverse effect on the continuing airworthiness of the aircraft.

(2) The relevant person is exempt from compliance with regulation 42.110 of CASR to the extent that it requires the person to comply with subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR in relation to the relevant change.

(3) The registered operator of the aircraft is exempt from compliance with subregulation 42.030(1), and paragraph 42.030(2)(a), of CASR to the extent that the provisions require the operator to ensure that the relevant person complies with subregulation 42.150(2) of CASR in relation to the relevant change.

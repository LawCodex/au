# Customs (Customs Place—Christmas Island) Approval 2026

LIN26/033

I, Gavan Reynolds AO, Comptroller of the Indian Ocean Territories Customs Service, make the following approval.

Dated 19 March 2026

Gavan Reynolds AO

Comptroller of the Indian Ocean Territories Customs Service

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Customs (Customs Place—Christmas Island) Approval 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 183UA(2) of the *Customs Act 1901* (C.I.)*.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Customs Act 1901* (C.I.).

***Customs place*** has the same meaning as in subsection 183UA(1) of the Act.

Note: The *Customs Act 1901* (C.I.) is the *Customs Act 1901* as modified by the *Customs Ordinance 1993* (C.I.).

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Approval of Customs place

For the purposes of paragraph 183UA(1)(f) of the Act, the Christmas Island Post Office located at 3 Jalan Pantai, Christmas Island, Western Australia, with the postcode 6798, is a Customs place.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Comptroller of the Indian Ocean Territories Customs Service Instrument of Approval No. 1 of 2015

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under:

(a) the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001*; and

(b) the *Business Names Registration Act 2011*; and

(c) the *Corporations Act 2001*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Personal details of certain officers

##### Corporations Regulations 2001

**1 After paragraph 2N.2.01(1)(d)**

Insert:

(da) electronic address;

**2 Paragraph 2N.2.01(1)(e)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(e) for each director, secretary and alternate director:

(i) the person’s personal details mentioned in subsection 205B(3) of the Act; and

(ii) if the person takes advantage of paragraph 205D(1)(b) of the Act to use an alternative address—the person’s usual residential address;

**3 At the end of subsection 2N.2.01(1)**

Add:

Note: For a person covered by subparagraph (e)(ii), the public will not be able to access the person’s usual residential address (see subparagraph 1274(2)(a)(iaab) of the Act).

**4 Paragraph 2N.4.01(1)(a)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(a) for each director, secretary and alternate director:

(i) the person’s personal details mentioned in subsection 205B(3) of the Act; and

(ii) if the person takes advantage of paragraph 205D(1)(b) of the Act to use an alternative address—the person’s usual residential address;

**5 At the end of subsection 2N.4.01(1)**

Add:

; (s) the electronic address of the company.

Note: For a person covered by subparagraph (a)(ii), the public will not be able to access the person’s usual residential address (see subparagraph 1274(2)(a)(iaac) of the Act).

**6 Regulation 9.1.02**

Before “For”, insert “(1)”.

**7 Subparagraph 9.1.02(a)(vi)**

Repeal the subparagraph, substitute:

(vi) for each of its directors, secretary and alternate directors—the person’s personal details mentioned in subsection 205B(3) of the Act;

(via) for each of its other officers (if any)—the person’s name and address;

**8 Subparagraph 9.1.02(d)(vi)**

Repeal the subparagraph, substitute:

(vi) for each of its directors—the person’s personal details mentioned in paragraph 601CB(c) of the Act;

(via) for each of its other officers (if any)—the person’s name and address;

**9 Paragraph 9.1.02(e)**

Omit “each company”, substitute “each foreign company”.

**10 Subparagraph 9.1.02(e)(vi)**

Repeal the subparagraph, substitute:

(vi) for each of its directors—the person’s personal details mentioned in paragraph 601CE(c) of the Act;

(via) for each of its other officers (if any)—the person’s name and address;

**11 At the end of regulation 9.1.02**

Add:

(2) For subsections 1274A(3) and (4) of the Act, information is prescribed if:

(a) the information relates to an entity that is:

(i) a company covered by paragraph (1)(a) of this regulation; or

(ii) a body covered by paragraph (1)(d) of this regulation; or

(iii) a foreign company covered by paragraph (1)(e) of this regulation; and

(b) for each such entity, the information is:

(i) for each of the entity’s officers who is a director or alternate director—whether the officer has a director identification number, and if so that number; and

(ii) how many of the entity’s directors and alternate directors have, and how many do not have, a director identification number; and

(c) ASIC considers it appropriate for:

(i) a person to search the information in accordance with subsection 1274A(3) of the Act; or

(ii) the information to be made available in accordance with subsection 1274A(4) of the Act.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 10 of the *Imported Food Control Regulations 2019*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Kava products and enoki mushrooms

##### Imported Food Control Order 2019

**1 Section 4 (definition of kava products)**

Omit “mentioned in section 2.6.3—3 of the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, as in force at the commencement of this definition”, substitute “covered by paragraph 2.6.3—3(a) or (b) of the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, as in force on 23 March 2022”.

**2 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (at the end of the table)**

Add:

| 26 | Enoki mushrooms that are fresh |
|---|---|

**3 Clause 1 of Schedule 2 (at the end of the table)**

Add:

| 4 | Kava products |
|---|---|

### Part 2—Melons

##### Imported Food Control Order 2019

**4 Section 7**

Omit “17 or 18”, substitute “17, 18 or 27”.

**5 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (at the end of the table)**

Add:

| 27 | Melons that are:<br>(a) both fresh and whole; or<br>(b) both:<br>(i) fresh or frozen; and<br>(ii) ready‑to‑eat |
|---|---|

### Part 3—Pufferfish

##### Imported Food Control Order 2019

**6 Subsection 6(1)**

Omit “12 or 23”, substitute “12, 23 or 28”.

**7 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (at the end of the table)**

Add:

| 28 | Pufferfish |
|---|---|

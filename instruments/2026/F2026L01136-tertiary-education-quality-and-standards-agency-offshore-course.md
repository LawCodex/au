###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 44H(3) of the *Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency Act 2011*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

Australian Qualifications Framework;

authorised offshore provider;

enrolled;

higher education award;

offshore premises;

offshore provided Australian course of study;

premises.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency Act 2011*.

###### 5 Manner of providing reports under section 44H of the Act

The manner in which reports must be provided to TEQSA by an authorised offshore provider under section 44H of the Act is by submitting the required information in a spreadsheet via: a secure upload link sent to the authorised offshore provider by TEQSA for this purpose; or another manner of submission approved by TEQSA.

###### 6 Information required to be provided in reports under section 44H of the Act

Reports provided to TEQSA under section 44H of the Act must contain the following information:

In relation to each offshore provided Australian course of study provided by the authorised offshore provider: the name of the course of study; the Australian Qualifications Framework level of the course of study (if applicable); whether the course of study leads to a higher education award offered or conferred by any entity other than the authorised offshore provider itself; the name of each country, and each city, at which the course of study is provided at offshore premises.

In relation to each offshore provided Australian course of study provided at each offshore premises by the authorised offshore provider: the start and end date of the academic year in which the course of study is delivered; the total student enrolment figure for the course of study for the academic year by headcount; the standard duration of the course of study for a full-time student; the standard duration for a full-time student of the component of the course of study that is delivered at offshore premises; where one or more entities other than other than the authorised offshore provider are involved in providing the curriculum, teaching or learning support for the course of study, the name of each entity other than the authorised offshore provider involved in providing the curriculum, teaching or learning support for the course of study.

In relation to each offshore premises at which one or more offshore provided Australian course of study is provided by the authorised offshore provider, the name of each entity with ownership of the offshore premises.

Note: A report containing this information must be provided in the manner set out in section 5 of this instrument.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Disability Insurance Scheme (Old Framework Plans) Determination 2024

**1 Section 4 (definition of pricing document)**

Repeal the definition, insert:

***pricing document*** means any instrument made under subsection 45C(1) of the Act and any document incorporated by reference into that instrument as in force from time to time.

Note: In 2026 the *National Disability Insurance Scheme (Pricing Arrangements) Determination 2026* could be found on the Federal Register of Legislation.

**2 Section 4 (definition of quotable)**

Repeal the definition, insert:

***quotable***: a support item is ***quotable*** if the document known as the *Assistive Technology, Home Modifications and Consumables Code Guide* (as it exists from time to time) provides that the support item is subject to quotation or otherwise indicates that a quote is required for the support item.

Note: In 2026 this document could be viewed on the Agency’s website (www.ndis.gov.au).

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Fisheries Management (E-monitoring Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery) Direction 2026

**1 Requirement to install and operate an e-monitoring system**

Section 6(1) after the words “A fishing concession holder (or a person acting on behalf of the fishing concession holder) fishing pelagic longline in the Eastern Tuna and Billfish Fishery” insert the word “must”.

**2 Requirement to monitor function of an e-monitoring system, arrange repair and provide e-monitoring data**

Section 7(1)(a)(i) after the words “data drive exchange;”, insert the word “and”.

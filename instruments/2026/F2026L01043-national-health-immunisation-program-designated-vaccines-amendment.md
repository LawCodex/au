# National Health (Immunisation Program — Designated Vaccines) Amendment Determination (No. 3) 2026

I, Dave McNally, delegate of the Minister for Health and Ageing, make the following Determination.

Dated 7 August 2026

Dave McNally

Assistant Secretary

National Immunisation Program Branch

Health Security and Immunisation Division

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *National Health (Immunisation Program — Designated Vaccines) Amendment Determination (No. 3) 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 9B(2) of the *National Health Act 1953*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health (Immunisation Program — Designated Vaccines) Determination 2014 (No.1)

**1 Section 4**

Insert:

**severe immunocompromise means a severely immunocompromising condition as described in the COVID-19 chapter of the Australian Immunisation Handbook as in force on 5 December 2025.**

Note: In 2026, the Australian Immunisation Handbook could be accessed on the Department’s website at https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/covid-19

**2 After item 223 of the table in Part 2 of Schedule 1**

Insert:

| 224 | Vaccine<br>BNT162b2 mRNA COVID-19 vaccine<br>Circumstances<br>Vaccine may be provided to a person who:<br>(a) has a severe immunocompromise and is at least 18 years of age but less than 65 years; or<br>(b) is at least 65 years of age but less than 75 years; or<br>(c) is at least 75 years of age; or<br>(d) is at least 50 years of age but less than 75 years and an Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander. | Comirnaty | Injection (0.3mL) | SARS-CoV-2 spike protein (mRNA), 30 µg | For a person who is at least 18 years of age but less than 65 years with severe immunocompromise—1 dose per calendar year.<br>For a person who is at least 65 years of age but less than 75 years—1 dose per calendar year.<br>For a person who is at least 75 years of age—2 doses per calendar year.<br>For an Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander person who is at least 50 years of age but less than 75 years—1 dose per calendar year. |

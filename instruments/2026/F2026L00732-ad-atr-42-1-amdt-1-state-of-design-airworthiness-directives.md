# AD/ATR 42/1 Amdt 1 – State of Design Airworthiness Directives

[image: Horizontal line to indicate the end of the first cell in the table.]\
**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the commencement date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below repeals Airworthiness Directive (**AD**) **AD/ATR 42/1**. and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001 (1) of CASR and subsection 33 (3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct an unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

[image: Horizontal line to indicate the end of the table.]

**ATR-GIE Avions de Transport Regional ATR 42 Series Aeroplanes**

**AD/ATR 42/1**

**Amdt 1**

**State of Design Airworthiness Directives 12/2026**

Applicability: ATR 42 series aeroplanes, as identified in an Airworthiness Directive (AD) listed in Table 1 or Table 2.

Requirement: 1. Determine the applicability of the ADs issued by the Direction Générale de l’Aviation Civile of France (DGAC), listed in Table 1, and the ADs issued by the European Aviation Safety Agency (EASA), listed in Table 2.

2\. If it is determined that an DGAC AD listed in Table 1 or an EASA AD listed in Table 2 is applicable and has not been previously accomplished, perform the actions required by that AD as it exists at the date of issue.

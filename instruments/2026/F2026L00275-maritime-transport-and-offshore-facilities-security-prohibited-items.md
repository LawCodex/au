# Maritime Transport and Offshore Facilities Security (Prohibited Items) Determination 2026

LIN 26/041

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following determination.

Dated 17 March 2026

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Maritime Transport and Offshore Facilities Security (Prohibited Items) Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) the day after the instrument is registered; and

(b) immediately after the commencement of Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the *Transport Security Amendment (Security of Australia’s Transport Sector) Act 2025*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 10A of the *Maritime Transport and Offshore Facilities Security Act 2003.*

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 10 of the Act, including ***prohibited item***.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Maritime Transport and Offshore Facilities Security Act 2003*.

###### 5 Prohibited items

Under section 10A of the Act, each of the following is determined to be a prohibited item for the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***prohibited item*** in section 10 of the Act:

(a) an imitation or replica of a firearm;

(b) an imitation or replica of a bomb, grenade, rocket, missile or mine.

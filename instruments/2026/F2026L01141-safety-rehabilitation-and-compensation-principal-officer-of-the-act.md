###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 4(14)(a) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

***Public Sector Workers Compensation Commissioner*** means the person appointed as the Public Sector Workers Compensation Commissioner under section 18 of the *Public Sector Workers Compensation Fund Act 2018* (ACT).

###### 6 Declaration

(1) I declare that the Public Sector Workers Compensation Commissioner is taken to be the principal officer of the Australian Capital Territory for the purposes of the Act.

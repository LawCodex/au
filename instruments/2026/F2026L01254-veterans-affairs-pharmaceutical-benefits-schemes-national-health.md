Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Affairs Pharmaceutical Benefits Schemes (National Health Act—Prescribing of Pharmaceutical Benefits) Amendment Determination 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 October 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 91(4) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* and 286(5) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme 2013 No. R43

**1 Section 3, after the definition of Authorised Nurse Practitioner**

**insert**

**authorised nurse prescriber** has the meaning given by subsection 84(1) of the National Health Act 1953.

**authorised optometrist** has the meaning given by subsection 84(1) of the National Health Act 1953.

**2 Section 3 (definition of Commission)**

*repeal the definition, substitute*

***Commission*** means the body corporate continued in existence by section 360B of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004.*

**3 Section 3, after the definition of paper‑based prescription**

*insert*

**participating dental practitioner** has the meaning given by subsection 84(1) of the National Health Act 1953.

**4 Section 3 (definition of RPBS prescriber, including the Note)**

*repeal the definition and the Note, substitute*

***RPBS prescriber*** means a *Medical Practitioner*, an *Authorised Midwife*, an *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, an *authorised nurse prescriber*, a *participating dental practitioner* or an *authorised optometrist*.

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**5 Paragraph 7(e), including the Note**

*repeal the paragraph and the Note, substitute*

(e) **basis for prescribing**: the prescribing of therapeutic substances other than for clinical diagnosis by a *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* shall be invalid;

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**6 Subsection 11(1)**

*repeal the subsection*, *substitute*

Prescriptions are to be written by a *Medical Practitioner, Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* and, except where inconsistent with the *Scheme,* are to:

satisfy the requirements for prescriptions in the *National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 2017*; and

in the case of a prescription written by an *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist*—only be for *Pharmaceutical benefits* the person is permitted to prescribe under the *National Health Act 1953* (including under instruments made under that Act).

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**7 Paragraph 11A(1)(e)**

*repeal the paragraph, substitute*

(e) for an *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner, authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist*—states in the prescription the number allotted to his or her approval under section 16 of the *National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 2017*; and

**8 Section 12**

*repeal the section, substitute*

**12 When prescriptions are invalid**

A *prescription* is not a valid *Pharmaceutical benefit* if the *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist*:

except where the prescription is a *medication chart prescription*, writes a prescription for a *Pharmaceutical benefit* for a person where the same *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* has already written another prescription for the same benefit for that person on the same day; or

prescribes, on the one form, a *Pharmaceutical benefit* that is a drug of addiction and another *Pharmaceutical benefit*, and directs that the supply of either *Pharmaceutical benefit* is to be repeated (but, if no repeats of either item are ordered, the *prescription* may be accepted provided that this is in accordance with the relevant State or Territory law); or

prescribes a narcotic drug for the *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* who is writing the prescription; or

prescribes, on a *Standard Prescription Form*, an item that is not listed in the *RPBS Schedule* or *PBS Schedule*; or

prescribes, on a *Standard Prescription Form*, a benefit in contravention of any of the restrictions set out in paragraph 7; or

where the prescription is written by an *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* for an *Eligible Person*—prescribes a *Pharmaceutical benefit* that is not available to the *Eligible Person* under the *PBS*.

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**9 Section 13**

*repeal the section*, *substitute*\
**13 Maximum quantity and repeats allowed**

The quantity and repeats for *Scheduled items* are to be confined to those specified in the *RPBS Schedule* or *PBS Schedule*. However, where inadequate, the *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* may seek *Prior Approval* to prescribe a quantity greater, or a greater number of repeats, than the maximum listed in the *RPBS Schedule* or *PBS Schedule*.

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**10 Section 15**

*repeal the section, substitute*

**15 Practitioners and others subject to this Scheme**

Where a *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* prescribes for an *Eligible Person*, the *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist* shall be subject to the terms and conditions of this *Scheme* and the *Explanatory Notes*.

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

**11 Section 17**

*repeal the section, substitute*

**17 Substitution of lesser priced alternative brand of drug**

Where a *valid prescription*, issued by a *Medical Practitioner*, *Authorised Midwife*, *Authorised Nurse Practitioner*, *authorised nurse prescriber*, *participating dental practitioner* or *authorised optometrist*, prescribes a brand of drug listed on the *PBS* or *RPBS* Schedule, a *Community Pharmacist* may substitute, with the approval of the prescriber, a lesser priced alternative *PBS* or *RPBS* listed brand of the drug in lieu of the brand prescribed and shall endorse the original, duplicate and repeat authorisation accordingly.

Note: an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* may only prescribe *Pharmaceutical benefits* listed on the *PBS*. A prescription issued by an *RPBS prescriber* who is not a *Medical Practitioner* for *Pharmaceutical benefits* that are not available on the *PBS* is not recognised under the *Scheme*.

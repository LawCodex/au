###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 232 of the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3 of the Act*,* including the following:

(a) National VET Regulator;

(b) NVR registered training organisation;

(c) registered provider; and

(d) VET accredited course.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011*.

***course***:

(a) in relation to an NVR registered training organisation and a VET accredited course, has the same meaning as in section 3 of the Act; and

(b) in relation to the performance of functions by the National VET Regulator as an ESOS agency, has the same meaning as in section 5AA of the ESOS Act.

***ESOS Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***ESOS agency*** has the same meaning as in the ESOS Act.

***provider*** has the same meaning as in the ESOS Act.

***Part 2 fee*** means a fee determined in Part 2 of this instrument.

***units of competency*** means the specification of the standards of performance required in the workplace as defined in a training product.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

### Part 3—Waiver of fees

###### 10 Application of Part

This Part applies for the purposes of paragraph 232(5)(c) of the Act.

###### 11 Withdrawal of application

(1) If:

(a) a person makes an application to the National VET Regulator under the Act or the ESOS Act; and

(b) the person has paid a Part 2 fee in relation to the application; and

(c) the person notifies the Regulator in writing that they wish to withdraw the application before the Regulator has commenced work on the application,

the Regulator must waive the fee.

###### 12 Special circumstances

(1) The National VET Regulator can waive a Part 2 fee, in whole or in part, if the Regulator determines that:

(a) special or unusual circumstances exist which cause the fee to be unreasonable or inequitable; or

(b) it would be uneconomical to pursue the amount of the fee that remains unpaid.

(2) The Regulator must waive a fee if:

(a) a person paid the fee in relation to an application under section 200 of the Act; and

(b) the Regulator does not consider the application because the application does not relate to a reviewable decision within the meaning of section 199 of the Act.

(3) To avoid doubt, the following circumstances do not constitute special or unusual circumstances for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a):

where the person required to pay the fee ceases to exist, or

where the person ceases to be registered under the Act and the ESOS Act; or

where the person ceases to carry on business as an NVR registered training organisation or as a registered provider.

###### 13 Refund of waived fees

If a person has already paid a Part 2 fee, and the National VET Regulator waives the whole or part of the fee in accordance with this Part, the Regulator must refund the amount waived.

### Part 4—Miscellaneous

###### 14 Delegated functions

There are no fees payable in respect of goods or services provided by a delegate under subsections 224(2), 225(1) or 226(1) of the Act.

###### 15 Set off of fees

(1) This section applies for the purposes of paragraph 232(5)(b) of the Act.

(2) The National VET Regulator may set off the amount of any fees or charges payable by a person to it under the Act, the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Charges) Act 2012* or the ESOS Act against any amount that is payable to the person by the National VET Regulator under the Act or any other law.

Example: In July, an NVR registered training organisation pays a National VET Regulator annual registration charge amount of $16,100 (as imposed by Part 1A of the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Charges) Act 2012*).

In September, the Regulator:

conducts a compliance audit of the organisation’s operations under section 35 of the Act, resulting in a charge of $11,700 (as imposed by Part 2 of the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Charges) Act 2012*; and

cancels the organisation’s registration under section 39 of the Act.

Because the organisation’s registration was cancelled in September, any remaining whole months of annual registration charge will be refundable. The Regulator adjusts the amount of National VET Regulator annual registration charge payable to $4,025, entitling the organisation to a refund of $12,075 ($16,100 – $4,025).

The organisation has not yet paid the compliance audit charge of $11,700, so the Regulator sets off this amount against the refund amount of $12,075. This reduces the refund payable to the organisation to $375 ($12,075 – $11,700).

## Part 5 – Transitional Provisions

###### 16 Transitional arrangements for applications in progress

This section applies if, before the commencement of this instrument:

an application fee has been paid or waived for any of the following services under the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Fees) Determination 2022*: item 1, table 1; item 3, table 1; item 5, table 1; item 7, table 1; item 1, table 2; item 3, table 2; item 5, table 2; item 1, table 3; item 3, table 3; and

the relevant assessment fee relating to a service listed in paragraph (a) has not been paid or waived.

The assessment fee that applies to an application referred to in subsection (1) is the relevant assessment fee set out in the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Fees) Determination 2022* as in force immediately before the commencement of this instrument.

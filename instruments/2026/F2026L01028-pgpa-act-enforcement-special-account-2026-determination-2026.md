###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 78(1) and 78(3) of the PGPA Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***ASIC*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001*.

***Enforcement Instrument*** means the *PGPA Act Determination (Enforcement Special Account 2016) — Establishment*.

***Enforcement Special Account 2016*** means the Enforcement Special Account 2016 established by the Enforcement Instrument.

***PGPA Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***the special account*** means the Enforcement Special Account 2026 established by this instrument.

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(a) of the PGPA Act, the *Enforcement Special Account 2026* is established as a special account.

### Part 2—Special account

###### 6 Amounts that may be credited to the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the following amounts may be credited to the special account:

(a) amounts received in the course of the performance of functions that relate to the purposes of the special account; and

(b) amounts received from any person for the purposes of the special account.

###### 7 Purposes of the special account

(1) For paragraph 78(1)(c) of the PGPA Act, the purposes of the special account, in relation to which amounts may be debited from the special account, are:

(a) to make payments to investigate and/or bring legal or administrative proceedings in the exercise of ASIC’s functions and powers, in relation to suspected breaches that are of significant public interest, including but not limited to breaches of:

(i) the *Corporations Act 2001*; and

(ii) the *Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001*;

(b) to make payments in relation to court costs which may be awarded against ASIC arising out of any such investigations or proceedings;

(c) to make payments of any other amounts for which ASIC may be liable, arising out of any proceedings or investigations, or anticipated proceedings or investigations, mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b), including as a result of agreements made in relation to these proceedings;

(d) to disburse amounts in relation to incidental activities;

(e) to reduce the balance of the special account (and, therefore, the available appropriation for the special account) without making a real or notional payment; and

(f) to repay amounts that have been credited to the special account where a court order, Act or other law requires or permits the repayment of an amount received.

(2) Without limiting paragraph (1)(a), a suspected breach of law is of significant public interest where it:

(a) relates to corporate or financial services regulation, and regulatory action in relation to the suspected breach (or a series of related suspected breaches of which it forms part) can reasonably be regarded as important for continued community confidence in the financial system; or

(b) is not covered by paragraph (a) but gives rise to a similar or greater level of public interest as a suspected breach that is covered by paragraph (a).

###### 8 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(d) of the PGPA Act, the accountable authority responsible for the special account is the Chairperson of ASIC.

### Part 3—Transitional, revocation and repeal provisions

###### 9 Opening balance of the special account

For paragraph 78(1)(b) of the PGPA Act, the amount in subsection 9(2) (***opening balance***) is required to be credited to the special account immediately after its establishment.

The opening balance is equal to the amount standing to the credit of the Enforcement Special Account 2016 immediately prior to the commencement of this instrument.

###### 10 Revocation of Enforcement Instrument

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the Enforcement Instrument is revoked.

###### 11 Additional repeal provisions

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, Part 3 of this instrument is repealed at the end of the seventh day after the revocation of the Enforcement Instrument under section 10 (***date of repeal***).

For subsection 78(3) of the PGPA Act, the following definitions in section 4 are repealed on the date of repeal:

definition of Enforcement Instrument; and

definition of Enforcement Special Account 2016.

Despite subsection 11(1), the effects of Part 3 of this instrument remain in force after the date of repeal.

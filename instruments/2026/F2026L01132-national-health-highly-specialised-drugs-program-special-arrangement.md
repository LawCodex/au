## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health (Highly Specialised Drugs Program) Special Arrangement 2021

**1 Section 6**

Repeal the following definitions:

definition of ***accredited prescriber of medication for the treatment of hepatitis B***;

definition of ***accredited prescriber of medication for the treatment of hepatitis C***.

**2 At the end of subsection 7(4)**

Add:

**; (f) a benefit that has a drug that is a medication for the treatment of hepatitis B.**

**3 Subsection 7(5) (heading)**

Omit “*hepatitis B, hepatitis C,* ”.

**4 Subsection 7(5) (table)**

Repeal the table, substitute:

<tr>
<td colspan="3"><b>Authorised prescribers for certain HSD pharmaceutical benefits</b></td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td><b>Column 2</b><br><b>is an</b> <b><i>authorised prescriber</b></i> <b>for an HSD pharmaceutical benefit that has a drug that is …</b></td>

**5 Subsection 7(6A) (heading)**

Omit “*erythropoietin stimulating agents*”*,* substitute “*specified medications*”*.*

**6 Subsection 7(6A)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

**An authorised nurse practitioner is an authorised prescriber for the following HSD pharmaceutical benefits:**

a benefit that has an erythropoietin stimulating agent;

**a benefit that has a drug that is a medication for the treatment of hepatitis B;**

**a benefit that has a drug that is a medication for the treatment of hepatitis C;**

a benefit that has the drug ruxolitinib.

**7 Subsection 8(10) (heading)**

Omit “*erythropoietin stimulating agents*”, substitute “*specified medications*”.

**8 Subsections 8(10) and (11)**

Omit “that has an erythropoietin stimulating agent”, substitute “mentioned in paragraph 7(6A)(a) or (d)”.

**9 Subsection 8(11) (heading)**

Omit “*erythropoietin stimulating agents*”, substitute “*specified medications*”.

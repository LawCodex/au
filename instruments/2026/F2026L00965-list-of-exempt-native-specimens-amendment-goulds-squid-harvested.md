# List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Gould’s squid harvested commercially in accordance with the Fisheries (Scalefish) Rules 2015 (Tas)) Instrument, July 2026

I, Belinda Jago, Branch Head, Ocean and Wildlife, as Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following instrument.

13 July 2026

BELINDA JAGO

Belinda Jago

Delegate of the Minister for the Environment and Water

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *List of Exempt Native Specimens Amendment (Gould’s squid harvested commercially in accordance with the* Fisheries (Scalefish) Rules 2015 *(Tas)) Instrument, July 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 303DC(1)(a) of the EPBC Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

**EPBC Act** means the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

| Commercially harvested specimens that are or are derived from Gould’s squid (*Nototodarus gouldi*), as defined in the *Fisheries (Scalefish) Rules 2015* (Tas), in force under the *Living Marine Resources Management Act 1995* (Tas), but not including:<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 209 of the EPBC Act (Australia’s List of Migratory Species), or<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 248 of the EPBC Act (Australia’s List of Marine Species), or<br>specimens that belong to eligible listed threatened species, as defined under section 303BC of the EPBC Act, or<br>specimens that belong to taxa listed under section 303CA of the EPBC Act (Taxa listed in the CITES Appendices). | Gould’s squid harvested commercially in accordance with the *Fisheries (Scalefish) Rules 2015* (Tas) | The specimen that is or is derived from Gould’s squid (*Nototodarus gouldi*) was taken lawfully;<br>Export approval has been provided on the basis that commercial catches of Gould’s squid will be managed in accordance with the *Fisheries (Scalefish) Rules 2015* (Tas) in force under the *Living Marine Resources Management Act 1995* (Tas);<br>During the term of the export approval, the Department of Natural Resources and Environment Tasmania must, consistent with Appendix B of the *Guidelines for the ecologically sustainable management of fisheries – 2nd edition*, provide the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (the department) with an annual report by 30 September each year;<br>In the interim, if there are any intended changes to legislative instruments or material changes to management arrangements that may affect the basis on which the approval was made, the department must be notified;<br>The specimens are included in the list until 13 July 2036. |

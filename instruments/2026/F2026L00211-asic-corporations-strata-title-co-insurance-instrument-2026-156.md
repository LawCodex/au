# ASIC Corporations (Strata Title Co-Insurance) Instrument 2026/156

ASIC Corporations (Strata Title Co-Insurance) Instrument 2026/156

I, Mary Zakaria, delegate of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, make the following legislative instrument.

Date 4 March 2026

Mary Zakaria

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Strata Title Co-Insurance) Instrument 2026/156*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The register may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 1020F(1)(c) of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Simplified outline of this instrument

This instrument provides relief by modifying the Act so that only one insurer is required to prepare a Product Disclosure Statement for a home building insurance product that is jointly issued by more than one insurer under a strata title co-insurance arrangement.\
Certain requirements must be satisfied for the relief to apply.

5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***home building insurance product*** has same meaning as in subparagraph 761G(5)(b)(ii) of the Act.

***participating share*** means, in relation to an insurer that has jointly issued a home building insurance product under a strata title co-insurance arrangement, the sum insured by the insurer under the product as a proportion of the total sum insured by all the insurers under the product.

***strata title co-insurance arrangement*** means an arrangement under which:

(a) a home building insurance product is jointly issued by more than one insurer to an owners corporation (however described) in relation to strata units held under a strata title system (or a similar system) established under the law of a State or a Territory; and

(b) each insurer is liable for a proportion of the total sum insured under the product.

***strata units*** means strata title units, community title interests or similar interests in real property.

Part 2—Declaration

6 Strata title co-insurance arrangement Product Disclosure Statements

Part 7.9 of the Act applies in relation to a home building insurance product under a strata title co-insurance arrangement as if section 1013A of the Act were modified or varied by:

(a) in subsection (1), after “the issuer of the financial product”, inserting:

“and, in the case of a jointly issued product, may be a document that is prepared by only one issuer of the product”; and

(b) in subsection (3), after “the ***responsible person*** for the financial product”, inserting:

“, provided that if a Product Disclosure Statement for a jointly issued product is prepared by one issuer of the product, that issuer is the ***responsible person*** for the product.”.

7 Where declaration applies

The declaration in section 6 applies in relation to a strata title co-insurance arrangement where one of the issuers (the ***lead issuer***) of the home building insurance product issued under the arrangement has prepared:

(a) a Product Disclosure Statement that contains the terms of the strata title co-insurance arrangement other than:

(i) the name and contact details of the other issuers (the ***following issuers***); and

(ii) the participating shares of the lead issuer and each following issuer; and

(b) a Supplementary Product Disclosure Statement that contains the name and contact details of each following issuer and the participating share of the lead issuer and each following issuer.

Part 3—Repeal

8 Repeal

This instrument is repealed at the start of 1 April 2031.

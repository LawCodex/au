###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*.

***bulk wheat*** means wheat to be loaded onto a ship for export but does not include wheat to be exported in a bag or container that is not capable of holding more than 50 tonnes of wheat.

***entity*** has the meaning given by section 64A of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***exporter*** means an entity seeking access to, or using, port terminal services for the purpose of exporting bulk wheat.

***port terminal facility*** means a facility (including a ship loader) for use at a port for loading bulk wheat onto a ship, including any of the following situated at the port and associated with the facility:

(a) an intake/receival facility;

(b) a grain storage facility;

(c) a weighing facility;

(d) a shipping belt.

***port terminal service*** means a service (within the meaning of Part IIIA of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*) provided by means of a port terminal facility, and includes the use of a port terminal facility.

***port terminal service provider*** means an entity that owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly) a port terminal facility that is used, or is to be used, to provide a port terminal service.

***reference prices*** means the standard prices a port terminal service provider charges for port terminal services on the standard terms at a port where the provider owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly) a port terminal facility.

***shipping stem report*** means a report referred to in subsection 10(1).

***standard terms*** means the standard terms and conditions, developed by a port terminal service provider, on which port terminal services are made available at a port where the provider owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly) a port terminal facility.

###### 6 Mandatory industry code

For the purposes of section 51AE of the Act, the industry code set out in Part 2 of this instrument:

(a) is prescribed for the purposes of Part IVB of the Act; and

(b) is declared to be a mandatory industry code.

###### 7 Repeal of this instrument

This instrument is repealed on 1 October 2029.

### Part 2—Wheat port code

#### Division 1—Purposes of industry code

###### 8 Purposes of industry code

The purposes of the industry code in this Part are:

(a) to promote and support good faith in commercial dealings between port terminal service providers and exporters of bulk wheat; and

(b) to provide information transparency to wheat export supply chain participants by making the following available to the public:

(i) shipping stem reports;

(ii) policies and procedures for managing demand for port terminal services;

(iii) standard terms and reference prices for port terminal services.

#### Division 2—General obligations of port terminal service providers and exporters

###### 9 Obligation to deal in good faith

A port terminal service provider and an exporter must at all times deal with each other:

(a) lawfully; and

(b) in good faith within the meaning of the unwritten law as in force from time to time.

Note 1: If there are 2 or more port terminal service providers for a port terminal facility, each of the providers for that facility must comply with this section.

Note 2: The disclosure of commercially sensitive information by an entity could found an action by another entity for breach of a duty of confidence.

###### 10 Port terminal service provider to make available shipping stem reports

(1) A port terminal service provider must on each business day make available to the public on its website a report that lists each ship scheduled to be loaded with grain through the use of a port terminal service provided at a port terminal facility that the provider owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly).

(2) The report must include the following information for each ship:

(a) a unique slot reference number for the ship;

(b) if the port terminal service provider knows the name of the ship—the ship’s name;

(c) the estimated time when the ship is to arrive at the port terminal facility through which the port terminal service is to be provided;

(d) the estimated time when grain is to start being loaded onto the ship;

(e) the estimated time when the ship is to leave the port terminal facility through which the port terminal service is being provided;

(f) the name of the exporter of the grain;

(g) the quantity of grain to be loaded onto the ship through the use of the port terminal service;

(h) the type of grain to be loaded onto the ship through the use of the port terminal service;

(i) if the grain has started to be loaded onto the ship, but the loading has not been completed—that fact;

(j) if the loading of grain has been completed—the time when the loading was completed.

Multiple port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility

(3) If:

(a) there are 2 or more port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility that is used, or is to be used, to provide a particular port terminal service; and

(b) one of the providers for that facility complies with subsection (1) in relation to a business day, that service and that facility;

then none of the other providers for that facility is required to comply with subsection (1) in relation to that business day, that service and that facility.

###### 11 Port terminal service providers to make available policies and procedures for managing demand for port terminal services

(1) A port terminal service provider must make available to the public on its website a statement setting out the provider’s policies and procedures for managing demand for the provider’s port terminal services provided at each port terminal facility that the provider owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly).

(2) The statement must include the port terminal service provider’s policies and procedures relating to the nomination and acceptance of ships to be loaded using those port terminal services.

Multiple port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility

(3) If:

(a) there are 2 or more port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility; and

(b) one of the providers for that facility complies with subsection (1) in relation to port terminal services provided at that facility;

then none of the other providers for that facility is required to comply with subsection (1) in relation to those services and that facility.

###### 12 Port terminal service providers to make available standard terms and reference prices

(1) A port terminal service provider must make available to the public on its website its current standard terms and reference prices for each port terminal facility that the provider owns (whether alone or jointly) or operates (whether alone or jointly).

(2) A port terminal service provider’s standard terms and reference prices for a port terminal facility may include different standard terms and reference prices for different seasons or periods of time.

Multiple port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility

(3) If:

(a) there are 2 or more port terminal service providers for a particular port terminal facility; and

(b) one of the providers for that facility complies with subsection (1) in relation to that facility;

then none of the other providers for that facility is required to comply with subsection (1) in relation to that facility.

### Part 3—Saving provisions

###### 13 Saving provisions—disputes

(1) Clauses 14, 16 and 18 to 21 of Schedule 1 to the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Port Terminal Access (Bulk Wheat)) Regulation 2014*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this section, continue to apply on and after that commencement in relation to the following:

(a) a request that was made under subclause 14(1) of that Schedule before that commencement;

(b) a dispute referred or submitted to mediation under an access agreement (within the meaning of that Schedule as so in force) before that commencement.

(2) Clauses 15 and 16 of Schedule 1 to the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Port Terminal Access (Bulk Wheat)) Regulation 2014*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this section, continue to apply on and after that commencement in relation to a notice that was given under subclause 15(1) of that Schedule before that commencement.

###### 14 Saving provisions—record keeping

(1) Clause 31 of Schedule 1 to the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Port Terminal Access (Bulk Wheat)) Regulation 2014*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this section, continues to apply on and after that commencement in relation to the following:

(a) an access agreement (within the meaning of that Schedule as so in force) entered into before that commencement;

(b) a variation made to such an access agreement before that commencement.

(2) Clause 32 of Schedule 1 to the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Port Terminal Access (Bulk Wheat)) Regulation 2014*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this section, continues to apply on and after that commencement in relation to a dispute that arose before that commencement.

(3) Clause 33 of Schedule 1 to the *Competition and Consumer (Industry Code—Port Terminal Access (Bulk Wheat)) Regulation 2014*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this section, continues to apply on and after that commencement in relation to each shipping window (within the meaning of that Schedule as so in force) that began before that commencement.

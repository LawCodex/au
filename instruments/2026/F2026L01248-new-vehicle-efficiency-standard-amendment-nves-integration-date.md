## Schedule 1 - Amendments

**New Vehicle Efficiency Standard Determination 2024**

**Subsection 4(1) (Interpretation)**

*Repeal the definition, substitute:*

***NVES Integration Date*** means:

if the vehicle type is required to comply with either the *Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 81/03 – Energy Consumption Labelling for Light Vehicles) 2025* or the *Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 114/00 – Carbon Dioxide Emissions Measurement) 2026* — the end of the day on 30 June 2027; or

in all other cases —the end of the day on 31 December of the relevant year after which compliance with an ADR on Carbon Dioxide Emissions is required for that vehicle type.

**Subsection 4(3) (Interpretation)**

*Repeal the subsection, substitute:*

Without limiting subsection (2), an ADR on Carbon Dioxide Emissions includes any of the following:

*Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 81/02 – Fuel Consumption Labelling for Light Vehicles) 2008*;

*Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 81/03 – Energy Consumption Labelling for Light Vehicles) 2025*;

*Vehicle Standard (Australian Design Rule 114/00 – Carbon Dioxide Emissions Measurement) 2026*.

**Subsection 6(1)(a) (Exempt Vehicles)**

*Repeal the paragraph, substitute*:

Any vehicle with a gross vehicle mass exceeding 3,500kg, until the first of the following date, whichever is earlier: the NVES Integration Date for that vehicle; or the date of repeal of this instrument.

###### 3 Authority

These Rules are made under the *Judiciary Act 1903*.

###### 4 Sittings of the High Court in 2027

(1) Sittings of a Full Court of the High Court are to be held at Canberra and other places as required during the following periods:

Monday 1 February 2027 to Friday 12 February 2027

Monday 1 March 2027 to Friday 12 March 2027

Monday 5 April 2027 to Friday 16 April 2027

Monday 3 May 2027 to Friday 14 May 2027

Monday 7 June 2027 to Friday 18 June 2027

Monday 2 August 2027 to Friday 13 August 2027

Monday 6 September 2027 to Friday 17 September 2027

Tuesday 5 October 2027 to Friday 15 October 2027

Monday 1 November 2027 to Friday 12 November 2027

Monday 29 November 2027 to Friday 10 December 2027

(2) The winter recess begins on Saturday 19 June 2027.

(3) The summer recess begins on Saturday 11 December 2027.

###### 5 Repeal of these Rules

These Rules are repealed at the start of 1 January 2028.

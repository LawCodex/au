# Aviation Transport Security (Prohibited Cargo—Yemen) Instrument 2026

LIN 26/030

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 17 March 2026

Tony Burke

Minister for Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Aviation Transport Security (Prohibited Cargo—Yemen) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 April 2026.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 65B(1) of the *Aviation Transport Security Act 2004*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Aviation Transport Security Act 2004*, including the following:

(a) Australian territory;

(b) aviation industry participant;

(c) cargo.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aviation Transport Security Act 2004*.

###### 5 Application

This instrument applies to aviation industry participants.

###### 6 Prohibited cargo

A person to whom this instrument applies must not bring, or cause to be brought, into Australian territory cargo that has originated from, or that has transited through, the Republic of Yemen.

###### 7 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

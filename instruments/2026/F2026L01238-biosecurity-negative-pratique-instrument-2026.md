###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) aircraft;

(b) Australian territory;

(c) biosecurity officer;

(d) incoming aircraft or vessel;

(e) landing place;

(f) listed human disease;

(g) operator;

(h) port;

(i) vessel.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

***cruise vessel*** means a passenger vessel that is usually used to provide a service of sea transportation, being a service that:

(a) is provided in return for a fee payable by persons using the service; and

(b) is available to the general public.

***disinsection measures***, for an incoming aircraft, means the disinsection measures described in the document titled *Schedule of Aircraft Disinsection Procedures for Flights into Australia and New Zealand*, as existing on 1 October 2026.

Note: The *Schedule of Aircraft Disinsection Procedures for Flights into Australia and New Zealand* could in 2026 be viewed on the Agriculture Department’s website (https://www.agriculture.gov.au).

***exempt aircraft***, in relation to an incoming aircraft, means an aircraft in a category of aircraft listed as exempt from disinsection measures and published on the Health Department website, as existing on 1 October 2026.

***non‑commercial vessel*** has the same meaning as in the *Biosecurity (Human Health) Regulations 2026*.

***pre‑arrival report***, in relation to an aircraft or vessel, means a report given by the operator of the aircraft or vessel under section 193 of the Act.

Note: The information that must be included in a pre‑arrival report, and other requirements in relation to the report, are prescribed by Part 1 of Chapter 3 of the *Biosecurity Regulations 2026*.

###### 6 Classes of incoming aircraft or vessels and requirements that must be complied with for pratique to be granted

The following paragraphs have effect:

(a) column 1 of an item of the following table specifies the classes of incoming aircraft or vessels in relation to which pratique is to be granted by a biosecurity officer;

(b) column 2 of that item specifies the requirements with which an incoming aircraft or vessel in that class must comply for pratique to be granted by a biosecurity officer.

Note: Certain incoming aircraft and vessels are exempt from the pratique requirements in sections 48 and 49 of the Act (see section 7 of the *Biosecurity (Human Health) Regulations 2026*).

<td>The operator of the vessel must:<br>(a) satisfy a biosecurity officer that the level of human health risk associated with the vessel is acceptable; and<br>(b) if requested, orally or in writing, by a biosecurity officer for the purpose of assessing or managing the level of human health risk associated with the vessel:<br>(i) allow a thing mentioned in the request to be unloaded from or loaded onto the vessel; or<br>(ii) allow a biosecurity officer, or another person mentioned in the request, to disembark from or embark onto the vessel</td>

Note 1: A failure to give a pre‑arrival report that includes the information required by Part 1 of Chapter 3 of the *Biosecurity Regulations 2026* may contravene subsection 193(4) of the Act.

Note 2: A failure to give additional or corrected information as required by subsection 194(1) of the Act may contravene subsection 194(2) of the Act.

Note 3: An effect of items 2 and 3 of the table is that the following activities are authorised for the purposes of subsection 48(3) of the Act:

(a) unloading or loading of a thing in accordance with a request under paragraph (b) of column 2 of those items;

(b) the disembarkation or embarkation of a person in accordance with a request under paragraph (b) of column 2 of those items.

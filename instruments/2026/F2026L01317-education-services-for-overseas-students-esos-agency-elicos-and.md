###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

ELICOS;

National VET Regulator;

provider;

registered higher education provider;

(e) registered provider;

(f) registered VET provider.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***entry arrangement*** means an arrangement between:

(a) the provider of an ELICOS; and

(b) a registered higher education provider,

under which overseas students undertake an ELICOS delivered by the provider described in paragraph (a) for entry to a higher education course, or Foundation Program, delivered by the provider described in paragraph (b).

###### 6 ESOS agency – ELICOS

For the purposes of item 4 in the table in subsection 6C(1) and subsection 6C(2) of the Act, the ESOS agency for a provider or registered provider, to the extent that the provider or registered provider is a person or entity that provides an ELICOS, is as follows:

(a) if the provider or registered provider is a registered higher education provider – TEQSA;

(b) if the provider or registered provider is a registered VET provider – the National VET Regulator;

(c) if the provider or registered provider is an approved school provider – the Secretary;

(d) if the provider or registered provider:

(i) is not a person or entity mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c); and

(ii) provides an ELICOS under an entry arrangement;

TEQSA;

(e) in any other case – the National VET Regulator.

###### 7 ESOS agency – Foundation Programs

For the purposes of item 4 in the table in subsection 6C(1) and subsection 6C(2) of the Act, the ESOS agency for a provider or registered provider, to the extent that the provider or registered provider is a person or entity that provides a Foundation Program, is TEQSA.

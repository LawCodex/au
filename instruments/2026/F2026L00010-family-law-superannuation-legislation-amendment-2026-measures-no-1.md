## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Family Law (Superannuation) (Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2025

**1 Schedule 9**

Repeal the Schedule, substitute:

## Schedule 9 Public sector superannuation plans — Northern Territory

(sections 5 and 6)

### Part 1 Northern Territory Government and Public Authorities’ Superannuation Scheme

###### 1 Definitions

In this Part:

***Northern Territory Government and Public Authorities’ Superannuation Scheme*** means the scheme established by Part 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1986* (NT).

###### 2 Method for interests in the Northern Territory Government and Public Authorities’ Superannuation Scheme

For an interest that is in the growth phase in the Northern Territory Government and Public Authorities’ Superannuation Scheme, the approved method for section 5 of this instrument is that the gross value of the superannuation interest on a date is equal to the value of the benefits (before any taxes or other charges have been deducted) that would have been payable in respect of the interest if the member spouse had voluntarily ceased to be a member of the Scheme on that date.

### Part 2 Northern Territory Supplementary Superannuation Scheme

###### 1 Definitions

In this Part:

***Northern Territory Supplementary Superannuation Scheme*** means the scheme established by an instrument made by the Treasurer of the Northern Territory on 4 January 1989.

###### 2 Method for interests in the Northern Territory Supplementary Superannuation Scheme

For an interest that is in the growth phase in the Northern Territory Supplementary Superannuation Scheme, the approved method for section 5 of this instrument is that the gross value of the superannuation interest on a date is equal to the value of the benefits (before any taxes or other charges have been deducted) that would have been payable in respect of the interest if the member spouse had voluntarily ceased to be a member of the Scheme on that date.

**2 Part 30 of Schedule 10**

Repeal the Part, substitute:

### Part 30 Ford superannuation plans

###### 1 Definitions

In this Part:

***Ford Employees Superannuation Plan*** means the plan of that name established in the Mercer Corporate Superannuation Division of the Mercer Super Trust in accordance with an agreement dated 23 December 2008 between Mercer Investment Nominees Limited (now Mercer Superannuation (Australia) Limited) and Ford Motor Company of Australia Limited.

***Ford Management Superannuation Plan*** means the plan of that name established in the Mercer Corporate Superannuation Division of the Mercer Super Trust in accordance with an agreement dated 23 December 2008 between Mercer Investment Nominees Limited (now Mercer Superannuation (Australia) Limited) and Ford Motor Company of Australia Limited.

***Mercer Super Trust*** means the trust of that name established by Deed dated 28 June 1995.

###### 2 Methods and factors for interests of members in the Ford superannuation plans

For an interest that is in the growth phase in the Ford Employees Superannuation Plan or the Ford Management Superannuation Plan mentioned in an item in the following table, the method or factor mentioned in the item is approved for section 5 of this instrument.

| Item | Interest in the growth phase | Method or factor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An interest that a person has as a member of the Ford Employees Superannuation Plan. | The method in subclause 35(2) of Schedule 3 to the Regulations, but as if:<br>(a) the reference in that subclause to 25% were a reference to 75%; and<br>(b) the reference in that subclause to 75% were a reference to 25%. |
| 2 | An interest that a person has as a member of the Ford Management Superannuation Plan. | The method in subclause 35(2) of Schedule 3 to the Regulations, but as if:<br>(a) the reference in that subclause to 25% were a reference to 75%; and<br>(b) the reference in that subclause to 75% were a reference to 25%. |

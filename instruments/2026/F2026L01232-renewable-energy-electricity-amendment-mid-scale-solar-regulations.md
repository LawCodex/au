## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Renewable Energy (Electricity) Regulations 2001

**1 Subregulation 3(1)**

Insert:

***mid‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device*** means a solar (photovoltaic) device, where:

(a) the device is installed on or after 1 October 2026; and

(b) at the time of installation of the device, the total kW rating of the following is more than 100 kW but no more than 1,000 kW:

(i) the device;

(ii) any related solar (photovoltaic) devices;

(iii) any related power stations.

Example 1: A solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 1,000 kW is installed on 13 January 2027. No related solar (photovoltaic) devices are already installed, and there are no related power stations. So the 1,000 kW device is a mid‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device.

Example 2: A solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 60 kW is installed on 1 November 2026. A related solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 30 kW was installed in 2024. A related power station with a kW rating of 700 kW was accredited in 2025. Therefore, the total kW rating for the purposes of paragraph (b) of this definition is 790 kW. So the 60 kW device is a mid‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device.

Example 3: A solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 400 kW is installed on 10 October 2026. A related power station with a kW rating of 700 kW was accredited in 2022. Therefore, the total kW rating for the purposes of paragraph (b) of this definition is 1,100 kW. As 1,100 kW is more than 1,000 kW, the 400 kW device is not a mid‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device.

***related power station***: see subregulation (4).

***related solar (photovoltaic) device***: see subregulation (3).

***small‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device*** means a solar (photovoltaic) device, where:

(a) the device has a kW rating of no more than 100 kW; and

(b) if the device was installed before 1 October 2026—the device generates no more than 250 MWh of electricity each year; and

(c) in any case—at the time of installation of the device, the total kW rating of the following is no more than 100 kW:

(i) the device;

(ii) any related solar (photovoltaic) devices.

Example: A solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 40 kW is installed on 12 December 2026. A related solar (photovoltaic) device with a kW rating of 50 kW was installed in 2025. Therefore, the total kW rating for the purposes of paragraph (c) of this definition is 90 kW. So the 40 kW device is a small‑scale solar (photovoltaic) device.

***solar (photovoltaic) device*** means a device whose energy source is solar (photovoltaic).

**3 Subregulations 3(2A) and (3)**

Repeal the subregulations, substitute:

(3) Solar (photovoltaic) devices are ***related solar (photovoltaic) devices*** if:

(a) the devices are connected to a grid at one or more connection points and not installed behind a commercial meter subsidiary to any of the connection points; or

(b) both:

(i) the devices are connected to a grid at one or more connection points and installed behind a single commercial meter subsidiary to one or more of the connection points; and

(ii) there is no connection subsidiary to the commercial meter that allows electricity to flow between the commercial meter and any other commercial meter or to another connection point; or

(c) both:

(i) the devices are connected to a grid at one or more connection points and installed behind more than one commercial meter subsidiary to one or more of the connection points; and

(ii) a connection subsidiary to the commercial meters allows electricity to flow between the commercial meters but not between any other commercial meter or to any other connection point; or

(d) at least 3 of the following apply:

(i) the devices are located at a single site or multiple adjoining sites in the same area;

(ii) the devices are installed for the primary purpose of generating electricity for export to a grid;

(iii) electrical infrastructure (other than metering equipment) is needed to connect the devices to a grid;

(iv) more than 50% of the total kW rating of all devices is from ground mounted systems.

(4) An accredited power station is a ***related power station*** of a solar (photovoltaic) device if:

(a) the application for accreditation as an accredited power station listed solar as an eligible energy source from which power is intended to be generated; and

(b) the power station and device are located at a single site or multiple adjoining sites in the same area.

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Rate of Interest—Permanent Impairment Compensation) Instrument 2026

I, Matthew James Keogh, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 24 March 2026

Matthew James Keogh

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Rate of Interest—Permanent Impairment Compensation) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the same time as the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rate of Interest Payable – s26(3)) Notice 2026*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 26(3) of the Act.

###### 4 Repeal of deemed legislative instrument

The *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Rate of Interest Payable – s26(3)) Notice 2016* is repealed to the extent that it is taken to have been made under the Act because of item 63 of Schedule 1 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Legislation Amendment (Defence Force) Act 2017*.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

###### 6 Rate of interest

(1) For subsection 26(3) of the Act, the rate of interest is the weighted average yield of 90 day bank-accepted bills, as published by the Reserve Bank of Australia, settled immediately prior to the last day of the thirty day settlement period.

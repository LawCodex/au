###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) ABSTUDY scheme;

(b) dental practitioner;

(c) dental provider;

(d) FTB(A) person;

(e) Human Services Department;

(f) item;

(g) medicare number;

(h) youth allowance.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Dental Benefits Act 2008*.

***carer payment*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***dental cap***: see Schedule 2.

***dental hygienist*** means a person who has general registration in the dental hygienists division of the dental profession under a law of a State or Territory.

***dental prosthetist*** means a person who has general registration in the dental prosthetists division of the dental profession under a law of a State or Territory.

***dental therapist*** means a person who has general registration in the dental therapists division of the dental profession under a law of a State or Territory.

***Department of Veterans’ Affairs*** means the Department of State that is administered by the Minister administering the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***disability support pension*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***double orphan pension*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***member of a couple*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***MRCAETS*** (short for Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act Education and Training Scheme) means the scheme determined under section 258 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004* and approved by the Minister administering that Act, as in force from time to time.

***oral health therapist*** means a person who has general registration in the oral health therapists division of the dental profession under a law of a State or Territory.

***parenting payment*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***partner***, in relation to a person who is a member of a couple, means the other member of the couple.

***provider number*** has the same meaning as in the *Health Insurance Regulations 2018*.

***public sector dental provider*** means:

(a) a dental provider employed by, contracted to, or providing dental services under an arrangement with:

(i) the Commonwealth; or

(ii) a State; or

(iii) a Territory; or

(iv) a local governing body; or

(v) an authority established by a law of the Commonwealth, a law of a State or a law of a Territory; or

(b) a dental provider on whose behalf another dental provider provides dental services under an arrangement with a person or body covered by subparagraph (a)(i), (ii), (iii), (iv) or (v).

***receive****:*

(a) in relation to a payment under the ABSTUDY scheme—has the same meaning as in subsection 24(4) of the Act; and

(b) in relation to the following payments—has the same meaning as in section 23 of the *Social Security Act 1991*:

(a) carer payment;

(b) disability support pension;

(c) double orphan pension;

(d) parenting payment;

(e) special benefit;

(f) youth allowance; and

(c) in relation to VCES and MRCAETS—has its ordinary meaning.

***registered dentist*** means a dental practitioner who has general registration, or specialist registration, in the dentists division of the dental profession under a law of a State or Territory.

***special benefit*** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***VCES*** (short for Veterans’ Children Education Scheme) means the scheme determined under subsection 117(1) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* and approved by the Minister administering that Act, as in force from time to time.

###### 6 Meaning of eligible dental patient

For the purposes of paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act, the following classes of eligible persons are eligible dental patients in relation to a dental service:

(a) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged under 18 years and receive any of the following payments:

(i) carer payment;

(ii) disability support pension;

(iii) parenting payment;

(iv) special benefit;

(v) youth allowance;

(b) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged under 18 years and either:

(i) who receive a payment under the ABSTUDY scheme; or

(ii) in respect of whom another person receives a payment under the ABSTUDY scheme;

(c) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged under 18 years and are an FTB(A) person;

(d) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged 16 or 17 years and either:

(i) who receive a payment under VCES or MRCAETS; or

(ii) in respect of whom another person receives a payment under VCES or MRCAETS;

(e) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged under 18 years and whose parent, guardian or carer receive either of the following payments in respect of the person:

(i) double orphan pension;

(ii) parenting payment;

(f) eligible persons who, at any time in the calendar year in which the dental service is rendered, are aged under 18 years and whose partner receives parenting payment.

###### 7 Meaning of dental provider

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 6(1)(b) of the Act, the following classes of persons are dental providers in relation to a dental service:

(a) dental hygienists;

(b) dental therapists;

(c) oral health therapists.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 6(2)(a) of the Act, the class of dental practitioners that are not dental providers in relation to a dental service are dental practitioners who have not been allocated a provider number in respect of the place of practice at which the service is rendered.

###### 8 Meaning of rendered on behalf of a dental provider

Dental services rendered on behalf of registered dentists

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 7(a) of the Act, a dental service may be rendered on behalf of a registered dentist who is not a public sector dental provider by a person who is included in one or more of the following classes of persons:

(a) dental hygienists;

(b) dental therapists;

(c) dental prosthetists;

(d) oral health therapists.

Dental services rendered on behalf of public sector dental providers

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 7(a) of the Act, a dental service may be rendered on behalf of a public sector dental provider who is a registered dentist by another public sector dental provider.

### Part 2—When dental benefit is not payable

#### Division 1—Dental benefit not payable unless certain conditions satisfied

###### 9 Particulars that must be recorded on accounts etc.

(1) For the purposes of subsection 17(2) of the Act, the particulars that must be recorded on an account or receipt for fees, or on a form of an assignment, in respect of a dental service, are the following:

(a) the name of the eligible dental patient in respect of whom the dental service was rendered;

(b) the date on which the dental service was rendered;

(c) the item that corresponds to the dental service;

(d) the name of the dental provider by whom, or on whose behalf, the dental service is rendered;

(e) the dental provider’s provider number in respect of the place of practice at which the dental service was rendered;

(f) for:

(i) an account or receipt for fees—the amount charged by the dental provider for the dental service, and the amount paid or outstanding (if any); or

(ii) a form of an assignment—the amount of dental benefit assigned to the dental provider.

(2) The particulars in subsection (1) are specified in relation to dental services generally.

###### 11 Condition that must be satisfied—consent to be provided and recorded

(1) For the purposes of subsection 18(1) of the Act, dental benefit is not payable in respect of a dental service unless:

(a) the person incurring the dental expenses in respect of the service:

(i) makes an assignment of the dental benefit in accordance with section 12 of the Act; and

(ii) has signed an approved form known as the Child Dental Benefits Schedule Bulk Billing Patient Consent Form in the calendar year in which the service is rendered; or

(b) the person incurring the dental expenses in respect of the service signs an approved form known as the Child Dental Benefits Schedule Non‑Bulk Billing Patient Consent Form on the day the service is rendered; or

(c) the dental provider by whom, or on whose behalf, the service is rendered:

(i) obtains and records consent to render the service from the eligible dental patient or the patient’s parent or guardian before the service is rendered; and

(ii) informs the person incurring the dental expenses in respect of the service of the amount of dental benefit likely payable for the service and any likely out‑of‑pocket costs before rendering the service; and

(iii) obtains and records the consent of the person mentioned in paragraph (ii) to render the service; and

(iv) obtains a signature from the person or persons providing the consents specified in subparagraph (i) and (iii) on the day the service is rendered.

Note: The approved forms known as the Child Dental Benefits Schedule Bulk Billing Patient Consent Form and the Child Dental Benefits Schedule Non‑Bulk Billing Patient Consent Form could, on 1 April 2025, be viewed on the Department’s website (www.health.gov.au).

Exception—dental services provided in an emergency

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if:

(a) the dental provider, or person on behalf of the dental provider, renders the dental service on an emergency basis outside the provider’s usual hours of practice or in a different location to the provider’s usual location of practice; and

(b) either:

(i) the person incurring the dental expenses in respect of the service did not sign a form mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) on the day the service is rendered; or

(ii) the dental provider is unable to record the consents or obtain the signature or signatures mentioned in paragraph (1)(c) on the day the service is rendered; and

(c) the person signs one of the forms, or the dental provider records the consents or obtains the signature or signatures (as the case requires), as soon as is reasonably practicable after the day the service is rendered.

Exception—other exceptional circumstances

(3) Subsection (1) does not apply if:

(a) the dental provider is unaware that the eligible dental patient is an eligible dental patient at the time the dental service is rendered; or

(b) the dental provider obtains and records the consents mentioned in subparagraph (1)(c)(i) and (iii) but a person providing a consent:

(i) refuses to provide the provider with the person’s signature; or

(ii) is incapacitated or dies before the person is able to provide the provider with their signature; or

(c) for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c), a person purports to be a person incurring dental expenses or to be a parent or guardian of an eligible dental patient, but the person is not such a person and the provider is unaware of this fact; or

(d) a requirement set out in subparagraph (1)(c)(i), (iii) or (iv) is not met due to an administrative error by the dental provider or a person working with the provider, where the error occurs outside the provider or person’s (as the case requires) standard operating procedures.

###### 12 Condition that must be satisfied—dental services rendered by States and Territories

For the purposes of subsection 18(1) of the Act, dental benefit is not payable in respect of a dental service rendered to an eligible dental patient by, on behalf of or under an arrangement with a State or Territory unless:

(a) the service is rendered before 1 January 2027; or

(b) the Minister directs that this section does not apply to the arrangement.

#### Division 2—Dental benefit not payable in certain circumstances

###### 13 Dental benefit not payable if no medicare number

For the purposes of subsection 21(1) of the Act, dental benefit is not payable in respect of a dental service rendered to an eligible dental patient if, at the time the service is rendered, the patient has not been assigned a medicare number.

###### 14 Dental benefit not payable if certain personal information not provided

For the purposes of subsection 21(1) of the Act, dental benefit is not payable in respect of a dental service rendered to an eligible dental patient if:

(a) the patient is an eligible dental patient because they are covered by paragraph 6(d) of this instrument; and

(b) the patient, or the person who receives a payment under VCES or MRCAETS in respect of the patient (as the case requires), has not consented to the Department of Veterans’ Affairs providing personal information (within the meaning of the *Privacy Act 1988*) about the patient to the Human Services Department.

###### 15 Dental benefit not payable if cap reached

(1) For the purposes of subsection 21(1) of the Act, dental benefit, or an amount of dental benefit, is not payable in respect of a dental service if the payment of the benefit or amount would result in the total amount of dental benefit paid:

(a) in respect of the eligible dental patient to whom the service is rendered; and

(b) in a 2 year period specified in subsection (2);

exceeding the dental cap specified in Schedule 2 to this instrument for the 2 year period.

(2) The 2 year periods specified in this subsection are the following periods:

(a) a 2 year period beginning on 1 January of the year in which dental benefit is first payable in respect of the patient;

(b) any 2 year period beginning on 1 January of the year in which dental benefit is first payable in respect of the patient after the expiry of a previous 2 year period (including a 2 year period under this paragraph).

###### 26 Dental benefit not payable for a certain general service

For the purposes of subsection 21(1) of the Act, dental benefit is not payable in respect of a dental service specified in item 88911 and rendered to an eligible dental patient by, or on behalf of, a dental provider on a day if any other dental service specified in an item in the Dental Benefits Schedule is rendered to the patient by, or on behalf of, the same provider on the same day and dental benefit is payable in respect of that other dental service.

### Part 4—Application, saving and transitional provisions

#### Division 1—Dental Benefits Rules 2026

###### 28 Definitions

In this Part:

***old rules*** means the *Dental Benefits Rules 2014*.

###### 29 Application—dental service provided on or after commencement

This instrument applies in relation to dental services rendered to an eligible dental patient on or after the commencement of this section.

###### 30 Transitional—dental benefit 2 year cap

(1) If a relevant 2 year period was in effect in relation to an eligible dental patient under section 14 of the old rules immediately before the commencement of this section, the relevant 2 year period continues in effect:

(a) in relation to the patient; and

(b) after commencement;

as if it were a 2 year period specified in subsection 15(2) of this instrument.

(2) The period remains in force for the balance of the relevant 2 year period.

###### 31 Something done under the old rules

(1) If:

(a) something was done for a particular purpose under the old rules as in force immediately before the commencement time; and

(b) the thing could be done for that purpose under this instrument;

the thing has effect for the purposes of this instrument as if it had been done for that purpose under this instrument.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), a reference in that subsection to something being done includes a reference to a notice, application or other instrument being given or made.

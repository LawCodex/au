### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Special Assistance—Motorcycle Purchase) Instrument 2026*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.

###### 3 Authority

(1) This instrument is made under section 424 of the Act.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), this instrument is made in reliance upon section 24A of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004* and section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in subsection 5(1) of the Act, including the following:

(a) Commission.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

***ANTS GST Act*** means the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*.

***ART Act*** means the *Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024*.

***applicant*** means the person who makes an application, or on whose behalf an application is made, for a motorcycle benefit.

***application***—see section 8.

***car*** has the meaning it has in section 995-1 of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997*.

***defence‑caused disease*** has the meaning it has in section 5Q of the VEA.

***defence-caused injury*** has the meaning it has in section 5Q of the VEA.

***GST*** has the meaning it has in section 195-1 of the ANTS GST Act.

***GST-free*** has the meaning it has in section 195-1 of the ANTS GST Act.

***motorcycle*** means a motor vehicle with not more than 4 wheels that is steered by means of handlebars.

***motorcycle benefit*** means a benefit under this instrument.

***part***, for a motorcycle:

(a) means a standard replacement part of equipment that is made specifically for the motorcycle; but

(b) does not include liquids or gases that are used in or on the motorcycle.

**Examples—liquids or gases:**

brake fluids

greases

hydraulic oils

oils

paints

petrol

petrol additives

radiator additives

refrigerant gases

***veteran*** has the meaning it has in section 5Q of the VEA.

***VEA*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlement Act 1986.*

***war‑caused disease*** has the meaning it has in section 5Q of the VEA.

***war-caused injury*** has the meaning it has in section 5Q of the VEA.

### Part 2—Eligibility for motorcycle benefit

###### 5 Who is eligible for a motorcycle benefit

For this instrument, a person is eligible for a motorcycle benefit if:

(a) the person is a veteran:

(i) to whom section 24 of the VEA applies and who receives a pension under Part II of the VEA; or

(ii) who has lost a leg or both arms because of a war‑caused injury, war‑caused disease, defence‑caused injury or defence‑caused disease; or

(iii) who has had a leg or both arms rendered permanently and completely useless because of a war‑caused injury, war‑caused disease, defence‑caused injury or defence‑caused disease; and

(b) sections 6 or 7 apply to the person.

Note Under Part VI of the VEA, ***veteran*** includes a member of the Forces and a member of a Peacekeeping Force.

**6 Eligibility—purchase of motorcycle**

(1) This section applies to a person who:

(a) on or after 1 July 2000, purchases a motorcycle for which GST is payable; and

(b) intends to use the motorcycle for the person’s personal transportation (whether or not as the driver); and

(c) unless the exception in subsection (2) applies, in the 2 years before the day the purchase mentioned in paragraph (a) is made:

(i) had not received a motorcycle benefit for another motorcycle; and

(ii) had not purchased a GST-free car or vehicle.

(2) Subparagraphs (1)(c)(i) and (ii) do not apply in relation to a person if:

(a) the car, vehicle or other motorcycle has been used to transport the person for distances totalling more than 40 000 kilometres; or

(b) the car, vehicle or other motorcycle:

(i) is no longer reasonably capable of being used for the purpose for which a car, vehicle or motorcycle of that kind is ordinarily used; and

(ii) is not insured for its replacement value; or

(c) the Commission is satisfied that there are special circumstances justifying the purchase.

**Example—special circumstances:**

The theft and non-recovery of the car, vehicle or other motorcycle if it is not insured for its replacement value.

###### 7 Eligibility—purchase of part for motorcycle

This section applies to a person who:

(a) on or after 1 July 2000, purchases a motorcycle part for which GST is payable; and

(b) intends to use the part for a motorcycle for which the person has been granted a motorcycle benefit, to replace a part of the motorcycle that:

(i) is no longer fit for its purpose because of wear and tear; or

(ii) was destroyed, damaged or stolen and was not insured for its replacement value.

### Part 3—Applications for motorcycle benefit

###### 8 Applications—formal requirements

(1) A person may apply to the Commission for a motorcycle benefit.

(2) The application must be:

(a) in writing; and

(b) if the Commission has approved a form for the purposes of this paragraph—in the approved form; and

(c) accompanied by either of the following as required under the ANTS GST Act to be issued for the purchase of the motorcycle or motorcycle part:

(i) a tax invoice that complies with subsection 29-70(1) and, if applicable, section 54-50 of the ANTS GST Act; or

(ii) a recipient-created tax invoice in accordance with subsection 29-70(3) of the ANTS GST Act.

(3) The application must be forwarded to, or delivered at, an office of the Department in Australia.

(4) An application is not valid unless the applicant is in Australia on the day the application is forwarded, or delivered, under subsection (3).

###### 9 Withdrawing application

(1) An applicant may withdraw an application that has not been decided by making a withdrawal orally, or in writing, to the Department.

(2) An application that is withdrawn is taken to have never been made.

(3) The Department must make a written record of a withdrawal that is made orally.

(4) If an application was made on behalf of a former member, the application may be withdrawn by another person only if the former member has authorised the person, in writing, to withdraw the application.

###### 10 Commission must investigate and decide application

(1) The Commission must investigate an application for a motorcycle benefit.

(2) The Commission must either approve or refuse an application.

(3) The Commission must approve the application if the Commission is satisfied that the applicant is eligible for a motorcycle benefit.

(4) If the Commission approves an application, the Commission must pay a motorcycle benefit to the applicant in the amount calculated under Part 4.

###### 11 Notification of decision

If the Commission has made a decision under section 10, the Commission must give the applicant a written notice stating all of the following:

(a) the decision;

(b) the day the decision takes effect;

(c) the reasons for the decision;

(d) the applicant’s right, if dissatisfied with the decision, to ask the Commission to review the decision.

### Part 4—Payment of motorcycle benefit

###### 12 Amount of motorcycle benefit

(1) The motorcycle benefit for a motorcycle or motorcycle part is:

(a) if its GST inclusive purchase price does not exceed the car limit for the financial year in which the motorcycle or part was purchased—an amount equal to one-eleventh of the value; or

(b) if its GST inclusive purchase price exceeds the car limit for the financial year in which the motorcycle or part was purchased—an amount equal to one-eleventh of the car limit.

(2) In this section:

***car limit***—see subsection 40-230(3) of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997*.

***GST inclusive purchase price***, for a motorcycle or part, means the amount of money paid by a former member to purchase the motorcycle or part:

(a) without discounting for an amount of GST; and

(b) excluding the amount of money paid for any modifications made solely for the purpose of adapting the motorcycle or part for driving or for transporting a person.

Note A payment under this instrument is exempt from income tax—see section 52-114 of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997*.

###### 13 Payment into bank account

A motorcycle benefit may be paid to the credit of a bank account, nominated and kept by a person, that is kept by the person alone, or jointly or in common with another person.

### Part 5—Review of decisions

###### 14 Applicant may request review of decision

(1) An applicant who is dissatisfied with a decision of the Commission under section 10, or another person on the applicant’s behalf, may ask the Commission to review the decision.

(2) A request for review must:

(a) be in writing; and

(b) set out the grounds on which the request is made; and

(c) be lodged with the Commission by forwarding to, or delivering at, an office of the Department in Australia within 3 months after the day the applicant received written notice of the decision.

###### 15 Commission to review decisions

(1) The Commission must review the decision:

(a) within 3 months after the day a request under section 14 was lodged; or

(b) within any longer period agreed in writing with the person who made the request.

(2) On review of a decision, the Commission must:

(a) affirm the decision; or

(b) set aside the decision.

(3) If the Commission sets aside a decision, it must make a decision in place of the decision set aside.

(4) If the Commission has delegated its power under this section to a person, that person must not review a decision that was originally made by the person.

###### 16 Commission to give notice of decision reviewed

(1) If the Commission has reviewed a decision under section 15, the Commission must give a written notice of the outcome of the review to:

(a) the person who requested the review; and

(b) any other person whose interests are affected by the decision.

(2) The written notice must:

(a) state the day the reviewed decision takes effect; and

(b) include a statement of reasons for the decision; and

(c) include notice of the right to apply to the Tribunal for further review of the decision.

Note: In giving the written notice, the Commission must have regard to any matters prescribed by the rules made under section 295 of the ART Act (see that Act, section 267).

(3) In this section:

***statement of reasons***, for a decision—see section 4 of the ART Act.

###### 17 Review by Tribunal

(1) A person whose interests are affected by a decision may apply under the ART Act to the Tribunal for review of a decision of the Commission under section 15.

(2) Despite anything else prescribed under subsection 18(1) of the ART Act, the person may apply to the Tribunal for review of the decision within 3 months after the day the person is given written notice of the decision.

(3) To remove any doubt, subsection (2) does not affect subsection 18(2) and section 19 of the ART Act.

## Part 6—Transitional provisions

**18 Meaning of commencement day**

In this Part:

***commencement day*** means the day this instrument commenced.

**19 Application made but not decided before the commencement day**

(1) This section applies to an application for a motorcycle benefit:

(a) made under the *Veterans' Entitlements (Special Assistance—Motorcycle Purchase) Instrument 2025*; and

(b) that had not been decided by the Commission immediately before the commencement day.

(2) This instrument applies in relation to the application as if the application was made under section 8.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 126(2AB) of the *Commonwealth Electoral Act 1918*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Commonwealth Electoral Act 1918*.

***CMYK*** (short for Cyan‑Magenta‑Yellow‑Black) is a colour model used in printing.

###### 6 Requirements for a logo set out in an application

For the purposes of paragraph 126(2AA)(b) of the Act, a logo set out in an application must:

(a) be a vector graphic in electronic format; and

(b) be 100% black in a CMYK colour space; and

(c) be contained within a frame of 10 mm by 10 mm; and

(d) be able to be reproduced correctly within a frame of 7 mm by 7 mm; and

(e) not include any of the following features:

(i) live text;

(ii) transparency or overprinting;

(iii) custom halftone, transfer curve or colour profile settings; and

(f) be a PDF file, of less than 5 megabytes, that complies with International Standard ISO 32000‑1:2008 *Document management—Portable document format—Part 1: PDF 1.7*, published by the International Organization for Standardization, as in force or existing at the time this instrument commences.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

In this instrument:

***Australian Standard*** means a standard published by, or on behalf of, Standards Australia.

Note: Standards Australia is defined in section 2B of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

***program*** means the Free Access to Australian Standards Program.

***the Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

### Part 2—Prescribed program

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the program is prescribed.

(2) The program provides funding to enable the delivery of free, online public access to Australian Standards referenced in Commonwealth, State or Territory legislation.

(3) The purpose of the program is to improve the understanding of, and reduce barriers to compliance with, legislative requirements that reference Australian Standards.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the powers of the Parliament to make laws with respect to the following are specified:

(a) postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution);

(b) enterprises and activities that are peculiarly adapted to the government of a nation and cannot otherwise be carried on for the benefit of the nation (see paragraph 51(xxxix) and section 61 of the Constitution).

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 3(1) of Schedule 2 to the *Universities Accord (Australian Tertiary Education Commission) (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2026*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***ATEC*** means the Australian Tertiary Education Commission established under section 8 of the *Universities Accord (Australian Tertiary Education Commission) Act 2026*.

***Panel*** means the Higher Education Standards Panel as established under section 166 of the TEQSA Act as in force immediately before the commencement of the *Universities Accord (Australian Tertiary Education Commission) (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2026*.

***TEQSA Act*** means the *Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency Act 2011*.

###### 5 Saving of acts in relation to the making of the Higher Education Standards Framework

(1) This section applies to the making of standards making up the Higher Education Standards Framework under section 58 of the TEQSA Act in the period beginning on the day this instrument commences and ending on 31 December 2026.

(2) For the purposes of paragraphs 58(3)(a) and (4)(a) of the TEQSA Act, a draft of a standard developed by the Panel before this instrument commences is taken to be a draft of the standard developed by the ATEC.

(3) For the purposes of subparagraph 58(4)(b)(i) of the TEQSA Act, advice or recommendations given to the Minister by the Panel before this instrument commences is taken to be advice or recommendations given to the Minister by the ATEC.

(4) Nothing in this section prevents the ATEC from further developing the standard or giving advice and recommendations to the Minister in relation to the standard for the purposes of section 58 of the TEQSA Act.

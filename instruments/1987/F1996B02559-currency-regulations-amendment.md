# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1987 No. 1421**

**Currency Regulations2 (Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulation under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 18 June 1987.

N. M. STEPHEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

[image: Signature of Minister of State for Community Services for and on behalf of the Treasurer.]

Minister of State for Community Services for

and on behalf of the Treasurer

**Designs of coins**

Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended by inserting after subparagraph (d) (viiie) the following subparagraph:

“(viiiea) in the case of a coin of the denomination of $10 that refers on its obverse side to the year 1987 as the year of the coin—the inscriptions ‘COAT OF ARMS NEW SOUTH WALES’ and ‘10 DOLLARS’ and a representation of the coat of arms of the State of New South Wales;”.

# AD/AT 600/4 Amdt 4 - Engine Mount

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/AT 600/4 Amdt 3 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The FAA received reports of cracked engine mounts. Such cracking, unless detected and corrected, could result in failure of the engine mount and possible separation of the engine from the aircraft.<br>Amendment 1 was prompted by reports received by the FAA of two Model AT-802A aircraft with cracked engine mounts below the initial compliance time. The FAA determined that the initial inspection is required when the aircraft reaches a total of 1,300 hours time in service.<br>Amendment 2 retained the inspection actions of FAA AD 2007-13-17 and required incorporation of engine mount gussets as a mandatory terminating action for the repetitive inspection requirement.<br>Amendment 3 was prompted by a report of a Model AT-602 aircraft with a crack completely through the gusset that was installed as required in FAA AD 2008-10-12. Consequently, this Directive requires repetitive inspections of engine mounts of all previously affected aircraft with or without gussets installed.<br>Amendment 4 is issued to correct minor typographical errors in Compliance section paragraphs 1.b. and 2.b. There is no change to the technical requirements. |

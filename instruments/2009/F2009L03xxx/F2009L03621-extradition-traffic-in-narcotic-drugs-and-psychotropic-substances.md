# Extradition (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Regulations 2009

Extradition (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Regulations 20091

Select Legislative Instrument 2009 No. 266

I, QUENTIN BRYCE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Extradition Act 1988*.

Dated 8 October 2009

QUENTIN BRYCE

Governor-General

By Her Excellency’s Command

BRENDAN O’CONNOR

Minister for Home Affairs

###### 1 Name of Regulations

These Regulations are the *Extradition (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Regulations 2009*.

###### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day after they are registered.

###### 3 Repeal

The Extradition (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Regulations are repealed.

###### 4 Definitions

In these Regulations:

***Act*** means the *Extradition Act 1988*.

***Convention*** has the same meaning as in the *Crimes (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Act 1990*.

###### 5 Extradition countries

For the definition of ***extradition country*** in section 5 of the Act, a country, or a colony, territory or protectorate of a country, for which the Convention is in force is an extradition country.

*Note 1* For when the Convention enters into force for a State, see Article 29 of the Convention in Schedule 1 to the *Crimes (Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances) Act 1990*.

*Note 2* The countries for which the Convention is currently in force are listed on the United Nations website at http://www.un.org.

###### 6 Application of the Act

The Act applies, subject to the Convention, to an extradition country mentioned in regulation 5.

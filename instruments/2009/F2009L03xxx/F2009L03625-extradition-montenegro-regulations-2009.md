# Extradition (Montenegro) Regulations 2009

Extradition (Montenegro) Regulations 20091

Select Legislative Instrument 2009 No. 261

I, QUENTIN BRYCE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Extradition Act 1988*.

Dated 8 October 2009

QUENTIN BRYCE

Governor-General

By Her Excellency’s Command

BRENDAN O'CONNOR

Minister for Home Affairs

###### 1 Name of Regulations

These Regulations are the *Extradition (Montenegro) Regulations 2009*.

###### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day after they are registered.

###### 3 Definition

In these Regulations:

***Act*** means the *Extradition Act 1988*.

###### 4 Declaration that Montenegro is an extradition country

Montenegro is declared to be an extradition country.

###### 5 Application of the Act in relation to Montenegro

For paragraph 11 (1) (b) of the Act, the Act applies to Montenegro subject to modification of paragraph 17 (2) (a) of the Act by omitting ‘45 days’ and substituting ‘60 days’.

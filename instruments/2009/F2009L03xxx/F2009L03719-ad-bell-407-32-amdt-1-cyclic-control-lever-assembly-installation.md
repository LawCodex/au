# AD/BELL 407/32 Amdt 1 - Cyclic Control Lever Assembly Installation

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/BELL 407/32 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 407 Series Helicopters

| AD/BELL 407/32 Amdt 1 | Cyclic Control Lever Assembly Installation | 12/2009 DM |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | All Model 407 helicopters. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | For helicopters with less than 50 flight hours in service **or** with a cyclic lever assembly installed within the last 50 flight hours:<br>Perform a one time inspection and if required, rectification, of the cyclic lever assembly installation, in accordance with accomplishment instructions provided in Bell Alert Service bulletin 407-09-85 dated 10 March 2009, or later Transport Canada approved revision.<br>Note: Transport Canada AD CF-2009-10R1 refers. |
| Compliance: | Within 10 flight hours after 30 September 2009, but no later than 30 calendar days after the effective date of this Directive; unless already accomplished. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 30 September 2009. |
| Background: | An incorrectly installed bearing Part Number 206-301-051-101, in the co-pilot cyclic control lever assembly has been discovered during manufacturing. It is possible, that an incorrectly installed bearing could be found in any aircraft with a recently installed cyclic control lever assembly.<br>Amendment 1 is issued to reflect the requirements of the latest Transport Canada AD, which, while retaining the requirements of the original AD issue, requires possible extra work on Model 206 helicopters. |

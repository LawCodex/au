# Tariff Concession Order 0906070

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 20.02.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4811.59.90 | PAPER, in sheets, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) adhesive laminated on one side with extruded high density<br>polyethylene film NOT exceeding 6 micron in<br>thickness;<br>(b) weight NOT less than 50 gsm and NOT greater than 60<br>gsm;<br>(c) thickness NOT less than 73 microns and NOT greater<br>than 79 microns<br>Op. 20.02.09 - TC 0906070 | 50 |

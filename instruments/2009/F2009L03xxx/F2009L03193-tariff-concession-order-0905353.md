# Tariff Concession Order 0905353

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 17.02.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7007.19.00 | PANELS, SOLAR GLASS, toughened (tempered) to<br>Australian Standard/New Zealand Standard 2208 (AS/NZS 2208),<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) thickness of 3.2 mm +/- 0.1 mm;<br>(b) solar energy transmittance (Te) NOT less than 91%;<br>(c) iron content (Ferric Oxide Fe2O3) NOT greater than 0.01%<br>Op. 17.02.09 - TC 0905353 | 50 |

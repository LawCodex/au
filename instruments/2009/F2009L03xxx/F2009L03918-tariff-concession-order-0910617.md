# Tariff Concession Order 0910617

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 30.03.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7411.29.00 | TUBING, copper, solid drawn, finned, internally enhanced, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) conforming to American Society of Mechanical Engineers<br>specification SB-75;<br>(b) continuous integrally rolled fins;<br>(c) NOT less than 40 fins per 25 mm;<br>(d) length NOT less than 1 000 mm<br>Op. 30.03.09 - TC 0910617 | 50 |

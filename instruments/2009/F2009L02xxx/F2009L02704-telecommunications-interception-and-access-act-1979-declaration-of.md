# Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979 - Declaration of eligible authority as agency - Crime and Misconduct Commission of Queensland

Declaration of eligible authority as agency — Crime and Misconduct Commission of Queensland

*Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*

I, ROBERT BRUCE McCLELLAND, Attorney‑General:

(a) acting under section 34 of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979* and at the request of the Premier of Queensland; and

(b) being satisfied that:

(i) the law of the State of Queensland makes satisfactory provision in relation to the matters mentioned in subsection 35 (1) of the Act; and

(ii) the State of Queensland has entered into an agreement as required by subsection 35 (2) of the Act;

declare the Crime and Misconduct Commission of Queensland to be an agency for the purposes of the Act.

Dated 7 July 2009

Robert McClelland

Attorney‑General

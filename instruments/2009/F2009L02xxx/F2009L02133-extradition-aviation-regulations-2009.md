# Extradition (Aviation) Regulations 2009

Extradition (Aviation) Regulations 20091

Select Legislative Instrument 2009 No. 92

I, QUENTIN BRYCE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Extradition Act 1988*.

Dated 4 June 2009

QUENTIN BRYCE

Governor-General

By Her Excellency’s Command

BOB DEBUS

Minister for Home Affairs

###### 1 Name of Regulations

These Regulations are the *Extradition (Aviation) Regulations 2009*.

###### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the day after they are registered.

###### 3 Repeal

The Extradition (Aviation) Regulations are repealed.

###### 4 Definitions

In these Regulations:

***Act*** means the *Extradition Act 1988*.

***Hague Convention*** has the same meaning as in the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991*.

***Montreal Convention*** has the same meaning as in the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991*.

***Protocol*** has the same meaning as in the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991.*

###### 5 Extradition countries — Hague Convention

For the definition of ***extradition country*** in section 5 of the Act, a country, or a colony, territory or protectorate of a country, for which the Hague Convention is in force is an extradition country.

*Note 1* For when the Hague Convention enters into force for a State, see Article 13 of the Convention in Schedule 1 to the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991*.

*Note 2* The countries for which the Hague Convention is currently in force are listed on the International Civil Aviation Organization website at http://www.icao.int.

###### 6 Extradition countries — Montreal Convention

For the definition of ***extradition country*** in section 5 of the Act, a country, or a colony, territory or protectorate of a country, for which the Montreal Convention is in force is an extradition country.

*Note 1* For when the Montreal Convention enters into force for a State, see Article 15 of the Convention in Schedule 2 to the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991*.

*Note 2* The countries for which the Montreal Convention is currently in force are listed on the International Civil Aviation Organization website at http://www.icao.int.

###### 7 Extradition countries — Protocol

For the definition of ***extradition country*** in section 5 of the Act, a country, or a colony, territory or protectorate of a country, for which the Protocol is in force is an extradition country.

*Note 1* For when the Protocol enters into force for a State, see Article VII of the Protocol in Schedule 3 to the *Crimes (Aviation) Act 1991*.

*Note 2* The countries for which the Protocol is currently in force are listed on the International Civil Aviation Organization website at http://www.icao.int.

###### 8 Application of the Act — Hague Convention

The Act applies, subject to the Hague Convention, to an extradition country mentioned in regulation 5.

###### 9 Application of the Act — Montreal Convention

The Act applies, subject to the Montreal Convention, to an extradition country mentioned in regulation 6.

###### 10 Application of the Act — Protocol

The Act applies, subject to the Protocol, to an extradition country mentioned in regulation 7.

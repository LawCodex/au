# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2009-2010) Determination 2009

Superannuation (PSS)

Maximum Benefits (2009-2010) Determination 2009

I, JONATHON EDWARD HALE, Manager of the Australian Public Sector Defined Benefit Schemes, Commonwealth Superannuation Administration, Position No. 44, and Delegate of Australian Reward Investment Alliance, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 30 June 2009

J HALE

Delegate of Australian Reward Investment Alliance

[image]

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2009-2010) Determination 2009*.

2 Commencement

This determination shall take effect from and including 1 July 2009.

# Telecommunications (Interception and Access) (Staff Members of Queensland Police Service) Declaration 2009

*Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*

I, ROBERT BRUCE McCLELLAND, Attorney-General, acting under section 6N of the *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979*, declare that unsworn staff members of the State Intelligence Group, State Crime Operations Command, Queensland Police Service, occupy positions corresponding to those of unsworn staff members of Australian Federal Police.

Dated 26 May 2009

Robert McClelland

Attorney-General

# Tariff Concession Order 0903184

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 30.01.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 3816.00.00 | MORTARS, REFRACTORY, TUYERE ZONE OR CUSHION, BLAST FURNACE,<br>resin bonded, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) alumina (AL203) content NOT less than 24%;<br>(b) silicon carbide (SiC) content NOT less than 24%;<br>(c) silicon dioxide (Si02) content NOT greater than 36%<br>Op. 30.01.09 - TC 0903184 | 50 |

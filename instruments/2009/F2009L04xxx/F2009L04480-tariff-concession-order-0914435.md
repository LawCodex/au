# Tariff Concession Order 0914435

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 30.04.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7308.90.00 | MARINE PILINGS, steel, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) thickness NOT less than 20 mm;<br>(b) length NOT less than 28.5 m;<br>(c) diameter NOT less than 900 mm;<br>(d) maximum of one splice per piling;<br>(e) maximum straightness difference of 1 mm per metre over<br>entire length<br>Op. 30.04.09 - TC 0914435 | 50 |

# Currency (Perth Mint) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)

Currency (Perth Mint) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)1

*Currency Act 1965*

I, NICK SHERRY, Assistant Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 17 November 2009

NICK SHERRY

Assistant Treasurer

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Perth Mint) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Determination is taken to have commenced as follows:

(a) on 29 April 2008 — sections 1 to 3 and Schedule 1;

(b) on 19 May 2009 — section 4 and Schedule 2.

3 Amendment of *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 1)*

Schedule 1 amends the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 1)*.

4 Amendment of *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2009 (No. 2)*

Schedule 2 amends the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2009 (No. 2)*.

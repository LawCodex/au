# Tariff Concession Order 0912405

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 15.04.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 8481.80.90 | GATE VALVES, DOUBLE BLOCK AND BLEED, motor operated, having ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) nominal bore diameter NOT less than 250 mm and NOT greater<br>than 450 mm;<br>(b) pressure rating at 1 000 degrees F NOT less than 19 bar<br>and at 100 degrees F NOT greater than 150 bar, in<br>accordance with American Society of Mechanical Engineers<br>class 900 (ASME class 900);<br>(c) complying with American Petroleum Institute Standard<br>6D (API 6D) and American Society of Mechanical Engineers<br>Standard B16.34 (ASME B16.34)<br>Op. 15.04.09 - TC 0912405 | 50 |

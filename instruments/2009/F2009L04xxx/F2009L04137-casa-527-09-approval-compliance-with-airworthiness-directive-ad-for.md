# CASA 527/09 - Approval - compliance with Airworthiness Directive (AD) for Hartzell Propellers

Instrument number CASA 527/09

I, William David, Manager, Continuing Airworthiness, Standards Development and Future Technology Division, a delegate of CASA, make this instrument under paragraph 39.004 (3) (a) of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

**[Signed William David]**

William David\
Manager, Continuing Airworthiness\
Standards Development and Future Technology Division

13 November 2009

Approval — compliance with Airworthiness Directive (*AD*) for Hartzell Propellers

1 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on the day of registration; and

(b) stops having effect if AD 2009-22-03, issued by the FAA, is amended or cancelled.

2 Application

This instrument applies to a registered operator who operates an aircraft with Hartzell Propeller Inc. propellers mentioned in AD 2009-22-03.

3 Approved means of compliance

I approve, as a means of compliance with AD 2009-22-03, other than that set out in that AD, the following means of compliance:

(a) for a propeller hub, mentioned in paragraph (d) of that AD and retired from service, compliance with AD/PHZL/75 instead of FAA AD 2003-01-03; and

(b) for propellers mentioned in paragraph (f) of AD 2009-22-03, compliance with AD/PHZL/74, Amendment 1, instead of FAA AD 2001-23-08.

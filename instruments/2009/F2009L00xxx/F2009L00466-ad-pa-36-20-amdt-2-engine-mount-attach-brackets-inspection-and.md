# AD/PA-36/20 Amdt 2 - Engine Mount Attach Brackets - Inspection and Modification

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/PA-36/20 Amdt 1 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Piper PA-36 (Pawnee Brave) Series Aeroplanes

| AD/PA-36/20 Amdt 2 | Engine Mount Attach Brackets - Inspection<br>and Modification | 3/2009 |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | Model PA-36-285 with serial numbers 36-736001 to 36-7660135 except aircraft incorporating Piper Engine Mount Attachment Modification Kit No. 763877 or equivalent. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | Inspect welds for cracks on the aft side of the gussets on the fuselage where the engine mount is attached, as shown in Piper SB No. 615. Use dye check or fluorescent penetrant inspection procedures or equivalent.<br>If cracks are found, install Piper Engine Mount Attachment Modification Kit No. 763877 or equivalent prior to further flight. During installation of the Kit, inspect the welds for cracks on the forward side of the gussets on the fuselage where the engine mount is attached, as shown in Piper SB No. 615. Use dye check or fluorescent penetrant inspection procedures or equivalent.<br>*Note: USA AD 79-12-09 (amdt 39-3494) refers.* |
| Compliance: | Inspect at each scheduled inspection for issue of a maintenance release.<br>Compliance with Amendment 1 of this AD constitutes compliance with this Amendment. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 12 March 2009. |
| Background: | The original issue of this AD in 1978 specified the same applicability and requirements as Piper SB No. 615.<br>Amendment 1 in 1983 extended the applicability to all PA-36 in response to reports of cracked engine mount brackets outside the serial number applicability of Piper SB No. 615. |

|  | This Amendment excludes aircraft incorporating Piper Kit No. 763877; restricts serial number applicability to the serial number range specified in USA AD 79-12-09; identifies USA AD 79-12-09; and specifies compliance period in terms of issue of a maintenance release. |
|---|---|

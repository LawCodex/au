# Tariff Concession Order 0823068

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 25.07.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8427.10.00 | REACH TRUCKS, retractable mast, rider seated, as defined in<br>Australian Standard AS2359.7-1995 clause 3.1.3.1.2, having ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) load capacity NOT less than 1 600 kg @ 600 mm load centre<br>at 5.75 m lift height;<br>(b) maximum lift height NOT less than 6 000 mm;<br>(c) height with collapsed mast NOT exceeding 4 800 mm;<br>(d) operator in a seated position within an operator's<br>compartment at right angles to the direction of travel;<br>(e) overhead operator protection guard;<br>(f) maximum travel speed NOT less than 10 km/h;<br>(g) reach NOT less than 355 mm;<br>(h) wheelbase greater than 1 420 mm;<br>(i) battery powered;<br>(j) power assisted steering;<br>(k) weight without battery exceeding 2 000 kg;<br>(l) lift speed greater than 0.45 m/sec<br>Op. 25.07.08 - TC 0823068 | 50 |

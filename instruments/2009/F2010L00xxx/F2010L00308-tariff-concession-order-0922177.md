# Tariff Concession Order 0922177

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 29.06.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 7209.16.00 | STEEL, cold rolled AND/OR cold reduced, complying with Japanese<br>Industrial Standard JIS G 3141:1996, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) yield strength NOT less than 115 N/mm2 and NOT greater than<br>225 N/mm2;<br>(b) tensile strength NOT less than 270 N/mm2;<br>(c) elongation NOT less than 40% and NOT greater than 52%;<br>(d) thickness 1.60 mm and width 1 055 mm<br><br>For the purposes of this Order, tolerances allowable for<br>specification (d) are + or - 5%<br>Op. 29.06.09 - TC 0922177 | 50<br>29.08.09 |

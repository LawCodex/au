# Tariff Concession Order 0841590

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 27.11.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8705.90.00 | VEHICLES, SWEEPER, SUCTION, ride in, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) two wheel steering;<br>(b) sweeping width NOT greater than 2 880 mm;<br>(c) dirt hopper capacity NOT greater than 1.85 m3;<br>(d) transport mode NOT greater than 50 km per hour;<br>(e) outer turning radius NOT greater than 3 850 mm;<br>(f) width NOT greater than 1 320 mm;<br>(g) height NOT greater than 1 995 mm<br>Op. 27.11.08 - TC 0841590 | 50 |
|---|---|---|

# Tariff Concession Order 0901776

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 20.01.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 3926.90.90 | ROLLERS, omni-directional, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) NOT less than 6 axle mounted spherical roller bearings;<br>(b) polyacetal or polyurethane or polyoxymethylene<br>rollers;<br>(c) diameter NOT less than 40 mm and NOT greater than 80 mm<br>Op. 20.01.09 - TC 0901776 | 50 |

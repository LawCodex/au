# Tariff Concession Order 0845335

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 24.12.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 7304.59.00 | PIPE AND PIPE FABRICATIONS, alloy steel, with or without clamps<br>AND/OR fittings AND/OR expansion joints, complying with American<br>Society for Testing and Materials specification A335 Grade P91<br>(ASTM A335 grade P91), having ALL of the following:<br>(a) NOT less than 8% chromium;<br>(b) NOT less than 0.7% molybdenum;<br>(c) NOT less than 0.18% vanadium<br>Op. 24.12.08 - TC 0845335 | 50 |

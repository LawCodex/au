# Tariff Concession Order 0843452

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 10.12.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 8481.40.00 | VALVES, GAS, BACKPRESSURE REGULATING, manual adjustment, spring<br>loaded, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) relief pressure NOT less than 2 pounds per square inch<br>gauge and NOT greater than 40 pounds per square inch gauge;<br>(b) gas flow NOT less than 83 Standard m3/h and NOT greater<br>than 105 Standard m3/h;<br>(c) stainless steel body;<br>(d) body size NOT less than 15 mm<br>Op. 10.12.08 - TC 0843452 | 50 |

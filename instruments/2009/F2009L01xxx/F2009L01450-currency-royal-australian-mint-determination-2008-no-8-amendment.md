# Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 8) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)

Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 8) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)1

*Currency Act 1965*

I, NICK SHERRY, Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 3 April 2009

NICK SHERRY

Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 8) Amendment Determination 2009 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

3 Amendment of *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 8)*

Schedule 1 amends the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 8).*

Schedule 1 Amendment

(section 3)

[1] Schedule 1, item 6, reverse design, subparagraph (b) (iii)

substitute

(iii) ‘OF POSTAL SERVICES IN AUSTRALIA’.

# Higher Education Provider Approval (No. 4 of 2009)

***INSTRUMENT OF APPROVAL***

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Higher Education Support Act 2003***

**Higher Education Provider Approval (No 4 of 2009)**

**Approval pursuant to section 16-25 and notice of approval pursuant to paragraph 16-50(1)(b)*****.***

I, FIONA BUFFINTON, a delegate of the Minister for Education for the purposes of section 16-25 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003* (‘the HESA’) have approved the Southbank Institute of Technology as a higher education provider under that section of the HESA, and:

a) note that the application of Southbank Institute of Technology for approval as a higher education provider has thereby been decided as required by paragraph 16-50(1)(a) of the HESA; and

b) pursuant to paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the HESA give notice of my approval to Southbank Institute of Technology.

In accordance with section 16-55 of the HESA and subsection 12(3) of the *Legislative Instruments Act 2003*, this notice of approval takes effect on the day after the last day on which a House of Parliament could have passed a resolution disallowing it.

*Dated this 8th day of May 2009*

*(signed)*

*FIONA BUFFINTON*

*Group Manager, Higher Education Group*

*Department of Education, Employment and Workplace Relations*

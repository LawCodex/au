# Tariff Concession Order 0926784

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Andrew Mumberson, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 27.07.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8427.10.00 | TRUCKS, TURRET, LATERAL AND FRONT STACKING, very narrow aisle,<br>rider seated, as defined in Australian Standard AS 2359.7-1995<br>Clause 3.1.3.1.10, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) load capacity NOT less than 1 500 kg at 600 mm load centre;<br>(b) maximum lift height NOT greater than 11 000 mm;<br>(c) height with collapsed mast NOT exceeding 5 100 mm;<br>(d) operator compartment seat;<br>(e) operator protection overhead guard;<br>(f) maximum travel speed NOT less than 9 km/h;<br>(g) battery power NOT less than 48 V;<br>(h) electronic power steering;<br>(i) wheelbase NOT less than 1 700 mm and NOT greater<br>than 2 100 mm;<br>(j) weight (without battery) NOT less than 3 500 kg<br>Op. 27.07.09 - TC 0926784 | 50 |

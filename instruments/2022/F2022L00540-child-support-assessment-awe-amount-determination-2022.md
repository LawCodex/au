This instrument is made under subsection 58AA(4) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: ***December quarter*** is defined in subsection 58AA(1) of the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Child Support (Assessment) Act 1989.*

###### 5 Determination of amount

The amount of $1328.90 is determined for the December quarter ending on 31 December 2021.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under paragraph 5H(12)(c) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this determination:

***“Act”*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

***“income support supplement”*** means the payment called the income support supplement payable under Part IIIA of the Act.

***“payment under the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme”*** means a payment made by the Commonwealth under the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme to an eligible survivor of the Stolen Generations.

***“service pension”*** has the same meaning as in subsection 5Q(1) of the Act.

***“Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme”*** has the same meaning as in section 3 of the *Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme (Facilitation) Act 2021.*

***“veteran payment”*** has the same meaning as in subsection 5Q(1) of the Act.

###### 5 Exempt lump sum

If:

a person receives a payment under the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme; and

the person is eligible for, or in receipt of:

an income support supplement; or

a service pension; or

a veteran payment;

the amount of payment under the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme received by the person is an exempt lump sum.

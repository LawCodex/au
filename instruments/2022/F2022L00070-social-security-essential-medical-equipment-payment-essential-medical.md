###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 917C(3)(a) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Essential Medical Equipment

Each of the following items of equipment is specified essential medical equipment, to the extent it is used in circumstances specified (if any) for the purposes of paragraph 917C(1)(a) of the Act:

Home Dialysis Machine;

Home Ventilator;

Home Respirator;

Home Parenteral or Enteral Feeding Device;

Oxygen Concentrator;

Heart Pump;

Suction Pump;

Infant Apnoea Monitor, in circumstances it is prescribed by a medical practitioner following apnoeic episodes;

Nebuliser, in circumstances it is used daily;

Positive Airways Pressure Device;

Phototherapy Equipment;

Airbed Vibrator;

Electric Wheelchair;

Insulin Pump.

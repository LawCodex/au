# Australian Citizenship (LIN 22/074: Classes of Persons Who May Receive a Pledge of Commitment) Authorisation 2022

LIN 22/074

Australian Citizenship (Classes of Persons Who May Receive Pledge of Commitment) Authorisation (LIN 22/074) 2022

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, make this instrument under subsection 27(5) of the *Australian Citizenship Act 2007* (the ***Act***)*.*

Dated 15 December 2022

**The Hon. Andrew Giles MP**

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

1 Name

This instrument is the *Australian* Citizenship (LIN 22/074: Classes of Persons Who May Receive a Pledge of Commitment) Authorisation 2022.

2 Commencement

(1) This instrument, other than section 3, is taken to have commenced on 8 September 2020.

(2) Section 3 of this instrument commences when the instrument is registered.

3 Repeal

*Citizenship (LIN 20/084: Class of Persons Who May Receive a Pledge of Commitment) Instrument 2020* is repealed.

4 Persons who may receive a pledge of commitment

(1) For subsection 27(5) of the Act, each class of persons mentioned in Schedule 1 is authorised to receive a pledge of commitment to become an Australian citizen.

(2) Despite subsection (1), a person who is a member of a class of persons mentioned in Schedule 1 is not authorised to receive a pledge of commitment to become an Australian citizen if:

(a) the Minister is satisfied that the person has received a pledge, or is likely to receive a pledge, in a way which is inconsistent with the character of a citizenship ceremony as non-commercial, apolitical, non-partisan and secular; and

(b) the Minister gives the person a notice to that effect.

A term in Column 1 of the following table refers to an Australian Public Service employee classified under the *Public Service Classification Rules 2000* as an employee at the level mentioned in Column 2 for the term.

Schedule 1 Classes of authorised persons

1 Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia.

2 A person who is Governor of a State.

3 A Member of the House of Representatives.

4 A Member of the Senate.

5 An entrusted person who is:

(a) an Australian citizen; and

(b) holding, occupying or performing the duties of any of the following positions in the Department of Home Affairs:

(i) Secretary;

(ii) SES 3;

(iii) SES 2;

(iv) Regional Directors in State and Territory offices;

(v) in the Citizenship Program:

(A) SES 1;

(B) EL 2 or EL 1;

(C) APS 6, APS 5 or APS 4.

6 An entrusted person who:

(a) is based in an Australian diplomatic mission; and

(b) is an Australian citizen; and

(c) holding, occupying or performing the duties of any of the following positions in the Department of Home Affairs:

(i) SES 3, SES 2, SES 1;

(ii) EL 2 or EL 1;

(iii) APS 6.

7 An Australian citizen holding, occupying of performing the duties of any of the following positions at an Australian diplomatic mission:

(a) High Commissioner;

(f) Chargé d'Affaires ad interim;

(g) Consul, at a post, serving as a Senior Australian Diplomatic Representative;

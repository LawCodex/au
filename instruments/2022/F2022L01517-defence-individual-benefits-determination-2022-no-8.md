###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 58B of the *Defence Act 1903*.

###### 4 Purpose

The purpose of this Determination is to provide a member commencing a long-term posting overseas travel benefits for a close relative to accompany the member to the overseas posting location and provide initial care arrangements for the member’s dependant.

###### 5 Definitions

In this Determination, the following apply.

**Baggage benefits** means benefits provided under Chapter 14 Part 4 Division 2 of the Defence Determination that the member’s close relative would be eligible for if they were a dependant of the member.

**Close relative** means a parent of the member to whom this Determination applies.

**Decision‑maker** means a person working in the Pay and Administration Centre – Victoria who is an Overseas Administration Team Leader.

**Defence Determination** means Defence Determination 2016/19, *Conditions of service*, as in force from time to time.

**Dependant** has the same meaning given by section 12.3.5 of the Defence Determination.

**Health care** means benefits provided under Chapter 15 Part 5 Division 2 of the Defence Determination that the member’s close relative would have been eligible for if they were a dependant of the member.

**Travel costs** means benefits under section 14.4.6 of the Defence Determination that the member’s close relative would be eligible for if they were a dependant of the member.

###### 6 Application

This Determination applies to the member holding the employee identification number 8097840.

###### 7 Period of eligibility

Benefits under this Determination apply between the following dates.

Starting on a date set by the decision‑maker that aligns with the member’s departure from Australia to the overseas posting location.

Ending on the date that is 3 months after the close relative arrives in the member’s posting location.

###### 8 Flights

The member is eligible for a return business class flight for their close relative to travel from Australia to the member’s next posting location.

###### 9 Travel costs, baggage and health care benefits

The member is eligible for all of the following for their close relative.

Travel costs.

Baggage benefits.

Health care.

###### 10 Effect of cancellation of posting

If the member’s long-term posting overseas is cancelled on request of the member before the posting commences, the member must repay the cost of benefits received under this Determination that cannot be refunded.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Bali bombings travel assistance payment*** means a lump sum payment made to an individual in accordance with the ‘*Memorial Services for Bali Bombings Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines*’ for attendance at a Memorial Service.

***Memorial Service*** means an official memorial service for the 2002 Bali bombings held on 12 October 2022 at a location identified in the Memorial Services for Bali Bombings Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines.

***Memorial Services for Bali Bombings Travel Assistance Payment Guidelines*** means the program guidelines dated 19 September 2022, and published on the GrantConnect website (www.grants.gov.au – GO ID:GO5763), for a grant program operated by the Australian Government to support the participation of eligible Australians attending a Memorial Service.

Note: The following expression used in this instrument is defined in subsection 8(11) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

(a) exempt lump sum

###### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a Bali bombings travel assistance payment is an exempt lump sum.

**6 Application provision**

Section 5 applies to the calculation of the rate of social security payment payable to Bali bombings travel assistance payment recipients for days on or after 30 June 2022.

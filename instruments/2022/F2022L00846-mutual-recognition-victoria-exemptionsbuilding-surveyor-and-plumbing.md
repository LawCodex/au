###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 42S(1) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* of the Commonwealth.

###### 5 Exemptions

(1) For the purpose of section 42S(1) of the Act, the following registrations or licences are excluded from the operation of automatic deemed recognition in Victoria:

a person who is a registered building practitioner under Part 11 of the *Building Act 1993 (Vic)* in the class of building surveyor;

a person who is a licensed plumber under Part 12A of the *Building Act 1993 (Vic)*;

a person who is a registered plumber under Part 12A of the *Building Act 1993 (Vic)* who is registered in any of the following classes of plumbing work specified in Part 4 or Part 5 of the Plumbing Regulations 2018:

(i) Gasfitting

(ii) Type A appliance conversion

(iii) Type A appliance servicing

(iv) Type B gasfitting

(v) Type B gasfitting advanced

(vi) Fire protection

(vii) Roofing (stormwater)

(viii) Mechanical services

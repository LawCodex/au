# Migration (Excluded health care and community services) Instrument (LIN 22/014) 2022

LIN 22/014

Migration (Excluded health care and community services) Instrument (LIN 22/014) 2022

I, Dr Gideon Gouws, Chief Medical Officer and delegate of the Minister, make this instrument under subclauses 4005(3) and 4007(1B) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations)*.*

Dated 14 April 2022

Dr Gideon Gouws

Chief Medical Officer

Department of Home Affairs

1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Excluded health care and community services) Instrument (LIN 22/014) 2022.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

3 Definitions

In this instrument:

***health care card*** or ***pensioner concession card*** is a card issued under a law of the Commonwealth.

***National Disability Insurance Scheme*** has the meaning given by the *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013*.

***pharmaceutical benefits scheme*** has the meaning given by the *National Health Act 1958*.

***social security payment*** has the meaning given by the *Social Security Act 1991*.

4 Specified health care and community services

For subclauses 4005(3) and 4007(1B) of Schedule 4 to the Regulations, the following health care and community services are specified:

(a) social security payments;

(b) benefits derived from holding a health care card or pensioner concession card;

(c) provision of pharmaceuticals listed under the pharmaceuticals benefits scheme that if the person ceases to take would not be seriously detrimental to their life or wellbeing;

(d) services provided under the National Disability Insurance Scheme.

5 Repeal

*SPECIFICATION OF HEALTH CARE AND COMMUNITY SERVICES (IMMI 11/073)* (F2011L02242) is repealed.

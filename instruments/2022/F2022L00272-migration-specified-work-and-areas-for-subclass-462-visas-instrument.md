# Migration (Specified work and areas for subclass 462 visas) Instrument (LIN 22/013) 2022

LIN 22/013

made under regulation 1.15FA of the

Migration Regulations 1994

**Compilation No. 3**

**Compilation date:** 5 April 2025

**Includes amendments:** F2025L00485

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Migration (Specified work and areas for subclass 462 visas) Instrument (LIN 22/013) 2022* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 05/04/2025 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au). The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. For more information on any uncommenced amendments, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Application, saving and transitional provisions for provisions and amendments**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. For more information on any modifications, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Specified Work and Areas for Subclass 462 visas) Instrument (LIN 22/013) 2022*.

###### 3 Purpose of Instrument

For the definition of ***specified Subclass 462 work*** in regulation 1.03 and regulation 1.15FA of the Regulations, this instrument specifies:

(a) areas of Australia; and

(b) kinds of work.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***bushfire affected area*** means one or more areas of Australia mentioned in item 1 of Schedule 1.

***COVID-19*** means the pandemic declared by the World Health Organization on 11 March 2020, caused by the coronavirus COVID-19.

***Northern Australia*** means one or more areas of Australia mentioned in item 2 of Schedule 1.

***Regional Australia*** means one or more areas of Australia mentioned in item 3 of Schedule 1.

***Remote and Very Remote Australia*** means one or more areas of Australia mentioned in item 4 of Schedule 1.

###### 5 Fishing and pearling work, and tree farming and felling work

(1) Northern Australia is specified if the work carried out in that area is a kind of work mentioned in subsection (2).

(2) A kind of work mentioned in the following table is specified if carried out in Northern Australia.

| Item | Kinds of work |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fishing and pearling:<br>(a) conducting operations relating directly to taking or catching fish and other aquatic species;<br>(b) conducting operations relating directly to taking or culturing pearls or pearl shell |
| 2 | Tree farming and felling:<br>(a) planting or tending trees in a plantation or forest that are intended to be felled;<br>(b) felling trees in a plantation or forest;<br>(c) transporting trees or parts of trees that were felled in a plantation or forest to the place where they are first to be milled or processed, or any other place from which they are to be transported to the place where they are to be milled or processed |

###### 6 Plant and animal cultivation work, and construction work

(1) Northern Australia and Regional Australia are specified if the work carried out in those areas is a kind of work mentioned in subsection (2).

(2) A kind of work mentioned in the following table is specified if carried out in Northern Australia and Regional Australia.

| Item | Kinds of work |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plant and animal cultivation:<br>(a) harvesting and/or packing of fruit and vegetable crops;<br>(b) pruning or trimming vines and trees directly associated with the cultivation and commercial sale of plant produce;<br>(c) general maintenance crop work;<br>(d) cultivating or propagating plants, fungi or their products or parts;<br>(e) immediate processing of plant products;<br>(f) maintaining animals for the purposes of selling them or their bodily produce, including natural increase;<br>(g) immediate processing of animal products including shearing, butchery, packing and tanning, but not including secondary processing;<br>(h) manufacturing dairy produce from raw material |
| 2 | Construction:<br>(a) building construction;<br>(b) heavy and civil engineering construction;<br>(c) land development and site preparation services;<br>(d) building structure services;<br>(e) building installation services;<br>(f) building completion services |

###### 7 Tourism and hospitality work

(1) Areas of Australia within postcodes 4406, 4416, 4498 and 7215, Northern Australia and Remote and Very Remote Australia are specified if the work carried out in those areas is:

(a) a kind of work mentioned in subsection (2); and

(b) carried out after 21 June 2021.

(2) Tourism and hospitality work is specified if carried out in areas of Australia within postcodes 4406, 4416, 4498 and 7215, Northern Australia and Remote and Very Remote Australia for a person providing one or more of the following services:

(a) accommodation, including:

(i) hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts, and backpacker hostels;

(ii) caravan parks and camping grounds;

(iii) commercial housekeeping services;

(iv) boarding houses, guest houses and reception centres;

(b) food and beverage, including:

(i) cafes and restaurants;

(ii) takeaway food services/businesses;

(iii) catering services;

(iv) pubs, taverns and bars;

(v) hospitality clubs;

(c) services provided directly to tourists, including:

(i) tourist guides and operators;

(ii) outdoor adventure or activity instructors;

(iii) tourist transport service workers;

(iv) gallery or museum workers, curators or guides;

(v) travel agency and tourist information workers;

(vi) event and entertainment venue workers.

###### 8 Bushfire recovery work

(1) Bushfire affected area is specified if the work carried out in that area is:

(a) a kind of work, paid or volunteer, mentioned in subsection (2); and

(b) carried out after 31 July 2019.

(2) Bushfire recovery work:

(a) is specified if carried out in a bushfire affected area; and

(b) includes:

(i) construction, farming, or any other work in association with recovery or restitution of land, property, farm animals or wildlife;

(ii) providing support services or assistance to people living, working or volunteering in the affected areas.

###### 9 Critical COVID-19 work in the healthcare and medical sectors

(1) Each area of Australia is specified if the work carried out in that area is:

(a) a kind of work mentioned in subsection (2); and

(b) carried out after 31 January 2020.

(2) Critical COVID-19 work in the healthcare and medical sectors:

(a) is specified if carried out in each area of Australia; and

(b) includes:

(i) medical treatment, nursing, contact tracing, testing and research;

(ii) support services such as cleaning of medical and health care facilities and equipment.

###### 9A Recovery work—flood, cyclone or other severe weather

(1) A kind of work is specified for the purposes of the definition of ***specified Subclass 462 work*** in regulation 1.03 of the Regulations if the work:

(a) is carried out in an area mentioned in item 5 of Schedule 1 as an area affected by flood, cyclone or other severe weather; and

(b) is carried out after 31 December 2021; and

(c) includes:

(i) clean-up, construction or any other work in association with restitution or restoration of services, land, waterways, property or infrastructure;

(ii) providing support services or assistance to people living, working or volunteering in the area affected by flood, cyclone or other severe weather.

(2) An area of Australia is specified for the purposes of the definition of ***specified Subclass 462 work*** in regulation 1.03 of the Regulations if the area is an area mentioned in item 5 of Schedule 1 as an area affected by flood, cyclone or other severe weather.

###### 10 Application of amendments made by LIN 24/020

The amendments made by the *Migration Legislation Amendment (Specified Work and Areas for Subclass 417 and 462 Visas) Instrument (LIN 24/020) 2024* apply in relation to an application for a Work and Holiday (Temporary) (Class US) visa:

(a) made, but not finally determined, before the commencement of that instrument; or

(b) made on or after the commencement of that instrument.

###### 11 Application of amendments made by LIN 25/048

The amendments made by the *Migration Legislation Amendment (Specified Work and Areas for Subclass 417 and 462 Visas) Instrument 2025* apply in relation to an application for a Work and Holiday (Temporary) (Class US) visa:

(a) made, but not finally determined, before the commencement of that instrument; or;

(b) made on or after the commencement of that instrument.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 123TGA(c) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999.*

###### 4 Definition

In this Determination:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Declared voluntary income management areas

(1) Each area specified in Schedule 1 is a declared voluntary income management area for the purposes of Part 3B of the Act.

(2) For Schedule 1, an area corresponds with a postcode that has been allocated by Australia Post in respect of Western Australia.

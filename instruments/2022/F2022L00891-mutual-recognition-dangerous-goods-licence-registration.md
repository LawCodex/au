# Mutual Recognition (Dangerous Goods Licence Registration Notification—Northern Territory) Determination 2022

Northern Territory of Australia

*Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth)

**Mutual Recognition (Dangerous Goods Licence Registration Notification—Northern Territory) Determination 2022**

I, Chanston James Paech, Attorney-General and Minister for Justice, under section 42J(4) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth), and with reference to section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* (Cth), determine that each licence issued under the *Dangerous Goods Act 1998* is a registration for which notification is required by section 42J(1) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth).

Chanston James Paech

…………………………………………..

Attorney-General and Minister for Justice

Dated 24 June 2022

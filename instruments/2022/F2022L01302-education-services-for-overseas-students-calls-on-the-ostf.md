###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 50B(5) of the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) OSTF.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***authorised deposit-taking institution*** has the same meaning as in the *Banking Act 1959*.

###### 5 Schedules

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable item in that Schedule.

###### 6 Requirements for payment

(1) If the TPS Director must pay an amount to a registered provider under paragraph 50B(3)(a) of the Act, the payment must be:

(a) made electronically;

(b) in Australian dollars paid into a transaction account nominated by, and in the name of, the provider; and

(c) paid into an account held with an authorised deposit-taking institution.

(2) If the TPS Director must pay an amount to a person under paragraph 50B(3)(b) of the Act, the payment must be:

(a) made electronically; and

(b) in Australian dollars paid into a transaction account nominated by, and in the name of, the person.

(3) If the TPS Director must pay an amount to a student under paragraph 50B(3)(c) of the Act, the payment must be:

(a) made electronically; and

(b) in Australian dollars paid into a transaction account nominated by, and in the name of, the student.

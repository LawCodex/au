# Telecommunications (Fibre-ready Facilities- Exempt Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Real Estate Development Project) Instrument 2022

I, MICHELLE ROWLAND, Minister for Communications, make the following instrument.

MICHELLE ROWLAND

Minister for Communications

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraphs 372K(1)(a), (c), (d), (e) and (f) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) building unit;

(b) real estate redevelopment project.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council*** means the local government entity, the Corporation of the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council (ABN 55 631 460 952).

***Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project*** means the real estate development project comprising the 25-lot subdivision owned by Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council located as described in column 1, and within the coordinates in column 2 of Schedule 1 to this instrument.

###### 5 Exemption

(1) Subject to subsection (2), for the purposes of paragraphs 372K(1)(a), (c), (d), (e) and (f) of the Act, the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project is specified as exempt from the requirements in sections 372E, 372F, 372G and 372H of the Act.

(2) The following condition is specified:

(a) Where there is an absence of fixed-line telecommunications infrastructure in respect of the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project, the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council must give notice in writing to all residents, including prospective residents, of a building unit situated on the land comprising the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project that:

(i) there is an absence of fixed-line telecommunications infrastructure in respect of the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project; and

(ii) telecommunications services will be delivered to the building units in the Lockhart River Aboriginal Shire Council Piiramo Street Subdivision Project through wireless networks or satellite networks, as the case may be.

Note: Subsection 372K(2) provides that an exemption under subsection 372K(1) of the Act may be unconditional or subject to such conditions (if any) as are specified in the exemption.

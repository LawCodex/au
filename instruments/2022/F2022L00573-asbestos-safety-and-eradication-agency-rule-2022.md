###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Asbestos Safety and Eradication Agency Act 2013*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3 of the Act, including the following:

(a) Agency;

(b) asbestos safety.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Asbestos Safety and Eradication Agency Act 2013*.

###### 5 Functions of the Agency

For the purposes of paragraph 8(1)(g) of the Act, the Agency has the following functions:

(a) to raise awareness of asbestos safety, including by developing and promoting materials on asbestos safety;

(b) to collect and analyse data required for measuring progress on preventing exposure to asbestos fibres and for informing evidence-based policies and strategies;

(c) to promote consistent messages, policies and practices in relation to asbestos safety.

# Cocos (Keeling) Islands Utilities and Services (Electricity Supply and Services Fees) Amendment (2022 Measures No. 2) Determination 2022

I, Natasha Griggs, Administrator of the Territory of Cocos (Keeling) Islands, make the following determination.

Natasha Griggs

Administrator of the Territory of Cocos (Keeling) Islands

Contents

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Category 2 Group Member*** has the meaning for this term given in the Settlement Deed approved by the Federal Court of Australia on 11 June 2021 in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (No 2)* [2021] FCA 634.

***Eligible Category 3 Group Member*** has the meaning for this term given in the Settlement Deed approved by the Federal Court of Australia on 11 June 2021 in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (No 2)* [2021] FCA 634.

***Eligible Group Member*** means a Category 2 Group Member or an Eligible Category 3 Group Member.

***Settlement Payment*** means a payment of a proportion of the settlement sum based upon interest notionally accrued on monies repaid by an Eligible Group Member to the Commonwealth, pursuant to the Settlement Deed approved by the Federal Court of Australia on 11 June 2021 in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (No 2)* [2021] FCA 634.

###### 5 Exempt lump sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, a Settlement Payment received by an Eligible Group Member is an exempt lump sum.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the Act.

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Approval

EQUALS International (Aust) Pty Ltd (ACN 078 991 858), a body corporate, is approved as a higher education provider under section 16‑25 of the Act.

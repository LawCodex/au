# Migration (Points for Business Innovation Stream and Investor Stream of Subclass 188 Visa) Instrument (LIN 22/083) 2022

LIN 22/083

Migration (Points for Business Innovation Stream and Investor Stream of Subclass 188 Visa) Instrument (LIN 22/083) 2022

I, Andrew Giles, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, under subclauses 188.222(1) and 188.242(1) of Schedule 2 to the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the ***Regulations***):

(a) Specify that for subclauses 188.222(1) and 188.242(1) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the applicant’s score on the business innovation and investment points test must be not less than 65 points.

(b) Repeal *Points for Business Innovation Stream and Investor Stream of Business Innovation and Investment (Provisional) Visa* (IMMI 12/041).

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

Dated 4 October 2022

ANDREW GILES

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

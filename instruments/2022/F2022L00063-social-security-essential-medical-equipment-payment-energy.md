###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 917E(3) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Forms of Energy

Each of the following forms of energy is a specified form of energy for the purposes of paragraph 917E(2)(b) of the Act:

Natural gas;

Liquid petroleum gas;

Diesel;

Heating oil;

Petrol;

Kerosene.

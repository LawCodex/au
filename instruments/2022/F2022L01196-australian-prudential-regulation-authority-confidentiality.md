# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 3 of 2022

**Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 3 of 2022**

Information provided by financial sector entities under relevant reporting documents

*Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998*

I, Alison Bliss, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 57(2)(b) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998* (the Act) determine all or a specified part of the reporting documents of a kind specified in Part 1, Part 2 or Part 3 of the Schedule do not contain confidential information.

This instrument commences on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

*Note:* The effect of this instrument is that the non-confidential information may be published under subsection 56(5C) of the Act.

Dated: 12 September 2022

Alison Bliss General Manager

Data Analytics & Insights

### Interpretation

In this determination

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***MySuper product*** means a MySuper product within the meaning given in section 10(1) of the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*.

***reporting document*** means a reporting document within the meaning of section 13 of the

*Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

# Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Extended operation of the Act in relation to Ovato Group of Companies in Administration) Declaration 01/2022

I, TONY BURKE, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated 23 September 2022

TONY BURKE

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Extended operation of the Act in relation to Ovato Group of Companies in Administration) Declaration 01/2022*.

###### 2 Commencement

###### This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of section 49 of the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012,* the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012* applies in relation to persons who were employed, but are no longer employed, by:

Ovato Limited (Administrators Appointed)

Ovato Print Cairns Pty Ltd (Administrators Appointed)

Ovato Packaging Pty Ltd (Administrators Appointed)

which are entities in administration under the *Corporations Act 2001*.

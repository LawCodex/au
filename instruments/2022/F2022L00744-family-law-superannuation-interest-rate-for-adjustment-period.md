###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Interest Rate for Adjustment Period) Determination 2022*.

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on 1 July 2022.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under regulation 45D of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

Note: Expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Federal Financial Relations Act 2009* as in force from time to time—see paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

### Part 2—Determination of national health reform payments for 2021-22

###### 5 National health reform payments for 2021-22

For the purposes of section 15A of the Act, each item of the following table specifies an amount that is to be paid to the State specified in the item, for the 2021-22 payment year, for the purpose of making a grant of financial assistance for the purpose of expenditure in accordance with the National Health Reform Agreement:

| Item | For this State: | The amount of financial assistance for the 2021‑22 payment year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | $9,179,806,921.34 |
| 2 | Victoria | $7,932,176,132.96 |
| 3 | Queensland | $6,316,504,375.91 |
| 4 | Western Australia | $3,188,498,985.14 |
| 5 | South Australia | $1,905,364,109.80 |
| 6 | Tasmania | $671,446,204.08 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | $548,173,370.47 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | $433,658,868.90 |
|  | **Total** | **$30,175,628,968.60** |

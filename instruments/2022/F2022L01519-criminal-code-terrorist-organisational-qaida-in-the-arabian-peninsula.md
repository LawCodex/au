###### 5 Terrorist organisation—Al‑Qa’ida in the Arabian Peninsula

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***terrorist organisation*** in subsection 102.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as al‑Qa’ida in the Arabian Peninsula is specified.

(2) Al‑Qa’ida in the Arabian Peninsula is also known by the following names:

(a) al‑Qa’ida in Yemen;

(b) Ansar al‑Sharia;

(c) AQAP;

(d) AQY;

(e) Sons of Hadramawt;

(f) Supporters of Sharia.

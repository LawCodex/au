# Migration (Designated regional areas for certain skilled and temporary graduate visas) Instrument (LIN 22/022) 2022

LIN 22/022

Migration (Designated regional areas for certain skilled and temporary graduate visas) Instrument (LIN 22/022) 2022

I, Alex Hawke, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs, make this instrument under the following provisions of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations):

(a) subregulations 1.15M(1) and (2);

(b) the definition of ***regional Australia*** in subregulation 5.19(16);

(c) subparagraph 888.226(2)(c)(i) of Schedule 2;

(d) paragraph 892.213(3)(b) of Schedule 2.

Dated 17 February 2022

Alex Hawke

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

1 Name

This instrument is the Migration (Designated regional areas for certain skilled and *temporary graduate visas) Instrument (LIN 22/022) 2022.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 5 March 2022.

(3) A part of Australia within a postcode mentioned in subsection (1) or (2) is specified for each of the following provisions of the Regulations:

(a) the definition of ***regional Australia*** in subregulation 5.19(16);

(b) subparagraph 888.226(2)(c)(i) of Schedule 2;

(c) paragraph 892.213(3)(b) of Schedule 2.

4 Application

This instrument applies to any of the following that occur after the instrument commences:

(a) a nomination made under paragraph 140GB(1)(b) of the *Migration Act 1958* (the ***Act***);

(b) an application for approval of a nomination made under subregulation 5.19(1) of the Regulations;

(c) an application made for any of the following visas:

(i) a second Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) visa in the Post-Study Work stream;

(ii) a Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)) visa;

(iii) a Subclass 888 (Business Innovation and Investment (Permanent)) visa;

(iv) a Subclass 892 (State/Territory Sponsored Business Owner) visa;

(d) an assessment made under section 93 of the Act;

(e) a grant of any of the following visas:

(i) a second Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate) visa in the Post-Study Work stream;

(ii) a Subclass 489 (Skilled—Regional (Provisional)) visa;

(iii) a Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)) visa.

5 Repeal

*Migration (Regional Areas) Instrument (LIN 20/292) 2020* (F2021L00044) is repealed.

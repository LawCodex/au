# Fair Work (State Declarations — employers not to be national system employers) Endorsement 2022 (No. 1)

*Fair Work Act 2009*

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Employment and Workplace relations, make this Endorsement under paragraph 14(4)(a) of the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

Dated 6 December 2022

Tony Burke

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Endorsement

This Endorsement is the *Fair Work (State Declarations — employers not to be national system employers) Endorsement 2022 (No. 1).*

###### 2 Commencement

This Endorsement commences on 1 January 2023.

###### 3 Definition

In this Endorsement:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

###### 4 Endorsement of State Declaration

For paragraph 14(4)(a) of the Act, a declaration as in force on 1 January 2023 under the *Industrial Relations (General) Regulations 1997* (WA) that an employer mentioned in Schedule 1 is not to be a national system employer for the purposes of the Act, is endorsed.

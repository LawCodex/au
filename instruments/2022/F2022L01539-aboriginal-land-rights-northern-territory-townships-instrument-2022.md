###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 3AB(3A) of the *Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976*.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976*.

(2) A reference in this instrument to a Survey Plan followed by a numerical identifier is a reference to the survey plan with that identifier that has been approved by the Surveyor‑General of the Northern Territory.

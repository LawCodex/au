# Mutual Recognition (Automatic Deemed Registration Notification – Northern Territory) Determination 2022 (No. 2)

Northern Territory of Australia

*Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth)

**Mutual Recognition (Automatic Deemed Registration Notification – Northern Territory) Determination 2022 (No. 2)**

I, Eva Dina Lawler, Treasurer, under section 42J(4) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth), determine that each registration specified in the Schedule is a registration for which notification is required by section 42J(1) of the Act.

Eva Dina Lawler

Treasurer

Dated 8 July 2022

**Schedule**

| 1 | A licence under the *Medicines, Poisons and Therapeutic Goods Act 2012* |
|---|---|
| 2 | A licence under the *Water Act 1992* |
| 3 | A registration under the *Construction Contracts (Security of Payments) Act 2004* |
| 4 | A licence under the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals (Control of Use) Act 2004* |
| 5 | A licence under the *Commercial Passenger (Road) Transport Act 1991* |
| 6 | A licence under the *Motor Vehicles Act 1949* |

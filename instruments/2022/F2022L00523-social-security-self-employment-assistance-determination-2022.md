###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1062A(2) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Activity

For the purposes of paragraph 1062A(1)(l) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the Self-Employment Assistance program is determined as an activity.

Note: Subsection 1062A(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991* enables the Employment Secretary, on behalf of the Commonwealth, to make, vary or administer an arrangement for the making of payments in relation to a range of purposes listed in that provision or in relation to an activity prescribed in a legislative instrument for the purposes of paragraph 1062A(1)(l).

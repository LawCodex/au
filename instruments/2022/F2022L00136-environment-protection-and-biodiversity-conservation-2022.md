# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation (2022 conservation theme for prioritising nominations of threatened species, threatened ecological communities and key threatening processes) Determination 2022

Commonwealth of Australia

Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation (2022 conservation theme for prioritising nominations of threatened species, threatened ecological communities and key threatening processes) Determination 2022

I, SUSSAN LEY, Minister for the Environment, under section 194D of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*, determine that the following conservation theme should be given priority in relation to the assessment period commencing 1 October 2022:

*‘Marine and freshwater species’*

This instrument commences the day after registration.

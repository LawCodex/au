# CASA 391/01 - Direction to retain navigation logs

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 391/01

### CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988

### CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS 1988

DIRECTION TO RETAIN NAVIGATION LOGS

I, MICHAEL ROBERT TOLLER, Director of Aviation Safety, under regulation 80 of the *Civil Aviation Regulations 1988,* direct that navigation logs for international operations conducted by Combined Aviation Services Pty Ltd (Aviation Reference Number 553573) be retained for a period of 6 months.

**[Signed M. Toller]**

Mick Toller

Director of Aviation Safety

27 November 2001

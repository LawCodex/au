# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/616A - Tariff Concession Order 0614614

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0001832 made on 23.05.00, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 9503.30.90 | CONSTRUCTION SETS, TOY OR MODEL, ABS AND/OR HVPC (high viscosity<br>polycarbonate) plastic, being EITHER of the following:<br>(a) having more than 50% of the pieces incorporating OR<br>representing ANY of the following:<br>(i) pieces with moving OR rotational joints;<br>(ii) vehicle parts;<br>(iii) mechanical parts;<br>(iv) motor components;<br>(v) electronic components,<br>(b) representing, when assembled, ANY of the following OR<br>combination thereof:<br>(i) sporting characters, fictional characters OR historical<br>characters;<br>(ii) fictional structures OR historical structures;<br>(iii) vehicles;<br>(iv) robots;<br>(v) cartoon characters;<br>(vi) movie characters, movie objects OR movie structures;<br>(vii) vessels;<br>(viii) animals;<br>(ix) weaponry<br><br>Op. 18.02.00 - TC 0001832 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

# Tariff Concession Order 0618078

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Coleman, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.11.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 6902.20.00 | BRICKS AND/OR SHAPES, REFRACTORY, BLAST FURNACE, fired, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) alumina (Al2O3) content NOT less than 66%;<br>(b) bulk density NOT less than 2.64%;<br>(c) apparent porosity NOT greater than 20%;<br>(d) cold crushing strength NOT less than 34 MPa;<br>(e) free carbon (F.C) content NOT less than 10% and NOT greater than 20%;<br>(f) silicon carbide (SiC) content NOT less than 2% and NOT greater than 5%<br>Op. 01.11.06 - TC 0618078 | 50 |

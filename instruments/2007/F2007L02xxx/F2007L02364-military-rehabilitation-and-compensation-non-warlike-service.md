# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Non-warlike Service) Determination 2007/1

I, Brendan Nelson, Minister for Defence, make this Determination under paragraph 6 (1) (b) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

Dated 20 June 2007

Signed by Brendan Nelson

BRENDAN NELSON

Minister for Defence

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (non-warlike service) Determination 2007/1.

###### 2 Nature of this Determination

For the avoidance of doubt, this Determination does not affect the operation of the previous Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (non-warlike service) Determination 2006/2.

###### 3 Commencement

This Determination commences on 1 July 2006.

###### 4 Non-warlike service

For paragraph 6 (1) (b) of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*, service on an operation specified in the Schedule is non-warlike service for the purposes of that Act.

Schedule Non-warlike service

(paragraph 6 (1) (b))

| Item | Name of operation | Nature of operation | Start date of operation | End date of operation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VIGILANCE | ADF support of the Australian whole-of-Government operation to enhance international peace and security | 1 July 2006 | Yet to be determined |

# AD/ENST 28/39 - Main Rotor Blade Retention Pin

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

For the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below issues the following Airworthiness Directive (AD) under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

|  | [image]<br>2. Determine the number of hours time in service (TIS) for any affected retention pin and replace the retention pin with a serviceable retention pin as follows:<br>a. | Compliance: | 1. Before further flight.<br>For Requirement 2.a: Within the next 5 hours TIS or within 30 days, whichever occurs first.<br>For Requirement 2.b: Before reaching 550 hours TIS or within 30 days, whichever occurs first.<br>This Directive shall be entered on the Maintenance Release as maintenance required. | Background: | The FAA received a report from the manufacturer that some retention pins were not manufactured in accordance with specifications cited on the engineering drawing. Failure of a retention pin would result in separation of a main rotor blade from the helicopter, and subsequent loss of control. |

# Tariff Concession Order 0706227

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 30.04.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7216.99.00 | STRIP, SOIL REINFORCEMENT, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) composition to Australian Standard AS3679.1 Grade 350<br>or equivalent;<br>(b) hot rolled;<br>(c) transverse ribs, having a height NOT less than 2.5 mm and<br>NOT greater than 4.5 mm;<br>(d) galvanized;<br>(e) attachment holes at one or both ends of the strip;<br>(f) width NOT less than 40 mm and NOT greater than 60 mm;<br>(g) thickness NOT less than 3 mm and NOT greater than 10 mm,<br>excluding height of ribs;<br>(h) length NOT greater than 15 metres<br>Op. 30.04.07 - TC 0706227 | 50 |

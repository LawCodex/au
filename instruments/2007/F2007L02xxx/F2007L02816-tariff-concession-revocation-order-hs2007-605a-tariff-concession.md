# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/605A - Tariff Concession Order 0614603

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0309837 made on 07.11.03, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 9502.10.00 | SETS, DOLL, comprising ALL of the following:<br>(a) solid, injection moulded torso, arms and legs;<br>(b) interchangeable body parts;<br>(c) head circumference greater than chest circumference;<br>(d) clothing;<br>AND having ANY of the following:<br>(i) sunglasses;<br>(ii) travel bags and/or handbags;<br>(iii) telephones and/or mobile phones;<br>(iv) umbrellas;<br>(v) furniture;<br>(vi) mirrors;<br>(vii) jewellery;<br>(viii) cosmetics;<br>(ix) towels;<br>(x) watches and/or clocks;<br>(xi) toys;<br>(xii) food;<br>(xiii) books;<br>(xiv) radios and/or cd players;<br>(xv) sun protection lotions<br><br>Op. 08.08.03 - TC 0309837 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 119/2007 - Tariff Concession Order 0710057

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Paul Coleman a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0701687 made on 04.06.07, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 31.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8208.10.00 | BLADES, SAW, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) cutting teeth per blade NOT less than 32;<br>(b) blade diameter NOT less than 40 mm;<br>(c) blade thickness NOT less than 1.5 mm;<br>(d) blade centre hole diameter NOT less than 40 mm<br><br>Op. 31.01.07 - TC 0701687 | 50<br>N/A |

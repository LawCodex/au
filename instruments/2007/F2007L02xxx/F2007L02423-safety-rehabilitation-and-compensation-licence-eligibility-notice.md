# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (2)

Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence

Eligibility) Notice 2007 (2)

**Notice No. 2 of 2007**

I, JOSEPH BENEDICT HOCKEY, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 18 July 2007

JOE HOCKEY

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (2).*

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (***the Act***), being satisfied that:

(a) Medibank Private Limited (ABN47 080 259) (‘the corporation’) is, but is about to cease to be, a Commonwealth authority; and

(b) it would be desirable for the Act to apply to the employees of the corporation,

I declare that the corporation is eligible to be granted a licence under Part VIII of the Act.

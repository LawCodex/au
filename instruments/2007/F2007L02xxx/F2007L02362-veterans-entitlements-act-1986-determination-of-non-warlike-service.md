# Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 - Determination of Non-warlike Service - Operation Vigilance

## Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986

**Determination of Non-warlike Service**

**Operation VIGILANCE**

I, Brendan Nelson, Minister for Defence:

determine that service rendered as a member of the Australian Defence Force assigned for service on Operation VIGILANCE on or after 1 July 2006 is non-warlike service for the purposes of the definition of “non-warlike service” in subsection 5C(1) of the *Veteran’s Entitlements Act 1986*, and

b. this Determination is taken to have commenced on 1 July 2006.

Dated 20 June 2007

*Signed Brendan Nelson*

BRENDAN NELSON

Minister for Defence

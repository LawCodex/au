# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/536A - Tariff Concession Order 0614732

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 8535103 made on 28.06.85, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8708.39 | PARTS FOR VEHICLES, being reproductions of goods fitted as<br>original equipment to vehicles, having BOTH of the following<br>characteristics:<br>(a) if imported, would fall within Chapter 87 of the Customs<br>Tariff Act 1987;<br>(b) manufactured prior to 1935,<br>being braking systems from the foot pedal to the brake drum or<br>disc inclusive<br><br>Op. 28.06.85 - TC 8535103 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

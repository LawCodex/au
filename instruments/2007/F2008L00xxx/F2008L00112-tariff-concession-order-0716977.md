# Tariff Concession Order 0716977

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 08.10.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 8537.20.90 | ELECTRICAL PROTECTION AND DISTRUBUTION SYSTEMS, comprising ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) isolated phase bus;<br>(b) generator circuit breakers;<br>(c) starting frequency converter breaker cabinets;<br>(d) power control centres, consisting of ALL of the following:<br>(i) low voltage switchgears;<br>(ii) control modules;<br>(iii) uninterruptible power supplies;<br>(iv) motor controllers;<br>(v) generator protection cabinets;<br>(e) low voltage transformers;<br>(f) battery back-ups;<br>(g) static excitation cabinets<br>Op. 08.10.07 - TC 0716977 | 50 |

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (5)

Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation

(Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (5)

**Notice No 5 of 2007**

I, JOSEPH BENEDICT HOCKEY, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 16 October 2007

JOE HOCKEY

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence*\
*Eligibility) Notice 2007 (5)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (the Act), being satisfied that it would be desirable for the Act to apply to the employees of the corporations listed in Schedule 1, I declare that the corporations are eligible to be granted a licence under Part VIII of the Act.

Note Each of the corporations is carrying on a business in competition with a Commonwealth authority or with another corporation that was previously a Commonwealth authority.

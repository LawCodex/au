# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (4)

Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation

(Licence Eligibility) Notice 2007 (4)

**Notice No 4 of 2007**

I, JOSEPH BENEDICT HOCKEY, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under section 100 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 16 October 2007

JOE HOCKEY

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence*\
*Eligibility) Notice 2007 (4)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration For section 100 of the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 (the Act), being satisfied that it would be desirable for the Act to apply to the employees of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia Limited (ACN 123 123 124) I declare that the corporation is eligible to be granted a licence under Part VIII of the Act.

*Note* The corporation was previously a Commonwealth authority.

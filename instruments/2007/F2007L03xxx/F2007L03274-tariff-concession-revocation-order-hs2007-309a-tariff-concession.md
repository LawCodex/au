# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/309A - Tariff Concession Order 0614266

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9703290 made on 01.07.97, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8421.91.90 | ROTORS, centrifuge, being ANY of the following:<br>(a) swinging bucket rotors having a capacity of 4 x 180 mL and<br>secondary containment covers;<br>(b) swinging bucket rotors capable of multiple microtitre and<br>deep-well 96 hole plates;<br>(c) rotors capable of carrying between 24 and 60 1.5 mL tubes;<br>(d) fixed angle rotors carrying 6 x 30 mL tubes with forces<br>greater than 8 500 g;<br>(e) fixed angle rotors carrying between 6 and 8 50 mL tubes with<br>forces greater than 7 500 g;<br>(f) fixed angle rotors with capacity of 6 x 85 mL and forces<br>greater than 6 000 g;<br>(g) fixed angle rotors 10 x 10 mL capacity with forces greater<br>than 16 500 g<br><br>Op. 01.07.97 - TC 9703290 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

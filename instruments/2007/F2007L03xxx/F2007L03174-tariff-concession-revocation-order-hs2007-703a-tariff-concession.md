# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/703A - Tariff Concession Order 0614779

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9600330 made on 11.01.96, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8527.31 | MUSIC SYSTEMS, audio only, integrated, comprising NOT less than<br>THREE of the following:<br>(a) turntable;<br>(b) cassette deck;<br>(c) preamplifier/control unit;<br>(d) amplifier;<br>(e) compact disc player;<br>(f) radio broadcast tuner,<br>but NOT including music systems as follows:<br>(i) imported with loudspeakers for use therewith; OR<br>(ii) incorporating ANY of the following:<br>(1) outputs for direct connection to a 100, 70, 50 or 25 volt<br>constant voltage line speaker system;<br>(2) more than one (mono or stereo) microphone input<br>connection;<br>(3) more than three speaker zone output selection switches;<br>(4) tone generators including any of bell (chime), pre<br>announce, alert or evacuate<br><br>Op. 11.01.96 - TC 9600330 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/300A - Tariff Concession Order 0614253

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9703277 made on 01.07.97, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8421.19.90 | CENTRIFUGES, table top, having at least 8 of the following:<br>(a) frequency controlled induction driven motor;<br>(b) microprocessor controlled;<br>(c) capable of accepting fixed angle rotor (maximum radius of 7.3<br>cm) producing greater than 10 790 g and capable of accepting<br>a haematocrit rotor (maximum radius 9.3 cm);<br>(d) speed control increments in 100 r/min;<br>(e) variable brake;<br>(f) swing out rotor capable of accepting microlitre plate<br>carriers and cyto adapters for slide preparation (maximum<br>radius 11.1 cm);<br>(g) CFC refrigerant free;<br>(h) software rotor identification;<br>(i) dimensions of 310 mm x 436 mm x 568 mm (HxWxD)<br><br>Op. 01.07.97 - TC 9703277 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

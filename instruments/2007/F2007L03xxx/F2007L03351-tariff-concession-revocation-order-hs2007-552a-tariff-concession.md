# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/552A - Tariff Concession Order 0614535

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9709679 made on 02.02.98, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8708.40.99 | GEARBOXES, automatic, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) maximum weight range from 220 kg to 300 kg;<br>(b) maximum engine torque range from 700 Nm to 1 600 Nm;<br>(c) maximum permissible input speed from 2 650 rpm to 2 900 rpm;<br>(d) gear ratios from 8.05 to 1.00;<br>(e) reverse gear ratio 5.67;<br>(f) maximum number of forward gears 6;<br>being gearboxes imported without ANY of the following:<br>(i) transfer gearboxes;<br>(ii) ancillary gearboxes;<br>(iii) ancillary transmission units<br><br>Op. 03.11.97 - TC 9709679 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/550A - Tariff Concession Order 0614533

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 9612594 made on 28.02.97, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8708.40.99 | GEARBOXES, AUTOMATIC OR MANUAL, being EITHER of the following:<br>(a) automatic gearboxes having ALL of the following:<br>(i) maximum weight range from 300 kg to 330 kg;<br>(ii) maximum engine torque range from 1 050 Nm to 1 600 Nm;<br>(iii) maximum permissible input speed from 2 650 rpm to<br>2 800 rpm;<br>(iv) design gear ratios from 3.43 to 0.59;<br>(v) design reverse gear ratio 4.84;<br>(vi) maximum number of forward gears 6;<br>(vii) with or without overdrive unit;<br>(b) manual gearboxes having ALL of the following:<br>(i) maximum weight range from 170 to 319 kg;<br>(ii) maximum engine torque range from 1 600 Nm to 2 300 Nm;<br>(iii) maximum number of forward gears 16;<br>(iv) with or without overdrive unit;<br>being gearboxes imported WITHOUT ANY of the following:<br>(a) transfer gear boxes;<br>(b) other ancillary gear boxes;<br>(c) other ancillary transmission units,<br><br>Op. 26.11.96 - TC 9612594 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order HS2007/528A - Tariff Concession Order 0614479

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 8809072 made on 26.10.88, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 01.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8544.59 | CABLES, flat ribbon type, containing 8 or more conductors,<br>but NOT including ANY of the following:<br>(a) cables fitted with connectors;<br>(b) cable jumpers being cables NOT more than 2 800 mm in<br>length, designed to connect printed circuit boards;<br>(c) cables having individual conductors with a cross-sectional<br>area of 0.5 sq mm or more<br><br>Op. 26.10.88 - TC 8809072 | 50<br>31.12.06 |

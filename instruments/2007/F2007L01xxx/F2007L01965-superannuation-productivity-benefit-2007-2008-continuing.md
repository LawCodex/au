# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2007-2008 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2007

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2007–2008 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 20071

I, niCK minchin, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 21 June 2007

NICK MINCHIN

Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2007-2008 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2007.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2007, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $ 2,805.38 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $ 2,805.38 | $252.48 |

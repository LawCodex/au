# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 97/2007 - Tariff Concession Order 0705899

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(3) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0701686 made on 30.04.07, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 31.01.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8424.89.90 | SPRAYERS, CONCRETE, UNDERGROUND MINING, diesel-hydraulic, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) spraying boom with:<br>(i) lateral coverage NOT greater than 8.5 metres;<br>(ii) vertical reach NOT greater than 9.5 metres;<br>(b) pump capacity NOT greater than 4 m3 and NOT greater<br>than 19 m3<br><br>Op. 31.01.07 - TC 0701686 | 50<br>N/A |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 84/2007 - Tariff Concession Order 0705893

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(2) of the Customs Act 1901, I Robert Crawford a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0702189 made on 15.02.07, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 16.11.06; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

| 8426.41.00 | HANDLERS, MATERIAL, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) diesel engine output NOT less than 95 kW and NOT greater<br>than 210 kW;<br>(b) action radius NOT less than 7 metres and NOT greater than<br>20 metres;<br>(c) operating weight NOT less than 20 tonnes and NOT<br>greater than 65 tonnes;<br>(d) compact boom NOT greater than 12 metres;<br>(e) loading stick NOT less than 2 metres and NOT greater than<br>10 metres;<br>(f) elevated cabin;<br>(g) 4-point outriggers;<br>(h) solid rubber tyres<br><br>Op. 16.11.06 - TC 0702189 | 50<br>N/A |

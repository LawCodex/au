# Tariff Concession Order 0701750

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Diane Morland, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 02.02.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8428.90.00 | PALLETISING SYSTEM having ALL of the following:<br>(a) robot controlled palletiser;<br>(b) pallet magazine;<br>(c) pallet conveyor;<br>(d) case and/or carton and/or tray conveyor;<br>(e) packing capacity NOT less than 350 layers per hour;<br>(f) grouping speed NOT less than 100 cycles per minute<br>Op. 02.02.07 - TC 0701750 | 50 |

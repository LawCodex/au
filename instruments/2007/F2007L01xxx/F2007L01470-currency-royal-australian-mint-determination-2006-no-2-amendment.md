# Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2006 (No. 2) Amendment Determination 2007 (No. 1)

*Currency Act 1965*

I, CHRISTOPHER JOHN PEARCE, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 16 May 2007

CHRIS PEARCE

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

This Determination is the *Currency (Royal Australian Mint) Determination 2006 (No. 2) Amendment Determination 2007 (No. 1)*.

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

# Tariff Concession Order 0618671

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 20.11.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8419.89.90 | TANKS, STORAGE, steel, complying with American Petroleum<br>Institute Standard 650 (API650), having ALL of the following:<br>(a) load capacity NOT less than 1 250 cubic metres;<br>(b) liquid density NOT greater than 1 500 kilograms per cubic<br>metre at full load capacity;<br>(c) diameter NOT less than 10 metres;<br>(d) height NOT less than 12 metres;<br>(e) internal heating coils;<br>(f) internal sloping floors<br>Op. 20.11.06 - TC 0618671 | 50 |

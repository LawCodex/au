# Determination of Assessment Period for Places for Inclusion in the National Heritage List and Commonwealth Heritage List (20/02/2007)

*Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*

**DETERMINATION OF ASSESSMENT PERIOD FOR PLACES FOR INCLUSION IN THE NATIONAL HERITAGE LIST AND COMMONWEALTH HERITAGE LIST**

In accordance with the provisions of sections 324G and 341G of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* I, Malcolm Bligh Turnbull, Minister for the Environment and Water Resources, determine, pursuant to paragraphs 324G(1)(a) and 341G(1)(a), that the assessment period for the Australian Heritage Council to assess places for inclusion in the National Heritage List and in the Commonwealth Heritage List will commence, for both lists, on 1 July 2007.

Dated 20 February 2007

Malcolm Bligh Turnbull

Minister for the Environment and Water Resources

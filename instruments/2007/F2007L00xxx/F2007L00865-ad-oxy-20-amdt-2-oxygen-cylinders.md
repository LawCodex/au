# AD/OXY/20 Amdt 2 - Oxygen Cylinders

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/OXY/20 Amdt 1 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The original issue of this Directive was issued following information concerning the risk of high-pressure oxygen cylinder rupturing causing rapid venting of the contents. These cylinders are used for missions at high altitudes or to ensure respiratory aid for passengers feeling sick.<br>It has been demonstrated that the material characteristics of the Aluminium Alloy 5283 from which the cylinders are manufactured deteriorate in the course of time and may possibly lead these oxygen cylinders to tear and abruptly vent onboard an aircraft. |
|  | Amendment 1 of this Directive was issued to correct a typographical error. |

|  | This Amendment primarily extends the compliance by six months for aircraft operated in normal climatic conditions and having already accumulated 25 years TIS, as of the effective date of this Amendment. The Amendment also allows seven days after the effective date to identify and, if necessary, empty spare cylinder, it also introduces some minor formatting changes. |

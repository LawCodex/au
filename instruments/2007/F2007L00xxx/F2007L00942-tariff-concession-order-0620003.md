# Tariff Concession Order 0620003

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Michael Perry, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 20.12.06 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 8414.59.90 | ASSEMBLIES, FAN, EVAPORATORS AND/OR CONDENSERS, DOMESTIC<br>REFRIGERATOR, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) motor power consumption NOT less than 4 watts and NOT<br>greater than 9 watts;<br>(b) blade pitch NOT less than 12 mm and NOT greater than 45 mm;<br>(c) shroud diameter NOT greater than 255 mm<br>Op. 20.12.06 - TC 0620003 | 50 |

# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - Determination of Assessment Period for Items for Inclusion in Subdivision A Lists (20/02/2007)

*Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*

**DETERMINATION OF ASSESSMENT PERIOD FOR ITEMS FOR INCLUSION IN SUBDIVISION A LISTS**

In accordance with the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* I, Malcolm Bligh Turnbull, Minister for the Environment and Water Resources, determine, pursuant to paragraph 194C(1)(a), that the assessment period relating to the assessment for inclusion of items in Subdivision A Lists will commence, for all lists, on 1 October 2007.

Dated 20 February 2007

Malcolm Bligh Turnbull

Minister for the Environment and Water Resources

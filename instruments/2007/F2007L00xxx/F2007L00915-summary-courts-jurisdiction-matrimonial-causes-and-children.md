# Summary Courts Jurisdiction (Matrimonial Causes and Children) Proclamation 2007

*Family Law Act 1975*

I, PHILIP MICHAEL JEFFERY, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make this Proclamation under subsections 39 (7) and 69J (3) of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

Signed and sealed with the\
Great Seal of Australia\
on 12 April 2007

P. M. JEFFERY\
Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

PHILIP RUDDOCK

Attorney‑General

###### 1 Name of Proclamation

This Proclamation is the *Summary Courts Jurisdiction (Matrimonial Causes and Children) Proclamation 2007*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Proclamation commences on 23 April 2007.

###### 3 Jurisdictional restriction

On and after 23 April 2007, proceedings in relation to matters arising under Parts V and VII of the *Family Law Act 1975* may not be instituted in, or transferred to, the following courts of summary jurisdiction located in the State of New South Wales.

| Court of summary jurisdiction | Location |
|---|---|
| Central Local Court | 98 Liverpool Street, Sydney |
| Downing Centre Local Court | Levels 4 and 5, Downing Centre<br>143–147 Liverpool Street, Sydney |
| Local Court Newtown | 222 Australia Street, Newtown |
| Local Court North Sydney | 94 Pacific Highway, North Sydney |

# Tariff Concession Order 1042798

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Michael Perry, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 17.09.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7312.10.00 | ROPE, WIRE, galvanised steel OR stainless steel, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) rope diameter NOT less than 16 mm and NOT greater than<br>18 mm;<br>(b) 6 strand laid rope, individual strands coated with<br>polyamide AND/OR polyester;<br>(c) standard wire tenacity NOT less than 1770 N/mm2<br>Op. 17.09.10 - TC 1042798 | 50 |

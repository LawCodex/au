# Tariff Concession Order 1017443

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 15.04.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8428.90.00 | RETORT POUCH LOADING AND UNLOADING MACHINES, comprising ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) conveyors;<br>(b) tray handling robots;<br>(c) crate turntables;<br>(d) servo motors;<br>(e) tray stackers and destackers;<br>(f) tray transporters;<br>(g) crates;<br>(h) control panels;<br>(i) stainless steel perimeter guarding;<br>(j) input capacity NOT less than 150 pouches per minute;<br>(k) output capacity NOT less than 200 pouches per minute<br>Op. 15.04.10 - TC 1017443 | 50 |

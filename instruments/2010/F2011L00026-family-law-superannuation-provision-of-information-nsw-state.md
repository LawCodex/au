# Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information - NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2010

Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme) Determination 20101

*Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*

I, ROBERT McCLELLAND, Attorney-General, make this Determination under subregulations 64 (7) and (7B) of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

Dated 21 December 2010

ROBERT McCLELLAND

Attorney-General

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2010*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Definitions

(1) In this Determination:

***early release debt account*** means the account created under subregulation 19 (3) of the *State Authorities Superannuation Regulation 2010* (NSW).

***flag lifting agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

***M & F Approval*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2003*.

***NSW Local Government Superannuation Act*** means the *Local Government and Other Authorities (Superannuation) Act 1927* (NSW).

***NSW State Authorities Superannuation Act*** means the *State Authorities Superannuation Act 1987* (NSW).

***NSW State Authorities Superannuation (Closed Local Government Schemes Transfer) Regulation*** means the *State Authorities Superannuation (Closed Local Government Schemes Transfer) (Savings and Transitional) Regulation 1990* (NSW).

***NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme*** means the superannuation scheme constituted by the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Act.

***Regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

***splitting order*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

***superannuation agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

(2) An expression used in this Determination and in the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Act or in a provision of that Act has the same meaning in this Determination as it has in the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Act or the provision of that Act.

###### 4 Application of Determination

This Determination applies to a superannuation interest in the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme for which a method and factors have been approved under Part 7 of Schedule 2 to the M & F Approval.

###### 5 Provision of information by trustee

For a superannuation interest mentioned in an item in the following table:

(a) for paragraphs 64 (7) (a) and (7B) (a) of the Regulations, the trustee of the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme is not required to provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 3 of the item; and

(b) for paragraphs 64 (7) (b) and (7B) (b) of the Regulations, the trustee of the NSW State Authorities Superannuation Scheme must provide any information about the interest mentioned in column 4 of the item.

# Tariff Concession Order 1031202

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 08.07.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8419.50.90 | HEAT EXCHANGERS, STEAM, OIL AND GAS WELL TESTING, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) adjustable chokes;<br>(b) working temperature NOT less than - 20 degrees C and NOT<br>greater than 160 degrees C;<br>(c) steam consumption NOT less than 2 200 kg/h;<br>(d) coil working pressure NOT less than 69 MPa;<br>(e) heating capacity NOT less than 4,600 J/h<br>Op. 08.07.10 - TC 1031202 | 50 |

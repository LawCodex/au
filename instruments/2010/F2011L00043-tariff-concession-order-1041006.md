# Tariff Concession Order 1041006

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 06.09.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 5602.10.00 | NEEDLE FELT, comprising polyimide fibre, scrim supported, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) weight NOT less than 450 g/m2 and NOT greater than<br>800 g/m2;<br>(b) thickness NOT less than 1.5 mm under 20 g/cm2 pressure and<br>NOT greater than 2.1 mm under 20 g/cm2 pressure;<br>(c) air permeability NOT less than 100 L/dm3/dm2/min at 20 mm<br>water gauge and NOT greater than 250 L/dm3/dm2/min at 20<br>mm water gauge;<br>(d) continuous operating temperature NOT greater than 180<br>degrees C with less than 35 percent moisture;<br>(e) polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) emulsion finish<br>Op. 06.09.10 - TC 1041006 | 50 |

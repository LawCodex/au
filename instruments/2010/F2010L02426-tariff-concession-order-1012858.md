# Tariff Concession Order 1012858

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Michael Perry, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 15.03.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8481.80.90 | VALVES, BALL, cast iron, comprising ALL of the following:<br>(a) working pressures NOT exceeding 3.4 MPa (500 psi);<br>(b) with OR without lockout;<br>(c) bore end dimensions NOT less than 19 mm and NOT greater<br>than 51 mm;<br>(d) end connection types, including ANY of the following:<br>(i) BSPF x BSPF (British Standard Pipe Female);<br>(ii) A-type x A-type (European Air Coupling);<br>(iii) BSPF x A-type;<br>(iv) SL x SL (Surelock);<br>(v) NPT x NPT (National Pipe Thread USA);<br>(vi) Shouldered Coupling x BSPF<br>Op. 15.03.10 - TC 1012858 | 50 |

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2010-2011 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2010

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2010-2011 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 20101

I, LINDSAY TANNER, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated June 17th 2010

LINDSAY TANNER

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2010-2011 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2010.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 July 2010.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2010, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $3,247.69 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $3,247.69 or more | $292.29 |

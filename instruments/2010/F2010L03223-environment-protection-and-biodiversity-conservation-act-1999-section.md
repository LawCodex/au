# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Instrument Adopting and Revoking Recovery Plans (26/11/2010) (NT, VIC)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

Instrument Adopting and Revoking Recovery Plans

I, CAROLYN WEISS CAMERON, Assistant Secretary, Approvals and Wildlife Division, delegate for the Minister for Sustainability, Environment, Water, Population and Communities:

(a) pursuant to subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, hereby revoke the following recovery plan that is currently adopted under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*:

\- Basalt Greenhood (*Pterostyylis basaltica*) Recovery Plan 2000-2004. Department of Natural Resources and Environment, Victoria, February 1999

(b) under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as recovery plans for the listed threatened species and ecological community specified below, the respective plans made by the Northern Territory and Victoria (or agencies of those States) specified opposite those listed threatened species or ecological communities:

| **Listed Threatened Species** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| *Croitana aestiva* Desert Sand-skipper, Aestiva Skipper | Palmer C. M. 2010. National Recovery Plan for the Desert Sand-skipper *Croitana aestiva*. Department of Natural Resources, Environment, The Arts and Sport, Northern Territory. |
| *Dasyurus hallucatus* Northern Quoll | Hill B.M. and Ward S.J. (2010). National Recovery Plan for the Northern Quoll *Dasyurus hallucatus*. Department of Natural Resources, Environment, The Arts and Sport, Darwin. |

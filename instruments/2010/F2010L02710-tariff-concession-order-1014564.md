# Tariff Concession Order 1014564

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Michael Perry, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 24.03.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8504.40.90 | POWER SUPPLY UNITS, DC AND/OR AC output, complying with<br>Australian and New Zealand Standard 60950 (AS/NZS 60950) AND<br>Australian and New Zealand Standard CISPR 22 (AS/NZS CISPR 22),<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) voltage NOT less than 2.5 V and NOT greater than 55 V;<br>(b) wattage NOT less than 0.02 W and NOT greater than 105 W;<br>(c) current output NOT less than 0.01 amp and NOT greater<br>than 2.5 amp<br>Op. 24.03.10 - TC 1014564 | 50 |

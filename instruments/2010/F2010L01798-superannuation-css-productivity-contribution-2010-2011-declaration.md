# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2010-2011) Declaration 2010

Superannuation (CSS)

Productivity Contribution

(2010-2011) Declaration 2010

I, SANDRA BAILY, Manager of Australian Public Sector Defined Benefit Schemes Administration Section (Acting), Commonwealth Superannuation Administration, Position No. 44, and Delegate of the Australian Reward Investment Alliance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 25 June 2010

S BAILEY\
Delegate of Australian Reward Investment Alliance

[image]

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2010-2011) Declaration 2010*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration shall take effect from and including 1 July 2010.

# Tariff Concession Order 0939596

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 21.10.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7210.49.00 | STEEL, COIL, hot dip zinc coated, complying with Japanese<br>Industrial Standard JIS G 3302:2007, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) yield strength NOT less than 275 N/mm2 and NOT greater<br>than 380 N/mm2;<br>(b) tensile strength NOT less than 440 N/mm2;<br>(c) elongation NOT less than 29% and NOT greater than 41%;<br>(d) coating mass NOT less than 45 g/m2 and NOT greater than<br>65 g/m2;<br>(e) thickness NOT less than 1.14 mm and NOT greater than 1.26<br>mm;<br>(f) width NOT less than 1 590 mm and NOT greater than 1 605 mm<br>Op. 21.10.09 - TC 0939596 | 50 |

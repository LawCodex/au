# Water Act 2007 - Accreditation of amendment of transitional water resource plan - River Murray Prescribed Watercourse Water Allocation Plan

**Commonwealth of Australia**

***Water Act 2007***\
**Section 65**

**Accreditation of amendment of transitional water resource plan – River Murray Prescribed Watercourse Water Allocation Plan**

I, PENELOPE YING YEN WONG, Minister for Climate Change and Water, under subsection 65(5) of the *Water Act 2007*, hereby accredit the amendments to the River Murray Prescribed Watercourse Water Allocation Plan that were made under s 89(2)(f) of the *Natural Resources Management Act 2004* (SA) on 15 July 2009. The River Murray Prescribed Watercourse Water Allocation Plan is a transitional water resource plan under item 7 of the table in Schedule 4 to the *Water Act 2007*.

This accreditation will come into force on the day after this instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

Dated this 11th February 2010

Penelope Ying Yen Wong

PENELOPE YING YEN WONG\
**Minister for Climate Change and Water**

# Tariff Concession Order 1009885

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 25.02.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4016.93.00 | SEAL RINGS, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) diameter NOT greater than 250 mm;<br>(b) temperature resistance NOT less than minus 40 degrees<br>Celsius and NOT greater than 200 degrees Celsius;<br>(c) rubber composition being ANY of the following:<br>(i) nitrile butadiene rubber(NBR);<br>(ii) fluorocarbon rubber;<br>(iii) ethylene propylene diene monomer(EPDM) rubber<br>Op. 25.02.10 - TC 1009885 | 50 |

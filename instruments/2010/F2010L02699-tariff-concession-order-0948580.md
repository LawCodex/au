# Tariff Concession Order 0948580

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 14.12.09 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8428.90.00 | SUPPORT FRAME AND WORK PLATFORMS, self propelled, truss AND/OR<br>rail operating, including ALL of the following:<br>(a) modular support frame;<br>(b) clearance envelope NOT less than 4 m horizontally and NOT<br>less than 4 m vertically;<br>(c) working platform maximum load capacity NOT greater than<br>600 kg supported and extended by hydraulic lifting arms;<br>(d) materials loading crane;<br>(e) membrane lining roll support frame and spool;<br>(f) hydraulic AND/OR electric drive engine<br>Op. 14.12.09 - TC 0948580 | 50 |

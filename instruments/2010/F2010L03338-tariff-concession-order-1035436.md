# Tariff Concession Order 1035436

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 02.08.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 5602.10.00 | NEEDLE FELT, comprising polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) and<br>polyimide on a polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) scrim, having ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) weight NOT less than 450 g/m2 and NOT greater than<br>800 g/m2;<br>(b) maximum operating temperature NOT greater than 250<br>degrees C;<br>(c) thickness NOT less than 1.1 mm and NOT greater than 2.2 mm;<br>(d) air permeability NOT less than 0.08 m/s and NOT greater<br>than 0.2 m/s;<br>(e) polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) emulsion finish<br>Op. 02.08.10 - TC 1035436 | 50 |

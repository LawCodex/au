# Tariff Concession Order 1010915

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 03.03.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8501.32.00 | MOTORS, ELECTRIC, DC, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) voltage rating NOT less than 11.5 V and NOT greater than<br>24.5 V;<br>(b) output range NOT less than 1 450 W and NOT greater<br>than 2 050 W;<br>(c) torque rating NOT less than 5 N.m<br>Op. 03.03.10 - TC 1010915 | 50 |

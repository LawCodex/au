# Tariff Concession Order 1011545

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 05.03.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 8421.21.90 | STERILISING AND DISINFECTING MACHINES, WASTEWATER TREATMENT,<br>including ALL of the following:<br>(a) chlorine sterilisers, including pumps;<br>(b) ultraviolet disinfectors, including pumps;<br>(c) steriliser and disinfector speed drives;<br>(d) steriliser and disinfector valves;<br>(e) steriliser and disinfector flow meters;<br>(f) steriliser and disinfector controllers;<br>(g) steriliser and disinfector tanks;<br>(h) steriliser and disinfector pipes;<br>(i) steriliser and disinfector fittings;<br>(j) steriliser and disinfector cables;<br>(k) steriliser and disinfector analysers<br>Op. 05.03.10 - TC 1011545 | 50 |

# Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information - NSW State Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2010

Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — NSW State Superannuation Scheme) Determination 20101

*Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*

I, ROBERT MCCLELLAND, Attorney-General, make this Determination under subregulations 64 (7) and (7B) of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

Dated 21 December 2010

ROBERT MCCLELLAND

Attorney-General

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — NSW State Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2010*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Definitions

(1) In this Determination:

***flag lifting agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

***M & F Approval*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2003*.

***NSW Superannuation Act*** means the *Superannuation Act 1916* (NSW).

***Regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

***splitting order*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

***SS Scheme*** means the superannuation scheme constituted by the NSW Superannuation Act.

***superannuation agreement*** has the same meaning as in section 90MD of the *Family Law Act 1975*.

(2) An expression used in this Determination and in the NSW Superannuation Act or in a provision of that Act has the same meaning in this Determination as it has in the NSW Superannuation Act or the provision of that Act.

###### 4 Application of Determination

This Determination applies to a superannuation interest in the SS Scheme for which a method and factors have been approved under Part 8 of Schedule 2 to the M & F Approval.

###### 5 Provision of information by trustee

In relation to a superannuation interest mentioned in an item in the following table:

(a) for paragraphs 64 (7) (a) and (7B) (a) of the Regulations, the trustee of the SS Scheme is not required to provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 3 of the item; and

(b) for paragraphs 64 (7) (b) and (7B) (b) of the Regulations, the trustee of the SS Scheme must provide any information about the interest mentioned in column 4 of the item.

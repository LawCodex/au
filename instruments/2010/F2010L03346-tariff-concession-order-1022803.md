# Tariff Concession Order 1022803

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 21.05.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8481.80.90 | VALVES, BALL, flanged end connection, split body, lockable,<br>lever OR gearbox operated, trunnion mounted ball OR floating<br>ball, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) DN(Diameter Nominal) NOT less than 15 mm and NOT greater<br>than 200 mm;<br>(b) rating complying with American Society of Mechanical<br>Engineers ASME B16.34a-1998;<br>(c) inlet pressure NOT less than 1 800 kPA at 38 degrees<br>Celsius<br>Op. 21.05.10 - TC 1022803 | 50 |

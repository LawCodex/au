# CASA 213/00 - Approval under subregulation 207(2)

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 213/00

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS 1988**

**APPROVAL UNDER SUBREGULATION 207 (2)**

I, EUGENE HOLZAPFEL, Acting Section Head Systems, Certification Standards Branch, CASA, a delegate of CASA, under subregulation 207 (2) of the *Civil Aviation Regulations 1988* approve the operation of the aircraft bearing the nationality and registration marks VH-LHD with an approved flight data recorder system that retains its last 8 hours of recording, provided that the aircraft is equipped with an approved cockpit voice recorder system as required by paragraph 6.1 of section 20.18 of the Civil Aviation Orders that retains its last 90 minutes of recording.

**[Signed E. Holzapfel]**

EUGENE HOLZAPFEL

Acting Section Head Systems

Certification Standards Branch

23 May 2000

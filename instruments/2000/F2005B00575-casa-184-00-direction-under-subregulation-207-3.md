# CASA 184/00 - Direction under subregulation 207 (3)

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 184/00

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS 1988**

**DIRECTION UNDER SUBREGULATION 207 (3)**

I, RICHARD GODFREY YATES, a delegate of CASA, under subregulation 207 (3) of the *Civil Aviation Regulations 1988*, direct that a flight crew member who is on flight deck duty in an unpressurised aircraft engaged in parachuting operations must be provided with, and continuously use, supplemental oxygen:

(a) if the aircraft operates above flight level 120; or

(b) if the aircraft operates above 10,000 feet above mean sea level:

(i) for more than 15 minutes during a sortie; or

(ii) at night; or

(iii) in I.M.C.

**[Signed R.G. Yates]**

Richard G. Yates

Assistant Director

Aviation Safety Standards

23 May 2000

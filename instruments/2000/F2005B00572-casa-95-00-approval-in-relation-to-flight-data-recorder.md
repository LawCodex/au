# CASA 95/00 - Approval in relation to flight data recorder

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 95/00

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS 1988**

**APPROVAL IN RELATION TO FLIGHT DATA RECORDER**

I, WILLIAM RONALD SMITH, Head of Certification Standards, Certification Standards Branch, CASA, under subregulation 207 (2) of the *Civil Aviation Regulations 1988*, approve the operation of the aircraft bearing nationality and registration marks VH-YAF with an approved flight data recorder system that complies with the requirements of section 103.19 of the Civil Aviation Orders except for item 2 of Appendix I.

The approval is subject to the aircraft being fitted with the other equipment required under section 20.18 of the Civil Aviation Orders.

**[Signed W.R. Smith]**

William Ronald Smith

Head of Certification Standards

Certification Standards Branch

13 April 2000

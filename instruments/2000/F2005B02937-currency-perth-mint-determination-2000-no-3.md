# Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2000 (No. 3)

I, JOE HOCKEY, Minister for Financial Services and Regulation, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 7 September 2000

JOE HOCKEY

Minister for Financial Services and Regulation

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2000 (No. 3)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Specification of coins

This Determination specifies, for a coin having the denomination and standard composition specified in columns 2 and 3 of Part 1 of Schedule 1, the standard weight, the allowable variation from that weight, the dimensions and the design specified in columns 4, 5 and 6 of that Part.

###### 4 Application of Determination

The specifications in this Determination do not affect any other Determination of the specifications of a coin.

| 205 | Reverse | R235 | A circle of raised beads, immediately inside the rim, enclosing:<br>(a) either:<br>(i) if the nominal weight of the coin is measured in ounces — the inscription ‘X OZ 999 SILVER’ (where ‘X’ is the nominal weight, in ounces, of the coin expressed as a common fraction, or whole number, in Arabic numerals); or<br>(ii) if the nominal weight of the coin is 1 kilogram — the inscription ‘1KILO 999 SILVER’; and<br>(b) the inscription ‘2001’; and<br>(c) the Chinese character for ‘snake’; and<br>(d) a pattern of bats and clouds;<br>that, in turn, surround:<br>(e) a representation of a snake with 3 unhatched eggs, 1 cracked egg and 1 egg from which a baby snake is hatching;<br>with or without the inscription ‘P’. |

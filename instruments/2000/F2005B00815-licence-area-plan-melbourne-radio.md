## Licence Area Plan – Melbourne Radio

(1) This plan for radio broadcasting services in the Melbourne area of Victoria is made under section 26(1) of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

(2) Seven national radio broadcasting services, eleven commercial radio broadcasting services, nine community radio broadcasting services and two open narrowcasting radio services are to be available in the area described at Attachment 1.1 to this plan with the use of the broadcasting services bands.

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2000–2001 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988,* the table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 2000, as if the amounts set out in the following table were substituted for the amounts set out in the table in that Schedule:

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $169.13 | $13.53 |
| $169.13 or more but less than $2023.08 | The amount that is 8% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $2023.08 or more | $161.85 |

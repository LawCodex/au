###### 33 Return of persons to the Territory

(1) Subsection (2) applies to a person who:

(a) was a prisoner in Western Australia; and

(b) before being imprisoned, was ordinarily resident in the Territory; and

(c) within a period of 21 days after being released from prison, asks the chief executive officer to return him or her to the Territory.

(2) The chief executive officer must either pay the person’s fare to the Territory, or make arrangements to return the person to the Territory.

(3) However, subsection (1) does not entitle a person who was a prisoner outside the Territory to be returned to the Territory if, after release, he or she became ordinarily resident outside the Territory.

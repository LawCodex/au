# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Declared Places - Afghanistan and Iraq) Notice 2013

Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation *(Declared Places – Afghanistan and Iraq)*\
Notice 2013

**Notice No. 1 of 2013**

I, WILLIAM RICHARD SHORTEN, Minister for Workplace Relations, make this Notice under paragraph 6(1)(h) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 5 August 2013

WILLIAM RICHARD SHORTEN

Minister for Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation *(Declared Places – Afghanistan and Iraq)* Notice 2013.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of paragraph 6(1)(h) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*, I declare that:

Afghanistan; and

Iraq

are places to which paragraph 6(1)(h) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* applies.

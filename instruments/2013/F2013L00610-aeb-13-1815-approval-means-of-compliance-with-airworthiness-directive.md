# AEB 13/1815 - Approval - means of compliance with Airworthiness Directive for Bombardier aircraft

Instrument number AEB 13/1815

I, MICHAEL JAMES HIGGINS, Manager Continuing Airworthiness Section, Airworthiness and Engineering Standards Branch, Standards Division, a delegate of CASA, make this instrument under paragraph 39.004(3)(a) of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

**[Signed M. Higgins]**

Mike Higgins\
Manager Continuing Airworthiness Section\
Airworthiness and Engineering Standards Branch\
Standards Division

29 March 2013

Approval — means of compliance with Airworthiness Directive for Bombardier aircraft

1 Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on the day of registration; and

(b) ceases to have effect if Canadian Airworthiness Directive CF 2011-03R2 (the ***AD***) is amended or cancelled.

2 Application

This instrument applies to any Bombardier Inc. aeroplane registered in Australia and engaged in commercial passenger-carrying operations within Australian territory (the ***aircraft***).

3 Approved means of compliance

I approve for the aircraft, as a means of compliance with the AD other than that set out in the AD, the retention of any original chemical oxygen generator installed in any lavatory.

# Higher Education Provider Approval No. 7 of 2013

***NOTICE OF APPROVAL***

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Higher Education Support Act 2003***

**Higher Education Provider Approval No 7 of 2013**

**Approval under section 16-25, decision under paragraph 16-50(1)(a), and Notice of Approval under paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the** ***Higher Education Support Act 2003*****.**

I, Alison Cleary, a delegate of the Minister for Education for the purposes of sections 16-25 and 16-50 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003* (HESA):

(a) approve the application of Torrens University Australia Limited (ACN: 154 937 005 ABN: 99 154 937 005) as a Higher Education Provider in accordance with section 16‑25 of HESA; and

(b) note that this approval decides the application of Torrens University Australia Limited as required by paragraph 16‑50(1)(a) of HESA; and

(c) hereby give Torrens University Australia Limited written notice of my approval as required by paragraph 16‑50(1)(b) of HESA.

In accordance with paragraph 16‑55(2) of HESA this Notice of Approval commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

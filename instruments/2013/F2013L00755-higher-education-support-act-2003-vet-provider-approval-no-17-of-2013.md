# Higher Education Support Act 2003 - VET Provider Approval (No. 17 of 2013)

***NOTICE OF APPROVAL***

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Higher Education Support Act 2003***

**VET Provider Approval No 17 of 2013**

**Approval under subclause 6(1), decision under paragraph 11(1)(a), and notice of approval under paragraph 11(1)(b) of Schedule 1A to the** ***Higher Education Support Act 2003*** **(the Act).**

I, Lyndal Groom, a delegate of the Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Science and Research

(a) approve the application of Cinimod Enterprises Pty Ltd (ACN: 101 136 574,\
ABN: 55 101 136 574) as a VET provider in accordance with subclause 6(1) of Schedule 1A to the Act; and

(b) note that this approval decides the application of Cinimod Enterprises Pty Ltd as required by paragraph 11(1)(a) of Schedule 1A to the Act; and

(c) hereby give Cinimod Enterprises Pty Ltd written notice of my approval as required by paragraph 11(1)(b) of Schedule 1A to the Act.

In accordance with subclause 12(2) of Schedule 1A to the Act this notice of approval commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

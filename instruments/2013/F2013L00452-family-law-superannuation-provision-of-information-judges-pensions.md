# Family Law (Superannuation-Provision of Information: Judges' Pensions Act Scheme) Determination 2013

Family Law (Superannuation—Provision of Information: Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme) Determination 2013

I, Mark Dreyfus, Attorney‑General, make the following determination under subregulations 64(7) and (7B) of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

Dated: 12 March 2013

Mark Dreyfus

Attorney‑General

Contents

###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation—Provision of Information: Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme) Determination 2013*.

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on 15 March 2013.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under subregulations 64(7) and (7B) of the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this determination:

***growth phase table*** means the table in clause 2 of Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Methods and Factors Approval.

***Judge*** has the same meaning as in the *Judges’ Pensions Act 1968*.

***Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme*** means the scheme constituted by the *Judges’ Pensions Act 1968*.

***Methods and Factors Approval*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation)(Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2003*.

***Regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

(2) For this determination, a reference to “the relevant date” in the definition of a factor in the Methods and Factors Approval mentioned in column 3 of an item of the table in section 6 is to be read as if it were a reference to “the appropriate date”.

###### 5 Application of determination

This determination applies to a superannuation interest in the Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme for which a method and factors have been approved under Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Methods and Factors Approval.

###### 6 Provision of information by trustee—growth phase interests

In relation to a superannuation interest mentioned in an item in the following table:

(a) for paragraph 64(7)(a) of the Regulations, the trustee of the Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme is not required to provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 2 of the item; and

(b) for paragraph 64(7)(b) of the Regulations, the trustee of the Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme must provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 3 of the item.

###### 7 Provision of information by trustee—payment phase interests

(1) This section is made for paragraph 64(7B)(a) of the Regulations, in relation to an interest that a person has in the Judges’ Pensions Act Scheme as a result of being paid a pension under the *Judges’ Pensions Act 1968*.

(2) The trustee of the Scheme is not required to provide the information mentioned in paragraphs 64(3)(a), (aa), (c), (d) and (e) of the Regulations.

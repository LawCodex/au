Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Act 2013

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Determination 2013* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 15 September 2026 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au).

**Application, saving and transitional provisions**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Editorial changes**

For more information about any editorial changes made in this compilation, see the endnotes.

**Presentational changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* provides for First Parliamentary Counsel to make presentational changes to a compilation. Presentational changes are applied to give a more consistent look and feel to legislation published on the Register, and enable the user to more easily navigate those documents.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. Any modifications affecting the law are accessible on the Register.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Determination 2013*.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Act 2013*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this determination:

***Act*** means the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards (Registration Fees) Act 2013*.

***charging year*** means a period of 12 months starting on 15 September.

***minor product*** has the same meaning as in the WELS Determination.

***WELS Determination*** means the legislative instrument made under section 26 of the *Water Efficiency Labelling and Standards Act 2005* as in force from time to time.

###### 5 Registration fees

(1) The fee payable for an application to register a WELS product, whether a new registration or a registration for a further period, is:

(a) for the first application by a person in a charging year—the fee worked out under subsection (2) for the number of products that the person is applying to register; and

(b) for subsequent applications by the person in the same charging year—the difference between:

(i) the fee worked out under subsection (2) for the total number of products the person has applied to register during the charging year including the current application; and

(ii) the fee worked out under subsection (2) for the total number of products the person has applied to register in previous applications during the charging year.

(2) The fee for a number (the ***relevant number***) of products is to be worked out as follows:

(a) the fee is the fee set out in Schedule 1 for the relevant number of products for the specified charging year;

(b) on and after 15 September 2030, the fee for the relevant number of products set out in Schedule 1 is indexed in accordance with section 6 of this instrument.

Minor products

(3) If the fee is to be worked out for a number of products that consists of 2 or more minor products, or 2 or more minor products and a product that is not a minor product, the relevant number of products for the purposes of subsection (2) is taken to be the number worked out as follows:

(a) on and after 15 September 2026 until 14 September 2027, divide the number of minor products by 10 and round the result up to the nearest whole number;

(b) on and after 15 September 2027, divide the number of minor products by 5 and round the result up to the nearest whole number;

(c) add the result of either paragraph (a) or (b) (as applicable) to the number of products that are not minor products (if any).

###### 6 Indexation

(1) If a provision of this instrument states that the fee is to be indexed in accordance with this section, then at the start of each charging year beginning on or after 15 September 2030, the fee for the relevant number of products set out in Schedule 1 is worked out using the following formula:

[image: Start formula Indexation factor for a charging year times Fee for the previous charging year for the relevant number of products end formula]

(2) The ***indexation factor*** for a charging year is the number worked out using the following formula:

[image: Start formula Consumer price index number for the reference month over Consumer price index number for the base month end fraction times 0.15 plus start fraction Wage price index number for the reference quarter over Wage price index number for the base quarter end fraction times 0.85 end formula]

where:

***base month*** means the December before the reference month.

***base quarter*** means the December quarter before the reference quarter.

***consumer price index number*** means the All Groups Consumer Price Index number for Australia published by the Australian Statistician for that month.

***reference month*** means the December immediately before the charging year.

***reference quarter*** means the December quarter immediately before the charging year.

***wage price index number*** means all sectors Australia Wage Price Index number published by the Australian Statistician for that quarter.

(3) If the fee worked out under subsection (1) is not a multiple of $10, the amount should be rounded to the nearest multiple of $10.

Amending laws are annotated in the legislation history and amendment history.

**Editorial changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* authorises First Parliamentary Counsel to make editorial and presentational changes to a compiled law in preparing a compilation of the law for registration. The changes must not change the effect of the law. Editorial changes take effect from the compilation registration date.

If the compilation includes editorial changes, the endnotes include a brief outline of the changes in general terms. Full details of any changes can be obtained from the Office of Parliamentary Counsel.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

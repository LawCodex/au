# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2013-2014) Determination 2013

**Superannuation (PSS)**

**Maximum Benefits (2013-2014)**

**Determination 2013**

I, STEPHEN PAUL WHITTON, Director, Australian Public Sector Payments and Entitlements, Position No. 44, and Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 6 June 2013

S WHITTON

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

**1 Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2013-2014) Determination 2013*.

**2 Commencement**

This determination shall take effect from and including 1 July 2013.

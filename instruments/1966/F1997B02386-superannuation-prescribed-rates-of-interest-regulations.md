# Superannuation (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Regulations

Statutory Rules 1966 No. 63 as amended

made under the

Superannuation Act 1922

This compilation was prepared on 8 June 2004\
taking into account amendments up to SR 1976 No. 201

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting,\
Attorney-General’s Department, Canberra

###### 1 Citation [see Note 1]

These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Regulations.

###### 2 Commencement [see Note 1]

These Regulations shall come into operation on the date to be fixed by the Treasurer by notice published in the Gazette under subsection (1) of section 69 of the *Superannuation Act 1965*.

###### 3 Prescribed rates of interest

For the purposes of subsection (3) of section 90 of the *Superannuation Act* 1922-1965, the rate specified in the second column of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite to a financial year specified in the first column of that Schedule is the prescribed rate of interest in respect of that financial year.

# Superannuation (CSS) Temporary Employee Approval No. 3

SUPERANNUATION ACT 1976

I, Peter Alexander Walsh, Minister of State for Finance, in pursuance of section 3 of the Superannuation Act 1976, hereby approve the following class of persons for the purposes of the definition of "temporary employee" in section 3 of the Superannuation Act 1976:

The class of persons engaged or appointed as the representatives in another country of the Government of Australia, or as the Australian representatives in another country of Her Majesty, who immediately before their engagement or appointment were eligible employees for the purposes of the Superannuation Act 1976.

Dated this 10th day of July 1985

PETER WALSH

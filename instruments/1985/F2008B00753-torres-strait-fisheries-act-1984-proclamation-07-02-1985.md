# Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984 - Proclamation (07/02/1985)

**PrOCLAMATION**

| commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>N. M. STEPHEN<br>Governor-General | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of<br>the Commonwealth of<br>Australia |
|---|---|

Whereas it is provided by sub-section 15 (1) of the *Torres*\
*Strait Fisheries Act 1984* that the Governor-General may, by Proclamation, declare an area of waters specified in the Proc-\
lamation that is adjacent to the Protected Zone and to the\
south of the line described in Annex 5 to the Torres Strait\
Treaty to be an area outside but near the Protected Zone for\
the purposes of the performance, in the course of commercial\
fishing, of any activity that is included in a class of activities by\
way of commercial fishing specified in the Proclamation:

and whereas it is provided by sub-section 15 (3) of\
that Act that the Governor-General shall not make a Procla-\
mation under sub-section 15 (1) in relation to an area that is\
wholly or partly within the coastal waters of Queensland un-\
less the Governor-General is satisfied that the Minister and\
the Queensland Minister have agreed that the Proclamation\
be made in relation to that area:

AND WHEREAS i am satisfied that the Minister and the Queensland Minister have agreed that this Proclamation be\
made under sub-section 15 (1) in relation to the areas of\
waters specified below:

NOW THEREFORE I, SIR NINIAN MARTIN\
STEPHEN, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of\
Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive\
Council, in pursuance of sub-section 15 (1) of the *Torres*\
*Strait Fisheries Act 1984*, hereby –

(a) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 1 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-\
cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
prawns;

(b) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 2 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-\
cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
tropical rock lobster;

(c) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 3 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-\
cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
Spanish mackerel;

(d) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 4 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-\
cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
dugong;

(e) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 5 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-\
cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
turtles;

(f) declare the area of waters specified in Schedule 6 to be\
an area outside but near the Protected Zone for the\
purposes of the performance, in the course of commer-

cial fishing, of any activity that is included in the class\
of activities that constitute commercial fishing for\
pearl shell; and

(g) declare that in a Schedule to this Proclamation\
"Fisheries Jurisdiction Line" means the line referred to\
in paragraph (a) of the definition of "area of Aus-\
tralian jurisdiction" in sub-section 3 (1) of the *Torres*\
*Strait Fisheries Act 1984.*

SCHEDULE 1

**AREA DECLARED IN RESPECT OF COMMERCIAL FISHING FOR PRAWNS**

The area of waters (excluding any waters within the limits of Queensland) bounded by a line –

(1) commencing at the point of Latitude 10°28'00" South, Longitude 142°09'00" East;

(2) running thence east along the parallel of Latitude\
10°28'00" South to its intersection by the meridian of\
Longitude 144°00'00" East;

(3) thence south along that meridian to its intersection by\
the parallel of Latitude 10°41'17" South;

(4) thence west along that parallel to its intersection by\
the meridian of Longitude 142°31'49" East;

(5) thence south along that meridian to its northernmost intersection by the coastline of Cape York Peninsula at\
low water;

(6) thence generally south-westerly along the western\
coastline of Cape York Peninsula, that is along the\
low water line on that coast and across any river\
mouth, to its intersection by the meridian of Longitude 142°09'00" East;

(7) thence north along that meridian to the point of\
commencement.

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Qualified Employees) Declaration No. 1

Statutory Rules 1990 No. 3221

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister of State for Finance, hereby make the following Declaration under subsection 3F (1) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit Act 1988*.

Dated 6 October 1990.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister of State for Finance

\_________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Qualified Employees) Declaration No. 1.

###### Commencement

**2.** This Declaration takes effect from 1 January 1988.

###### Declared class of persons

**3.** For the purposes of subsection 3F (1) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the members of the staff of the Central Land Council established under section 21 of the *Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976* are a class of persons who are qualified employees.

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 17 October 1990.

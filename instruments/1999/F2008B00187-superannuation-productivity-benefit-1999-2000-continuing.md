###### 1 Name of declaration

This declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (1999-2000 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 1999*.

###### 2 Commencement

This declaration commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988,* the table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 1999, as if the amounts set out in the following table were substituted for the amounts set out in the table in that Schedule:

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $193.28 | $13.53 |
| $193.28 or more but less than $1941.54 | The amount that is 7% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $1941.54 or more | $135.91 |

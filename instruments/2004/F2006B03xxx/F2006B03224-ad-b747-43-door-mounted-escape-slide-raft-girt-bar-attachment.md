# AD/B747/43 - Door Mounted Escape Slide/Raft Girt Bar Attachment Assembly - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/43 Door Mounted Escape Slide/Raft Girt Bar**

**Attachment Assembly - Inspection and Replacement**

**7/85**

Applicability: All models equipped with BF Goodrich (BFG) light weight slide/raft P/Nos 7A1340, 7A1342, 7A1373, 7A1437, 7A1439, 7A1447 or 7A1448.

Requirement: Boeing SB 25A2665 R1. and B.F. Goodrich SB 25-093.

*Note: FAA AD 84-24-04 refers.*

Compliance: As per Requirement Documents.

Background: During an emergency evacuation, the sewn quick disconnect loops separated from the girt bar assembly allowing the slide to separate from the aeroplane.

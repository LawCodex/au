# AD/BAL/10 - Wire Rope Vent and Rip Lines

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

AD/BAL/10 Wire Rope Vent and Rip Lines 7/90

Applicability: All hot air balloons.

Requirement: 1. Gain access to the lower section of the rip and vent control lines.

2\. Examine the lower 7 metres for evidence of wire rope.

3\. If wire rope is found replace affected lines with same diameter kevlar core polyester covered line or other suitable non-conductive alternative.

*Note: Kavanagh Balloons Engineering Order KB / 90 / 03 provides a suitable means of compliance.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1990.

Background: To reduce potential electrical shock hazards to the pilot and passengers, all metal control lines are replaced with non-conductive control lines.

# AD/BAe 146/32 - Centre Section Fuel Tank Coating

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/32 Centre Section Fuel Tank Coating 7/91

Applicability: Model 146 with series and constructors numbers listed in BAe 146 SB 28-18. Requirement: Action in accordance with Mandatory BAe SB 28-18.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement document.

Background: Discrepancies have been found in the fuel proof polymer coating on the centre section fuel tank.

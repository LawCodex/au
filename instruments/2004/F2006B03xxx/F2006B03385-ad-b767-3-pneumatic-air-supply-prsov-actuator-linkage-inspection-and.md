# AD/B767/3 - Pneumatic Air Supply PRSOV Actuator Linkage - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B767/3 Pneumatic Air Supply PRSOV Actuator**

**Linkage - Inspection and Modification**

**7/85**

**TX**

Applicability: All models equipped with Pressure Regulating and shut off Valves Part Nos. 773288- 4 and 773288-5.

Requirement: Boeing Alert Service Bulletin 767-36A0015. Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer has reported an incident where a failure of the PRSOV caused the pneumatic ducting to be subjected to high pressure air. Considerable damage resulted and jamming of the flap and breakage of the throttle control occurred.

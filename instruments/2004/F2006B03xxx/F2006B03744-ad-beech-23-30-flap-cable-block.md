# AD/BEECH 23/30 - Flap Cable Block

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 19, 23 and 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 23/30 Flap Cable Block 7/76

Applicability: All models B19 with S/Nos. MB-732 to MB-788; C23 with S/Nos. M-1600 to M- 1769; B24R with S/Nos. MC-305 to MC-398; which are equipped with mechanical flaps.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0831-161.

*Note: FAA AD 76-10-09 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 July 1976.

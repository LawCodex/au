# AD/B767/35 - Entry/Service Door Acoustic Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/35 Entry/Service Door Acoustic Seal 4/90

Applicability: Model 767 Line Position 1 through 244 and Line Position 246 through 262. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 767-25-0123 R1.

*Note: FAA AD 90-01-05 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 15 June 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Migration of acoustic seal may result in incomplete arming of emergency evacuation slide. This must be armed for correct slide deployment during an emergency.

# AD/BAe 146/57 - MLG Direction Link Sub-Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/57 MLG Direction Link Sub-Assembly 13/95

Applicability: All Series BAe 146 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Avro International Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 32-143.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, as specified in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 7 December 1995.

Background: The manufacturer received an operator report of very heavy corrosion on the threads of the direction link tube and eye ends, which led to failure of the direction link sub- assembly.

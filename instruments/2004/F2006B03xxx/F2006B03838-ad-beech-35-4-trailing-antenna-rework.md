# AD/BEECH 35/4 - Trailing Antenna Rework

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/4 Trailing Antenna Rework NK

Applicability: Model 35 and A35 equipped with an automatic reel trailing antenna. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SB Model 35 - No. 13; Model A35 - No. 4.

*Note: FAA AD 49-26-1 refers.*

Compliance: Forthwith.

# AD/BAe 146/3 - Evacuation Slide Cover Assembly Latch Tension

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/3 Evacuation Slide Cover Assembly Latch Tension 4/86

Applicability: All Series 100, 100A, 200 and 200A listed in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 146 S.B. 25-58-35069A.

Compliance: Prior to 31 May 1986.

Background: A case has occurred where excessive force was necessary to release the slide cover and this interfered with the opening of the door.

# AD/B747/91 - EFIS Control Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/91 EFIS Control Panel 6/91

Applicability: All model 747-400 series with line numbers 1 through 791.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-06-17.

*Note: Collins Service Bulletin No DCP-7000-31-04 also refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Country of Origin AD to prevent failure of either pilots EFIS control panel, with resultant smoke in the flight deck.

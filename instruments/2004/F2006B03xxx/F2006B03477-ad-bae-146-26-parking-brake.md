# AD/BAe 146/26 - Parking Brake

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/26 Parking Brake 3/91** Applicability: Model 146 with Constructor’s numbers listed in BAe SB 32-104-01159A. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Mandatory SB 32-104-01159A.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive is raised to introduce a modification that installs a special washer in the park brake selector assembly. This prevents the cable tensioning spring in the selector assembly from inadvertently selecting the park brake.

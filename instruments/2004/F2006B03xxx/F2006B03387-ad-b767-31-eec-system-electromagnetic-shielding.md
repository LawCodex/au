# AD/B767/31 - EEC System Electromagnetic Shielding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/31 EEC System Electromagnetic Shielding 1/90

Applicability: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 767-71-0041 R2 or later revision.

*Note: FAA AD 89-21-04 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 25 April 1991.

Background: Boeing have advised that at the maximum probable lightning threat level, lightning induced voltage transients in excess of the EEC qualification requirement may result. A lightning strike at take-off could therefore adversely affect EEC operation and could result in uncommanded thrust loss greater than the loss of one engine.

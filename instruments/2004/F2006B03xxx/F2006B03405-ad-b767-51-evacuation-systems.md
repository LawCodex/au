# AD/B767/51 - Evacuation Systems

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/51 Evacuation Systems 2/91

Applicability: Model 767 equipped with BFGoodrich escape slides or slide/rafts identified in BFGoodrich SB 25-212.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-22-04 Amdt 39- 6776.

*Note: BFGoodrich SB 25-212 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with revised effective date of 21 February 1991.

Background: Country of origin AD. To prevent evacuation system malfunctions which may jeopardize successful emergency evacuation of the aircraft.

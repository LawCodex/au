# AD/BEA 121/20 - Throttle Cable Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/20 Throttle Cable Assembly 4/78

Applicability: All model B121 Series 2 and 3.

Requirement: Replace throttle cable assembly P/N BE.47.20017 with P/N BE.47.20037 for Series 2 aircraft and P/N BE.47.30019/1 with P/N BE.47.30025 for Series 3 aircraft.

*Note: Scottish Aviation SB No. B121/74 also refers.*

Compliance: Before 31 May 1978.

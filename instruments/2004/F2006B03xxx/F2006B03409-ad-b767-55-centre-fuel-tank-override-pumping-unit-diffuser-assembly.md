# AD/B767/55 - Centre Fuel Tank Override Pumping Unit Diffuser Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B767/55 Centre Fuel Tank Override Pumping**

**Unit Diffuser Assembly**

Applicability: Model 767 line number 1 through 359, 361 and 362. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 767-28A0036.

*Note: Sundstrand Alert SB 5006286-28-A4 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 May 1991.

**7/91**

**TX**

Background: On certain S/N override pumping units the brazed joint attaching the diffuser ring to the diffuser sleeve may separate. If this occurred during a dry pump operation an ignition source may occur within the centre tank.

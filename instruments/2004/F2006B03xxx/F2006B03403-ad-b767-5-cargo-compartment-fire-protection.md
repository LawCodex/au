# AD/B767/5 - Cargo Compartment Fire Protection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/5 Cargo Compartment Fire Protection 9/85

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify and test the environmental control system according to the requirements detailed in Boeing SB 767-21-0041 latest revision.

*Note: FAA AD 85-13-02 Amdt 39-5085 refers.*

Compliance: Before 31 December 1985.

Background: The rework is necessary to assure the effectiveness of the cargo compartment fire protection system.

# AD/BEECH 200/10 - Wing Deicer Lines

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 200/10 Wing Deicer Lines 8/76

Applicability: Model 200 with S/Nos BB-3 to BB-41.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0822-193. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1976.

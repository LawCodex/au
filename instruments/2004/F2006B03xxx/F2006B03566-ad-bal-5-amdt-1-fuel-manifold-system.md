# AD/BAL/5 Amdt 1 - Fuel Manifold System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

**AD/BAL/5**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuel Manifold System 6/86**

Applicability: All balloons in Aerial work and Charter operations which are not fitted with dual blast valves or burners.

Requirement: LPG cylinders used for flight shall be connected to a manifold system approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Prior to issue of a certificate of airworthiness.

Background: This Directive has been amended to delete those balloons fitted with dual blast valves or burners.

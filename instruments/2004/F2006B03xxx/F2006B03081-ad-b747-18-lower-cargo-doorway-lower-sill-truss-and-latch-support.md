# AD/B747/18 - Lower Cargo Doorway Lower Sill Truss and Latch Support Fitting - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/18 Lower Cargo Doorway Lower Sill Truss**

**and Latch Support Fitting - Inspection**

**11/79**

Applicability: All models.

Document: 1. FAA AD 79-17-02, Amdt. 39-3526;

2\. (Boeing SB 747-53-2200 refers). Compliance: As per requirement document No. 1.

Background: Overseas it has been found that the 7079-T6 truss and related 7079-T6 latch support fittings on the lower sill of forward and lower cargo doorways can exhibit corrosion, cracking and blocked drainage paths.

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

**Amdt 1**

**Left Cabin Window Forward or Rear Cabin Door 12/78**

Applicability: All models 58P & 58PA with S/Nos. TJ-2 to TJ-58. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft No. 0828-110, Rev.1.

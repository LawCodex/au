# AD/BEECH 33/16 - Fuel System Placards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 33/16 Fuel System Placards 7/73

Applicability: Models 35-33, 35-A33, 35-B33, 35-C33, E-33, F33, G33 with S/Nos. CD1 to CD- 1276.

Models 35-C33A, E33A, F33A with S/Nos. CE-1 to CE-366. Models E33C, F33C with S/Nos. CJ-1 to CJ-30.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI 0491-281 or 0493-281 as applicable. Tank inspections are to be performed on all aircraft with baffled fuel cells unless previously inspected in accordance with AD/BEECH 33/7.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1973.

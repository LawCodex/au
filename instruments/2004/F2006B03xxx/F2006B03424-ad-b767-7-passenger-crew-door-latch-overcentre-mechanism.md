# AD/B767/7 - Passenger/Crew Door Latch Overcentre Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/7 Passenger/Crew Door Latch Overcentre Mechanism 4/86

DM

Applicability: All model 767 with line position 2 to 121 and 124 to 127. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 767-52-0041.

*Note: FAA AD 86-02-01 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1986.

Background: Boeing Co have reported that a significant percentage of self locking nuts P/N BACN10JC6CM in stock do not meet specified locking torque requirements.

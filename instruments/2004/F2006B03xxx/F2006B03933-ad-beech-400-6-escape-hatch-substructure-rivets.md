# AD/BEECH 400/6 - Escape Hatch Substructure Rivets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 400 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 400/6 Escape Hatch Substructure Rivets 4/94

Applicability: Model 400A aircraft with serial numbers RK-1 through RK-57, RK-59, RK-60, and RK-62 through RK-66.

Model 400T aircraft with serial numbers TT-02 through TT-30, and TT-32.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory Service Bulletin 2509.

*Note: FAA AD 93-25-15 Amdt 39-8779 refers.*

Compliance: Within 200 hours’ time in service after 31 March 1994.

Background: The FAA received reports that, during production, incorrect rivets may have been installed in the escape hatch substructure.

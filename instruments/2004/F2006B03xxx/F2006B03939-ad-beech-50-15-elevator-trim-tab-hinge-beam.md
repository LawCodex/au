# AD/BEECH 50/15 - Elevator Trim Tab Hinge Beam

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 50 (Twin Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 50/15 Elevator Trim Tab Hinge Beam 12/65

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Visually inspect the elevator trim tab hinge beam (P/N 50/610000/94) for cracking in the flange and web.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 250 hours’ time in service.

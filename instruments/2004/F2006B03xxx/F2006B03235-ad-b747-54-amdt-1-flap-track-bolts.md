# AD/B747/54 Amdt 1 - Flap Track Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/54**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Track Bolts 13/87**

Applicability: All models with line numbers 001 to 632, except 747SP aeroplanes. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 747-57A2234 R1.

*Note: FAA AD 86-18-06 refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Background: This Amendment revises the Compliance statement to incorporate a 5000 flight cycle inspection threshold in accordance with the manufacturers SB.

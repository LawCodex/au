# AD/BAe 146/68 - Eye Bolt and Piston Rod Threads of MLG Retraction Actuator

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/68 Eye Bolt and Piston Rod Threads**

**of MLG Retraction Actuator**

**13/97**

Applicability: All BAe 146 and Avro 146-RJ Series aircraft, fitted with main landing gear actuators Part/Type No. 104628002, 104628003 (as listed in Dowty Aerospace Hydraulics- Cheltenham Service Bulletin 146-32-507).

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 32-145. Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 4 December 1997.

Background: Instances of corrosion of the eyebolt and piston rod threads on the retraction actuator has occurred on in-service aircraft main landing gears.

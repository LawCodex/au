# AD/BEECH 35/46 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/46 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly 4/75

Applicability: Models 35, A35, B35, C35, D35, E35, F35, G35, 35R, H35, J35, K35, M35, N35,

P35, S35, V35, N35A, V35B, which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA2653WE (Installation of engine crankcase breather and vacuum pump air-oil separator).

Requirement: 1. For Beech Models 35, A35, B35, C35, D35, E35, F35, G35 and 35R, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N B-1119-1 and install either P/N B-1119-M or P/N B- 1119-RM.

2\. For Beech Models S35, V35, V35A and V35B, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N B-1116-1 and install either P/N B-1116-M or P/N B-1116-RM.

3\. For Beech Models H35, J35, K35, M35, N35 and P35, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N B-1114-1 and install either P/N B-1114-M or P/N

B-1114-RM.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1974.

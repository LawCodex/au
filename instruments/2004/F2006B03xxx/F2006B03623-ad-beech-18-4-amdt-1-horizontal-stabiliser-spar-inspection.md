# AD/BEECH 18/4 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Spar - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 18 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 18/4**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Spar - Inspection NK**

Applicability: Model C-18S, D-18S and AT-11, not modified to Beech SB D-18-48.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the horizontal stabiliser front spar and sub-spar for cracks at the points of attachment to the fuselage.

2\. Remove the stabiliser and stabiliser lower skin panel and inspect for cracks in the front spar and sub-spar.

3\. If cracking has occurred modify the stabiliser attachment in accordance with Beechcraft Service Bulletin No. D-18-48 or to the satisfaction of the Secretary before further flight.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - At intervals not exceeding 10 hours time in service.

For Para. 2 - At intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service from the last inspection.

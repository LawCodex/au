# AD/B767/49 - Collins Flight Control Computers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/49 Collins Flight Control Computers 2/91

Applicability: All models fitted with Collins flight control computers.

Requirement: Inspect and/or modify as appropriate in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-11-10 (Amdt 39-6608).

Compliance: Part A. Prior to 31 December 1992.

Part B. Prior to 31 December 1991.

Background: To prevent intermittent altitude hold, altitude capture, and/or flight director mistracking anomalies.

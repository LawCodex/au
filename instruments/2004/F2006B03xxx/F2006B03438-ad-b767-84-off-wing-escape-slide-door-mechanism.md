# AD/B767/84 - Off-Wing Escape Slide Door Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/84 Off-Wing Escape Slide Door Mechanism 11/95

Applicability: Model 767 series aircraft equipped with off-wing escape slides.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 95-08-11 Amdt 39- 9200.

*Note: Boeing Service Bulletin 767-25-0216 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 12 October 1995.

Background: The FAA received reports that indicated that the requirements of a previously issued FAA AD did not adequately preclude leakage from the door opening actuators. This Directive requires replacement of the currently installed door opening actuators of the off-wing escape system with new, improved actuators.

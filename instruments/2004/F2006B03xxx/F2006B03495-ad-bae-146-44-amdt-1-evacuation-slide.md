# AD/BAe 146/44 Amdt 1 - Evacuation Slide

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/44**

**Amdt 1**

**Evacuation Slide 1/96**

Applicability: All Series BAe 146 and Avro 146-RJ aircraft equipped with Air Cruisers Evacuation Slide Part No. D31005-Series and Slide Serial Numbers as listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Inspect and modify the evacuation slide in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 25-328 Rev. 3.

*Note: Air Cruisers SB 201-25-17 Rev. 2 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document, with a revised modification compliance date of 4 April 1996.

Background: The Evacuation Slide manufacturer informed BAe that a batch of inflation valves installed to certain Evacuation Slides, may fail to deploy the slide automatically on demand. This amendment incorporates a modification to the inflation valves.

# AD/B747/66 Amdt 2 - Lower Lobe Cargo Door Locking System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/66**

**Amdt 2**

**Lower Lobe Cargo Door Locking System 3/91**

Applicability: Model 747 series line number 001 and subsequent.

Requirement: Unless already accomplished; action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-09-06 Amdt 39-6581.

*Note: Boeing SB’s 747-52A2206 and 747-52A2209 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: A lower lobe forward cargo door partially opened in flight due to damaged lock sectors. Amendment 1 was raised in response to the FAA decision, following a second occurrence, to accelerate the compliance time previously required by AD 88- 12-04, and for installation of certain modifications as well as additional inspections and tests in the interim.

This amendment is raised to reflect an additional requirement of FAA Amdt 39-6581, AD 90-09-02; which supersedes Amdt 39-6166, AD 89-05-54.

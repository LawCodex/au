# AD/BEA 121/30 - Brake Torque Tube Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/30 Brake Torque Tube Assembly 4/96

Applicability: Beagle B121 Series 1, 2, and 3 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Aircraft Mandatory Service Bulletin B121/103.

*Note: UK CAA AD 003-10-95 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Reports have been received of cracked brake torque tube assemblies on Bulldog aircraft. The brake torque tube assemblies installed on B121 aircraft, are of similar construction.

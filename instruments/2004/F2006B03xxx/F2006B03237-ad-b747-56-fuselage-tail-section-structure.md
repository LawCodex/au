# AD/B747/56 - Fuselage - Tail Section Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/56 Fuselage - Tail Section Structure 2/87

Applicability: All models with line numbers 001 to 554. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 747-53-2220 R2.

*Note: FAA AD 86-16-05 refers.*

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances of in-flight cabin pressure loss due to aft pressure bulkhead corrosion or deterioration of the composite Auxiliary Power Unit (APU) duct pressure pan.

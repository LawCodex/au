# AD/BAe 146/28 - Main Landing Gear Lock Jack

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/28 Main Landing Gear Lock Jack 3/91

Applicability: All BAe 146 aircraft which have an Uplock Jack part number 104275001 installed with the serial number listed in Dowty Rotol SB 146-32-93.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dowty Rotol Mandatory SB 146-32-93 Rev 1.

*Note: BAe146 SB 32-103 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: A batch of lock jack assemblies have been manufactured with a forging defect in the bodies. A total of forty one units have been identified as being manufactured from the defective material.

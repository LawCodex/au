# AD/BEECH 400/1 - Aft Fuel Tank Vent

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 400 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 400/1 Aft Fuel Tank Vent 12/92 DM

Applicability: Model 400A serial number RK-1 to RK-28 inclusive.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with the Accomplishment Instructions contained in Beechcraft mandatory service bulletin No 2445.

*Note: FAA AD 92-16-14 amdt 39-8323 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 25 September 1992.

Background: To prevent a potential fire or explosive situation a one time inspection of the aft fuselage vent system to ensure aluminium honeycomb material is installed, is required.

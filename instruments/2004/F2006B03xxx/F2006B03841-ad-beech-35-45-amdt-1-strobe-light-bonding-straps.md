# AD/BEECH 35/45 Amdt 1 - Strobe Light Bonding Straps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 35/45**

**Amdt 1**

**Strobe Light Bonding Straps 1/75**

Applicability: All models V35B with S/No. D-9468 thru D-9692 fitted with Grimes P/N 30-0467-1 or Grimes P/N 30-0467-3 Strobe Light Assemblies.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0655-362 Rev. 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1975.

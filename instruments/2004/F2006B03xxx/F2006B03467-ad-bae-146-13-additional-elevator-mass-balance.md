# AD/BAe 146/13 - Additional Elevator Mass Balance

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/13 Additional Elevator Mass Balance 8/89

DM

Applicability: Series 100, 100A, 200 and 200A with serial numbers listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe S.B. 55-7-00955D. Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1989.

Background: This modification introduces additional mass balance forward of the elevator hinge line to assist in damping control column oscillations.

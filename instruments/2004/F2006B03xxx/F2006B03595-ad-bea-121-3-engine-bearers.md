# AD/BEA 121/3 - Engine Bearers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/3 Engine Bearers 1/70

Applicability: All Series 2 and 3 models prior to S/No. B121/107 which have 300 hours time in service.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beagle SB B121/11 (Mod. BE349). Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service from 31 January 1970.

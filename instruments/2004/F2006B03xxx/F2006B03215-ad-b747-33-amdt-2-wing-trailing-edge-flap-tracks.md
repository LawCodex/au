# AD/B747/33 Amdt 2 - Wing Trailing Edge Flap Tracks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/33**

**Amdt 2**

**Wing Trailing Edge Flap Tracks 8/90**

Applicability: Model B747 line numbers 1 through 584 except B747SP. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB747-57A2225 Rev. 4.

*Note: FAA AD 83-21-02 refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Background: Water accumulation in the track aft cavity has resulted in corrosion and consequent aft web cracking. Amendment 1 references the latest revision of the Requirement document, which adds terminating action. Amendment 2 clarifies aircraft affected.

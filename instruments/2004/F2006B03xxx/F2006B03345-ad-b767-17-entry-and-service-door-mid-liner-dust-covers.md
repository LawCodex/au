# AD/B767/17 - Entry and Service door Mid Liner Dust Covers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/17 Entry and Service door Mid Liner Dust Covers 3/88

Applicability: All model 767 listed in the Requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 767-25A0092.

*Note: FAA AD 87-25-09 refers.*

Compliance: Inspect at intervals not to exceed 90 days until modified. Modify prior to 19 June 1989.

Background: A loose inner window (dust cover) interfered with the movement of the upper liner and prevented the door from opening fully.

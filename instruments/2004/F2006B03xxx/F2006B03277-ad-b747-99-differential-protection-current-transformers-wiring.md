# AD/B747/99 - Differential Protection Current Transformers - Wiring

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B747/99 Differential Protection Current Transformers - Wiring

Applicability: As specified in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Boeing Service Bulletin 747-24-2154.

*Note: FAA AD 91-18-05 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 18 March 1992.

## 12/91

Background: This AD is issued following a 747 Safety Assessment which demonstrated that the potential exists for a single fault to cause the loss of all normal sources of aircraft power. Compliance with this AD requires re-routing and adding shielded wiring associated with the differential protection current transformers in the P6 panel.

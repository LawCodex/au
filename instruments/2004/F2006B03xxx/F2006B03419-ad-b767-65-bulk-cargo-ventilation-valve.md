# AD/B767/65 - Bulk Cargo Ventilation Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/65 Bulk Cargo Ventilation Valve 5/92

Applicability: Model 767 Series as listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Remove the bulk cargo ventilation flapper valve in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 767-21A0098.

*Note: FAA AD 92-02-12 Amdt 39-8148 refers.*

Compliance: Within 4000 Hours Time in Service after 2 March 1992.

Background: The FAA has received a report of a cargo compartment flapper valve that did not close during a functional check of the bulk cargo compartment fire extinguishing system. This condition could result in loss of the extinguishing agent through an open flapper valve.

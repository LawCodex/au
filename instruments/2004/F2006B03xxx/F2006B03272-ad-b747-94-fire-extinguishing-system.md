# AD/B747/94 - Fire Extinguishing System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/94 Fire Extinguishing System 6/91

Applicability: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-05-04. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: This AD requires modification of the fire extinguishing system in order to preclude the possibility of improper connection of the system wiring during maintenance.

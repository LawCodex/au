# AD/BEECH 33/25 - Tachometer Drive Cable Housing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 33/25 Tachometer Drive Cable Housing 4/76

Applicability: Models 35-B33, 35-C33, E33, F33 and G33 serials CD-388 thru CD-981 and CD-983 thru CD1304.

[image]\
Beagle B206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 206/4 Main and Nose Undercarriage Legs 11/68

Applicability: Model B206 Series 1 and Series 2.

Compliance: Inspect within 25 hours time in service after 13 January 1969. Modify within 200 hours time in service after 13 January 1969.

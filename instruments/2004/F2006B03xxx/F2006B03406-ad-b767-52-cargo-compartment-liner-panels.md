# AD/B767/52 - Cargo Compartment Liner Panels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/52 Cargo Compartment Liner Panels 3/91

Applicability: Model 767 series; equipped with class “C” lower lobe cargo compartments that do not have complete cargo compartment floors.

Requirement: Action IAW the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-25-08 Amdt 39-6816. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: An FAA investigation into the service history of cargo compartment fires and full- scale fire testing, revealed that, without additional cargo liner panels, fire could migrate up behind the cargo liner and cause major structural damage.

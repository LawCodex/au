# AD/BEECH 200/8 - Seat Locking Pins

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 200/8 Seat Locking Pins 5/76

Applicability: All model 200 with S/Nos BB-6 to BB-87, BB-89 to BB-99 and BB-101 to BB-106; All model A200 with S/Nos BC-1 to BC-8 and BD-1 to BD-8.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0789-314.

*Note: FAA AD 76-04-02 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1976.

# AD/BEECH 200/63 - Landing Gear Hand Pump - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 200/63 Landing Gear Hand Pump - Modification 9/99

Applicability: All B200, B200C, B200T, B200CT and A200CT model airplanes with serial numbers specified in Raytheon Service Bulletin SB 32-3073 Revision 1, dated July 1998.

Requirement: Install a filter element in the landing gear hand pump suction line in accordance with Raytheon Mandatory Service Bulletin SB 32-3073 Revision 1, dated July 1998.

*Note: US FAA AD 99-09-10 Amdt 39-11143 dated 26 April 1999 refers.*

Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service (TIS), but no later than two years, from the effective date of this Directive.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 9 September 1999.

Background: Experience has shown that debris can get into the landing gear hand pump system and prevent the successful operation of the system. This Directive introduces a modification to filter the suction line and prevent debris from migrating further into the system.

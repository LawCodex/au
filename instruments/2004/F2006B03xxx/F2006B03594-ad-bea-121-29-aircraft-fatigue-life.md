# AD/BEA 121/29 - Aircraft Fatigue Life

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/29 Aircraft Fatigue Life 9/95

Applicability: B121 Pup, all models.

Requirement: Retire the airframe from service before exceeding 17900 fatigue hours. Fatigue hours shall be calculated in accordance with Jetstream Alert Service Bulletin B121/101.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 014-03-95 refers.*

Compliance: Effective 17 August 1995.

Background: The Beagle Pup has been allocated a mandatory fatigue life by the United Kingdom Civil Aviation Authority.

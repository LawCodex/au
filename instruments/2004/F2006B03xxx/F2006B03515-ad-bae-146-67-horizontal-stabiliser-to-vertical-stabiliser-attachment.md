# AD/BAe 146/67 - Horizontal Stabiliser to Vertical Stabiliser Attachment Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BAe 146/67 Horizontal Stabiliser to Vertical Stabiliser Attachment Brackets

Applicability: All series BAe 146 aircraft.

All Avro 146-RJ70 aircraft up to E1267. All Avro 146-RJ85 aircraft up to E2300. All Avro 146-RJ100 aircraft up to E3301.

## 8/97

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 55-15.

*Note 1: UK CAA AD 001-04-97 refers.*

*Note 2: Maintenance Review Board Report refers.*

Compliance: At the thresholds detailed in the requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 17 July 1997.

Background: Reports were received of corrosion on the inner faces of the horizontal stabiliser to vertical stabiliser attachment fitting, and on the adjoining vertical stabiliser lugs.

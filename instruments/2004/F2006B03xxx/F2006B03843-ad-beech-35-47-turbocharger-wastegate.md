# AD/BEECH 35/47 - Turbocharger Wastegate

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/47 Turbocharger Wastegate 6/75

Applicability: All model V35TC, V35A-TC and V35B-TC with S/Nos. as listed in Beechcraft SI No. 0710-245, also all model S35, V35, V35A and V35B fitted with Continental TSIO-520-D engines in accordance with STC SA 1035 WE or Beech Drawing No. 35-010023.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0710-245.

*Note: FAA AD 75-01-07 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1975.

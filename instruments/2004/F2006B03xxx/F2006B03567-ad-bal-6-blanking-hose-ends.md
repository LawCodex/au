# AD/BAL/6 - Blanking Hose Ends

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

AD/BAL/6 Blanking Hose Ends 5/86 DM

Applicability: All balloons.

Requirement: Any hose disconnected from a system shall have a pressure seal blank fitting installed on the unattached end.

Compliance: Any time one end of a hose is disconnected and the other end remains connected to a source of pressurized LPG.

Background: Some operations are conducted with one or more cylinders of a manifold system removed. In these cases operators rely on the seals of the hose end fitting disconnect check valve to retain pressurized LPG.

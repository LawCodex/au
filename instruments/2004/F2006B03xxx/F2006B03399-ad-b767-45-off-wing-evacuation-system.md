# AD/B767/45 - Off-Wing Evacuation System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/45 Off-Wing Evacuation System 11/90

Applicability: Model 767 series with manufacturers variable numbers VB051 through VB055, VE061 through VE063, VE066 through VE069, and VN081 through VN 084.

Requirement: 1. Unless already accomplished, modify the off-wing evacuation system in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 767-25-0120.

2\. Unless already accomplished, modify the off-wing evacuation system by replacing the escape system actuator in accordance with Boeing Alert Service Bulletin 767- 25A 0131 or Boeing Service Bulletin 767-25-0011 Revision 2 or Revision 3.

*Note: FAA AD 90-15-05 Amdt 39-6654 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 January 1992.

Background: This AD is issued in response to reports of failures of the evacuation system due to installation errors and equipment malfunctions.

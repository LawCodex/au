# AD/BEECH 55/46 - Current Limiter Terminal Block Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 55/46 Current Limiter Terminal Block Assemblies 10/76

Applicability: All models E55 & E55A S/Nos. TE-969 thru TE-1009, TE-1011 thru TE-1021, TE- 1023 thru TE-1029, TE-1032 thru TE-1034; 58 and 58A S/Nos. TH-441 thru TH-482, TH-484 thru TH-541, TH-543 thru TH-547, TH-549 thru TH-555, TH-557 thru TH- 595, TH-597 thru TH-602, TH-604, TH-605, TH-607 and TH-609.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0754-350.

*Note: FAA AD 76-13-05 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 October 1976.

# AD/B747/120 Amdt 1 - Lower Lobe Cargo Doors and Main Deck Side Cargo Door

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/120**

**Amdt 1**

**Lower Lobe Cargo Doors and Main Deck Side Cargo Door**

**8/93**

**DM**

Applicability: Model 747 series; as listed in Boeing SB 747-52-2186, Revision 4, and Boeing ASB 747-52A2233.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 93-10-01 Amendment 39-8580.

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

The compliance of the initial issue of this Directive remains unchanged. This Amendment is effective from 10 June 1993.

Background: The FAA has received numerous reports of corroded or cracked fittings, cam latch bellcranks, and cam latches.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a final rule correction of FAA AD 93-06-03 which clarifies the compliance time for accomplishing optional repetitive inspections of the cam latches.

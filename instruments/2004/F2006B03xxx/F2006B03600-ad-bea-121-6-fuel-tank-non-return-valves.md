# AD/BEA 121/6 - Fuel Tank Non-Return Valves

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/6 Fuel Tank Non-Return Valves 2/71

Applicability: All Series 1, 2 and 3 aircraft which do not have non-return valve P/N 703-AA-25 fitted to each fuel cell outlet connection.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beagle SB B121/21. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 26 February 1971.

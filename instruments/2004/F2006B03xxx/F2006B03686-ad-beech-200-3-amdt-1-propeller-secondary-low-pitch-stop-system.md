# AD/BEECH 200/3 Amdt 1 - Propeller Secondary Low Pitch Stop System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 200/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Propeller Secondary Low Pitch Stop System 5/77**

Applicability: Models 200, S/Nos BB-2, BB-6 to BB-162.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0808-247, Rev 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1977.

*Note: Flight Manual amendment is required following incorporation of this modification.*

# AD/B747/42 - Evacuation Slides/Rafts - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/42 Evacuation Slides/Rafts - Modification 6/85

Applicability: All models equipped with B.F. Goodrich evacuation slides and slide/rafts, part numbers as specified in B.F. Goodrich Service Bulletins listed below.

Requirement: B.F. Goodrich S.B. 25-090 R.1 B.F. Goodrich S.B. 25-084 B.F. Goodrich S.B. 25- 088 R.1

*Note: FAA AD 85-02-01 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1986.

Background: Evacuation slides/rafts have been subject to malfunction or non-inflation to an unacceptable level. The modifications contained in this directive should restore successful deployment rates.

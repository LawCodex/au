# AD/B747/27 - Escape Slide Flapper Valve - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/27 Escape Slide Flapper Valve - Modification 2/81

Applicability: All models 747 with B.F. Goodrich escape slides and turbo fans identified in B.F. Goodrich SB No. 25-060 dated 14 July 1980.

Documents: 1. FAA AD 80-20-02, Amendment 39-3928; or

2\. Boeing SB 747-25-2543; OR 3. B.F. Goodrich SB 25-060.

Compliance: Before 1 January 1981.

Background: Some escape slides on overseas aeroplanes have failed to inflate correctly during emergency test inflation.

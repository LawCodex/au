# AD/BEA 121/17 - Nosewheel Steering Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/17 Nosewheel Steering Mechanism 1/77

Applicability: All model B121 not incorporating Mod. No. BE314. Requirement: Action in accordance with Scottish Aviation SB No. B121/69.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

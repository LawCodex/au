# AD/B767/69 Amdt 1 - Engine Bleed Air Crossover Duct

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B767/69**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Bleed Air Crossover Duct 13/92 DM**

Applicability: All Boeing 767 series aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Unless previously carried out, action IAW Boeing ASB 767-36A0041. Compliance: Prior to 1 April 1993 or accumulation of 7,000 flight cycles, whichever occurs later.

*Note: Aircraft not involved in ETOPS operations may be eligible for an extension to the compliance requirements.*

Background: Due to a rupture of a crossover bleed air duct loss of aircraft pressurisation, bled air to the air driven hydraulic pump, wing thermal anti-icing and also, damage to the air conditioning access panels occurred. Failure of the pressurisation may force the aircraft to descend into icing conditions with no wing anti-icing capabilities.

Amendment 1 changed Compliance by replacing 31 January 1993 with 1 April 1993 and replacing the words ‘occurs first’ with ‘occurs later’.

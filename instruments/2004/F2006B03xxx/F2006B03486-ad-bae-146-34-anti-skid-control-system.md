# AD/BAe 146/34 - Anti-Skid Control System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/34 Anti-Skid Control System 10/91

Applicability: As specified in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 32-124-70491A&B.

*Note: Compliance with this SB is classified MANDATORY by the UK CAA, and*

*includes mandatory modification of Anti Skid Control Boxes held as spares.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1992.

Background: A loss of normal braking has been reported which has been attributed to incorrect operation of the Anti Skid Control Box which may result in a single fault causing failure of the normal breaking system.

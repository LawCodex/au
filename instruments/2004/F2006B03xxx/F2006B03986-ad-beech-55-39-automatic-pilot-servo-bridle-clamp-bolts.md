# AD/BEECH 55/39 - Automatic Pilot Servo Bridle Clamp Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 55/39 Automatic Pilot Servo Bridle Clamp Bolts 1/74

Applicability: Models 95-55, 95-A55, 95-B55, 95-B55A, Serial TC1 to TC1573, 95-C55, 95-C55A,

D55, D55A, E55, E55A Serial TE-1 to TE-919 and TC-350 equipped with Edo-Aire Mitchell AK292 Automatic pilot.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0601-180. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1974.

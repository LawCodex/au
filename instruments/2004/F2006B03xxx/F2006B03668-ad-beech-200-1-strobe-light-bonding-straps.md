# AD/BEECH 200/1 - Strobe Light Bonding Straps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 200/1 Strobe Light Bonding Straps 1/75

Applicability: All model 200 with S/Nos BB-1 thru BB-14, BB-16 thru BB-21,and BB-29 fitted with Grimes P/N 30-0467-1 or Grimes P/N 30-0467-3 Strobe Light Assemblies.

Document: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0655-362, Rev 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1975.

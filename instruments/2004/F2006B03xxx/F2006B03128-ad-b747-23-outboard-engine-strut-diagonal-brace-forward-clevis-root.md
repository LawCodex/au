# AD/B747/23 - Outboard Engine Strut Diagonal Brace Forward Clevis Root - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B747/23 Outboard Engine Strut Diagonal Brace Forward

**Clevis Root - Inspection and Modification**

Applicability: All model 747 equipped with CF6, JT9D-3 or JT9D-7 engines. Document: 1. FAA AD 80-08-02, Amdt. 39-3738; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 747-54-2070.

Compliance: As per requirement document No. 1.

## 7/80

Background: An overseas operator has detected transverse cracks in clevis root of brace. Defect is attributed to local thickness being less than specified.

# AD/BEECH 55/45 Amdt 1 - Wing Strobe Lights

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 55/45**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Strobe Lights 3/77**

Applicability: All models 95-B55 & 95-B55A, S/Nos. TC-1299 thru TC-1525; E55 & E55A S/Nos. TE-768 thru TE-903; 58 & 58A S/Nos. TH-1 thru TH-302; that have either Hoskins P/N 30-0187-21 or Grimes P/N 30-0669-1 wing strobe lights installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0800-362. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 March 1977.

*Note: Aircraft complying with the original issue of this Directive satisfy this issue of the Directive.*

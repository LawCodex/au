# AD/BEA 121/15 - Flap Actuating Lever Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/15 Flap Actuating Lever Assemblies 4/75

Applicability: All series 1, 2 and 3 aeroplanes.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Scottish Aviation SB No. B121/61.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 19 May 1975 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

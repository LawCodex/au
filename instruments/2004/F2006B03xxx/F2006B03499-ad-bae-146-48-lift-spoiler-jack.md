# AD/BAe 146/48 - Lift Spoiler Jack

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/48 Lift Spoiler Jack 2/94

Applicability: BAe 146 series aircraft fitted with either lift spoiler jack P/N P308-45-0002 or P308- 45-0102.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Inspection Service Bulletin 27-137.

*Note: Both UK CAA AD 005-11-92 and FAA AD 93-21-07 amdt 39-8721 refer.*

Compliance: At the next schedule maintenance check of the lift spoilers configuration warning microswitches or by 31 December 1994, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that the lift spoiler unlocked indication system may suffer from a dormant failure.

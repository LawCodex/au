# AD/BEECH 50/18 Amdt 3 - Elevator Control Rod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 50 (Twin Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 50/18**

**Amdt 3**

**Elevator Control Rod 12/78**

Applicability: All model 50 with S/Nos. H-1 to H-11;

All models B50 and C50 with S/Nos. CH-12 to CH-360;

All models D50, D50A, D50B, D50C and D50E with S/Nos. DH-1 to DH-347; All model E50 with S/Nos. EH-1 to EH-70;

All model F50 with S/Nos. FH-71 to FH-96;

All model G50 with S/Nos. GH-94, GH-97 to GH-119; All model H50 with S/Nos. HH-120 to HH-149;

All model J50 with S/Nos. JH-150 to JH-176.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0334-152, Rev. III. Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

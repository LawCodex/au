# AD/B767/43 - Passenger Oxygen - Oxygen Generators

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/43 Passenger Oxygen - Oxygen Generators 10/90

Applicability: All model 767 series aeroplanes listed in Boeing Alert SB 767-35A0015. Requirement: Action in Accordance with Boeing Alert SB 767-35A0015.

*Note: FAA AD 90-10-09 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1991.

Background: This modification is to prevent the oxygen generators failing to activate due to the release pin bending or breaking.

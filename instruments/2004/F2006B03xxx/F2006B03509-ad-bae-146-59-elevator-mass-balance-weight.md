# AD/BAe 146/59 - Elevator Mass Balance Weight

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/59 Elevator Mass Balance Weight 3/96

Applicability: All Series BAe 146 and Avro 146/RJ aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace, Avro International Division Mandatory Service Bulletin 55-014-01510A.

Compliance: Before 30 June 1997.

Background: The manufacturer requires a modification which introduces revised mass balance weights to the elevators in order to counteract possible accumulation of water at the trailing edge of the elevators in severe precipitation.

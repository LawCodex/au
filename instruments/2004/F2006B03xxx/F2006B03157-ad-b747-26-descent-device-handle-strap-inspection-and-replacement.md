**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B747/26 Descent Device Handle Strap - Inspection

**and Replacement**

Applicability: All models 747 with line numbers 1 to 467. Document: 1. Boeing Alert SB No. 747-25-A2551; and

2\. Frost Engineering SB No. 379103-25-002. Compliance: Before 1 March 1981.

## 2/81

Background: On overseas aeroplanes several descent devices were found with one or both handle straps broken.

# AD/BEECH 1900/13 - Cabin Partition

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 1900 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 1900/13 Cabin Partition 4/96

Applicability: Model 1900D aircraft with serial numbers UE-2 through UE-92.

Requirement: (a) For aircraft with one of the following serial numbers: UE-2 through UE-68, UE- 70 through UE-72, or UE-74 through UE-77; inspect the cabin partition to ensure that a right-hand partition bracket, part number 129-530043-79, exists. If this bracket does not exist, install this bracket with part number MS27039-1-09 screws and part number AN960PD10 washers in accordance with Kit Drawing No. 129-5007 as referenced in Beech Service Bulletin 2556 Revision 1.

(b) For all affected serial numbers (UE-2 through UE-92), improve the right-hand forward partition installation in accordance with Kit Drawing No. 129-5007, as referenced in Beech Service Bulletin 2556 Revision 1.

*Note: FAA AD 95-26-14 Amdt 39-9474 refers.*

Compliance: Within 400 hours time in service after 28 March 1996.

Background: The FAA requires the above actions to be taken to prevent cabin partition failure because of a structural deficiency, which could cause passenger injury if the partition could not withstand the load incurred with the baggage compartment loaded to its 250 lb limit.

# AD/BEECH 200/7 - Rudder Push Rod Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 200/7 Rudder Push Rod Assembly 5/76

Applicability: All models with S/Nos BB-2 to BB-58.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0778-153.

*Note: FAA AD 76-03-07 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1976.

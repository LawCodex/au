**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B767/54 Engine and Cargo Compartment Fire Extinguishing Systems

Applicability: As detailed in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-05-06. Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

Background: This AD requires modification of the engine and cargo compartment fire extinguishing wiring and plumbing to preclude improper connection during maintenance. Compliance with this AD constitutes terminating action for the

## 6/91

repetitive inspections and functional checks required by AD/B767/22 Amdt 2.

# AD/BAe 146/12 Amdt 1 - Elevator `G Weight Damper

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator \`G’ Weight Damper 6/89**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos referenced in the Requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 27-77-00955 A & C Rev 2. Compliance: Prior to 30 September 1989.

Background: This AD contains two concurrent modifications. One to overcome control column oscillation at 2.5 Hz and the other to increase stick centring force following an autopilot cutout. Amendment 1 requires additional work, includes other series aircraft and includes variations dependent on modification state.

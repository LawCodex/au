# AD/BEECH 200/26 Amdt 1 - Flight Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 200/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Controls 1/79**

Applicability: All models 200 with S/Nos BB-2 to BB-185, BB-187 to BB-199; 200T with S/Nos BT-1 and all 200 and 200T series fitted with a rudder shipped from Beech during period 1 May 1973 to 1 July 1976.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0629-150, Rev II.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1979.

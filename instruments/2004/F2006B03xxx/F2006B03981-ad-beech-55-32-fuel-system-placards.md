# AD/BEECH 55/32 - Fuel System Placards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 55/32 Fuel System Placards 7/73

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. TC-1 to TC-1425, TE-1 to TE-851; TF-1 to TF-70 and TH-1 to TH-200.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI’s Nos. 0492-281 or 0484-281 as applicable.

Tank inspections are to be performed on all aircraft with baffled fuel cells unless previously inspected in accordance with AD/BEECH 55/17.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 July 1973.

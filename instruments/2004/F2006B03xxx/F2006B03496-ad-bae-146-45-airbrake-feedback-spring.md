# AD/BAe 146/45 - Airbrake Feedback Spring

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/45 Airbrake Feedback Spring 5/93

Applicability: All BAe 146 series aircraft fitted with Servo-Valve assemblies P/N 3799H1 (Fairy Hydraulics) incorporating Servo-Valve P/N 72189 (Abex) in the S/N range as specified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 146 mandatory Service Bulletin 27-133.

*Note: UK CAA AD 018-01-92 and DGAC 92-096-IMP(B) refer.*

Compliance: By 30 June 1993.

Background: Advice has been received that due to faulty manufacture the Servo-Valve feedback spring may be fractured.

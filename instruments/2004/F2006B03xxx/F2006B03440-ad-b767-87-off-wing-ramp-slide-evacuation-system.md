# AD/B767/87 - Off-Wing Ramp/Slide Evacuation System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/87 Off-Wing Ramp/Slide Evacuation System 13/95

Applicability: Model 767 series aircraft, equipped with BFGoodrich off-wing ramp/slide evacuation systems having part number 101630, 101655, or 101656.

Requirement: Modify the off-wing ramp/slide evacuation systems in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 767-25-0218, and BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 101630/655/656-25- 269.

*Note: FAA AD 95-18-03 Amdt 39-9351 refers.*

Compliance: Before 10 October 1998.

Background: The FAA received reports of punctured tubes on certain off-wing ramp/slide evacuation systems installed on the affected aircraft.

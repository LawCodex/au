# AD/BEECH 36/27 Amdt 1 - Flight Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 36/27**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Controls 1/79**

Applicability: All models A36 with S/Nos. E323 and E441 to E954; and all 36 series fitted with rudder and/or elevators shipped from Beech during the period 1 May 1973 and

1 July 1976.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0629-150, Rev. II.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1979.

# AD/B747/31 - Aileron Control System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/31 Aileron Control System - Modification 6/83

Applicability: All model 747 with S/Nos. referenced in Boeing SB. 27-2065, SB 27-2095, SB 27- 2161 and SB 51-2032 as applicable.

Document: Boeing SBs 747-27-2065, 747-27-2095, 747-27-2161, 747-51-2032.

*Note: FAA AD 82-02-10 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, within 3000 hours time in service after 30 June 1983.

Background: Numerous occurrences of binding in the aileron control system are attributed to the accumulation of ice on the aileron control system in the wing gear wheel well area.

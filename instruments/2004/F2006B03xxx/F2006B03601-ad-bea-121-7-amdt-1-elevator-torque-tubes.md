# AD/BEA 121/7 Amdt 1 - Elevator Torque Tubes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEA 121/7

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Elevator Torque Tubes 2/92

Requirement: Action in accordance with mandatory British Aerospace SB B121/28 Revision 1. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: A severely corroded torque tube assembly has been found on an aircraft at a major inspection. SB has been revised and made mandatory by UK CAA.

# AD/BEECH 400/4 - Fuselage Rivets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 400 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 400/4 Fuselage Rivets 13/93

Applicability: Model 400A with serial numbers RK-1 through RK-41. Model 400T with serial numbers TT-3 through TT-20.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory Service Bulletin 2482.

*Note: FAA AD 93-16-12 Amdt 39-8670 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 9 December 1993.

Background: The FAA received reports that during manufacture, rivets may have been inadvertently omitted from the area adjacent to the upper edge of the emergency exit door.

# AD/B747/22 - Escape Slide/Raft Lanyard Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/22 Escape Slide/Raft Lanyard Assembly - Inspection 3/80

Applicability: All model 747 equipped with escape slide/raft assemblies P/N 7A1238, 7A1239, 7A1255, 7A1256, 7A1257 and 7A1261, manufactured prior to January 1979.

Document: FAA AD 79-18-02, Amdt. 39-3537.

*Note: Boeing Alert SB No. 747-25A2467 and B.F. Goodrich Alert SB No. 25-051 refer.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished initially inspect before 1 June 1980 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1 year.

Background: During slide/raft refurbishment a number of firing lanyard assemblies have been found corroded and/or frayed.

# AD/BAe 146/38 - Generator Control Unit (GCU) Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/38 Generator Control Unit (GCU) Replacement 1/92

TX

Applicability: As specified in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 146 Mandatory Alert Service Bulletin 24-A97. Compliance: Prior to 17 November 1991.

Background: BAe have advised that failure of affected GCUs to detect a single phase feeder line open circuit may lead to inaccurate instrument readings.

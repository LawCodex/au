# AD/B767/83 - Flight Station Window Latch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/83 Flight Station Window Latch 11/95

Applicability: Model 767 series with line numbers 1 through 114.

Requirement: Modify the latch hook installation for the number two flight station window frame in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 767-56-0002.

*Note: FAA AD 94-18-01 Amdt 39-9014 refers.*

Compliance: Before 11 April 1997.

Background: The FAA received reports of the flight crew executing rejected takeoffs and air turnbacks due to false “closed” indications for the number two flight station window.

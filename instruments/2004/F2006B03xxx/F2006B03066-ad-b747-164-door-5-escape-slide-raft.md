# AD/B747/164 - Door 5 Escape Slide/Raft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/164 Door 5 Escape Slide/Raft 11/96

Applicability: Model 747 series aircraft equipped with BFGoodrich Evacuation Slide/Rafts at door 5; having slide/raft assembly part number 7A1469-1, -2, -3, -4, -7, -8, -9, -10, -11, or - 12 (all unit serial numbers).

Requirement: Modify the door 5 evacuation slide/rafts in accordance with BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1469-25-283.

*Note 1: Modification previous to the issue of this Directive in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 747-25A3096, which references BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1469-25-283, is considered acceptable for compliance with the modification requirements of this Directive.*

*Note 2: FAA AD 96-15-08 Amdt 39-9698 refers.*

Compliance: Before 3 September 1999.

Background: The FAA received reports that the door 5 evacuation slide/raft failed to deploy properly due to adverse loads caused by the geometry of this evacuation slide/raft. Failure to deploy properly could contribute to injury to passengers on the slide, and could delay or impede the evacuation of passengers during an emergency.

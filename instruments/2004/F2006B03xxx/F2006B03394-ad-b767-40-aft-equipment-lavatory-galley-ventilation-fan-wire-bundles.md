# AD/B767/40 - Aft Equipment/Lavatory/Galley Ventilation Fan Wire Bundles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B767/40 Aft Equipment/Lavatory/Galley Ventilation Fan Wire Bundles

Applicability: All Boeing 767 series aeroplanes as detailed in the Requirement document.

## 9/90

Requirement: Re-route the aft equipment/lavatory/galley ventilation fan wire bundles along the frame assembly at Stn 1540 IAW Boeing ASB 767-21A0074.

*Note: FAA AD 90-08-13 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 6 September 1991.

Background: This AD is prompted by a report of charred insulation blankets aft of station 1540 which resulted from arcing and burning of damaged wire bundles. This condition, if not corrected, could result in a fire behind the aft cargo compartment wall.

Compliance with this AD is required in order to minimise the possibility of inadvertent damage to the wire bundles.

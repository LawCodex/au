# AD/BEA 206/6 - Exhaust Bellows - Retirement Life

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 206/6 Exhaust Bellows - Retirement Life 9/69

Applicability: Model B206 Series 2.

Requirement: Retire from service exhaust bellows P/N CE20901 and P/N CE20806. Compliance: 1. For exhaust bellows with more than 500 hours total time in service as at 29

September 1969:

Within the next 100 hours time in service after 29 September 1969.

2\. For exhaust bellows with less than 500 hours time in service:

Before 600 hours time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service.

*Note: Beagle B206, Series 2, Maintenance Schedule, Volume 5, page 40-8, dated 30 April 1967 also refers.*

# AD/BEECH 33/23 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 33/23 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly 10/75

Applicability: Models 35-33, 35-A33, 35-B33 and 35-C33 which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA 2219 WE (installation of engine crank-case breather and vacuum pump air-oil separator).

Requirement: Remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/No. 1000-1 and install P/No. B-1114- M, P/No. B-1114-RM or P/No. 1000-1 RM.

*Note: FAA AD No. 75-15-08 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 November 1975.

# AD/B747/25 - Wing Leading Edge Flap System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/25 Wing Leading Edge Flap System - Modification 1/81

Applicability: All models 747.

Document: 1. FAA AD 80-19-05, Amdt. 39-3906; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 747-27-2204.

Compliance: As per requirement document No. 1.

Background: Investigation has revealed that a single electrical fault in the leading edge flap circuits could cause an unscheduled deployment of the flaps.

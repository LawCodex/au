# AD/B767/34 - Control Wheel Aids Transducer

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/34 Control Wheel Aids Transducer 4/90

Applicability: All models listed in the referenced Service Bulletin. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 767-31A-0029.

*Note: FAA AD 89-24-05 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1990.

Background: Removal of the AIDS transducer is required to prevent binding of the control wheel.

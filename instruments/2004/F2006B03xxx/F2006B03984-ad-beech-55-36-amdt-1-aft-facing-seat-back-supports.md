# AD/BEECH 55/36 Amdt 1 - Aft Facing Seat Back Supports

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 55/36**

**Amdt 1**

**Aft Facing Seat Back Supports 7/74**

Applicability: All models 58 and 58A equipped with optional aft facing seats. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0591-314.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 December 1973.

# AD/B747/156 - Door 2 Escape Slide/Raft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/156 Door 2 Escape Slide/Raft 2/96

Applicability: Model 747SP series aircraft equipped with BFGoodrich evacuation systems identified in BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1255-25-275.

Requirement: Modify the escape slide/raft on Door 2 in accordance with BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1255-25-275.

*Note: FAA AD 95-24-02 Amdt 39-9434 refers.*

Compliance: Before 31 December 1998.

Background: The FAA received a report of a Door 2 escape slide/raft that did not inflate automatically after deployment onto the wing.

# AD/BEA 121/26 - Rudder Control Tube Bolt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/26 Rudder Control Tube Bolt 10/88 DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Alert SB B121/94.

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 27 July 1988 and thereafter at each removal/replacement of the subject bolt.

Background: Certain combinations of rudder and elevator control settings have resulted in this bolt fouling the elevator control tube. This may restrict or jam control movement.

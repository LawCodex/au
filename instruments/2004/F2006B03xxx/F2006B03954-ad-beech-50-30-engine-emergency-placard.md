# AD/BEECH 50/30 - Engine Emergency Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 50 (Twin Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 50/30 Engine Emergency Placard 9/79

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect emergency engine shutdown procedure placard on overhead or instrument panel for legibility and condition. If defaced or missing, fabricate a new placard using letters of 3mm height and affix to either overhead or instrument panel.

Placard to read:

**EMERGENCY ENGINE SHUTDOWN PROCEDURES**

**MIXTURE CONT-IDLE CUT-OFF**

**2. FUEL SELECTOR-OFF**

**3. OIL SHUT-OFF HDL-UP AND LKD**

**4. PROP CONTROL-FEATHERED**

**5. THROTTLE-FULLY OPEN**

**6. BOTH BOOST PUMPS-OFF**

**7. MAGNETO SWITCH-OFF**

**8. ALTNTR/GEN SWITCH OFF**

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 September 1979.

Background: As the original engine emergency shutdown procedure decal may have become defaced or be otherwise illegible a more durable placard is to be installed as per a recent FAA requirement.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEECH 55/15

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Control Wheel Adaptor 6/78

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0254-156, Rev. IV. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 August 1972.

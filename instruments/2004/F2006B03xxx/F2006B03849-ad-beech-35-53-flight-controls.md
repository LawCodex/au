# AD/BEECH 35/53 - Flight Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/53 Flight Controls 9/78

Applicability: All models V35B & V35TC with S/Nos. D-9478 to D-9480, D-9482 to D-9531, D- 9533 to D-9536, D-9539 to D-9968, and all 35 series fitted with a rudder and/or elevator shipped from Beech during period 1 May 1973 to 1 July 1976.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beech SI No. 0629-150 Rev. 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1978.

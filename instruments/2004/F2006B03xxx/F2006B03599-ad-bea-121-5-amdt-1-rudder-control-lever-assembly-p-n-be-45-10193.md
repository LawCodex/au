# AD/BEA 121/5 Amdt 1 - Rudder Control Lever Assembly P/N BE 45.10193

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEA 121/5**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

**Rudder Control Lever Assembly P/N BE 45.10193**

**5/71**

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beagle SB B121/25.

Compliance: 1. For aircraft not already inspected in accordance with AD/BEA 121/5, and, for those having more than 40 hours time in service since last inspected in accordance with AD/BEA 121/5 - within 10 hours time in service after 13 May 1971; and;

2\. For aircraft having less than 40 hours time in service since last inspected in accordance with AD/BEA 121/5 - within 50 hours time in service from last inspection, and

3\. Thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

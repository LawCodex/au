# AD/BEECH 200/15 - Aileron Control System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 200 (Super King Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 200/15 Aileron Control System 6/77** Applicability: All model with S/Nos BB-2 to BB-250. All model 200T with S/Nos BT-1 and BT-2. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0906.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1977.

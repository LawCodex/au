# AD/BAe 146/50 - Generator Control Unit

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/50 Generator Control Unit 8/94

Applicability: All series BAe 146 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Avro International Aerospace Mandatory Inspection Service Bulletin S.B.24-103 dated 24 March 1994.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 014-03-94 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1994.

Background: The CAA(UK) have advised that investigation of in-service generator control unit (GCU) failures has highlighted the possibility that GCUs repaired or rectified by workshops other than GEC-Marconi, may have the under-frequency trip level set too high. This Directive requires the one time inspection of GCUs for possible incorrect under-frequency trip level settings and, if necessary, adjustment of the settings. The CAA(UK) compliance time is extended by two calendar months.

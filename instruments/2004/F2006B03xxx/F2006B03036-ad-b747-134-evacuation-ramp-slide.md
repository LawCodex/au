# AD/B747/134 - Evacuation Ramp/Slide

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/134 Evacuation Ramp/Slide 13/94

Applicability: Model 747-100, -200, -300, and -400 series aircraft equipped with BFGoodrich evacuation ramp/slides; as listed in BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1418-25-253, or Revision 2.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-17-09 Amdt 39- 9004.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The FAA received reports of several evacuation ramp/slide malfunctions.

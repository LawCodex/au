# AD/B767/77 Amdt 2 - Override/Jettison Fuel Pump

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B767/77**

**Amdt 2**

**Override/Jettison Fuel Pump 13/94**

Applicability: Model 767 series aircraft line position 001 to 545 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert Service Bulletin 767-28A0045 Revision 2.

*Note: Sundstrand SB 5006286-28-A5 Revision 2 and FAA AD 94-11-05 Amdt 39- 8921 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously carried out, within 30 days from 8 December 1994.

Thereafter, repeat the inspection at intervals not exceeding 1000 hours time in service until the requirements of Boeing SB 767-28-0046 have been incorporated.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that a loose diffuser ring may cause damage to the impeller and pumping unit housing causing the pump to lock-up.

During dry fuel pump operation, a loose diffuser ring may result in metal-to-metal contact creating a potential ignition source.

This amendment adds terminating action for this directive.

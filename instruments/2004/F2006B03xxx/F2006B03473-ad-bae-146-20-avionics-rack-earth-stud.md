# AD/BAe 146/20 - Avionics Rack Earth Stud

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/20 Avionics Rack Earth Stud 9/90

Applicability: All BAe 146 series aeroplanes as detailed in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 146 Service Bulletin 24-60.

*Note: Compliance with this Service Bulletin has been classified mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Prior to 6 November 1990.

Background: BAe have advised that one operator has experienced chafing of an electrical loom on this earth stud.

# AD/B747/55 - Nacelle Strut Attachment Fitting Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/55 Nacelle Strut Attachment Fitting Bolts 1/87

Applicability: All models with line numbers 1 to 644.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Para’s A,B,C,D,E & F of FAA AD 86-23-01.

*Note: Boeing SB 747-57A2235 refers.*

Compliance: As per Requirement Document from 31 January 1987.

Background: The manufacturer reports of fractured H-11 bolts which secure the nacelle strut attachment fittings. One instance reported was of both bolts fractured at one fitting.

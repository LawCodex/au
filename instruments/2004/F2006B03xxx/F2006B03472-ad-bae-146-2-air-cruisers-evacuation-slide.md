# AD/BAe 146/2 - Air Cruisers Evacuation Slide

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/2 Air Cruisers Evacuation Slide 3/86

Applicability: Series 100 and 100A; up to and including E1017 and Series 200 and 200A; up to and including E2048.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Alert S.B. 25-A57. Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1986.

Background: A case has been reported of incorrect routing of the evacuation slide inflation lanyard.

This has resulted in the slide deploying in an inverted attitude.

# AD/B747/59 - Fuselage Upper Body Tear Strap Disbonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/59 Fuselage Upper Body Tear Strap Disbonding 11/87

Applicability: All models listed in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 747-53A2279 R1.

*Note: FAA AD 87-16-13 refers.*

Compliance: Inspect in accordance with the Requirement Document until terminating action carried out.

Background: Disbonding of the upper body hot bonded skin tear straps has been reported.

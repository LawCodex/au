# AD/B747/124 - H-11 Steel Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/124 H-11 Steel Bolts 7/94

Applicability: Model 747 series aircraft with line numbers 641 through 708, excluding Model 747- 400 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-07-06 Amendment 39-8864.

*Note: Boeing SB 747-51-2048 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Reports were received of cracked and fractured H-11 steel bolts installed at certain locations of the airframe structure. The actions specified in this Directive are intended to prevent the failure of attachment bolts at critical locations.

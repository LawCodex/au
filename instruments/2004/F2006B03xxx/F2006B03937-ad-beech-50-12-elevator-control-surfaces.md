# AD/BEECH 50/12 - Elevator Control Surfaces

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 50 (Twin Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 50/12 Elevator Control Surfaces NK

Applicability: All models prior to S/Nos. DH-170 and FH-89 except H-1 to H-11.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SB B50 - No. 35; C50 - No. 26; D50 - No. 20; E50 - No. 11; F50 - No. 4.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service.

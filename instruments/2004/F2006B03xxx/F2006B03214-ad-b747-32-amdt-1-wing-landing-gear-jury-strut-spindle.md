# AD/B747/32 Amdt 1 - Wing Landing Gear Jury Strut Spindle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/32**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Landing Gear Jury Strut Spindle 13/89**

Applicability: All model B747 aeroplanes listed in Boeing SB 747-32-2261R2. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing S.B. 747-32-2261R2.

*Note: FAA AD 83-05-09 Amdt. 39-4585 refers.*

Compliance: As per requirement document until modification in accordance with Boeing SB 747- 32-2261R2.

Background: The manufacturer reports two cases of wing landing gear jury strut spindle failures.

Amendment 1 references Revision 2 to the Requirement Document.

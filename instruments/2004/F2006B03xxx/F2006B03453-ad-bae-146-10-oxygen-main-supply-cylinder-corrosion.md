# AD/BAe 146/10 - Oxygen Main Supply Cylinder Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/10 Oxygen Main Supply Cylinder Corrosion 13/88

Applicability: As detailed in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 146 SB 35-8.

Compliance: Within three months time in service after 29 December 1988 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 12 months until embodiment of Mod HCM 36032A.

Background: Corrosion of the cylinder has been reported in the contact area between the cylinder and the cradle.

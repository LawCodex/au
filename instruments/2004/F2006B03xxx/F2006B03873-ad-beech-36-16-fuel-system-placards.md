# AD/BEECH 36/16 - Fuel System Placards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 36/16 Fuel System Placards 7/73

Applicability: Models 36 and A36 with S/Nos. E-1, E-310.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI 0491-281 or 0493-281 as applicable. Tank inspections are to be performed on all aircraft with baffled fuel cells unless previously inspected in accordance with AD/BEECH 36/6.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1973.

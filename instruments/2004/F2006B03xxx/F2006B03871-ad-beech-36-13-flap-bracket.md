# AD/BEECH 36/13 - Flap Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 36/13 Flap Bracket 8/72

Applicability: All models which have achieved 1000 or more hours time in service.

Requirement: Inspect flap actuator bracket and adjacent structure on LH and RH flaps for cracks or signs of distress.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

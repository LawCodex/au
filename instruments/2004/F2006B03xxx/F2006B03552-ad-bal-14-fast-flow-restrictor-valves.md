# AD/BAL/14 - Fast Flow Restrictor Valves

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

AD/BAL/14 Fast Flow Restrictor Valves 11/95 DM

Applicability: All hot air balloons.

Requirement: Inspect LP gas cylinders for fitment of fast flow restrictor valves and remove if fitted.

*Note: Inspection of LP cylinders for fitment of restrictor valves may be accomplished by an AS2337- approved gas cylinder test station or by an operational check of each cylinder, with burner alight.*

Compliance: Before further flight. Thereafter, following any maintenance, or inspection for date stamping.

Background: There have been cases reported of fast flow restrictor valves being fitted internally to LP gas cylinders during maintenance. Because a balloon burner uses fuel within the operating range of the fast flow restrictor valve, a serious accident could result.

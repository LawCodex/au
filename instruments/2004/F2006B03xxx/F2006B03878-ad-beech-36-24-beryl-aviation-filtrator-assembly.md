# AD/BEECH 36/24 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 36/24 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly 11/75

Applicability: All models 36 and A36 which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA2653WE (Installation of engine crankcase breather and vacuum pump air-oil separator).

Requirement: 1. Remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N B-1116-1 and install either P/N B-1116-M or P/N B-1116-RM.

*Note: FAA AD 75-05-02 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 November 1975.

# AD/BAe 146/8 Amdt 1 - Flaptrack 3 Lower Carriage Roller Bearings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/8**

**Amdt 1**

**Flaptrack 3 Lower Carriage Roller Bearings 7/90**

Applicability: Series 100, 100A, 200 and 200A with serial nos listed in the Requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 57-24-00911A Revision 2.

Compliance: Prior to 31 May 1989.

Background: Development testing has revealed that the roller bearing pins have insufficient strength to withstand the loads developed if the flaps jam under power. Amendment 1 updates the Requirement Document to Revision 2.

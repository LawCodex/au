# AD/BEA 121/16 Amdt 1 - Fuselage/Mainplane Attachments

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEA 121/16**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage/Mainplane Attachments 10/78**

Applicability: All B121 series 2 and 3 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Pup Service Bulletin No. B121/76. Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

*Note: Role factors must be assessed by the manufacturer in accordance with appendices B and C of SB 121/76, and evidence of the assessment provided to the Secretary.*

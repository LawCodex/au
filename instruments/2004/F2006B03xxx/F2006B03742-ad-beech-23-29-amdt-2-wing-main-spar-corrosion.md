# AD/BEECH 23/29 Amdt 2 - Wing Main Spar Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 19, 23 and 24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 23/29**

**Amdt 2**

**Wing Main Spar Corrosion 12/86**

Applicability: All 19, 23 and 24 series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0824-035 Rev. II.

Compliance: Prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 December 1986 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 12 months.

Background: A history of Major Defect Reports has warranted an increase in the periodicity of this Directive to that originally called up by Beech.

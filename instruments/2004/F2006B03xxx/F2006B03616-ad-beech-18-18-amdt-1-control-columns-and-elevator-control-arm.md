# AD/BEECH 18/18 Amdt 1 - Control Columns and Elevator Control Arm Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 18 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 18/18**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

**Control Columns and Elevator Control Arm Assembly - Inspection**

**12/87**

Requirement: Inspect the Control Columns and Elevator Control Arm Assembly as detailed in Beechcraft SI 0889-155. In addition inspect for cracks on the Control Column Assembly P/N 187510, tubing, outer surface for a distance of 11 cm up the tube above the lower welded tube splice. The same area on the co-pilot’s Control Column is also to be inspected. The inspection is to be performed using visual and either magnetic particle, or penetrant procedures; or

FAA AD 77-19-07.

Compliance: Within 100 hours after 31 December 1977 and thereafter at 300 hour intervals.

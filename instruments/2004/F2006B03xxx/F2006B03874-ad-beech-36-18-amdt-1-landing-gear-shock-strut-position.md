# AD/BEECH 36/18 Amdt 1 - Landing Gear Shock Strut Position

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 36/18**

**Amdt 1**

**Landing Gear Shock Strut Position 1/74**

Applicability: All model A36 with S/Nos. E-425 to E-475 except E-431. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 612-200.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1974 unless already accomplished.

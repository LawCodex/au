# AD/BAe 146/9 Amdt 1 - Steering Ring Cuff Nut

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BAe 146/9

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Steering Ring Cuff Nut 4/89

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with BAe SB 32-A95.

2\. Modify in accordance with BAe SB 32-95-70409A.

*Note: UK CAA AD 014-08-88 refers.*

Compliance: 1. As detailed in the Requirement Document.

2\. Prior to 30 June 1989.

Background: Insecurity of the steering cuff ring nut has resulted in unscrewing of the ring nut. This allows the steering cuff to drop resulting in a loss of nose wheel steering. Amdt 1 provides for terminating action.

# AD/BEECH 33/5 Amdt 2 - Control Wheel Adaptor

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEECH 33/5

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Control Wheel Adaptor 8/72

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0254-156 Rev. IV.

*Note: Compliance with a previous issue of this Directive satisfies the requirement of this issue.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1972.

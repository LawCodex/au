# AD/BAe 146/4 Amdt 1 - Flight Controls - Stall Identification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/4**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Controls - Stall Identification 1/87**

Applicability: Series 100 and 100A, and Series 200 and 200A, fitted with the suspect valves identified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Service Bulletin 27-58.

*Note: SB 27-58 supersedes SB 27-53.*

Compliance: Inspect within 60 flights after 2 May 1986 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 60 flights until replaced with modified valve.

For spares prior to installation in an aircraft and thereafter a intervals not exceeding 60 flights until replaced with modified valve.

Background: A number of stick push valves in service may become defective as a result of internal corrosion within the solenoid assembly. This could lead to a dormant failure not apparent on the flight deck.

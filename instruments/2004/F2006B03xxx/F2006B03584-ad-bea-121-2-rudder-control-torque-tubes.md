# AD/BEA 121/2 - Rudder Control Torque Tubes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/2 Rudder Control Torque Tubes 8/69

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 001 to 092.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beagle SB No. B121/8.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 September 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

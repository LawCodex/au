# AD/BAe 146/22 - Stall Ident and Warning

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/22 Stall Ident and Warning 10/90

Applicability: BAe 146 models 200 and 200A S/No E 2012 and subsequent with modification HCM 00503B embodied before delivery, or retrofitted by SB 27-94-00503B.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 27-109-00503C.

*Note: UK CAA Mandatory SB.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1991.

Background: The stall identification summing unit Part No C81606-3 has incorrect speed law.

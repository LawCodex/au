**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B747/30 Off-Wing Slides Mechanical Restraints - Modification

Applicability: All models 747 equipped with B.F. Goodrich off-wing slides. Document: 1. FAA AD 80-25-03, Amendment 39-3982; or

2\. B.F. Goodrich SB No. 25-054. Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

## 2/82

Background: Modified shear pin mechanical restraints have greatly increased reliability of slide deployment.

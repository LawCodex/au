# AD/B747/110 - Doorstop Support Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/110 Doorstop Support Fitting 6/92

Applicability: Model 747 series, line numbers 001 through 113.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 92-02-01 Amdt 39- 8137.

*Note: Boeing ASB 747-53A2326 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Reports have been received of cracks in certain doorstop fittings at the forward edge of number 5 main entry doors. This Directive requires on-going repetitive inspections until optional terminating action is instigated.

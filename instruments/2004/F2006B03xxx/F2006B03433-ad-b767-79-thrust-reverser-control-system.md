# AD/B767/79 - Thrust Reverser Control System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/79 Thrust Reverser Control System 10/94

Applicability: All Boeing 767 series aircraft equipped with Pratt and Whitney PW4000 series engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-12-10. Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, as specified in the Requirement Document, effective

15 September 1994.

Background: This AD is issued to ensure the integrity of the fail safe features of the thrust reverser system to prevent inadvertent deployment of a thrust reverser in flight.

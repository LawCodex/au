# AD/B747/58 - Hydraulic Fuse

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/58 Hydraulic Fuse 10/87

Applicability: Model 747 series aircraft as listed in Boeing Service Bulletin 747-29-2063.

Requirement: In accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 747-29-2063 dated 23 December 1986 or later FAA approved revision.

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1989.

Background: The installation of a hydraulic fuse in the No. 4 hydraulic system is prompted by the incident in which all four hydraulic systems were lost when failure of the rear pressure bulkhead resulted in loss of the vertical stabiliser.

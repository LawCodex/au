# AD/BEECH 35/48 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/48 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly 8/75

Applicability: Models 35, A35, B35, C35, D35, E35, F35,35R, H35, J35, K35, M35, N35 and P35

which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA 2219 WE (Installation of engine crankcase breather and vacuum pump air-oil separator).

Requirement: 1. For Beech Models 35, A35, B35, C35, D35, E35, F35 and 35R, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N 1000-1 and install P/N B-1119-M, P/N

B-1119-RM, or P/N 1000-1-RM.

2\. For Beech Models H35, J35, K35, M35, N35 and P35, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N 1000-1 and install P/N B-1114-M, P/N B-1114-RM or P/N 1000-1-RM.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1975.

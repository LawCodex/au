# AD/B767/46 - Vertical Stabiliser

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/46 Vertical Stabiliser 12/90

Applicability: Model 767 line numbers 002 through 299.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB767-55A0007.

*Note: FAA AD 90-18-05 Amdt 39-6713 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, prior to 30 April 1992.

Background: Country of origin AD. To prevent overpressurization of the vertical stabilizer, which could cause structural failure. This could happen if rapid decompression of the fuselage occurred in the stabilizer area.

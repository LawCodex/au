# AD/BAL/8 Amdt 1 - Instruments in Hot Air Balloons

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

**AD/BAL/8**

**Amdt 1**

**Instruments in Hot Air Balloons 9/90**

Applicability: All balloons for which a system of maintenance has not been approved pursuant to CAR 38(3).

Requirement: Carry out a calibration check of all instruments in accordance with manufacturer’s instructions/specifications.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding three years.

Background: Whilst CAR 2 definition of “aircraft” covers balloons, these aircraft do not have a major inspection requirement nor are ambient or envelope temperature indicators currently addressed.

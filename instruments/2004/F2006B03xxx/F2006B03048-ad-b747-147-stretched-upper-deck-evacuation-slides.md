# AD/B747/147 - Stretched Upper Deck Evacuation Slides

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/147 Stretched Upper Deck Evacuation Slides 6/95

Applicability: Model 747-300 and -400 series aircraft equipped with BFGoodrich stretched upper deck evacuation slides, part number 7A1323-1, -2, -3, -4, -105, -106, -107, -108, -

109, or -110.

Requirement: Modify the BFGoodrich stretched upper deck evacuation slide, part number 7A1323- ( ), in accordance with the Accomplishment Instructions of BFGoodrich Service Bulletin 7A1323-25-266, Revision 1.

*Note 1: Installation of the “product improvements”, specified in paragraph 2.J. of the Accomplishment Instructions of the Service Bulletin, is not required by this Directive.*

*Note 2: FAA AD 95-04-08 Amdt 39-9160 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 6 April 1998.

Background: The FAA received reports of loss of air pressure and non-inflation of the inflatable tubes of the slide due to problems associated with the restraint strap, regulator assembly, and turbo fan flapper retaining roll pins.

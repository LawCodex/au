# AD/B747/159 - Forward Pressure Bulkhead

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/159 Forward Pressure Bulkhead 8/96

Applicability: All model 747s, line numbers 1 to 886 excepting 80, 85 and 151.

Requirement: Inspect the forward pressure bulkhead structure in accordance with Boeing SB 747- 53-2355.

*Note: This Service Bulletin supersedes Boeing SB 747-53-2210.*

Compliance: Inspect at the flight thresholds and intervals specified in Boeing SB 747-53-2355, Paragraph D. Aircraft over these times must be inspected within 500 flights after 18 July 1996.

Modification or repair of each area in accordance with Boeing SB 747-53-2355 cancels the inspection requirements for that area.

*Note: For the purposes of this AD, flights on which the cabin pressure differential does not exceed 2.0 psi need not be counted.*

Background: Numerous cracks caused by cyclic pressure loads have been found in the forward pressure bulkhead on aircraft in service and on both the 747 forward fuselage fatigue test articles. A series of inspections and structural changes are required to limit the fatigue damage to the bulkhead. This Directive replaces outer ring chord inspections previously specified in AD/B747/46 Amdt 4.

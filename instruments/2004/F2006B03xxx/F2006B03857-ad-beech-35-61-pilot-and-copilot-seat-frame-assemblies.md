# AD/BEECH 35/61 - Pilot and Copilot Seat Frame Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/61 Pilot and Copilot Seat Frame Assemblies 13/88

Applicability: Model V35B with S/Nos D-9830 through D-10403.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2010 Rev 1 Part II.

*Note: FAA AD 88-21-02 refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 29 December 1988.

Background: To prevent failure of the pilot and copilot seat attachment during an emergency landing.

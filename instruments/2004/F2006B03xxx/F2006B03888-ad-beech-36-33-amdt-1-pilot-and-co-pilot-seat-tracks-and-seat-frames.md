# AD/BEECH 36/33 Amdt 1 - Pilot and Co-pilot Seat Tracks and Seat Frames

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 36 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 36/33**

**Amdt 1**

**Pilot and Co-pilot Seat Tracks and Seat Frames 11/89**

Applicability: 1. Model A36 with S/Nos E-632 through E-789.

2\. Model A36 with S/Nos E-790 through E-1945 E-1947 through E-2103 E-2105 through E-2110.

3\. Model A36TC, B36TC with S/Nos EA-2 through EA-319. EA-321 through EA-388.

Requirement: For Para 1. Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2010 Rev 1 Part I.

For Para 2 & 3. Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2010 Rev 1 Part I and Part II.

*Note 1: FAA AD 88-21-02 refers.*

*Note 2: Compliance with Beechcraft SB 2233 is an acceptable alternative to modification per SB 2010 Part I.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 29 December 1988.

Background: To prevent failure of the pilot and copilot seat attachment during an emergency landing. Amendment 1 clarifies Applicability and Requirement.

# AD/BEA 121/25 - Control Rod End Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/25 Control Rod End Fittings 7/87

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB B121/91.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1987 or prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 30 June 1987 whichever is later.

Background: There has been an incident where the forward elevator control tube fork end became detached from the control tube assembly with consequent loss of elevator control.

# AD/B747/60 - Ceiling Lighting Wire Bundles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/60 Ceiling Lighting Wire Bundles 2/88

Applicability: All aeroplanes modified with Heath Tecna Superbins in accordance with STC SA2365 NM.

Requirement: Action in accordance with FAA AD 87-23-01. Compliance: Prior to 26 April 1988.

Background: Operators have reported instances of chafing and/or burning of the ceiling lighting wire bundles caused by contact between the outboard edges of the ceiling panels and the bin back panels. This condition if not corrected could result in fires.

# AD/BEECH 55/42 Amdt 1 - Strobe Light Bonding Straps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 55/42**

**Amdt 1**

**Strobe Light Bonding Straps 1/75**

Applicability: All models 95-B55 and 95-B55A, S/No. TC-1526 thru TC-1804 and TC-1806 thru TC-1811; E55 & E55A, S/No. TE-904 thru TE-1000, TE-1002 and TE-1003; 58 &

58A S/No. TH-303 thru TH-482, and TH-484 thru TH-527, fitted with Grimes P/N 30-0467-1 or Grimes P/N 30-0467-3 Strobe Light Assemblies.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0655-362, Rev. 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1975.

# AD/BEA 121/24 - Fuselage Angle, Diaphragm and Flange at Tailplane Spar Attachments

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEA 121/24 Fuselage Angle, Diaphragm and Flange**

**at Tailplane Spar Attachments**

**7/84**

Applicability: All B121 series 1, 2 and 3 aeroplanes which have exceeded 2450 hours time in service.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace (Scottish Division) SB No. B121/86.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 8 June 1984 and thereafter a intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer reports that cases of rear fuselage cracks in the area of the tailplane attachment have been discovered in service.

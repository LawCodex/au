# AD/B767/56 - Vertical Stabiliser

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/56 Vertical Stabiliser 8/91

Applicability: Model 767 series with line numbers 300 through 307.

Requirement: Action IAW the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 91-10-09 Amdt 39-6993.

*Note: Boeing SB 767-55A0007 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Modification of the vertical stabilizer forward closure rib by installation of a lower plate and a panel assembly over lightening and access holes, was not incorporated during production on certain aircraft.

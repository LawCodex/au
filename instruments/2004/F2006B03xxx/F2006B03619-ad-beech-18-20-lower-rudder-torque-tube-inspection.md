# AD/BEECH 18/20 - Lower Rudder Torque Tube - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 18 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 18/20 Lower Rudder Torque Tube - Inspection 6/78

Applicability: All models D18C, D18S, E18S, E18S-9700, G18S, H-18, C-45G TC-45G, C-45H,

TC-45J (SNB-5), JRB-6, 3N, 3M and 3NM modified in accordance with STC SA 1243WE, S1016WE or SA360WE.

Requirement: Visually inspect the lower rudder torque tube P/N 452606-7 for cracks in the area of the access hole.

*Note 1: Cracked tubes in which the length of cracks does not exceed 0.50 inch (12.5 mm) may be repaired to a scheme approved by the Secretary. If cracks exceed 0.50 inch the tube shall be replaced before further flight.*

*Note 2: FAA AD 78-10-1 AMDT. 39-3210 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

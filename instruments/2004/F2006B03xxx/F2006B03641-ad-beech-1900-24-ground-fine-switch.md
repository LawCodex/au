# AD/BEECH 1900/24 - Ground Fine Switch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 1900 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 1900/24 Ground Fine Switch 10/98

Applicability: Model 1900D aeroplanes with serial numbers UE-1 through UE-271 inclusive. Requirement: Modify the ground idle low pitch stop system by incorporating Raytheon ‘Ground

Fine Switch Installation Kit’ No 129-5200-1 in accordance with the Accomplishment

Instructions of Raytheon Aircraft Mandatory Service Bulletin No 2714 issued June 1997.

*Note: FAA AD 98-13-11 Amdt 39-10599 refers.*

Compliance: Within 800 hours time in service after the effective date of this directive.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 10 September 1998.

Background: This directive is intended to prevent a loose or misrigged ground fine switch, which could result in a hard landing causing structural damage to the airframe and possible passenger injury.

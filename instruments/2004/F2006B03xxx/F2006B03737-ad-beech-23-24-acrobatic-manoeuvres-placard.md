# AD/BEECH 23/24 - Acrobatic Manoeuvres - Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 19, 23 and 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 23/24 Acrobatic Manoeuvres - Placard 8/74

Applicability: Model B-19 with S/Nos.MB-481 to MB-634and MB-649 and Model C-23 with S/Nos. M-1285 to M-1493 except M-1437 and M-1491 - not fitted with spin improvement Kit No. 23-4007-1S.

Requirement: Remove all placards pertaining to intentional spins and acrobatic flight and replace with a placard, conspicuously located on the instrument panel, as follows :

“THIS AEROPLANE MUST BE OPERATED AS A NORMAL OR UTILITY CATEGORY AEROPLANE ONLY. INTENTIONAL SPINS ARE PROHIBITED. NO ACROBATIC MANOEUVRES APPROVED EXCEPT CHANDELLES, LAZY EIGHTS, STEEP TURNS AND STALLS (EXCEPT WHIP STALLS).”

Compliance: Before issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

# AD/B747/29 - Fairing Mounted Escape Slide Firing Mechanism - Rigging Revision

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/29 Fairing Mounted Escape Slide Firing**

**Mechanism - Rigging Revision**

**12/81**

Applicability: All model B747 aeroplanes with S/Nos. referenced in Boeing SB 747-25A2581 or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues or revisions of that SB.

Document: Boeing SB 747-25A2581.

Compliance: Within 1200 hours time in service after 31 December 1981.

Background: Failure of escape slide to inflate due to reduced fairing door travel restricting the inflation firing mechanism.

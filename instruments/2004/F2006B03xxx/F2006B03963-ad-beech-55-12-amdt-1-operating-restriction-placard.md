# AD/BEECH 55/12 Amdt 1 - Operating Restriction Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 55/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Operating Restriction Placard 2/76**

Applicability: Models 95-55, 95-A55, 95-B55, 95-B55A, 95-B55B, 95-C55, 95-C55A, D55 and D- 55A.

Requirement: 1. For aircraft fitted with auxiliary fuel tanks, install a placard on the leading edge of the fuel selector panel with the following wording:

"USE AUXILIARY FUEL TANKS AND CROSSFEED IN LEVEL FLIGHT ONLY."

2\. For aircraft not fitted with baffled fuel cells, install a placard on the leading edge of the fuel selector panel with the following wording:

"TURNING TYPE TAKE-OFFS OR TAKE-OFFS IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING FAST TAXI TURN ARE PROHIBITED."

Compliance: Before 1 September 1971.

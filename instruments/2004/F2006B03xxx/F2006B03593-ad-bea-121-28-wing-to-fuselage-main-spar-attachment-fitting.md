# AD/BEA 121/28 - Wing to Fuselage Main Spar Attachment Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEA 121/28 Wing to Fuselage Main Spar Attachment Fitting 9/95

Applicability: All Beagle B121 Pup Series 2 and Series 3 aircraft.

Requirement: Perform a high frequency eddy current inspection of the wing to fuselage main spar attachment fitting in accordance with Jetstream Alert Service Bulletin (ASB) B121/100.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 007-01-95 refers.*

Compliance: Inspect at the thresholds and intervals specified in Jetstream ASB B121/100 as adjusted by the role factors assigned by Jetstream SB B121/76.

Background: Fatigue calculations by the manufacturer have indicated that additional inspections are required on the wing to fuselage main spar attachment fitting.

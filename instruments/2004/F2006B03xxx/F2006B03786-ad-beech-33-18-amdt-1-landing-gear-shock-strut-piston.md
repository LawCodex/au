# AD/BEECH 33/18 Amdt 1 - Landing Gear Shock Strut Piston

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 33/18**

**Amdt 1**

**Landing Gear Shock Strut Piston 1/74**

Applicability: All model F33A with S/Nos. CE-436 to CE-463 except CE-439 and CE-462. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0612-200.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1974 unless already accomplished.

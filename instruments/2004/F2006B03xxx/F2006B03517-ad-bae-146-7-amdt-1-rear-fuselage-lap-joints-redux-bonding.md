# AD/BAe 146/7 Amdt 1 - Rear Fuselage Lap Joints - Redux Bonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BAe 146/7**

**Amdt 1**

**Rear Fuselage Lap Joints - Redux Bonding 11/88**

Applicability: As listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 53-70 Revision 1. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: Unsatisfactory Redux bonding may render lengths of lap joint reinforcing ineffective and lead to cracking of the rear fuselage skin at lap joint rivet holes. Amendment 1 requires action in accordance with Revision 1 of British Aerospace SB 53-70.

# AD/B747/127 - Engine Mount Bolt Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/127 Engine Mount Bolt Inspection 9/94

Applicability: Boeing 747 aircraft fitted with Pratt & Whitney JT9D-3 and -7 series engines. Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of FAA AD 94-10-05.

Compliance: Effective 18 August 1994, as specified in the FAA AD.

Background: There have been reports of the forward lower engine mount bolts migrating out of the mount due to loss of the retaining nuts.

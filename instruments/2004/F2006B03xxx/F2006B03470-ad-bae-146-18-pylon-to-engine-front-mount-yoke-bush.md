# AD/BAe 146/18 - Pylon to Engine Front Mount Yoke Bush

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/18 Pylon to Engine Front Mount Yoke Bush 2/90

Applicability: Series 200 S/No. E2106, E2111, E2115, E2121 and E2127. Series 300 S/No. E3128, E3131 and E3134.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 54-4. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: There has been a report of the subject bush being found incorrectly installed.

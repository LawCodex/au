# AD/B747/123 - Stretched Upper Deck Escape System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/123 Stretched Upper Deck Escape System 5/94

Applicability: Model 747 series aircraft with a stretched upper deck, all line numbers up to and including line number 980.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-01-15 Amdt 39- 8797.

*Note: Boeing ASB 747-25A3056 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 October 1994.

Background: The FAA received a report that, during deployment of a stretched upper deck emergency evacuation slide, the slide inflated, but subsequently lost air from one chamber.

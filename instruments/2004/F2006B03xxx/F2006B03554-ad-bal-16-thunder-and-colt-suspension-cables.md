# AD/BAL/16 - Thunder and Colt Suspension Cables

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hot Air Balloons

AD/BAL/16 Thunder and Colt Suspension Cables 10/96

Applicability: Thunder and Colt models AX7-77 S/N 1515 and AX7-65 S/N 335.

Requirement: Inspect the crimping of cables in accordance with Chaise SB No.1, and replace any defective cables.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-114(A) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 12 September 1996.

Background: The suspension cables fitted to the applicable balloons may be defective.

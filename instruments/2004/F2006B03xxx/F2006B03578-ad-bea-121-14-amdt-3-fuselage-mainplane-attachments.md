# AD/BEA 121/14 Amdt 3 - Fuselage/Mainplane Attachments

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beagle B121 (Pup) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEA 121/14**

**Amdt 3**

**Fuselage/Mainplane Attachments 11/77**

Applicability: All model B121 Series 1 fitted with fuselage to mainplane attachment fittings NOT modified in accordance with a scheme approved by the manufacturer.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Scottish Aviation SB No. B121/75. Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

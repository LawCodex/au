# AD/B747/76 Amdt 1 - H-11 Steel Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/76**

**Amdt 1**

**H-11 Steel Bolts 7/90**

Applicability: Model 747 with line numbers 001 through 648.

Requirement: 1. Visually inspect H-11 steel bolts for cracks or fractures at locations in accordance with Boeing SB 747-51-2043 Rev 1.

2\. Replace all H-11 steel bolts with Inconel 718 bolts at locations in accordance with Boeing SB 747-51-2043 Rev 1.

*Note: FAA AD 89-23-07 refers.*

Compliance: For Requirement 1: Prior to exceeding 4 years since date of manufacture or within 15 calendar months after 22 February 1990, whichever occurs later, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 18 calendar months until H-11 bolts are replaced with Inconel 718 bolts.

For Requirement 2: Prior to 22 February 1994.

Background: Country of origin AD. Fracture or cracking of H-11 steel bolts at critical locations has been reported. This condition if not corrected could result in severe structural damage. Amendment 1 references the latest revision to the Requirement document.

# AD/B747/49 - Vertical Fin Access Cover Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/49 Vertical Fin Access Cover Installation 6/86

Applicability: All models with line numbers to 625.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 747-53A2264.

*Note: FAA AD 86-08-02 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1986.

Background: The FAA has determined that the vertical fin could be over pressurised to the point of structural failure in the event of failure of the aft pressure bulkhead.

# AD/B747/102 - P180-1 Terminal Strip Nuts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/102 P180-1 Terminal Strip Nuts 2/92 DM

Applicability: As specified in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-23-09. Compliance: Prior to 10 January 1992.

Background: The FAA have advised that a report has been received of loss of battery power due to a loose power feeder terminal connection. This condition, if not corrected, could result in fire and/or loss of electrical power to flight critical systems in the event of failure of the aircraft primary electrical power system.

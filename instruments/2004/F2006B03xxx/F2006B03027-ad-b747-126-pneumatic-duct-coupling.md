# AD/B747/126 - Pneumatic Duct Coupling

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/126 Pneumatic Duct Coupling 9/94

Applicability: All model 747 series aeroplanes with line position 1 through 219 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-09-13 Amdt 39-

8902\.

*Note: Purolator Service Bulletin FSC-912 dated December 1972 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Failures of certain duct couplings installed on the wing leading edge pneumatic duct have been reported.

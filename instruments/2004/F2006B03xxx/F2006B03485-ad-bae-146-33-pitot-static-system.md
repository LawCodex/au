# AD/BAe 146/33 - Pitot-Static System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/33 Pitot-Static System 7/91

Applicability: All series 200 and 200A Constructor’ Nos E2164 and subsequent, and all series 300 and 300A Constructor’s Nos E3118, E3163 and subsequent.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mandatory British Aerospace Service Bulletin 34-128- 00950J.

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1991.

Background: It has been found that water traps form in the hoses fitted to post Mod. HCM 00950A&D (introduction of EFIS) aircraft.

# AD/BEECH 33/34 - Pilot and Co-Pilot Seat Frame Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 33 and 35-33 (Debonair/Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 33/34 Pilot and Co-Pilot Seat Frame Assemblies 13/88

Applicability: Models F33A with S/Nos CE-621 through CE-1024.

Models F33C with S/Nos CJ-112 through CJ-155.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2010 Rev 1 Part II.

*Note: FAA AD 88-21-02 refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 29 December 1988.

Background: To prevent failure of the pilot and copilot seat attachment during an emergency landing.

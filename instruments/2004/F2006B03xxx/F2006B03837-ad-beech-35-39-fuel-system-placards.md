# AD/BEECH 35/39 - Fuel System Placards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 35 (Bonanza) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 35/39 Fuel System Placards 7/73

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. D-1 to D-9319.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SIs 0491-281, 0493-281 or 0495-281 as applicable. Tank inspections are to be performed on all aircraft with baffled fuel cells unless previously inspected in accordance with AD/BEECH 35/28.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1973.

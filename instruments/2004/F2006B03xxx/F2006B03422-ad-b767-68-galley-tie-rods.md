# AD/B767/68 - Galley Tie Rods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/68 Galley Tie Rods 8/92

Applicability: Model 767 series, as listed in Boeing SB 767-25-0160.

Requirement: Replace the aft galley tie rods IAW Boeing SB 767-25-0160 or SB 767-25-0160 Revision 1.

*Note: FAA AD 92-08-05 Amdt 39-8216 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 23 September 1992.

Background: The FAA requires tie rod replacement following a determination that certain aft galley installations do not meet the requirements for emergency landing conditions. The effective date required by the FAA is extended by one month.

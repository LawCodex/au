# AD/BEECH 55/34 Amdt 1 - Landing Gear Shock Strut Piston

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 55, 58 and 95-55 (Baron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 55/34**

**Amdt 1**

**Landing Gear Shock Strut Piston 1/74**

Applicability: All model 95-B55 with S/Nos. TC-1552 to TC-1607 except TC-1557, TC-1558, TC- 1559 and TC-1604.

All model E55 and E55A with S/Nos. TE-914 to TE-940 except TE-919, TE-920 and TE-938.

All model 58 & 58A with S/Nos. TH-325 to TH-383 except TH-336, TH-337, TH-363 and TH-382.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0612-200.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1974 unless already accomplished.

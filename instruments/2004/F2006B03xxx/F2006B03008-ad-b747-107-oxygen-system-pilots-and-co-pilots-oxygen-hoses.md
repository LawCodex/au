# AD/B747/107 - Oxygen System - Pilots and Co-Pilots Oxygen Hoses

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B747/107 Oxygen System - Pilots and Co-Pilots Oxygen Hoses

Applicability: Model 747-400 series airplanes listed in the Requirement Document.

## 4/92

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 92-02-06 Amdt 39- 8142.

*Note: Boeing Alert SB 747-35A2075 Revision 1 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 31 May 1992 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: This Directive requires inspection, and if necessary, replacement or rerouting of the oxygen hose supplying oxygen to the Captain’s and First Officer’s oxygen mask stowage boxes.

# AD/B747/50 - Inboard Pylon Upper Link

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B747/50 Inboard Pylon Upper Link 6/86** Applicability: All models, line No’s 1 to 616, with JT9D-3 or -7, or CF6-45 or -50 engine struts. Requirement: Inspect in accordance with:

FAA AD 86-07-06 (Sections A and C) or,

2\. Boeing Service Bulletin 747-54-2111, Revision 1.

Compliance: As detailed in Requirement Document No. 1. until modified in accordance with Requirement Document No.2.

Background: Cracks have occurred in the forward lug of the inboard pylon upper link. If not corrected, this could result in separation of an engine from an aircraft.

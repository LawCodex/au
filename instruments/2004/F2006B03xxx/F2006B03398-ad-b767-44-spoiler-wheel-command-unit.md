# AD/B767/44 - Spoiler Wheel Command Unit

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B767/44 Spoiler Wheel Command Unit 10/90

Applicability: Line no’s 1 thru 243, 257, 262, 263, 269, 273 thru 275, 282, 285, 289 and 291. Requirement: Boeing SB 767-27-0085 R1 or later revision.

*Note: FAA AD 90-10-07 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1992.

Background: To prevent uncommanded extension of three flight spoilers.

# AD/B747/45 - Aft Pressure Bulkhead Damage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/45 Aft Pressure Bulkhead Damage 2/86

DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 85-22-12. Compliance: Prior to 20 December 1985.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of aft bulkhead damage and repairs which are unairworthy.

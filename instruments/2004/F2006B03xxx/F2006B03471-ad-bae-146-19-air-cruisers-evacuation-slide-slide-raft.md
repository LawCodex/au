# AD/BAe 146/19 - Air Cruisers Evacuation Slide/Slide Raft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 146 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BAe 146/19 Air Cruisers Evacuation Slide/Slide Raft 9/90

DM

Applicability: All models equipped with slide or slide raft systems that are pre-mod HCM 70440B. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 25-198.

*Note: UK CAA AD 008-05-90 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1990.

Background: There has been a report of a slide failing to deploy correctly due to an incorrectly routed slide valve release cable.

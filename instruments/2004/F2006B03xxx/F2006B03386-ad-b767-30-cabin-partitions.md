# AD/B767/30 - Cabin Partitions

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 767 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B767/30 Cabin Partitions 13/89** Applicability: Model 767 aeroplanes as listed in Boeing Alert SB 767-25A0135 dated 6 July 1989. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 767-25A0135.

*Note: FAA AD 89-19-03 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 January 1990 unless previously accomplished.

Background: Country of origin AD. Defective attachment bolts allowed collapse of cabin partition on which an attendant seat is mounted.

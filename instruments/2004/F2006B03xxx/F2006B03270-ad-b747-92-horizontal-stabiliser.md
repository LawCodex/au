# AD/B747/92 - Horizontal Stabiliser

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/92 Horizontal Stabiliser 6/91

Applicability: Model 747 series with line numbers 002 through 226.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-05-17 Amdt 39- 6917.

*Note: Boeing Alert SB 747-53A 2332 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 13 June 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: The FAA has received reports of additional cracking detected by the inspections required by a current Airworthiness Directive. This condition, if not corrected, could result in loss of the horizontal stabilizer. Effective date required by the FAA is extended by two months.

# AD/B747/77 - APU Fire Detection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 747 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B747/77 APU Fire Detection 5/90

Applicability: All model Boeing 747-400 series aeroplanes as listed in Boeing SB 747-26-2135 Revision 1, or later revision.

Requirement: Modify the APU electrical power circuitry in accordance with Boeing SB 747-26- 2135 Revision 1 or later revision.

Compliance: Prior to 17 August 1990.

Background: In the current configuration if the main battery switch is turned off whilst the APU is operating, power to the APU fire detection system is removed. The APU goes through a 60 second cool down cycle with the fire detection system inoperative.

Compliance with this AD is required to correct this unacceptable condition.

# Extradition (Kyrgyzstan) Regulations 2004

Statutory Rules 2004 No. 3681

I, PHILIP MICHAEL JEFFERY, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Extradition Act 1988*.

Dated 16 December 2004

P. M. JEFFERY

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command

CHRISTOPHER MARTIN ELLISON

Minister for Justice and Customs

###### 1 Name of Regulations

These Regulations are the *Extradition (Kyrgyzstan) Regulations 2004*.

###### 2 Commencement

These Regulations commence on the date of their notification in the *Gazette*.

###### 3 Definitions

***Act*** means the *Extradition Act 1988*.

***Kyrgyzstan*** means the Kyrgyz Republic.

###### 4 Declaration that Kyrgyzstan is an extradition country

Kyrgyzstan is declared to be an extradition country.

###### 5 Application of the Act in relation to Kyrgyzstan

For the purposes of the application of the Act in relation to Kyrgyzstan and relying on paragraph 11 (1) (b) and subsection 11 (2) of the Act, paragraph 17 (2) (a) of the Act is modified by omitting ‘45 days’ and substituting ‘90 days’.

**Note**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 23 December 2004.

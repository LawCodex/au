# AD/BEECH 65/40 Amdt 3 - Flight Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/40**

**Amdt 3**

**Flight Controls 3/89**

Applicability: All model Queen Air B80 with S/Nos. LD-441 to LD-511, 70 series wit S/Nos LB-31 through LB-35 and all 65, 65-80, 70 and 80 series fitted with a rudder shipped from Beech during period 1 May 1973 to 1 July 1976.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0629-150, Rev. 11.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1979. Background: Amendment 3 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

# AD/BEECH 95/7 - Operating Restriction Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 95 (Travelair) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 95/7 Operating Restriction Placard 2/69

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install a placard on the leading edge of the fuel selector panel with the following wording:

“USE AUXILIARY FUEL TANKS AND CROSSFEED IN LEVEL FLIGHT ONLY.

TURNING TYPE TAKE-OFFS OR TAKE-OFFS IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING FAST TAXI TURNS ARE PROHIBITED".

Compliance: Before 1 September 1971.

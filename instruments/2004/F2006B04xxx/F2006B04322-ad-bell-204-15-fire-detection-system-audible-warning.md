# AD/BELL 204/15 - Fire Detection System - Audible Warning

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 204 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 204/15 Fire Detection System - Audible Warning NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify the engine fire detection system to include an audible warning. The warning shall be unmistakeable from any other audible warning and shall be capable of being manually silenced.

Compliance: Before 1 January 1975.

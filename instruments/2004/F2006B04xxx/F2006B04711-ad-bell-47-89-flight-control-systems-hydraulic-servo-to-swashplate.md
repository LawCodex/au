**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

## AD/BELL 47/89 Flight Control Systems - Hydraulic Servo to

**Swashplate Control Plate Attachment Bolts**

Applicability: All 47G, J and K series aircraft. Document: Bell Alert Service Bulletin 47-83-8. Compliance: By 31 December 1983.

## 10/83

Background: There have been two reported failures on AN Standard bolts, installed in the above flight control application where BHTI standard bolts should have been used.

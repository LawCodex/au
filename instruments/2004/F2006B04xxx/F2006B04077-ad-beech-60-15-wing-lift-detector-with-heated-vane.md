# AD/BEECH 60/15 - Wing Lift Detector With Heated Vane

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/15 Wing Lift Detector With Heated Vane 2/72

Applicability: Model 60 with S/Nos. P-3 to P-122 and P-124 to P-126, and Model A60 with S/Nos. P-123, P-127 to P-151, P-153, P-154, P-157 and P-159 to P-162, used for flight into known icing conditions.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0431-355 and Beechcraft SI No. 0430- 355, Rev. 2.

Compliance: Before 31 October 1972.

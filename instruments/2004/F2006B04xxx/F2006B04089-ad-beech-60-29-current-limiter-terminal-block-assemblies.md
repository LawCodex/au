# AD/BEECH 60/29 - Current Limiter Terminal Block Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/29 Current Limiter Terminal Block Assemblies 10/76

Applicability: All models B60, S/Nos. P-272 thru P-307, P-309 thru P-326, P-328 thru P-330. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0754-350.

*Note: FAA AD 76-13-05 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 October 1976.

# AD/BN-2/42 Amdt 3 - Elevator Trim Tab Cracking

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/42**

**Amdt 3**

**Elevator Trim Tab Cracking 4/86**

Applicability: All models listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2/S.B 142 Issue 4. Compliance: 1. Modify prior to the issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

2\. Inspect in accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: Issue 4 of the Requirement Document has extended the post mod. inspection out to every 100 hours T.I.S.

# AD/BEECH 95/15 - Fuel System Placards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 95 (Travelair) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 95/15 Fuel System Placards 7/73

Applicability: Models 95, B95, B95A, D95A, E95 with S/Nos TD-1 to TD-721. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI 0492-281 or 0484-281 as applicable. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 July 1973.

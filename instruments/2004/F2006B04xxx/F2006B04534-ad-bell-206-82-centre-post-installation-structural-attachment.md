# AD/BELL 206/82 - Centre Post Installation - Structural Attachment - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/82 Centre Post Installation - Structural**

**Attachment - Inspection and Modification**

**2/80**

Applicability: All model 206L with S/Nos. 45004 to 45153 and 46601 to 46617. All model 206L-1 with S/Nos. 45154 to 45299.

Document: Bell Technical Bulletin No. 206L-79-41. Compliance: As per the requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that cracks have been found during periodic inspections of the centre post structural attachment area.

# AD/CAP 10/2 - Fuel System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Avions Mudry Cap Series Aeroplanes

AD/CAP 10/2 Fuel System 2/92

Applicability: All CAP 10B aircraft from S/N 01 to 208 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Avions Mudry & CIE Service Bulletin CAP10B-28-001.

*Note: DGAC AD 91-112(A) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: Loss of engine power through air entering the fuel system via the inverted flight valve hose has been reported.

# AD/BEECH 90/90 - Leading Edge Fuel Tanks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/90 Leading Edge Fuel Tanks 12/93

Applicability: All B90, C90 and C90A aircraft S/N LJ-489 thru to LJ-1318 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory Service Bulletin No 2475.

*Note: FAA AD 93-17-01 amdt 39-8671 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously carried out within 150 hours’ time in service from 11 November 1993.

Background: Misalignment of the fuel line resulting in cracking of the fuel cell nipples have been reported. Failure to detect and repair a damaged fuel cell nipple could result in fuel leaks and possible aircraft fire.

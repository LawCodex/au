# AD/BEECH 90/50 - Goodyear Fuel Cells - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/50 Goodyear Fuel Cells - Inspection 6/78

Applicability: Models 65-90, 65-A90, B90 and C90 with S/Nos. LJ-1 to LJ-608; E90 with S/Nos.

LW-1 to LW-73, fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0895.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 June 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding twelve months.

# AD/BELL 206/76 Amdt 2 - Horizontal Stabiliser - Inspection and Modification or Retirement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/76**

**Amdt 2**

**Horizontal Stabiliser - Inspection and Modification or Retirement**

**11/81**

Applicability: All model 206L and 206L-1 with S/Nos. listed in Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-23 and fitted with a horizontal stabiliser assembly of affected P/N detailed in that document.

Document: Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-23.

Compliance: 1. Inspect at each daily or overnight inspection until modified IAW with Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-23.

*Note: Pilot in command may perform and certify for this inspection.*

2\. Retire or modify in accordance with the requirements of Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-23, not later than 31 March 1982.

Background: Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-23 extends the requirement to addition P/N stabilisers and provides for mandatory retirement of certain P/N items.

# AD/BN-2/34 - Rudder Bar Lower Mounting - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/34 Rudder Bar Lower Mounting - Inspection NK

and Modification

Applicability: All model BN-2A which have exceeded 1000 hours total time in service, except those which have incorporated Mod. No. NB/M/463 Part A.

Document: Britten Norman SB No. BN-2/SB.74, Issue 2.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 24 May 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service until modified. Modify not later than 31 August 1976.

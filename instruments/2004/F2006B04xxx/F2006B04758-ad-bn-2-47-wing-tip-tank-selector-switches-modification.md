# AD/BN-2/47 - Wing Tip Tank Selector Switches - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/47 Wing Tip Tank Selector Switches - Modification 5/83

Applicability: All models fitted with wing tip tanks. Document: Britten Norman SB No. BN-2/SB 157. Compliance: Before further flight after 31st Dec. 1983.

Background: Arising from recommendations contained in the reports of two accidents, the wing tip fuel tank selector switches, system placarding, and selection indicator lamps have been changed by the aircraft manufacturer.

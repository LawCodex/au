# AD/BEECH 65/39 Amdt 1 - Nacelle Interior Fire Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/39**

**Amdt 1**

**Nacelle Interior Fire Seal 3/89**

Applicability: All Models Queen Air 65, A65 and A65-8200 with S/Nos. L-1, L-2, L-6, LF-7, LF-8 & LC-1 to LC-335. Models 65-80, 65-A80, 65-A80-8800 and 65-B80 with S/Nos.

LD-1 to LD-475. Model 65-88 with S/Nos. LP-1 to LP-26, LP-28 & LP-30 to LP-47.

Model 70 with S/Nos LB-1 and on.

Requirement: Install Beech Nacelle Interior Fire Seal Kits P/N 65-9008-1, for all listed models except Model 65-88, for which use P/N 65-9008-3.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 September 1974. Background: Amendment 1 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

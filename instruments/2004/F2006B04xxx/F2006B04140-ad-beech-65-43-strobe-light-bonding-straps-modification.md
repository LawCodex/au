# AD/BEECH 65/43 - Strobe Light Bonding Straps - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 65/43 Strobe Light Bonding Straps - Modification 1/75

Applicability: All model 65-B80 S/Nos. LD-441 thru LD-490 fitted with Grimes P/N 30-0467-1 or Grimes P/N 30-0467-3 Strobe Light Assemblies.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0655-362, Rev. 1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1975.

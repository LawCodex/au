# AD/BO 105/8 - Tail Boom Skin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BO 105 Series Helicopters

AD/BO 105/8 Tail Boom Skin 3/87

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with MBB BO 105 Alert Bulletin No 15 Revision 1. Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Background: Cracked tail boom skins have been found in service. LBA Directive 76-317/2 MBB refers.

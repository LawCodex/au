# AD/CESSNA 150/13 - Glove Box

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 150, F150, 152 & F152 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 150/13 Glove Box 11/67

Applicability: All model 150G and 150H.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the forward upper edge of the glove box for adequate clearance from the aileron control chain and sprocket with the control wheel in the elevator full up position.

*Note: This inspection may be performed and certified by the pilot.*

2\. Modify the glove box mountings by bracing to ensure there is no possibility of fouling controls or alternatively remove the glove box.

Compliance: Inspect before further flight. Modify within 5 hours time in service from 23 November 1967.

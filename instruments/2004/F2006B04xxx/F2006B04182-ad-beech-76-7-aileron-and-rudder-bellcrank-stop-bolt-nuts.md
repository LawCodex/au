# AD/BEECH 76/7 - Aileron and Rudder Bellcrank Stop Bolt Nuts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 76 (Duchess) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 76/7 Aileron and Rudder Bellcrank Stop Bolt Nuts 12/79

Applicability: As detailed in Beechcraft SI No. 1087. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1087.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service from 16 November 1979.

Background: There is a possibility that non self-locking nuts are fitted to the affected stop bolts.

# AD/BN-2/68 - Rudder Lower Hinge Doubler

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/68 Rudder Lower Hinge Doubler 6/99

Applicability: Model BN2, BN2A, BN2B, and BN2T Islander aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Britten Norman Mandatory Service Bulletin BN2/SB.240. Compliance: Before 17 July 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 17 June 1999.

Background: The manufacturer received reports of damage to the rudder lower hinge. This Directive requires installation of a doubler at the left and right side of the rudder lower hinge bracket.

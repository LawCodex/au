# AD/CASA/20 - Corrosion of Rudder Torsion Tube

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/20 Corrosion of Rudder Torsion Tube 9/96

DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with CASA SB 212-27-34.

*Note: Spanish DGAC AD 06/96 refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 August 1996.

Background: Corrosion has been detected in the rudder lower shaft and support structure. This has been caused by water entry through the space between the upper bearing and the fuselage structure.

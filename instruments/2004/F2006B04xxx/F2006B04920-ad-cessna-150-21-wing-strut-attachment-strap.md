**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 150, F150, 152 & F152 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 150/21 Wing Strut Attachment Strap 10/73

Applicability: Model 150 with S/Nos. 15074635 to 15074799 and Model A150 with S/Nos.

A1500415 to A1500427.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE 73-20. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 24 September 1973.

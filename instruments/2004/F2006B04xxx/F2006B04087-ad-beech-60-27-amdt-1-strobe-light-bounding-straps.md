# AD/BEECH 60/27 Amdt 1 - Strobe Light Bounding Straps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 60/27**

**Amdt 1**

**Strobe Light Bounding Straps 11/74**

Applicability: All model A60 & B60, S/Nos. P-223 thru P-307, P-309 and P-310, fitted with Grimes P/N 30-0467-1 or Grimes P/N 30-0467-3 strobe light assemblies.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0655-362, Rev. 1. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1975.

# AD/BELL 47/85 - Engine Mount Strut Rod Ends - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 47/85 Engine Mount Strut Rod Ends - Replacement 3/81

Applicability: All models 47 equipped with Soloy turbine conversion. Document: Soloy Conversions Ltd. SB 03-660.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1981.

Background: Replacement of stainless steel rod ends is necessary to assure the continued airworthiness of engine mount struts.

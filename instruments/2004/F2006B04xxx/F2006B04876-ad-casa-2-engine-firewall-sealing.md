# AD/CASA/2 - Engine Firewall Sealing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/2 Engine Firewall Sealing 12/82

Applicability: Model 212 with S/Nos. as detailed in Casa SB No. 212-73-03, Rev. 1. Document: Casa SB No. 212-73-03, Rev. 1.

Compliance: If not already accomplished within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1982. Background: Manufacturer’s requirement to improve fireproofing at the engine firewall.

# AD/BELL 206/14 Amdt 2 - Cyclic Control Bellcrank Supports - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/14**

**Amdt 2**

**Cyclic Control Bellcrank Supports - Inspection and Replacement**

**8/69**

Applicability: All models equipped with magnesium Cyclic Bellcrank Supports P/N 206-001-521. Requirement: 1. Inspect the right and left hand support assemblies as follows:

(a) Remove the upper forward cowling, P/N 206-061-801, to expose the hydraulic power cylinders.

(b) Inspect the attachment of the support assemblies to the fuselage for security. If a support assembly is found to be loose torque the NAS 1304-9 bolt to 80 to 100 inch pounds and the NAS 1305-9 bolt to 120 to 145 inch pounds.

(c) Inspect visually the right and left hand support assemblies P/No. 206-001-521- 5 and 206-001-521-6, for cracks in the spotfaced areas around the attachment bolts and in the fillet radii between the base and vertical section of the supports, using a bright flashlight or equivalent.

*Note: Both the forward and aft flange fillet radii and spotfaced areas must be inspected.*

(d) Smooth out any nicks or corrosion visible in the forward and aft flange fillet radii and spotfaced areas using a fine file and crocus cloth.

2\. Remove and replace the support assembly before further flight if cracks are found. Inspect the cyclic control rigging in accordance with paras. 4-30 of the Model 206A Maintenance and Overhaul Instructions when a support assembly is replaced.

3\. Replace the right and left hand magnesium support assemblies with new assemblies.

Compliance: For Paras. 1 and 2 - At each daily or overnight inspection.

For Paras. 3 - Within 100 hours time in service after 29 August 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until replaced with new aluminium support assemblies P/N 206-00-544 (Bell SB No. 206A-11 dated 9 May 1969 refers).

Background: AD/Agusta 206/11 equivalent.

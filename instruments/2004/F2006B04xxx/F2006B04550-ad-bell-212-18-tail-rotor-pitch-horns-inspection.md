# AD/BELL 212/18 - Tail Rotor Pitch Horns - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron 212 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 212/18 Tail Rotor Pitch Horns - Inspection 10/77

Applicability: All models fitted with pitch change horns P/N 212-010-716-5 or -9. Document: 1. Bell SB No. 212-77-7; or

2\. FAA AD 77-17-03.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 15 September 1977 and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 100 hours.

# AD/BO 105/3 - Tail Rotor Gearbox Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BO 105 Series Helicopters

AD/BO 105/3 Tail Rotor Gearbox Attachment 7/86

DM

Applicability: All model BO 105 up to and including S/No 780. Requirement: Action in accordance with MBB Alert Bulletin No. 28.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 20 June 1986 but no later than 31 July 1986.

Background: Inspection of bolts supplied to the manufacturer has revealed flaws necessitating the replacement of the existing bolts.

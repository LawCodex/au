# AD/BELL 47/66 Amdt 4 - Stabilizer Bar Tubes and Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

## AD/BELL 47/66

**Amdt 4**

Applicability: All models.

## Stabilizer Bar Tubes and Assembly 10/86

Document: Bell SB No. 47-(04-2)-73-1, Rev. A. Kawasaki-Bell S.B. KSB-Bell 338. Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

*Note: This inspection may be accomplished by the pilot-in-command, in which case the precise A.D. reference shall be entered in a certification made on the maintenance release coincident with that for the daily inspection. This inspection may also be performed and certified by any Licensed Aircraft Maintenance Engineer.*

Background: To detect cracked stabilizer bar tubes and/or tie rods that are in tension. This Directive has been amended to include Kawasaki-Bell Helicopters.

# AD/BELL 205/48 - Fuselage Main Beams Splice - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron 205 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 205/48 Fuselage Main Beams Splice - Modification 4/80

Applicability: All model 205A-1 with S/Nos. 30001 to 30287 and 30289 to 30296. Documents: 1. Bell Technical Bulletin No. 205-79-34; or

2\. FAA AD 80-03-07 Amdt. 39-3681.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 3 April 1980.

Background: At manufacture aluminium rivets were installed in the subject splice instead of Monel rivets.

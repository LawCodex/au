# AD/BN-2/58 - 70 Amp DC Generation System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/58 70 Amp DC Generation System 4/96 DM

Applicability: All BN2 Series aircraft with either Modification NB/M/1148, “70 amp DC Generation System”, incorporated or Modification NB/M/1571, “Introduction of Increased Rated Diode - 70 amp DC Generation System” not incorporated.

Requirement: Replace diodes on STBD and PORT GEN Switches in accordance with Pilatus Britten-Norman Mandatory Service Bulletin BN 2-228 Issue 1.

*Note: Compliance with this Service Bulletin is classified Mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 14 February 1996.

Background: Incorporation of this Directive will increase the circuit reliability of the 70 amp DC Generation System.

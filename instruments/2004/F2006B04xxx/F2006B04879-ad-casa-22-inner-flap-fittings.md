# AD/CASA/22 - Inner Flap Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/22 Inner Flap Fittings 9/96

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with CASA SB 212-57-10.

*Note: Spanish DGAC AD 03/96 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 15 August 1996, thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours until modified.

Background: Cracking has been detected in the inner flap fittings. This Directive is issued to prevent an unsafe condition developing and going undetected.

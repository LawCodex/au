# AD/BELL 205/65 - Vertical Fin Spar Cap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron 205 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 205/65 Vertical Fin Spar Cap 12/99 DM

Applicability: Model 205A-1 helicopters with Vertical Fin Spar Cap PNo 212-030-447-001 or -101 fitted.

Model 205B helicopters with Vertical Fin Spar Cap PNo 212-030-447-101. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 99-18-02

Amendment 39-11268.

*Note: The FAA calls out Bell Helicopter Textron Inc. Alert Service Bulletin 205-98- 70 Revision A dated 21 Sept 1998. However, the FAA AD requires inspections and maintenance beyond that of the Bell ASB.*

Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document, but with a revised with effect date of 6 October 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 6 October 1999.

Background: The FAA have issued an AD that inspects the vertical fin spar cap for corrosion, cracking and disbonding. The FAA justification cites 5 accidents involving helicopters of a similar design.

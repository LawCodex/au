# AD/BN-2/41 Amdt 1 - Elevator and Flap Control Rods - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/41**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator and Flap Control Rods - Inspection NK**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Inspect elevator and flap control rods for corrosion and rectify in accordance with Britten Norman SB No. BN-2/SB.101.

2\. Bonding clips and leads may be removed completely provided that:

(a) Radio system still performs satisfactorily; and

(b) Control rod fork end bearings are of all metal construction.

*Note: If bearings are fitted with “plastic” inserts it will be necessary for the bonding clips and leads to be replaced.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1977.

# AD/BELL 206/89 - Emergency Flotation Kit Valve Assembly P/N 206-073-915-5 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/89 Emergency Flotation Kit Valve Assembly**

**P/N 206-073-915-5 - Inspection**

**8/81**

Applicability: All models 206L and 206L-1 fitted with emergency flotation kit P/Nos. referenced in Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-21.

Document: Bell Alert SB No. 206L-81-21.

Compliance: Before 1 July 1981, unless already accomplished.

Background: The manufacturer has advised of a recent occurrence involving an overwater emergency in which the emergency floats malfunctioned due to a defective valve of the referenced P/N.

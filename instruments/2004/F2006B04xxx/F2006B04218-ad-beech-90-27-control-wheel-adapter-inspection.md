# AD/BEECH 90/27 - Control Wheel Adapter - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/27 Control Wheel Adapter - Inspection 6/72

Applicability: All models.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0497-155.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 10 May 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours’ time in service until adapter P/No. 99-524036-33 is installed.

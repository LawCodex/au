# AD/BEECH 65/33 Amdt 1 - Placard - Emergency Engine Shut-Down Procedure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/33**

**Amdt 1**

**Placard - Emergency Engine Shut-Down Procedure**

**3/89**

Applicability: Model 65 with S/Nos L-1, L-2, L-6, LF-7, LF-8 and LC-1 to LC-239.

Model A65 & A65-8200 with S/Nos LC-240 to LC-335.

Models 65-80, 65-A80, 65-A80-8800 and 65-B80 with S/Nos LD-1 to LD-456.

Model 65-88 with S/Nos LP-1 to LP-47. Model 70 with S/Nos LB-1 through LB-35.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0509-016. Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 23 August 1972. Background: Amendment 1 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 Aircraft.

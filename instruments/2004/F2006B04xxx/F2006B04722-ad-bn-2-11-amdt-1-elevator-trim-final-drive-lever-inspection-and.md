# AD/BN-2/11 Amdt 1 - Elevator Trim Final Drive Lever - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Trim Final Drive Lever - Inspection NK and Modification**

Applicability: All models not incorporating Modification No. NB/M/394. Document: Britten-Norman SB No. BN-2/SB.24.

Compliance: Inspect within 10 hours time in service after 23 February 1970 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service until modified.

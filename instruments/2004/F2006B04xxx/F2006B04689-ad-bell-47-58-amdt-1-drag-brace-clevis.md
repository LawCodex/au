# AD/BELL 47/58 Amdt 1 - Drag Brace Clevis

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 47/58**

**Amdt 1**

**Drag Brace Clevis 11/67**

Applicability: All model 47 series with wooden main rotor blades.

Requirement: 1. Remove drag brace clevises P/N 47-110-235-1 and inspect for cracking in the root of the first six threads from the fork end of the clevis before re-installation. The inspection is to be performed using a fluorescent magnetic particle technique such as Magnaglow or equivalent with residual longitudinal magnetisation.

2\. Alternatively, replace existing clevises with -

(a) new parts,

(b) serviceable used parts that have either not been involved with main rotor damage or, if so, have been inspected in accordance with para. 1 above.

Compliance: 1. Before further flight following damage to the main rotor blades sustained through contact with foreign objects whilst rotating.

2\. For parts inspected in accordance with paragraph 1 above, repeat the inspection at between 40 and 50 hours time in service following re-installation.

# AD/BELL 206/41 - Vertical Fin - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 206/41 Vertical Fin - Inspection 2/73

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. detailed in requirement documents with vertical fin P/N 206-020-113-5, -7 or -9.

Document: Bell SB No. 206-01-73-1, or Agusta SB No. BT206-57.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 8 January 1973, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service until incorporation of Bell SL No. 206-203.

Background: AD/Agusta 206/29 equivalent.

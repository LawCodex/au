# AD/CESSNA 170/28 - Wing Strut Attachment Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 170, 172, F172, FR172 and 175 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 170/28 Wing Strut Attachment Strap 9/73

Applicability: All model 172 with S/No. 17261664 to 17261808. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE 73-20.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 24 September 1973.

# AD/BEECH 65/17 Amdt 1 - Plastic Control Wheel - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/17**

**Amdt 1**

**Plastic Control Wheel - Inspection 12/70**

Applicability: All models fitted with non-reinforced plastic control wheels.

Requirement: Visually inspect all plastic non-reinforced control wheels for cracking. Control wheels in which cracks are present shall be replaced with a serviceable item before further flight. It is recommended that replacements should be of the metal reinforced type.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service after 31 December 1970.

# AD/BEECH 95/1 Amdt 2 - Plastic Control Wheel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 95 (Travelair) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 95/1**

**Amdt 2**

**Plastic Control Wheel 12/70**

Applicability: All models fitted with non-reinforced plastic control wheels.

Requirement: Visually inspect all plastic non-reinforced control wheels for cracking. Control wheels in which cracks are present shall be replaced with a serviceable item before further flight. It is recommended that replacements should be of the metal reinforced type.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service after 1 September 1971.

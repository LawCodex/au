# AD/CESSNA 170/29 Amdt 1 - Aft Fuselage Bulkhead Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 170, 172, F172, FR172 and 175 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 170/29**

**Amdt 1**

**Aft Fuselage Bulkhead Assembly 2/74**

Applicability: All models 172 with S/Nos. 17260759 to 17261495, F17200905 to F17201034, FR17200351 to FR17200440.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE73-37. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1974.

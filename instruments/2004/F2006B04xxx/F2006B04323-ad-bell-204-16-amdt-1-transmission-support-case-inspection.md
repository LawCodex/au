# AD/BELL 204/16 Amdt 1 - Transmission Support Case - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 204 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 204/16**

**Amdt 1**

**Transmission Support Case - Inspection NK**

Applicability: All model 204B.

Requirement: Visually inspect the two lift link attachment lugs on the transmission main support case for cracking.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service after 17 July 1974.

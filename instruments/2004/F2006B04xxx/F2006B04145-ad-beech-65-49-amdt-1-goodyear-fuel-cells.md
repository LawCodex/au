# AD/BEECH 65/49 Amdt 1 - Goodyear Fuel Cells

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/49**

**Amdt 1**

**Goodyear Fuel Cells 3/89**

Applicability: Models 65, A65 and A65-8200 with S/Nos. LC-148 to LC-335; 65-80, 65-A80, 65- A80-8800 and 65-B80 with S/Nos. LD-56 to LD-472; 65-88 with S/Nos. LP-1 to LP- 26, LP-28 and LP-30 to LP-47; 70 with S/Nos LB-1 to LB-35; fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0895.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 June 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding twelve months.

Background: This amendment consolidates Queen Air 65 and 70 series aircraft. Previous compliance with AD/BEECH 70/19 is equivalent compliance with this AD.

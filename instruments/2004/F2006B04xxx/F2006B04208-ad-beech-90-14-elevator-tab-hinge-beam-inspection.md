# AD/BEECH 90/14 - Elevator Tab Hinge Beam - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/14 Elevator Tab Hinge Beam - Inspection 7/68

Applicability: All models in excess of 1000 hours total time in service.

Requirement: Inspect the upper and lower flanges of the LH and RH elevator tab hinge beam P/N 50-610000-94 for cracking along the rivet line.

Compliance: Forthwith and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 200 hours’ time in service.

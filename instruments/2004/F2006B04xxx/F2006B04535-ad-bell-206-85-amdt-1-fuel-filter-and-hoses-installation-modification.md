# AD/BELL 206/85 Amdt 1 - Fuel Filter and Hoses Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/85**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuel Filter and Hoses Installation - Modification**

**9/81**

Applicability: All models Bell 206L-1 with S/Nos. 45154 to 45448 except as noted in Bell ASB 206L-80-13, and single pilot IFR equipped models listed in ASB 206L-81-19.

Document: Bell SB 206L-80-13 & for single pilot IFR helicopters Bell SB 206L-81-19.

*Note: FAA AD 81-06-05 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1981.

Background: The Airworthiness Directive is amended to include a modification to the freewheeling vent hose on single pilot IFR equipped helicopters.

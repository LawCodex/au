# AD/BELL 47/75 Amdt 2 - Control Tubes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

## AD/BELL 47/75

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Control Tubes 10/86

Requirement: 1. Visually inspect the control tubes referenced in Bell SB 47-11-73-1 and Kawasaki- Bell S.B. KSB-Bell 334 for evidence of corrosion pits, particularly in the lower four (4) inches of each tube assembly; or

2\. Inspect the control tubes referenced in Bell SB 47-11-73-1 and Kawasaki-Bell S.B. KSB-Bell 334 for evidence of corrosion using a suitable radiographic technique; or

3\. Remove the lower end fittings from the control tubes and visually inspect the internal surface of the tube for evidence of corrosion.

Compliance: 1. Within 25 hours time in service after 31 December 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

2 & 3. Within 25 hours time in service after 31 December 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 3 years.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of failure of fore and aft control tubes caused by corrosion. This Directive has been amended to include Kawasaki-Bell Helicopters.

# AD/BELL 412/30 - Swashplate Support Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 412 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 412/30 Swashplate Support Assembly 4/92

TX

Applicability: All model 412 helicopters if fitted with Swashplate Support Assembly P/N 412-010- 453-101.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bell Alert Service Bulletin 412-92-57 Rev A and 412-92- 58.

Compliance: 1. For compliance with ASB 412-97-57 Rev A,

Part I Daily after receipt of this Directive, and

Part II Within 10 hours time in service after 10 February 1992; and

2\. For compliance with ASB 412-92-58, before further flight after receipt of this directive.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of cracking in the stainless steel Swashplate Support Assembly.

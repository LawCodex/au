# AD/BELL 206/80 - Fuel Feed Tube and Vent Tube - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 206/80 Fuel Feed Tube and Vent Tube - Replacement 1/80

Applicability: All models 206L and 206L-1. Document: Bell Alert SB No. 206L-79-7.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1980.

Background: Moisture accumulation in the insulating material adjacent to the fuel feed tube and a fuel vent tube can cause corrosion to the extent of the tube wall penetration.

# AD/CASA/6 - Fuel Shut-Off Valves - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/6 Fuel Shut-Off Valves - Replacement 9/83

Applicability: All Casa 212 models as referenced in requirement document. Document: Casa SB No. 212-28-15.

Compliance: Before further flight after 31st August 1983.

Background: The manufacturer advises that failures have been discovered in the operation of fuel shut-off valves.

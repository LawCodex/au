# AD/BELL 47/97 - Main Rotor Flight Control System Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 47/97 Main Rotor Flight Control System Bolts 2/90

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bell Alert Service Bulletin No 47-89-16.

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release after 30 March 1990 or by 30 June 1990, whichever occurs first. Thereafter, reinspect at intervals not exceeding 24 months calendar time.

Background: Bell have advised that some of the rotating control bolts have been found corroded, fretting and/or with excessive wear on the bolt shank.

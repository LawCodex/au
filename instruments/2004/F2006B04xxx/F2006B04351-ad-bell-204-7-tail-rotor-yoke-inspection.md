# AD/BELL 204/7 - Tail Rotor Yoke - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 204 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 204/7 Tail Rotor Yoke - Inspection NK

Applicability: All 204B models fitted with tail rotor yoke P/N 204-010-781-9 or 204-010-781-5.

Requirement: Inspect yoke assembly P/N 204-010-781-5 or 204-010-781-9 by magnetic particle method with particular emphasis on the threaded portion of the yoke spindle.

Maximum amperage 1400 amps. DC or 1100 amps. AC. Do not exceed 4200 ampere turns.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service, after 6 October 1968.

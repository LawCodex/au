# AD/CESSNA 170/33 Amdt 1 - Flap Actuator

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 170, 172, F172, FR172 and 175 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 170/33**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Actuator 10/82**

Applicability: Model 172RG with S/Nos. 172RG0001 to 172RG0005, 172RG0007 to 172RG0012, 172RG0014, 172RG0016 and 172RG0019.

Requirement: Modify the flap actuator assembly P/N C301002-0108 by installing a new mechanical stop nut in accordance with Cessna Service Information Letter SE79-57, or install a replacement flap actuator assembly, P/N C301002-0110.

*Note: Although Cessna SIL SE79-59 does not include the model 172RG, the actuator modification for this model is the same as that provided for the model 152.*

FAA AD 80-01-06, Amendment 39-3649 refers.

Compliance: If not already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1982.

Background: This amendment includes the option to replace the actuator with a new type which provides shorter travel. Overtravel may lead to an asymmetric flap condition.

# AD/BN-2/31 Amdt 1 - Engine Mounting Frames - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/31**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Mounting Frames - Inspection 5/74**

Applicability: All models with engine mounting frames P/Nos. NB-20-G- 1901, NB-20-G-5231, NB-51-H-1021 and NB-20-D-5209.

Document: Britten Norman SB BN-2/SB.72.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 10 hours time in service after 31 May 1974.

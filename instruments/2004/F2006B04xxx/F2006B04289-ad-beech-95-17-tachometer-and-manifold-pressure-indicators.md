# AD/BEECH 95/17 - Tachometer and Manifold Pressure Indicators - Calibration and Engine Limitation Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 95 (Travelair) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEECH 95/17 Tachometer and Manifold Pressure

**Indicators - Calibration and Engine Limitation Placard**

Applicability: Models 95, B95, B95A, D95A, E95 with S/Nos TD-2 to TD-721.

## 9/75

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SIs No 0723-241, or Hartzell Propeller SB No 107A.

*Note: FAA AD 75-13-02 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Placard installation and instrument and governor calibration to be performed within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 September 1975.

2\. Instrument calibrations to be performed at intervals not exceeding 2 years thereafter.

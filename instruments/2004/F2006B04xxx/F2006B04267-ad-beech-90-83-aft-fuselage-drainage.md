# AD/BEECH 90/83 - Aft Fuselage Drainage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/83 Aft Fuselage Drainage 8/90

Applicability: Models 65-90, 65-A90, B90, C90 and C90A with S/Nos LJ-1 through LJ-1222.

Models E90, F90, H90 all S/Nos.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2312.

*Note: FAA AD 90-08-17 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to exceeding 100 hours’ time in service after 9 August 1990, or at the next scheduled inspection for issue of Maintenance Release after 9 August 1990, whichever occurs later.

Background: Country of origin AD. A blocked drainage system may allow water to accumulate, freeze and restrict movement of the flight controls.

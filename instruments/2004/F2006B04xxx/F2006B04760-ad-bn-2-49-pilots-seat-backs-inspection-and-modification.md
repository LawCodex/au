# AD/BN-2/49 - Pilots Seat Backs - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/49 Pilots Seat Backs - Inspection and Modification 7/84

Applicability: All models with individual pilots sliding seat units - P/N NBC-010-H-001 (LH) and P/N NBC-010-H-002 (RH) or NBC-012-H-001, not embodying mod. NB/M/1217.

Document: Britten Norman SB No. BN2/SB163.

Compliance: INSPECT: Prior to the issue of the next maintenance release after 31 July 1984 and

thereafter at each issue of a new maintenance release.

MODIFY: Before 30 November 1984.

Background: Some individual seat units, installed in the flight compartment, have been affected by distortion and fracture at the attachment of the folding back to the seat frame.

# AD/BEECH 56/17 - Goodyear Fuel Cells

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 56TC (Turbo Baron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 56/17 Goodyear Fuel Cells 6/78

Applicability: Models 56TC and A56TC with S/Nos. TG-2 to TG-94, fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0895.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 June 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding twelve months.

# AD/CESSNA 150/2 Amdt 1 - Main Undercarriage U Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 150, F150, 152 & F152 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 150/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Undercarriage “U” Bolts 11/67**

Applicability: All models fitted with “U” bolts P/N 0441161 types.

Requirement: 1. Visual: Inspect the main undercarriage “U” bolts P/N 0441161 in situ for cracks and other damage and check that the “U” bolt retaining nuts are torqued to the values specified in the Cessna Service Manual.

2\. Magnetic Particle: Remove the main undercarriage “U” bolts P/N 0441161 and inspect by the magnetic particle method for cracks particularly in the radius of the bends and in the root of the threads.

Compliance: Visual: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Magnetic Particle: At intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

# AD/CASA/15 - Flight Idle Stops

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/15 Flight Idle Stops 9/92 TX

Applicability: All model C-212- series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA Telegraphic AD T92-13-51.

*Note: CASA SB 212-76-07 provides service data on the backup blocking system.*

Compliance: Within 48 hours from 24 June 1992. Thereafter reinspect at intervals not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

Background: During investigation of a fatal accident a backup power lever flight idle stop was found to be inoperative. This directive introduces a functional test of the system.

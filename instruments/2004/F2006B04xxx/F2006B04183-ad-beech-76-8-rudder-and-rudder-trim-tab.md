# AD/BEECH 76/8 - Rudder and Rudder Trim Tab

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 76 (Duchess) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 76/8 Rudder and Rudder Trim Tab 4/80

Applicability: All model 76 with S/Nos. ME-1 to ME-316. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1116. Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 11 March 1980.

Background: There is a possibility of water accumulating in the rudder and/or trim tab.

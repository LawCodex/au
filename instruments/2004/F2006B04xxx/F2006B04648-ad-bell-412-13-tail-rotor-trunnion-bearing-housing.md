# AD/BELL 412/13 - Tail Rotor Trunnion Bearing Housing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 412 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 412/13 Tail Rotor Trunnion Bearing Housing 9/86

DM

Applicability: All model 412 S/N 33001 to 33118, 33120 and 33121.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bell Alert Service Bulletin No. 412-86-25, Revision A. Compliance: Part I: At the next 20 hour inspection and thereafter at each 20 hour inspection until

Part II is complied with.

Part II: Within 150 hours time in service or 60 days, whichever occurs first after 31 July 1986.

Background: A trunnion bearing housing end web has been found below specified dimensions.

Failure of the housing caused excessive tail rotor vibration and loss of control.

# AD/BN-2/9 Amdt 4 - Empennage - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/9**

**Amdt 4**

**Empennage - Inspection and Modification NK**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Modify the elevator trim tab installation in accordance with Britten Norman Modification Leaflet No. BN-2/NB/M/507.

2\. Perform the inspections detailed in Britten Norman SB No. BN-2/SB49.

3\. Perform the additional torque inspections detailed in Britten Norman SB No. BN- 2/SB49A.

*Note: The inspection detailed in para. 2 may be performed and certified by the pilot in command.*

Compliance: For para. 1 - Before 30 September 1971.

For para. 2 - At intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service after 28 November 1975. For para. 3 - Within 5 hours time in service after 9 May 1974.

# AD/BN-2/52 Amdt 1 - Elevator Mass Balance Weights

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/52**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Mass Balance Weights 5/89**

Applicability: All models that do not have modification NB/M/990 embodied. Requirement: Action in accordance with Pilatus Britten-Norman SB BN2/SB113 Issue 3. Compliance: Prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 18 May 1989.

Background: This amendment extends the applicability to aircraft manufactured after the issue of Issue 2 of the SB.

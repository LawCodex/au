# AD/BN-2/48 - Wing Tip Tank Fuel Contents Transmitter Floats - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BN-2/48 Wing Tip Tank Fuel Contents Transmitter Floats - Inspection

Applicability: All models BN-2 Islander with wing tip fuel tanks. Document: Britten Norman SB BN-2/SB 154.

## 6/83

Compliance: As per requirement document within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1983.

Background: Wing tip fuel tank contents transmitter floats are prone to erosion and require repetitive inspections to prevent fuel system contamination which could result in loss of engine power.

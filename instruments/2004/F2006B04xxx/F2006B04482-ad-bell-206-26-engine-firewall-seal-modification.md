# AD/BELL 206/26 - Engine Firewall Seal - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/26 Engine Firewall Seal - Modification 8/70** Applicability: All model 206A and AB-206A with S/Nos. referenced in requirement documents. Document: 1. Bell SB 206A-142; or

2\. FAA AD 70-14-6; or

3\. Agusta SB BT206-43.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 31 August 1970. Background: AD/Agusta 206/18 equivalent.

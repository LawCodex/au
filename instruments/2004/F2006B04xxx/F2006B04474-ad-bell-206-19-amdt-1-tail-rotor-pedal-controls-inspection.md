# AD/BELL 206/19 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Pedal Controls - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/19**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Pedal Controls - Inspection 6/70**

Applicability: All model AB-206A fitted with hydraulically boosted tail rotor pitch control; and All model 206A with S/Nos. 4 to 497.

Requirement: Check the tail rotor pedal spring and friction adjustment and re- adjust as necessary in accordance with the requirements of Section IV the Maintenance and Overhaul Instructions.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service. Background: AD/Agusta 206/10A equivalent.

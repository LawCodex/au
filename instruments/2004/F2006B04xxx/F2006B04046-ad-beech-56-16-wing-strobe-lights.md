# AD/BEECH 56/16 - Wing Strobe Lights

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 56TC (Turbo Baron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 56/16 Wing Strobe Lights 10/76

Applicability: All models A56TC, S/Nos. TG-84 thru TG-94, that have either Hoskins P/N 30-0187- 21 or Grimes P/N 30-0669-1 wing strobe light installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0800-362. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 October 1976.

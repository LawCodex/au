# AD/CESSNA 150/45 - STOL Conversion Wing Stall Fence

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 150, F150, 152 & F152 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 150/45 STOL Conversion Wing Stall Fence 10/96

Applicability: Any model fitted with "Bush Conversions Inc." Short Take Off and Landing (STOL) kit installed in accordance with FAA STC SA1371SW.

Requirement: Measure the height of the wing stall fence at the trailing edge. If the height exceeds

32.5 mm (1.28 inches) install a smaller fence in accordance with a scheme approved pursuant to CAR35 through a District Office of the Authority.

*Note 1. FAA AD 96-12-23 refers.*

*Note 2. The diagram below is extracted from the FAA AD for guidance.*

[image]

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 12 September 1996.

Background: This Directive is issued to prevent modified aircraft from entering a stall condition due to excessive stall fence height, which could result in loss of control of the aircraft.

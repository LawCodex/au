# AD/BELL 407/11 - Door Locking Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 407 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 407/11 Door Locking Mechanism 10/99

Applicability: Model 407 helicopters, serial numbers 53000 through 53334.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bell Helicopter Textron Canada Alert Service Bulletin 407-99-30, dated 10 May 1999.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-99-19 refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 November 1999, unless already accomplished.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 7 October 1999.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of in-service problems with the doors. A possibility exists for the locking mechanism to become completely disabled and could impede an emergency egress, should critical circumstances arise.

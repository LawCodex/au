# AD/CA21/6 - Life Limited Components

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Skyfox Aviation (Calair/Hedaro) CA21 and CA22A Series Aeroplanes

| **AD/CA21/6** | **Life Limited Components** | **3/96** |
|---|---|---|
| Applicability: | All CA21 and CA22A aircraft. |  |
| Requirement: | The following components are to be retired from service at the times shown: |  |

Component Maximum Time in Service

Wing Front and Rear Spars 4000 Hours Wing Lift Struts and Carry-Through Structure 4000 Hours Horizontal Stabiliser Spar 8000 Hours All Other Structure, Except Doors and Fairings 8000 Hours

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement, with effect from 29 February 1996.

Background: The fatigue life limits in this Directive are the best estimates, based on stress and fatigue analyses supplied by the manufacturer.

# AD/BEECH 65/47 Amdt 1 - Wing Strobe Lights

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/47**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Strobe Lights 3/89**

Applicability: All models 65-B80, serial numbers LD-270 thru LD-480 and models 70 with S/Nos LB-1 through LB-35 that have either Hoskins P/N 30-0187-21 or Grimes P/N 30- 0669-1 wing strobe lights installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0800-362. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 October 1976. Background: Amendment 1 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

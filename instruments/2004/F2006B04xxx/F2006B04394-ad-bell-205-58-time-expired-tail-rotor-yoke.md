# AD/BELL 205/58 - Time Expired Tail Rotor Yoke

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron 205 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 205/58 Time Expired Tail Rotor Yoke 4/90

Applicability: All models fitted with aluminium tail rotor grips P/N 204-011-728-19. Requirement: Inspect and remove from service any tail rotor grip with any of the following S/Nos:

| A3-66287 | A3-66365 | A3-67171 | A3-66333 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A3-66370 | A3-67184 | A3-66356 | A3-66377 |
| A3-67207 | A3-66361 | A3-67149 |  |

*Note: FAA Priority Letter AD 90-03-10 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 19 February 1990.

Background: Advice has been received that a number of tail rotor grips previously operated for 300 hours time in service are being misrepresented as new items.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1984 or at the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 January 1984 whichever is the later.

Background: Possible migration and loss of the aileron hinge pin could result if the retaining split pin holes are incorrectly located.

# AD/BEECH 90/28 - Flap Bracket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/28 Flap Bracket - Inspection 8/72

Applicability: All models which have achieved 1000 or more hours’ time in service.

Requirement: Inspect flap actuator bracket and adjacent structure on RH and LH flaps for cracks or signs of distress.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 August 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 300 hours’ time in service.

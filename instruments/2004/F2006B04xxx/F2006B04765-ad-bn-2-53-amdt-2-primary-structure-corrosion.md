# AD/BN-2/53 Amdt 2 - Primary Structure Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BN-2/53**

**Amdt 2**

**Primary Structure Corrosion 4/98**

Applicability: All models BN-2 Series Islanders, and BN2T Turbine Islanders.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Pilatus Britten-Norman Mandatory Service Bulletin BN- 2/SB.190 Issue 3.

*Note: UK CAA AD 020-02-90 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document

The compliance periods of the previous issue of this Directive remain unchanged. This amendment becomes effective on 26 March 1998.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that the recommended inspections had only been partially successful in controlling major corrosion.

Amendment 1 provided additional information and actions, and incorporated the active parts of earlier service bulletins for the purpose of centralising all effective information relating to corrosion.

Amendment 2 is issued in response to Issue 3 of the requirement document which gives clearer inspection and repair procedures for corrosion control.

Amendment 1 of this airworthiness directive became effective on 4 March 1993.

The original issue of this airworthiness directive became effective on 30 March 1990.

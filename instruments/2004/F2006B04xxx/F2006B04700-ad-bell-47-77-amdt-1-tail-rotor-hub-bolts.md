# AD/BELL 47/77 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Hub Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 47/77**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Hub Bolts 10/86**

Applicability: Models 47B, 47B3, 47D, 47D-1, 47G, 47G-2 and 47H-1. Document: Bell SB No. 47-76-1. Kawasaki-Bell S.B. No. KSB-Bell 341.

Compliance: Before 1 March 1976. For Kawasaki-Bell: Prior to issue of Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: The existing tail rotor hub bolts have been subject to failure due to fatigue. This Directive has been amended to include Kawasaki- Bell Helicopters.

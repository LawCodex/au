# AD/BELL 204/27 Amdt 1 - External Cargo Suspension Kit - Load Restriction and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 204 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 204/27**

**Amdt 1**

**External Cargo Suspension Kit - Load NK Restriction and Modification**

Applicability: All model 204B with External cargo suspension kit P/N 204-706-106-1.

Requirement: 1. Restrict load on external cargo hook in accordance with Bell SB No. 204-78-1 until modified in accordance with Bell SB No. 204-78-4.

2\. Modify external cargo suspension kit P/N 204-706-104-1 in accordance with Bell SB No. 204-78-4.

Compliance: 1. On any flight utilising the external cargo hook.

2\. Before 1 September 1978.

# AD/CESSNA 150/24 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 150, F150, 152 & F152 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 150/24 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly 9/75

Applicability: Models 150, 150A, 150B, 150C, 150D, 150E, 150F, 150G, 150H, 150J, 150K,

A150K, 150L, A150L series which have been modified in accordance with STC SA2219WE.

Requirement: For Cessna Models 150, 150A, 150B, 150C, 150D, 150E, 150F, 150G, 150H, 150J,

150K, A150K, 150L and A150L Series Airplanes, remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N 1000-1 and install P/N B-1119-M, P/N B-1119-RN or P/N 1000-1-RM.

*Note: FAA AD 75-15-08 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1975.

# AD/BELL 206/77 - Tail Rotor Drive Shaft - Replacement of Bearing Hangars

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 206/77 Tail Rotor Drive Shaft - Replacement**

**of Bearing Hangars**

**12/78**

Applicability: Models 206A, 206B, AB206 & AB206B with S/Nos. as per Requirement Doc’s. Document: 1. Bell SB No. 206-77-9; or

2\. FAA AD 78-16-01 Amendment 39-3271; or

3\. Agusta SB No. BT206-123.

Compliance: No later than the next scheduled 1200 hourly inspection completed after 31 January 1979.

Background: AD/Agusta 206/61 equivalent.

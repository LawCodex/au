# AD/CASA/17 - MLG Shock Absorber

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/17 MLG Shock Absorber 3/95

Applicability: All Model C-212 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Spanish DGAC AD 07/94.

*Note: CASA SB 212-32-38 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 2 March 1995.

Background: The DGAC requires the incorporation of a modification which improves the attachment of the restrictor piston on the sliding tube in the MLG shock absorbers, by increasing from one to three the number of screw pins installed.

# AD/BEECH 60/38 - Air Conditioning Compressor Pulley Guard and Retaining Bolt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 60/38 Air Conditioning Compressor Pulley**

**Guard and Retaining Bolt**

**4/83**

Applicability: All models 60, A60 and B60 with S/Nos. P-4 to P-577, P-579 to P-587 and P-589 and P-592.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1236. Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 30 May 1983.

Background: The installation of a compressor pulley guard is considered mandatory by the manufacturer to reduce the possibility of the clutch coming in contact with a fuel line.

# AD/BEECH 77/1 - Wing Attachment Bolts and Nutplates

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 77 (Skipper) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 77/1 Wing Attachment Bolts and Nutplates 2/80** Applicability: All model 77 with S/Nos. WA-1 to WA-39, WA-41, WA-42 and WA-44 to WA-46. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1088.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to further flight.

Background: It is possible that thread damage may have occurred to the wing attach bolts and/or the internal nut plates.

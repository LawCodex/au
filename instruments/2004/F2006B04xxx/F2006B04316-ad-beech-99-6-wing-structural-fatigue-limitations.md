# AD/BEECH 99/6 - Wing Structural Fatigue Limitations

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 99 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 99/6 Wing Structural Fatigue Limitations 12/96

Applicability: All model 99 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect and retire the wing structural components in accordance with the Beechcraft 99 Airliner Series Continued Airworthiness Program Inspection Manual P/N 98- 30131 Rev. A1, Sections 57-10 and 57-20.

*Note: FAA AD 92-15-13 refers.*

Compliance: This Directive takes effect 7 November 1996.

Background: This Directive requires life limited components to be retired to prevent fatigue failure of the wing spar and associated structure.

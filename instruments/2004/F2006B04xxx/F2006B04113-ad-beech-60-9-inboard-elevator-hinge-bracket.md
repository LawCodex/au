# AD/BEECH 60/9 - Inboard Elevator Hinge Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/9 Inboard Elevator Hinge Bracket 12/70

Applicability: Models 60 and A60 with S/Nos. P-3 to P-151 which have more than 150 hours’ time in service.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beech SI No. 0342-132.

*Note: FAA AD 70-22-3 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 December 1970.

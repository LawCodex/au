# AD/CESSNA 170/52 - Muffler

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 170, 172, F172, FR172 and 175 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 170/52 Muffler 3/86

Applicability: Cessna aircraft S/N 172RG 1135 to 172RG 1187.

Requirement: Inspect muffler core below each end plate attachment screw for damage in accordance with Cessna SB No SEB85-17.

Compliance: Prior to issue of Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: A possibility exists that holes may have been drilled through the muffler allowing exhaust gases into the cabin during cabin heat operation.

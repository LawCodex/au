# AD/BEECH 60/41 - Elevator Hinge Attachment Areas

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/41 Elevator Hinge Attachment Areas 13/89

Applicability: Models 60, A60, and B60 with S/Nos P-3 through P-596. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft Mandatory SB 2328.

Compliance: Prior to exceeding 100 hours’ time in service after 28 December 1989, or at the next scheduled inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release after 28 December 1989, whichever occurs later.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of partial outboard elevator separations, and considers this inspection Mandatory to ensure integrity of the elevator to stabilizer attachments.

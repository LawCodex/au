# AD/BEECH 76/5 - Main Landing Gear Fork Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 76 (Duchess) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 76/5 Main Landing Gear Fork Pin 8/79

Applicability: All model 76 with S/Nos. ME-1 to ME-228. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1073.

Compliance: Within 50 hours’ time in service, unless already accomplished, after 28 August 1979.

Background: Failure of the existing fork pin retaining pin could result in separation of the wheel and fork assembly.

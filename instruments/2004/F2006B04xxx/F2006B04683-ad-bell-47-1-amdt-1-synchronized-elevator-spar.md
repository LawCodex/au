# AD/BELL 47/1 Amdt 1 - Synchronized Elevator Spar

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

**AD/BELL 47/1**

**Amdt 1**

**Synchronized Elevator Spar 11/76**

Applicability: Model 47J S/Nos. 1420 to 1802. All model 47K. Document: 1. Bell SB No. 135; or

2\. FAA AD 76-21-05.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 25 November 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

# AD/BEECH 65/56 - Cabin, Baggage and Pilot Compartment Side Windows - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEECH 65/56 Cabin, Baggage and Pilot Compartment

**Side Windows - Inspection** Applicability: All models 65-88 with S/Nos. LP-1 to LP-26, LP-28 and LP-30 to LP-47. Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0711-110, Rev. 111.

*Note: FAA AD 81-12-01 Amdt. 39-4126 refers.*

## 8/81

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, initially inspect within 50 hours’ time in service after 7 July 1981 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 300 hours’ time in service or 1 year, whichever occurs first, until windows are replaced in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0711-110, Rev. 111.

Background: In service window failures have occurred both in Australia and overseas in similar type aeroplanes.

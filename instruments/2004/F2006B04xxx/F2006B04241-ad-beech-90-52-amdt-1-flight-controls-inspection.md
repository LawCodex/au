# AD/BEECH 90/52 Amdt 1 - Flight Controls - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 90/52**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Controls - Inspection 3/79**

Applicability: All models : B90 & C90 with S/Nos. LJ-500 to LJ-706; E90 with S/Nos. LW-1 to LW-209 and all 65-90 and 90 series fitted with a rudder shipped from Beech during period 1 May 1973 to 1 July 1976.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0629-150, Rev. II.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 January 1979.

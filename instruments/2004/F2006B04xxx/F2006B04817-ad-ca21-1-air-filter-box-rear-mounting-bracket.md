# AD/CA21/1 - Air Filter Box Rear Mounting Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Skyfox Aviation (Calair/Hedaro) CA21 and CA22A Series Aeroplanes

AD/CA21/1 Air Filter Box Rear Mounting Bracket 1/96

Applicability: CA 22A aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Visually inspect the rivets securing the air filter box rear mounting bracket. If the rivets are loose, broken or missing, replace with MS20470AD4-4 solid rivets.

2\. Remove the air filter box from the aircraft and replace the pop rivets securing the rear mounting bracket with MS20470AD4-4 or MS20470AD5-4 solid rivets.

*Note: Skyfox Aviation Service Bulletin 9 Iss.2 refers.*

Compliance: For Requirement 1, before next flight after 4 January 1996.

For Requirement 2, at the next inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release or within 100 hours time in service, whichever occurs first.

Background: The pop rivets originally fitted could work loose and be ingested into the engine.

This AD requires fitment of solid rivets.

# AD/BELL 205/52 - Tail Rotor Trunnion Bearing Housing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Bell Helicopter Textron 205 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 205/52 Tail Rotor Trunnion Bearing Housing 9/86

DM

Applicability: All model 205A and 205A-1 helicopters fitted with kit P/N 212-704-129-101 (refer SI 212-68).

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bell Alert Service Bulletin No. 205-86-24, Revision A. Compliance: Part I of the Requirement Document:

Within 25 hours time in service or seven days whichever occurs first after

6 August 1986 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service or 7 days, whichever occurs first, until Part II is complied with.

Part II of the Requirement Document:

Within 150 hours time in service or 60 days whichever occurs first after 31 July 1986.

Background: A trunnion bearing housing end web has been found below specified dimensions.

Failure of the housing caused excessive tail rotor vibration and loss of control.

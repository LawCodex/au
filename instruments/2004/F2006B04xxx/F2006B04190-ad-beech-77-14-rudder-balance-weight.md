# AD/BEECH 77/14 - Rudder Balance Weight

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 77 (Skipper) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 77/14 Rudder Balance Weight 12/83

Applicability: All model 77 with S/Nos. WA-1 to WA-312. Requirement: 1. Inspect rudder balance weight attachments for security.

2\. Modify I.A.W. Beechcraft S.I No. 1256.

Compliance: 1. Within 25 hours’ time in service after 4 November 1983 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours’ time in service until modified.

2\. Prior to 31 December 1983.

Background: To provide a reinforced attachment of the rudder balance weight.

# AD/CASA/9 - Engine Controls - Propeller Feathering System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/9 Engine Controls - Propeller Feathering System 6/87

Applicability: All model CASA-212 with S/No’s referenced in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with CASA S.B. No. 212-76-04.

*Note: FAA AD No. 87-05-06 Amendment 39-5561 also refers.*

Compliance: Modify prior to 31 December 1987.

Background: The possibility of loss of propeller control system integrity is not sufficiently remote for this class of aeroplane.

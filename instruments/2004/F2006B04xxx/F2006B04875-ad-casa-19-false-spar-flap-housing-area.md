# AD/CASA/19 - False Spar, Flap Housing Area

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/19 False Spar, Flap Housing Area 9/96

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with CASA document COM 212-224 dated 28 November 1990.

2\. Repair any cracking in accordance with a scheme approved pursuant to CAR 35.

*Note: Spanish DGAC AD 02/96 refers.*

Compliance: Within 1200 hours time in service after 15 August 1996. Where no cracking is found repeat inspection at intervals not exceeding 1200 hours time in service.

Background: Cracking has been detected in the area defined in the Requirement Document.

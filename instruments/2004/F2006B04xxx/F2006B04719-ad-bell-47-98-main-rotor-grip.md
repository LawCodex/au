# AD/BELL 47/98 - Main Rotor Grip

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 47/98 Main Rotor Grip 1/92

Applicability: Model 47G2, 47G2A, 47G2A1, 47G3, 47G3B1, 47G3B2, 47G3B2A, 47G4, 47G4A,

47G5, 47G5A, 47J, 47J2 and 47J2A helicopters, with main rotor grip part number 47-120-252-11, installed.

If one of the M/R grips listed above is installed, remove and replace with a serviceable part.

*Note: FAA AD 91-20-10 Amdt 39-8044 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 12 January 1992.

Background: The FAA has issued a Directive in response to reports that a vendor to BHTI produced and directly distributed 156 M/R grips which by-passed the FAA-approved quality control system. As a result, these M/R grips are potentially unsafe and must be removed from service. The effective date required by the FAA is extended by two months.

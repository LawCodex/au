# AD/BEECH 90/16 - Rudder Spar and Hinges - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/BEECH 90/16 Rudder Spar and Hinges - Inspection

**and Modification** Applicability: Models 65-90, 65-A90 and B90 with S/Nos. LJ-1 to LJ-428. Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0101-135.

## 5/69

Compliance: 1. Inspection within 100 hours’ time in service after 12 May 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service until modified.

2\. Modify before 1 August 1969.

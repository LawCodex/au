# AD/BEECH 76/9 Amdt 1 - Engine Mount Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 76 (Duchess) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 76/9**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Mount Assembly 7/82**

Applicability: All models 76 with S/Nos. ME 1 to ME-415. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1147 R1. Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: Cracks may develop in the lower tubes of the engine mount assembly. Amdt. 1 is issued to extend aircraft serial number effectivity.

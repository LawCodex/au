# AD/BELL 47/59 - Main Rotor Blades

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 47 (All Variants) Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 47/59 Main Rotor Blades 10/65

Applicability: All model 47 series with metal main rotor blades.

Requirement: Carry out a visual inspection for cracking of the upper and lower surfaces of the main rotor blades adjacent to the outboard extremity of the root reinforcement doublers at the trailing edge.

Compliance: Before 1500 hours blade time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

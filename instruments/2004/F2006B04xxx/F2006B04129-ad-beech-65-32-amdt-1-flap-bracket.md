# AD/BEECH 65/32 Amdt 1 - Flap Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/32**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Bracket 3/89**

Applicability: All models Queen Air 65 and 70 which have achieved 1000 or more hours’ time in service.

Requirement: Inspect flap actuator bracket and adjacent structure on RH and LH flaps for cracks or signs of distress.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 August 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 300 hours’ time in service.

Background: Amendment 1 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

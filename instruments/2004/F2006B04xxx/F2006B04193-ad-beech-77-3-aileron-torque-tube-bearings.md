# AD/BEECH 77/3 - Aileron Torque Tube Bearings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 77 (Skipper) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 77/3 Aileron Torque Tube Bearings 4/80

Applicability: All model 77 with S/Nos. WA-1 to WA-78, WA-80 to WA-86 and WA-88. Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 1119.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 17 March 1980.

Background: It is possible for the aileron torque tube to bind in the flap spool during flight.

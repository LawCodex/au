# AD/CASA/16 - Rudder Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/16 Rudder Control 3/94 DM

Applicability: All Model C-212 aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with CASA Communication COM 212-245.

2\. Action in accordance with CASA Service Bulletin 212-27-47.

*Note: Spanish DGCA AD 01/93 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Prior to each flight, until CASA Service Bulletin 212-27-47 is accomplished.

2\. Prior to 31 May 1994.

Background: The Spanish DGCA requires an interim limitation on rudder pedal adjustment and then modification action in order to achieve the appropriate values of rudder surface deflection for required flying handling qualities, regardless of the selected rudder pedal assembly adjustment.

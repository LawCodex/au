# AD/BEECH 65/14 Amdt 4 - Elevator Control Rods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/14**

**Amdt 4**

**Elevator Control Rods 3/89**

Applicability: All models Queen Air 65, A65 and A65-8200 with S/Nos. L-1, L-2, L-6, LF-7, LF-8 and LC-1 to LC-335; All models 65-80, 65-A80 and 65-B80 with S/Nos. LD-1 to LD-511; All models 65-88 with S/Nos. LP-1 to LP-28, LP-30 to LP-47. Models Queen Air 70 with S/Nos LB-1 through LB-35.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No. 0334-152, Rev. III. Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: Amendment 4 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

# AD/CA21/3 - Rudder Actuator Horn

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Skyfox Aviation (Calair/Hedaro) CA21 and CA22A Series Aeroplanes

AD/CA21/3 Rudder Actuator Horn 1/96

Applicability: CA21 aircraft, serial numbers CA21004 through CA21035.

Requirement: Inspect the rudder actuator horn for drain hole in accordance with Skyfox Aviation Service Bulletin No.4.

Compliance: At the next inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release or within 100 hours time in service, from 4 January 1996 whichever occurs first.

Background: Skyfox advise that a number of rudder actuator horns may not have had a drain hole installed at manufacture. This AD requires a drain hole to be drilled if not already fitted.

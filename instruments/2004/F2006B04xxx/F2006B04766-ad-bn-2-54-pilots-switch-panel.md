# AD/BN-2/54 - Pilots Switch Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Britten-Norman BN-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/BN-2/54 Pilots Switch Panel 9/91

Applicability: All models BN2 Islander, 2T Turbine Islander Series Aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of Pilatus Britten-Norman SB BN-2/SB.202.

*Note: Compliance with this SB is classified Mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Prior to 5 November 1991.

Background: Investigation has revealed the possibility of an electrical short circuit accidentally occurring between adjacent switches behind the pilots switch panel.

# AD/BEECH 90/45 - Wing Strobe Lights - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/45 Wing Strobe Lights - Replacement 10/76

Applicability: All models B90 and C90, S/Nos. LJ-318 thru LJ-502; E90 S/No. LW-1; that have either Hoskins P/N 30-0187-21 or Grimes P/N 30-0669-1 wing strobe lights installed.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0800-362.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 October 1976.

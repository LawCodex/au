# AD/BELL 206/22 - Hydraulic Actuator - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 206/22 Hydraulic Actuator - Replacement 12/69

Applicability: All model 206A and AB-206A. Document: Bell SB No. 206A-12.

*Note: Agusta SB No. BT206-35 refers.*

Compliance: 1. For actuators with 850 hours or more total time in service at 17 November 1969 - within the next 25 hours time in service.

2\. For actuators with less than 850 hours total time in service - before exceeding 875 hours total time in service.

Background: AD/Agusta 206/15 equivalent.

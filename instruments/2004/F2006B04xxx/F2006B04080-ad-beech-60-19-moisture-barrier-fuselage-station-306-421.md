# AD/BEECH 60/19 - Moisture Barrier Fuselage Station 306.421

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/19 Moisture Barrier Fuselage Station 306.421 11/72

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: To prevent possible interference with elevator movement, accomplish the following:

Starting at the cutout for the elevator bell crank, slit the moisture barrier vertically upward using a knife, razor blade or similar tool. Extend this cut through the “velcro” strip which attaches the moisture barrier to the front spar of the horizontal stabiliser.

*Note: In making the slit do not damage the spar.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 3 November 1972 unless already accomplished.

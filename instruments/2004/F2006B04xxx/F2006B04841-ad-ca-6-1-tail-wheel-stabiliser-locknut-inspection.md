# AD/CA-6/1 - Tail Wheel Stabiliser Locknut - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
C.A.C. CA-6 (Wackett Trainer) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CA-6/1 Tail Wheel Stabiliser Locknut - Inspection NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Check the tightness of the tailwheel bungee stabiliser locknut (Part No. 06-34041). Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service.

# AD/BEECH 65/31 Amdt 1 - Control Wheel Adapter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/31**

**Amdt 1**

**Control Wheel Adapter 3/89**

Applicability: All models Queen Air 65 and 70.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Beechcraft SI No 0497-155.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 10 May 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours’ time in service until adapter P/N 99-524036-33 is installed.

Background: Amendment 1 consolidates Queen Air series 65 and 70 aircraft.

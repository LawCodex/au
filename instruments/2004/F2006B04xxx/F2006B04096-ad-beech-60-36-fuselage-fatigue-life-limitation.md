# AD/BEECH 60/36 - Fuselage Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 60 (Duke) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 60/36 Fuselage Fatigue Life Limitation 3/81

Applicability: All model 60, A60 and B60 Series. Requirement: Retire the pressure cabin from service. Compliance: Before exceeding 12,800 pressurised flights.

Background: Analysis and fatigue tests by the manufacturer have established the above pressure cabin life for these aircraft.

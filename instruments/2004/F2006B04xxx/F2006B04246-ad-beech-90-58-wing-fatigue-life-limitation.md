# AD/BEECH 90/58 - Wing Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 90 and 65-90 (King Air) Series Aeroplanes

AD/BEECH 90/58 Wing Fatigue Life Limitation 3/81

Applicability: All model F90 Series.

Requirement: Retire the wing main spar and wing centre section carry-thru spar lower cap. Compliance: Before exceeding 10,300 hours in service.

*Note 1: This life may be revised following the completion of further tests and analysis currently being carried out by the manufacturer.*

*Note 2: On the basis of currently available fatigue substantiating data only one spar replacement is permitted. This will allow a single lifetime extension from spar cap replacement.*

Background: Analysis and test data supplied by the manufacturer has established the above retirement life for the wing spar.

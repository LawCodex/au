# AD/BEECH 65/12 - Rudder Spar and Hinges - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Beechcraft 65 and 70 (Queen Air) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/BEECH 65/12 Rudder Spar and Hinges - Inspection**

**and Modification**

**5/69**

Applicability: Models A65 & A65-8200 with S/Nos. LC-240 to LC-319. Models 65-80, 65-A80 & 65-B80 with S/Nos. LD-1 to LD-398.

Document: Beechcraft SI No. 0101-135.

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 100 hours’ time in service after 12 May 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service until modified.

2\. Modify before 1 August 1969.

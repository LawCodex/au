# AD/CASA/3 - Wing Flap Control Hinge Bearing - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Casa 212 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CASA/3 Wing Flap Control Hinge Bearing - Replacement 3/83

Applicability: All models CASA-212 with S/Nos. referenced in the requirement document. Document: Construcciones Aeronauticas SB 212-27-19.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1983 unless previously accomplished.

Background: The manufacturer reports breakage and corrosion of premod bearings.

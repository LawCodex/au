# AD/BELL 206/121 - Tail Rotor Blade Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron Canada (BHTC) 206 and Agusta Bell 206 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL206/121 Tail Rotor Blade Assembly 9/90

TX

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Determine the part number and serial number of the tail rotor blade assembly installed on the helicopter.

2\. If the tail rotor blade assembly installed is P/N 206-016-201-113 or P/N 206-016- 201-127 and is identified with any serial number listed below, remove and replace the blade assembly.

|  | T -44222 | T-44300 | T-44638 |

*Note: FAA Emergency AD 90-13-01 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 26 June 1990.

Background: The FAA has listed the blades above as being life expired. They may have been represented as airworthy newly overhauled blades using falsified documentation.

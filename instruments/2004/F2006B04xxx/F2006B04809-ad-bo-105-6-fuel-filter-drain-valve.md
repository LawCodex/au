# AD/BO 105/6 - Fuel Filter Drain Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BO 105 Series Helicopters

AD/BO 105/6 Fuel Filter Drain Valve 9/86 DM

Applicability: All model BO 105 helicopters fitted with engine fuel filter drain valve P/No P101- 228.

Requirement: Action in accordance with MBB Alert Service Bulletin ASB BO 105-60-101.

*Note: German AD 86-148 MBB Refers.*

Compliance: Corrective action 1 of Requirement Document:

Before further flight after 22 August 1986. Corrective action 2 of Requirement Document:

At each daily inspection after 22 August 1986.

Corrective action 3 of Requirement Document:

This action may be carried out before further flight after 22 August 1986 in which case corrective actions 1 and 2 need not be complied with.

*Note: Corrective action in accordance with action 3 is an interim measure until manufacturer can supply improved drain valves.*

Background: Draining the fuel filters at preflight checks may loosen the handle of the shutoff plunger and under adverse conditions may cause the drain valve to disintegrate.

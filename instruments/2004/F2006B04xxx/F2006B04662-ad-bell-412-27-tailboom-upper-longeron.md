# AD/BELL 412/27 - Tailboom Upper Longeron

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bell Helicopter Textron 412 Series Helicopters

AD/BELL 412/27 Tailboom Upper Longeron 12/90

DM

Applicability: Model 412 with serial numbers:

33001 through 33211

34001 through 34013

36001 through 36013

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Part 1 of Bell Alert Service Bulletin No 412-90-49.

2\. Action in accordance with Part II of Bell Alert Service Bulletin No 412-90-49. Compliance: 1. Prior to 30 November 1990.

2\. At intervals not exceeding 300 hours time in service after incorporation of Requirement 1.

Background: Incidents of cracked longeron splices have created a requirement for additional access to inspect upper longeron splices, and modification action to increase structural integrity.

Compliance: Before 18 September 1997.

Background: The seat manufacturer advised that a different anti corrosion treatment was used on the affected leg collars and that treatment may cause the collars not to meet the seat structural certification requirement. The manufacturer has advised that Ansett Australia is the only known operator in Australia.

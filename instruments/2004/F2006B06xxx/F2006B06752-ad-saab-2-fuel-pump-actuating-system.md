# AD/SAAB/2 - Fuel Pump Actuating System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB Safir Series Aeroplanes

AD/SAAB/2 Fuel Pump Actuating System 5/86

Applicability: Model SAAB Safir 91B, 91B-2, 91C and 91D. Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB SB 91.4.003.

*Note: Swedish AD No 1-017 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1986.

Background: The manufacturer has received a report of the cable assembly of the manually actuated fuel pump, failing during operation in an emergency situation.

# AD/S-76/6 - Hydraulic Pumps - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

AD/S-76/6 Hydraulic Pumps - Replacement 1/80

Applicability: All model S-76 with S/Nos. SS No. 76005, 76006 and 760001 through 760043. Document: Sikorsky CSB 76-29-1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1980.

Background: The manufacturer advises of the need to replace certain models of hydraulic pumps.

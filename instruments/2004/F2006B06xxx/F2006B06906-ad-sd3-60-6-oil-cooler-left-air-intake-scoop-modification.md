# AD/SD3-60/6 - Oil Cooler Left Air Intake Scoop - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/6 Oil Cooler Left Air Intake Scoop - Modification 6/84

Applicability: All model SD3-60 with S/Nos SH 3601 to SH 3629, SH 3631 and SH 3632. Document: Shorts SB SD360-71-05.

Compliance: Modify prior to 30 November 1984.

Background: The manufacture reports that high oil temperature readings have been recorded while operating in severe icing conditions.

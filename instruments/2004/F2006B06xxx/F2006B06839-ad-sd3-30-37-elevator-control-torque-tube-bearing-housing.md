# AD/SD3-30/37 - Elevator Control Torque Tube Bearing Housing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/37 Elevator Control Torque Tube Bearing Housing 3/99

Applicability: All SD3-30 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD330-27-37. Compliance: Before 25 September 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 25 March 1999.

Background: Experience shows that existing bolts securing the elevator control torque tube bearing housing retaining plate to pilot’s seat pedestal have been found loose; probably because of the significance of the structure has not been recognised during maintenance.

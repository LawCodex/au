# AD/SD3-60/44 - Strobe Lighting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/44 Strobe Lighting 1/97

Applicability: This Directive is applicable to all SD3-60-200 variant aeroplanes.

Requirement: Incorporate Modification No A8651 (Introduction of New Strobe Light and Power Supply with Associated Hardware) in accordance with Shorts Service Bulletin SD360-33-25 Revision 1 dated July 1996.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 007-05-96 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1997.

Background: The CAA(UK) has advised that the existing tail strobe light does not meet the certification requirements regarding minimum intensity. Action specified by this Directive rectifies this deficiency.

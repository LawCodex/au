# AD/R22/3 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Transmission - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robinson R22 Series Helicopters

**AD/R22/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Transmission - Inspection 4/81**

Applicability: All R22 series helicopters with serial numbers 0002 to 0099. Documents: 1. Robinson S.L. 6 (Serial Nos. 0002 to 0058 only.) and

2\. Robinson S.B. 7.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 4 March 1981.

*Note: Helicopters previously inspected in accordance with AD/R22/3 need only have the drive shaft damper inspected additionally in accordance with para. 5 of S.B. 7.*

Background: This directive is intended to prevent failure of A193-2 forward tail rotor coupling flex plate and loss of power to tail rotor and is issued as the result of receiving FAA AD 80-26-06 Amendment 39-3997. The Directive is raised in issue to include an expanded serial number range, and to introduce an inspection of the tail rotor drive shaft damper. Robinson S.L. 7 is replaced by Robinson S.B. 7.

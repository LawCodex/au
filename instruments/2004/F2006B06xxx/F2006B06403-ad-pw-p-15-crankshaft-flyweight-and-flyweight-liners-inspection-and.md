# AD/PW-P/15 - Crankshaft Flyweight and Flyweight Liners - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Piston Engines

AD/PW-P/15 Crankshaft Flyweight and Flyweight NK

Liners - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: R985 series.

Document: Pratt & Whitney SB 1758 Rev. A.

Compliance: Installed Engines - Before installation in an aircraft following each overhaul. Spare engines - Before installation in an aircraft after 2 November 1967 unless previously inspected and reworked or replaced as necessary in accordance with Pratt & Whitney SB No. 1758 or P&W Wasp Junior Overhaul Manual P/N 123440 at last overhaul.

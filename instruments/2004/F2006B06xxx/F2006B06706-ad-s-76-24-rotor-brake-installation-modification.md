# AD/S-76/24 - Rotor Brake Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

AD/S-76/24 Rotor Brake Installation - Modification 10/82

Applicability: All models S-76A.

Requirement: Modify the rotor brake system by incorporation of the following Sikorsky documents: 1. CSB 76-66-7.

2\. CSB 76-66-8A.

3\. CSB 76-66-10B.

4\. CSN 76-51B.

*Note: FAA AD 82-17-03 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October, 1982.

Background: The manufacturer has introduced a number of modifications to the rotor brake system to reduce the possibility of brake drag and overheating.

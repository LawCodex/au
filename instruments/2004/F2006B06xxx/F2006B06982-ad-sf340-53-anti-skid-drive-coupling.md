# AD/SF340/53 - Anti-Skid Drive Coupling

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/53 Anti-Skid Drive Coupling 5/96 DM

Applicability: All SAAB SF340A S/N 004 through 159 and SF340B S/N 160 through 378 aircraft. Requirement: Unless previously carried out, action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin 340-

32-107 dated 18 January 1996.

*Note: Swedish AD No 1-085 R1 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1996.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that numerous occurrences of unexpected brake pressure reductions have been reported. In one incident an aircraft ran into an airport building.

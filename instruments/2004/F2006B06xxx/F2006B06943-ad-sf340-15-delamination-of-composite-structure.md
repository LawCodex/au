# AD/SF340/15 - Delamination of Composite Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/15 Delamination of Composite Structure 5/89

DM

Applicability: Model SF340A S/No 103 to 139 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB SB SF340-51-006.

*Note: Swedish AD 1-031 refers.*

Compliance: At the next A-check/E-check after 29 March 1989 and thereafter at each heavy landing or flight in turbulence per AMM 05-50-00 until replaced or repaired in accordance with a Scheme approved by the Authority.

Background: The manufacturer has found that due to production difficulties and insufficient documentation some composite parts may be delaminated. Water intrusion may cause further delamination and affect structural integrity.

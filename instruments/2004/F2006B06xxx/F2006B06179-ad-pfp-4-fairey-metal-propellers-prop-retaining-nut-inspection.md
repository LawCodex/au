# AD/PFP/4 - Fairey Metal Propellers Prop Retaining Nut - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Fixed Pitch

AD/PFP/4 Fairey Metal Propellers Prop NK Retaining Nut - Inspection

Applicability: All Fairey metal propellers.

Requirement: Check the propeller hub retaining nuts for tightness.

Compliance: Within 5 hours time in service after each installation and there-after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

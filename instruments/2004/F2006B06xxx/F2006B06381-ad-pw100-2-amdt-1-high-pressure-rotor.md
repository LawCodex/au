# AD/PW100/2 Amdt 1 - High Pressure Rotor

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Canada Turbine Engines - PW100 Series

**AD/PW100/2**

**Amdt 1**

**High Pressure Rotor 4/91**

Applicability: All PW100 series engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Transport Canada Airworthiness Directive CF-89-14 Rev 1.

Compliance: 1. PW115, PW118, PW120, PW120A, PW121 engines; at next access to affected parts.

2\. PW118A, PW123, PW124B, PW125B, PW126 engines; at next scheduled engine shop visit, but no later than 31 December 1991.

Background: This Directive has been amended to reflect a revision to the Requirement Document. The revision alleviates the compliance requirements for engines with PW115/PW120 series turbine components, and includes reference to alternative service bulletins acceptable to Transport Canada as meeting the intent of Airworthiness Directive CF- 89-14.

# AD/PL 12/5 - Rudder Pedal Assemblies - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

| **AD/PL 12/5** | **Rudder Pedal Assemblies - Inspection** | **5/69** |
|---|---|---|
| Applicability: | All models not modified to Transavia Dwg. 7-254 Issue C or later. |  |
| Requirement: | Inspect the rudder pedal assemblies for cracking in all welded areas. |  |

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 4 July 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/SD3-30/5 - Aileron, Pilots Handwheel Stop - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/5 Aileron, Pilots Handwheel Stop - Modification 1/82

Applicability: SD 3-30 aircraft with S/Nos. SH 3002 to SH 3067 fitted with PT6A-45R engines (SB SD3-71-11), and S/Nos. SH 3068 to SH 3071, SH 3073, SH 3075, SH 3077 and SH

3078\.

Document: Shorts SB No. SD3-27-23.

(Modification No. 6255).

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 400 hours time in service after 31 January 1981.

Background: During flight testing after fitting PT6A-45R engines, it was found that in some low weight configurations the aircraft was marginally below the required VMCL rate of roll.

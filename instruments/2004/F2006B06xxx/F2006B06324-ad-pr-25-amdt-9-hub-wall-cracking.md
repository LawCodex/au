# AD/PR/25 Amdt 9 - Hub Wall Cracking

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Dowty Rotol

**AD/PR/25**

**Amdt 9**

**Hub Wall Cracking 5/95**

Applicability: ALL R354/4-123-F/13, R354/4-123-F/20 and R375/4-123-F/21 propellers fitted to SAAB 340A or 340B aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Dowty Aerospace Propellers Mandatory Service Bulletin SF340-61-A42 Revision 6.

2\. Action in accordance with Dowty Aerospace Propellers SB SF340-61-61 Revision 4.

Compliance: 1. As specified in the Requirement Document until Requirement 2 has been complied with.

2\. Unless previously carried out, at the next overhaul of the propeller. Incorporation of SB SF340-61-61 Rev 4 provides terminating action for this directive.

Background: Previous amendments have been raised to provide terminating action, to correct several P/N errors and to vary the repetitive inspection requirements.

This amendment mandates compliance with Revision 6, latest revision, to Dowty Mandatory SB SF340-61-A42.

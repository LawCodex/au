# AD/PC-6/23 - Rudder Bottom Nose Rib - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/PC-6/23 Rudder Bottom Nose Rib - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models prior to S/No. 685. Document: Pilatus SB No. 80.

## 7/68

Compliance: Inspect within 100 hours time in service after 11 July 1968 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified. Modify before 1 January 1969.

# AD/SD3-60/5 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Frame 475 - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/5**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage Frame 475 - Modification 4/85**

Applicability: All model SD3-60 with S/Nos SH 3601 to SH 3633. Document: Shorts SB SD360-53-11 R1.

Compliance: Prior to December 1985.

Background: The manufacturer reports difficulty in obtaining MS 21141 blind fasteners. The service bulletin has been re-issued and specifies alternate fasteners. The compliance date is also revised.

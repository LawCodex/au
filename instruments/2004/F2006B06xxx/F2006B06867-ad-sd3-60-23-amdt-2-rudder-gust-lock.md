Background: Damage has occurred to the rudder control system due to wind gust and jet blast.

This prompted the issue of the original Requirement, ie Part 1. Since then there have been reports of unsolicited engagement of the rudder gust lock. Investigation indicates that these were caused by inaccurate adjustment of microswitch WM2.

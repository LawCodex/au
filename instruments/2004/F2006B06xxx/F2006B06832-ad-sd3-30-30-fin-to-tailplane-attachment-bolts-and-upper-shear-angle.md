# AD/SD3-30/30 - Fin to Tailplane Attachment Bolts and Upper Shear Angle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-30/30 Fin to Tailplane Attachment Bolts**

**and Upper Shear Angle**

**7/95**

**DM**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Service Bulletin SD330-55-18.

Compliance: Within 60 days after 03 May 1995; thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1500 hours time in service.

Background: Fin to tailplane attach bolts have been found loose, and rivets in the upper shear angle have been found pulled or loose.

# AD/PA-46/20 - Exhaust Transition Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-46 (Malibu) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-46/20 Exhaust Transition Bolts 8/91 TX

Applicability: All model PA-46-350P fitted with Textron Lycoming TI0-540-AE2A engine with engine S/No up to but not including L9161-61A and excluding S/No L9157-61A.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 91-10-04 Amdt 39-6981.

*Note: Textron Lycoming SB No 491A refers.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service from 31 May 1991.

Background: An aircraft accident has occurred caused by engine power loss, which resulted from exhaust transition flange bolt loss and flange separation.

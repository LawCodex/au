# AD/PC-6/13 - Elevator Attachment Bracket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PC-6/13 Elevator Attachment Bracket - Inspection 1/67

Applicability: All models prior to S/Nos. 621 not modified in accordance with SB No. 67. Document: Pilatus SB No. 67.

Compliance: Inspect with the aid of dye penetrant at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 15 January 1967.

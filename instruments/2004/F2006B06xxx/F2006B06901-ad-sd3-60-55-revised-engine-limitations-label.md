# AD/SD3-60/55 - Revised Engine Limitations Label

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/55 Revised Engine Limitations Label 6/99

Applicability: SD3-60 aircraft serial number SH3716 through SH3763.

Requirement: Affix a revised engine limitations label to the ditching hatch in accordance with the requirements of Short Brothers Service bulletin SD360-11-23.

*Note: CAA (UK) Airworthiness Directive 015-11-98 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 17 June 1999.

Background: This directive has been issued to require a revised engine limitations label which reflects the aircraft manufacturer’s amendment of the propeller speed tolerance.

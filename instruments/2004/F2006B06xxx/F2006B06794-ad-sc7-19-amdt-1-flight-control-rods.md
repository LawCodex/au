# AD/SC7/19 Amdt 1 - Flight Control Rods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SC7/19**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Control Rods 13/95**

Applicability: All Model SC7 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Service Bulletin 27-74 Revision 1.

Compliance: Within 60 days after 25 May 1995; thereafter at intervals not to exceed 1200 hours time in service or 2 calendar years, whichever occurs first.

The compliance date of the previous issue of this Directive remains unchanged. This Amendment is effective from 7 December 1995.

Background: Pitting Corrosion, and Stress Corrosion cracking has been discovered in the flight control rods.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement Document which increases the calendar time repetitive inspection period.

# AD/SF340/21 - Auto-Coarsening

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/21 Auto-Coarsening 2/91 DM

Applicability: All SAAB SF340 Series aircraft S/No 004 through 159 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-21-16 Amdt 39- 6763.

*Note 1: This directive cancels AD/SF340/13.*

*Note 2: Swedish AD No 1-037 refers and cancels Swedish AD No 1-028.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1991

Background: As previously incorporated Electrical Control Unit (ECU) modifications may affect single engine climb performance. The FAA, Swedish Airworthiness Authority and the Aircraft Manufacturer requires these modifications to be removed and a modified ECU to be installed.

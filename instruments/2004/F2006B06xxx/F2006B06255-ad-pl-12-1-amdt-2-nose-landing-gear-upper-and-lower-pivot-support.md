# AD/PL 12/1 Amdt 2 - Nose Landing Gear Upper and Lower Pivot Support Assemblies - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PL 12/1**

**Amdt 2**

**Nose Landing Gear Upper and Lower Pivot Support Assemblies - Inspection**

**9/73**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Thoroughly clean and inspect the nose landing gear upper and lower pivot support assemblies for cracking in the area of the welds using a 6X magnification lens.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 10 September 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

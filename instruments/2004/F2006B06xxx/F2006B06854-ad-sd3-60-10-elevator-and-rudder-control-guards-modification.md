# AD/SD3-60/10 - Elevator and Rudder Control Guards - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SD3-60/10 Elevator and Rudder Control Guards - Modification

Applicability: All model SD 3-60 with S/Nos SH 3601 to SH 3665. Requirement: Shorts S.B. SD 360-27-04.

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1985.

## 6/85

**DM**

Background: An instance was reported to the manufacturer in which an ashtray was dropped behind a furnishing panel in the passenger compartment adjacent to the spring pot in the elevator control circuit. Fitting of extended/additional guards will protect the controls from possible interference.

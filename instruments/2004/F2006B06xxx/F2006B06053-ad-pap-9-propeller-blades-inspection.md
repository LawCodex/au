# AD/PAP/9 - Propeller Blades - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Allison/Aeroproducts

AD/PAP/9 Propeller Blades - Inspection 9/80

Applicability: All models A6441 FN-606 and -606A propeller blades.

Requirement: Inspect propeller blades in accordance with Part 2 of Allison CSL 240, Rev. 3. (FAA AD 79-24-07 refers).

Compliance: At periods not exceeding 3250 hours.

Background: The manufacturer has introduced additional blade inspection requirement.

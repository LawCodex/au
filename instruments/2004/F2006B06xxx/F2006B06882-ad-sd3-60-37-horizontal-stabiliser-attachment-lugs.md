# AD/SD3-60/37 - Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Lugs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/37 Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Lugs 6/93

Applicability: All SD3-60 aircraft with tailplanes which have accumulated 20,000 or more flights.

Requirement: Modify the aircraft and ultrasonically inspect the forward and aft tailplane attachment lugs, upper and lower surfaces, in accordance with SB SD360-55-19.

*Note: SB SD360-55-19 has been classified mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Before 29 July 1993. Reinspect at intervals of not more than 4000 flights.

Background: The manufacturer has determined by continued testing that additional inspections are required on the horizontal stabiliser attachment lugs.

# AD/PC-6/41 - Rudder Pedal Supports

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PC-6/41 Rudder Pedal Supports 5/95

Applicability: All PC-6 aircraft S/N 001 to 824 inclusive fitted with steel rudder pedal support tube P/N 6232.0196.00.

Requirement: Visually inspect the rudder pedal support for cracks using a 10X glass. Inspect in accordance with Pilatus Service Bulletin (SB) PC-6-A-162, Accomplishment Instructions A(2).

*Note: Swiss FOCA AD HB 89-301 refers.*

Compliance: Initially inspect within 5 hours time in service from 15 April 1995. Re-inspect at intervals not to exceed 100 hours time in service.

Background: Cracking has been reported on in-service aircraft. Failure of the rudder pedal support tube could result in a reduction in control of the aircraft.

# AD/SD3-30/33 - Elevator and Rudder Skin Flukes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/33 Elevator and Rudder Skin Flukes 7/97

Applicability: All SD3-30 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD330-55-19.

*Note: UK CAA AD 006-02-97 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 19 September 1997.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 19 June 1997.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that a low standard of skin may be installed on some empennage control surfaces.

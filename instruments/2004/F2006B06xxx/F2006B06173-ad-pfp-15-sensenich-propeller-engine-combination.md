# AD/PFP/15 - Sensenich Propeller - Engine Combination

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Fixed Pitch

AD/PFP/15 Sensenich Propeller - Engine Combination 6/89

Applicability: All Sensenich model M76AM-2 or 76AM6-2 series propellers installed on Lycoming O-290-D2 engines.

Requirement: Remove from service and metal stamp the word "UNSERVICEABLE" on the propeller hub.

*Note: M74DM or 74DM6 propellers may be installed as replacements.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 April 1989 or by 31 May 1989 which ever occurs first.

Background: Due to recurring tip failures, Sensenich issued SB No 3, dated November 1956, requiring M76AM-2 or 76AM6-2 propellers to be retired from service. This was advised via AD/PA-20/6 but due to a recurrence of this combination on other aircraft types it has now been extended to all installations involving this engine and propeller combination.

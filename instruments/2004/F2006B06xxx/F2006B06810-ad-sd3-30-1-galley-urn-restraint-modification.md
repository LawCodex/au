# AD/SD3-30/1 - Galley Urn Restraint - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/1 Galley Urn Restraint - Modification 5/81

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install, to a scheme approved by the Secretary, a restraint system to secure the galley urn against flight and emergency alighting loads, independent of the urn compartment door.

Compliance: Before initial issue or renewal of Australian Certificate of Airworthiness or within 2 months time in service after 30 April 1981 for aircraft already in service.

Background: During a First of Type Inspection it was noted that the urn was restrained only by the compartment door and this could present a hazard to the cabin attendant and passengers during an emergency landing.

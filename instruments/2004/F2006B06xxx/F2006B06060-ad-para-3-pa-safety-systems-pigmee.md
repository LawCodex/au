# AD/PARA/3 - PA Safety Systems Pigmee

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Parachute Equipment

AD/PARA/3 PA Safety Systems Pigmee 3/93

Applicability: All Pigmee S007; 007(A), (B), (C) and (D) parachutes that incorporate the single operation system to activate the reserve.

Requirement: Action in accordance with PA Safety Systems Product Recall titled “Pigmee Red Handle (reserve ripcord) Proof Test and dated 16 November 1992.

Compliance: Prior to further use after 4 March 1993.

Background: During a periodic inspection a Red Handle ripcord was found to be in poor condition.

When tested the handle failed at ten percent of the required load.

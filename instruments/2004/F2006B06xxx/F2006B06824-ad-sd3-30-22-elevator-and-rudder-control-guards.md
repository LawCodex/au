# AD/SD3-30/22 - Elevator and Rudder Control Guards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/22 Elevator and Rudder Control Guards 5/87

Applicability: All model SD3-30 with S/Nos SH3002 to SH3099, SH3102 and SH3105. Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD330-27-31.

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1987.

Background: Flight control interference occurred as a result of an excessive load being applied to the back panel of a baggage locker.

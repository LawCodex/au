# AD/SD3-60/46 - Inner Flap Sub-Assembly Lever Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/46 Inner Flap Sub-Assembly Lever Bracket 7/97

Applicability: All SD3-60 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD360-27-26.

*Note: UK CAA AD 011-01-97 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 19 September 1997 for the initial inspection; thereafter as specified in the requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 19 June 1997.

Background: The manufacturer received reports of corrosion and cracking on inner flap sub- assembly lever brackets.

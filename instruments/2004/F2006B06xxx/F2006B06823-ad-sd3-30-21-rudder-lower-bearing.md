# AD/SD3-30/21 - Rudder Lower Bearing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/21 Rudder Lower Bearing 5/87

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD3-55-05 R2 Part A.

Compliance: At next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 7 May 1987 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service or 6 months, whichever occurs first.

Background: This requirement will reduce possible ice formation due to water ingress in the rudder lower bearing.

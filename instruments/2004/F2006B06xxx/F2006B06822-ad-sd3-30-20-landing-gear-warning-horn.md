# AD/SD3-30/20 - Landing Gear Warning Horn

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/20 Landing Gear Warning Horn 12/86

Applicability: All models SD3-30 with S/Nos. SH3002 through SH3107 and SH3109 through SH3120.

Requirement: 1. Upon replacement of Lucas Horn P/No. V.0603L with Keystone Horn P/No.

AC155F, relocate mounting of horn according to the requirements of Shorts SD3- 30 Service Bulletin SD330-32-114.

2\. For aeroplanes previously fitted with Keystone Horn P/No. AC155F, relocate mounting of horn according to the requirements of the document referenced in Para 1 above.

Compliance: For Para 1: Upon replacement.

For Para 2: Within 30 hours time in service after 31 January 1987.

Background: Reports have been received that the Keystone P/No. AC155F landing gear warning horn is inaudible when located between station 3 and 13.45 on the aircraft top centre line.

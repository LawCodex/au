# AD/SC7/10 - Automatic Pilot Aileron Servo Control Cable - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SC7/10 Automatic Pilot Aileron Servo Control Cable - Inspection

Applicability: Skyvan Series 3, 3A and 3M fitted with automatic pilot. Document: Short SB 22-A59.

Compliance: Before issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

## 7/79

Background: A malfunction has occurred where the cable connecting to the aileron automatic pilot servo motor capstan jammed between the two guide pulleys at Stn. 226. The inspection should avoid any repetition of this fault.

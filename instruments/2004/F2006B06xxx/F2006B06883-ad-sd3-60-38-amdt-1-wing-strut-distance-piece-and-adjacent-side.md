# AD/SD3-60/38 Amdt 1 - Wing Strut Distance Piece and Adjacent Side Plates Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/38**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All Models.

**Wing Strut Distance Piece and Adjacent Side Plates Corrosion**

**9/93**

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory SB SD360-53-38 Revision 1.

*Note: Accomplishment of SB SD360-53-39 constitutes terminating action for the inspections required by SB SD360-53-38.*

Compliance: a. Unless the inspection of both the distance piece and adjacent side plates has already been accomplished, within 30 days after 19 August 1993.

b. Reinspect reworked distance pieces and/or side plates at intervals not to exceed 600 hours time in service or 3 months, whichever occurs first.

Background: Corrosion had been detected on the horizontal leg of the distance piece during non routine maintenance.

Amendment 1 is issued to reflect a revision of the Requirement Document which introduces an inspection of the side plates in addition to the distance piece, and a repeat inspection of any reworked parts.

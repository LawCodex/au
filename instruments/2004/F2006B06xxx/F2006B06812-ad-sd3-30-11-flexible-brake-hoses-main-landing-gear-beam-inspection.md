# AD/SD3-30/11 - Flexible Brake Hoses/Main Landing Gear Beam - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SD3-30/11 Flexible Brake Hoses/Main Landing Gear

**Beam - Inspection and Modification**

Applicability: All models SD3-30 with S/Nos. SH3002 to SH3063. Document: Shorts SB No. SD3-32-92, Rev. 1.

## 6/82

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 300 hours time in service after 30 June 1982.

Background: Instances have occurred where the flexible brake hoses have been rubbing on the main landing gear beam causing wear in the internal bore.

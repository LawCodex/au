# AD/SD3-30/31 - Flap Hydraulic Unit Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/31 Flap Hydraulic Unit Brackets 2/96

Applicability: All SD330 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD330-27-34.

*Note: UK CAA AD 005-09-95 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, an initial inspection is required before 29 February 1996; thereafter as specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer received a report of exfoliation corrosion on brackets in flap hydraulic units.

# AD/SF340/42 - Nose and Main Landing Gear

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/42 Nose and Main Landing Gear 2/94

Applicability: Model SF340A with serial numbers 004 through 159.

Model 340B with serial numbers 160 through 346.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin 340-32-094.

*Note: LFV AD 1-061 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with short term action to be performed within 600 landings after 3 February 1994.

Background: Extension and retraction problems have been experienced in service as a result of loose retract actuator brackets. An investigation revealed that bolt shear had occurred at the head of one of the two bracket retaining bolts, which resulted in bracket movement.

# AD/SA-TP/26 Amdt 1 - Flap Operating System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Scottish Aviation) Twin Pioneer Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SA-TP/26

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Flap Operating System 1/87

Requirement: Action in accordance with Scottish Aviation TNS 50 issue 3.

Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service after last compliance with the previous issue of this Directive prior to 31 January 1987 and thereafter every 200 hours time in service.

Background: This amendment reflects the later issue of the TNS which extends compliance to 200 hours time in service.

# AD/PARA/4 - Rigging Innovations Skyhook Reserve Pilotchute

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Parachute Equipment

AD/PARA/4 Rigging Innovations Skyhook Reserve Pilotchute 8/93

Applicability: Skyhook reserve pilotchutes P/n 2233-( ) serial numbers 2405 to 5551 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Rigging Innovations SB 1513 Rev A dated 22 June 1992.

*Note: FAA AD 93-08-02 Amdt. 39-8549 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1993.

Background: This Directive requires testing of the pilotchutes to verify spring tension. It is prompted by an overseas incident of total pack closure of the pilotchute.

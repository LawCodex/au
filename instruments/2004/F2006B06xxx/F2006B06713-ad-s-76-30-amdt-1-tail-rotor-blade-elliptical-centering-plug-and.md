# AD/S-76/30 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Blade Elliptical Centering Plug and Retaining Pad

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/30**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: Model S76A.

**Tail Rotor Blade Elliptical Centering Plug and Retaining Pad**

**12/94**

Requirement: Action in accordance with Sikorsky SB 76-65-35 Rev A.

*Note: FAA AD 94-14-20 refers. This supersedes FAA AD 84-06-02.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 25 hours time in service after 13 October 1994.

The effective date of the initial issue was 27 February 1984.

Background: An operator encountered severe vibration during flight while executing a left turn after take-off. Investigation revealed disbonding of the aluminium elliptical spar plug which allowed the tail rotor blade to be displaced in a radial direction. Amendment 1 withdraws the repetitive inspection requirement as a result of no further reports of failure following the inspection and modification in accordance with the Service Bulletin.

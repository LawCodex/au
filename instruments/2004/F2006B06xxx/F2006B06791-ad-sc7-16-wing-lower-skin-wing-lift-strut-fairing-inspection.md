# AD/SC7/16 - Wing Lower Skin/Wing Lift Strut Fairing - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

AD/SC7/16 Wing Lower Skin/Wing Lift Strut Fairing - Inspection 2/80

Applicability: All Skyvan series 3, 3A and 3M. Document: Shorts SB No. 57-63.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1980 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified in accordance with SB No. 57-64.

Background: Damage to the wing lower skin can occur over a period of time due to incorrect location of the wing lift strut fairing.

# AD/SD3-30/7 - Aft Baggage Compartment - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/7 Aft Baggage Compartment - Modification 3/82

Applicability: All model SD3-30 with S/Nos. SH-3002 to SH-3071, SH-3073, SH-3075, SH-3077 and SH-3078.

Requirement: Shorts SB SD3-25-30.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, modify prior to 30 June 1982.

Background: An opening in the furnishings of the aft baggage compartment can result in an inadequate concentration of the fire extinguishant.

# AD/SF340/24 - Wing and Stabilizer Rework and Reinforcements

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/24 Wing and Stabilizer Rework and Reinforcements 5/91

Applicability: All Model SF340 with:

Serial Nos SF340A -004 through -108 for Service Bulletin SAAB 340-57-017, and

Serial Nos SF340A -004 through -138 for Service Bulletin SAAB 340-55-027. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Swedish Airworthiness

Directive No 1-035 Revision A, issued 29 June 1990.

*Note: Service Bulletins: SAAB 340-55-027 and SAAB 340-57-017 refer.*

Compliance: Prior to exceeding 16000 flight cycles.

Background: Full scale fatigue testing has indicated that the four referenced wing attach fastener holes, and the front and rear horizontal stabilizer spars are fatigue critical.

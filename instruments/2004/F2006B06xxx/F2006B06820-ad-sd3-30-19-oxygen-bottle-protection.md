# AD/SD3-30/19 - Oxygen Bottle Protection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/19 Oxygen Bottle Protection 6/86

Applicability: All model Serial Nos. SH3100, SH3101, SH3103, SH3104, SH3106, SH3107, SH3109 to SH3113.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD 330-25-47. Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1986.

Background: To prevent grease from control cables entering the area of the oxygen bottles.

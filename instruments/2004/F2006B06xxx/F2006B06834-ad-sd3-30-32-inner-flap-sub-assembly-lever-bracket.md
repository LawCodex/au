# AD/SD3-30/32 - Inner Flap Sub-Assembly Lever Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/32 Inner Flap Sub-Assembly Lever Bracket 5/97

Applicability: All SD3-30 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD3-27-36.

Compliance: Before 27 July 1997 for the initial inspection; thereafter as specified in the Requirement Document for the repeat inspection periods.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 24 April 1997.

Background: The manufacturer received reports of corrosion and cracking on inner flap sub- assembly lever brackets.

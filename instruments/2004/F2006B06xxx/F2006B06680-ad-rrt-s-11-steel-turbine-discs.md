# AD/RRT-S/11 - Steel Turbine Discs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Spey Series

AD/RRT-S/11 Steel Turbine Discs 13/92

Applicability: Spey series engines as detailed in Rolls Royce Service Bulletin Sp72-1044. Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of Rolls Royce Service Bulletin Sp

72-1044.

Compliance: Effective 10 December 1992: At next engine shop visit during which, either a low pressure turbine or a high pressure turbine overhaul is scheduled.

Background: This Directive requires steel turbine discs which have completed at least 40% of their published service life, to be inspected in accordance with the overhaul manual. The requirement addresses concern with evidence of low cycle fatigue cracks originating from corrosion pits in a number of Spey steel turbine discs.

# AD/S-76/12 - Upper Deck Flight Controls Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

AD/S-76/12 Upper Deck Flight Controls Installation - Modification

7/80

Applicability: All models S76 with S/Nos. 76003 and 76005, 760001 to 760006, 760009 and

760011 to 760015.

Document: Sikorsky CSB 76-67-1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1980.

Background: Rework of a bracket and angle is necessary to increase the clearances for rigging pin installation and roll gradient spring cylinder operation.

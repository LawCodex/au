# AD/ROBIN/15 - Lateral Console Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/15 Lateral Console Panel 7/92

Applicability: ATL, all types, to S/No 89 inclusive, except S/No 22, 28, 53, 71 and 82. Requirement: Action in accordance with ROBIN SA SB No 7.

*Note: DGAC AD 91-018(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; prior to further flight after 30 June 1992 if contact is evident, if no contact is evident accomplish modification prior to 31 July 1992.

Background: The DGAC requires an inspection and modification in order to prevent contact between the rudder pedals and the lateral console panel which could lead to the rudder pedals jamming.

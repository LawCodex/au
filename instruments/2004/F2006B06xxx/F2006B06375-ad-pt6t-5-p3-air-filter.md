# AD/PT6T/5 - P3 Air Filter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Canada Turbine Engines - PT6T Series

AD/PT6T/5 P3 Air Filter 10/85

Applicability: PT6T-3, -3B, -6 and ST6T-75, -76 with S/Nos. as per SB No. 5206, Rev. 3. Document: 1. Pratt & Whitney Aircraft of Canada SB No. 5124, Rev. 5; and

2\. Pratt & Whitney Aircraft of Canada SB No. 5180, Rev. 2; or

3\. Pratt & Whitney Aircraft of Canada SB No. 5206, Rev. 3; or

4\. AD/UAC-T/33 Amdt 3.

*Note: Canadian Airworthiness Directive No. CF-83-04 refers.*

Compliance: Not later than next overhaul.

Background: This Directive is amended in order to specify Service Bulletin No. 5206, Rev. 3 as an alternative means of compliance and to reference the Canadian Airworthiness Directive concerned.

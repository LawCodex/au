# AD/SD3-60/8 Amdt 2 - Elevator Torque Shaft Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/8**

**Amdt 2**

**Elevator Torque Shaft Assembly 6/85 DM**

Applicability: All Models SD-360 aircraft. Requirement: 1. Shorts SB SD360-27-A06 Rev 1.

2\. Shorts SB SD360-27-06.

Compliance: 1. Prior to 14 March 1985 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 60 hours time in service or pre-first flight following parking in severe wind gust conditions.

2\. Prior to 15 August 1985.

Background: This Directive was issued after loss of elevator control on a SD3-30 aircraft. Rev 1 extended the inspection area and introduced a repetitive inspection. Rev 2 introduces mandatory modification to reinforce the attachment of the elevator torque shaft housings.

# AD/ROCK-114/3 - Cabin Air Vent - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Rockwell) 114 Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROCK-114/3 Cabin Air Vent - Modification 5/78

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. listed in requirement document. Document: Rockwell SB No. SB-114-6.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978.

*Note: When this Directive has been complied with the operator may apply to a Regional Airworthiness Office of the Department for the withdrawal of the flight manual “P” amendment to para. 3.4.*

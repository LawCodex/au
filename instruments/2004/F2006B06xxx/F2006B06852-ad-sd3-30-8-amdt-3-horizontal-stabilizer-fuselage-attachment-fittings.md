# AD/SD3-30/8 Amdt 3 - Horizontal Stabilizer/Fuselage Attachment Fittings, Pins and Bushes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-30/8**

**Amdt 3**

**Horizontal Stabilizer/Fuselage Attachment Fittings, Pins and Bushes**

**8/92**

Applicability: All Models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory SB SD3-55-16 Revision 4. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

The compliance of the previous issue of this Directive is as stated. This Amendment is effective from 23 July 1992.

Background: Service experience reported by the Manufacturer indicates that under certain conditions corrosion and/or wear may occur in the fittings. Amendment 2 of this Directive clarified the repeat inspection operating hours and introduces calendar limits. Amendment 3 is issued to reflect a revision of the Requirement Document which alters the content of the accomplishment instructions.

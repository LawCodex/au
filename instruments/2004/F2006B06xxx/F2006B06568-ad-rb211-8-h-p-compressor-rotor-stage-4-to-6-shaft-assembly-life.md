# AD/RB211/8 - H.P. Compressor Rotor Stage 4 to 6 Shaft Assembly - Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - RB211 Series

**AD/RB211/8 H.P. Compressor Rotor Stage 4 to 6**

**Shaft Assembly - Life Limitation**

**5/83**

CANCELLED.

Background: Extensive laboratory investigation and stress analysis has now shown that the life restriction placed upon the affected assemblies by Rolls Royce RB211 SB211-72- 6420 can be removed. They can now assume the same life as their unaffected equivalent parts.

# AD/SF340/48 - Rudder Nose Rib

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/48 Rudder Nose Rib 13/95

Applicability: Model SF 340A with serial numbers 004 through 159.

Model 340B with serial numbers 160 through 369.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin 340-55-032.

*Note: LFV AD 1-074 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 7 December 1995.

Background: A report was received of a cracked nose rib on the front spar of the rudder. This condition could result in jamming between the fin and rudder due to deformation of the cracked nose rib web.

# AD/SF340/11 - Pitot Systems Water Traps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/11 Pitot Systems Water Traps 11/88

Applicability: Model SF340A with serial numbers 004 through 138.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Scania AB Service Bulletin SF340-34-056 dated 1 August 1988 or later revision.

*Note: Swedish Airworthiness Directive No. 1-027 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1988.

Background: Water intrusion into the pitot systems may occur in heavy rain. This can result in ice blocking the systems leading to erroneous air data signals.

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Propellers - Variable Pitch - Dowty Rotol

**AD/PR/24**

**Amdt 6**

**Beta Tube 2/90**

Applicability: All models R354/4-123-F/13 R354/4-123-F/20 and R375/4-123-F/21.

# AD/RB211/10 Amdt 1 - High Pressure Compressor Disc

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - RB211 Series

**AD/RB211/10**

**Amdt 1**

**High Pressure Compressor Disc 9/87**

Applicability: RB211-524 Series Engines.

Requirement: Remove from service, stage 1 and stage 2 high pressure compressor disc assemblies identified by serial number in Rolls Royce Service Bulletin RB211-72-A7774 Rev 2 Appendix 3 and Appendix 4.

*Note: FAA AD 86-15-01 Amdt 39-5346 refers.*

Compliance: In accordance with Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive has been amended to reflect the manufacturers latest revision to Service Bulletin RB211-72-A7774. The revision, in part, increases the number of discs in Appendix 3 and Appendix 4.

# AD/SA-TP/28 - Wing Lift Strut Fretting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Scottish Aviation) Twin Pioneer Series Aeroplanes

AD/SA-TP/28 Wing Lift Strut Fretting 12/86

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with Scottish Aviation MNS No 965. Compliance: Prior to the issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: This modification is to eliminate fretting on the surface of the wing lift strut at the stabilizer strut fitting.

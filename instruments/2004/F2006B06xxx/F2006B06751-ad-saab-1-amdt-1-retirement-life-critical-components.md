# AD/SAAB/1 Amdt 1 - Retirement Life - Critical Components

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB Safir Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SAAB/1**

**Amdt 1**

**Retirement Life - Critical Components 5/95**

Applicability: All Saab Safir 91B, 91B-2, 91C and 91D.

Requirement: Inspect, check and retire structure and components in accordance with Saab Service Bulletin (SB) 91.1.005 Revision 2.

*Note: Swedish LFV SAD 1-058 Revision B refers.*

Compliance: Effective 30 May 1995.

Background: This Amendment references the latest issue of the applicable Service Bulletin which has superseded Saab SB’s 91.1.003 and 91.3.001.

# AD/ROBIN/17 - Safety Harness Link

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/17 Safety Harness Link 7/92

Applicability: ATL, all types.

Requirement: Action in accordance with ROBIN SA SB No.6 Rev 1.

*Note: DGAC AD 89-075(A) R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; Within 50 hours time in service after 25 June 1992. Background: The DGAC requires modification action in order to prevent the nut of the quick-

disconnect link to unscrew.

# AD/SD3-30/12 Amdt 1 - Rib/Skin Attachment Cleats at Left Wing Station 160

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-30/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Rib/Skin Attachment Cleats at Left Wing Station 160**

**13/93**

Applicability: Model SD3-30 with serial numbers SH3002 through SH3090.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD3-57-10 Revision 2.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document, with flight hours to count from previous certified compliance.

This Amendment is effective from 9 December 1993.

Background: The manufacturer received reports of cases where cracks have been detected in the rib/skin attachment cleats at the wing upper surface, Station 160. Amendment 1 is issued in response to the latest issue of the Requirement Document which aligns SB Paragraph 1.E, Part A,4 with the "Not Exceed Time" specified in the Maintenance Programme.

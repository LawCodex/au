# AD/RES/14 - Integral Buckle Assembly, Pacific Scientific P/N 1107261-01, -05, -09 Replace Lower Cover P/N 1107270-01 with Cover Assembly P/N 1107525-01

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Restraint Equipment

**AD/RES/14 Integral Buckle Assembly, Pacific Scientific P/N 1107261-01, -05, -09 Replace Lower Cover**

**P/N 1107270-01 with Cover Assembly P/N 1107525-01**

**8/83**

Applicability: All Pacific Scientific buckle assemblies P/N 1107261-01, 05, -09 manufactured prior to 1982.

Document: Pacific Scientific SB No. 1107261-25-01, Revision 1.

*Note: FAA AD 83-11-03, Amdt. 39-4662 refers.*

Compliance: Within six months after 31st August, 1983.

Background: Entrapment of occupants could result from inability to release the subject restraint system assembly.

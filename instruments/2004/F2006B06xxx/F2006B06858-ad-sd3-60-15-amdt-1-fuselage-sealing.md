# AD/SD3-60/15 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Sealing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/15**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage Sealing 8/86**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos SH3601 to SH3678.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB360-53-22, Revision 1. Compliance: Prior to 31 September 1986.

Background: This AD has been amended to include S.B. 360-53-22 Revision 1 to compliance document, which adds to the positions requiring sealing.

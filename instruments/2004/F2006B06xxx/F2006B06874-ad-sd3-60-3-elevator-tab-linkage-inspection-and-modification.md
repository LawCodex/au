# AD/SD3-60/3 - Elevator Tab Linkage - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/3 Elevator Tab Linkage - Inspection and Modification 1/84

Applicability: All model SD3-60 aircraft with S/Nos SH 3601 to SH 3623. Requirement: 1. Shorts S.B. SD360-55-A02.

2\. Shorts S.B. SD360-55-02 modification 7448.

Compliance: 1. Within 60 hours ("A" Check) time in service after 12 December 1983 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 60 hours ("A" check) until modified.

2\. Before 31 March 1984.

Background: Analysis shows that in the event of elevator tab disconnect there could be an unacceptable level of vibration.

# AD/SC7/1 - Left Front Engine Shockmount - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SC7/1 Left Front Engine Shockmount - Inspection

**and Replacement** Applicability: All models of series 3 not incorporating Mod. 763. Document: Short SB No. 71-50.

## 6/69

Compliance: 1. Inspect in accordance with Part 1 of SB No. 71-50 at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified to Part 2.

2\. Modify in accordance with Part 2 of SB No. 71-50 before exceeding 1500 hours total time in service.

# AD/SEATS/11 - First Class Seats Motor System Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Seats and Berths

AD/SEATS/11 First Class Seats Motor System Cable 11/97

Applicability: Britax Rumbold first class seats fitted on Boeing 747, Boeing 777, and McDonnell Douglas DC-10 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Britax Rumbold Service Bulletin SBM13000-25-929.

*Note: UK CAA AD 003-05-97 refers.*

Compliance: Before 9 January 1998 for the initial inspection, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 6 months; until Britax Rumbold Service bulletin SBM13000-25-911 (Modification CRR SE012) is incorporated.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 9 October 1997.

Background: The CAA requires certain actions because damage and wear to the cable could be caused by movement of the seat sliding trolley.

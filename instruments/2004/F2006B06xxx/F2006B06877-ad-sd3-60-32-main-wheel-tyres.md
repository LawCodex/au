# AD/SD3-60/32 - Main Wheel Tyres

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/32 Main Wheel Tyres 4/91

Applicability: Model SD3-60 Series 300; plus aircraft Serial Numbers SH3708, SH3712, SH3713, SH3714, SH3715.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mandatory Dunlop Limited SB 32-1044.

*Note: Shorts SD3-60 IL-171 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document but with a revised effectivity date of 18 April 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Premature failure of main landing gear tyres part number DR19922T has occurred.

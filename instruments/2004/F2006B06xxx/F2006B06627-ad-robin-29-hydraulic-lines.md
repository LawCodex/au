# AD/ROBIN/29 - Hydraulic Lines

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/29 Hydraulic Lines 11/96

Applicability: All HR 100/250 and HR 100/285 series aircraft fitted with retractable landing gear.

Requirement: Replace the inlet and outlet pipes of the Retractable Gear (R/G) hydraulic pump as required by Avions Pierre Robin SB No 148 dated April 26, 1996.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-147(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously carried out, at the next scheduled aircraft maintenance inspection and thereafter, at every second disassembly of the pipes.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that frequent removal and re-tightening of the tube fitting nuts located at the end of the fluid pipes has resulted in cracking of the tubing flares.

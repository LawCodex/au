# AD/SD3-30/26 Amdt 1 - Pitot Static Heaters

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SD3-30/26

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All SD3-30.

## Pitot Static Heaters 12/91

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD3-30-24-25 Revision 2.

*Note: UK CAA mandatory.*

Compliance: At next 'D' check or 4800 flight hours after 30 November 1990, whichever occurs later, but no later than 1 June 1992.

Background: To prevent the loss of pitot heat whenever a generator fails. Amendment 1 to this AD is issued to reflect revised procedures introduced by Revision 2 to SB SD3-30-24-25.

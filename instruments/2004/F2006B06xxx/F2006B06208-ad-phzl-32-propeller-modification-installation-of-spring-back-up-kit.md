**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

**AD/PHZL/32 Propeller Modification - Installation**

**of Spring Back-up Kit**

**7/72**

Applicability: Models HC-E2YK-2RB, HC-E2YR-2RB and HC-E2YL-2( ) propellers fitted with 8465-7R, 7663-4 or J7663-4 non counterweighted blades.

2\. FAA AD 71-21-9.

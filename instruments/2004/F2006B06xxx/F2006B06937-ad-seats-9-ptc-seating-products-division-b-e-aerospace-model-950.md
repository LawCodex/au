# AD/SEATS/9 - PTC Seating Products Division B/E Aerospace Model 950

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Seats and Berths

AD/SEATS/9 PTC Seating Products Division B/E Aerospace Model 950

12/96

Applicability: PTC Seating Products Division, B/E Aerospace model 950 equipped with a footrest assembly.

Requirement: 1. Remove footrest assembly arms P/N 98440-1, or -2, in accordance with PTC Aerospace SB 25-1192 Rev A.

2\. Install conversion kit P/N 122966-1 in accordance with PTC Aerospace SB 251330.

*Note: FAA AD 96-12-09 refers.*

Compliance: Before 12 June 1997.

Background: This Directive is prompted by finger injuries that have occurred during attempts to either extend or retract the footrest assembly.

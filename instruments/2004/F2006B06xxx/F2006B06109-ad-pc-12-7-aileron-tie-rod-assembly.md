# AD/PC-12/7 - Aileron Tie Rod Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus PC-12 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PC-12/7 Aileron Tie Rod Assembly 8/97

Applicability: Model PC-12 and PC-12/45 aircraft, with serial numbers MSN 101 through to and including MSN 169.

Requirement: Inspect and modify in accordance with Pilatus Mandatory Service Bulletin 27-001.

*Note: FOCA Switzerland AD HB 97-174 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 17 October 1997.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 17 July 1997.

Background: A report was received where the aileron tie-rod jam-nuts, on the aileron movement chain of the control wheels, became loose.

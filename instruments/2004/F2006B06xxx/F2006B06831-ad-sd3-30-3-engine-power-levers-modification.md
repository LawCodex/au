# AD/SD3-30/3 - Engine Power Levers - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/3 Engine Power Levers - Modification 11/81

Applicability: Model SD3-30 with S/Nos. SH3002 to SH3068. Document: Shorts SB No. SD3-76-01.

Compliance: Before 1 April 1982.

Background: Modification of the engine power lever cam mechanism will inhibit take-off with the flight control locks still engaged.

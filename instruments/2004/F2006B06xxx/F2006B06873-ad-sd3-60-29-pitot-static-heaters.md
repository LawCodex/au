# AD/SD3-60/29 - Pitot Static Heaters

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/29 Pitot Static Heaters 10/90

Applicability: SD3-60 aircraft S/No's SH3601 to SH3760 inclusive. Requirement: Shorts SD3-60 SB SD360-24-18.

*Note: UK CAA mandatory.*

Compliance: At next 'D' check or 4800 flight hours after 30 November 1990, whichever occurs later.

Background: To prevent the loss of pitot heat whenever a generator fails.

# AD/SD3-30/4 - Fuselage Frame at Station 340.46 - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/4 Fuselage Frame at Station 340.46 - Modification 12/81

Applicability: All model SD-330 with S/Nos. referenced in Shorts SB No. SD3-53-43 or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues or revisions of that SB.

Document: Shorts SB NO. SD3-53-43.

Compliance: Within 400 hours time in service after 31 December 1981.

Background: Manufacturers inspection of production aircraft has revealed omission of reinforcing plates.

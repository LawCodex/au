# AD/S-76/40 - Power Train Tail Drive Shaft Bearing Support Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/40 Power Train Tail Drive Shaft Bearing Support Assembly**

**9/85**

**TX**

Applicability: All model S76 which have bearing support assembly bearings manufactured by MRC installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Sikorsky Alert Service Bulletin No. 76- 66-20. Compliance: Before further flight after 31 July 1985.

Background: It has been determined that some bearings manufactured by MRC may have been assembled without grease.

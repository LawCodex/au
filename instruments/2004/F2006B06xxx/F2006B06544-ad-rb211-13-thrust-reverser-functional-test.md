# AD/RB211/13 - Thrust Reverser Functional Test

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - RB211 Series

AD/RB211/13 Thrust Reverser Functional Test 6/91

Applicability: All RB211-524G and 524H engines not incorporating the requirements of Rolls Royce Service Bulletin RB211-78-9347.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of Rolls Royce Mandatory Service Bulletin RB211-78-9274.

Compliance: As detailed in Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive requires a functional test to be carried out following any abandoned take-off where the thrust reversers have been deployed. This requirement reflects advice that following an abandoned take-off, thrust reverser operation times may become extended (10 seconds or more).

# AD/S-76/3 Amdt 1 - Engine Air Inlet Cowl and Aft Cowl Assemblies - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Air Inlet Cowl and Aft Cowl Assemblies - Modification**

**6/80**

Applicability: All model S-76 with S/Nos. 76005 to 76007, 760001 to 760037.

Documents: 1. Sikorsky SB 76-53-10;

2\. Sikorsky Drawings 76070-30001 and 76080-30007;

3\. Sikorsky EO 83184 against Drawing 76303-07001; EO 83185 against Drawing 76302-07001; and EO 83189, EO 83190 and EO 83191 against Drawing 76080- 30006; or

4\. FAA AD 80-02-10, Amendment 39-3717.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1980.

Background: This Directive is varied to prevent structural failures of the engine air inlet cowl doors and in-flight opening of their attaching latch handles.

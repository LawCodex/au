# AD/R22/12 Amdt 2 - Upper Steel Tube Frames

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robinson R22 Series Helicopters

**AD/R22/12**

**Amdt 2**

**Upper Steel Tube Frames 11/86**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 0002 to 0311 having A020-2 steel tube frames S/Nos. 0002 to 0399 installed.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Robinson S.B. No. 47.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 31 May 1986 and thereafter as detailed in the Requirement Document.

Background: Service Bulletin No. 47 supersedes S.B.’s 3, 18 and 23; and requires inspection of all welded areas of the A020-2 frame. This amendment reduces the frame applicability serial numbers from 0002-0400 to 0002-0399.

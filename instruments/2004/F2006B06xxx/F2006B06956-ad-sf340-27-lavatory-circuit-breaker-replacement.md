# AD/SF340/27 - Lavatory Circuit Breaker Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/27 Lavatory Circuit Breaker Replacement 7/91

Applicability: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements Swedish AD 1-045.

*Note: SAAB-SCANIA SB 340-25-181 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 8 December 1991.

Background: Action in accordance with this AD is necessary in order to prevent possible wire overload if a failure occurs in the forward or aft lavatory electrical system.

*Note: The compliance date specified in the Requirement document has been extended from 8 September 1991 to 8 December 1991.*

# AD/SD3-60/14 - Tailplane Attachment Fitting Bushes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/14 Tailplane Attachment Fitting Bushes 1/86

Applicability: All Models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD360-55-10. Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1986.

Background: Interference fit bushes have been found loose in the tailplane attachment fittings.

# AD/SEATS/6 - Aviatech Flight Crew Seats

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Seats and Berths

AD/SEATS/6 Aviatech Flight Crew Seats 7/91

Applicability: Aviatech Inc. pilot & co-pilot seats Model 394, P/N's 394-(000)-01, -02, -03, -04, -05

and -06, and 394-(001)-01, -02, -03, -04, -05 and -06, approved by Transport Canada under TSO Design Approval C39A used on de Havilland DHC-8 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Requirements of Transport Canada AD CF-91-04.

*Note: Aviatech SB 2 Rev A refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 September 1991.

Background: There have been cases of excessive wear and fraying of the pilot and co-pilot shoulder harness caused by rubbing of the shoulder harness on the guides located on top of the seat-back. Compliance date required by Transport Canada is extended by one month due to receipt date of Canadian Directive.

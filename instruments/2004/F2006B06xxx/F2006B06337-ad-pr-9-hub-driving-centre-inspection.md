# AD/PR/9 - Hub Driving Centre - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Dowty Rotol

**AD/PR/9 Hub Driving Centre - Inspection NK** Applicability: All model (c) R184/4-30-4/50 propellers not incorporating Mod. No. (c) VP 2486. Document: Dowty Rotol SB No. 61-633.

Compliance: Before exceeding 2500 hours total time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 750 hours time in service.

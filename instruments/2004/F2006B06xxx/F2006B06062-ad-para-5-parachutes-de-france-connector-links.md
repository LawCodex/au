# AD/PARA/5 - Parachutes De France - Connector Links

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Parachute Equipment

AD/PARA/5 Parachutes De France - Connector Links 10/95

Applicability: Parachutes De France emergency parachutes P512()01 delivered after 21 June 1994, and with the following serial numbers:

| DE039 to DE046 | DF001 to DF024 | DG001 to DG017 |
|---|---|---|
| DH001 to DH006 | DJ001 to DJ019 | DM001 to DM025 |
| EC001 to EC031 | ED001 to ED025 | EE001 to EE019 |

Requirement: Inspect the connector links between the canopy and the harness container, and verify full thread engagement and torque in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

*Note: DGAC AD 95-131(AB) and Parachutes De France ASB P512-25-002 refer.*

Compliance: Prior to further use after 14 September 1995.

Background: During a periodic inspection a parachute was found with loose connector links.

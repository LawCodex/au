# AD/SF340/14 - Emergency Lighting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/14 Emergency Lighting 3/89

Applicability: All SAAB SF 340A aircraft S/Nos 004 through 159 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin SF 340-33-028.

*Note: SAD 1-030 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 22 July 1989.

Background: The FAA have determined that the SAAB SF 340 emergency lighting system does not fully comply with FAR 25.812(f)(3).

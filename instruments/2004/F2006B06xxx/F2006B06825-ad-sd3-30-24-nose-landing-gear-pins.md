# AD/SD3-30/24 - Nose Landing Gear Pins

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/24 Nose Landing Gear Pins 2/89

Applicability: All models with NLG Assemblies Part No 18001 Serial Nos 017 thru 097. Requirement: Action in accordance with Menasco Service Bulletin No.32-69.

*Note: Shorts Service Bulletin SD 330-32-119 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to the accumulation of 30,000 landings.

Background: It has been found during fatigue testing that pins P/No 18109-3 and 18110-3 have a limited service life.

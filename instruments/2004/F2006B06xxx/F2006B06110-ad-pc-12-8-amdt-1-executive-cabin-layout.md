# AD/PC-12/8 Amdt 1 - Executive Cabin Layout

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus PC-12 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PC-12/8**

**Amdt 1**

**Executive Cabin Layout 9/98**

Applicability: Lavatory Wall Modification: All PC-12 and PC-12/45 aircraft, MSN 101 up to and including MSN 180, which have an executive cabin layout.

Passenger Seat Configuration Modifications: All PC-12 and PC-12/45 aircraft, MSN 101 up to and including MSN 171, which have an executive cabin layout.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with Pilatus Aircraft Service Bulletin (SB) 25-003 Revision 1.

*Note: FOCA Switzerland AD HB 97-249A refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished in accordance with SB 25-003, before 13 September 1998.

This amendment becomes effective on 13 August 1998.

Background: In order to comply with the Head Injury Criteria requirements of FAR 23.562 (c)(5), this Directive requires modification of the executive cabin type layout.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to revision of the FOCA AD which introduces a revised Requirement document which clarifies certain requirements.

The original issue of this airworthiness directive became effective on 2 July 1997.

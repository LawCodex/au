# AD/ROTAX/4 - Electronic Module With "Pi" Filter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rotax Piston Engines

AD/ROTAX/4 Electronic Module With  Filter 2/96

Applicability: This Directive is applicable to all 912A series engines from serial number 4,076.064 to serial number 4,380.753 inclusive.

Requirement: Replace the electronic ignition module part number 965 356 with part number 965 358 in accordance with Bombardier Rotax Technical Bulletin 912-08 dated 16 August 1995.

*Note: Austro Control (Austria) AD 84 dated 4 October 1995 refers.*

Compliance: At the next 100 hour inspection, but not later than 1 March 1996.

Background: Electronic modules with integrated pi filter protect the ignition from electromagnetic interference. EMI tests have revealed that poor engine performance, or engine cut- out, could result in areas of heavy radio transmission.

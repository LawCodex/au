# AD/PA-44/9 - Aileron Balance Weight Attachment Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-44 (Seminole) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-44/9 Aileron Balance Weight Attachment**

**Inspection and Modification**

**10/81**

Applicability: All models PA-44-180 with serial numbers 44-7995001 to 44-8195016. All models PA-44-180T with serial numbers 44-8107001 to 44-8107044.

Document: Piper S.B. No. 725A. FAA AD 81-16-10 refers.

Compliance: Part I: Initially inspect before further flight and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service until modified in accordance with Piper S.B. No.

725A.

*Note: The pilot in command may perform and certify for this inspection.*

Part II: Within 200 hours time in service after 21 August 1981.

Background: It is possible that the aileron balance weights may become loose in the outboard leading edge of the aileron.

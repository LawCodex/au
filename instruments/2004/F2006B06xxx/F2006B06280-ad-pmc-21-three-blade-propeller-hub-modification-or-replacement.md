# AD/PMC/21 - Three Blade Propeller Hub - Modification or Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - McCauley

**AD/PMC/21 Three Blade Propeller Hub - Modification**

**or Replacement**

**7/71**

Applicability: Models D3A32C77, C79, C88 and C90 models with no model number change letter or letter “A” to “J” fitted to Cessna 206 series aircraft with normally aspirated Continental IO-520 engine not modified in accordance with McCauley SB No. 88 (AD/PMC/20 refers).

Document: 1. Cessna SL SE71-15; or

2\. McCauley SB No. 89.

Compliance: 1. For propellers which have completed 600 hours or more total time in service - within 25 hours time in service after 30 July 1971.

2\. For propellers which have completed 500 hours total time in service - before achieving 600 hours total time in service.

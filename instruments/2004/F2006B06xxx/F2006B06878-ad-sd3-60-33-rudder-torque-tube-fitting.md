# AD/SD3-60/33 - Rudder Torque Tube Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/33 Rudder Torque Tube Fitting 8/91

Applicability: Model SD360.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mandatory Shorts Service Bulletin SD360-55-17. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 8 August

1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Instances have been reported where the rudder torque tube fitting at rib 1 has been subject to exfoliation corrosion. The effective date required by the UK CAA is extended by three months.

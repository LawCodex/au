# AD/PL 12/14 Amdt 1 - Nose Landing Gear Pivot Pin and Bushes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PL 12/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Nose Landing Gear Pivot Pin and Bushes 2/89**

Applicability: PL 12 and PL 12U.

Requirement: 1. Replace nose landing gear Pivot Pin P/N 6-24 Issue “C” and Bushes P/N 6-22 Issue “A” with Pin P/N 6-24 Issue “D” and Bushes P/N 6-22 Issue “B” or with Pin P/N 6-33 and Bushes P/N 6-34.

2\. Inspect nose landing gear Pivot Pin P/N 6-24 Issue “D” or 6-33 for tooling or machine marks. Reject those pins with tooling or machine marks.

*Note: Pin P/N 6-24 Issue “D” is same as Pin P/N 6-33 and Bush P/N 6-22 Issue “B” is same as Pin P/N 6-34.*

| Compliance: | For Para. 1 - | Unless already accomplished, within 25 hours time in service after 31 October 1973. |
|---|---|---|
|  | For Para. 2 - | (i) For Installed Pins: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1973. |
|  |  | (ii) For Spare Pins: Before installation. |

Background: This Amendment deletes “All models” from the “Applicability” and reflects design and manufacturing improvements to later model aircraft.

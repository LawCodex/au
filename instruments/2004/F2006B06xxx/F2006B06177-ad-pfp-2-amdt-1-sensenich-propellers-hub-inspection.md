# AD/PFP/2 Amdt 1 - Sensenich Propellers - Hub Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Fixed Pitch

**AD/PFP/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Sensenich Propellers - Hub Inspection NK**

Applicability: All model M74DM installed on Lycoming 0-320-B series engines except propellers with an "A" or "K" S/No. prefix and all Model M74DM regardless of S/No. prefix, installed on all Lycoming 0-320 series engines when used in agricultural operations.

Document: Sensenich SB No. R-8.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/ROBIN/14 - Fuel Tank Selector Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/14 Fuel Tank Selector Valve 7/92 DM

Applicability: All ATL and ATL"S" Series Robin aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of DGAC AD 88-050(A) dated 30 March 1988.

*Note: Robin SA Service Bulletin No 5 refers.*

Compliance: By 22 May 1992. Thereafter, reinspect at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer reports that due to an operational incident a repetitive inspection of the fuel selector valve is required.

# AD/ROBIN/20 - Main Gear Torque Link Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/20 Main Gear Torque Link Attachment 13/94

Applicability: Model HR100/250TR and HR100/285TR.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Avions Pierre Robin Service Bulletin 134.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-129(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 25 hours time in service after 8 December 1994, and thereafter at intervals as specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The DGAC requires an inspection in response to field reports of cracks on the strut torque link welded attachment points.

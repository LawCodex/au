# AD/ROBIN/12 Amdt 1 - Safety Harness Link

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

**AD/ROBIN/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Safety Harness Link 6/92**

Applicability: Models as listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Avions Pierre Robin SB 114 Rev.1.

*Note: DGAC AD 89-074(A)R1 Refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 21 May 1992. The compliance of the previous issue of this Directive was:

Prior to exceeding 25 hours time in service after 2 August 1989, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

Background: Country of Origin AD. Manufacturer reports possibility of defective quick-disconnect link on safety belt.

Amendment 1 is raised in response to a revision of the related DGAC AD which requires a modification to the link. This requirement constitutes terminating action in lieu of repetitive inspections.

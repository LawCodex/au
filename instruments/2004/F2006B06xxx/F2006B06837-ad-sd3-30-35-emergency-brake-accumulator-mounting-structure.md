# AD/SD3-30/35 - Emergency Brake Accumulator Mounting Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/35 Emergency Brake Accumulator Mounting Structure

2/99

Applicability: All SD3-30 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD330-29-19.

*Note: UK CAA AD 008-10-98 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 25 May 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 25 February 1999.

Background: The manufacturer received reports of cracking of the emergency brake accumulator mounting structure. This Directive requires an inspection for cracks, and if required, the replacement of the mounting angle.

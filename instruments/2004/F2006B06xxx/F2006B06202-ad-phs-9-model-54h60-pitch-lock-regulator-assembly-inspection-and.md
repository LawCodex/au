Propellers - Variable Pitch - Hamilton Standard

AD/PHS/9 Model 54H60 Pitch Lock Regulator Assembly - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: Propeller models 54H60-81, 91, 117, 125. Document: Hamilton Standard SB 54H60, No.-59 Rev. 1.

*Note: FAA AD 75-03-05 refers.*

Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

Background: To reduce the possibility of separation of the pitchlock extension sleeve, modifications are introduced to the retention thread and surge valve housing.

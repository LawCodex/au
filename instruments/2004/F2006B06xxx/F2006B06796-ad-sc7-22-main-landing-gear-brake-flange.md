# AD/SC7/22 - Main Landing Gear Brake Flange

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

AD/SC7/22 Main Landing Gear Brake Flange 10/95

Applicability: Skyvan MK 3, 3A, 3M, and Skyliner series aeroplanes.

Requirement: Inspect, using the fluorescent penetrant method, the main landing gear brake flanges in accordance with Dowty Mandatory Service Bulletin (MSB) 32-10M, Revision 3.

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 017-12-81 refers.*

Compliance: Before the thresholds specified in Dowty MSB 32-10M, Revision 3 or before 17 September 1995, whichever occurs later.

Re-inspect at intervals not to exceed 1000 landings.

Background: Dowty has completed a fatigue analysis of the main landing gear and inspections of the brake flange are required to take advantage of the increase in service life which resulted.

# AD/RB211/9 - Fan Cowl Support Strut Stowage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - RB211 Series

AD/RB211/9 Fan Cowl Support Strut Stowage 7/86

Applicability: RB211-524 series engines.

Requirement: Modify the fan cowl support strut mechanism in accordance with Rolls Royce S.B. RB 211-71-7254 Rev 1.

Reference Document FAA AD 86-07-03 Amdt 39-5276.

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1987.

Background: This Directive is issued to require modification of the left rear fan cowl door support stowage mechanism. The modification is required to prevent an unrestrained cowl support jamming the throttle control linkages.

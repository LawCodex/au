# AD/PZL/3 - Upper Fin Rudder Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
PZL M18 Dromader Series Aeroplanes

AD/PZL/3 Upper Fin Rudder Attachment 8/91

Applicability: Serial Number 1Z001-01 to 1Z021-30.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 90-17-15, Amendment 39-6678.

*Note: PZL-Mielec Mandatory Engineering Bulletin (MEB) No. K/02.135/89, dated 29 December 1989, refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with revised effective date of 8 August 1991.

Background: This AD provides for inspection and reinforcement of the fin in the area of the upper rudder attach point, following two reports of cracks and damage to the area.

# AD/SD3-30/6 - Passenger Cabin Window - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/6 Passenger Cabin Window - Modification 1/82

Applicability: SD 3-30 aircraft with S/Nos. SH 3002 to SH 3071; and SH 3073, SH 3075, SH 3077

and SH 3078.

Document: Shorts SB No. SD3-56-02.

(Modification No. 6159).

Compliance: Accomplish before 1 April, 1982.

Background: An instance has been reported where ice cast by the propeller penetrated the right hand window immediately aft of the propeller plane.

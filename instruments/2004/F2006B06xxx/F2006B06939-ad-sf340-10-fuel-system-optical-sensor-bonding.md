# AD/SF340/10 - Fuel System Optical Sensor Bonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/10 Fuel System Optical Sensor Bonding 8/88

Applicability: SAAB SF 340 aircraft S/No’s -004 through -106.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin SF340-28-006.

*Note: Swedish Airworthiness Directive SAD No 1-025 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 6 October 1988.

Background: SAAB have advised that they have discovered that in some aircraft the fitting of a jumper assembly on the mid level and tank full optical sensors has been omitted. If not corrected, this could create a hazardous situation if a lightning strike occurs.

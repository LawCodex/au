# AD/PMC/37 - Blade Counterweight Bolt Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - McCauley

AD/PMC/37 Blade Counterweight Bolt Replacement 5/83

Applicability: All 3AF32C504, 3AF32C505, 3AF32C506, 3AF32C507, 3AF32C508 and

3AF32C509 models with serial numbers 803546 through 816154.

Document: McCauley Service Bulletin 147.

Compliance: By the next maintenance release inspection, after 31st May 1983.

Background: The manufacturer reports failures of blade counterweight bolts. This Directive introduces a new maintenance practise to nullify this problem.

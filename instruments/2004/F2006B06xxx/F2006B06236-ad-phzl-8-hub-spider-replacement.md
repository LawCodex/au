# AD/PHZL/8 - Hub Spider - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Hartzell

AD/PHZL/8 Hub Spider - Replacement NK

Applicability: All HC12X20 models.

Requirement: 1. Replace all Hartzell hub spiders having S/Nos. as follows: 1 to 4303, 4307 to 4316, 4318, 4319, 4321, 4323, 4324, 4325, 4328, 4329, 4332 to 4336 and 4341.

2\. Hub spiders with S/nos. other than those listed above may be used as replacements; or

FAA AD 59-26-1; or ANO 107.1.11.0.1.7.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft.

# AD/PL 12/17 - Pilot Compartment/Restraint - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PL 12/17 Pilot Compartment/Restraint - Modification 9/78

Applicability: All PL-12 Series.

Requirement: Modify fuel pump switch and hopper door level installations to improve in-flight accessibility according to a scheme approved by the Secretary, or modify the pilot's safety harness by fitment of an inertia reel satisfying the requirements of ANO 108.42.

Compliance: Before 1 October 1979.

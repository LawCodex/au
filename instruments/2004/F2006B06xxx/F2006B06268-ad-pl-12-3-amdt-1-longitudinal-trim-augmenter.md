# AD/PL 12/3 Amdt 1 - Longitudinal Trim Augmenter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PL 12/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Longitudinal Trim Augmenter 2/89**

Applicability: PL 12, PL 12U, PL 12/T320, PL 12/T300. Requirement: Action in accordance with Transavia SB No. 16. Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Background: This Amendment deletes “All models” from the “Applicability” to reflect the design and manufacturing improvements in the later model aircraft.

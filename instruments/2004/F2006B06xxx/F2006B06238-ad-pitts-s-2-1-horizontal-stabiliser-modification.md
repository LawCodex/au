# AD/PITTS S-2/1 - Horizontal Stabiliser - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pitts S-1 and S-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PITTS S-2/1 Horizontal Stabiliser - Modification 6/73

Applicability: All model S-2A.

Requirement: Modify horizontal stabiliser structure in accordance with Pitts SB No. 7 dated 12 March 1973.

Compliance: Before issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

# AD/SD3-30/25 - Airworthiness Limitations

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/25 Airworthiness Limitations 8/89

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: The approved system of maintenance of all SD3-30 aeroplanes shall include all relevant inspections and life limitations in the Shorts SD3-30 Maintenance Programme, Revision 17 dated 23 November 1987.

Compliance: Forthwith.

Background: This Directive has been raised to formally promulgate the Airworthiness limitations inspections and life limitations, which had been previously overlooked. The Maintenance Programme is a separate document but which forms Chapter 5 of the Maintenance Manual.

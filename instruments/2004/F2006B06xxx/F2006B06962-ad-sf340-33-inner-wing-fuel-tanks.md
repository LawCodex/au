# AD/SF340/33 - Inner Wing Fuel Tanks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/33 Inner Wing Fuel Tanks 5/92

Applicability: Model 340B S/N 160 through 226 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin 340-28-013. *Note 1: Swedish Airworthiness Directive (SAD) No 1-046 refers. Note 2: FAA AD 92-03-10 Amdt 3908165 refers.*

Compliance: Within 250 hours time in service from 20 April 1992 or by 31 July 1992, whichever occurs first.

Background: During pressure refuelling, a piece of foam blocked the vent line between the inner and outer fuel cell. This blockage caused a pressure build up which deformed the ribs and wing skin.

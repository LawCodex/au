# AD/SC7/18 - Ground/Air Lever Spring Heel Block - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

AD/SC7/18 Ground/Air Lever Spring Heel Block - Modification 2/81

Applicability: All models SC7, Series 3, 3A and 3M. Document: Shorts SB No. 76-58, Revision No. 1.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 31 March 1981.

Background: An improved method of security the retaining pin in the ground/air lever spring heel block has now been developed by the manufacturer. The bulletin has been classified as mandatory by the UK CAA.

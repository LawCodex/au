# AD/ROBIN/16 - Ball and Socket Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/16 Ball and Socket Fitting 7/92

Applicability: ATL, all types, and R3000, all types.

Requirement: For ATL: Action in accordance with ROBIN SA SB No.8 Rev.1.

For R3000: Action in accordance with AVIONS PIERRE ROBIN SB No.121 Rev.1.

*Note: DGAC AD 91-154(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; Prior to further flight after 30 June 1992.

Background: The DGAC requires an inspection of the ball and socket fittings. This action is required in order to prevent jamming of certain ball and socket fittings, due to a surface treatment problem during manufacture.

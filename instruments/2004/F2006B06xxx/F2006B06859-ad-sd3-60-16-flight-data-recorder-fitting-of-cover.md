# AD/SD3-60/16 - Flight Data Recorder - Fitting of Cover

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/16 Flight Data Recorder - Fitting of Cover 6/86

Applicability: All model SD3-60 with S/No's SH3601 to SH3677. Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts. S.B. SD360-31-04. Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1986.

Background: Corrosion of the circuit cards by moisture in the electronic section of the Flight Data Recorder causes a loss of data.

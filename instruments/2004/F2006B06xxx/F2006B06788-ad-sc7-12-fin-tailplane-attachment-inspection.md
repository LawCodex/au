# AD/SC7/12 - Fin/Tailplane Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

AD/SC7/12 Fin/Tailplane Attachment - Inspection 8/79

Applicability: All Skyvan series 3, 3A and 3M. Document: Short SB No. 55-53.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1979.

Background: It has been reported to the manufacturer that bolts connecting the fin to tailplane have been found not fully tightened.

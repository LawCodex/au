# AD/PMC/28 - Propeller Hub - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - McCauley

AD/PMC/28 Propeller Hub - Replacement 10/75

Applicability: Models D2AF34C52/80GF-0 and D2AF34C52A/ 80GF-0 with S/Nos. 63000 to 659999 which have not been modified in accordance with AD/PMC/20.

Document: McCauley SB No. 111.

Compliance: For propellers which have completed 1500 hours total time in service as at 28 November 1975 - within 100 hours time in service after 28 November 1975. For propellers with less than 1500 hours total time in service -before achieving 1600 hours total time in service.

# AD/PB/2 - Beech Model 215, 272 and 278 Series - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Beechcraft

AD/PB/2 Beech Model 215, 272 and 278 Series - Inspection 10/69

Applicability: All model 215, 272 and 278 propellers fitted to Beechcraft Bonanza and Twin Bonanza aircraft.

Document: Beech Propeller SB No. 5.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

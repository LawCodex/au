# AD/R22/1 Amdt 1 - Main Rotor Blade

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robinson R22 Series Helicopters

## AD/R22/1

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: Model R22 Series.

## Main Rotor Blade 7/92

Requirement: Remove from service all main rotor blades with Serial Numbers 1 through 91.

*Note: FAA AD 80-11-07 Amdt 39-3834 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; prior to 30 June 1992.

Background: Delamination of the trailing edges and root end doublers of the main rotor blades may occur in normal service.

Amendment 1 is raised to reflect the FAA requirement that the above affected main rotor blades be removed from service in order to prevent possible blade structural failure.

# AD/PITTS S-2/5 - Induction Air Box Door - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pitts S-1 and S-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PITTS S-**

**2/5**

**Induction Air Box Door - Inspection and Modification**

**9/73**

Applicability: Model S-2A with S/Nos. 2001 to 2059. Requirement: 1. Inspection:

Visually inspect the induction air box flapper door assembly, Pitts P/N 2-7112-7, for cracks in the welded areas.

*Note: This inspection may be performed by the pilot.*

2\. Modification:

Install stiffener, Pitts P/N 2-7112-16, to the induction air box flapper door assembly in accordance with Pitts SL No. 2; or

Pitts SL No. 2 - For Aircraft with more than 50 hours time in service from new.

Compliance: 1. Inspection: Prior to first flight of each day until incorporation of the modification.

2\. Modification: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 September 1973.

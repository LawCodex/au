# AD/SD3-60/43 - Flap Hydraulic Unit Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/43 Flap Hydraulic Unit Brackets 4/96

Applicability: All SD3-60 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Service Bulletin SD360-27-24.

*Note: UK CAA AD 007-09-95 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously carried out, in accordance with the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 28 March 1996.

Background: The manufacturer has reported exfoliation corrosion on the flap hydraulic support brackets.

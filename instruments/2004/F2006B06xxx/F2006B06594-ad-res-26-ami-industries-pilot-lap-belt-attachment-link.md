# AD/RES/26 - AMI Industries Pilot Lap Belt Attachment Link

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Restraint Equipment

AD/RES/26 AMI Industries Pilot Lap Belt Attachment Link 7/93

Applicability: Pilot and Co-Pilot Seats, Model 1108, serial numbers 004 through 189; as installed in, but not limited to, de Havilland Model DHC-8 series aircraft.

Requirement: Remove the lap belt attachment links and install the improved lap belt attachment link kit, P/N 1B9014-3R, in accordance with AMI Industries, Inc., Service Bulletin 25- 1108-03.

*Note: FAA AD 93-07-05 Amdt 39-8537 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1993.

Background: The FAA received a report of a lap belt link fitting failure due to fatigue.

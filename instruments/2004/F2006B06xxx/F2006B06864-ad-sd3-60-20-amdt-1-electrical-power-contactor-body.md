# AD/SD3-60/20 Amdt 1 - Electrical Power - Contactor Body

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/20**

**Amdt 1**

**Electrical Power - Contactor Body 2/87**

Applicability: All model SD3-60 Aircraft with S/Nos SH 3601 through SH 3695.

Requirement: Modify the electrical power contactors according to the requirements of Shorts S.B. SD360-24-06 Revision 1.

*Note: The United Kingdom CAA has classified this service bulletin as mandatory.*

Compliance: Before 30 April 1987.

Background: 1. In service experience has shown that the bodies of the contactors require to be insulated.

2\. This Directive has been amended to reflect the manufacturers revised torque procedure reference.

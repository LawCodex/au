# AD/PHS/2 Amdt 1 - Model 23260, 43E-60 and 43D-50 Reverse Solenoid Valve - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Hamilton Standard

**AD/PHS/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Model 23260, 43E-60 and 43D-50 Reverse Solenoid Valve - Modification**

**6/66**

Applicability: All model 23260, 43E60 and 43D50 propellers.

Requirement: Remove Hamilton Standard Model 67000 reverse solenoid valves and replace with approved, redesigned valves.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft after 28 July 1968.

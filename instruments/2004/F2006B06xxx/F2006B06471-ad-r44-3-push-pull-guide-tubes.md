# AD/R44/3 - Push-Pull Guide Tubes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robinson R44 Series Helicopters

AD/R44/3 Push-Pull Guide Tubes 8/95 DM

Applicability: All R44 helicopters S/N 0001 through 0150 inclusive.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Robinson R44 Service Bulletin No 4.

*Note: FAA AD 95-09-07 Amdt 39-9210 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 26 May 1995 or before 30 June 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of cyclic control binding.

The binding is caused by excessive guide wear in which the P/N C121-7 push-pull tubes slide.

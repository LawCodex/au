# AD/RES/13 Amdt 1 - Teleflex Morse Inertia Reels - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Restraint Equipment

**AD/RES/13**

**Amdt 1**

**Teleflex Morse Inertia Reels - Modification 1/84**

Applicability: All Teleflex morse inertia reel assemblies P/N. D73766. and D73766/001. Document: Teleflex Morse S.B. 25-00-185429 Issue 2.

*Note: CAA AD No. 011-10-82 refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 September 1984.

Background: Cases have occurred of spring failure, leading to failure of the rewind function.

This Directive is raised in issue to incorporate the latest issue the requirement document which extends effectivity and amends accomplishment instructions.

# AD/SD3-60/9 Amdt 1 - Nose Undercarriage Main Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/9**

**Amdt 1**

**Nose Undercarriage Main Fitting 13/87**

Applicability: All model SD3-60 aircraft with nose undercarriage units listed in Dowty Rotol Service Bulletin No 32-26 SD.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dowty Rotol Service Bulletin No 32-26 SD. Compliance: Prior to the issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: The manufacturer has reported an instance of cracking on the cylindrical wall of the main fitting. This amendment makes typographical changes only.

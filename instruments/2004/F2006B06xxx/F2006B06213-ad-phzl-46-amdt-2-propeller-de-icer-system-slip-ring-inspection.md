# AD/PHZL/46 Amdt 2 - Propeller De-Icer System Slip Ring - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Hartzell

**AD/PHZL/46**

**Amdt 2**

**Propeller De-Icer System Slip Ring - Inspection 4/90**

Applicability: All propeller models HC-B3TN-3C or -3D equipped with propeller de-icer system using spinner bulkhead P/N D-3472 or D-3474, and fitted to Embraer EMB-110 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer Service Bulletin No 110-030-0006 Revision 2.

*Note: Brazilian AD 79-12-02 refers to SB 110-030-0006 initial issue.*

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 50 hours time in service after 28 November 1979.

2\. Repeat inspection at periods not exceeding 50 hours time in service, for an additional 5 inspections.

3\. Thereafter, inspect at periods not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

4\. Inspection cycle to be repeated for replacement propellers.

Background: The aircraft manufacturer advises of the need to inspect for cracking of the resin that attaches the de-icer slip ring. The manufacturer has further revised his Service Bulletin which increased the 200 hour inspection to 300 hours.

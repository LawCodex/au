# AD/ROBIN/22 - Carburettor Spacer

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/22 Carburettor Spacer 7/95

Applicability: Robin DR 400 and R 2160 aircraft as detailed by serial number in Pierre Robin Airplanes Service Bulletin No. 139.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Pierre Robin Airplanes Service Bulletin No. 139.

*Note: DGAC Airworthiness Directive 94-275(A) refers.*

Compliance: Effective 22 June 1995; within 10 hours time in service.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require an inspection of the carburettor to air heater box spacer for evidence of adhesive tape. The requirement follows a report of pieces of adhesive tape being found in the bore of a carburettor spacer. Should adhesive tape in the carburettor spacer bore become dislodged in flight, the tape may interfere with the carburettor operation with a subsequent loss of engine power.

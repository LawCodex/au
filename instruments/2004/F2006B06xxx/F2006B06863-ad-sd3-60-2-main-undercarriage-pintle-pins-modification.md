# AD/SD3-60/2 - Main Undercarriage Pintle Pins - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/2 Main Undercarriage Pintle Pins - Modification 11/83

Applicability: All model SD-360 aircraft with main undercarriage units listed in Dowty Service Bulletin No. 32-11SD.

Document: Dowty Service Bulletin No. 32-11SD.

*Note: Shorts Service Bulletin SD 360-32-10 refers.*

Compliance: Before 31st October 1983.

Background: Pintle nut P/No. 200952602 is below the specified heat treatment standard.

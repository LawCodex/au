# AD/SC7/11 - Air/Ground Lever Spring Heel Block - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

AD/SC7/11 Air/Ground Lever Spring Heel Block - Inspection 8/79

Applicability: All models SC7 Series 3, 3A and 3M. Document: Shorts Alert SB No. 76-A56.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 27 August 1979.

Background: The air/ground lever spring heel block is to be checked for security. Loss of this block will permit inadvertent selection of the power lever into ground range.

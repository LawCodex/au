# AD/PMC/44 - Oil Filled Hub

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - McCauley

AD/PMC/44 Oil Filled Hub 9/95

Applicability: All McCauley 2A37C223-[X]B/90RB-[X] propellers installed on, but not limited to, Beech Debonair and Beech Bonanza 35 and 36 series aircraft.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with McCauley Service Bulletin 208A.

*Note: FAA AD 95-09-08 amdt 39-9211 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 17 August 1995 or by 31 December 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: This directive is being raised after receipt of Country of Origin AD.

# AD/PT6A/18 - No. 2 Bearing Oil Pressure Tube

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Canada Turbine Engines - PT6A Series

AD/PT6A/18 No. 2 Bearing Oil Pressure Tube 10/85

Applicability: PT6A-38, PT6A-41, PT6A-45A engines with S/Nos. as specified in the requirement document.

Document: 1. Pratt & Whitney of Canada SB No. 3099, Rev. 2; or

2\. AD/UAC-T/28A.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February, 1978.

Background: This Directive is reissued in order to reflect the manufacturers latest reference document which completes the listing of engine serial numbers affected and clarifies new part assembly instructions.

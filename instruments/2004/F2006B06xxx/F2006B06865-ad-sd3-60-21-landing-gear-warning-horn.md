# AD/SD3-60/21 - Landing Gear Warning Horn

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/21 Landing Gear Warning Horn 12/86

Applicability: All models SD3-60 aeroplanes with S/Nos. SH3601 through SH3689. Requirement: 1. Upon replacement of Lucas horn P/No V.0603L with Keystone horn P/No.

AC155F, relocate mounting of horn according to the requirements of Shorts SD3-

60 Service Bulletin SD360-32-23.

2\. For aeroplanes previously fitted with Keystone horn P/No. AC155F, relocate mounting of horn according to the requirements of the document referenced in Para 1 above.

Compliance: For Para 1: Upon replacement.

For Para 2: Within 30 hours time in service after 31 January 1987.

Background: Reports have been received that the Keystone P/No. AC155F landing gear warning horn is inaudible when located between Station 3 and 13.45 on the aircraft top centre line.

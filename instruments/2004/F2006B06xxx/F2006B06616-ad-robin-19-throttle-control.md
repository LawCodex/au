# AD/ROBIN/19 - Throttle Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/19 Throttle Control 9/94

Applicability: Model ATL, all types except ATL “L”, with serial number range of 110 and above. Requirement: Modify the throttle lever control support in accordance with ROBIN Service Bulletin

ATL No.9 R1.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-118(A)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 18 August 1994.

Background: A report was received of a throttle cable casing stop failure at the control lever in the cabin.

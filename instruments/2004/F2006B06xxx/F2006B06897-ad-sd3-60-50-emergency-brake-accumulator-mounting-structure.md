# AD/SD3-60/50 - Emergency Brake Accumulator Mounting Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/50 Emergency Brake Accumulator Mounting Structure

1/99

Applicability: All SD360 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts Mandatory Service Bulletin SD360-29-06. Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 28 April 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 28 January 1999.

Background: The manufacturer received a report of cracking of the emergency brake accumulator mounting structure. This Directive requires an inspection to detect cracking, and, if necessary, replacement of a support bracket angle.

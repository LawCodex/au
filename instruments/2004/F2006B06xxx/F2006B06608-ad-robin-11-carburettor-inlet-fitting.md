# AD/ROBIN/11 - Carburettor Inlet Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/11 Carburettor Inlet Fitting 7/89

Applicability: All models equipped with Lycoming O-235, O-320 or O-360 series engine. Requirement: Action in accordance with Avions Pierre Robin Service Bulletin No 112.

*Note: DGAC AD 89-052(A) refers.*

Compliance: By 26 May 1989.

Background: The manufacturer requires inspection and immediate replacement of the inlet fitting if leaks are detected. If no leaks are evident fitting must be replaced at next 50 hourly inspection.

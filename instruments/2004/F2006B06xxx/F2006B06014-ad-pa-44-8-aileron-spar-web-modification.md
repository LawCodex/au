# AD/PA-44/8 - Aileron Spar Web - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-44 (Seminole) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-44/8 Aileron Spar Web - Modification 3/81

Applicability: All model PA-44-180 with S/Nos. 44-7995001 to 44-8095006. Document: Piper SB No. 702.

Compliance: Before 1 July 1981.

Background: Reports have been received by the manufacturer of an abnormal flexing in the aileron spar web at the push-pull rod attachment.

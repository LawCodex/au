# AD/ROBIN/23 - Anti-Tab Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robin Aviation Series Aeroplanes

AD/ROBIN/23 Anti-Tab Control 7/95

Applicability: All Model R 2000 and HR 200 aircraft, serial numbers 250 through 271. Requirement: Action in accordance with Avions Pierre Robin Mandatory Service Bulletin 137.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-183(A)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 22 June 1995.

Background: The DGAC requires installation of a friction lock device on the sliding stop tube in order to improve the operating condition of the anti-tab control.

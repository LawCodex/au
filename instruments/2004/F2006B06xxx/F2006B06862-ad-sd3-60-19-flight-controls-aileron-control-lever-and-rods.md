# AD/SD3-60/19 - Flight Controls - Aileron Control Lever and Rods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/19 Flight Controls - Aileron Control Lever and Rods 10/86

Applicability: All model S/NO SH3601 to SH3676.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts. S.B. SD360-27-09 Revision 1. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: This modification prevents incorrect installation of the aileron control lever and rods.

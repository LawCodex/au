# AD/PITTS S-2/10 - Rudder Control Cable Sleeves - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pitts S-1 and S-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PITTS S-2/10 Rudder Control Cable Sleeves - Inspection 7/76

Applicability: All models with rudder cables of Pitts manufacture.

Requirement: 1. Inspect all “NICROPRESS” sleeves on rudder cable terminations for correct pressing; or

2\. Replace rudder cables with cables of local manufacture.

*Note: FAA AD 43-13-1, Chapter 4, contains information for checking NICOPRESS cable fittings.*

Compliance: Before further flight after 11 June 1976.

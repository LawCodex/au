# AD/S-76/21 Amdt 1 - Main Gear Box Assembly - Shaft and Spur Pinion Inspection and Retirement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/21**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Gear Box Assembly - Shaft and Spur Pinion Inspection and Retirement**

**3/83**

Applicability: All model S-76 using 76351-09000 main gear box assemblies, prior to and including - 062.

Document: Sikorsky Alert SB 76-66-16B.

*Note: FAA AD 82-06-51 Amendment 39-43-87 dated 5th March 1982 refers in part.*

Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: Manufacturer’s Amendment “B” includes the -062 main gear box assembly incorporating the -104 shaft and spur pinions plus an additional inspection requirement.

# AD/SD3-60/1 Amdt 1 - Engine Flexible Mounts - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

**Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes**

**AD/SD3-60/1**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Flexible Mounts - Inspection and Modification**

**12/84**

Applicability: All models SD-360 aircraft fitted with Barry engine mounts P/No. 93880-12, which have not been modified in accordance with Shorts Service Bulletin SD 360-71-08.

Document: Shorts Service Bulletin SD 360-71-A02, Rev 1.

Compliance: Inspect - prior to issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 December 1984, and thereafter at each issue of a new Maintenance Release.

Background: The manufacturer has reported instances of metal to rubber bond separation of moulded assembly.

# AD/SD3-60/18 - Take-Off Trim Range

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/18 Take-Off Trim Range 12/86 DM

Applicability: All Shorts SD3-60, fitted with Pratt and Whitney PT6A-65R engines, and Shorts

SD3-60 with serial numbers SH3601 to SH3685, fitted with Pratt and Whitney PT6A- 65AR engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with either Shorts Service Bulletin:

SD360-27-10 Revision No. 1 for PT6A-65R engines; or

2\. SD360-27-11 Revision No. 2 for PT6A-65AR Engines. Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1986.

Background: Operational experience has found that excessive control column force is required during take-off under certain conditions.

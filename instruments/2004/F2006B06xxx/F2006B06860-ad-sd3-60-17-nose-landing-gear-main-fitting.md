# AD/SD3-60/17 - Nose Landing Gear Main Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SD3-60/17 Nose Landing Gear Main Fitting 8/86** Applicability: All model SD3-60 with units referenced in Dowty Service Bulletin No. 32-45SD. Requirement: Action in accordance with Dowty S.B. No. 32-45SD.

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer reports premature wear of the ferobestos bearing has caused wear in the bore of the nose undercarriage main fitting.

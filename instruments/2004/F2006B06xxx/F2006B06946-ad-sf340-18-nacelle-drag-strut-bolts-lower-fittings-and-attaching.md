# AD/SF340/18 - Nacelle Drag Strut Bolts, Lower Fittings and Attaching Fasteners

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SF340/18 Nacelle Drag Strut Bolts, Lower Fittings**

**and Attaching Fasteners**

Applicability: SF340A S/No -004 to -159 SF340B S/No -160 to 166. Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Service Bulletin 340-54-026.

*Note: Swedish AD 1-034 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Para 2B (1) to (4); within 200 hours time in service after 15 January 1990.

**3/90**

**DM**

2\. Para 2B (5); at the next engine removal or within 1500 hours time in service after 15 January 1990.

Background: There have been reports of loose nuts at the lower drag strut support fitting. If not corrected could, in the case of a double failure, result in the loss of structural integrity of the power plant installation.

# AD/PW4000/1 - Thrust Reverser Aft Cascade Support

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - PW4000 Series

AD/PW4000/1 Thrust Reverser Aft Cascade Support 2/97

Applicability: PW4000 series engines installed on Airbus A300-600 and A310-300 series aircraft incorporating a thrust reverser per Supplemental Type Certificate No. SJ514NE.

Requirement: Inspect the thrust reverser aft cascade support frame in accordance the technical requirements of Pratt and Whitney Service Bulletin No. PW4NAC 78-78, Revision 6 dated 6 March 1996, Part 1 - Interim inspection.

Incorporation of aft cascade support frames, Part Nos. 221-0516-505 (LH) and 221- 0516-506 (RH), constitutes terminating action for this Directive. Refer to Pratt and Whitney Service Bulletin No. PW4NAC 78-78, Revision 6, dated 6 March 1996, Part 2 - Permanent Modification.

*Note 1: FAA AD 96 -13-08 also refers.*

*Note 2: Inspection and modification to later issues of the referenced service bulletin satisfies the intent of this Directive.*

Compliance: Effective 30 January 1997; as detailed in the requirement document.

Background: There have been reports of thrust reverser aft cascade support failures which have resulted in liberation of thrust reverser hardware during reverse thrust operation.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PC-6/37 Flat End Rib - Inspection 6/76

Applicability: All models prior to S/No. 744. Document: Pilatus SB No. 124.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 19 May 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified.

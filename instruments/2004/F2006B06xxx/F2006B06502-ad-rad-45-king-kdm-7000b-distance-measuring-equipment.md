# AD/RAD/45 - King KDM 7000B Distance Measuring Equipment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Radio Communication and Navigation Equipment

AD/RAD/45 King KDM 7000B Distance Measuring Equipment 2/89

Applicability: King model KDM 7000B distance measuring equipment, Part Number 066-1019-21/- 23/-26/-33/-43/-57 with Serial Numbers 21855 to 22526 inclusive.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with Bendix/King Alert Service Bulletin KDM 7000B-34-14 dated 4 November 1988.

*Note: FAA AD 88-25-01 refers.*

Compliance: If fitted to an aircraft - within 150 hours time in service after 23 February 1989 or before 23 May 1989 whichever is the sooner.

Otherwise - before fitment to an aircraft.

Background: Failure of the DME or its power supply can cause erroneous channel selection on an associated NAV receiver when the DME and NAV channelling lines are paralleled.

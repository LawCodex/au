# AD/S-76/16 Amdt 2 - Main Rotor Blade Root End - Inspection for Fatigue Cracking

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/16**

**Amdt 2**

**Main Rotor Blade Root End - Inspection for Fatigue Cracking**

**4/85**

Applicability: All models fitted with main rotor blades P/N 76150-09000 or 76150-09100. Requirement: Sikorsky S76 Alert Customer Service Bulletin no. 76-65-18C.

Compliance: As detailed in requirement document. Inspections carried out in accordance with the previous issue of this directive, meet the requirements of this issue.

Background: The directive has been amended to nominate ACSB 76-65-18C as a more appropriate requirement document than the Composite Materials Manual. FAA AD 84-10-02 also refers to this subject.

# AD/SC7/14 - Flap Mechanism Support Structure - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SC7 (Skyvan) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SC7/14 Flap Mechanism Support Structure - Inspection

**and Modification** Applicability: All models as referenced in Alert SB No. 27-A64, Rev. 4. Document: Short Alert SB No. 27-A64, Rev. 4.

## 9/79

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 50 hours time in service after 2 October 1979.

Background: In service cracking has developed in some overseas operated Short SC7s in the support structure for the flap mechanism located in the centre section.

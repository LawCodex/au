# AD/RAD/68 Amdt 2 - Mode C Transponder Altitude Checks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
[image]\
Radio Communication and Navigation Equipment

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

[image]

For the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive AD/RAD/68 Amdt 2.

[image]

**AD/RAD/68**

**Amdt 2**

**Mode C Transponder Altitude Checks 6/2000**

Requirement: CANCELLED.

Background: This Directive was based on United States Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) AD 99-23-22R1. The FAA AD was rescinded with effect 20 April 2000 as the FAA has determined that repetitive tests are unnecessary and they increase component wear.

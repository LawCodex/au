# AD/RRT-S/4 Amdt 1 - H.P. Fuel Shut Off Valve Primary Strainer - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Spey Series

**AD/RRT-S/4**

**Amdt 1**

**H.P. Fuel Shut Off Valve Primary Strainer - Inspection**

**5/78**

Applicability: Spey 511-8 engines fitted with HP shut-off valves featuring Lucas SB LSB Sp 73- 158 but not fitted with H.P. fuel pumps featuring either LSB Sp 73-127 or LSB Sp 73-212.

Requirement: Rolls Royce Spey SB No. Sp 73-182. Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

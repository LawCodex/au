# AD/SD3-30/18 - Fuselage Sealing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-30 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-30/18 Fuselage Sealing 4/86

Applicability: All models with S/Nos SH3002 to SH3116. Requirement: Short SB SD330-53-58.

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1986.

Background: Following an incident of a fuel purge valve failure, investigation indicated that, to safeguard against the possibility of fuel ingress into the main cabin, an improvement in fuselage top skin sealing is desirable.

# AD/PL 12/11 Amdt 1 - Nose Wheel Pivot Pin - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Transavia PL 12 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PL 12/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Nose Wheel Pivot Pin - Inspection and Replacement**

**5/75**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. prior to G576 not incorporating SB No. 27.

Requirement: 1. Check nosewheel pivot pin P/N 6-24 for correct torque in accordance with Transavia PL-12 Maintenance Manual.

2\. Replace nose wheel pivot pin if torque is found incorrect.

3\. Remove pivot pin and inspect for wear, cracks and signs of abnormal loading. Compliance: For Para. 1 - Within 10 hours time in service after 10 September 1973.

For Para. 2 - Before further flight.

For Para. 3 - At next inspection for issue of maintenance release completed after 10 September 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/RES/19 - Am-Safe Harness Buckle Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Restraint Equipment

AD/RES/19 Am-Safe Harness Buckle Assemblies 4/87

Applicability: Am-Safe restraint system assemblies P/Nos 501825-403, 501907-401, 501907-403,

501907-405 502061-401, 502147-401, 502147-403;

Requirement: Inspect the male portion/s of the buckle assembly. Remove from service any connector identified as Lot 04. Am-Safe Mandatory SB AS 001 refers.

[image]

Compliance: Prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 9 April 1987 or within 100 hours time in service after 9 April 1987 whichever is later.

Background: There is a possibility that items from the subject batch may dislodge from the buckle if a side load is applied to the assembly.

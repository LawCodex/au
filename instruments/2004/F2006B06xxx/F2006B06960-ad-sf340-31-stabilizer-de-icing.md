# AD/SF340/31 - Stabilizer De-Icing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/31 Stabilizer De-Icing 3/92

Applicability: Model 340B Serial Number 240 through 299 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of LFV (Sweden) AD No 1- 051.

*Note: SAAB SB SAAB 340-30-039 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: Freezing of the control valve and the pressure switch located in the dorsal fin may result in continuously inflated or deflated de-ice boots.

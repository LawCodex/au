# AD/PC-6/25 Amdt 1 - Aileron Balance Weight Arms - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PC-6/25**

**Amdt 1**

**Aileron Balance Weight Arms - Inspection 11/72**

Applicability: All models fitted with aileron mass balance weight assembly P/No. 6106.11. Document: Pilatus SB No. 89A.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1972 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until replaced by aileron balance weight assembly P/No. 111.45.08.001.

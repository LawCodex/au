# AD/SD3-60/28 - Horizontal Stabilizer - Rear Spar Web/Boom

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Short SD3-60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SD3-60/28 Horizontal Stabilizer - Rear Spar Web/Boom 7/88

DM

Applicability: All models with S/Nos SH3601 to SH3691 and SH3694. Requirement: Action in accordance with Shorts SB SD360-55-16.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to 31 July 1988 and thereafter as per Requirement Document until modified in accordance with Shorts SB SD360-55-12.

Background: Testing on a horizontal stabilizer with service usage has shown failure of some rivets connecting the rear spar web to the booms.

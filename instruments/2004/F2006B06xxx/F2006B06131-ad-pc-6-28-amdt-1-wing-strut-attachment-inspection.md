# AD/PC-6/28 Amdt 1 - Wing Strut Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pilatus Porter PC-6 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PC-6/28**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Strut Attachment - Inspection 12/80**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 338 to 701. Document: Pilatus SB No. 93.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Background: Service experience indicates that the inspection for corrosion in the wing strut attachment can be extended to 100 hourly intervals.

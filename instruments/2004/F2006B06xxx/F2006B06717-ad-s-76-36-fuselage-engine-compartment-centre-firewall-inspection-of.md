# AD/S-76/36 - Fuselage - Engine Compartment Centre Firewall - Inspection of No. 1 Tail Drive Shaft for Adequate Clearance

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

**AD/S-76/36 Fuselage - Engine Compartment Centre**

**Firewall - Inspection of No. 1 Tail Drive Shaft for Adequate Clearance**

Applicability: All model S-76A Helicopters.

Requirement: Sikorsky Alert S.B. 76-53-24. Sikorsky CBT-TP-84-098.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before further flight after 12 February 1985.

Background: A report indicates No. 1 section tail rotor drive shaft became severed due to interference with doubler P/N 76070-20057-101.

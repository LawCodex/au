# AD/S-76/9 - Cyclic Torque Tube - Inspection of Centre Support Bearing Housing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Sikorsky S-76 Series Helicopters

AD/S-76/9 Cyclic Torque Tube - Inspection of Centre Support Bearing Housing

3/80

Applicability: Model S-76. Document: Sikorsky CSB 76-67-7.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 14 February 1980.

Background: Some bearing support housings have been incorrectly manufactured, with the bearing retaining shoulder on the wrong side. This would permit axial displacement of the bearing along the torque tube.

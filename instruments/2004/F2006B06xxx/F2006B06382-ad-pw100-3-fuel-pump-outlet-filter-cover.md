# AD/PW100/3 - Fuel Pump Outlet Filter Cover

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Canada Turbine Engines - PW100 Series

AD/PW100/3 Fuel Pump Outlet Filter Cover 5/91

DM

Applicability: All PW100 series engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Transport Canada Airworthiness Directive CF-90-21.

*Note: Netherlands Airworthiness Directive 90-130 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1991.

Background: This Directive requires modification of the engine driven fuel pump outlet filter cover assembly. This requirement reflects the requirements of Transport Canada AD CF- 90-21, and advice of a recent in-flight fuel leak event involving an Australian registered DHC-8. Investigation of this event indicated the leak source to be a cut fuel pump outlet filter cover O-ring.

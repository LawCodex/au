# AD/PA-44/5 - Engine Priming System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-44 (Seminole) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-44/5 Engine Priming System - Modification 8/79

Applicability: Model PA-44-180 with S/Nos. 44-7995001 to 44-7995190. Document: 1. Piper SB No. 634; or

2\. FAA AD 79-12-11, Amendment 39-3490.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 29 June 1979.

Background: The engine fuel primer system of this aircraft is subject to cracking of lines and breakage of fittings and poses a fire hazard.

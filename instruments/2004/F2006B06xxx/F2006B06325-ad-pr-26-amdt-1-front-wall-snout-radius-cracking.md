# AD/PR/26 Amdt 1 - Front Wall/Snout Radius Cracking

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propellers - Variable Pitch - Dowty Rotol

**AD/PR/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Front Wall/Snout Radius Cracking 5/90**

Applicability: All R212/4-30-4/22, R212/4-30-4/22/1 and R251/4-30-4/49 propellers.

Requirement: Inspect the hub front wall/snout radius area for evidence of cracking in accordance with Dowty Rotol SB 61-1053 Revision 2.

*Note: Hubs previously exempt or certified as having complied with AD/PR/20 must be reinspected in accordance with this Directive.*

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: Cracks have been found in the front wall/snout radius area in a limited number of hubs modified to VP 2388 standard. The manufacturer now requires all hubs, modified or unmodified, to be inspected and if a hub is found cracked during overhaul a fleet wide recurring inspection is required.

This Amendment incorporates Revision 2 of the Requirement document.

# AD/R22/32 - Carburettor Air Box Latches

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Robinson R22 Series Helicopters

AD/R22/32 Carburettor Air Box Latches 8/90

Applicability: All R22 series helicopters.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out, action in accordance with Robinson SB No 61. Compliance: At next inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release after 31 August 1990 or by 31

December 1990, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has received several reports of the air box cover opening in flight.

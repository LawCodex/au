# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Adoption of State Plans as Recovery Plans (19/02/2004)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

**Adoption of State Plans as Recovery Plans**

I, DAVID ALISTAIR KEMP, Minister for the Environment and Heritage, under section 269A of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as recovery plans for the listed threatened species specified below, the respective plans made by New South Wales specified opposite those listed threatened species:

**New South Wales Recovery Plans (10)**

| **Listed Threatened Species** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| *Litoria castanea*<br>(Yellow-spotted Bell Frog) and *Litoria piperata*<br>(Peppered Tree Frog) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2001). Yellow-spotted Bell Frog (*Litoria castanea*) and Peppered Tree Frog (*Litoria piperata*) Recovery Plan. NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Thersites mitchellae*<br>(Mitchell’s Rainforest Snail) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2001). Mitchell’s<br>Rainforest Snail *Thersites mitchellae* Recovery Plan. NSW PWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Emydura macquari*<br>(Bellinger River Emydura) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2001). Bellinger River Emydura *Emydura macquarii* (Bellinger River) Recovery Plan. NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Boronia granitica*<br>(Granite Boronia) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2002). Recovery Plan for *Boronia granitica* (Granite Boronia). NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Pterostylis gibbosa* (R.Br.) (Illawarra Greenhood Orchid) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2002). *Pterostylis gibbosa* (R.Br.) Illawarra Greenhood Orchid Recovery Plan. NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Grevillea obtusiflora* subsp*. obtusiflora* and subsp*. fecunda* | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2001). *Grevillea obtusiflora* subsp*. obtusiflora* and subsp*. fecunda* Recovery Plan. NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Elaeocarpus* sp. Rocky Creek (syn E. sp. 2 'Minyon') | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2003). Recovery Plan for the *Elaeocarpus* sp. Rocky Creek (*syn E. sp. 2 'Minyon'*). NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Pseudophryne corroboree*<br>(Southern Corroboree Frog) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2001). Recovery Plan for the Southern Corroboree Frog (*Pseudophryne corroboree*). NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Genoplesium plumosum*<br>(Tallong Midge Orchid) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2002). Recovery Plan for the Tallong Midge Orchid (*Genoplesium plumosum*). NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Genoplesium vernale*<br>(East Lynne Midge Orchid) | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2002). Recovery Plan for the East Lynne Midge Orchid (*Genoplesium vernale*). NSW NPWS, Hurstville, NSW. |

The recovery plans will come into force on the date of signing of this instrument.

Dated this19th day of February 2004.

DAVID KEMP

**Minister for the Environment and Heritage**

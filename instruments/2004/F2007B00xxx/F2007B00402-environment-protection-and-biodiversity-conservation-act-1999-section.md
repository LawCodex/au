# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Adoption of State Plans as Recovery Plans (18/08/2004)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

**Adoption of State Plans as Recovery Plans**

I, IAN GORDON CAMPBELL, Minister for the Environment and Heritage, under section 269A of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as recovery plans for the listed threatened species specified below, the respective plans made by New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland and Western Australia (or agencies of that State/Territory) specified opposite those listed threatened species:

**New South Wales Recovery Plans (2)**

| **Listed Threatened Species** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| *Zieria Formosa, Zieria buxijugum* and *Zieria parrisiae* | NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service (2002). Recovery Plan for *Zieria formosa, Zieria buxijugum and Zieria parrisiae*. NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, Hurstville, NSW. |
| *Zieria lasiocaulis* | NSW National Parks & Wildlife Service (2002). Recovery Plan for *Zieria lasiocaulis*, NSW National Parks & Wildlife Service, Hurstville, NSW. |

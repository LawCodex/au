# Cheques Act 1986 - Determination No. 1 of 2004

| [image] | DETERMINATION under the CHEQUES ACT 1986 |
|---|---|

**Determination No 1 of 2004**

The RESERVE BANK OF AUSTRALIA makes the following determination under section 70A(4) of the *Cheques Act 1986*:

The Australian Paper Clearing System (APCS) is hereby determined to be a recognised settlement system in terms of the *Cheques Act*.

This approval is a disallowable instrument under section 46A of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* and, accordingly, is being tabled before each House of the Commonwealth Parliament.

Subject to disallowance, this determination will take effect from the date of notification in the *Gazette*.

This determination may be reviewed from time to time.

Signed

IJ Macfarlane\
Governor\
Reserve Bank of Australia\

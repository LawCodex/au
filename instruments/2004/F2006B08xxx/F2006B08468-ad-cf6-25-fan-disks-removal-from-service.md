# AD/CF6/25 - Fan Disks - Removal From Service

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

AD/CF6/25 Fan Disks - Removal From Service 10/92

Applicability: CF6-50 and 80A series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-23-13. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 17 September

1992\.

Background: This AD requires removal of certain Stage 1 Fan Disks from service. It results from the possibility of a metallurgical defect which could adversely affect the service life of the disc.

# AD/CF700/6 - Fuel Nozzle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF700 Series

AD/CF700/6 Fuel Nozzle 9/85

Applicability: All CF700 engines with fuel nozzles P/N 37D401657P104, P107 and P109. Document: General Electric SB (CF700) 72-27 and SB (CF700) 73-28; or

AD/GE-T/18.

Compliance: 1. Inspect in accordance with SB (CF700) 73-27 during next hot section inspection after 30 September 1971.

2\. Modify in accordance with SB (CF700) 73-28 before installation in an aircraft following next reconditioning completed after 31 December 1971.

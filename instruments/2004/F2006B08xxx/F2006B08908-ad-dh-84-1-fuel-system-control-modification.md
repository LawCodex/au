# AD/DH 84/1 - Fuel System Control - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 84 (Dragon) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH 84/1 Fuel System Control - Modification NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify the fuel system control as follows:

Incorporate a positive method of retaining and clamping the fuel control cable housing at both ends, and at the bend where it enters the wing.

2\. Incorporate a means of locking the pilot’s control lever in the “ON” position such that the lever can be moved rapidly to the “OFF” position in an emergency. OR Part 100, Appendix “A” Notice No. 10/2; or

ANO 105.1.14.0.1.1.

Compliance: Forthwith.

# AD/CESSNA 310/29 - Wing Leading Edge Fuel Lines and Wiring - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 310 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 310/29 Wing Leading Edge Fuel Lines and**

**Wiring - Inspection and Modification**

**6/73**

Applicability: All models 310C to 310Q with S/Nos. 35772 to 310Q0709 except those S/Nos. detailed in SL ME73-5, and all turbo 310 with S/Nos. 310P0001 to 310Q0709.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL ME73-5 and supplement 2. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 June 1973.

# AD/CF6/27 - High Pressure Turbine Thermal Shield Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

AD/CF6/27 High Pressure Turbine Thermal Shield Inspection 12/93

Applicability: CF6-45, -50, -80A and -80C2 series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 93-14-18.

*Note: General Electric Service Bulletins 72-1021,-596, and -565 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the FAA AD with effectivity commencing 11 Nov 1993.

Background: This directive is raised because of a HPT thermal shield separation. Such separations could result in uncontained failure.

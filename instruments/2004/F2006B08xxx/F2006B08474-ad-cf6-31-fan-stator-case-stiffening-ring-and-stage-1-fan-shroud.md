# AD/CF6/31 - Fan Stator Case Stiffening Ring and Stage 1 Fan Shroud

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

## AD/CF6/31 Fan Stator Case Stiffening Ring and Stage 1 Fan Shroud

Applicability: General Electric CF6-45/-50 series engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-08-05.

*Note: General Electric CF6-50 Service Bulletin 72-573 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the FAA AD with an effective date of 18 August 1994.

## 9/94

Background: The FAA AD was raised as a result of an uncontained stage one fan blade failure which resulted in an inflight engine shutdown and damage to the aircraft.

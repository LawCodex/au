# AD/DH114/34 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Keel Frame at Wing Rear Spar - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DH114/34

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Fuselage Keel Frame at Wing NK Rear Spar - Inspection

Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS Heron (114) No. F.17, Issue 1.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service or thirty days, after 1 May 1972 whichever occurs first and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service.

*Note: Inspections performed in accordance with the preceding issue of this Directive may be regarded as the initial inspection required by para. 3 of the requirement document.*

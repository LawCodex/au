# AD/CL-600/6 - Branson Fuel Tank - Electrical Harness - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CL-600/6 Branson Fuel Tank - Electrical Harness - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All CL600 aeroplanes listed in Canadair Alert SB A600-0250. Document: 1. Canadair Challenger Alert SB No. A600-0250; and

2\. Canadair Challenger Alert SB No. A600-0268. Compliance: Before issue of Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

## 3/83

Background: To ensure adequate clearance between electrical harnesses and Branson tank and install a barrier between the forward Branson tank and electrical harnesses.

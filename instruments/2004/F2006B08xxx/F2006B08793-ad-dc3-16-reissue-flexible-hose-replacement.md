# AD/DC3/16 Reissue - Flexible Hose - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DC3/16

**Reissue**

Applicability: All models.

## Flexible Hose - Replacement 1/84

Requirement: Remove propeller feathering hose assembly, P/No ASC-A14F, and replace with hose assembly approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 29 February 1984.

Background: This Directive is reissued with no changes made to the requirement.

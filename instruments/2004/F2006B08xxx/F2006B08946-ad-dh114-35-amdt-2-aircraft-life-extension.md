# AD/DH114/35 Amdt 2 - Aircraft Life Extension

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH114/35**

**Amdt 2**

**Aircraft Life Extension NK**

Applicability: All models incorporating life extension by wing modification or replacement. Document: For Series 1B aeroplanes: British Aerospace TNS Heron (114) No. M.11, Issue 2.

For Series 2 aeroplanes: British Aerospace TNS Heron (114) No. M.9, Issue 4.

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement documents.

*Note: The safe lives referred to in para. 1.1 of each requirement document are superseded by those promulgated in the current issue of AD/DH114/13. The manufacturer has advised that the aircraft life cannot be extended beyond two wing spar boom lifetimes.*

# AD/DH114/41 - Front (Pilots) Seats Restraint Installations - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/41 Front (Pilots) Seats Restraint NK

Installations - Modification

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify each front (pilot) seat restraint installation by fitting a safety harness incorporating an inertia reel in accordance with a scheme approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Before 1 March 1974.

# AD/CESSNA 210/42 - Fuel Flow Indication System - Hose Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 210/42 Fuel Flow Indication System - Hose**

**Inspection and Replacement**

**11/79**

Applicability: Models 210L and T210L with S/Nos. 21061040 and subsequent Models 210M, T210M and P210N modified to incorporate Symbolic Displays Inc. fuel flow indicating system per STC.SA3835WE.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the fuel flow transducer installation on the upper left side of the engine adjacent to the fuel distributor manifold.

2\. If an NAS 424-4 coupling is installed between transducer and distributor manifold, remove coupling and replace with either hose assembly Aeroquip P/No. AE7010001E0040 or Stratoflex P/No. 111D417-45-0040.

*Note: FAA AD 79-19-06 Amendment 39-3558 also refers to this subject.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 3 October 1979.

Background: The hose assembly connecting the fuel flow transducer with the fuel distributor manifold has proved unsuitable in service and may leak fuel over the engine.

# AD/DAUPHIN/17 - Hydraulic System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/17 Hydraulic System 1/87 DM

Applicability: All Model SA360C, SA365C, C1, C2 and C3 helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SA360 and 365 Service Bulletin No. 29-01.

*Note: French AD 86-176-27(B) refers.*

Compliance: Before further flight after 31 December 1986.

For spares: before installation in an aircraft after 31 December 1986.

Background: A spring spacer is fitted to prevent an overpressure in the hydraulic systems if the flange of the regulator valve stem should come off.

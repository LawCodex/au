**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Cessna 337 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 337/11 Aileron Control Tube - Inspection and

**Automatic Pilot Roll Servo - Removal**

Applicability: Model 337 fitted with Mitchell Automatic Pilot Models AK155, AK155E or

## 2/72

AK155E-3 not modified in accordance with Edo-Aire Mitchell Service Letter ML 32.

Document: 1. Edo-Aire Mitchell Service Bulletin MB6; or 2. FAA AD 72-2-6.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 February 1972.

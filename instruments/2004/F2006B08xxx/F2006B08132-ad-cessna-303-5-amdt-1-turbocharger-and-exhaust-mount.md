# AD/CESSNA 303/5 Amdt 1 - Turbocharger and Exhaust Mount

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna T303 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 303/5**

**Amdt 1**

**Turbocharger and Exhaust Mount 4/87**

Applicability: Cessna model T303/Crusader, 1982 through 1984 with serial numbers T30300001 through T30300315.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna Service Bulletin MEB86-19 Rev 1.

*Note: S.B. MEB86-20 has also been issued and is recommended by the manufacturer to be accomplished concurrently when complying with S.B. MEB86-19.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 May 1987.

Background: The manufacturer has recognised a problem with the turbocharger and exhaust system mounting, and is supplying a modification kit to improve the service life. This amendment is raised to incorporate Revision 1 of the Requirement Document which changes nearly all areas of the original issue and to alter the compliance time.

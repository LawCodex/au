# AD/CFM56/13 - Accessory Gear Box - Starter Gear Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
CFM International Turbine Engines - CFM56 Series

**AD/CFM56/13 Accessory Gear Box - Starter Gear Replacement**

**10/98**

**DM**

Applicability: CFM International (CFMI) CFM56-3, -3B and -3C model turbofan engines identified and listed in Table 1 of CFMI Service Bulletin 72-877 Revision 1 and installed on Boeing 737 and other aeroplanes.

Requirement: Replace the starter gear (line 5) in the accessory gearbox (AGB) of applicable engines in accordance with CFMI Service Bulletin 72-877 Rev 1.

*Note: DGAC 98-198(B)R1 dated 01 July 1998 refer.*

Compliance: 1. For aircraft with two applicable engines fitted, perform above requirement on one engine before 1 October 1998.

2\. For aircraft which have only one applicable engine, perform above action before 1 February 1999.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 29 July 1998.

Background: Two in flight shut downs were experienced due to a failure of the starter gear (line5) in the AGB.

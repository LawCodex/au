# AD/CESSNA 336/3 Amdt 1 - Plastic Control Wheel - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 336 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 336/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Plastic Control Wheel - Inspection 5/69**

Applicability: All models fitted with plastic control wheels. Document: 1. Cessna SL 62-44; and

2\. Cessna SL 64-8.

Compliance: 1. Inspect in accordance with document 1 - at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 12 May 1969.

2\. Inspect in accordance with document 2 - at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service after 12 May 1969.

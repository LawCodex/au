# AD/DAUPHIN/37 - Tripod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/37 Tripod 5/94

Applicability: Model SA 365 N helicopters equipped with tripod part number 360A23-0024-09. Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter Telex Service Bulletin 01.28b.

*Note: DGAC AD 88-188-024(B)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, as specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: A crack was found at the root of one of the three arms on the tripod ring. An investigation revealed that the crack originated at a corrosion site.

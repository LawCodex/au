# AD/DHA-3/1 - Electrical Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHA-3 (Drover) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHA-3/1 Electrical Installation - Modification 9/51

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify the electrical installation in accordance with the following:

| **Circuit** | **Remove** | **Install** | **Fuse Chart Amendment** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing Lights | 10 Amp. Type S Fuses | 15 Amp. Type S fuses in circuit and also spares | Delete “10 Amp.”<br>Insert “15 Amp.” |
| Generators | 20 Amp. Type S Fuses | Two Type 5AG fuses boxes and Two 25 Amp. Type 5AG fuses. Connect in places of original Type S fuses. Install spare fuses | Delete “Generators 20 Amp.” From original Fuse Box. Identify new fuse box “Generator 25 Amp.” |

or ANO 105.1.14.14.5.1

Compliance: Forthwith.

# AD/DAUPHIN/22 - TRGB Mounting Tripod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/22 TRGB Mounting Tripod 3/89 DM

Applicability: SA 365C, C1, C2, C3 helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SB 01.22A.

*Note: DGAC AD 88-189-030 (B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 25 January 1989 and thereafter every 400 hours time in service.

Background: Cracking initiated by corrosion has been detected at the root of one of the three arms of the tail rotor gearbox mounting tripod.

# AD/CESSNA 400/49 Amdt 2 - Pilot and Co-Pilot Windshield Attach Hole - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 400/49**

**Amdt 2**

**Pilot and Co-Pilot Windshield Attach Hole - Inspection and Modification**

**2/84**

Applicability: 1. All models 1978 thru 1982 414 and 1977 thru 1982 421 aircraft with S/Nos 414A0001 thru 414A0833, 414A0835 thru 414A0851, 414A0853 thru 414A0856,

414A0858 and 421C0273 thru 421C1257.

2\. All models 425 with S/Nos 425-0002 thru 425-0177.

*Note: This inspection is only applicable to aircraft with factory installed windshields.*

Document: 1. Cessna S.L. ME77-5

2\. Cessna S.L. ME83-33 REV 1

3\. Cessna S.L. PJ83-18 REV 1

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 50 hours time in service after 17 February 1984.

Until compliance is accomplished, all flight operations are restricted to a maximum cabin differential pressure of 3.0 P.S.1.

Background: This Directive is raised in issue to reflect revised aircraft applicability and compliance requirements.

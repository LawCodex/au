# AD/CESSNA 320/3 - Nose Gear Main Drive Tube Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 320 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 320/3 Nose Gear Main Drive Tube Assembly - Inspection

Applicability: All models which have exceeded 1000 hours time in service.

## 9/68

Requirement: 1. Inspect the bearing P/N 4NBC612ZP for movement between the outer race and the eye end of the nose gear main drive tube P/N 0842120-1.

2\. If movement is found inspect the entire nose gear assembly for damage or incorrect adjustment. The inflation of the shock leg is of major importance.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service from 5 September 1968 and there-after at intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

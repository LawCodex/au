# AD/DAUPHIN/8 - Horizontal Stabiliser Spar Tube - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/8 Horizontal Stabiliser Spar Tube - Inspection 11/81

Applicability: All SA360C & SA365C/C1 & C2. Document: Dauphin Telex SB No. 05.06.

*Note: Magnetic particle inspection may be used in lieu of dye penetrant.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 9 October 1981 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

Background: There have been three cases of fatigue failure of the stabiliser spar tube on overseas operated helicopters.

# AD/DART/25 - High Pressure Impeller Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Dart Series

AD/DART/25 High Pressure Impeller Replacement 13/94

Applicability: All Rolls Royce Dart Series engines that incorporate a pre-Mod.1475 standard High Pressure Impeller.

Requirement: Replace the high pressure impeller in accordance with Rolls Royce Service Bulletin Da 72-307, Revision 5 dated Sept.94 (RR Mod.1475).

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 004-09-94 also refers.*

Compliance: Effective 8 December 1994, at the first engine shop visit at which the HP compressor area is exposed but no later than 30 June 1995.

Background: Pre-Mod 1475 H.P. Impellers have experienced vane tip failures caused by a particular mode of vibration being excited within the engine operating R.P.M. range.

# AD/CH-3/3 - Front Seat Restraint Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Chrislea CH-3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CH-3/3 Front Seat Restraint Installation - Modification 9/72

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify each front seat restraint installation by fitting a safety harness in accordance with a scheme approved by the Secretary.

*Note: Guidelines for installation schemes are outlined in Airworthiness Advisory Circular No. 62 dated March 1972.*

Compliance: Before 1 October 1973.

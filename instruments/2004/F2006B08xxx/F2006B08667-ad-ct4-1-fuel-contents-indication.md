# AD/CT4/1 - Fuel Contents Indication

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pacific Aerospace Corporation CT/4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CT4/1 Fuel Contents Indication 9/99

Applicability: Model CT/4, CT/4A and CT/4B series aeroplanes with Serial Numbers 001 through 077 and 097 through 114 inclusive.

Requirement: 1. Re-calibrate and re-mark the fuel contents indicator in accordance with Pacific Aerospace Corporation (PAC) Service Bulletin PACSB/CT/117 Revision 1 dated 7 December 1998.

2\. Install a placard, in accordance with PACSB/CT/117 Revision 1, stating:

**WHEN FUEL INDICATOR READS ZERO, FUEL REMAINING IN TANK CANNOT BE SAFELY USED IN FLIGHT**

*Note: CAA(NZ) AD DCA/CT4/6 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Prior to 31 December 1999.

2\. Prior to 31 December 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 9 September 1999.

Background: This Directive provides action that ensures that the fuel quantity indicating system only displays useable fuel contents.

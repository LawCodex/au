# AD/CESSNA 400/101 Amdt 1 - Seat Back Reinforcing Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 400/101**

**Amdt 1**

**Seat Back Reinforcing Strap 7/94**

Applicability: Aeroplanes in the Serial Number ranges:

401B0101 to 401B0221

402B0354 to 402C1020

414-0151 to 414A1212

421B0101 to 421C1807

425-0002 to 425-0236

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna MEB 89-3 Rev 1 and CQB 90-7 as applicable.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 1 April 1993 for all applicable models, except the model 425 which is, within 100 hours time in service after 23 June 1994.

Background: In Australia there is a history of seat back failure in the aircraft referred to in the MEB. The most recent being a total failure in IMC conditions, which in normal single pilot operation would have resulted in total loss of control near the ground.

Amendment 1 changes the Applicability to include the model 425 which has similar seats. It also includes Revision 1 to the MEB which requires no further work.

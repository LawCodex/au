# AD/CESSNA 210/20 Amdt 3 - Aft Tailcone Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 210/20**

**Amdt 3**

**Aft Tailcone Assembly 12/85**

Applicability: All model 210 Series Serial Nos 21057841 to 21059739. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE72-29.

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

*Note: Inspections carried out in accordance with the previous issue of this Directive may be taken as meeting the requirements of Cessna SL SE72-3.*

Background: This Directive is amended to reflect production changes to later serial no. aircraft.

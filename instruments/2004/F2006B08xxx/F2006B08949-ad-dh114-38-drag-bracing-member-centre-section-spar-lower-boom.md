# AD/DH114/38 - Drag Bracing Member, Centre Section Spar Lower Boom - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/38 Drag Bracing Member, Centre Section Spar Lower NK

Boom - Inspection and Replacement

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. prior to 14135 not incorporating modification 1102 or 1103. Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS Heron (114) No. F.18.

Compliance: 1. Inspect fittings in accordance with Para. 4 of TNS Heron (114) No. F18. within 100 hours time in service after 29 June 1973 or before 1 August 1973 whichever occurs first.

2\. Replace fittings in accordance with Para. 5 of TNS Heron (114) No. F.18 before 1 July 1974.

# AD/DH 104/19 Amdt 1 - Engine Mounting Frames - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH 104/19**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Mounting Frames - Inspection 8/71**

Applicability: All models fitted with engine mount frames No. 4EM201A and 4EM203A.

Requirement: Inspect the engine mounting frames for evidence of internal corrosion by visual and radiographic means.

*Note: The inspections shall be carried out in accordance with Hawker Siddeley TNS CT(104) No. 190 Issue 2. Radiographs and any associated reports shall be submitted to the Secretary for examination.*

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 4 years.

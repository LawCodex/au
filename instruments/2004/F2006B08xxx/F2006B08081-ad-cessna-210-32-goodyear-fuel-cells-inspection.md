# AD/CESSNA 210/32 - Goodyear Fuel Cells - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 210/32 Goodyear Fuel Cells - Inspection 6/78

Applicability: All models 210 and T210 fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells. Document: Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978 and annually thereafter as detailed in Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

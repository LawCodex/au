# AD/DH114/12 Amdt 1 - Aileron Lever Part No. 14 WA. 199 - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH114/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Aileron Lever Part No. 14 WA. 199 - Inspection NK and Modification**

Applicability: All models not incorporating Mod. No. 662. Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS Heron (114) No. W.3, Issue 3.

Compliance: Inspect at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service until modified. Modify before 1 June 1970.

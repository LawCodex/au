# AD/CHA/13 - Stall Warning Horn

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CHA/13 Stall Warning Horn 8/75

Applicability: Models 7EC, 7FC, 7ECA, 7GCAA, 7GCBC and 7KCAB. Requirement: Install an approved stall warning horn system.

The following Bellanca-Champion drawings detail approved systems:

Dwg. No. 7-1065 for models 7EC and 7FC; OR Dwg. No. 7-1401 for models 7ECA, 7GCAA, 7GCBC and 7KCAB.

Compliance: Before 31 January 1976.

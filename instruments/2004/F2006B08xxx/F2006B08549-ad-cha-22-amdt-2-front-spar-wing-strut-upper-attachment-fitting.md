# AD/CHA/22 Amdt 2 - Front Spar Wing Strut Upper Attachment Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CHA/22**

**Amdt 2**

**Front Spar Wing Strut Upper Attachment Fitting 11/90**

Applicability: All model 8KCAB aircraft .

*Note: Although the Applicability is limited to the 8KCAB, the requirement may in future be extended to other models (particularly the aerobatic 7 series) if subsequent service experience and/or analysis so indicates.*

Requirement: 1. For P/N 2-1976 fittings. Remove the left and right front spar wing strut upper attachment fittings P/N 2-1976 from the aircraft and conduct a magnetic particle inspection in accordance with the procedures outlined in CAO 108-8 Issue 1, paying particular attention to the welds which connect the strut attachment bolt hole reinforcement plates to the forward and aft “U” channels.

*Note : Ensure that the fitting is re-protected after inspection.*

2\. For P/N 3-1658 fittings. Retire P/N 3-1658 fittings from service.

Compliance: For Requirement 1: Prior to the accumulation of 500 hours component time in service or within 50 hours component time in service after 3 September 1990, whichever occurs later, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 250 hours component time in service.

*Note: For aircraft with fittings which have exceeded 500 hours, aerobatic manoeuvres should not be flown until the initial inspection has been carried out.*

For Requirement 2: Prior to exceeding 1500 hours component time in service.

Background: There have been five reported overseas occurrences of fatigue cracking emanating from the weld area, including one in-flight failure in P/N 2-1976 fittings on 8KCAB aircraft. This amendment limits the Applicability to the Decathlon, replaces the previous one time fluorescent dye penetrant inspection with a repetitive off-aircraft magnetic particle inspection, and promulgates the recommended 1500 hour life limitation for the replacement fittings P/N 3-1658. FAA AD 90-15-15 and American Champion Aircraft Corporation Service Letter 402 refer.

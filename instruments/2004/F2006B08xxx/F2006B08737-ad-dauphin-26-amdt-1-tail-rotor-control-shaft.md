# AD/DAUPHIN/26 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Control Shaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

**AD/DAUPHIN/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Control Shaft 1/92**

Applicability: All model SA 360, 365C, C1, C2, C3, N and FS Helicopters. Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SB 05.15 or 05.24, as applicable.

*Note: D6AC AD 90-204-033(B) refers to SB 05.15 only.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement document.

Background: Excessive wear of the tail rotor control shaft bearings could result in loss of tail rotor control. This amendment extends the Requirement to all 365 series helicopters.

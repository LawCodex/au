# AD/CESSNA 210/43 Amdt 1 - Alternator Ground Strap - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 210/43**

**Amdt 1**

**Alternator Ground Strap - Installation 9/82**

Applicability: All model 210/P210/T210 with S/Nos. 21058783 to 21062666 except 21062650 and 21062662, P21000001 to P21000026, T210-0001 to T210-0454.

Document: 1. Cessna SIL SE79-59; or

2\. FAA AD 79-25-07.

Compliance: 1. Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1982.

2\. Each time an engine is installed in an aircraft following initial compliance.

Background: Compliance has been amended by item 2 due to reports from industry that ground straps are being omitted from refitment on engine reinstallations.

# AD/CESSNA 340/28 - Auxiliary Fuel Pump Wiring Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 340/28 Auxiliary Fuel Pump Wiring Modification 12/89

Applicability: All models 340 which have been modified in accordance with Cessna Service Bulletin MEB 88-3.

Requirement: Unless already accomplished, action in accordance with Cessna Service Bulletin MEB 88-3 Revision 1.

*Note: This Requirement does not apply to aeroplanes that have not incorporated SB MEB 88-3.*

Compliance: Prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 December 1989.

Background: Compliance with the original issue of SB MEB 88-3 was not considered to warrant the issue of an Airworthiness Directive. This AD is raised to ensure that necessary features of Revision 1 are incorporated into modified aeroplanes.

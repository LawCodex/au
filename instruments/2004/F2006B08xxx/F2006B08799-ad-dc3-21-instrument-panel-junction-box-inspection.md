# AD/DC3/21 - Instrument Panel Junction Box - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/21 Instrument Panel Junction Box - Inspection NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the undercarriage warning light dimming resistors inside the instrument panel junction box for possible short circuiting to terminals of frame.

2\. Re-position resistors as necessary to preclude any possibility of short circuiting. OR ANOs Part 100 Appendix “A” Notice No. 7/31;

OR ANO 105.1.16.1.6.8.

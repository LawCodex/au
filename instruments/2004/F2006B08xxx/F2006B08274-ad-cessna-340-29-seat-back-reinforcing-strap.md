# AD/CESSNA 340/29 - Seat Back Reinforcing Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 340/29 Seat Back Reinforcing Strap 4/93

Applicability: S/N 340-0001 to 340A1817.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna MEB 89-3. Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1993.

Background: In Australia there is a history of seat back failure in the aircraft referred to in the MEB. The most recent being a total failure in IMC conditions, which in normal single pilot operation would have resulted in total loss of control near the ground.

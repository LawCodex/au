# AD/CF6/28 - CF6-80C2 Stage 1 High Pressure Turbine Disc Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

AD/CF6/28 CF6-80C2 Stage 1 High Pressure Turbine Disc Inspection

7/94

Applicability: CF6-80C2 Engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-06-03.

*Note: General Electric Service Bulletin No 72-614 Revision 1 and All Operator’s Wire 92-80C-16 of 22 April 1992 refer.*

Compliance: This directive is effective from 27 Jun 1994.

Background: This directive requires an inspection for cracks in the stage 1 high pressure turbine disc rim bolt holes and replacement as necessary with serviceable parts.

It originates from an uncontained stage 1 high pressure turbine failure.

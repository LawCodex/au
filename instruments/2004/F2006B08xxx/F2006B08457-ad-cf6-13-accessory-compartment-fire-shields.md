# AD/CF6/13 - Accessory Compartment Fire Shields

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

AD/CF6/13 Accessory Compartment Fire Shields 11/88

DM

Applicability: CF6-80A Series.

Requirement: Install fire shields in accordance with the requirements of FAA AD 89-16-08 Paras

(a) and (b) unless already accomplished. Compliance: Prior to 27 October 1989.

Background: The FAA advises of one event involving a CF6-80A engine where an external fire resulted from failure of a number 5R bearing. The fire progressed into the accessory compartment damaging fuel carrying lines causing fuel leakage which sustained the fire.

# AD/DC4/38 - Lower Main Plane Stringers Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC4/38 Lower Main Plane Stringers Inspection NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect for cracking of stringers 18, 19, 20 and 21 between the front and centre lower mainplane spars from wing station 167 to wing station 281 using a radiographic technique acceptable to the Secretary.

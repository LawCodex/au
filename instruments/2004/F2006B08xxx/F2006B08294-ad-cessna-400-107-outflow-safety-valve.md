# AD/CESSNA 400/107 - Outflow Safety Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 400/107 Outflow Safety Valve 5/96

Applicability: All Cessna 441 series aircraft.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out, action in accordance with Allied Signal Service Bulletin 103576-21-4054.

*Note: FAA AD 95-25-10 Amdt 39-9456 refers.*

Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service from 25 April 1996 or prior to 31 July 1996, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that cracking has been found in two valve poppets of a particular batch. This cracking may cause failure of the outflow safety valve which could result in rapid decompression of the aircraft.

# AD/DAUPHIN/40 - Main Rotor Shaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/40 Main Rotor Shaft 7/94 DM

Applicability: All model SA 365 helicopters versions C, C1, C2, C3, N, N1 and N2 equipped with main rotor shaft P/N 365A31-1179-00, -01, -02, -03, -04 or -21 with serial number

less than 10000.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter France Telex Service No 01-31 or 01-39, as applicable.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-066-037(B)R1 and 93-065-034(B)R1 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that corrosion has been found on the mast during overhaul.

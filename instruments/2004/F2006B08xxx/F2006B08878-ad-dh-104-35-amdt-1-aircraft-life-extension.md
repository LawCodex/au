## AD/DH 104/35

**Amdt 1**

## Aircraft Life Extension 7/77

Document: British Aerospace TNS Dove (104) No. 240 Issue 2.

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document. NOTE: Wing spar boom safe lives are detailed in the current issue of AD/DH 104/5. the manufacturer has advised that the aircraft life cannot be extended beyond two wing spar boom lifetimes.

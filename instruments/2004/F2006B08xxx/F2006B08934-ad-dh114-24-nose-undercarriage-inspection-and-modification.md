# AD/DH114/24 - Nose Undercarriage - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/24 Nose Undercarriage - Inspection and Modification NK

Applicability: All Series 2 aeroplanes not incorporating Heron Mod. 1093. Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS Heron (114) No. U.13.

Compliance: Inspection: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 May 1970 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified.

Modification: Before 1 August 1970.

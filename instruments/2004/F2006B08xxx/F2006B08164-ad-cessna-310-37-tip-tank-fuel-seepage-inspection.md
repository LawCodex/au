# AD/CESSNA 310/37 - Tip Tank Fuel Seepage - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 310 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 310/37 Tip Tank Fuel Seepage - Inspection 4/75

Applicability: All models fitted with strobe lights in the wing tip fuel tank nose cap. Document: 1. Cessna SL ME73-4, or

2\. FAA AD 75-05-08.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1975 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

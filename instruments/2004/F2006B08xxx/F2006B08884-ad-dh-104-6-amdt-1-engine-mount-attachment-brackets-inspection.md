# AD/DH 104/6 Amdt 1 - Engine Mount Attachment Brackets - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH 104/6**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Mount Attachment Brackets - Inspection 3/75**

Applicability: All models not modified in accordance with TNS CT (104) Nos. 50 and 65. Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS CT(104) Nos. 50 Issue 3 and 65 Issue 4.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service after 30 April 1973.

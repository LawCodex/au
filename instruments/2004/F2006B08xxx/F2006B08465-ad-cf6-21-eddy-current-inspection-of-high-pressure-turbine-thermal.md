# AD/CF6/21 - Eddy Current Inspection of High Pressure Turbine Thermal Shields

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

## AD/CF6/21 Eddy Current Inspection of High Pressure

**Turbine Thermal Shields**

Applicability: CF6-45/-50 series engines.

## 11/91

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 91-15-16, Amendment 39-7080.

*Note: General Electric Service Bulletin 72-879 Revision 6 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 28 November 1991.

Background: Four HPT stage one discs have been found cracked during inspection. The condition, if not corrected could result in uncontained engine failure.

# AD/DART/26 - Low Torque Switch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Dart Series

AD/DART/26 Low Torque Switch 6/97

Applicability: All Rolls Royce Dart series engines.

Requirement: To ensure unapproved low torque switches are removed from service, inspect installed and spare low torque switches in accordance with Rolls Royce Service Bulletin Da61-13.

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 002-12-96 also refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, no later than 30/6/97.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 22 May 1997.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that unapproved low torque switches have been found in service. These switches may give rise to difficulties during activation of the autofeathering system.

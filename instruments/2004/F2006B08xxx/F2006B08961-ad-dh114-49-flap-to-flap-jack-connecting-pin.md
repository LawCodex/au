# AD/DH114/49 - Flap To Flap Jack Connecting Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/49 Flap To Flap Jack Connecting Pin 1/89

DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe TNS CF-20 Issue 1. Compliance: Within seven days time in service after 7 December 1988.

Background: Incorrectly manufactured connecting pins have been found in service, one of which caused an in-flight asymmetric flap condition.

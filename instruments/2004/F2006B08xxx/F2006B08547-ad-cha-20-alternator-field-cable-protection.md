# AD/CHA/20 - Alternator - Field Cable Protection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CHA/20 Alternator - Field Cable Protection 6/78

Applicability: All models fitted with an Alternator System.

Requirement: Install a 5 AMP circuit protective device (i.e. in line fuse or circuit breaker) in the field cable as close as practical to the master switch.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978.

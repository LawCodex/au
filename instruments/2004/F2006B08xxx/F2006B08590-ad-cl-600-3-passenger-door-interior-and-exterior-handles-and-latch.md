# AD/CL-600/3 - Passenger Door Interior and Exterior Handles and Latch Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CL-600/3 Passenger Door Interior and Exterior Handles and Latch Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 1024 to 1075. Document: Canadair Challenger Alert SB No. A600-0203.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 31 March, 1983.

## 3/83

Background: Passenger door latch mechanisms and associated fittings have been found incorrectly rigged.

# AD/COOL/1 - Stewart Warner Oil Coolers - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Coolers

AD/COOL/1 Stewart Warner Oil Coolers - Inspection NK

and Replacement

Applicability: Stewart Warner oil coolers:

Model 8406J with S/Nos. 101 to 1500 10599A with S/Nos. 101 to 700

10568B with S/Nos. 101 to 579

10578B with S/Nos. 101 to 634

Requirement: Inspect the engine oil cooler for applicability within the affected model and serial number range.

Any affected cooler shall be retired from service and replaced with a serviceable cooler not of the subject serial numbered range.

*Note: The oil coolers are fitted to, but not necessarily limited to, the following aircraft:*

*1976 and 1977 Model Cessna 172 and 177.*

*2. 1976 Model Cessna 177RG.*

*3. Consolidated Aeronautics Lake LA-4-200.*

*4. Grumman American Model AA-5A and AA-5B.*

*5. Mooney M20 Series.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1976.

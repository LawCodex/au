# AD/DH 89/1 - Fuel System Control - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 89 (Dragon Rapide) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH 89/1 Fuel System Control - Modification NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify the fuel system controls as follows:

Incorporate a positive method of retaining and clamping the fuel control cable housing at both ends, and at the bend where it enters the wing.

2\. Incorporate a means of locking the pilot’s control lever in the “ON” position such that the lever can be moved rapidly to the “OFF” position in the event of an emergency; or

Part 100, Appendix “A” Notice No. 10/2; or ANO 105.1.14.0.1.1.

Compliance: Forthwith.

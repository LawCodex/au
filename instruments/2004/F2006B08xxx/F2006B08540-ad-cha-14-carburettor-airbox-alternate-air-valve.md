# AD/CHA/14 - Carburettor Airbox Alternate Air Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CHA/14 Carburettor Airbox Alternate Air Valve 10/75

Applicability: Models: 7ECA with S/Nos 723-70 and subsequent. 7GCAA with S/Nos 205-70 and subs. 7GCBC with S/Nos 202-70 and subs. 7KCAB with S/Nos 202-70 and subs.

8KCAB with S/Nos 4-71 and subs. 8GCBC with S/Nos 1-74 and subs.

Requirement: Inspect the carburettor airbox alternate air valve for cracking and security of attachment.

*Note: Incorporation of a Bellanca Service Kit does not cancel this repetitive inspection.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1975 and there-after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

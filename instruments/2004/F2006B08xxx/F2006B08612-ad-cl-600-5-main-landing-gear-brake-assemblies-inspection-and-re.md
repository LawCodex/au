# AD/CL-600/5 - Main Landing Gear Brake Assemblies - Inspection and Re-Identification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CL-600/5 Main Landing Gear Brake Assemblies - Inspection

**and Re-Identification**

Applicability: 1. All models S/No. 1002 and subsequent.

2\. All brake assemblies, P/No. 600-85083-55, held as spares. Document: Canadair Challenger SB No. A600-0045, Rev. 2.

Compliance: Prior to the issue of an Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

## 3/83

Background: Inspection and re-identification of brake assemblies is required to ensure that previous rework has not created a condition that could result in a dragging brake.

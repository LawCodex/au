# AD/DAUPHIN/44 - Tail Rotor Hub Fairing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/44 Tail Rotor Hub Fairing 9/95 TX

Applicability: Model SA 365C, C1, C2, and C3, and 365N.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter Telex Service No. 01.34 or 01.43 as applicable.

*Note: DGAC ADs 95-112-040(B) and 95-107-039(B) R1 refer.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 7 July 1995, thereafter in accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: There have been in service reports of ruptured springs, which both DGAC and Eurocopter consider to be an urgent airworthiness matter.

# AD/DH114/42 - Nose Gear Inner Casing - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/42 Nose Gear Inner Casing - Inspection NK

and Replacement

Applicability: All models.

Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS Heron (114) No. U.17.

Compliance: Inspect casing in accordance with para. 3 of TNS No. U.17 within 50 hours time in service after 31 December 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service and after any reported heavy landings.

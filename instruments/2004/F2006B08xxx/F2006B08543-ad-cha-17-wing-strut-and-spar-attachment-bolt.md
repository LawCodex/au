# AD/CHA/17 - Wing Strut and Spar Attachment Bolt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

| **AD/CHA/17** | **Wing Strut and Spar Attachment Bolt** | **11/77** |
|---|---|---|
| Applicability: | All model 8GCBC and 8KCAB. |  |
| Requirement: | Retire from service: |  |

The front and rear wing strut upper and lower attachment bolts.

2\. The front and rear wing spar to fuselage attachment bolts.

Compliance: 1. For aircraft which have achieved 1000 hours time in service: within 200 hours time in service after 30 November 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1200 hours time in service.

2\. For all other Aircraft: at intervals not exceeding 1200 hours time in service.

*Note: For 8KCAB aircraft each hour engaged in aerobatics shall be counted as equivalent to 10 hours normal flight.*

# AD/CF6/5 - Fuel Supply Manifold

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

AD/CF6/5 Fuel Supply Manifold 9/85

Applicability: General Electric CF6-80A, -80A2 engines. Requirement: FAA TAD No. T85-15-51.

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: General Electric has advised of 3 fuel supply manifolds failing due to cracking at the “Y” section. The manifolds’ Part No 9327M46G01, 02, are the subject of FAA Emergency Telegraphic Airworthiness Directive T85-15-51 which requires a cycle life limit of 2500 cycles. Any manifold in service with a cycle life in excess of 2500 cycles must be replaced within 100 cycles following receipt of FAA TAD No T85- 15-51.

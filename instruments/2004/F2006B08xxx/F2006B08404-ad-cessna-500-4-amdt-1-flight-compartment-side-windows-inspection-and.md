# AD/CESSNA 500/4 Amdt 1 - Flight Compartment Side Windows - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 500 (Citation) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 500/4**

**Amdt 1**

**Flight Compartment Side Windows - Inspection and Replacement**

**11/74**

Applicability: All model 500 except those fitted with flight compartment side windows P/Nos. 5511285-34-4.

Requirement: 1. Inspect in accordance with Cessna Citation SL 56-2 Rev. 1.

2\. Replace in accordance with SB 56-4.

Compliance: Before achieving 400 hours time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified.

Modify before 1 September 1975.

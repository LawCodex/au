# AD/DC3/33 - Supplemental Inspection Program

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/33 Supplemental Inspection Program 7/94

Applicability: All Models.

Requirement: Incorporate the inspection program outlined in McDonnell Douglas Report L26-013, “DC-3 Supplemental Inspection Document” (SID), into the maintenance schedule for the aircraft.

Compliance: Incorporate the SID inspection program before 26 May 1995.

Background: McDonnell Douglas Aircraft Corporation has produced a supplemental inspection program to ensure the continued safety of older DC-3 aircraft. This Airworthiness Directive mandates the application of the inspection program to Australian registered aircraft.

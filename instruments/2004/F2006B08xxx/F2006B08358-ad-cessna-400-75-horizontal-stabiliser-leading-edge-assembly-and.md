# AD/CESSNA 400/75 - Horizontal Stabiliser Leading Edge Assembly and Elevator Trim Tab Actuator - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 400/75 Horizontal Stabiliser Leading Edge Assembly

**and Elevator Trim Tab Actuator - Replacement**

Applicability: All models 404 with S/Nos. 404-0001 to 404-0637.

Requirement: 1. Modify the horizontal stabiliser by replacing the leading edge assembly in accordance with Cessna Service Kit SK404-31 as revised 23 June 1980.

## 10/80

2\. Modify the elevator trim system by replacing the trim tab actuator in accordance with Cessna Service Kit SK404-31 as revised 23 June 1980.

Compliance: 1. Before 1 March 1981.

2\. Unless already accomplished, before 1 March 1981.

Background: These replacements will eliminate the RPM limitation and the recurring trim tab actuator screw replacement requirement of Cessna SIL ME80-2, Revision 2.

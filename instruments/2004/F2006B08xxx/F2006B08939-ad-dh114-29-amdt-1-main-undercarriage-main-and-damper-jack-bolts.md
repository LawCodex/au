# AD/DH114/29 Amdt 1 - Main Undercarriage - Main and Damper Jack Bolts - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH114/29**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Undercarriage - Main and Damper NK Jack Bolts - Inspection and Modification**

Applicability: All Series 2 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect the attachment bolts at each end of the damper jack and at the top of the main jack for fatigue cracking as follows:

For aircraft fitted with attachment bolts P/No. 14-2U.299:-

(a) Remove the bolts and inspect for cracking around the centre grease hole using a magnetic particle method.

(b) Incorporate Modification No. 1536.

2\. For aircraft fitted with attachment bolts P/No. 14-2U.631:-

Remove the bolts and inspect for cracking around the centre grease hole using a magnetic particle method.

Compliance: For Para. 1:

(a) For aircraft inspected in accordance with the previous issue of this Directive - within 300 hours time in service from the previous inspection; and

For all other aircraft - within 150 hours time in service after 31 May 1972; and

For all other aircraft - thereafter at intervals not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

(b) Before 31 December 1972. For Para. 2:

Within 2400 hours time in service after the incorporation of modification 1536 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 2400 hours time in service.

# AD/DAUPHIN/41 - Free Wheel Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/41 Free Wheel Assembly 9/94

Applicability: All model SA 365 helicopters versions C, C1, C2 and C3 equipped with free wheel assembly P/N 365A35.1004.00.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Eurocopter Telex Service No 10040.SI/0145/94 dated 25 May 1994.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-145-039(B) refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 20 August 1994.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that following an incident the free wheel assembly in the helicopter was found without oil.

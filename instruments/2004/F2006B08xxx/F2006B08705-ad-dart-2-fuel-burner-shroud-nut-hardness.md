# AD/DART/2 - Fuel Burner Shroud Nut Hardness

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Dart Series

AD/DART/2 Fuel Burner Shroud Nut Hardness 11/87

Applicability: All Dart RDa-6, RDa-7 series engines fitted with fuel burners of any modification standard other than Mod. 1626.

Requirement: 1. Inspect fuel burner shroud nuts for hardness in accordance with the requirements of Rolls Royce SB Da 73-68 and Da 73-A81 as applicable; or

2\. AD/RRT/15 Amdt 1.

Compliance: As detailed in Requirement documents. Background: Reissue of AD/RRT/15 Amdt 1.

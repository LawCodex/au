# AD/CON/65 - Fuel Pump Screen

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Teledyne Continental Motors Piston Engines

AD/CON/65 Fuel Pump Screen 13/92

Applicability: All A-65, A75, C75, C85, C90, A-100, C125, O-200 series engines fitted with AC fuel pumps P/N 40585, 40695 or 631391.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Continental Aircraft Service Bulletin M81-8.

*Note: FAA Airworthiness Directive 81-07-06 refers.*

Compliance: Effective 10 December 1992: At intervals not to exceed 12 months time in service. Background: The requirements of AD/CON/39A have been reissued following the review of all

Continental Piston Engine Directives. The requirements of FAA Airworthiness

Directive 81-07-06 are still effective and appropriate.

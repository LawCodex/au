# AD/DH 104/36 - Engine Mounting Frame Front Engine Support Spigots - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DH 104/36 Engine Mounting Frame Front Engine

**Support Spigots - Inspection** Applicability: All models equipped with front engine support spigot P/N 4EM.681. Document: British Aerospace TNS Dove (104) No. 244 Issue 2.

Compliance: Before 15 September 1981.

## 5/81

Background: A case has been reported to the manufacturer where the front engine support spigot, on an engine mounting frame, was found fractured.

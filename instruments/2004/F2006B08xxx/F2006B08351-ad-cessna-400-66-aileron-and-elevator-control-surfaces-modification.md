# AD/CESSNA 400/66 - Aileron and Elevator Control Surfaces - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 400/66 Aileron and Elevator Control

**Surfaces - Modification**

Applicability: All model 441 with S/Nos. 441-0001 to 441-0090. Document: Cessna Customer Care Owner Advisory No. PJ 79-3A.

(FAA AD 79- 09-02 Amdt. 39-3453 refers).

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1979.

## 6/79

Background: An inflight aileron vibration/flutter incident was caused by an accumulation of water in one of the aileron control surfaces.

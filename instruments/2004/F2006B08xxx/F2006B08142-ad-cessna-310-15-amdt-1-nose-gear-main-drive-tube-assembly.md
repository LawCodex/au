# AD/CESSNA 310/15 Amdt 1 - Nose Gear Main Drive Tube Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 310 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA310/15**

**Amdt 1**

**Nose Gear Main Drive Tube Assembly 9/91**

Applicability: All models which have exceeded 1000 hours time in service; and with bearing P/N 4NBC612ZP fitted to nose gear drive tube P/N 0842120-1.

Requirement: Inspect the affected bearing for movement between the outer bearing race and the eye end of the nose gear main drive tube.

Compliance: At intervals not to exceed 200 hours time in service. The compliance date of the original issue of this Directive was 5 September 1968. This reissue is effective from 5 September 1991.

Background: The initial issue of this Directive was raised in response to three incidents; one of which resulted in an accident. This amendment is raised to clarify applicability.

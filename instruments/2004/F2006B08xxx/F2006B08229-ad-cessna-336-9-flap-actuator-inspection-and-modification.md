# AD/CESSNA 336/9 - Flap Actuator - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 336 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 336/9 Flap Actuator - Inspection and Modification 2/73

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 336-0001 to 336-0195.

Requirement: PART 1 - Inspect actuator jack screw and clean and relubricate in accordance with Cessna SL SE60-16.

PART 2 - Modify the aircraft in accordance with Cessna SL ME72-19.

Compliance: For Part 1 : At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1973 until modified.

For Part 2 : Before 1st June 1973.

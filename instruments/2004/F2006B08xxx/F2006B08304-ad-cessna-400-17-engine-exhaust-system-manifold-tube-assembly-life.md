# AD/CESSNA 400/17 - Engine Exhaust System Manifold Tube Assembly - Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 400/17 Engine Exhaust System Manifold Tube**

**Assembly - Life Limitation**

**4/70**

Applicability: All 401 and 402 series.

Requirement: Retire from service exhaust manifold tube assemblies P/Nos. 0850712-5; 0850712-6; 0850712-32 and 0850712-33.

Compliance: 1. For assemblies with more than 1700 hours time in service at 29 May 1971 - within 100 hours time in service.

2\. For assemblies with less than 1700 hours time in service - before achieving 1800 hours time in service.

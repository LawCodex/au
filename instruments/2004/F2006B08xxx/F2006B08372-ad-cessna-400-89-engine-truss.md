# AD/CESSNA 400/89 - Engine Truss

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 400/89 Engine Truss 7/86

Applicability: All models 441 with S/Nos. 441-001 to 441-0362 Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna Service Bulletin CQB85-24 Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: In service cracking may occur at the welded joints in the engine truss as described in the Service Bulletin.

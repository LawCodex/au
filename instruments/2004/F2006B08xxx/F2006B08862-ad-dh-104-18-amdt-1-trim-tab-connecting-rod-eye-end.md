# AD/DH 104/18 Amdt 1 - Trim Tab Connecting Rod Eye End

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH 104/18**

**Amdt 1**

**Trim Tab Connecting Rod Eye End 11/91**

Applicability: Model DH 104 series.

Requirement: Remove the rudder and elevators, and the rudder and elevator trim tab connecting rod assemblies; carry out an inspection for cracking of the eye ends P/N CM-2A and

CM-2B.

*Note: Hawker Siddeley Technical News sheet CT (104) No. 187 issue 1 refers.*

Compliance: At each check 4 inspection. The compliance time of the previous issue of this Directive was at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

Background: A cracked eye end was found on a Heron aircraft elevator trim tab connecting rod.

The same part is fitted to Dove aircraft. This amendment extends compliance to align with Country of Origin AD.

# AD/DAUPHIN/36 - Electronic/Electric Panels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/36 Electronic/Electric Panels 7/93

Applicability: Dauphin SA 365 helicopters versions N, N1 and N2.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter France Mandatory SA 365 Service Bulletin No 39.03R1 (date code 93-13).

*Note: DGAC AD 87-167-022(B)R1 dated 28 April 1993 refers.)*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 24 June 1993.

Background: Reports have been received by the DGAC of chafing of electrical wiring harnesses, which could in turn cause electrical shorts and smoke and/or fire in the cockpit.

Compliance with this Directive will detect any damage and decrease the possibility of further damage being caused to wiring harnesses.

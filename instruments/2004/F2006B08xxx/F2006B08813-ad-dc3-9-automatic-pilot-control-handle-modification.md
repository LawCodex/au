# AD/DC3/9 - Automatic Pilot Control Handle - Modification

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/9 Automatic Pilot Control Handle - Modification NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Modify the automatic pilot control handle in such a manner that confusion will not exist between it and the adjacent fuel cross-feed control handle.

2\. Figures 1 and 2 of this Order provide a suitable method of incorporating this modification.

OR ANOs Part 100 Appendix “A” Notice 7/4; OR ANO 105.1.16.1.5.9.

# AD/CESSNA 210/65 - Fuel Line Chafing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 210/65 Fuel Line Chafing 1/92 DM

Applicability: All Cessna 210 series with S/N 21059062 through 21064535, T210-0393 through T210-0454 and P21000001 through P21000760.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Cessna Service Information Letter SE82-32. A minimum clearance of 6.5mm (0.25 inches) must be obtained between the fuel line/electrical wiring loom.

*Note: FAA AD 91-22-01 Amdt 39-8061 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service from 22 November 1991 or by 30 April 1992, whichever occurs first.

Background: Chafing of the fuel line by the wiring loom in the nose gear tunnel area could result in an inflight fire.

# AD/CON/64 - Induction Elbow

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Teledyne Continental Motors Piston Engines

AD/CON/64 Induction Elbow 13/92

Applicability: All TSIO-520-J and N series engines fitted with a P/N 629138 intake elbow tube. Requirement: Inspect and assemble P/N 635930 flexible intake elbows in accordance with

Continental Aircraft Engine Service Bulletin M78-5 Rev.3.

*Note: FAA Airworthiness Directive 78-06-01 refers.*

Compliance: Effective 10 December 1992: At intervals not to exceed 110 hours time in service. Background: The requirements of AD/CON/35A have been reissued following review of all

Continental Piston Engine Directives. The requirements of FAA Airworthiness

Directive 78-06-01 are still effective and appropriate.

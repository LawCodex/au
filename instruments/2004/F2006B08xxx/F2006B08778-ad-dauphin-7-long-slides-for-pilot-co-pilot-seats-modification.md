# AD/DAUPHIN/7 - Long Slides for Pilot & Co-Pilot Seats - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/7 Long Slides for Pilot & Co-Pilot Seats - Modification

8/81

Applicability: Models fitted with bucket seats & long slides (prior to mod AMS 365A.07.1653). Document: Dauphin SB No. 25.08.

Compliance: Before 31 July 1981.

Background: Installation of a doubler plate is required to prevent deformation of the long slides.

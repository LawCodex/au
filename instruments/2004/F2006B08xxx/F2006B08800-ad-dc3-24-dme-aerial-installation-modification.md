# AD/DC3/24 - DME Aerial Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/24 DME Aerial Installation - Modification NK

Applicability: All models with DME aerials type VJN-3, VJN-3A or VJN-3B mounted forward of station 136 1/2.

Requirement: Relocate the aerial to a position aft of station 136 1/2 or alternatively replace the aerial with a type AT-256A/ARC and associated matching stub.

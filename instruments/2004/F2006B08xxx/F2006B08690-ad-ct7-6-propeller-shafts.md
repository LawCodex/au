# AD/CT7/6 - Propeller Shafts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CT7 Series

AD/CT7/6 Propeller Shafts 11/94 DM

Applicability: General Electric Aircraft Engines Models: CT7-5A2, -5A3, -7A, -7A1, -9B, -9B2,

-9C, -9D.

Requirement: Action in accordance with General Electric CT7-TP Series Service Bulletin Number A72-350 Revision 3 of 9 June 1994.

*Note: FAA AD 94-15-01 refers.*

Compliance: At the next shop visit after 12 September 1994 or before 30 November 1994 whichever occurs first.

Background: A one time ultrasonic inspection of a suspect population of propeller shafts for metallurgical defects and the replacement of any found to be faulty is required because of an inflight propeller separation which resulted from a metallurgical defect.

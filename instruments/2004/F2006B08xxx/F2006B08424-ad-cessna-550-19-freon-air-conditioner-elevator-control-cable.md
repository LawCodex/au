# AD/CESSNA 550/19 - Freon Air Conditioner/Elevator Control Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 550 (Citation II) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 550/19 Freon Air Conditioner/Elevator**

**Control Cable**

**7/90**

**TX**

Applicability: Model 550, 551 and S550 series fitted with STC SA 2698SW freon air conditioning systems.

Requirement: 1. Deactivate the air conditioning system by pulling the airconditioner circuit breakers on the main junction box and install tie wraps to preclude inadvertent re- setting.

2\. Visually inspect the elevator control cables under and in the vicinity of the aft baggage compartment floor for evidence of damage caused by either arcing or chafe.

3\. Replace mounting hardware on the inboard mounting rail and replace existing earth wire in accordance with Kieth Service Bulletin 108.

*Note: FAA Emergency AD 90-07-10 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Prior to further flight after 18 April 1990.

2\. Within 10 hours time in service after 18 April 1990.

3\. Prior to reactivating the airconditioning system.

Background: There has been a report of arcing from the freon airconditioning unit that has burned through an elevator control cable. A defect, if uncorrected, could lead to loss of control.

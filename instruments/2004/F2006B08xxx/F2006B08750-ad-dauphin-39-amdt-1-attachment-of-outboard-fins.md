# AD/DAUPHIN/39 Amdt 1 - Attachment of Outboard Fins

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

**AD/DAUPHIN/39**

**Amdt 1**

**Attachment of Outboard Fins 3/97**

Applicability: Models 365N, N1 and N2 fitted with outboard fins that are secured with studs P/n 365A13-3017-24, on which Mod 365A07-55B08 has not been embodied.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter SB SA365 01.40 R1.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-076-036(B)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished in accordance with the previous issue of this Directive, before further flight after 27 February 1997.

This Amendment has effect from 27 February 1997.

Background: Following the loss of an outboard fin, other aircraft were found to have loose fin attaching studs.

Amendment 1 reflects the latest revisions of the Requirement document, and the DGAC AD, it also revises the Applicability to exclude aircraft at a particular mod state.

The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 20 April 1994.

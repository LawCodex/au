# AD/CF700/11 - Improved Combustion Liner Rivets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF700 Series

AD/CF700/11 Improved Combustion Liner Rivets 9/85

Applicability: All CF700-2C engines with S/Nos. prior to 24532. CF700-2D engines with S/Nos. prior to 299133. CF700-2D-2 engines with S/Nos. prior to 304096.

Document: General Electric SB (CF700) 72-108, Rev. 1; or AD/GE-T/24.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft following next hot section inspection or following next reconditioning whichever occurs first.

# AD/DC3/30 - Front (Pilots) Seats Restraint Installations - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/30 Front (Pilots) Seats Restraint NK Installations - Modification

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify each front (pilot) seat restraint installation by fitting a symmetric safety harness incorporating an inertia reel in with a scheme approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Before 1 July 1973 unless already accomplished.

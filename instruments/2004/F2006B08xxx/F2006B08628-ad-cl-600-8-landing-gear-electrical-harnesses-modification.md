# AD/CL-600/8 - Landing Gear Electrical Harnesses - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CL-600/8 Landing Gear Electrical Harnesses - Modification 3/83

Applicability: All model CL600 aeroplanes as per requirement document. Document: Canadair Challenger SB No. 600-0159.

Compliance: Before issue of Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: To prevent landing gear proximity switches from being affected by electromagnetic interference. This may result in loss of aircraft brakes while landing and taxiing with anti-skid system in use.

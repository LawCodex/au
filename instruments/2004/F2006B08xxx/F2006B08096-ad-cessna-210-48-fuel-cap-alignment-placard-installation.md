# AD/CESSNA 210/48 - Fuel Cap Alignment Placard - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 210/48 Fuel Cap Alignment Placard - Installation 9/80

Applicability: Models 210 and T210 with S/Nos. 616, 618, 57001 to 57575 and 21057576 to 21063870; Model P210 with S/Nos. P21000001 to P21000457.

Document: Cessna SIL No. SE 80-59.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1980.

Background: Correct alignment of flush fitting type fuel caps is essential for efficient operation of the fuel tank secondary vent system.

# AD/CHA/21 - Wing Rib/Spar Attachment and L.E. Support Block Nails

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CHA/21 Wing Rib/Spar Attachment and**

**L.E. Support Block Nails**

**4/80**

Applicability: All models 7GC, 7GCA, 7GCB, 7GCBA, 7HC, 7KC and 7KCAB; All models 7ECA with S/Nos. 1 to 1350-80; All models 7GCAA with S/Nos. 1 to 396-80; All models 7GCBC with S/Nos. 1 to 1213-80; All models 8KCAB with S/Nos. 3-70 to 638-80; All models 8GCBC with S/Nos. 1-74 to 355-80.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bellanca SL No. C-139. Compliance: In accordance with Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of the rib/spar attachment and leading edge support block nails loosening and backing out of the spar.

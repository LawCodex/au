# AD/CHA/15 - Wing Front Strut Aerobatic Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CHA/15 Wing Front Strut Aerobatic Fatigue Life Limitation 4/76

Applicability: All model 8KCAB aircraft.

Requirement: Retire the wing front strut assembly, including the aluminium end fittings, from service.

Compliance: Before achieving 1100 aerobatic flying hours.

*Note: The hours are those actually spent in aerobatic flight and do not include positioning time.*

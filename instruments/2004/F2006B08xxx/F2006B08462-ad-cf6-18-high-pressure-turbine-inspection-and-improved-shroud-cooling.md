# AD/CF6/18 - High Pressure Turbine Inspection and Improved Shroud Cooling

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF6 Series

## AD/CF6/18 High Pressure Turbine Inspection and Improved Shroud Cooling

Applicability: All model CF6-80C2, A5, B6, B6 F and DIF.

Requirement: Carry out the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-05-21 Amdt 39-6900.

## 6/91

Compliance: As detailed in Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 13 June 1991.

Background: The AD introduces a borescope inspection program to detect high pressure turbine (HPT) stage 1 shroud and blade distress. Also, it requires installation of improved HPT hardware which increases the cooling to the HPT stage 1 shroud. The AD is prompted by one HPT failure which resulted in aircraft damage.

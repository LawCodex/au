# AD/CESSNA 550/14 - Indirect (Fluorescent) Lighting System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 550 (Citation II) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 550/14 Indirect (Fluorescent) Lighting System 6/88

Applicability: 1. All models 550 S/No 0561, 0562, 0564 through 0566, 0568 and 0569.

2\. All models S550 S/No 0140 through 0146, 0148, 0149.

Requirement: Pull and disable the indirect (fluorescent) lighting system circuit breaker CB275.

*Note 1: Cessna Alert Service Letters SLA 550-33-02, SLA S550-33-01 and FAA AD 88-06-02 refer.*

*Note 2: Compliance with SB 550-33-9 or SB S550-33-5 as applicable will constitute terminating action for this AD.*

Compliance: Before next flight following receipt of this AD.

Background: Cessna has determined that effected aircraft were wired with low voltage wiring instead of the required high voltage wiring. Wire replacement will be accomplished by incorporation of SB550-33-9 and SB S550-33-5 as applicable when issued.

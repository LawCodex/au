# AD/DH114/37 Amdt 1 - Main Undercarriage Damper Ram - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH114/37**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Undercarriage Damper Ram - Inspection NK**

Applicability: All Series 2 aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Check that the lower attachment bolt of the damper ram can be turned easily in situ using a spanner. If the bolt cannot be easily turned the ram shall be replaced with a serviceable unit before further flight.

*Note: Experience has shown that an unserviceable ram becomes internally pressurised and exerts sufficient end load to make bolt turning extremely difficult or impossible.*

2\. Replace existing damper rams P/N AC 11164 with units of same P/N modified in accordance with Dunlop SB No. 32-824 or new damper rams P/N A/C 65528.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - At each daily or overnight inspection until modified or replaced in accordance with Hawker TNS No. U.15, Issue 2.

For Para. 2 - Before 1 July 1974.

# AD/CESSNA 210/54 - Wing Main Spar Attachment Fittings - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 210/54 Wing Main Spar Attachment

**Fittings - Inspection**

Applicability: All model 210/T210 with S/No. 21058819 thru 21058968, T210-0198 thru T210-0338.

Document: Cessna S.I.L. No. SE 83-28

Compliance: For fittings that have achieved 4000 hours T.I.S., inspect at next 100 hourly inspection and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

## 1/84

Background: Cracking could occur in the wing attachment lugs, in particular the lower centre lugs.

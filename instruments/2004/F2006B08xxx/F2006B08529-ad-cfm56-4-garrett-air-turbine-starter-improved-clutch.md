# AD/CFM56/4 - Garrett Air Turbine Starter - Improved Clutch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
CFM International Turbine Engines - CFM56 Series

**AD/CFM56/4 Garrett Air Turbine Starter - Improved Clutch 8/94** Applicability: All CFM56-5A1 engines equipped with Garrett air turbine starter, P/N 3505582-1. Requirement: Incorporate CFM International Service Bulletin CFM56-5 No 80-003.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-099(B) also refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, at the first starter shop visit after 21 July 1994 but no later than 31 December 1994.

Background: This Directive has been issued to prevent starter failures and consequent heat damage to the electronic engine control (FADEC) harnesses.

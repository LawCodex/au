# AD/DC3/14 Reissue - Fuel Tank Filler Cap - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DC3/14

**Reissue**

Applicability: All models.

## Fuel Tank Filler Cap - Modification 1/84

Requirement: 1. Provide a water-tight cover over each of the four fuel tank filler cap wells.

The cover shall consist of a water-tight cap or lid, which may be opened to give unobstructed access to the fuel filler cap, hinged on a suitable mounting. The mounting shall make a water-tight seal with the wing skin.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours after 29 February 1984. Background: This Directive is reissued with no changes made to the requirement.

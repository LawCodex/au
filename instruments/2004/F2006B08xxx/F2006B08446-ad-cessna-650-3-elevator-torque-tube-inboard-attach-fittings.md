# AD/CESSNA 650/3 - Elevator Torque Tube Inboard Attach Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 650 (Citation III) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 650/3 Elevator Torque Tube Inboard**

**Attach Fittings**

Applicability: Model 650 S/n -0001 to -0219 and 7001 to -7013 inclusive.

Requirement: Inspect the inboard elevator torque tube fittings visually for cracking, and in accordance with Cessna Alert Service Letter SLA650-27-30.

*Note: FAA AD 92-27-08 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 January 1993.

**3/93**

**DM**

Background: There has been a report of cracking of the elevator inboard attach fitting and this Directive is issued to preclude potential loss of elevator control.

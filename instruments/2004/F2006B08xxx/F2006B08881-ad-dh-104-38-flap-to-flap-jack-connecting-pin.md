# AD/DH 104/38 - Flap To Flap Jack Connecting Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH 104/38 Flap To Flap Jack Connecting Pin 1/89 DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe TNS 246 Issue 1. Compliance: Within seven days time in service after 7 December 1988.

Background: Incorrectly manufactured pins have been found in service on DH 114 aircraft, one of which caused an in-flight assymetric flap condition. The same pins may have been fitted to DH 104 aircraft.

# AD/CON/68 - Incorrect Oil Pick-Up Tube

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Teledyne Continental Motors Piston Engines

AD/CON/68 Incorrect Oil Pick-Up Tube 1/94

Applicability: TCM O-470, IO-470, IO-520, TSIO-520 and IO-550 engines, as detailed by serial number in Teledyne Continental Motors Service Bulletin M91-10 Rev.1.

*Note: This Directive is only applicable to engines rebuilt or overhauled by the manufacturer Teledyne Continental Motors, Mobile Alabama USA, prior to November 1991.*

Requirement: Inspect the oil sump for evidence of an incorrect oil pick-up tube in accordance with the requirements of TCM Service Bulletin M91-10 Rev. 1. If an incorrect oil tube is installed, the oil tube and sump shall be replaced before further flight.

*Note: FAA Airworthiness Directive 93-08-17 refers.*

Compliance: Effective 6 January 1994:

Within 25 hours time in service.

2\. Prior to installation in an aircraft.

Background: This Directive requires an inspection of the oil sump for evidence of an incorrect oil pick-up tube being fitted during assembly. The requirement follows advice of a number of engines released by the manufacturer with incorrect oil pick-up tubes fitted.

# AD/DH 104/14 Amdt 3 - Wing/Fuselage Lower Main Root Joint - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DH 104/14**

**Amdt 3**

Applicability: All models.

**Wing/Fuselage Lower Main Root Joint - Inspection**

**9/71**

Requirement: Inspect the wing to fuselage lower main root joint for corrosion, fretting and cracking in accordance with Hawker Siddeley Technical News Sheet CT(104) No. 168 Issue 4.

Note: Magnetic particle inspection to para. 1.2 of the previous issue of this Directive, or fluorescent magnetic particle inspection in accordance with ANO 108.8 are acceptable alternatives to the eddy current technique specified in Hawker Siddeley TNS CT (104) No. 217 and Appendix 2 of TNS CT(104) No. 168 Issue 4.

Compliance: As detailed in TNS CT(104) No. 168 Issue 4.

# AD/CESSNA 400/70 - Elevator Horn Inboard Tip Rib - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 400 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 400/70 Elevator Horn Inboard Tip Rib - Modification

Applicability: All model 441 with S/Nos. 441-0001 to -0106 and 0109. Document: Cessna Propjet SIL No. PJ79-32.

## 4/80

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 25 hours time in service after 28 February 1980.

Background: Sealing of the inboard tip rib of the elevator horn is required to preclude foreign material from entering or exiting the elevator tip rib.

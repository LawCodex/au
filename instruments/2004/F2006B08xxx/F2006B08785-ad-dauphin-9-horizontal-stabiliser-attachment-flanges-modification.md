# AD/DAUPHIN/9 - Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Flanges - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

**AD/DAUPHIN/9 Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment**

**Flanges - Modification**

**1/83**

Applicability: All model SA365 helicopters which have been modified in accordance with Dauphin SB No. 55.04 (8 setting of lateral fins).

Document: Dauphin SB No. 55.05.

*Note: DGAC AD 82-136-14(B) refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

Background: Several cases of failure of the stabiliser attachment flanges have been reported in aircraft with the stabiliser setting.

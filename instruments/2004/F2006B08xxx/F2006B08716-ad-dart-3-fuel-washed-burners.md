# AD/DART/3 - Fuel Washed Burners

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Dart Series

AD/DART/3 Fuel Washed Burners 11/87

Applicability: All Dart RDa-6, RDa-7 series engines featuring Mod. 827 and/or Mod. 1224 fuel washed burners.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Rolls Royce SB Da 73-A54 Rev 3; or

2\. AD/RRT/10.

Effectivity: Forthwith.

Background: Reissue of AD/RRT/10.

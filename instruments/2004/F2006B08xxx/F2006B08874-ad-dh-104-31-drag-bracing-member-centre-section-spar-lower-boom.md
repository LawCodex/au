# AD/DH 104/31 - Drag Bracing Member, Centre Section Spar Lower Boom - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH 104 (Dove) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DH 104/31 Drag Bracing Member, Centre Section Spar

**Lower Boom - Inspection and Replacement**

Applicability: All models.

Document: Hawker Siddeley TNS CT(104) No. 230.

## 5/73

Compliance: 1. Inspect fittings in accordance with para. 4 of TNS CT(104) No. 230 within 100 hours time in service after 29 June 1973 or before 1 August 1973 whichever occurs first.

2\. Replace fittings in accordance with para. 5 of TNS CT(104) No. 230 before 1 July 1974.

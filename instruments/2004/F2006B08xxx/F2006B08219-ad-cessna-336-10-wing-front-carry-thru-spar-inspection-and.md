# AD/CESSNA 336/10 - Wing Front Carry Thru Spar Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 336 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 336/10 Wing Front Carry Thru Spar**

**Inspection and Modification**

**5/74**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 336-0001 to 336-0195 not modified in accordance with Service Kit SK 336-19A.

Document: Cessna SL ME74-4.

Compliance: For aircraft with more than 2975 hours total time in service as at 14 May 1974 - within 25 hours time in service after 14 May 1974, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

For all other aircraft - before exceeding 3000 hours total time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

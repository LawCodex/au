# AD/CF700/15 - Fan Guard Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CF700 Series

AD/CF700/15 Fan Guard Replacement 11/97

Applicability: General Electric CF700 series turbine engines.

Requirement: Replace existing fan guards in accordance with General Electric Service Bulletin number (CF700) 72-154.

*Note: FAA AD 97-13-10 also refers.*

Compliance: No later than 9 October 1999.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 9 October 1997.

Background: This directive is prompted by reports of uncontained fan blade failures following engine overspeed events.

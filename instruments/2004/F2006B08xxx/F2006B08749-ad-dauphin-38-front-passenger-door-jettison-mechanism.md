# AD/DAUPHIN/38 - Front Passenger Door Jettison Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/38 Front Passenger Door Jettison Mechanism 5/94

Applicability: Model SA 365 N helicopters, versions N, N1, and N2 which have not been modified per modification AMS 365A07-56B15, with serial numbers 6008, 6033, 6083, 6084,

6085, 6093, and helicopters with serial numbers higher than 6120; except 6282

through 6299 and those delivered after 25 October 1993.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter Mandatory SA 365 SB No.01.38.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-052-035(B) refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: A door jettison mechanism anomaly was found during production.

# AD/DC3/10 - Rear Luggage Compartment Tie Down Fittings - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DC3/10 Rear Luggage Compartment Tie NK

Down Fittings - Modification

Applicability: All models modified in accordance with DCA 1900 series conversion drawings.

Requirement: 1. Replace rear luggage compartment tie-down fittings of the type called up in Drawing DCA 2188, Issue 1 by the larger type as detailed in Issue 3; or

2\. ANOs Part 100 Appendix “A” Notice 7/10; or 3. ANO 105.1.16.1.5.10.

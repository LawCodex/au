# AD/CON/53 Amdt 1 - Starter Adaptor Shafts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Teledyne Continental Motors Piston Engines

**AD/CON/53**

**Amdt 1**

**Starter Adaptor Shafts 12/88**

Applicability: IO-520BA, IO-520BB, IO-550B, TSIO-520BE, TSIO-520LB, TSIO-520UB, TSIO-

520WB engines fitted with a starter adaptor shaft driving a freon compressor.

Requirement: Part 1 Action in accordance with TCM SB M87-24.

*Note: FAA AD 88-17-03 Amdt 39-5943 Para (a) refers.*

Part 2 Replace starter drive assy in accordance with (IAW) TCM SB M88-5 and drive belt IAW TCM SB M88-6.

*Note: FAA AD 88-17-03 Amdt 39-5943 Para (b) refers.*

Compliance: For Part 1 Before further flight after 1 February 1988.

For Part 2 Prior to refitment of compressor drive belt and reactivation of airconditioning system.

*Note: Compliance with CAO 106 AD/CON/50 does not constitute compliance with this Directive.*

Background: This Directive is amended to reflect and reference FAA AD 88-17-03 Amdt 39-5943 which supersedes FAA AD 87-26-01 Amdt 39-5882.

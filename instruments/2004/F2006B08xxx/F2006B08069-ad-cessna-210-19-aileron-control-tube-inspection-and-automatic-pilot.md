**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 210 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 210/19 Aileron Control Tube - Inspection and**

**Automatic Pilot Roll Servo - Removal**

**2/72**

Applicability: Models 210B, 210C, 210-5, 210-5A, 210-D and 210E fitted with Mitchell Automatic Pilot Models AK086, AK086R, AK086E, AK112, AK120, AK120E, AK120E-3,

AK126, AK126E or AK126E-3 not modified in accordance with Edo-Aire Mitchell SL No. ML-32.

2\. FAA AD 72-2-6.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 February 1972.

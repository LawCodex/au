# AD/DAUPHIN/42 - Fuel Filter Elements

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA 360 and SA 365 (Dauphin) Series Helicopters

AD/DAUPHIN/42 Fuel Filter Elements 11/94

Applicability: All model SA 365 C, C1, C2, C3, and AS 365 N, N1 and N2 helicopters. Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter AS 365 C mandatory SB No 28-04 or

Eurocopter SA 365 N mandatory SB No 28-22, as applicable.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-149-038(B) refers to C series helicopters while DGAC AD 94- 148-037(B) refers to N series helicopters.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service from 13 October 1994 or by 31 January 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that incorrect P/N airframe fuel filter elements have been supplied.

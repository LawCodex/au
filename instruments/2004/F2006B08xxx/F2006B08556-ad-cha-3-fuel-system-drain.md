# AD/CHA/3 - Fuel System Drain

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
American Champion (Aeronca, Bellanca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CHA/3 Fuel System Drain 10/71

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install an approved fuel drain cock at the lowest point of the fuel system plumbing. Compliance: Before 29 October 1971.

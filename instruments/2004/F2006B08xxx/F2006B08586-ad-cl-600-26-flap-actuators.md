# AD/CL-600/26 - Flap Actuators

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CL-600/26 Flap Actuators 10/93 DM

Applicability: Model CL-600-2A12 with serial numbers 3002 through 3066. Model CL-600-2B16 with serial numbers 5001 through 5131.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Canadair Challenger Alert Service Bulletin A601-0415 or Alert Wire TA601-0415-036.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-93-18 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 10 hours time in service after 11 August 1993 or prior to 11 September 1993, whichever occurs first.

Background: The recent failure of a flap actuator was found to be caused by the incorrect installation of the shoulder bolt which secures the flap actuator to the wing trailing edge.

# AD/DC3/28 Reissue - Hydraulic System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DC3/28

**Reissue**

Applicability: All models.

## Hydraulic System - Modification 1/84

Document: Douglas SB No 239.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1984.

Background: This Directive is reissued with no changes to the requirement. In the event of an engine failure during the take-off phase, this modification ensures the availability of hydraulic pressure to raise the undercarriage if required.

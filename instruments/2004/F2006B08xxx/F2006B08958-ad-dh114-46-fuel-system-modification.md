# AD/DH114/46 - Fuel System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DH114 (Heron) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DH114/46 Fuel System - Modification 6/85 TX

Applicability: DH114 model 2D/A1 aircraft which have been in modified in accordance with AVDEV Airlines drawing No AA17.

Requirement: 1. Isolate outboard tank system in accordance with East Coast Airlines E.O. Sydney Q2/84 issue 2, or

2\. Modify fuel system in a manner acceptable to the Secretary.

3\. Approval of AVDEV Airlines Drawing No AA17 is withdrawn. Compliance: Before further flight.

Background: The modified fuel system has been found not to comply with BCAR section D5-2.

# AD/CL-600/7 - Horizontal Stabiliser Pitch Trim Actuator - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Canadair) CL-600 (Challenger) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CL-600/7 Horizontal Stabiliser Pitch Trim Actuator - Modification

Applicability: Pitch trim actuators as listed in requirement document. Document: Canadair Challenger Alert SB No. A600-0084.

*Note: Canadian Dept. of Transport AD CF-82-05 refers.*

Compliance: Before issue of Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

## 3/83

Background: Pitch trim actuators are suspected of having possible loose motor drive gear pinions.

This could result in damage to internal control wiring.

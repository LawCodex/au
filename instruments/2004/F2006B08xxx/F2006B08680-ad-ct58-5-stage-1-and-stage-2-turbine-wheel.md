# AD/CT58/5 - Stage 1 and Stage 2 Turbine Wheel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
General Electric Turbine Engines - CT58 Series

AD/CT58/5 Stage 1 and Stage 2 Turbine Wheel 9/85

Applicability: All CT58 engine fitted with Stage 1 and 2 turbine wheels listed in SB (CT58) 72-126, Rev. 1.

Document: General installation in an aircraft following next complete overhaul after 23 March 1970; or

AD/GE-T/14A.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft following next complete overhaul after 23 March 1970.

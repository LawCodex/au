# AD/DC3/8 Reissue - Fire Extinguisher Release Cable Conduit - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DC3/8**

**Reissue**

Applicability: All models.

**Fire Extinguisher Release Cable Conduit - Replacement**

**1/84**

Requirement: Replace aluminium fire extinguisher control conduit with stainless steel tubing and nickel steel fittings.

*Note: Douglas SB DC3 No. 246 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 29 February 1984.

Background: This Directive is reissued with no changes made to the requirement.

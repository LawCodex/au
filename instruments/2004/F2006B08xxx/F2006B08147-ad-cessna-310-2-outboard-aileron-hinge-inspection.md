# AD/CESSNA 310/2 - Outboard Aileron Hinge - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 310 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 310/2 Outboard Aileron Hinge - Inspection NK

Applicability: All model 310 prior to S/No. 35392 except those incorporating hinges P/N 0824006-1 and -2.

Document: 1. Cessna SL No. 310-7; or

2\. FAA AD 57-4-1.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

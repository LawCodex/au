# AD/DC3/4 Reissue - Engine Section Throttle Control Rod - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Douglas DC3 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DC3/4**

**Reissue**

**Engine Section Throttle Control Rod - Replacement**

**1/84**

Applicability: DC3/C47 aircraft - All models fitted with Pratt and Whitney engines. Document: Douglas SB (DC3) No. 219 Section V; or

\* CAA (US) AD 44-7-3; or

\* ANO 105.1.16.1.5.4.

\* Included for reference purposes as these documents are no longer a requirement.

Compliance: Before the issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: This Directive is re-issued to cover aircraft that are to be added the Register and aircraft requiring Certificate of Airworthiness renewal.

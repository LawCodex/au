# Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information - Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2004

Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information—Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2004

made under subregulation 64(7) of the

Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001

**Compilation No. 4**

**Compilation date:** 13 March 2024

**Includes amendments:** F2024L00293

**Registered:** 26 July 2024

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information—Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2004* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 13 March 2024 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au). The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. For more information on any uncommenced amendments, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Application, saving and transitional provisions for provisions and amendments**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Editorial changes**

For more information about any editorial changes made in this compilation, see the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. For more information on any modifications, see the Register for the compiled law.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

Contents

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information—Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Determination 2004*.

###### 3 Definitions

(1) In this Determination:

***growth phase table*** means the table in clause 2 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Methods and Factors Approval.

***Methods and Factors Approval*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Methods and Factors for Valuing Particular Superannuation Interests) Approval 2003*.

***payment phase table*** means the table in clause 3 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Methods and Factors Approval.

***PSS Scheme*** means the Public Sector Superannuation Scheme mentioned in section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990*.

***Regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

***Rules*** has the meaning given by section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990*.

(2) An expression used in this Determination and in rule 1.2.1 of the Rules has the same meaning in this Determination as it has in that rule.

(3) For this Determination, a reference to ‘the relevant date’ in the definition of a factor in the Methods and Factors Approval mentioned in column 4 of an item of the table in section 5 or 6 is to be read as if it were a reference to ‘the appropriate date’.

###### 4 Application of Determination

This Determination applies in relation to a superannuation interest in the PSS Scheme for which a method and factors have been approved under Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Methods and Factors Approval.

###### 6 Provision of information by trustee—payment phase interests

In relation to a superannuation interest mentioned in an item in the following table:

(a) for paragraph 64(7B)(a) of the Regulations, the trustee of the PSS Scheme is not required to provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 3 of the item; and

(b) for paragraph 64(7B)(b) of the Regulations, the trustee of the PSS Scheme must provide the information about the interest mentioned in column 4 of the item.

| Item | Superannuation interest | Information not required to be provided | Information that must be provided |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An interest mentioned in item 1 of the payment phase table | The information mentioned in paragraph 64(3)(b) of the Regulations | The information mentioned in the definition of P in item 1 of the payment phase table<br>The following information in relation to the person who has the interest:<br>(a) the person’s date of birth;<br>(b) the type of pension payable to the person;<br>(c) if the pension is not an associate standard pension, an associate preserved pension or a pension that became payable on the person’s invalidity retirement before age 60—whether or not the person has chosen a Reduced Pension under rule 9.2.1 of the Rules; |
|  |  |  | (d) if the pension is an associate standard pension—whether the pension is a normal associate standard pension or an invalidity associate standard pension;<br>(e) if the pension is an associate preserved pension—whether the pension is a normal associate preserved pension or an invalidity associate preserved pension;<br>(f) the annual amount, if any, of the person’s pension that is payable, in accordance with the Rules, in respect of 1 or more eligible children or partially dependent children |

###### 7 Application of amendments made by the Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Amendment Determination 2024

The amendments of the table in section 5 made by the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Provision of Information — Public Sector Superannuation Scheme) Amendment Determination 2024* apply in relation to applications, to the trustee of the PSS Scheme for information about a superannuation interest, that:

(a) were made before the time that instrument commences, if at that time, the trustee has not yet provided the requested information; or

(b) are made on or after that time.

Amending laws are annotated in the legislation history and amendment history.

**Editorial changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* authorises First Parliamentary Counsel to make editorial and presentational changes to a compiled law in preparing a compilation of the law for registration. The changes must not change the effect of the law. Editorial changes take effect from the compilation registration date.

If the compilation includes editorial changes, the endnotes include a brief outline of the changes in general terms. Full details of any changes can be obtained from the Office of Parliamentary Counsel.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

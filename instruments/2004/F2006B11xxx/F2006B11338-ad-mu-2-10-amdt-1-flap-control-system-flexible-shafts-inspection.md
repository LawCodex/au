# AD/MU-2/10 Amdt 1 - Flap Control System Flexible Shafts - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MU-2/10**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Control System Flexible Shafts - Inspection 12/84**

Applicability: All models MU-2B aircraft referred to in the requirement document. Document: Mitsubishi MU-2 S.B. No. 162 Rev. A.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1975.

Background: This directive is amended to clarify the aircraft applicability and amend the revision status of the requirement document.

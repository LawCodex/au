# AD/M20/25 - Dukes Electric Landing Gear Actuator - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/25 Dukes Electric Landing Gear Actuator - Inspection 11/76

Applicability: All model M20 series fitted with Dukes P/N 4196-00-1C electric landing gear actuator.

Document: Mooney SB No. 190, Part 1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1976 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

# AD/ML-M4/17 - Gascolator and Electric Fuel Pump - Proximity to Dual Exhaust System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Maule M-4 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/ML-M4/17 Gascolator and Electric Fuel Pump - Proximity**

**to Dual Exhaust System**

**9/96**

Applicability: All Maule M-4-210 S/N 1001 to 1045 and M-4-210C S/N 1001C to 1080C aircraft with dual exhaust system 5230F installed.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Maule Aerospace Technology, Inc. SB No 10 dated 9/16/94 (16 September 1994).

*Note: FAA AD 96-10-05 Amdt 39-9610 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service from 15 August 1996 or by 31 December 1996, whichever occurs first.

Background: When dual exhaust system 5230F has been installed the proximity of the L/H exhaust pipe to the gascolator is too close requiring repositioning.

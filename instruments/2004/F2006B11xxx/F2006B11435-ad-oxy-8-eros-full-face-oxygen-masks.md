# AD/OXY/8 - Eros Full Face Oxygen Masks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Oxygen Systems

AD/OXY/8 Eros Full Face Oxygen Masks 5/92

Applicability: EROS full face oxygen masks with the following P/N:

| MF 10-02-01 | MF 10-02-02 | MF 10-02-03 | MF 10-02-04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| MF 10-02-05 | MF 10-03-01 | MF 10-03-02 | MF 10-03-03 |
| MF 10-03-04 | MF 10-03-05 | MF 10-04-01 | MF 10-05-01 |
| MF 10-05-04 | MF 10-08-01 | MF 10-30-01 | MF 10-30-02 |
| MF 10-30-03 | MF 10-50-01 |  |  |

Requirement: 1. Inspect in accordance with EROS Service Bulletin MF 10-35-43 (Revision 1).

2\. Modify in accordance with EROS Service Bulletin MF 10-35-46 (modification 45416) or Service Bulletin MF 10-35-44 (modification 45415).

*Note: DGAC AD 91-175(AB)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 May 1992 for Requirement 1.

Prior to 31 January 1993 for Requirement 2.

Background: This inspection/modification is required to ensure the integrity of the oxygen mask.

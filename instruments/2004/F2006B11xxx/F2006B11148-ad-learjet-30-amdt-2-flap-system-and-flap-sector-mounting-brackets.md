# AD/LEARJET/30 Amdt 2 - Flap System and Flap Sector Mounting Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/30**

**Amdt 2**

**Flap System and Flap Sector Mounting Brackets**

**1/87**

Applicability: All model Learjet with S/Nos. listed in requirement documents. Requirement: Modify in accordance with Gates Learjet SB AMK 86-4.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service or at the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 January 1987, whichever is later.

Background: This Directive was originally issued due to cracking in the flap sector mounting brackets. This amendment replaces the aluminium brackets with steel items and terminates any further requirement.

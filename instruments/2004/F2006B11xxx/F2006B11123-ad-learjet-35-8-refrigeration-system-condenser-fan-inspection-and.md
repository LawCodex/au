# AD/LEARJET 35/8 - Refrigeration System Condenser Fan - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET 35/8 Refrigeration System Condenser**

**Fan - Inspection and Replacement**

**9/78**

Applicability: Model 35 with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-194. Model 36 with S/Nos. 36-001 to 36-040. And any 35/36 Series Learjet which has had a Condenser Fan changed since 1 July 1977.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the P/No. rubber-stamped on a blade of the refrigeration condenser fan.

2\. If the fan bears the P/No. 6608059-3, attach a placard worded as follows to the instrument panel adjacent to the refrigeration system control switch:

WARNING

DO NOT OPERATE REFRIGERATION SYSTEM UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES ON THE GROUND OR IN THE AIR UNTIL FAN HAS BEEN REPLACED.

*Note: The correct fans have blades which taper in thickness towards the tip and bear the P/Nos. 6608059-2 or 6608059-4.*

or Gates Learjet SB 35/36-21-3.

Compliance: Before further flight.

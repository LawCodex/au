# AD/P166/19 - Carburettor Air Mixer Valve - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piaggio P166 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P166/19 Carburettor Air Mixer Valve - Inspection**

**and Modification**

**7/62**

Applicability: Aeroplanes equipped with carburettor air mixer valve having a cylinder shaped valve body.

Document: Piaggio SB P166-19. Compliance: Modification - Forthwith.

Inspection - At intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

# AD/MU-2/51 - Autopilot Engage Switch Guard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/51 Autopilot Engage Switch Guard 3/92

Applicability: All Models MU-2 fitted with Bendix M4C or M4D autopilots. Requirement: Action in accordance with MHI MU-2 Service Bulletin 076/22-008.

*Note: JCAB TCD-3464-91 also refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 6 March 1992. Background: To preclude the unintentional engagement of the autopilot.

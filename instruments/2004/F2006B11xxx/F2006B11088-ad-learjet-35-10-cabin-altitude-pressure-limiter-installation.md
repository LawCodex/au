**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/10 Cabin Altitude Pressure Limiter - Installation 1/79

Applicability: Model 35 with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-112 except S/No. 35-107;

Model 36 with S/Nos. 36-001 to 36-031.

Document: Learjet Airplane Mod. Kit AMK 78-5.

Compliance: Within 600 hours time in service after 31 January 1979.

# AD/JT9D/30 - First Stage High Pressure Turbine Disk

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/30 First Stage High Pressure Turbine Disk 6/98

Applicability: Pratt and Whitney JT9D series engines incorporating high pressure turbine disks specified in FAA AD 97-25-10.

Requirement: Inspect affected high pressure turbine disks in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 97-25-10.

*Note: FAA AD 97-25-10 supersedes FAA AD 91-04-10.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, as detailed in the Requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 21 May 1998.

Background: This Directive is prompted by reports of cracks in certain high pressure turbine disks at cooling hole and tie bolt hole locations.

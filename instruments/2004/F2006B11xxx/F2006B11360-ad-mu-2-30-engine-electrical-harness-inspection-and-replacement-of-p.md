# AD/MU-2/30 - Engine Electrical Harness - Inspection and Replacement of P/No MS25471 Electrical Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/MU-2/30 Engine Electrical Harness - Inspection and Replacement of P/No MS25471 Electrical Cable

Applicability: MU-2B-10/-15/-20/-25/-26 S/Nos 005 to 347 (except S/Nos 313 and 321).

MU-2B-30/-35/-36 S/Nos 501 to 696 (except S/Nos 652 and 661).

Document: Mitsubishi Heavy Industries SB No. 191.

Inspect engine electrical harness in accordance with SB No. 191.

## 1/84

2\. Replace unacceptable electrical wiring Part No. MS 25471 in accordance with SB No. 191.

*Note: JCAB AD No. TCD 2113-82 refers.*

Compliance: Part 1 - Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1984 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service if required.

Part 2 - Within 300 hours time in service after 31 January 1984.

Background: The conducting portion of the electrical cable has been found protruding through the insulation.

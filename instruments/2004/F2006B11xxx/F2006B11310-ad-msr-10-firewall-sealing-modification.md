# AD/MSR/10 - Firewall Sealing - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/10 Firewall Sealing - Modification 10/70

Applicability: All models 880B, 885, 893A and 894A.

Requirement: 1. Replace the existing firewall sealant with a fireproof sealant; or 2. FAA AD 70-17-6.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 October 1970.

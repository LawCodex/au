# AD/LEARJET 35/17 - Cabin Interior Liner in Areas of Emergency Exit Door - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET 35/17 Cabin Interior Liner in Areas of**

**Emergency Exit Door - Inspection**

**2/81**

Applicability: 35 and 36 series aircraft with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-332, 35-334, 35-335, 35-336, 35-

339, 35-340; and 36-001 to 36-045.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 35/36-52-5 dated 4 November 1980. Compliance: At next periodic inspection after 30 January 1981.

Background: This Directive is issued to ensure cabin interior liner does not interfere with emergency exit door operation.

# AD/LEARJET/12 - Control System Stop Bolts - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/12 Control System Stop Bolts - Replacement 9/75

Applicability: All model 24 with S/Nos. 24-100 to 24-304 and 24-309 to 24-322, and all model 25

with S/Nos. 25-003 to 25-197. Document: Gates Learjet SB 23/24/25-266.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 31 October 1975.

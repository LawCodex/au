# AD/OPTICA/2 - Engine Pod Mounting Feet

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
FLS Aerospace OA7 Optica Series Aeroplanes

AD/OPTICA/2 Engine Pod Mounting Feet 7/94

Applicability: Model OA7 Optica aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with FLS Aerospace Service Bulletin OA7/SB/0040.

*Note: UK CAA AD 010-03-94 refers.*

Compliance: At the next 50 hour inspection after 23 June 1994, and thereafter at every subsequent 50 hour inspection.

Background: The UK CAA requires a periodic inspection of the engine pod mounting feet, and replacement of any mounting foot found to contain a crack.

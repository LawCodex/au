# AD/JT8D-200/3 - Low Pressure Turbine Anti-Rotation Pins

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT8D-200 Series

AD/JT8D-200/3 Low Pressure Turbine Anti-Rotation Pins 10/92

Applicability: JT8D-209, -217, -217A, -217C and -219 turbofan engines.

Requirement: As detailed in the technical requirements of FAA Airworthiness Directive 88-04-02. Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement document, with an effective date of 17 September

1992\.

Background: This Directive requires inspection, replacement and modification of the low pressure turbine anti-rotation pins.

# AD/LEARJET/34 - Flap Attachment Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/34 Flap Attachment Fittings 9/86

Applicability: All model Learjet 24 & 25 with Serial Numbers. 24-328 to 24-357, 25-135, 25-188

and 25-205 to 25-367.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Gates Learjet S.B. 24/25-333. Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service after 30 September 1986.

Background: Cracking of the flap attachment fitting has become evident in service. Failure of a flap attach fitting may result in an asymmetric flap condition.

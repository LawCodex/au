# AD/LEARJET 35/23 Amdt 2 - Flap System and Flap Sector Mounting Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET 35/23**

**Amdt 2**

**Flap System and Flap Sector Mounting Brackets**

**1/87**

Applicability: All models Learjet with serial numbers listed in Requirement Document. Requirement: Modify in accordance with Gates Learjet SB AMK 86-4.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service or at the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 January 1987 whichever is later.

Background: This Directive was initially issued due to cracking of the flap sector mounting brackets. A repetitive inspection was later included because of further cracking after inspection. This amendment now requires modification using steel sector mounting brackets and deletes the repetitive inspection.

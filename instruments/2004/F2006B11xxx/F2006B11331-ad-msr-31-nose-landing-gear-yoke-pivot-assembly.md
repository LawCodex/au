# AD/MSR/31 - Nose Landing Gear - Yoke Pivot Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/31 Nose Landing Gear - Yoke Pivot Assembly 5/95

Applicability: All models, all serial numbers Morane Saulnier and Rallye aeroplanes except Rallye 235F model.

Requirement: Inspect the yoke pivot assembly for cracks using dye penetrant.

Inspect in accordance with Socata Service Bulletin 151.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-143(A) refers.*

Compliance: 1. Inspect in accordance with Requirement within 50 hours time in service from 27 April 1995.

Background: Reports have been received that fatigue cracks could form in the nose landing gear yoke pivot assembly due to a manufacturing defect.

This AD requires a once only inspection for cracks.

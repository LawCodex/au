# AD/LEARJET/28 - Automatic Flight Control Systems - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/LEARJET/28 Automatic Flight Control Systems - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All models 25, 25A with S/Nos. 25-003 thru 25-066 except 25-061.

All models 25B, 25C with S/Nos. 25-061, 25-067 thru 25-205.

## 4/83

Requirement: 1. Modify the flight control systems in accordance with the following modification kits:

MODEL MODIFICATION KITS

25B and 25C AMK 81-9, \*AMK 81-8 and AMK 80-13 Change 3.

25 and 25A AMK 81-10 Change 1, \*AMK 81-8 and AMK 80-13 Change 3.

\* Kit AMK 81-8 does not apply unless Kit AAK 76-4 has been installed.

2\. Inspect the stall warning accelerometer in accordance with Gates Learjet Service Bulletin 23, 24, 25-301B.

*Note. FAA AD 82-01-04 Amendment 39-4296 refers.*

Compliance: On or before 30 April 1984.

Background: This action is necessary to reduce the hazard created by a possible malfunction which if not detected could result in a hazardous flight attitude.

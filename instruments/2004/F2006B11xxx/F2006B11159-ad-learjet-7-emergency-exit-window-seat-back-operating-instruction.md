# AD/LEARJET/7 - Emergency Exit Window, Seat-Back Operating Instruction Placard - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/7 Emergency Exit Window, Seat-Back Operating**

**Instruction Placard - Installation**

**3/75**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install a placard above the cabin emergency exit window, immediately below the existing window operating instruction placard, as follows:

“PUSH SEAT-BACK FORWARD TO CLEAR EXIT”.

*Note: Lettering shall be a minimum of 3/16" high in red on a white background.*

Compliance: Before 30 June 1975.

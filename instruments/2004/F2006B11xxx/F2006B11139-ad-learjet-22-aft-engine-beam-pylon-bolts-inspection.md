# AD/LEARJET/22 - Aft Engine Beam Pylon Bolts - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/22 Aft Engine Beam Pylon Bolts - Inspection 5/80

Applicability: All model 23 S/Nos. 23-003 to 23-099;

All model 24 S/Nos. 24-100 to 24-357;

All model 25 S/Nos. 25-003 to 25-286 and 25-288.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 23/24/25-299. Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: To ensure that the aft engine beam pylon bolts are secure and properly torqued.

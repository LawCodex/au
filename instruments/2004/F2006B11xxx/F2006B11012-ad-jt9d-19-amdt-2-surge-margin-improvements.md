# AD/JT9D/19 Amdt 2 - Surge Margin Improvements

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

**AD/JT9D/19**

**Amdt 2**

**Surge Margin Improvements 3/93**

Applicability: JT9D-7R4D, E series engines installed in Boeing B767 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with FAA Airworthiness Directive 90-14-08, paragraphs (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f), (g), (h) and (i).

For the purpose of this Directive, compliance with the requirements of PWA SB JT9D-7R4-75-101 is deemed to be an alternative method of compliance with the requirements of FAA AD 90-14-08, paragraph (d), (2).

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document, with an effective date of 4 March 1993.

Background: This Directive has been amended to reflect the release of an additional procedure which, if incorporated, will improve the JT9D-7R4 surge margin.

# AD/LEARJET 35/6 - Wing to Fuselage Attachment - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/6 Wing to Fuselage Attachment - Modification 9/77

Applicability: All models 35 with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-110 except 35-107;

All models 36 with S/Nos. 36-001 to 36-031.

Requirement: Modify the wing to fuselage attachment to accordance with Gates Learjet Kit No.

AMK 77-6.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 30 September 1977.

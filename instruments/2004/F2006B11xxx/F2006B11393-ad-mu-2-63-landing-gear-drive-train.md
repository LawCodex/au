# AD/MU-2/63 - Landing Gear Drive Train

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

[image]11/98

Requirement: 1. Inspect the torque shaft (part number 030A-38605-21) for correct spline connection in accordance with paragraph 4 of Mitsubishi Heavy Industry MU-2 Service Bulletin (SB) 235, dated 4 August 1998.

2\. If the distance measured in the Requirement 1 inspection is less than 12mm, before further flight, replace or modify the torque shaft assembly in accordance with paragraph 5 of SB 235.

*Note: JCAB AD TCD-4838-98 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 8 October 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 8 October 1998.

Background: This Directive is to prevent disconnection within the landing gear drive train, which could result in a situation with the nose gear extended and the main gear retracted.

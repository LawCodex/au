# AD/L.40/9 Amdt 1 - Wing Structural Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Meta Sokol L.40 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/L.40/9**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Structural Life Limitation 2/73**

Applicability: All models with wing main spar lower caps exceeding 4000 hours time in service.

Requirement: Retire from service the centre section and outer wing main spar lower caps, including the centre section to outer wing attachment fittings.

Compliance: 31 March 1975.

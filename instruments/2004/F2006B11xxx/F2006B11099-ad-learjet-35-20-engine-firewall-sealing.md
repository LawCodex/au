# AD/LEARJET 35/20 - Engine Firewall Sealing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/20 Engine Firewall Sealing 8/82

Applicability: Learjet 35-001 to 35-482; Learjet 36-001 to 36-050. Document: Gates Learjet Service Bulletin SB35/36-71-2 refers. Compliance: Within 150 hours after 31 August 1982.

Background: Manufacturers requirement to improve fireproofing at the engine pylon firewall.

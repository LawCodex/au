**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

## AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE

[image]

For the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive AD/LEARJET 35/30.

[image]

## AD/LEARJET 35/30 Flight Management Systems 6/2000

Requirement: CANCELLED.

Background: This Directive was based on United States Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) AD 95-19-04. The FAA AD was rescinded with effect 27 March 2000, following the shut down of the Omega navigation system.

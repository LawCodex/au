# AD/LEARJET 35/25 - Flap Attachment Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/25 Flap Attachment Fittings 9/86

Applicability: All model Learjet 35 and 36 with Serial Numbers listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Gates Learjet 35 S.B. 35/36-27-16. Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service after 30 September 1986.

Background: Cracking of the flap attachment fitting has become evident in service. Failure of a flap attach fitting may result in an asymmetric flap condition.

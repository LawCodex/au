# AD/MU-2/35 - Horizontal Stabiliser Leading Edge Skin - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/35 Horizontal Stabiliser Leading Edge Skin - Inspection and Modification

5/85

Applicability: All models referenced in Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Service Recommendation No 062A.

Requirement: Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Service Recommendation No. 062A.

Compliance: Prior to issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 May 1985 and thereafter at each issue of a new Maintenance Release until modified.

Background: This Directive is issued to ensure cracked horizontal stabiliser leading edge skins are replaced and reinforced in accordance with the manufacturers repair scheme.

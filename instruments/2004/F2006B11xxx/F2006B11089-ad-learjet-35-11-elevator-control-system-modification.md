# AD/LEARJET 35/11 - Elevator Control System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/11 Elevator Control System - Modification 1/79

Applicability: All model 35 with S/Nos. 35-002 to 35-041 and all model 36 with S/Nos. 36-002 to 36-016, unless modified in accordance with Kit Nos. AMK 75-10 or AMK 75-10A.

Requirement: To provide for increased reliability of the elevator control system: Modify the aeroplane by installing Kit No. AMK 75-10A.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 31 January 1979.

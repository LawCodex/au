# AD/M20/44 - Baggage Door Inside Latch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/44 Baggage Door Inside Latch 2/89 DM

Applicability: M20J S/No 24-0084, 24-0378 to 24-1645 and 24-3000 to 24-3056 M20K S/No 25-

0001 to 25-1160.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mooney Aircraft Corporation SBM20-239.

*Note: FAA AD 88-25-11 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 11 January 1989.

Background: There have been reports overseas of in-flight opening of the baggage door, one of which involved fatalities.

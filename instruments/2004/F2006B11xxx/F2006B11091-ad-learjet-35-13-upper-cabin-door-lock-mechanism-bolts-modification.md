# AD/LEARJET 35/13 - Upper Cabin Door Lock Mechanism Bolts - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/LEARJET 35/13 Upper Cabin Door Lock Mechanism Bolts - Modification

Applicability: All model 35 with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-274 and 278;

All model 36 with S/Nos. 36-001 to 36-044.

## 4/80

Requirement: Modify the upper cabin door lock mechanism bolts by installing Gates Learjet Kit No. AMK 79-9.

Compliance: Within 150 hours time in service after 30 April 1980.

Background: Modification kit AMK 79-9 provides an additional safety feature to the upper cabin door lock mechanism by installing drilled bolts and lockwiring these bolts in-situ.

# AD/MU-2/46 - Anti-Ice and De-Ice Systems

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/46 Anti-Ice and De-Ice Systems 5/90

Applicability: Model MU2.

Requirement: Carry out an operation check of the airframe and engine anti-ice and de-ice systems in accordance with Chapter 9 of the MU2 Maintenance Manual.

Compliance: At each 100 hour maintenance interval.

Background: A recent accident investigation revealed that ice accumulation may have been a contributing factor. A surveillance of the M2 fleet revealed an unacceptably high incidence of anti-ice and de-ice systems unserviceability.

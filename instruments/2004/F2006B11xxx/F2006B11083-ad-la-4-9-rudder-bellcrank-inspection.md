# AD/LA-4/9 - Rudder Bellcrank - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Consolidated Aeronautics, Colonial and LA-4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LA-4/9 Rudder Bellcrank - Inspection 3/74

Applicability: All models.

Document: Consolidated Aeronautics SL L-50.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1974, and there- after at intervals not exceeding 1 year or at anytime rudder pedals are subjected to abnormal loads.

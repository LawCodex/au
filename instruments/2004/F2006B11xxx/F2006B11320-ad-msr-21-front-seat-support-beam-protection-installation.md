# AD/MSR/21 - Front Seat Support Beam Protection - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/21 Front Seat Support Beam Protection - Installation 7/74

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install a front seat support beam protection cover in accordance with a scheme approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 August 1974.

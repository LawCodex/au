# AD/LYC/104 - Crankshaft Drive Gear Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Lycoming Piston Engines

AD/LYC/104 Crankshaft Drive Gear Bolts 12/99

Applicability: Textron Lycoming Model O-540-F1B5 engines as detailed by serial number in FAA Airworthiness Directive 99-03-05 and installed in, but not limited to, Robinson R44 helicopters.

*Note: Copies of FAA AD 99-03-05 are available from CASA. CASA contact position is the Airworthiness Directive Publishing Controller. Contact number is 131 757.*

Requirement: Replace crankshaft drive gear bolt part number (P/N) STD-2209. Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service from the effective date of this directive.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 2 December 1999.

Background: This directive has been issued to prevent failure of the crankshaft bolt resulting in an engine failure. The requirement follows advice of a manufacturing error in bolts supplied to the Robinson Helicopter Company.

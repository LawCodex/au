# AD/ML-M4/7 - Aileron Control System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Maule M-4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/ML-M4/7 Aileron Control System - Modification 10/69

Applicability: All models.

Requriement: Remove the bolt attaching the most forward aileron control system pulley to the control column.

Replace the pulley assembly with the addition of washers AN970-5 and AN960-516, and bolt AN5-27 instead of the original bolt in the following order from front to rear:

AN5-27 Bolt - head forward.

2\. AN970-5 Washer

3\. AN960-516 Washer

4\. Original Pulley

5\. Control Column

6\. AN960-516 Washer 7. AN365-524 Nut.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 1 October 1969.

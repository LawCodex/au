# AD/M20/42 - Autopilot Servo Cable Guards

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/42 Autopilot Servo Cable Guards 2/86 DM

Applicability: All model M20J and M20K with KAP 100/150/KFC 150 autopilot to STC SA 1561CE-D or KFC 200 autopilot to STC SA 1771CE-D.

Requirement: King SB KS 177/178-3.

*Note: FAA AD 85-25-02 also refers.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 16 January 1986.

Background: An installation problem has been identified which can result in jamming of primary pitch and/or roll control. This condition has occurred in service.

# AD/JT9D/21 - Drain Tube Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/21 Drain Tube Replacement 5/90

Applicability: JT9D-7R4 engines fitted to Boeing 767 aircraft.

Requirement: Replace three existing drain tubes and a tee fitting as detailed in Boeing Service Bulletin 767-71A0057.

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1990.

Background: This Directive requires replacement of certain drain lines and a tee fitting to prevent possible oil loss due to chafing of the number three oil pressure line against a drain tube tee fitting.

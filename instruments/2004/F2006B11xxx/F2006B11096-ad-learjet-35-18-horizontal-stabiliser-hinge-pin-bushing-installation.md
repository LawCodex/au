# AD/LEARJET 35/18 - Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Pin Bushing Installation - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET 35/18 Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Pin Bushing**

**Installation - Inspection**

**12/81**

Applicability: All models 35 & 36 with S/Nos. referenced in Gates Learjet Service Bulletin No.

35/36-27-13 or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues of that Service Bulletin.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 35/36-27-13.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December, 1981.

Background: The horizontal stabiliser hinge pin bushings may move out of their mating fittings during normal service.

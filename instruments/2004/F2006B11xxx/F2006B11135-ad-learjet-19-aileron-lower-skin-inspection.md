# AD/LEARJET/19 - Aileron Lower Skin - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/19 Aileron Lower Skin - Inspection 2/80

Applicability: All model 23 with S/Nos. 23-003 to 23-099;

All model 24 with S/Nos. 24-100 to 24-357;

All model 25 with S/Nos. 25-003 to 24-291.

Document: Gates Learjet SB No. 23/24/25-295.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1980.

Background: The drain holes in the aileron lower skin may not be correctly installed or serviceable.

# AD/LEARJET/21 - Aft Engine Beam - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/21 Aft Engine Beam - Inspection 5/80

Applicability: All models 25 with S/Nos. 25-266 to 25-300. Document: Gates Learjet SB 25-298.

Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: To inspect the aft engine beam forward web lightening hole flanges for cracks.

# AD/M20/38 - Fuselage Tubular Structure - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/38 Fuselage Tubular Structure - Modification 2/80

Applicability: All model M20K with S/Nos. 25-0001 to 25-0247. Document: Mooney SB No. M20-220.

Compliance: Before 1 July 1980.

Background: The manufacturer advises of the possibility of the lower fuselage longeron buckling under severe positive gust conditions.

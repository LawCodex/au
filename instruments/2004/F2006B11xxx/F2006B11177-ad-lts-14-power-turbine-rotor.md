# AD/LTS/14 - Power Turbine Rotor

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Lycoming) Turbine Engines - LTS 101 Series

AD/LTS/14 Power Turbine Rotor 10/96

Applicability: All LTS101 series engines, other than the LTS101-750B-2 model.

Requirement: To prevent power turbine rotor failure resulting in the loss of engine power, identify the serial number of the power turbine rotor assembly installed in applicable engines and remove from service rotor assemblies as listed by serial number in Textron Lycoming Service Bulletin Number LT 101-72-50-0144.

*Note: FAA Airworthiness Directive 96-12-05 refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document with an effective date of 12 September 1996.

Background: This directive has been issued to require the removal from service of certain power turbine rotor assemblies. The requirement reflects advice from the manufacturer of workmanship deficiencies in certain power turbine discs that could result in a reduction of the fatigue life of those discs.

# AD/JT9D/26 - Diffuser Case Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/26 Diffuser Case Inspection 9/94

Applicability: Pratt and Whitney JT9D-7F engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of FAA AD 93-19-02. Compliance: Effective 18 August 1994, as specified in the FAA AD.

Background: There have been reports of cracking in the diffuser case at the rear rail location.

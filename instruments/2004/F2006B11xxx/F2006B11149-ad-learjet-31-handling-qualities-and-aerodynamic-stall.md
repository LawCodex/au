# AD/LEARJET/31 - Handling Qualities and Aerodynamic Stall Characteristics - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/LEARJET/31 Handling Qualities and Aerodynamic Stall

**Characteristics - Modification**

Applicability: All model 23 with S/Nos 23-003 to 23-099.

All model 24 with S/Nos 24-100 and subsequent. All model 25 with S/Nos 25-003 to 25-061.

25-067 to 25-201.

25-204 and subsequent.

## 7/85

With unmodified wings.

Requirement: 1. Install modification kit AMK 83-4, or

2\. Install modification kit AMK 84-5.

*Note: FAA AD No 80-19-11 R1 refers.*

Compliance: Before 1st October, 1986.

Background: GLC and the FAA have recently found evidence of undesirable handling characteristics at the limits of the flight envelope.

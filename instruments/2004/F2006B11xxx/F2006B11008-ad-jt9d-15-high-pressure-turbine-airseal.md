# AD/JT9D/15 - High Pressure Turbine Airseal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/15 High Pressure Turbine Airseal 12/86

TX

Applicability: JT9D-7R4D, E models.

Requirement: Carry out the requirements of Pratt and Whitney ASB JT9D-7R4-72-318, Paragraph 2 "Accomplishment Instructions".

Compliance: According to a program agreed to by the Secretary. Effective: 1 December 1986.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require compliance with the Accomplishment Instructions of P.W.A. A.S.B. JT9D-7R4-72-318. The A.S.B. details inspection and modification of the high pressure turbine to preclude cracking of the stage 1-2 airseal.

# AD/MU-2/5 Amdt 3 - Wing Structural Fatigue Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MU-2/5**

**Amdt 3**

**Wing Structural Fatigue Limitation 4/79**

Applicability: MU-2B-30 and MU-2B-36A aircraft.

Requirement: 1. Retire from service the outer wing front spar joint fitting.

[image]2. Retire the aircraft from service.

Compliance:

Note 1: The lives for the MU-2B-36A are conservative interim values and will be reviewed on receipt of further data from the manufacturer.

Note 2: Similar restrictions may be applied to other Models of the type should they enter the Australian Register.

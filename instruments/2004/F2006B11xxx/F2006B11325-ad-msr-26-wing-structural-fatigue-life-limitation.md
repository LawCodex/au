# AD/MSR/26 - Wing Structural Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/26 Wing Structural Fatigue Life Limitation 3/80

Applicability: All series 235E aircraft.

Requirement: Retire the wing main spar and the centre section splice plates from service.

*Note: This fatigue life limitation will be reviewed upon receipt of future substantiation data from the manufacturer.*

Compliance: Upon or prior to achieving 2800 hours component time in service.

Background: Based on information provided by the manufacturer, a safe fatigue life has been determined for the wing structure.

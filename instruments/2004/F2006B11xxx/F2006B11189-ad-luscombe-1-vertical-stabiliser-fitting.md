# AD/LUSCOMBE/1 - Vertical Stabiliser Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Luscombe Model 8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LUSCOMBE/1 Vertical Stabiliser Fitting 11/94

Applicability: All Model 8 Series aircraft that have round-tipped vertical stabiliser installations.

Requirement: Replace the aluminium vertical stabiliser forward attach fitting, Luscombe part number 28415, 28444, or 28453, with either a Luscombe part number 28455 fitting manufactured by the Don Luscombe Aviation History Foundation (DLAHF); a welded steel fitting manufactured by the Univair Aircraft Corporation, part number U28444; or an FAA approved part.

Accomplish this replacement in accordance with the procedures included in DLAHF Service Recommendation #1, dated November 28, 1993.

*Note: FAA AD 94-16-02 Amdt 39-8991 refers, which supersedes AD 79-25-05 Amdt 39-3630.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 13 October 1994.

Background: The FAA requires replacement of the aluminium vertical stabiliser forward attach fitting with a steel attach fitting, on aircraft that have round-tipped vertical stabiliser installations. This action is intended to prevent the possible failure of the vertical stabiliser.

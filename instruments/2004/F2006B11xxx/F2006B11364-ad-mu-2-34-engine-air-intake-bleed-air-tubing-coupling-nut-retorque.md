# AD/MU-2/34 - Engine Air Intake Bleed Air Tubing Coupling Nut Retorque, Inspection, Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MU-2/34 Engine Air Intake Bleed Air Tubing Coupling Nut**

**Retorque, Inspection, Modification**

**7/84**

Applicability: All models MU-2B/-10/-15/-20/-25/-26 with S/Nos. 008 to 347 (except S/Nos. 313SA and 321SA) and MU-2B-30/-35/ -36 with S/Nos. 501 to 696 (except S/Nos. 652SA and 661SA).

Document: Mitsubishi SB No. 196 Rev A.

*Note: JCAB AD No. TCD-2342-84 refers.*

Compliance: Before further flight after 31 July 1984.

Background: Loss of engine bleed air at the intake anti-ice coupling nut can cause malfunction of the engine air intake anti-ice system. The manufacturer has therefore introduced an inspection and a minor modification to ensure permanent security of the coupling nut.

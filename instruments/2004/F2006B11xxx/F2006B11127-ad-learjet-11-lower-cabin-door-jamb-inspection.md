# AD/LEARJET/11 - Lower Cabin Door Jamb - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET/11 Lower Cabin Door Jamb - Inspection 6/75

Applicability: Model 24 with S/Nos. 24-272, 24-295 to 319 except 24-303, 24-304 and 24-305.

Model 25 with S/Nos. 25-134, 25-168 to 25-195 with 24 inch door installed.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 24/25-260.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1975.

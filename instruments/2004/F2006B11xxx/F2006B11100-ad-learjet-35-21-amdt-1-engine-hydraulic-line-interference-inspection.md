# AD/LEARJET 35/21 Amdt 1 - Engine Hydraulic Line Interference - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET 35/21**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Hydraulic Line Interference - Inspection**

**10/83**

Applicability: All model 35 and 36 series aircraft as detailed in Gates Learjet Service Bulletins 35/36-29-1 and 35/36-29-2.

Document: 1. Gates Learjet SB 35/36-29-1.

*Note: FAA AD 83-08-3 refers.*

2\. Gates Learjet SB 35/36-29-2.

Compliance: Requirement Document No. 1: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1983.

Requirement Document No. 2: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1983.

Background: This directive has been raised in issue to include a further manufacturers SB related to interference between hydraulic lines and adjacent components in the tailcone area.

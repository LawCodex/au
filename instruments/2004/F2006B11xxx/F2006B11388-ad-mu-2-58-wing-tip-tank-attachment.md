# AD/MU-2/58 - Wing Tip Tank Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/58 Wing Tip Tank Attachment 8/96

Applicability: Model MU-2B-10, -15, -20, -25, -26, with serial numbers 008 through 347 (except

313, 321).

Model MU-2B-30, -35, -36, with serial numbers 501 through 696 (except 652, 661).

Requirement: Inspect the wing tip tank fitting attachment bolts in accordance with Mitsubishi Heavy Industry MU-2 Service Bulletin 225.

*Note: JCAB TCD-4310-96 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 18 July 1996.

Background: Incorrect wing tip attachment bolts have been found corroded. Failure of corroded bolts could result in the separation of the wing tip tank from the aircraft.

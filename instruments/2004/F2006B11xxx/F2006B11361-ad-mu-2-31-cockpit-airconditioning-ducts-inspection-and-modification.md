# AD/MU-2/31 - Cockpit Airconditioning Ducts - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/MU-2/31 Cockpit Airconditioning Ducts - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models MU-2B-26A/-40 with S/Nos. 321SA, 348SA, 350SA to 457SA.

## 1/84

MU-2B-36A/-60 with S/Nos. 661SA, 697SA to 699SA, 1501SA to 1542SA, 1544SA to 1547SA.

Document: Mitsubishi SB No. 040/21-002.

Compliance: Prior to issue of the next maintenance release after 21 December 1983.

Background: The manufacturer advises that it may be necessary to re-route and modify the airconditioning ducting located above the electrical overhead panel due to the possibility of electrical arcing or shorting occurring in that area.

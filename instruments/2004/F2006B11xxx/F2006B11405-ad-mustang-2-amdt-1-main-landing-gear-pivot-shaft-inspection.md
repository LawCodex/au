# AD/MUSTANG/2 Amdt 1 - Main Landing Gear Pivot Shaft - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
C.A.C. CA-18 (Mustang) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MUSTANG/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Landing Gear Pivot Shaft - Inspection 1/84**

Applicability: All models engaged in aerial work or charter operations.

Requirement: Inspect the main landing gear pivot shaft P/N 17-33163 or 66584 for cracking using an approved magnetic particle method.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 250 landings, and within 10 landings following any heavy landing or other shock loading.

Background: This Directive has been revised to delete aircraft not involved in aerial work or charter operations, and to clarify the part numbers of affected components.

# AD/ML-M5/5 Amdt 1 - Rudder Bar - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Maule M-5 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/ML-M5/5**

**Amdt 1**

**Rudder Bar - Inspection and Modification 10/82**

Applicability: All models with rudder bars which have not been modified in accordance with Maule Aircraft Corporation Service Bulletin No. 2.

Requirement: Inspect and modify the rudder bar in accordance with Maule Aircraft Corporation Service Bulletin No. 2.

Compliance: Within the next 100 hours time in service after 31 October, 1982.

Background: Reinforcement of the rudder bar in accordance with SB No. 2 reduces the possibility of fatigue cracking and will obviate the necessity for the repetitive special inspections previously required.

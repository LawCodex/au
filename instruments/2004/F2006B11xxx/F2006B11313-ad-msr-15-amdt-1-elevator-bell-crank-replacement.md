Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MSR/15**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Bell Crank - Replacement 11/71**

Applicability: All models prior to S/No. 1874. Document: Rallye SB No. 92.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service or three months after 31 December 1971, whichever occurs first.

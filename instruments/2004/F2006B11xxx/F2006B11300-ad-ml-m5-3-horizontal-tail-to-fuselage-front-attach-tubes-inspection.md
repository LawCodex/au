# AD/ML-M5/3 - Horizontal Tail to Fuselage Front Attach Tubes - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Maule M-5 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/ML-M5/3 Horizontal Tail to Fuselage Front Attach**

**Tubes - Inspection and Modification**

**3/80**

Applicability: All M-5 series with S/Nos. as referenced in Maule Aircraft Corporation SB No. 1. Document: Maule Aircraft Corporation SB No. 1.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1979.

Background: There is a possibility that the fuselage tube attaching the front attach point of the horizontal tail may be cracked.

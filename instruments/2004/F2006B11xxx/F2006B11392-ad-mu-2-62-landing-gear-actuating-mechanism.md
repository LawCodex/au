# AD/MU-2/62 - Landing Gear Actuating Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/62 Landing Gear Actuating Mechanism 2/98

Applicability: Models MU-2B, MU-2B-10, -15, -20, -25, and -26 aircraft with serial numbers 008

through 347, except serial numbers 313 and 321; and Models MU-2B-30, -35, and -36

aircraft with serial numbers 501 through 696, except serial numbers 652 and 661.

Requirement: Modify the bearing box(es) in the landing gear actuating mechanism in accordance with Mitsubishi MU-2 Service Bulletin 230.

*Note: JCAB AD TCD-4684-97 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 29 January 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 29 January 1998.

Background: The JCAB requires modification action in order to prevent the possible inability to completely extend the landing gear.

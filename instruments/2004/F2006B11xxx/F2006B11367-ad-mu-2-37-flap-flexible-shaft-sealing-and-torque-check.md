# AD/MU-2/37 - Flap Flexible Shaft Sealing and Torque Check

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

AD/MU-2/37 Flap Flexible Shaft Sealing and Torque Check 12/85

Applicability: All models MU-2B/-10/-15/-20/-25/-26 with S/Nos. 008 to 347 except S/Nos. 313 and 321. All models MU-2B-30/-35/- 36 with S/Nos.501 to 696 except S/Nos. 652

and 661.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mitsubishi SB No. 198.

*Note: JCAB AD No. TCD-2451-85 refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 December 1985.

Background: The manufacturer reports failures of the flexible drive shaft attributable to ingress of moisture.

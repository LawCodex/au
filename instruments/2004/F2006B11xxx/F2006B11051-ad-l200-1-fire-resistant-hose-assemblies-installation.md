# AD/L200/1 - Fire Resistant Hose Assemblies - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Morava L200 Series Aeroplanes

AD/L200/1 Fire Resistant Hose Assemblies - Installation 3/63

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install approved fire-resistant hose assemblies in all flexible fuel, oil and hydraulic lines forward of the firewall.

Compliance: Forthwith.

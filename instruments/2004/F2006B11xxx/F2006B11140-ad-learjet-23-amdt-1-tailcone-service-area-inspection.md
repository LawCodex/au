# AD/LEARJET/23 Amdt 1 - Tailcone Service Area - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/23**

**Amdt 1**

**Tailcone Service Area - Inspection 1/81**

Applicability: All models 24 & 25 not incorporating Mod. Kit. No. AMK 80-7.

Requirement: Immediately after the engines are shut down, open the tailcone service area access cover and accomplish the following, using a flashlight or supplemental light as necessary:

Check the motive flow valves for leaks or stains indicating leaks.

2\. Check all vents and drains to ensure that they are clean and free from obstruction.

3\. Any leaking motive flow valve must be replaced with a new or factory rebuilt valve prior to further flight. 4. Secure access cover.

*Note 1. This inspection may be performed and certified by the pilot in command. Note 2. FAA AD 80-19-09 Amdt. 39-3913 refers.*

Compliance: After every flight until modified to AMK 80-7.

Background: Undetected leakage of flammable fluids in the tailcone service area may, when combined with a source of ignition, result in fire or explosion. Incorporation of modification kit AMK 80-7 will eliminated the requirement for post-flight inspection of this area.

# AD/LEARJET/15 - Refrigeration System Condenser Fan - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/15 Refrigeration System Condenser**

**Fan - Inspection and Replacement**

**7/77**

Applicability: Model 24 with S/Nos. 24-100 to 24-356. Model 25 with S/Nos. 25-002 to 25-253.

And any 23/24 or 25 Series Learjet which has had a Condenser Fan changed since 1 July 1977.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the P/No. rubber-stamped on a blade of the refrigeration condenser fan.

2\. If the fan bears the P/No. 6608059-3, attach a placard worded as follows to the instrument panel adjacent to the refrigeration system control switch:

WARNING

DO NOT OPERATE REFRIGERATION SYSTEM UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES ON THE GROUND OR IN THE AIR UNTIL FAN HAS BEEN REPLACED.

*Note: The correct fans have blades which taper in thickness towards the tip and bear the P/Nos. 6608059-2 or 6608059-4.*

or Gates Learjet SB 23/24/25-284.

Compliance: Before further flight after 31 July 1977.

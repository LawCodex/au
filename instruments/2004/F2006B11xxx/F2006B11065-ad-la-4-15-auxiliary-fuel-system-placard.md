# AD/LA-4/15 - Auxiliary Fuel System - Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Consolidated Aeronautics, Colonial and LA-4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LA-4/15 Auxiliary Fuel System - Placard 1/76

Applicability: All models LA-4 and LA-4-200 fitted with auxiliary fuel tanks to Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA 876EA.

Requirement: 1. Install a placard adjacent to the auxiliary tank fuel transfer switches as follows:

WARNING

AUXILIARY TANK FUEL TRANSFER PROHIBITED UNLESS AUXILIARY TANK SUMPS ARE DRAINED FREE OF WATER AFTER EACH WATER OPERATION

or

2\. FAA AD 76-02-01, Amendment 39-2494.

Compliance: Before 16 April 1976.

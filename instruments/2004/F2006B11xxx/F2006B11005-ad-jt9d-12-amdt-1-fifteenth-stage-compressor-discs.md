# AD/JT9D/12 Amdt 1 - Fifteenth Stage Compressor Discs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

**AD/JT9D/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Fifteenth Stage Compressor Discs 9/89**

Applicability: JT9D-3A, -7, -7A, -7H, -7AH, -7A(SP), -7F, -7J, -20, -20J.

Requirement: Inspect 15th stage compressor discs P/N 658615 and 672915 in accordance with the requirements of PWA SB No.4908 Rev.1.

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement document.

Background: This Directive has been amended to reflect the manufacturers latest revision to S.B.

4908\. The revision, in part, reduces the initial inspection requirement from 6,500 cycles to 3,500 cycles.

# AD/LEARJET/27 - Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Pin Bushing Installation - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/27 Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Pin Bushing**

**Installation - Inspection**

**12/81**

Applicability: All Gates Learjet models 24 and 25 with S/Nos. referenced in Gates Learjet Service Bulletin No. 23/24/ 25-311 or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues of that Service Bulletin.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 23/24/25-311.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1981.

Background: The horizontal stabiliser hinge pin bushings may move out of their mating fittings during normal service.

# AD/LTS/10 - Number Two Bearing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Lycoming) Turbine Engines - LTS 101 Series

AD/LTS/10 Number Two Bearing 3/95

Applicability: LTS101 series engines incorporating Number 2 Bearing P/N 4-301-362-01 as detailed by serial number in Textron Lycoming Alert Service Bulletin Number A-LT101-72- 50-0163 Revision 1.

Requirement: Replace the number 2 bearing in accordance with Textron Lyoming Alert Service Bulletin No. A-LT101-72-50-0163 Revision 1.

*Note: FAA Airworthiness Directive 94-18-06 refers.*

Compliance: Effective 2 March 1995: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require the replacement of certain number 2 bearings. The requirement follows advice that a machining discrepancy may have occurred during the manufacturing process. This discrepancy may result in bearing failure and subsequent engine failure.

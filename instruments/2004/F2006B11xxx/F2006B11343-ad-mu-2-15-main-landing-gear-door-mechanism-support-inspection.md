# AD/MU-2/15 - Main Landing Gear Door Mechanism Support - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/MU-2/15 Main Landing Gear Door Mechanism

**Support - Inspection**

Applicability: All model MU-2B-30/-35 with S/Nos. 501 to 585. Document: Mitsubishi SB No. 172.

## 1/76

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 27 February 1976 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified in accordance with SB No. 172.

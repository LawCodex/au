# AD/L200/2 - Horizontal Stabiliser Attach Fittings - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Morava L200 Series Aeroplanes

AD/L200/2 Horizontal Stabiliser Attach Fittings - Modification 7/63

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Omnipol Information Bulletin No. L200/076 Part C has been classified as mandatory. This section of the Bulletin requires the replacement of the horizontal stabiliser attach fittings and the modification of the horizontal stabiliser front spar.

Compliance: 1. Replacement of the horizontal stabiliser rear attach fittings, lower fuselage attach fittings on bulkhead No. 17 and strengthening of the horizontal stabiliser as specified on page 9 of the Information Bulletin, is to be carried out before the completion of 2,000 flights.

2\. Replacement of the front fuselage attach fittings on bulkhead No. 16 and strengthening of the front spar of the horizontal stabiliser as specified on page 10 of the information bulletin is to be carried out before the completion of 3,500 flights.

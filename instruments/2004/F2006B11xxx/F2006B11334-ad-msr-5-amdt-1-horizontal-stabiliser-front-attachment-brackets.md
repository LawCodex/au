# AD/MSR/5 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Front Attachment Brackets - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MSR/5**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Front Attachment Brackets - Inspection and Modification**

**8/62**

Applicability: Models MS880, 881, 885 and 886 with S/Nos. 1 to 395.

Requirement: Part 1 - Inspect the horizontal stabilizer forward attachment brackets as detailed in Rallye Service Bulletin No. 12/1.

Part 2 - Replace the light alloy attachment brackets with steel brackets as detailed in Rallye Service Bulletin No. 12/1.

Compliance: For Part 1 - at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service until incorporating of Part 2.

For Part 2 - within 100 hours time in service after 13 April 1972.

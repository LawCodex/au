# AD/JT9D/18 - Low Pressure Turbine Airseal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/18 Low Pressure Turbine Airseal 3/89

Applicability: JT9D-3A,-7,-7A,-7AH, -7H, -7F, -7J, -20 engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with FAA AD 88-24-05 amdt 39-6018, items (a)(1)(2), (b), (c).

*Note: Subject airseals modified in accordance with the requirements of PWA SB 4835 revision 4 are considered to be in compliance with the requirements of this Directive.*

Compliance: 1. Airseals conforming to FAA AD 88-24-05 amdt 39-6018 item (a)(1) - Within the next 500 cycles in service after 1 March 1989.

2\. Airseals conforming to FAA AD 88-24-05 amdt 39-6018 item (a)(2) - At next disassembly of the low pressure turbine module.

3\. FAA AD 88-24-05 amdt 39-6018 items (b), (c) - At next disassembly of the low pressure turbine module.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require modification and/or inspection of certain sixth stage low pressure turbine airseals. The Requirement follows advice that airseal rub could result in seal rupture and uncontained engine failure.

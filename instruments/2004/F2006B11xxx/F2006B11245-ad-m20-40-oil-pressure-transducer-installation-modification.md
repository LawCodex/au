# AD/M20/40 - Oil Pressure Transducer Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/40 Oil Pressure Transducer Installation - Modification 9/80

Applicability: Model M20J with S/Nos. 24-0084, 24-0378 to 24-0906, 24-0908 to 24-0925, 24-0927

to 24-0942 and 24-0946.

Document: Mooney SB M20-221.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1980.

Background: Inadequate clearance between the oil pressure transducer housing and the upper right hand engine mount may cause the transducer to chafe through and lose all engine oil.

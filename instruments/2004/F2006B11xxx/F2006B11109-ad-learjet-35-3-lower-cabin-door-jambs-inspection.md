# AD/LEARJET 35/3 - Lower Cabin Door Jambs - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

AD/LEARJET 35/3 Lower Cabin Door Jambs - Inspection 4/76

Applicability: All model 35 with S/Nos. 35-001 to 35-035. And all model 36 with S/Nos. 36-001 to 36-016 with the 24 inch cabin door.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 35/36-52-1.

Compliance: Within 75 hours time in service after 30 April 1976.

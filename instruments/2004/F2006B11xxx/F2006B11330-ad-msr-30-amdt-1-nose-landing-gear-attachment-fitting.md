# AD/MSR/30 Amdt 1 - Nose Landing Gear Attachment Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MSR/30**

**Amdt 1**

**Nose Landing Gear Attachment Fitting 13/95**

Applicability: All serial numbers of models MS 880, 883, 885, 886, 887, 890, 892, 893, 894; and RALLYE 100S, 100ST, 110ST, 150T, 150ST, 150SV, 150SVS, 180T, 180TS, and 235E;

Requirement: Action in accordance with SOCATA Rallye SB 152-32 Amdt.1.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-182(A)R1 refers.*

Compliance: After a hard landing, or after a significant nose landing gear shimmy occurrence.

This amendment becomes effective 7 December 1995.

Background: This Directive is to ensure the integrity of the attachment fittings of the NLG lower arms following an incident as described in the Compliance statement.

# AD/LA-4/3 Amdt 1 - Flap Pushrod Bearings - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Consolidated Aeronautics, Colonial and LA-4 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LA-4/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Pushrod Bearings - Inspection and Replacement**

**1/73**

Applicability: All Colonial C1 and C2, and Lake LA-4 and LA-4-200 models with 2-7811-7 pushrod assemblies installed which are not fitted with Heim HM-5G (Magnafluxed) rod ends.

Document: Consolidated Aeronautics SL No. 39A.

Compliance: Within 15 hours time in service after 5 January 1973, unless already accomplished within the last 10 hours time in service, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service.

# AD/MSR/22 - Rear Fuselage Structure - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/22 Rear Fuselage Structure - Inspection 6/76

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Remove the fuselage tail-cone and fin dorsal fairing and, with the aid of a torch, visually inspect the fuselage internal and external stringers for corrosion.

2\. If corrosion is detected remove the empennage components and perform a detailed visual inspection of the rear fuselage structure for corrosion damage.

Compliance: Before 15 July 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 3 years.

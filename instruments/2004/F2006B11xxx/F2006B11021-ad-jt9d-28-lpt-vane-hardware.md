# AD/JT9D/28 - LPT Vane Hardware

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT9D Series

AD/JT9D/28 LPT Vane Hardware 1/97

Applicability: All Pratt and Whitney JT9D-7R4 series engines.

Requirement: Replace certain 5th stage Low Pressure Turbine (LPT) vanes, and 3rd, 4th and 5th stage vane retention hardware in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 96-17-11.

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 2 January 1997.

Background: There have been reports of failures of LPT vane retention hardware which resulted in uncontained engine failures.

# AD/LEARJET/20 - Upper Cabin Door Lock Mechanism Bolts - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/LEARJET/20 Upper Cabin Door Lock Mechanism

**Bolts - Modification**

Applicability: All model 24 with S/Nos. 24-100 to 24-357,

All model 25 with S/Nos. 25-003 to 25-291.

## 3/80

Requirement: Modify the upper cabin door lock mechanism bolts by installing Gates Learjet Kit No. AMK 79-9.

Compliance: Within 150 hours time in service after 30 April 1980.

Background: Modification kit AMK 79-9 provides an additional safety feature to the upper cabin door lock mechanism by installing drilled bolts and lockwiring these bolts in situ.

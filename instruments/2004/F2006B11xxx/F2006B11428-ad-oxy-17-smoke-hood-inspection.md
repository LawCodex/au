# AD/OXY/17 - Smoke Hood Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Oxygen Systems

AD/OXY/17 Smoke Hood Inspection 2/98

Applicability: AIR LIQUIDE Smoke hood type 15-40 F installed on any type of aircraft with the following lot numbers and are not further identified with a “R1” on the label:

| 8141 | 8283 | 8359 | 8389 | 8413 | 8473 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8474 | 8475 | 8477 | 8524 | 8569 | 8570 |
| 8656 | 8657 | 8682 | 8744 | 8801 | 8840 |
| 8841 | 8842 | 9003 | 9007 | 9254 |  |

Requirement: 1. Inspect and remove from service all type 15-40 F smoke hoods installed on aircraft and identified by the lot numbers above.

2\. Smoke hoods identified above are not to be fitted to any aircraft.

*Note: DGAC AD 97-088(AB)R1 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Unless previously accomplished, no later than 16 July 1998.

2\. As of the effective date of this airworthiness directive.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 29 January 1998.

Background: The DGAC has been informed that some visors on the smoke hoods, identified by the lot numbers detailed become opaque (white) within a few minutes following the opening of the vacuum package. In order to eliminate the potential risk presented by these hoods, this airworthiness directive requires all smoke hoods identified to be removed from service.

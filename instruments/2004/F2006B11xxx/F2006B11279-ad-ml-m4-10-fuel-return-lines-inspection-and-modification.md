# AD/ML-M4/10 - Fuel Return Lines - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Maule M-4 Series Aeroplanes

AD/ML-M4/10 Fuel Return Lines - Inspection and Modification

11/75

Applicability: Model M-4-210 with S/Nos. 1001 to 1045. Model M-4-210C with S/Nos. 1001C to 1117C.

Requirement: 1. Inspect line P/No. 5182X-21 for leakage and cracking in accordance with Maule SL No. 31.

2\. Replace line P/No. 5182X-20 in accordance with Maule SL No. 31 or in accordance with a scheme approved by the Secretary; or

3\. FAA AD 75-11-02.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 November 1975.

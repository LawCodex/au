# AD/LEARJET/14 Amdt 1 - Upper Cabin Door Locking Mechanism -Inspection And Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 24 & 25 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/LEARJET/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Upper Cabin Door Locking Mechanism - Inspection And Modification**

**2/80**

Applicability: All models 23, 24 and 25 with S/Nos. 23-003 to 23-099, 24-100 to 24-356 and 25-003 to 25-238, with 36 inch cabin door.

Document: Gates Learjet SB 23/24/25-280A.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after achieving 3000 hours time in service and thereafter as per requirement document or until Gates Learjet Modification Kit No. AMK 78-2 is fitted.

Background: Failure of the door locking mechanism during door closing can result in a hidden door unsafe condition.

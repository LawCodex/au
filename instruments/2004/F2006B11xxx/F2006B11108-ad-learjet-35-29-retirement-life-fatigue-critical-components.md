# AD/LEARJET 35/29 - Retirement Life - Fatigue Critical Components

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gates Learjet 35 and 36 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/LEARJET 35/29 Retirement Life - Fatigue Critical Components

Applicability: All Learjet 35 & 36 series aeroplanes.

## 7/95

Requirement: Retire from service, at or before the times specified therein, all components listed in the Learjet 35 & 36 series Maintenance Manual, Revision 61, Chapter 5, Section

5-11-00.

Compliance: This Directive is effective 22 June 1995.

Background: This Directive reflects the manufacturer’s mandatory retirement life schedule for fatigue critical components.

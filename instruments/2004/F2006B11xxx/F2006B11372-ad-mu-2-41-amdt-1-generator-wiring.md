# AD/MU-2/41 Amdt 1 - Generator Wiring

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MU-2/41**

**Amdt 1**

**Generator Wiring 1/89**

Applicability: MU 2B-25, MU 2B-26 and MU 2B-35 Serial No’s 264 to 312, 314 to 320, 586 to 651

and 653.

Requirement: Action in accordance with MU 2 SB 201A.

*Note: FAA AD 87-12-02 and Japanese AD TCD - 2545-1-88 refer.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 26 January 1989.

Background: To prevent burning of generator shield jumper wires. Amdt 1 to this Directive is issued to amend the Requirement document only.

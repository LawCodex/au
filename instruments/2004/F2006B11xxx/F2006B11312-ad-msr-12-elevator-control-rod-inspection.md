# AD/MSR/12 - Elevator Control Rod - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale Rallye Series Aeroplanes

AD/MSR/12 Elevator Control Rod - Inspection 8/71

Applicability: All models not modified in accordance with Rallye SB No. 85. Document: Rallye SB No. 85.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1971 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/M20/49 - Fuel Selector Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mooney M20 Series Aeroplanes

AD/M20/49 Fuel Selector Valve 13/95 DM

Applicability: All M20K series aircraft fitted with Gerdes P/N A-2580 fuel selector valve. Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Mooney Aircraft Corporation

Service Bulletin M20-256.

*Note: FAA AD 95-18-12 Amdt 39-9359 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours times in service from 1 November 1995.

Background: The manufacturer has advised of a Gerdes selector valve malfunction which could result in an unscheduled landing of the aircraft.

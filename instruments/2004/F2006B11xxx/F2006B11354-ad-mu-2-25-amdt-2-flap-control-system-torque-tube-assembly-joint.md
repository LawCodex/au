# AD/MU-2/25 Amdt 2 - Flap Control System Torque Tube Assembly Joint

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Mitsubishi MU-2 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/MU-2/25**

**Amdt 2**

**Flap Control System Torque Tube Assembly Joint 10/90**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Mitsubishi SB No 189A or SB No 067/27-008 as applicable by serial number.

*Note: Japan Civil Aviation Bureau AD No TCD-2252-1-87 refers.*

Compliance: Initially inspect prior to exceeding 4000 hours component time in service or within 100 hours component time in service after 1 November 1990, whichever occurs later. Repeat the inspections at intervals not exceeding 100 hours component time in service.

*Note: Modification in accordance with the applicable Requirement document constitutes terminating action for this Directive.*

Background: Amendment 1 extended the repeat inspection requirements, and introduced a new terminating action for the inspections.

Amendment 2 introduces SB 067/27-008 which extends the inspection requirements to include all remaining serial numbers, and includes terminating action acceptable for those serial numbers.

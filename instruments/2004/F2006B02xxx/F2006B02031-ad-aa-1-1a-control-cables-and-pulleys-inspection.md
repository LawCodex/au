# AD/AA-1/1A - Control Cables and Pulleys - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-1 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AA-1/1A Control Cables and Pulleys - Inspection NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Thoroughly clean and inspect all rudder, aileron and elevator control pulleys and cables where the cables pass under the pulley grouping forward of the wing centre spar.

*Note: To adequately inspect the cable it will be necessary to actuate the controls to the full deflection to expose the complete cable pulley contact area for examination.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 25 February 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service.

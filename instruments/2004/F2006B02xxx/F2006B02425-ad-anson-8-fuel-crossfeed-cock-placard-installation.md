# AD/ANSON/8 - Fuel Crossfeed Cock Placard - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Avro Anson and Avro 19 Series Aeroplanes

AD/ANSON/8 Fuel Crossfeed Cock Placard - Installation 3/52

Applicability: Anson.

Requirement: Install a placard adjacent to the fuel crossfeed cock, indicating clearly the “ON” and “OFF” positions of the cock when viewed from the pilot’s seat; or

ANO 105.1.31.4.5.9.

Compliance: Forthwith.

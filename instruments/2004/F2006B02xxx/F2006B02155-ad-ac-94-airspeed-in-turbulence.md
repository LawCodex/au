# AD/AC/94 - Airspeed in Turbulence

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Twin Commander (Gulfstream/Rockwell/ Aerocommander 500, 600 and 700) Series Aeroplanes

AD/AC/94 Airspeed in Turbulence 13/95 DM

Applicability: The following aircraft models and serial numbers: Models Serial Numbers

680T and 680V 1473 through 1720

680W 1721 through 1850

681 6001 through 6072

690 11001 through 11079

690A 11100 through 11344

690B 11350 through 11566

690C 11600 through 11735

690D 15001 through 15042

695 95000 through 95084

695A 96000 through 96100

695B 96201 through 96208

Requirement: Attach a placard on the windshield centre post in accordance with the Accomplishment Instructions of Twin Commander Service Bulletin 220.

A separate direction under CAR 138(3) for incorporation of the applicable flight manual amendment is being provided to Certificate of Registration holders.

*Note: FAA AD 95-19-18 Amdt 39-9379 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours’ time in service after 25 October 1995.

Background: This Directive is issued in response to the recent issue of the above referenced AD by the FAA. Although no limitations have changed, the country of origin regulatory authority requires a placard and a flight manual amendment to warn the pilot to observe turbulent air penetration speeds.

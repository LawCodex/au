# AD/B737/80 Amdt 1 - Wing Leading Edge Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B737/80**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Leading Edge Panel 4/95**

Applicability: Model 737-300, -400, and -500 series aircraft, with line numbers 1001 through 1976,

1978 through 2197, and 2199.

Requirement: Modify the leading edge slat and internal structure at Front Spar Station 250.663 in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 737-57-1221.

*Note: FAA AD 94-06-11 Amdt 39-8858 and AD 94-26-13 Amdt 39-9108 refer.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to 21 April 1996.

Background: Reports were received of fuel leakage from the fuel line at front spar station 250.663, which then flowed through a drain hole in a slat access panel and into the turbine exhaust area.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a new FAA AD which expands the applicability to include additional affected aircraft.

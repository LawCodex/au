# AD/AC/77 - Induction Air Elbow Duct - Replacement, Exhaust System - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Twin Commander (Gulfstream/Rockwell/ Aerocommander 500, 600 and 700) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AC/77 Induction Air Elbow Duct - Replacement,

**Exhaust System - Inspection** Applicability: Model 685 with S/Nos. 12000 to 12043 and 12045 to 12066. Document: Gulfstream American SB 192.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 December 1982.

## 11/82

Background: A combination of exhaust system leakage and displaced induction ducting may cause exhaust gases to enter the cockpit and cabin.

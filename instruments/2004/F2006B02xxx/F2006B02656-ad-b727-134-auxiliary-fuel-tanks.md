# AD/B727/134 - Auxiliary Fuel Tanks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/134 Auxiliary Fuel Tanks 9/91

Applicability: All model 727 aircraft as listed in Boeing SB 727-28-0110.

Requirement: 1. Inspect aircraft in accordance with Boeing SB 727-28-0110 or CAA approved inspection procedure until modified in accordance with Requirement 2.

2\. Modify aircraft in accordance with Boeing SB 727-28A0067 Revision 1.

*Note: FAA AD 91-09-06 Amdt 39-6977 refers to Boeing SB 727-28A0067 Revision 1.*

Compliance: 1. As specified in the Requirement Document.

2\. Prior to issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: An uncontrolled fuel leak into the baggage compartment due to damage of an auxiliary fuel tank cell, has occurred.

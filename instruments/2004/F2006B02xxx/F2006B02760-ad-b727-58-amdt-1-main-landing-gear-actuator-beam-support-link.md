# AD/B727/58 Amdt 1 - Main Landing Gear Actuator Beam Support Link - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/58**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Landing Gear Actuator Beam Support Link - Inspection and Modification**

**9/71**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect the main landing gear actuator beam support link P/N 65-19657-4, -5 or -8 in accordance with Boeing SB No. 32-90 Rev. 2 and link P/N 65-19657-13 in accordance with Boeing SB No. 32-189 until replaced by link P/N 65-19657-11.

Compliance: Forthwith.

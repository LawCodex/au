**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B727/129 Fuselage Circumferential Joint Doubler

**BS259, 360, 441, 481 and 681**

Applicability: 727-100 line position 1 through 547.

727-200 line position 433 through 549.

## 3/91

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-26-09 Amdt 39- 6835.

*Note: Boeing SB 727-53-0084 Rev. 4 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Country of origin AD. Delamination, corrosion and/or cracking if not corrected, could result in loss of cabin pressurisation.

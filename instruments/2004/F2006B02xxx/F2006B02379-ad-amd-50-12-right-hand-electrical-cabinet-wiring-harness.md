# AD/AMD 50/12 - Right Hand Electrical Cabinet Wiring Harness

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 50/12 Right Hand Electrical Cabinet Wiring Harness 8/96

Applicability: This Directive is applicable to Mystere-Falcon 50 aeroplanes with equipment racks fitted between frames 9 and 11, aft of the right hand electrical cabinet.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dassault Aviation Falcon 50 Mandatory Service Bulletin F50-256 dated 24 April 1996.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-094-017(B) dated 24 April 1996 refers.*

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 18 July 1996 or prior to 31 December 1996, whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive is issued following a report from Dassault Aviation advising of an incident resulting from a chafed wiring harness aft of the right hand electrical cabinet. The actions specified by this Directive are designed to decrease the likelihood of wire harness chafing occurring in this area.

# AD/B727/124 - Fuselage Crown Skin Circumferential Joint BS 1080

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B727/124 Fuselage Crown Skin Circumferential

**Joint BS 1080**

Applicability: Model 727-100 and 727-100C aircraft listed in the Requirement document.

## 2/91

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-21-10 Amdt 39-6761.

*Note: Boeing SB 727-53-0109 Rev 1, Rev 2 and Rev 3 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement document with effective date 13 November 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. To detect delamination, cracking, and/or corrosion that may lead to depressurisation.

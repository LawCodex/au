# AD/B737/38 - Fuselage Lap Joints BS259 to BS1016

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/38 Fuselage Lap Joints BS259 to BS1016 5/90

Applicability: Line number 001 to 291 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 89-09-03, paras A to D.

*Note: Incorporation of the repairs/modifications in this Directive partially terminate the requirements of AD/B373/37.*

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive requires terminating action for parts of AD/B737/37. This is in recognition that inspection procedures alone may not ensure an adequate level of structural integrity at the skin splices.

# AD/B737/65 - Radio Control Panels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/65 Radio Control Panels 2/92

Applicability: Boeing 737 series aircraft equipped with Boeing part number 285U0037-104, -105, - 201, -202 or -203 series radio control panels.

Requirement: Remove radio control panels, part numbered 285U0037-104, -105, -201, -202 and - 203 and replace with part numbers 285U0037-304, -305, -301, -302 and -303 respectively.

*Note: FAA AD 91-21-12 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 12 January 1993.

Background: Country of origin AD. To prevent uncommanded frequency changes of the high frequency (HF) and very high frequency (VHF) radios.

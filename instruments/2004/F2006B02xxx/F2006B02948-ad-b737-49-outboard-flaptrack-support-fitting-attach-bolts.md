# AD/B737/49 - Outboard Flaptrack Support Fitting Attach Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/49 Outboard Flaptrack Support Fitting Attach Bolts 12/90

Applicability: Models 737 with line numbers 1 through 324.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-17-20 Amdt 39- 6707.

*Note: Boeing SB 737-57A1079 Rev 4 refers.*

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement document, except that the effective date of this AD is 29 November 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Failure of the subject bolts could result in loss of the flap and consequent control difficulties.

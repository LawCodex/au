# AD/AC/64 - Fatigue Life Limitation - Pressurised Fuselage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Twin Commander (Gulfstream/Rockwell/ Aerocommander 500, 600 and 700) Series Aeroplanes

| **AD/AC/64** | **Fatigue Life Limitation - Pressurised Fuselage** | **6/77** |
|---|---|---|
| Applicability: | Models 680T, 680V, 680W, 680FL(P), 681, 685, 690, 690A and 690B. |  |
| Requirement: | Retire the pressure hull and related structure from service. |  |

Compliance: For models 680T, 680V, 680W, 680FL(P), 681 and 690: On or before achieving 7500 pressurised flight cycles.

For models 685, 690a and 690B: On or before 10,000 pressurised flight cycles.

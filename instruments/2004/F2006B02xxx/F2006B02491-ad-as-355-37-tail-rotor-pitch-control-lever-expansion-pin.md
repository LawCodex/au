# AD/AS 355/37 - Tail Rotor Pitch Control Lever Expansion Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/37 Tail Rotor Pitch Control Lever Expansion Pin 9/91

DM

Applicability: All AS 355 helicopters versions E, F, F1, F2 and N.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of DGAC TAD T91-138-043 (B).

*Note: Aerospatiale TSB 01-34 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with revised effective date of 10 July 1991.

Background: One lug of the tail rotor gearbox control lever hinge yoke failed through incorrect assembly of the bushes on the expansion pin.

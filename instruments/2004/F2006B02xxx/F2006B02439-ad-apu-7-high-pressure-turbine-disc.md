# AD/APU/7 - High Pressure Turbine Disc

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auxiliary Power Units

AD/APU/7 High Pressure Turbine Disc 4/92

Applicability: Garrett Auxiliary Power Division model TSCP700-4B auxiliary power units, prior to S/N 90697 and model TSCP700-5 auxiliary power units prior to S/N 80443.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-25-04.

*Note: Garrett Service Bulletin TSCP700-49-5892 Revision 2 dated 10 October 1990, refers.*

Compliance: Within 24 months after the issue date of this Directive.

Background: This Directive has been raised due to reports of HPT disc ruptures. This condition, if not corrected, could lead to an uncontained HPT disc failure.

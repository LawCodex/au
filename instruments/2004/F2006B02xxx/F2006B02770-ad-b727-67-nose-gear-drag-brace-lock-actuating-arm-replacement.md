# AD/B727/67 - Nose Gear Drag Brace Lock Actuating Arm - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/67 Nose Gear Drag Brace Lock Actuating Arm - Replacement

3/71

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Replace nose gear drag brace lock actuating arms P/Nos. 65-20687-6, -7 or -8 in accordance with Boeing SB No. 32-162, Rev. 1.

Compliance: 1. For components with less than 4000 landings as at 15 March 1971 - before exceeding 5000 landings.

2\. For components with between 4000 and 9000 landings as at 15 March 1971 - within 1000 landings from 15 March 1971.

3\. For components with between 9000 and 9800 landings as at 15 March 1971 - before exceeding 10,000 landings.

4\. For components with more than 9800 landings as at 15 March 1971 - within 200 landings from 15 March 1971.

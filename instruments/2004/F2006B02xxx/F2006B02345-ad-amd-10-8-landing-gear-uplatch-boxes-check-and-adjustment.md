**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AMD 10/8 Landing Gear Uplatch Boxes - Check**

**and Adjustment**

Applicability: Falcon 10 aeroplanes with S/Nos. 1 to 127. Document: AMD SB BA F10-0164 R1. (F10-32-021).

Compliance: For Part A: Within 25 hours time in service after 18 October 1978.

For Part B: Before 31 December 1978.

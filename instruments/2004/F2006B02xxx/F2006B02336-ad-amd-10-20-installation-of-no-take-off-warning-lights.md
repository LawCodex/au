# AD/AMD 10/20 - Installation of No Take Off Warning Lights

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 10/20 Installation of No Take Off Warning Lights 3/99

Applicability: All Dassault Aviation Falcon 10 aircraft up to and including serial number 152 without modification M801 or Service Bulletin F10-280 incorporated.

Requirement: 1. Install “NO TAKE OFF” warning lights in accordance with Dassault Aviation Service Bulletin F10-280 dated 6 September 1995 or Revision 1 dated 10 February

1999\.

*Note: DGAC AD 98-547-022(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 25 December 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 25 March 1999.

Background: To prevent take off with the park brake engaged, a indicating light stating “NO TAKE OFF”, is to be added to the pilots and co-pilots instrument panel.

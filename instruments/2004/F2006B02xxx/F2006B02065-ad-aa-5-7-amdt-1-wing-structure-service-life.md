Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-5 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AA-5/7

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Wing Structure - Service Life 9/75

Requirement: Retirement of “Service Life Limited Components” shall be carried out on or before reaching the Manufacturer’s service lives stated below:

| Component | Aircraft Model | Part Number | Service Life Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inboard Spar Assembly | AA-5 & 5A AA-5B | 5102310-501<br>5102310-502 | 12,000<br>12,000 |
| Shoulder Bolt | AA-5 & 5A AA-5B | 901044-1/2<br>901044-2 | 7,250<br>7,250 |
| Wing Spar Assembly | AA-5 & 5A AA-5A & 5B | 5201002-501<br>5201004-501 | 12,500<br>12,500 |
| Wing Outboard Spar Assembly | AA-5 & 5A AA-5B | 5201189-501<br>5201189-501 | 12,500<br>12,500 |

Compliance: As detailed in requirement.

# AD/APU/14 - TSCP700 First Stage LPC Disc

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auxiliary Power Units

AD/APU/14 TSCP700 First Stage LPC Disc 3/98

Applicability: AlliedSignal Inc. TSCP700 series auxiliary power units (APUs) incorporating stage one low pressure compressor (LPC1) disc, P/No 3606429-1.

Requirement: Remove from service affected TSCP700 LPC1 discs in accordance with AlliedSignal Service Bulletin TSCP700-49-7266.

*Note: FAA AD 97-19-18 also refers*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document, with a revised effective date of 26 February 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective 26 February 1998.

Background: The manufacturer has advised there has been LPC1 disc rim separation events.

Subsequent investigation has resulted in a reduction in the LPC1 life limit.

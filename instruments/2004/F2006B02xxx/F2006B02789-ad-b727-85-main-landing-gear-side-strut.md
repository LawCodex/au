# AD/B727/85 - Main Landing Gear Side Strut

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/85 Main Landing Gear Side Strut 1/80

Applicability: All model 727 with line numbers 1 to 1361 inclusive. Document: 1. FAA AD 79-20-06, Amdt. 39-3581; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 727-32-268, Rev. 1.

Compliance: As per requirement document No. 1.

Background: Incorrect parts may have been installed in the M-L-G side strut assemblies at manufacture.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/83 Mid-Cabin Galley Door Evacuation Slide - Inspection

1/79

Applicability: All 727-100 series.

Document: 1. FAA AD 78-24-05 Amdt. 39-3355; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 727-25-A247.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, initially inspect within 300 hours time in service after 15 December 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1500 hours time in service.

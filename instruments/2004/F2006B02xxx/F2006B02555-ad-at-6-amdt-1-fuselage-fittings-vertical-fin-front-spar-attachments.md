# AD/AT/6 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Fittings - Vertical Fin Front Spar Attachments - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airtractor AT-300, 400 and 500 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AT/6**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage Fittings - Vertical Fin Front Spar Attachments - Inspection and Modification**

**11/83**

Applicability: All models AT-300, AT-301, AT-302 with S/Nos. 0001 to 0240. Document: 1. Snow Engineering Co. Service Letter No. 26 and

2\. Snow Engineering Co. Service Letter No. 47.

*Note: FAA AD 83-18-01 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 100 hours time in service after 31st July 1983 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

2\. Modification - prior to 2000 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer reports that service history indicates cracking of attachment fittings. Amendment 1 of this AD details Snow Engineering Co. Service Letter No. 26 for the repetitive inspection and notes the issue of FAA AD 83-18-01.

# AD/AMD 10/13 - Passenger Door Latch System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 10/13 Passenger Door Latch System - Modification 2/80

Applicability: All Falcon 10 aeroplanes with S/Nos. 1 to 153. Document: AMD SB BA F10-0190. (F10-52-009).

Compliance: Before 30 June 1980.

Background: The manufacturer advises that it is possible to operate the door closing and locking mechanisms and yet not have all the latches engaged.

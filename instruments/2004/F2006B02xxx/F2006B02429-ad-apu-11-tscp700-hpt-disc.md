# AD/APU/11 - TSCP700 HPT Disc

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auxiliary Power Units

AD/APU/11 TSCP700 HPT Disc 1/97

Applicability: AlliedSignals TSCP700 Series Auxiliary Power Units, incorporating a High Pressure Turbine (HPT) disc identified with a serial number listed in AlliedSignals Service Bulletin TSCP700-49-A7168.

Requirement: Remove from service affected HPT discs as identified in AlliedSignals Service Bulletin TSCP700-49-A7168.

*Note: FAA AD 96-18-17 also refers.*

Compliance: Effective 2 January 1997; prior to accumulating 7500 cycles since new or no later than 2 January 2000, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that some HPT discs have been manufactured from nonconforming material which has required a reduction in service life.

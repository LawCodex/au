# AD/B727/92 - Take-Off Warning System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/92 Take-Off Warning System - Modification 3/83

Applicability: All model 727 aeroplanes with S/Nos. listed in the requirement document. Document: Boeing SB 727-31-52.

*Note: FAA AD 80-22-12 Amendment 39-3951 refers.*

Compliance: Before 31 March 1985.

Background: To ensure that flight crew have positive warning when the leading edge devices are not extended for take-off.

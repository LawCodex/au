# AD/AMD 50/26 Amdt 1 - Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

[image]

[image]\
For the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/AMD 50/26 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.1 (1) of CAR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

**AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes**

**AD/AMD 50/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Structure 7/2002**

Applicability: Dassault Aviation Mystere Falcon 50 Aircraft, serial numbers 253 through 278. Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of Dassault Aviation Service Bulletin

F50-332.

*Note: DGAC AD 2002-033-039(B) R1 refers.*

Compliance: Within 6 years and 6 months after 13 June 2002.

The compliance time remains unchanged by this issue. This amendment becomes effective on 11 July 2002.

Background: The DGAC requires certain inspections to address ageing aircraft issues of fatigue, corrosion, and fretting of the aircraft structure.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the related DGAC AD which corrects an applicability serial number error.

The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 13 June 2002.

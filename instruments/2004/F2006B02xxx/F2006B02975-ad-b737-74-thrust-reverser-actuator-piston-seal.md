# AD/B737/74 - Thrust Reverser Actuator Piston Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/74 Thrust Reverser Actuator Piston Seal 2/93

DM

Applicability: Boeing 737-300,-400 and -500 series aeroplanes as listed in Boeing Service Bulletin 737-78A1055, dated 2 April 1992.

*Note: FAA AD 92-24-04 refers.*

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 737-78A1055 dated 2 April 1992. Compliance: By 1 January 1994.

Background: Analysis by the manufacturer indicates that a potential exists for leakage across the piston seal in the thrust reverser actuator. The actions specified in this AD are intended to prevent uncommanded deployment.

# AD/B727/120 - Fuel Tank Access Doors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/120 Fuel Tank Access Doors 9/90

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 727-28-0101. Compliance: Prior to 30 October 1991.

Background: The incorporation of the Service Bulletin enables aircraft to comply with FAR 25.963(e) which provides for some fuel tank access panels to have a higher resistance to impact to reduce possibility of penetration from engine debris or objects thrown by the aircraft tyres.

# AD/AMD 50/14 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AMD 50/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge 9/99**

Applicability: Mystere Falcon 50 aircraft with serial numbers 242 through 262, Mystere Falcon 900 aircraft with serial numbers 139 through 169, and Falcon 900 EX aircraft with serial numbers 1 through 21.

Requirement: 1. Incorporate the following temporary changes and revisions, as applicable: For the MF 50:

Aircraft Flight Manual, TC 46 (TC 12 for S/N 251, 253 on).

Maintenance Manual Chapter 5-40, Revision 10.

For the MF 900:

Aircraft Flight Manual, TC 69.

Maintenance Manual Chapter 5-40, Revision 9.

For the F 900 EX:

Aircraft Flight Manual, TC 14.

Maintenance Manual Chapter 5-40, Revision 2.

2\. Perform the check specified in Chapter 5 of the Maintenance Manual at the revision status referenced in Requirement 1 of this Directive, as applicable.

3\. Action in accordance with Dassault Aviation Service Bulletin F 50-274, F 900- 203, or F 900 EX-37, as applicable.

*Note: DGAC AD 1997-370-020 (B) R2 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Unless already accomplished, before 23 May 1998.

2\. Before 27 February 2004 or supplementary 3750 flight cycles, whichever occurs first.

3\. Before 6 years and 2 Months or 3750 flight cycles since new, whichever occurs first.

The compliance remains unchanged for Requirement 1 of this Directive. This Amendment becomes effective on 9 September 1999.

Background: A check performed on a test aircraft revealed deformation of the horizontal stabiliser attachment and hinge outer rings which may create excessive play in the hinge.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a new DGAC AD which deletes the mandatory inspection to be performed after a stall as required in Maintenance Manual Chapter 5.50

The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 23 April 1998.

# AD/B727/84 Amdt 3 - Main Landing Gear Lock System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/84**

**Amdt 3**

**Main Landing Gear Lock System 9/88**

Applicability: All models to line number 1125.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing S.B. 727-32-0237 Rev 4.

*Note: FAA AD 79-04-01 R3 is referenced for earlier issues of this Directive.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1988.

Background: Aircraft were initially modified to provide higher strength components in the MLG lock system. Since that time there have been reports of missing nuts and the loss of a bolt.

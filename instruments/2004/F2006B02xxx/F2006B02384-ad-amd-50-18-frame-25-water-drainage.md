# AD/AMD 50/18 - Frame 25 Water Drainage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 50/18 Frame 25 Water Drainage 13/99

Applicability: MF 900 S/n 1 to 177 and F900 EX S/n 1 to 41.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with Dassault Aviation SB MF900-232 or F900EX-093 as appropriate.

*Note: DGAC AD 1999-446-028(B) refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 June 2000.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 30 December 1999.

Background: Manufacturer flight testing revealed that water does not drain properly through the Frame 25 filter and if it froze could lead to jamming of the flight controls.

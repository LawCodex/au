# AD/AS 355/45 - Cyclic Pitch Change Rod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/45 Cyclic Pitch Change Rod 11/94

Applicability: Models E, F, F1, F2 and N fitted with cyclic pitch change control rod P/No 704A34- 113-279, and not fitted with an auto-pilot.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter AS 355 Service Bulletin 01-38.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-179-051(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 13 October 1994.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that some control rods may not be ‘in safety’.

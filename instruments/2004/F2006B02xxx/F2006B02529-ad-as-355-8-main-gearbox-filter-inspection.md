# AD/AS 355/8 - Main Gearbox Filter - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/8 Main Gearbox Filter - Inspection 3/83

Applicability: All model AS 355 helicopters, having the main gearbox fitted with a TEDECO magnetic plug, Part Number B4439 with electrical indication (optional SB 63.02 refers) and not modified in accordance with A.M.S. 07.1211.

Document: AS 355 SB 05.07.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 82-180-9(B) refers.*

Compliance: At periods not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 31st March 1983.

Background: The manufacturer advises of the need to reduce the inspection interval for the main gearbox oil filter, on helicopters fitted with the TEDECO magnetic plug installation.

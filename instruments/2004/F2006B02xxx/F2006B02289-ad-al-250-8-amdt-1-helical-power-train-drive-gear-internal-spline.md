# AD/AL 250/8 Amdt 1 - Helical Power Train Drive Gear Internal Spline Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Allison Turbine Engines - 250 Series

**AD/AL 250/8**

**Amdt 1**

**Helical Power Train Drive Gear Internal Spline Inspection**

**2/71**

Applicability: All 250-C18 series non Blue Ribbon gearboxes and all Blue Ribbon gearboxes used with non Blue Ribbon turbines.

Document: Allison 250 CSL - 35 Rev. 1.

Compliance: Between 700 and 775 hours’ time in service since new or last overhaul after 26 February 1961.

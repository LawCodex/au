# AD/AS 355/5 - Upper Fin Attachment Fitting - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/5 Upper Fin Attachment Fitting - Inspection 10/82

Applicability: All model AS 355 not modified in accordance with AMS 350A07.1047 (SB No.

55.02).

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SB No. 05.03.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 82-99-6(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 1 September 1982 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 10 hours time in service.

Background: Modification in accordance with SB 55.02 provides terminating action for the mandatory repeat inspection.

# AD/B727/90 - Ground Spoiler Hydraulic Tube Assemblies - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B727/90 Ground Spoiler Hydraulic Tube Assemblies - Modification

Applicability: All models 727 with line numbers 1 to 1612. Document: 1. FAA AD 82-10-03 Amdt. 39-4375; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 727-27-202, Rev. 1.

Compliance: As per requirement document.

## 7/82

Background: Three overseas operators have reported inadvertently cross connecting a ground spoiler up-line with a ground spoiler-down line during routine maintenance.

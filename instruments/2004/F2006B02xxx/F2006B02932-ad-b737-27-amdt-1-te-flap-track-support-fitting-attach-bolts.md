# AD/B737/27 Amdt 1 - TE Flap Track Support Fitting Attach Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B737/27**

**Amdt 1**

**TE Flap Track Support Fitting Attach Bolts 8/91**

Applicability: Model 737-300 and -400 series as listed in Boeing ASB 737-57A1202 Revision 1.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 91-09-17 Amdt 39-6984.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 8 August 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Incorrect material bolts and nuts may have been used to attach the outboard flap inboard track support fitting.

Amendment 1 is prompted by information from the manufacturer which indicated that additional aircraft were not inspected for the proper nuts before they left the factory.

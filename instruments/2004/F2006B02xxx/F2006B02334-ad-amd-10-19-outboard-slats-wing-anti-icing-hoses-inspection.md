# AD/AMD 10/19 - Outboard Slats Wing Anti-Icing Hoses - Inspection, Replacement, Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AMD 10/19 Outboard Slats Wing Anti-Icing Hoses -

**Inspection, Replacement, Modification**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 1 to 260. Requirement: AMD SB No. F10-A246. (ATA F10-A30-21).

Compliance: 1. Preliminary Inspection Before 31 October 1984.

## 10/84

2\. Replacement Should no defects be found; as determined by the results of the preliminary inspection as described in the Requirement Document, replace hoses before 30 June 1985.

Background: Further to the Requirements of AD/AMD-10/4 AMDT 1, the manufacturer has produced a supplement to AMD SB No. F10/239 requiring further attention to the outboard slats wing anti-icing hoses to eliminate unacceptable tolerances subjecting hoses to compression loads.

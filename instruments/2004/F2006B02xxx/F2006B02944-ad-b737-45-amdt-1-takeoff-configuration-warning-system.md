# AD/B737/45 Amdt 1 - Takeoff Configuration Warning System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B737/45**

**Amdt 1**

**Takeoff Configuration Warning System 12/90**

Applicability: All model Boeing 737 series aircraft as listed in Boeing Service Bulletin 737-31-1033 Revision 1.

Requirement: Modify the logic which enables Takeoff Configuration Warning Systems (TOCWS) in accordance with Boeing Service Bulletin 737-31-1033 Revision 1 or later revision.

Compliance: Prior to 6 September 1992.

Background: Compliance with this AD is required in order to prevent takeoff configuration warning system nuisance warnings when taxi operations are conducted with the flaps intentionally retracted.

Amendment 1 to this AD is issued to clarify the applicability.

# AD/B727/154 - Fuel Valve Actuator Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/154 Fuel Valve Actuator Replacement 13/98

Applicability: Model 727 aircraft equipped with J. C. Carter Company Fuel Valve Actuators having part number (P/N) 40574-2 (Kearfott Models 3715-7 and -8) or 40574-5 (Kearfott

Model 3715-9).

Requirement: Replace all effected fuel valve actuators with acceptable items in accordance with the directions given in either J.C. Carter Company Service Bulletin 61163-28-09 dated 28 September 1995, or J. C. Carter Company Service Bulletin 61163-28-09 dated

1 May 1996.

*Note: FAA AD 98-18-20 Amendment 39-10736 refers.*

Compliance: Within 36 months of the effective date of this Directive.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 3 December 1998.

Background: Service experience has revealed a possible fault in the subject actuators which could prevent the pilot from shutting off the fuel to the engine following an engine failure and/or fire. This Directive has been raised to replace the effected components with serviceable items.

# AD/B727/95 Amdt 2 - Outer Wing Upper Stringers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B727/95

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Outer Wing Upper Stringers 12/88

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB No. 727-57A159 Rev 3 including Notice of Status Change No 727-57A159 NSC1.

*Note: FAA AD 88-17-06 Amdt 39-5988 refers.*

Compliance: As per Requirement document.

Background: Extensive cracking has been found in wing upper stringers at various rib locations.

Previous repairs may not have been effective. Revision 3 to the ASB introduced further inspection and some rework. This amendment references the latest FAA AD but does not change the requirement.

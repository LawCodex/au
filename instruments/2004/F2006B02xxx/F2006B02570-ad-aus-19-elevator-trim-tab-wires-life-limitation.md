# AD/AUS/19 - Elevator Trim Tab Wires - Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auster/Beagle A.61 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AUS/19 Elevator Trim Tab Wires - Life Limitation 5/68

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Replace the elevator trim tab control wires. Compliance: Within 1000 hours time in service after installation.

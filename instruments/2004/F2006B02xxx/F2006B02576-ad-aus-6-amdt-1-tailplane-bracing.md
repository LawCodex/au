# AD/AUS/6 Amdt 1 - Tailplane Bracing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auster/Beagle A.61 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AUS/6**

**Amdt 1**

**Tailplane Bracing 5/95**

Applicability: All models manufactured before January 1947.

Requirement: Visually inspect the tailplane bracing wire strap fittings for cracks near the retaining bolts.

*Note: The pilot in command may perform, endorse and certify on the Maintenance release for this inspection.*

Compliance: Before the first flight of the day.

This AD is effective from 27 April 1995.

Background: Cracks have been detected in the bracing wire attachment fittings.

This amendment allows the pilot in command to perform the inspection.

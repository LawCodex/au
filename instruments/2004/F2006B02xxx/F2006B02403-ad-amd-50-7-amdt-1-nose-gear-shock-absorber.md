# AD/AMD 50/7 Amdt 1 - Nose Gear Shock Absorber

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AMD 50/7

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: Falcon 900 aircraft.

## Nose Gear Shock Absorber 11/94

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dassault Aviation Alert Service Bulletin F900-150.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-098-014(B)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to 13 November 1994.

Background: The manufacturer had defined a procedure to check the charge of the low pressure chamber of the nose gear shock absorber. This action was required to prevent the possibility of a gear retraction when the nose gear is not fully extended.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the relevant DGAC AD which requires a modification that is designed to interrupt retraction of the landing gear when the NLG shock absorber is not fully extended.

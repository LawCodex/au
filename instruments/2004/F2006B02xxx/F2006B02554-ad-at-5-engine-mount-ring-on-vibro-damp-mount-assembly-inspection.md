# AD/AT/5 - Engine Mount Ring on Vibro-Damp Mount Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airtractor AT-300, 400 and 500 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AT/5 Engine Mount Ring on Vibro-Damp Mount Assembly - Inspection

Applicability: All models AT-300 and AT-301.

Document: Snow Engineering Co. Service Letter No. 50.

Compliance: 1. Within 100 hours time in service after 31st July 1983 and thereafter as per requirement document.

2\. Repeat bolt removal at each 1000 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer reports cracking of attach brackets and excessive wear of attachment bolts.

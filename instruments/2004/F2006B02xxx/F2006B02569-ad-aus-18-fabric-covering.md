# AD/AUS/18 - Fabric Covering

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auster/Beagle A.61 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AUS/18 Fabric Covering 3/64

Applicability: Mk. III with wings not covered to Dwg. EEH-48.

Mk. IV and V with wings not covered to Dwg. EGH-37. J1 with wings not covered to Dwg. EJH-72.

J1B, J1N with wings not covered to Dwg. EJH-72. J2 and J4 with wings not covered to Dwg. EJH-52. J5 with wings not covered to Dwg. EJH-72.

Other J5 series with wings not covered to Dwg. EJH-437.

Requirement: 1. Modify the wing rib stringing by introducing additional stringing in the leading edge area - Beagle Drawing No. CSK 3483 refers.

2\. When it becomes necessary to recover the wing it shall be recovered to the appropriate scheme called up in paragraph 1 or a scheme approved by the Secretary.

*Note: Beagle Service Bulletin (Auster Series) No. 53 contains all relevant information.*

Compliance: Forthwith.

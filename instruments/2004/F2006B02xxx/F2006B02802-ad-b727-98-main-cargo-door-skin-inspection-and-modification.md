# AD/B727/98 - Main Cargo Door Skin - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/98 Main Cargo Door Skin - Inspection and Modification

1/84

Applicability: All model 727-100 series aircraft with main cargo door of Boeing manufacture. Document: Boeing SB 727-52-79, Revision 5.

Compliance: As detailed in requirement document.

Background: Cargo door skin cracks have been found on aircraft in service, and have been assessed as a Safety of Flight item.

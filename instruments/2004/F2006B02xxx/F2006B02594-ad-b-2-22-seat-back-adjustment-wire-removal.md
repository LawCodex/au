# AD/B-2/22 - Seat Back Adjustment Wire - Removal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Brantly B-2 Series Helicopters

AD/B-2/22 Seat Back Adjustment Wire - Removal NK

Applicability: All model B-2 fitted with seat back adjustment wire P/N B2-334-32.

Requirement: Remove the seat back adjustment wire P/N B2-334-32 from the left and right seat backs; or

FAA AD 62-6-1.

Compliance: Forthwith.

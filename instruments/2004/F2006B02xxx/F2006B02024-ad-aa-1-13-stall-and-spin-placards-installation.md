# AD/AA-1/13 - Stall and Spin Placards - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-1 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AA-1/13 Stall and Spin Placards - Installation 8/73

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Install a placard on the instrument panel in full view of the pilot stating “SPINS PROHIBITED”.

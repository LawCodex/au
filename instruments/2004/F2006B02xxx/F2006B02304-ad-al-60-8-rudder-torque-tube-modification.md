# AD/AL 60/8 - Rudder Torque Tube - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aermacchi - Lockheed AL 60 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AL 60/8 Rudder Torque Tube - Modification 6/64

Applicability: Models AL 60 and AL 60B.

Requirement: Modify the rudder torque tube by drilling a 3/16 in. hole in the lower portion of the torque tube 3/8 in. above the lower bearing as shown in Figure 1 of Aeronautica Macchi Modification Bulletin No. 19.

Compliance: Before 1 September 1971.

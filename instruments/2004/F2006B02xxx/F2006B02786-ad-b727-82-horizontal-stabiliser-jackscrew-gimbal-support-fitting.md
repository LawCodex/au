**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/B727/82 Horizontal Stabiliser Jackscrew Gimbal

**Support Fitting Assembly - Inspection**

Applicability: All model 727-100 series with S/Nos. 001 to 889. Document: 1. FAA AD 78-11-07 Amdt. 39-3228; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 727-55-A73.

## 8/78

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, initially inspect within 1000 hours time in service after 29 June 1978 and thereafter at intervals as per requirement document, No. 1.

# AD/B727/22 - Main Landing Gear Actuator Beam - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/22 Main Landing Gear Actuator Beam - Inspection and Modification

3/66

Applicability: All models. Document: Boeing SB No. 32-76.

Compliance: Inspect before 3,000 hours total time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service until modified in accordance with SB No. 32-76.

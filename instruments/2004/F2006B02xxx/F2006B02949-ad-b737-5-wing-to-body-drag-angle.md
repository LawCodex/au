# AD/B737/5 - Wing-To-Body Drag Angle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/5 Wing-To-Body Drag Angle 8/85

Applicability: All model 737 aircraft, Line Numbers 1 to 316.

Requirement: 1. FAA AD 85-03-06, Amendment 39-4998, up to and including Section C.

2\. Boeing SB 737-53-1031 Revision 3. Compliance: As detailed in Requirement Document No 1.

Background: There have been numerous reports of cracking in the wing-to-body drag angles.

Continued operation with failed angles could lead to progressive cracking of adjacent structure and rapid decompression.

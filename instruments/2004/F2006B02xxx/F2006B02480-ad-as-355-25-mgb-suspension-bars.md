# AD/AS 355/25 - MGB Suspension Bars

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/25 MGB Suspension Bars 6/87 DM

Applicability: All models AS 355E, F, F1 and F2.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SB NO 05.16.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD T87-038-032(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 3 April 1987 and thereafter at each daily inspection.

*Note: The daily inspection may be accomplished by the pilot-in-command, in which case the precise AD reference shall be entered in a certification made on the Maintenance Release coincident with that for the daily inspection. A copy of the Reference Document is to be carried in the aircraft.*

Background: The manufacturer has had reports including Australian MDR’s of the MGB suspension bar end bearings being displaced in the tube ends.

Turbomeca Turbine Engines - Artouste Series

AD/ARTOUSTE/6 Fuel Shutoff Valve - Control 9/87

Applicability: All Artouste III B-B1-BF & BF1.

Requirement: Turbomeca SB Artouste 80-218-0083 (Modification TU 153). Compliance: Before 31 December 1987.

Background: The fuel shutoff valve has been reported partially closing causing engine power loss.

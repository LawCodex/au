# AD/B727/106 - Main Landing Gear Support Link

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/106 Main Landing Gear Support Link 3/89

Applicability: All models listed in Boeing SB 727-32-306.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the requirements of FAA AD 88-24-01 paras A, B and C. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement document.

Background: There have been reports of corrosion and cracking of the support link shaft, failure of which, could cause damage to the wing and/or hydraulic system.

# AD/AMD 10/14 - Pilot and Co-Pilot Seats - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 10/14 Pilot and Co-Pilot Seats - Modification 3/80

Applicability: All model 10 with S/Nos. 1 to 133 except 103, 124, 129 to 132. Document: AMD SB BA F10-0193 R1. (F10-25-010).

Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: Repeated stresses in the pilots’ seats can result in failures in the seat backrest housings.

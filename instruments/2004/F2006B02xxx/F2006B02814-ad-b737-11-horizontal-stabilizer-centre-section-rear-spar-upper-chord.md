# AD/B737/11 - Horizontal Stabilizer Centre Section - Rear Spar Upper Chord Attach Lugs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B737/11 Horizontal Stabilizer Centre Section - Rear**

**Spar Upper Chord Attach Lugs**

**10/86**

Applicability: All 737-100, -200 and -200C models with Line Numbers 316 to 322, and 325 and subsequent.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with the Flight Safety Inspection Program specified in Boeing S.B. 737-55-1033.

*Note: FAA AD 86-12-05 refers.*

Compliance: (a) Before exceeding 40,000 landings; or

(b) Before exceeding 40,000 landings after a chord replacement or modification in accordance with Option 1 of the Requirement Document,

whichever is the later, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 5,700 landings.

Background: Cracks in the lugs, if undetected, could lead to loss of the horizontal stabilizer.

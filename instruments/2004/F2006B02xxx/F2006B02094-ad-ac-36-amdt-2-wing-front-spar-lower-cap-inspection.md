# AD/AC/36 Amdt 2 - Wing Front Spar Lower Cap - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Twin Commander (Gulfstream/Rockwell/ Aerocommander 500, 600 and 700) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AC/36**

**Amdt 2**

**Wing Front Spar Lower Cap - Inspection 11/70**

Applicability: All models 560E, 680 and 680E which do not embody an external or internal spar reinforcement extending to W.S. 127 approved by the Secretary.

Document: Aero Commander SB No. 92.

Compliance: At or before 3000 hours’ time in service unless already accomplished.

# AD/B737/36 - Cabin Door Blowout Panels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/36 Cabin Door Blowout Panels 4/90

Applicability: 737 Aircraft line position 1 through 1587.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 737-52A1105 Rev. 2.

*Note: FAA AD 89-04-03 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Improper use of sealants on blowout panels in the control (cockpit) cabin door could preclude proper operation of blowout panels if a pressure differential greater than 1.5 P.S.I occurred between passenger cabin and control cabin. As a result damage to control cabin floor might occur, with possible interference of control cables.

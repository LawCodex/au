# AD/B727/77 Amdt 1 - Hydraulic System B Pump Motor

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/77**

**Amdt 1**

**Hydraulic System “B” Pump Motor 11/87**

Applicability: All models equipped with ABEX P/N 57186 pump motor. Requirement: Action in accordance with FAA AD 87-02-05 R1 Amendment 39-5675.

*Note: Boeing Service Bulletins 727-29-47 Revision 2, and 727-29-55 refer.*

Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

Background: This amendment is raised to incorporate FAA AD 87-02-05 R1 which introduces terminating action, and supersedes FAA AD 76-22-08 Amendment 39-2762 as the requirement document for this AD.

# AD/B727/88 Amdt 2 - Main Cargo Door Cam Support Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/88**

**Amdt 2**

**Main Cargo Door Cam Support Fittings 7/84**

Applicability: All 727-100 series aircraft listed in the requirement document.

Document: Boeing Alert Service Bulletin 727-52A124. Revision 6 including Addendum.

*Note: FAA AD 80-08-10 also refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in requirement document.

Background: Cracking of these fittings has been found in service and has been attributed to cabin pressurisation fatigue. Alert SB 727-52A124 has been revised to incorporate all pertinent inspection requirements into applicable sections of the Service Bulletin.

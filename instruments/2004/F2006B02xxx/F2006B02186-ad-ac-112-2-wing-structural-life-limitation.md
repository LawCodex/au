# AD/AC-112/2 - Wing Structural Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Rockwell) 112 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AC-112/2 Wing Structural Life Limitation 1/76

Applicability: All models with wing main spar caps exceeding 11200 hours’ time in service. Requirement: Retire the wing main spar from service.

Compliance: 30 January 1976.

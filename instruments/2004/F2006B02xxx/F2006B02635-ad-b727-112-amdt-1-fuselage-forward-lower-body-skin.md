# AD/B727/112 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Forward Lower Body Skin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/112**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage Forward Lower Body Skin 7/90**

Applicability: All Boeing 727-200.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Alert SB 727-53A0203 Rev 1.

*Note: FAA AD 89-23-13 Amendment 39-6388 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 5 December 1989, unless previously accomplished within the last 6 months, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 12 calendar months until preventive modification is carried out.

Background: Country of origin AD. Overseas aircraft found to have cracks 1200 mm long. Initial inspection has found cracks on one local aircraft. Amendment 1 references Revision 1 to the Requirement document. This requires repeat inspections until preventive modification is carried out, deletes specific line numbers and quotes FAA AD.

# AD/AA-5/8 Amdt 1 - Control Cables and Pulleys - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-5 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/AA-5/8**

**Amdt 1**

**Control Cables and Pulleys - Inspection 10/74**

Applicability: All model AA-5 with S/Nos. AA5-0001 to AA5-0209.

Requirement: Thoroughly clean and inspect all rudder, aileron and elevator cables as per Grumman American SB No. 127A.

*Note: To adequately inspect the cables it will be necessary to actuate the controls to the full deflection to expose the complete cable pulley contact area for examination.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 29 November 1974 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service.

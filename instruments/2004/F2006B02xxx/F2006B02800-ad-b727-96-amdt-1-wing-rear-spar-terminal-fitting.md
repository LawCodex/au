# AD/B727/96 Amdt 1 - Wing Rear Spar Terminal Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/96**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Rear Spar Terminal Fitting 11/87**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. specified in Boeing SB 727-57-103 R4.

Requirement: Action in accordance with FAA AD 87-04-15 and Boeing SB 727-57-103 R4. Compliance: As per Requirement documents.

Background: Cracks have been reported in 21 fittings on aircraft in service. Amdt 1 of this Directive references the latest FAA AD.

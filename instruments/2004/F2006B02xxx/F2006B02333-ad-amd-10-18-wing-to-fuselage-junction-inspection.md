# AD/AMD 10/18 - Wing-to-Fuselage Junction - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 10/18 Wing-to-Fuselage Junction - Inspection 7/84

Applicability: All model Falcon 10 with S/Nos 1 to 70, 73, 74 and 75. Requirement: AMD S.B. F10-0205.

*Note: Consigne De Navigabilite No. 84-41-19(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1985.

Background: Corrosion of wing recesses and wing to fuselage attachments has been reported due to deterioration of the original recess filling and sealing compound.

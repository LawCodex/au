# AD/B727/32 - Nose Landing Gear Actuating Bracket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/32 Nose Landing Gear Actuating Bracket - Inspection 6/66

Applicability: All models with nose landing gear actuator brackets, P/N 65-16370-1, which have accumulated 1000 hours total time in service.

Requirement: Remove Bracket P/N 65-16370-1 and inspect for cracks initiating at all bolt holes using a fluorescent dye penetrant or eddy current technique.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service.

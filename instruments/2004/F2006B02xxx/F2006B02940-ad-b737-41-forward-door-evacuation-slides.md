# AD/B737/41 - Forward Door Evacuation Slides

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/41 Forward Door Evacuation Slides 8/90

Applicability: Models 737-300 and 737-400 equipped with Air Cruisers Company forward door evacuation slides P/N D31591-448 through -454 with S/Nos 0001 to 1602.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Air Cruisers Company SB 103-25-17.

*Note 1: FAA AD 90-09-11 refers.*

*Note 2: Boeing SB 737-25-1260 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 9 August 1993.

Background: Country of origin AD. Evacuation slides may fail to automatically inflate upon deployment, and if not corrected could delay emergency evacuations.

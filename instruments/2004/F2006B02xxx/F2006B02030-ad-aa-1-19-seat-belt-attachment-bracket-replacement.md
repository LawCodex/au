# AD/AA-1/19 - Seat Belt Attachment Bracket - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-1 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AA-1/19 Seat Belt Attachment Bracket - Replacement 5/85

Applicability: All models AA-1, AA-1A, AA-1B and AA-1C. Requirement: Gulfstream Aerospace S.B. No. 173.

Compliance: Within 50 hours’ time in service after 31 May 1985.

Background: It is possible for the existing P/N 60152-2 to fail on some aircraft in the event of severe deceleration.

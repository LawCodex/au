# AD/B727/126 - Main Deck Cargo Door

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/126 Main Deck Cargo Door 3/91

Applicability: Model 727 and 727-100 series aircraft equipped with main deck cargo doors installed in accordance with STC SA1368SO.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-21-19 Amdt 39-6765.

*Note: Aeronautical Engineers, Inc. (AEI), Service Bulletin Report No R-690 refers.*

Compliance: Before 1 April 1991.

Background: The results of an FAA audit of the cargo door installation revealed that six cargo door latch lockpins were removed by virtue of a revision to STC SA1368SO.

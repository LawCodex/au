# AD/B727/148 - Fuel Actuator Shutoff Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/148 Fuel Actuator Shutoff Valve 6/95

Applicability: All model 727 series aircraft fitted with J.C. Carter actuator P/N 40574-2 used in any of the following J.C. Carter fuel system valves.

| J.C Carter P/N | Boeing P/N |
|---|---|
| 6563 | 10-60529-1 |
| 60128 | 10-60529-5 |
| 61163 | 10-60529-8 |
| 61183 | 10-60529-10 |
| 61185 | 10-60529-11 |

*Note: The above valve assemblies are used on the 727 fuel system crossfeed positions.*

Requirement: Action in accordance with J.C. Carter SB 61163-28-08. Compliance: By 31 May 1997.

Background: The valve manufacturer has advised that while functional testing a Boeing 737 gate valve, when the valve was selected to either the “open” or “close” position the actuator suffered a clutch slippage problem whenever the temperature dropped below minus twenty degrees C (minus five degrees F).

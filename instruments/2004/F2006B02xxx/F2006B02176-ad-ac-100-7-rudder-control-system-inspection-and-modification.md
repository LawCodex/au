# AD/AC-100/7 - Rudder Control System - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
S.L. Industries Commander 100 Series and Volair 10 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AC-100/7 Rudder Control System - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 001 to 250.

## 8/68

Requirement: 1. Remove paint over an area extending 3/4 of an inch aft of the cable attachment hole in the left and right rudder control arms P/N 35321 and inspect for cracking using a 10 power glass.

2\. Modify both arms P/N 35321 in accordance with Aero Commander SB No. 1009. Compliance: Inspect daily until modified. Modify within 100 hours’ time in service from 1 August

1968\.

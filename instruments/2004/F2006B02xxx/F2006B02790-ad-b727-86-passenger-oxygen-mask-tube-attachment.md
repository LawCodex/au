# AD/B727/86 - Passenger Oxygen Mask Tube Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/86 Passenger Oxygen Mask Tube Attachment 4/80

Applicability: All models with “Carry-all” interior passenger service units. Document: 1. Boeing SB No. 727-35-A18; or

2\. FAA AD 79-23-02.

Compliance: Within 2000 hours time in service after 30 April 1980.

Background: Advice from overseas indicates that modification of the passenger oxygen manifold orifice fittings is necessary to prevent passenger oxygen supply tubes from inadvertently disconnecting.

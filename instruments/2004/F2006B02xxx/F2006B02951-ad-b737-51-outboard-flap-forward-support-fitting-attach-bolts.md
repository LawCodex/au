# AD/B737/51 - Outboard Flap Forward Support Fitting Attach Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/51 Outboard Flap Forward Support Fitting Attach Bolts 2/91

Applicability: Model 737-200, -300 and -400 listed in the Requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 90-24-04 Amdt 39- 6809.

*Note: Boeing Alert SB 737-57A1206 and Alert SB 737-57A1208 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement document with effective date 24 December 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Fastener fracture in fitting attachment could result in loss of outboard flap.

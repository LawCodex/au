# AD/AMD 50/8 - Cockpit Brake Pressure Lines

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 50 and 900 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 50/8 Cockpit Brake Pressure Lines 13/95

DM

Applicability: All Falcon 900 aircraft.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Dassault Aviation Alert Service Bulletin F900-A29-4.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service from 17 October 1995.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that chaffing of the cockpit LH side brake pressure lines may occur.

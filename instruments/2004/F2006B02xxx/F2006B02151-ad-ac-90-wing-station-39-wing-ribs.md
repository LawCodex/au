# AD/AC/90 - Wing Station 39 Wing Ribs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Twin Commander (Gulfstream/Rockwell/ Aerocommander 500, 600 and 700) Series Aeroplanes

AD/AC/90 Wing Station 39 Wing Ribs 5/95

Applicability: Models 690, 690A, and 690B S/n 11001 to 11566 and Model 685 S/n 12001 to

12066\.

Requirement: Modify the wing ribs at WS 39 in accordance with Twin Commander Service Bulletin 211 Rev 1 Part II.

Compliance: Within 50 hours’ time in service after 25 May 1995, or at the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 25 May 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive is issued to prevent an under-strength condition developing in the wing ribs at WS 39, and is part of the requirements of FAA AD 94-04-12.

This Directive will be amended to include the spar cap and stringer inspections of FAA AD 94-04-12, if subsequent information is received indicating that this is necessary.

# AD/B727/113 - Aft Mount Support Fittings Engines 1 and 3

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/113 Aft Mount Support Fittings Engines 1 and 3 2/90

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing SB 727-54-0017.

*Note: FAA AD 89-23-17 refers.*

Compliance: 1. For aircraft with fittings made of 7079-T6 material. Unless previously accomplished within the last 2000 flight cycles prior to 22 February 1990 - Prior to exceeding 1000 flight cycles or 1 year after 22 February 1990, whichever occurs first, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 3000 flight cycles.

*Note: 7079-T6 material is used in the aft support fitting on number 1 engine strut on aircraft line numbers 001 through 883, and number 3 engine strut on aircraft line number 001 through 880.*

2\. For aircraft with fittings made of 7075-T73 material. Unless previously accomplished within the last 2000 flight cycles prior to 22 February 1990 - Prior to exceeding 40,000 flight cycles, or within 1000 flight cycles after 22 February 1990 whichever occurs later, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 3000 flight cycles.

*Note: 7075-T73 material is used in the aft support fitting on number 1 engine strut on aircraft line number 884 and later, and on number 3 engine strut on aircraft line number 881 and later.*

Background: Country of origin AD. Cracking has been reported as a result of stress corrosion and fatigue.

# AD/B727/57 - Thrust Reverser Deflector Doors - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/57 Thrust Reverser Deflector Doors - Modification 8/68

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Modify the thrust reverser deflector doors in accordance with Boeing SB No. 78- 51, Rev. 1.

2\. Modify the thrust reverser deflector doors in accordance with Boeing SL 6-7132- 3375 dated 1 March 1967 (or later revision).

3\. Replace bolt P/N BAC-B30EK8-48 or P/N BAC-C30EK8-48C. Compliance: Forthwith.

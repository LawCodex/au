# AD/AS 355/9 - Main Gear Box Bevel Reduction Gear Module - Inspection of Bevel Ring Gear/Vertical Shaft Attachment Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

**AD/AS 355/9 Main Gear Box Bevel Reduction Gear**

**Module - Inspection of Bevel Ring Gear/Vertical Shaft Attachment Bolts**

**3/83**

Applicability: All model AS 355 helicopters fitted with a main gearbox containing bevel reduction gear assembly, Part Number 355A.32.0600.00 and 01, not modified under A.M.S.

07.7082 or A.M.S. 07.7083.

Document: AS 355 SB 05.05.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 82-178-8(B) refers.*

Compliance: PART 1: Within 10 hours time in service after 31st March 1983 every 50 hours thereafter, prior to compliance with Part 2: Carry out the visual check.

PART 2: Within 300 hours time in service after 31st March 1983: Carry out the screw tightening check, as a one time only inspection.

PART 3: Every 300 hours time in service, after compliance with Part 2: Carry out the visual check.

Background: The manufacturer advises that there have been several cases of sheared and loose bolts, securing the bevel ring gear to the vertical shaft, found during maintenance operations.

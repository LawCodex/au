# AD/B727/145 - Lavatory Drain System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/145 Lavatory Drain System 2/95

Applicability: All model 727 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 94-23-10 Amdt 39- 9073.

*Note: FAA AD 94-23-10 Amdt 39-9073 supersedes FAA AD 86-05-07 Amdt 39-5250.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 2 February 1995.

Background: This Directive has been raised to prevent engine damage or separation, airframe damage, and/or hazard to persons or property on the ground which could occur if “blue ice” has formed from leakage of the lavatory drain system and is subsequently dislodged from the aircraft.

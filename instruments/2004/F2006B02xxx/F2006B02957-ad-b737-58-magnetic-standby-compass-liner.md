# AD/B737/58 - Magnetic Standby Compass Liner

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B737/58 Magnetic Standby Compass Liner 7/91** Applicability: All model 737 aeroplanes line numbers 1683, 1687, 1693, 1697 & 1700 thru 1769. Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of FAA AD 91-07-14.

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1991.

Background: To prevent a high voltage electrical shock hazard to flight deck personnel.

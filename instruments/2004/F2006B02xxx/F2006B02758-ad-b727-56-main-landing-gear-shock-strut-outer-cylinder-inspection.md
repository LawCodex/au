# AD/B727/56 - Main Landing Gear Shock Strut Outer Cylinder - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/56 Main Landing Gear Shock Strut Outer Cylinder - Inspection

4/68

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect for cracking, all chrome plated main landing gear outer cylinder aft trunnion extensions which have been overhauled in a manner other than specifically described by Boeing 727 Overhaul Manual Section 32/12/1.

Compliance: Forthwith.

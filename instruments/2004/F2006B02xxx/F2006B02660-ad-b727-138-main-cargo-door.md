# AD/B727/138 - Main Cargo Door

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B727/138 Main Cargo Door 1/92

Applicability: Model 727 series as listed in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-22-04 Amdt 39-8064.

*Note: Boeing SB 727-52-0142 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with a revised effective date of 12 January 1992 for Australian Registered Aircraft.

Background: This AD arises from reports of worn latch cams and worn pressure relief door hinge fittings, and a report of a main cargo door that opened in flight. The effective date required by the FAA is extended by approximately one month.

# AD/AL 250/12 Amdt 2 - Pc Filter Flanged Insert - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Allison Turbine Engines - 250 Series

**AD/AL 250/12**

**Amdt 2**

**Pc Filter Flanged Insert - Inspection 1/71**

Applicability: All “Blue Ribbon” Allison 250-C18 series. Document: Allison 250 CEB-112, Rev. 1.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 12 January 1971 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours’ time in service until the insert is installed with EPOXYLITE 814.

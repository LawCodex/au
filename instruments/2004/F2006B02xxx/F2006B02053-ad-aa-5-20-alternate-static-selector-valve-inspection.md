# AD/AA-5/20 - Alternate Static Selector Valve - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-5 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AA-5/20 Alternate Static Selector Valve - Inspection NK

Applicability: Aeroplanes S/Nos. AA5A-0001 to AA5A-0340 and AA5B-0001 to AA5B-0451. Also all aeroplanes modified by the addition of Gerdes A-1390 alternate static valve shipped from Grumman prior to 19 March 1977.

Document: 1. Grumman American SB 160; or

2\. FAA AD 77-08-03.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 May 1977.

# AD/ARRIEL/4 - Turbine Case Identification Plate Removal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Turbomeca Turbine Engines - Arriel Series

AD/ARRIEL/4 Turbine Case Identification Plate Removal 8/86

Applicability: All Arriel Series I engines.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bureau Veritas Directive 86-29(B).

Reference Document - Turbomeca S.B. Arriel No. 72-292-0093.

Compliance: In accordance with Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require compliance with Bureau Veritas Directive 86-29(B). The Directive requires removal of the turbine module identification plate to allow inspection of the turbine case for corrosion. This requirement has become necessary following blow-out of a turbine case in service with a reported loss of power.

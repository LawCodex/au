# AD/B727/79 - Static Sensing Holes - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/79 Static Sensing Holes - Modification 6/77** Applicability: All model B727-100 and -200 aircraft with static sensing holes of .047 inch diameter. Document: 1. FAA AD 77-02-02; or

2\. Boeing SB No. 727-34-94.

Compliance: Within 3000 hours time in service after 30 June 1977.

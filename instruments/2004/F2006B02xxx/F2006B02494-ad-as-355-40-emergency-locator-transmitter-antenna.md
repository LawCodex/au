# AD/AS 355/40 - Emergency Locator Transmitter Antenna

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/40 Emergency Locator Transmitter Antenna 10/92

Applicability: All AS 355 helicopters, versions E, F, F1 and F2, fitted with a “Jolliet” emergency locator transmitter antenna, not modified per AMS 350A.07.2492 (Eurocopter France Service Bulletin AS 355 No 25.31 dated 30 March 1992).

Requirement: Modify the antenna in accordance with Eurocopter France Service Bulletin AS 355 No 25.33 dated 2 June 1992.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-142-045(B) dated 8 July 1992 refers.*

Compliance: Within 400 hours time in service from 17 September 1992 or by 1 April 1993, whichever occurs first.

Background: Compliance with this Directive improves the antenna resistance to vibrations and reduces the risk of loss in flight.

# AD/AL 60/11 - Engine Mounting Plates - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aermacchi - Lockheed AL 60 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/AL 60/11 Engine Mounting Plates - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models not incorporating Modification MB-L-001.

## 2/75

Requirement: 1. Inspect the engine mounting plates for cracks using a Dye Penetrant Technique in accordance with Aermacchi Lockheed Service Bulletin No. SB-L-0003.

2\. Modify the engine mounting plates in accordance with Aermacchi Lockheed Modification Bulletin No. MB-L-0001.

Compliance: For para. 1 - Within 10 hours’ time in service after 20 January 1975 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service until modified.

For para. 2 - Before achieving 1200 hours total time in service.

# AD/AS 355/23 - Hydraulic System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 355 (Twin Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/AS 355/23 Hydraulic System 12/86 DM

Applicability: All model AS 355 E, F, F1 and F2 helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale AS 355 Service Bulletin No. 29-01.

*Note: French AD 86-150-30(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 30 November 1986 or before 31 January 1987 whichever occurs first.

For spares: before installation in an aircraft after 30 November 1986.

Background: A spring spacer is fitted to prevent an overpressure in the hydraulic systems if the flange of the regulator valve stem, fitted to the filter unit, should come off.

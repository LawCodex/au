**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Lycoming) Turbine Engines - ALF502 and LF507 Series

## AD/ALF/4 Compressor Vanes, Turbine Blades and Turbine Nozzle - Inspection and Replacement

Applicability: Lycoming Turbo Fan Engine Models ALF 502-L/L2 and ALF 502-R3/R4. Document: 1. Avco Lycoming SB ALF 502-72-0012, Rev. 2.

2\. Avco Lycoming SB ALF 502-72-0025, Rev. 1.

3\. Avco Lycoming SBALF 502-72-0027.

4\. Avco Lycoming SBALF 502-72-0021; or

5\. AD/LYC-T/21.

*Note: FAA AD 82-25-10 Amendment 39-4511 refers.*

## 1/86

Compliance: In accordance with requirement documents.

Background: There have been reports of engine failures and loss of power on ALF 502-L/L2 engines caused by separation of first stage compressor vane airfoils and fourth stage turbine blades in addition there has been a non contained fourth stage turbine blade failure.

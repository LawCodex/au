# AD/AA-1/16 - Structural and Control Surface Bondlines - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American/ American Aviation) AA-1 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AA-1/16 Structural and Control Surface Bondlines - Inspection

9/76

Applicability: All models AA1 and AA1A with S/Nos. 0001 and subsequent and all model AA1B with S/Nos. 0001 to 0641.

Document: 1. Grumman American SB No. 155; or 2. FAA AD 76-17-03.

Compliance: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 21 September 1976 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours’ time in service until modified in accordance with SB No.

155\.

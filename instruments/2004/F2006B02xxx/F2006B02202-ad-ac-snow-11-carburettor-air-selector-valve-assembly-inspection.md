## AD/AC-SNOW/11 Carburettor Air Selector Valve Assembly - Inspection

Applicability: Model S-2R with S/Nos. 5000 and up. Document: Rockwell International SB No. SB-AG-8.

## 3/78

Compliance: Within 50 hours’ time in service after 30 April 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours’ time in service.

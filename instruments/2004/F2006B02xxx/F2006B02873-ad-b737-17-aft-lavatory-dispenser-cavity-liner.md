# AD/B737/17 - Aft Lavatory Dispenser Cavity Liner

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/17 Aft Lavatory Dispenser Cavity Liner 12/88

Applicability: All model 737 aeroplanes with line numbers 001 through 1425, equipped with non- modular aft lavatories.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the area behind each aft lavatory towel and cup dispenser if the dispenser cavities are not enclosed by a liner, and remove any foreign material.

2\. Unless already accomplished, install a protective liner to enclose each aft lavatory towel and cup dispenser cavity in accordance with Boeing SB 737-25-1219.

*Note: FAA AD 88-14-07 Amdt 39-5966 refers.*

Compliance: 1. At intervals not exceeding 1000 hours time in service, until modified in accordance with Boeing SB 737-25-1219.

2\. Before 31 December 1989.

Background: The FAA has determined that the installation of a liner at each lavatory towel and cup dispenser cavity will prevent the accumulation of combustible material behind the aircraft sidewall, and alleviate a possible fire hazard.

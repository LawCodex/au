# AD/APU/12 - Compressor Wheel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Auxiliary Power Units

AD/APU/12 Compressor Wheel 8/97

Applicability: Sundstrand Aerospace T-62T-40C series Auxiliary Power Units incorporating Compressor Wheel, P/No 162690-1, 165111-1 or 167200-1

Requirement: Replace affected compressor wheels in accordance with Sundstrand Aerospace Service Bulletin T-62T-49-120 Revision 2 dated 5 November 1996.

*Note: FAA AD 96-25-12 also refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 17 July 1997.

Background: The manufacturer has advised three compressor wheel failures have occurred as a result of fatigue cracks propagating from screw holes on the backface of the compressor wheel.

Compressor wheel rupture could result in an uncontained APU failure and damage to an aircraft.

# AD/ANSON/10 - Fuel Crossfeed Cock - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Avro Anson and Avro 19 Series Aeroplanes

AD/ANSON/10 Fuel Crossfeed Cock - Inspection 3/52

Applicability: Anson.

Requirement: Inspect the fuel crossfeed cock and ensure that the stop ring is NOT in a position which will prevent the cock from being moved to the ‘OFF’ position; or

ANO 105.1.31.4.6.2.

Compliance: At any time the fuel crossfeed cock is disturbed.

# AD/AL 250/48 Amdt 3 - Fourth Stage Turbine Nozzle P/N 6898694 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Allison Turbine Engines - 250 Series

**AD/AL 250/48**

**Amdt 3**

**Fourth Stage Turbine Nozzle P/N 6898694 - Inspection**

**5/83**

Applicability: Allison 250-C30, C30P and C30S engines and turbine assemblies as detailed in the requirement document.

Document: Allison 250-C30, C30P, C30S : CEB-A-72-3056, Rev. 3.

*Note: As FAA Airworthiness Directive No. 81-13-12 refers in part only. Compliance to this level does not constitute full compliance with Amendment 3 of this Directive.*

Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document upon receipt of this Directive. Background: This Directive was initially issued as the result of the receipt of FAA Directive No.

81-13-12. It is raised in issue to promulgate the changes made in Revision 3 of CEB-

A-72.3056 which includes wider model affectivity, additional inspections and adds parts life limitations.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AMD Falcon 10 Series Aeroplanes

AD/AMD 10/3 Total Pressure Probes - Modification 7/77

Applicability: All Falcon 10 aircraft not modified to SB F100096 and which are equipped with Badin Crouzet total pressure probes P/N 33790-10.

Document: 1. AMD SB BA F10-0095 (F10-34-006); or 2. SGAC AD 75-138-1.

Compliance: Forthwith.

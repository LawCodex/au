# AD/B737/70 - Thrust Reverser - Auxiliary Slider Track Liner

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 737 Series Aeroplanes

AD/B737/70 Thrust Reverser - Auxiliary Slider Track Liner 11/92

Applicability: All Model 737-300, -400 and -500 series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-25-06.

*Note: Boeing Service Bulletin 737-78-1048 Revision 1 refers.*

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 15 October 1992. Background: Inspection, repair and replacement as necessary of each thrust reverser auxiliary track,

track liner and slider fitting has been prompted by two in-flight incidents in which a

thrust reverser translating sleeve panel has separated from the aircraft.

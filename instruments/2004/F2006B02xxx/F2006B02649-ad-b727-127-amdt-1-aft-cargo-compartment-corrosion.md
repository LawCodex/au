# AD/B727/127 Amdt 1 - Aft Cargo Compartment Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Boeing 727 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/B727/127**

**Amdt 1**

**Aft Cargo Compartment Corrosion 2/93**

Applicability: Model 727-100 and -100C series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 92-19-10 Amdt 39-8368.

*Note: Boeing SB 727-53-0085 Revision 3 and Revision 4 refer.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document. The compliance of the previous issue of this Directive remains unchanged. This Amendment is effective from 4 February 1993.

Background: This Directive was prompted by reports to the FAA of corrosion in the cold bonded skin, doubler and triplets in the skin panels beneath the aft cargo compartment floor. Amendment 1 is issued in response to a new FAA AD which expands the applicability to include all Model 727-100 and -100C aircraft.

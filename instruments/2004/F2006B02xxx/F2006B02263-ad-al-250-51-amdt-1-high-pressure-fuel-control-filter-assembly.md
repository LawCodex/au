# AD/AL 250/51 Amdt 1 - High Pressure Fuel Control Filter Assembly - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Allison Turbine Engines - 250 Series

**AD/AL 250/51**

**Amdt 1**

**High Pressure Fuel Control Filter Assembly - Modification**

**6/83**

Applicability: All models 250-C20, C20B and C20J engines fitted with CECO P/N 6895173 or P/N 6896727 high pressure fuel control filter assemblies.

Document: Allison 250-C20, C20B and C20J Alert CEB-A-1144, Rev. 4.

*Note: FAA AD No. 82-13-03 refers.*

Compliance: For model 250-C20 and C20B : Forthwith.

For model 250-C20J : Not later than 1st August 1983.

Background: This Directive is issued to prevent possible metal contamination of the CECO Model MC-40 fuel control system power turbine governor which can result in power turbine rotor overspeed. It is raised in issue to Amdt 1 to include the model C20J as specified in Revision 4 of the Allison Document.

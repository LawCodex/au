# AD/P68/18 - Oil Cooler Elbow Fitting - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/18 Oil Cooler Elbow Fitting - Replacement 12/79

Applicability: All models P68 and P68B with S/Nos. 182 and below, except 74, 89, 133, 142, 155,

163 and 171.

Requirement: Replace the oil cooler left hand elbow fitting P/N AN822-6-6D in accordance with page No. 3 of Partenavia SB No. 45.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1979.

Background: In order to reduce strain on hose and fitting the oil cooler left hand elbow is replaced with an elbow of a more suitable angle.

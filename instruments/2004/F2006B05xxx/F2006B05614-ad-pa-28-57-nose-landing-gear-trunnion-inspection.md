# AD/PA-28/57 - Nose Landing Gear Trunnion - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-28/57 Nose Landing Gear Trunnion - Inspection 8/76

Applicability: All model 28R-200 with S/Nos. 7635001 to 7635249, 7635251 to 7635299, 7635302 to 7635318 and 7635321. In addition all models PA-28R-180 and PA-28R-200 that have had nose landing gear trunnion assembly P/N 67054-00V replaced since August 1975.

Document: Piper SB No. 510.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1976.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

Background: 1. The existing voltage regulator installation has been shown to interfere with the ADF system such as to cause erroneous bearing information to be displayed.

2\. The engine start circuit and the auto feather circuit are at present connected to the same circuit breaker. Opening this circuit breaker by an electrical fault or by pilot action will render both circuits inoperative.

# AD/PA-28/77 - Fuel Line Union Fittings - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-28/77 Fuel Line Union Fittings - Inspection 6/79

Applicability: Models: PA28-161 with S/Nos. 28-7816340 to 28-7916410.

PA28-181 with S/Nos. 28-7890276 to 28-7990429.

PA28-236 with S/Nos. 28-7911001 to 28-7911167.

PA28-201T with S/Nos. 28-7921001 to 28-7921028. PA28R-201 with S/Nos. 28R-7837150 to 28R-7837317. PA28R-201T with S/Nos. 28R-7803185 to 28R-7803373. PA28RT-201 with S/Nos. 28R-7918001 to 28R-7918128. PA28RT-201T with S/Nos. 28R-7931001 to 28R-7931187.

Document: Piper SB No. 638.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service from 30 June 1979.

Background: A number of fuel line union fittings throughout the aircraft fuel system may have been tightened insufficiently during manufacture and must be retorqued.

# AD/P68/39 - Fuel Selector Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/39 Fuel Selector Valve 4/94 DM

Applicability: All P68 series aircraft.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Partenavia Service Bulletin No 90.

*Note: RAI AD 94-014 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 18 February 1994.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that loss of a key from a fuel selector keyway has been reported to them.

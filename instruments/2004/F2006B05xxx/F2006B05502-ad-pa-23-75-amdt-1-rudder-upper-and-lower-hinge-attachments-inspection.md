# AD/PA-23/75 Amdt 1 - Rudder Upper and Lower Hinge Attachments - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-23 (Apache and Aztec) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-23/75**

**Amdt 1**

**Rudder Upper and Lower Hinge Attachments - Inspection**

**9/80**

Applicability: All models PA-23 and PA-23-160 with S/Nos. 23-1 and subsequent.

Requirement: 1. Remove the rudder upper hinge P/N 17063-03 and inspect the rudder upper hinge reinforcement bracket P/N 17067-18 or equivalent for cracks using a dye penetrant technique.

2\. Inspect the rudder lower hinge bracket assembly P/N 17289-00 in the bearing area for cracks using a dye penetrant technique.

3\. If cracks are found in the reinforcing bracket during the inspection of para. 1, remove the bracket and inspect the exposed spar web for cracks using a dye penetrant technique.

*Note 1: A 10X glass may be used in lieu of a dye penetrant technique for recurring inspections.*

*Note 2: FAA AD 78-08-03 Amdt. 39-3339 refers to this inspection.*

Compliance: 1 & 2 - Unless already accomplished, initially inspect within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1980 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1000 hours time in service or 3 years whichever occurs first.

Background: This amendment reflects the current FAA inspection requirements as referenced in FAA AD 78-08-03 Amdt. 39-3339.

# AD/CESSNA 180/43 - Fuel Quantity Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 180/43 Fuel Quantity Placard 4/76

Applicability: As detailed in Requirement Document.

Requirement: Install fuel quantity placards as detailed in Cessna SL SE75-7 and Supplement No. 1. Compliance: Cessna 180

Model 180 with S/Nos. as detailed in Cessna SL SE75-7 - within 100 hours time in service after 16 April 1976.

2\. Model 180 with S/Nos. prior to 52364:

(a) if currently fitted with one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later - within 100 hours time in service after 16 April 1976.

(b) Concurrent with fitting one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later.

Cessna 182

3\. Model 182 with S/Nos. as detailed in Cessna SL SE75-7 - within 100 hours time in service after 16 April 1976.

4\. Model 182 with S/Nos. prior to 62251:

(a) if currently fitted with one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later - within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1976.

(b) Concurrent with fitting one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later.

# AD/CESSNA 206/1 Amdt 1 - Plastic Control Wheel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 206/1**

**Amdt 1**

**Plastic Control Wheel 5/69**

Applicability: All models fitted with plastic control wheels.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL 62-44 and Cessna SL 64-8.

Compliance: Inspect in accordance with Cessna SL 62-44 at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Inspect in accordance with Cessna SL 64-8 at 500 hours time in service from new and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

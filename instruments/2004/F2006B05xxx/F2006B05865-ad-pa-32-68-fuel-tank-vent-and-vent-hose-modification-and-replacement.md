# AD/PA-32/68 - Fuel Tank Vent and Vent Hose - Modification and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-32 (Cherokee Six) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-32/68 Fuel Tank Vent and Vent Hose - Modification**

**and Replacement**

**9/80**

Applicability: Model PA-32-260 with S/Nos. 32-7800001 to 32-7800008; Model PA-32-300 with S/Nos. 32-7740109 to 32-7940290; Model PA-32R-300 with S/Nos. 32R-7780215 to 32R-7880068; Model PA-32RT-300 with S/Nos. 32R-7885001 to 32R-7985105; Model PA-32RT-300T with S/Nos. 32R-7787001, 32R-7887002 to 32R-7987126.

Document: Piper SB No. 646A.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1980.

Background: The fuel vent hose material fitted to these aircraft has proved unsuitable in service and may result in leakage of fuel and accumulation of fumes in the wing areas.

# AD/P166/32 - Flap Actuating Cylinder Attachment Bracket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piaggio P166 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P166/32 Flap Actuating Cylinder Attachment**

**Bracket - Inspection**

**11/66**

Applicability: All models P166, P166B and P166C fitted with bracket P/N 1409-01 which have exceeded 1500 hours time in service.

Document: Piaggio SB No. 166-53.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

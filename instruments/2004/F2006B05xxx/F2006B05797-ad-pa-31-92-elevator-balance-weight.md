# AD/PA-31/92 - Elevator Balance Weight

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-31/92 Elevator Balance Weight 9/80

Applicability: All models PA-31 & PA31-325 with S/Nos. 31-7401201 to 31-8012076; All models PA-31-350 with S/Nos. 31-7405401 to 31-8052172.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Piper SB No. 690.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1980.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that some of the referenced aeroplanes may have been fitted with incorrect elevator balance weights.

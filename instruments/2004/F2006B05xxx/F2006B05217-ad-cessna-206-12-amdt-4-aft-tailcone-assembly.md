# AD/CESSNA 206/12 Amdt 4 - Aft Tailcone Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 206/12**

**Amdt 4**

**Aft Tailcone Assembly 12/85**

Applicability: All model 206 Series Serial Nos. P2060001 to P2060647 and U2060001 to U20601905.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna S.L. SE72-29. Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

*Note: Inspections carried out in accordance with the previous issues of this Directive may be taken as meeting the requirements of Cessna SL SE72-3.*

Background: This Directive is amended to reflect the manufacturer’s modification to the subject structure during production of aircraft with subsequent serial numbers.

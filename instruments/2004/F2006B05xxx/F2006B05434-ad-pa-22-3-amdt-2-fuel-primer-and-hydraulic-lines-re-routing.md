# AD/PA-22/3 Amdt 2 - Fuel Primer and Hydraulic Lines - Re-Routing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-22 (Tri-Pacer and Colt) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-22/3**

**Amdt 2**

**Fuel Primer and Hydraulic Lines - Re-Routing 4/85 DM**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. To prevent contact with the exhaust muffler, re-route fuel primer line, and wheel brake hydraulic line if necessary, to ensure that they do not pass behind the muffler.

2\. Using clamps, secure the lines to the firewall - refer to Piper SL No. 213 for fuel primer line clamping instructions.

*Note: FAA AD 53-24-4 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 31 March 1985.

Background: This Directive has been amended to combine the intent of Piper SL No. 213 and FAA AD 53-24-4 into a single requirement. Re-routing, as applicable, of the fuel primer line and wheel brake hydraulic line is necessary to preclude the possibility of an in- flight fire.

# AD/P68/40 - Stabilator Torque Tube Attachment Shelves, Flap and Aileron Hinge Shelves

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/P68/40 Stabilator Torque Tube Attachment Shelves,

**Flap and Aileron Hinge Shelves**

Applicability: All Model P68 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Partenavia Mandatory Service Bulletin 91.

*Note: RAI AD 94-026 refers.*

## 5/94

Compliance: At the next 100 hour inspection after 28 April 1994, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed one calendar year.

Background: A case of corroded shelves was reported to the manufacturer.

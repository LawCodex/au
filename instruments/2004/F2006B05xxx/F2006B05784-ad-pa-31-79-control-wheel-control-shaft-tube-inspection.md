# AD/PA-31/79 - Control Wheel Control Shaft Tube - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-31/79 Control Wheel Control Shaft Tube - Inspection 6/79

Applicability: All models PA-31, PA-31-300 and PA-31-325 with S/Nos 31-5 to 31-7912059; PA- 31-350 S/Nos. 31-5001 to 31-7952111. All model PA-31P with S/Nos. 31P-1 to 31P- 7730012. All model PA-31T with S/Nos. 31T- 7400002 to 31T-7920060. All model PA-31T1 with S/Nos. 31T-7804001 to 31-7904033.

Requirement: To avoid possible separation of the control wheel control shaft stud from the control shaft tube due to the absence of two roll pins, and subsequent loss of aileron control of the affected control wheel, accomplish the following:

Secure control wheels in the aft position.

2\. Gain access to the control shaft tube assembly (P/N 42939-00) and to stud (P/N 42938-00) connection located just aft of the control wheel stop collar (P/N 40950- 00).

3\. Inspect the forward end of the control shaft tube assembly (P/N 42939-00) to determine if two roll pins (P/N 480732) are installed.

4\. If one or both roll pins are missing, install as required, roll pin Piper P/N 48732 prior to further flight.

*Note: Piper SB No. 651 refers to this subject.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 5 hours time in service after 18 May 1979. Background: The subject roll pins may not have been installed at aircraft manufacture.

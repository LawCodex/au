# AD/CESSNA 188/3 Amdt 1 - Main Undercarriage Legs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 188/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Undercarriage Legs 2/69**

Applicability: All models not fitted with spring leg P/No. 1641007.

Requirement: Inspect the main undercarriage spring legs for cracking in the vicinity of the bend adjacent to fuselage attachment points using the magnetic particle method.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

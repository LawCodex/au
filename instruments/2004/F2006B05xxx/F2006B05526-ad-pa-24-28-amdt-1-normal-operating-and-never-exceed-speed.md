# AD/PA-24/28 Amdt 1 - Normal Operating and Never Exceed Speed Limitations - Reduction

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-24 (Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-24/28**

**Amdt 1**

**Normal Operating and Never Exceed Speed Limitations - Reduction**

**2/75**

Applicability: Models PA24, PA24-250 and PA24-260. Requirement: Reduce Vno and Vne speeds as follows:

Aircraft not incorporating Rudder Balance Weight Installations Kit, Piper P/N 760705:

PA-24 reduce Vne to 160 kts. IAS; PA24-250 and PA24-260 reduce Vno to 147 Kts. IAS and Vne to 165 Kts. IAS.

2\. Aircraft incorporating Rudder Balance Weight Installation Kit, Piper P/N 760705:

PA-24 reduce Vne to 172 kts. IAS; PA24-250 and PA24-260 reduce Vno to 158 kts. IAS and Vne to 179 kts. IAS.

*Note 1: For PA24-250 and PA24-260 type aeroplanes incorporating Stabilator Piper Service Kit No. 760747 in addition to Rudder Piper Service Kit No. 760705 - Vne may be increased to 201 knots.*

*Note 2: This Directive reflects the contents of FAA AD Piper 72-22-5 Amdt. 39-1545 as amended by Amdt. 39-2052.*

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Before 10 November 1972.

For Para. 2 - Within 30 days after 10 November 1972.

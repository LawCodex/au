# AD/CESSNA 180/51 - Goodyear Fuel Cells

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 180/51 Goodyear Fuel Cells 6/78

Applicability: All Cessna models 180 and 182 and Wren 460 fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978 and annually thereafter as detailed in Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

# AD/CESSNA 207/7 Amdt 2 - Aft Tailcone Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 207/7**

**Amdt 2**

**Aft Tailcone Assembly 12/85**

Applicability: All 207 Series Serial Nos. 20700001 to 20700216. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE72-29.

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

*Note: Inspections carried out in accordance with the previous issues of this Directive may be taken as meeting the requirements of Cessna Service Letter SE72-3.*

Background: This Directive is amended to reflect the production changes to later model aircraft.

# AD/CESSNA 206/38 - Retirement Lives - Fatigue Critical Components

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 206/38 Retirement Lives - Fatigue Critical Components

6/85

Applicability: All model U206G and TU206G which have been modified in accordance with US FAA STC SA2353NM (Soloy Turbine Conversion).

Requirement: Action in accordance with Soloy Turbine Pac Maintenance and Overhaul Manual, Revision No. 2 dated 1 August 1984.

Compliance: All life limited components listed in Table I-8 of the Requirement document shall be retired from service before exceeding the lives quoted.

Background: The components listed have been assessed as critical to flight safety. Operation beyond their published retirement lives may increase the risk of failure.

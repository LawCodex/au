# AD/PA-36/17 - Rudder Spar - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-36 (Pawnee Brave) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-36/17 Rudder Spar - Inspection 4/77

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 36-7360001 to 36-7760003. Document: Piper SB No. 518.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until rudder is replaced.

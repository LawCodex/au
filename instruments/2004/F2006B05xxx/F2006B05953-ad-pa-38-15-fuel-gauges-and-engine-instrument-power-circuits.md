# AD/PA-38/15 - Fuel Gauges and Engine Instrument Power Circuits - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-38 (Tomahawk) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-38/15 Fuel Gauges and Engine Instrument**

**Power Circuits - Modification**

**11/79**

Applicability: All model PA-38-112 with S/Nos. 38-78A0002 to 38-78A0027, 38-78A0029 to 38- 78A0037, 38-78A0039 to 38-78A0041, 38-78A0043, 38-78A0045 to 38-78A0064,

38-78A0066 to 38-78A0072 and 38-78A0074 to 38-78A0104.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 603; or

2\. FAA AD 79-17-05.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1979.

Background: Fuel gauges and engine instruments have a common grounding point which under operating conditions has been shown to be unreliable. The modification installs a ground wire which will ensure normal operation.

# AD/PA-38/23 - Upper Forward and Aft Vertical Fin Spar - Inspection and Reinforcement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-38 (Tomahawk) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/PA-38/23 Upper Forward and Aft Vertical Fin

**Spar - Inspection and Reinforcement** Applicability: All model PA-38-112 with S/Nos. 38-78A0001 to 38-82A0122. Document: Piper Service Bulletin No. 763.

## 9/83

Compliance: On vertical fins which have achieved 500 hours time in service, within 25 hours time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until Kit 764-965 is installed.

Background: Field reports indicate that cracks have occurred in the area where the horizontal stabiliser attachment brackets attach to the upper end of the vertical fin forward and aft spars.

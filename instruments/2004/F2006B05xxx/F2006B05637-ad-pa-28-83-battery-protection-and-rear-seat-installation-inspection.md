# AD/PA-28/83 - Battery Protection and Rear Seat Installation - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-28/83 Battery Protection and Rear Seat Installation - Inspection

1/82

Applicability: All models PA-28-151 & PA-28-161 with S/Nos. referenced in Piper SB No. 631B or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues or revisions of that SB.

Document: Piper SB No. 631B. (FAA AD 81-23-05 Refers).

Compliance: 1. Part I: Before further flight after 25 November 1981, unless already

accomplished.

2\. Part II: Within 50 hours time in service after 25 November 1981, unless already accomplished.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of the seat base springs chafing the battery box cover and the springs subsequently contacting the battery terminals.

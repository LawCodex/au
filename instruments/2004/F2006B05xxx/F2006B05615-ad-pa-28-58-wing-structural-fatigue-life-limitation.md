# AD/PA-28/58 - Wing Structural Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-28/58 Wing Structural Fatigue Life Limitation 12/76

Applicability: All model PA-28-235. Requirement: Retire the wing from service.

Compliance: At or before 12,500 hours time in service.

# AD/P68/35 - LH and RH Muffler Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/35 LH and RH Muffler Assemblies 6/91

Applicability: All models excluding the ‘TC’ series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of RAI AD No 91-125/P68- 42.

*Note: Partenavia Service Bulletin No 83 refers.*

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document with revised effective date 30 June 1991.

Background: Due to the possibility of carbon monoxide entering the aircraft cabin through the heating system a repetitive inspection of the muffler assemblies has been introduced.

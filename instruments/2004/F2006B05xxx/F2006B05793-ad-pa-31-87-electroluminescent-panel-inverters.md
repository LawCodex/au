# AD/PA-31/87 - Electroluminescent Panel Inverters

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-31/87 Electroluminescent Panel Inverters 12/79

Applicability: Models PA-31T1 Cheyenne I S/Nos. 31T-7804001 to 31-7904016, and PA-31T1 Cheyenne/Cheyenne II S/Nos. 31T-7400002 to 31T-7920036.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Piper SB No. 640.

*Note: (FAA AD 79-19-09 Amdt. 39-3560 refers).*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1979.

Background: There have been a number of cases overseas where shorting has occurred in the electroluminescent panel inverters resulting in smoke in the cockpit.

# AD/CESSNA 207/29 - Rear Wing Spar Doubler

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/29 Rear Wing Spar Doubler 8/85

Applicability: All model 207 with S/Nos 20700001 to 20700767 (1969 to 1983).

(Normally aspirated and turbocharged models which have had a rear wing spar replaced with one shipped from Cessna Supply Division).

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna S.B. SEB 85-9.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 August 1985 or at the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 August 1985 whichever is the later.

Background: The manufacturer suspects that a quantity of spare rear wing spar assemblies may have been shipped from the Cessna Supply Division without a doubler installed near the attach fitting.

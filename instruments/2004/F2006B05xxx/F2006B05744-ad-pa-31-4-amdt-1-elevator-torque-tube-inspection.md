# AD/PA-31/4 Amdt 1 - Elevator Torque Tube - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-31/4**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Torque Tube - Inspection 12/68**

Applicability: All models not modified in accordance with Section 11 of Piper SB No. 284.

Requirement: Remove the elevator torque tube assembly from the aircraft and dismantle the horn assembly P/N 40006-00 and brackets P/N 40066-00 from the tube. Remove the finish from the tube and inspect for cracking using dye penetrant or magnetic particle methods.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

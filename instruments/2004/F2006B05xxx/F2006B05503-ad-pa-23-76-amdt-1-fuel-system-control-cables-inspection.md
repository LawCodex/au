# AD/PA-23/76 Amdt 1 - Fuel System Control Cables - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-23 (Apache and Aztec) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-23/76**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuel System Control Cables - Inspection 1/81**

Applicability: Models PA-23 and PA-23-160 with S/Nos. 23-1 to 23-2046 with auxiliary fuel systems only. Models PA-23, PA-23-160, PA-23-235, PA-23-250 and PA-E23-250

with S/Nos. 27-1 to 27-7954089.

Requirement: Using a 10X power magnification, inspect all fuel valve control cable wires at each swivel fitting, idler control arm and actuating lever for cracks, sharp radius bends or kinks, referring to Piper SB No. 507 for inspection locations.

*Note: FAA AD 80-18-10, Amdt. 39-3903 also refers to this subject.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1981 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Background: This Directive has been amended to include the additional serial numbered aircraft listed in FAA AD 80-18-10.

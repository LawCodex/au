# AD/PA-38/3 Amdt 1 - Vertical Fin/Rudder Leading Edge - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-38 (Tomahawk) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-38/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Vertical Fin/Rudder Leading Edge - Inspection and Modification**

**1/79**

Applicability: All models PA-38-112 with S/Nos. 38-78A0001 to 38-78A0215 and 38-78A0217 to 38-78A0226.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 607A, Parts I and II; or

2\. FAA AD 78-22-01, Amdt. 39-3323.

Compliance: 1. Inspect at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service; and

2\. Modify within 50 hours time in service after 20 November 1978.

# AD/PA-20/14 - Fire Hazard Prevention - Inspection and Rework

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-20 (Pacer) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-20/14 Fire Hazard Prevention - Inspection and Rework 4/85

DM

Applicability: All models. Requirement: Piper SB No 161A.

*Note: FAA AD 57-22-1 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time-in-service after 31 March 1985 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours.

Background: Following an in-flight fire it has been considered necessary to re-issue the requirements of cancelled AD/PA-20/4.

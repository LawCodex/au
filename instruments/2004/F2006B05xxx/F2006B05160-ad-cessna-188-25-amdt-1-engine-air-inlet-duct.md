# AD/CESSNA 188/25 Amdt 1 - Engine Air Inlet Duct

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 188/25**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Air Inlet Duct 6/75**

Applicability: All model A188 and A188B with S/Nos. 18800833 to 18801824 including those S/Nos. with a suffix "T".

Requirement: Action in accordance with either Cessna SL SE75-5 or SL SE74-23.

*Note: FAA AD’s 74-23-07 and 75-09-06 refer.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 12 May 1975.

# AD/CESSNA 185/44 - Instrument Panel Light Dimming Rheostat

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 185 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 185/44 Instrument Panel Light Dimming Rheostat 3/94

Applicability: Cessna Model 185D, 185E and 185F aircraft with serial numbers 185-0777 through 18502310 inclusive.

Requirement: Unless previously accomplished, replace the existing instrument panel light dimming rheostat (some installations will also require the fitting of a doubler) in accordance with the Accomplishment Instructions of Cessna Single Engine Service Bulletin SEB92-33 Revision 2 dated 10 September 1993.

*Note: FAA AD 93-24-15 Amdt 39-8764 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 3 October 1994.

Background: The FAA has advised of a report of an inflight fire in a Cessna Model 172.

The fire was caused by a short in the electrical wiring controlled by the instrument panel light dimming rheostat. This Directive requires the rheostat to be replaced with an improved version, which is both current-limiting and heat-protected, thus decreasing the probability of an inflight fire being caused by a defective rheostat.

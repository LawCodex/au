**Elevator and Aileron System Control Rod End Bearings - Inspection and Replacement**

**12/78**

Applicability: All models PA-36-285/300 with S/Nos. 36-7360001 to 36-7860116 and All model PA-36-375 with S/Nos. 36-7802002 to 36-7802049 except those S/Nos. omitted in SB No. 608A.

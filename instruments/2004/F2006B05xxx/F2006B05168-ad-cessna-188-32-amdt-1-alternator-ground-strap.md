# AD/CESSNA 188/32 Amdt 1 - Alternator Ground Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 188/32**

**Amdt 1**

**Alternator Ground Strap 9/82**

Applicability: All model 188 with S/Nos. 18800050 to 18803240. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL SE79-59.

*Note: FAA AD 79-25-07 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1982.

2\. Each time an engine is installed in an aircraft following initial compliance.

Background: Compliance has been amended with the addition of item 2 due to reports from industry that ground straps are being omitted from refitment on engine reinstallations.

# AD/P166/33 - Engine and Propeller Teleflex Controls - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piaggio P166 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/P166/33 Engine and Propeller Teleflex Controls - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All models P166, P166B and P166C incorporating Piaggio SB No. 166-51.

## 1/68

Requirement: 1. Inspect P/N 5206 swing bearings for security in the swinging joint terminal fitting P/N 6161.02, of all engine and propeller teleflex controls.

2\. Modify the joint and bearing of the engine and propeller controls in accordance with Piaggio SB No. 166-54.

Compliance: For para. 1 - Within 25 hours time in service after 10 January, 1968 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service until modified as required in para. 2.

For para. 2 - Before 1 June, 1968.

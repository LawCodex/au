# AD/CESSNA 188/8 Amdt 1 - Engine Mount

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 188/8

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Engine Mount 6/85

Requirement: Visually inspect the engine mount for cracking, paying particular attention to the rear engine mount bearers, rear cross tube, and upper firewall attach fittings.

Compliance: At each issue of a new Maintenance Release.

Background: This Directive has been amended to include the firewall attachment fittings following a recent fatigue failure.

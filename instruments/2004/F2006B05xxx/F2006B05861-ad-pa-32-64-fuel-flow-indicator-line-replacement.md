# AD/PA-32/64 - Fuel Flow Indicator Line - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-32 (Cherokee Six) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-32/64 Fuel Flow Indicator Line - Replacement 9/79

Applicability: Model PA-32RT-300T with S/Nos. 32R-7887001 to 32R-7987124. Document: 1. Piper SB No. 652; or

2\. FAA AD 79-13-04, Amendment 39-3500.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 19 July 1979.

Background: There are overseas reports of broken fuel flow indicator lines which have resulted in fuel leakage, engine power loss and one instance of a forced landing.

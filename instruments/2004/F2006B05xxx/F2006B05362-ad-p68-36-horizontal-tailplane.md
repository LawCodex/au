# AD/P68/36 - Horizontal Tailplane

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/36 Horizontal Tailplane 12/91 DM

Applicability: Model P68 series with serial numbers up to and including 327. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of RAI AD 91-272.

*Note: Partenavia SB 85 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; as specified in the Requirement Document but with a revised modification accomplishment date of 31 January 1992 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: This Directive initially requires the introduction of airspeed limitations. These limitations are subsequently removed when a modification to the horizontal tailplane is incorporated. The compliance time required by the RAI is extended by one month.

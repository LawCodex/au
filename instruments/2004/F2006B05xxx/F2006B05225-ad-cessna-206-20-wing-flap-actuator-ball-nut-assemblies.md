# AD/CESSNA 206/20 - Wing Flap Actuator Ball Nut Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 206/20 Wing Flap Actuator Ball Nut Assemblies 3/77

Applicability: Model U206 with S/Nos. as detailed in Cessna SL SE76-25.

Requirement: 1. Inspect P/N C301002-0101 or P/N C301002-0102 wing flap actuator for date stamp code as detailed in Cessna SL SE76-25.

2\. If the date stamp code on the actuator is OH, HH, WH or ZH, install a placard near the flap control which reads:

“FLAP EXTENSION PROHIBITED”

*Note: This placard may be removed when the Paragraph 3 requirement is satisfied.*

3\. Replace affected wing flap actuator ball nut assemblies in accordance with Cessna SL SE76-25.

Compliance: Paragraphs 1 and 2 - Before further flight after 28 January 1977.

Paragraph 3 - Within 100 hours time in service after 28 January 1977.

# AD/CESSNA 188/11 - Throttle Bellcrank

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 188/11 Throttle Bellcrank 4/69

Applicability: All models fitted with P/N 0750163-1 throttle control bellcranks. Requirement: Action in accordance with Supplement 1 of Cessna SL No. SE 68-28.

*Note: FAA AD 69-4-2 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 15 April 1969.

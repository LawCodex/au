# AD/P166/6 - Fire Detection System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piaggio P166 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P166/6 Fire Detection System - Modification NK

Applicability: All P166 series in transport category.

Requirement: Replace the existing nacelle heat detector system by an approved fire detector system complying with the requirement of ANO Section 101.1.3.1, Issue 2, paragraph 2.4.

Compliance: Forthwith.

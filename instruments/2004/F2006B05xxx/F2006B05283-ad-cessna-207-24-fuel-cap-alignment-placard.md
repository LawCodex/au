# AD/CESSNA 207/24 - Fuel Cap Alignment Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/24 Fuel Cap Alignment Placard 9/80

Applicability: Models 207 and T207 with S/Nos 20700001 to 20700588. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No SE 80-59.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1980.

Background: Correct alignment of flush fitting type fuel caps is essential for efficient operation of the fuel tank secondary vent system.

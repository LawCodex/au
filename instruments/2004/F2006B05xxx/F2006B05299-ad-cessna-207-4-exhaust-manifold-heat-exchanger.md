# AD/CESSNA 207/4 - Exhaust Manifold Heat Exchanger

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/4 Exhaust Manifold Heat Exchanger 6/71

Applicability: All turbocharged models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE71-11.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 9 June 1971 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

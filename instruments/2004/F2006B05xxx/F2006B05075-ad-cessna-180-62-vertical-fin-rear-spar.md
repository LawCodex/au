# AD/CESSNA 180/62 - Vertical Fin Rear Spar

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 180/62 Vertical Fin Rear Spar 12/80

Applicability: Model 180 with S/Nos. 18052385 to 18053147. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No. SE80-85. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1980.

Background: Overseas, cracks have been found in the flanges and web of the spar below the lower rudder hinge.

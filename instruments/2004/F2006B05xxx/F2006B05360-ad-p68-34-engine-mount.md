# AD/P68/34 - Engine Mount

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/34 Engine Mount 5/91 DM

Applicability: All P68C-TC up to serial number XXX-46TC.

All P68 OBS-TC up to serial number XXX-030 BTC.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of R.A.I. AD 91-83.

*Note: Partenavia SB 82 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 27 March 1991 or prior to 30 April 1991; whichever occurs first.

Background: Reports of cracks in the engine mount structure have been received. This Directive requires a detail inspection in the area of the lower attachment.

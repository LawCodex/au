# AD/CESSNA 207/23 - Turbocharger

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/23 Turbocharger 6/80

Applicability: Model 207 S/Nos. 20700001 thru 20700603. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No SE 80-24.

*Note: FAA AD 80-07-01, Amdt 39-3719 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1980.

Background: This Directive is issued to preclude a failure of the turbocharger anti-rotation pin and its ingestion into the scavenge pump. This in turn could cause a failure of the pressure and scavenge pump.

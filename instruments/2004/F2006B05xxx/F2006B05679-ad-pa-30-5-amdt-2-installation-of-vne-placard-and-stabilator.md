# AD/PA-30/5 Amdt 2 - Installation of Vne Placard and Stabilator - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-30/5**

**Amdt 2**

**Installation of Vne Placard and Stabilator - Modification**

**8/67**

Applicability: Model PA-30 with S/Nos. 853 and 902 to 1253.

Requirement: Rebalance the stabilator in accordance with Piper SB No. 229A and placard the airspeed indicator to show a never exceed speed (Vne) of 206 Kts. IAS.

Compliance Forthwith.

# AD/CESSNA 177/16 - Oil Pressure Gauge Line

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 177 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 177/16 Oil Pressure Gauge Line 3/74

Applicability: Model 177 with S/Nos. 17700001 to 17701160.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the oil pressure gauge line along the entire length from firewall to instrument panel, paying particular attention to the following areas:

(a) in the vicinity of the flap control switch;

(b) adjacent to the blast cooling nozzle of those aircraft fitted with radio cooling equipment.

2\. Adjust the line as required to prevent chafing. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 March 1974.

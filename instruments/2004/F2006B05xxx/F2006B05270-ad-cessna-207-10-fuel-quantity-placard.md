# AD/CESSNA 207/10 - Fuel Quantity Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/10 Fuel Quantity Placard 4/76

Applicability: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Install fuel quantity placards as detailed in Cessna SL SE75-7 and Supplement No 1.

Compliance: 1. Model 207 with S/Nos as detailed in Cessna SL SE75-7: within 100 hours time in service after 16 April 1976.

2\. Model 207 with S/Nos prior to 00226:

(a) if currently fitted with one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later - within 100 hours time in service after 16 April 1976.

(b) concurrent with fitting one or more replacement fuel cells bearing a manufacturing date of June 1973 or later.

# AD/CESSNA 180/46 - Horizontal Stabiliser Rear Spar Flange

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 180/46 Horizontal Stabiliser Rear Spar Flange 7/77

Applicability: All model 182 with S/Nos. listed in Requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE77-11.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1977.

# AD/CESSNA 190/4 - Upper Landing Gear Bulkhead

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 190 and 195 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 190/4 Upper Landing Gear Bulkhead 9/73

Applicability: All models 190 and 195.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE73-15.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 28 September 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

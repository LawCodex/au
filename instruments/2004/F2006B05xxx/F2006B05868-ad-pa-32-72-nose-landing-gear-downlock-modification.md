Applicability: PA-32R-300 Lance S/Nos. 32R-768001 to 32R-7880068. PA-32RT-300 Lance II S/N 32R-7885001 to 32R-7985105. PA-32RT-300T Turbo Lance II S/N 32R-7787001,

# AD/PA-18/11 Amdt 1 - Lower Horizontal Stabiliser Brace Wire Fuselage Attachment Tab - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-18 and 19 (Super Cub) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-18/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Lower Horizontal Stabiliser Brace Wire Fuselage Attachment Tab - Inspection and Modification**

**12/81**

Applicability: All models PA-18-150 with S/Nos. 18-8602 to 18- 8109013.

Document: Piper Service Bulletin No. 706A. (FAA AD 81-06-80 Letter of 13-3-81 refers).

*Note: Aircraft modified to Piper SB No. 706 do not require further rework.*

Compliance: 1. Unless already accomplished, inspect within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1981.

2\. Unless already accomplished, modify within 300 hours time in service after 31 December 1981.

Background: Revision “A” of Piper SB introduces a revised template shape for horizontal stabiliser brace wire additional support plate.

# AD/CESSNA 188/36 - Vertical Fin Rear Spar

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 188/36 Vertical Fin Rear Spar 12/80** Applicability: Model 188 with S/Nos. 18801375 to 18803725 and T18803307T to T18803725T. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No. SE80-85.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1980.

Background: Overseas, cracks have been found in the flanges and web of the spar below the lower rudder hinge.

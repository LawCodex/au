# AD/PA-30/9 - Installation of Vne Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-30/9 Installation of Vne Placard 7/70

Applicability: Model PA-30 with S/Nos. 30-1 to 30-852 and 30-854 to 30-901.

Requirement: Attach the following operating limitation placard to the airspeed indicator in full view of the pilot:

“DO NOT EXCEED 206 Kts. IAS”.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 8 July 1970.

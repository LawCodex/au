# AD/P68/12 - Propeller Control Rod End - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/P68/12 Propeller Control Rod End - Inspection

**and Replacement** Applicability: All models P68 and P68B with S/Nos. below 160. Document: Partenavia SB No. 37, Revision 1.

## 11/78

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 17 October 1978 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service until Part 2 of Partenavia SB No. 37, Revision 1 is completed.

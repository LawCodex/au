# AD/PA-34/9 - Rudder Servo Trim Tab - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-34 (Seneca) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-34/9 Rudder Servo Trim Tab - Inspection 6/73

Applicability: All models not incorporating modification kit P/N 760 800 V. Document: Piper SB No. 390A.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 25 June 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

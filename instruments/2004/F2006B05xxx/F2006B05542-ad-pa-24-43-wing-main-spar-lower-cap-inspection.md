# AD/PA-24/43 - Wing Main Spar Lower Cap - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-24 (Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-24/43 Wing Main Spar Lower Cap - Inspection 9/83

Applicability: All model PA-24-400 with S/Nos. 26-2 and subsequent. All model PA-24-180, PA- 24-250 and PA-24-260 with S/Nos. 24-1 and subsequent.

Document: Piper Service Bulletin No. 751.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30th September 1983.

Background: The manufacturer reports cases of chafing of the wing main spar lower cap where the spar enters the fuselage.

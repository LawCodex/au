# AD/CESSNA 185/34 - Fuel Cap Alignment Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 185 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 185/34 Fuel Cap Alignment Placard 9/80

Applicability: Model 185 with S/Nos. 632, 185-0001 to 185-1599 and 18501600 to 18503988. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No. SE 80-59.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1980.

Background: Correct alignment of flush fitting type fuel caps is essential for efficient operation of the fuel tank secondary vent system.

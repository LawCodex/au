# AD/CESSNA 188/29 - Induction Air Box Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 188 (Agwagon) Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 188/29 Induction Air Box Seal 6/77

Applicability: Model 188 with S/Nos. 18800001 to 18802348. Model A188 with S/Nos. 18800001 to 18800707.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE76-18.

*Note: FAA AD 77-04-05 Amendment 39-2838 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1977.

# AD/CESSNA 185/12 - Engine Mount

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 185 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 185/12 Engine Mount 7/68

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Visually inspect the engine mount for cracking, with particular attention to the rear engine bearers and rear cross tube.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

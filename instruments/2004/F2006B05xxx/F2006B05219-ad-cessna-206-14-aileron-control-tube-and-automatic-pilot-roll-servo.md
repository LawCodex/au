# AD/CESSNA 206/14 - Aileron Control Tube and Automatic Pilot Roll Servo

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 206/14 Aileron Control Tube and Automatic Pilot Roll Servo

2/72

Applicability: Models 206, P206 and U206 fitted with Mitchell Automatic Pilot Models AK120, AK120E or AK120E-3 not modified in accordance with Edo-Aire Mitchell SL ML32.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Edo-Aire Mitchell SB MB6.

*Note: FAA AD 72-2-6 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 February 1972.

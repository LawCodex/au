# AD/PA-24/35 - Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-24 (Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-24/35 Beryl Aviation Filtrator Assembly - Replacement 4/75

Applicability: Models PA24, PA24-250, PA24-260 series which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate No. SA2653WE (installation of engine crankcase breather and vacuum pump air-oil separator).

Requirement: 1. Remove the Beryl Aviation filtrator assembly P/N B-1113-1 and install either P/N B-1113-M or P/N B-1113-RM; or

2\. FAA AD No. 75-05-02.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1975.

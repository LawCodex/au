# AD/CESSNA 177/23 - Stabilator Trim Actuator Attach Bolt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 177 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 177/23 Stabilator Trim Actuator Attach Bolt 9/76

Applicability: All models 177 and 177RG with S/Nos. 17702255 to 02494, and 177RG0700 to 0937.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE76-14.

*Note: FAA AD 76-14-08 AMDT. 39-2671 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 18 August 1976.

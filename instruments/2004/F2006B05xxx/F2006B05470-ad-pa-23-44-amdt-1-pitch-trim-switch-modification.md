# AD/PA-23/44 Amdt 1 - Pitch Trim Switch - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-23 (Apache and Aztec) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-23/44**

**Amdt 1**

**Pitch Trim Switch - Modification 9/77**

Applicability: Models PA-23-250 and PA-E23-250 with S/Nos. 27-3837 27-4796, fitted with Rocker Type Trim Switch on Pilot’s Control Wheel.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 527; or 2. FAA AD 77-09-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1977.

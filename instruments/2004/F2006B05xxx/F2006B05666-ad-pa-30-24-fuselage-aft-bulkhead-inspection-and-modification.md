# AD/PA-30/24 - Fuselage Aft Bulkhead - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-30/24 Fuselage Aft Bulkhead - Inspection**

**and Modification**

**3/74**

Applicability: All model PA-30 with S/Nos. 30-1 to 30-2000 and all model PA-39 with S/Nos. 39-1 to 39-155 not incorporating kit Piper P/N 760783.

Document: Piper SL No. 679.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 March 1974 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified.

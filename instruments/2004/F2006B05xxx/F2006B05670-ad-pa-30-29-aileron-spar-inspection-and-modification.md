# AD/PA-30/29 - Aileron Spar - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-30/29 Aileron Spar - Inspection and Modification 1/77

Applicability: All models PA-30 and PA-39 with S/Nos. 30-2 to 30-2000; and 39-1 to 39-155 not incorporating Piper Kit No. 760914.

Document: Piper SL No. 787.

Compliance: Inspect within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified.

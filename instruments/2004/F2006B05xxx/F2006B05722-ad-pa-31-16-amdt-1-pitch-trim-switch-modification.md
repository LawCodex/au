# AD/PA-31/16 Amdt 1 - Pitch Trim Switch - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-31/16**

**Amdt 1**

**Pitch Trim Switch - Modification 9/77**

Applicability: Model PA-31 and PA-31-300 S/Nos. 31-2 to 31-797; PA-31-350 S/Nos. 31-5001 to 31-5004; and PA-31P S/Nos 31P-1 to 31P-109; fitted with Rocker Type switch on pilot’s control wheel.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 527; or

2\. FAA AD 77-09-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1977.

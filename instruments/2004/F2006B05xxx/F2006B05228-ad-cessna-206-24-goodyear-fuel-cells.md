# AD/CESSNA 206/24 - Goodyear Fuel Cells

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 206/24 Goodyear Fuel Cells 6/78

Applicability: All models 206, P206, U206 and TU206 fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978 and annually thereafter as detailed in Cessna SL No. SE78-10.

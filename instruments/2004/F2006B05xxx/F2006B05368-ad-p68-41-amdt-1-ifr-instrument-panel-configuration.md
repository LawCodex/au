# AD/P68/41 Amdt 1 - IFR Instrument Panel Configuration

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P68/41**

**Amdt 1**

**”IFR” Instrument Panel Configuration 1/95**

Applicability: P68 Observer, P68-TC Observer, and P68 Observer 2 aircraft with IFR flight approval.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Partenavia Service Bulletin 89.

*Note: RAI AD 94-242 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1995.

Background: The RAI requires modification of the “IFR” instrument panel to eliminate interference with the structure.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to the issue of a new RAI AD, which extends the time required for compliance.

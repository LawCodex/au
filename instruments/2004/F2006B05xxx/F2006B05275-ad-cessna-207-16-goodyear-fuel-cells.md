# AD/CESSNA 207/16 - Goodyear Fuel Cells

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 207 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 207/16 Goodyear Fuel Cells 6/78

Applicability: All models 207 and T207 fitted with Goodyear BTC-39 fuel cells. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No SE78-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1978 and annually thereafter as detailed in Cessna SL No SE78-10.

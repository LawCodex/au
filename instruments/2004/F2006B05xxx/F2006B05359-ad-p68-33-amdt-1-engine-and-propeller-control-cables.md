# AD/P68/33 Amdt 1 - Engine and Propeller Control Cables

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P68/33**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine and Propeller Control Cables 3/95 DM**

Applicability: Model P68 series aircraft, up to and including serial number 394, excluding serial number 391.

Requirement: 1. Inspect in accordance with Partenavia Mandatory Service Bulletin 81 Revision 1.

2\. Replace the cables in accordance with Partenavia Mandatory Service Bulletin 81 Revision 1.

Compliance: 1. Within 25 hours time in service after 11 January 1995 and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 25 hours time in service, until replacement in accordance with Requirement 2 of this Directive.

2\. Prior to 11 January 1996.

Background: A report was received of the failure of a part number NOR 7.171-6 propeller control cable. Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement Document which requires a more frequent inspection period prior to replacement of the cables.

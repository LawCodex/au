# AD/PA-31/21 Amdt 3 - Rudder and Nose Gear Steering Cable Assemblies - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-31/21**

**Amdt 3**

**Rudder and Nose Gear Steering Cable Assemblies - Inspection and Replacement**

**8/75**

Applicability: All model PA-31P with S/Nos. 31P-1 to 31P-7530019. All model PA-31T with S/Nos. 31T-7400002 to 31T-7520022.

Requirement: 1. Inspect rudder and nose gear steering cable assemblies for wear at all pulley and fair-lead locations.

2\. Replace rudder and nose gear steering cable assemblies by installing Piper Kit No. 760-923 in accordance with SB No. 446A.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1975 and

thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until replaced.

For Para. 2 - Within 300 hours time in service after 30 September 1975.

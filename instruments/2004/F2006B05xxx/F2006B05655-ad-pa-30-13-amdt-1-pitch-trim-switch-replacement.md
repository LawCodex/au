# AD/PA-30/13 Amdt 1 - Pitch Trim Switch - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-30/13**

**Amdt 1**

**Pitch Trim Switch - Replacement 2/77**

Applicability: Model PA-30 with S/Nos. 30-1717 and 30-1745 to 30-2000 and all model PA-39 with S/Nos. 39-1 to 39-155 fitted with rocker type switch on pilot’s control wheel.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 527; or FAA AD 77-09-10. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1977.

*Note: Aircraft complying with previous issues of this Directive, satisfy this issue of the Directive.*

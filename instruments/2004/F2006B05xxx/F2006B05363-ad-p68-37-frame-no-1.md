# AD/P68/37 - Frame No. 1

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P68/37 Frame No. 1 12/91

Applicability: Model P68C and P68C-TC with serial numbers up to and including 392. Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of RAI AD 91-243.

*Note: Partenavia SB 84 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 12 December 1991.

Background: Reports of cracks on frame No 1 around the nose landing gear attachment rivets.

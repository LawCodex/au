# AD/PA-30/8 - Vmc Placard - Revision

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-30/8 Vmc Placard - Revision 1/70

Applicability: Model PA-30 not incorporating Piper Kit 760 368 (ref. SL 552).

Requirement: Change the existing Vmc placard or install a new placard to reflect the following: “MINIMUM SINGLE ENGINE CONTROL SPEED 80 Kts. IAS”.

*Note: The work required here-in may bve performed and certified by the pilot or owner. Minimum lettering size shall not be less than that of the existing placard.*

Compliance: Before further flight after 26 November 1969.

# AD/PA-30/28 - Fin Forward Spar Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-30 & 39 (Twin Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-30/28 Fin Forward Spar Attachment - Inspection 9/76

Applicability: All models PA-30 and PA-39 with S/Nos. 30-1 to 30-2000 and 39-1 to 39-155.

Requirement: Visually inspect, with the aid of at least a 5X glass, the fin forward spar to fuselage attachment for cracks in the bend relief cutout radius of channel P/N 22903-00.

*Note 1: The part number quoted may not appear in the Parts Catalogue as it is a component of the fin front spar which is listed as a complete assembly under a separate part number.*

*Note 2: Piper SL No. 777 provides instructions for modifying the Fin Assembly to gain Visual access to the channel relief holes.*

*Note 3: This inspection is the subject of FAA AD 76-18-05.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 22 September 1976 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/CESSNA 185/21 - Engine Air Inlet Duct

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 185 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 185/21 Engine Air Inlet Duct 5/74

Applicability: All model A185 with S/Nos. 18502008 to 18502650. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE75-5.

*Note: FAA AD 75-09-06 refers.*

Compliance: Within 500 hours time in service after 12 May 1975.

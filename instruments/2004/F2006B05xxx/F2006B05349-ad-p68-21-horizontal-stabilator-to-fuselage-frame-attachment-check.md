# AD/P68/21 - Horizontal Stabilator to Fuselage Frame Attachment - Check Torque

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P68/21 Horizontal Stabilator to Fuselage Frame**

**Attachment - Check Torque**

**4/83**

Applicability: All Partenavia P68, P68B, P68C, P68C-TC and P68 Observer aircraft with S/Nos. 1 to 289 except S/Nos. 234, 256, 259, 269 and 288.

Requirement: Check torque AN4-6A bolts securing the horizontal stabilator torque tube brackets to fuselage frame. If necessary retorque to correct torque value 78-83 LB inches.

*Note: RAI AD 83-3 and Partenavia SB No. 57 refer.*

Compliance: Before further flight.

# AD/CESSNA 177/9 - Fuel Quantity Transmitter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 177 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 177/9 Fuel Quantity Transmitter 4/70

Applicability: Models 177 and 177A with S/Nos. 17700001 through 17701160, 17701165 through

17701168, 17701171, 17701174 through 17701178 and 17701180.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE69-25.

*Note: FAA AD 70-1-2 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 April 1971.

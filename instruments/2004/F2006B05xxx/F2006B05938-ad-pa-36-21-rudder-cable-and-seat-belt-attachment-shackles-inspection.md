# AD/PA-36/21 - Rudder Cable and Seat Belt Attachment Shackles - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-36 (Pawnee Brave) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-36/21 Rudder Cable and Seat Belt Attachment Shackles - Inspection

12/81

Applicability: All models PA-36-285/300 and PA-36-375 with S/Nos. referenced in Piper SB No.

729 or with S/Nos. referenced in subsequent issues or revisions of that service bulletin.

Document: Piper SB No. 729.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 10 November 1981.

Background: Some shackles P/N 486-566 (MS 20115-5) installed for rudder cable/horn attachment and the upper seat belt/cable attachment may have cracks.

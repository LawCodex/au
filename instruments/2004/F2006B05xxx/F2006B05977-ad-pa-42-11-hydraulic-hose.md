# AD/PA-42/11 - Hydraulic Hose

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-42 (Cheyenne III) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-42/11 Hydraulic Hose 6/86 DM

Applicability: All model PA-42 Cheyenne III with S/Nos 42-7800001, 42-7800002, 42-7801003, 42-7801004 and 42-8001001 to 42-8001106.

All model PA-42 Cheyenne IIIA with S/Nos 42-8301001, 42-8301002, 42-5501003 to 42-5501023, 42-5501025.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Piper Service Bulletin No. 822. Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 19 May 1986.

Background: A group of hydraulic hoses were manufactured using material that does not meet the pressure requirements for operation in the aircraft hydraulic system.

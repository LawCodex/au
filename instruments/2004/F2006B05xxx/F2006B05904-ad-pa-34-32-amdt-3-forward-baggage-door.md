# AD/PA-34/32 Amdt 3 - Forward Baggage Door

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-34 (Seneca) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-34/32**

**Amdt 3**

**Forward Baggage Door 7/88**

Applicability: Models PA 34-200 S/Nos. 34-7250001 to 34-7450220, PA 34-200T S/Nos.

34-7570001 to 34-8170092 and PA 34-220T S/Nos. 34-8133001 to 34-8633031 and

3433001 to 3433088.

Requirement: 1. Unless previously accomplished; action in accordance with Piper SBs 633B Parts 3 and 4.

2\. Unless previously accomplished; action in accordance with Piper SB 872.

*Note: FAA AD 88-04-05 refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 31 July 1988.

Background: The manufacturer has additional reports of the forward baggage door opening in flight. Investigation has indicated that damaged keys and keylocks are a factor. Amendment 3 of this Directive requires modification, if not already accomplished and additional work to Piper SB 872.

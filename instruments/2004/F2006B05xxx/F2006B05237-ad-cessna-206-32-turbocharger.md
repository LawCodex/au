# AD/CESSNA 206/32 - Turbocharger

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 206 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 206/32 Turbocharger 6/80

Applicability: Model U206 S/Nos. U2060487 thru U20605619;

Model P206 S/Nos. P2060001, P2060191 thru P20600647.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL No. SE 80-24.

*Note: FAA AD 80-07-01 Amdt. 39-3719 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1980.

Background: This Directive is issued to preclude a failure of the turbocharger anti-rotation pin and its ingestion into the scavenge pump. This in turn could cause a failure of both the pressure and scavenge pumps.

# AD/PA-38/24 - Elevator Control Travel - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-38 (Tomahawk) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-38/24 Elevator Control Travel - Modification 2/85

Applicability: All model PA-38-112 with S/Nos. 38-78A0001 to 38-82A0122. Requirement: Piper SB No. 800.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February 1985 or at the next scheduled inspection for maintenance release issue after 28 February 1985, which ever is the later.

Background: It has been reported that when either control wheel is full AFT and an upward-and- forward pressure is applied, the potential exists that the pilot may be unable to move the control yoke out of the full AFT position.

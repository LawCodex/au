# AD/PA-31/57 - Elevator Balance Weights - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-31/57 Elevator Balance Weights - Inspection 9/77

Applicability: All model PA-31 and PA-31-300 with wing luggage lockers per nayak STC. SA 986 SW.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the elevator balance weights fitted in the nose channel of the elevator for security of attachment (“Rivnut” pull-up) and check elevator unbalance (96.4 inch lbs. tail heavy maximum); or

2\. FAA AD 77-12-02 amdt. 39-2915.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 19 August 1977.

# AD/CESSNA 180/30 - Aileron Control Tube and Automatic Pilot Roll Servo

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 180/30 Aileron Control Tube and Automatic**

**Pilot Roll Servo**

**2/72**

Applicability: Models 182E, 182F, 182G and 182H fitted with Mitchell Automatic Pilot Models AK088, AK088R, AK088E, AK110, AK110E, AK110E-3, AK154, AK154E or

AK154E-3 not modified to Edo-Aire Mitchell Service Letter ML32. Requirement: Action in accordance with Edo-Aire Mitchell Service Bulletin MB-6.

*Note: FAA AD 72-2-6 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 February 1972.

# AD/PA-24/26 Amdt 1 - Pitch Trim Switch - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-24 (Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/PA-24/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Pitch Trim Switch - Modification 5/71**

Applicability: Model PA-24-260 with S/Nos. 24-4783 to 24-5047 fitted with rocker type trim switch on pilot’s control wheel.

Document: 1. Piper SB No. 527; or

2\. FAA AD 77-09-10.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February, 1977.

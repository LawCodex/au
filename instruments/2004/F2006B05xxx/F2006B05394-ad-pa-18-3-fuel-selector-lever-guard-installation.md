# AD/PA-18/3 - Fuel Selector Lever - Guard Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-18 and 19 (Super Cub) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-18/3 Fuel Selector Lever - Guard Installation NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Install a suitable guard to prevent the possibility of inadvertent operation of the fuel selector lever. A wooden block at least 1 inch thick, shaped to clear the arc of the lever, and attach to the existing indicator plate, would be suitable for the purpose.

Compliance: Forthwith.

Cessna 205 (210-5) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 205/6**

**Amdt 2**

**Auxiliary Fuel Pump Electrical System 12/70**

Applicability: Model 205 and 205A with S/Nos. 205-0001 to 205-0577. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE69-9, or Cessna SL SE70-25. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 August 1969.

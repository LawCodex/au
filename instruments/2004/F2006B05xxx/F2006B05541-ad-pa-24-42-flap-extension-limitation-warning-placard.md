# AD/PA-24/42 - Flap Extension Limitation - Warning Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-24 (Comanche) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-24/42 Flap Extension Limitation - Warning Placard 6/83

Applicability: Models PA-24, PA-24-250, PA-24-260 series which have been modified in accordance with FAA Supplemental Type Certificate SA2495WE (Robertson STOL modification kit).

Requirement: 1. To prevent possible loss of aeroplane control due to an asymmetric flap extension or retraction, install a placard on the panel near the flap actuator which reads:

“DO NOT EXTEND FLAPS BEYOND 15 DEGREES (TAKEOFF POSITION)”.

2\. The placard may be removed when a positive flap retraction system is installed in accordance with Robertson Aircraft Corporation Service Bulletin 19 dated 22 October 1982.

*Note: FAA AD 82-23-01 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 16 May 1983.

Background: An accident investigation has shown that a loss of aeroplane lateral control can occur if an asymmetric flap condition is allowed to develop, in subject aircraft fitted with the Robertson STOL modification kit. A Robertson Service Bulletin has been issued in order to overcome the problem.

# AD/CESSNA 180/59 Amdt 1 - Alternator Ground Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 180/59**

**Amdt 1**

**Alternator Ground Strap 9/82**

Applicability: All model 180 with S/Nos. 18051762 to 18052981 except 18052975.

All models 182/A182/F182 with S/Nos. 18257446 to 18266590, A1820001 to A1820146, F18200001 to F18200094.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SIL SE79-59.

*Note: FAA AD 79-25-07 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1982.

2\. Each time an engine is installed in an aircraft following initial compliance.

Background: Compliance has been amended with the addition of Para 2 due to reports from industry that ground straps are being omitted from refitment on engine reinstallations.

# AD/CESSNA 185/18 - Wing Strut Attachment Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 185 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 185/18 Wing Strut Attachment Strap 9/73

Applicability: All model 185 with S/Nos. 18502199 to 18502238. Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE73-20.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 24 September 1973.

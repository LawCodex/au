# AD/PA-31/119 - Aileron Control System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-31 Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-31/119 Aileron Control System 4/93

Applicability: Model PA-31 Series.

Requirement: Colour code the aileron balance cable and the bellcrank lug of both aileron control systems with red enamel paint in accordance with Figure 1 of this Directive.

*Note: FAA AD 92-27-05 Amdt 39-8438 refers.*

Compliance: The next time the aileron cables are disconnected for any reason or prior to 1 April 1994, whichever occurs first.

Background: The FAA has received several reports of improper connections of the aileron control systems on the affected aircraft.

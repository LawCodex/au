# AD/P68/29 Amdt 1 - Elevator Trim Tab

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Partenavia P68 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/P68/29**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Trim Tab 1/89**

Applicability: All P68 up to serial number 381 included. Requirement: Action in accordance with Partenavia SB 73 Rev 1.

*Note: RAI AD 88-167 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 20 July 1988.

Background: To reduce the flexibility of stabilator tabs with seven ribs or eight ribs, modification or replacement is required. This will increase control authority. Amendment 1 calls up Rev 1 of the Requirement document which may alleviate the action required for aircraft with more than 3500 hours T.I.S.

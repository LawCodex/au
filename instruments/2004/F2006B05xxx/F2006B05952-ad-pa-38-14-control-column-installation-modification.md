# AD/PA-38/14 - Control Column Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piper PA-38 (Tomahawk) Series Aeroplanes

AD/PA-38/14 Control Column Installation - Modification 11/79

Applicability: All models PA-38-112 with S/Nos. 38-78A0001 to 38-79A1160. Document: Piper SB No. 661.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1979.

Background: It has been reported to the manufacturer that roughness or binding of the control shaft in the instrument panel can occur at the extremes of control movement.

# AD/CESSNA 180/37 Amdt 1 - Wing Strut Attachment Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/CESSNA 180/37**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Strut Attachment Strap 9/73**

Applicability: All model 180 with S/Nos. 18052335 to 18052349 and all model 182 with S/Nos.

18261960 to 18262101 and 18262104.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL SE73-20 (Supplement No. 1). Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 24 September 1973.

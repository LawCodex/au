# AD/CESSNA 208/2 - Fuel Reservoir Tank and Wing Fuel Bay Primer

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 208 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/CESSNA 208/2 Fuel Reservoir Tank and Wing Fuel Bay Primer

Applicability: Models 208 and 208A, S/Nos 20800001 through 20800108.

Model 208B, S/Nos 208B0001 through 208B0008.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna S.B. No. CAB 87-14.

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement document after 20 November 1987.

## 1/88 DM

Background: The manufacturer has requested that a fuel sample be taken and inspected to determine if primer particles are in the fuel system. Additional inspections and possible repairs will be required for the fuel reservoir tank and/or wing fuel bays if primer particles are found.

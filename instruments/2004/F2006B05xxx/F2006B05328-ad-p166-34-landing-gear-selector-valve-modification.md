# AD/P166/34 - Landing Gear Selector Valve - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Piaggio P166 Series Aeroplanes

AD/P166/34 Landing Gear Selector Valve - Modification 11/68

Applicability: All model P166 fitted with selector valve P/N 5-5233 which does not have the suffix Rev. A on valve name plate.

Document: 1. Piaggio SB No. 166-56; or

2\. RAI AD 68-173/P166-31, Rev. 1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 November 1968.

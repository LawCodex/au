# AD/CESSNA 180/45 - Induction Air Box Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Cessna 180, 182 and Wren 460 Series Aeroplanes

AD/CESSNA 180/45 Induction Air Box Seal 6/77

Applicability: Model 180 with S/Nos. 18050662 to 18052711. Model 182 and Wren 460 with

S/Nos. 18251557 to 18264790.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Cessna SL No. SE76-18.

*Note: FAA AD 77-04-05 Amendment 39-2838 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1977.

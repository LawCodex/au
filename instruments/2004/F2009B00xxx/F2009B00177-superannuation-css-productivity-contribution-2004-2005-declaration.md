# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2004-2005) Declaration 2004

Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution\
(2004–2005) Declaration 2004

Statutory Rules 2004 No. 1381

I, ARTHUR ZIKAS, Manager of the Member Accounts Section, Commonwealth Superannuation Administration and delegate of the CSS Board, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 17 June 2004

ARTHUR ZIKAS

Delegate of the CSS Board

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2004–2005) Declaration 2004.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on the date of its notification in the *Gazette*.

###### 3 Productivity contributions

For section 110D of the S*uperannuation Act 1976*, the table in subsection 110C (1) of that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 2004, as if the amounts specified in the following table were substituted for the amounts specified in that table.

Table

| Fortnightly rate of salary | Fortnightly productivity contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $1 409.33 | $42.28 |
| $1 409.33 or more, but less than $2 270.67 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $2 270.67 or more, but less than $3 406.00 | $68.12 |
| $3 406.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**Note**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 24 June 2004.

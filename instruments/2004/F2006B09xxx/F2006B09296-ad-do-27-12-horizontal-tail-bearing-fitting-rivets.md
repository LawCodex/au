# AD/DO-27/12 - Horizontal Tail Bearing Fitting Rivets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Dornier DO-27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DO27/12 Horizontal Tail Bearing Fitting Rivets 13/96 DM

Applicability: All Series all Serial Numbers.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Daimler Benz Aerospace/Dornier SB1140-0000 dated 29 September 1995.

*Note*: *LBA AD 96-271 refers.*

Compliance: Within 30 days after 09 October 1996.

Background: Loosening of rivets is possible at the bearing flange, and “L” profile and profile of the frame.

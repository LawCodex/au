# AD/EMB-110/30 Amdt 1 - Flap Control and Protection Unit - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/30**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Control and Protection Unit - Modification 4/85**

Applicability: All models equipped with multiposition flap control switch. Document: Embraer SB 110-27-073 R1.

*Note: Brazilian AD 84-01-01 refers.*

Compliance: Part I: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1984. Part II: Within 250 hours time in service after 30 April 1985.

Background: a. Inadvertent flap extension due to short circuit in flap system control and protection unit have been reported.

b. Amendment 1 includes a further modification to prevent tripping the Flap control circuit breaker when the fire extinguishing system is actuated.

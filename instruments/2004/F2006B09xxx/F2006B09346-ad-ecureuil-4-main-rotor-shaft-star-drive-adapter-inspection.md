# AD/ECUREUIL/4 - Main Rotor Shaft Star Drive Adapter - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 350 (Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

## AD/ECUREUIL/4 Main Rotor Shaft Star Drive Adapter - Inspection

Applicability: All models fitted with main rotor shaft assembly P/N 350A.37.0003. Document: AS 350 SB 65.01.

## 8/79

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 5 July 1979, and thereafter in accordance with paragraph D(2) of the requirement document.

Background: It is possible to install the rotating star drive adapter incorrectly on the rotor shaft.

# AD/F50/7 - Dorsal Fin Gutter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/7 Dorsal Fin Gutter 3/89 DM

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20144. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-53-003.

*Note: RLD AD 88-85 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1989.

Background: Freezing of cable systems has occurred at high altitude. Rain, high humidity and low OAT were factors. Installation of a gutter is to prevent ingress of rainwater.

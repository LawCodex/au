# AD/DO-27/11 - Wing Flap Position Selector

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Dornier DO-27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DO-27/11 Wing Flap Position Selector 2/89

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dornier Service Bulletin 1116-0000.

*Note: LBA AD 88-109 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 March 1989.

*Note: Flight Manual to be amended to G1 status restricting maximum flap deflection to 35 degrees prior to 1 March 1989 unless accomplished previously.*

Background: This modification is to improve the longitudinal controllability of the aircraft in the landing configuration.

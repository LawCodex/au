# AD/F28/42 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Bolt Retention - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F28/42**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Hinge Bolt Retention - Inspection and Modification**

**8/80**

Applicability: All models F28 with S/Nos. 11003 to 11145, 11147, 11150, 11151, 11991 and 11992.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the retention of the horizontal stabiliser hinge bolts in accordance with RLD AD No. 79-89-55, or Fokker F28 SB No. F28/55-20.

2\. Modify the retention of the horizontal Stabiliser hinge bolts in accordance with RLD AD No. 80-37-55, OR Fokker SB No. F28/55-21.

Compliance: 1. Before 1 June 1980.

2\. Before 1 November 1980.

An overseas operator experienced migration of a horizontal stabiliser hinge bolt after loss of the retaining pin. This AD amendment adds a modification to provide back-up to bolt retention.

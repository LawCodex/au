# AD/EMB-110/17 - Fire Detector Bracket Assembly P/N 4A-513-21-02 - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/EMB-110/17 Fire Detector Bracket Assembly P/N 4A-513-21-02 - Modification

Applicability: All models EMB-110 with S/Nos. referenced in Embraer SB No. 110-26-003. Document: 1. Embraer SB No. 110-26-003; or

2\. CTA AD No. 82-02-02.

Compliance: Within 250 hours time in service after 30 September, 1982.

## 5/82

Background: Damage to the fire detector installation may occur during removal and/or installation of the engine upper cowling.

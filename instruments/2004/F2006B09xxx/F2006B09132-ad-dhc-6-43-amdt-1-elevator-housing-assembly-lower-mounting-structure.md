# AD/DHC-6/43 Amdt 1 - Elevator Housing Assembly Lower Mounting Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-6/43**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Housing Assembly Lower Mounting Structure**

**9/91**

Applicability: Model DHC-6 with serial numbers 2 through 494, 497 through 503, 505 and 507.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of Transport Canada AD CF- 77-05 R1.

*Note: De Havilland SB 6/348 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: This amendment is raised to reflect transport Canada’s ageing aircraft program, which requires terminating action rather than repetitive inspections for known critical service problems.

# AD/DHC-6/63 - Gust Lock Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-6/63 Gust Lock Assembly 3/90 DM

Applicability: S/Nos 1 to 690, 692, 693, 695 and 696. Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Canada SB 6/508.

*Note: Canadian AD CF-90-01 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1990.

Background: Three instances have been reported where aircraft took off with pre-mod 6/1676 gust locks still engaged.

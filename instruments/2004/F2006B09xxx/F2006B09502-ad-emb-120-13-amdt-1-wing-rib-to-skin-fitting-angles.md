# AD/EMB-120/13 Amdt 1 - Wing Rib to Skin Fitting Angles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-120 (Brasilia) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-120/13**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Rib to Skin Fitting Angles 12/95**

Applicability: All Model EMB-120 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of CTA AD 95-05-01R1 Amdt 39-746.

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

The original issue of this directive became effective on 20 July 1995.

Background: The Brazilian CTA received reports of cracks in the wing rib to skin fitting angles (shear clips) in both the upper and lower wing skins.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement Document which requires a final corrective action.

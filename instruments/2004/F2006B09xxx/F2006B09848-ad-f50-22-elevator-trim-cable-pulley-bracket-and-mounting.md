# AD/F50/22 - Elevator Trim Cable Pulley Bracket and Mounting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/22 Elevator Trim Cable Pulley Bracket and Mounting 8/89

Applicability: Serial numbers 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20123.

Requirement: Reinforce elevator trim cable pulley brackets and mountings in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-27-008 dated 3 February 1989.

*Note: Netherlands AD 89-24 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to the accumulation of 8000 flight cycles.

Background: Stress analysis has shown that the increased tension in the elevator trim cables will decrease the fatigue life of the pulley brackets and mountings.

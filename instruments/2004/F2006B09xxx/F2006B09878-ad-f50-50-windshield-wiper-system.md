# AD/F50/50 - Windshield Wiper System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/50 Windshield Wiper System 1/92

Applicability: Model F27 MK 050 series with serial numbers 20213 through 20227. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of RLD AD 91-130.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-30-016 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with a revised effective date of 12 January 1991 for Australian Registered Aircraft.

Background: This Directive requires the windshield Piper system to be modified as it does not meet the specifications of the vendor.

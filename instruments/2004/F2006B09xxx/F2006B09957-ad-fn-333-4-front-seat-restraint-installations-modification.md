# AD/FN-333/4 - Front Seat Restraint Installations - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti FN-333 (Riviera) Series Aeroplanes

AD/FN-333/4 Front Seat Restraint Installations - Modification NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Modify each front seat restraint installation by fitting a safety harness in accordance with a sche me approved by the Secretary.

*Note: Guidelines for installation schemes are outlined in Airworthiness Advisory circular No. 62 dated March 1972.*

Compliance: Before 1 January 1974.

# AD/EMY/10 - Inflatable Escape Slide Regulators - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/10 Inflatable Escape Slide Regulators - Inspection 6/73

Applicability: All escape slides fitted with international inflatables regulator P/N 70003.

Requirement: 1. Inspect all International Inflatables regulators P/N 70003 for evidence of corrosion both inside and outside of the regulator assembly.

If any evidence of corrosion exists replace the regulator before further flight with a corrosion free regulator inspected in accordance with this Directive or a regulator approved by the Secretary.

2\. Inspect all spare International Inflatables regulators P/No. 70003 in accordance with paragraph 1.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Before 1 September 1973 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 4 months.

For Para. 2 - Immediately before installation.

# AD/DHC-8/12 Amdt 1 - Emergency Lighting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-8/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Emergency Lighting 9/91**

Applicability: All DHC-8 series aircraft S/No 1 through 179.

Requirement: Action IAW Boeing Canada de Havilland SB 8-33-17 Revision B or later revision.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-90-08 refers.*

Compliance: The compliance date of the previous issue of this Directive was 17 May 1991. This revision is effective from 5 September 1991.

Background: In the current configuration the aircraft does not comply with the emergency lighting requirements of FAR 25.812. Transport Canada have advised of their intention to mandate compliance with this SB.

Amendment 1 to this AD is issued to correct a typographical error in the Requirement Document reference number.

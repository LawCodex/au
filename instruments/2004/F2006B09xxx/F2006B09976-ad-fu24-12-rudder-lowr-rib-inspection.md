# AD/FU24/12 - Rudder Lowr Rib - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/12 Rudder Lowr Rib - Inspection 6/68

Applicability: All models not incorporating James Aviation Mod. HA/FU24/116.

Requirement: Detach the torque tube from the rudder and examine the rudder lower rib assembly P/N 242406 at the attachment of the rudder torque tube flange P/N 242410 for cracking and corrosion.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

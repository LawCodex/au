# AD/F50/51 - Auto Feather Control Unit

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/51 Auto Feather Control Unit 2/92

Applicability: All F50 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Netherlands AD BLA 91-134 dated 22 November 1991.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-61-011 and Pratt and Whitney Canada SB No 21046 refers.*

Compliance: By 30 September 1992.

Background: A number of inadvertent auto feather incidents have been reported.

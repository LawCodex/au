# AD/DHC-6/58 - Wing Struts Internal Beam Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-6/58 Wing Struts Internal Beam Corrosion 9/85

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with De Havilland Aircraft of Canada Service Bulletin No.

6/474.

Compliance: As per “Requirement” Document.

Background: The manufacturer reports two struts on the same aircraft displaying exfoliation corrosion in the strut beam flanges enclosed by the strut skins.

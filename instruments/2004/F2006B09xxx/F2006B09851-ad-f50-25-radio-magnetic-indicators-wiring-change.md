# AD/F50/25 - Radio Magnetic Indicators Wiring Change

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/25 Radio Magnetic Indicators Wiring Change 13/89

Applicability: F27 Mark 50 aircraft with Serial Numbers 20103 to 20108 inclusive and 20110 to 20141 inclusive

Requirement: Modify in accordance with Fokker 50 Service Bulletin SBF 50-34-013.

*Note: RLD Airworthiness Directive BLA 89-097 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 April 1990.

Background: Errors of up to 5 degrees are possible on the RMIs due to incorrect wiring. These exceed permissible limits.

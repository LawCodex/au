# AD/F50/3 Amdt 1 - Wing Structure - Skin Splice WS 7915

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F50/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Structure - Skin Splice WS 7915 4/89**

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20127.

Requirement: 1. Restrict Vmo in accordance with Fokker Telefax ME 88.5877 dated 29 June 1988 until modified in accordance with Fokker S.B. F50/57-001.

*Note: Speed restriction may be achieved by placing placards adjacent to ASIs and including the Fokker Temporary Change in the Flight Manual.*

2\. Action in accordance with Fokker S B F50-57-001 Rev 1.

*Note: RLD BLA 88-54 part 3 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Before further flight after 1 July 1988.

2\. Prior to 31 December 1989.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that due to faulty heat treatment the strength of the skin splice at WS 7900 is less than specified. Amdt 1 includes terminating modification action.

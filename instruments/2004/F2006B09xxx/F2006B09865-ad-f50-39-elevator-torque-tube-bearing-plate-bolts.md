# AD/F50/39 - Elevator Torque Tube Bearing Plate Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/39 Elevator Torque Tube Bearing Plate Bolts 2/91

DM

Applicability: F27 Mark 050 aircraft with S/Nos 20103 through 20184.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Netherlands AD BLA 90-104.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-55-004 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to accumulating 500 hours time in service after revised date of 4 December 1990, or prior to revised date of 1 June 1991, whichever occurs first.

Background: Country of origin AD. It was found that, during production, bolts were tightened to a torque load that was too high. To prevent failure, replacement of bolts is required.

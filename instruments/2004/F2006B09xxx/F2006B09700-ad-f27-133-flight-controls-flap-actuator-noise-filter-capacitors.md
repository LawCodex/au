# AD/F27/133 - Flight Controls - Flap Actuator Noise Filter Capacitors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F27/133 Flight Controls - Flap Actuator Noise Filter Capacitors

Applicability: All Model F27 (except Mk 050).

## 11/92

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F27/27-135 dated 1 June 1992 and Lucas Aerospace Power Equipment Corporation SB D7-9-27-01 dated 15 May 1991.

*Note: RLD AD 92-076 dated 10 July 1992 refers.*

Compliance: Within 500 time in service from 15 October 1992 or before 1 January 1993, whichever occurs first.

Background: Fokker advises that one operator recently experienced the detachment of a flap actuator noise filter capacitor, which subsequently caused damage to the lower aileron cable. This Directive requires the capacitors to be inspected and, if necessary, replaced to prevent similar occurrences.

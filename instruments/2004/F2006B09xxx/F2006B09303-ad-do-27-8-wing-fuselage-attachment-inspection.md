# AD/DO-27/8 - Wing Fuselage Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Dornier DO-27 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DO-27/8 Wing Fuselage Attachment - Inspection NK** Applicability: All D0-27Q and H series not modified in accordance with Dornier TB no. 27-33. Document: Dornier TB No. 27-33.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 15 April 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

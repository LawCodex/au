# AD/F27/134 Amdt 1 - Main Wing Tank and Collector Tank Fuel Line

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F27/134**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Wing Tank and Collector Tank Fuel Line 1/93**

Applicability: Fokker F27 aircraft (except Mk 050), all Serial Numbers.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of the Netherlands Directive BLA 92-102 Issue 2 dated 23 October 1992.

*Note: Fokker Service Bulletin F27/28-61 dated 3 September 1992 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with an effective date of 7 January 1993. Background: This amendment is raised to incorporate Issue 2 of the Netherlands AD into this

Directive. The AD requires inspection of a fuel line between the main wing tank and

the collector tank for damage which could result in fuel leaks.

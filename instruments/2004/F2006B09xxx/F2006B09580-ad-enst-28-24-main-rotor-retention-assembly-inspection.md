# AD/ENST 28/24 - Main Rotor Retention Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

AD/ENST 28/24 Main Rotor Retention Assembly - Inspection 10/82

Applicability: All model F-28A, F-28C, F-28F, 280, 280C and 280F. Document: Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No. 0057.

*Note: FAA AD 82-16-09 Amdt. 39-4429 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1982, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

Background: Manufacture reports misalignment of the nylon shims has worn grooves in the inner surfaces of the blade grip.

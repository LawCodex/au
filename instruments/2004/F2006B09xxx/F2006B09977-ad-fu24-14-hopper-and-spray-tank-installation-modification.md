# AD/FU24/14 - Hopper and Spray Tank Installation - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/14 Hopper and Spray Tank Installation - Modification 6/68

Applicability: All models manufactured prior to S/Nos. 120 not modified in accordance with James Aviation JA/FU24/M92 or Air Parts SB AP14.

Requirement: Modify the hopper installation in a manner approved by the Secretary to withstand a 12g forward loading in an emergency alighting case.

Compliance: Forthwith.

# AD/ECUREUIL/3 - Vibration Absorber Mounting Flange - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 350 (Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

## AD/ECUREUIL/3 Vibration Absorber Mounting Flange - Inspection

Applicability: All models not incorporating modification AMS 350.07.6063. Document: Aerospatiale AS 350 SB 05.03.

## 8/79

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 4 July 1979 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service.

Background: Several cracks have been found at the blending radius of the vibration absorber mounting flange installed on the main rotor shaft.

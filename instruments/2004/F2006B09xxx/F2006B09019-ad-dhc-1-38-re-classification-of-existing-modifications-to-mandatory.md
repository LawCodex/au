# AD/DHC-1/38 - Re-Classification of Existing Modifications to Mandatory Status

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
De Havilland DHC-1 (Chipmunk) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DHC-1/38 Re-Classification of Existing Modifications

**to Mandatory Status**

Applicability: All DHC-1 Chipmunk aircraft.

## 7/97

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Technical News Sheet (TNS) CT (C1) No. 200.

*Note 1: TNS CT (C1) No.200 also informs operators how to determine that the required modifications have been incorporated on their aircraft.*

*Note 2: UK CAA AD 006-03-97 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 19 September 1997.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 19 June 1997.

Background: The UK CAA requires that certain modifications be incorporated, in order to ensure the continued airworthiness of Chipmunk aircraft.

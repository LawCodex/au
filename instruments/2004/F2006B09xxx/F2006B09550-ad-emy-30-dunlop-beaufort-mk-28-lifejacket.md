# AD/EMY/30 - Dunlop-Beaufort Mk 28 Lifejacket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/30 Dunlop-Beaufort Mk 28 Lifejacket 9/93

Applicability: All lifejackets if used in conjunction with an immersion suit. Requirement: Action in accordance with Dunlop-Beaufort Service Bulletin No 131. Compliance: Prior to 19 May 1994.

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 008-04-93 refers.*

Background: The Service Bulletin introduces a modification to increase the nominal buoyancy of the lifejacket to ensure immersion suit compatibility.

# AD/F50/64 - Seat Rails Tapered Shims

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/64 Seat Rails Tapered Shims 5/94

Applicability: Model F27 Mk 050 Series aircraft, serial numbers 20103 through 20258. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-25-045.

*Note: RLD AD 94-033 (A) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 November 1994.

Background: The ageing of the tapered nylon shims that are installed under the seat rails from fuselage STA.14025 aft, may result in a reduction in strength of the seat rail attachments. This Directive requires the removal of the shims and the replacement of the current seat rail attachment bolts with shorter ones.

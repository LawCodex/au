# AD/F50/9 - Door External Handles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/9 Door External Handles 3/89

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-52-007.

*Note: RLD AD 88-91 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1989.

Background: Excessive wear in the door external handle mechanism allows moisture ingress and may subsequently freeze the door operating mechanism.

# AD/EMB-110/23 - Trim Tab Control Systems - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/EMB-110/23 Trim Tab Control Systems - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models EMB-110 with S/Nos. 110001 to 110364 and 110366 to 110384. Document: Embraer SB No. 110-27-062 Parts I and II.

*Note: Brazilian AD 82-11-03 refers.*

Compliance: PART 1: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 January 1983.

## 3/83

PART II: Within 125 hours time in service or one month, whichever occurs first, after 25 January, 1983.

Background: Investigation by the manufacturer revealed that the attaching nuts of the actuating rod ends of the trim cab systems may become loose.

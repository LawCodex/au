# AD/EMB-110/20 Amdt 1 - Primary Flight Control Cables

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/20**

**Amdt 1**

**Primary Flight Control Cables 11/89**

Applicability: All model EMB-110 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer Service Bulletin 110-27-064 Rev 2.

*Note: Brazilian AD 82-09-08 R2 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 3 January 1983, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1200 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer reports that cases of primary control cables with excessive wear and broken wires have been noted. Amendment 1 references latest Requirement document which extends repeat inspection period.

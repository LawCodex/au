# AD/F50/2 - Aircraft Flight Manual and Minimum Equipment List Amendment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F50/2 Aircraft Flight Manual and Minimum Equipment List Amendment**

**9/88**

**TX**

Applicability: F27 Mk 050 aircraft serial numbers 20103 to 20108 inclusive, and 20110 to 20120 inclusive.

Requirement: (1) Amend Aircraft Flight Manual to incorporate RLD approved amendment AFM/50-081, dated May 09/88 concerning 2.09.01, 5.00.00, 5.02.01, 5.05.01.

(2) Aircraft cannot be dispatched with the TAT indication inoperative unless the engine anti-icing system and the ignition system is operative and switched on in visible moisture.

(3) Amend Master Minimum Equipment List AIA 34 page 2, version 01, issue 003.

*Note 1: Netherlands Airworthiness Directive BLA nr. 88.37 refers.*

*Note 2: Formal Flight Manual amendment will be issued by applicable Regional Airworthiness Office.*

Compliance: (1) Prior to 25 June 1988.

(2) Effective 25 June 1988.

(3) Prior to 9 July 1988.

Background: This Directive has been issued to reflect the requirements of Netherlands Airworthiness Directive BLA nr. 88.37. The Directive requires amendment to the Aircraft Flight Manual and the Master Minimum Equipment List. The amendments are required to prevent power loss in heavy rain and icing conditions.

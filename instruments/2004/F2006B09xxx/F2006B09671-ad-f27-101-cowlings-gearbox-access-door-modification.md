# AD/F27/101 - Cowlings - Gearbox Access Door - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/101 Cowlings - Gearbox Access Door - Modification 11/80

Applicability: All model F27 with S/Nos. as detailed in Fokker F27 SB No. F27/71-27. Document: Fokker F27 SB No. F27/71-27.

Compliance: Before 1 June 1981.

Background: SB No. F27/71-27 introduces a modification designed to prevent departure of the aeroplane with an unlocked gearbox access door.

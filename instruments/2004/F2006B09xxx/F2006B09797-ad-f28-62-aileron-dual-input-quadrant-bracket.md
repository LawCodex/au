# AD/F28/62 - Aileron Dual Input Quadrant Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/62 Aileron Dual Input Quadrant Bracket 13/90

Applicability: F28 aircraft with S/Nos 11003 through 11241, 11991 and 11992. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F28/27-177.

*Note: AD BLA 90-097 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1991, unless previously accomplished.

Background: Country of origin AD. Severe pitting corrosion on the mounting brackets has been reported, together with incorrect installation of bushings.

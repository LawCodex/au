# AD/EMY/29 - RFD Liferaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/29 RFD Liferaft 7/93

Applicability: RFD liferafts Type 10R MK 1 and SAR MK 1 with Serial Numbers 10R 152 through 10R 1027 and liferafts Type 14R MK 1 with Serial Numbers 14R 213 through 14R 1020.

Requirement: Action in accordance with RFD LIMITED Service Bulletin 25-69.

*Note: UK CAA AD 001-02-93 refers.*

Compliance: At the next repack or scheduled overhaul interval of the liferaft after 24 June 1993.

Background: This Directive requires a check to be made of the liferafts for the correct location of the bridge piece attached patches, as detailed in Service Bulletin 25-46, together with repair procedures if the installation is found to be incorrect. This condition could result in some restriction of gas flow into the fender tubes and boarding ramps.

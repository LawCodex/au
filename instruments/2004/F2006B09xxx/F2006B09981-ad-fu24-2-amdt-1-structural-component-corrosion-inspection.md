# AD/FU24/2 Amdt 1 - Structural Component - Corrosion - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/FU24/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Structural Component - Corrosion - Inspection 7/68**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect fuselage longerons, centre wing, main and rear spars, wing attachment fittings and other major structural components for signs of corrosion and effect any necessary repairs.

*Note: This inspection will involve dismantling the aircraft as it is not provided with sufficient inspection panels to complete inspection of the main structure.*

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 3 years for aircraft engaged in agricultural operations. At intervals not exceeding 5 years for aircraft engaged in operations other than agricultural.

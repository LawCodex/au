# AD/F27/100 - Main Entrance Door - Check of Emergency Operation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F27/100 Main Entrance Door - Check of Emergency Operation

Applicability: All models F27 with S/Nos. as listed in RLD AD 79-106-52. Document: 1. RLD AD 79-106-52; or

2\. Fokker SB No. F27-52-61.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 1 May 1980.

## 5/80

Background: There is a possibility of malfunction of the emergency operation of the main cabin door.

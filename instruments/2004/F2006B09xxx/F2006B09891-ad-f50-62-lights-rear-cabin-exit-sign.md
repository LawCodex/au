# AD/F50/62 - Lights - Rear Cabin Exit Sign

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/62 Lights - Rear Cabin Exit Sign 12/93

Applicability: F27 Mk 050 serial numbers identified in the requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of RLD Airworthiness

Document BLA 93-106 (A).

*Note: Fokker Service Bulletin SBF50-33-028 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 11 May 1994.

Background: In some aircraft configurations, the exit sign on the cargo compartment door is obscured from view.

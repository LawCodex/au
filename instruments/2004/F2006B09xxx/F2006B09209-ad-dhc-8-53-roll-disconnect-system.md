# AD/DHC-8/53 - Roll Disconnect System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/53 Roll Disconnect System 6/98

Applicability: DHC-8 aircraft, Models 102, 103, 106, 201, 202, 301, 311, 314, and 315, with serial

numbers 3 through 433.

Requirement: Install Modification 8/2376 in accordance with de Havilland Service Bulletin 8-27- 79, Revision A.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-98-04 refers.*

Compliance: Before 21 November 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 21 May 1998.

Background: Several incidents have been reported of uncommanded roll system disconnects during landing.

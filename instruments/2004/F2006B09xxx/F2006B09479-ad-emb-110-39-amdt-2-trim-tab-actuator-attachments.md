# AD/EMB-110/39 Amdt 2 - Trim Tab Actuator Attachments

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/EMB-110/39

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Trim Tab Actuator Attachments 12/87

Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer SB110-027-0083. Compliance: Within 500 hours time in service after 3 December 1987.

Background: The Department has received reports of fractured spring pins on Australian aircraft. Pin failure could result in control surface flutter. Amdt 1 of this Directive extended compliance to all trim tab actuators. Amdt 2 of this Directive references SB110-027- 0083 which replaces existing spring pins with solid pins.

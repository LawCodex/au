# AD/F50/4 - Fire Sensing Element (Fire Wire) Bonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/4 Fire Sensing Element (Fire Wire) Bonding 1/89

Applicability: All F27 MK 050 aircraft serial numbers 20103 to 20108, and 20110 to 20123 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-26-003.

*Note: Dutch AD BLA 88-46 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 26 February 1989.

Background: Fokker have advised that undetected unserviceabilities of the fire sensing element (fire wire) can occur due to faulty bonding of the element.

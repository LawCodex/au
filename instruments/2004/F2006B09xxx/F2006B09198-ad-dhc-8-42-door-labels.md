# AD/DHC-8/42 - Door Labels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/42 Door Labels 12/95

Applicability: Model DHC-8 aircraft, all series, with serial numbers 3 through 402.

Requirement: Carry out the label replacement procedure of the Accomplishment Instructions of de Havilland Service Bulletin 8-11-14.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-87-07R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 30 November 1995.

Background: Instances have been reported of passengers mistaking the passenger exit door handle for the toilet door handle and depressurising the aircraft while searching for the toilet.

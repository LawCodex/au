# AD/F50/55 - Autoflight - Autopilot Computer - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/55 Autoflight - Autopilot Computer - Replacement 6/92

Applicability: All aircraft specified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: In accordance with the Requirement Document Fokker Service Bulletin F50-22-005. Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document

Background: The autopilot engage button on the Flight Mode Panel apparently exhibits a tendency to stick in the \`engage’ position. As a result the autopilot re-engages when either an autopilpot disconnect button on the control wheel or a \`go-around’ button on the power levers is released. A modification to the autopilot computer is required.

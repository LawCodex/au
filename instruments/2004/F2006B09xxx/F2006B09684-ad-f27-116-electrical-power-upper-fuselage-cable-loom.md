# AD/F27/116 - Electrical Power - Upper Fuselage Cable Loom

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/116 Electrical Power - Upper Fuselage Cable Loom 1/89

Applicability: All F27 Mk 500 aircraft serial numbers 10623 to 10692 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F27/24-77 or later.

*Note: RLD BLA 88-40 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 26 April 1989.

Background: Chafing of the cable loom on the upper fuselage has been reported by Fokker.

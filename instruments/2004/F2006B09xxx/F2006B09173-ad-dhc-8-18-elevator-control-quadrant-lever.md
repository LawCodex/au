# AD/DHC-8/18 - Elevator Control Quadrant Lever

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/18 Elevator Control Quadrant Lever 4/92

Applicability: Model DHC-8 series 100 with serial number 1 through 126 inclusive. Requirement: In accordance with Boeing Canada de Havilland Division SB A8-27-45 Rev A.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-88-25 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; within 50 hours time in service after 2 April 1992. Background: An elevator control quadrant lever was found to be incorrectly installed on a Dash 8.

The incorrect installation caused permanent deformation of the associated push rod.

If not corrected, this condition could result in loss of associated elevator control.

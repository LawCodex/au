# AD/F50/11 - Wing Centre Section Cable Tray Blanking Plate

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/11 Wing Centre Section Cable Tray Blanking Plate 3/89

DM

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20129. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-57-003.

*Note: RLD AD 88-95 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1989.

Background: Freezing of the cable system has occurred at high altitude, rain, high humidity and low OAT were factors. Installation of this blanking plate will prevent water ingress to this area.

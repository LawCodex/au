# AD/DHC-2/20 - Control Column Torque Tubes - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-2/20 Control Column Torque Tubes - Inspection 9/80

Applicability: All models DHC-2 and DHC-2 Mk. III. Document: De Havilland Aircraft of Canada SB No. 2/28.

Compliance: Initially inspect within 25 hours time in service after 18 September 1980 and thereafter as noted in De Havilland Aircraft of Canada SB No. 2/28.

Background: Three overseas operators have reported cracks in the horizontal torque tube at the base of the control column.

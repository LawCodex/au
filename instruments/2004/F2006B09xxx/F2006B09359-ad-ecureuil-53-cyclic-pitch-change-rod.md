# AD/ECUREUIL/53 - Cyclic Pitch Change Rod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 350 (Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/ECUREUIL/53 Cyclic Pitch Change Rod 11/94

Applicability: Models B, BA, D, B1 and B2 fitted with cyclic pitch change control rod P/No 704A34-113-279, and not fitted with an auto-pilot.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter AS 350 Service Bulletin 01-42.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-180-069(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 13 October 1994.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that some control rods may not be “in safety”.

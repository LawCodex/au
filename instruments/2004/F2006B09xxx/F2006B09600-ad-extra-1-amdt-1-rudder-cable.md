# AD/EXTRA/1 Amdt 1 - Rudder Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Extra EA-300 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/EXTRA/1

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All Models EA-300 aircraft.

## Rudder Cable 4/93 TX

Requirement: Inspect the rudder control cables for correctly swaged Nicopress type sleeves at each end of the cable by using the appropriate "go, no-go" gauge. If a compressed sleeve does not conform to the above inspection, the cable assembly shall be replaced.

*Note: EXTRA Mandatory SB 300-1-93 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 12 February 1993.

Background: A report has been received of an incident in which a loss of rudder control was implicated in an accident. Although still under investigation, it was reported that the cable had pulled through an incorrectly swaged sleeve. Amendment 1 is issued in response to the requirements of EXTRA Service Bulletin 300-1-93.

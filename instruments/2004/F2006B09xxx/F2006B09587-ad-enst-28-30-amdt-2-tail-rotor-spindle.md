# AD/ENST 28/30 Amdt 2 - Tail Rotor Spindle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

**AD/ENST 28/30**

**Amdt 2**

**Tail Rotor Spindle 3/93**

Applicability: All F-28F, 280F or 280FX model helicopters and any F-28C, F-28C-2, or 280C models that have been converted to use the 28-150064-11 or -13 spindle.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No 0077 Revision B. Compliance: As specified in the requirement document but with a revised effective date of

26 March 1992.

Background: This amendment raised in sequence to add the -13 spindle to the AD requirements and to place a service life limit of 1200 hours on the 28-150064-11 and -13 spindles.

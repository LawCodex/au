# AD/DHC-2/11 - Aileron Hinge Arm - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-2/11 Aileron Hinge Arm - Inspection 3/65

Applicability: All models with aileron hinge arms having more than 3000 hours time in service. Document: Hawker de havilland TNS DHC-2 Aust. No. 3.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 200 hours time in service.

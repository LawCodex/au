# AD/F50/17 - Fuel Collector Tank Supply Line

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/17 Fuel Collector Tank Supply Line 5/89

Applicability: Model F27 Mark 050 with serial numbers 20104 through 20108.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the fuel collector tank supply line (fwd line looking in the direction of flight) and the sheepskin - covered edges of the feedthrough holes at STA 3038 of both engine nacelles for evidence of chafing.

2\. Action in accordance with Fokker 50 SB No. F50-28-003.

*Note: RLD AD BLA 89-18 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 65 hours time in service after 31 May 1989, until modified in accordance with Fokker 50 SB No. F50-28-003.

2\. Prior to 31 August 1989.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that a modification to enlarge the STA 3038 nacelle feedthrough hole and fit ant-chafe material to the fuel supply line, will eliminate interference and possible fuel leakage.

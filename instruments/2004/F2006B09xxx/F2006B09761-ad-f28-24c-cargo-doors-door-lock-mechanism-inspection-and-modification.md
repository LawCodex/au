# AD/F28/24C - Cargo Doors - Door Lock Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/24C Cargo Doors - Door Lock NK Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 11003 to 11993.

Requirement: 1. Inspect door locking mechanism as per Part 2 of SB F28/52-53 by aircraft S/No. as referenced in applicable SB.

2\. Modify door locking mechanism by fitting a reinforced safety catch and stop assembly, detailed in SB F28/52-51 on aircraft with S/nos. referenced in that service bulletin.

3\. Further modify door locking mechanism by fitting a revised switch operating mechanism as detailed in SB F28/52-49 on aircraft with S/No. referenced in that service bulletin.

4\. Further modify the cargo door locking mechanism by reinforcing the door locking handle access panel as detailed in SB F28/52-81 on aircraft with S/Nos. and cargo door configurations referenced in that SB. RLD AD A118/77 also refers.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Before 1 September 1974.

For Para. 2 - Before 1 January 1975.

For Para. 3 - Before 1 January 1975.

For Para. 4 - Before 15 July 1978.

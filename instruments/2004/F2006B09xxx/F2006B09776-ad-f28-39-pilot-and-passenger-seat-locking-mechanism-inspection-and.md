# AD/F28/39 - Pilot and Passenger Seat Locking Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/39 Pilot and Passenger Seat Locking Mechanism - Inspection and Modification

4/79

Applicability: All model F28 aeroplanes fitted with “IPECO” Seats in pilot compartment or in locations other than pilot compartment.

Document: 1. RLD AD No. 79/05/25; or

2\. Fokker SB No. F28/25-82.

Compliance: In accordance with requirement document No. 1 except that initial inspection shall be performed within 50 hours time in Service after 1 March 1979.

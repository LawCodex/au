**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/46 Aileron Control Cables - Inspection 1/83

Applicability: All model F28 S/Nos. 11003 and subsequent. Document: 1. Netherlands AD 82-41-27.

2\. Fokker Alert Service Bulletin F28/27-A150. Compliance: As per requirement document 1.

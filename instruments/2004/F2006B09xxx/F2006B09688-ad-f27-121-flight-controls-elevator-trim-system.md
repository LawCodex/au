# AD/F27/121 - Flight Controls - Elevator Trim System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/121 Flight Controls - Elevator Trim System 2/91

Applicability: F27 aircraft with S/No’s 10102 to 10684 inclusive, 10686, 10687 and 10689 to 10692 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Netherlands AD BLA 90-106.

*Note: Fokker SB F27/27-130 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement document with effective date 5 October 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. To modify the elevator and trim tab systems so that they are considerably more resistant to flutter and easier to maintain.

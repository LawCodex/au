# AD/DHC-2/19 Amdt 1 - Aileron Control Cables - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-2/19**

**Amdt 1**

**Aileron Control Cables - Inspection 10/80**

Applicability: All models DHC-2 Mk. II and Mk. III.

Document: De Havilland Aircraft of Canada SB No. 2/27, Rev. A. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1980.

Background: Investigations by the manufacturer have revealed that incorrect rigging tensions are being applied to the non- flexible steel cables in some cases.

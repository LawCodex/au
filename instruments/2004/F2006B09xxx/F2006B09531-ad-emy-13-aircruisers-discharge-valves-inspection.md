# AD/EMY/13 - Aircruisers Discharge Valves - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/13 Aircruisers Discharge Valves - Inspection 1/78

Applicability: All Air Cruisers Company inflatables with discharge valves P/N D29517 series and D18259 series.

Document: Air Cruisers Company SIL 114-77-2.

Compliance: Before 1 June 1978 and thereafter at any time inflatable is removed for inspection.

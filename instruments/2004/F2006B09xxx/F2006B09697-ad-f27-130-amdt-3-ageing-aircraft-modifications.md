# AD/F27/130 Amdt 3 - Ageing Aircraft Modifications

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F27/130**

**Amdt 3**

**Ageing Aircraft Modifications 11/94**

Applicability: Model F27 (except Mk 050), with serial numbers as listed in Appendices I and II of the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of RLD AD 91-058/5 (A) Correction.

*Note: Fokker Report No SE-278, Issue No 3 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

The compliance of the previous issue of this Directive remains unchanged. This Amendment is effective from 13 October 1994.

Background: The Country of Origin Airworthiness Authority requires terminating action in lieu of special repetitive inspections to insure the continued structural integrity of older aircraft.

Amendment 1 introduced a revision of SB F27/55-61.

Amendment 2 introduced a revision of SB F27/57-68, and the re-issue of Fokker Report No SE-278.

Amendment 3 is issued to introduce a correction of Fokker SB F27/57-68 Revision 1 in Appendix I of the Requirement Document.

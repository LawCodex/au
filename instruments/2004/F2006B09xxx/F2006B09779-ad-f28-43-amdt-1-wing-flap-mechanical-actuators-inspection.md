# AD/F28/43 Amdt 1 - Wing Flap Mechanical Actuators - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F28/43**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Flap Mechanical Actuators - Inspection 3/82**

Applicability: All models with flap actuators modified to Menasco SBs 27-63 or 27-66 but NOT modified to Menasco SB 27-81.

Document: 1. Fokker SB No. F28/27-137; or

2\. RLD AD. BLA 80-08-27.

Compliance: As detailed in requirement document.

This Directive has been raised in issue to include reference to Menasco SB No. 27-81, which introduces an improved steel ball return tube no longer subject to repetitive inspection.

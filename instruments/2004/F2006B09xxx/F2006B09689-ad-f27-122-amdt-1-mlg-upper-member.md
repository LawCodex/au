# AD/F27/122 Amdt 1 - MLG Upper Member

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F27/122**

**Amdt 1**

**MLG Upper Member 12/91**

Applicability: Model F27 series with serial numbers 10102 through 10684, 10686 and 10687, 10689

through 10692.

Requirement: Inspect and modify in accordance with Fokker SB F27/32-162.

*Note: RLD AD 91-041 refers.*

Compliance: Before 1 January 1992. (Same as previous issue of this Directive.)

Background: Several main undercarriage upper members were found on inspection to have a fatigue crack at the intersection of the side face and the cross bore. Therefore Fokker is calling for a one time inspection of the rest of the fleet, and a rework to radius the edge of the cross bore to prevent further cracking.

Amendment 1 is raised to align this Directive with the related RLD AD, which calls up the above Fokker bulletin, and in doing so now includes the Mk 500.

# AD/DHC-2/22 Amdt 1 - Aileron Centre Hinge Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-2/22**

**Amdt 1**

**Aileron Centre Hinge Bracket 1/88 DM**

Applicability: All models DHC-2 Mk1 and MkII and DHC-2 MkIII. Turbo- Beaver Aircraft not incorporating Modification 2/1536.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with De-Havilland Canada S.B 2/37 Rev C.

*Note: Canadian AD CF-84-02-R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished within 200 hours time in service after 19 November 1987 or prior to 19 January 1988 whichever is earlier.

Background: The initial issue of this Directive allowed an open ended continued inspection. However, due to failure of a cast bracket in flight this option is now terminated.

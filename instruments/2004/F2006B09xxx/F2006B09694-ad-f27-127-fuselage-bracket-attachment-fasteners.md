# AD/F27/127 - Fuselage Bracket Attachment Fasteners

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/127 Fuselage Bracket Attachment Fasteners 12/91

Applicability: Model F27 S/No’s 10102 thru 10433, 10435 thru 10443, & 10446 thru 10450. Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of RLD AD 91-112.

*Note: Fokker SB F27/57-23 Revision 6 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 12 December 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Reports of loose bolts and hi-shear pins in the brackets on the front and rear spars. The effective date required by the RLD is extended by approximately two months.

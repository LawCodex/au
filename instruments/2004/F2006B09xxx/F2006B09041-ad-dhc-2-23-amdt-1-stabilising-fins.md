# AD/DHC-2/23 Amdt 1 - Stabilising Fins

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC2/23**

**Amdt 1**

**Stabilising Fins 8/91**

Applicability: Model DHC-2 Mk I and Mk II Beaver aircraft equipped with EDO Model 679-4930 floats; or floats or amphibious floats listed in Type approval Number A-22, at Issue 20, but not equipped with fins as specified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action IAW the Technical Requirements of Transport Canada AD CF-83-09 R2. Compliance: Unless already accomplished; prior to further flight after 8 August 1991.

Background: Flight tests indicate that a DHC-2 Beaver Mk I or Mk II equipped as a floatplane or an amphibious floatplane, may exhibit hazardous adverse directional stability when not equipped with fins as specified.

This amendment is raised in response to Revision 2 of the Requirement Document, which has been issued to combine AD CF-76-15 (25 Aug 76) and AD CF-83-09 R1 (2 Mar 89), and to include wipline ventral fin and stabilising fins.

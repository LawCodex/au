# AD/F50/10 - Outboard Nacelle Ventilation Air Inlets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/10 Outboard Nacelle Ventilation Air Inlets 3/89

DM

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20128. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-54-003.

*Note: RLD AD 88-94 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1989.

Background: There have been several reports of ice build-up on cable systems. These occurred at high altitude and high humidity conditions with low OAT.

# AD/ELECT/13 Amdt 2 - Magneto - BTH - Slow Speed Wheel - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Electrical Equipment

**AD/ELECT/13**

**Amdt 2**

**Magneto - BTH - Slow Speed Wheel - Inspection 2/74**

Applicability: BTH Type AG4.

Requirement: Thoroughly inspect the teeth of the CX133241/1 Slow Speed Wheel for cracks or damage using at least a X5 magnifying glass. Remove from service any defective Slow Speed Wheels.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft following each reconditioning completed after 28 February 1974.

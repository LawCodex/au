# AD/DHC-8/25 - Power Cable Connectors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/25 Power Cable Connectors 1/93 TX

Applicability: All DHC-8 100 & 300 Series without modification 8/1970 incorporated.

Requirement: Action in accordance with de Havilland Inc Service Bulletin No A8-24-44 dated 23 October 1992, or later approved versions.

*Note: Canadian Emergency AD CF-92-20 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 9 January 1993.

Background: This action is required because electrical DC feeder cables are loosening and corroding. The cause is attributed to the use of aluminium washers.

# AD/EMB-120/1 - Generator Control Unit (GCU)

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-120 (Brasilia) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-120/1 Generator Control Unit (GCU) 2/91

Applicability: Embraer 120 aircraft S/No’s 120004; and 120006 through to 120090 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Brazilian AD 90-09-03.

*Note: Embraer SB 120-024-0021 Revision 3 also refers.*

Compliance: Within 1000 hours time in service after 30 September 1990.

Background: Advice has been received that GCU failures may result in momentary overvoltages of sufficient amplitude to cause failure of critical aircraft systems and components.

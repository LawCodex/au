# AD/EMY/23 - Aerazur Life Rafts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/23 Aerazur Life Rafts 12/90

Applicability: Aerazur life rafts with part numbers listed in Aerazur SB BS 332.60.002 and up to S/N 175 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerazur SB BS 332.60.002.

*Note: DGAC AD 90-128 AB refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, at the next scheduled inspection for issue of Maintenance Release after 29 November 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Raft package vent pipes have been found bent and closed off.

Failure of package venting may cause deployment of raft during flight at altitude.

# AD/F27/107 - Inboard Aileron Hinge Bolt Retention - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F27/107 Inboard Aileron Hinge Bolt Retention - Inspection and Replacement

Applicability: All model F27 with S/Nos. 10105 to 10635. Document: Fokker SB F27/57-58.

*Note: RLD AD BLA 83-20-57 refers.*

Compliance: Before 1st January 1984.

## 8/83

Background: Restricted aileron movement was reported due to migration of hinge bolt and bush.

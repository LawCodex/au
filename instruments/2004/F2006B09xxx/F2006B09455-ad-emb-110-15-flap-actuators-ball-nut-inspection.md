# AD/EMB-110/15 - Flap Actuators Ball Nut - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/15 Flap Actuators Ball Nut - Inspection 10/81** Applicability: All models EMB-110 equipped with linear actuators P/Nos. D2246-3 and D2246-4. Document: 1. Embraer Alert S.B. No. 110-27-A54, or

2\. C.T.A. AD NR.81-07-01.

Compliance: At the next 250 hourly inspection of actuators carried out after 30 September 1981. Background: In addition to the repetitive 250 hourly inspection of actuators laid down in AD/EMB-

110/8B, a once only inspection of the ball return tube retaining clamp is now required. Loosening of a ball return tube may cause loss of the balls and result in an asymmetric flap condition.

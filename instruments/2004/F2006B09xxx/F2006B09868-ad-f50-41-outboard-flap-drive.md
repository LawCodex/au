# AD/F50/41 - Outboard Flap Drive

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/41 Outboard Flap Drive 3/91

Applicability: Model F27 MK 50 with serial numbers 20103 and 20107. Requirement: Action in accordance with RLD AD 90-148.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-27-026 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Several outboard flap drive assemblies have been identified as not being in compliance with current regulations.

# AD/F50/37 - Auto Flight Idle Stop Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/37 Auto Flight Idle Stop Mechanism 10/90

Applicability: Model F50 series with serial numbers 20103 through 20192 with Service Bulletin F50-76-008 Revision 1 incorporated.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-76-009.

*Note: RLD AD 90-044 refers.*

Compliance: Within 250 hours time in service after incorporation of SB F50-76-008 Revision 1, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 250 hours time in service.

Background: This Directive requires repetitive inspection of the auto flight idle stop mechanism to ensure correct solenoid stroke, which is affected by the gradual wear of the parts.

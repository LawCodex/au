# AD/F27/81 - Engine Mount Tubes - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/81 Engine Mount Tubes - Inspection 12/73

Applicability: All model F27 on which engine mount P/N 27.1-8101-000-403 with S/Nos. 590 to 725 have been installed.

Requirement: 1. Inspect referenced engine mount tubes in accordance with Fokker F27 SB F27/71- 25 for cracks using the radiographic technique as detailed; or

2\. Inspect the engine mount tubes detailed in Fokker F27 SB F27/71-25, for cracks using an alternative non destructive technique approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Within 500 hours time in service after 31 December 1973 and there-after at intervals not exceeding 1 year.

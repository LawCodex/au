# AD/F50/20 Amdt 1 - Engine Tailpipe Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F50/20**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Tailpipe Assembly 1/92**

Applicability: All F50 series aircraft equipped with P/N F8200-08-401 or F8200-08-403 exhaust tailpipe assemblies.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of Netherlands Airworthiness Directive BLA No 89-038 Issue 2.

Compliance: As detailed in the Requirement document with a revise effective date of 12 January 1992.

Background: This Directive has been amended to reflect the revision of Netherlands Airworthiness Directive BLA No. 89-038. The revision includes additional inspection requirements for P/N F8200-08-403 tailpipes, and introduces a terminating action.

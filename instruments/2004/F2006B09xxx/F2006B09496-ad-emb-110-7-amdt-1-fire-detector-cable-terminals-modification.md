# AD/EMB-110/7 Amdt 1 - Fire Detector Cable Terminals - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/7**

**Amdt 1**

**Fire Detector Cable Terminals - Modification 5/82**

Applicability: All models EMB-110 with S/Nos. 110001 to 110340. Document: 1. Embraer SB No. 110-26-002 Rev. 02. or

2\. CTA AD 82-02-01.

*Note: Aircraft which have complied with the initial issue of this AD require further work to comply with AD/EMB-110/7 Amdt “1".*

Compliance: Within 250 hours time in service after 30 September 1982.

Background: The manufacturer is aware of several instances of failure of the L"Hotellier fire detector cable terminals. Stronger replacement terminals are now available.

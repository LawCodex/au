# AD/FU24/40 - Cockpit Ventilators - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/40 Cockpit Ventilators - Modification 4/71

Applicability: Model FU24-950 and other FU24 Series aeroplanes fitted with Mod. AP55 Part A with cockpit ventilators to Mod. AP23 or similarly located air intakes.

Requirement: Seal off subject cockpit ventilators.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 19 April 1971.

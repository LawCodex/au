# AD/FPE/7 - Kidde Water Based Fire Extinguisher Cartridge Holder - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fire Protection Equipment

AD/FPE/7 Kidde Water Based Fire Extinguisher Cartridge Holder - Inspection

7/81

Applicability: Walter Kidde water based fire extinguishers P/N 970317, 871685, 890017, 890275,

891318, 892480 and 893948.

Document: 1. Walter Kidde SB No. 162, or,

2\. Kidde Belleville SB No. 26-20-240, or,

3\. A scheme approved by the Secretary which will confirm the integrity and dimension of the cartridge holder internal peg.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished before 31 August 1981.

Background: A Walter Kidde water based fire extinguisher P/N 890275 failed to operate when recently required for use. Subsequent investigation revealed a corroded and shortened cartridge holder inner peg, which had caused the holder to reach the end of its thread before actuating the cartridge.

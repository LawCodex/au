# AD/F27/113 - RH Elevator Stn 300 Rib

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/113 RH Elevator Stn 300 Rib 7/87

Applicability: All models listed in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F27/55-63.

*Note: RLD BLA 87-32 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1987.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances of heavy corrosion and stress corrosion cracking in the milled rib.

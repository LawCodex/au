# AD/FA-200/23 Amdt 1 - Control Column

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/FA-200/23**

**Amdt 1**

**Control Column 1/90**

Applicability: All models which have not been modified in accordance with Fuji Technical Bulletin 200-020, dated 30 May 1989.

Requirement: Using an approved dye penetrant method, inspect the control column tube for cracking in the lock pin hole area.

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 5 January 1987 and thereafter at each scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue.

Background: The Authority has received a report of control column failure in flight. The tube fractured at the lock pin hole.

Amendment 1 introduces a modification which constitutes terminating action for the inspection requirements.

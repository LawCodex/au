# AD/EMB-110/36 Amdt 1 - Elevator Trim Tab Actuation - Dual Control Rod Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/36**

**Amdt 1**

**Elevator Trim Tab Actuation - Dual Control Rod Installation**

**11/85**

Applicability: All model EMB-110 with S/Nos referenced in the Requirement Document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer SB 110-27-068 Rev 1.

*Note: Brazilian AD 82-06-01 R5.*

Compliance: Within 250 hours time in service after 30 November 1985.

*Note: Aircraft which have complied with previous issues of this Directive require further rework in accordance with SB 110-27-068 R1.*

Background: To preclude the effect of rod failure the installation of dual rods with bearings and increased weight to the elevator mass balance is now a mandatory requirement. This amendment references Rev 1 to the Requirement Document.

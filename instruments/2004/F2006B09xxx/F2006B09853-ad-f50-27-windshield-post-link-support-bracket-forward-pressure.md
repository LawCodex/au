# AD/F50/27 - Windshield - Post Link Support Bracket- Forward Pressure Bulkhead

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F50/27 Windshield - Post Link Support Bracket- Forward Pressure Bulkhead

Applicability: Serial Numbers 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20135.

## 13/89

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-53-008, dated 26 July 1989.

*Note: RLD AD BLA 89-83 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to accumulating 20000 flight cycles.

Background: Cracking of the subject bracket was discovered during full-scale fatigue tests.

Substitution with an improved bracket is called for by this AD.

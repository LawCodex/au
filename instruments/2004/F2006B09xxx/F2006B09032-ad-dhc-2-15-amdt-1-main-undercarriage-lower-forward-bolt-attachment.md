# AD/DHC-2/15 Amdt 1 - Main Undercarriage Lower Forward Bolt Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-2/15**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

**Main Undercarriage Lower Forward Bolt Attachment - Inspection**

**8/84**

Requirement: Inspect the main undercarriage lower forward attachment bolts for cracking as follows:

P/N C2U-487ND (part of bolt assembly No. C2U-185A) : magnetic particle inspect in accordance with ANO 108.8 or fluorescent penetrant inspect in accordance with ANO 108.10.

2\. P/N 02-B-16 (hard chrome plated bolts): fluorescent penetrant inspect in accordance with ANO 108.10.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service for aircraft engaged in agricultural operations, and 500 hours time in service for other aircraft.

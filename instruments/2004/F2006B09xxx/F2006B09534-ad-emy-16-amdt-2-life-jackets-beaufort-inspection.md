# AD/EMY/16 Amdt 2 - Life Jackets (Beaufort) - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

**AD/EMY/16**

**Amdt 2**

**Life Jackets (Beaufort) - Inspection 9/84**

Applicability: Beaufort (AIR-SEA) Equipment Life Jackets Mark 6A. Requirement: Accomplish one of the following:-

(a) Inspect for discolouration, brittleness to touch or porosity. and

(b) Pressure test - inflate life jacket with clean dry air to 3 I.S.I. No loss of pressure is permitted over 1 minute. Inflate to 300 mm water gauge. Max. loss permitted, 20mm over 5 minutes.

2\. Test in accordance with a scheme acceptable to the Secretary.

3\. Return life jacket to Beaufort Service Agent for inspection and test.

*Note: Major Defect Reports must be submitted detailing serial number of the life jacket and the nature of the deterioration.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished within the last 6 months, before 31 October 1984 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding six months.

Background: Further reports of deterioration and test failure have been received on life jackets which were satisfactory when tested in compliance with Amendment 1 to this Directive.

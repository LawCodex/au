# AD/F50/31 - Accessory Compartment Cable Covers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/31 Accessory Compartment Cable Covers 2/90

DM

Applicability: F27 Mk50 with S/Nos 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20134, in post Fokker SB F50-27-010 configuration.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-27-024.

*Note: Netherlands AD 89-99 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished - prior to 31 March 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Water draining from covers may splash onto control cables and freeze, causing control difficulty.

# AD/F50/6 - Control Cables in Accessory Compartment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/6 Control Cables in Accessory Compartment 3/89

DM

Applicability: F27-050 S/Nos 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20138. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-27-010.

*Note: RLD AD 88-84 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1989.

Background: Freezing of cable systems has occurred at high altitude. Rain, high humidity and low OAT were factors. Installation of a cover is to prevent moisture from falling onto the cables pulleys etc.

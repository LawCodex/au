# AD/F28/17 - Main Landing Gear Wheel - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/17 Main Landing Gear Wheel - Inspection NK

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect Goodyear Main Landing Gear wheels in the area of the internal flange radius using either an approved eddy current or ultrasonic technique.

Compliance: At each tyre removal after 3 August 1971.

# AD/F27/143 - Port Elevator Torque Tube

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/143 Port Elevator Torque Tube 4/97

Applicability: F27 Mk 100 to Mk 700 all serial numbers. Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Fokker SB F27/55-66.

*Note: RLD AD 1995-007 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to the accumulation of 45 000 flight cycles or before 31 July 1997, whichever occurs last.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 27 March 1997.

Background: This Directive is raised following the report of cracking found on the torque tube support of the port elevator.

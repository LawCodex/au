# AD/EMB-110/42 - Undercarriage Wiring Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-110/42 Undercarriage Wiring Inspection 13/89

Applicability: All model EMB-110 aircraft.

Requirement: Carry out an inspection of the main and nose landing gear wiring in accordance with Embraer SB 110-32-052R3.

*Note: Brazilian AD 82-10-02R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished,

Within 25 hours time in service from 28 December 1989, and

2\. Thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours time in service or 3 months.

Background: Embraer have advised that the main and nose landing gear wiring are subject to wear in operation which can produce open and/or short circuits which may result in erratic indication and/or uncommanded operation of the landing gear.

# AD/F50/15 - Engine Air Intake Seal

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/15 Engine Air Intake Seal 5/89 DM

Applicability: Model F27 mark 050 with serial numbers: 20103 through 20108

20110 through 20144

20146 and 20147

20150 through 20152

20155 through 20161

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker 50 Service Bulletin No. F50-30-004.

*Note: RLD AD BLA 89-32 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 May 1989.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that ice accumulating at the rear of the engine air intake and on the flexible seal, can shed into the engine and cause a momentary flame-out.

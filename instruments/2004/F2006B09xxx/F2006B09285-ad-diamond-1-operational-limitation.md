# AD/DIAMOND/1 - Operational Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Diamond (HOAC) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DIAMOND/1 Operational Limitation 9/97 DM

Applicability: Diamond Aircraft Model DA 20 A1 “Katana” aircraft, with serial numbers 10002 through 10287.

Requirement: 1. Install a placard on the cockpit instrument panel, in letters at least 2.54 mm (0.10 inch) high, in the vicinity of the existing limitations placard located above the airspeed indicator, to read:

**SPINS PROHIBITED**

A flight manual amendment is required.

2\. Modify the aircraft in accordance with the Accomplishment Instructions of Diamond Aircraft Service Bulletin DA20-53-01A, dated 5 June 1997.

*Note: Transport Canada Emergency AD CF-97-09 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Before further flight.

2\. Before 18 July 1997.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 18 June 1997.

Background: There has been an occurrence of a rudder pedal jam during a practice spin with a Katana aircraft. The jam was caused by the pilot’s shoe being caught between the rudder pedal and battery hardware.

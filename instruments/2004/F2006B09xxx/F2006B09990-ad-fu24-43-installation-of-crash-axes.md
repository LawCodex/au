# AD/FU24/43 - Installation of Crash Axes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/43 Installation of Crash Axes 9/71

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Aircraft engaged in agricultural operations - Install a crash axe on the left hand inner side of the cockpit immediately below the canopy opening.

2\. Aircraft fitted out for passenger carrying in the fuselage - Install a crash axe on the left hand inner side of the cockpit immediately below the canopy opening, and another on the inside surface of the cargo door.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1971.

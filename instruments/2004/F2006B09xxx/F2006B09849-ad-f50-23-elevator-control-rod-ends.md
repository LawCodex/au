# AD/F50/23 - Elevator Control Rod Ends

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/23 Elevator Control Rod Ends 10/89

Applicability: F27 Mark 050 aircraft with serial numbers 20103 through 20108, 20110 through

20147\.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-27-006.

*Note: RLD AD BLA89-56 refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled 3000 hours inspection after 5 October 1989 or prior to 31 March 1990, whichever occurs first.

Background: Country of origin AD. Present rod ends have a semi-octagonal section, which may catch and retain foreign objects, leading to a potential jamming of elevator controls. Replacement rod ends required by this AD are less liable to jamming.

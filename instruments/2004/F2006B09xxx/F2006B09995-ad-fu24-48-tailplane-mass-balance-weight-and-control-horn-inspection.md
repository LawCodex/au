# AD/FU24/48 - Tailplane Mass Balance Weight and Control Horn - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/FU24/48 Tailplane Mass Balance Weight and Control

**Horn - Inspection and Modification**

Applicability: All models fitted with piston engines. Document: Aerospace SB ASB/FU/005.

## 5/75

Compliance: Inspect before 30 April 1975. Modify, where necessary, before 31 May 1975.

*Note: Aircraft with an incorrectly balanced tailplane may continue in service until modified.*

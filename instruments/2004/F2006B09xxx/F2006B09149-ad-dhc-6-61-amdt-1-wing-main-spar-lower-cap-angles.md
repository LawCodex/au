# AD/DHC-6/61 Amdt 1 - Wing Main Spar Lower Cap Angles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/DHC-6/61

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Wing Main Spar Lower Cap Angles 7/91

Requirement: Action in accordance with the Technical Requirements of Transport Canada AD CF- 87-02, R1.

*Note: De Havilland SB 6/492 Revision A refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised effective date of 11 July 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Exfoliation corrosion in the main spar cap lower angles has been reported on older aircraft. Amendment 1 is raised to reflect a revision of the related Country of origin AD which was issued to remove flight hour limitations from the compliance time requirements.

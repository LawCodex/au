# AD/FA-200/19 Amdt 2 - Rudder Pedal System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/FA-200/19**

**Amdt 2**

**Rudder Pedal System 11/89**

Applicability: All models FA-200 with S/Nos. 12 to 297. Requirement: Action in accordance with FHI SB No. 200-002 Rev C.

*Note: JCAB AD TCD-1455A-1-89 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 3 December 1984 or prior to the first flight during which aerobatics are to be performed, whichever is earlier. Thereafter at:

each inspection for Maintenance Release issue, or

b. periods not to exceed 1000 hours time in service if Technical Bulletin 200-22 is embodied.

Background: Overtravel of the rudder pedals and incorrect rudder control system operation has been experienced. Amendment 2 introduces a relieving modification to extend the inspection periodicity to 1000 hours.

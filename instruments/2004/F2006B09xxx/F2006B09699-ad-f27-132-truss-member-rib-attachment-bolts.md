# AD/F27/132 - Truss Member/Rib Attachment Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/132 Truss Member/Rib Attachment Bolts 4/92

Applicability: Model F27 Series, if in Pre SB F27/57-25 configuration. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F27/57-25 Revision 1.

*Note: RLD AD 91-110 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 January 1995.

Background: At present the truss-members at designated wing stations are attached with one long bolt at each end. To obtain a better clamping effect this bolt is replaced by two short ones. The new bolts also have a longer service life.

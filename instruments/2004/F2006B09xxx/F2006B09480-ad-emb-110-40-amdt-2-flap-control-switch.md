# AD/EMB-110/40 Amdt 2 - Flap Control Switch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/EMB-110/40**

**Amdt 2**

**Flap Control Switch 8/89**

Applicability: All EMB-110 aircraft serial number 110001 through 110467 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer SB 110-027-0084 Revision 1.

*Note: Brazilian AD 88-09-01 Revision 1 also refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 2 November 1989.

Background: Embraer have advised that chafing between the attaching screws of the flap control switch potentiometer and resistor R273 can cause inadvertent flap extension.

Amendment 1 to this AD was issued to reflect an addition to the applicable part numbers in the Requirement documents.

Amendment 2 to this Directive is issued to amend the Requirement Document.

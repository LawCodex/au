# AD/FA-200/9 Amdt 1 - Engine Driven Fuel Pump Outlet Tee - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/FA-200/9**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Driven Fuel Pump Outlet Tee - Replacement**

**3/83**

Applicability: All model FA-200-180 with S/No. 12 and subsequent. Requirement: Install new engine driven fuel pump outlet tee P/No. AN783-6.

*Note: Fuji SB No. 200-004 and Japanese CAB AD TCD-2213-82 refer.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1983 and thereafter at intervals of 3000 hours time in service.

Background: Instances of fatigue cracking have been reported by the manufacturer. This AD has been amended to restrict the service life of the component.

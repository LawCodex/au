# AD/EMB-110/18 - Tailplane Deicer System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-110/18 Tailplane Deicer System - Modification 8/82

Applicability: All models equipped with pneumatic deicing systems. Document: 1. SB No. 110-30-012;

2\. SB No. 110-30-013;

3\. SB No. 110-55-020.

*Note: Brazilian CTA AD 82-05-03 refers.*

Compliance: Modify in accordance with documents 1, 2 and 3 within 100 hours time in service after 31 October, 1982.

Background: Sharp and unexpected pitching down moments have occurred on approach due to fractured tailplane deicer hoses causing ice build-up on horizontal stabilizer leading edges.

# AD/ENST 28/25 - Clutch Engagement Over Centre Lock Warning Light

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

## AD/ENST 28/25 Clutch Engagement Over Centre

**Lock Warning Light** Applicability: All models F-28A, 280, F-28C, 280C, F-28F and 280F helicopters. Document: Document SDB No. 0061.

FAA AD 82-24-01 refers.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1983.

## 1/83

Background: To ensure that the clutch engaging mechanism is locked and over centre, a warning light indicator is installed.

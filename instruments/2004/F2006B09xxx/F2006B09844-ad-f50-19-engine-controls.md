# AD/F50/19 - Engine Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/19 Engine Controls 7/89

Applicability: All F27-050 S/No 20103 thru 20108, 20110 thru 20135. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin SB F50-76-002.

*Note: RLD AD 89-39 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 15 September, 1989.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that when a failure occurs in the power lever control cable system, the automatic flight idle stop mechanism will be ineffective. In certain conditions this may result in an uncontained propeller overspeed.

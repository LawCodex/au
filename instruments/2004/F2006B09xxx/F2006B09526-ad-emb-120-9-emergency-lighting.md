# AD/EMB-120/9 - Emergency Lighting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-120 (Brasilia) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-120/9 Emergency Lighting 9/93

Applicability: EMB 120 aircraft serial numbers 120004 and 120006 through 120727 inclusive,

except 120181 and 120208.

Requirement: Modify the emergency lighting system in accordance with Embraer Service Bulletin 120-33-0033 dated 15 December 1992.

*Note: CTA (Brazil) AD 93-03-01 Amdt 39-712 dated 10 April 1993 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 December 1993.

Background: Compliance with this Directive corrects a deficiency in automatic activation of the emergency lighting system.

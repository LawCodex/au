# AD/FU24/45 - Fuel Quantity Indication - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/45 Fuel Quantity Indication - Modification 4/72

Applicability: All models with mechanical type fuel quantity indicators fitted to inboard end of left hand fuel tank system.

Requirement: Modify the fuel quantity indicating system by:

Fitting electrical indicators; or

2\. Repositioning the left hand indicator assembly to a scheme approved by the Secretary.

*Note: Airparts (NZ) Ltd. drawing 246158 is an acceptable means of compliance with*

*1. (above).*

Compliance: Before 30 September 1973.

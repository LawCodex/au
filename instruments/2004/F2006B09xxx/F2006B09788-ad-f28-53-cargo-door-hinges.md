# AD/F28/53 - Cargo Door Hinges

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/53 Cargo Door Hinges 8/87 TX

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Remove forward, centre and aft cargo door hinges.

2\. Remove hinge wire and disassemble hinge.

3\. Inspect hinge lugs for corrosion and cracking.

Compliance: Unless accomplished within the pat six months within 50 hours time in service after 9 June 1987.

Background: The Department has received reports of intergranular corrosion and cracking of the cargo door hinges. In one case nearly half of the lugs were cracked.

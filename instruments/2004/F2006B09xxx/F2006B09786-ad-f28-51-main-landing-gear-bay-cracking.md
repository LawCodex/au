# AD/F28/51 - Main Landing Gear Bay Cracking

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/51 Main Landing Gear Bay Cracking 2/86

Applicability: All model F28 with S/Nos 11003 to 11091, 11094 to 11135, 11991 and 11992. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F28/57-76.

*Note: RLD AD 85-87 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1986.

The manufacturer reports an instance of a crack in the area of Station 2635 and the rear spar.

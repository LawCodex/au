# AD/F27/114 Amdt 1 - Main Landing Gear Torque Links

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F27/114

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Main Landing Gear Torque Links 11/87

Requirement: Action in accordance with Dowty Rotol SB 32-161B R2. Compliance: Prior to 1 January 1989.

Background: The manufacturer reports a number of torque links have been incorrectly machined during manufacture. Amendment 1 of this Directive references Revision 2 of the Requirement Document which extends the batch numbers identified as being incorrectly machined.

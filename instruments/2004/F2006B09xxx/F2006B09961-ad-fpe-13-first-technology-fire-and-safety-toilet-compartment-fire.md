# AD/FPE/13 - First Technology Fire and Safety Toilet Compartment Fire Extinguishers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fire Protection Equipment

**AD/FPE/13 First Technology Fire and Safety Toilet**

**Compartment Fire Extinguishers**

**5/98**

Applicability: All First Technology and Safety spherical type toilet compartment fire extinguishers Halon 1211 and 1301 with serial numbers 7967 to 10356 inclusive.

Requirement: Remove applicable fire extinguishers from aircraft, check and replace if necessary in accordance with First Technology Fire and Safety Service Bulletin 26-110.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 007-11-97 refers.*

Compliance: Before 23 July 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 23 April 1998.

Background: At Serial Number 7967 a manufacturing change resulted in a leakage problem at the eutectic tip.

# AD/ENST 28/31 - Main Rotor Transmission Clutch Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

AD/ENST 28/31 Main Rotor Transmission Clutch Assembly 5/89

DM

Applicability: All model F28 series helicopters equipped with P/N 28- 13401-1, Formsprag CL 40526-1 through -7 main rotor transmission clutch assembly.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No 0068. Additionally, for F-28F, F280F and 280FX helicopters inspect clutch assembly for part number. If clutch P/N 28- 13401-1 fitted, replace with clutch P/N 28-13401-2 before further flight.

*Note: FAA AD 89-04-09 refers.*

Compliance: At next inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release or by 31 March 1989 whichever occurs first.

Background: Failure of the 28-13401-1 clutch has resulted in severe damage to helicopters.

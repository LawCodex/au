# AD/EMB-120/22 - Wing and Empennage Leading Edge

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-120 (Brasilia) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-120/22 Wing and Empennage Leading Edge 1/98

Applicability: All models EMB-120 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Embraer Alert Service Bulletin 120-51-A004.

*Note: CTA AD 97-09-07 Amdt 39-802 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 75 hours time in service after 1 January 1998.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 1 January 1998.

Background: An occurrence was reported of delaminations in the wing leading edge top layer, immediately behind the ice boot upper edge. This Directive requires a visual inspection for erosion, delamination and condition of sealing and anti-static paint.

# AD/DHC-6/40 - Wing Struts - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-6/40 Wing Struts - Inspection and Modification 4/76** Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 1 to 462, 464, 465, 468, 469, 472, 473, 474, 478 and 481. Document: De Havilland of Canada SB No. 6/342.

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 120 hours time in service after 31 May 1976.

2\. Modify within 360 hours time in service after 31 May 1976.

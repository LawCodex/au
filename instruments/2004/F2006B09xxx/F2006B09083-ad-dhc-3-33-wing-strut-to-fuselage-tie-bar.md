# AD/DHC-3/33 - Wing Strut to Fuselage Tie Bar

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-3 (Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-3/33 Wing Strut to Fuselage Tie Bar 13/87

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with de Havilland Canada SB 3/37 Rev B.

*Note: Canadian AD CF-82-26 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to the initial issue of a Certificate of Airworthiness and thereafter at periods not exceeding 12 months.

Background: Tie bar lugs have been found corroded as well as the internal channel section under the cabin floor.

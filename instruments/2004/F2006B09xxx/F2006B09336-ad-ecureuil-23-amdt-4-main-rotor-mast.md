# AD/ECUREUIL/23 Amdt 4 - Main Rotor Mast

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 350 (Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

**AD/ECUREUIL/23**

**Amdt 4**

**Main Rotor Mast 5/88**

Applicability: All models with main rotor masts P/N 350A37.1076 .00 to .06

Requirement: 1. (a) Perform the visual check and if necessary, the dye penetrant check called for in Aerospatiale SB 05-13 Rev 3 para’s C(1) and C(2).

(b) Protect any reworked surfaces in accordance with Aerospatiale SB 05-13 Rev 3 para C(3).

2\. Aerospatiale SB 05-13 Rev 3.

*Note 1: Magnetic particle inspection may be carried out in lieu of dye penetrant and is the preferred technique.*

*Note 2: Bureau Veritas AD 85-95-40(B) and Bureau Veritas AD 83-173-36(c) refer.*

Compliance: 1. Within 10 hours time in service after 30 November 1985, unless already accomplished.

2\. As detailed in the requirement document - Aerospatiale SB 05-13 Rev 3 para D.

Background: A fatigue crack initiated from an undetected corrosion pit on an overseas operated helicopter, and propagated to a critical size in 170 hours time in service. This AD has been amended in accordance with the requirement of Bureau Veritas AD 85-94-40(B) which required a one time 10 hour inspection of the main rotor shaft. The requirements of Bureau Veritas AD 85-95-40(B) supplement those of Bureau Veritas AD 83-173-36(c), which were noted in the previous issue of this AD. This Amendment incorporates Revision 3 of the S.B.

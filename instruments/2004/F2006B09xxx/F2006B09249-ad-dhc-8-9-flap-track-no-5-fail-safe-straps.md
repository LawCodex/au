# AD/DHC-8/9 - Flap Track No.5 Fail Safe Straps

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/9 Flap Track No.5 Fail Safe Straps 10/89

Applicability: Serial number 1 through 119.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Boeing Canada DHC-8 Service Bulletin No. 8-57-10 dated 31 March 1989.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-89-02 refers.*

Compliance: Within 500 hours time in service after 5 October 1989, or prior to 5 January 1990, whichever occurs first.

Background: Some fatigue failures have occurred on subject straps because of incorrect installation.

# AD/F50/42 Amdt 1 - External Power Connection Relay Fitment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F50/42**

**Amdt 1**

**External Power Connection Relay Fitment 5/91**

Applicability: All F 27 MK50 aircraft as listed in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of RLD Airworthiness Directive 90-137 dated 14 December 1990.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-24-015 dated 29 November 1990 also refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

Background: Reports indicate that the potential exists for serious damage to occur to contactors and circuit breakers during ground power to aircraft power change-over.

*Note: Amendment 1 to this AD is issued to clarify the compliance statement.*

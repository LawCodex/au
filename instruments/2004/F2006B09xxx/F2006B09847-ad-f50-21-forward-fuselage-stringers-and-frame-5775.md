# AD/F50/21 - Forward Fuselage - Stringers and Frame 5775

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/21 Forward Fuselage - Stringers and Frame 5775 8/89

Applicability: Serial numbers 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20121.

Requirement: Reinforce fuselage frame to stringer connection by installing connecting angles in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-53-004 dated 14 October 1988.

*Note: Netherlands AD 88-81 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to accumulating 10000 flight cycles.

Background: Full scale fatigue testing has shown the possibility of cracks occurring on the subject area, due to insufficient frame to stringer connection.

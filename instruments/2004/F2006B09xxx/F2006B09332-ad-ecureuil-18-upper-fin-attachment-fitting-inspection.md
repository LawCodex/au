# AD/ECUREUIL/18 - Upper Fin Attachment Fitting - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 350 (Ecureuil) Series Helicopters

AD/ECUREUIL/18 Upper Fin Attachment Fitting - Inspection 10/82

Applicability: All model AS350 not modified in accordance with AMS 350A07.1047 (SB No.

55.03).

Document: Aerospatiale SB No. 05.09.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 82-98-26(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 1 September 1982, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 10 hours time in service.

Background: Modification in accordance with SB 55.03 provides terminating action for the mandatory repeat inspection.

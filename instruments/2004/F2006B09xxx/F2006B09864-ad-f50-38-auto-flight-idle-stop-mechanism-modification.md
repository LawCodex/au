# AD/F50/38 - Auto Flight Idle Stop Mechanism Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/38 Auto Flight Idle Stop Mechanism Modification 10/90

Applicability: Model F50 series with serial numbers 20103 through 20108, 20110 through 20159,

20161 through 20173 and 20176 through 20178.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F50-76-008 Revision 1.

*Note: RLD AD 90-043 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 January 1991.

Background: Reports have been received of incidents where the power levers could not be retarded below flight idle during landing. This Directive requires a modification of the mechanism adjustment of the auto flight idle stop located on the engine

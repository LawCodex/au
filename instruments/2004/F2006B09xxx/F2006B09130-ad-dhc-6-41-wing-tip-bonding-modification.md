**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-6/41 Wing Tip Bonding - Modification 5/76

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 1 to 430 incorporating wing tip strobe lighting. Document: 1. De Havilland SB No. 6/318.

2\. Canadian AD CF-76-04.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1976.

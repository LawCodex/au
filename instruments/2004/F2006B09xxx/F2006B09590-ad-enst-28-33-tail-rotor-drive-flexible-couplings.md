# AD/ENST 28/33 - Tail Rotor Drive Flexible Couplings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

AD/ENST 28/33 Tail Rotor Drive Flexible Couplings 11/90

DM

Applicability: All model F-28, F-28A, F-28C, F-28C-2, F-28F, 280, 280C, 280F and 280 FX series helicopters.

Requirement: 1. Replace the 5 plate flex pack coupling P/N A005-1991 with 7 plate flex pack coupling P/N A005-1992. During pack replacement inspect flange condition in accordance with Paragraph 5.2 of Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No 0080.

2\. Inspect 7 plate flex pack coupling in accordance with Paragraph 11 and 12 of Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No 0080.

*Note: FAA AD 90-17-02 Amdt 39-6688 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 25 hours time in service after 7 September 1990.

2\. At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Background: Due to excessive distortion of the 5 plate coupling and the results of subsequent strain gauge testing by the manufacturer, replacement with a 7 plate coupling is mandatory.

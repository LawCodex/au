# AD/ENST 28/21 - Tail Rotor Blade Grip - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

**AD/ENST 28/21 Tail Rotor Blade Grip - Inspection**

**and Modification**

**5/79**

Applicability: All models F-28C and 280C with Tail Rotor Blade Grip P/N 28-150013-1 wide chord.

Document: Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No. 0048. Compliance: As per requirement document.

The Preflight Inspections may be carried out by the pilot in command in which case certification is not required.

Background: The manufacturer has received several field reports of crack indications being found in the wide chord tail rotor blade retentions grip.

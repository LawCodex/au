# AD/DHC-8/32 - Landing Gear Hydraulic Selector Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Bombardier (Boeing Canada/De Havilland) DHC-8 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-8/32 Landing Gear Hydraulic Selector Valve 7/94

Applicability: All DHC-8-100 series aircraft S/N 003 through 038 not incorporating modification 8/0530.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out action in accordance with de HAVILLAND Dash 8 Alert Service Bulletin A8-32-117.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-94-05 refers.*

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service from 30 June 1994 or by 30 October 1994, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that pilot valve leakage which has not been corrected may, when combined with a low voltage condition, lead to an inadvertent landing gear retraction.

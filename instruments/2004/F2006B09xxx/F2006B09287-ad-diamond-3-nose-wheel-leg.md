# AD/DIAMOND/3 - Nose Wheel Leg

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Diamond (HOAC) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DIAMOND/3 Nose Wheel Leg 7/98 DM

Applicability: Model DV20 with serial numbers 20.005 through 20.160.

Requirement: Check the nose wheel leg weld for cracks using the fluorescent penetrant method, in accordance with Diamond Aircraft Industries Service Bulletin 20-32.

*Note: Austrian AD No. 86 refers.*

Compliance: Unless the initial inspection is already accomplished, within 20 hours time in service after 6 May 1998; thereafter at intervals not to exceed 100 hours time in service, until a version ‘B’ nose wheel leg is installed.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 6 May 1998.

Background: The manufacturer has identified the possibility of cracks occurring in the weld of the nose wheel tappet.

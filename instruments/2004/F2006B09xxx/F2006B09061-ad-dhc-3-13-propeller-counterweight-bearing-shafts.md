# AD/DHC-3/13 - Propeller Counterweight Bearing Shafts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-3 (Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-3/13 Propeller Counterweight Bearing Shafts NK

Applicability: All models fitted with 3D40-227 propellers. Requirement: Action in accordance with De H Canada EB “O” No 39.

*Note: Canadian AD 56-13 refers.*

Compliance: Before installation on an aircraft following each propeller reconditioning.

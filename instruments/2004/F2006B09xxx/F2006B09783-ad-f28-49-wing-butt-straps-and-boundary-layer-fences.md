# AD/F28/49 - Wing Butt Straps and Boundary-Layer Fences

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F28/49 Wing Butt Straps and Boundary-Layer Fences 9/85

Applicability: All model F28 with S/Nos 11092, 11093, 11111 to 11203 and 11205 to 11231. Requirement: Fokker SB F28/57-70.

*Note: Netherlands AD 85-44 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1985.

Air-leakage through butt straps and boundary layer fences has an adverse effect on the aerodynamic behaviour of the wings.

# AD/F100/9 - Forward Partial Pressure Bulkhead

**OMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F100 (F28 Mk 100) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F100/9 Forward Partial Pressure Bulkhead 4/99

Applicability: F.28 Mk.0100 aircraft, serial numbers 11244 through 11286, 11289, 11291 through

11293, 11295, 11297, 11300, 11303, 11306 and 11308.

Requirement: Install new doubler assemblies at the avionics cooling outlet valve fitting on the LH forward partial pressure bulkhead, in accordance with Part 1 of the Accomplishment Instructions of Fokker Service Bulletin (SB) F100-53-052 dated 17 January 1992 and at the cargo bay outlet valve fitting on the RH forward partial pressure bulkhead, in accordance with Part 2 of the Accomplishment Instructions of Fokker SB F100-53- 052 Revision 1 dated 7 June 1993.

*Note: RLD BLA 92-029/2(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, before accumulating 15,000 flight cycles since new.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 22 April 1999.

Background: During fatigue tests on the F.28 Mk.0100, cracks were found in the area of the avionics cooling outlet valve fitting. This AD requires a modification to prevent cracks developing in service and to improve the fatigue life of the affected structure to above 45,000 flight cycles.

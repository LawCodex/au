# AD/DHC-3/24 - Main Landing Gear Fuselage Attachment Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-3 (Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-3/24 Main Landing Gear Fuselage Attachment Bolts 3/69

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Remove and inspect using a magnetic particle method main landing gear to fuselage attachment bolts P/Ns C3U72-3 and C3U142-5 for cracking particularly around the grease outlet hole.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 15 April 1969 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

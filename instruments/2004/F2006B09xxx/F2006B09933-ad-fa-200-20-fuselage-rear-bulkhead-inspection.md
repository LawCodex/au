# AD/FA-200/20 - Fuselage Rear Bulkhead - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FA-200/20 Fuselage Rear Bulkhead - Inspection 2/83

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. up to and including S/N 296. Document: Fuji Service Bulletin No. FAS 200-003.

Compliance: Within the next 100 hours time in service and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service, until modified in accordance with SB FAS 200-003.

Background: Cracks have been found in the upper corners of the fuselage bulkhead at F.S. 7105, near the horizontal stabiliser main spar attachment.

# AD/DHC-2/21 - Flight Controls, Elevator Root Rib - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-2/21 Flight Controls, Elevator Root Rib - Inspection 10/80

Applicability: All Model DHC-2 Mk. I, Mk. II and Mk. III. Document: De Havilland Aircraft of Canada SB No. 2/30.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 December 1980 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 400 hours time in service.

Background: Cracking of the elevator root rib at the torque tube attachment and cracking of the reinforcing doubler in the same area has been reported to the manufacturer.

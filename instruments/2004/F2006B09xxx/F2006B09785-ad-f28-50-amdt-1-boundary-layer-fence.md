# AD/F28/50 Amdt 1 - Boundary Layer Fence

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F28 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F28/50**

**Amdt 1**

**Boundary Layer Fence 10/86**

Applicability: All model F28 with S/Nos 11092, 11093, 11111 to 11234, 11237 and 11238. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker S.B. F28/57-75, Rev. 1.

*Note: RLD AD 85-45 Rev. 2 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1987.

Air-leakage through boundary layer fences results in a deterioration of the wing performance. Modified boundary layer fences provide labyrinth-type seals to overcome this problem. Amendment 1 of this Directive references Rev 1 of Fokker SB F28/57-75 which requires additional work.

# AD/ENST 28/7 Amdt 1 - Main Rotor Transmission Attachment Bolts - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

**AD/ENST 28/7**

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

**Main Rotor Transmission Attachment Bolts - Inspection and Replacement**

**4/77**

Requirement: 1. Inspect the 4 bolts attaching the main rotor transmission assembly to the fuselage frame for a torque preloading of 100-110 inch pounds.

2\. Modify the attachment of the main rotor transmission assembly to fuselage attachment as per Enstrom Service Information Letter No. 0057.

Compliance: For Para. 1: At intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service until modified and then once only within 100 hours time in service after modification as per SIL No. 0057.

For Para. 2: Modify within 300 hours time in service after 1 June 1977.

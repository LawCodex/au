# AD/DO-27/1 - Steel Band Tensioners - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Dornier DO-27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DO-27/1 Steel Band Tensioners - Replacement 8/61

Applicability: All models Q-1, Q-3, Q-4 and H2.

Requirement: 1. Replace the steel band tensioners (Drawing No. 27-404-01 H02) with tension chains (Drawing No. 27-416-01 H02).

2\. SB No. 27/11 dated 22 February 1961, issued by Dornier-Merke G.m.b.H Munchen-Neuaubing, Federal Republic of Germany, covers the modification work required. or

3\. LBA AD 6.27. Compliance: Before 1 September 1971.

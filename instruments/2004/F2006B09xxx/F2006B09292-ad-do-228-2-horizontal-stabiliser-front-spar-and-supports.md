# AD/DO 228/2 - Horizontal Stabiliser Front Spar and Supports

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Dornier 228 Series Aeroplanes

AD/DO 228/2 Horizontal Stabiliser Front Spar and Supports 10/89

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Dornier Service Bulletin 228-035 Rev 1 dated 18 February 1986.

2\. Action in accordance with Dornier Service Bulletin 228-024, Rev 1 dated 25 April 1986.

*Note 1. Accomplishment of Requirement 2 constitutes terminating action for the inspection requirements of Requirement 1.*

*Note 2. West German AD 85-97/3 dated 22 July 1986 refers.*

Compliance: 1. As detailed in Requirement Document 1.

2\. Prior to accumulating 5000 flight cycles.

Background: During ground runs and take-offs with 100% power setting the horizontal stabiliser is subject to asymetrical loads, which could result in cracking in the stabiliser front spar and connection areas.

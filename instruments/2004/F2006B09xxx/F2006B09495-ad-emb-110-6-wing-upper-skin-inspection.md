# AD/EMB-110/6 - Wing Upper Skin - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-110/6 Wing Upper Skin - Inspection 6/79

Applicability: All models.

Document: Embraer Service Bulletin 110-57-012. Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: This inspection is a precautionary measure following discovery of skin cracks in the manufacturer’s fatigue test.

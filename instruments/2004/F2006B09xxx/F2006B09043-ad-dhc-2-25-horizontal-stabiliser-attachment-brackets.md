# AD/DHC-2/25 - Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-2/25 Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Brackets 11/87

Applicability: All models S/Nos 1 to 1056 Pre-mod 2/1338 and Pre-mod 2/984 (Agricultural). Requirement: Action in accordance with De Havilland of Canada SB 2/42.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished within the previous 800 hours time in service, within 200 hours time in service after 31 October 1987, thereafter, every 1000 hours time in service.

Background: The original issue of this Service Bulletin was a Country of Origin Airworthiness Directive and as such was an initial Australian CofA requirement. However the manufacturer has expanded the applicability and introduced a repeat inspection requirement which necessitates the issue of this Directive.

# AD/F50/40 - Fire Wire Connector Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/40 Fire Wire Connector Replacement 2/91

Applicability: All Fokker F27 MK 50 aircraft as listed in the Requirement document. Requirement: In accordance with the technical Requirements of the Netherlands AD BLA 90-127.

*Note: Fokker SB F50-26-008 also refers.*

Compliance: As specified in Requirement document with an effective date of 9 November 1990.

Background: Advice has been received that the ingress of moisture into the fire wire connectors can cause false fire warnings.

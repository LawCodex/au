# AD/ENST 28/15 Amdt 2 - Tail Rotor Spindle and Drive Shaft - Inspection and Retirement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

**AD/ENST 28/15**

**Amdt 2**

**Tail Rotor Spindle and Drive Shaft - Inspection and Retirement**

**8/77**

Applicability: All models F-28 and 280.

Requirement: 1. Remove tail rotor spindles P/N 28-15202 from service and replace with a new spindle P/N 28-15202-13 or a re- worked spindle which may be identified by an 8- character serial number acid etched on the outside of the spindle centre section.

2\. Install tail rotor strike tabs in accordance with Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No. 0037 Revision A or FAA AD 77-04-04 Amendment 39-2839.

3\. Inspect the tail rotor spindle in accordance with the above Bulletin or FAA AD.

4\. Inspect the tail rotor drive shaft in accordance with Enstrom ServiceDirective Bulletin No. 0039.

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Within 10 hours time in service after 17 August 1977.

For Para. 2 - Forthwith.

For Para. 3 and 4 - Before further flight following any tail rotor strike.

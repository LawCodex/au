# AD/F50/24 - Engine Control Rod Clearance

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/24 Engine Control Rod Clearance 13/89

DM

Applicability: F27 MK50 aircraft with serial numbers 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20167.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-54-006.

*Note: Netherlands AD 89-100 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1989.

Background: Country of origin AD. Freezing of water on control rod at feedthrough hole could cause jamming.

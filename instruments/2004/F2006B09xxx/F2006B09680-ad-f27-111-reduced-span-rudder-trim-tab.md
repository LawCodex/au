# AD/F27/111 - Reduced Span Rudder Trim Tab

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/111 Reduced Span Rudder Trim Tab 12/86

Applicability: All model F27 with S/Nos. 10102 to 10684; 10686, 10687 and 10689 to 10692. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker S.B. F27/55-62.

*Note: RLD BLA 86-53 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1988.

Background: This modification alleviates most of the problems associated with the previous design rudder trim tab.

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/EMB-110/28

**Amdt 2**

Applicability: All models.

## Control Surfaces - Static Balance 10/83

Document: Embraer S.B. No. 110-55-021.

*Note: Brazilian AD 83-05-03R2 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 500 hours time in service after 31st July 1983.

Background: Amdt 2 of AD/EMB110/28 is issued to reflect revision 2 of Brazilian AD 83-05-03 which increases some maximum mass balance weight values to achieve static balance.

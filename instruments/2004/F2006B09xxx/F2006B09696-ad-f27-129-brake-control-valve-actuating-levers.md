# AD/F27/129 - Brake Control Valve Actuating Levers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F27 Series Aeroplanes

AD/F27/129 Brake Control Valve Actuating Levers 3/92

Applicability: All model F27, all serial numbers.

Requirement: Replace the brake control valve actuating levers in accordance with Fokker SB F27/32-161 Revision 1 dated 14 June 1991.

*Note: Netherlands RLD AD 91-038 refers.*

Compliance: Before 1 May 1992.

Background: Country of origin AD. Reports have been received of brake control valve actuating levers that fractured during operation of the braking system. Subsequent investigation showed that the levers initially cracked due to fatigue and finally fractured due to overload.

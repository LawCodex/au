# AD/F50/18 - Door Mechanism Gearbox Cover

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/18 Door Mechanism Gearbox Cover 5/89

Applicability: Model F27 Mark 050 with serial numbers 20103 through 20108 and 20110 through 20145.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker 50 SB No F50-52-005.

*Note: RLD AD BLA 88-96 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1989.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that the operating mechanism gearbox of the passenger, cargo, and service/emergency doors, can collect water as a result of condensation; which may freeze at low temperatures and prevent the mechanism from operating correctly.

# AD/ENST 28/16 - Tail Rotor Pitch Link Retainer and Guide Bolt - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Enstrom F-28 Series Helicopters

**AD/ENST 28/16 Tail Rotor Pitch Link Retainer and**

**Guide Bolt - Replacement**

**10/77**

Applicability: Models F-28C and 280C fitted with the Wide Cord tail rotors and S/Nos. as listed in the requirement document.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the P/N 28-16320 pitch link retainer in accordance with Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No. 0040.

2\. Replace the P/N 28-16320 pitch link retainer and guide bolts in accordance with Enstrom Service Directive Bulletin No. 0040 and 0043.

FAA AD 77-15-04 refers.

Compliance: 1. For Part 1 of the requirement document - before each flight, until part 2 is complied with.

2\. For Part 2 of the requirement document - within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1977.

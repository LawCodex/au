# AD/DHC-6/65 - Wing Strut Chafing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-6 (Twin Otter) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-6/65 Wing Strut Chafing 4/92

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect for the presence of the protective nylon shield incorporated in the wing strut upper fairings by de Havilland Modification 6/1581.

If the shield is not installed, visually inspect the struts for damage in accordance with de Havilland Service Bulletin 6/342, and incorporate Modification 6/1581.

*Note: Transport Canada Airworthiness Directive CF-91-30 dated 8 August 1991 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 25 hours time in service after 2 April 1992.

2\. Modify before 25 June 1992.

Background: Transport Canada’s aging aircraft review identified this as mandatory action.

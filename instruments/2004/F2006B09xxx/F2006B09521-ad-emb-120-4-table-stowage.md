# AD/EMB-120/4 - Table Stowage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-120 (Brasilia) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-120/4 Table Stowage 1/92

Applicability: Model 120 with serial numbers 120014, 120024, 120027, 120061, 120062, 120063,

120064, 120070, 120080, 120094, 120097, 120099, 120147 and 120154.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of Brazil CTA AD 91-10-01 Amdt 39-688.

*Note: Embraer SB 120-025-0163 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with a revised effective date of 12 January 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Investigation has revealed that the plug-in table stowage assembly may prevent the rear partition blow-out panel from operating properly between the cargo and passenger compartment.

# AD/FA-200/17 Amdt 1 - Aerobatic Fatigue Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/FA-200/17

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Aerobatic Fatigue Life Limitation NK

Requirement: Retire the aircraft from service.

Note: Assessments of fatigue equivalent flying hours previously calculated in accordance with the original issue of this Directive may still be used but should be divided by 1.5 to be compatible with the retirement life of this issue. This issue of the requirement is a simplification and alleviation resulting from a further assessment of non aerobatic flight load spectra.

Compliance: Before achieving 1700 aerobatic flying hours.

Note: The hours are those actually spent in aerobatic flight and do not include positioning time.

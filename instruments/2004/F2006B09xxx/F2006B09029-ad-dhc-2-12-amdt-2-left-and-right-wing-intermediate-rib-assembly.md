# AD/DHC-2/12 Amdt 2 - Left and Right Wing Intermediate Rib Assembly Stations 15.50, 29.00 and 42.50 - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/DHC-2/12**

**Amdt 2**

**Left and Right Wing Intermediate Rib Assembly Stations 15.50, 29.00 and 42.50 - Inspection and Modification**

**10/84**

Applicability: All model DHC-2 aircraft.

Requirement: De Havilland of Canada EB Series “B” No. 35.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished inspect within 250 hours time in service after 17 September 1984 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service until modification 2/1497 is incorporated.

Background: This Directive is amended to correct a rib station number, include an additional rib assembly and clarify inspection and modification requirements.

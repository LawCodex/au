# AD/EMB-110/41 - Engine Electrical Connectors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-110/41 Engine Electrical Connectors 2/89

Applicability: All model EMB-110 with S/Nos 110001 through 110444 which comply with SB 110-071-0009/1; and S/Nos 110445 through 110478.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Embraer SB 110-071-0023.

*Note: Brazilian AD 88-11-01 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 9 August 1989.

Background: Some electrical connectors installed to the engine “C” flange have rear insulators which are out of specification; thus potentially affecting the reliability of various engine systems.

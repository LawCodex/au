# AD/F50/1 - Central Windshield Post Connection to Pressure Bulkhead

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/F50/1 Central Windshield Post Connection to Pressure Bulkhead

Applicability: All model F27 Mk 050 aircraft with serial numbers 20104 to 20107 inclusive. Requirement: Modify in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin F 50-53-001.

*Note: RLD BLA No 88-18 refers.*

Compliance: Before exceeding 10,000 flights.

## 7/88

Background: The lug at the bottom of the central windshield post has broken on the manufacturer’s fatigue test. A modification has been designed to improve the fatigue resistance of this critical structural joint.

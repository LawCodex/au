# AD/FU24/49 - Centre Wing, Outboard Rear Fitting - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/49 Centre Wing, Outboard Rear Fitting - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: All model FU24-950 with S/Nos. prior to 189. Document: Aerospace SB ASB/FU/018.

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 25 hours time in service after 15 December 1975.

2\. Modify within 100 hours time in service after 15 December 1976.

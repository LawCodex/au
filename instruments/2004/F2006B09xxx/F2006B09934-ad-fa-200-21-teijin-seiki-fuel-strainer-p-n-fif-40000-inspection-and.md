# AD/FA-200/21 - Teijin Seiki Fuel Strainer P/N Fif-40000 - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fuji FA-200 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/FA-200/21 Teijin Seiki Fuel Strainer**

**P/N Fif-40000 - Inspection and Modification**

**4/84**

Applicability: All FA-200-160, 180 and 180 AO aircraft equipped with Teijin Seiki fuel strainer P/N FIF-40000.

Document: Fuji SB No. 200-005 including Teijin Seiki SB No TS-SB-72.

Compliance: Phase I: Within 10 hours time in service after 9 March 1984 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until Phase II as per SB is accomplished.

Phase II: After obtaining the necessary spare part, but not later than 1 February 1985.

Background: There have been instances reported where the stack pipe inside the fuel strainer become lodged upwards into the fuel inlet gallery, thus restricting the fuel flow.

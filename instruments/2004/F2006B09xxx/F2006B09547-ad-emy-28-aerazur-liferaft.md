# AD/EMY/28 - Aerazur Liferaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Emergency Equipment

AD/EMY/28 Aerazur Liferaft 4/93

Applicability: Aerazur Type 346 Liferaft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerazur Mandatory Service Bulletin 346.60.001.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-246(AB) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 15 May 1993.

Background: Reports have been received of liferaft manual inflation/deflation valves with deteriorated flange material.

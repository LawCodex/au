# AD/DHC-2/28 - Wing Strut Lower Steel End-Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
DHC-2 (Beaver) Series Aeroplanes

AD/DHC-2/28 Wing Strut Lower Steel End-Fitting 12/93

Applicability: Model DHC-2 aircraft with steel end-fittings on C2W1115-1/-2 wing struts. Requirement: Visually inspect the wing strut in accordance with de Havilland Alert Service Bulletin

No. A2/48. If damage is detected, repair or replace the strut in accordance with the

above mentioned Service Bulletin.

*Note: Transport Canada AD CF-93-21 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 11 November 1993, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 12 calendar months.

Background: Service difficulty reports received by Transport Canada indicated the occurrence of skin corrosion and fastener damage at the wing strut lower steel end-fittings.

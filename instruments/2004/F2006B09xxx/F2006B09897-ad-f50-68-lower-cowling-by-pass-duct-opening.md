# AD/F50/68 - Lower Cowling By-Pass Duct Opening

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F50/68 Lower Cowling By-Pass Duct Opening 11/95

Applicability: Fokker Aircraft B.V. Model F.27 Mk.050 series, serial numbers as listed in the requirement document.

Requirement: Modify the air outlet opening of the engine by-pass duct in accordance with Fokker

S.B. F50-71-041 dated 10 November 1993.

*Note: Dutch AD BLA 1995-065 (A) dated 30 June 1995 also refers.*

Compliance: Required within the next three (3) calendar months after the 12 October 1995, unless accomplished previously.

Background: There have been a number of in-flight power fluctuations in the service experience of the Fokker Model F.27 Mk.050 series aircraft. In certain weather conditions ice can build up in the engine air intake leading to a loss of engine power as a result of ice ingestion.

This Directive mandates the by-pass duct modification to reduce the possibility of ingestion of ice into the engine.

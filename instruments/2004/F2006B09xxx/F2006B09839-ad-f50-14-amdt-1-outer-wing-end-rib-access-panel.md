# AD/F50/14 Amdt 1 - Outer Wing End Rib Access Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F50 (F27 Mk 50) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/F50/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Outer Wing End Rib Access Panel 6/89**

Applicability: S/No. 20103 to 20108 and 20110 to 20145 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Fokker SB F50-57-004. Rev. 1.

*Note: RLD AD 89-017 Issue 2 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 May 1989.

Background: Deformation of the access panel increases the possibility of ice accretion and can cause heavy operation of the aileron during icing conditions. Amendment 1 references Revision 1 to the Requirement Document which lists additional aircraft affected, none of which are presently on the Australian Register.

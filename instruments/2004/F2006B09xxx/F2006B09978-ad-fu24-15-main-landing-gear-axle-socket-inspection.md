# AD/FU24/15 - Main Landing Gear Axle Socket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airparts (NZ) Ltd. FU 24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/FU24/15 Main Landing Gear Axle Socket - Inspection 6/68

Applicability: All models except those fitted with disc brakes.

Requirement: Inspect visually, the Main Landing Gear Axle Sockets, P/N 245101, for cracks across the lugs containing the lower links.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

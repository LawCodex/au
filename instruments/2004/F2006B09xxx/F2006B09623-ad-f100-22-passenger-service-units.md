# AD/F100/22 - Passenger Service Units

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fokker F100 (F28 Mk 100) Series Aeroplanes

AD/F100/22 Passenger Service Units 4/99

Applicability: F.28 Mk.0100 aircraft, serial numbers 11244 through 11437, if equipped with Grimes Aerospace Passenger Service Units, Part Number (P/N) 10-1178-( ), all serial numbers.

Requirement: Modify affected Grimes Aerospace Passenger Service Units in accordance with Fokker Service Bulletin (SB) F100-25-061 dated 8 March 1994, and Grimes Aerospace SB 10-1178-33-0036 dated 14 May 1993 and SB 10-1178-33-0039

Revision 1 dated 31 October 1193.

*Note: RLD BLA 94-078(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 22 July 1999.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 22 April 1999.

Background: Through service experience with the F.28 Mk.0100 aircraft, it has been determined that the **NO SMOKING** and **FASTEN SEATBELTS** signs, installed in the overhead Passenger Service Units, may not be sufficiently readable to each seated passenger under all probable conditions of cabin illumination.

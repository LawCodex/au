# AD/EMB-110/21 - Fuel Filter Drain Hose - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Embraer EMB-110 (Bandeirante) Series Aeroplanes

AD/EMB-110/21 Fuel Filter Drain Hose - Replacement 2/83

Applicability: All models EMB-110. Document: Embraer SB No. 110-28-024.

*Note: Brazilian CTA AD 82-10-01 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 28 February, 1983.

Background: Existing fuel filter drain hoses are to be replaced with rigid tubes in order to minimise the possibility of failure. Such a failure could lead to the draining of the aircraft fuel tanks.

# AD/DHE/27 - Fuel Control Unit - Boost Capsule - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
De Havilland Piston Engines

AD/DHE/27 Fuel Control Unit - Boost Capsule - Replacement 2/84

Applicability: All Gipsy Queen Series 70.

Document: Hants and Sussex: Gipsy Modification 3014 Issue 2. Compliance: In accordance with requirement document.

Background: This directive is issued to require compliance with the quoted modification to preclude boost capsule failure causing loss of engine power.

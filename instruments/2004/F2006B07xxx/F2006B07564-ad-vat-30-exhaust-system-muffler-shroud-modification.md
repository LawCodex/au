# AD/VAT/30 - Exhaust System Muffler Shroud - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Victa and A.E.S.L. Airtourer Series Aeroplanes

AD/VAT/30 Exhaust System Muffler Shroud - Modification 1/68

Applicability: Victa Airtourer 100.

Requirement: Drill a 3/16 inch diameter drain hole just forward of the rear face bottom centre line of each exhaust system muffler shroud P/N 42124/1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 25 January 1968.

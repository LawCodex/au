# AD/SUPP/11 - Bronzavia-Air Equipment Rescue Hoist Attach Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Supplementary Equipment

AD/SUPP/11 Bronzavia-Air Equipment Rescue Hoist Attach Brackets

12/88

Applicability: Electric Rescue Hoists P/N 76370 76370-010 76370-011 76370-020 76370-030. Requirement: Action in accordance with Bronzavia - Air Equipment SB 25-62-OJ.

*Note 1: DGAC AD 88-087(B) refers.*

*Note 2: Installation of CRS bracket P/N 709883-100 and 709884-100 terminates the inspection requirements.*

*Note 3: For the purposes of the repetitive inspection requirement, prior inspections to AD/ECUREUIL/38 Amdt 1 and AD/AS 355/27 are acceptable as compliance with this Directive.*

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 1 December 1988 and thereafter every 200 hours time in service.

Background: The manufacturer has reported that angle brackets were seriously cracked. Since that time a terminating modification has been developed.

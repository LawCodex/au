# AD/TB20/9 - Main Landing Gear Bay

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/9 Main Landing Gear Bay 7/87

Applicability: All models S/Nos 275 to 709.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale Socata SB No 30.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 87-030(A) refers.*

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 30 June 1987.

Background: The manufacturer reports the possibility of the main landing gear contacting the landing gear bay structure.

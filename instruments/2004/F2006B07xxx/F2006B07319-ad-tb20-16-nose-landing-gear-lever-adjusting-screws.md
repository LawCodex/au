# AD/TB20/16 - Nose Landing Gear Lever Adjusting Screws

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/16 Nose Landing Gear Lever Adjusting Screws 3/90

Applicability: TB20 and TB21 aircraft with S/Nos 1 to 701 inclusive, S/No 707 and 709 except S/No 664 and 665.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale Socata TB SB 46/1.

*Note: DGAC AD 89-108-(A) R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished - Prior to exceeding 100 hours time in service after 22 March 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. To prevent adjusting screws becoming loose.

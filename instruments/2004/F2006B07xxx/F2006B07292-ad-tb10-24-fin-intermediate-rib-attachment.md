# AD/TB10/24 - Fin Intermediate Rib Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/24 Fin Intermediate Rib Attachment 3/95

Applicability: Model TB9, TB10 and TB200 aircraft with serial numbers 1 through 1609. Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata Mandatory Service Bulletin 10.083.55.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-248(A) refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, as specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The fin intermediate rib attachment rivets have been found to be susceptible to working, and loosening as a result.

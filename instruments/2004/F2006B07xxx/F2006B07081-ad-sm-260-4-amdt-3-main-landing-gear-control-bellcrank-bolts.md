# AD/SM-260/4 Amdt 3 - Main Landing Gear Control Bellcrank Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti 260 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SM-260/4**

**Amdt 3**

**Main Landing Gear Control Bellcrank Bolts 5/88**

Applicability: Models F260, F260B, F260C and F260D with S/Nos listed in SIAI-Marchetti SB No 260B36 revision C.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SIAI-Marchetti Service Bulletin No 260B36 revision C.

*Note: RAI AD No 87-227/F260-29 Rev 3 refers.*

Compliance: At maintenance inspection intervals as listed in the Requirement document.

*Note: Improved replacement bolts P/No 260-14-35-5 are now inspected at maintenance intervals not exceeding 1500 hours time in service.*

Background: This amendment has been raised to expand applicability and inspection intervals in accordance with a revision of the Requirement document.

# AD/TPE 331/25 - Third Stage Turbine Wheel P/N 3101516/1 - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Garrett/AiResearch) Turbine Engines - TPE 331 Series

AD/TPE 331/25 Third Stage Turbine Wheel P/N 3101516/1 - Replacement

3/86

Applicability: TPE-331-10-501G models fitted with third stage turbine wheel P/N 3101516/1.

Requirement: Remove the forged third stage turbine wheel, P/No. 3101516/1, and replace it with a cast third stage turbine wheel, P/No. 3102655/1; or

AD/AIR-T/34.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 11 June 1979.

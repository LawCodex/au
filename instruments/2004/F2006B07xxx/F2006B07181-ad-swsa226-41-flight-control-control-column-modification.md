# AD/SWSA226/41 - Flight Control, Control Column - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/41 Flight Control, Control Column - Modification 2/82

Applicability: All model SA226-T S/N T201 to T290. All model SA226-AT S/N AT001 to AT066 and all model SA226-TC S/N TC201 to TC225.

Document: Swearingen SB27-022.

*Note: FAA AD 81-26-04 Amendment 39-4284 refers.*

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 28 February, 1982.

Background: There has been a failure of the control column cable pulley system with subsequent loss of lateral control reported overseas.

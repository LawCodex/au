# AD/SM-205/36 - Control Cables

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti S205 and S208 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SM-205/36 Control Cables 10/95

Applicability: Models S205, S208, and S208A.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SIAI Marchetti Service Bulletin 205B58.

*Note: RAI AD 95-119 refers.*

Compliance: For installed cables at the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 14 September 1995 or prior to 31 December 1995 whichever occurs first.

For uninstalled parts supplied as spare parts prior to 31 December 1992, inspect before installation.

Background: The manufacturer reports that there has been cracking of the cable eyes.

# AD/TSA-600/43 - Fuselage Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/TSA-600/43 Fuselage Horizontal Stabiliser

**Attachment Fittings**

Applicability: All models up to and including Airframe Number 0849.

## 13/95 TX

Requirement: Inspect the forward and aft fuselage horizontal stabiliser attachment fittings, P/N 210006-001 and 210007-001, in accordance with the instructions in Aerostar Service Bulletin SB600-130.

*Note: A copy of this Service Bulletin is held in the CASA District Offices.*

Compliance: Initially inspect before 5 hours time in service after 16 October 1995. Re-inspect at intervals not to exceed 1 year.

Replace any fitting found cracked before further flight.

*Note: The compliance time of this directive differs from that given in Aerostar SB600-130.*

Background: The Authority has received a report of a very large crack in the fuselage horizontal stabiliser attachment fitting. Failure of the fitting may be catastrophic.

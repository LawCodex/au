# AD/TBM 700/10 - Wing Tip Draining

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/10 Wing Tip Draining 3/95

Applicability: Model TBM 700 aircraft with serial numbers 1 through 92.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SOCATA Mandatory Service Bulletin 70.052.57.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-268(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 17 December 1995.

Background: The DGAC requires that certain action be taken to prevent water accumulation in the wing tips between ribs N18 and N19.

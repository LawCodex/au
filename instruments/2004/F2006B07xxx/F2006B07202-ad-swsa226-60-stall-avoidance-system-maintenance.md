# AD/SWSA226/60 - Stall Avoidance System Maintenance

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/60 Stall Avoidance System Maintenance 1/91

Applicability: All models as listed in Requirement Document.

Requirement: Inspect and/or modify as appropriate in accordance with FAA Airworthiness Directive 85-22-06 R1.

Compliance: As per Requirement Document within 100 hours time in service from 24 Jan 1991. Background: To assure proper operation of the Stall Avoidance System.

# AD/TBM 700/2 - Elevator Horn Anti-Icing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/2 Elevator Horn Anti-Icing 6/93

Applicability: Aerospatiale TBM 700 Series aircraft Serial Numbers 1 to 49 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TBM SB 70-20-30.

*Note: GAC AD 93-041(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1993.

Background: This Directive requires the installation of pneumatic de-icing equipment to the elevator horns to avoid ice accumulation at low speed and in icing conditions.

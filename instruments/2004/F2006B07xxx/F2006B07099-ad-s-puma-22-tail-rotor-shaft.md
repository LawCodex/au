# AD/S-PUMA/22 - Tail Rotor Shaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 332 (Super Puma) Series Helicopters

AD/S-PUMA/22 Tail Rotor Shaft 7/96 DM

Applicability: All model AS 332 helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter Mandatory Service Bulletin 05.00.41 dated January 29, 1996.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-074-057(B) refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that a crack in one of the tail rotor flapping hinge retainers has been found.

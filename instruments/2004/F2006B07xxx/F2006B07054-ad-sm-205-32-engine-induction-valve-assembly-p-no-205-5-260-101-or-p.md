# AD/SM-205/32 - Engine Induction Valve Assembly P/No. 205-5-260-101 or P/No. 205-5-260-01 - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti S205 and S208 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SM-205/32 Engine Induction Valve Assembly P/No. 205-5-260-**

**101 or P/No. 205-5-260-01 - Modification**

**5/82**

Applicability: All models S205/20F and S205/20R with S/Nos. referenced in SIAI Marchetti SB No.

205 B55 or with S/Nos. listed in subsequent issues of that SB. Document: 1. SIAI Marchetti SB No. 205B55. or

2\. RAI AD 82-10/5205-47.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1982.

Background: Breakage or loss of the MS 9048-006 pins securing the butterfly to the valve has been experienced overseas.

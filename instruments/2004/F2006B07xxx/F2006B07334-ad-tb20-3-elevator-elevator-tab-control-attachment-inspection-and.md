# AD/TB20/3 - Elevator/Elevator Tab Control Attachment Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/TB20/3 Elevator/Elevator Tab Control Attachment**

**Inspection and Modification**

Applicability: All models TB20 with S/Nos up to and including 479. Requirement: Socata TB SB No. 24 Modification No. 48.

Compliance: Operation 1: before further flight after 22 February 1985.

Operation 2: within 100 hours time in service after 22 February 1985.

Background: Socata warns of the risk of play in the tab rod/lever attachment.

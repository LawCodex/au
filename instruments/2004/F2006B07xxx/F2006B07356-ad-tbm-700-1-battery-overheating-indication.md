# AD/TBM 700/1 - Battery Overheating Indication

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/1 Battery Overheating Indication 5/93

Applicability: TBM 700 series airplanes identified in TBM 700 Socata Mandatory Service Bulletin No. 70-026-24 dated February 1993.

Requirement: Action in accordance with TBM 700 Socata Mandatory Service Bulletin No.

70-026-24 dated February 1993.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-019(B) dated 3 March 1993 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time-in-service from 29 April 1993.

Background: Compliance with this Directive will correct a wiring discrepancy in the “Battery Overheat” indication circuitry.

# AD/TSA-600/21 - Engine Nacelle Drain Tubes - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/21 Engine Nacelle Drain Tubes - Modification 6/79

Applicability: All models with Airframe Sequence Numbers below 0660. Document: 1. Piper Aerostar SB No. SB600-80, Part II; or

2\. FAA AD 79-08-05, Amendment 39-3449.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1979.

Background: In the event of a wing tank fuel leak there is a possibility that fuel will enter and accumulate in the lower nacelle compartment aft of the firewall to be ignited during engine startup.

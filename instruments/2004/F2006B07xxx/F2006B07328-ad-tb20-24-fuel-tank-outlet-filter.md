# AD/TB20/24 - Fuel Tank Outlet Filter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/24 Fuel Tank Outlet Filter 2/92

Applicability: Model TB20 and TB21 with serial number 1038 to 1308 inclusive. Aircraft S/N 1213, 1264 through 1267, 1284 through 1287, 1301 through 1304 and 1307 have previously been modified.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirement of DGAC AD 91-246(A).

*Note: Socata TB airplanes SB Nr 61 refers.*

Compliance: During the next periodic inspection after the 25 January 1992 or by 30 June 1992, whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive has been raised to ensure sump volume and unusable fuel quantity are correct.

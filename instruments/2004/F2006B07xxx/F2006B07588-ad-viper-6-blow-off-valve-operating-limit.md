# AD/VIPER/6 - Blow-Off Valve Operating Limit

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Rolls Royce Turbine Engines - Viper Series

AD/VIPER/6 Blow-Off Valve Operating Limit 4/90

Applicability: Viper Mk 601-22.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Rolls Royce SB Viper Mk 601-22 75-All. Compliance: Effective 19 April 1990.

Background: This Directive has been issued to require a revised blow-off valve operating point.

The Requirement reduces the possibility of engine surge or stall.

# AD/SM-205/28 - Flap Control Lever - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti S205 and S208 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SM-205/28 Flap Control Lever - Inspection 12/77

Applicability: All SIAI Marchetti models S-205/18F; S-205/18R, S205/20F, S-205/20R, S205/22R equipped with manual flap control.

Document: SIAI Marchetti SB No. 205B47.

Compliance: Initially within 25 hours time in service after 10 November 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service.

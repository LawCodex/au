# AD/TFE 731/14 - First Stage L.P. Turbine Disc

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Garrett/AiResearch) Turbine Engines - TFE731 Series

AD/TFE731/14 First Stage L.P. Turbine Disc 9/88 TX

Applicability: TFE 731-5 series engine with suspect first stage L.P. turbine disc whose serial numbers are identified in the Requirement document.

Requirement: Remove from service affected first stage L.P. turbine discs in accordance with

G.T.E.C. Alert SB TFE 731-A72-3371.

Compliance: For discs with less than 750 operating cycles: Prior to exceeding 750 operating cycles.

For discs with more than 750 operating cycles: Replace within 25 operating cycles after 27 June 1988.

Background: This Directive is issued to reflect GTEC Alert SB TFE 731-A72-3371 which advises the recent in-flight shutdown caused by separation of 2 adjacent firtree posts and release of three turbine blades. Engineering investigation determined that discs had been manufactured from a suspect forging lot.

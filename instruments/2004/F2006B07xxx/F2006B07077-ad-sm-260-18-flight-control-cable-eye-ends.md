# AD/SM-260/18 - Flight Control Cable Eye Ends

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti 260 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SM-260/18 Flight Control Cable Eye Ends 6/94

Applicability: Model F260, F260B, F260C, and F260D aircraft with serial numbers up to and including serial number 848.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SIAI Marchetti Service Bulletin 260B55.

*Note: RAI AD 94-016 refers.*

Compliance: At the next 100 hour inspection after 26 May 1994, or prior to 31 December 1994, whichever occurs first.

Background: Reports have been received of cracked control cable eye ends. This Directive requires a detailed inspection of the eye ends in the aileron, elevator, and rudder control cable runs.

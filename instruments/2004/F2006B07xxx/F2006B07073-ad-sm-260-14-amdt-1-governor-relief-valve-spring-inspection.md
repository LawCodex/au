# AD/SM-260/14 Amdt 1 - Governor Relief Valve Spring - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti 260 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SM-260/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Governor Relief Valve Spring - Inspection 9/83**

Applicability: All SIAI-Marchetti F260, F260B and F260C models equipped with the following propeller governors;

| HARTZELL | SIAI EQUIVALENT |
|---|---|
| F4-4A | 260-13-272-01 |
| F4-4A (Reset into FA-11) | 260-23-272-05 |
| F4-112 | 260-13-272-03 |
| F6-15A | 260-13-272-07 |
| F4-4A | 260-13-272-09 |

Document: SIAI Marchetti Service Bulletin 260 B35.

*Note: FAA AD 83-06-10 refers.*

Compliance: For those governors with a relief valve body which has a rectangular port, by 31 July 1983 and every maintenance release inspection thereafter. For those governors with a relief valve body which has a round port, by 31st July 1983 and every 500 hours time in service thereafter.

Background: 1. The manufacturer advises instances of spring P/No. A3107 breakage. Breakage of this spring can cause lowering of governor oil pressure and a harmful “overspeed” of the propeller.

2\. This AD has been reprinted to correct a typographical error under applicability for Hartzell FA-112, SIAI equivalent should read 260-13-272-03.

# AD/S-PUMA/3 Amdt 2 - Tail Rotor Head Spindles

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 332 (Super Puma) Series Helicopters

**AD/S-PUMA/3**

**Amdt 2**

**Tail Rotor Head Spindles 2/93**

Applicability: All models fitted with tail rotor head P/N 332A33-0001, all dash/point numbers. Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter S.B. AS 332 01.30 Revision 2.

*Note: DGAC AD 91-012-042 (B) Rev. 3 refers.*

Compliance: Unless the initial inspections are already accomplished in accordance with the previous issues of this Directive, within 30 hours time in service after 4 September 1991; thereafter, in accordance with the Requirement Document, with an effective date of 4 February 1993.

Background: The initial version of the Directive was issued following the fatigue failure of a tail rotor head spindle. Amendment 1 limited the inspections to specific part number tail rotor heads. Amendment 2 reflecting the DGAC requirement calls up the manufacturers requirements which have revised the inspection result analysis.

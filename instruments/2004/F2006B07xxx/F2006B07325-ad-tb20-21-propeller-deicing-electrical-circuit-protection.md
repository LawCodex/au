# AD/TB20/21 - Propeller Deicing Electrical Circuit Protection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/21 Propeller Deicing Electrical Circuit Protection 10/91

Applicability: All TB20 and TB21 aircraft as specified in the requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale TB Airplanes - SB Nr. 59.

Compliance: Prior to 3 October 1992.

Background: Compliance with this AD is necessary in order to ensure the integrity of the propeller de icing circuit by installing the correct rating fuse.

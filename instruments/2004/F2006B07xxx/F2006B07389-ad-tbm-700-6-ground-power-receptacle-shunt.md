# AD/TBM 700/6 - Ground Power Receptacle Shunt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/6 Ground Power Receptacle Shunt 13/94

Applicability: Model TBM 700 series airplanes with serial numbers 1 to 22 and 24 to 91 inclusive. Requirement: Unless previously accomplished action in accordance with Socata TBM 700

Mandatory Service Bulletin SB 70-038-24 dated February 1994 and associated

Erratum dated June 1994.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-112(B) dated 11 May 1994 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 8 December 1994.

Background: Compliance with this Directive is designed to prevent corrosion developing on the ground power receptacle shunt attaching screws.

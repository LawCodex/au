# AD/TB20/25 - Autopilot Master Switch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/25 Autopilot Master Switch 2/92

Applicability: All TB20 and TB21 with S/No’s 275 thru 1222 fitted with one of the following options: 668, G668, 669, G669, 670 or G670.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale SOCATA Service Bulletin No 60.

*Note: DGAC AD 91-145A refers.*

Compliance: Prior to the next Maintenance Release after 28 February 1992. Background: To reduce the risk of a short circuit at the “A/P Master” switch.

# AD/TB20/7 - Stabilator Control Rod Ends

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/7 Stabilator Control Rod Ends 4/87 DM

Applicability: All models with S/Nos 1 to 709, except S/Nos 652, 653, 661, 664, 665, 672, 691, 701

to 706.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata S.B. No. 29.

*Note: Bureau Veritas AD 87-003(A) refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time-in-service after 6 February 1987 and thereafter at each scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue until modified.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances of stabilator operating rod ends with excessive play due to loose rivets.

# AD/TB10/22 Amdt 1 - Front Seats

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/TB10/22**

**Amdt 1**

**Front Seats 13/94**

Applicability: Model TB9 and TB10 aircraft front seats with a tilting back-rest, installed from February 1991 on aircraft up to serial number 1479; except aircraft serial numbers 1192, 1193, 1308, 1426 through 1433, 1448, 1461, 1462, 1464 through 1469, 1471,

and 1473 through 1477.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TB Service Bulletin 10-075-25 Amendment 1.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-114(A)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 50 hours time in service after 8 December 1994, or at the next inspection for maintenance release issue, whichever occurs first.

Background: The DGAC requires reinforcement of the front seat structure.

Amendment 1 is issued to reflect an amendment of the manufacturer’s bulletin which clarifies applicability and weld filler material.

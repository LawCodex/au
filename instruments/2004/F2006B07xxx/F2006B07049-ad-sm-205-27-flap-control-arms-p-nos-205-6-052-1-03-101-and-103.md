# AD/SM-205/27 - Flap Control Arms P/Nos. 205-6-052-1, -03, -101 and -103 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti S205 and S208 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SM-205/27 Flap Control Arms P/Nos. 205-6-052-1,**

**-03, -101 and -103 - Inspection**

**7/77**

Applicability: All models 205.

Requirement: Visually inspect the reference P/No. flap control arms for cracks with the aid of a 10X glass or an approved dye penetrant technique.

*Note: RAI AD No. 77-91/S205-41 and SIAI MARCHETTI SB No. 205B47 refer to*

*this inspection.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1977.

# AD/VW/3 - Rear Bearing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Volkswagen Derivative Piston Engines

AD/VW/3 Rear Bearing 8/88

Applicability: Revmaster R2100 series engines.

Requirement: Modify the No 1 main bearing (rear) by providing a locating dowel for both bearing halves.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, at next disassembly of the crankcase assembly. Background: This Directive has been issued to reflect the requirements of cancelled Airworthiness

Directive ANO 106 AD/REV/3.

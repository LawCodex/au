# AD/T53/14 - Torque Meter Boost Pump - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Lycoming) Turbine Engines - T53 Series

AD/T53/14 Torque Meter Boost Pump - Replacement 1/86

Applicability: All T53 series engines.

Document: 1. Remove torque meter boost pump P/No. 1-300-221-01 or P/No. 1-300-221-02 and replace with P/No. 1-300-221.04; or

*Note: Avco Lycoming SB No. 0031 pertains to this subject.*

2\. FAA AD 75-22-02, Amendment 39-2385; or

3\. AD/LYC-T/15.

Compliance: Within 200 hours time in service after 31 December 1975.

# AD/TB20/33 - Upper Attachment of Front Seat Belts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/33 Upper Attachment of Front Seat Belts 11/96 DM

Applicability: S/Nos. 1 to 1701, 1707 to 1750, 1758 to 1763, 1767 and 1769. Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata SB 10-103-25.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-143(A) refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service, or 30 days, after 14 August 1996 whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive is issued to ensure the integrity of the upper attachment of the front seat belts.

# AD/TSA-600/22 - Cabin Door Upper Section - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/22 Cabin Door Upper Section - Modification 11/79

Applicability: All model 600 with S/Nos. 60-0001 to 60-0056; and All model 601 with S/Nos. 61-

0001 to 61-0070.

Requirement: Install in the cabin door upper section latching mechanism, spacers and the locking position visual indicators in accordance with Aerostar SB No. 600-35.

*Note: FAA AD 79-18-10, Amdt. 39-3535 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1979.

Background: This Directive adds the modification action of Aerostar SB 600-35 to prevent inflight loss of the cabin upper door section.

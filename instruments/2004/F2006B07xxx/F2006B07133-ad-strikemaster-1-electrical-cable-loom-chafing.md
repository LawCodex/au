# AD/STRIKEMASTER/1 - Electrical Cable Loom Chafing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
BAe (BAC 167) Strikemaster Series Aeroplanes

AD/STRIKEMASTER/1 Electrical Cable Loom Chafing 12/96 DM

Applicability: This Directive is applicable to all British Aerospace Corporation (BAC) 167 Strikemaster aeroplanes.

Requirement: Inspect, protect, restrain and if necessary repair the DC generating system electrical cables in accordance with British Aerospace Defence Limited, Military Aircraft Division, Company Special Technical Instruction (CSTI) Strikemaster 162 dated 4 April 1995.

*Note: CAA(UK) Mandatory Permit Directive 1995-102 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 8 October 1996.

Background: The CAA(UK) has received a report of a short circuit in the DC generating system caused by heavy duty cables chafing against the thread/anchor nut of the port mainplane trailing edge fairing attachment bolt. The CSTI provides for a visual examination for cable chafing or damage, repair or replacement of the damaged cables, cable protection and encapsulation of the anchor nut assembly in sealing compound.

# AD/TSA-600/12 - Fuel Crossfeed Placard - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/12 Fuel Crossfeed Placard - Installation 3/78

Applicability: All models with Airframe Sequence Numbers below 0475.

Requirement: Install placard P/N TSA 030100-1 on the instrument panel in accordance with Ted Smith Corp. SB No. SB600-70.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1978.

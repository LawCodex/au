# AD/SM-260/16 - Wing Main Spar Web

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti 260 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SM-260/16 Wing Main Spar Web 6/87

Applicability: All model F260, F260B, F260C and F260D with Serial Numbers up to and including 735.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SIAI Marchetti Service Bulletin No. 260-B50.

*Note: RAI AD 86-199/F260-32 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Initial Inspection.

(a) For aircraft which have achieved less than 1000 hours time in service as at 4 June 1987 - Prior to exceeding 1000 hours time in service or within 500 hours time in service after 4 June 1987, whichever is the later.

(b) For aircraft which have achieved 1000 or more hours time in service as at 4 June 1987, but less than 3000 hours - Within 500 hours time in service after 4 June 1987 or prior to exceeding 3100 hours, whichever is the sooner.

(c) For aircraft which have achieved 3000 or more hours time in service as at 4 June 1987 - Within 100 hours time in service after 4 June 1987.

2\. Repeat Inspections. For all aircraft, repeat the inspections at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service until modified in accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive introduces an inspection for cracks in the wing main spar web.

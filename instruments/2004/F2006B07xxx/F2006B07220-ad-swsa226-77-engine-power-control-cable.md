# AD/SWSA226/77 - Engine Power Control Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/77 Engine Power Control Cable 5/96

Applicability: SA226-T, SA226-T(B), SA226-AT, SA226-TC, SA227-TT, SA227-AT, SA227-AC, SA227-BC, SA227-CC, and SA227-DC.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the following Fairchild Service Bulletins as applicable: a. 226-76-009,

b. 227-76-004, or

c. CC7-76-001.

*Note: FAA AD 96-03-03 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 25 April 1996.

Background: There have been reports of cable attaching hardware failing, one failure of which resulted in engine shut down. This Directive is intended to preclude such a failure.

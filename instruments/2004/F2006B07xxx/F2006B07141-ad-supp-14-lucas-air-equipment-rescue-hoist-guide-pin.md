# AD/SUPP/14 - Lucas-Air Equipment Rescue Hoist Guide Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Supplementary Equipment

AD/SUPP/14 Lucas-Air Equipment Rescue Hoist Guide Pin 2/91

Applicability: Electric Rescue Hoists P/No's 76370, 76370-010, 76370-011, 76370-020 and

76370-030.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of French DGAC AD 90-121 (AB).

*Note: Lucas Air Equipment SB 25-62-OK refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with effective date 28 July 1990.

Background: Country of origin AD. Reduces likelihood of reversion of cable winding direction due to wear of guide pin.

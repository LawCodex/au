# AD/X-TS/3 - Canard Rear Spar Reinforcement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eagle X-TS Series Aeroplanes

AD/X-TS/3 Canard Rear Spar Reinforcement 2/2000 TX

Applicability: Models X-TS 150 and 150B serial numbers 001 to 003 and 005 to 030 inclusive. Requirement: Modify the canard rear spar bracket in accordance with Eagle Service Bulletin 1074. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after the effective date of this directive.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 24 December 1999.

Background: This Directive is issued to provide additional strength to the canard rear spar bracket, to enable the aircraft to meet certification requirements.

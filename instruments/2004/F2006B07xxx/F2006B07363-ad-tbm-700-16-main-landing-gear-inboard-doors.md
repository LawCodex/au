# AD/TBM 700/16 - Main Landing Gear Inboard Doors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/16 Main Landing Gear Inboard Doors 5/96 DM

Applicability: All aircraft with modification No. MOD 70-065-32 embodied or option No.

OPT 70-57-001 installed.

Requirement: Remove the main landing gear inboard doors in accordance with SOCATA TBM 700 SB No. 70-073-32.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-037(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 15 March 1996.

Background: This Directive is issued to avoid the possibility of the main landing gear remaining locked "up" due to the presence of corrosion products. Inboard door removal is an interim solution.

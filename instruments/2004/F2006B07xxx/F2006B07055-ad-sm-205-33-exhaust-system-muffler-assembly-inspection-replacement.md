# AD/SM-205/33 - Exhaust System Muffler Assembly - Inspection - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SIAI Marchetti S205 and S208 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SM-205/33 Exhaust System Muffler Assembly - Inspection - Replacement

9/83

Applicability: All S205-22R aircraft fitted with muffler assembly P/Ns. 870-8 or P/Ns. 870-9. Document: SIAI Marchetti SB No. 205B56.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30th September 1983, or, when muffler assembly has accumulated 500 hours time in service, which- ever occurs later, after 30 September 1983. Thereafter, at intervals of 100 hours time in service.

Background: Instances of detachment of the muffler baffling have been reported by the manufacturer which can result in partial or complete loss of engine power.

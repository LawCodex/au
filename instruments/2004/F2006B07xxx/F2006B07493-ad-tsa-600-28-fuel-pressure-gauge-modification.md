# AD/TSA-600/28 - Fuel Pressure Gauge - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/28 Fuel Pressure Gauge - Modification 11/80

Applicability: Model 601 and 601P series.

Requirement: Mark the left hand and right hand engine fuel pressure gauges with a yellow arc over the 12 psi to 18 psi range.

*Note: FAA AD 80-18-03 Amdt. 39-3900 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1980.

Background: This directive is issued to prevent possible engine failures due to excessively lean fuel/air mixture operation and supplements corresponding flight manual amendment.

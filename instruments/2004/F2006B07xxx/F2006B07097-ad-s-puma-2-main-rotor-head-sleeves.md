# AD/S-PUMA/2 - Main Rotor Head - Sleeves

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 332 (Super Puma) Series Helicopters

AD/S-PUMA/2 Main Rotor Head - Sleeves 2/91

Applicability: Versions C, C1, L and L1 which are fitted with main rotor head sleeve P/N 330A31 1376.04 or 330A31 1376.16.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of DGAC AD 86-121-025(B) Revision 1, dated 19 September 1990.

*Note: Aerospatiale AS 332 SB No 05.09 R3.*

Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

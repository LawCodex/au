# AD/S-PUMA/9 - Electrical Power System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 332 (Super Puma) Series Helicopters

AD/S-PUMA/9 Electrical Power System 11/92

Applicability: All AS 332 helicopters, versions C, C1, L and L1, not modified per AMS 332A

07.26.044 or by kit No 332A.08.8133.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter France Service Bulletin AS 332 No 24.20 dated 2 June 1992.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-143-048(B) dated 8 July 1992 refers.*

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service or three calendar months from 15 October 1992, whichever occurs first.

Background: Eurocopter advises that that a pressurisation system hydraulic line was perforated, following continued interference with a wire harness. Compliance with this Directive removes the possibility of the wire harness interference with and subsequent failure of the hydraulic line.

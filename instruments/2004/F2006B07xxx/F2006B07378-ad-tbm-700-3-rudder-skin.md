# AD/TBM 700/3 - Rudder Skin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/3 Rudder Skin 1/94

Applicability: Model TBM 700 aircraft with serial numbers 1 through 19, 21, 22, 25 through 34, 38,

39, 46, 49, 50, 52, 53, 57, 59, 61 through 63, 67, 68, and 71 through 75.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TBM 700 Service Bulletin 70-027-55.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-178(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 6 January 1994.

Background: The DGAC determined that unbonding between the rudder skin and the rear spar could occur unless certain actions are instigated.

# AD/TB10/4 - Wing Flap Deflection - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/4 Wing Flap Deflection - Modification 5/82

Applicability: All TB10 aircraft from S/No. 1 up to and including S/No. 239. Document: Rallye TB SB No. 10 GR.27.17.

*Note: Upon incorporation of this modification aircraft flight manuals should have amendment 4 incorporated and the contents of amendment 3 removed.*

Compliance: Before certification.

Background: In order to maintain adequate longitudinal stability with full flap extended, maximum flap extension has been reduced from 32o to 25o30’. Additionally the forward C.G. limit is amended to 11% M.A.C. or .949m.

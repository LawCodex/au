# AD/TFE 731/18 - Digital Electronic Engine Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Garrett/AiResearch) Turbine Engines - TFE731 Series

AD/TFE731/18 Digital Electronic Engine Control 2/90

Applicability: TFE 731-2, -5, -5AR, -5R series engines as listed in Garrett SB TFE 731-76-3049.

Requirement: Replace suspect digital electronic engine controls (DEEC) as listed by serial number in Garrett SB TFE 731-76-3049.

*Note: FAA AD 89-22-16 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1990.

Background: This Directive reflects advice from the manufacturer of a possibility that uncommanded thrust increase or decrease may occur due to a defect in certain electronic engine controls.

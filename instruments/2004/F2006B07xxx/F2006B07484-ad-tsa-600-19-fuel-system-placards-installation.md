# AD/TSA-600/19 - Fuel System Placards - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/19 Fuel System Placards - Installation 5/79

Applicability: All models.

Document: Piper Aerostar SB No. SB600-79. Compliance: Before 30 June 1979.

Background: Resulting from a recent review of the Aerostar fuel system by the FAA, additional and revised placards are now required to be installed in accordance with SB No.

SB600-79.

# AD/SWSA226/42 Amdt 1 - Prohibition of Flight into Known Icing Conditions - Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SWSA226/42**

**Amdt 1**

**Prohibition of Flight into Known Icing Conditions - Placard**

**12/82**

Applicability: Models SA227-AC, SA227-AT, SA227-TT, not modified in accordance with SA227 Service Bulletin A61-002 or Service Bulletin 30-001.

Requirement: Install a placard, minimum 1/4" lettering on the instrument panel in plain view of the pilot stating “Flight into known icing conditions prohibited”.

Compliance: Forthwith.

Background: Overseas operational experience indicates that propeller de-icing is not adequate.

FAA Directive 82-05-05 withdraws approval for operation in icing conditions.

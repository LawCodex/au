# AD/WILGA/2 - Stall Warning System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
PZL 104 (Wilga) Series Aeroplanes

AD/WILGA/2 Stall Warning System 2/95

Applicability: All PZL-104 \`Wilga 80’ aircraft.

Requirement: Install a guard on the Stall Warning System automatic switch in accordance with PZL-Warszawa-Okecie Mandatory Service Bulletin 10493023.

*Note: This Service Bulletin has been classified as Mandatory by the General Inspectorate of Civil Aviation Poland.*

Compliance: Prior to 1 August 1995.

Background: This Directive introduces measures whereby the Stall Warning System cannot be inadvertently switched off, preventing the operation of the acoustic warning horn.

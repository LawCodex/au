# AD/VAT/29 - Elevator Trim Adjustment Segment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Victa and A.E.S.L. Airtourer Series Aeroplanes

AD/VAT/29 Elevator Trim Adjustment Segment - Inspection 6/67

Applicability: Victa Airtourer 115.

Requirement: Inspect the elevator trim adjustment segment P/N 52528/2 on the trim lever assembly for wear or damage to the locking teeth.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified in accordance with Victa SB No. 61.

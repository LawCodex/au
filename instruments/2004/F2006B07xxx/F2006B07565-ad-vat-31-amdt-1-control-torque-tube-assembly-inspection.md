# AD/VAT/31 Amdt 1 - Control Torque Tube Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Victa and A.E.S.L. Airtourer Series Aeroplanes

**AD/VAT/31**

**Amdt 1**

**Control Torque Tube Assembly - Inspection 9/79**

Applicability: Airtourer 100 and 115.

Requirement: Inspect both upper and lower cut outs in the Control Column Torque Tube P/N 52131/30 for cracking. The area can be inspected by removing the arm rest front panel P/N 53018/1.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 30 September 1979.

# AD/TB10/2 - Horizontal Stabilizer Rivets - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/TB10/2 Horizontal Stabilizer Rivets - Inspection

**and Modification** Applicability: All models TB.9 and TB.10 with s/Nos. prior to 172. Document: Rallye TB SB No. 8 GR.55-07.

## 8/81

Compliance: 1. Initially inspect within 25 hours time in service after 23 June 1981 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until all rivets referenced in SB No. 8 have been replaced.

2\. Modify by replacement of rivets in accordance with requirement document within 300 hours time in service after 23 June 1981.

Background: Service experience has shown that certain rivets in the horizontal stabilizer are prone to work loose.

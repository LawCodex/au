# AD/Z 526/2 - Main Wing Spar Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Morovan Zlin Z 526 Series Aeroplanes

AD/Z 526/2 Main Wing Spar Corrosion 11/96

Applicability: All models Z 526 aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with Morovan Mandatory Service Bulletin Z 526/69a.

*Note: Czech CAI AD 2-016/96 refers.*

Compliance: Before 10 November 1996, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 12 months.

Background: The Czech civil aviation authority received reports of exfoliation corrosion on the main wing spar flanges which seriously degraded the structural strength of the wing.

# AD/TFE 731/7 Amdt 2 - Turbine Interstage Transition Duct

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Garrett/AiResearch) Turbine Engines - TFE731 Series

**AD/TFE731/7**

**Amdt 2**

**Turbine Interstage Transition Duct 11/87**

Applicability: TFE 731-2, -3, -3A, -3AR, -3B, -3BR and -3R engines whose serial numbers are detailed in the Requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with GTEC SB TFE-731-72-3159R6.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, at the next major periodic inspection or at the next access of affected part whichever occurs first after 30 June 1986.

*Note: Compliance with the previous issue of this Directive satisfies compliance with this amendment.*

Background: This Directive is amended to reflect the manufacturers revision 6 of SB

TFE 731-72-3159 which updates engine application and includes reference to FAA AD 81-24-08 which addresses this subject.

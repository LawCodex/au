# AD/TB10/14 Amdt 1 - Front Seat Safety Belts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/TB10/14**

**Amdt 1**

**Front Seat Safety Belts 3/95**

Applicability: All Model TB9, TB10 and TB200 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SOCATA Mandatory Service Bulletin 10.044.25 Amendment 1.

*Note: DGAC AD 89-097(A)R1 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 2 March 1996, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 12 calendar months.

Background: Seat belt anchorage fittings are hidden by aircraft trim and therefore are not readily inspectable for corrosion.

Amendment 1 is issued to align this Directive with a revision of the Country of Origin AD which reduces the inspection interval.

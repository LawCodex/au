# AD/TBM 700/4 - Elevator and Rudder Drainage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/4 Elevator and Rudder Drainage 1/94

Applicability: Model TBM 700 aircraft with serial numbers 2 through 19, 21, 22, 24 through 34, 38,

39, 46, 49, 50, 52, 53, 57, 59 through 63, 67, 68, 70 through 78, 80, and 82 through

85\.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TBM 700 Service Bulletin 70-028-55.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-179(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 6 January 1994.

Background: The DGAC determined that inadequate drainage exists at the central bearing of the empennage control surfaces.

# AD/TB10/28 - Upper Attachment of Front Seat Belts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/28 Upper Attachment of Front Seat Belts 11/96 DM

Applicability: S/Nos. 1 to 1701, 1707 to 1750, 1758 to 1763,1767, 1768, and 1769. Requirement: For TB9 aircraft not equipped with upholstering on the upper duct post:

\- action in accordance with Socata SB 10-104-25.

For TB9, TB10 and TB200 aircraft with upholstering on the upper duct post:

\- action in accordance with Socata SB 10-103-25.

*Note: DGAC ADs 96-142(A) and 96-143(A) refer.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service, or 30 days, after 14 August 1996 whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive is issued to ensure the integrity of the upper attachment of the front seat belts.

# AD/TSA-600/26 - Nacelle Fairing - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerostar (Piper/Ted Smith) 600 and 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TSA-600/26 Nacelle Fairing - Modification 8/80

Applicability: Models 600, 601 and 601P with S/Nos. 0001 to 0799. Document: 1. Piper SB No. SB600-83; or

2\. FAA AD 80-12-14, Amendment 39-3801.

Compliance: Before 1 March 1981.

Background: The lower engine nacelle aft fibreglass fairing is inadequate to prevent flames from a possible engine fire entering the lower wing structure.

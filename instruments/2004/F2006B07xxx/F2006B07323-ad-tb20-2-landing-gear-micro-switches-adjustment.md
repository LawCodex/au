# AD/TB20/2 - Landing Gear Micro Switches - Adjustment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/2 Landing Gear Micro Switches - Adjustment 3/85

Applicability: All model TB20 with S/Nos up to and including 459.

Requirement: Adjust the undercarriage micro switches according to the requirements contained in Socata TB SB No 23.

*Note: French AD 85-4(A) refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 31 March 1985.

Background: Compliance with this Directive will minimise the possibility of inadvertent undercarriage collapse.

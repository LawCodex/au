# AD/TPE 331/44 - Pitch Control Gasket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
AlliedSignal (Garrett/AiResearch) Turbine Engines - TPE 331 Series

AD/TPE 331/44 Pitch Control Gasket 2/93 TX

Applicability: TPE 331-1, -2, -3, -5, -6, -8, -10, -11, -12, -14, -25, -43, -47A, -55B and -61A engines

that have had the propeller pitch control gasket replaced after 10 May 1992.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Garrett (Allied-Signals) Service Bulletin TPE331-A72- 7092 or TPE331-A72-0857, as applicable.

*Note: FAA Priority Letter Airworthiness Directive 92-26-07 refers.*

Compliance: A. For installed engines. Within the next 10 hours time in service from 21 December 1992.

B. For uninstalled engines. Prior to installation in an aircraft.

Background: Several failures of the propeller pitch control gasket have been reported. Failure of the gasket resulted in a sudden loss of engine oil and propeller control. In one incident the failure occurred during the landing run when reverse pitch was applied.

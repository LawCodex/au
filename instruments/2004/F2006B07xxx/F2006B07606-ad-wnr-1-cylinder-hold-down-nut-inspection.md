**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Warner Piston Engines

AD/WNR/1 Cylinder Hold-Down Nut - Inspection NK

Applicability: All Scarab and Super Scarab engines not modified to Warner SL No. A-15. Document: 1. Warner SL No. A-15; or

2\. ANO 106.1.16.0.2.1; or

3\. FAA AD 51-4-2.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

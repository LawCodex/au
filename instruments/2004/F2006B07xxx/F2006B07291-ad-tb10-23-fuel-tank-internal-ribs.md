# AD/TB10/23 - Fuel Tank Internal Ribs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/23 Fuel Tank Internal Ribs 3/95

Applicability: TB aircraft serial numbers 1038 through 1442, 1444 through 1462, 1464 through

1581, 1583 through 1606, 1608 through 1619, and 1629; and aircraft serial numbers 1 through 1037 on which the tank rib has been repaired since the application of modification No.78.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata Mandatory Service Bulletin 10.084.57.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-247(A) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 2 March 1995 or prior to 31 December 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: Tank sealant has been found obstructing the fuel tank internal rib drain holes. This Directive requires fuel tank internal inspections to ensure the necessary drain holes are clear.

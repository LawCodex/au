*Note: FAA Priority Letter AD 95-24-10 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to further flight after 30 November 1995.

Background: There have been two reports of failure (rupture) of the main landing gear tyre during operations on Cessna Citation VII aircraft. The cause of the failures is attributed to separations that developed in the crown region of the tyre due to low adhesion caused by misplaced rubber compound.

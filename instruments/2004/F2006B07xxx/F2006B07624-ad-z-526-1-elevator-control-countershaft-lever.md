# AD/Z 526/1 - Elevator Control Countershaft Lever

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Morovan Zlin Z 526 Series Aeroplanes

AD/Z 526/1 Elevator Control Countershaft Lever 3/95

Applicability: All Z 526 F, Z 526 L and Z 526 AFS models.

Requirement: 1. Visually inspect the elevator control countershaft lever for cracking with a 10x glass. Inspect in accordance with Moravan Service Bulletin (SB) Z 526/58 Process of Work, Part 1.

2\. Replace the elevator control countershaft P/N Z 326.314.1 with new P/N Z

326.314.2 in accordance with Moravan SB Z 526/58 Process of Work, Part 2.

Compliance: 1. Initially inspect before 10 hours time in service after 2 March 1995 or before 2 April 1995, whichever occurs first. Re-inspect at intervals not to exceed 100 hours time in service.

2\. Replace the countershaft before 2 September 1995.

*Note: Countershaft replacement does not cancel the Requirement 1 inspection.*

Background: There is a potential for fatigue cracking in the elevator control countershaft.

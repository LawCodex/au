# AD/SWSA226/76 - Main Landing Gear Door Actuator

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/76 Main Landing Gear Door Actuator 4/96

Applicability: Models SA226-T, SA226-T(B), SA226-AT and SA226-TC S/N range as specified in the requirement document.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out, action in accordance with Fairchild Aircraft Service Bulletin 226-32-059 dated February 14, 1991.

*Note: FAA AD 95-25-07 amdt 39-9452 refers.*

Compliance: Within 1000 hours time in service from 28 March 1996.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that if the gear door actuator is misrigged the gear doors may fail to open.

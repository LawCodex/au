# AD/TB20/8 - Aileron Balance Weight Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/8 Aileron Balance Weight Attachment 5/87

Applicability: All TB20 with S/Nos 275 to 700 except S/No 343, 638, 639, 643, 644, 645, 653, 664,

665, 672, 673, 683, 691, 693, 697, 698.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TB SB No 28.

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 7 May 1987 and thereafter at each scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue until modified.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances of loosening of balance weights and skin cracking in the balance weight attachment area.

# AD/SUPP/10 - Breeze-Eastern Hoists

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Supplementary Equipment

AD/SUPP/10 Breeze-Eastern Hoists 11/88

Applicability: All Breeze-Eastern rescue hoists.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Breeze-Eastern Alert Advisory Bulletin No. CAB-100-30. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: The manufacturer has determined that damage to a particular area of the load cable may evade detection during normal inspection, which could result in a serious compromise of cable integrity. This AD introduces regular inspections which are intended to significantly improve the operational safety of the rescue hoist system.

# AD/SWSA226/23 - Fuel and Hyraulic Lines Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/23 Fuel and Hyraulic Lines Inspection 11/76

Applicability: All SA-226 series not incorporating Swearingen SB No. 54-003. Document: Swearingen SB 54-002.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 28 October 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service.

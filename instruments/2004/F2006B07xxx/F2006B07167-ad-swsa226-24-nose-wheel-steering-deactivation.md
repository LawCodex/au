# AD/SWSA226/24 - Nose Wheel Steering - Deactivation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SWSA226/24 Nose Wheel Steering - Deactivation 8/77

Applicability: All models fitted with variable authority nose wheel steering. Document: Swearingen Alert SB No. 32-012.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 3 August 1977.

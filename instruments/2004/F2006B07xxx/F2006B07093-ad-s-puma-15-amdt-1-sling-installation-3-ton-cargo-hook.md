# AD/S-PUMA/15 Amdt 1 - Sling Installation - 3 Ton Cargo Hook

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter AS 332 (Super Puma) Series Helicopters

**AD/S-PUMA/15**

**Amdt 1**

**Sling Installation - 3 Ton Cargo Hook 12/94**

Applicability: Models C, C1, L and L1 equipped with a sling installation fitted with cargo hook Part Number AS21-7 with a mechanical emergency jettison control Part Number AS22- 25.

Requirement: Replace the mechanical emergency jettison control P/N AS22-25 with control P/N AS22-12.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-150-052(B)R1 and AS 332 SB No. 01-38 R1 refer.*

Compliance: The compliance of the original AD remains; Prior to next use of the cargo hook after 27 July 1994. This amendment is effective 10 November 1994.

Background: This Directive is issued to preclude uncommanded load jettison.

Amendment 1 reflects the revision status of the DGAC AD and includes reference to the Eurocopter SB.

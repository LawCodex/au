# AD/TBM 700/8 - Propeller Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/8 Propeller Control 13/94

Applicability: All TBM 700 series aircraft within the S/N 1 to 22, 24 to 92 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TBM 700 Service Bulletin 70-042-76.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-109-(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service from 8 December 1994.

Background: To improve the governor control the manufacturer requires the propeller governor screws to be lockwired.

# AD/VW/2 - Cast Crankshafts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Volkswagen Derivative Piston Engines

AD/VW/2 Cast Crankshafts 8/88

Applicability: Revmaster R2100D engines fitted with ductile steel crankshafts.

Requirement: Replace cast crankshafts fitted to applicable engines with the equivalent forged crankshaft.

Compliance: Before installation in an aircraft.

Background: This Directive has been issued to reflect, in part, the requirements of cancelled Airworthiness Directive ANO 106 AD/REV/1A. The inspection requirements of AD/REV/1A are n ow contained in Airworthiness Directive ANO 106 AD/VW/1.

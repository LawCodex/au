# AD/TB10/11 Amdt 1 - Fuselage Frame 9

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/TB10/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuselage Frame 9 6/88**

Applicability: TB9 and TB10 series aeroplanes with S/N 1 to 795 without modification No 70 embodied.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale (Socata) Service Bulletin No 35/1.

Compliance: (a) For aircraft with 1500 hours or more total time in service - inspect before further flight after 9 October 1987.

(b) For aircraft with less than 1475 hours total time in service - inspect within 25 hours time in service after 9 October 1987.

(c) Thereafter, reinspect at each issue of a new Maintenance Release.

*Note: Embodiment of Aerospatiale (Socata) modification No 70 constitutes terminating action for this Directive.*

Background: Notification has been received from the manufacturer of in service cracking on overseas aircraft. This Directive has been amended to reflect the terminating action specified in the Requirement document.

# AD/SWSA226/58 Amdt 1 - Main Landing Gear Doors

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/SWSA226/58**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Landing Gear Doors 10/90**

Applicability: As listed in the applicable Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Fairchild SBs SA226-32-055 or SA227-32-027 as applicable.

*Note: FAA AD 90-05-06 R1 Amendment 39-6519 as revised by Amendment 39-6668 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 12 July 1990 or at the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 12 July 1990 whichever is the later.

Background: There have been incidents overseas where the MLG doors have jammed against the nacelle and thus preventing gear extension.

Amendment 1 reflects the revised FAA AD status.

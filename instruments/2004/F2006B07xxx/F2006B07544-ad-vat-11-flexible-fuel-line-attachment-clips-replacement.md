# AD/VAT/11 - Flexible Fuel Line Attachment Clips - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Victa and A.E.S.L. Airtourer Series Aeroplanes

AD/VAT/11 Flexible Fuel Line Attachment Clips - Replacement 2/64

Applicability: Victa Airtourer 100.

Requirement: Replace all plastic clips supporting flexible fuel lines in the engine compartment with suitable metal clips.

Compliance: Before 1 September 1971.

# AD/TB20/19 - Oil Cooler Inlet and Outlet Elbows

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/19 Oil Cooler Inlet and Outlet Elbows 1/91

Applicability: All model TB20 and TB21 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata Service Bulletin NR 50-1.

*Note: DGAC AD 90-143(A) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 January 1991.

Background: Leakage from the oil cooler inlet and outlet elbow has resulted in total engine oil loss.

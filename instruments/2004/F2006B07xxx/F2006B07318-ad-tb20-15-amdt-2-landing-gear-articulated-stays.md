# AD/TB20/15 Amdt 2 - Landing Gear Articulated Stays

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/TB20/15**

**Amdt 2**

**Landing Gear Articulated Stays 3/91**

Applicability: Model TB20 and TB21 series with serial numbers 275 through 1119, except S/N 1109.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of DGAC AD 89-096 (A) R2.

*Note: Socata TB SB 40 and 45/2 refer.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished - Prior to exceeding 100 hours time in service after 22 March 1990, and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service, or 1 year whichever occurs first, until modified in accordance with SB 45/1 or 45/2.

Background: To prevent the landing gear from jamming.

Amendment 1 referenced latest Requirement Documents. SB 45/1 listed kits 9165 and 9166 which if installed terminate Directive requirements.

Amendment 2 is raised to introduce the Country of origin AD as the Requirement Document plus additional information contained in Rev 2 of SB 45.

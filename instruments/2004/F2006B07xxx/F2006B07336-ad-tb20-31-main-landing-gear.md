# AD/TB20/31 - Main Landing Gear

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/31 Main Landing Gear 8/95 DM

Applicability: TB20 and TB21 aircraft, on which the ring on the attachment bearing of the LH or RH main landing gear has been removed and replaced during maintenance.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Socata TB Service Bulletin SB10.091.57.

*Note: DGAC AD 95-084(A) refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 31 May 1995, or prior to 31 July 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: This Directive is issued to ensure that the rings, when replaced, have been correctly installed on the attachment bearing of the hinged strut.

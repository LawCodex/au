# AD/SUPP/4 - Air Equipment Rescue Hoist - Inspection and Modification of Squib

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Supplementary Equipment

**AD/SUPP/4 Air Equipment Rescue Hoist - Inspection**

**and Modification of Squib**

**10/82**

Applicability: Air Equipment Rescue Hoists P/Nos. 76370, 76370-010, 76370-020 and 76375.

*Note: These hoists may be installed on (but not limited to) the following helicopters:- Aerospatiale SA 341, SA 342, 3160, 316, 319, AS350, AS 355, SA 360C, SA 365C and SA 365N.*

Requirement: 1. Inspect and modify hoists P/Nos. 76370, 76370-010 and 76370-020 in accordance with Air Equipment Service Bulletin 25-62-0C Rev. 1 or later approved revisions.

2\. Inspect and modify hoist P/No. 76375 in accordance with Air Equipment Service Bulletin 25-69-0B Rev. 1 or later approved revisions.

3\. For hoists not modified in accordance with paras. 1 and 2 provide a hand cable cutter capable of ensuring a positive quick cable cut.

Compliance: For Paras. 1 and 2 - Before 1 October 1982. For Para. 3 - Before operation of the hoist.

Background: There have been several reported overseas defects which could cause the pyrotechnical shearing device to become inoperative.

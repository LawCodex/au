# AD/TB10/16 - Lower Engine Cowl Carburettor Air Supply

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/16 Lower Engine Cowl Carburettor Air Supply 3/91

Applicability: All TB9 & TB10 series aircraft from S/No’s 1 to 944 inclusive fitted with lower cowling carburettor air supply.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of DGAC AD 90-240 (A). Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with revised effective date of 31 March

1991\.

Background: The lower engine cowl \`NACA’ fitting can be damaged if the supply hose clamp is overtightened. If the fitting is damaged the intake hose could come loose possibly restricting engine operation.

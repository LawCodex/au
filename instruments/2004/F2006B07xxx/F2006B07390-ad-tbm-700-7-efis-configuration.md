# AD/TBM 700/7 - EFIS Configuration

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TBM 700 Series Aeroplanes

AD/TBM 700/7 EFIS Configuration 13/94

Applicability: Model TBM 700 series airplanes with serial numbers 14, 19, 21, 24, 25, 27, 49, 50,

53, 62, 74, 84 and 85.

Requirement: Unless previously accomplished action in accordance with Socata TBM 700 Mandatory Service Bulletin SB 70-030-34 dated March 1994.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-068(B) dated 30 March 1994 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 8 December 1994.

Background: Compliance with this Directive ensures that the same symbology is used on the EADI and the standby horizon ADI.

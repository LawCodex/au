# AD/TB20/4 - Wing Drainage - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB20 (Trinidad) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB20/4 Wing Drainage - Modification 4/85 DM

Applicability: All model TB20 with S/Nos up to 433 and No 444. Requirement: Aerospatiale SOCATA TB SB No. 26.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within the next 50 hours time in service after 8 March 1985.

Background: The purpose of this modification is to provide proper drainage of the wing tip forward spar box.

# AD/SWSA226/34 - Fuselage Skin and Aft Belly Fairing - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Fairchild (Swearingen) SA226 and SA227 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/SWSA226/34 Fuselage Skin and Aft Belly Fairing - Inspection

Applicability: All model SA226T with S/Nos. T201 to T275 and T277 to T291; All model

## 9/81

SA226AT with S/Nos. AT001 to AT074; All model SA226TC with S/Nos. TC201 to TC407.

Document: 1. Swearingen SB No. 53-006, and

2\. FAA AD 81-15-01 refers.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 250 hours time in service after 30 September 1981.

Background: Chafing may occur on the lower fuselage skin due to contact between the aft belly fairing stringers and the fuselage.

# AD/SF340/72 - MLG Hydraulic Swivel Brackets

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
SAAB SF340 Series Aeroplanes

AD/SF340/72 MLG Hydraulic Swivel Brackets 10/99

Applicability: Model SF340A aircraft, serial numbers 004 through 159, and Model 340B aircraft, serial numbers 160 through 339.

Requirement: Action in accordance with SAAB Aircraft AB Mandatory Service Bulletin 340-29- 009 Revision 2, dated 2 July 1999.

*Note: LFV AD 1-145 refers.*

Compliance: Before 2 July 2000.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 7 October 1999.

Background: A misalignment of the downlock or brake swivels in the main landing gear dragbrace and shock strut trunnions has, due to excessive loads during extension/retraction of the landing gear, resulted in abnormally high failure rate of the hydraulic swivels; and subsequent loss of hydraulic systems.

# AD/TB10/17 - Horizontal Stabilizer Balance Weight

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale (Socata) TB9 and TB10 (Tobago) Series Aeroplanes

AD/TB10/17 Horizontal Stabilizer Balance Weight 4/91 DM

Applicability: Model TB9 and TB10 series with serial numbers 1 through 1217. Requirement: Action in accordance with Alert SB 57.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 13 February 1991.

Background: Directive requires an inspection to determine whether both attaching nuts of the horizontal stabilizer balance weight are within the required safety dimension.

Detachment of balance weight may lead to a dangerous flutter condition.

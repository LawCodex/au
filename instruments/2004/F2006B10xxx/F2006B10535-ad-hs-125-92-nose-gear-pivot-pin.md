# AD/HS 125/92 - Nose Gear Pivot Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/92 Nose Gear Pivot Pin 7/88 DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe-125 SB 32-217.

*Note: CAA AD 008-02-88 refers.*

Compliance: For components with more than 4000 landings, prior to 31 August 1988. For components with less than 4000 landings, before exceeding 4000 landings.

Background: Recent analysis by the manufacturer has resulted in a requirement to crack detect pivot pins fitted to all nose leg assemblies which have achieved or exceeded 4000 landings.

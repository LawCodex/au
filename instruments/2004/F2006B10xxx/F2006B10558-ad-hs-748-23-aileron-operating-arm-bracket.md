# AD/HS 748/23 - Aileron Operating Arm Bracket

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/23 Aileron Operating Arm Bracket 5/98

Applicability: All HS 748 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Aircraft Mandatory Service Bulletin HS748-27- 124.

*Note: UK CAA AD 007-11-95 refers.*

Compliance: Unless the initial inspection is already accomplished; within 1,000 flying hours or 6 months after 23 April 1998, whichever occurs first; thereafter as specified in the requirement document.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 23 April 1998.

Background: Cracks have been found in the forward flanges of the aileron operating arm bracket assembly.

# AD/GAZELLE/1 - Main Gearbox - Inspection During Initial Operations

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/1 Main Gearbox - Inspection During Initial Operations

8/76

Applicability: All models SA341 fitted with main gear box P/N 341A.32. 1000.01, .02, .03 or .05 (not modified to AMS 07.7077) with less than 25 hours time in service since new or recondition.

Document: Aerospatiale Gazelle SB 341 05.10. Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

# AD/G21/1 - Below Floor Control System Components - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Grumman G21 (Goose) Series Aeroplanes

AD/G21/1 Below Floor Control System Components - Inspection

10/77

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Remove and disassemble:

(i) Elevator Torque Tube Assembly P/N 12755,

(ii) Rudder Torque Tube Assembly P/N 12756,

(iii) Rudder Pedal Torque Tube Assembly LH and RH P/N 12757 and 12758,

(iv) Hinge Support Assembly P/N 12725.

2\. Inspect all parts for corrosion and/or cracks using visual and dye penetrant or magnetic particle techniques.

*Note: FAA AD 73-12-6 Amendment 39-1657 refers.*

Compliance: Initially before issue of Certificate of Airworthiness and there- after at intervals not exceeding 2000 hours time in service or two years whichever occurs first.

# AD/JETSTREAM/26 - Propeller Governor Low Speed Setting From 94% to 96%

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/26 Propeller Governor Low Speed

**Setting From 94% to 96%**

Applicability: All Jetstream 3100 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe ASB No 76-A-JA 880442 Rev 1.

*Note: FAA AD 88-13-02 refers.*

## 10/88 DM

Compliance: 1. Unless already accomplished within 50 hours TIS or by 22 August 1988 whichever occurs first.

2\. Prior to first flight after installation of an engine or propeller governor.

Background: During a recent overseas accident investigation the pilot noted dual torque oscillation prior to the accident. To ensure take-off power is available this Directive requires operators to check that the governor low speed stop is correctly set between 95.5 and

96.0 percent.

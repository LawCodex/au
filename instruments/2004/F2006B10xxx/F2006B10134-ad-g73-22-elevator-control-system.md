# AD/G73/22 - Elevator Control System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Frakes (Grumman) G73 Series Aeroplanes

AD/G73/22 Elevator Control System 3/88

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Frakes Aviation S.B. G-73-FA26.

*Note: FAA AD 8-25-08 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 March 1988.

Background: This Directive is prompted by overseas reports of elevator control system jamming in the full nose-up position during take-off.

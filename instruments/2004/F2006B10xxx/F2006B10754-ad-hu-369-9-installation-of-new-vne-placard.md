# AD/HU 369/9 - Installation of New Vne Placard

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/9 Installation of New Vne Placard 4/72** Applicability: All models with S/Nos. 0101S to 0357S, 0101M to 0213M, 0101E to 0215E. Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-45.

Compliance: Within 10 hours time in service after 11 April 1972.

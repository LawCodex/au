# AD/INST/19 - ARC PA 500A Actuators - Cessna Auto Pilot, AFCS & Yaw Damper - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Instruments and Automatic Pilots

## AD/INST/19 ARC PA 500A Actuators - Cessna Auto Pilot, AFCS & Yaw Damper - Inspection and Modification

Applicability: ARC PA500A actuators S/Nos. 6265 and below. Document: 1. ARC Field Engineering SB No. 181; or

2\. Cessna Avionics SL AV75-10; or 3. FAA AD 76-04-03.

## 6/76

Compliance: Inspection - within 100 hours time in service after 30 July 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until actuator is modified.

Modification -Before 30 April 1977.

# AD/HS 125/98 - Electrical Power - Starting - Hartman Contactor

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/98 Electrical Power - Starting - Hartman Contactor 3/89

Applicability: 1. All BAe 125 series aircraft with Garrett TFE 731 engines.

2\. Series 700 aircraft pre mod 256990 (cold start battery series contactor; Aircraft with post mod 252817 part F (SB 24-237-2817) embodied; Series 600 aircraft S/No 256070.

Requirement: 1. Applicable to 1. above. Action in accordance with BAe SB 24-268-3223A and B Revision 1 part A.

2\. Applicable to 2. above. Action in accordance with BAe SB 24-268-3223A and B Revision 1 part B.

Compliance: Prior to 22 June 1989.

Background: Overheating and short circuit to earth can occur with this type of relay.

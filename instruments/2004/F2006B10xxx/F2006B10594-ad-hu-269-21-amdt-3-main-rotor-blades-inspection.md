Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/21**

**Amdt 3**

**Main Rotor Blades - Inspection 1/78**

Applicability: All models fitted with main rotor blades P/Nos. 269A1125, 269A1131, 269A1131-1, 269B1145, 269B1145-1 and 259B1145-25.

Document: Hughes SIN No. N-9.2.

Compliance: 1. Initial inspection as per requirement document except those blades previously inspected to AD/HU 269/21B.

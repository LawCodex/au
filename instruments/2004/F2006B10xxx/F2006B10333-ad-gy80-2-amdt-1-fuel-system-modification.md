# AD/GY80/2 Amdt 1 - Fuel System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale GY 80 (Horizon) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/GY80/2

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Fuel System - Modification 1/76

Requirement: Blank off the mid position on the fuel selector plate and install in its place a placard as follows:

“DO NOT USE MID POSITION”. (See Flight Manual) Compliance: Before 1 April 1976.

# AD/HU 269/27 Amdt 1 - Lower Coupling Drive Shaft - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/27**

**Amdt 1**

**Lower Coupling Drive Shaft - Inspection 2/69**

Applicability: Model 269A with S/Nos. 0011 thru 0893, Model 269A-1 with S/Nos. 0001 thru 0041, Model 269A-2 with S/Nos. 0001 and 0002, Model 269B with S/Nos. 0001 thru 0359.

Document: 1. Hughes SIN No. N-55.1; or

2\. FAA AD 68-26-1.

Compliance: For installed shafts - within 25 hours time in service after 4 November 1968.

For new replacement shafts - before installation on a helicopter after 4 November 1968.

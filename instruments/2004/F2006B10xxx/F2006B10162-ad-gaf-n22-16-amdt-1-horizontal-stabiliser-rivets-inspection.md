**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/16**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Rivets - Inspection 1/78**

Applicability: All models N22 with S/Nos. N22-2, N22-3, N22-4, N22-8, N22-9, N22-17, N22-24, N22B-5, N22B-6, N22B-7, N22B-11, N22B-12, N22B-13, N22B-15, N22B-16, N22B-18 to N22B-23, N22B-25 to N22B-28 and N222-31.

# AD/GAF-N22/49 - Emergency Exit - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/49 Emergency Exit - Modification 10/84

Applicability: All models N22 and N24.

Requirement: Nomad Service Bulletin AS/B ANMD-52-6.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 October 1984 or at the next scheduled inspection for maintenance release after 31 October 1984 whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances of excessive force being required to push the emergency exit clear of the aircraft.

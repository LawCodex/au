# AD/GAF-N22/12 Amdt 1 - Rudder Control System - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/12**

**Amdt 1**

**Rudder Control System - Inspection and Modification**

**4/77**

Applicability: All models N22 and N22B with S/Nos. as listed in SB No. N22.27.7.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the mounting of the rudder pulley bracket at FUS. STA. 67.95 in accordance with GAF ALERT SB No. N22.27.6.

2\. Modify the mounting of the rudder pulley bracket at FUS. STA. 67.95 and affix towbar maximum deflection indication in accordance with GAF SB No. N22.27.7.

*Note 1: Flight restrictions referred to in Alert SB Telex N22.27.5 must be adhered to until compliance with para. 2 is completed.*

*Note 2: Incorporation of MODIFICATION N137 is an acceptable means of compliance with para. 2.*

Compliance: 1. Within 50 hours time in service after 24 December 1976 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 50 hours time in service until modified.

2\. Within 200 hours time in service after 1 April 1977.

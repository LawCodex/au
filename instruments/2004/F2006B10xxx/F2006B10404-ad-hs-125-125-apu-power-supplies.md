# AD/HS 125/125 - APU Power Supplies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/125 APU Power Supplies 3/92

Applicability: As specified in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Service Bulletin 49-37-25A253A&B.

*Note: Action is accordance with this SB is classified mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 March 1992.

Background: The APU power supply connections are relocated to protect against damage from engine debris.

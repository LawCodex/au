# AD/HS 125/61 - Electrical Power - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/61 Electrical Power - Modification 5/83

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: British Aerospace 125 Service Bulletin 24-239-(2885). Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1983.

Background: To ensure that resetting a circuit breaker onto a system fault will not result in rupture of the ‘PE’ busbar feeder fuses.

# AD/HU 269/23 Amdt 8 - Main Rotor Gear Drive Assembly - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/23**

**Amdt 8**

**Main Rotor Gear Drive Assembly - Inspection and Replacement**

**11/81**

Applicability: All models fitted with P/N 269A5175, 269A5175-3 or 269A5175-5 main rotor gear drive, incorporating P/N 269A5179 or 269A5179-3 drive shaft.

Document: Hughes SIN N-179.

*Note: FAA AD 81-17-04 refers.*

Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

Background: The Directive is raised in issue to reflect current manufacturers data and FAA requirements. Hughes SIN N-179 supersedes N-114-3 N-143, and additionally reduces the life of P/N 269A5179 or 269A5179-3 shaft and coupling assemblies. Installation of P/N 269A5194 carrier assembly(N-142-1) is now mandatory.

# AD/GBK 117/4 - Landing Gear Electrical Bonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BK 117 Series Helicopters

AD/GBK 117/4 Landing Gear Electrical Bonding 12/91

Applicability: All MBB-BK 117 aircraft serial No 7001 through 7261 inclusive. Requirement: Action in accordance with MBB Alert SB 117 -90-104.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 12 December 1991.

Background: The existing bonding of the landing gear to the helicopter has been determined by the manufacturer to be inadequate to ensure satisfactory elimination of electrostatic charges.

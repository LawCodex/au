# AD/G159/46 - MLG Retract Cylinder Attachment Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

AD/G159/46 MLG Retract Cylinder Attachment Fittings 10/97

Applicability: Serial Numbers 1 to 12, 14 to 112, 114 to 148 and 322 and 323

Requirement: Replace MLG retract cylinder attachment fitting assemblies with assemblies containing Part Number 159WM10276-1 and -2, and balls Part Number 159WM10277-1.

*Note: FAA AD 97-14-10 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before issue of Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

This airworthiness directive becomes effective on 11 September 1997.

Background: This directive provides terminating action for a repetitive inspection in the FAA AD.

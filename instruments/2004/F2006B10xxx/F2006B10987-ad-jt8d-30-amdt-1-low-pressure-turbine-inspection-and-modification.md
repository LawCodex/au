# AD/JT8D/30 Amdt 1 - Low Pressure Turbine Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 106 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Pratt and Whitney Turbine Engines - JT8D Series

**AD/JT8D/30**

**Amdt 1**

**Low Pressure Turbine Inspection and Modification 2/98**

Applicability: JT8D-1 through -17R series engines.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the third and fourth stage turbine blades of affected engines in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 97-19-14 Amdt 39-10135.

*Note 1: The extent of the inspection requirements is dependent on modification/build status of individual engines.*

2\. Modify affected engines to incorporate improved low pressure turbine containment hardware and improved No.6 bearing scavenge pump attach bracket bushings in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 97-19-14 Amdt 39-10135.

Incorporation of these modifications is terminating action for the inspections specified in Requirement 1 of this directive.

Compliance: Effective 29 January 1998, as detailed in the requirement document.

*Note 2: This directive supersedes AD/JT8D/28.*

This amendment becomes effective on 29 January 1998.

Background: Uncontained engine failures have occurred as a result of low pressure turbine blade and shaft fractures.

This amendment revises the original Requirement Document to reflect FAA AD 97- 19-14 in lieu of FAA AD 94-20-08. FAA AD 97-19-14 reduces the compliance period for the modifications specified in Requirement 2 for engines in which certain 4th stage low pressure turbine hubs are installed. AD/JT8D/34 also refers.

The original issue of this airworthiness directive became effective on 2 February 1995.

# AD/G159/17 - Wing Front Beam - Wing to Fuselage Fitting - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

**AD/G159/17 Wing Front Beam - Wing to Fuselage Fitting -**

**Inspection**

**1/72**

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Inspect the wing to fuselage fittings at butt line 9.0 and associated areas as detailed in GRUMMAN GULFSTREAM 1 Service Change No. 190. Rework as necessary as detailed in that Service Change or a variation approved by the manufacturer.

Compliance: Within 6 months after 28 January 1972 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1 year.

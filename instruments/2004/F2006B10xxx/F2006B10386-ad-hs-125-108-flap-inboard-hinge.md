# AD/HS 125/108 - Flap Inboard Hinge

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/108 Flap Inboard Hinge 8/90

Applicability: As listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Hawker Siddeley SB 27-112. Compliance: In accordance with the Requirement Document.

Background: This Directive is to reduce the possibility of flap damage when landing on heavily watered or slush covered runways.

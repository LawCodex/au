# AD/GAF-N22/72 - Upper Fin Rear Attachment Points

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/72 Upper Fin Rear Attachment Points 8/95 TX

Applicability: All Nomad N22 and N24 series aircraft.

Requirement: Inspect the bores of the upper fin rear attachment bolt holes in the stub fin rear face for radial cracks. Inspect using one of the methods described in ASTA Service Bulletin ANMD-53-19, Accomplishment Instructions, Part 1.

Compliance: Inspect before 5000 hours time in service or before further flight after 18 May 1995, whichever occurs later.

Background: A fatigue analysis has indicated that the upper fin rear attachment points on the stub fin structure are fatigue sensitive. The repeat inspections for this area are subject to on-going analysis and will be published shortly.

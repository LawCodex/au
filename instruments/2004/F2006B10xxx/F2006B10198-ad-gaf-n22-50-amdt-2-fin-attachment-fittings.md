# AD/GAF-N22/50 Amdt 2 - Fin Attachment Fittings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/50**

**Amdt 2**

**Fin Attachment Fittings 11/89**

Applicability: All Nomad N22 and N24 series aeroplanes which do not incorporate modification N602 together with either modification N600A or N600B.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with ASTA Alert Service Bulletin ANMD-55-23, dated 27 June 1989.

2\. Action in accordance with GAF Service Bulletin NMD-53-5 and GAF Service Bulletin NMD-55-21.

*Note: Compliance with Requirement 2 constitutes terminating action for Requirement 1.*

Compliance: 1. Not later than the first issue of a Maintenance Release after 30 November 1989, unless Requirement 2 has been accomplished.

2\. Before exceeding 3000 hours component time in service.

Background: The original retirement life of 3000 hours was promulgated based on fatigue analysis. Recent service experience of pre-mod N331 aircraft has revealed that the original bolt P/N 2/N-00-43 may be susceptible to cracking before reaching 3000 hours component time in service. This Amendment calls up a bolt replacement as an interim alternative to incorporating modification N600A or N600B.

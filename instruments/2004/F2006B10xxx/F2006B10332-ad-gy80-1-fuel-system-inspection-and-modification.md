# AD/GY80/1 - Fuel System - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale GY 80 (Horizon) Series Aeroplanes

AD/GY80/1 Fuel System - Inspection and Modification 10/69

Applicability: All models with S/Nos. prior to No. 125.

Requirement: 1. Inspect the fuel supply lines from each wing tank where they pass through the wing-root/fuselage structure for any evidence of chafing. If chafing has occurred replace the affected pipes.

2\. In the region where the fuel supply lines pass through the wing-root and fuselage structure, protect the pipes against possible chafing by means of a suitable piece of split rubber hose secured around the pipe in the critical area; or

3\. Sud Aviation SB No. 28. Compliance: 25 November 1969.

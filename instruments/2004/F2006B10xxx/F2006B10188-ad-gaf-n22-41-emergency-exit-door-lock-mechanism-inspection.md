# AD/GAF-N22/41 - Emergency Exit Door Lock Mechanism - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/GAF-N22/41 Emergency Exit Door Lock Mechanism - Inspection

Applicability: All models N22 and N24.

Document: GAF Telex Nomad Alert SB ANMD 52-2.

## 5/81

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 10 hours time in service after 16 April 1981.

Background: Instances have occurred in which the lock mechanism spindle on the emergency exit door has been incorrectly positioned before fitting the handle.

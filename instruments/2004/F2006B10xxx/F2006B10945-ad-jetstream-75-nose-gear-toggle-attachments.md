# AD/JETSTREAM/75 - Nose Gear Toggle Attachments

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/75 Nose Gear Toggle Attachments 2/97

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 and Jetstream 3200 Series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Aircraft Alert Service Bulletin 32-JA 960601.

*Note: UK CAA AD 002-10-96 and AAPH SB 32-66 Revision 1 refer.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before 30 April 1997.

Background: The attachment brackets for the nose gear upper and lower toggles may be of reduced thickness. A reduced wall thickness affects the fatigue life of the nose landing gear assembly and a new fatigue limit may be required.

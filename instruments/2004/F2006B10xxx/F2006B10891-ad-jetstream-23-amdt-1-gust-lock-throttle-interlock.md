# AD/JETSTREAM/23 Amdt 1 - Gust Lock/Throttle Interlock

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/23**

**Amdt 1**

**Gust Lock/Throttle Interlock 2/89 DM**

Applicability: Series 3100 constructors numbers 601 to 749. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 27-JM7561 Rev 1. Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1989.

Background: There has been an incident where take-off was attempted with the rudder gust lock partially engaged. Incorporation of this SB ensures that take-off power cannot be achieved without all three gust locks having been disengaged. Rev 1 of the Service Bulletin provides amended rigging instructions for those aircraft that could not physically meet the initial requirement.

# AD/GAF-N22/33 - Rudder Structure and Trim Tab Control Rod - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/33 Rudder Structure and Trim Tab Control**

**Rod - Inspection and Modification**

**3/80**

Applicability: All N22 and N24 series with S/Nos. listed in GAF Alert SB No. ANMD-55-11. Document: GAF Alert SB No. ANMD-55-11.

Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of partial failure of an intercostal member and chafing of the trim tab control rod in service.

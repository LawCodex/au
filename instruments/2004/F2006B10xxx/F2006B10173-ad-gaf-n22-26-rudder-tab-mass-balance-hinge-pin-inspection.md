# AD/GAF-N22/26 - Rudder Tab Mass Balance Hinge Pin - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/26 Rudder Tab Mass Balance Hinge Pin - Inspection

5/79

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N24 and N24A with Mod. N39 fitted and delivered before 1 March 1979.

Document: GAF Alert SB No. ANMD-55-6.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 16 March 1979 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until modified in accordance with Alert SB No. ANMD-55-6.

Background: An instance has occurred where the rudder tab mass balance hinge pin seized in its bush and sheared the split pin retaining the hinge pin.

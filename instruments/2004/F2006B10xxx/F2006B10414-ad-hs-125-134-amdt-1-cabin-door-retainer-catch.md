# AD/HS 125/134 Amdt 1 - Cabin Door Retainer Catch

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/134**

**Amdt 1**

**Cabin Door Retainer Catch 5/98**

Applicability: Model 125 series 800 with serial numbers as listed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 25-68- 25A440A.

*Note: UK CAA AD 004-08-92 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 September 1993.

The compliance of the original issue of this directive remains unchanged. This amendment becomes effective on 23 April 1998.

Background: This Directive requires the existing cabin door retainer catch fitted in the upper luggage bay shelf to be replaced by an improved catch in order to prevent closing of the door during take off and landing.

Amendment 1 is issued to re-instate this directive to the series.

The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 30 September 1993.

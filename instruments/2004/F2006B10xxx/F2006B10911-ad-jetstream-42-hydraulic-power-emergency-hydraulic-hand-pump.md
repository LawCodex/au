# AD/JETSTREAM/42 - Hydraulic Power - Emergency Hydraulic Hand Pump

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/42 Hydraulic Power - Emergency**

**Hydraulic Hand Pump**

**4/91 DM**

Applicability: Jetstream Series 3100 Aircraft construction number 601 to 646, 648 to 655, 658 and

660\.

Requirement: Inspect and/or modify subject aircraft in accordance with British Aerospace Service Bulletin, CAA (UK) Mandatory, 29-JM 7360 Revision 1.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document but with a revised effectivity date of 18 April 1991 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: Cases have been reported of difficulty in gaining access to the emergency hydraulic hand-pump following hydraulic systems failures.

# AD/HS 125/55 - Fuel Filler Neck Flap Valve - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/55 Fuel Filler Neck Flap Valve - Installation 7/81

Applicability: As detailed in the requirement document.

Document: British Aerospace 125 SB No. 28-60-(2465), Rev. No. 2. Compliance: Before 31 January 1982.

Background: A rubber flap valve is fitted to each of the fuel tank filler necks to minimise the inflight effects of an improperly closed fuel filler cap.

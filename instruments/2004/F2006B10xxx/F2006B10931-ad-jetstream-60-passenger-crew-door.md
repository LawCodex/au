# AD/JETSTREAM/60 - Passenger/Crew Door

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/60 Passenger/Crew Door 12/93

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 and Series 3200 aircraft with constructors numbers up to and including 972.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 52-JM 7793.

*Note: UK CAA AD 010-08-93 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 April 1994.

Background: There have been reports of spurious indications from the door warning system. This Directive affects the passenger/crew drawbolt installations and the door warning system microswitch rigging.

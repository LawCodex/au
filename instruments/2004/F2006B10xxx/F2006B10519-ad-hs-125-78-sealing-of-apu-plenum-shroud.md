# AD/HS 125/78 - Sealing of APU Plenum Shroud

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/78 Sealing of APU Plenum Shroud 7/86

Applicability: All DH/HS/BH/BAe 125 series up to and including aircraft 258030 post mod 251605 and 258748.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 49-31-(9286A) Rev 1.

*Note: C.A.A. Airworthiness Directive 007-02-86 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 December 1986.

Background: To reduce the possibility of APU fuel leakage into the rear equipment bay.

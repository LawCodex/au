# AD/GA-7/7 - Fuel Line Chafing - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American) GA-7 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GA-7/7 Fuel Line Chafing - Inspection NK

Applicability: Model GA-7 with S/Nos. GA-7-0001 to GA-7-0076. Document: 1. GAC SB No. ME-14; or

2\. FAA AD 79-05-06 amendment 39-3423.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 9 April 1979.

Background: The main fuel line aft of the left hand engine firewall may be chafed by the propeller control cable.

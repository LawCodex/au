# AD/HU 269/104 - Rudder Pedal Bulkhead Gusset

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 269/104 Rudder Pedal Bulkhead Gusset 12/94

Applicability: Models 269A, A-1, B, and C.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Schweizer SB B-247.

*Note: FAA AD 94-16-04 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 10 November 1994.

Background: Tail rotor pedal gussets were not installed on 269A aircraft, and other aircraft have been found with the gussets cracked or with loose or missing rivets.

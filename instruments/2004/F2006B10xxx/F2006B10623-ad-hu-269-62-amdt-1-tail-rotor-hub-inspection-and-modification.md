# AD/HU 269/62 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Hub - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/62**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Hub - Inspection and Modification 3/82**

Applicability: All model 269C with S/Nos. 0004 to 0716.

All model 269A, TH-55A, 269A-1 and 269B with LTS tail rotor assembly P/N 269A6034 Series.

All P/N 369A1725and 369A1725-501 tail rotor hub assemblies. All P/N 369A1725-5 tail rotor assemblies with S/Nos. 001 to 862.

Document: Hughes SIN No. N-153.1.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 March, 1982.

Background: Manufacturer’s SI Notice Revision 1 extends part number effectivity.

# AD/GENERAL/45 Amdt 2 - Mitchell Automatic Pilot Servo Bridle Cable Clamp - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aircraft - General

**AD/GENERAL/45**

**Amdt 2**

**Mitchell Automatic Pilot Servo Bridle Cable Clamp - Replacement**

**8/80**

Applicability: All aircraft fitted with automatic pilot servo bridle clamps Mitchell Part No. 42A173 or 42A184 except aircraft fitted with Piper III Series automated Flight Control System bridle clamps incorporating a safety sleeve.

Document: 1. Edo-Aire Mitchell Service Bulletin MBI dated 1 June 1970; or 2. FAA AD 70-15-18.

Compliance: Within 300 hours time in service after 30 September 1970 for aircraft specified in FAA AD 70-15-18. Other aircraft within 300 hours time in service after 31 January 1974.

Background: The applicability has been revised to exempt bridle clamps installed by Piper which have a safety sleeve fitted. (Refer to Piper Service Manual instructions for Autocontrol III and Altimatic III for details of safety sleeves).

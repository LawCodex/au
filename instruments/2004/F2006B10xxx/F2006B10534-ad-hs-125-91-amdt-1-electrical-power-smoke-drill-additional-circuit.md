# AD/HS 125/91 Amdt 1 - Electrical Power - Smoke Drill - Additional Circuit Breaker Identical Labels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/91**

**Amdt 1**

**Electrical Power - Smoke Drill - Additional Circuit Breaker Identical Labels**

**5/88**

Applicability: As detailed in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 24-263-9486A Revision 1 dated 27 January 1988.

*Note: CAA AD 002-12-87 also refers.*

Compliance: Before 21 July 1988.

Background: 1. BAe SB 24-263-9486A revision 1 requires installing self adhesive colour coded labels to the listed circuit breakers in compliance with airworthiness requirements.

2\. Amendment 1 to this AD has been issued to amend the Requirement document only.

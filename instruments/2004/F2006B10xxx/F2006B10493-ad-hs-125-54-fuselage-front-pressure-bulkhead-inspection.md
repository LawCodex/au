# AD/HS 125/54 - Fuselage Front Pressure Bulkhead - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/54 Fuselage Front Pressure Bulkhead - Inspection 10/80

Applicability: All HS/DH/BH 125 models up to and including Series 400. Document: British Aerospace 125 SB No. 53-52.

Compliance: As per requirement document except that stated flights shall be pressurised flights.

Background: Cracking in the corner bend radii of both control pressings on one aircraft was discovered on an overseas aeroplane after 6700 flights.

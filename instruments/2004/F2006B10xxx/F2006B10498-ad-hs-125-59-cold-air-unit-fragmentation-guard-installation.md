# AD/HS 125/59 - Cold Air Unit Fragmentation Guard - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/59 Cold Air Unit Fragmentation Guard - Installation 3/82

Applicability: As detailed in the requirement document. Document: British Aerospace HS 125 SB No. 21-123-(2815). Compliance: Before 31 March 1983.

Background: A fragmentation guard is installed around the fan housing of the cold air unit to protect the main engine beam in the event of a fan bursting failure.

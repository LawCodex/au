# AD/HS 748/11 - Nose Landing Gear Retraction Jack Bracket Mounting Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 748/11 Nose Landing Gear Retraction Jack**

**Bracket Mounting Bolts**

**2/95**

Applicability: All HS748 aircraft.

Requirement: The nose landing gear retraction jack bracket mounting bolts are lifed items and shall be replaced in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 53/16 Revision 3.

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 0115 refers.*

Compliance: Replace bolts before they achieve 3000 flights.

Background: Fatigue tests have shown that the nose landing gear retraction jack bracket mounting bolts are too highly loaded to attain a long fatigue life. Distortion of the bolts has been experienced on aircraft in service.

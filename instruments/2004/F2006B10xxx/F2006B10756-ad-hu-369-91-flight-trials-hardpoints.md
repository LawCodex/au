# AD/HU 369/91 - "Flight Trials" Hardpoints

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/91 "Flight Trials" Hardpoints 8/96 DM

Applicability: Models 369D, 369E, 369F, 369FF, and 500N that have been modified using Flight Trials Helicopter Inc. hardpoint assemblies in accordance with either United States FAA:

Supplementary Type Certificate SH6080NM approved by this Authority, or

b. Form 337 "Major Repair and Alteration", prior to import into Australia. Requirement: Remove from service any Flight Trials Helicopter Inc. hardpoints not marked with:

a Part Number (P/N) FTH 105 LH Mod 1 for the port side and FTH 105 RH Mod 1 for the starboard side; and

b. a Serial Number (S/N).

*Note 1. Access may be gained by removing the NAS 1351-3 cap screw that secures the hardpoint to the P/N 369H2521-1 and -2 jacking fittings, and slipping the hardpoint assembly out of the step mount.*

*Note 2. FAA AD 96-08-03 refers.*

Compliance: Before further flight after 22 May 1996.

Background: This Directive is raised to prevent failure of a hardpoint assembly with separation from the helicopter, and subsequent possible contact with the fuselage or rotor system.

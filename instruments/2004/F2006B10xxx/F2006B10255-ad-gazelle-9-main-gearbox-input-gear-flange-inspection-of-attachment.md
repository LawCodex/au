# AD/GAZELLE/9 - Main Gearbox Input Gear Flange - Inspection of Attachment Nut

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/9 Main Gearbox Input Gear Flange - Inspection of Attachment Nut

5/80

Applicability: Models SA-341 and SA-342 fitted with main gearbox P/N 341A.32.1000.01 to .05, not modified in accordance with AMS 07.7076/S374.

Document: Gazelle SB 65.19.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1980.

Background: The manufacturer has introduced an inspection to ensure that the correct torque load has been applied to the notched nut which retains the input gear flange.

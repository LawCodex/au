# AD/GROB 115/7 Amdt 2 - Cancellation of the Reduction of the Never Exceed Speed

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Grob G115 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GROB 115/7**

**Amdt 2**

**Cancellation of the Reduction of the Never Exceed Speed**

**4/98**

Applicability: Models G115C, G115C2, G115D, G115D2, all serial numbers. Requirement: Action in accordance with Grob Service Bulletin 1078-69.

*Note: LBA AD 96-270/3 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before the next flight after 26 March 1998 .

This amendment becomes effective on 26 March 1998.

Background: The investigation of an incident of buffeting when an aircraft approached the never exceed speed, identified a vibration in the aircraft structure that had originated at the engine shock mounts as the cause.

Amendment 1 required certain inspections to clear aircraft for aerobatic manoeuvres while maintaining the airspeed limitation of the previous issue of this Directive.

Amendment 2 is issued in response to a revision of the related LBA AD which cancels the previously required airspeed limitation following successful completion of the buffeting investigation.

Amendment 1 of this airworthiness directive became effective on 9 October 1997.

The original issue of this airworthiness directive became effective on 14 February 1996.

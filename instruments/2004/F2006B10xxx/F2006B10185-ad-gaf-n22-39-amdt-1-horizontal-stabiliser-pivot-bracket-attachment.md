# AD/GAF-N22/39 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Pivot Bracket Attachment - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/39**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Pivot Bracket Attachment - Inspection and Modification**

**9/80**

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N22S, N24 and N24A.

Documents: GAF Telex Alert SB No. ANMD-55-13 dated 22 July 1980 and Revision to SB ANMD-55-13 dated 24 July 1980.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 120 hours time in service after 16 July 1980 or before 16 September, whichever occurs first.

Background: This amendment ‘A’ references GAF SB No. ANMD-55-13 as the requirement document which extends scope of inspection and provides detailed inspection instructions.

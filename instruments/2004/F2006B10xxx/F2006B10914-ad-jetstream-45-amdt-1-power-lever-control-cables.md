# AD/JETSTREAM/45 Amdt 1 - Power Lever Control Cables

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/45**

**Amdt 1**

**Power Lever Control Cables 3/95**

Applicability: All HP137 Mk1, Jetstream Series 200, Jetstream Series 3100; and Jetstream Series 3200 with constructors numbers 790 through 960.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Mandatory ASB 76-A-JA 910542.

*Note: Compliance with BAe SB 76-JA 911042 constitutes terminating action for the Requirement of this Directive.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document, until compliance with BAe SB 76-JA 911042. This Amendment is effective from 2 March 1995.

Background: Cases were reported of failures of the power lever control cable during engine ground runs. The UK CAA found it necessary to introduce a life limitation on the affected cables.

Amendment 1 is issued to align this Directive with the manufacturer’s optional terminating action.

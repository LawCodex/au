**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/3 Wing/Fuselage Attachment Spigot Bush - Inspection

3/85

Applicability: All models with S/Nos referenced in British Aerospace Jetstream S.B. 57-A-JA- 840917.

Requirement: British Aerospace Alert Service Bulletin 57-A-JA-840917. Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer reports a case of a repair bush becoming dislodged on an aircraft in service.

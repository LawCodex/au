# AD/IAI-W/5 - Galley Electrical Wiring - Inspection and Re-Routing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Israel Aircraft Industries 1123 and 1124 (Westwind) Series Aeroplanes

AD/IAI-W/5 Galley Electrical Wiring - Inspection and Re-Routing

12/80

Applicability: Westwind Series S/N 239 to 298 except 241, 252, 257, 261, 264, 265, 290, 294, 295

and 297.

Document: IAI 1124-Westwind SB No. WW-24-20 (24-03).

*Note: FAA AD No. 80-19-15 refers.*

Compliance: Part 1 - Within 10 hours time in service after 11 November 1980.

Part 2 - To be accomplished as soon as practicable but not later than 100 hours time in service after 11 November 1980.

Background: The FAA advise of an incident in which thrust reversers, flaps, spoilers and some lights were lost because a hot liquid container in the galley rubbed against a loom and created an electrical short circuit which resulted in a fire.

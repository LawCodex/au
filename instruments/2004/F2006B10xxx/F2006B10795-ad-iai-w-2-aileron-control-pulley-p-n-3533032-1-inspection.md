# AD/IAI-W/2 - Aileron Control Pulley P/N 3533032-1 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Israel Aircraft Industries 1123 and 1124 (Westwind) Series Aeroplanes

AD/IAI-W/2 Aileron Control Pulley P/N 3533032-1 - Inspection 11/80

Applicability: All model 1124 with S/Nos. 240 to 275. Document: IAI SB No. 1124-27-02.

Compliance: Within 150 hours time in service after 30 November 1980.

Background: The control pulleys at the base of the control column may be chafed by the cables which could cause abnormal wear to the pulleys.

# AD/HYD/1 - Sprague Engineering Hydraulic Accumulator - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hydraulic Equipment

AD/HYD/1 Sprague Engineering Hydraulic NK Accumulator - Modification

Applicability: All Sprague Engineering Accumulator assemblies P/N A-200-25 and A-200-50 not fitted with steel end caps.

Document: 1. Sprague Engineering Mod. Bulletin No. 2; or 2. FAA AD 70-10-4.

Compliance: Components with 15,000 hours or more total time in service at 30 September 1970 - within the next 1000 hours time in service. Components with less than 15,000 hours total time in service at 30 September 1970 - before completing 16,000 hours total time in service.

# AD/G159/30 Amdt 1 - Engine Nacelle Firewall - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

AD/G159/30 Amdt 1 Engine Nacelle Firewall - Inspection 10/82

Applicability: All models.

Document: Grumman Customer Bulletin No. 293, Amendment 1.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 150 hours time in service after 31 October 1982.

Background: Operators have found additional cracks in the centre of the firewall between the two locations described in the original Customer Bulletin.

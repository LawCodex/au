# AD/GAF-N22/42 - Emergency Exit - External Label

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/42 Emergency Exit - External Label 6/81

Applicability: As listed in Nomad Service Bulletin SB NMD-11-3. Document: Nomad Service Bulletin SB NMD-11-3 dated 31 March 1981. Compliance: At next 100 hour inspection after receipt of the correct label.

Background: Some aircraft have been fitted with a label which incorrectly describes operation of the emergency exit. (i.e. Shows incorrect position of handle and incorrect direction of rotation to operate).

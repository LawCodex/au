AD/GOV/6 Governor Pressure Relief Valve Roll Pin - Modification

8/79

Applicability: All McCauley governors model C290D3/T(), C290D4/T(), CF() () 310D2/T(), ()CF()S31D3/T(), DCF()S310D4/T(), DCF()S290D4/T(), DCF() ()290D2/T(), DCF()S290D5/T().

# AD/HU 369/21 - Stabiliser Strut Damper Plunger - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/21 Stabiliser Strut Damper Plunger - Inspection**

**and Replacement**

**12/76**

Applicability: All models fitted with P/N 369A2001-601 shock mounted stabiliser strut assembly. Document: 1. Hughes SIN HN-102.1; or

2\. FAA AD 76-19-03.

Compliance: For Part 1 - Within 25 hours time in service after 25 Nov. 1976.

For Part 2 (if required) - Within 100 hours time in service after 25 Nov. 1976.

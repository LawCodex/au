# AD/HILLER 12/19 - Main Rotor Blades - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

AD/HILLER 12/19 Main Rotor Blades - Inspection 11/71

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: 1. Carry out a visual inspection for cracks in the main rotor blades P/No. 53100.

*Note: This inspection may be performed and certified by the pilot.*

2\. Inspect blades P/N 53100 for cracks using an approved dye penetrant method.

*Note: Any blades found defective shall be removed from service before further flight.*

Compliance: For Para. 1 - Before further flight.

For Para. 2 - Within 10 hours’ time in service after 16 Nov. 1971.

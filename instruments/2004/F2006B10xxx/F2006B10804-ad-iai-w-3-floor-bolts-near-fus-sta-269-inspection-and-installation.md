# AD/IAI-W/3 - Floor Bolts, Near Fus. Sta. 269 - Inspection and Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Israel Aircraft Industries 1123 and 1124 (Westwind) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/IAI-W/3 Floor Bolts, Near Fus. Sta. 269 - Inspection

**and Installation** Applicability: All model 1124 with S/Nos. 240 to 260, 262, 263, 264 and 266. Document: IAI SB No. 1124-53-02.

Compliance: Within 150 hours time in service after 30 November 1980.

## 11/80

Background: The manufacturer advises that some bolts may have been omitted from the floor beam attachment near FUS. STA. 269 during manufacture.

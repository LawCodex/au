# AD/JETSTREAM/59 - Main Wing Lower Skin and Lower Front Spar Structure

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/59 Main Wing Lower Skin and Lower Front Spar Structure

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 aircraft.

## 9/93

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Jetstream Mandatory Service Bulletin 57-JA 921203.

*Note: UK CAA AD 014-05-93 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document, with a completion date of 19 February 1994 for Accomplishment Instructions Parts 1 and 2 of the Requirement Document.

Background: Reports of exfoliation corrosion damage of the lower main skin at bolts which attach the skin to the front spar structure between Stations 36 and 40, LH and RH.

# AD/GBK 117/12 - Transmission and Engine Cowling Hook System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BK 117 Series Helicopters

AD/GBK 117/12 Transmission and Engine Cowling Hook System

13/99

Applicability: All Model MBB BK-117 helicopters with serial numbers 7001 through 7253, and 7500 through 7523.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter Deutschland Service Bulletin MBB BK 117- 20-109 Revision 2, dated April 30 1999.

*Note 1: Accomplishment of Alert Service Bulletin MBB BK 117-20-104 is a prerequisite for compliance with this Directive for certain serial number helicopters, and is addressed by AD/GBK 117/3 Amdt 1.*

Compliance: Before 30 June 2000, unless already accomplished.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 30 December 1999.

Background: The LBA requires installation of a hook system to the doors of the transmission and engine cowlings to prevent the doors from opening fully during flight; in the event of the door fasteners not being correctly latched during pre-flight check.

## AD/JETSTREAM/19 Cracking of MLG Cylinder Upper Attach Lug

Applicability: As listed in the requirement document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 32-JA860812. Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

## 5/87

Background: During fatigue testing of the main landing gear a crack occurred in the cylinder near the upper toggle attachment lug.

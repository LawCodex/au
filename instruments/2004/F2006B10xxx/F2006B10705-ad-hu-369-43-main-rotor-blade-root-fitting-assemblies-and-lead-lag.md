# AD/HU 369/43 - Main Rotor Blade Root Fitting Assemblies and Lead-Lag Assemblies - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/43 Main Rotor Blade Root Fitting Assemblies**

**and Lead-Lag Assemblies - Inspection**

**2/80**

Applicability: All model 369H with component S/Nos. as listed in the requirement document. Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-140.

Compliance: Part 1 - Initial Inspection - within 25 hours time in service after 3 January 1980.

Part 2 - Periodic Inspection - as detailed in requirement document.

Background: Cracking of the main rotor hub lead-lag link attach lugs has been reported in service.

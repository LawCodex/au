# AD/G159/40 - Main and Nose Landing Gear Uplock Cylinder Attach Bolts - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

## AD/G159/40 Main and Nose Landing Gear Uplock Cylinder Attach

**Bolts - Replacement**

Applicability: All models G159-1, -1C and -TC4C.

Document: Gulfstream American Customer Bulletin No. 285A.

## 5/82

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1982.

Background: Loss of a main landing gear uplock cylinder attach bolt has resulted in a gear up

landing involving an overseas aeroplane.

# AD/GAF-N22/57 - Oxygen - Cabin Supply Pipe

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/57 Oxygen - Cabin Supply Pipe 4/90

Applicability: All series with oxygen outlets in the passenger cabin. (Customer Options G71B, G71C, or R72).

Requirement: Action in accordance with ASTA Nomad SB NMD-35-02. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1990.

Background: To prevent electrical contact with cabin oxygen lines.

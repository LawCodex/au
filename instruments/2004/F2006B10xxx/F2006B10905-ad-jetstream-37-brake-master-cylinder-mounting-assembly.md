# AD/JETSTREAM/37 - Brake Master Cylinder Mounting Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/37 Brake Master Cylinder Mounting Assembly

Applicability: Model 3100 and 3200 with constructors numbers 601 to 870. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Alert SB 27-A-JA 880145. Compliance: Effective forthwith, in accordance with the Requirement Document.

## 9/90 DM

Background: There has been a report of cracking of the brake master cylinder lower mounting bracket. Failure of the bracket would result in loss of braking on the affected wheel.

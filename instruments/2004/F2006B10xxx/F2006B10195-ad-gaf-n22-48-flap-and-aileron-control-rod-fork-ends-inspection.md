# AD/GAF-N22/48 - Flap and Aileron Control Rod Fork Ends - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/GAF-N22/48 Flap and Aileron Control Rod Fork Ends - Inspection

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N24 and N24A with S/Nos. listed in Service Bulletin NMD-27-24.

Document: GAF Service Bulletin NMD-27-24.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30th September 1983.

## 9/83

Background: Instances have occurred where rod fork ends P/Ns. 1/N-45-351 and 1/N-45-1059 have been found full of water.

# AD/HILLER 12/27 Amdt 2 - Outer Gimbal Forward Attach Fitting - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

**AD/HILLER 12/27**

**Amdt 2**

**Outer Gimbal Forward Attach Fitting - Inspection**

**12/84**

**TX**

Applicability: All models UH-12D and UH-12E series which have been converted to turbine power by STC SH177WE or SH 178WE.

Requirement: 1. Remove the paint from the outer gimbal forward attach fitting of the Hiller Engine Mount P/No. 63181-5.

2\. Using a dye penetrant or a magnetic particle inspection technique, inspect the gusset weld beads for cracks on both the front and rear sides of the gusset.

*Note: FAA AD B4-19-08 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Within 20 hours’ time in service after 06 November 1984 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 600 hours’ time in service.

2\. Upon installation or reinstallation of engine mount assy P/N 63181-5.

Background: A recent overseas accident resulted from fatigue failure of the outer gimbal forward attach fitting. This directive has been amended to include repetitive inspections of the attach fitting.

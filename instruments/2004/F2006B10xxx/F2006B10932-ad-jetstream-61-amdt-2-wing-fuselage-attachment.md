# AD/JETSTREAM/61 Amdt 2 - Wing/Fuselage Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/61**

**Amdt 2**

**Wing/Fuselage Attachment 2/95**

Applicability: Model HP137 Mk1, Jetstream Series 200, and Jetstream Series 3100 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 57-JA 930941 Revision 2.

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with an effective date of 2 February 1995. Background: Cracks and corrosion on the spigot housing plate at the wing/fuselage forward-

attachment sliding-joint, have been reported on an in-service Jetstream 3100 aircraft. Amendment 1 split the inspection of the spigot from the inspection of the spigot housing plate in order to ease operator workload, and added new compliance instructions for repeat inspections.

Amendment 2 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement document which introduces several changes including an optional ultrasonic test and an extension of compliance.

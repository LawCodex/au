# AD/HU 269/25 Amdt 1 - Lateral Control Bellcrank (P/N 269A7506) - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/25**

**Amdt 1**

**Lateral Control Bellcrank (P/N 269A7506) - Replacement**

**7/69**

Applicability: Model 269A with S/Nos. 0011 to 1033.

Model 269A-1 S/Nos. 0001 to 0041. Model 269B with S/Nos. 0001 to 0398.

Document: Hughes SIN N-37.2.

Compliance: 1. At or before 900 hours total time in service for bellcranks having less than 800 hours total time in service at 4 July 1969.

2\. Within 100 hours time in service for bellcranks having 800 hours or more total time in service as at 4 July 1969. Bellcranks having over 900 hours total time in service are also subject to the inspection requirements of Step II Paras. a.1, 2 and 3 of Hughes SIN N-37.2 during the 100 hour compliance period.

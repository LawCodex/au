# AD/GA-7/6 - Rudder Bellcrank Torque Tube - Inspection and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American) GA-7 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GA-7/6 Rudder Bellcrank Torque Tube - Inspection NK

and Modification

Applicability: All model GA-7 with S/Nos. GA7-0002 to GA-70038, GA7- 0040, GA7-0042 to GA7-0087.

Document: Gulfstream American SB No. ME-16.

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 10 hours time in service after 28 February 1979.

2\. Modify as per requirement document.

Background: Partial loss of rudder control may occur if free play in the rudder circuit exceeds 1 degree.

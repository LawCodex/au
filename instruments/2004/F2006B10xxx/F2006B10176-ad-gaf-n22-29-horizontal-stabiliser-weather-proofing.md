# AD/GAF-N22/29 - Horizontal Stabiliser - Weather Proofing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/29 Horizontal Stabiliser - Weather Proofing 11/79

Applicability: All models N22, N22B and N24 referenced in SB No. NMD 55-7. Document: Nomad SB No. NMD-55-7.

Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

Background: It has been found that water can be ingested and stored in the horizontal stabiliser.

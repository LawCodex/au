# AD/HS 748/22 - Main Hydraulic Accumulators - Corrosion Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/HS 748/22 Main Hydraulic Accumulators - Corrosion

**Inspection**

Applicability: British Aerospace HS748 aircraft.

## 7/97

Requirement: Inspect the main hydraulic accumulators for corrosion and repair as required, in accordance with British Aerospace Service Bulletin HS748-29-49 issued Feb 25/97.

Compliance: Within 30 days from effective date of this Directive and thereafter every two years.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 19 June 1997.

Background: Extensive corrosion has been found under the mounting brackets of the main hydraulic accumulators of an operator's aircraft during unscheduled maintenance which required removal of the accumulators.

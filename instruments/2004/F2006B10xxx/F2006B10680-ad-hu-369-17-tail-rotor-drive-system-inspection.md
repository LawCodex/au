# AD/HU 369/17 - Tail Rotor Drive System - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/17 Tail Rotor Drive System - Inspection 6/75

Applicability: All models equipped with metal tail rotor blades and all models with drive shafts not previously indexed.

Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-86.

Compliance: For Part 1 - Within 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1975.

For Part 2 - At intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service after 30 June 1975.

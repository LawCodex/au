# AD/HU 369/11 Amdt 1 - Main Transmission Ring Gear Bolts - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Main Transmission Ring Gear Bolts - Inspection 2/79**

Applicability: All model 369H Series except helicopters modified in accordance with HL. 39 (main transmission P/N 369A5100- 705M, -707M, -709).

Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-61.

Compliance: At next scheduled main transmission oil change and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 150 hours time in service after 28 September 1973.

# AD/GAF-N22/32 - Trim Tab Hinge to Horizontal Stabiliser Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/32 Trim Tab Hinge to Horizontal Stabiliser**

**Attachment - Inspection**

**3/80**

Applicability: All N22 and N24 series which have had replacement trim tab hinge P/N MS20001-4 fitted in service.

Document: GAF Telex Alert SB ANMD-27-16.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 20 hours time in service after 14 February 1980.

Background: An operator has had a replacement hinge fitted in which the rivet holes in the horizontal stabiliser attach section of the hinge were drilled with nil edge distance.

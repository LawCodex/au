# AD/GAF-N22/24 - Audio System Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/24 Audio System Modification 11/78

Applicability: All N22 and N22B aircraft fitted with Customer option G50 and not modified to Nomad Mod. N97 standard.

Document: GAF SB NMD-23-1 or an alternative modification which has been approved by the Secretary.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 November 1978.

# AD/IAI-W/6 - Audio Intercom System - Warning Placard and Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Israel Aircraft Industries 1123 and 1124 (Westwind) Series Aeroplanes

AD/IAI-W/6 Audio Intercom System - Warning Placard and Modification

5/81

Applicability: All models 1123, 1124 and 1124A with S/Nos. 151 to 315 except 294, 296, 297, 309,

310 and 314.

Document: IAI Service Bulletin WW-25 for Model 1123 aeroplanes. IAI Service Bulletin WW- 24-23 for Model 1124 and 1124A aeroplanes.

*Note: FAA Emergency Airworthiness Directive 81-07-07 dated 20 March 1981 refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 May 1981.

Background: In an incident overseas a resistor in the intercom system overheated and initiated a fire on the ground. A check of Australian aircraft of this type revealed that overheating of the same resistor has occurred but not to the degree necessary to initiate a fire.

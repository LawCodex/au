# AD/GBK 117/3 Amdt 1 - Cowling Latches

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter BK 117 Series Helicopters

**AD/GBK 117/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Cowling Latches 7/95**

Applicability: Eurocopter BK 117 series helicopters S/N 7001 to 7201. Requirement: Action in accordance with ASB-MBB-BK 117-20-104. Compliance: Prior to the issue of an Australian Certificate of Airworthiness.

This Amendment is effective 22 June 1995.

Background: Loss of a cowl resulted in the loss of an Australian aircraft.

Amendment 1 is to reference the Eurocopter (MBB) document rather than that of Kawasaki. The Eurocopter document also references the aircraft built specifically by MBB.

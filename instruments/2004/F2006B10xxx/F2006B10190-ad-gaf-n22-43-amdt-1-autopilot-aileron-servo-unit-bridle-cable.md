# AD/GAF-N22/43 Amdt 1 - Autopilot Aileron Servo Unit Bridle Cable Assembly P/N 1/N-45-1317 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/43**

**Amdt 1**

**Autopilot Aileron Servo Unit Bridle Cable Assembly P/N 1/N-45-1317 - Inspection**

**8/82**

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N22S, N24 and N24A fitted with 2 axis autopilot (option G48, G48-24 and G48-22S) except those modified to Mod. N554 or SB NMD-27-22.

Document: GAF Alert SB No. ANMD-27-20.

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished initially inspect within 10 hours time in service after 11 September 1981 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 120 hours time in service.

Background: The autopilot aileron servo bridle cable has failed inflight on a local aeroplane causing restriction to aileron control movement. Amdt. 1 removes from the applicability those aircraft embodying Mod. 554 or SB NMD-27-22.

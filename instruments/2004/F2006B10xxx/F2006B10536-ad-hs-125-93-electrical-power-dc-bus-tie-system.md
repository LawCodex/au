# AD/HS 125/93 - Electrical Power - DC Bus Tie System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/93 Electrical Power - DC Bus Tie System 8/88

Applicability: All BAe 125 aircraft post mod 251335. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 125 SB 24-264. Compliance: Prior to 6 October 1988.

Background: BAe have advised that a case has occurred where the PS1 and PS2 DC bus bars remained electrically connected with the bus-tie contactor opened.

# AD/HU 369/13 - Main Rotor Blade - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/13 Main Rotor Blade - Inspection 10/74

Applicability: All 500 model 369H series. Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-73.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 7 October 1974 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

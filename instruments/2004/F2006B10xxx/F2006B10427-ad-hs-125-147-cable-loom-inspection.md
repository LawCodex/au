# AD/HS 125/147 - Cable Loom Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/147 Cable Loom Inspection 3/95

Applicability: Hawker 1000, BAe 125 Series 1000A and B aircraft identified in Hawker Raytheon Corporate Jets Mandatory Service Bulletin SB 24-313 dated 19 December 1994.

Requirement: Inspect the electrical cable loom behind the right-hand throttle box cover in accordance with Hawker Raytheon Corporate Jets Mandatory Service Bulletin SB 24- 313 dated 19 December 1994.

*Note: This Service Bulletin has been classified as Mandatory by the CAA(UK).*

Compliance: Prior to 31 July 1995.

Background: Hawker Raytheon has received a report of damage to the cable loom behind the right- hand throttle box cover caused by the flap selector spring strut. Failure of electrical systems could result if this condition is not rectified. This Directive provides a method of early detection of damage to the cable loom.

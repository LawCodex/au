# AD/HU 369/14 - Aft Fuselage Skin - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/14 Aft Fuselage Skin - Inspection 4/75

Applicability: All models: 369HE with S/Nos. 0101E to 0215E, 369HS with S/Nos. 0001S to 0661S, 369HM with S/Nos. 0001 to 0004, and 005M to 260M.

Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-82.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 30 April 1975 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

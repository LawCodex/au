# AD/HU 369/58 - Main Rotor Swashplate Bearing - Inspection - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/58 Main Rotor Swashplate Bearing - Inspection - Replacement

2/84

Applicability: All 500 model 369 Series Aircraft equipped with, swashplate bearing assembly 369A 7003-3 and main rotor installation 369A 1000-502 or 369D 21000 and main rotor swashplate installation 369A 7609-501, 369A 7609-605 or 369D 27609-501 installed after 1 June 1983 except those 369A 7003-3 swashplate bearing assemblies with 9xxx series serial numbers or marked with a blue lacquered dot on the outer surface of the bearing seal retention snap ring.

Document: Hughes Service Information Notices HN 191, EN12 and DN-125. Compliance: Before further flight after 5th January 1984.

Background: The manufacturer reports that a main rotor swashplate bearing assembly, has been found without the ball bearing cages installed, consequently a one time inspection is required to ensure correct assembly.

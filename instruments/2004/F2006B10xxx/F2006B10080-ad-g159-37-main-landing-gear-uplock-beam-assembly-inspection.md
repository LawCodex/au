# AD/G159/37 - Main Landing Gear Uplock Beam Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

**AD/G159/37 Main Landing Gear Uplock Beam Assembly -**

**Inspection**

**3/80**

Applicability: All Model G159.

Document: Gulfstream American Customer Bulletin No. 12. Compliance: At each daily or overnight inspection.

*Note: This inspection may be performed and certified for by the pilot in command.*

Background: Failures of the M.L.G. uplock beam assembly have occurred in overseas registered areoplanes.

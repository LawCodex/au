# AD/JETSTREAM/5 - Battery Temperature Monitor Unit - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/5 Battery Temperature Monitor Unit - Inspection 7/85

Applicability: Jetstream Series 3100 Aircraft: S/N 603, 610, 611, 615, 617-620, 622, 623, 627, 629,

633, 645, 648, and 649.

Requirement: Jetstream Service Bulletin 24-A-JA 850518. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1985.

Background: A number of Battery Temperature Monitor Units have been found to have internal crossed electrical connections which can lead to wrong temperature information being presented to the pilots.

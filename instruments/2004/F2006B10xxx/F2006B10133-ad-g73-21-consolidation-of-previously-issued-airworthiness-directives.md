# AD/G73/21 - Consolidation of Previously Issued Airworthiness Directives

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Frakes (Grumman) G73 Series Aeroplanes

AD/G73/21 Consolidation of Previously Issued Airworthiness Directives

9/83

Applicability: All G73 Models.

Document: Grumman Service Bulletins Nos. 1, 3, 6, 10, 12, 18, 20, 23, 24 and Grumman

Customer Bulletins G73-3, -4, -5, -27, -29, -43, -46, -66, -71, -83, -88.

Compliance: Prior to issue of Certificate of Airworthiness.

Background: To reduce operators administrative workload, the requirements for initial modification and one time inspections have been consolidated above.

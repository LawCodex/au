# AD/HU 369/2 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Gearbox - Inspection and/or Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/2**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Gearbox - Inspection and/or Replacement**

**6/70**

Applicability: All models fitted with tail rotor gearbox P/N 369A5400 with gearbox S/Nos. listed in Hughes SIN No. HN-1.1 and Addendum.

Document: Hughes SIN No. HN-1.1 and addendum XI.

Compliance: Before achieving 90 hours component total time in service, after 1 September 1971.

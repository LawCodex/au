**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/13**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuel Crossfeed Selector Knob - Inspection and Replacement**

**8/77**

Applicability: All models N22 and N22B. Document: 1. GAF Alert SB No. N22.28.4’ and

2\. GAF SB No. N22.28.5.

Compliance: For GAF Alert SB No. N22.28.4: Before further flight after 7 February 1977 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service until GAF SB No. N22.28.5 is incorporated.

For GAF SB No. N22.28.5: Before 1 November 1977.

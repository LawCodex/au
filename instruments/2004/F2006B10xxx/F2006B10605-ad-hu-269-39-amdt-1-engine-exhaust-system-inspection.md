# AD/HU 269/39 Amdt 1 - Engine Exhaust System - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/39**

**Amdt 1**

**Engine Exhaust System - Inspection 2/72**

Applicability: Models 269A with S/Nos. 0011 to 0314, 269A-1 with S/Nos. 0004 to 0041, 269B with S/Nos. 0001 and subsequent, 269C with S/Nos. 0001 and subsequent equipped with 269A4201 or 269A4869 exhaust cabin heater installation.

Document: Hughes SIN No. N-88.1.

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 29 October 1971 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

# AD/HU 369/80 - Bolts in Main Transmission

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/80 Bolts in Main Transmission 7/90

Applicability: 1. All Model 369D, 369E serial numbers 0001E through 0352E and 369F/FF serial number 0001F through 0067FF series helicopters.

2\. All spare MS21250-04036 bolts purchased from MDHC or directly from Air Industries since August 1988. Any spare transmission delivered prior to September 1989.

Requirement: Action in accordance with McDonnell Douglas SIN DN-166, EN-57 or FN-45, as applicable.

Compliance: For Helicopters: Unless previously carried out, within 300 hours time in service from 12 July 1990 or by 30 April 1991, whichever occurs first.

For Spares: Before installation on a helicopter after 30 May 1991.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that certain bolts may not meet industry standards.

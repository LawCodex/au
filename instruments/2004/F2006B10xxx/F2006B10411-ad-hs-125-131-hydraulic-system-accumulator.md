# AD/HS 125/131 - Hydraulic System Accumulator

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/131 Hydraulic System Accumulator 12/92

Applicability: All model 125 up to and including the 800 series aircraft.

Requirement: Unless previously carried out, action in accordance with “2. Accomplishment Instructions” contained in Mandatory Service Bulletin No 29-89-3269A.

*Note: Compliance with this SB has been classified as Mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1993.

Background: Due to possible hydraulic fluid contamination of the accumulator air charge, resulting in possible compression ignition, the accumulators are to be charged with nitrogen.

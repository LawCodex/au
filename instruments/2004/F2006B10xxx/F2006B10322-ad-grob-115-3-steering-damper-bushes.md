# AD/GROB 115/3 - Steering Damper Bushes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Grob G115 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GROB 115/3 Steering Damper Bushes 3/91

Applicability: Model Grob G115A with serial numbers 8090 through 8109.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of German LBA AD 90-344.

*Note: Grob SB TM 1078-17 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished; before the next flight after 21 March 1991.

Background: During the manufacture of the G 115A, some oversize bushes were installed in the steering damper 115-4220. Under certain circumstances this can cause the rudder to jam.

# AD/JETSTREAM/31 - Inverter Circuit Protection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/31 Inverter Circuit Protection 1/89

Applicability: All Jetstream 3100 series aircraft constructors numbers 696 to 794, 796 to 799, 801 to

804, 806 to 809, 811 to 813, 815 to 817, and 820.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe ASB 24-A-JA7672A. Compliance: Prior to 26 April 1989.

Background: BAe have advised that the 26 Vac output cable of AII inverters installed by modification JM 7518 are not protected by a thermal fuse or circuit breaker. ASB 24- A-JA 7672A installs circuit breaker protection for the 26 Vac output cable.

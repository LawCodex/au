# AD/HS 125/74 - Wire Loom Chafe

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/74 Wire Loom Chafe 2/86

Applicability: All HS-125 series 400B to 700B (Post Mod. 252049) aeroplanes.

Requirement: Inspect the area adjacent to the TKS de-icing pump for wire loom chafing damage according to the requirements of British Aerospace 125 S.B. 24-255.

*Note: CAA Airworthiness Directive 005-10-85 refers.*

Compliance: Before 31 March 1986.

Background: An instance of wire loom chafing has occurred resulting in arcing and an electrical overheat.

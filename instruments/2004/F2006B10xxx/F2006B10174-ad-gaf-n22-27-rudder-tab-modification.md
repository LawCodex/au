# AD/GAF-N22/27 - Rudder Tab - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/27 Rudder Tab - Modification 8/79

Applicability: All models N22 and N22B not modified in accordance with GAF SB No. NMD-27-3, Rev. 1.

Requirement: Modify in accordance with GAF SB No. NMD-27-3, Rev. 1.

*Note: The speed of 180 Kts IAS should not be exceeded on aircraft not yet modified.*

Compliance: Before 1 December 1979.

Background: Manufacturer’s tests indicate that wear and backlash which may develop in the rudder tab system during normal service may erode the tab/rudder flutter speed margins.

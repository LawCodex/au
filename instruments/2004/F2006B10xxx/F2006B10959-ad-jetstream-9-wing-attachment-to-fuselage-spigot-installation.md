# AD/JETSTREAM/9 - Wing Attachment to Fuselage Spigot Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/9 Wing Attachment to Fuselage

**Spigot Installation**

Applicability: All Jetstream 3100 aircraft with S/Nos 601 to 666.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Alert SB 57-A-JA 85-941.

## 11/85 T

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 25 September 1985 or 7 days after 25 September 1985, whichever occurs first.

Background: An operator has reported that a Spigot P/No 13781 B7 attaching the wing front spar to fuselage STN 199 had become disengaged from the Spigot housing.

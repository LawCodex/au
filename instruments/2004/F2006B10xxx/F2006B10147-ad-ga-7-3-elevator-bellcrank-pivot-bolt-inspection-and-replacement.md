# AD/GA-7/3 - Elevator Bellcrank Pivot Bolt - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Gulfstream (Grumman American) GA-7 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GA-7/3 Elevator Bellcrank Pivot Bolt - Inspection NK

**and Replacement** Applicability: All models GA-7 with S/Nos. GA7-00002, 0005 to 0021. Document: Gulfstream American SB No. ME-3.

Compliance: 1. Inspect within 100 hours time in service after 31 March 1979 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

2\. Replace within 500 hours time in service after 31 March 1979.

# AD/HS 125/152 - Hydraulic Pipes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/152 Hydraulic Pipes 7/95

Applicability: All BAe 125 series 1000A, 1000B and Hawker 1000 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Hawker Raytheon Corporate Jets Mandatory Service Bulletin SB.29-95.

*Note: This SB has been classified as Mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: By 31 June 1995.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that chafing between the equipment bay structure and hydraulic lines has been reported.

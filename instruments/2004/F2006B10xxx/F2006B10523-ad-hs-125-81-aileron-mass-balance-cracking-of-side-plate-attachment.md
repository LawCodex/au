# AD/HS 125/81 - Aileron Mass Balance - Cracking of Side Plate Attachment Lugs

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/HS 125/81 Aileron Mass Balance - Cracking of

**Side Plate Attachment Lugs**

Applicability: All models to -700 inclusive.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace 125 S.B. 57-66, Rev 1.

## 12/86

Compliance: Prior to 31 March 1987 and thereafter every 600 hours time in service until repaired in accordance with Repair Scheme 25WG/R143.

Background: Cracking of the attachment lugs of the aileron mass balance side plate may result in displacement of the side plate and interfere with aileron movement.

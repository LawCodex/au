# AD/HU 269/65 Amdt 1 - Tail Rotor Blade Corrosion

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 269/65**

**Amdt 1**

**Tail Rotor Blade Corrosion 7/86**

Applicability: All models, fitted with tail rotor blades P/No. 269A6035-21 with S/Nos. prior to 0877 and tail rotor blades P/No. 269A6035-23 with S/Nos. prior to 2710.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Hughes SIN No. N-162.

*Note: FAA AD 79-23-03 R1 Amdt 39-5282 also refers.*

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: Evidence of corrosion in the interior of the steel spar in the subject tail rotor blades has been found. Amdt 1 limits applicability to those T.R. blades manufactured prior to the introduction of improved corrosion protection at manufacture.

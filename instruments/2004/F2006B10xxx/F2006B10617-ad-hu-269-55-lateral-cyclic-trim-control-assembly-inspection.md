**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 269/55 Lateral Cyclic Trim Control Assembly - Inspection 8/76

Applicability: Model 269C with S/Nos. 0162 to 0455, 0458 to 0463, 0465 to 0469, 0471 to 0475, and any model 269A, 269A-1, 269Bor 269C equipped with a P/N 269A7316-3,-5, -7,

2\. FAA AD 76-10-02.

# AD/HU 369/88 - Tailrotor Swashplate Locknut Lock Washer

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/88 Tailrotor Swashplate Locknut Lock Washer 1/95

DM

Applicability: Models 369D, 369E and 369F/FF Series.

Requirement: Action in accordance with MDHC SINs DN-185, EN-78 or FN-64 as applicable.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 9 November 1994, thereafter inspect at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

Background: There have been reports of the MS172209 lockwashers failing due to a flaw in the tang. Failure of the tang could allow the locknut to lose torque, and thereby causing eventual disengagement of the assembly resulting in loss of tail rotor control.

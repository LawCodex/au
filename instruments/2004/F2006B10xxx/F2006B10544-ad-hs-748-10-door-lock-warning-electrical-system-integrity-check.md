# AD/HS 748/10 - Door Lock Warning Electrical System Integrity Check

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/10 Door Lock Warning Electrical System Integrity Check

1/95

Applicability: All HS 748 aircraft.

Requirement: Carry out electrical indication system integrity check in accordance with paragraph 2.C.(2) of British Aerospace Aircraft Group, Manchester Division HS 748 Alert Service Bulletin 52/90 Revision 1 dated 17 December 1985.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 008-07-81 refers.*

Compliance: At each \`A’ check after 5 January 1995.

Background: This Directive is introduced to ensure the integrity of the electrical door lock indication system.

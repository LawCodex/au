# AD/GAF-N22/34 - Horizontal Stabiliser Gust Lock - Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/34 Horizontal Stabiliser Gust Lock - Installation 4/80** Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N24 and N24A with S/Nos. referenced in SB NMD-55-9. Document: GAF SB No. NMD-55-9.

Compliance: Before 1 August 1980.

Background: The horizontal stabiliser and control system have been damaged by strong wind gusts even with the pilot’s control lock engaged.

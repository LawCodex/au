# AD/HS 125/63 - Main Landing Gear Retraction Jack Cylinder Head Attachment Lugs - Inspection, Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/63 Main Landing Gear Retraction Jack Cylinder Head

**Attachment Lugs - Inspection, Replacement** Applicability: All models HS/DH/BH 125 series aircraft up to and including series 700. Document: British Aerospace 125 SB 32-A197.

Compliance: Before 31 October 1983.

Background: The manufacturer advises an inspection of MLG retraction jack cylinder head attachment lugs after reports of in-service cracking.

# AD/HILLER 12/1 - Main Rotor Blade - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

AD/HILLER 12/1 Main Rotor Blade - Inspection 10/60

Applicability: All models fitted with main rotor blades P/No. 2253-1101-03 with 250 or more hours time in service.

Document: 1. Hiller SIL No. 3007; or

2\. FAA AD 60-17-2.

Compliance: At each daily or overnight inspection after 7 October 1970.

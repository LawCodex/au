# AD/HS 125/64 - Audio Integrating System Wiring - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/64 Audio Integrating System Wiring - Modification 4/84

Applicability: All Hawker Siddeley HS-125 series listed in requirement document.

Document: Hawker Siddeley HS-125 Service Bulletin No. 23-21-(8765) November 1983 (Revision 2).

CAA AD No. 046-04-83 refers.

Compliance: Before 31 May, 1984.

Background: A single failure in the electrical supply to the ESB31 and EA76 Audio integrating systems, can produce a complete failure of all aircraft audio systems. Modification involves segregation of power supplies.

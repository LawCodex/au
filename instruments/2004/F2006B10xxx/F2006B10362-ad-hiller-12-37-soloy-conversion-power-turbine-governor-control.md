# AD/HILLER 12/37 - Soloy Conversion: Power Turbine Governor Control System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

## AD/HILLER 12/37 Soloy Conversion: Power Turbine

**Governor Control System - Modification** Applicability: All Soloy turbine conversion UH12D/E and E (4 Place) helicopters. Requirement: Soloy Conversions S.B. 06-560.

Compliance: Prior to the issue of the next Maintenance Release after 30 June 1985.

## 6/85

Background: The design, applicable prior to the issue of the Requirement Document, could result in loss of collective pitch control. This modification includes a spring bungee unit that will allow the collective lever to operate independently, if the governor cable seizes.

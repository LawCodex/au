# AD/HS 125/53 Amdt 2 - Flap Nose Ribs - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/53**

**Amdt 2**

**Flap Nose Ribs - Inspection 6/84**

Applicability: All models not incorporating mod 252772 (revised flap hinge arrangement), up to and including:

700A: aircraft NA0311 plus NA0313, NA0323, NA0325, NA0326, NA0339.

700B: aircraft 7163.

Document: British Aerospace 125 Series SB 57-58 Revision 4 dated 10 March 1984. Compliance: As detailed in the requirement document.

Background: This Directive has been amended to require compliance with the latest revision of the requirement document. It adds five aircraft previously omitted from the effectivity list.

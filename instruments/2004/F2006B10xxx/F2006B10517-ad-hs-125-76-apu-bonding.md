# AD/HS 125/76 - APU Bonding

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/76 APU Bonding 4/86

Applicability: All DH/HS/BH/B.Ae.125 series aircraft up to and including aircraft 258034 fitted with an Auxiliary Power Unit to Mod. 251605 or 258748.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 49-32-(9210A).

*Note: The British CAA have classified this Service Bulletin as mandatory.*

Compliance: Before 31 December 1986.

Background: To protect the A.P.U. system against a possible starter/generator live casing condition, and potential fire hazard.

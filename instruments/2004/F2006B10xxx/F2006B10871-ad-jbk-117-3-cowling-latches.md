# AD/JBK 117/3 - Cowling Latches

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Kawasaki BK 117 Series Helicopters

AD/BK 117/3 Cowling Latches 2/90

Applicability: All models S/No. 1001 to 1006, 1008 to 1015 and 1017. Requirement: Action in accordance with Kawasaki SB KSB-117-047-1. Compliance: Prior to the issue of an Australian CofA.

Background: Loss of the cowl resulted in the loss of an Australian aircraft.

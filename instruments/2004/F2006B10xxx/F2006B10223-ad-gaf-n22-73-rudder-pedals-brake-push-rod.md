# AD/GAF-N22/73 - Rudder Pedals Brake Push Rod

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/73 Rudder Pedals Brake Push Rod 8/95

Applicability: All models N22 and N24.

Requirement: Action in accordance with ASTA Nomad SB NMD-27-47 Revision 1. Compliance: At the next inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 20 July 1995, or prior to

31 October 1995, whichever occurs first.

Background: There has been a reported failure of the brake push rod end fitting. Investigation revealed that excessive depth of the lightening hole in the end fitting caused the rod fitting to fail.

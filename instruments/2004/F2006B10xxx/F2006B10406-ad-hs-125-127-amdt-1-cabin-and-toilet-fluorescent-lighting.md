# AD/HS 125/127 Amdt 1 - Cabin and Toilet Fluorescent Lighting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/127**

**Amdt 1**

**Cabin and Toilet Fluorescent Lighting 9/92**

Applicability: All HS 125 aircraft specified in the Requirement Document. Requirement: IAW CAA (UK) mandatory BAe SB SB.33-45-25A027A&B Revision 2.

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document with a revised compliance date of 31 September 1992 for Australian registered aircraft.

Background: This modification introduces power units with an integral circuit for system fault protection. The units will provide protection against faults which may cause overheating of components, wiring. Amendment 1 is issued to reflect a revision in the Requirement Document which renders the compliance with Mandatory Inspection SB.33-43 and requirement for repeat inspection in the Maintenance Schedule no longer necessary.

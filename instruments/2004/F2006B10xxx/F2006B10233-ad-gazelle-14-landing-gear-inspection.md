# AD/GAZELLE/14 - Landing Gear - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/14 Landing Gear - Inspection 7/83

Applicability: All models SA341 and SA342 fitted with landing gear 341A41.5200 and 341A41.5300 all dash-numbers.

Document: Aerospatiale Gazelle SB No. 32.02.

Compliance: Unless previously accomplished, within 30 days after 6th June 1983. Background: The manufacturer reports a failure of the rear cross tube/rear arch tube junction.

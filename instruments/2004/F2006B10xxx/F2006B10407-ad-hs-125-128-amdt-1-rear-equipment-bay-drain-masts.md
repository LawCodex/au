# AD/HS 125/128 Amdt 1 - Rear Equipment Bay Drain Masts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/128**

**Amdt 1**

**Rear Equipment Bay Drain Masts 9/92**

Applicability: Model 125 Series as listed in BAe 125 SB 53-74-3193 C&D Revision 1. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe 125 Mandatory SB 53-74-3193 C&D Revision 1.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished in accordance with the initial issue of this Directive; prior to 31 October 1992. The compliance of the previous issue of this Directive is as stated. This Amendment is effective from 20 August 1992.

Background: Modification action is required in order to provide fluid venting clear of the rear fuselage structure, thereby avoiding the possibility of reingestion. Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement Document which expands applicability and includes the removal of additional access panels in the accomplishment instructions.

# AD/HS 748/19 - Supplementary Structural Inspection Document

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/19 Supplementary Structural Inspection Document 6/95

Applicability: All HS748 aircraft.

Requirement: Incorporate into the Maintenance Schedule the requirements of the HS748 Supplementary Structural Inspection Document Revision 2 with the following exceptions and additions:

(i) A high frequency eddy current inspection approved by the Authority shall be used in lieu of the visual method specified in item 57-40-1.

(ii) When complying with the SSID operators shall ensure the full requirements of the following Service Bulletins (SB) are met:

SB 57/75, SB 57/55, SB 57/34, SB 55/19, SB 53/49.

(iii) The inspection intervals and associated inspection procedures of SB 52-101 shall be observed when complying with item 52-30-1.

*Note: CAA(UK) AD 010-09-85 and British Aerospace Alert Service Bulletin 12/11 Revision 1 refer.*

Compliance: Effective 25 May 1995.

Background: The Supplementary Structural Inspection Document has been produced as part of a programme to ensure the continuing airworthiness of high time HS748 aircraft.

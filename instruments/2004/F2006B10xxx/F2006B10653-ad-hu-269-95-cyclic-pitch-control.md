# AD/HU 269/95 - Cyclic Pitch Control

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 269/95 Cyclic Pitch Control 7/90 DM

Applicability: All model 269C helicopters S/N 0004 thru 1456.

Requirement: Unless previously accomplished, action in accordance with Schweizer Service Bulletin B-237.

*Note: FAA Emergency AD 90-09-07 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 18 May 1990.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that movement of the lower longitudinal cyclic control rod spring attachment clamp, P/N AN735-9, has occurred. Movement of this clamp could restrict cyclic control which could result in loss of helicopter control.

# AD/HS 748/17 - Engine Subframe Eye Bolt

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/17 Engine Subframe Eye Bolt 5/95

Applicability: All HS748 Aircraft.

Requirement: 1. On both left and right engine subframe assemblies inspect the installation of the eye bolt (P/N 14b.0.3975) into the “Y” plate (P/N 14a.0.3975) in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin (MSB) 54/31.

2\. Rectify any deficient eye bolt installations in accordance with British Aerospace MSB 54/31.

*Note: CAA (UK) AD 009-08-88 refers.*

Compliance: 1. Inspect before 11 May 1995.

2\. Rectify, if required, within 60 days of the Requirement 1 inspection.

Background: An in-service failure of an eye bolt revealed the possibility of incorrect bedding of the eye bolt head to the sub-frame “Y” plate.

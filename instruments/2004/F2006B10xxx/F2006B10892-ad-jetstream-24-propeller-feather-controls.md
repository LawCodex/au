# AD/JETSTREAM/24 - Propeller Feather Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/24 Propeller Feather Controls 6/88

Applicability: All Jetstream 3100 series aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe ASB 76-A-JA880440. Compliance: As per requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that incorrect propeller control rigging has caused feathering difficulty during emergency engine shut down.

# AD/HS 125/72 - Engine Mounting Cowling Damage

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/72 Engine Mounting Cowling Damage 10/85

Applicability: All models fitted with Garrett TFE 731-3R-1H engines.

Requirement: Inspect and repair in accordance with British Aerospace 125 S.B. 71-38.

*Note: If engine mounts are found to be damaged on aircraft fitted with thrust reversers, their fatigue life is limited. Refer AD/HS 125/45 at latest amendment.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 November 1985.

Background: Several cases of damage to the forward engine mounting caused by fouling with the cowling have been reported. This damage could result in reduction of fatigue life.

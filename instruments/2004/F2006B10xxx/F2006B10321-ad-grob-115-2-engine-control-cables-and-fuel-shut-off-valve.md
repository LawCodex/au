# AD/GROB 115/2 - Engine Control Cables and Fuel Shut-Off Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Grob G115 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GROB 115/2 Engine Control Cables and Fuel Shut-Off Valve 12/89

Applicability: All Grob G115 series aircraft, except those already modified as listed in Grob SB TM1078-8.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Grob Service Bulletin TM1078-8.

*Note: LBA AD 89-141 Grob refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 January 1990.

Background: To maintain aircraft airworthiness, replacement of the engine control cables and fuel shut-off valve insert is required.

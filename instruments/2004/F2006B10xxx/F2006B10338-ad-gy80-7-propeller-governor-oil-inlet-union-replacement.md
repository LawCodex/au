# AD/GY80/7 - Propeller Governor Oil Inlet Union - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Aerospatiale GY 80 (Horizon) Series Aeroplanes

AD/GY80/7 Propeller Governor Oil Inlet Union - Replacement 6/79

Applicability: All GY80 aircraft fitted with variable pitch Hartzell propeller. Document: Aerospatiale Horizon SB No. 37.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 July 1979.

Background: This Directive is issued as the result of overseas notification of oil loss caused by failure of the crankcase mounted union.

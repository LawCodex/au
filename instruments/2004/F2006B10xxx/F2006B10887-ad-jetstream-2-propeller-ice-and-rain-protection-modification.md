# AD/JETSTREAM/2 - Propeller Ice and Rain Protection - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/2 Propeller Ice and Rain Protection - Modification

Applicability: Jetstream Series 3100 aircraft. Constructors numbers 601 to 645. Requirement: Service Bulletin 30-JM7453 Revision 1.

Compliance: Before further flight after 31 May 1985.

## 1/85

Background: The manufacturer has introduced a revised propeller de-icing system to resolve the problems of induced vibrations occuring during icing encounters at low ambient temperatures.

# AD/HS 125/70 - EFIS Power Supply

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/70 EFIS Power Supply 8/85

Applicability: All BAe-125 series aeroplanes with the following S/Nos: Series 700B - 257208 to 257210, 257214 and 257215

Series 800A and B - 258001 to 258020, 258022 to 258024 and 258026.

Requirement: Modify the EFIS power supply according to the requirements detailed in British Aerospace 125 S.B. 24-248-(3024).

*Note: United Kingdom CAA AD No. 004-04-85 Refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 October 1985.

Background: The aircraft manufacturer advises of an overheat of the heat sink diode assembly on a completed aircraft.

# AD/GAF-N22/22 - Fuselage - Wing Centre Section Spar Support Bracket Attachment - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/22 Fuselage - Wing Centre Section Spar Support**

**Bracket Attachment - Inspection**

**8/78**

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N24 and N24A delivered from factory prior to 4 July 1978. Document: GAF Alert SB ANMD-16-1 (ANMD-53-2) and Revision 1.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 10 hours time in service after 12 July 1978.

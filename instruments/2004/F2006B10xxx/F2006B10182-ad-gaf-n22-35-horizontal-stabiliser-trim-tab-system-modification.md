# AD/GAF-N22/35 - Horizontal Stabiliser Trim Tab System - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/GAF-N22/35 Horizontal Stabiliser Trim Tab System - Modification

Applicability: All models N22, N22B and N22S with S/Nos. referenced in SB NMD-55-8. Document: GAF SB No. NMD-55-8.

Compliance: Before 1 August 1980.

## 4/80

Background: The modification is introduced to improve the durability of the stabilator trim tab control system.

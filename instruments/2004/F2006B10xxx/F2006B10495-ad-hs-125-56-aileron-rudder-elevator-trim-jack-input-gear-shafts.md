# AD/HS 125/56 - Aileron, Rudder & Elevator Trim Jack Input Gear Shafts - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/56 Aileron, Rudder & Elevator Trim Jack

**Input Gear Shafts - Inspection** Applicability: All HS/DH/BH125 aircraft all series up to and including series 700. Document: British Aerospace HS 125 SB No. 27-130, Rev. 1.

Compliance: At next removal of control surfaces.

Background: Failure of a trim jack input gear shaft has occurred in service.

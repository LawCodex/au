# AD/JETSTREAM/63 - Aileron Control System Mounting Spigot Nut

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/63 Aileron Control System Mounting Spigot Nut

5/94

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 aircraft, and all Jetstream 3200 aircraft up to and including constructors number 979.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Mandatory Service Bulletin 57-JA 921140 or Service Bulletin 57-JA 921140 Revision 1.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to 1 January 1995

Background: There have been reports of cracks and failures of the threaded portion of the aileron control mounting spigots. The cracking was attributed to stress corrosion.

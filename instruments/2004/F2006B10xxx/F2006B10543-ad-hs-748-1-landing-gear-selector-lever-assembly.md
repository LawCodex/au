# AD/HS 748/1 - Landing Gear Selector Lever Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/1 Landing Gear Selector Lever Assembly 12/94

Applicability: Model HS748 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Alert Service Bulletin HS748-A-32-95.

*Note: UK CAA AD 007-07-94 refers.*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, within 250 landings after 10 November 1994, and thereafter at intervals not to exceed 1500 hours time in service.

Background: The UK CAA received reports of incidents where the landing gear selector lever cam detached from the shaft causing the landing gear extension/retraction to malfunction.

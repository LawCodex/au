# AD/HS 125/145 - Electrical Wiring Chafing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/145 Electrical Wiring Chafing 11/94

Applicability: Hawker 1000 and BAe 125 Series 1000A and B aircraft identified in Hawker Raytheon Corporate Jets Mandatory Service Bulletin SB A29-92 Revision 1 dated 1 August 1994.

Requirement: If not previously accomplished, action in accordance with Hawker Raytheon Corporate Jets Mandatory Service Bulletin SB A29-92 Revision 1 dated 1 August 1994.

*Note: This Service Bulletin has been classified as mandatory by the CAA(UK).*

Compliance: Prior to 30 October 1994.

Background: The aircraft manufacturer has received reports of chafing of an electrical cable/loom on a hydraulic pipe, this caused an insulation breakdown and electrical short circuit which produced a pin-hole failure of the hydraulic pipe. Action required by this Directive is designed to decrease the likelihood of a loss of hydraulic pressure, electrical malfunction or an in-flight fire.

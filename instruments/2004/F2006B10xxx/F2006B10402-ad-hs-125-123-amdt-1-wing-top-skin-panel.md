# AD/HS 125/123 Amdt 1 - Wing Top Skin Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/123**

**Amdt 1**

**Wing Top Skin Panel 6/93**

Applicability: Model 125 aircraft up to and including Series 700A and 700B.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 57-73 Revision 1.

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, prior to 31 August 1993.

Background: Reports of wing top skin corrosion in the area beneath the boundary layer fence.

Amendment 1 is issued in response to a revision of the Requirement Document which extends applicability to include 700 A and B Series aircraft.

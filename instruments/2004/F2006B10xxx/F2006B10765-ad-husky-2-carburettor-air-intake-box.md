# AD/HUSKY/2 - Carburettor Air Intake Box

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Christen Industries A-1 (Husky) Series Aeroplanes

AD/HUSKY/2 Carburettor Air Intake Box 1/92

Applicability: Model A-1 with serial number 1001 through 1045.

Requirement: Action in accordance with the technical requirements of FAA AD 91-23-02 Amdt 39-8071.

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document.

Background: Reports of valve engine failures on the air intake box has occurred. This failure could result in loss of engine power.

# AD/HS 748/9 - Auto Feather Micro Switch Operating Levers

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/9 Auto Feather Micro Switch Operating Levers 1/95

Applicability: All HS 748 series aircraft not incorporating modification 6909.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Revision 4 of Jetstream Aircraft Ltd Mandatory SB HS748-76-15.

*Note: UK CAA AD 0132 refers.*

Compliance: Within 1000 hours time in service from 5 January 1995. Thereafter, reinspect at intervals not exceeding 1000 hours time in service until modification 6909 has been incorporated.

Background: Cracks in the fork end of the levers which actuate the auto feather inhibiting micro switches, located on the engine sub frame, have been reported. In the event of a failed micro switch being followed by an engine failure, propeller auto feathering will not take place.

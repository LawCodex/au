# AD/INST/23 Amdt 1 - Edo-Aire Mitchell NSD-360 Compass System - Addition of Nav Flag Adapter

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Instruments and Automatic Pilots

**AD/INST/23**

**Amdt 1**

**Edo-Aire Mitchell NSD-360 Compass System - Addition of Nav Flag Adapter**

**1/78**

Applicability: NSD-360 Compass Systems using VOR/Localiser Converters other than Edo-Aire Mitchell types AK423, IC707 or IC707-1, and using navigation instruments P/N 52D136- 0020, 52D136-0120, 52D136-0220, 52D136-1020, 52D136-1120, 52D136-

1220, 52D137-1020, 52D137-1120, 52D137-1220.

Document: 1. Edo-Aire Mitchell SB No. MB-13; or

2\. Piper SB No. 565, 566 or 600-63; or

3\. Aircraft Manufacturer SBs which refer to Edo-Aire Mitchell SB No. MB-13. Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31 January 1978.

*Note: Compliance with previous issue of this Directive, satisfies compliance with this issue.*

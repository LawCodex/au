# AD/G159/11 Amdt 1 - Engine Mount Chafing

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

[image]AD/G159/11 Amdt 1 Engine Mount Chafing 11/97

Applicability: All models

Requirement: In accordance with Grumman Gulfstream Service Change 180:

Inspect the applicable engine mount trusses, and

b. Install chafe guard Part Number 159WP10017-11

*Note: FAA AD 97-14-13 refers*

Compliance: Unless already accomplished, before the issue of an Australian Certificate of Airworthiness, thereafter inspect the chafe guard at intervals not exceeding 2 500 hours time in service.

This directive has effect from 9 October 1997.

Background: This directive provides terminating action for a short time repetitive inspection in the FAA AD.

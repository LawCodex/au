# AD/G159/16 - Engine Breather Overheat Detector - Max Setting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

AD/G159/16 Engine Breather Overheat Detector - Max Setting 11/71

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Set engine breather overheat detector setting to operate at a temperature not exceeding 260oC.

*Note: Rolls Royce Notice to Operators No. 1018, Issue 3 also refers to this subject.*

Compliance: Before 30 November 1971.

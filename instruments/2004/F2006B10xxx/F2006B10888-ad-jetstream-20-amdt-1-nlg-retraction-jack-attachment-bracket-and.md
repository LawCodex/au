# AD/JETSTREAM/20 Amdt 1 - NLG Retraction Jack Attachment Bracket and Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/20**

**Amdt 1**

**NLG Retraction Jack Attachment Bracket and Bolts**

**3/95**

Applicability: All HP137 Mk1 and Jetstream Series 200 aircraft with Modification 5127 embodied, and Jetstream Series 3100 and Series 3200 with constructors numbers 601 through 840.

Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Mandatory ASB 53-A-JA 870510.

*Note: Compliance with BAe SB 53-JM 5285 constitutes terminating action for the requirement of this directive.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with landings to count from previous certified compliance; until compliance with BAe SB 53-JM 5285. This Amendment is effective from 2 March 1995.

Background: Reports were received of in-service failures of the bolts securing the nose gear retraction jack attachment bracket. Amendment 1 is issued to align this Directive with the manufacturer’s optional terminating action.

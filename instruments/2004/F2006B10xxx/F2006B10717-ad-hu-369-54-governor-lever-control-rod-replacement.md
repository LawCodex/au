# AD/HU 369/54 - Governor Lever Control Rod - Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/54 Governor Lever Control Rod - Replacement 3/82

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Remove P/N 369A7706-3 RPM Governor Lever Control Rod and replace with P/N 369A7706-5 RPM Governor Lever Control Rod.

*Note: FAA AD 82-01-18, Amendment 39-4292, Hughes SIN No. DN-87 and SIN No. HN-169 also refer to this subject.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 25 January 1982.

Background: Failure of the aluminium control rod will result in loss of engine power control. The replacement rod is fabricated from steel.

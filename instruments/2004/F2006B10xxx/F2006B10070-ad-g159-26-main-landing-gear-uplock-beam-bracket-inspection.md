# AD/G159/26 - Main Landing Gear Uplock Beam Bracket - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY PART 105 SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GULFSTREAM (GRUMMAN) G.159 SERIES AEROPLANES

AD/G159/26 Main Landing Gear Uplock Beam Bracket - Inspection 8/75

Applicability: All Gulfstream 1 and TC4C aeroplanes. Document: Grumman Customer Bulletin No. 272.

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 29 August 1975 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service.

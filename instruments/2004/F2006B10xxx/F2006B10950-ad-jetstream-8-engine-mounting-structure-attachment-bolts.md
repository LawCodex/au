# AD/JETSTREAM/8 - Engine Mounting Structure Attachment Bolts

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/8 Engine Mounting Structure Attachment Bolts

10/85

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 with S/Nos. 601 to 607, 609 to 633, 635 to 640 and 649. Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace S.B. 71-JA850625.

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: Each engine mounting structure incorporates two special bolts. It has been found that some batches have been insufficiently heat treated during manufacture.

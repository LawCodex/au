# AD/JETSTREAM/1 - Fuselage Drain Holes - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/1 Fuselage Drain Holes - Modification 8/84

Applicability: All Jetstream Series 3100 aircraft with S/Nos 601, 603, 604, 608 to 624 and 627. Requirement: British Aerospace Service Bulletin 53-JM7389.

Compliance: Prior to issue of the next Maintenance Release after 31 August 1984.

Background: Cases have occurred in service of water draining from the fuselage drain valve at STN 255 onto the flap micro-switches. This water has frozen causing malfunction of the flap operating system.

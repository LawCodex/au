# AD/JETSTREAM/35 - Passenger/Crew Door Restraint Cable

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/35 Passenger/Crew Door Restraint Cable 3/90

Applicability: Constructor numbers 757, 770 to 840, 842 to 847 and 850. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe Alert SB 52-A-JM7704.

Compliance: Prior to 30 June 1990.

Background: Disconnection of the upper attachment has prevented door opening. The main door must always be useable as an emergency exit.

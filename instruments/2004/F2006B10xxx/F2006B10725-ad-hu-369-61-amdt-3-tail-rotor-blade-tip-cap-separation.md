# AD/HU 369/61 Amdt 3 - Tail Rotor Blade Tip Cap Separation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/61**

**Amdt 3**

**Tail Rotor Blade Tip Cap Separation 4/86**

Applicability: All Model 369 Helicopters with affected P/N tail rotor blades. Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Hughes S.I.N. DN-129, EN-18 and HN-195.

2\. Action in accordance with Hughes S.I.N. DN-132.1, EN-21.1, FN-9.1 and HN-199.1.

3\. Rectify, in accordance with the procedures detailed in Requirement Document 2, all those blades listed in Requirement Documents 1 and 2 that require removal of installed rivets.

*Note: FAA AD 86-01-04 Amdt 39-5187 refers.*

Compliance: 1. As per Requirement Document.

2\. As per Requirement Document.

3\. When necessitated as a result of compliance with Requirement Documents 1 or 2.

Background: Reports of tail rotor blade tip rib separation or partial separation have been received. Failure to detect and rectify this defect could result in loss of tail rotor control. Amdt 3 to the Directive changes the applicable FAA AD reference.

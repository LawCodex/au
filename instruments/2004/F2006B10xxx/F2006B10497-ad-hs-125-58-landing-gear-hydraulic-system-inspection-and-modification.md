## AD/HS 125/58 Landing Gear Hydraulic System - Inspection

**and Modification**

Applicability: All models without Modification No. 252837. Document: British Aerospace 125 SB No. 32-190.

Compliance: Before 1 April 1982.

## 11/81

Background: Blockage of a landing gear restrictor valve may prevent lowering of the gear by either the normal or the auxiliary hydraulic system. Filters are introduced at each side of the restrictor valves.

# AD/GAF-N22/11 Amdt 1 - Relocation of Pilot Seat Installations

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/11**

**Amdt 1**

**Relocation of Pilot Seat Installations 5/77**

Applicability: Model N22 with S/Nos. 2 and 4. Model N22B with S/Nos. 7, 25, 26, 27 and 28. Requirement: Modify position of both Pilot seat installations in accordance with GAF SB N22.25.1. Compliance: Before 1 May 1977.

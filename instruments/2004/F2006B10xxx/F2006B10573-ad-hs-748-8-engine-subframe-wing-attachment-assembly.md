# AD/HS 748/8 - Engine Subframe/Wing Attachment Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/8 Engine Subframe/Wing Attachment Assembly 13/94

Applicability: All HS748 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 54/29.

*Note: UK CAA AD 026-11-86 refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: Undetected abnormal movement and wear of the engine subframe/wing attachment assembly can lead to the failure of the split bush and/or taper bolt, which can cause severe stress and damage to the engine support structure.

# AD/HILLER 12/9 Amdt 1 - Horizontal Stabiliser Spar - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

**AD/HILLER 12/9**

**Amdt 1**

**Horizontal Stabiliser Spar - Inspection 3/78**

Applicability: All models UH-12, UH-12A, UH-12B and UH-12C.

Requirement: Remove the horizontal stabiliser, and with the aid of a lens of not less than 8X power, inspect the spar tube for cracking in the vicinity of the stabiliser attachment bolt holes.

Compliance: At intervals not exceeding 300 hours’ time in service.

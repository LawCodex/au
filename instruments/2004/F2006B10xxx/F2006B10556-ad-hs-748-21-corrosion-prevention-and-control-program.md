# AD/HS 748/21 - Corrosion Prevention and Control Program

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/21 Corrosion Prevention and Control Program 10/96

Applicability: British Aerospace HS 748 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Jetstream Aircraft Corrosion Prevention and Control Programme document Reference JS/CPCP/02 dated 1 April 1996.

*Note: UK CAA AD 002-04-96 refers.*

Compliance: At the thresholds specified in the Requirement Document.

Background: The Country of Origin Airworthiness Authority requires implementation of a corrosion prevention and control program to prevent the degradation of the structural capabilities of the affected aircraft.

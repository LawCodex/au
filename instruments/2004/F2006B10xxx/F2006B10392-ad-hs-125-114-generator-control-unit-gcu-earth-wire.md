# AD/HS 125/114 - Generator Control Unit (GCU) Earth Wire

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/114 Generator Control Unit (GCU) Earth Wire 2/91

DM

Applicability: All BAe 125 aircraft as listed in the Requirement document. Requirement: Action in accordance with BAe SB 24-A278.

*Note: Compliance with this SB has been classified Mandatory by the UK CAA.*

Compliance: Prior to 31 January 1991.

Background: Loss of GCU earth connection can result in a DC generator overvoltage which can cause battery overheat and subsequent failure.

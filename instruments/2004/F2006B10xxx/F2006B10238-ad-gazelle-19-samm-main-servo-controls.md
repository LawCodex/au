# AD/GAZELLE/19 - SAMM Main Servo Controls

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/19 SAMM Main Servo Controls 13/94

DM

Applicability: All model SA 341/342 helicopters with SAMM Main Servo Control SC-5041-A1 (704A44-831-042) installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Eurocopter SA 341/342 Mandatory SB No 05.31.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-194-037(B) refers.*

Compliance: As specified in the requirement document with a revised effective date of 14 October 1994.

Background: The manufacturer has advised that the servo control rod may have been incorrectly torqued during assembly.

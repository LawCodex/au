# AD/G164/17 - Lower Wing Attachment Fittings - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Grumman) G-164 (Ag-Cat) Series Aeroplanes

AD/G164/17 Lower Wing Attachment Fittings - Inspection 12/78

Applicability: All models G-164, G-164A and G-164B which have achieved 500 flying hours or 6 months time in service.

Document: Gulfstream American AG-CAT (G164) SB No. 65.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 6 November 1978 and there- after at intervals not exceeding 500 hours time in service or 6 months whichever occurs first.

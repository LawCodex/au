# AD/JETSTREAM/66 - Wing - Main Spar Fuel Fitting Attachment Holes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/66 Wing - Main Spar Fuel Fitting Attachment Holes

Applicability: Jetstream 3200 Series aircraft up to and including constructor’s number 950.

## 10/94

Requirement: Modify the fuel tank drainage hole at wing station 64.8 on the left and right hand sides in accordance with British Aerospace SB 57-JM 8175 revision 1.

Compliance: Prior to 6000 landings or before 15 March 1995 whichever is the later.

Background: Cracking of the wing main spar webs has been found at this location on the Jetstream 3200 series wing test specimen.

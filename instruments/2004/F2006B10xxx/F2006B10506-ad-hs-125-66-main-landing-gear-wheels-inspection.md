# AD/HS 125/66 - Main Landing Gear Wheels - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 125/66 Main Landing Gear Wheels - Inspection 8/84

Applicability: All models HS/DH/BH 125 Series aircraft up to and including Series 700. Requirement: British Aerospace 125 SB No. 32-201.

*Note: The eddy current scan pattern shall be as shown in Fig. 1 of Appendix A and the probe shall be jigged to ensure that the scan index is accurately maintained.*

Compliance: Inspect prior to 31 August 1984, as detailed in the Requirement Document. Background: Main wheel flange fracture has occurred with resultant structural damage.

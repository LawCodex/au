# AD/HU 369/47 Amdt 2 - Over-Running Clutch - Sprag Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

**AD/HU 369/47**

**Amdt 2**

**Over-Running Clutch - Sprag Assembly 1/86**

Applicability: All models of 369D, E, F and H series aircraft equipped with any cargo hook. Requirement: Hughes Service Information Notice DN 81-1 EN-3, HN 164-1 and FN-3.

Compliance: Inspection - Within 600 hours total time in service, unless already accomplished, and then at periods not exceeding 300 hours time in service.

Retirement: Prior to the assembly exceeding 1800 hours time in service.

Background: This Directive is amended to include the E series aircraft and its Requirement Documents.

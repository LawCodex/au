# AD/JETSTREAM/33 - Inhibition of TTL Test and Stall Protection Test Functions During Flight

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/JETSTREAM/33 Inhibition of TTL Test and Stall Protection

**Test Functions During Flight**

Applicability: As listed in Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace SB 24-JM7657A. Compliance: Prior to 31 August 1989.

## 6/89

Background: This AD is raised to ensure that inadvertent selection of the TTL or stall protection test switches during flight will not result in an unsafe condition.

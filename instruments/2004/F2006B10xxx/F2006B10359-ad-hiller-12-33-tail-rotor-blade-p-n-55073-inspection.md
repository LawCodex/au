# AD/HILLER 12/33 - Tail Rotor Blade P/N 55073 - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Hiller UH-12 Series Helicopters

AD/HILLER 12/33 Tail Rotor Blade P/N 55073 - Inspection 11/81

Applicability: All UH-12 series fitted with tail rotor blades P/N. 55073. Document: Hiller Aviation SB No. 55-1.

Compliance: At each daily or overnight inspection.

*Note: This inspection may be performed and certified for by the pilot in command.*

Background: There is a possibility of the vent holes located at the inboard and outboard ends of the blade becoming obstructed with dirt or debris.

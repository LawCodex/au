# AD/G21/2 - Horizontal Stabiliser Lower Strut Fittings - Inspection and Life Limitation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Grumman G21 (Goose) Series Aeroplanes

## AD/G21/2 Horizontal Stabiliser Lower Strut Fittings - Inspection and Life Limitation

Applicability: All model G21A.

## 10/77

Requirement: 1 Inspect the threaded portion of the strut lower adjustable end fitting P/N 12561-3 for cracks using a magnetic particle inspection technique.

2\. Retire from service strut lower adjustable end fittings P/N 12561-3.

*Note: Grumman Customer Bulletin G21A No. 3 and FAA AD 53-31-2 refer.*

Compliance: 1. Initially, before issue of Certificate of Airworthiness and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 1000 hours time in service.

2\. Before exceeding 3200 hours time in service.

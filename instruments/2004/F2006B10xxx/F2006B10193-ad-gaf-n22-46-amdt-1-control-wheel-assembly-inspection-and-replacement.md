# AD/GAF-N22/46 Amdt 1 - Control Wheel Assembly - Inspection and Replacement

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/GAF-N22/46**

**Amdt 1**

**Control Wheel Assembly - Inspection and Replacement**

**2/83**

Applicability: All Nomad N22 and N24 series aircraft. Document: GAF Nomad Alert SB ANMD-27-27 Revision 1.

Compliance: For Part A of the SB: As per requirement document.

For Part B: Within 300 hours time in service after 28 February, 1983, but not later than 1 April 1983.

Background: Instances have been reported in which partial failure of the control wheel assembly has occurred.

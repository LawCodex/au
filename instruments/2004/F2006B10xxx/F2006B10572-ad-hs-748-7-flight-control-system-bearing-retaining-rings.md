# AD/HS 748/7 - Flight Control System Bearing Retaining Rings

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace (Hawker Siddeley) HS 748 Series Aeroplanes

AD/HS 748/7 Flight Control System Bearing Retaining Rings 13/94

Applicability: All HS748 aircraft.

Requirement: Action in accordance with British Aerospace Mandatory Service Bulletin 27/110.

*Note: UK CAA AD 006-03-87 refers.*

Compliance: At the inspection intervals specified in paragraph 2.A and 2.B of the Requirement Document Accomplishment Instructions.

Background: The UK CAA requires an on-going check for the security of bearings in housings held in place by Walker Schofield retaining rings.

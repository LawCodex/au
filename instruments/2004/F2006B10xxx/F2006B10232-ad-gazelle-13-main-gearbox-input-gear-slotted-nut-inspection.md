# AD/GAZELLE/13 - Main Gearbox Input Gear Slotted Nut - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/13 Main Gearbox Input Gear Slotted Nut - Inspection

5/83

Applicability: All SA 341 and SA 342 models fitted with main gearbox Part No. 341A 32.1000.01 through 06 with the embodiment of Modification A.M.S. 07.70.76/S.374.

Document: SA 341-342 Service Bulletin 65-24.

*Note: For gearboxes not modified as above refer to AD/GAZELLE/9 and Service Bulletin 65.19.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours time in service after 31st May 1983 carry out the inspection as per the requirement document.

Background: The manufacturer has introduced an inspection method to ensure that the correct torque load had been applied to the slotted nut which retains the input gear flange.

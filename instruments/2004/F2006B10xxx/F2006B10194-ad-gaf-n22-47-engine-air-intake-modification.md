# AD/GAF-N22/47 - Engine Air Intake Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/47 Engine Air Intake Modification 9/82

Applicability: All Nomad N22 and N24 series aeroplanes. Document: GAF SB No. NMD-71-6 Rev. 2.

Compliance: Before 30 June 1983.

Background: This modification is required to provide engine inlet icing protection over the full certification envelope.

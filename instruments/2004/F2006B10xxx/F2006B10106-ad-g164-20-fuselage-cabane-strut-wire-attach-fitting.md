# AD/G164/20 - Fuselage Cabane Strut Wire Attach Fitting

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Grumman) G-164 (Ag-Cat) Series Aeroplanes

AD/G164/20 Fuselage Cabane Strut Wire Attach Fitting 11/87

Applicability: All models G164B with S/Nos 660B to 0790B and G164B-15T,-34T with S/Nos 001E to 003E.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Schweizer SB No.82.

Compliance: At the next scheduled inspection for maintenance release issue after 30 September and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 400 hours time in service or annually, whichever occurs first.

Background: The manufacturer reports possible cracking of the welded fuselage cabane strut wire attachment fittings.

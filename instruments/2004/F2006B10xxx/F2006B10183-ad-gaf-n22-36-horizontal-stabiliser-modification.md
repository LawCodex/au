# AD/GAF-N22/36 - Horizontal Stabiliser - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
GAF N22 and N24 Series Aeroplanes

AD/GAF-N22/36 Horizontal Stabiliser - Modification 4/80

Applicability: All models N22, N22B, N24 and N24A with S/Nos. referenced in SB No.

NMD-55-10.

Document: GAF SB No. NMD-55-10.

Compliance: Before 1 August 1980.

Background: In several cases the horizontal stabiliser has developed loose rivets, skin cracking and internal structural cracking during service.

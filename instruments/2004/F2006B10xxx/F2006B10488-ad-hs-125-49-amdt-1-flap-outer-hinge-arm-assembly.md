# AD/HS 125/49 Amdt 1 - Flap Outer Hinge Arm Assembly

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 125 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/HS 125/49**

**Amdt 1**

**Flap Outer Hinge Arm Assembly 9/80**

Applicability: All HS/DH/BH 125 series aeroplanes except those with modification 252670 incorporated.

Document: British Aerospace 125 SB No 57-54, Rev 1.

Compliance: Initially inspect before further flight (unless already accomplished) and thereafter inspect at intervals not exceeding 3 months.

Background: The service bulletin revision supersedes the previous alert bulletin, it calls for the removal of the hinge arm for the inspection and applies to additional serial numbered aircraft.

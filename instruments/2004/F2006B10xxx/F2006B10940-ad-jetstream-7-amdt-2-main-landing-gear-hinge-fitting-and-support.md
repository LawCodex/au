# AD/JETSTREAM/7 Amdt 2 - Main Landing Gear Hinge Fitting and Support Angle

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

**AD/JETSTREAM/7**

**Amdt 2**

**Main Landing Gear Hinge Fitting and Support Angle**

**5/95**

Applicability: All Jetstream 3100 series aircraft with constructors numbers 601 to 695 inclusive, without Service Bulletin 57-JM 5218 incorporated.

Requirement: Inspect in accordance with British Aerospace Service Bulletin (BAe SB) 32-A-JA 850127, Revision 2, dated 11 November 1994.

Compliance: Initially inspect before exceeding 4000 component landings.

Re-inspect at the intervals specified in BAe SB 32-A-JA 850127, Revision 2. The inspection requirements are unchanged by this Amendment.

This Amendment is effective from 27 April 1995.

Background: Cracks have been found at the MLG hinge fittings on the Jetstream fatigue test specimen. In addition, a number of cases of cracks have been reported by operators of earlier series versions of the Jetstream. Amendment 2 redefines the applicability and excludes aircraft incorporating Mod No JM5218 (SB 57-JM 5218 refers).

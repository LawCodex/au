# AD/HU 369/77 - Main and Tail Rotor Control Tubes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 369/77 Main and Tail Rotor Control Tubes 9/89

DM

Applicability: All model 369 series helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with McDonnell Douglas SIN No HN-217, DN-158, EN-48 or FN-36, as applicable.

Compliance: Part 1 of Requirement Document: Unless already accomplished, within 25 flight hours from 14 July 1989.

Part 2 of Requirement Document: At next inspection for issue of a Maintenance Release but not later than 31 December 1989.

Background: The manufacturer advises that cracking of the main and tail rotor control tubes, mounted in the vertical tunnel, has been discovered.

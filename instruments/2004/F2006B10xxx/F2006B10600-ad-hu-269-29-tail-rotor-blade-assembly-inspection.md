# AD/HU 269/29 - Tail Rotor Blade Assembly - Inspection

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Hughes) 269 Series Helicopters

AD/HU 269/29 Tail Rotor Blade Assembly - Inspection 12/68

Applicability: All models with tail rotor blade assemblies S/Nos up to and including 126-3312. Document: Hughes SIN N-58.

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in service after 9 December 1968 and thereafter at intervals not exceeding 25 hours time in service.

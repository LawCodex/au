# AD/GAZELLE/17 - Intermediate Gearbox Attachment

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/17 Intermediate Gearbox Attachment 4/92

Applicability: All model SA 341 and SA 342 helicopters equipped with an intermediate gearbox unmodified by AMS 341A07.1636.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Aerospatiale Service Bulletin No 65-43.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-011-036(B) refers.*

Compliance: Before 8 February1992. Thereafter reinspect at each removal/installation of the intermediate gearbox until modification AMS 341A07.1636 incorporated.

Background: Wear has been found on the forward attachment lug bolt. Allowed to continue this wear could lead to loss of the intermediate gearbox.

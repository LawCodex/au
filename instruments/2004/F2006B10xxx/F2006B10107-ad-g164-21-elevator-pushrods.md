# AD/G164/21 - Elevator Pushrods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Schweizer (Grumman) G-164 (Ag-Cat) Series Aeroplanes

AD/G164/21 Elevator Pushrods 12/89 DM

Applicability: All models.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Schweizer G164 SB No 85.

*Note: FAA AD 89-18-02, Amendment 39-6300 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 18 November 1989 and thereafter at periods not exceeding 12 months.

Background: The manufacturer has reported corrosion and cracking of the elevator pushrods which in one case led to loss of the aircraft.

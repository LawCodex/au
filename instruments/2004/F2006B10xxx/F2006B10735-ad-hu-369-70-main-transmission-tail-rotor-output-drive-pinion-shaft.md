# AD/HU 369/70 - Main Transmission Tail Rotor Output Drive Pinion Shaft

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

## AD/HU 369/70 Main Transmission Tail Rotor Output Drive Pinion Shaft

Applicability: All model 369D, 369E, 369F and 369FF series Helicopters.

## 8/87

**TLX**

Requirement: Action in accordance with McDonnell Douglas SIN No. DN-147, EN-35, FN-24, as applicable.

Compliance: For helicopters fitted with transmission assemblies which have the Serial Numbers listed in the “Time of Compliance” section of the Requirement Document: Within 25 hours time in service after 1 June 1987.

All other helicopters: Within 100 hours time in service after 1 June 1987. For spares: Before installation on a Helicopter after 1 June 1987.

Background: The manufacturer has received reports of the output drive pinion shafts fracturing.

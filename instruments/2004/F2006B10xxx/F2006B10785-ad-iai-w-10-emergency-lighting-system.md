# AD/IAI-W/10 - Emergency Lighting System

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Israel Aircraft Industries 1123 and 1124 (Westwind) Series Aeroplanes

AD/IAI-W/10 Emergency Lighting System 8/85

Applicability: As detailed in Requirement Document.

Requirement: Inspect and Modify the emergency lighting system components according to Accomplishment Instructions Parts 1 and 2 contained in IAI Westwind S.B. 1124-33-010.

Compliance: Within 150 hours time in service after 31 August 1985. Background: The aircraft manufacturer advises that:

a) the inertial switch may not be aligned with the longitudinal axis of the aircraft; and

b) the possibility exists for an inadvertent battery pack short circuit to surrounding structure.

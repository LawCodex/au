# AD/JETSTREAM/6 - Inboard Wing Leading Edge Hydraulic Lines

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
British Aerospace BAe 3100 (Jetstream) Series Aeroplanes

AD/JETSTREAM/6 Inboard Wing Leading Edge Hydraulic Lines 8/85

Applicability: All model Jetstream Series 3100 with S/Nos. 601 to 636. Requirement: British Aerospace SB29-JA840508 Rev. 1.

Compliance: Within 300 flying hours from 31 AUGUST 1985.

Background: Chafing of the suction and pressure hydraulic lines in the inboard wing leading edge can result in complete hydraulic failure due to loss of fluid.

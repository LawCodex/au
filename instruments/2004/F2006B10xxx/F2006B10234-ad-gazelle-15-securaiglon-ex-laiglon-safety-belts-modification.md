# AD/GAZELLE/15 - Securaiglon (ex LAiglon) Safety Belts - Modification

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Eurocopter SA341 and SA342 (Gazelle) Series Helicopters

AD/GAZELLE/15 Securaiglon (ex L’Aiglon) Safety Belts - Modification

9/84

Applicability: All models SA341 and SA342 equipped with passenger seat belts identified in Paragraph 1 of Service Bulletin TRW No. 01.

Requirement: Aerospatiale SB No. 01.20 and TRW RAPA SA SB No. 01. Compliance: Modify prior to 31 January 1985.

Background: The manufacturer reports instances where the buckle had jammed in the locked position.

# AD/GOV/8 - Woodward Combined Power Turbine and Propeller Governor - Feather Check

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 107 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Propeller Governors

**AD/GOV/8 Woodward Combined Power Turbine and**

**Propeller Governor - Feather Check**

**10/82**

Applicability: Woodward propeller governors P/N 8210-018 (DDA 6875708), installed on Allison 250-B15G, B17, B17B, B17C engines, and included in the serial number schedule listed in the compliance document.

Document: 1. Woodward SB. 33577; or

2\. Allison 250 CEB A-87 and Allison 250 CEB A-1151.

*Note: FAA AD 82-12-04 refers.*

Compliance: Within 50 hours time in service after 20 September, 1982.

Background: A manufacturing calibration error which could cause slow propeller feathering or the inability to feather may exist in a group of governors.

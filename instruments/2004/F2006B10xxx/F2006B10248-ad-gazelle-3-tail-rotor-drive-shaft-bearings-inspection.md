AD/GAZELLE/3 Tail Rotor Drive Shaft Bearings - Inspection 9/76

Applicability: All models SA341 and SA342 fitted with:

Horizontal transmission shaft 341A.34.1106.00, .01 or .03 without elastomer sleeve 341A.34.1034.20 (Modification S208-AMS 07.9016).

2\. a. Horizontal transmission shaft 341A.34.1106.00, .01, .02 or .03. 2. b. Complying shaft 341A.34.1114.00, .01 or .02.

3\. Bearing attachment straps 341A.34.1033.00. Document: Aerospatiale Gazelle SB 341-542 05.07. Compliance: In accordance with the requirement document.

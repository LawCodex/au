# AD/HU 369/67 Amdt 1 - Main Rotor Retention Strap

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
McDonnell Douglas (Hughes) and Kawasaki 369 Series Helicopters

## AD/HU 369/67

**Amdt 1**

Applicability: All models.

## Main Rotor Retention Strap 4/88

Requirement: Action in accordance with Part 1 of McDonnell Douglas SIN HN214, DN154, EN44 or FN33 as applicable.

Compliance: As applicable by helicopter model, either:

(a) within three months or one hundred hours time in service after the previous compliance with AD/HU 369/28D whichever occurs first; or

(b) within nine months or three hundred hours time in service after the previous compliance with the initial issue of this Directive whichever occurs first;

(c) and thereafter in accordance with the Requirement document. Background: The manufacturer has consolidated the inspection within the single SIN.

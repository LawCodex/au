# AD/A320/29 Amdt 1 - Ram Air Turbine Bearing End-Float

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/A320/29**

**Amdt 1**

**Ram Air Turbine Bearing End-Float 9/95**

Applicability: All A320 series aircraft fitted with P/N 680203020 or 680203021 or 680203027 Ram Air Turbine and not incorporating Airbus Industries modification 24701 or Airbus Industries SB A320-29-1065.

Requirement: 1. Action in accordance with Airbus Service Bulletin A320-29-1061.

2\. Unless previously carried out action in accordance with Airbus Service Bulletin A320-29-1065.

*Note: DGAC AD 93-057-041(B)R1 and Dowty Aerospace Hydraulics - Cheltam Service Bulletin No 600-29-171 refers.*

Compliance: 1. The effective date specified in the original issue remains unchanged at 13 July 1993.

2\. Prior to 30 June 1997.

*Note: Compliance with Airbus Industries SB A320-29-1065 provides terminating action for this directive.*

Background: The manufacturer has advised that a Ram Air Turbine became detached from its leg during a ground functional test. This amendment provides terminating action for this directive.

# AD/A320/47 Amdt 2 - Fuselage - Pressure Floor Between Frames 42 and 43

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/A320/47**

**Amdt 2**

**Fuselage - Pressure Floor Between Frames 42 and 43**

**6/96**

Applicability: Serial Numbers 002 to 008, 010 to 014, 016 to 078, 080 to 104, and 106 and 107, without Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1023 Rev.7 incorporated.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1024 Rev. 2.

*Note 1. DGAC AD 92-205-033(B)R2 refers.*

*Note 2. For aircraft with Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1025 Rev. 6 or earlier Revisions incorporated, refer to AD/A320/67.*

Compliance: Prior to attaining 12 000 flights; thereafter at the intervals specified in the Requirement Document until modified, and this amendment is effective from 23 May 1996.

Background: During the full scale fatigue test, cracking was experienced in the outer floor beams, box beams, and other associated structure.

Amendment 1 provides relief for operators that have already incorporated SB A320-53-1023 at issues before Revision 3.

Amendment 2 amends the applicability and provides terminating action through Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1023 Rev. 7.

The original issue of this AD became effective on 27 April 1995. Amendment 1 of this AD became effective on 20 July 1995.

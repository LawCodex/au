# AD/A320/55 - Centre Wing, Bracket Between Shroud Box and Fixed Shroud

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/A320/55 Centre Wing, Bracket Between Shroud Box and Fixed Shroud

Applicability: As specified in the Requirement Document.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-57-1034 Rev. 1.

*Note: DGAC AD 95-100-068(B) refers.*

## 9/95

Compliance: Prior to attaining 17 000 flight cycles, thereafter at the intervals specified in the Requirement Document.

*Note: Full incorporation of Airbus Industrie SB A320-57-1035 terminates this Directive.*

Background: This Directive is issued to detect and/or prevent any damage related to structural fatigue.

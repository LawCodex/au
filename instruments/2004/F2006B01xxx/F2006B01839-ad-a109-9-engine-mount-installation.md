# AD/A109/9 - Engine Mount Installation

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/9 Engine Mount Installation 6/87

Applicability: All models A109A and A109AII up to and including S/N 7361 except S/N 7359. Requirement: Action in accordance with Bollettino Tecnico 109-61 Part I and Part II.

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 4 June 1987 or at the next scheduled inspection for Maintenance Release issue after 4 June 1987, whichever is later.

*Note: RAI AD 87-43/109-42 refers.*

Background: Chafing of an oil scavenge line against a mount leg has initiated corrosion which has in one case penetrated through the tubing.

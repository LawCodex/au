# AD/A320/51 - Main Landing Gear Rear Pintle Pin

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

AD/A320/51 Main Landing Gear Rear Pintle Pin 5/95

Applicability: A320 S/N 002 to 008, 010 to 014 and 016 to 021.

Requirement: Replace the rear pintle pin in the main landing gear in accordance with Airbus Industrie Service Bulletin A320-32-1024.

*Note: DGAC AD 94-240-061(B) refers.*

Compliance: Before 20000 landings or during the next main landing gear overhaul, whichever occurs first.

Background: Three cracked pins have been found on the fatigue specimen. To improve the fatigue performance, A320-100 pins are replaced by A320-200 pins on aircraft in service.

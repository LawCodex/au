# AD/A109/10 - Nose Landing Gear Actuator

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/10 Nose Landing Gear Actuator 12/87

Applicability: All Model A109A and A109AII Helicopters.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta Bollettino Tecnico No. 109-62, Revision A.

*Note: RAI AD No 87-93/A109-43 refers.*

Compliance: As per the Requirement Document.

Background: Additional maintenance, particularly where operations are conducted in highly humid and saline conditions, is required the nose gear retraction servo to prevent the nose landing gear collapsing during ground operation.

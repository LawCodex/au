# AD/A320/26 Amdt 1 - Fuel Quantity Probes

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/A320/26**

**Amdt 1**

**Fuel Quantity Probes 7/92**

Applicability: All A320 series aircraft without Airbus Industrie modifications 22647 and 22855.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Airbus Industrie Service Bulletin A320-28-1040 Revision 1 dated 3 April 1992.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-048-024(B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 21 February 1993.

Background: This Directive requires action to prevent arcing between fuel quantity probes and fuel tank structures in the event of a lightning strike.

Amendment 1 is issued to reflect a revision to the Requirement Document.

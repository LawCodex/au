# AD/A109/19 Amdt 2 - Tail Rotor Hub

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

**AD/A109/19**

**Amdt 2**

**Tail Rotor Hub 9/95**

Applicability: All A109C helicopters equipped with Fork Assy P/N 109-8131-05-109 and -113 installed on Tail rotor Assy P/N 1009-8131-02-115.

Requirement: 1. Inspect in accordance with Bollettino Technico Telegrafico No. 109-8 Rev. A, and

2\. Modify in accordance with Bollettino Technico No. 109-100.

*Note: RAI AD 95-101 refers.*

Compliance: 1. In accordance with the Requirement Document until modified, and

2\. Prior to 31 March 1997.

For Requirement 1 the effective date was 25 January 1992. This amendment is effective 17 August 1995.

Background: The initial issue of this Directive was to address cracking of the flange of the tail rotor grip. Amendment 1 extended the inspection to the -113 part number grip.

Amendment 2 provides terminating action through modification.

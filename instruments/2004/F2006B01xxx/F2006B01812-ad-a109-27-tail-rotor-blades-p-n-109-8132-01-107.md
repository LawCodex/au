# AD/A109/27 - Tail Rotor Blades P/N 109-8132-01-107

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/27 Tail Rotor Blades P/N 109-8132-01-107 7/97

DM

Applicability: Model A109C with tail rotor blades Part Number 109-8132-01-107 with Serial Numbers prefixed A5- installed.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta Bullettino Technico 109-105.

*Note: RAI AD 97-090 refers.*

Compliance: Within 25 hours time in Service after 12 May 1997.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 12 May 1997.

Background: This Directive is raised to inspect the bonding of the tail rotor blades tip fairing for voids and disbonding. Loss of a tip fairing will cause severe vibration, and may cause severe structural damage that may result in loss of control.

# AD/A109/21 - Aircruiser Emergency Floats

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/21 Aircruiser Emergency Floats 3/94

Applicability: All models fitted with emergency floats P/N 109-0715-01 not modified in accordance with Aircruiser Service Bulletin 902-25-1.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta B.T. 109-93.

*Note: RAI AD 93-347 refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 30 September 1994.

Background: There has been an instance of uncommanded inflation of a float at high forward airspeed.

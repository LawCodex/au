# AD/A320/15 - Low Pressure Fire Fuel Shut-Off Valve

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

AD/A320/15 Low Pressure Fire Fuel Shut-Off Valve 2/91

Applicability: A320, all models.

Requirement: Inspect and/or modify subject aeroplanes IAW French AD 90-190-015(B).

*Note: French AD 90-190-015(B) refers to Airbus Industrie SB A320-28-1028 rev.1.*

Compliance: As per Requirement Document.

Background: To avoid an incorrect closure of the low pressure shut-off valves in the low pressure fuel shut-off valve actuator.

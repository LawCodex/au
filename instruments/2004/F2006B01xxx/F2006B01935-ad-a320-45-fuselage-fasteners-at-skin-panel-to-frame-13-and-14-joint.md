# AD/A320/45 - Fuselage - Fasteners at Skin Panel to Frame 13 and 14 Joint

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

## AD/A320/45 Fuselage - Fasteners at Skin Panel to Frame 13 and 14 Joint

Applicability: A320 series aircraft MSN 005 to 030 inclusive.

## 5/95

Requirement: Perform an external detailed visual inspection for missing or broken rivet heads at fuselage frames 13 and 14 on the left and right hand sides between stringers 1 and 7. Inspect in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1036.

*Note: DGAC AD 92-199-028(B) refers.*

Compliance: Inspect before 10000 flights or before 27 May 1995, whichever is the later. Repeat inspections at intervals not exceeding 6000 flights.

Modification in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-53-1010 cancels the requirement of this AD.

Background: A number of damaged or missing rivet heads were found on the fatigue test article.

# AD/A109/14 - Main Landing Gear Wheels

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/14 Main Landing Gear Wheels 13/88

Applicability: A109A and A109AII.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Bollettino Tecnico 109-71.

*Note: RAI AD 88-142 refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 March 1989.

Background: Excessive torque on the retaining nut has caused failure of the aluminium retaining washer and loss of the wheel.

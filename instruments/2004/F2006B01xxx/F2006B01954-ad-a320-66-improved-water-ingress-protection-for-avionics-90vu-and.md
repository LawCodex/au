# AD/A320/66 - Improved Water Ingress Protection for Avionics 90VU and 91VU

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

**AD/A320/66 Improved Water Ingress Protection**

**for Avionics 90VU and 91VU**

**5/96**

Applicability: All models A320 without Airbus Industrie modifications 21999 or 22119 embodied, or without Airbus Industrie SB A320-24-1054 incorporated.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-24-1054.

*Note: DGAC AD 96-040-076 (B) refers.*

Compliance: Prior to 28 February 1999.

This Directive has effect from 25 April 1996.

Background: This Directive is issued to improve the protection in the avionics compartment from accidental spillage of liquids in cabin or galley area.

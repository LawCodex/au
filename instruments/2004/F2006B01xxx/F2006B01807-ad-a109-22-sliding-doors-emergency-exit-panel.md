# AD/A109/22 - Sliding Doors Emergency Exit Panel

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/22 Sliding Doors Emergency Exit Panel 8/94

DM

Applicability: A109A and A109AII.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta Bollettino Tecnico 109-95.

*Note: RAI AD 94-71 refers.*

Compliance: Part I: Within 25 hours’ time in service after 26 May 1994.

Part II: At each 100 hour inspection after compliance with Part I. Part III: Prior to 30 June 1994.

Background: This Directive is issued following the loss of an emergency exit in flight. This was due to incomplete engagement of the locking pins and improper safety wire installation.

# AD/A320/13 - Passenger Oxygen

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Airbus Industrie A319, A320 and A321 Series Aeroplanes

AD/A320/13 Passenger Oxygen 10/90

Applicability: All models A320.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Airbus Industrie SB A320-35-1002 including change notice 0A or later.

*Note: DGAC AD 90-108-012(B) refers.*

Compliance: Within 100 hours’ time in service after 31 October 1990. Background: To prevent the failure of the passenger oxygen doors to open.

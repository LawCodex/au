# AD/A109/28 - Tail Rotor Blade Grip Assemblies

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/28 Tail Rotor Blade Grip Assemblies 7/97

DM

Applicability: All A109C up to Serial Number 7670, excluding 7630, 7633, 7645, 7651, 7653, 7657,

7661, 7663, 7665, 7667, and 7669.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta Bollettino Tecnico No. 109-100, Rev A.

*Note: RAI AD 97-084 refers.*

Compliance: Before 30 June 1997.

This Airworthiness Directive becomes effective on 14 May 1997.

Background: Cracking has been discovered in the grip assemblies P/N 109-8131-05-109 and -113.

This Directive is issued to replace the grip assemblies with an improved item and rework of the blades to accept larger diameter bolts.

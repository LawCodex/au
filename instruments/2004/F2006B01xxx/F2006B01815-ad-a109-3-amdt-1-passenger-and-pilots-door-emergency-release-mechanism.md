# AD/A109/3 Amdt 1 - Passenger and Pilots Door Emergency Release Mechanism

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

**AD/A109/3**

**Amdt 1**

**Passenger and Pilots’ Door Emergency Release Mechanism**

**9/86**

Applicability: All models A109A and A109AII.

Requirement: Action in accordance with Agusta Bollettino Tecnico No. 109-48, Revision A. Compliance: As per the Requirement Document.

Background: An operator has experienced the loss of a passenger door during flight as a result of a failure to observe the correct refitting procedures. Amendment 1 introduces a modification that simplifies the inspection procedure.

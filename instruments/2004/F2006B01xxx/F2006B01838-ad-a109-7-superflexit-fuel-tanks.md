# AD/A109/7 - Superflexit Fuel Tanks

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA** ***(Civil Aviation Regulations 1998)*****, PART 39 - 105 CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY AUTHORITY**

**SCHEDULE OF AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVES**

[image]\
Agusta A109 Series Helicopters

AD/A109/7 Superflexit Fuel Tanks 10/86

Applicability: All models A109A and A109AII fitted with Superflexit fuel tanks P/No. 109-0611- 02-7/-8 (Superflexit P/No. 18408-1-2) and P/No. 109-0611-02-9/-10 (Superflexit P/No. 18631-1/-2).

Requirement: Inspect and modify in accordance with Agusta B.T. No. 109-57. Compliance: Part I of Requirement Document:

At the next Maintenance Release inspection after 31 October 1986 or before 31 January 1987 whichever occurs first.

Part II of Requirement Document: Each 4 years after initial inspection.

Background: Some instances of fuel leaking have been caused by damage to the bladder due to excessive stress of the pressure hose on the retaining clamp inside the tank.

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Commonwealth authority) Notice 2004

**Notice No. 1 of 2004.**

I, KEVIN JAMES ANDREWS, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 30th June. 2004

Kevin Andrews

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of Commonwealth authority) Notice 2004*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on Gazettal.

###### 3 Declaration

For subparagraph 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (***the Act***), I declare that ARTC Services Company Pty Ltd (ACN 105 451 483) is a body corporate to which the Act applies under the definition of *Commonwealth authority* in Part I of the Act.

*Note* The body corporate is a body corporate in which a body corporate declared under paragraph (c) has a controlling interest.

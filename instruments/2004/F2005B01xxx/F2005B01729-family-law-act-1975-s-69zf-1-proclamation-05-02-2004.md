# Family Law Act 1975 s 69ZF(1) - Proclamation (05/02/2004)

Proclamation

*Family Law Act 1975*

I, PHILIP MICHAEL JEFFERY, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under subsection 69ZF (1) of the *Family Law Act 1975*, declare that all the child welfare law provisions of Part VII of that Act extend to Queensland.

Signed and sealed with the\
Great Seal of Australia\
on 5 February 2004

P. M. JEFFERY

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command

philip ruddock

Attorney-General

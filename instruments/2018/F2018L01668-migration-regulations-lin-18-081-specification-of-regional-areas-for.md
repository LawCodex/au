# Migration Regulations (LIN 18/081: Specification of Regional Areas for a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa) Instrument 2018

I, David Coleman, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 29/11/18

David Coleman

The Hon. David Coleman MP

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

**Contents**

**Part 1—Preliminary 1**

**Part 1 – Preliminary**

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration Regulations (LIN 18/081: Specification of Regional Areas for a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa) Instrument 2018*.

This instrument may be cited as LIN 18/081.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 1404(4) of Schedule 1 to the\
*Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Part 2 – Specified regional areas

###### 6 Specified regional areas

For the purposes of paragraph 1404(3)(e) of Schedule 1 to the Regulations and subparagraphs 2.06AAB(2)(a)(i) and (ii) of the Regulations, the following regional areas are specified:

in the Australian Capital Territory, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 1;

in New South Wales the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 2;

in the Northern Territory, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 3;

in Queensland, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 4;

in South Australia, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 5;

in Tasmania, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 6;

in Victoria, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 7;

in Western Australia, the areas identified by postcode listed in Schedule 8.

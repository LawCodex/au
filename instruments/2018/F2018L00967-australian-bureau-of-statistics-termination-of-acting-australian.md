# Australian Bureau of Statistics – Termination of Acting Australian Statistician – Appointment 2018

I, the Honourable Paul de Jersey AC QC, Administrator of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under subsection 15(1) of the *Australian Bureau of Statistics Act 1975*, terminate the appointment made on 2 March 2018 of Jonathan Palmer, Randall Brugeaud and Luise McCulloch to act in the office of Australian Statistician.

Dated: 29 June 2018

Paul de Jersey

Administrator

By His Excellency’s Command

Michael Sukkar

Assistant Minister to the Treasurer

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

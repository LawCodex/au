###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Defence Force Reparation Scheme Payment*** means a reparation payment made by the Secretary of the Defence Department as a result of a recommendation made by the Defence Force Ombudsman under section 14A or 14B of the *Ombudsman Regulations 2017*.

###### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a Defence Force Reparation Scheme Payment is an exempt lump sum.

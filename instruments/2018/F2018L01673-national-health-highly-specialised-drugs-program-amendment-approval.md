## Schedule 1 Amendments

***National Health (Highly specialised drugs program) Special Arrangement 2010***

**1 Section 4 (subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition of** ***approved hospital authority*****)**

Omit 'Chief Executive Medicare', substitute 'Secretary'.

**2 Section 4 (definition of** ***Regulations*****)**

Omit '*National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 1960*', substitute '*National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefit) Regulations 2017*'.

**3 Section 19 (including heading)**

Repeal the section, substitute:

**19 Application of section 51 of the Regulations**

Section 51 of the Regulations does not apply to the supply of HSD pharmaceutical benefits.

**4 Paragraph 21(a)**

Omit 'regulation 19', substitute 'section 40'.

**5 Paragraph 21(b)**

Omit 'regulation 19AA', substitute 'section 41'.

**6 Section 21 (note)**

Omit 'Regulation 13', substitute 'section 30'.

**7 Section 23**

Omit 'regulation 19', substitute 'section 40'.

**8 Section 26 (including heading)**

Repeal the section, substitute:

**26 Application of section 30 of the Regulations in relation to CAR drugs**

Section 30 of the Regulations does not apply in relation to a prescription for an HSD pharmaceutical benefit that has a CAR drug supplied under this Special Arrangement.

**9 Paragraph 51(b)**

Omit 'regulation 8A', substitute 'section 16'.

**10 Subsection 52(4)**

Omit 'subregulation 8A(1)', substitute 'subsection 16(4)'.

**11 Section 52**

Omit 'Chief Executive Medicare' (wherever appearing), substitute 'Secretary'.

**12 Subsection 54(4)**

Omit 'the Regulations', substitute 'the *National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 1960* as in force immediately before the commencement of the Regulations'.

## Schedule 2 Transitional provisions

**1 Definitions**

(1) In this Schedule:

***commencement day*** means the day on which the *National Health (Highly specialised drugs program) Amendment (Approval of certain hospital authorities) Special Arrangement 2018* commences.

***new Special Arrangement*** means the *National Health (Highly specialised drugs program) Special Arrangement 2010* as in force on the commencement day.

***old Special Arrangement*** means the *National Health (Highly specialised drugs program) Special Arrangement 2010* as in force immediately before the commencement day.

**2 Applications for approval of public hospital authority**

(1) This item applies where an application under section 52 of the old Special Arrangement for approval of the hospital authority of a public hospital has been made but a decision whether to approve the hospital authority has not been made before the commencement day.

(2) From the commencement day the decision may be made by:

(a) the Secretary, as if the application had been made to the Secretary; or

(b) the Chief Executive Medicare, as if the amendments made by the *National Health (Highly specialised drugs program) Amendment (Approval of certain hospital authorities) Special Arrangement 2018* had not commenced;

**3 Transition of approvals granted and things done by the Chief Executive Medicare**

(1) This item applies in relation to an approval for a hospital authority for a public hospital, granted under section 52 of the old Special Arrangement, that was in force immediately before the commencement day.

(2) From the commencement day:

(a) the approval is taken to have been granted by the Secretary;

(b) the conditions (if any) determined by the Chief Executive Medicare to which the approval was subject are taken to have been determined by the Secretary; and

(c) any number allotted to the approval by the Chief Executive Medicare is taken to have been allotted by the Secretary.

**4 Transition of suspensions issued by Chief Executive Medicare**

(1) This item applies where, immediately before the commencement day, the approval of the hospital authority for a public hospital is suspended following a notice issued by the Chief Executive Medicare under subsection 52(7) of the old Special Arrangement.

(2) The notice of suspension is taken to have been issued by the Secretary.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1157Q(4A) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Schedule – Revocation of previous Determination

Schedule 1 repeals the *Social Security (Loan Fringe Benefits) (Market Rate of Interest) Determination No. 1.*

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 6 Loan fringe benefits – market rate of interest

For subsection 1157Q(4A) of the Act, the following banks are specified:

(a) Australia and New Zealand Banking Group Limited;

(b) Commonwealth Bank of Australia;

(c) National Australia Bank Limited;

(d) Westpac Banking Corporation.

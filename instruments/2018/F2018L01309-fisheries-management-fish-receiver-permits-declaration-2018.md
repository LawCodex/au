# Fisheries Management (Fish Receiver Permits) Declaration 2018

I, Nick Rayns, Executive Manager Fisheries of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, make the following declaration.

Dated 14 September 2018

Nick Rayns

Executive Manager Fisheries

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fisheries Management (Fish Receiver Permits) Declaration 2018*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 91 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act, including the following:

(a) fishery;

(b) fishing permit.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

***Coral Sea Fishery*** means the fishery in the area described in Part 2 of Schedule 1A of the Regulations for which fishing permits have been granted under section 32 of the Act.

***Eastern Tuna and Billfish Fishery*** means the fishery described as the area of the fishery in Schedule 1 of the *Eastern Tuna and Billfish Management Plan 2010*.

***Norfolk Island Offshore Demersal Finfish Fishery*** means the fishery in the area described in Part 5A of Schedule 1A of the Regulations for which fishing permits have been granted under section 32 of the Act.

***Regulations*** means the *Fisheries Management Regulations 1992*.

***Small Pelagic Fishery*** means the fishery described as the sub-areas of the fishery in Schedule 1 of the *Small Pelagic Fishery Management Plan 2009*.

***Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery*** means the fishery described as the area of the fishery in Part 1 of Schedule 1 of the *Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Management Plan 2003*.

***Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery*** means the fishery described as the SBT Fishery in section 3.1 of the *Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery Management Plan 1995*.

***Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery*** means the fishery described as the area of the fishery for an Australian boat in section 3 of the *Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery Management Plan 2005*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Declaration of fisheries to which Division 2 of Part 6 of the Act applies

(1) It is declared that Division 2 of Part 6 of the Act applies to the fisheries listed by subsection (2).

(2) This subsection lists the following fisheries:

(a) the Coral Sea Fishery; and

(b) the Eastern Tuna and Billfish Fishery; and

(c) the Norfolk Island Offshore Demersal Finfish Fishery; and

(d) the Small Pelagic Fishery; and

(e) the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery; and

(f) the Southern Bluefin Tuna Fishery; and

(g) the Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery.

###### 3 Revocation

The Social Security (Crisis Payment - Qualifying Humanitarian Visas) Determination 2007 (No.1) is revoked.

###### 4 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1061JI(2) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Class of visas

For the purposes of paragraph 1061JI(1)(b) of the *Social Security Act 1991* the qualifying humanitarian visas are:

(a) Subclass 200 (Refugee);

(b) Subclass 201 (In-country Special Humanitarian);

(c) Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian);

(d) Subclass 203 (Emergency Rescue); and

(e) Subclass 204 (Woman at Risk).

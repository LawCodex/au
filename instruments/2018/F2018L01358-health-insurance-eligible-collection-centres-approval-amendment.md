# Health Insurance (Eligible Collection Centres) Approval Amendment (Duration of Approvals) Principles 2018

I, CELIA STREET, delegate of the Minister for Health, determine the following principles under subsection 23DNBA(4) of the *Health Insurance Act 1973.*

Dated 25 September 2018

Celia Street

Assistant Secretary\
Diagnostic Imaging and Pathology Branch\
Medical Benefits Division

Department of Health

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Health Insurance (Eligible Collection Centres) Approval Amendment (Duration of Approvals) Principles 2018.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 December 2018.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 23DNBA(4) of the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance (Eligible Collection Centres) Approval Principles 2010

**1 Subsection 6(3)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(3) An approval granted in respect of an application must be expressed to be valid for two years, inclusive of the day of commencement of the approval.

Note: An approval may be revoked or cancelled under the Act.

**2 Subsection 6(3A) (including note)**

Repeal the subsection.

**3 Subsection 6(5)**

Repeal the subsection.

**4 Part 4 Transitional**

Repeal the Part.

## Schedule 2—Application

**1 Application**

(1) Despite the amendments made by Schedule 1, section 6 of the *Health Insurance (Eligible Collection Centres) Approval Principles 2010*, as in force immediately before the commencement of Schedule 1, continues to apply in relation to an application for approval made before that date.

(2) For subsection (1):

(a) an application made online is taken to be made at the time the applicant uploads it to the website specified in the prescribed form;

(b) an application sent by post is taken to be made at the time the applicant posts it to the address specified in the prescribed form; and

(c) an application sent by facsimile is taken to be made at the time the applicant dispatches it to the facsimile number specified in the prescribed form.

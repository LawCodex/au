# PGPA Act Determination (Home Affairs SOETM Special Account 2018)

### Part 1—Establishment

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *PGPA Act Determination (Home Affairs SOETM Special Account 2018)*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 78(1) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013*.

***Home Affairs*** means the Department of Home Affairs.

***SOETM*** means Services for Other Entities and Trust Moneys

***the special account*** means the *Home Affairs SOETM Special Account 2018*.

###### 5 Establishment of the special account

For subsection 78(1)(a) of the Act, the *Home Affairs SOETM Special Account 2018* is established as a special account (***the special account***).

###### 6 Accountable authority responsible for the special account

For subsection 78(1)(d) of the Act, the accountable authority responsible for the special account is the Secretary of Home Affairs.

### Part 2—Special account credits and debits

###### 7 Amounts that may be credited to the special account

For subsection 78(1)(b) of the Act, amounts up to and equal to the following may be credited to the special account:

(a) an amount that is seized, found or otherwise comes under the control of the Home Affairs in accordance with an Act or other law;

(b) an amount forfeited to the Commonwealth as a result of a court order;

(c) an amount received from another Commonwealth entity, Commonwealth company, government, organisation or person, in order to carry out joint activities;

(d) an amount received in the course of the performance of functions that relates to a purpose of the special account in clause 8;

(e) an amount received from any person for a purpose of the special account in clause 8;

(f) an amount appropriated by the Australian Parliament in relation to a purpose in clause 8.

###### 8 Purposes of the special account

For subsection 78(1)(c) of the Act, an amount may be debited from the special account for one of the following purposes:

(a) to disburse an amount held on trust or otherwise for the benefit of a person other than the Commonwealth;

(b) to disburse an amount in connection with joint activities performed on behalf of another Commonwealth entity, Commonwealth company, government, organisation or person;

(c) to disburse an amount in connection with an agreement between the Commonwealth and another government;

(d) to repay an amount where an Act, other law, or court order requires or permits the repayment of an amount credited to the special account;

(e) to reduce the balance of the special account (and, therefore, the available appropriation for the special account) without making a real or notional payment.

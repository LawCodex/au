# Social Services Laws (Present Value of Unpaid Amount –Interest Rate) Determination 2018

Social Services Laws (Present Value of Unpaid Amount – Interest Rate) Determination 2018

I, Dan Tehan, Minister for Social Services, make the following Determination.

Dated 27 February 2018

Dan Tehan

Minister for Social Services

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Social Services Laws (Present Value of Unpaid Amount –Interest Rate) Determination 2018.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on 1 March 2018.

###### 3 Authority for this Determination

This Determination, other than Schedule 1, is made under:

section 1237AAB of the *Social Security Act 1991*;

section 43E of the *Student Assistance Act 1973*; and

section 100 of the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999.*

###### 4 Authority for Schedule 1 to this Determination

Schedule 1 to this Determination is made under section 198 of the *Paid Parental Leave Act 2010*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Annual interest rate

For the definition of ***interest*** in the subsections specified in subsection (2), the specified annual rate of interest is 5%.

The following subsections are specified:

subsection 1237AAB(6) of the *Social Security Act 1991;*

subsection 43E(6) of the *Student Assistance Act 1973*;

subsection 100(6) of the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999.*

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Paid Parental Leave Rules 2010

**1 At the end of Division 4.3.2**

Add:

## Division 4.3.3 Waiver of debts – settlement of civil actions

## 4.22 Settlement interest

For the definition of ***settlement interest*** in subsection 198(6) of the Act, the prescribed annual rate of interest is 5%.

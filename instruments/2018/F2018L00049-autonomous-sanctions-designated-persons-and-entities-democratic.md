# Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2018

I, JULIE BISHOP, Minister for Foreign Affairs, make the following legislative instrument.

Dated 15 January 2018

JULIE BISHOP

Minister for Foreign Affairs

Contents

#### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2018.*

#### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

#### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*.

#### 4 Designation declared to continue to have effect (person)

For subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, the person listed in Schedule 1 is a person that the Minister is satisfied is, or has been, associated with the DPRK’s weapons of mass destruction program or missiles program, and the person’s designation as a designated person in Schedule 1, Part 1 of the *Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) List 2012* is declared to continue to have effect.

#### 5 Designations and declarations declared to continue to have effect (persons)

For subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, each of the persons listed in Schedule 2 is a person that the Minister is satisfied is, or has been, associated with the DPRK’s weapons of mass destruction program or missiles program, and each person’s designation as a designated person and declaration as a declared person in Schedule 1, Part 2 of the *Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) List 2012* is declared to continue to have effect.

#### 6 Designations declared to continue to have effect (entities)

For subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, each of the entities listed in Schedule 3 is an entity that the Minister is satisfied is mentioned in Item 1 of the table in subregulation 6(1) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, and each entity’s designation as a designated entity in Schedule 1, Part 3 of the *Autonomous Sanctions (Designated Persons and Entities – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) List 2012* is declared to continue to have effect.

Note: Subregulation 9(1) and 9(2) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011* (the Regulations) provide that designations under paragraph 6(1)(a) of the Regulations and declarations under paragraph 6(1)(b) of the Regulations cease to have effect on the third anniversary of the day on which the designation or declaration took effect (if no declaration under subregulation 9(3) has been made in relation to the designation or declaration) or, otherwise, the third anniversary of the making of the most recent declaration in relation to the designation or declaration.

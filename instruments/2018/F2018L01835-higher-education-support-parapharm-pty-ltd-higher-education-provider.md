# Higher Education Support (Parapharm Pty Ltd) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018

Higher Education Support (Parapharm Pty Ltd)

Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018

I, Drew Menzies-McVey, delegate of the Minister for Education and Training, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 20 December 2018

Drew Menzies-McVey

Branch Manager

HELP and Provider Integrity Branch

Higher Education Group

Department of Education and Training\
Delegate of the Minister for Education and Training

###### 1 Name

This is the Higher Education Support (Parapharm Pty Ltd) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered in the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument **Act** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval as a Higher Education Provider

The approval of Parapharm Pty Ltd (ACN: 098564099, ABN: 45098564099) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act.

The legislative instrument *Higher Education Approval No.2 of 2014* (F2014L00373) is repealed.

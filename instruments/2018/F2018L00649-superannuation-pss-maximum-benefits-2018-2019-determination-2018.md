# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2018-2019) Determination 2018

**Superannuation (PSS)**

**Maximum Benefits (2018-2019)**

**Determination 2018**

I, STEPHEN PAUL WHITTON, Senior Manager, Quality, Knowledge and Change, Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 16 May 2018

S WHITTON

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

**1 Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2018-2019) Determination 2018*.

**2 Commencement**

This determination takes effect on 1 July 2018.

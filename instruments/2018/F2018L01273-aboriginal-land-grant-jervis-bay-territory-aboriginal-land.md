###### 3 Authority

This declaration is made under subsection 9(1) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The expression ***Aboriginal Land*** used in this declaration is defined in section 2 of the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986*.

###### 5 Declaration of Aboriginal Land

The land described in Schedule 1 is Aboriginal Land.

Note: For paragraph 9(1)(a) of the Act, the land described in Schedule 1 is vacant Crown land in the Jervis Bay Territory that adjoins Aboriginal Land.

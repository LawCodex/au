**1 Name**

#### This instrument is the Veterans' Entitlements (Veterans’ Children Education Scheme - Long Tan Bursary) Determination 2018.

**2 Commencement**

#### This instrument commences immediately after the commencement of Schedule 4 to the Veterans’ Affairs Legislation Amendment (Veteran-centric Reforms No. 2) Act 2018.

#### Note: Schedule 4 to the Veterans’ Affairs Legislation Amendment (Veteran-centric Reforms No. 2) Act 2018 commences on 1 July 2019.

**3 Authority**

#### This instrument is made under subsection 116CA(1) of the Act.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986.*

***eligible grandchild of a Vietnam veteran*** – see subsection 116(1) of the *Act*.

***grandchild*** of a veteran – see subsection 116(1) of the *Act*.

***veteran*** – see paragraph (a) of the definition of “veteran” in subsection 5C(1) of the *Act*.

***Vietnam service*** – see subsection 116(1) of the *Act*.

**5 Purpose**

#### The purpose of this instrument is to determine a class of persons for the purposes of the definition of eligible grandchild of a Vietnam veteran in subsection 116(1) of the Act.

*Note 1:* A person will be an “*eligible grandchild of a Vietnam veteran*” as defined in subsection 116(1) of the *Act* if the Repatriation Commission determines, under subsection 116CC(2) of the *Act,* that the person is included in the class determined by this instrument.

*Note 2:* A person who is an “*eligible grandchild of a Vietnam veteran*” may qualify for a Long Tan Bursary – see paragraph 7.3.2 of the Veterans’ Children Education Scheme (Instrument 2015 No. R43).

**6 Determined classes of persons**

#### For subsection 116CA(1) of the Act, the following class of persons is determined:

A person:

(a) who is a *grandchild* of a *veteran* who has rendered *Vietnam service*; and

(b) who is making the transition to tertiary study and is academically able to complete an undergraduate degree (or similar tertiary course of study); and

(c) whose family circumstances are such that the person may require additional financial assistance.

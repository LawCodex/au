# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility —DHL Express (Australia) Pty Ltd) Declaration 2018

Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility—DHL Express (Australia) Pty Ltd) Declaration 2018

I, CRAIG LAUNDY, Minister for Small and Family Business, the Workplace and Deregulation, make the following declaration.

Dated 19th March 2018

CRAIG LAUNDY

Minister for Small and Family Business, the Workplace and Deregulation

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Licence Eligibility —DHL Express (Australia) Pty Ltd) Declaration 2018*.

###### 2 Commencement

###### This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 100 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

***DHL Express*** means DHL Express (Australia) Pty Ltd (ACN 001 112 929).

**5 Declaration**

Being satisfied that it would be desirable for the Act to apply to employees of DHL Express, I declare that DHL Express is eligible to be granted a licence under Part VIII of the Act.

*Note*: DHL Express is a corporation that is carrying on business in competition with a Commonwealth authority or with another corporation that was formerly a Commonwealth authority.

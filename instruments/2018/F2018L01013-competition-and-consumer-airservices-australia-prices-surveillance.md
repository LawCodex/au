# Competition and Consumer (Airservices Australia Prices Surveillance) Declaration 2018

I, Scott Morrison, Treasurer, make the following declaration.

Dated 4 July 2018

The Hon Scott Morrison MP

Treasurer

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Competition and Consumer (Airservices Australia Prices Surveillance) Declaration 2018*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 95X(1) and (2) of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*.

***Airservices Australia*** means the body called Airservices Australia established by subsection 7(1) of the *Air Services Act 1995*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Notified Services

(1) For the purposes of Part VIIA of the Act, services of the following kinds are notified services if a charge for the services has been set in a determination that is made under section 53 of the *Air Services Act 1995* and in force at the time the services are supplied:

(a) terminal navigation;

(b) fire fighting and rescue;

(c) en route air route and airway facilities; and

(d) other safety related activities.

###### 7 Declared Persons

(1) For the purposes of Part VIIA of the Act, Airservices Australia is a declared person in relation to services that are notified services under section 6.

(2) The declaration in subsection (1) ceases to have effect when this instrument is repealed.

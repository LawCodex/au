# Higher Education Support (Churchill Education Pty Ltd as trustee for Decelle Investment Trust, ACN 120 692 159) VET Provider Approval Revocation 2018

Higher Education Support (Churchill Education Pty Ltd as trustee for Decelle Investment Trust) VET Provider Approval Revocation 2018

I, Shelley Owen, delegate of the Minister for Small and Family Business, Skills and Vocational Education, make this instrument under clause 39 of Schedule 1A to the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 22 November 2018.

Shelley Owen

Director\
VET Student Loans

Delegate of the Minister

###### 1 Name

This is the *Higher Education Support (Churchill Education Pty Ltd as trustee for Decelle Investment Trust, ACN 120 692 159) VET Provider Approval Revocation 2018.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under clause 39 of Schedule 1A to the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

***body*** means Churchill Education Pty Ltd as trustee for Decelle Investment Trust, ACN 120 692 159.

Note: The terms ***VET Guidelines*** and ***VET provider*** have the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of revocation of approval as a VET provider

The body’s approval as a VET provider is revoked in accordance with subclause 39(1) of Schedule 1A to the Act subject to subclause 39(2A) of Schedule 1A to the Act.

The *Higher Education Support Act 2003 - VET Provider Approval (No. 46 of 2013)* is revoked.

**6 Provisions of Act and VET Guidelines which continue to apply**

For the purposes of subparagraph 39(2A)(b)(ii) of Schedule 1A to the Act, the following provisions of the Act and VET Guidelines continue to apply to the body as if the body were still approved as a VET provider:

(1) clauses 23, 26, 46, 48-51 and 56 of Schedule 1A to the Act; and

(2) sections 31(3), 33, 39, 42, 43 and 65 of the VET Guidelines.

Note: Paragraph 39(2A)(a) of Schedule 1A to the Act also provides that, after the body’s revocation, clauses 45E and 46B of Schedule 1A to the Act continue to apply to the body as if the body were still approved as a VET provider.

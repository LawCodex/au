# Higher Education SupportHigher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018

Higher Education Support (Harvest Bible College Inc)

Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018

I, Damian Coburn, delegate of the Minister for Education and Training, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 23 August 2018

Damian Coburn

Branch Manager

Student Information and Learning Branch

Higher Education Group

Department of Education and Training\
Delegate of the Minister for Education and Training

###### 1 Name

This is the *Higher Education SupportHigher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2018* .

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered in the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument **Act** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval as a Higher Education Provider

The approval of Harvest Bible College Inc (ABN: 97 621 239 356) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection

22-2(1) of the Act.

# Research Involving Human Embryos (Corresponding State Law—QLD) Declaration 2018

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Research Involving Human Embryos Act 2002***

***Subsection 7(1)***

**Research Involving Human Embryos (Corresponding State Law—QLD) Declaration 2018**

I, Greg Hunt, Minister for Health, under subsection 7(1) of *the Research Involving Human Embryos Act 2002* (‘the Commonwealth Act’), declare the *Research Involving Human Embryos and Prohibition of Human Cloning for Reproduction Act 2003* of Queensland to be a corresponding State law for the purposes of the Commonwealth Act.

This Instrument commences the day after registration.

Dated 26 September 2018

…………………………………………………………………………….

Greg Hunt

Minister for Health

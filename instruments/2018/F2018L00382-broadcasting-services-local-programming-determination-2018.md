# Broadcasting Services Local Programming Determination 2018

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following determination under subsection 61CZ(1) of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

Dated: 22 March 2018

###### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This is the *Broadcasting Services Local Programming Determination 2018*.

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences at the start of whichever is the later of the following days:

(a) the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation; or

(b) 17 April 2018.

Note 1: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

Note 2: Both of the events referred to in this section must occur before this determination commences.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under subsection 61CZ(1) of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this determination:

***Act*** means *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

***file footage*** means any footage other than:

(a) live footage; or

(b) footage that has been specifically obtained for the purpose of accompanying the broadcast of the news material in question.

***licensee*** means, for a licence to which this determination relates, the holder of the licence.

Note: A number of other expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) licence area;

(b) licence area plan;

(c) regional aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence;

(d) regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence; and

(e) timing period.

(See sections 6 and 61CU of the Act).

###### 5 References to other instruments

In this determination, unless the contrary intention appears:

(a) a reference to any other legislative instrument is a reference to that other legislative instrument as in force from time to time; and

(b) a reference to any other kind of instrument is a reference to that other instrument as in force at the commencement of this determination.

Note 1: For references to Commonwealth Acts, see section 10 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*; and see also subsection 13(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003* for the application of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* to legislative instruments.

Note 2: All Commonwealth Acts and legislative instruments are registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### Part 2—Material of local significance

###### 6 Material of local significance

(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), material that is broadcast to a local area is material of local significance if it relates directly to:

(a) the local area; or

(b) the licensee’s licence area.

Note: Material of local significance may accumulate 1, 2 or 3 points per minute, depending on the characteristics of the material. See subsection 61CY(3) if the Act and section 9 of this determination.

(2) Material in the form of advertising or sponsorship matter, whether or not of a commercial kind, is not material of local significance unless it is a community service announcement.

Note: Subsections 61CY(7) and (8) of the Act limit the way community service announcements may be counted towards points accumulated for a licence during a timing period.

(3) Material (including a station promotion) that constitutes an announcement that material of local significance will appear later is not material of local significance.

###### 8 Local area for regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licences

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the local area for a regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence is the area identical to the licence area for that licence.

Note 1: The licence area for a regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence is set out in the relevant licence area plan made under subsection 26(1B) of the Act.

Note 2: The licence area for a regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence covered by subsection (1) is, at the time of making this instrument, set out in whichever is applicable of the following instruments:

*Television Licence Area Plan (Broken Hill) 2011*

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a regional non-aggregated commercial television broadcasting licence for the licence area Remote and Regional WA TV1. The local area for such a licence is the combined area of the following licence areas:

(a) Geraldton TV1;

(b) Kalgoorlie TV1; and

(c) South West and Great Southern TV1.

###### Part 4—Additional requirements for certain material of local significance

###### 9 Additional requirements to accumulate 3 points per minute

For the purposes of paragraph (d) of item 1 in the table in subsection 61CY(3) of the Act, the following additional requirements are specified:

(a) the news item must relate directly to the local area; and

(b) the depiction of people, places or things must predominantly consist of moving images; and

(c) when considering the news item as a whole, file footage, if used, must:

(i) not be the only kind of footage used;

(ii) be visually relevant to the news item;

(iii) be recently obtained or provide a different perspective to the news item; and

(iv) not be extensively used unless it is directly relevant to the news item.

# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 1 of 2018

**Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 1 of 2018**

Provision of statistical information about financial sector entities to the Reserve Bank of Australia and the Australian Bureau of Statistics during the 2017-18 financial year.

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Steve Matthews, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 51(1)(a) of the *Australian*

*Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998,* fix the charges specified in the attached Schedule of charges in respect of the specified services and facilities provided by APRA.

This instrument commences on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation under the *Legislation Act 2003.*

Dated: 16 May 2018

[Signed]

Steve Matthews

Executive General Manager

Interpretation

In this instrument

***ABS*** means the Australian Bureau of Statistics.

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***Financial sector entity*** has the meaning in section 5 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***RBA*** means the Reserve Bank of Australia.

## Schedule of charges

| **Column 1**<br>**Services for which the charge is imposed** | **Column 2**<br>**Amount of the charge** | **Column 3**<br>**Person required to pay the charge** | **Column 4**<br>**When the charge is to be paid** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provision to the ABS during the 2017 – 18 financial year of the statistical information specified in Schedule<br>1 concerning financial sector entities. | $319,949 | ABS | Twenty-eight days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |
| Provision to the RBA during the 2017 – 18 financial year of the statistical information specified in Schedule<br>2 concerning financial sector entities. | $74,959 | RBA | Twenty-eight days after receipt of APRA’s invoice for the charge. |

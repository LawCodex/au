# Child Care Subsidy (Transition of approved child care services) Determination 2018

I, Dr Michele Bruniges AM, Secretary of the Department of Education and Training, make this instrument under subitem 9(2) of Schedule 4 to the *Family Assistance Legislation Amendment (Jobs for Families Child Care Package) Act 2017.*

Dated 9 June 2018

Dr Michele Bruniges AM

Secretary, Department of Education and Training

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Child Care Subsidy (Transition of approved child care services) Determination 2018.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 2 July 2018.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 9(2) of Schedule 4 to the *Family Assistance Legislation Amendment (Jobs for Families Child Care Package) Act 2017*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999.*

*Note 1* The following expressions are defined in the Act immediately before commencement day:

approved centre based long day care service

approved child care service

approved family day care service

approved in-home care service

approved occasional care service

approved outside school hours care service

*Note 2* The following expressions are defined in the Act on and from commencement day:

approved child care service

approved provider

*Note 3* ***commencement day*** has the same meaning as in Schedule 4 to the *Family Assistance Legislation Amendment (Jobs for Families Child Care Package) Act 2017* (being 2 July 2018).

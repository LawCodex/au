# Australian Education (Financial Assistance for Non-participating States and Territories) Determination 2018

*Australian Education Act 2013*

I, DAN TEHAN, Minister for Education, pursuant to item 44 of Schedule 1 to the *Australian Education Amendment Act 2017*, make the following determination in relation to financial assistance for the year 2017 under subsections 65(3) and 65(7) of the *Australian Education Act 2013* (as in force immediately before 1 January 2018)

Dated 6 December 2018

DAN TEHAN

Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Australian Education (Financial Assistance for Non-participating States and Territories) Determination 2018.*

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made, pursuant to item 44 of Schedule 1 to the *Australian Education Amendment Act 2017*, under subsections 65(3) and 65(7) of the *Australian Education Act 2013* as in force immediately before 1 January 2018*.*

###### 4 Definition

In this determination:

***the Act*** means the *Australian Education Act 2013* as in force immediately before 1 January 2018, being the *Australian Education Act 2013* (Compilation No.5) (Federal Register of Legislation reference no. C2017C00205).

###### 6 Total amount

For subsection 65(3) of the Act, the total amount of financial assistance that is payable to all non-participating States and Territories for 2017 for the non-participating schools located in those States and Territories under Part 4 of the Act is $4,082,663,937.

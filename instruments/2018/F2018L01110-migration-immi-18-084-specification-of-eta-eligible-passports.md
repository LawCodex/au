# Migration (IMMI 18/084: Specification of ETA-Eligible Passports) Instrument 2018

IMMI 18/084

Migration (IMMI 18/084: Specification of ETA-Eligible Passports) Instrument 2018

made under the *Migration Regulations 1994*

Compilation no. 1

Compilation date 20 February 2022

This compilation was prepared by the Department of Home Affairs on 20 February 2022 taking into account amendments up to *Migration (Specification of ETA-Eligible Passports) Amendment Instrument (LIN 22/040) 2022.*

**Contents**

Part 1 – Preliminary

1 Name

(1) This instrument is the *Migration (IMMI 18/084: Specification of ETA-Eligible Passports) Instrument 2018*.

(2) This instrument may be cited as IMMI 18/084.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under regulation 1.11B(1) of the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

4 Definitions

In this instrument:

ETA means Electronic Travel Authority.

Regulations means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

Part 2 – Specified ETA-Eligible Passports

6 ETA- Eligible Passports

For the purposes of subregulation 1.11B(1) of the Regulations, a passport is an ETA-eligible passport in relation to an application for a visa if in the table in Schedule 1:

(a) the passport is listed in Column 1; and

(b) the conditions specified in Column 2 for the corresponding passport specified in Column 1 are satisfied.

Part 3 – Application

7 Application of this instrument

This instrument applies to an application for a visa made on or after the commencement of this instrument.

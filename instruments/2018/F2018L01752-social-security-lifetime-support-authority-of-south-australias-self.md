**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Personal care support scheme

The scheme for the provision of personal care support known as the *Lifetime Support Authority of South Australia’s Self-Directed Supports Program*, established under the *Motor Vehicle Accidents (Lifetime Support Scheme) Act 2013* (SA), is determined to be an approved scheme for the purposes of paragraph 8(8)(zi) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

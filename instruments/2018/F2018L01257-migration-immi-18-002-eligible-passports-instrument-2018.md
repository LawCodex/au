###### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (IMMI 18/002: Eligible Passports) Instrument 2018*.

This instrument may be cited as IMMI 18/002.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 175A of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expression used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the term *passport*.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means *Migration Act 1958*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### Part 2—Determination of Eligible Passports

###### 6 Eligible passports for section 165 of the Act

Determine that for the purposes of the definition of ‘eligible passport’ in section 165 of the Act, all valid passports are ‘eligible passports’ with the exception of the following specified travel documents:

travel documents with certain characteristics specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument; and

travel documents specified in Schedule 2 to this instrument.

## Schedule 1 – Travel documents with certain characteristics that are not ‘eligible passports’ for section 165 of the Act

Group passports (eg. a passport issued to a sporting team) other than a family;

Travel document or passport which has been damaged, accidentally or otherwise, in such a way that the name, the photograph, the personal details or other important information have been obscured or pages are missing or where it is not possible to confirm conclusively the holder’s identity because of such damage;

In the case of permanent visas, travel document or passport endorsed by the issuing authority to the effect that it is not valid for migration;

In the case of temporary visas, travel document or passport which is not endorsed, or is not accompanied by separate evidence of, a right of re-entry to the issuing country or another country;

Travel document or passport where endorsement of the document would indicate recognition of the issuing authority contrary to Australian Government foreign policy; or

Travel document or passport issued by an entity which is not recognised by the Australian Government as empowered to issue travel documents.

# Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Extended operation of the Act in relation to Custom Bus Australia Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 01/2018

**Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Extended operation of the Act in relation to Custom Bus Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 01/2018**

*Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012*, section 49

I, CRAIG LAUNDY, Minister for Small and Family Business, the Workplace and Deregulation, make this declaration under section 49 of the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012*.

Dated on the 6th day of April 2018.

Craig Laundy

Minister for Small and Family Business, the Workplace and Deregulation

**1. Name of declaration**

This declaration is the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Extended operation of the Act in relation to Custom Bus Australia Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 01/2018*.

**2. Commencement**

This declaration commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

**3. Declaration**

The *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012* applies in relation to persons who were employed, but are no longer employed, by Custom Bus Australia Pty Ltd which is an entity in administration under the *Corporations Act 2001*.

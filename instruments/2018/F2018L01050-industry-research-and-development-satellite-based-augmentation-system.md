###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 5 Prescribed program

For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the program specified in item 1 of the following table is prescribed.

<table>
<tr>
<td colspan="3">Prescribed program</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Item</td>
<td>Column 1<br>Program</td>
<td>Column 2<br>Description and purpose of the program</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>1</td>
<td>Satellite‑Based Augmentation System Program</td>
<td>The program provides funding for a Satellite‑Based Augmentation System (SBAS). SBAS is an international standard used to deliver precise and high‑integrity positioning, navigation and timing (PNT) data via satellite. The SBAS will improve and augment the accuracy, integrity and availability of basic Global Navigation Satellite System signals by delivering precise and high‑integrity PNT data.</td>
</tr>
</table>

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the power of the Parliament to make laws with respect to postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution) is specified.

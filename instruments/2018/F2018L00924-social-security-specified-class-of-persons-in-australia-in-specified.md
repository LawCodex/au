###### 4. Interpretation

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991;*

***Health care card income test*** has the meaning given by section 1071A of the Act.

### Part 2 Specified class of persons in Australia in specified circumstances

###### 5. Qualification for a health care card

###### (1) A person who is the holder of any of a:

(a) subclass 060 (Bridging F) visa;

(b) subclass 070 (Bridging (Removal Pending)) visa;

(c) subclass 309 (Partner (Provisional)) visa;

(d) subclass 449 (Humanitarian Stay (Temporary)) visa;

(e) subclass 785 (Temporary Protection) visa;

(f) subclass 786 ((Temporary) Humanitarian Concern) visa;

(g) subclass 790 (Safe Haven Enterprise) visa;

(h) subclass 820 (Partner) visa; and

###### (2) satisfies the health care card income test; and

###### (3) is, or has been, in Australia as the holder of a relevant visa

###### is declared to be a person to whom section 1061ZO of the Act applies.

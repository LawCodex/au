###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) (Modifications of the Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme) Instrument 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2019.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 20 of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019.*

###### 4 Schedule—Modifications of the Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

The *Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme* is modified as set out in the Schedule to this instrument for the purposes of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019*.

## Schedule—Modifications of the Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

##### Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

**1 Sections 1A to 2**

Substitute:

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Scheme*.

###### 2 Authorisation of Scheme

The *Scheme* is authorised by section 20 of the *Act*.

###### 2A Purpose of Scheme

The *Scheme* enables *Community Pharmacists* to supply *Pharmaceutical benefits* to *Eligible Persons*.

**2 Section 3 (definition of Act)**

Substitute:

***Act*** means the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019*;

**3 Section 3 (definition of accepted disability)**

Omit the definition.

**4 Section 3 (definition of Eligible Person)**

Substitute:

***Eligible Person*** means an eligible person within the meaning of the *Act*;

**5 Section 3**

Omit the following definitions:

(a) definition of ***income support payment”***;

(c) definition of ***MRCA supplement***;

**6 Section 3 (definition of Repatriation Health Card ‑ For All Conditions)**

Substitute:

***Repatriation Health Card ‑ For All Conditions*** means an identification card, or written authorisation, provided by the Department of Veterans’ Affairs to an *Eligible Person* in relation to treatment for all injuries or diseases;

# Approval of a means of compliance with Transport Canada Airworthiness Directive (AD) CF-2014-41 – Fuel Spray Nozzle and Manifold Assembly Configuration

## Instrument Number AEB 19/8295

I, Pieter van Dijk, Manager Continued Operational Safety, a delegate of the Civil Aviation Safety Authority, make this instrument under paragraph 39.004(3)(a) of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

Pieter van Dijk

Manager Continued Operational Safety

20 August 2019

Approval of a means of compliance with Transport Canada Airworthiness Directive (AD) CF-2014-41 – Fuel Spray Nozzle and Manifold Assembly Configuration

Duration

This instrument:

(a) commences on the date of registration; and

(b) ceases to have effect if Transport Canada AD CF-2014-41 (the AD) is amended.

2\. Application

This instrument applies to a registered operator of an aircraft fitted with a Pratt & Whitney Canada PW100 series engine mentioned in the AD, as in force at the date of this instrument.

3\. Approval

I approve, as an acceptable means of compliance with the AD, the modification of the fuel spray nozzle manifold installation in accordance with Pratt & Whitney Canada service bulletin PW100-72-21891 as in force from time to time.

**Note**: The words “as in force from time to time” require compliance with the most recent version of the referenced document.

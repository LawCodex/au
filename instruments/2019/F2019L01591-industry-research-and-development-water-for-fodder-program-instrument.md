###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***Desertification Convention*** means the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification in those Countries Experiencing Serious Drought and/or Desertification, particularly in Africa, done at Paris on 17 June 1994.

Note: The Convention is in Australian Treaty Series 2000 No. 18 ([2000] ATS 18) and could in 2019 be viewed in the Australian Treaties Library on the AustLII website (http://www.austlii.edu.au).

***program*** means the Water for Fodder Program.

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the program is prescribed.

(2) The program is for the administration by the Commonwealth of the sale of water allocations by South Australia to drought‑affected farmers in the southern Murray‑Darling Basin, including by:

(a) entering into arrangements or understandings with such farmers, including enforceable undertakings provided by such farmers in favour of the Commonwealth (whether or not provided under a deed poll); and

(b) enforcing or otherwise giving effect to such arrangements, understandings or enforceable undertakings.

(3) The purpose of the program is to provide support to drought‑affected farmers in the southern Murray‑Darling Basin by assisting them to produce fodder.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the power of the Parliament to make laws with respect to external affairs (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xxix) of the Constitution) is specified, as it relates to measures to give effect to Australia’s obligations under the Desertification Convention, particularly Articles 4 and 5.

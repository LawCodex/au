### Part 2—Determination of GST revenue sharing relativities for 2019‑20

###### 5 GST revenue sharing relativities for the 2019-20 payment year

For the purposes of section 8 of the Act, each item of the following table sets out a factor that is the GST revenue sharing relativity, for the 2019-20 payment year, for the State specified in the item:

| Item | For this State: | The GST revenue sharing relativity for the 2019‑20 payment year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | 0.87013 |
| 2 | Victoria | 0.98273 |
| 3 | Queensland | 1.05370 |
| 4 | Western Australia | 0.51842 |
| 5 | South Australia | 1.46552 |
| 6 | Tasmania | 1.75576 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | 1.23759 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | 4.26735 |

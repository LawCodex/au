# Social Security (Financial Investment) (Requirements for Annuity Contracts) Determination 2019

**Social Security (Financial Investment) (Requirements for Annuity Contracts) Determination 2019**

I, Paul Fletcher, Minister for Families and Social Services, make the following Determination.

Dated 16 March 2019

Paul Fletcher

Minister for Families and Social Services

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Social Security (Financial Investment) (Requirements for Annuity Contracts) Determination 2019.*

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 9(1EA) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***SIS Regulations*** means the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Regulations 1994.*

**5 Requirements**

For paragraph (j) of the definition of financial investment in subsection 9(1) of the Act, the requirements are the standards mentioned in:

(a) subregulation 1.05(4) of the SIS Regulations; or

(b) subregulation 1.05(11A) of the SIS Regulations.

Note: If an asset-tested income stream (long term) that is an annuity (within the meaning of *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*) is provided under a contract that meets the requirements in either paragraph 5(a) or (b), that asset-tested income stream (long term) is a financial investment under subsection 9(1) of the Act.

# Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement 2019 (No.1)

*Fair Work Act 2009*

I, Kelly O’Dwyer, Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations, make this Endorsement under paragraph 14(4)(a) of the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

Dated 23 January 2019

Kelly O’Dwyer

Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations

###### 1 Name of Endorsement

This Endorsement is the *Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement 2019 (No.1)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Endorsement commences on 1 March 2019.

###### 3 Definition

In this Endorsement:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

###### 4 Endorsement of State Declaration

For paragraph 14(4)(a) of the Act, a declaration as in force on 21 December 2018 under the *Industrial Relations Act 1996* (NSW) that an employer mentioned in Schedule 1 is not to be a national system employer for the purposes of the Act, is endorsed.

## Schedule 1 Employer declared by or under a law of New South Wales not to be a national system employer

(section 4)

Employer

Far North West Joint Organisation

Far South West Joint Organisation

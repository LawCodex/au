# Telecommunications (Revocation of International Mobile Roaming Industry Standard) Direction 2019

I, PAUL WILLIAM FLETCHER, Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts, issue the following Direction under subsection 125AA(4) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

Dated 26 November 2019

PAUL FLETCHER

Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts

Contents

###### 1 Name

This Direction is the *Telecommunications (Revocation of International Mobile Roaming Industry Standard) Direction 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Direction commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Repeal of this Direction

This Direction is repealed on the day after the Standard referred to in section 6 of this instrument is revoked.

###### 4 Authority

This Direction is made under subsection 125AA(4) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 5 Definitions

In this Direction:

***ACMA*** means the Australian Communications and Media Authority.

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***Standard*** means the *Telecommunications (International Mobile Roaming) Industry Standard 2013.*

###### 6 Direction

(1) I direct the ACMA to revoke the Standard made under subsection 125AA(1) of the Act.

(2) The Standard is to be revoked no later than 9 months after the date this Direction commences.

# Migration (LIN 19/214: Regional Certifying Bodies) Instrument 2019

I, David Coleman, Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 8 November 2019

David Coleman

Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (LIN 19/214: Regional Certifying Bodies) Instrument 2019.*

This instrument may be cited as LIN 19/214.

###### 2 Commencement

The whole of this instrument commences at the same time as Schedule 2 to the *Migration Amendment (New Skilled Regional Visas) Regulations 2019*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subregulation 2.72C(20) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations).

###### 4 Regional Certifying Bodies

For the purposes of paragraph 2.72C(19)(a) of the Regulations, each body listed in a table in subsection (3) is specified as a Regional Certifying Body.

A Regional Certifying Body listed in subsection (3) has responsibility for a part of Australia that is a designated regional area listed in an instrument in writing made under the Regulations.

###### 5 Application

This instrument applies in relation to the following:

an application for approval of a nomination for a Subclass 494 (Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional)) visa made on or after 16 November 2019; and

an application for a Subclass 494 (Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional)) visa made on or after 16 November 2019, if the related application for approval of a nomination is made on or after that date.

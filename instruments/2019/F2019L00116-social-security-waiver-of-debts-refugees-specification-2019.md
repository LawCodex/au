# Social Security (Waiver of Debts – Refugees) Specification 2019

Social Security (Waiver of Debts - Refugees) Specification 2019

I, Paul Fletcher, Minister for Families and Social Services, make the following Specification.

Dated 2 February 2019

Paul Fletcher

Minister for Families and Social Services

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Social Security (Waiver of Debts – Refugees) Specification 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the end of the period for disallowing it.

*Note:* For that period, see section 42 of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1237AB (1) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

###### 5 Specification of class of debts

For subsection 1237AB (1) of the Act, a debt in the following class of debts is specified:

a debt of a person that arises because the person receives or has received payments on the basis that a person who held a Refugee and Humanitarian (Class XB) visa from 5 October 2001 onwards was the holder of a permanent protection visa within the meaning of subparagraph (c)(i) of subsection 7(6B) of the Act.

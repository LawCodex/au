# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 4 of 2019

**Australian Prudential Regulation Authority instrument fixing charges No. 4 of 2019**

Provision of statistical information about financial sector entities to the Reserve Bank of Australia and the Australian Bureau of Statistics during the 2018-19 financial year.

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Stephen Brian Matthews, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 51(1)(a) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998,* fix the charges specified in the attached Schedule of charges in respect of the specified services and facilities provided by APRA.

This instrument commences on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation*.*

Dated 17 June 2019

[Signed]

……………………….........................................

Stephen Brian Matthews

Executive General Manager

Interpretation

In this instrument

***ABS*** means the Australian Bureau of Statistics.

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***financial sector entity*** has the meaning in section 5 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***EFS*** means Economic and Financial Statistics

***RBA*** means the Reserve Bank of Australia.

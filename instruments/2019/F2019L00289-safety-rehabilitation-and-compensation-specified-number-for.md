# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Number for Redemption of Compensation) Instrument 2019

I, KELLY O’DWYER, Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations, make the following instrument.

Dated 7 March 2019

KELLY O’DWYER

Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Number for Redemption of Compensation) Instrument 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 30(4) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

**4 Schedules**

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in that Schedule, and any other item in that Schedule has effect according to its terms.

**5 Specification**

For the purposes of subsection 30(2) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*, the specified number is 0.03.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 - Notice Specifying a Number for Redemption of Compensation (Notice No. R1 of 1994)

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) (Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles) Instrument 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2019.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 19 of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019*.

###### 4 Schedule—Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles

The Repatriation Private Patient Principles are modified as set out in the Schedule to this instrument for the purposes of the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019.*

## Schedule—Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles

##### Repatriation Private Patient Principles

**1 Paragraphs 1.1 and 1.1A**

Omit the paragraphs, substitute:

###### 1.1 Name

This instrument is the Principles.

###### 1.1A Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2019.

**2 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Act)**

Substitute:

***Act*** means the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Act 2019.*

**3 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Commission)**

Substitute:

***Commission*** means the Repatriation Commission continued in existence by section 179 of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

**4 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of dependent of a Vietnam veteran)**

Omit the definition.

**5 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of entitled person)**

Substitute:

***entitled person*** means an eligible person within the meaning of the *Act*.

**6 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of former Repatriation Hospital)**

Substitute:

***former Repatriation Hospital*** means a hospital or other institution that was formerly operated by the Commission under paragraph 89(1)(a) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

**7 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Principles)**

Substitute:

***Principles*** means the Repatriation Private Patient Principles as modified by the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) Modifications of the Private Patient Principles Instrument 2019*.

**8 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of private patient)**

Substitute:

**private patient has the same meaning given by subsection 19(8) of the Act.**

**9 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of revoked Repatriation Private Patient Principles)**

Omit the definition.

**10 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Treatment Principles)**

Substitute:

***Treatment Principles*** means the Treatment Principles as modified by the *Treatment Benefits (Special Access) (Modifications of the Treatment Principles) Instrument 2019*.

**11 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of veteran partnering private hospital)**

Omit “the *Act*”, substitute “the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*”.

**12 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Vietnam veteran)**

Omit the definition.

**13 Paragraph 3.6**

Omit the paragraph.

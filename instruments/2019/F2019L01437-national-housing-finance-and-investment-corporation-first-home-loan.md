# National Housing Finance and Investment Corporation (First Home Loan Deposit Scheme Commencement) Determination 2019

I, MICHAEL SUKKAR, Minister for Housing and Assistant Treasurer, determine 1 January 2020, for the purposes of item 16 of Schedule 1 to the *National Housing Finance and Investment Corporation Amendment Act 2019*.

Note: The effect of this Determination is to allow the National Housing Finance and Investment Corporation to make a decision to issue a guarantee under the First Home Loan Deposit Scheme on and after 1 January 2020.

Dated 8/11/2019

MICHAEL SUKKAR

Minister for Housing and Assistant Treasurer

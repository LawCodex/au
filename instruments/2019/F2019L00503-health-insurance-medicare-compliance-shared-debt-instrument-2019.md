# Health Insurance (Medicare Compliance Shared Debt) Instrument 2019

I, GREG HUNT, Minister for Health, make the following legislative instrument.

Dated 26 March 2019

Greg Hunt

Minister for Health

Contents

1 Name

This instrument is the *Health Insurance (Medicare Compliance Shared Debt) Instrument 2019.*

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 July 2019.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 129ACA(9)(c) of the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Health Insurance Act 1973*.

5 Percentage

For the purposes of paragraph 129ACA(9)(c) of the Act, the prescribed percentage is 35%.

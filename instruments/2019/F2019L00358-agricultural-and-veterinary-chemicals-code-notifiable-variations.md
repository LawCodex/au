# Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code (Notifiable Variations) Instrument 2019

I, Chris Parker, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Pesticides and Veterinary Medicines Authority, make the following legislative instrument.

Dated 21 March 2019

Chris Parker

Chief Executive Officer

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code (Notifiable Variations) Instrument 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the day it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 26AB(5) of the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code, as scheduled to the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Act 1994*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in Part 1, Division 1 of the Code, including the following:

(a) active constituent;

(b) agricultural chemical product;

(c) chemical product;

(d) instruction;

(e) notifiable variation;

(f) Register;

(g) relevant particulars;

(h) variations;

(i) veterinary chemical product.

In this instrument:

***Agricultural Labelling Code*** means the *Agricultural Labelling Code* made by the APVMA, as in force from time to time.

***Code*** means the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code, as scheduled to the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Act 1994*.

***FAISD Handbook*** means the document entitled *Handbook of First Aid Instructions, Safety Directions, Warning Statements and General Safety Precautions for Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals*, published by the Office of Chemical Safety in the Therapeutic Goods Administration of the Department of Health, as in force from time to time.

***interchangeable constituent determination*** means that term as defined in the Regulations.

***Regulations*** means the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Regulations 1995* and a reference to subregulations and paragraphs has a corresponding meaning.

***Veterinary Labelling Code*** means the *Veterinary Labelling Code* made by the APVMA, as in force from time to time.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

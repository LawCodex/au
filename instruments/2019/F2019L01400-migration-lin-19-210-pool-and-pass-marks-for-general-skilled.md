###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsections 96(1) and (2) of the *Migration Act 1958*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Migration Act 1958*.

***invitation***, in relation to an application for a class of visa, means an invitation by the Minister to apply for that class of visa.

###### 6 Pool and pass marks—applications made on or after 16 November 2019

(1) This section applies in relation to a class of visas to which both of the following paragraphs apply:

(a) the visas are of any of the following subclasses:

(i) Subclass 189 (Skilled—Independent) visa;

(ii) Subclass 190 (Skilled—Nominated) visa;

(iii) Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)) visa;

(b) the applications for the visas are made on or after 16 November 2019.

(2) For the purposes of the Act and the regulations:

(a) the pool mark in relation to the class of visas; and

(b) the pass mark in relation to the applications;

is 65 points.

###### 7 Pool and pass marks—applications made before 16 November 2019

(1) This section applies in relation to a class of visas to which all of the following paragraphs apply:

(a) the visas are of a subclass mentioned in column 1 of an item of the table in subsection (3);

(b) the requirements specified in column 2 of the item are met;

(c) the applications for the visas were made before 16 November 2019.

(2) For the purposes of the Act and the regulations:

(a) the pool mark in relation to the class of visas; and

(b) the pass mark in relation to the applications;

is the mark specified in column 3 of the item.

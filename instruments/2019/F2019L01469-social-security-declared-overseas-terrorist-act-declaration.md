# Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2019—Nairobi 2019

Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act—Nairobi 2019) Declaration 2019

I, SCOTT JOHN MORRISON, Prime Minister, make the following declaration.

Dated 11 October 2019

SCOTT JOHN MORRISON

Prime Minister

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2019—Nairobi 2019*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 35B(1) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

(1) In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***terrorist act*** has the meaning given by subsection 100.1(1) of the *Criminal Code.*

(2) Dates and times in this instrument are expressed in terms of Eastern African Time.

###### 5 Declared overseas terrorist act

For the purposes of subsection 35B(1) of the Act, the following terrorist act is a declared overseas terrorist act:

(a) the complex attack at the DusitD2 hotel complex in Nairobi, Kenya, from approximately 2.30 pm on 15 January 2019 to approximately 10 am on 16 January 2019.

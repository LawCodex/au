### Part 2—Final factor for the year 2018‑19

###### 5 Determination of final factor for 2018‑19

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2018‑19 year is 1.0300.

###### 6 How the final factor was worked out

(1) Under subsection 8(1) of the Act, the factor in relation to the 2018‑19 year is to be worked out by applying the formula set out in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act and then adjusting the result under whichever of paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are applicable.

Paragraph 8(1)(a) formula

(2) The factor calculated under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act is as follows:

Note 2: Under section 4A, the Statistician made the estimate of the population of Australia as at 31 December 2016 on 21 June 2018 (ABS Catalogue Number 3101.0).

Note 3: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 24 April 2019 by the Statistician in respect of the 2019 March quarter (ABS Catalogue Number 6401.0).

Note 4: The formula uses the All Groups Consumer Price Index number, being the weighted average of the 8 capital cities, published on 24 April 2019 by the Statistician in respect of the 2018 March quarter (ABS Catalogue Number 6401.0).

Paragraph 8(1)(b) modification

Paragraph 8(1)(c) modification

(4) The result of subsection (3) was modified under paragraph 8(1)(c) of the Act as a result of special circumstances, being the need to account for the Commonwealth’s decision to bring forward the first 2 quarterly payments in the 2018-19 year into the 2017-18 year and the first 2 quarterly payments in the 2019-20 year into the 2018-19 year.

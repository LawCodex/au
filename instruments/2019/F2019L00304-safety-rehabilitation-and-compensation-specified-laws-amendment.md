# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2019

I, KELLY O’DWYER, Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations, make the following instrument*.*

Dated 7 March 2019

KELLY O’DWYER

Minister for Jobs and Industrial Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 119(7) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Declaration 2017

**1 Schedule 1 (after table item 1)**

Insert:

| 1A | *Motor Accident Injuries Act 2017* |
|---|---|

**2 Transitional—operation for the purposes of the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988**

The amendments of this instrument made by the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2019* do not apply in relation to the operation of this instrument for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

Note: This instrument operates for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988* because of item 63 of Schedule 1 to the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Legislation Amendment (Defence Force) Act 2017*.

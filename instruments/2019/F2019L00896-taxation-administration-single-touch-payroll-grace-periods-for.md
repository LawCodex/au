# Taxation Administration – Single Touch Payroll – Grace periods for correcting statements

I, Deborah Jenkins, Deputy Commissioner of Taxation, make this legislative instrument under subsection 389-25(5) of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953*.

**Deborah Jenkins**

Deputy Commissioner of Taxation

21 June 2019

**Name of instrument**

This determination is the *Taxation Administration – Single Touch Payroll – Grace periods for correcting statements* legislative instrument.

**2. Effective dates**

This instrument commences on 1 July 2019.

**3. Application**

This instrument applies to all entities that make a statement notifying an amount under item 1 or 2 of the table in subsection 389-5(1) of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953*.

**4. Determination**

(a) For the purposes of subsection 389-25(5) of Schedule 1 to the *Taxation Administration Act 1953*, the period within which an entity may correct a statement made in an Single Touch Payroll report is set out in the following table:

<table>
<tr>
<td><b><i>ITEM</b></i></td>
<td><b><i>COLUMN A</b></i><br><b><i>If the original statement was made in relation to…</b></i></td>
<td><b><i>COLUMN B</b></i><br><b><i>Then the period commences on…</b></i></td>
<td><b><i>COLUMN C</b></i><br><b><i>And the period ends on the earlier of…</b></i></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>1</td>
<td>information or amounts relating to a person which the entity reasonably expects they will not be required to make another statement about within the same financial year</td>
<td rowspan="2">the day the entity becomes aware that the statement is false or misleading</td>
<td>(a) the 14th day after the day mentioned in column B, or<br>(b) the 14th day after the end of the financial year in which the original statement was made.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2</td>
<td>a person which the entity reasonably expects it will be required to make another statement about within the same financial year</td>
<td>(a) the day that, having regard to the pattern of statements made by the entity to the Commissioner under section 389-5 of Schedule 1 to the <i>Taxation Administration Act 1953</i> in respect of that person in the preceding six months, the entity would ordinarily next make a statement under that section in respect of that person<i>,</i> or<br>(b) the 14th day after the end of the financial year in which the original statement was made.</td>
</tr>
</table>

(b) The day in column B can be no later than the 14th day after the end of the financial year in which the original statement was made.

# Defence Determination (Prohibited Substances – Authorised Persons) 2019/2

I, General Angus J Campbell, AO, DSC, Chief of the Defence Force, make the following Determination under section 93A of the *Defence Act 1903.*

Dated 5 November 2019

Angus J Campbell

General\
Chief of the Defence Force

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is *Defence Determination (Prohibited Substances – Authorised Persons) 2019/2*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 93A of the *Defence Act 1903.*

###### 4 Repeals

This instrument repeals *Defence Determination (Prohibited Substances – Authorised Persons) 2019/1,* signed 21 March 2019.

###### 5 Authorised persons

The following persons are authorised persons:

Vice Chief of the Defence Force;

Chief Joint Operations;

Deputy Chief Joint Operations;

A Commander of a Joint Task Force holding a rank specified at item 5 of the table in subsection 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the *Defence Act 1903*.

The following persons are authorised persons for the purposes of a member in the Royal Australian Navy:

Chief of Navy;

Deputy Chief of Navy;

Director General Navy People;

Commander Australian Fleet;

Head Navy People Training and Resources;

Commodore Training;

Commander Surface Force;

Commander Fleet Air Arm;

Commodore Flotillas;

Commander Information Warfare Force;

Deputy Fleet Commander;

Chief of Staff Navy Headquarters.

The following persons are authorised persons for the purposes of a member in the Australian Army:

Chief of Army;

Deputy Chief of Army;

Chief of Staff Army Headquarters;

Director General Army People Capability.

The following persons are authorised persons for the purposes of a member in the Royal Australian Air Force:

Chief of Air Force;

Deputy Chief of Air Force;

Director General Personnel – Air Force.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

**Act** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**Expense Payment** means, in the case of an individual participating in the New Employment Services Trial, an amount:

(a) paid by, or on behalf of, the Commonwealth, to assist with the individual’s expenses associated with, or incidental to, the individual’s participation in the New Employment Services Trial; and

(b) received by any means including:

(i) a reimbursement to the individual; or

(ii) a direct payment to a supplier who provides a good or service to the individual.

**New Employment Services Trial** means the program established by the Commonwealth and known as the “New Employment Services Trial”.

**5 Exempt Lump Sum**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, if an individual participating in the New Employment Services Trial receives an Expense Payment, that amount is an exempt lump sum.

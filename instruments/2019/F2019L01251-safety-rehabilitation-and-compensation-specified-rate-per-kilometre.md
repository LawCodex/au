# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Instrument 2019

I, CHRISTIAN PORTER, Attorney-General and Minister for Industrial Relations, make the following instrument.

Dated 13 September 2019

CHRISTIAN PORTER

Attorney-General and Minister for Industrial Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Instrument 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

###### This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 16(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988.*

**5 Schedule**

Each instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in that Schedule, and any other item in that Schedule has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Specification

For the purposes of subsection 16(6) of the Act, the ***specified rate per kilometre*** on and from the date of commencement of this instrument is $0.68.

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Notice 2008 (3)

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

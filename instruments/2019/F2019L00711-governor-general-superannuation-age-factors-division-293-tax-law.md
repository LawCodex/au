# Governor-General Superannuation Age Factors (Division 293 Tax Law) Determination 2019 (No. 1)

Governor-General Superannuation Age Factors (Division 293 Tax Law) Determination 2019 (No. 1)1

*Governor-General Act 1974*

I, ROSEMARY THERESE HUXTABLE, Secretary of the Department of Finance, make this Determination under subsection 4BA(10) of the *Governor-General Act 1974*.

Dated 20 May 2019

**ROSEMARY THERESE HUXTABLE**

Secretary of the Department of Finance

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Governor-General Superannuation Age Factors (Division 293 Tax Law) Determination 2019 (No. 1)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Age factors

For the purposes of section 4BA of the *Governor-General Act 1974*, the age factor for a person is specified in the Table in the Schedule to this Determination, based on:

(a) whether the person is male or female;

(b) the age of the person at their last birthday prior to the start of their pension.

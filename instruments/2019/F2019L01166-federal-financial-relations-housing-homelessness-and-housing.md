Note: Paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003* has the effect that expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Federal Financial Relations Act 2009* as in force from time to time.

### Part 2—Determination of housing, homelessness and housing affordability payments for 2018‑19

###### 5 Housing, homelessness and housing affordability payments for 2018‑19

For the purposes of subsection 15C(2) of the Act, each item of the following table specifies an amount that is to be paid to the State specified in the item, for the purpose of making a grant of financial assistance for the 2018-19 financial year, for the purpose of expenditure in accordance with the primary housing agreement, and a supplementary housing agreement that relates to that financial year to which that State is a party:

| Item | For this State: | The amount of financial assistance for the 2018‑19 financial year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | $476,464,270.58 |
| 2 | Victoria | $395,181,358.81 |
| 3 | Queensland | $314,257,569.99 |
| 4 | Western Australia | $163,542,999.98 |
| 5 | South Australia | $107,545,804.89 |
| 6 | Tasmania | $33,232,624.52 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | $25,962,187.49 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | $19,657,105.97 |
|  | **Total** | **$1,535,843,922.23** |

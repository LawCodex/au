# Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Fees for GEMS Regulator Services) Instrument 2019

I, Sean Sullivan, GEMS Regulator, acting in accordance with my powers under section 77 of the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012*, make this instrument for the purpose of specifying the fees payable in relation to certain services provided by, or on behalf of, me in the performance of my functions.

Sean Sullivan

GEMS Regulator

Dated: 29 March 2019

**Part 1 Preliminary**

###### 1 Name of Instrument

This instrument is the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Fees for GEMS Regulator Services) Instrument 2019.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

**3 Revocation**

This instrument revokes the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (Variation of a Model’s Registration – Application Fees) Instrument 2014.*

###### 4 Purpose

This instrument specifies the fees payable for identified services provided by the GEMS Regulator associated with registrations under the *Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards Act 2012* (Act).

###### 5 Power

Subsection 77(1) of the GEMS Act provides that the GEMS Regulator may charge fees for services provided by, or on behalf of, the GEMS Regulator in the performance of his or her functions.

###### 6 Interpretation

Unless the contrary intention appears words and expressions used in this instrument have the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 7 Application

This instrument applies to the services identified in Schedule 1.

###### Part 2 Fees

###### 8 Fees

Column 2 of Schedule 1 specifies the fee payable for the service provided by the GEMS Regulator identified in Column 1 of Schedule 1.

**Schedule 1: Fees payable**\
(section 8)

| **Column 1**<br>**GEMS Regulator service** | **Column 2**<br>**Fee amount** |
|---|---|
| Application to vary a registration to cover additional models<br>(Act section 46) | $210 |
| Assessment of a voluntarily submitted additional test report for a registered product, where a revised test method has been specified in a replacement determination (Act sections 36 and 40) | $210 |

# Conservation theme for prioritising nominations of threatened species, threatened ecological communities and key threatening processes under Section 194D of the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (Cth) (217)

Commonwealth of Australia

**Conservation theme for prioritising nominations of threatened species, threatened ecological communities and key threatening processes under Section 194D of the** ***Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*** **(Cth) (217)**

I, SUSSAN LEY, Minister for the Environment under section 194D of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999* (Cth), determine that the following conservation theme should be given priority in relation to the assessment period commencing 1 October 2020:

**‘*****Listed threatened species which require reassessment to harmonise their listing status across range states and territories’***

Sussan Ley

Minister for the Environment

Dated 28 November 2019

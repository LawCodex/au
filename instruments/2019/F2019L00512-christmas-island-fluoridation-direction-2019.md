# Christmas Island Fluoridation Direction 2019

I**, SUSSAN PENELOPE LEY**, Assistant Minister for Regional Development and Territories, acting in accordance with section 8G of the *Christmas Island Act 1958* and paragraph 9(1)(b) of the *Fluoridation of Public Water Supplies Act 1966* (WA)(CI):

(a) **Revoke** the *Direction under section 9(1)(b) - Fluoridation of Public Water Supply Direction*; and

(b) **Direct** the Administrator to give effect to the recommendation of the Fluoridation of Public Water Supplies Advisory Committee of Western Australia that 0.7 mg/l of fluoride be added to the water supply of the Territory.

This Direction commences on the day after this Direction is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Dated 28 March 2019

SUSSAN LEY

Assistant Minister for Regional Development and Territories

# Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2019—Hyderabad (2007)

*Social Security Act 1991*

I, SCOTT JOHN MORRISON, Prime Minister, make this declaration under subsection 35B(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

Dated 7 August 2019

SCOTT JOHN MORRISON

Prime Minister

###### 1 Name of declaration

This declaration is the *Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2019—Hyderabad (2007)*

###### 2 Commencement

This declaration commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Definitions

In this declaration:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***terrorist act*** has the same meaning as in subsection 100.1(1) of the *Criminal Code.*

###### 4 Declared overseas terrorist act

For the purposes of subsection 35B(1) of the Act, the following terrorist act is a declared overseas terrorist act:

1\) The bombings at approximately 19.50 (local time) on 25 August 2007, in the Gokul Chat Bhandar restaurant on Gokul Kanchi Guda Station Road, Koti, and Lumbini Amusement Park in Hyderabad, India.

# Telecommunications (Carrier Licence and Nominated Carrier Declaration Exemption—Wireless Network Unit) Determination 2019

I, PAUL FLETCHER, Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts, make the following determination.

Dated 18 September 2019

Paul Fletcher

Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence and Nominated Carrier Declaration Exemption—Wireless Network Unit) Determination 2019*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 51(1) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) base station that is part of a terrestrial radiocommunications customer access network;

(b) carriage service;

(c) carrier licence;

(d) distinct places;

(e) line link;

(f) network unit;

(g) nominated carrier declaration.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***fixed-line network*** means a network made up of one or more line links.

***Wireless Network Unit*** means a network unit which is a base station that is part of a terrestrial radiocommunications customer access network.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Exemption

Section 42 of the Act does not apply in relation to a Wireless Network Unit where:

(a) the Wireless Network Unit is used to supply a carriage service to users where the users are not in a distinct place from the Wireless Network Unit; and

(b) a carrier licence or a nominated carrier declaration is not, or would not be, required under section 42 of the Act in respect of the use of a fixed-line network to supply a carriage service under the same circumstances as referred to in paragraph (a).

## Schedule 1—Repeals

##### Determination under subsection 51(1) (No. 1 of 2002)

**1 The whole of the instrument**

Repeal the instrument.

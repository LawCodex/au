### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This is the *Biosecurity (First Point of Entry—Port of Sydney) Determination 2019*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 229(1) of the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 9 of the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

***container*** has the same meaning as in the *Biosecurity Regulation 2016*.

***freight container*** means a re‑usable container:

(a) that is designed to be used to transport goods by one or more modes of transport; and

(b) that can be transferred from one mode of transport to another without unloading the goods from the container; and

(c) that is known as a 20‑foot‑long container or a 40‑foot‑long container.

***general goods*** means goods other than baggage, freight containers, live horses or waste.

***goods*** means goods (as defined by section 19 of the Act):

(a) that are subject to biosecurity control; or

(b) in relation to which an exposed goods order is in force.

***inorganic bulk goods*** means chemicals, ores, minerals or gases that:

(a) are brought or imported into Australian territory in bulk; and

(b) do not contain animals, plants, organic compounds or microbes.

***non‑commercial vessel*** has the same meaning as in the *Biosecurity Regulation 2016*.

***non‑commercial vessel baggage*** means baggage carried on a non‑commercial vessel.

***non‑commercial vessel waste*** means waste carried on a non‑commercial vessel.

***passenger vessel baggage*** means baggage carried on a passenger vessel.

***vessel*** means a vessel (as defined by section 9 of the Act) that is subject to biosecurity control.

***waste*** means goods:

(a) that are subject to biosecurity control; and

(b) that are intended, by the person in charge of the vessel on which the goods are carried, to be disposed of.

### Part 2—First point of entry

###### 5 First point of entry—vessels

Port of Sydney is a first point of entry for vessels generally.

###### 6 First point of entry—goods

Port of Sydney is a first point of entry for goods other than the following:

(a) freight containers;

(b) live horses.

Note: For other matters relating to whether particular goods may be brought into a particular first point of entry, see sections 173 and 174 of the Act (which deal with prohibited goods and conditionally non‑prohibited goods) and any determinations made under those sections.

### Part 4—Conditions

###### 9 Conditions—notifying Agriculture Department of changes

(1) The determination of Port of Sydney as a first point of entry by this instrument is subject to the conditions in this section.

(2) If the owner (or, if there is more than one owner, an owner) of Port of Sydney becomes aware that there has been, or will be, a change to the business entity operating:

(a) Port of Sydney; or

(b) a biosecurity entry point at Port of Sydney;

the owner (or, if there is more than one owner, an owner) must give the Agriculture Department, in writing, notice of the change as soon as practicable after becoming aware that the change has occurred or will occur.

(3) If the lessee (if any) (or, if there is more than one lessee, a lessee) of Port of Sydney becomes aware that there has been, or will be, a change to the business entity operating:

(a) Port of Sydney; or

(b) a biosecurity entry point at Port of Sydney;

the lessee (or, if there is more than one lessee, a lessee) must give the Agriculture Department, in writing, notice of the change as soon as practicable after becoming aware that the change has occurred or will occur.

(4) If a person or body that is responsible for carrying out operations at Port of Sydney proposes to make a change referred to in subsection (5), the person or body must give the Agriculture Department, in writing, reasonable notice of the proposed change.

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4), the changes are as follows:

(a) a change to the procedures at Port of Sydney providing for biosecurity measures to be taken to manage the level of biosecurity risk associated with operations carried out at Port of Sydney;

(b) a change to the facilities or amenities available at Port of Sydney for biosecurity officials and human biosecurity officers to perform functions or exercise powers under the Act at Port of Sydney;

(c) a change to the procedures at Port of Sydney that may affect the ability of a person who carries out operations at Port of Sydney to identify biosecurity risks associated with those operations;

(d) a change to the procedures at Port of Sydney that may affect the ability for biosecurity officials or human biosecurity officials to be informed of any identified biosecurity risks associated with operations carried out at Port of Sydney;

(e) a change to the procedures at Port of Sydney for managing any other factors that may contribute to, or affect, the level of biosecurity risk associated with operations carried out at Port of Sydney.

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fuel Quality Standards (Ethanol E85) Information Standard 2019*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22A of the *Fuel Quality Standards Act 2000*.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fuel Quality Standards Act 2000*.

***E85*** means a fuel blend consisting of:

(a) 70% to 85% ethanol; and

(b) petrol that meets the requirements of the *Fuel Quality Standards (Petrol) Determination 2024*.

***service station*** means premises at which E85 is sold by retail from a fuel pump.

###### 6 Supply of E85 from service stations

(1) This section specifies:

(a) the information about E85 that should be provided with the supply of E85 from a service station; and

(b) the way in which that information is to be provided.

(2) One of the following is to be clearly displayed on any fuel pump at the service station dispensing E85:

(a) the words:

(i) “Contains 70–85% ethanol”; and

(ii) “Not petrol or diesel”;

(b) the words:

(i) “Contains x% ethanol”, where x is a number equal to or more than 70 but not more than 85 and is no less than the percentage of ethanol in the ethanol blend; and

(ii) “Not petrol or diesel”.

(3) For the purposes of subparagraph (2)(b)(i), if a percentage is not a whole number, it may be rounded to the nearest whole number, with a number ending with 0.5 being rounded up.

###### 7 Other retail supply of E85

(1) This section specifies:

(a) the information about E85 that should be provided in relation to the supply of E85, by retail sale, other than from a service station; and

(b) the way in which that information is to be provided.

(2) Either of the following:

(a) before the E85 is supplied to a buyer, the buyer is to be given a document that prominently includes the words specified in paragraph 6(2)(a) or (b);

(b) if the buyer is present when the E85 is supplied, the words specified in paragraph 6(2)(a) or (b) are to appear on each container of E85 so that the words can be easily read by the buyer.

###### 8 Supply of E85 to retailers

(1) This section specifies:

(a) the information about E85 that should be provided with the supply of E85 to a retailer; and

(b) the way in which that information is to be provided.

(2) At the time of the supply, the retailer is to be given a document that prominently displays:

(a) a statement that the E85 contains:

(i) 70% to 85% ethanol; or

(ii) x% ethanol, where x is a number equal to or more than 70 but not more than 85 and is no less than the percentage of ethanol in the ethanol blend; and

(b) a statement that the E85 is the subject of this instrument.

**Editorial changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* authorises First Parliamentary Counsel to make editorial and presentational changes to a compiled law in preparing a compilation of the law for registration. The changes must not change the effect of the law. Editorial changes take effect from the compilation registration date.

If the compilation includes editorial changes, the endnotes include a brief outline of the changes in general terms. Full details of any changes can be obtained from the Office of Parliamentary Counsel.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

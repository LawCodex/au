# AusCheck (Major National Event—International Cricket Council T20 World Cup Australia 2020) Declaration 2019

LIN 19/270

AusCheck (Major National Event—International Cricket Council T20 World Cup Australia 2020) Declaration 2019

I, Peter Dutton, Minister for Home Affairs, acting under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*, declare the International Cricket Council T20 World Cup Australia 2020 to be a major national event.

8th November 2019

Peter Dutton\
Minister for Home Affairs

# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 2 of 2019

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination

No. 2 of 2019

Information provided by authorised deposit-taking institutions under Reporting Standards ARS 720.0, ARS 720.1, ARS 720.2 and ARS 720.3

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Alison Bliss, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 57(2)(b) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998,* DETERMINE that specified parts of a reporting document of a kind specified in the Schedule does not contain confidential information.

This determination commences on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

*Note:* The effect of this determination is that the non-confidential information may be published under subsection 56(5C) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998.*

Dated: 22 August 2019

[Signed]

Alison Bliss

General Manager

Data Analytics and Insights

**Interpretation**

In this determination:

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***authorised deposit-taking institution*** **(ADI)** has the meaning given in section 5 of the *Banking Act 1959*.

***reporting document*** means a reporting document within the meaning of section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data Act) 2001*.

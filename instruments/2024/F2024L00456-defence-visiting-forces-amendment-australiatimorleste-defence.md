David Hurley

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Mark Dreyfus KC

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Defence (Visiting Forces) Regulations 1963

**1 Subregulation 6(5)**

Omit “the civilian component of a visiting force of Japan”, substitute “a civilian component of a visiting force of Japan or Timor‑Leste”*.*

**2 Subregulation 6(5)**

Omit “civilian component of the visiting force”, substitute “relevant civilian component of the relevant visiting force”.

**3 Schedule 2**

Omit “East Timor”.

**4 Schedule 2**

After “Thailand”, insert “Timor‑Leste”.

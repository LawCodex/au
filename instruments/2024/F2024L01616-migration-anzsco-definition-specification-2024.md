# Migration (ANZSCO Definition) Specification 2024

LIN 24/105

I, Julian Hill, Assistant Minister for Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 6 December 2024

Julian Hill

Assistant Minister for Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (ANZSCO Definition) Specification 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 7 December 2024.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under regulation 1.03 of the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Definition of ANZSCO

(1) For the purposes of the definition of ***ANZSCO*** in regulation 1.03 of the Regulations, ANZSCO has the meaning given by subsections (2) and (3).

(2) Subject to subsection (3), ANZSCO means the Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics, as in force on 27 June 2013.

(3) For the following provisions of the Regulations, ANZSCO means the Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics, as in force on 23 November 2022:

(a) regulation 2.72;

(b) regulation 2.73;

(c) subregulation 5.19(5).

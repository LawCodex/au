# Broadcasting Services (Regulated Television Services) Determination 2024

I, Michelle Rowland, Minister for Communications, make the following determination.

Dated 29 November 2024

Michelle Rowland

Minister for Communications

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Broadcasting Services (Regulated Television Services) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 130ZZJ(2) of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

###### 5 Determination of regulated television service

Channel 44, an open narrowcasting television service provided for community or educational non-profit purposes, by C44 Adelaide Limited, ABN 17 104 643 736, is a regulated television service for the purpose of paragraph 130ZZJ(1)(c) of the Act.

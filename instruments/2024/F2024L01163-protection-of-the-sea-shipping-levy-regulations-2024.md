I, the Honourable Sam Mostyn AC, Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Governor‑General

By Her Excellency’s Command

Catherine King

Minister for Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development and Local Government

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Protection of the Sea (Shipping Levy) Act 1981*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

Note 1: The *Protection of the Sea (Shipping Levy Collection) Act 1981* is incorporated in, and is to be read as one with, the *Protection of the Sea (Shipping Levy) Act 1981* (see section 4 of that Act).

Note 2: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Protection of the Sea (Shipping Levy Collection) Act 1981*, including the following:

(a) quarter;

(b) ship;

(c) tonnage.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Protection of the Sea (Shipping Levy) Act 1981*.

###### 6 Rate of levy

For the purposes of section 6 of the Act, the rate of levy in respect of a ship for a quarter commencing on or after 1 October 2024 is 11.25 cents per ton of the tonnage of the ship.

###### 7 Minimum amount of levy

For the purposes of section 7 of the Act, the amount for a quarter commencing on or after 1 October 2024 is $10.

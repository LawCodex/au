# Migration (Arrangements for Pacific Engagement Visa Applications) Instrument (LIN 24/012) 2024

LIN 24/012

I, Patrick Bryson, delegate of the Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 13 May 2024

Patrick Bryson

A/g SES Band 1 / A/g Assistant Secretary

Skilled Visas Branch

Immigration Programs Division

Department of Home Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Arrangements for Pacific Engagement Visa Applications) Instrument (LIN 24/012) 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subregulation 2.07(5) of the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Migration Regulations, including *Immigration*.

In this instrument:

***ImmiAccount*** means the interactive portal for online services available through the Department’s website at https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.

***Internet form*** means a form mentioned in paragraph 1.18(2)(b) of the Migration Regulations.

***Migration Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

***written notice*** means an email from Immigration to a person that:

(a) authorises the person to make an application using an approved form specified in paragraph 5(1)(b); and

(b) includes an Immigration email address and instructions to the person setting out how to send the form to that email address.

###### 5 Applying for a Subclass 192 (Pacific Engagement) visa—specified form and manner

(1) For the purposes of item 1140 of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the following approved forms are specified:

(a) form 1556 (Internet);

(b) form 1556.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 1140(3)(a) of Schedule 1 to the Migration Regulations, the manner in which an application must be made, using a form specified in subsection (1), is specified in the following table.

| Item | If the form used in making the visa application is… | the application must be made… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | form 1556 (Internet) | via ImmiAccount. |
| 2 | form 1556 | by sending the application by email to the Immigration email address that is set out in a written notice. |

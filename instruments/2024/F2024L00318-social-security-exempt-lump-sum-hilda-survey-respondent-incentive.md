# Social Security (Exempt Lump Sum) (HILDA Survey Respondent Incentive Payment) Determination 2024

I, GILLIAN BEER, Delegate of the Secretary of the Department of Social Services, make the following Determination.

Dated 14 March 2024

Gillian Beer

Branch Manager, Payment Structures and Seniors Branch

Delegate of the Secretary of the Department of Social Services

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Social Security (Exempt Lump Sum) (HILDA Survey Respondent Incentive Payment) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after the instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***HILDA Survey*** means the Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia survey.

***respondent incentive payment*** means a payment to a person for their participation in the HILDA Survey.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of the Act, a respondent incentive payment is an exempt lump sum under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act.

# Higher Education Support Provider Approval (Adelaide College of Divinity Incorporated) Revocation 2024

I, Larissa Hinds, delegate of the Minister for Education, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Dated 03 September 2024

Larissa Hinds

Assistant Secretary

HELP, Student and Provider Integrity Branch

Department of Education

Delegate of the Minister for Education

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Support Provider Approval (Adelaide College of Divinity Incorporated) Revocation 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 5 Notice of Revocation

The approval of Adelaide College of Divinity Incorporated (ABN 866 809 542 46) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22‑2(1) of the Act.

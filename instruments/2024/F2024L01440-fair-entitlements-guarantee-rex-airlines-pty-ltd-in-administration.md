# Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Rex Airlines Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2024

I, MURRAY WATT, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated 8 November 2024

Murray Watt

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Rex Airlines Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 49 of the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012*.

###### 4 Declaration

The *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012* applies in relation to persons who were employed, but are no longer employed, by Rex Airlines Pty Ltd (Administrators Appointed) A.C.N. 642 400 048 which is an entity in administration under the *Corporations Act 2001*.

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Defence Trade Controls (Australian Military Sales Program item) Determination 2024* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 29 July 2026 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au).

**Application, saving and transitional provisions**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. Any modifications affecting the law are accessible on the Register.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Defence Trade Controls (Australian Military Sales Program item) Determination 2024*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 5AA of the *Defence Trade Controls Act 2012*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) Australian Military Sales Program item;

(b) Defence and Strategic Goods List;

(c) DSGL goods;

(d) DSGL technology.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Defence Trade Controls Act 2012.*

***development*** has the same meaning as in the *Defence and Strategic Goods List*.

***DSGL Reference Number*** means the item number in Part 1—Munitions List of the *Defence and Strategic Goods List* that covers the DSGL goods or DSGL technology.

***production*** has the same meaning as in the *Defence and Strategic Goods List*.

***required*** has the same meaning as in the *Defence and Strategic Goods List*.

***software*** has the same meaning as in the *Defence and Strategic Goods List*.

***technology*** has the same meaning as in the *Defence and Strategic Goods List*.

###### 5 Australian Military Sales Program item

(1) This section specifies DSGL goods or DSGL technology that is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(2) DSGL goods or DSGL technology specified in column 1 of an item in the following table that is covered by the DSGL reference number specified in column 2 is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

|  | **Column 1** | **Column 2** |
|---|---|---|
| **Item** | **DSGL goods or DSGL technology** | **DSGL reference number** |
| 1 | Ghost Shark Extra-Large Autonomous Undersea Vehicle | ML9. |
| 2 | Active Electronically Scanned Array | ML5. |
| 3 | Jindalee Operational Radar Network high-frequency over-the-horizon radar | ML5. |

(3) A component specially designed for DSGL goods mentioned in subsection (2) is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(4) Software covered by DSGL reference number ML21. specially designed or modified for the development, production, operation or maintenance of:

(a) DSGL goods or DSGL technology mentioned in subsection (2); or

(b) a component covered by subsection (3);

is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

(5) Technology covered by DSGL reference number ML22. specially required for the development, production, operation, installation, maintenance (checking), repair, overhaul or refurbishing of:

(a) DSGL goods or DSGL technology mentioned in subsection (2); or

(b) a component covered by subsection (3);

is an Australian Military Sales Program item.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

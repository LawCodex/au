###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 7AAA of the *Therapeutic Goods Act 1989*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3(1) of the Act, including the following:

(a) therapeutic goods;

(b) vaping goods.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Therapeutic Goods Act 1989*.

***tobacco product*** has the same meaning as in section 9 of the *Public Health (Tobacco and Other Products) Act 2023.*

###### 5 Goods determined to be therapeutic goods

(1) The goods specified in Schedule 1 are determined to be therapeutic goods for the purposes of the Act.

(2) For each item of the table in Schedule 2, the goods specified in column 2, when used, advertised, or presented for supply in a way specified in column 3, are determined to be therapeutic goods for the purposes of the Act.

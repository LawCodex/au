# Fisheries Management (Multiple Fishery Closures) Direction 2024

I, Wez Norris, the Chief Executive Officer, make the following instrument.

Dated 9 January 2024

Wez Norris

Chief Executive Officer

Australian Fisheries Management Authority

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fisheries Management (Multiple Fishery Closures) Direction 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences 30 April 2024.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 41A(2) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in definitions section of the Act, including the following:

(a) Australian Fishing Zone;

(b) Fishing.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

***Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery*** has the same meaning as in the *Western Tuna and Billfish Management Plan 2005*.

***Western Skipjack Fishery*** has the same meaning as in the *Fisheries Management Regulations 2019*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 To Whom this instrument applies

(1) This instrument applies to a person who is permitted to engage in in fishing (however described) in the following fisheries:

(a) Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery; and

(b) Western Skipjack Fishery.

###### 7 Direction not to engage in fishing

Fishing is not to be engaged in in the areas specified in Annexure A and Annexure B.

###### 8 Geographic coordinates

(1) Except as otherwise indicated, geographic coordinates in this instrument are expressed in terms of the Geocentric Datum of Australia 1994 (commonly known as GDA94).

Note: The Geocentric Datum of Australia 1994 was published in. Gazette No. GN35 of 6 September 1995 at page 3369.

(2) In this instrument, a reference to WGS84 is a reference to the World Geodetic System 1984.

(3) In this instrument, GDA94 and WGS84 are taken to be equivalent.

Note: See US National Imagery and Mapping Agency, Technical Report - NIMA TR8350.2 Third Edition (including amendments to 23 June 2004) - Department of Defense World Geodetic System 1984—Its Definition and Relationships with Local Geodetic Systems, in particular Chapters 2.2.1 and 7.

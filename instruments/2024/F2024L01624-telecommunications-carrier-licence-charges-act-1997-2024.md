# Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997 - 2024 Determination under paragraph 15(1)(b)

AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION

Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997

###### 2024 DETERMINATION UNDER PARAGRAPH 15(1)(b)

The AUSTRALIAN COMPETITION & CONSUMER COMMISSION (ACCC) makes this determination under paragraph 15(1)(b) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997* and determines that the proportion of the ACCC’s costs for the 2022-23 financial year that is attributable to the ACCC’s telecommunications functions and powers is $13,041,969.

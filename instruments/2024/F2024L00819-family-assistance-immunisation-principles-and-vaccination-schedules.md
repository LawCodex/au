## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Family Assistance (Immunisation Principles and Vaccination Schedules) (DSS) Determination 2018

**1 Subsection 8(1) (note 2)**

Repeal the note, substitute:

*Note 2* As of 4 June 2024, the Australian Immunisation Handbook can be found at: https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/

**2 Subsection 15(1) (cell at table item dealing with children born on or after 1 January 2017, column headed “If the child was born…”)**

After “on or after 1 January 2017”, insert “and before 1 July 2024”.

**3 Subsection 15(1) (at the end of the table)**

Add:

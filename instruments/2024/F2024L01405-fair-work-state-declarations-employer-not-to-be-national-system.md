# Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement (No. 2) 2024

*Fair Work Act 2009*

I, Murray Watt, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Endorsement under paragraph 14(4)(a) of the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

Dated 31 October 2024

Murray Watt

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Endorsement

This Endorsement is the *Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement (No. 2) 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Endorsement commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Definition

In this Endorsement:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

###### 4 Endorsement of State Declaration

For paragraph 14(4)(a) of the Act, a declaration as in force on 4 October 2024 under the *Industrial Relations Act 1996* (NSW) that an employer mentioned in Schedule 1 is not to be a national system employer for the purposes of the Act, is endorsed.

## Schedule 1 Employer declared by or under a law of New South Wales not to be a national system employer

(section 9A(1))

**Employer**

The Pavilion: Performing Arts Centre Sutherland Limited

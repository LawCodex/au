# Higher Education Provider Approval (No. 11 of 2012) Amendment Instrument 2024

I, Peta Brill, delegate of the Minister, make the following instrument.

Dated 11 January 2024

Peta Brill

A/g Assistant Secretary\
Student and Provider Integrity Branch

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Higher Education Provider Approval (No. 11 of 2012) Amendment Instrument 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 16-25 and paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

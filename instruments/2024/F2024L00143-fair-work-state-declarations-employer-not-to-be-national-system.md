# Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement (No. 1) 2024

*Fair Work Act 2009*

I, Tony Burke, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make this Endorsement under paragraph 14(4)(a) of the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

Dated 1 February 2024

Tony Burke

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Endorsement

This Endorsement is the *Fair Work (State Declarations — employer not to be national system employer) Endorsement (No. 1) 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Endorsement commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Definition

In this Endorsement:

***Act*** means the *Fair Work Act 2009*.

###### 4 Endorsement of State Declaration

For paragraph 14(4)(a) of the Act, a declaration as in force on 19 January 2024 under the *Industrial Relations Act 1996* (NSW) that an employer mentioned in Schedule 1 to this instrument is not to be a national system employer for the purposes of the Act, is endorsed.

## Schedule 1 Employer declared by or under a law of New South Wales not to be a national system employer

(section 9A(1))

**Employer**

Murray Art Museum Albury Limited

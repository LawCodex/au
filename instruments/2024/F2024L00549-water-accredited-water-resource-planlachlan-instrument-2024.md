# Water (Accredited Water Resource Plan—Lachlan) Instrument 2024

I, Tanya Plibersek, Minister for the Environment and Water, make the following instrument.

Dated 15 May 2024

Tanya Plibersek

Minister for the Environment and Water

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Water (Accredited Water Resource Plan—Lachlan) Instrument 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 63 of the *Water Act 2007*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 4 of the Act, including the following:

(a) Authority;

(b) Basin Plan;

(c) water resource plan;

(d) water resource plan area.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Water Act 2007.*

###### 5 Accredited water resource plan

(1) For the purposes of subparagraph 63(5)(b)(i) of the Act, the proposed water resource plan for the Lachlan water resource plan area specified in subsection (2) is accredited.

(2) The plan is the document titled *Lachlan Surface Water Resource Plan* as in force on the day that this instrument is made, excluding any material identified as not forming part of the plan.

Note 1: The plan could in 2024 be accessed on the Authority website at www.mdba.gov.au.

Note 2: The plan meets the requirements that a water resource plan for a water resource plan area must comply with to be accredited: see table item 11 of subsection 22(1) of the Act and Chapter 10 of the Basin Plan.

Note 3: The accreditation of the plan ceases to have effect in accordance with the Act: see sections 54 and 64.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***ABN*** has the meaning given by the *A New Tax System (Australian Business Number) Act 1999*.

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***ADI*** (short for ***authorised deposit-taking institution***) has the meaning given by the *Banking Act 1959*.

***electronic service*** has the meaning given by the *Online Safety Act 2021*.

***program***: see subsection 5(1).

***telecommunications industry*** has the meaning given by the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the Streamlining External Dispute Resolution for Scams Program (the ***program***) is prescribed.

(2) The program provides funding by way of a grant to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority Limited (ABN 38 620 494 340).

(3) The purpose of the program is to facilitate the Australian Financial Complaints Authority Limited to prepare for, and implement all systems necessary for, operating an external dispute resolution scheme for dealing with complaints to one or more of the following about scams:

(a) ADIs;

(b) participants in the telecommunications industry;

(c) providers of electronic services.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the power of the Parliament to make laws with respect to the following matters are specified:

postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution);

banking, other than State banking; also State banking extending beyond the limits of the State concerned, the incorporation of banks, and the issue of paper money (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xiii) of the Constitution);

foreign corporations, and trading or financial corporations formed within the limits of the Commonwealth (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xx) of the Constitution).

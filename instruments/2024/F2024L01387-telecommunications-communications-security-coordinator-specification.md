# Telecommunications (Communications Security Coordinator) Specification 2024

I, Tony Burke, the Home Affairs Minister (within the meaning of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*), make this instrument.

Dated 30 October 2024

Tony Burke

Home Affairs Minister

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Telecommunications (Communications Security Coordinator) Specification 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) the day after this instrument is registered; and

(b) the day Schedule 4 of the *Crimes and Other Legislation Amendment (Omnibus No. 1) Act 2024* commences.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 7A(2) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 7 of the Act, including the following:

(a) Communications Security Coordinator;

(b) Home Affairs Department; and

(c) Home Affairs Minister.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

***APS employee*** has the meaning given by the *Public Service Act 1999*.

***Cyber and Infrastructure Security Group*** means the Group of the same name in the Home Affairs Department.

***SES employee*** has the meaning given by the *Public Service Act 1999*.

###### 5 Specification

(1) For the definition of Communications Security Coordinator in paragraph 7A(1)(b) of the Act, APS employees in the Home Affairs Department holding, occupying or performing the duties of a SES employee in the Cyber and Infrastructure Security Group are specified.

(2) For subsection 7A(4) of the Act, all of the functions and powers of a Communications Security Coordinator under a provision of the Act are specified to be functions and powers that a person mentioned in subsection (1) may perform or exercise.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Australian Crime Commission (National Policing Information Charges) Act 2016*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***accredited body*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Crime Commission Act 2002*.

***Act*** means the *Australian Crime Commission (National Policing Information Charges) Act 2016*.

***nationally coordinated criminal history check*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Crime Commission Act 2002*.

###### 6 Amount of national policing information charge for nationally coordinated criminal history check

For the purposes of subsection 7(1) of the Act, for a national policing information service of a kind mentioned in column 1 of an item in the following table that is provided by the Australian Crime Commission to an entity mentioned in column 2 of that item, the amount of the national policing information charge is the amount mentioned in column 3 of that item.

| Item | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | If the national policing information service is … | and is provided by the Australian Crime Commission to … | the amount of the charge is … |
| 1 | a nationally coordinated criminal history check | the Australian Federal Police or a police force of a State or Territory | $28.00 |
| 2 | a nationally coordinated criminal history check | an accredited body | $28.00 |
| 3 | a nationally coordinated criminal history check relating to a volunteer | any of the following:<br>(a) the Australian Federal Police;<br>(b) a police force of a State or Territory;<br>(c) an accredited body | $7.00 |

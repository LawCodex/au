###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***appointed person*** means a person appointed by the Commonwealth to act as a nominee director on the board of directors of SQC.

***program***: see subsection 5(1).

***remuneration*** includes salary, allowances, benefits and superannuation.

***SQC*** means Silicon Quantum Computing Pty Ltd (ACN 619 102 608).

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the Silicon Quantum Computing Support Program (the ***program***) is prescribed.

(2) The program is to support an appointed person to perform their functions, powers and duties as a nominee director of SQC, including by providing for their engagement and remuneration.

(3) The purpose of the program is to support SQC in carrying out its activities, particularly nationally significant research and development in the field of quantum computing.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the powers of the Parliament to make laws with respect to the following are specified:

(a) foreign corporations, and trading or financial corporations formed within the limits of the Commonwealth (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xx) of the Constitution);

(b) enterprises and activities that are peculiarly adapted to the government of a nation and cannot otherwise be carried on for the benefit of the nation (see paragraph 51(xxxix) and section 61 of the Constitution).

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 85BB(4) of the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999*.

***member of the family unit*** of a person has the same meaning as in the *Migration Act 1958*.

###### 5 Eligibility for child care subsidy—prescribed classes of persons

For the purposes of paragraph 85BB(4)(b) of the Act, the classes of persons determined for the purposes of paragraph 85BB(3)(c) of the Act are:

(a) persons who have been approved by a Department, in the course of administering the Pacific Australia Labour Mobility scheme, to bring members of the family unit of the person to Australia; and

(b) members of the family unit of such persons.

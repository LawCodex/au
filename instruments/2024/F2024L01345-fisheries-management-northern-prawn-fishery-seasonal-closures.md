# Fisheries Management (Northern Prawn Fishery Seasonal Closures) Amendment Direction No. 1 2024

I, Wez Norris, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, make the following direction.

Dated: 18 October 2024

Wez Norris

Chief Executive Officer

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fisheries Management* (*Northern Prawn Fishery Seasonal Closures) Amendment Direction No. 1 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 41A(3) of the *Fisheries Management Act 1991*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment

##### Fisheries Management (Northern Prawn Fishery Seasonal Closures) Direction 2021

**1 Subsection 11(3)**

Omit the subsection, substitute:

(3) Fishing is not to be engaged in the fishery defined in Schedule 1 during the period commencing at 2200 hours UTC on 30 October 2024 and ending at 2200 hours UTC on 30 November 2024.

**2 Subsection 11(4)**

Omit the subsection, substitute:

(4) Fishing is not to be engaged in the fishery defined in Schedule 2 during the period commencing at 2200 hours UTC on 30 October 2024 and ending at 2200 hours UTC on 30 November 2024.

# CASA EX45/24 — Operation of Remotely Piloted Aircraft Over Populous Area Exemption 2024

**CASA EX45/24 — Operation of Remotely Piloted Aircraft Over Populous Area Exemption 2024**

as amended

made under regulation 11.160 of the Civil Aviation Safety Regulations 1998.

This compilation takes into account amendments up to instrument *CASA EX92/25 — Amendment of CASA EX45/24 (Operation of Certain Remotely Piloted Aircraft Over Populous Area) Instrument 2025.* It is a compilation of *CASA EX45/24 — Operation of Remotely Piloted Aircraft Over Populous Area Exemption 2024*, as amended and in force on 16 November 2025.

Prepared by the Legal Services Branch, Legal, International & Regulatory Affairs Division, Civil Aviation Safety Authority, Canberra.

Compilation No. 1.

1 Name

This instrument is *CASA EX45/24 — Operation of Remotely Piloted Aircraft Over Populous Area Exemption 2024*.

2 Duration

This instrument:

(b) is repealed at the end of 31 July 2027.

3 Definitions

In this instrument:

***approved area*** has the meaning given by regulation 101.236 of CASR.

***certificated RPA*** means a relevant RPA for which a certificate of airworthiness has been issued.

***populous area*** has the meaning given by regulation 101.025 of CASR.

***relevant approval*** means any of the following approvals, whether granted before or after the commencement of this definition:

(a) an approval under regulation 101.029 of CASR, for the purposes of subregulation 101.245(5) of CASR, to operate a relevant RPA that is not a certificated RPA within 30 metres of a person who is not directly associated with the operation;

(b) an approval given for the purposes of paragraph 7(3)(a) of *CASA 20/25 – Operation of Certain Unmanned Aircraft and Rockets – Directions Instrument 2025* (***CASA 20/25***) to operate a relevant RPA in, or over, another person’s safety zone.

*Note* The term ***safety zone*** is defined in subsection 7(2) of CASA 20/25.

***relevant person***: see section 4.

***relevant RPA*** means a very small RPA, small RPA, medium RPA or large RPA.

4 Application

This instrument applies if:

(a) a person (the ***relevant person***) holds a relevant approval; and

(b) the relevant approval is not suspended; and

(c) the relevant person complies with the conditions of the relevant approval.

5 Exemptions — operating over a populous area

(1) Subsection (2) applies if the relevant RPA is a very small RPA, small RPA or medium RPA.

(2) If the relevant person operates the relevant RPA outside an approved area over a populous area, the relevant person is exempt from compliance with paragraph 101.250(1)(b) of CASR in relation to the operation.

(3) If the relevant person operates the relevant RPA over a populous area, the relevant person is exempt from compliance with subregulation 101.280(2) of CASR in relation to the operation.

**Contents**

Part 1—Preliminary

1 Name of legislative instrument

This is the *ASIC Corporations (Approval of February 2025 Banking Code of Practice) Instrument 2024/523*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 28 February 2025.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 1101A(1) of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***February 2025 Banking Code of Practice*** means the *Banking Code of Practice* provided to ASIC by the Australian Banking Association Limited (ABN 60 117 262 978) on 24 June 2024.

Note: The February 2025 Banking Code of Practice will be available on the website of the Australian Banking Association Limited shortly after this instrument is made. The February 2025 Banking Code of Practice, under its terms, takes effect from 28 February 2025.

Part 2—Approval

5 Approval of February 2025 Banking Code of Practice

The February 2025 Banking Code of Practice is approved.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Social Security (Assurances of Support) Determination 2018

**1 Section 2A**

Repeal the section.

**2 Paragraph 14(2)(a)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(a) if:

(i) the person does not have current available evidence of assessable income for the first financial year; or

(ii) the person’s assessable income for the first financial year was reduced due to a temporary change in the person’s circumstances;

\- the current financial year, and the second financial year; and

**3 After paragraph 25(c)**

**Insert:**

**(ca) a body corporate that has given an assurance of support is deregistered (within the meaning of the Corporations Act 2001) and, in the opinion of the Secretary, the circumstances justify cancellation of the assurance of support;**

**4 Application provision**

(1) The amendment made by item 2 of this Schedule applies to an assurance of support given on or after commencement of this instrument, in relation to financial years starting before, on or after that commencement.

**(2) The amendment made by item 3 of the Schedule applies in relation to an assurance of support given before, on or after commencement of this instrument, in relation to deregistration that occurred before, on or after that commencement.**

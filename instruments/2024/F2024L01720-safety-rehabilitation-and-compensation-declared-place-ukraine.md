# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Declared Place – Ukraine) Declaration 2024

I, MURRAY WATT, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated 18 December 2024

Murray Watt

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Declared Place – Ukraine) Declaration 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of subparagraph 6(1)(h)(ii) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*, I declare that:

Ukraine

is a place to which paragraph 6(1)(h) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* applies.

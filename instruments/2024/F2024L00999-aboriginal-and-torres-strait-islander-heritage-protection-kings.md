###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 10 of the *Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Heritage Protection Act 1984*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Heritage Protection Act 1984*.

***bulk earthworks*** means the excavation or other movement of large quantities of soil or rock with the intent or effect of changing the level or elevation of an area of land.

***declared area*** means the area specified in section 5.

***land*** includes soil and vegetation.

***mining activities*** means activities that include, or are related to, the extraction of materials or minerals from the ground.

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 3 of the Act, including the following:

(a) area;

(b) significant Aboriginal area.

### Part 2—Declaration of an area as a significant Aboriginal area to be preserved and protected from injury or desecration

###### 5 Declared area

(1) For the purposes of subsection 10(1) and paragraph 11(a) of the Act, the area specified in subsection (2), that is a significant Aboriginal area, is declared for protection and preservation in accordance with section 6.

(2) The declared area is the area which comprises part of the Belubula River, its headwaters and its springs, at Kings Plains, near Blayney in New South Wales.

(3) The declared area is indicated by the map in Schedule 1.

###### 6 Protection and preservation of declared area

(1) For the purposes of subsection 10(1) and paragraph 11(b) of the Act, the declared area is a significant Aboriginal area that is to be preserved and protected from injury and desecration.

(2) A person must not engage in any conduct that will, or is likely to, injure or desecrate the declared area.

Note 1: Subsection 3(2) of the Act relevantly states that for the purposes of the Act, an area shall be taken to be injured or desecrated if:

(a) it is used or treated in a manner inconsistent with Aboriginal tradition;

(b) by reason of anything done in, on or near the area, the use or significance of the area in accordance with Aboriginal tradition is adversely affected; or

(c) passage through or over, or entry upon, the area by any person occurs in a manner inconsistent with Aboriginal tradition.

Note 2: A person commits an offence if the person engages in conduct that contravenes this provision: see subsection 22(1) of the Act.

(3) Without limiting subsection (2), a person must not:

(a) undertake any mining activities within the declared area; or

(b) construct any bulk earthworks on the declared area; or

(c) seal any part of the declared area, including with concrete; or

(d) undertake any activity that will, or is likely to, significantly alter the landform or course of water flowing within the declared area; or

(e) conduct any drilling activities or preliminary activities associated with drilling; or

(f) conduct any clearing activities or preliminary activities associated with clearing; or

(g) disturb native vegetation or soil in a way that will, or is likely to, damage the declared area.

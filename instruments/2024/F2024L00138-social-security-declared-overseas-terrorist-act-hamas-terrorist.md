# Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act— Hamas Terrorist Attacks—7 October 2023) Declaration 2024

Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act—Hamas Terrorist Attacks—\
7 October 2023) Declaration 2024

I, ANTHONY NORMAN ALBANESE, Prime Minister, make the following declaration.

Dated 2 February 2024

Anthony Albanese

Prime Minister

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act— Hamas Terrorist Attacks—7 October 2023) Declaration 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 35B(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument, ***terrorist act*** has the same meaning as in subsection 100.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*.

###### 5 Declared overseas terrorist act

The following terrorist acts in locations in Israel on 7 October 2023 are declared overseas terrorist acts:

(a) the attack on the Be’eri Kibbutz;

(b) the attack on Sderot;

(c) the attack on the Supernova Sukklot Gathering music festival, in the Re’im Kibbutz;

(d) the attack on Nahal Oz Kibbutz;

(e) the attack on the military base adjacent to the Nahal Oz Kibbutz near the northern Gaza Strip;

(f) the attack on the Kfar Aza Kibbutz;

(g) the attack on the Nir Oz Kibbutz;

(h) the attack on the Netiv HaAsara Moshav;

(i) the attack on the Alumim Kibbutz;

(j) the attack on the Kissufim Kibbutz;

(k) the attack on the Nirim Kibbutz;

(l) the attack on Zikim Beach;

(m) the attack on the Holit Kibbutz;

(n) the attack on the Ein HaShlosha Kibbutz; and

(o) the attack on the Nir Yitzhak Kibbutz.

Note: If the Prime Minister makes a declaration under subsection 35B(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, then subsection 35B(2) provides that the Minister is taken to have made a determination under subsection 36(1) that the terrorist act is a major disaster.

# Defence (Non-relevant foreign country) Determination 2024

I, Richard Marles, Minister for Defence, make the following determination.

Dated 1 May 2024

Richard Marles

Minister for Defence

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Defence (Non-relevant foreign country) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 115(3) of the *Defence Act 1903* (the Act).

###### 4 Countries that are not relevant foreign countries

Each of the following countries is not a relevant foreign country for the purposes of the definition of relevant foreign country in section 113 of the Act:

(a) Canada;

(b) New Zealand;

(c) United Kingdom;

(d) United States of America.

# Classification (Accredited Persons) (Application for Revocation of Classification) Determination 2024

I, Jim Betts, Secretary of the Department of Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development, Communications and the Arts, make the following determination under subsection 22L(8) of the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

Dated 8 March 2024

Jim Betts

Secretary of the Department of Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development, Communications and the Arts

Contents

###### 1 Name

This determination is the *Classification (Accredited Persons) (Application for Revocation of Classification) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the later of:

(a) the start of the day after this instrument is registered; and

(b) the same time as the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Amendment (Industry Self‑Classification and Other Measures) Act 2023* commences.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under subsection 22L(8) of the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) Board;

(b) classified;

(c) computer game;

(d) film;

(e) participating Minister;

(f) publish.

In this determination:

***Act*** means the *Classification (Publications, Films and Computer Games) Act 1995*.

***application for revocation*** means an application made under paragraph 22L(2)(c) of the Act for the Board to exercise its power to revoke a classification made by an accredited person.

***publisher*** means a person who has published or intends to publish the film or computer game.

###### 5 Class of persons who may make an application for revocation

For the purposes of paragraph 22L(7)(b) of the Act, the following constitute a class of persons who may make an application for revocation:

(a) a participating Minister;

(b) the publisher of the film or computer game.

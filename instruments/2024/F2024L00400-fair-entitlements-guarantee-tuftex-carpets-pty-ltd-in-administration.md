# Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Tuftex Carpets Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2024

I, TONY BURKE, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated: 27 March 2024

Tony Burke

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee (Tuftex Carpets Pty Ltd in Administration) Declaration 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 49 of the *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012*.

###### 4 Declaration

The *Fair Entitlements Guarantee Act 2012* applies in relation to persons who were employed, but are no longer employed, by Tuftex Carpets Pty Ltd (Administrators Appointed) which is an entity in administration under the *Corporations Act 2001*.

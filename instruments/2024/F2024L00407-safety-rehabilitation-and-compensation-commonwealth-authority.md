# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Declaration 2024

I, TONY BURKE, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated 27 March 2024

TONY BURKE

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Declaration 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This declaration is made under subparagraph (d)(ii) of the definition of *Commonwealth authority* in subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (the Act).

###### 4 Declaration

For the purposes of subsection 4(1) of the Act in relation to the definition of *Commonwealth authority,* I declare that under subparagraph (d)(ii) of that definition that Inland Rail Pty Ltd (ACN 094 819 520) is a body corporate to which the Act applies.

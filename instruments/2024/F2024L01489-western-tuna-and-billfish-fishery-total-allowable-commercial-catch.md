Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery (Total Allowable Commercial Catch) Determination (No.2) 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on 1 February 2025.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made pursuant to paragraph 17(6)(aa) of the Act and under sections 3 and 11 of the Management Plan.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of the expressions used in this instrument are defined in the section 3 of the Management Plan, including the following:

*fishing season;*

*Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery;*

*total allowable commercial catch; and*

*quota species.*

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Fisheries Management Act 1991.*

***Management Plan*** means the *Western Tuna and Billfish Fishery Management Plan 2005.*

###### 5 Cessation

This instrument ceases on 31 January 2028 unless revoked earlier.

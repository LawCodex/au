# Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code (Interchangeable Constituent) Determination 2024

I, Dr Melissa McEwen, Acting Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Pesticides and Veterinary Medicines Authority, make the following instrument.

Dated 4 June 2024

Dr Melissa McEwen\
Acting Chief Executive Officer

Contents

###### Name

This determination is the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code (Interchangeable Constituent) Determination 2024.*

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under subregulation 19AEA(1) of the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Regulations 1995*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this determination:

***non-active constituent*** means any constituent of a registered chemical product other than an active constituent.

Note: Words and expressions used in this Determination have the same meaning as in the Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code as scheduled to the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Act 1994* and the *Agricultural and Veterinary Chemicals Code Regulations 1995.*

###### 5 Interchangeable constituent determination

The constituent of a chemical product listed in Column 1 of the Table below may be interchanged with the constituent listed in Column 2 if the requirements specified in Column 3 are met.

| **Column 1** | **Column 2** | **Column 3** |
|---|---|---|
| Tersperse 4896 | Tersperse 4894 | The chemical product is a registered chemical product; and<br>Tersperse 4896 is a non-active constituent of the chemical product; and<br>Tersperse 4896 is for use as a surfactant; and<br>the registered chemical product is a suspension concentrate or flowable concentrate formulation type; and<br>the concentration of Tersperse 4894 to be interchanged is the same as the concentration of Tersperse 4896 in the registered chemical product. |

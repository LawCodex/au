# Digital ID (Phasing-in of Participation in the Australian Government Digital ID System) Determination 2024

I, Katy Gallagher, Minister for Finance, make the following determination.

Dated 7 November 2024

Katy Gallagher

Minister for Finance

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Digital ID (Phasing-in of Participation in the Australian Government Digital ID System) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the same time as the *Digital ID Act 2024* commences.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 60(1) of the *Digital ID Act 2024*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) accredited entity;

(b) Digital ID Regulator;

(c) entity;

(d) participating relying party;

(e) relying party.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Digital ID Act 2024*.

***applicant*** means an entity that has applied for accreditation under section 14 of the Act.

***Corporations Act company*** means a body corporate that is incorporated, or taken to be incorporated, under the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***owned***, in relation to a Corporations Act company, means holding at least half of the issued share capital of the company.

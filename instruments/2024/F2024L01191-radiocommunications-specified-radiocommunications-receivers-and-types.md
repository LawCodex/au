# Radiocommunications (Specified Radiocommunications Receivers and Types of Transmitter Licences and Receiver Licences) Determination 2024

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following determination under paragraph 7(1)(b) and subsection 98(1) of the *Radiocommunications Act 1992*.

Dated: 19 September 2024

Adam Suckling

[signed]

Member

Michael Brealey

[signed]

Member/General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### 1 Name

This is the *Radiocommunications (Specified Radiocommunications Receivers and Types of Transmitter Licences and Receiver Licences) Determination 2024*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the start of the day after the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed free of charge at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under made under paragraph 7(1)(b) and subsection 98(1) of the Act.

Note 1: A number of other expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) radiocommunications receiver;

(b) receiver licence;

(c) transmitter licence.

Note 2: Other expressions used in this instrument may be defined in a determination made under section 64 of the *Australian Communications and Media Authority Act 2005* including:

(a) Act;

(b) area-wide receive station;

(c) defence receive station;

(d) earth receive station;

(e) fixed receive station;

(f) major coast receive station;

(g) space receive station.

###### 4 Repeal

The *Radiocommunications (Specified Radiocommunications Receivers and Types of Transmitter Licences and Receiver Licences) Determination 2014* [F2014L01790] is repealed.

###### 5 References to other instruments

In this instrument, unless the contrary intention appears:

(a) a reference to any other legislative instrument is a reference to that other legislative instrument as in force from time to time; and

(b) a reference to any other kind of instrument or writing is a reference to that other instrument or writing as in force or existence from time to time.

Note 1: For references to Commonwealth Acts, see section 10 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*; and see also subsection 13(1) of the *Legislation Act 2003* for the application of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* to legislative instruments.

Note 2: All Commonwealth Acts and legislative instruments are registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note 3: See section 314A of the Act.

###### 6 Radiocommunications receivers

For the purposes of paragraph 7(1)(b) of the Act, a radiocommunications receiver is specified if it is, or forms part of:

(a) an area-wide receive station;

(b) a defence receive station;

(c) an earth receive station;

(d) a fixed receive station;

(e) a major coast receive station; or

(f) a space receive station.

###### 7 Transmitter licences

For the purposes of subsection 98(1) of the Act, the ACMA may issue a transmitter licence of a type specified in column 2 of an item in Schedule 1.

###### 8 Receiver licences

For the purposes of subsection 98(1) of the Act, the ACMA may issue a receiver licence of a type specified in column 2 of an item in Schedule 2.

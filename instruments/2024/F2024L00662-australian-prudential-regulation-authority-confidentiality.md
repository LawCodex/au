# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 1 of 2024

**Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 1 of 2024**

Information provided by financial sector entities under relevant reporting documents

*Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998*

I, Andrew Robertson, delegate of APRA:

(a) under section 57 of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998* (the Act) and subsecton 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, revoke the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No. 3 of 2023*; and

(b) under paragraph 57(2)(b) of the Act determine all or a specified part of the reporting documents of a kind specified in Part 1, Part 2 or Part 3 of the Schedule do not contain confidential information.

This instrument commences at the start of the day after the day the instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

*Note:* The effect of this instrument is that the non-confidential information may be published under subsection 56(5C) of the Act.

Dated: 7 June 2024

Andrew Robertson

General Manager - Chief Data Officer

Technology and Data

**Interpretation**

In this determination

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

***MySuper product*** means a MySuper product within the meaning given in section 10(1) of the *Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993*.

***reporting document*** means a reporting document within the meaning of section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

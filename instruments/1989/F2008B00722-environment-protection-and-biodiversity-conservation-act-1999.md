# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - Proclamation - Christmas Island National Park (14/12/1989)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of<br>Australia<br>BILL HAYDEN<br>Governor-General |  | By His Excellency the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia |
|---|---|---|

I, WILLIAM GEORGE HAYDEN, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under section 7 of the *National Parks and Wildlife Conservation Act 1975*:

(a) hereby revoke:

(i) the Proclamation relating to the Christmas Island National Park published in the *Gazette* on 21 February 1980; and

(ii) the Proclamation relating to the Christmas Island (Stage 2) National Park and the Christmas Island (Stage 3) National Park published in the *Gazette* on 31 January 1986; and

(b) after consideration by the Federal Executive Council of a report by the Director of National Parks relating to the area that was not within any of the parks referred to in subparagraph (a) (i) or (ii), hereby:

(i) declare the area specified in the Schedule to be a park; and

(ii) assign to the park the name "Christmas Island National Park”; and

(iii) specify the depth of 1,000 metres below the surface of any land within the area so declared as the depth to which the subsoil beneath that land shall be taken to be within the park; and

(iv) specify the depth of 1,000 metres below the sea-bed beneath any sea within the area so declared as the depth to which the subsoil beneath that sea-bed shall be taken to be within the park.

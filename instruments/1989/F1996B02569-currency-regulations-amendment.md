# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1989 No. 2311**

**Currency Regulations2 (Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 29 August 1989.

BILL HAYDEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

Peter Morris

Minister of State for Industrial Relations for and on behalf of the Treasurer

**Weight, dimensions and remedy of coins**

**1.** Regulation 3 of the Currency Regulations is amended:

(a) by omitting from subregulation (1) “In respect of a coin” and substituting “Subject to subregulation (1a), in respect of a coin”;

(b) by inserting after subregulation (1) the following subregulation:

“(1a) The standard weight, dimensions and remedy in respect of weight specified in item 10a in the Schedule are prescribed only in respect of a coin bearing, on the reverse side, a design prescribed by sub-subparagraph 4 (d) (viiiec) (b).”.

**Designs of coins**

**2.** Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended by omitting subparagraph (d) (viiiec) and substituting the following subparagraph:

“(viiiec) in the case of a coin of the denomination of $10 that refers on its obverse side to the year 1989 as the year of the coin:

(a) being a coin the weight, dimensions and remedy of which are as specified in item 10 in the Schedule—the inscriptions ‘QUEENSLAND’ and “10 DOLLARS’ and a representation of the coat of arms of the State of Queensland; or

(S.R. 265/89)—Cat. No. 14/21.8.1989

(b) being a coin the weight, dimensions and remedy of which are as specified in item 10 in the Schedule or a coin the weight, dimensions and remedy of which are as specified in item 10a in the Schedule—the inscription ‘TEN DOLLARS’ and a representation of 2 kookaburras perched at the fork of a tree branch;”.

**Schedule**

**3.** The Schedule to the Currency Regulations is amended by inserting after item 10 the following item:

“10a $10 92.5% silver and 7.5% other metal 40.00 34.10 5.10 0.50”.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Australian Thalidomide Survivors Support Program*** means the program established in the 2020-21 Budget under which a payment is made by the DOH or Services Australia on behalf of the DOH to recognised Australian thalidomide survivors.

***DOH*** means the Department of Health.

***relevant amount*** means, in relation to a person, any lump sum amount received by the person under the Australian Thalidomide Survivors Support Program.

Note: The following expression used in this instrument is defined in subsection 8(11) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

(a) exempt lump sum

###### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, all relevant amounts constitute a class of amounts that are exempt lump sums.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the following:

(a) section 504 of the *Social Security Act 1991*;

(b) section 557 of the *Social Security Act 1991*;

(c) section 646 of the *Social Security Act 1991*;

### Part 2—COVID‑19 supplement

###### 5 COVID‑19 supplement—parenting payment

(1) For the purposes of subsection 504(3) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the initial period, as previously extended, is extended by the period:

(a) beginning on 19 December 2020; and

(b) ending at the end of 31 December 2020.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 504(7) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the amount of a person’s COVID‑19 supplement per fortnight for the extension period is $250 for an instalment period in relation to the person that begins on or after 19 December 2020 and before the end of 31 December 2020.

###### 6 COVID‑19 supplement—youth allowance

(1) For the purposes of subsection 557(3) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the initial period, as previously extended, is extended by the period:

(a) beginning on 19 December 2020; and

(b) ending at the end of 31 December 2020.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 557(7) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the amount of a person’s COVID‑19 supplement per fortnight for the extension period is $250 for an instalment period in relation to the person that begins on or after 19 December 2020 and before the end of 31 December 2020.

###### 7 COVID‑19 supplement—jobseeker payment

(1) For the purposes of subsection 646(3) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the initial period, as previously extended, is extended by the period:

(a) beginning on 19 December 2020; and

(b) ending at the end of 31 December 2020.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 646(7) of the *Social Security Act 1991*, the amount of a person’s COVID‑19 supplement per fortnight for the extension period is $250 for an instalment period in relation to the person that begins on or after 19 December 2020 and before the end of 31 December 2020.

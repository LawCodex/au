# Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Determination 2020 (No. 1)

I, MICHAEL SUKKAR, Minister for Housing and Assistant Treasurer, make this determination under subsection 8 (1) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015.*

Dated 22 April 2020

MICHAEL SUKKAR

Minister for Housing and Assistant Treasurer

### 1. Name of Determination

This determination is the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Determination 2020 (No. 1).

### 2. Commencement

This determination commences on the day on which it is registered.

*Note:* Section 42 of the *Legislation Act 2003* (which deals with disallowance of legislative instruments) does not apply to this instrument: see subsection 8 (3) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

### 3. Authority

This determination is made under subsection 8 (1) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

### 4. Crediting of amounts

An amount of $730,000,000.00 is to be credited to the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account on 28 April 2020.

*Note:* The Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account is the special account established by section 7 of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

# Governance Order 2020

THE AUSTRALIAN NATIONAL UNIVERSITy

I, Professor Brian P. Schmidt, Vice-Chancellor, make the following order.

Dated 22 December 2020

Professor Brian P. Schmidt AC FAA FRS

Vice-Chancellor

Contents

#### 1 Name

##### This is the Governance Order 2020.

#### 2 Commencement

##### This instrument commences on 1 January 2021.

#### 3 Authority

##### This instrument is made under the Governance Statute, section 68(1) (General power to make rules and orders).

#### 4 Deans and research school heads

##### For the ANU Act, the Governance Statute and the Governance Rule:

###### (a) the bodies mentioned in column 2 of the table in Schedule 1 are the bodies that are to be regarded as faculties or research schools; and

###### (b) the person holding the position mentioned in column 1 of an item in the table is to be regarded as the dean or head of the body specified in column 2 of the item.

[Note: If the name of a body or position mentioned in the table in Schedule 1 changes, a reference in the table to the body or position by its previous name is taken, after the change, to be a reference to the body or position by its new name (see Legislation Statute, section 10(3) (Application of general references to University entities etc.)).]

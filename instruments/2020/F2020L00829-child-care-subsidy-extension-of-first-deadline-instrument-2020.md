###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Extension of the first deadline

(1) For subsection 103B(2) of the Family Assistance Administration Act, each affected individual is allowed until the end of the period ending on 31 March 2021 to meet the CCS reconciliation conditions for the 2018-19 income year.

(2) For subsection (1), an ***affected individual*** is an individual who has not met the CCS reconciliation conditions for the 2018-19 income year before the end of the 2019-20 income year.

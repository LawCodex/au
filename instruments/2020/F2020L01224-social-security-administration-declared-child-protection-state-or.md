###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 123TF of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedule

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the Schedule.

###### 6 Declared child protection State or Territory

For section 123TF of the Act, the Northern Territory is a declared child protection State or Territory.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 99AEI of the *National Health Act 1953*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Health Act 1953*.

###### 5 Subsection 99ACB(1) is taken not to have applied

(1) For the purposes of subsection 99ACB(3) of the Act, subsection 99ACB(1) of the Act is taken not to have applied to the MERCAPTOPURINE-LINK brand of mercaptopurine, tablet containing mercaptopurine monohydrate 50 mg, oral.

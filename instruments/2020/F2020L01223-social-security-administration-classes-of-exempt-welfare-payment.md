###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 123UGB(2) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Social Security Act 1991*, including the following:

(a) ***nominated visa holder;***

(b) ***principal carer;***

(c) ***receiving***; and

(d) ***special benefit***.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedule

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the Schedule.

###### 6 Exempt welfare payment recipients — specified classes of persons

For subsection 123UGB(2) of the Act, the following class of persons is specified to be exempt welfare payment recipients:

the class of persons each member of which:

is at least 16 years old; and

is receiving special benefit; and

is not a nominated visa holder; and

is not the principal carer of a child who is aged under 6 years.

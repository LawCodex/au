###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Medical Indemnity (Run‑off Cover Support Payment) Act 2004*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Medical Indemnity (Run‑off Cover Support Payment) Act 2004*.

###### 6 Applicable percentage for working out amount of run‑off cover support payment

For the purposes of paragraph 6(2)(b) of the Act, the percentage of 5% is specified for a contribution year beginning on or after 1 July 2020.

###### 7 Kind of amounts by which premium income is reduced

(1) This section specifies a kind of amounts for the purposes of subsection 7(2) of the Act (which provides for amounts by which a medical indemnity insurer’s premium income for a period under subsection 7(1) of the Act is reduced).

(2) The kind is amounts of refund payable by the insurer to a medical practitioner in respect of an overpayment of a premium for medical indemnity cover (whether or not the overpayment was made during the period for which the insurer’s premium income is being calculated).

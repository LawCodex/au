# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2020

I, CHRISTIAN PORTER, Minister for Industrial Relations, make the following instrument*.*

Dated 27 August 2020

CHRISTIAN PORTER

Minister for Industrial Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2020*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 119(7) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

###### 4 Schedule

Each instrument that is specified in the Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in the Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Declaration 2017

**1 Schedule 1 (after table item 15)**

Insert:

| 15A | *Motor Accident Injuries Act 2019* |
|---|---|

**2 Transitional—operation for the purposes of the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988**

The amendments of this instrument made by the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Amendment Declaration 2020* do not apply in relation to the operation of this instrument for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

Note: This instrument operates for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988* because of item 63 of Schedule 1 to the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Legislation Amendment (Defence Force) Act 2017*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 84AG(1) of the *National Health Act 1953.*

###### 4 Repeal of previous instrument

The *National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits—Therapeutic Groups) Determination 2010* is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

In this Determination:

***Act*** means the *National Health Act 1953.*

***ATRA group*** means the Angiotensin II receptor antagonist group.

###### 6 Therapeutic groups

For paragraph 84AG(1)(a) of the Act, a therapeutic group is a group mentioned in a Part of Schedule 1.

###### 7 Drugs in therapeutic groups

For paragraph 84AG(1)(b) of the Act, each listed drug mentioned in a Part of Schedule 1 is in the therapeutic group mentioned in the Part.

# Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Determination 2020

***Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004***

**Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Determination 2020**

Instrument 2020 No. M37

I, Darren Chester, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following legislative instrument under section 293 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004*.

Dated this 29th day of October 2020.

….Darren Chester…….

**DARREN CHESTER**

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Determination 2020.*

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on 1 November 2020.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under section 293 of the *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004* (the Act).

**4 Revocation**

The *Military Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Determination 2019* Instrument 2019 No. M16 dated 28 March 2019 is revoked.

**5 Specified rate per kilometre**

For the purposes of the definition of ***specified rate per kilometre*** in subsection 293(3) the Act, the rate per kilometre is determined to be 68 cents.

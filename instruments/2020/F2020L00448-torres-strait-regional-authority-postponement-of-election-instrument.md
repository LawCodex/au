###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 142S of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Act 2005.*

***TSRA election*** has the meaning given in subsection 4(1) of the Act.

###### 5 Postponement of TSRA election

(1) This section:

(a) is made for the purposes of subsection 142S(1) of the Act; and

(b) displaces the operation of subsection 142Y(1) of the Act.

(2) The next TSRA election, which is due to be held in July 2020, is postponed until a date to be fixed in accordance with section 6.

###### 6 Timing of next TSRA election

For the purposes of subsection 142Y(2) of the Act, the day or days fixed for polling in the next TSRA election shall be no later than 31 March 2021.

###### 7 Subsequent TSRA elections

Subsection 142Y(1) of the Act applies to subsequent TSRA elections held after 31 March 2021.

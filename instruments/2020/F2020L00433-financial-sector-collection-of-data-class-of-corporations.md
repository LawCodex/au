# Financial Sector (Collection of Data) class of corporations determination No. 1 of 2020

## Financial Sector (Collection of Data) class of corporations determination No. 1 of 2020

## Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001

### [image]

Since APRA considers that the business activities of each corporation of the class of corporations specified in the Schedule includes the provision of finance, I, Sean Carmody, delegate of APRA, under paragraph 7(1A)(b) of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001* (the Act) determine the class of corporations specified in the Schedule for the purposes of paragraph 7(1)(b) of the Act.

This instrument commences on 16 April 2020.

Dated: 16 April 2020

[Signed]

Sean Carmody

Executive Director

Cross-Industry Insights and Data Division

**Interpretation**

In this Determination:

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority.

**Schedule – class of corporations that are registrable corporations**

Corporations granted a guarantee under section 5 of the *Guarantee of Lending to Small and Medium Enterprises (Coronavirus Economic Response Package) Act 2020* that are not immediately prior to the grant:

(i) registrable corporations within the meaning of section 7 of the Act; or

(ii) authorised deposit-taking institutions within the meaning of the *Banking Act 1959*.

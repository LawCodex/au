# Prescription of a non-corporate Commonwealth entity for the purposes of Recoverable Payments

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

*Remuneration Tribunal Act 1973*

Prescription of a non-corporate Commonwealth entity for the purposes of\
Recoverable Payments

I, BEN MORTON, Parliamentary Secretary to the Prime Minister, under section 16A (8) of the *Remuneration Tribunal Act 1973* (the Act), prescribe the Department of the Treasury, a non-corporate Commonwealth entity, as the relevant Commonwealth entity for the purposes of Recoverable Payments in relation to recovery of an amount paid by the National Competition Commission under s 16A(1) of the Act.

Dated 27 May 2020

BEN MORTON

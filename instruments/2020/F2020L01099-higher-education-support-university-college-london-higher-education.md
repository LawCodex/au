# Higher Education Support (University College London) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2020

Higher Education Support (University College London)

Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2020

I, Damian Coburn, delegate of the Minister for Education, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 23 July 2020

Damian Coburn

Assistant Secretary

HELP Provider and Integrity Branch

Higher Education Division

Department of Education, Skills and Employment\
Delegate of the Minister for Education

###### 1 Name

This is the *Higher Education Support (University College London) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2020.*

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered in the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument **Act** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval as a Higher Education Provider

The approval of University College London (ABN: 54 728 710 374) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 123XPA(3)(b) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Social Security Act 1991* and *Student Assistance Act 1973*, including the following:

(a) ***ABSTUDY;***

(b) ***living allowance;***

(c) ***pensioner education supplement***

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 5 Schedule

The instrument that is specified in Schedule 1 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the Schedule.

###### 6 Deductible Portions

For paragraph 123XPA(3)(b) of the Act:

50% is specified as the deductible portion of an instalment of a category I welfare payment, other than a category I welfare payment specified in subparagraph (b)(i) or (ii); and

100% is specified as the deductible portion of an instalment of the following category I welfare payments: a payment under the scheme known as the ABSTUDY scheme that includes an amount identified as the living allowance; and a payment under the scheme known as the ABSTUDY scheme that includes an amount identified as pensioner education supplement.

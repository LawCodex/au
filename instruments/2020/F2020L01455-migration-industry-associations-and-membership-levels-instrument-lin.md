# Migration (Industry Associations and Membership Levels) Instrument (LIN 20/061) 2020

I, Alan Tudge, Minister for Population, Cities and Urban Infrastructure, make the following instrument.

Dated 13 November 2020

The Hon Alan Tudge MP

Minister for Population, Cities and Urban Infrastructure, on behalf of the Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs

Contents

### Part 1— Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Migration (Industry Associations and Membership Levels) Instrument (LIN 20/061) 2020*.

This instrument may be cited as LIN 20/061.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraphs 132.232(3)(a) and (b) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Regulations*** means the *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Repeals

In accordance with subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, the instrument IMMI 12/052, *Industry Associations and Membership Levels* (F2012L01309), is repealed.

### Part 2—Industry associations and membership levels

###### 6 Australian Investment Council Limited

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 132.232(3)(a) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the Australian Investment Council Limited is specified as an industry association.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 132.232(3)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Regulations, the category of membership of the industry association mentioned in subsection (1) is specified as:

(a) Corporate Venture Capital Membership; or

(b) Venture Capital Fund Manager Membership.

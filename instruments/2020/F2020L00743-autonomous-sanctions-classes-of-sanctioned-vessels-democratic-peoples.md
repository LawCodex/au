# Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2020

I, MARISE PAYNE, Minister for Foreign Affairs, make the following instrument.

Dated 23 May 2020

MARISE PAYNE

Minister for Foreign Affairs

Contents

###### 1 Name

This Instrument is the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Continuing Effect Declaration 2020*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This Instrument is made under subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this Instrument:

***DPRK*** means the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.

***DPRK vessel class*** has the same meaning as in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017.*

***sanctioned vessel*** means a vessel designated under regulation 8 of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011.*

***vessel*** has the same meaning as in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017.*

###### 5 Designation to continue to have effect

For subregulation 9(3) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011*, the designation of each vessel within the DPRK vessel class as a sanctioned vessel for the DPRK in the *Autonomous Sanctions (Classes of Sanctioned Vessels – Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Designation 2017* is declared to continue to have effect.

Note: Subregulation 9(1) of the *Autonomous Sanctions Regulations 2011* (the Regulations) provide that designations under paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Regulations cease to have effect on the third anniversary of the day on which the designation took effect (if no declaration under subregulation 9(3) has been made in relation to the designation) or, otherwise, the third anniversary of the making of the most recent declaration in relation to the designation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subitem 1(2) of Schedule 16 to the *Coronavirus Economic Response Package Omnibus Act 2020*.

###### 4 Deferral of sunsetting—income management and cashless welfare arrangements

Income management

(1) For the purposes of subitem 1(2) of Schedule 16 to the *Coronavirus Economic Response Package Omnibus Act 2020*, section 123UF of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*:

(a) continues to operate until 31 December 2020; and

(b) ceases to operate on 31 December 2020.

Cashless welfare arrangements

(2) For the purposes of subitem 1(2) of Schedule 16 to the *Coronavirus Economic Response Package Omnibus Act 2020*, Part 3D of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*:

(a) continues to operate until 31 December 2020; and

(b) ceases to operate on 31 December 2020.

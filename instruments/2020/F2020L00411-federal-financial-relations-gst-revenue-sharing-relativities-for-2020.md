Note: Paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Legislation Act 2003* has the effect that expressions have the same meaning in this instrument as in the *Federal Financial Relations Act 2009* as in force from time to time.

### Part 2—Determination of GST revenue sharing relativities for 2020‑21

###### 5 GST revenue sharing relativities for the 2020-21 payment year

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, each item of the following table sets out a factor that is the GST revenue sharing relativity, for the 2020-21 payment year, for the State specified in the item:

| Item | For this State: | The GST revenue sharing relativity for the 2020‑21 payment year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | 0.91808 |
| 2 | Victoria | 0.95992 |
| 3 | Queensland | 1.04907 |
| 4 | Western Australia | 0.44970 |
| 5 | South Australia | 1.35765 |
| 6 | Tasmania | 1.89742 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | 1.15112 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | 4.76893 |

# Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) (Paragraph 15(1)(d)) Determination 2020 (No. 1)

**Commonwealth of Australia**

**TELECOMMUNICATIONS (CARRIER LICENCE CHARGES) (PARAGRAPH 15(1)(d)) DETERMINATION 2020 (No. 1)**

I, PAUL FLETCHER, Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts, under paragraph 15(1)(d) of the *Telecommunications (Carrier Licence Charges) Act 1997*, determine that the estimated total amount of grants likely to be made during the 2019‑2020 financial year under section 593 of the *Telecommunications Act 1997* is $2,322,000.

Dated 11 March 2020.

PAUL FLETCHER

Minister for Communications, Cyber Safety and the Arts

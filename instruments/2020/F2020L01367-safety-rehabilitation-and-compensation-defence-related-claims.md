# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Notice 2020

***Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988***

**Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Notice 2020**

Instrument 2020 No. M36

I, Darren Chester, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following legislative instrument under subsection 16(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

Dated this 29th day of October 2020.

……Darren Chester…….

**DARREN CHESTER**

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Notice 2020.*

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on the 1 November 2020.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 16(6) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

**4 Revocation**

The *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Rate per Kilometre) Notice 2019* Instrument No. M15 of 2019 dated 28 March 2019 is revoked.

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988.*

**6 Specified rate per kilometre**

For the purposes of the definition of ***specified rate per kilometre*** in paragraph 16(6)(c) of the Act, the rate per kilometre is specified to be 68 cents.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under sections 500WA, 549CA and 620 of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Schedule

Each instrument that is specified in the Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule, and any other item in the Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Ordinary waiting period exemptions

(1) For paragraph 500WA(2)(b) of the Act, the following class of people is exempted from application of subsection 500WA(1) of the Act.

(a) The class of persons undertaking the activity specified in section 7 of the *Social Security (Exemptions from Non-payment and Waiting Periods - Activities) Specification 2017*, as amended by this instrument.

(2) For paragraph 549CA(3)(b) of the Act, the following class of people is exempted from application of subsection 500CA(2) of the Act.

(a) The class of persons undertaking the activity specified in section 7 of the *Social Security (Exemptions from Non-payment and Waiting Periods - Activities) Specification 2017*, as amended by this instrument.

(3) For paragraph 620(2)(b) of the Act, the following class of people is exempted from application of subsection 620(1) of the Act.

(a) The class of persons undertaking the activity specified in section 7 of the *Social Security (Exemptions from Non-payment and Waiting Periods - Activities) Specification 2017*, as amended by this instrument.

## Schedule 1—Amendments to other instruments

**Social Security (Exemptions from Non-payment and Waiting Periods - Activities) Specification 2017**

**1 At the end of section 6**

Add:

7 Specification of activities for ordinary waiting period exemptions

(a) For paragraphs 500WA(2)(a), 549CA(3)(a) and 620(2)(a) of the Act, the following activity is specified:

the activity of making a claim for a social security payment during the period commencing 12 March 2020 and ending on 12 June 2020 and then receiving a social security payment for part or all of that period.

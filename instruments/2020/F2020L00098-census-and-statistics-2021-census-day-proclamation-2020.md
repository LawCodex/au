# Census and Statistics (2021 Census Day) Proclamation 2020

I, General the Honourable David Hurley AC DSC (Retd), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Proclamation.

Signed and Sealed with the

Great Seal of Australia on

6 February 2020

David Hurley

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Michael Sukkar

Minister for Housing and Assistant Treasurer

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Census and Statistics (2021 Census Day) Proclamation 2020*.

This instrument is made under subsection 8(2) of the *Census and Statistics Act 1905.*

###### 4 Census day 2021

The Census day for 2021 is appointed as 10 August 2021.

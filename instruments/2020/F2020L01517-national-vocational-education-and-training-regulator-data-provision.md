## Part 1—Preliminary

#### 1 Name

This instrument is the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Data Provision Requirements) Instrument 2020*.

#### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 187 of the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011*.

#### 3A Simplified outline of this instrument

This instrument sets out the Data Provision Requirements with which an NVR registered training organisation must comply.

There are requirements to provide data on an ongoing basis, on request and as part of annual reporting.

The main ongoing data provision requirement (see Division 1 of Part 2) is for an NVR registered training organisation to provide data about VET students and VET courses either to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research (under what is called the direct reporting pathway) or to a state training authority (under what is called the indirect reporting pathway). The data must be provided before the end of the first month after the quarter in which the data becomes available to the organisation.

The National VET Regulator may grant exemptions from the main ongoing data provision requirement, under which an NVR registered training organisation can provide certain aggregate data instead. An exemption may be subject to conditions.

Other ongoing data provision requirements (see Division 2 of Part 2) include providing nil activity reports, providing privacy notices to individuals and providing data on cessation of registration.

Different ongoing data provision requirements apply in relation to nationally recognised training delivered by NVR registered training organisations as part of a school curriculum (see Division 3 of Part 2).

An NVR registered training organisation is also required to provide certain data on request by the National VET Regulator and within any timeframe specified, and to provide annual reporting in relation to quality indicators (see Part 3).

## Part 2—Ongoing data provision requirements

#### Division 1—Requirements for provision of reportable data etc.

#### 6 NVR registered training organisations to provide data

(1) If an NVR registered training organisation’s VET dataset does not comply with subsection (2), the organisation must provide data, in accordance with this Division, that will result in the VET dataset complying with subsection (2).

Note: Subsection 6A(3) provides that, where an exemption has been granted to an NVR registered training organisation under subsection 6A(1), the organisation may provide certain aggregate data instead of data otherwise required under this subsection.

(2) An NVR registered training organisation’s VET dataset complies with this subsection at a particular time if it contains all data, for each relevant VET IS described element, that is available to the organisation at that time, including in respect of:

(a) each VET student that was or is enrolled with the organisation; and

(b) each VET course that was or is provided by, or on behalf of, the organisation.

(3) Data is taken to be ***available*** to an NVR registered training organisation for the purposes of this instrument at any time after any of the following generates, or comes into possession of, the data:

(a) the organisation;

(b) a contractor or subcontractor of the organisation;

(c) an employee of a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b);

(d) a person who provides, on a voluntary basis, services relating to VET to, or on behalf of, a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b);

(e) a third party that delivers assessment or training on the organisation’s behalf.

#### 6A Exemptions from requirement to provide data

Granting of exemptions

(1) The National VET Regulator may, for the purposes of this section, grant an NVR registered training organisation an exemption from subsection 6(1) in accordance with the VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy.

Note: For variation and revocation of an exemption granted under this subsection, see subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

(2) In addition to complying with the VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy, the exemption must:

(a) be in writing; and

(b) set out:

(i) the kind of data that, under the exemption, does not need to be provided; and

(ii) the period in respect of which data does not need to be provided (which may be a period that ends at a time that the National VET Regulator later notifies in writing under subsection (3)); and

(c) specify the conditions of the exemption (if any).

(3) If:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation has been granted an exemption under subsection (1); and

(b) according to the exemption, the period in respect of which data covered by the exemption does not need to be provided is to end at a time later notified in writing by the National VET Regulator;

then the National VET Regulator must, in writing, notify the organisation of the time at which that period is to end.

Note: For variation and revocation of a notification given under this subsection, see subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

Effect of an exemption

(4) If an NVR registered training organisation has been granted an exemption under subsection (1) in relation to a kind of data in respect of a period, then:

(a) despite subsection 6(1), the organisation does not need to provide data of that kind in respect of that period; and

(b) the organisation must provide, in accordance with this Division, aggregate data of the kind set out in the VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy in respect of each quarter that starts during the period (unless the organisation is required to make a notification under section 6G in respect of the quarter); and

(c) for the purposes of this instrument, aggregate data mentioned in paragraph (b) of this subsection in respect of a quarter is taken to be first available to the organisation on the last day of that quarter.

NVR registered training organisation to comply with any applicable requirements in VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy

(5) An NVR registered training organisation that has been granted an exemption under subsection (1) must comply with any applicable requirements relating to the exemption that are set out in the VET Data Provision Exemptions Policy.

#### 6B Ways to provide reportable data

If subsection 6(1) or paragraph 6A(4)(b) requires an NVR registered training organisation to provide certain data (***reportable data***), then the organisation must provide the data in accordance with the direct reporting pathway or the indirect reporting pathway.

#### 6C Direct reporting pathway

An NVR registered training organisation provides particular reportable data in accordance with the ***direct reporting pathway*** if the organisation provides the data to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research:

(a) through a National VET data system; and

(b) in a format that satisfies any applicable requirements set out in the VET Information Standard; and

(c) within the period that:

(i) starts on the day the data was first available to the organisation; and

(ii) ends 1 month after the start of the next quarter.

#### 6D Indirect reporting pathway

An NVR registered training organisation provides particular reportable data in accordance with the ***indirect reporting pathway*** if:

(a) the organisation and a state training authority have an arrangement under which the authority agrees that, if the organisation provides any reportable data to the authority, then the authority will provide that data to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research:

(i) through a National VET data system or another VET data system; and

(ii) in a format that satisfies any applicable requirements set out in the VET Information Standard; and

(b) the organisation provides the particular reportable data to the state training authority within the period that:

(i) starts on the day the data was first available to the organisation; and

(ii) ends 1 month after the start of the next quarter.

#### 6E Inaccurate or incomplete data notifications

If an NVR registered training organisation provides reportable data that is inaccurate or incomplete, the organisation is taken, for the purposes of this instrument, not to have provided the reportable data.

#### Division 2—Other data provision requirements

#### 6F Maintaining appropriate VET data system

(1) An NVR registered training organisation must maintain a VET data system that:

(a) enables the organisation to collect and store reportable data in digital form; and

(b) if the organisation proposes to provide reportable data in accordance with the direct reporting pathway (see section 6C)—is capable of being used to provide reportable data in that way; and

(c) if the organisation proposes to provide reportable data in accordance with the indirect reporting pathway (see section 6D)—is capable of being used to provide reportable data in that way.

(2) If the NVR registered training organisation provides reportable data in accordance with the direct reporting pathway or the indirect reporting pathway, the organisation must do so using the VET data system maintained by the organisation.

#### 6G Nil activity reports

(1) An NVR registered training organisation must notify the National Centre for Vocational Education Research if, in a quarter, it has done neither of the following:

(a) enrolled new VET students;

(b) provided VET courses.

(2) The notification must be made:

(a) before the end of 1 month after the start of the next quarter; and

(b) through a National VET data system or other means agreed to in writing by the National Centre for Vocational Education Research.

#### 6H Providing privacy notices to individuals

(1) If:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation collects personal information about an individual who enrols in all or part of a VET course provided by, or on behalf of, the organisation; and

(b) the organisation reasonably believes that the personal information may be included in reportable data the organisation is required to provide in accordance with this instrument;

the organisation must provide the individual with a privacy notice in the form set out in Schedule 1.

Note: The requirement in this section is in addition to any obligation that the *Privacy Act 1988* or other laws of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory may impose on an NVR registered training organisation.

(2) The organisation must provide the individual with the privacy notice before or at the time the individual enrols in all or part of a VET course provided by, or on behalf of, the organisation.

#### 6J Providing reportable data on cessation of registration

(1) This section applies to an entity whose registration as an NVR registered training organisation is cancelled or withdrawn.

(2) The entity must ensure that all reportable data available to the entity immediately before the time of the cancellation or withdrawal has, before the time of that cancellation or withdrawal:

(a) been provided to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research:

(i) through a National VET data system; and

(ii) in a format that satisfies any applicable requirements set out in the VET Information Standard; or

(b) if the entity has an arrangement with a state training authority of the kind described in paragraph 6D(a)—been provided to that state training authority.

(3) If:

(a) the entity has provided reportable data in relation to a matter in accordance with Division 1 of this Part or subsection (2) of this section; and

(b) any of the data provided by the entity in relation to that matter is inaccurate or incomplete; and

(c) more accurate or more complete data in relation to that matter is available to the entity before the end of the extended reporting period for the entity (see subsection (5) of this section);

then the entity must, before the end of the extended reporting period, provide that more accurate and more complete data in accordance with paragraph (2)(a) or (b) of this section.

(4) This section applies despite any other provision in this Part.

Note: One effect of subsection (4) is that an entity may be required under this section to provide particular reportable data earlier than the entity would otherwise be required to provide the data under Division 1 of this Part.

(5) In this section:

***entity*** means a person or a body.

***extended reporting period*** for an entity means the period of 30 days beginning on the day on which the entity’s registration as an NVR registered training organisation is cancelled or withdrawn.

#### Division 3—Nationally recognised school training

#### 7 Providing data in relation to nationally recognised school training

(1) This section applies in relation to nationally recognised school training delivered by, or on behalf of, an NVR registered training organisation.

(2) If the organisation is not required under Division 1 of this Part to provide data in relation to the training, then the organisation must instead comply with any requirements to submit AVETMISS data in relation to the training that apply under the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy.

Note: Whether a particular NVR registered training organisation is required under Division 1 of this Part to provide data in relation to nationally recognised school training will depend on the details of the VET Information Standard, including the details of the VET IS described elements specified in that standard.

#### 7A Providing outstanding AVETMISS data before the switch day

(1) If:

(a) Division 1 of this Part requires an NVR registered training organisation to provide data in relation to nationally recognised school training delivered by, or on behalf of, the organisation; and

(b) immediately before the start of the switch day for the organisation (see subsection (2) of this section), there is AVETMISS data that relates to training of that kind that:

(i) is available to the organisation; and

(ii) has not previously been submitted by the organisation in accordance with the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy;

then the organisation must, before the switch day, submit that AVETMISS data in accordance with the requirements (except those relating to reporting timeframes) in the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1), the *switch day* for the NVR registered training organisation is the first day on which the organisation provides data on nationally recognised school training in accordance with the requirements of Division 1 of this Part.

## Part 3—Provision of data on request

#### 8 Data to be provided on request to the National VET Regulator

(1A) The National VET Regulator may, by notice in writing given to:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation; or

(b) a person (an ***applicant***) or body (also an ***applicant***) that has applied under Division 1 of Part 2 of the Act for registration, or renewal of registration, as an NVR registered training organisation;

request that the organisation or applicant provide the data specified in the request to the National VET Regulator.

(1B) The data specified in the request must be data that is covered by subsection (1) and that relates to the organisation or applicant.

(1C) The organisation or applicant must provide the data specified in the request to the National VET Regulator:

(a) within the period (if any) specified in the request; and

(b) in the manner (if any) specified in the request.

(1) For the purposes of subsection (1B), the following data is covered :

(a) information about the kind of legal entity the NVR registered training organisation is (for example, corporation, sole trader, incorporated association, partnership or government entity);

(b) copy of the organisation’s certificate of incorporation (which displays all current and historical owners and directors), or a copy of the organisation’s constitution or articles of association;

(c) any information required to assess compliance with the Financial Viability Risk Assessment Requirements;

(d) business name registration certificate;

(e) ACN;

(f) ABN;

(g) information about the type of training organisation the NVR registered training organisation is (for example, school, university, community-based adult education, other training provider);

(h) the following address details for the NVR registered training organisation:

(i) the physical address and postal address for the head office;

(ii) the physical address of the organisation’s principal place of business;

(iii) the physical address of the sites or campuses from which VET courses are delivered on a permanent basis (whether in Australia or offshore); and

(iv) website address;

(j) names of governing persons for the NVR registered training organisation;

(k) contact details for:

(i) public enquiries;

(ii) registration enquiries; and

(iii) the chief executive or other head manager;

(l) any previous identifier code as displayed on the National Register (including any such code that relates to registration with a VET Regulator in a non-referring State);

(m) whether or not the applicant, the NVR registered training organisation, or a governing person for the NVR registered training organisation, hold a similar role with respect to any other application for registration currently lodged (including where it is still being considered or where it was granted) under the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*, the *Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency Act 2011*, the *Australian Education Act 2013* or for registration with a VET Regulator in a non-referring State;

(n) any information required to assess compliance with the Fit and Proper Person Requirements;

(o) a list of the national code and title of the training packages, VET qualifications, VET accredited courses, modules or units of competency that the NVR registered training organisation or applicant (see paragraph (1A)(b) of this section) delivers or intends to deliver;

(oa) for each VET qualification, VET accredited course, module or unit of competency that is covered by paragraph (o)—evidence to demonstrate that each trainer and each assessor has the necessary training and assessment credentials (as required under the Standards for NVR Registered Training Organisations), and the industry competencies, skills and knowledge, to deliver or assess the qualification, course, module or unit;

(p) where another licensing or authorising body is responsible for licensing or otherwise authorising in relation to a VET course offered by the NVR registered training organisation, evidence of whether a licence or other relevant authorisation has been, or is to be, granted;

(q) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation delivers, or intends to deliver, any training online or by distance;

(r) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation delivers, has delivered, or intends to deliver, any training overseas;

(ra) if the NVR registered training organisation has delivered training overseas at a particular site, but no longer does so—when the organisation ceased to deliver training at that site;

(rb) if the NVR registered training organisation delivers, or intends to deliver, any training overseas at a particular site—when the organisation expects to cease to deliver training at that site (if known);

(s) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation offers, or intends to offer, or has otherwise applied to offer any training to overseas students, and if so, details of approval held under the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000* (in relation to applications that are pending, have been granted or rejected);

(t) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation intends to apply for, or already receives, Commonwealth, State or Territory government funding for training;

(u) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation delivers, or intends to deliver, training or assessment through a contract with another organisation (other than employment contracts, lease agreements or hire arrangements);

(v) where training is delivered through a contract as referred to in paragraph (u):

(i) details of the other organisation’s ABN,

(ii) contact details of the other organisation;

(iii) details about the term of the contract and the services to be provided, including the VET courses it relates to;

(iv) details about where the training services will be provided; and

(v) details about which organisation is to retain VET records associated with the training services provided under the contract;

(w) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation collects, or intends to collect, $1,500 or more in fees, including enrolment fees, tuition fees, administrative fees, fees for materials required for a course or any other amount that a learner is (or may be) required to pay to enrol, learn, train or undertake assessment with the NVR registered training organisation, and which are (or may be) paid before any relevant goods or services for which those fees have been paid are delivered by the NVR registered training organisation;

(x) whether or not the NVR registered training organisation delivers, or intends to deliver, training to students under the age of 18, and if so:

(i) details about working with children checks for any trainer, assessor or other staff member of the NVR registered training organisation who is required to hold one under a law of a state or territory; and

(ii) policies and procedures for managing these students;

(y) evidence of appropriate financial records management systems;

(z) evidence of systems that allow the NVR registered training organisation to comply with the requirements of Part 2 of this instrument;

(za) information about the ability of the NVR registered training organisation to meet requirements about information technology set out in any legislative instrument made under subsection 211(2B) of the Act; and

(zb) copy of documentation demonstrating public liability insurance coverage.

(2) If the details of any information provided under paragraphs (1)(a), (d), (e), (f), (g), (h), (j), (k), (p), (q), (r), (ra), (rb), (s), (t), (u), (v), (w) or (x) (and for paragraph (x) only in respect of whether or not training is delivered to students under 18) change because of an event, the NVR registered training organisation is required to provide updated details of the change as soon as practicable following the event.

(3) If an event occurs that results in an NVR registered training organisation no longer complying with the Financial Viability Risk Assessment Requirements or the Fit and Proper Person Requirements, the NVR registered training organisation is to provide details about its non-compliance as soon as practicable following the event.

## Part 4—Annual reporting

#### 9 Annual reporting in relation to quality indicators

An NVR registered training organisation must provide data to the National VET Regulator, by 30 June of each year, in the form of an annual summary report that details its performance during the previous calendar year against the quality indicators agreed by the Ministerial Council.

## Part 5—Application and transitional provisions

#### Division 1—Amendments made by the National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Data Provision Requirements) Amendment (Data Streamlining) Instrument 2026

#### 10 Definitions for this Division

In this Division:

***amending instrument*** means the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Data Provision Requirements) Amendment (Data Streamlining) Instrument 2026*.

***new law*** means this instrument as amended by the amending instrument.

***transition day***: see subsections 11(3), (7) and (8).

#### 11 Application of the new law—main rules

Scope of this section

(1) This section deals with the application of the new law, except to the extent that it relates to nationally recognised school training.

Note: For the application of the new law as it relates to nationally recognised school training, see section 12.

General rule

(2) The new law applies to an NVR registered training organisation:

(a) on and after the transition day for the organisation; and

(b) in relation to training that:

(i) begins to be delivered on or after the transition day; or

(ii) begins to be delivered before the transition day and has not been completed before the transition day.

Note: In relation to training other than nationally recognised school training, an NVR registered training organisation must, until the transition day for the organisation, continue to submit AVETMISS data as required under the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy (see section 7 of this instrument, as in force immediately before the commencement of the amending instrument).

Transition day

(3) Subject to this section, the ***transition day*** for an NVR registered training organisation is:

(a) for an organisation that is first registered on or after 1 January 2028—the day when the organisation is first registered; and

(b) for an organisation that is first registered before 1 January 2028, and is registered immediately before 1 January 2029—1 January 2029; and

(c) for an organisation that is first registered before 1 January 2028, but is not registered immediately before 1 January 2029—the earliest day, being 1 January 2029 or a later day, on which the organisation is next registered.

Note: The transition day may be a different day in certain circumstances (see subsections (7) and (8)).

Providing outstanding AVETMISS data before the transition day

(4) If:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation is registered immediately before the start of the transition day for the organisation; and

(b) there is AVETMISS data that:

(i) is available to the organisation immediately before the start of the transition day; and

(ii) has not previously been submitted by the organisation in accordance with the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy;

then the organisation must, before the start of the transition day, submit that AVETMISS data in accordance with the requirements (except those relating to reporting timeframes) in the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy.

Voluntary early provision of data in accordance with the new law

(5) Subsection (6) applies if:

(a) on a day (the ***early provision day***) that is before the transition day for an NVR registered training organisation, the organisation provides data for a VET IS described element to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research or a state training authority; and

(b) the data is provided in accordance with the requirements that would have applied to the organisation under Division 1 of Part 2 of the new law if the organisation’s transition day had been before the early provision day; and

(c) the provision of the data by the organisation does not contravene any arrangement between the organisation and a state training authority.

(6) To the extent that the data provided by the organisation on the early provision day is accurate and complete, the organisation is taken to satisfy any requirement to submit corresponding AVETMISS data under the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy.

Alternative arrangements—early transition

(7) If:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation is covered by paragraph (3)(b) (so that the transition day for the organisation would, apart from this subsection, be 1 January 2029); and

(b) the organisation has an arrangement with a state training authority; and

(c) the arrangement requires that, from a specified day (the ***early transition day***) that is before 1 January 2029, the organisation must provide data for VET IS described elements to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research or the state training authority; and

(d) the organisation is registered on the early transition day;

then, despite subsection (3), treat the ***transition day*** for the organisation as being the early transition day.

Alternative arrangements—late transition

(8) If:

(a) an NVR registered training organisation is first registered on or after 1 January 2028 but before 1 January 2029 (so that the transition day for the organisation would, apart from this subsection, be the day on which the organisation is first registered); and

(b) the organisation has an arrangement with a state training authority; and

(c) the arrangement requires the organisation to submit AVETMISS data in accordance with the National VET Provider Collection Data Requirements Policy for a period that starts on the day on which the organisation is first registered;

then, despite subsection (3), treat the ***transition day*** for the organisation as being:

(d) if the period mentioned in paragraph (c) of this subsection ends before 1 January 2029—the first day after the end of that period; and

(e) otherwise—1 January 2029.

#### 12 Application of the new law—nationally recognised school training

(1) The new law, to the extent that it relates to nationally recognised school training, applies to an NVR registered training organisation:

(a) on and after the alternative start day for the organisation (see subsection (2)); and

(b) in relation to nationally recognised school training that:

(i) begins to be delivered on or after the alternative start day; or

(ii) begins to be delivered before the alternative start day and has not been completed before that day.

(2) The ***alternative start day*** for an NVR registered training organisation is:

(a) if the organisation is registered on the day on which the amending instrument commences—the day on which the amending instrument commences; or

(b) otherwise—the first day, after the day on which the amending instrument commences, on which the organisation is registered.

Why we collect your personal information

As a registered training organisation (RTO), we collect your personal information so we can process and manage your enrolment in a vocational education and training (VET) course with us.

**Note to RTOs—delete this box before providing to students**: In accordance with APP 5.2(e), a collection notice should include the consequences for the individual if the personal information is not collected. How we use your personal information

We use your personal information to enable us to deliver VET courses to you, and otherwise, as needed, to comply with our obligations as an RTO.

How we disclose your personal information

We may be required by law, under the *National Vocational Education and Training Regulator Act 2011* (Cth) (NVETR Act), to disclose the personal information we collect about you to the National Centre for Vocational Education Research Ltd (NCVER). We may also be required to provide personal information to a state training authority which may then disclose that information to NCVER. NCVER is the National VET Data Custodian and is responsible for collecting, managing, analysing and communicating research and statistics about the Australian VET sector.

**Note to RTOs—delete this box before providing to students**: If any disclosures of students’ personal information to overseas recipients are likely, please include a statement notifying students of this likely disclosure and the countries in which such recipients are likely to be located (if it is practicable to specify those countries in the notice or to otherwise make the individual aware of them) in accordance with the requirements set out in APP 5.2(i) and (j).

How NCVER and other bodies handle your personal information

NCVER will collect, hold, use and disclose your personal information in accordance with the law, including the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) (Privacy Act), the NVETR Act, and the *Student Identifiers Regulation 2014* (Cth). NCVER may use and disclose your personal information for purposes that include populating authenticated VET transcripts; administration of VET; facilitation of statistics and research relating to education, including surveys and data linkage; and understanding the VET market.

NCVER may disclose your personal information to the Australian Government Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR), other relevant Commonwealth authorities, State and Territory authorities (other than registered training organisations) that deal with matters relating to VET and VET regulators for the purposes for which the legislation above permits.

NCVER may also disclose personal information to persons engaged by NCVER to conduct research on NCVER’s behalf. NCVER does not intend to disclose your personal information to any overseas recipients.

Commonwealth and State and Territory government authorities who deal with or have responsibility for VET also have contracted IT operators that design, build, test, operate, maintain and host a VET data system used to collect and/or store VET data. VET data system operators are subject to a range of strict privacy, security, and confidentiality obligations. Your personal information may be used by VET data system operators to fulfil their contractual obligations, including ensuring that the VET data system is operating properly. VET data system operators and their contractors will be required to abide by the Australian Privacy Principles.

Data submitted through a VET data system may undergo validation and quality assurance checks, which involve checking the submitted data against data held in other information systems, for example, the Unique Student Identifier Registry or the Australian Business Register.

For more information about how NCVER will handle your personal information please refer to NCVER’s Privacy Policy at https://www.ncver.edu.au/privacy.

DEWR is authorised by law, including the Privacy Act and the NVETR Act, to collect, use and disclose your personal information to fulfil its functions and activities, which include the following:

administration of VET, such as program administration, regulation, monitoring and evaluation;

facilitation of statistics and research relating to education, including surveys and data linkage;

understanding how the VET market operates for policy, workforce planning and consumer information.

DEWR may be authorised by law to share “public sector data” with third parties, including under the *Data Availability and Transparency Act 2022* (Cth) (DAT Act). “Public sector data” is defined in the DAT Act to mean “data lawfully collected, created or held by or on behalf of a Commonwealth body…” and therefore includes any data collected by the Department as part of performing VET related functions.

For more information about how DEWR will handle your personal information, please refer to the DEWR VET Privacy Notice at https://www.dewr.gov.au/national‑vet‑data/vet‑privacy‑notice.

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Laws) Declaration 2020

Instrument 2020 No. M27

I, Darren Chester, Minister for Veterans’ Affairs, make the following legislative instrument under subsection 119(7) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

Dated this 8th day of October 2020.

…Darren Chester……

**DARREN CHESTER**

Minister for Veterans’ Affairs

Contents

5 Declaration……………………………………………………………………............. 1

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) (Specified Laws) Declaration 2020*.

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on the day after this instrument is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under subsection 119(7) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*.

**4 Repeal for the purposes of the** ***Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988***

The *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Declaration 2017* as in force for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988* under item 63 of Schedule 1 to the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Legislation Amendment (Defence Force) Act 2017* is taken to be repealed.

Note: The *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Specified Laws) Declaration 2017* was in force for the purposes of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988* because of item 63 of Schedule 1 to the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Legislation Amendment (Defence Force) Act 2017.*

**5 Declaration**

For the purposes of subsection 119(7) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Defence-related Claims) Act 1988*, a law of a State or of a Territory that provides for the payment of compensation, other than workers' compensation, specified in column 1 of an item of the table in the Schedule is a specified law.

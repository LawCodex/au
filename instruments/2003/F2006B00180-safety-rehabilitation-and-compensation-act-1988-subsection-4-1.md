# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 - subsection 4(1) - Definition of Commonwealth authority

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

## Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988

### Subsection 4(1) — Definition of Commonwealth authority

Declaration by Minister of a body corporate to which this Act applies

I, ANTHONY JOHN ABBOTT, Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations, acting under subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (the Act) in relation to the definition of ***Commonwealth authority***, hereby declare under subparagraph (c)(iii) of that definition that the Australian Strategic Policy Institute Limited (ABN 77 097 369 045) is a body corporate to which this Act applies.

Dated 23/9/2003

Signed:

TONY ABBOTT

Minister for Employment and Workplace Relations

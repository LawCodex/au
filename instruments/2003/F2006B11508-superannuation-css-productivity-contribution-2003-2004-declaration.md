# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2003-2004) Declaration 2003

Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution\
(2003–2004) Declaration 2003

Statutory Rules 2003 No. 1731

I, SANDRA KAY WILSON, Branch Manager of the Superannuation Branch, Department of Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 11 June 2003

SANDRA WILSON

Delegate of the Minister for Finance and Administration

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2003–2004) Declaration 2003.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Productivity contributions

For section 110D of the S*uperannuation Act 1976*, the table in subsection 110C (1) of that Act has effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 2003, as if the amounts specified in the following table were substituted for the amounts specified in that table:

Table

| Fortnightly rate of salary | Fortnightly productivity contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $1 337.33 | $40.12 |
| $1 337.33 or more, but less than $2 155.33 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $2 155.33 or more, but less than $3 233.00 | $64.66 |
| $3 233.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**Note**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 30 June 2003.

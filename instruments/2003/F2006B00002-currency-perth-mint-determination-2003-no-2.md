# Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2003 (No. 2)

I, IAN GORDON CAMPBELL, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make this Determination under paragraph 13 (2) (b) and subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 15 September 2003

IAN CAMPBELL

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2003 (No. 2)*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on gazettal.

###### 3 Specification of coins

This Determination specifies, for a coin:

(a) the denomination and standard composition in columns 2 and 3 of Part 1 of Schedule 1; and

(b) the standard weight, the allowable variation from that weight, the dimensions and the design in columns 4, 5 and 6 of that Part.

###### 4 Application of Determination

The specifications in this Determination do not affect any other Determination of the specification of a coin.

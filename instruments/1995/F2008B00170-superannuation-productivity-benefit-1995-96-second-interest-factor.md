# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1995-96 Second Interest Factor Declaration

Statutory Rules 1995 No. 1761

\__________________

I, KIM CHRISTIAN BEAZLEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under paragraph 3E (1) (b) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 21 June 1995.

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1995-96 Second Interest Factor Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Interpretation

**2.** In this Declaration:

**“Act”** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*;

**“Days”** means:

(a) in the case of an employee who becomes a fund employee during the financial year commencing on 1 July 1995—the number of days in the period:

(i) commencing on the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1995; and

(ii) ending at the end of the day on which an amount first becomes payable under section 4f of the Act; or

(b) in the case of an employee in respect of whom an interim benefit becomes payable under section 6 of the Act during the financial year commencing on 1 July 1995—the number of days between:

(i) the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1995; and

(ii) the day on which the interim benefit became payable; or

(c) in the case of an employee who does not become a fund employee during the financial year commencing on 1 July 1995 and in respect of whom an interim benefit does not become payable during that year—the number of days between:

(i) the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1995; and

(ii) 30 June 1996.

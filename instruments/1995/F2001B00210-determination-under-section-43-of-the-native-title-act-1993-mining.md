# Determination under section 43 of the Native Title Act 1993 (Mining Act 1971 (SA)) (18/10/1995)

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

**DETERMINATION UNDER SECTION 43**

**OF THE NATIVE TITLE ACT 1993**

I, GARY THOMAS JOHNS, Special Minister of State, acting under subsection 43(1) of the *Native Title Act 1993,* determine that the following provisions comply with subsection 43(2) of the *Native Title Act 1993*:

Section 58 and Part 9B of the *Mining Act 1971 (South Australia)* as amended by the *Mining (Native Title) Amendment Act 1995 (South Australia)*

GARY THOMAS JOHNS

Special Minister of State

18 October 1995

# Declaration of Aboriginal Land (11/10/1995)

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986*

**Declaration of Aboriginal Land**

I, ROBERT EDWARD TICKNER, Minister for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Affairs, under subsection 9A (1) of the *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986* and being satisfied that:

(a) the land within the Jervis Bay National Park is of significance to the Aboriginals who are members of the Wreck Bay Aboriginal Community Council; and

(b) the land within the Jervis Bay Botanic Gardens is of significance to those Aboriginals; and

(c) it would be appropriate to grant the land referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b) to the Council; and

(d) the Council and the Director of National Parks and Wildlife have entered an agreement under which the Council agrees to grant to the Director a lease of the land referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b);

declare that all the land within the Jervis Bay National Park and all the land within the Jervis Bay Botanic Gardens is Aboriginal Land.

Dated 11th October 1995.

R E Tickner

Minister for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Affairs

[NOTE: The *Aboriginal Land Grant (Jervis Bay Territory) Act 1986* identifies the Jervis Bay National Park and the Jervis Bay Botanic Gardens.]

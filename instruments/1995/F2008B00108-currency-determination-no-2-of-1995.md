# Currency Determination No. 2 of 1995

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 2 of 1995**

I, PAUL ELLIOTT Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, acting for the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 7 April 1995.

[image]Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 2 of 1995.

**Commencement**

**2.** This Determination commences on 19 April 1995.

**Application of the Determination**

**3.** This Determination does not affect any other determination of the specifications or design of a coin.

**Specifications and design of a $2 silver coin**

**4. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of $2 the standard composition of which (except for the cameo referred to in paragraph (9) (a)) includes not less than 99.9% silver.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 62.770 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.500 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 50.30 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 4.50 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II", "AUSTRALIA", and "2 DOLLARS";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin,

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a kookaburra in flight and a smaller representation, in gold cameo, of 2 doves in flight; and

(b) the following inscriptions:

(i) "THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 2 OZ. 999 SILVER";

(ii) "1995";

(iii) the words "50 YEARS BEYOND WW II";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

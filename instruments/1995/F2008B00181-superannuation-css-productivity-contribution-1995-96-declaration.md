# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1995-96) Declaration

Statutory Rules 1995 No. 1731

\__________________

I, KIM CHRISTIAN BEAZLEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 19 June 1995.

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1995-96) Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Productivity contributions

**2.** In relation to the financial year commencing on 1 July 1995, the Table in subsection 110C (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in that Table.

**TABLE**

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $985.33 | $29.56 |
| $985.33 or more, but less than $1,587.33 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,587.33 or more, but less than $2,381.00 | $47.62 |
| $2,381.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 30 June 1995.

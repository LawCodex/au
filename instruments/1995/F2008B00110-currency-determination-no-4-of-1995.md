# Currency Determination No. 4 of 1995

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 4 of 1995**

I, PAUL ELLIOTT, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, acting for the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 11 September 1995.

[image]Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 4 of 1995.

**Commencement**

**2.** This Determination commences on 13 September 1995.

**Application of the Determination**

**3.** This Determination does not affect any other determination of the specifications or design of a coin.

**Specifications and design of a $1 silver coin**

**4. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of $1 the standard composition of which includes not less than 99.9% silver.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 31.635 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.500 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 40.60 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 4.00 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions:

(i) "ELIZABETH II"; and

(ii) "AUSTRALIA"; and

(iii) "1 DOLLAR";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a kookaburra perched on a branch of a tree; and

(b) a smaller encircled representation of a giant panda; and

(c) the inscriptions:

(i) "THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 1 OZ. 999 SILVER"; and

(ii) "1995";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**Specifications and design of a $30 silver coin**

**5. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of $30 the standard composition of which includes not less than 99.9% silver.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 1002.502 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 1.50 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 101.00 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 14.60 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions:

(i) "ELIZABETH II"; and

(ii) "AUSTRALIA"; and

(iii) "30 DOLLARS";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a kookaburra in flight; and

(b) a smaller encircled:

(i) representation of the Liberty Head (based on the United States 1895 "Morgan" dollar); and

(ii) inscription "1895"; and

(c) the inscriptions:

(i) "THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 1 KILO 999 SILVER"; and

(ii) "1995";

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

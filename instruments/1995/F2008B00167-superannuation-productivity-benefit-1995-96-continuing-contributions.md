# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1995-96 Continuing Contributions Declaration

Statutory Rules 1995 No. 1741

\__________________

I, KIM CHRISTIAN BEAZLEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 21 June 1995.

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1995-96 Continuing Contributions Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Substitution of amounts

**2.** For the purposes of section 3d of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, in relation to the financial year commencing on 1 July 1995, as if the amounts set out in the following Table were substituted for the amounts set out in the Table in that Schedule:

TABLE

| Less than $225.50 | $13.53 |
|---|---|
| $225.50 or more but less than $1,670.76 | The amount that is 6% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $1,670.76 or more | $100.25 |

# Currency Determination No. 3 of 1995

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 3 of 1995**

I, PAUL ELLIOTT, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, acting for the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 4 September 1995.

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

\______________

**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 3 of 1995.

**Commencement**

**2.** This Determination commences on 13 September 1994.

**Application of the Determination**

**3.** This Determination (other than clause 33) does not affect any other determination of the specifications or design of a coin.

**Schedule to the Act**

**4.** The Act has effect, on and after the commencement of this Determination, as if:

(a) there were included in the Schedule to the Act a reference to a denomination of “One hundred and fifty dollars”; and

(b) there were specified in that Schedule, opposite to that denomination, as the standard composition of coins of that denomination, the expression “not less than 99.99% gold”.

**Specifications and design of a 20 cent coin**

**5. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of 20 cents the standard composition of which includes copper and nickel.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 11.30 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.33 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 28.65 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 2.52 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions “ELIZABETH II”, “AUSTRALIA” and “1995”.

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) the logo of the United Nations; and

(b) the inscriptions “UNITED NATIONS FIFTIETH ANNIVERSARY” and “20 cents”.

# Private Health Insurance (Risk Equalisation Levy) Rules 2017

I, GREG HUNT, Minister for Health, make these Rules under section 10A of the *Private Health Insurance (Risk Equalisation Levy) Act 2003*.

\____________________________________

Greg Hunt

Minister for Health

19 April 2017

##### Contents

## Part 1 Preliminary

## 1. Name of Rules

These Rules are the *Private Health Insurance (Risk Equalisation Levy) Rules 2017*.

## 2. Commencement

These Rules commence on 20 May 2017.

## 3. Authority

This Rule is made under section 10A of the *Private Health Insurance (Risk Equalisation Levy) Act 2003*.

## 4. Definitions

In these rules:

***Act*** means the *Private Health Insurance (Risk Equalisation Levy) Act 2003.*

***amendment Act*** means the *Private Health Insurance (Reinsurance Trust Fund Levy) Amendment Act 2007*.

***quarter*** means the period of 3 months ending on the last day of March, June, September or December in a year.

## 5. Risk equalisation levy day

For the purposes of paragraph 6 (1) (a) of the Act, the 21st day of the second month of each quarter in a financial year is specified as a risk equalisation levy day for that financial year.

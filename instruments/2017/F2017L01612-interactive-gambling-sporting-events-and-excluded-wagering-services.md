# Interactive Gambling (Sporting Events and Excluded Wagering Services) Determination 2017

*Interactive Gambling Act 2001*

I, MITCH FIFIELD, Minister for Communications make the following determination.

Dated: 11 December 2017

MITCH FIFIELD

Minister for Communications

### Part 1—Introduction

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Interactive Gambling (Sporting Events and Excluded Wagering Services) Determination 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the following provisions of the *Interactive Gambling Act 2001*:

(a) subsection 8A(4);

(b) subsection 10A(2);

(c) subsection 10A(3).

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means *Interactive Gambling Act 2001*.

***day of play***:

(a) in relation to a multiple-day cricket match, means the part, or all of the parts, of the match that occur on a particular day (in the local time where the match occurs); and

(b) in relation to a multiple-day golf tournament, means the part, or all of the parts, of the tournament that occur on a particular day (in the local time where the tournament occurs).

***day of racing***, in relation to a multiple-day cycling race, means the part, or all of the parts, of the race that occur on a particular day (in the local time where the race occurs).

***multiple-day cricket match*** means a cricket match that occurs on more than one day (in the local time where the match occurs).

***multiple-day cycling race*** means a cycling race that occurs on more than one day (in the local time where the race occurs).

***multiple-day golf tournament*** means a golf tournament that occurs on more than one day (in the local time where the tournament occurs).

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) bet;

(b) excluded wagering service;

(c) sporting event.

### Part 2—Sporting Events

###### 5 Things taken not to be sporting events

For the purposes of subsection 10A(3) of the Act, each of the following things is taken not to be a sporting event:

(a) a multiple-day cricket match;

(b) a multiple-day cycling race;

(c) a multiple-day golf tournament.

###### 6 Things taken to be sporting events

For the purposes of subsection 10A(2) of the Act, each of the following things is taken to be a sporting event, and the outcomes of each of those things is taken not to be a contingency for the purposes of paragraph 10B(b) of the Act:

(a) a day of play of a multiple-day cricket match;

(b) a day of racing of a multiple-day cycling race;

(c) a day of play of a multiple-day golf tournament.

Note 1: Due to section 5 and this section:

(a) each day of play of a multiple-day cricket match is a sporting event; and

(b) the multiple-day cricket match is a series of sporting events.

Note 2: Due to section 5 and this section:

(a) each day of racing of a multiple-day cycling race is a sporting event; and

(b) the multiple-day cycling race is a series of sporting events.

Note 3: Due to section 5 and this section:

(a) each day of play of a multiple-day golf tournament is a sporting event; and

(b) the multiple-day golf tournament is a series of sporting events.

### Part 3—Excluded wagering services

###### 7 Conditions

Scope

(1) This section determines conditions under subsection 8A(4) of the Act for the purposes of services mentioned in subsection 8A(3) of the Act.

Note: Due to subsection 8A(3) of the Act, a service will be an excluded wagering service:

(a) to the extent to which the service relates to betting on, or on a series of, sporting events; and

(b) to the extent to which the service is not an in-play betting service;

so long as the conditions determined in this section (and any other conditions determined under subsection 8A(4) of the Act) are satisfied.

Conditions—multiple-day cricket match, cycling race or golf tournament

(2) It is a condition of a service mentioned in subsection 8A(3) of the Act that the service does not relate to betting on the outcome of, or on a contingency that may or may not happen in the course of:

(a) a multiple-day cricket match; or

(b) a day of play of a multiple-day cricket match;

where the bets are placed, made, received or accepted during a period:

(c) beginning at the start of any day of play of the match; and

(d) ending at the conclusion of that day of play.

(3) It is a condition of a service mentioned in subsection 8A(3) of the Act that the service does not relate to betting on the outcome of, or on a contingency that may or may not happen in the course of:

(a) a multiple-day cycling race; or

(b) a day of racing of a multiple-day cycling race;

where the bets are placed, made, received or accepted during a period:

(c) beginning at the start of any day of racing of the race; and

(d) ending at the conclusion of that day of racing.

(4) It is a condition of a service mentioned in subsection 8A(3) of the Act that the service does not relate to betting on the outcome of, or on a contingency that may or may not happen in the course of:

(a) a multiple-day golf tournament; or

(b) a day of play of a multiple-day golf tournament;

where the bets are placed, made, received or accepted during a period:

(c) beginning at the start of any day of play of the tournament; and

(d) ending at the conclusion of that day of play.

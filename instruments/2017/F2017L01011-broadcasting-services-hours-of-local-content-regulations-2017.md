# Broadcasting Services (Hours of Local Content) Regulations 2017

I, General the Honourable Sir Peter Cosgrove AK MC (Ret’d), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Dated 10 August 2017

Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Mitch Fifield

Minister for Communications

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In these Regulations:

***Act*** means the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*.

###### 6 Prescribed daytime hours

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of ***daytime hours*** in subsection 43C(8) of the Act, 5 am is prescribed.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***daytime hours*** in subsection 43C(8) of the Act, 8 pm is prescribed.

###### 7 Prescribed prime‑time hours

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of ***prime‑time hours*** in section 61CA of the Act, 5 am is prescribed.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***prime‑time hours*** in section 61CA of the Act, 8 pm is prescribed.

# Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Determination 2017 (No. 1)

## Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015

I, SCOTT JOHN MORRISON, Treasurer, make this determination under subsection 8 (1) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015.*

Dated 20 April 2017

SCOTT JOHN MORRISON

Treasurer

### 1. Name of Determination

This determination is the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Determination 2017 (No. 1).

### 2. Commencement

This determination commences on the day on which it is registered.

*Note:* Section 42 of the *Legislation Act 2003* (which deals with disallowance of legislative instruments) does not apply to this instrument: see subsection 8 (3) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

### 3. Crediting of amounts

For subsection 8 (1) of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*, $275,000,000 is to be credited to the Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account on 1 May 2017.

*Note:* The Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) special account is the special account established by section 7 of the *Fuel Indexation (Road Funding) Special Account Act 2015*.

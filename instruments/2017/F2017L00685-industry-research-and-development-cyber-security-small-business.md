###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: ***Entity*** is defined in the Act.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***CREST ANZ*** means CREST Australia New Zealand Ltd.

Note: CREST is short for the Council of Registered Ethical Security Testers.

***small business*** means an entity that:

(a) has an ABN, and is registered, within the meaning given by section 195‑1 of the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*; and

(b) employs less than 20 people.

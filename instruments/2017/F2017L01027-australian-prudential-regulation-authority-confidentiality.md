# Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination No 1 of 2017

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (confidentiality) determination

No 1 of 2017

Information provided by ADIs under Reporting Standard ARS 731.1, ARS 731.3A, ARS 731.3B and ARS 731.4

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998

I, Katrina Ellis, a delegate of APRA, under paragraph 57(2)(b) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998*, DETERMINE that information contained in a reporting document described in the Schedule does not contain confidential information.

This determination has effect on the date of registration on the Federal Register of Legislation under the *Legislation Act 2003.*

*Note:* The effect of this determination is that the non-confidential information may be published under subsection 56(5C) of the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act.*

Dated: 08 August 2017

[Signed]

\_____________________

Katrina Ellis

General Manager

Data Analytics

**Interpretation**

In this determination:

***ADI*** means an authorised deposit-taking institution within the meaning of the *Banking Act 1959*.

***APRA*** means the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority established under the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998.*

***ARS 731.1*** means *Reporting Standard ARS 731.1 International Banking Statistics – Locational Data*, or another reporting standard in substantially the same terms replacing it, made under section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***ARS 731.3A*** means *Reporting Standard ARS 731.3A International Banking Statistics – Immediate and Ultimate Risk Exposures – Domestic Entity*, or another reporting standard in substantially the same terms replacing it, made under section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***ARS 731.3B*** means *Reporting Standard ARS 731.3B International Banking Statistics – Immediate and Ultimate Risk Exposures – Foreign Entity*, or another reporting standard in substantially the same terms replacing it, made under section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***ARS 731.4*** means *Reporting Standard ARS 731.4 International Banking Statistics – Balance Sheet Items*, or another reporting standard in substantially the same terms replacing it, made under section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

***reporting document*** means a reporting document within the meaning of section 13 of the *Financial Sector (Collection of Data) Act 2001.*

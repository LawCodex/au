# Migration (IMMI 17/126: Impairment Rating) Instrument 2017

I, Peter Dutton, Minister for Immigration and Border Protection, make the following instrument.

Dated 10 October 2017

PETER DUTTON

THE HON PETER DUTTON MP

Minister for Immigration and Border Protection

Contents

###### Name

This instrument is the *Migration (IMMI 17/126: Impairment Rating) Instrument 2017.*

This instrument may be cited as IMMI 17/126.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 1.15AA(1)(c) of the Regulations.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Regulations*** means *Migration Regulations 1994*.

###### 5 Impairment Rating

For the purposes of paragraph 1.15AA(1)(c) of the Regulations the impairment rating is 30.

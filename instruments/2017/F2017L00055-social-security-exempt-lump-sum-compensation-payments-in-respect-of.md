**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Revocation**

*Social Security Exempt Lump Sum (Compensation payments in respect of certain World War 2 internments) (DEWR) Determination 2007*

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***compensation payment*** means either:

(a) a payment made to World War Two Japanese Internees under the *Compensation (Japanese Internment) Act 2001*;

(b) a payment for North Korean Internees under Part 2 of the *Veterans’ Entitlement (Clarke Review) Act 2004*; or

(c) a payment for World War Two European Internees under *Schedule 5 of the Social Security and Veterans’ Affairs Legislation Amendment (One-off Payments and Other 2007 Budget Measures) Act*.

**6 Exempt Lump Sums**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the amount of a compensation payment is an exempt lump sum.

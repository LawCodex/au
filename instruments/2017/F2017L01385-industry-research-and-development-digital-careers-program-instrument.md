###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***ICT*** means information and communications technology.

###### 5 Prescribed program

For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the program specified in item 1 of the following table is prescribed.

<tr>
<td colspan="3">Prescribed program</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Item</td>
<td>Column 1<br>Program</td>
<td>Column 2<br>Description and purpose of the program</td>

<tr>
<td>1</td>
<td>Digital Careers Program</td>
<td>Financial assistance to address Australia’s ICT skills shortage by:<br>(a) increasing awareness, interest and participation among school students in computational thinking and digital technologies; and<br>(b) increasing awareness of career diversity and job opportunities for students studying ICT, science, technology, engineering, mathematics and related disciplines; and<br>(c) providing educational and training material and professional development for educators delivering the digital technology curriculum and coordinating associated activities.</td>
</tr>
</table>

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the powers of the Parliament to make laws with respect to the following are specified:

(a) external affairs (within the meaning of paragraph 51(xxix) of the Constitution);

(b) postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution).

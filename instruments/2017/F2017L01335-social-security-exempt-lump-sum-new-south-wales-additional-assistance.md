3 **Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

4 **Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Additional Assistance Hire Vehicle Payment*** means a one-off lump sum payment made by Transport for NSW under Schedule 3 of the *Point to Point Transport (Taxis and Hire Vehicles) Act 2016 No 34* (NSW) to the holder of an eligible hire vehicle licence.

**5 Exempt lump sum**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of an Additional Assistance Hire Vehicle Payment is an exempt lump sum.

# Determination of Day Applications for HFC Quotas for the First HFC Quota Allocation Period Must Be Given 2017

I, Josh Frydenberg, Minister for the Environment and Energy, under subregulation 44(3) of the *Ozone Protection and Synthetic Greenhouse Gas Management Regulations 1995* (the ***Regulations***), determine 22 September 2017 as the day on or before which applications for HFC quotas for the calendar years in the first HFC quota allocation period must be given.

Note: Pursuant to regulation 41 of the Regulations:

(a) An HFC quota allocation period is 2 years.

(b) The first HFC quota allocation period starts on 1 January 2018.

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

Dated 1 September 2017

JOSH FRYDENBERG

Minister for the Environment and Energy

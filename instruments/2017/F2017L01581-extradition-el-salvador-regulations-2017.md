Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Michael Keenan

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Extradition Act 1988*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Extradition Act 1988*.

###### 5 Declaration of El Salvador as an extradition country

For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of ***extradition country*** in section 5 of the Act, El Salvador is declared to be an extradition country.

###### 6 Application of Act

For the purposes of paragraph 11(1)(b) of the Act, the Act applies in relation to El Salvador subject to modification of paragraph 17(2)(a) of the Act by omitting ‘45 days’ and substituting ‘60 days’.

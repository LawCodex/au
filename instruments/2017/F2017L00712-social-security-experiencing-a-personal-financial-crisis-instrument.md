###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 19DA(5) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this Instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*;

***Administration Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*;

***extreme circumstance*** includes, but is not limited to:

(a) a flood;

(b) a fire;

(c) an earthquake; and

(d) a storm;

***humanitarian visa*** means:

(a) a Subclass 200 (Refugee) visa;

(b) a Subclass 201 (In-country Special Humanitarian) visa;

(c) a Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian) visa;

(d) a Subclass 203 (Emergency Rescue) visa; and

(e) a Subclass 204 (Woman at Risk) visa;

***start day*** means the day worked out in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Administration Act.

*Note:* The following expressions are defined in the Act:

in gaol (see subsection 23(5)); and

psychiatric confinement (see subsection 23(8)).

###### 5 Experiencing a personal financial crisis

A person satisfies the circumstances in subsection 19DA(4) of the Act if at some time in the 4 weeks immediately before the person’s start day (worked out disregarding clause 5 of Schedule 2 to the Administration Act):

(a)the person was in gaol or psychiatric confinement;

(b)the person:

(i) first entered Australia; and

(ii) was the holder of a humanitarian visa on that entry to Australia; or

(c)the person’s principal place of residence was lost or sustained major damage as a result of an extreme circumstance.

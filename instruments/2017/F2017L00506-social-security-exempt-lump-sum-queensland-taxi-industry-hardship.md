### 3 Authority

### This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Social Security Act 1991.

### 4 Definitions

### In this instrument:

### Act means the Social Security Act 1991.

### Queensland Taxi Industry Hardship Assistance Payment means a one-off lump sum payment made by the Queensland Government under the Taxi and Limousine Industry Assistance Scheme Regulation 2016 (Qld) to either:

(a) the holder and operator of a taxi or limousine service licence; or

(b) the holder or operator of a taxi or limousine service licence; or

(c) both (a) and (b).

### For the holder or operator of more than one licence, the total payment amount is capped at the sum calculated for a maximum of 10 taxi or limousine service licences.

### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

### For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of an Queensland Taxi Industry Hardship Assistance Payment is an exempt lump sum.

3 **Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

4 **Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***pensioner concession card*** has the same meaning as in the Act.

***seniors health card*** has the same meaning as in the Act.

***Special Energy Bonus*** means a one-off payment of $125 made by Aurora Energy Pty Ltd, an organisation owned by the State of Tasmania, during the 2017-18 financial year, to pensioner concession card and seniors health card holders in Tasmania to assist with their electricity bills.

**5 Exempt lump sum**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a Special Energy Bonus is an exempt lump sum.

Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

George Brandis QC

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Freedom of Information Act 1982*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Freedom of Information Act 1982*.

###### 6 Prescribed authorities

For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***prescribed authority*** in subsection 4(1) of the Act, Aboriginal Hostels Limited is declared to be a prescribed authority for the purposes of the Act.

###### 8 Information Commissioner’s annual report—giving information

For the purposes of paragraph 93(3)(a) of the Act, the information must be given:

(a) if guidelines under section 93A of the Act refer to information being given on a quarterly basis—before the end of 21 days after each quarter ending on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September or 31 December; and

(b) if guidelines under section 93A of the Act refer to information being given on an annual basis—before the end of 31 July after each financial year.

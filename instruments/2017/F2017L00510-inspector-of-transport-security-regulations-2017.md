###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Inspector of Transport Security Act 2006*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act, including the following:

(a) coronial inquiry;

(b) Inspector;

(c) international agreement.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Inspector of Transport Security Act 2006*.

###### 6 Form of identity card

For the purposes of paragraph 40(2)(a) of the Act, the form of an identity card is a form that includes the following information legibly:

(a) the identifying number of the card;

(b) the date of issue, and the date of expiry, of the card;

(c) the name of the person to whom the card is issued;

(d) an example of the signature of the person;

(e) identification of the powers under Division 2 of Part 5 of the Act that the person can exercise.

###### 7 Australia’s international obligations affecting Inspector’s powers and functions

For the purposes of section 81 of the Act (which requires the Inspector’s powers and functions to be exercised and performed consistently with obligations under international agreements), the obligations are those in paragraphs 5.12 and 6.2 of Annex 13 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation done at Chicago on 7 December 1944 (that was adopted in accordance with that Convention).

Note: The Convention is in Australian Treaty Series 1957 No. 5 ([1957] ATS 5) and could in 2017 be viewed in the Australian Treaties Library on the AustLII website (http://www.austlii.edu.au).

###### 8 Criteria for person to be delegated Inspector’s powers and functions

For the purposes of subparagraph 82(1)(c)(ii) of the Act (which is about criteria for a person to be delegated the Inspector’s powers and functions), the criteria are:

(a) the person has experience or other expertise relevant to the mode of transport to which the powers or functions to be delegated relate; or

(b) the person has suitable experience in, or training relevant to, security investigations; or

(c) the person works in a transport industry to which the powers or functions to be delegated relate; or

(d) the person has experience or other technical expertise relevant to the principal matter to which the powers or functions to be delegated relate.

###### 9 Fee for attendance at coronial inquiry

(1) For the purposes of subsection 88(2) of the Act, this section provides for the amount, and due date for payment, of a fee payable by a State or Territory under subsection 88(1) of the Act for a person’s attendance as a witness at a coronial inquiry.

(2) The amount of the fee is the sum of the following amounts:

(a) the person’s salary for the duration of his or her:

(i) attendance at the inquiry; and

(ii) necessary travel to and from the inquiry;

subject to subsection (3);

(b) reasonable costs incurred by the person, and payable by the Commonwealth, for transport between the person’s usual place of residence and the place where he or she attends the inquiry;

(c) if the attendance requires the person’s absence overnight from his or her usual place of residence—reasonable costs incurred by the person, and payable by the Commonwealth, for meals and accommodation during the absence;

(d) if:

(i) the Commonwealth incurs legal expenses relating to the attendance (such as the provision of legal representation at the inquiry); and

(ii) the Inspector is satisfied that it is appropriate to seek reimbursement of those expenses from the State or Territory;

the amount of those expenses.

(3) The amount described in paragraph (2)(a) cannot exceed:

(a) in any case—$1,000; and

(b) in the case of attendance and travel for less than a full day—the amount worked out assuming that the person’s salary for each hour, or part of an hour, of attendance or travel were $133.33.

(4) The due date for payment of the fee is the 30th day after the last day of the inquiry.

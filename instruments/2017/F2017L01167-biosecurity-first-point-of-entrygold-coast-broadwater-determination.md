For more information on any uncommenced amendments, see the series page on the Legislation Register for the compiled law.

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

Contents

### Part 1—Preliminary

###### 1 Name

This is the *Biosecurity (First Point of Entry—Gold Coast Broadwater) Determination 2017*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 229(1) of the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in the *Biosecurity Act 2015*, including the following:

approved arrangement;

goods;

vessel.

In this instrument:

***goods*** means goods:

(a) that are subject to biosecurity control; or

(b) in relation to which an exposed goods order is in force.

***non‑commercial vessel*** has the meaning given by section 5 of the *Biosecurity Regulation 2016*.

***vessel*** means vessels that are subject to biosecurity control.

***waste*** means goods subject to biosecurity control, or in relation to which an exposed goods order is in force, that have been surrendered by the owner as waste goods.

### Part 2—First point of entry

###### 5 First point of entry—vessels

Gold Coast Broadwater is a first point of entry for non‑commercial vessels.

###### 6 First point of entry—goods

Gold Coast Broadwater is a first point of entry for each of the following classes of goods:

(a) personal effects carried as accompanied traveller baggage;

(b) waste.

### Part 4—Conditions

###### 8 Conditions

Waste from vessels must be:

(a) stored, transported and treated under an approved arrangement with the Department of Agriculture and Water Resources; or

(b) removed, stored and transported from vessels under the supervision of a Biosecurity Officer.

### Part 5—Period of effect

###### 9 Period of effect

This instrument is in effect until 16 June 2019.

**Editorial changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* authorises First Parliamentary Counsel to make editorial and presentational changes to a compiled law in preparing a compilation of the law for registration. The changes must not change the effect of the law. Editorial changes take effect from the compilation registration date.

If the compilation includes editorial changes, the endnotes include a brief outline of the changes in general terms. Full details of any changes can be obtained from the Office of Parliamentary Counsel.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe the amendment to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, the amendment is incorporated into the compiled law and the abbreviation “(md)” added to the details of the amendment included in the amendment history.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the abbreviation “(md not incorp)” is added to the details of the amendment included in the amendment history.

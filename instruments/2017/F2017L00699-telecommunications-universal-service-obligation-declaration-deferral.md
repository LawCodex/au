# Telecommunications Universal Service Obligation (Declaration Deferral Period) Declaration 2017

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Telecommunications (Consumer Protection and Service Standards) Act 1999*

**Telecommunications Universal Service Obligation**

**(Declaration Deferral Period)**

**Declaration 2017**

I, MITCH FIFIELD, Minister for Communications, make the following Declaration.

Dated 16 June 2017

MITCH FIFIELD

Minister for Communications

###### Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Telecommunications Universal Service Obligation (Declaration Deferral Period) Declaration 2017*.

###### 2. Commencement

This Declaration commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

3\. Authority

This Declaration is made under paragraphs 8J(1)(b) and 8K(1)(b) of the Act*.*

###### 4. Definitions

In this Declaration:

***Act*** means the *Telecommunications (Consumer Protection and Service Standards) Act 1999*.

Note: The term ***declaration deferral period*** is defined in sections 8J and 8K of the Act.

###### 5. Declaration

The 24-month period starting immediately after this Declaration is made is the declaration deferral period for the purposes of sections 8J and 8K of the Act.

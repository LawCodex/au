# Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Heritage Protection Regulations 2017

I, General the Honourable Sir Peter Cosgrove AK MC (Ret’d), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Peter Cosgrove

Governor‑General

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Heritage Protection Act 1984*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Heritage Protection Act 1984*.

### Part 2—Matters prescribed for purposes of Act

###### 6 Identity cards for authorized officers

(1) For the purposes of subsection 17(2) of the Act, the form in Schedule 1 is prescribed.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 17(3)(b) of the Act, the following particulars of an identity card are prescribed:

(a) the name of the authorized officer;

(b) the identity card number;

(c) the date that appears on the identity card.

###### 7 Prescribed authority

For the purposes of paragraph 21(1)(c) of the Act, the National Museum of Australia is a prescribed authority for safekeeping.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 93B(2) of the *Defence Act 1903*.

###### 4 Prohibited substance tests

A test is a prohibited substance test for the purposes of Part VIIIA of the *Defence Act 1903* if it involves testing a sample of hair or saliva.

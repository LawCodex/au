# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Declaration 2017

*Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*

I, MICHAELIA CASH, Minister for Employment, make this declaration under subparagraph (c) (iii) of the definition of *Commonwealth authority* in subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 20 November 2017

MICHAELIA CASH

Minister for Employment

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This is the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Declaration 2017.

###### 2 Commencement

This declaration commences on 1 October 2017.

###### 3 Authority

This declaration is made under subparagraph (c)(iii) of the definition of *Commonwealth authority* in subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (the Act).

###### 4 Declaration

For the purposes of subsection 4(1) of the Act in relation to the definition of *Commonwealth authority,* I declare that under subparagraph (c)(iii) of that definition that Aboriginal Hostels Ltd (ACN 008 504 587) is a body corporate to which the Act applies.

**5 Repeal**

Item 3 of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Declaration and Specification) Notice 2007(1)* (F2007L02005) is repealed.

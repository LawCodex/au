**3. Authority**

This instrument is made under section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4. Revocation**

This instrument revokes the *Social Security (Local Area Coordination Scheme) Determination 2000.*

**5. Personal care support scheme**

(1) The scheme for the provision of personal care support known as *Direct Consumer Funding* is approved for the purposes of section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

(2) In this instrument, *Direct Consumer Funding* means an agreement by the Northern Territory Department of Health to provide funding through its Individual Support Package to a person with a disability, or on that person’s behalf to their carer, for the payment of support or services by a third party.

# Education Services for Overseas Students (Publishing Results of Enforcement Action) Instrument 2017

I, Karen Sandercock, Group Manager, International Group, Department of Education and Training, make the following legislative instrument under subsection 170A(4) of the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

Dated 26 October 2017

Karen Sandercock

Group Manager, International Group, Department of Education and Training

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Education Services for Overseas Students (Publishing Results of Enforcement Action) Instrument 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 170A(4) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***enforcement action*** means action taken in relation to a provider under Part 6 of the *Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000*.

***home website*** means the website the ESOS agency normally uses to communicate information to the public.

Note: A number of expressions used in this instrument are defined in section 5 of the Act, including the following:

(a) ESOS agency; and

(b) provider.

###### 5 Publication of enforcement action information

(1) An ESOS agency may publish information about the matters specified in subsection 170A(2) of the Act, on its home website.

# AD/DHC-8/34 Amdt 2 - Black Thermal Insulation

AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE

On the commencement date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below repeals Airworthiness Directive (**AD**) AD/DHC-8/34 Amdt 1 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001 (1) of CASR 1998 and subsection 33 (3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct an unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

<td>Background:</td>
<td colspan="3">Operators have reported corrosion forming on areas of the aircraft where black film thermal insulation blankets are being used. De Havilland’s investigation found that the black film, which covers these insulation blankets, contains carbon. If condensation is present, corrosion may occur in places where the black film is in contact with any aluminium structure.<br>Amendment 1 of this AD was issued in response to a revision of the Transport Canada AD which changed the applicability to align with the applicability of the latest revision of the mandated service bulletins.<br>Amendment 2 of this AD is issued to correct two minor typographical errors in the serial number range of the last row of the Requirement Table.</td>

**Schedule 1—Amendments to health support allowance**

***Defence Determination 2016/19, Conditions of service***

<table>
<tr>
<td>1</td>

</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="2">Omit section, substitute:</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>4.9.17</td>
<td colspan="2">Health support allowance – Members on Reserve service</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>1.</td>
<td colspan="2">A member of the Reserves who meets all of the following conditions during a financial year may be eligible for a payment of $600 under this section.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td>They serve 20 days of Reserve service during the financial year.</td>
</tr>

</tr>
<tr>

<td>They have not been paid an allowance under section 4.9.16 during the same financial year.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2.</td>
<td colspan="2">To be entitled to the payment a member must meet all of the following conditions before 30 January of the following financial year.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>a.</td>
<td>The member must make a declaration in the form and manner authorised by the CDF.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>b.</td>
<td>The member must provide the information specified by the CDF.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td>c.</td>
<td>The member must provide the documents specified by the CDF.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3.</td>
<td colspan="2">Defence is authorised to collect sensitive information for the purposes of this benefit.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>

</tr>

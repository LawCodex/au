### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***NSW Stolen Generations Reparations Scheme*** means a scheme operated by the NSW Department of Education to provide a one-off ex gratia lump sum payment of up to $75,000, paid by the NSW Department of Education to Stolen Generations survivors found to have been removed by, committed to, or otherwise come into the care of the Aborigines Protection Board or Aborigines Welfare Board up until the *Aborigines Protection Act 1909* was repealed on 20 March 1969.

***Stolen Generations Funeral Assistance Fund*** means a fund to provide a one-off lump sum payment of $7,000 made by the NSW Department of Education to contribute to funeral costs for Stolen Generations survivors found to have been removed by, committed to, or otherwise under the care of the Aborigines Protection Board or the Aborigines Welfare Board up until the *Aborigines Protection Act 1909* was repealed on 20 March 1969, or otherwise deemed eligible under the Stolen Generations Reparations Scheme.

***Aboriginal Trust Fund Repayment Scheme*** means an *ex gratia* repayment of $11,000 made by the NSW Department of Education on a case by case basis as part of the residual management of this Scheme to eligible claimants who did not receive a repayment when the Scheme was operational during the period 2005 to 2011.

### 5 Exempt lump sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a payment made under the Stolen Generations Reparations Scheme, the Stolen Generations Funeral Assistance Fund or the Aboriginal Trust Fund Repayment Scheme is an exempt lump sum.

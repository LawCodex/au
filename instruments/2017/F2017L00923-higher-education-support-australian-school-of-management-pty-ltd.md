# Higher Education Support (Australian School of Management Pty Ltd) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2017

I, Katerina Lawler, delegate of the Minister for Education and Training, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 11 July 2017

Katerina Lawler

A/g Branch Manager

Funding Policy and Legislation Branch

Higher Education Group

Department of Education and Training\
Delegate of the Minister for Education and Training

###### 1 Name

This is the *Higher Education Support (Australian School of Management Pty Ltd) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered in the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument **Act** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval as a Higher Education Provider

The approval of the Australian School of Management Pty Ltd

(ACN: 138 545 605, ABN: 42 138 545 605) is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act:

The legislative instrument *Higher Education Provider Approval No. 2 of 2012* (F2012L00977) is repealed.

# Australian Education (Financial Assistance for Non-participating States and Territories) Determination 2017

*Australian Education Act 2013*

I, SIMON BIRMINGHAM, Minister for Education and Training, make the following determination under subsections 65(3) and 65(7) of the *Australian Education Act 2013*.

Dated 19 October 2017

SIMON BIRMINGHAM

Minister for Education and Training

###### 1 Name of determination

This determination is the *Australian Education (Financial Assistance for Non-participating States and Territories) Determination 2017.*

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This determination is made under subsections 65(3) and 65(7) of the *Australian Education Act 2013.*

###### 4. Definition

In this determination:

***the Act*** means the *Australian Education Act 2013*.

###### 6 Total amount

For subsection 65(3) of the Act, the total amount of financial assistance that is payable to all non-participating States and Territories for 2016 for the non-participating schools located in those States and Territories under Part 4 of the Act is $3,692,666,005.

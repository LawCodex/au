### 3 Authority

### This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Social Security Act 1991.

### 4 Definitions

### In this instrument:

### Act means the Social Security Act 1991.

### Victorian industry transition assistance payment means a one-off ex-gratia lump sum payment made by the Victorian Taxi Services Commission to an entity that:

### (a) has been registered with the Commission as a taxi or hire car licence holder continuously from the 15 August 2016, and

### (b) is registered with the Commission as a taxi or hire car licence holder at the time of payment.

### Where an entity holds more than four taxi or hire car licences, the payment will be in respect of the four highest value licences.

### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

### For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a Victorian industry transition assistance payment is an exempt lump sum.

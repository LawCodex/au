3 **Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

4 **Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Queensland Taxi Transitional Assistance Payment*** means a one-off lump sum payment made by the Queensland Government under the *Taxi and Limousine Industry Assistance Scheme Regulation 2016* (Qld) to the holder of an appropriate taxi or limousine service licence. For the holder of more than one taxi licence, the payment is capped at $40,000.

**5 Exempt lump sum**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of a Queensland Taxi Transitional Assistance Payment is an exempt lump sum.

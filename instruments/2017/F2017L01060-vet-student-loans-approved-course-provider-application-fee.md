# VET Student Loans (Approved Course Provider Application Fee) Determination 2017

I, Michele Bruniges, Secretary of the Department of Education and Training, make this legislative instrument under subsection 31(1) of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016*.

Dated 17 August 2017

Dr Michele Bruniges AM

Secretary of the Department of Education and Training

###### 1 Name of instrument

This instrument is the *VET Student Loans (Approved Course Provider Application Fee) Determination 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 31(1) of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016*.

###### 4 Fees for applications

For subsection 31(1) of the *VET Student Loans Act 2016*, the fee for making an application for approval as an approved course provider is $5 110.

# Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2017—Barcelona and Cambrils, Spain

**Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2017 — Barcelona and Cambrils, Spain**

*Social Security Act 1991*

I, MALCOLM BLIGH TURNBULL, Prime Minister, make this declaration under subsection 35B(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991.*

Dated 20 September 2017

MALCOLM BLIGH TURNBULL

Prime Minister

**1 Name of declaration**

This declaration is the *Social Security (Declared Overseas Terrorist Act) Declaration 2017—Barcelona and Cambrils, Spain*

**2 Commencement**

This declaration commences on the day after this instrument is registered.

**3 Definitions**

In this declaration:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***terrorist act*** has the same meaning as in subsection 100.1(1) of the *Criminal Code.*

**4 Declared overseas terrorist act**

For the purposes of subsection 35B(1) of the Act, the following terrorist acts are a declared overseas terrorist act:

1\) The attack at approximately 17:00 hours (local time) on Thursday 17 August 2017 along Las Ramblas between Plaça de Catalunya and Plaça Boqueria in the Las Ramblas district, Barcelona, Spain.

2\) The attack at approximately 1:00 hours (local time) on Friday 18 August 2017 on the seafront promenade near the Nautical Club of Cambrils, Passeig de les Palmeres, Cambrils, Spain.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *VET Student Loans (Charges) Act 2016*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The following expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act:

(a) approved course provider;

(b) approved course provider charge.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *VET Student Loans (Charges) Act 2016*.

***approved course*** has the same meaning as in the *VET Student Loans Act 2016.*

***census day*** has the same meaning as in the *VET Student Loans Act 2016.*

***large provider***: see section 5.

***medium provider***: see section 6.

***small provider***: see section 7.

***VET student loan*** has the same meaning as in the *VET Student Loans Act 2016.*

###### 5 Large providers

An approved course provider is a ***large provider*** for a financial year if the total number of VET student loans approved for students of the provider by the Secretary for approved courses with at least one census day in the financial year is more than 1,000.

###### 6 Medium providers

An approved course provider is a ***medium provider*** for a financial year if the total number of VET student loans approved for students of the provider by the Secretary for approved courses with at least one census day in the financial year is not less than 200 nor more than 1,000.

###### 7 Small providers

An approved course provider is a ***small provider*** for a financial year if the total number of VET student loans approved for students of the provider by the Secretary for approved courses with at least one census day in the financial year is less than 200.

###### 8 Amount of charge

For the purposes of paragraph 7(a) of the Act, the prescribed amount of approved course provider charge payable by an approved course provider for a financial year is as follows:

(a) if the approved course provider is a small provider for the financial year—$1,280;

(b) if the approved course provider is a medium provider for the financial year—$12,480;

(c) if the approved course provider is a large provider for the financial year—$62,870.

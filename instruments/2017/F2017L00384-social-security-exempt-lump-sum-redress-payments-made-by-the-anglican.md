###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Anglican Diocese of Brisbane Redress Payment*** means a payment made by The Corporation of the Synod of the Diocese of Brisbane (ACN 32 025 287 736) under the Anglican Diocese of Brisbane’s policies and procedures for redress payments to persons who were victims of child abuse or sexual misconduct by a church worker in the context of, or related to, their church role.

###### 5 Exempt Lump Sum

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of an Anglican Diocese of Brisbane Redress Payment is an exempt lump sum.

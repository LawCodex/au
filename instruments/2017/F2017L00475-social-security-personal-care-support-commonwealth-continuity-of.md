#### 3. Personal care support scheme

(1) The scheme for the provision of personal care support known as the *Commonwealth Continuity of Support (CoS) Programme – Direct Funding Individual Support Packages* is an approved scheme for the purposes of section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

(2) In this determination, the *Commonwealth Continuity of Support Programme – Direct Funding Individual Support Packages* means the Direct Funding Model under the Individual Support Packages sub-programme which operates under the Commonwealth Continuity of Support (CoS) Programme.

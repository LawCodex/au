# Radiocommunications (Register of Radiocommunications Licences) Determination 2017

made under sections 144, 147 and 149 of the

Radiocommunications Act 1992

**Compilation No. 5**

**Compilation date:** 23 September 2025

**Includes amendments up to:** F2025L01056

Prepared by the Australian Communications and Media Authority, Melbourne

**About this compilation**

**This compilation**

This is a compilation of the *Radiocommunications (Register of Radiocommunications Licences) Determination 2017* that shows the text of the law as amended and in force on 23 September 2025 (the ***compilation date***).

The notes at the end of this compilation (the ***endnotes***) include information about amending laws and the amendment history of provisions of the compiled law.

**Uncommenced amendments**

The effect of uncommenced amendments is not shown in the text of the compiled law. The details of amendments made up to, but not commenced at, the compilation date are underlined in the endnotes. Any uncommenced amendments affecting the law are accessible on the Register (www.legislation.gov.au).

**Application, saving and transitional provisions**

If the operation of a provision or amendment of the compiled law is affected by an application, saving or transitional provision that is not included in this compilation, details are included in the endnotes.

**Modifications**

If the compiled law is modified by another law, the compiled law operates as modified but the modification does not amend the text of the law. Accordingly, this compilation does not show the text of the compiled law as modified. Any modifications affecting the law are accessible on the Register.

**Self‑repealing provisions**

If a provision of the compiled law has been repealed in accordance with a provision of the law, details are included in the endnotes.

###### 1 Name

This is the *Radiocommunications (Register of Radiocommunications Licences) Determination 2017*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under sections 144, 147 and 149 of the *Radiocommunications Act 1992*.

###### 5 Definitions

In this Determination:

***ACN*** has the same meaning as it has in section 9 of the *Corporations Act 2001*.

***Act*** means the *Radiocommunications Act 1992.*

***effective occupied bandwidth****,* in relation to a transmitter, means the minimum width of a frequency band having fixed upper and lower limits that is necessary to contain 99% of the true mean power of the transmitter’s emission at any time.

***emission centre frequency****,* in relation to a transmitter, means the frequency midway between the lower and upper frequency limits of the transmitter’s effective occupied bandwidth.

***emission designator*** has the meaning given by section 7.

***fixed receiver*** means a receiver that is principally located at a fixed point.

***fixed transmitter*** means a transmitter that is principally located at a fixed point.

***group of receivers*** has the meaning given by section 9.

***group of transmitters*** has the meaning given by section 8.

***interference impact certificate*** means a certificate issued by an accredited person under subsection 145(3) of the Act.

***Register*** means the Register of Radiocommunications Licences established under section 143 of the Act.

***s.145 determination*** means, in respect of a spectrum licence, the determination of unacceptable levels of interference, made under section 145 of the Act, that is specified in that spectrum licence.

Note: Each s.145 determination is a legislative instrument and is available, free of charge, from the Federal Register of Legislation and www.legislation.gov.au.

***Tax Determination*** means:

(a) the *Radiocommunications (Transmitter Licence Tax) Determination 2025*; or

(b) if a later instrument replaces that determination – the later instrument.

Note 1: The *Radiocommunications (Transmitter Licence Tax) Determination 2025* is a legislative instrument and is available, free of charge, from the Federal Register of Legislation at www.legislation.gov.au.

Note 2: A number of other expressions used in this instrument are defined in the Act or the *Radiocommunications (Interpretation) Determination 2015*, including the following:

ACMA;

(aa) area-wide licence;

(ab) area-wide receive licence;

(aba) fixed licence;

(ac) PTS licence;

radiocommunications receiver;

radiocommunications transmitter;

Radio Regulations;

receiver; and

transmitter.

(2) In this instrument:

(a) a site is in a ***high density area*** if it is in the Sydney area, the Melbourne area or the Brisbane area, within the meaning given to those expressions by the Tax Determination; and

(b) a site is in a ***medium density area*** if it is in the Perth area, the Adelaide area or the Newcastle area, within the meaning given to those expressions by the Tax Determination; and

(c) a site is in a ***low density area*** if it is both:

(i) in the East Australian area, the Western Australian area, the Tasmania area or the Darwin area, within the meaning given to those expressions by the Tax Determination; and

(ii) not in a high density area or a medium density area.

###### 7 Emission designator

In this Determination, a reference to an emission designator, in relation to a transmitter operated under an apparatus licence or a spectrum licence, is a reference to the designation of the transmitter’s emission worked out in accordance with the Radio Regulations published by the International Telecommunication Union.

Note: The Radio Regulations are available from the International Telecommunication Union’s website at: http://www.itu.int.

(2) For the purpose of working out the designation of the emission of a transmitter operated under a spectrum licence, the references to necessary bandwidth for a given class of emission are taken to be references to the effective occupied bandwidth of the transmitter.

###### 8 Group of transmitters

(1) In this Determination, two or more fixed transmitters are a ***group of transmitter***s if:

(a) they have the same centre frequency and emission designator;

(b) they are operated for the purpose of communicating with the same radiocommunications receiver or group of radiocommunications receivers;

(c) each has an antenna of the same type, model and manufacturer;

(d) the antenna used with each fixed transmitter is located on the same structure and within 20 metres of the phase centre of all antennas within the group of radiocommunications transmitters; and

(e) the identification number assigned by the ACMA to the antenna used with each radiocommunications transmitter is the same.

(2) Despite subsection (1), two or more fixed transmitters are not a group of transmitters if any of those transmitters already belong to a group of transmitters, the details of which are included in the Register.

(3) The location of a group of transmitters is calculated in accordance with the relevant s.145 determination.

###### 9 Group of receivers

(1) In this Determination, two or more fixed receivers are a group of receivers if:

they are operated for the purpose of communicating with the same transmitter or group of transmitters;

each has an antenna of the same type, model and manufacturer;

the antenna used with each fixed receiver is located on the same structure and within 20 metres of the phase centre of all antennas within the group of radiocommunications receivers; and

the identification number assigned by the ACMA to the antenna used with each radiocommunications receiver is the same.

Despite subsection (1), two or more fixed radiocommunications receivers are not a group of receivers if any of those receivers already belong to a group of receivers, the details of which are included in the Register.

(3) The location of a group of radiocommunications transmitters is calculated in accordance with the relevant s.145 determination.

###### 10 Details for apparatus licences

(1) The part of the Register relating to apparatus licences must contain the details set out in:

(a) for a fixed licence – subsection (2) and section 10A;

(b) for any other apparatus licence – this section;

about each such licence and the devices operated under the licence.

Note: Under paragraphs 147(1)(a) and (b) of the Act, the Register must contain, for each apparatus licence, the licensee’s name and postal address, and the dates of issue and expiry of the licence. The further details mentioned in subsections 10(2) to 10(7), and in section 10A, have been determined by the ACMA under paragraphs 147(1)(c), (d) or (e) of the Act, as the case may be, or are otherwise considered to be details necessary or convenient for the purposes of the Act: see subsection 147(2) of the Act.

(2) The details about each licensee of an apparatus licence are:

the client number assigned by the ACMA to the licensee;

the licensee’s trading name (if any); and

if the licensee is a company—its ACN.

(3) The details about each apparatus licence are:

the licence number;

the date of effect;

the licence callsign;

the licence type;

the status of the licence (for example, whether the licence is suspended);

the ship station name (where applicable);

the special conditions in the licence (if any); and

the advisory notes in the licence (if any).

(4) The details about the use of the spectrum by each device authorised to be operated under the licence are:

the number assigned by the ACMA to that use;

the carrier frequency;

the assigned frequency;

the bandwidth;

the status (that is, whether approved or not);

the hours of operation of the device;

the date the use of the spectrum was approved;

if the operation of the device is related to a geographic area—that area;

whether the coverage is by means of a low power spectrum access within the meaning of that term in the Tax Determination; and

the identifier allotted by the International Telecommunication Union Radiocommunication Bureau.

(4A) However, for subsection (4), the part of the Register relating to apparatus licences need not contain the details about the use of the spectrum by the devices authorised to be operated under:

(a) an area-wide licence; or

(b) an area-wide receive licence; or

(c) a PTS licence;

unless there is a condition in:

(d) the licence; or

(e) an instrument made under subsection 110A(1) or subsection 110A(2) of the Act that applies to the licence;

to the effect that the Register must contain the details.

(5) The details about each device authorised to be operated under the licence are:

the registration number assigned by the ACMA;

whether the device is a transmitter or a receiver;

the emission designator;

for a device whose power is measured as equivalent isotropically radiated power—the maximum equivalent isotropically radiated power;

the transmitter power;

the form the power of the radiofrequency transmitter is expressed in as defined in the Radio Regulations published by the International Telecommunication Union;

the antenna height above ground level;

the azimuth for a device using a directional antenna, being the bearing along the centre of the beamwidth;

the intended polarisation of the radiation; and

the antenna tilt relative to the perpendicular.

(5A) However, for subsection (5), the part of the Register relating to apparatus licences need not contain the details about the devices authorised to be operated under:

(a) an area-wide licence; or

(b) an area-wide receive licence; or

(c) a PTS licence;

unless there is a condition in:

(d) the licence; or

(e) an instrument made under subsection 110A(1) or subsection 110A(2) of the Act that applies to the licence;

to the effect that the Register must contain the details.

(6) The details about each antenna are:

the identification number assigned by the ACMA;

the forward gain for the intended polarisation, in units of dBi;

the 3 dB beamwidth for the intended polarisation, in degrees;

the front to back ratio for the intended polarisation, in decibels;

the antenna size;

the antenna type;

the antenna model; and

the antenna manufacturer.

(6A) However, for subsection (6), the part of the Register relating to apparatus licences need not contain the details about the antennas used by, for or in relation to devices authorised to be operated under:

(a) an area-wide licence; or

(b) an area-wide receive licence; or

(c) a PTS licence;

unless there is a condition in:

(d) the licence; or

(e) an instrument made under subsection 110A(1) or subsection 110A(2) of the Act that applies to the licence;

to the effect that the Register must contain the details.

(7) The details about each site where a device is located are:

the site identification number assigned by the ACMA;

the site name;

the site address;

the site latitude and longitude and the precision of those coordinates; and

whether the site is within a high density area, a medium density area or a low density area.

(7A) However, for subsection (7), the part of the Register relating to apparatus licences need not contain the details about the sites where devices authorised to be operated under:

(a) an area-wide licence; or

(b) an area-wide receive licence; or

(c) a PTS licence;

are located, unless there is a condition in:

(d) the licence; or

(e) an instrument made under subsection 110A(1) or subsection 110A(2) of the Act that applies to the licence;

to the effect that the Register must contain the details.

**10A Details for fixed licences**

(1) The details about each fixed licence are:

(a) the licence number;

(b) the date of effect;

(c) the licence callsign;

(d) the licence type;

(e) the status of the licence (for example, whether the licence is suspended);

(f) the special conditions in the licence (if any); and

(g) the advisory notes in the licence (if any).

(2) For:

(a) each base station authorised to be operated under a fixed licence (point to multipoint station); and

(b) for each radiocommunications transmitter authorised to be operated under any other fixed licence (other than a fixed licence (point to multipoint system));

the details about the use of the spectrum by the licence are:

(c) the number assigned by the ACMA to that use;

(d) the carrier frequency (if any is specified);

(e) the assigned frequency (if any is specified);

(f) the bandwidth;

(g) the status (that is, whether approved or not);

(h) the hours of operation of the station or transmitter (if any);

(i) if the operation of the station or transmitter is related to a geographic area – that area;

(j) whether the coverage is by means of a low power spectrum access within the meaning of that term in the Tax Determination;

(k) the identifier allotted by the International Telecommunication Union Radiocommunication Bureau.

Note 1: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint station), the details of radiocommunications transmitters other than base stations operated under the licence do not need to be registered.

Note 2: For some fixed licences, a frequency band is specified (whether by specifying the upper and lower limits of the band, or by specifying the centre frequency and bandwidth), rather than a carrier frequency or an assigned frequency.

Note 3: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), no details of any radiocommunications transmitter need to be registered.

(3) For:

(a) each base station authorised to be operated under a fixed licence (point to multipoint station); and

(b) for each radiocommunications transmitter authorised to be operated under any other fixed licence (other than a fixed licence (point to multipoint system));

the details about the station or transmitter are:

(c) the registration number assigned by the ACMA;

(d) except for a sound outside broadcast station, television outside broadcast network station, television outside broadcast station or a television outside broadcast system station:

(i) the emission designator; and

(ii) for a station or transmitter whose power is measured as EIRP – the maximum EIRP; and

(iii) the form the power of the station or transmitter is expressed in, as defined in the Radio Regulations; and

(iv) the antenna height above ground level; and

(v) the azimuth for a station or transmitter using a directional antenna, being the bearing along the centre of the beamwidth; and

(vi) the antenna tilt relative to the perpendicular; and

(vii) the intended polarisation of the radiation.

Note 1: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint station), the details of radiocommunications transmitters other than base stations operated under the licence do not need to be registered.

Note 2: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), no details of any radiocommunications transmitter need to be registered.

(4) For:

(a) each base station authorised to be operated under a fixed licence (point to multipoint station); and

(b) for each radiocommunications transmitter authorised to be operated under any other fixed licence (other than a fixed licence (point to multipoint system)), except for a sound outside broadcast station, television outside broadcast network station, television outside broadcast station or a television outside broadcast system station;

the details about each antenna are:

(c) the identification number assigned by the ACMA;

(d) the forward gain for the intended polarisation, in units of dBi;

(e) the 3 dB beamwidth for the intended polarisation, in degrees;

(f) the front to back ratio for the intended polarisation, in dB;

(g) the antenna size;

(h) the antenna type;

(i) the antenna model; and

(j) the antenna manufacturer.

Note 1: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint station), the details of radiocommunications transmitters other than base stations operated under the licence do not need to be registered. However, some details about the radiocommunications receivers to which those transmitters send transmissions may be included.

Note 2: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), no details of any radiocommunications transmitter need to be registered.

(5) Subject to subsection (6), for:

(a) each base station authorised to be operated under a fixed licence (point to multipoint station); and

(b) for each radiocommunications transmitter authorised to be operated under any other fixed licence (other than a fixed licence (point to multipoint system));

the details about each site where the station or transmitter is located are:

(c) the site identification number assigned by the ACMA;

(d) the site name;

(e) the site address;

(f) the site latitude and longitude and the precision of those coordinates; and

(g) whether the site is within a high density area, a medium density area or a low density area.

Note 1: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint station), the details of radiocommunications transmitters other than base stations operated under the licence do not need to be registered.

Note 2: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), no details of any radiocommunications transmitter need to be registered.

(6) If:

(a) a fixed licence (point to multipoint station) authorises the operation of a base station in a defined area, rather than at a specified site; or

(b) any other fixed licence, other than a fixed licence (point to point) or a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), authorises the operation of a radiocommunications transmitter in a defined area, rather than at a specified site;

the details about the defined area are:

(c) the defined area;

(d) whether the defined area is within a high density area, a medium density area or a low density area, or is the whole of Australia;

(e) if the ACMA assigns an identification number to the defined area – that identification number.

Note 1: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint station), the details of radiocommunications transmitters other than base stations operated under the licence do not need to be registered.

Note 2: For a fixed licence (point to multipoint system), no details of any radiocommunications transmitter need to be registered.

(7) In this section, each of the expressions ***base station***, ***fixed licence (point to multipoint station)***, ***fixed licence (point to multipoint system)*** and ***fixed licence (point to point station)*** has the meaning given by:

(a) the *Radiocommunications Licence Conditions (Fixed Licence) Determination 2025*; or

(b) if a later instrument replaces that determination and defines the expression – the later instrument.

###### 11 Details for spectrum licences

The part of the Register relating to spectrum licences must contain the details set out in this section about each spectrum licence.

Note: Under paragraphs 144(1)(a) and (b) of the Act, the Register must contain, for each spectrum licence, the licensee’s name and postal address, and the dates of issue and expiry of the licence. The further details mentioned in subsections 11(2), 11(3) and 12(1) to 12(6) have been determined by the ACMA under paragraphs 144(1)(c), (d) or (e) of the Act, as the case may be, or are otherwise considered to be details necessary or convenient for the purposes of the Act: see subsection 144(2) of the Act.

The details about each licensee of a spectrum licence are: the client number assigned by the ACMA to the licensee; the licensee’s trading name (if any); and if the licensee is a company—its ACN.

The details about each spectrum licence are:

the licence number;

the date of effect;

the identification code assigned by the ACMA to the frequency band in relation to which the licence is issued;

the core conditions of the licence;

the periods specified in the licence during which operation of devices is authorised under the licence;

the conditions included in the licence by the ACMA under section 71 of the Act;

the title of the s.145 determination;

the title of the guidelines (if any) issued by the ACMA under section 262 of the Act about the co-ordination of devices operated under the licence with other specified devices, whether or not those other devices are also operated under the licence; and

if particular kinds of transmitters are exempt from meeting the requirements of the ACMA under Part 3.5 of the Act for registration under that Part—a description of the kinds of transmitters.

###### 12 Details for devices operated under spectrum licences

(1) The part of the Register relating to spectrum licences must contain the details set out in this section about:

each transmitter authorised to be operated under each spectrum licence, unless registration of the transmitter is exempt under a condition included in the licence relating to the transmitter under subsection 69(2) of the Act; and

if a licensee registers a receiver—each receiver that is so registered.

The details are:

the licence number;

the device identification number assigned by the ACMA to each device;

the registration identification number assigned by the ACMA to each device;

whether the device is a transmitter or a receiver;

the number assigned by the ACMA to the person who applied to register the device;

if the device is a transmitter—the client number assigned by the ACMA to the person accredited under section 263 of the Act who issued the interference impact certificate for the device;

the date and time of registration of the device; and

an indication of whether the operation of the device is authorised during certain times only.

(3) The details about each antenna are:

the identification number assigned by the ACMA;

the forward gain for the intended polarisation, in units of dBi;

the 3 dB beamwidth for the intended polarisation, in degrees;

the front to back ratio for the intended polarisation, in decibels;

the antenna size;

the antenna type;

the antenna model; and

the antenna manufacturer.

(4) The details about each site where a device is located are:

the site identification number assigned by the ACMA;

the site name;

the site address;

the site latitude and longitude and the precision of those coordinates; and

whether the site is within a high density area, a medium density area or a low density area*.*

(5) If the device is a transmitter, the details are:

the emission centre frequency;

the emission designator;

the effective occupied bandwidth;

for a device whose power is measured as equivalent isotropically radiated power—the maximum equivalent isotropically radiated power;

the transmitter power;

the form the power of the radiofrequency transmitter is expressed in as defined in the Radio Regulations published by the International Telecommunication Union;

the antenna height above ground level;

the azimuth for a device using a directional antenna, being the bearing along the centre of the beamwidth;

the intended polarisation of the radiation;

the antenna tilt relative to the perpendicular;

the parameters relating to the operation of devices that are used in working out unacceptable levels of interference under the s.145 determination; and

whether the device is registered as part of a group of transmitters.

(6) If the device is a receiver, the details are:

the emission centre frequency of the intended received signal;

the emission designator of the intended received signal;

the effective occupied bandwidth of the intended received signal;

the antenna height above ground level;

the azimuth for a device using a directional antenna, being the bearing along the centre of the beamwidth;

the polarisation of the intended received signal;

the antenna tilt relative to the perpendicular; and

whether the device is registered as part of a group of receivers.

###### 13 Details for class licences

The part of the Register relating to class licences must contain, for each class licence, the title of the class licence.

Note: Class licences issued by the ACMA under s. 132 of the Act are available on the Federal Register of Legislation which may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

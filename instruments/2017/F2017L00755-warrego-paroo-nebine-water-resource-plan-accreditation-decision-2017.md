# Warrego-Paroo-Nebine Water Resource Plan Accreditation Decision 2017

I, Barnaby Joyce, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Agriculture and Water Resources, accredit, under section 63 of the *Water Act 2007*, the *Warrego-Paroo-Nebine Water Resource Plan* set out, or identified in accordance with section 10.04 of the Basin Plan 2012, in the document titled *Warrego-Paroo-Nebine Water Resource Plan* published on the date that this instrument was signed at www.mdba.gov.au/publications/policies-guidelines/water-resource-plan-warrego-paroo-nebine.

This is the *Warrego-Paroo-Nebine Water Resource Plan Accreditation Decision 2017*.

This instrument commences the day after registration.

Dated

15 June 2017

Barnaby Joyce

Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Agriculture and Water Resources

# Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) (Prescribed Ship or Unit — Intra-State Trade) Declaration 2017

**COmmonwealth of Australia**

*Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) Act 1993*

***Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) (Prescribed Ship or Unit — Intra-State Trade) Declaration 2017***

I, Michaelia Cash, Minister for Employment, pursuant to sections 4A and 4B of the *Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) Act 1993* (‘the Act’), hereby declare:

***(1)*** That the *Occupational Health and Safety (Maritime Industry) (Prescribed Ship or Unit — Intra-State Trade) Declaration 2015 (No. 2****)*** **(**F2015L00863**)** registered on the Federal Register of Legislation on 22 June 2015, is repealed.

(2) That, subject to clause (3), a ship or a vessel which is only engaged in intra-state trade is not a prescribed ship or a prescribed unit for the purposes of the Act.

(3) Clause (2) does not apply to any ship or vessel to which subsections 6(3) or 6(3A) of the Act apply.

(4) This declaration takes effect on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Michaelia Cash

Minister for Employment

Dated 12 June 2017

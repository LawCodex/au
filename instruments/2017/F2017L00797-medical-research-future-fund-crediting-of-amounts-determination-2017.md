# Medical Research Future Fund (Crediting of Amounts) Determination 2017

We, SCOTT MORRISON, Treasurer, and MATHIAS HUBERT PAUL CORMANN, Minister for Finance, having had regard to the object of the *Medical Research Future Fund Act 2015*, make this determination under section 15 (1) (a) of that Act.

Dated June 22 2017

SCOTT MORRISON MATHIAS HUBERT PAUL CORMANN

Treasurer Minister for Finance

###### 1 Name

This determination is the *Medical Research Future Fund (Crediting of Amounts) Determination 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This determination commences on the day on which it is registered.

*Note:* Section 42 of the *Legislation Act 2003* (which deals with disallowance of legislative instruments) does not apply to this instrument: see section 15 (3) of the *Medical Research Future Fund Act 2015*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 15 (1) (a) of the *Medical Research Future Fund Act 2015*.

###### 4 Crediting of Amounts

As per section 15 (1) (a) of the *Medical Research Future Fund Act 2015*, $2,242,200,000.00 is to be credited to the Medical Research Future Fund Special Account on 3 July 2017.

*Note:* The ***Medical Research Future Fund Special Account*** is established by section 14 of the *Medical Research Future Fund Act 2015*.

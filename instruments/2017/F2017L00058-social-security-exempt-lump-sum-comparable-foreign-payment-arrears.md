**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Revocation**

The following instrument is revoked:

*Social Security Exempt Lump Sum Determination No. 2 of 2004 (FACS)*

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***comparable foreign payment*** has the same meaning as under section 1228A of the Act.

**6 Exempt Lump Sums**

#### For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act the payment of an amount of arrears of a comparable foreign payment is an exempt lump sum.

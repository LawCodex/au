###### 4 Schedule 1—Modifications of the Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

The *Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme* is modified as set out in Schedule 1 to this instrument for the purposes of the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) Act 2006*.

## Schedule 1—Modifications of the Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

##### Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme

**1 Sections 1A to 2**

Omit the sections, substitute:

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Scheme*.

###### 2 Authorisation of Scheme

The *Scheme* is authorised by section 18 of the *Act*.

###### 2A Purpose of Scheme

The *Scheme* enables *Community Pharmacists* to supply *Pharmaceutical benefits* to *Eligible Persons*.

###### 2B Transitional provision

If:

(a) a thing was started under the *Repatriation Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests) 2006* (No. R45/2013) before 1 July 2017; and

(b) immediately before that day, the thing has not been finished;

then, the thing may be finished under this *Scheme* on or after that day as if it had been started under this *Scheme*.

**2 Section 3 (definition of Act)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Act*** means the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) Act 2006*;

**3 Section 3 (definition of accepted disability)**

Omit the definition.

**4 Section 3 (definition of Eligible Person)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Eligible Person*** means an eligible person within the meaning of the *Act*;

**5 Section 3**

(b) definition of ***income support payment under the Social Security Act 1991***;

(c) definition of ***MRCA supplement***;

**6 Section 3 (definition of Repatriation Health Card ‑ For All Conditions)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Repatriation Health Card ‑ For All Conditions*** means an identification card, or written authorisation, provided to an *Eligible Person* in relation to treatment for all injuries or diseases;

**8 Section 3 (definition of Scheme)**

**9 Section 3**

(a) definition of ***social security pension supplement***;

**10 Section 3 (definition of Treatment Principles)**

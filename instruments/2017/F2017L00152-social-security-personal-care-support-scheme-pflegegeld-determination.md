#### 3. Revocation

This instrument revokes the Social Security (Personal Care Scheme – Pflegegeld) Determination 2004.

#### 4. Personal care support scheme

(1) The scheme for the provision of personal care support known as *Pflegegeld* is an approved scheme for the purposes of section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

(2) In this instrument, *Pflegegeld* means the scheme of that name established by the Republic of Austria with effect on 1 July 1993 under which an allowance for long-term care is paid to certain disabled persons.

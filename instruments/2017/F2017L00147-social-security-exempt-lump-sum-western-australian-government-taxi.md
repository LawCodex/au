3 **Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

4 **Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Eligible taxi plate owners*** means current holders of Perth metropolitan taxi plates purchased prior to 18 December 2015.

***Transition Assistance Package*** means a package established by the Western Australian Government to assist existing taxi and omnibus industry participants transition to the new operating environment following regulatory and policy reforms, including funds for the provision of a one-off ex-gratia lump sum payment to assist taxi plate owners adjust to a new, more competitive environment.

***Adjustment Assistance Grant*** means a payment made from the Transition Assistance Package by the Western Australian Department of Transport to eligible taxi plate owners. This grant is capped at $20 million.

**5 Exempt lump sum**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the gross amount of an Adjustment Assistance Grant made from the Transition Assistance Package is an exempt lump sum.

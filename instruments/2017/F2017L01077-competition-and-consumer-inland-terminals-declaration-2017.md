# Competition and Consumer (Inland Terminals) Declaration 2017

Competition and Consumer (Inland Terminals) Declaration 2017

An instrument made under section 10.02A of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010* declaring that a specified facility is an inland terminal.

I, **Darren Chester**, Minister for Infrastructure and Transport, make this instrument.

Dated 18 July 2017

Darren Chester\
Minister for Infrastructure and Transport

### Part 1 Preliminary

1 Name

This is the *Competition and Consumer (Inland Terminals) Declaration 2017*.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 10.02A(1) of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*.

4 Declaration

A facility specified in an item of a table in Schedule 1 is an inland terminal for the purposes of Part X of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*.

5 Revocation

This instrument revokes the *Instrument of Declaration of Inland Terminals 2008* (Instrument No: Part X 10.02A 1/2008).

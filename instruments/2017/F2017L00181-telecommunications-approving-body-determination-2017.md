# Telecommunications (Approving Body) Determination 2017

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following determination under subsection 410(1) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

Dated: 27 February 2017

Richard Bean

[signed]

Member

James Cameron

[signed]

Member/General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### 1 Name

This is the *Telecommunications (Approving Body) Determination 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences at the start of the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 410(1) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Repeal of the Telecommunications (Approving Bodies) Determination 2007

The *Telecommunications (Approving Bodies) Determination 2007* (F2007L02578) is repealed on 1 July 2017.

###### 5 Determination of approving body

###### The Joint Accreditation System of Australia and New Zealand (ABN 49 614 982 550) is an approving body for the purposes of subsection 410(1) of the Telecommunications Act 1997.

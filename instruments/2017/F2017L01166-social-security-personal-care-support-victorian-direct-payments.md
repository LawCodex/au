###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 35A of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991.*

***Direct Payments Project*** is the project, known by that name, which:

is administered by the Victorian Department of Health and Human Services; and

funds individuals with a disability who elect to self-manage their Individual Support Package under a direct payment model.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Personal Care Support Determination

The Direct Payments Project is an approved scheme for the purposes of the Act.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 8(11)(d) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**4 Revocation**

The following instruments are revoked:

*Social Security Exempt Lump Sum (Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme Payment) (FaCSIA) Determination 2007*

*Social Security Exempt Lump Sum (Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme Payment) (DEWR) Determination 2007*

*Social Security Exempt Lump Sum (Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme Payment) (DEST) Determination 2007*

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security Act 1991*.

***Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme*** means a scheme operated by the Anglican Church Diocese of Sydney and the Sydney Anglican Home Mission Society Council to provide one off payments to persons who were victims of child abuse or sexual misconduct by a church worker in the context of, or related to, their church role.

***Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme Payment*** means a payment made by the Anglican Church, Diocese of Sydney or the Sydney Anglican Home Mission Society Council under the *Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme*.

**6 Exempt Lump Sums**

For the purposes of paragraph 8(11)(d) of the Act, the amount of a *Pastoral Care and Assistance Scheme Payment* is an exempt lump sum.

# Extension of the Ban Period for the Interim Ban on Certain Decorative Alcohol Fuelled Devices

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

*COMPETITION AND CONSUMER ACT 2010*

**EXTENSION OF THE BAN PERIOD FOR THE INTERIM BAN ON CERTAIN DECORATIVE ALCOHOL FUELLED DEVICES**

I, MICHAEL McCORMACK, Minister for Small Business, pursuant to section 111(2) of the Australian Consumer Law, which is Schedule 2 to the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*, extend the interim ban titled ‘*Imposition of interim ban on certain decorative alcohol fuelled devices’* registered on the Federal Register of Legislation on 16 March 2017, by a period of 30 days.

The interim ban commenced on 17 March 2017 and is in force for 60 days, until 15 May 2017. The effect of this Notice is to extend the interim ban period by 30 days from 16 May 2017.

This Notice commences on 16 May 2017.

Dated this 8th day of May 2017

MICHAEL McCORMACK

Minister for Small Business

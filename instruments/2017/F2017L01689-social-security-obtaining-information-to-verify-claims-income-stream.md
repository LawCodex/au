Contents

###### 1. Name of specification

This specification is the *Social Security (Obtaining Information to Verify Claims – Income Stream Recipients) Specification 2017*.

###### 3. Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 195(3A) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4. Definitions

**Act** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

**Human Services Department** has the same meaning as in the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**Secretary** means the Secretary of the Department of Social Services.

**special reduction amount** has the same meaning as in subsection 1099AAA(1) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5. Specification of Information

For the purpose of subsection 195(3A) of the Act, the following information in relation to an income stream received a person is specified:

(i) The unique identifier allocated to the income stream provider or their administrator by the Human Services Department;

(ii) The Australian Business Number of the income stream provider, as reported on the income stream schedule;

(iii) The name of the income stream product;

(iv) If the owner is a reversionary beneficiary – whether they became a reversionary beneficiary in the period specified in the notice issued;

(v) If the owner is a reversionary beneficiary – the date on which the income stream reverted to the reversionary beneficiary;

(vi) The latest available account balance of the income stream;

(vii) The date of the latest available account balance of the income stream;

(viii) If the income stream is commuted – the total value of commutations since commencement of the income stream;

(ix) If the income stream is commuted – the total number of commutations made in the period specified in the notice issued;

(x) If the income stream is a market-linked income stream and is commuted – the reason that a commutation has been made;

(xi) If the income stream is a market-linked income stream and is in its final year of term – the expiry date of the income stream’s term;

(xii) Whether payments from the income stream have been suspended;

(xiii) If payments from the income stream have been suspended, the date on which payments were suspended;

(xiv) Whether the income stream is jointly owned;

(xv) If the income stream is jointly owned, the division of ownership of the income stream, as a percentage;

(xvi) If the income stream is an account-based or market-linked income stream – the gross annual payment for the current financial year made under the income stream;

(xvii) If the income stream is a defined benefit income stream or a military invalidity pension income stream – the gross fortnightly payment made under the income stream;

(xviii) If the income stream is a lifetime, life expectancy or term income stream – the gross amount of each payment made under the income stream and the frequency of the payment; and

(xix) If the income stream is a military invalidity pension income stream – the special reduction amount for the year in which the notice is given and the method used to work out the amounts that would be the tax free components for the purposes of determining that special reduction amount.

**Editorial changes**

The *Legislation Act 2003* authorises First Parliamentary Counsel to make editorial and presentational changes to a compiled law in preparing a compilation of the law for registration. The changes must not change the effect of the law. Editorial changes take effect from the compilation registration date.

If the compilation includes editorial changes, the endnotes include a brief outline of the changes in general terms. Full details of any changes can be obtained from the Office of Parliamentary Counsel.

**Misdescribed amendments**

A misdescribed amendment is an amendment that does not accurately describe how an amendment is to be made. If, despite the misdescription, the amendment can be given effect as intended, then the misdescribed amendment can be incorporated through an editorial change made under section 15V of the *Legislation Act 2003*.

If a misdescribed amendment cannot be given effect as intended, the amendment is not incorporated and “(md not incorp)” is added to the amendment history.

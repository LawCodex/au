# Telecommunications (Section of the Telecommunications Industry – Public Number Directory Publishers) Determination 2017

The Australian Communications and Media Authority makes the following Determination under subsection 110(3) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

Dated: 22 September 2017

Richard Bean

[signed]

Member

James Cameron

[signed]

Member/General Manager

Australian Communications and Media Authority

###### 1 Name

This is the *Telecommunications (Section of the Telecommunications Industry – Public Number Directory Publishers) Determination 2017*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences at the start of the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

Note: The Federal Register of Legislation may be accessed at www.legislation.gov.au.

###### 3 Authority

This Determination is made under subsection 110(3) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*.

###### 4 Repeal of the Telecommunications (Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2007

The *Telecommunications (Section of the Telecommunications Industry) Determination 2007* (F2007L01242) is repealed.

###### 5 Definitions

In this Determination:

***public number directory*** has the meaning given by subsection 285(2) of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*, as in force from time to time.

***public number directory publisher*** has the same meaning as in the *Telecommunications Integrated Public Number Database Scheme 2017*, as in force from time to time.

Note 1: Section 14 of the *Legislation Act 2003* allows legislative instruments to make provision in relation to matters by incorporating Acts and disallowable legislative instruments, either as in force at a particular time or as in force from time to time.

Note 2: All Commonwealth Acts and legislative instruments are registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 6 Application of Determination

This Determination applies to public number directory publishers.

###### 7 Specification of telecommunications activity

For the purposes of this Determination, the specified telecommunications activity is the publication and maintenance of a public number directory.

###### 8 Section of the telecommunications industry

Persons to whom this Determination applies who carry on, or propose to carry on, the telecommunications activity mentioned in section 7 are determined to constitute the section of the telecommunications industry to be identified as “public number directory publishers”.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 17 of the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) Act 2006.*

###### 4 Schedule 1—Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles

The Repatriation Private Patient Principles are modified as set out in Schedule 1 to this instrument for the purposes of the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) Act 2006*.

###### 5 Schedule 2—Repeals

Each instrument that is specified in Schedule 2 to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable item in that Schedule, and any other item in that Schedule has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles

##### Repatriation Private Patient Principles

**1 Paragraphs 1.1 and 1.1A**

Omit the paragraphs, substitute:

###### 1.1 Name

This instrument is the Principles.

###### 1.1A Commencement

The Principles commence on 1 July 2017.

###### 1.1B Transitional provision

If:

(a) a thing was started under the *Veterans’ Affairs (Modified Repatriation Private Patient Principles ‑ Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests) Instrument* 2006 before 1 July 2017; and

(b) immediately before that day, the thing has not been finished;

then, the thing may be finished under these Principles on or after that day as if it had been started under these Principles.

**2 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Act)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Act*** means the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) Act 2006.*

**3 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Commission)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Commission*** has the meaning given by section 5A of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

**4 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of dependent of a Vietnam veteran)**

Omit the definition.

**5 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of entitled person)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***entitled person*** means an eligible person within the meaning of the *Act*.

**6 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of former Repatriation Hospital)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***former Repatriation Hospital*** means a hospital or other institution that was formerly operated by the *Commission* under paragraph 89(1)(a) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

**7 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Principles)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Principles*** means the Repatriation Private Patient Principles as modified by the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) (Modifications of the Repatriation Private Patient Principles) Instrument 2017*.

**8 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of private patient)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***private patient*** has a meaning affected by subsection 17(8) of the *Act*.

**9 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Treatment Principles)**

Omit the definition, substitute:

***Treatment Principles*** means the Treatment Principles as modified by the *Australian Participants in British Nuclear Tests and British Commonwealth Occupation Force (Treatment) (Modifications of the Treatment Principles) Instrument 2013*.

**10 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of veteran partnering private hospital)**

Omit “the *Act*”, substitute “the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*”.

**11 Paragraph 1.2 (definition of Vietnam veteran)**

Omit the definition.

**12 Paragraph 3.6**

Omit the paragraph.

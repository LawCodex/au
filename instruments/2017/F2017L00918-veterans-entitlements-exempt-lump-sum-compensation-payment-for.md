# Veterans’ Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum –Compensation Payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe) Determination 2017

*Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*

**Veterans’ Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum – Compensation Payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe) Determination 2017**

Instrument 2017 No. R13

I, MARK HARRIGAN, delegate of the Repatriation Commission, make the following determination under paragraph 5H(12)(c) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*.

Dated this 7th day of July 2017

M Harrigan

…………………………………………………………

MARK HARRIGAN

**1 Name**

This instrument is the *Veterans’ Entitlements (Exempt Lump Sum –Compensation Payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe) Determination 2017.*

**2 Commencement**

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

**3 Authority**

This instrument is made under paragraph 5H(12)(c) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* (VEA).

**4 Revocation**

The *Veterans’ Entitlements Income (Exempt Lump Sum - Ex Gratia Payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe) Determination* (Instrument No.R16/2007) dated 31 May 2007 is revoked.

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***“compensation payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe”*** means a one-off payment made under Schedule 5 of the *Social Security and Veterans’ Affairs Legislation Amendment (One-off Payments and Other 2007 Budget Measures) Act 2007*;

***“income support supplement”*** means the payment called the income support supplement payable under Part IIIA of the VEA;

***“service pension”*** has the same meaning as in subsection 5Q(1) of the VEA.

**6 Exempt lump sum**

If a person or a person’s partner receives a c*ompensation payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe* and the person is eligible for, or in receipt of a *service pension* or *income support supplement,* then the amount of the c*ompensation payment for Australian Prisoners of War in Europe* received by the person or the person’s partner is an exempt lump sum.

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Qualified Employees) Declaration No. 4

Statutory Rules 1998 No. 1681

\__________________

I, JOHN JOSEPH FAHEY, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under subsection 3F (1) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 22 June 1998.

JOHN FAHEY\
Minister for Finance and Administration

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (Qualified Employees) Declaration No. 4.

###### Commencement

**2.** This Declaration is taken to have commenced on 1 July 1994.

###### Interpretation

**3.** In this Declaration:

**“ACT Public Service”** means the Service established by subsection 12 (1) of the *Public Sector Management Act 1994* of the Australian Capital Territory;

**“the Act”** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

###### Declared class of persons—officers and employees of the ACT Public Service

**4.** For the purposes of subsection 3F (1) of the Act, the class of persons comprising officers and employees of the ACT Public Service is a class of persons who are qualified employees.

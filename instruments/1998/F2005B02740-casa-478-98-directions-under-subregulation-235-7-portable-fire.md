# CASA 478/98 - Directions under subregulation 235(7) – portable fire extinguishers for refuelling of aircraft

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 478/98

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION ORDERS**

**DIRECTIONS UNDER SUBREGULATION 235 (7)**

I, DAVID ALAN VILLIERS, Acting General Manager, Airworthiness Branch, CASA, a delegate of CASA, issue the following directions under subregulation 235 (7) of the Civil Aviation Regulations.

**PORTABLE FIRE EXTINGUISHERS FOR REFUELLING OF AIRCRAFT**

1\. The following portable fire extinguishers are of an approved type for the purposes of paragraph 4.4.4 of section 20.9 of the Civil Aviation Orders:

(a) fire extinguishers of the powder extinguisher type that have a rating under Australian Standard (AS) 1940-1993 that is 2A/60B or higher, combined with a rating under that standard for dealing with electrical fires; and

(b) fire extinguishers of the foam extinguisher type that have an AS 1940‑1993 rating that is 2A/20B or higher.

2\. The approved capacity of a fire extinguisher referred to above is a capacity that enables it to cover the maximum floor area that an extinguisher with its rating should cover in accordance with AS 1940-1993.

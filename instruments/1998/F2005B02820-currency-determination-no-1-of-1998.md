# Currency Determination No. 1 of 1998

as amended

made under subsection 13A (1) of the

Currency Act 1965

This compilation was prepared on 28 March 2006\
taking into account amendments up to *Currency Determination No. 6 of 1998*

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting and Publishing,\
Attorney‑General’s Department, Canberra

###### 1 Citation [see Note 1]

This Determination may be cited as the *Currency Determination No. 1 of 1998*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on 15 February 1998.

###### 3 Specifications of coins

This Determination specifies, for a coin having the denomination and standard composition specified in columns 1 and 2 of Part 1 of the Schedule, the standard weight, the allowable variation from that weight, the dimension and the design set out in columns 3, 4 and 5 of that Part.

###### 4 Application of Determination

This Determination does not affect any other determination of the specifications of a coin.

Part 2 Explanation of symbols

| Design Feature | Symbol | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | S1 | Circular |
| Edge | E1 | Milled |
| Obverse | O1 | A circle of raised beads, immediately inside the rim, enclosing:<br>(a) the inscriptions:<br>(i) “ELIZABETH II”; and<br>(ii) “AUSTRALIA”; and<br>(b) an inscription stating, in arabic numerals, the amount, in dollars, of the coin, followed by the word “DOLLARS”;<br>that, in turn, surround an effigy of the Queen. |
| Reverse | R60 | A circle of raised beads, immediately inside the rim, enclosing the inscriptions:<br>(a) ‘THE AUSTRALIAN NUGGET X OZ. 9999 GOLD’ (where ‘X’ is the nominal weight, in ounces, of the coin expressed as a common fraction, or whole number, in arabic numerals); and<br>(b) ‘1998’ or ‘1999’;<br>that, in turn, surround:<br>(c) a representation of a joey kangaroo; and<br>(d) either:<br>(i) for a coin dated 1998 — the inscription ‘P’; or<br>(ii) for a coin dated 1999 — the inscription ‘P100’;<br>superimposed on a sunburst design. |

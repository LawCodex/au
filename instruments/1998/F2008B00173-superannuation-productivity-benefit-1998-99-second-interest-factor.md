# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1998-99 Second Interest Factor Declaration

Statutory Rules 1998 No. 1721

\__________________

I, JOHN JOSEPH FAHEY, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under paragraph 3E (1) (b) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 22 June 1998.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance and Administration

\____________

Citation:

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1998-99 Second Interest Factor Declaration.

*Note* This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss 46A and 48.

Interpretation

**2.** In this Declaration:

**“Act”** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*;

**“Days”** means:

a. in the case of an employee who becomes a fund employee during the financial year starting on 1 July 1998—the number of days in the period:

(i) starting on the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1998; and

(ii) ending at the end of the day on which an amount first becomes payable under section 4F of the Act; or

b. in the case of an employee in respect of whom an interim benefit becomes payable under section 6 of the Act during the financial year starting on 1 July 1998—the number of days between:

(i) the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1998; and

(ii) the day on which the interim benefit became payable; or

c. in the case of an employee who does not become a fund employee during the financial year starting on 1 July 1998 and in respect of whom an interim benefit does not become payable during that year—the number of days between:

(i) the day following the last day of the last pay period of the employee ending before 1 July 1998; and

(ii) 30 June 1999.

Declared second interest factor

**3.** For section 8A of the Act, the declared second interest factor for the financial year starting on 1 July 1998 is:

0.0589 x Days

365

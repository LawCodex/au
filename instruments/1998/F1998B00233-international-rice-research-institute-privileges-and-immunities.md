# International Rice Research Institute (Privileges and Immunities) Regulations 1998

Statutory Rules 1998 No. 2521

\__________________

I, WILLIAM PATRICK DEANE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *International Organisations (Privileges and Immunities) Act 1963*.

Dated 30 July 1998.

WILLIAM DEANE\
Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

ALEXANDER DOWNER

Minister for Foreign Affairs

\____________

###### Name of Regulations

**1.** These Regulations are the *International Rice Research Institute (Privileges and Immunities) Regulations 1998*.

###### Commencement

**2.** These Regulations take effect from a day specified in a determination made by the Minister under subsection 13 (2) of the *International Organisations (Privileges and Immunities) Act 1963.*

###### Definition

**3.** In these Regulations:

***International Rice Research Institute*** means the organisation of that name provided for by the Agreement Recognizing the International Legal Personality of the International Rice Research Institute done at Manila on 19 May 1995.

###### Legal capacity and personality

**4.** The International Rice Research Institute:

(a) is a body corporate with perpetual succession; and

(b) is capable, in its corporate name:

(i) of entering into contracts; and

(ii) of acquiring, holding and disposing of real and personal property; and

(iii) of suing and being sued.

**NOTE**

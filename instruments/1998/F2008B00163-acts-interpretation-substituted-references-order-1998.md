# Acts Interpretation (Substituted References) Order 1998

I, WILLIAM PATRICK DEANE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following order under subsections 19B (1), (2) and (3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

Signed and sealed with the\
Great Seal of Australia\
on 12 November 1998.

WILLIAM DEANE

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

DARYL WILLIAMS

Attorney-General

###### 1 Name of order

This order is the *Acts Interpretation (Substituted References) Order 1998*.

###### 2 Commencement

This order commences on 12 November 1998.

###### 3 Definition

In this order:

***provision*** means a provision in an Act or in an instrument under an Act.

###### 4 References to Ministers

A provision mentioned in column 4 of an item in Schedule 1, that mentions the Minister, or Ministers, mentioned in column 2 of the item, has effect as if there were substituted a reference to the Minister, or Ministers, mentioned in column 3 of the item.

###### 5 References to Departments

A provision mentioned in column 4 of an item in Schedule 2, that mentions the Department mentioned in column 2 of the item, has effect as if there were substituted a reference to the Department mentioned in column 3 of the item.

###### 6 References to Secretaries

A provision mentioned in column 4 of an item in Schedule 3, that mentions the Secretary mentioned in column 2 of the item, has effect as if there were substituted a reference to the Secretary mentioned in column 3 of the item.

*Note* A provision mentioned in a Schedule to this order:

(a) may have been affected by other substituted references under other orders made, under section 19B or 19BA of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, before the commencement of this order; and

(b) may, after the commencement of this order, be affected by other substituted references under other orders made under section 19B or 19BA of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*.

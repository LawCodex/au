# Family Law Act 1975 s 69ZF(1) - Proclamation (09/12/1998)

*Family Law Act 1975*

proclamation

I, WILLIAM PATRICK DEANE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under subsection 69ZF (1) of the *Family Law Act 1975*, declare that all the child welfare law provisions of Part VII of that Act extend to Victoria on and from the date of the gazettal of this Proclamation.

Signed and sealed with the\
Great Seal of Australia\
on 9 December 1998

WILLIAM DEANE

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

Daryl Williams

Attorney General

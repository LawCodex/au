# Currency Determination No. 3 of 1998

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

I, RODERICK CHARLES KEMP, Assistant Treasurer, acting for the Treasurer, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 12 March 1998.

C.R KEMP

Assistant Treasurer

###### Name of Determination

**1.** This Determination is Currency Determination No. 3 of 1998.

###### Commencement

**2.** This Determination commences on 1 April 1998.

###### Specification of coins

**3.** This Determination specifies, for a coin having the denomination and standard composition specified in columns 1 and 2 of Part 1 of the Schedule, the standard weight, the allowable variation from that weight, the dimensions and the design set out in columns 3, 4 and 5 of that Part.

###### Application of Determination

**4.** This Determination does not affect any other determination of the specifications of a coin.

**PART 2—EXPLANATION OF SYMBOLS**

| **Design Feature** | **Symbol** | **Explanation** |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | S1 | Circular |
| Edge | E1 | Continuous milled |
| Edge | E5 | Plain edge with the inscription “GAMES OF THE XXVII OLYMPIAD” (twice appearing) evenly spaced around the edge to read with the reverse of the coin facing up. |
| Obverse | O3 | An effigy of the Queen, surrounded by the inscriptions:<br>(a) “ELIZABETH II”; and<br>(b) “AUSTRALIA 2000”. |
| Obverse | O4 | An effigy of the Queen, surrounded by:<br>(a) the inscriptions:<br>(i) “ELIZABETH II”; and<br>(ii) “AUSTRALIA 2000”; and<br>(b) an inscription stating, in Arabic numerals, the amount in dollars of the denomination of the coin, followed by the word “DOLLARS”. |
| Reverse | R70 | A representation of a football player striking at a football superimposed on a representation of Olympic flames, surrounded by:<br>(a) the inscriptions:<br>(i) “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) “5 DOLLARS”; and<br>(b) a representation of the Olympic rings. |
| Reverse | R71 | A representation of a triathlete emerging from the water superimposed on a representation of Olympic flames, surrounded by:<br>(a) the inscriptions:<br>(i) “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) “5 DOLLARS”; and<br>(b) a representation of the Olympic rings. |
| Reverse | R72 | A representation of a cyclist riding a bicycle superimposed on a representation of Olympic flames, surrounded by:<br>(a) the inscriptions:<br>(i) “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) “5 DOLLARS”; and<br>(b) a representation of the Olympic rings. |
| Reverse | R73 | A circle, immediately inside the ring, comprising:<br>(a) representations of coral; and<br>(b) a tablet, enclosing:<br>(i) the inscription “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) a representation of the Olympic rings in Olympic colours;<br>and surrounding:<br>(c) a representation of 2 great white sharks; and<br>(d) the letter “C”. |
| Reverse | R74 | A circle, immediately inside the ring, comprising:<br>(a) representations of sailing ships; and<br>(b) a tablet, enclosing:<br>(i) the inscription “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) a representation of the Olympic rings in Olympic colours;<br>and surrounding:<br>(c) a motif border, surrounding:<br>(i) a representation of Australia with superimposed figures, footprints and motifs; and<br>(ii) the letter “C”. |
| Reverse | R75 | Representations of elements of an traditional analog stopwatch and a modern digital heart‑rate monitor, and the inscription “DEDICATION”, arranged in a circle, immediately inside the rim, enclosing:<br>(a) a representation of a tracksuit‑clad athlete training in the rain; and<br>(b) the letter “P”; and<br>(c) a tablet, enclosing:<br>(i) the inscription “Sydney 2000”; and<br>(ii) a representation of the Olympic rings. |

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 - Notice of Declaration (Notice No. 3 of 1998)

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

*SAFETY, REHABILITATION AND COMPENSATION ACT 1988*

NOTICE OF DECLARATION – MEDIBANK PRIVATE LIMITED

Notice No. 3 of 1998

I, PETER KEASTON REITH, Minister for Workplace Relations and Small Business, for the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition of “Commonwealth authority” in subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988:*

(a) revoke the previous declaration made in respect of Medibank Limited on 5 December 1997; and

(b) declare that on and from 1 May 1998 the following body corporate is a body corporate to which that Act applies:

Medibank Private Limited (ACN 080 890 259)

Peter Reith

Minister for Workplace Relations and Small Business

Dated 8th May 1998

# CASA 103/98 - Approval under subregulation 207(2)

INSTRUMENT NUMBER: CASA 103/98

**CIVIL AVIATION ACT 1988**

**CIVIL AVIATION REGULATIONS**

**APPROVAL UNDER SUBREGULATION 207(2)**

I, FRANK RICHARD HAMILTON GRIMSHAW, a delegate of CASA:

(a) revoke instrument number CASA 51/98; and

(b) under subregulation 207 (2) of the Civil Aviation Regulations, approve the operation of the aircraft bearing nationality and registration marks VH‑LHH:

(i) with an approved flight data recorder system that retains its last 8 hours of recording; and

(ii) with an approved cockpit voice recorder system that retains its last 60 minutes of recording and complies with the requirements of subsection 2 of section 103.20 of the Civil Aviation Orders except for recording simultaneously on only 3 channels.

This approval is subject to both recorder systems being operated in accordance with paragraphs 6.3, 6.4 and 6.6 of section 20.18 of the Civil Aviation Orders.

**[Signed Frank Richard Hamilton Grimshaw]**

FRANK RICHARD HAMILTON GRIMSHAW

General Manager

Airworthiness Branch

27 April 1998

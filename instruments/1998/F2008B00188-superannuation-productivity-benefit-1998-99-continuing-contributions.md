# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1998-99 Continuing Contributions Declaration

Statutory Rules 1998 No. 1701

\__________________

I, JOHN JOSEPH FAHEY, Minister for Finance and Administration, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 22 June 1998.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance and Administration

\____________

Citation:

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1998-99 Continuing Contributions Declaration.

*Note* This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss 46A and 48.

###### Substitution of Amounts

**2.** For Section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 1998, as if the amounts set out in the following table were substituted for the amounts set out in the table in that Schedule:

**TABLE**

| **Pay period rate of salary** | **Pay period contribution** |
|---|---|
| Less than $193.28 | $13.53 |
| $193.28 or more but less than $1883.08 | The amount that is 7% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $1883.08 or more | $131.82 |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 29 June 1998.

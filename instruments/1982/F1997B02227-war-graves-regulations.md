# War Graves Regulations

Statutory Rules 1982 No. 142 as amended

made under the

War Graves Act 1980

This compilation was prepared on 26 May 2004\
taking into account amendments up to SR 1983 No. 162

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting,\
Attorney-General’s Department, Canberra

###### 1 Citation [see Note 1]

These Regulations may be cited as the War Graves Regulations.

###### 2 Commencement

Regulation 3 shall be deemed to have come into operation on 22 June 1981.

###### 3 Allowances of Director

For the purposes of subsection 6 (2) of the *War Graves Act 1980*, the following allowances are prescribed, namely, the allowances payable to an officer included in the Third Division of the Australian Public Service.

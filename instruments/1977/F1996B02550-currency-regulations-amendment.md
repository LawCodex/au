# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

Statutory Rules

**1977 No. 17**

REGULATION UNDER THE CURRENCY ACT 1965.\*

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulation under the *Currency Act* 1965.

Dated this eleventh day of February*,* 1977.

John R. Kerr

Governor-General.

[image: Signature of Minister of State for Aboriginal Affairs for and on behalf of the Treasurer]

By His Excellency’s Command,

Minister of State for Aboriginal Affairs for and on behalf of the Treasurer.

Amendments of the Currency Regulations†

**Designs of coins.**

Regulation 4 of the Currency Regulations is amended—

(a) by omitting from sub-paragraph (vi) of paragraph (d) the words “ the next succeeding sub-paragraph ” and substituting the words and letters “ sub-paragraph (vii) or (viii) ”;

(b) by omitting from sub-paragraph (vi) of paragraph (d) the word “ and ” (last occurring); and

(c) by adding at the end of paragraph (d) the following word and sub-paragraph:—

“ ; and (viii) in the case of a coin of the denomination of Fifty cents that refers on its obverse side to the year 1977 as the year of the coin—the inscriptions ‘ SILVER JUBILEE ’ and ‘ 50 CENTS ’ and a representation of a wattle blossom surrounded by a representation of 25 overlapping crowns.”.

\* Notified in the *Australian Government Gazette* on 16 February. 1977

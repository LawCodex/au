# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 3

Statutory Rules 1993 No. 1331

\__________________

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 7 June 1993.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 3.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46a and 48.]

###### Productivity contributions

**2.** In relation to the period commencing on 1 July 1993, the Table in subsection 110C (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the following Schedule were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table:

### SCHEDULE

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $ 933.33 | $28.00 |
| $933.33 or more but less than $1,503.33 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,503.33 or more but less than $2,255 | $45.10 |
| $2,255 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 23 June 1993.

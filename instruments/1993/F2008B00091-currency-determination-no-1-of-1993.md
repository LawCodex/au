# Currency Determination No. 1 of 1993

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 1 of 1993**

I, ROBERT FRANCIS MCMULLAN, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 16 February 1993.

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer for and on behalf of the Treasurer

[image]**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 1 of 1993.

[NOTE: This Determination commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901,* ss. 46A and 48.]

**Interpretation**

**2**. In this Determination:

**“1 dollar kookaburra coin”** means a coin of the denomination of $1 whose:

(a) composition is not less than 99.9% silver; and

(c) dimensions are specified in clause 4; and

(d) design is specified in clause 5;

**“Act”** means the *Currency Act 1965.*

**Standard weight of 1 dollar kookaburra coin**

**3. (1)** The standard weight of the 1 dollar kookaburra coin is 31.635 grams.

**(2)** A variation of 0.500 grams from the standard weight is allowable.

**Dimensions of 1 dollar kookaburra coin**

**4**. **(1)** The diameter of the 1 dollar kookaburra coin is 40.60 millimetres.

**(2)** The thickness of the coin is 4.00 millimetres.

**Design of the 1 dollar kookaburra coin**

**5**. **(1)** The shape of the 1 dollar kookaburra coin is circular.

**(2)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(3)** The obverse of the coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions “ELIZABETH II AUSTRALIA” and “1 DOLLAR”, all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(4)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) the representation of a kookaburra perched on a branch with a lizard in its beak; and

(b) the inscription “THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 1 OZ 999 SILVER”; and

(c) the inscription in figures of a year; and

(d) a small eagle motif;

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

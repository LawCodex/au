# Currency Determination No. 4 of 1993

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 4 of 1993**

I, ROBERT McMULLAN, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, acting for and on behalf of the Treasurer, make the following Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 10 March 1993.

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

[image]**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 4 of 1993.

**Commencement**

**2.** This Determination commences on 19 March 1993.

**Specifications and design of a $2 coin**

**3. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of $2 the standard composition of which includes not less than 99.9% silver.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin consists of alternating segments as follows:

(a) coarse milled segments approximately 10.25 millimetres in length; and

(b) plain segments approximately 29 millimetres in length.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 62.770 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.5 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 50.3 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 4.5 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions “ELIZABETH II”, “AUSTRALIA” and “2 DOLLARS”;

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a kookaburra perched on a tree stump; and

(b) a representation of 2 Australian flora; and

(c) a representation of 2 whales within a circle; and

(d) the inscriptions “THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 2 OZ. 999 SILVER” and “1993”;

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(10)** This clause does not affect any other determination of the specifications or design of a coin.

**Specifications and design of a $2 coin**

**4. (1)** This clause sets out the specifications and design of a coin of the denomination of $2 the standard composition of which includes not less than 99.9% silver.

**(2)** The shape of the coin is circular.

**(3)** The edge of the coin consists of alternating segments as follows:

(a) coarse milled segments approximately 10.25 millimetres in length; and

(b) plain segments approximately 29 millimetres in length.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 62.770 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.5 grams from the standard weight is allowed.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 50.3 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 4.5 millimetres.

**(8)** The obverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of the Queen; and

(b) the inscriptions “ELIZABETH II”, “AUSTRALIA” and “2 DOLLARS”;

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(9)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a kookaburra perched on a tree stump; and

(b) a representation of 2 Australian flora; and

(c) a representation of the obverse of the coin:

(i) issued in 1813 in the Colony of New South Wales; and

(ii) commonly referred to as “the Dump”; and

(d) the inscriptions “THE AUSTRALIAN KOOKABURRA 2 OZ. 999 SILVER” and “1993”;

all encircled by raised beads inside the rim of the coin.

**(10)** This clause does not affect any other determination of the specifications or design of a coin.

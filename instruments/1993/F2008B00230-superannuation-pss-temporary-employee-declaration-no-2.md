# Superannuation (PSS) Temporary Employee Declaration No. 2

Statutory Rules 1993 No. 2221

\__________________

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister for Finance, make the following declaration under section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990*.

Dated 9 August 1993.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This declaration may be cited as Superannuation (PSS) Temporary Employee Declaration No. 2.

###### Commencement

**2.** This declaration is taken to have commenced on 1 July 1990.

###### Temporary employees

**3.** The definition of “temporary employee” in section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990* applies to a person who is engaged as a temporary employee by the Australian Broadcasting Corporation for employment outside Australia only, if:

(a) the person was a member of the Superannuation Scheme immediately before becoming so engaged; or

(b) the person:

(i) was an eligible employee for the purposes of the *Superannuation Act 1976* immediately before becoming so engaged; and

(ii) while an eligible employee, made an election under subsection 244 (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* that has not been revoked and a declaration under that subsection;

and, if the person became so engaged before the date of notification in the *Gazette* of the making of this declaration, the person made a request, in writing, before that date, to be treated as a member of the Superannuation Scheme while so engaged.

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 13 August 1993.

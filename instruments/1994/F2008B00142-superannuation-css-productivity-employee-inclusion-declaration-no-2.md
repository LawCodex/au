# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Employee Inclusion Declaration No. 2

Statutory Rules 1994 No. 291

\__________________

I, kim christian beazley, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110F of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 14 February 1994.

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Employee Inclusion Declaration No. 2.

###### Commencement

**2.** This Declaration is taken to have commenced on 1 July 1992.

###### Interpretation

**3.** In this Declaration:

**“the Act”** means the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

###### Productivity Employees

**4.** An eligible employee who is an employee of the Legal Aid Commission of New South Wales is declared to be a productivity employee for the purposes of Part VIA of the Act.

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 4 March 1994.

# Superannuation (CSS) Approved Part-time Employee Declaration No. 5

**SUPERANNUATION (CSS) APPROVED PART-TIME**

**EMPLOYEE DECLARATION No. 5**

**I, KIM C BEAZLEY, Minister for Finance, hereby make the following declaration under the definition of the term "approved part-time employee" in subsection 3(1) of the** ***Superannuation Act 1976.***

Dated 16 September 1994

\____________________________

**Citation**

**1.** This declaration may be cited as "Superannuation (CSS) Approved Part-time Employee Declaration No 5".

**Approved Part-time Employee**

**2.** An eligible employee who holds a statutory office and who performs his or her duties on a part-time basis who:

(a) on appointment, performed the duties of that office on a full-time basis; and

(b) is not remunerated for the performance of those duties on a per diem basis;

is included in a class of part-time employees which is an approved class of part-time employees for the purposes of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

**Declaration Renamed**

**3.** Part-time Employment Declaration No. 4 is renamed "Superannuation (CSS) Approved Part-Time Employee Declaration No. 4".

# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 4

**Statutory Rules 1994 No. 2121**

————————

**Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 4**

I, KIM CHRISTIAN BEAZLEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 20 June 1994.

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

————

**Citation**

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 4 Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

**Productivity contributions**

**2.** In relation to the period commencing on 1 July 1994 and ending at the end of 30 June 1995, the Table in subsection 110C (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the Schedule to this Declaration were substituted for the amounts specified in that Table.

**SCHEDULE** Clause 2

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $947.33 | $28.42 |
| $947.33 or more, but less than $1,526.00 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,526.00 or more, but less than $2,289.00 | $45.78 |
| $2,289.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 30 June 1994.

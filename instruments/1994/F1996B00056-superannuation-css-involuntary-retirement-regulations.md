# Superannuation (CSS) (Involuntary Retirement) Regulations

Statutory Rules 1994 No. 2311

\__________________

I, The Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 30 June 1994.

BILL HAYDEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

KIM C. BEAZLEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) (Involuntary Retirement) Regulations.

[NOTE: These Regulations commence on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, s. 48.]

###### Interpretation

**2.** In these Regulations, unless the contrary intention appears:

**“the Act”** means the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

###### Prescribed circumstances in which a person retires, or has employment or appointment terminated

**3.** For the purposes of paragraph 58 (3) (h) of the Act, a person’s retirement under section 127, 128 or 137 of the *Public Sector Management Act 1994* of the Australian Capital Territory is a prescribed circumstance in which the person retires otherwise than under the Public Service Act.

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 30 June 1994.

# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1997-98 First Interest Factor Declaration

Statutory Rules 1997 No. 1451

\__________________

I, JOHN FAHEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under paragraph 3E (1) (a) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 18 June 1997.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1997-98 First Interest Factor Declaration.

[Note: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Interpretation

**2.** In this Declaration:

**“Act”** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*;

**“Days”** means:

(a) in the case of an employee who becomes a fund employee during the financial year starting on 1 July 1997—the number of days in the period:

(i) starting on the last day of the first pay period of the employee ending after 1 July 1997; and

(ii) ending at the end of the day on which an amount first becomes payable under section 4F of the Act; or

(b) in the case of an employee in respect of whom an interim benefit becomes payable under section 6 of the Act during the financial year starting on 1 July 1997—the number of days between:

(i) the last day of the first pay period of the employee ending after 1 July 1997; and

(ii) the day on which the interim benefit became payable; or

(c) in the case of an employee who does not become a fund employee during the financial year starting on 1 July 1997 and in respect of whom an interim benefit does not become payable during that year—the number of days between:

(i) the last day of the first pay period of the employee ending after 1 July 1997; and

(ii) 30 June 1998.

###### Declared first interest factor

**3.** For section 8A of the Act, the declared first interest factor for the financial year starting on 1 July 1997 is:

**0.0783  Days**

**730**.

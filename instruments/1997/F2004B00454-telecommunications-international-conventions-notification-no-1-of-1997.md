# Telecommunications (International Conventions) Notification No. 1 of 1997

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

*Telecommunications Act 1997*

**Telecommunications (International Conventions)**

**Notification No. 1 of 1997**

I, RICHARD KENNETH ROBERT ALSTON, Minister for Communications and the Arts, under section 580 of the *Telecommunications Act 1997*, notify the Australian Communications Authority of each convention specified in the Schedule.

Dated 18 June 1997.

RICHARD ALSTON

Minister for Communications and the Arts

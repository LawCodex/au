# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1997-98) Declaration

Statutory Rules 1997 No. 1491

\__________________

I, john joseph faheY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 10 June 1997.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (1997-98) Declaration.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Productivity contributions

**2.** In relation to the financial year commencing on 1 July 1997, the Table in subsection 110C (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in that Table.

## TABLE

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $1,034.67 | $31.04 |
| $1,034.67 or more, but less than $1,667.33 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,667.33 or more, but less than $2,501.00 | $50.02 |
| $2,501.00 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
|  |  |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 25 June 1997.

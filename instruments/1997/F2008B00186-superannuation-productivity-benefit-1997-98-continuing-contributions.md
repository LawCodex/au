# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1997-98 Continuing Contributions Declaration

Statutory Rules 1997 No. 1441

\__________________

I, JOHN FAHEY, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 18 June 1997.

JOHN FAHEY

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) 1997-98 Continuing Contributions Declaration.

[Note: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Substitution of amounts

**2.** For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, for the financial year starting on 1 July 1997, as if the amounts set out in the following table were substituted for the amounts set out in the table in that Schedule:

TABLE

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $225.50 | $13.53 |
| $225.50 or more but less than $1817.69 | The amount that is 6% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $1817.69 or more | $109.06 |

**NOTE**

1\. Notified in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette* on 25 June 1997.

# Currency Determination No. 6 of 1992

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 6 of 1992**

I, ROBERT MCMULLAN, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 17 January, 1992.

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

for and on behalf of the Treasurer

[image]**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 6 of 1992.

[NOTE: This Determination commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901,* ss.46a and 48.]

**Interpretation**

**2.** In this Determination:

**"$25 coin"** means:

(a) a Princess Margaret coin; or

(b) a Princess of Wales coin; or

(c) a Princess Royal coin; or

(d) a Queen Mother coin;

**"Act"** means the *Currency Act 1965*;

**"Princess Margaret coin"** means a coin:

(a) of the denomination of $25; and

(b) the specifications of which are set out in clauses 3 to 6 (inclusive) of this Determination; and

(c) the design of which is set out in clause 7 of this Determination;

**"Princess of Wales coin"** means a coin:

(a) of the denomination of $25; and

(b) the specifications of which are set out in clauses 3 to 6 (inclusive) of this Determination; and

(c) the design of which is set out in clause 8 of this Determination;

**"Princess Royal coin"** means a coin:

(a) of the denomination of $25; and

(b) the specifications of which are set out in clauses 3 to 6 (inclusive) of this Determination; and

(c) the design of which is set out in clause 9 of this Determination;

**"Queen Mother coin"** means a coin:

(a) of the denomination of $25; and

(b) the specifications of which are set out in clauses 3 to 6 (inclusive) of this Determination; and

(c) the design of which is set out in clause 10 of this Determination.

**Schedule to the Act**

**3.** The Act has effect, on and after 19 July 1992, as if the reference in the Schedule to the Act, opposite to the reference to the denomination "Twenty-five dollars", to the standard composition of coins of that denomination were a reference to the standard compositions "not less than 99.99% gold", "not less than 99.95% platinum" and "92.5% silver and 7.5% other metal".

**Shape and edge of $25 coin**

**4. (1)** The shape of a $25 coin is circular.

**(2)** The edge of the coin is milled

**Composition of $25 coin**

5\. The composition of a $25 coin is 92.5% silver and 7.5% other metal.

**Weight and dimensions of $25 coin**

**6. (1)** The standard weight of a $25 coin is 33.63 grams.

(2) A variation of 0.65 grams from the standard weight is allowable.

(3) The diameter of a $25 coin is 38.90 millimetres.

(4) The thickness of a $25 coin is 3.10 millimetres.

**Design of the Princess Margaret coin**

**7. (1)** The obverse of the Princess Margaret coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II" and "AUSTRALIA 1992".

**(2)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of Her Royal Highness The Princess Margaret, surrounded by a circle of crowns; and

(b) the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II 40TH ANNIVERSARY" and "25 DOLLARS".

**Design of the Princess of Wales coin**

**8. (1)** The obverse of the Princess of Wales coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II" and "AUSTRALIA 1992".

**(2)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of Her Royal Highness the Princess of Wales, surrounded by a circle of crowns; and

(b) the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II 40TH ANNIVERSARY" and "25 DOLLARS".

**Design of the Princess Royal coin**

**9. (1)** The obverse of the Princess Royal coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II" and "AUSTRALIA 1992".

**(2)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of Her Royal Highness the Princess Royal, surrounded by a circle of crowns; and

(b) the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II 40TH ANNIVERSARY" and "25 DOLLARS".

**Design of the Queen Mother coin**

**10. (1)** The obverse of the Queen Mother coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II" and "AUSTRALIA 1992".

**(2)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) an effigy of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth, the Queen Mother, surrounded by a circle of crowns; and

(b) the inscriptions "ELIZABETH II 40TH ANNIVERSARY" and "25 DOLLARS".

**Issue price of $25 coin**

**11.** For the purposes of subsection 14A (1) of the Act, the issue price of a $25 coin is $195.

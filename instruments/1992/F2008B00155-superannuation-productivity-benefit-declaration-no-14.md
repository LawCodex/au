# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Declaration No. 14

Statutory Rules 1992 No. 4621

\__________________

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 21st December 1992.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Declaration No. 14.

[NOTE: This Declaration commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss. 46A and 48.]

###### Substitution of amounts

**2.** For the purposes of section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act is to have effect, in relation to the period commencing on 1 January 1993 and ending at the end of 30 June 1993, as if the amounts set out in the following Table were substituted for the amounts set out in the Table in that Schedule:

**TABLE**

\____________________________________________________________

Less than $270.60 $13.53

$270.60 or more but less than $1538.47 The amount that is 5% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary

$1538.47 or more $76.92

# Superannuation Act 1976 (Eligible Superannuation Schemes) Declaration No. 1

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

**SUPERANNUATION ACT 1976**

**DECLARATION**

The Commonwealth Superannuation Board of Trustees No. 2, pursuant to subsection 134(1) of the Superannuation Act 1976 DECLARES as follows:

**Citation:**

1\. This declaration may be cited as the “Superannuation Act 1976 (Eligible Superannuation Schemes) Declaration No. 1”.

**Interpretation:**

2\. (1) In this declaration:

**“Act”** means the Superannuation Act 1976.

(2) Words and expressions defined in s.3(1) of the Act have the same meaning in this declaration.

**Commencement**

3\. Clause 5 of this determination shall take effect from and

including 1 October 1991.

**Declaration of Eligible Superannuation Schemes**

4\. The following superannuation schemes are hereby eligible superannuation schemes for the purposes of Division 3 of Part IX of

the Act:

the AIDC Executive Superannuation Plan; and

the AIDC Staff Superannuation Plan.

5\. The following superannuation scheme is hereby an eligible superannuation scheme for the purposes of Division 3 of Part IX of

the Act:

the CAA Staff Superannuation Fund.

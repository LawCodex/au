# Currency Determination No. 8 of 1992

**Commonwealth of Australia**

*Currency Act 1965*

**Currency Determination No. 8 of 1992**

I, ROBERT McMULLAN, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, make the following Determination under the *Currency Act 1965.*

Dated 4 February 1992.

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer for and on behalf of the Treasurer

[image]**Citation**

**1.** This Determination may be cited as Currency Determination No. 8 of 1992.

[NOTE: This Determination commences on gazettal: see *Acts Interpretation Act 1901*, ss.46A and 48.]

**Interpretation**

**2.** In this Determination:

**“$5 silver satellite coin”** means a coin of the denomination of $5:

(a) the specifications of which are set out in clause 4; and

(b) the design of which is set out in clause 5;

**“Act”** means the *Currency Act 1965.*

**Specifications of coins**

**3.** On and after 5 September 1992, the standard weight, the allowable variation from that standard weight, the design and the dimensions of a $5 silver satellite coin are as set out in this determination.

**Specifications of $5 silver satellite coin**

**4 (1)** The shape of a $5 silver satellite coin is circular.

**(2)** The edge of the coin is milled.

**(3)** The composition of the coin is 92.5% silver and 7.5% other metal.

**(4)** The standard weight of the coin is 35.79 grams.

**(5)** A variation of 0.63 grams from the standard weight is allowable.

**(6)** The diameter of the coin is 38.90 millimetres.

**(7)** The thickness of the coin is 3.50 millimetres.

**Design of $5 silver satellite coin**

**5. (1)** The obverse of a $5 silver satellite coin bears an effigy of the Queen and the inscriptions “ELIZABETH II AUSTRALIA” and “1992”.

**(2)** The reverse of the coin bears:

(a) a representation of a group of satellites in the form of the Southern Cross orbiting a globe on which the Australian continent appears; and

(b) the inscription “5 DOLLARS”.

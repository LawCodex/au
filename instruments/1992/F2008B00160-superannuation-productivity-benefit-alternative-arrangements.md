# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Alternative Arrangements Declaration No. 4

Statutory Rules 1992 No. 3661

\__________________

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister of State for Finance, make the following Declaration under subsections 4A (1) and (2) of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 30th October 1992.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister of State for Finance

\____________

###### Citation

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Alternative Arrangements Declaration No. 4.

###### Commencement

**2.** This Declaration takes effect from 1 July 1990.

###### Interpretation

**3.** In this Declaration:

**“relevant person”** means a person specified in the Schedule by reference to the name and Australian Government Staff Number of the person;

**“the Act”** means the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

###### Declaration of certain employees as a class

**4.** For the purposes of subsection 4a (1) of the Act, relevant persons who:

(a) are qualified employees; and

(b) are not scheme employees;

are a class of employees who are not fund employees or interim arrangement employees.

###### Arrangements specified in relation to declared class of employees

**5.** For the purposes of subsection 4a (2) of the Act, the following arrangements apply to a member of the class of employees referred to in clause 4:

(a) the member is taken to be a fund employee and a remainder employee;

(b) the superannuation fund specified in the Schedule in relation to the member is taken to be an approved fund;

(c) the Act, other than subsections 4c (2) and (3), is taken to apply to the member.

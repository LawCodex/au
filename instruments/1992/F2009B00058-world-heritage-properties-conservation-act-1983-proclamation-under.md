# World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983 - Proclamation under section 9 - Lune River (19/08/1992)

**PROCLAMATION**

| Commonwealth of Australia | By His Excellency the<br>Governor-General of the<br>Commonwealth of Australia |
|---|---|

BILL HAYDEN

Governor-General

I, WILLIAM GEORGE HAYDEN, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council and under subsection 6 (3) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983*, being satisfied that the property described in the Schedule is likely to be damaged,

declare that property to be property to which section 9 of that Act applies.

\__________________

SCHEDULE

All the land in the vicinity of Lune River in the State of Tasmania that:

(a) is part of the land:

(i) described in the indenture between the Honourable Francis Roger Groom and Benders Spreading Services (1964) Pty Ltd made on 7 June 1984, as amended and in force on 11 June 1992; and

(ii) demised and leased to Benders Spreading Services (1964) Pty Ltd under the indenture; and

(b) is also included in the World Heritage List referred to in subparagraph 3A (1) (a) (iii) of the *World Heritage Properties Conservation Act 1983.*

GIVEN under my Hand and\
the Great Seal of Australia\
on 3 August 1992

(LS)

By His Excellency’s Command,

WENDY FATIN

Minister of State for the Arts and Territories

GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!

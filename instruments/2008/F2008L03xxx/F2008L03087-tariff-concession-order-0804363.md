# Tariff Concession Order 0804363

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 18.04.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8422.30.90 | MACHINES, LABELLING, PAIL OR CAN, in line, metal OR plastic,<br>having BOTH of the following:<br>(a) pail or can capacity NOT less than 0.5 litres and NOT<br>greater than 15 litres;<br>(b) labelling capacity NOT less than 15 labels per minute<br>Op. 18.04.08 - TC 0804363 | 50 |

# Tariff Concession Order 0814287

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 24.06.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8536.69.90 | CONNECTORS, switching, electrical, having ALL of the<br>following:<br>(a) single OR three phase switching;<br>(b) current rating NOT less than 16 A and NOT greater<br>than 250 A;<br>(c) operating voltage NOT greater than 690 V;<br>(d) AC23 rating for load switching;<br>(e) dependant manual operation;<br>(f) IP (International Protection) 20 rating<br>Op. 24.06.08 - TC 0814287 | 50 |

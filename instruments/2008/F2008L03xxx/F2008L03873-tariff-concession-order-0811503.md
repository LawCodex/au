# Tariff Concession Order 0811503

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 05.06.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| 8705.90.00 | PLATFORMS, TRUCK MOUNTED, hydraulic elevating mobile, having ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) 4x4 drive chassis;<br>(b) elevated height capacity NOT greater than 29 metres;<br>(c) horizontal outreach capacity NOT greater than 19.5 metres<br>Op. 05.06.08 - TC 0811503 | 50 |

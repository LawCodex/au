# Tariff Concession Order 0815096

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 27.06.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7207.11.00 | BILLETS, square, semi-finished non-alloy steel, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) thickness and width NOT less than 130 mm X 130 mm;<br>(b) length NOT less than 11 800 mm and NOT greater than<br>12 200 mm;<br>(c) chemical composition by weight of ALL of the following:<br>(i) carbon 0.13 % - 0.17%;<br>(ii) manganese 0.35% - 0.55%;<br>(iii) sulphur 0.035 % maximum;<br>(iv) chromium 0.2 % maximum;<br>(v) copper 0.3% maximum;<br>(vi) phosphorous 0.035% maximum;<br>(vii) silicon 0.14% - 0.24%<br>Op. 27.06.08 - TC 0815096 | 50 |
|---|---|---|

# Tariff Concession Order 0808968

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 20.05.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7616.99.00 | CASES, aluminium, with or without internal dividers, having ALL<br>of the following:<br>(a) Medium Density Fibreboard (MDF) and/or foam lining;<br>(b) length NOT less than 300 mm and NOT greater than 500 mm;<br>(c) width NOT less than 200 mm and NOT greater than 350 mm;<br>(d) depth NOT less than 50 mm and NOT greater than 200 mm<br>Op. 20.05.08 - TC 0808968 | 50 |

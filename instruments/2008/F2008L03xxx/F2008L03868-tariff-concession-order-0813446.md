# Tariff Concession Order 0813446

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 18.06.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 3918.10.00 | COVERINGS, FLOOR, vinyl, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) NOT less than three layers;<br>(b) thickness NOT less than 2 mm and NOT greater than 6 mm;<br>(c) abrasive wear loss of 300 mg + or - 50 mg as determined in<br>accordance with International Standard for Organization<br>9352 (ISO 9352);<br>(d) burning rate NOT exceeding zero mm per minute<br>determined in accordance with International Standard for<br>Organization 3795 (ISO 3795)<br>Op. 18.06.08 - TC 0813446 | 50 |

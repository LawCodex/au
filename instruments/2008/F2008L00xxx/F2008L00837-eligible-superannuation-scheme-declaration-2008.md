# Eligible Superannuation Scheme Declaration 2008

*Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits Act 1973*

**Eligible Superannuation Scheme Declaration 2008**

I, LINDSAY TANNER, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, pursuant to section 73 of the *Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits Act 1973* (the Act), do hereby:

(a) revoke all existing declarations made pursuant to section 73 of the Act;

(b) declare that the superannuation schemes specified in the Schedule to this Declaration are eligible superannuation schemes for the purposes of Division 3 of Part IX of the Act; and

(c) specify 1 January 2008 as the day on which this Declaration is deemed to come into force.

Dated: 29 February 2008

Lindsay Tanner

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

SCHEDULE

1\. The superannuation schemes established under the following Acts:

(a) *Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Act 1948*;

(b) *Superannuation Act 1976*;

(c) *Superannuation Act 1990*.

2\. The superannuation schemes known as:

(a) AvSuper;

(b) Northern Territory Government and Public Authorities Superannuation Scheme;

(c) QSuper;

(d) Queensland Electricity Supply Industry Superannuation Scheme;

(e) Queensland Local Government Superannuation Scheme;

(f) Queensland Parliamentary Contributory Superannuation Scheme;

(g) UniSuper – Accumulation Plan 2.

# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Instrument adopting Recovery Plans (08/01/2008) (NT, QLD, NSW)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

Instrument adopting Recovery Plans

I, PETER ROBERT GARRETT, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and the Arts:

Under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as the recovery plan for the listed threatened species specified below, the plans made by the Northern Territory, Queensland and New South Wales governments (or agencies of the State/Territory) specified opposite the listed threatened species:

**Northern Territory Recovery Plans (2)**

| **Listed Threatened Species** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| *Acacia latzii, Acacia peuce, Acacia pickardii, Acacia undoolyana, Ricinocarpos gloria-medii* | Nano, C., Harris, M. and Pavey, C.R. (2007). National recovery plan for threatened Acacias and *Ricinocarpos gloria-medii* in central Australia. Northern Territory Department of Natural Resources, Environment and the Arts, Alice Springs. |
| Gove Crow Butterfly *Euploea alcathoe enastri* | Braby, M.F. (2007). National recovery plan for the<br>Gove Crow Butterfly *Euploea alcathoe enastri.*<br>Northern Territory Department of Natural Resources, Environment and the Arts. |

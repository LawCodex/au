# AD/ERJ-170/3 Amdt 1 - Air Data Smart Probes

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/ERJ-170/3 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Embraer ERJ-170 Series Aeroplanes

| AD/ERJ-170/3 Amdt 1 | Air Data Smart Probes | 4/2008 DM |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | ERJ-170( ) model aeroplanes, equipped with air data smart probes part number (P/N) 2015G2H2H-4( ), 2015G2H2H-5( ), 2015G2H2H-6( ) or 2015G2H2H-7( ). |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | 1. Carry out a detailed inspection of the four "Air Data Smart Probes" drain holes and remove any accumulated moisture in accordance with Embraer Service Bulletin (SB) 170-34-0007, original issue, dated 28 April 2005 or further revisions approved by the Brazilian Agência Nacional de Avição Civil (ANAC). |
|  | Note 1: For the purpose of this Directive, a detailed inspection is defined as: "An intensive examination of a specific item, installation or assembly to detect damage, failure or irregularity. Available lighting is normally supplemented with a direct source of good lighting at an intensity deemed appropriate. Inspection aids such as mirrors, magnifying lenses, etc., may be necessary. Surface cleaning and elaborate access procedures may be required." |
|  | The optional installation of air data smart probes P/N 2015G2H2H-8( ) in accordance with the procedures detailed in the Embraer SB 170-34-0018 original issue, dated<br>19 September 2006 or further revisions approved by ANAC, constitutes terminal action to the repetitive inspections. |
|  | 2. Air data smart probes part number (P/N) 2015G2H2H-4( ), 2015G2H2H-5( ), 2015G2H2H-6( ) or 2015G2H2H-7( ) may not be reinstalled on any aeroplane as a replacement for probe P/N 2015G2H2H-8( ). |
|  | Note 2: ANAC AD 2006-05-05R1 refers. |
| Compliance: | For Requirement 1 - Either within the next 600 hours time in service (TIS) after the first issue of an Australian Certificate of Airworthiness or within 600 hours TIS after the last inspection in accordance with the original issue of this Directive, which ever occurs first, and thereafter, at intervals that do not exceeds 600 hours TIS until the air data smart probe is replaced by a probe P/N 2015G2H2H-8( ). |
|  | For Requirement 2 - As of the effective date of this Amendment. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 4 April 2008. |

| Background: | The ANAC has received reports of moisture being found in the pneumatic passage between the pitot port and the pressure sensors in the air data smart probes. That moisture may freeze and block the sensors, causing erroneous information to the Air Data System. |
|---|---|
|  | Since this condition may affect the safety of the aeroplane, the original issue of this Directive required the repetitive inspection of the air data smart probes for moisture and, if necessary, the removal of any accumulated moisture. |
|  | This Amendment continues the repetitive inspection of the probes until the probe is optionally replaced with a probe P/N 2015G2H2H-8( ). |
|  | The original issue of this Directive became effective on 4 April 2007. |

# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 28/2008 - Tariff Concession Order 0801962

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(3) of the Customs Act 1901, I Michael Perry a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 0717718 made on 16.01.08, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 17.10.07; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 5903.10.90 | FABRICS, POLYESTER AND/OR COTTON AND POLYESTER AND/OR RAYON AND<br>POLYESTER, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) polyvinyl chloride coatings;<br>(b) thickness NOT less than 0.3 mm and NOT greater<br>than 1.8 mm;<br>(c) width NOT less than 1 100 mm and NOT greater than 2 100 mm;<br>(d) weight NOT less than 200 g/m2 and NOT greater<br>than 1 800 g/m2;<br>(e) length NOT less than 15 m<br><br>Op. 17.10.07 - TC 0717718 | 50<br>N/A |

# Tariff Concession Order 0719070

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 09.11.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 5407.84.00 | FABRIC, printed, 50% polyester, 50% cotton, having ALL of the<br>following:<br>(a) thread count NOT less than 76 x 64 picks and NOT greater<br>than 110 x 90 picks;<br>(b) yarn gauge NOT less than 16/16 and NOT greater than 40/40;<br>(c) width NOT less than 240 cms and NOT greater than 285 cms<br>Op. 09.11.07 - TC 0719070 | 50 |

# Tariff Concession Order 0721282

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 13.12.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 5903.20.90 | FABRICS, KNITTED, flame retardant, anti-microbial, anti-fungal,<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) polyamide base fabric weight NOT less than 110 gm/2 and<br>NOT greater than 170 gm/2;<br>(b) polyurethane layer weight NOT less than 80 gm/2 and NOT<br>greater than 130 gm/2;<br>(c) width NOT less than 1 200 mm and NOT greater than 1 500 mm<br>Op. 13.12.07 - TC 0721282 | 50 |

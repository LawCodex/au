# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2008-2009 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2008

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2008–2009 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 20081

I, niCK SHERRY, Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law, acting for and on behalf of the Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 19 June 2008

NICK SHERRY

Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law

for and on behalf of the Minister for Finance and Deregulation

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2008-2009 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2008.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 July 2008.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2008, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $2,936.92 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $2,936.92 or more | $264.32 |

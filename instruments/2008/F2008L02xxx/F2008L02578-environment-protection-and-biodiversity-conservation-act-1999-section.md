# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Instrument Adopting Recovery Plans (06/07/2008) (WA, QLD)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

Instrument Adopting Recovery Plans

I, PETER ROBERT GARRETT, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and the Arts:

Under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as the recovery plan for the listed threatened species and ecological community specified below, the plans made by Western Australia and Queensland Governments (or agencies of the State) specified opposite the listed threatened species/ecological community:

**Western Australia (10)**

| **Listed Threatened Species/Ecological Community** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| Kundip Wattle *Acacia rhamphophylla* | Hartley, R., & Barrett, S., (2008), Recovery Plan for the Kundip Wattle *Acacia rhamphophylla.* |
| Slender Andersonia *Andersonia gracilis* | Stack, G., Taylor, H., Sage, L., Evans, R., Broun, G., & English, V., (2008), Recovery Plan for the Slender Andersonia *Andersonia gracilis.* |
| Feather-leaved Banksia *Banksia brownii* | Gilfillan, S., & Barrett, S., (2008), Recovery Plan for the Feather-Leaved Banksia *Banksia brownii.* |
| Manypeaks Rush *Chordifex abortivus* | Hartley, R., Gilfillan, S., & Barrett, S., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Manypeaks Rush *Chordifex abortivus.* |
| Dunsborough Spider-orchid *Caladenia viridescens* | Patten, J., Webb, A., & Batty, A., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Dunsborough Spider Orchid *Caladenia viridescens.* |
| Grass Conostylis *Conostylis misera* | Hartley, R., & Barrett, S., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Grass Conostylis *Conostylis misera.* |
| Yellow Mountain Bell *Darwinia collina* | Hartley, R., & Barrett, S., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Yellow Mountain Bell *Darwinia collina.* |
| Wittwer’s Mountain Bell *Darwinia wittwerorum* | Hartley, R., Gilfillan, S., & Barrett, S., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Wittwer’s Mountain Bell *Darwinia wittwerorum.* |
| Long-sepalled Daviesia *Daviesia megacalyx* | Hartley R., & Barrett, S., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Long-sepalled Daviesia *Daviesia megacalyx.* |
| Naturaliste Nancy *Wurmbea calcicola* | Taylor, H., English, V., & Webb, A., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Naturaliste Nancy *Wurmbea calcicola.* |

**Queensland Recovery Plans (3)**

| **Listed Threatened Species/Ecological Community** | **Recovery Plan** |
|---|---|
| Boggomoss Snail *Adclarkia dawsonensis* | Dr Stanisic, J., Queensland Museum (2008) Recovery Plan for the Boggomoss Snail *Adclarkia dawsonensis.* |
| Yellow Chat (Dawson) *Epthianura crocea macgregori* | Houston, W., & Melzer, A., (2008) Recovery Plan for the Yellow Chat (Dawson) *Epthianura crocea macgregori.* |
| Mabi Forest (Complex Notophyll Vine Forest 5b) | Latch, P., for the Mabi Forest Recovery Team (2008) Recovery Plan for the Mabi Forest (Complex Notophyll Vine Forest 5b) Ecological Community. |

The adopted recovery plans will come into force on the day after they are registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

Dated this sixth day of July 2008.

PETER ROBERT GARRETT

**Minister for the Environment, Heritage and the Arts**

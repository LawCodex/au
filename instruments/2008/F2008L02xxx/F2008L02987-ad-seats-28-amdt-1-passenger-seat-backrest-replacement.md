# AD/SEATS/28 Amdt 1 - Passenger Seat Backrest - Replacement

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/SEATS/28 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | Failure of seat backrest recline actuator metal fittings having tool marks have been reported on in-service aircraft. The actions specified by this Airworthiness Directive (AD) are intended to prevent further failures of the seat backrests which could result in injury to passengers or crew members during an emergency landing.<br>Since the issuance of the original issue of this Airworthiness Directive, Aviointeriors has identified a life limitation for backrests that do not have tool marks. Consequently, this Airworthiness Directive mandates the replacement of those backrests before reaching the specified threshold.<br>The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective 15 February 2007. |

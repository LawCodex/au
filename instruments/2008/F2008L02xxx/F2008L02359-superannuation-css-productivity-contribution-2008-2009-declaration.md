# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2008-2009) Declaration 2008

Superannuation (CSS)

Productivity Contribution

(2008-2009) Declaration 2008

I, JONATHON EDWARD HALE, Manager of Australian Public Sector Defined Benefit Schemes Administration Section, Commonwealth Superannuation Administration, Position No. 44, and Delegate of the Australian Reward Investment Alliance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 30 June 2008

J HALE\
Delegate of Australian Reward Investment Alliance

[image]

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2008-2009) Declaration 2008*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration shall take effect from and including 1 July 2008.

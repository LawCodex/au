# Tariff Concession Order 0803932

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 12.03.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8501.34.00 | MOTORS, DC, water and/or air cooled, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) output exceeding 375 kW;<br>(b) speed NOT less than 650 RPM and NOT greater than 1 400 RPM;<br>(c) armature voltage NOT less than 460 V DC and NOT greater<br>than 750 V DC<br>Op. 12.03.08 - TC 0803932 | 50 |

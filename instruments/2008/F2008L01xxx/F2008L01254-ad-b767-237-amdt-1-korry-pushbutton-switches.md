# AD/B767/237 Amdt 1 - Korry Pushbutton Switches

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/B767/237 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The FAA has received a report indicating that the integrated drive generator failed in flight due to a possible switch malfunction. This Directive is issued to ensure that certain lighted pushbutton switches in the flight compartment do not malfunction and cause the flightcrew to be unable to control critical systems and continue safe operation of the aeroplane. |
|  | The original issue of this Directive required an inspection of certain lighted pushbutton switches in the flight compartment for configuration \`D' master modules and part numbers together with any necessary corrective action. The Directive also provided an option to inspect panel assemblies for part numbers. |
|  | This Amendment continues the requirements of the original issue of the Directive and corrects a typographical error in Requirement 3. |
|  | The original issue of this Directive became effective on 10 April 2008. |

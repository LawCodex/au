# Tariff Concession Order 0800279

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 07.01.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4810.31.00 | KRAFT PAPER, silicone base, bleached, clay coated on both sides,<br>having ALL of the following:<br>(a) weight NOT less than 124 g/sq m and NOT greater than<br>128 g/sq m;<br>(b) Bendtsen International Standards Organisation ISO 8791/2<br>roughness on coated sides NOT exceeding 110 ml/min;<br>(c) reel width NOT less than 1 000 mm and NOT greater than<br>1 525 mm<br>Op. 07.01.08 - TC 0800279 | 50 |

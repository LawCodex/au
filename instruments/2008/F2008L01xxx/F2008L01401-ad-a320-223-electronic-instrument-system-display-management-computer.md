# AD/A320/223 - Electronic Instrument System Display Management Computer Software

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

For the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below issues the following Airworthiness Directive (AD) under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

<td>Requirement:</td>
<td colspan="7">1. The following dispatch limitations are mandatory for all flights:</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="7"><i>For aircraft equipped with EIS2 LCD Display Units and with conventional standby instruments:</i><br><i>- Dispatch with the standby IAS (Indicated Airspeed) indicator inoperative (MMEL 34-21-01) is no longer authorised.</i><br><i>- Dispatch with inoperative standby horizon or inoperative standby altimeter may be authorised provided that the crew maintains VMC conditions during the entire flight and day-light conditions.</i><br><i>For aircraft equipped with EIS2 LCD Display Units and with the Integrated Standby Instrument System (ISIS):</i><br><i>- Dispatch with the ISIS airspeed function inoperative (MMEL</i><br><i>34-22-03-C) is no longer authorised.</i><br><i>- Dispatch with inoperative ISIS horizon function or inoperative ISIS altitude function may be authorised provided that the crew maintains VMC conditions during the entire flight and day-light conditions.</i><br><i>Reminder: For dispatch with one Display Unit inoperative as per MMEL</i><br><i>31-63-01, it is reminded that the subject Display Unit must be switched-off as per associated MMEL operational procedure.</i></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="7">Incorporation of the Master Minimum Equipment List (MMEL) changes or a copy of this Directive in the Aircraft Operations Manual is a satisfactory method of compliance with this Requirement.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="7">2. Install EIS2 standard S7 (DMC disk part number F1461768) in accordance with the instructions of Airbus SB A320-31-1276 dated 18 April 2007 or later revision approved by the European Aviation Safety Agency (EASA).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="7">This action is not required if Airbus SB A320-31-1263 Revision 1 or Airbus SB A320-31-1276 Original Issue has been embodied.</td>

<tr>
<td>Background:</td>
<td colspan="7">The EASA has advised that two incidents have occurred due to the lack of visibility on the Primary Flight Display (PFD) of the Traffic Alert and Collision Avoidance System (TCAS) indications.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td></td>
<td colspan="7">EIS2 standard S7 introduces modifications to the vertical speed indication to improve the legibility in case of TCAS Resolution Advisory. The modifications consist in changing the colour of the needle and increasing the width of the TCAS green band.</td>
</tr>

# Tariff Concession Order 0718565

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Conor Annesley, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 30.10.07 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 5512.29.00 | FABRIC, ACRYLIC, woven staple yarn, fluorocarbon treated, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) hydrostatic pressure resistance NOT less than 300 mm<br>and NOT greater than 315 mm;<br>(b) weight 290 grams per square metre;<br>(c) tensile strength NOT less than 1 250 N (warp) and NOT less<br>than 700 N (weft)<br><br>For the purpose of this order, all measurements have a tolerance<br>of + or - 5%<br>Op. 30.10.07 - TC 0718565 | 50 |

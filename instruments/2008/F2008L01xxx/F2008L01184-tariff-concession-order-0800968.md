# Tariff Concession Order 0800968

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 17.01.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4412.31.00 | DOOR SKINS, PLYWOOD, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) thickness NOT greater than 3.6 mm;<br>(b) width NOT less than 500 mm and NOT greater than 1 332 mm;<br>(c) length NOT less than 2 000 mm and NOT greater than 3 050 mm<br>Op. 17.01.08 - TC 0800968 | 50 |

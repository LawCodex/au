# AD/PA-20/9 Amdt 1 - Exhaust System - Inspection

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/PA-20/9 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Piper PA-20 (Pacer) Series Aeroplanes

| AD/PA-20/9 Amdt 1 | Exhaust System - Inspection | 6/2008 |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | All models. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | In accordance with Piper Service Letter No. 324B.<br>Note: FAA AD 68-05-01 also refers. |
| Compliance: | Inspection at time intervals not exceeding 100 hours time in service. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 5 June 2008. |
| Background: | Correcting identification of Piper service instruction and adding FAA AD. |

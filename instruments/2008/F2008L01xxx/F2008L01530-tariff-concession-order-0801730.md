# Tariff Concession Order 0801730

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 31.01.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8414.80.19 | COMPRESSORS, BREATHABLE AIR, RECIPROCATION PISTON, complying<br>with Australian/New Zealand Standard AS/NZS 2299.1:1999 AND<br>AS/NZS 1715:1994, having BOTH of the following:<br>(a) continuous working pressure NOT less than 25 000 kPa;<br>(b) compressed air delivery NOT less than 100 L per minute<br>Op. 31.01.08 - TC 0801730 | 50 |

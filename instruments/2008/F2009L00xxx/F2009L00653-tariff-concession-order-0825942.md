# Tariff Concession Order 0825942

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 11.08.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 7217.90.00 | WIRE, STEEL, being hard drawn low carbon (HDLC) wire, phosphate<br>coated, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) diameter NOT less than 18.00 mm but NOT greater than<br>20.00 mm;<br>(b) chemical composition by weight of ALL of the following:<br>(i) carbon 0.04% to 0.10%;<br>(ii) phosphorus 0.03% maximum;<br>(iii) manganese 0.40% to 0.60%;<br>(iv) silicon 0.12% to 0.24%;<br>(v) sulphur 0.03% maximum<br>Op. 11.08.08 - TC 0825942 | 50 |

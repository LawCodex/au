# Tariff Concession Order 0826127

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 12.08.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 6815.99.00 | REFRACTORY BRICKS AND SHAPES, MAGNESIA CARBON, unfired or<br>unburned, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) magnesia content NOT less than 70%;<br>(b) carbon content NOT less than 5%;<br>(c) bulk density NOT less than 2.7 gm/cc<br>Op. 12.08.08 - TC 0826127 | 50 |

# Tariff Concession Order 0828028

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 25.08.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4811.90.90 | PAPER, THERMAL IMAGING, in rolls, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) roll width NOT less than 1 175 mm;<br>(b) weight NOT greater than 77 grams per square metre;<br>(c) thickness NOT less than 81 microns and NOT greater than 85<br>microns<br>Op. 25.08.08 - TC 0828028 | 50 |

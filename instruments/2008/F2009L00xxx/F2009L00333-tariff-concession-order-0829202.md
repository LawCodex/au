# Tariff Concession Order 0829202

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Damien Beaumont, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 03.09.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

|---|---|

| 3924.90.00 | HOUSEHOLD ARTICLES, plastic, being ANY of the following:<br>(a) organisers;<br>(b) cosmetic holders;<br>(c) jewellery holders;<br>(d) cleaning aid holders;<br>(e) stationery holders;<br>(f) paper towel holders;<br>(g) toilet roll holders;<br>(h) toilet brush holders;<br>(i) toothbrush holders;<br>(j) jewellery stands;<br>(k) soap dishes or soap savers<br>Op. 03.09.08 - TC 0829202 | 50 |

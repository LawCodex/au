# Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 2) Amendment Determination 2008 (No. 1)

Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 2) Amendment Determination 2008 (No. 1)1

*Currency Act 1965*

I, NICK SHERRY, Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law, make this Determination under subsection 13A (1) of the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 15 December 2008

NICK SHERRY

Minister for Superannuation and Corporate Law

1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 2) Amendment Determination 2008 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Determination is taken to have commenced on 1 November 2008.

3 Amendment of *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 2)*

Schedule 1 amends the *Currency (Perth Mint) Determination 2008 (No. 2)*.

# Tariff Concession Order 0823069

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 25.07.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8427.10.00 | PALLET TRUCKS, as defined in Australian Standard AS 2359-1995<br>clause 3.1.3.2.1, end rider with seated operator, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) maximum load capacity NOT less than 2 000 kg;<br>(b) lift height NOT greater than 200 mm;<br>(c) weight (excluding battery) NOT less than 1 600 kg;<br>(d) maximum speed NOT less than 15 km/h<br>Op. 25.07.08 - TC 0823069 | 50 |

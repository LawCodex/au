# AD/JBK 117/6 Amdt 6 - Main Rotor Blade

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/JBK 117/6 Amdt 5 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

#### Kawasaki BK 117 Series Helicopters

| AD/JBK 117/6 Amdt 6 | Main Rotor Blade | 13/2008 |
|---|---|---|

| Applicability: | Model BK117, BK117A-3, BK117A-4, BK117B-1, BK117B-2, and BK117C-1 helicopters; equipped with main rotor blade part numbers 117-150041, 117-150051, 117-150071, 117-150081, 117-151311, 117-151331, 117-151371, and<br>117-151371V001. |
|---|---|
| Requirement: | 1. Revise the KHI-BK117 Maintenance Manual to Revision No. 14 (or later approved revision) for Models KHI-BK117, BK117A-3, BK117A-4, BK117B-1, BK117B-2, and Revision No. 2 (or later approved revision) for Model<br>KHI-BK117C-1.<br>2. Perform an inspection for bulging on the upper and lower surfaces of the main rotor blades in the area of the outboard lead balance weight in accordance with the KHI-BK117 Maintenance Manual, revised by Requirement 1. Inspect at the intervals specified in the KHI-BK117 Maintenance Manual, revised by Requirement 1.<br>If the inspection results exceed the allowable limit specified in the KHI-BK117 Maintenance Manual, before further flight, contact KHI Customer Service for technical advice.<br>Note 1: Kawasaki Service Bulletin No. KSB-117-115A-1 dated 11 September 2008, refers.<br>*Note 2: JCAB AD TCD-4114A-1-2008 refers.* |
| Compliance: | Unless Requirement 1 is already accomplished, before further flight after<br>18 December 2008. Thereafter, inspect as specified in Requirement 2. |
|  | This Amendment becomes effective on 18 December 2008. |
| Background: | In response to incidents, the manufacturer recommended an immediate inspection for bulging on the upper and lower surfaces of the main rotor blades in the area of the outboard lead balance weight.<br>Amendment 1 referenced the JCAB AD and the Kawasaki service bulletin rather than the LBA AD and the MBB service bulletin of the initial issue. |

|  | Amendment 2 introduced an additional blade part number to applicability.<br>Amendment 3 was issued in response to a new JCAB AD, which expanded applicability to all main rotor blade part numbers.<br>Amendment 4 was issued in response to a new JCAB AD, which transferred the inspection requirements of this Directive to the KHI-BK117 Maintenance Manual. This also introduced an additional inspection.<br>Amendment 5 excluded certain Main Rotor Blade part numbers from inspection, with no change to the requirements or compliance.<br>Amendment 6 is issued in response to a revision of the related JCAB AD, which specifies the part numbers of affected main rotor blades.<br>The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on<br>13 October 1994. |

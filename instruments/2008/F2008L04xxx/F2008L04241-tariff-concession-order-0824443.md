# Tariff Concession Order 0824443

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 01.08.08 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8429.51.90 | WHEEL LOADERS, ARTICULATED, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) operating weight exceeding 16 000 kg;<br>(b) standard bucket capacity NOT less than 3 m3;<br>(c) maximum dumping height NOT less than 3 000 mm;<br>(d) maximum dumping reach NOT less than 1 000 mm;<br>(e) maximum breakout force NOT less than 150 kN<br>Op. 01.08.08 - TC 0824443 | 50 |

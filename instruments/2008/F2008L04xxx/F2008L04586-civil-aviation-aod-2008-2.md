# Civil Aviation AOD 2008/2

**Instrument number Civil Aviation AOD 2008/2**

I, ANTHONY ALBANESE, Minister for Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development and Local Government, make this instrument under subsection 33(2) of the *Civil Aviation Act 1988*.

Anthony Albanese\
Minister for Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development and Local Government

2 December 2008

Testable drugs for Part IV of the *Civil Aviation Act 1988*

1 Name of instrument

This instrument is *Civil Aviation AOD 2008/2* (the ***testable drugs specification***).

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

3 Revocation

Instrument *Civil Aviation AOD 2008/1* is revoked.

4 Specification of testable drugs for Part IV of the Civil Aviation Act

I specify each drug mentioned in an item in Schedule 1 for the purposes of the definition of ***testable drug*** in subsection 33 (1) of the *Civil Aviation Act 1988*.

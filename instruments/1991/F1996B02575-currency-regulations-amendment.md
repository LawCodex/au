# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1991 No. 31**1

**__________________**

**Currency Regulations**2 **(Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 27 February 1991.

BILL HAYDEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency's Command,

R. McMULLAN

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

for and on behalf of the Treasurer

\____________

**1. Amendment**

1.1 The Currency Regulations are amended as set out in these Regulations.

**2. Regulation 4 (Designs of coins)**

2.1 Subparagraph 4 (d) (vi):

Omit the subparagraph, insert:

“(vi) in the case of a coin of the denomination of 50 cents, other than a coin referred to in subparagraph (vii), (viii), (viiia), (viiib), (viiibaa), (viiibab) or (viiibac)—the figures ‘50’ and a representation of the arms of the Commonwealth;”.

**3. Regulation 7 (The Schedule to the Act)**

3.1 Paragraphs 7 (8) (h) and (i):

Omit “expressions”, substitute “standard composition”.

# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 1

**Statutory Rules 1991 No. 128**1

**__________________**

**Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 1**

I, RALPH WILLIS, Minister of State for Finance, make the following Declaration under section 110d of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 5 June 1991.

RALPH WILLIS

Minister of State for Finance

\____________

**Citation**

**1.** This Declaration may be cited as the Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution Declaration No. 1.

**Productivity contributions**

**2.** In relation to the period commencing on 1 July 1991, the Table in subsection 110c (1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976* is to have effect as if the amounts specified in the following Schedule were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table:

**SCHEDULE**

| *Fortnightly rate of salary* | *Fortnightly productivity contribution* |
|---|---|
| Less than $904 | $27.12 |
| $904 or more but less than $1,442 | The amount that is 3% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary |
| $1,442 or more but less than $2,163 | $43.26 |
| $2,163 or more | The amount that is 2% of the employee’s fortnightly rate of salary. |

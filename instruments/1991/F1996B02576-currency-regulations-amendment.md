# Currency Regulations (Amendment)

**Statutory Rules 1991 No. 149**1

**__________________**

**Currency Regulations** 2 **(Amendment)**

I, THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following Regulations under the *Currency Act 1965*.

Dated 19 June 1991.

BILL HAYDEN

Governor-General

By His Excellency’s Command,

R. McMULLAN

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

for and on behalf of the Treasurer

\____________

**1. Amendment**

1.1 The Currency Regulations are amended as set out in these Regulations.

**2. Regulation 4 (Designs of coins)**

2.1 Subparagraphs 4 (b) (i) and (ii):

Add at the end “and”.

2.2 Paragraph 4 (b):

After subparagraph (iii), insert:

“(iiia) in the case of a coin of the denomination of $10 the composition of which is not less than 99.9% silver—4 coarse milled segments each approximately 10.25 millimetres in length separated by 4 plain segments each approximately 29 millimetres in length; and

(iiib)in the case of a coin of the denomination of $50 the composition of which is not less than 99.9% silver—4 coarse milled segments each approximately 15.40 millimetres in length separated by 4 plain segments each approximately 43 millimetres in length; and

(iiic)in the case of a coin of the denomination of $150 the composition of which is not less than 99.9% silver—4 coarse milled segments each approximately 20.50 millimetres in length separated by 4 plain segments each approximately 58 millimetres in length; and”.

# Higher Education Support (John Paul ll Institute for Marriage and Family, Melbourne) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2021

Higher Education Support (John Paul ll Institute for Marriage and Family, Melbourne)

Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2021

I, Damian Coburn, delegate of the Minister for Education, make this instrument under section 22-2 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003.*

Dated 9 April 2021

Damian Coburn

Assistant Secretary

HELP and Provider Integrity Branch

Department of Education, Skills and Employment

Delegate of the Minister for Education

###### 1 Name

This is the Higher Education Support (John Paul ll Institute for Marriage and Family, Melbourne) Higher Education Provider Approval Revocation 2021.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day it is registered in the Federal Register of Legislation.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 22-2 of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument **Act** means the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

Note: The term ***higher education provider*** has the same meaning as given in the Act.

###### 5 Notice of Decision to Revoke Approval as a Higher Education Provider

The approval of John Paul ll Institute for Marriage and Family, Melbourne (ABN: 27 788 943 586) as a higher education provider is revoked in accordance with subsection 22-2(1) of the Act.

The legislative instrument *Higher Education Provider Approval No 14 of 2012* (F2012L02517) is repealed.

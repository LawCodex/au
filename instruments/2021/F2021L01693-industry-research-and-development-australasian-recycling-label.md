###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 33 of the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Industry Research and Development Act 1986*.

***program***: see subsection 5(1).

###### 5 Prescribed program

(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the Australasian Recycling Label Program (the ***program***) is prescribed.

(2) The program provides funding to industry representative bodies to develop and deliver (online and by telephone) education, resources and assistance to encourage and support small to medium enterprises:

(a) to adopt the Australasian Recycling Label on their packaging; and

(b) to improve the sustainability of their packaging.

###### 6 Specified legislative power

For the purposes of subsection 33(3) of the Act, the power of the Parliament to make laws with respect to postal, telegraphic, telephonic, and other like services (within the meaning of paragraph 51(v) of the Constitution) is specified.

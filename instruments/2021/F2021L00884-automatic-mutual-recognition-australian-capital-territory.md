## 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* of the Commonwealth.

***covered activity*** means an activity of the kind mentioned in schedule 1, table 1.2, item 29 of the *Environment Protection Act 1997* of the Australian Capital Territory authorised to be carried on under an environmental authorisation under that Act.

***local registration authority*** means the Environment Protection Authority established under the *Environment Protection Act 1997* of the Australian Capital Territory.

## 6 Notification requirement

For subsection 42J(4) of the Act, a person intending to carry on a covered activity in the Australian Capital Territory must notify the local registration authority before the person begins to carry on the activity.

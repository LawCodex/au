### Part 2—Determination of GST revenue sharing relativities for 2021‑22

###### 5 GST revenue sharing relativities for the 2021-22 payment year

For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, each item of the following table sets out a factor that is the GST revenue sharing relativity, for the 2021-22 payment year, for the State specified in the item:

| Item | For this State: | The GST revenue sharing relativity for the 2021‑22 payment year is: |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New South Wales | 0.95617 |
| 2 | Victoria | 0.92335 |
| 3 | Queensland | 1.05918 |
| 4 | Western Australia | 0.41967 |
| 5 | South Australia | 1.34719 |
| 6 | Tasmania | 1.96067 |
| 7 | Australian Capital Territory | 1.16266 |
| 8 | Northern Territory | 4.79985 |

# Mutual Recognition (Notification Requirement for Agents – Northern Territory) Determination 2021

Northern Territory of Australia

*Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth)

**Mutual Recognition (Notification Requirement for Agents – Northern Territory) Determination 2021**

I, Michael Patrick Francis Gunner, Treasurer, under section 42J(4) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth) and with reference to section 4 of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* (Cth), determine that registration under the *Commercial and Private Agents Licensing Act 1979* is a registration for which notification is required by section 42J(1) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* (Cth).

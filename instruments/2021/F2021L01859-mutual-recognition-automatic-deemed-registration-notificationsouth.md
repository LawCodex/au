###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 42J(4) of the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* of the Commonwealth.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* of the Commonwealth.

###### 5 Notifications required before automatic deemed registration used

For the purposes of subsection 42J(1) of the Act, a person who intends to carry on an activity covered by an occupation in column 1 of the table, in reliance on automatic deemed registration, must notify the local registration authority for the occupation in South Australia before the person begins to carry on the activity in South Australia.

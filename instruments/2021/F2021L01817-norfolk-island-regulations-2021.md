# Norfolk Island Regulations 2021

I, General the Honourable David Hurley AC DSC (Retd), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Dated 16 December 2021

David Hurley

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Nola Marino

Assistant Minister for Regional Development and Territories\
Parliamentary Secretary to the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Infrastructure, Transport and Regional Development

This instrument is made under the *Norfolk Island Act 1979*.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Norfolk Island Act 1979*.

### Part 2—Applied law jurisdiction

###### 5 Applied law jurisdiction

For the purposes of paragraph 5(2)(a) of the Act, Queensland is an applied law jurisdiction.

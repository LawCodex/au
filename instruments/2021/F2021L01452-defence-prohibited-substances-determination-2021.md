# Defence (Prohibited Substances) Determination 2021

I, Angus J Campbell, Chief of the Defence Force, make the following Determination under subsection 93B(1) of the *Defence Act 1903.*

Dated 5 October 2021

Angus J Campbell, AO, DSC

General\
Chief of the Defence Force

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is *Defence (Prohibited Substances) Determination 2021*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 93B(1) of the *Defence Act 1903.*

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***World Anti-Doping Code International Standard Prohibited List 2021*** means the document of that name published by the World Anti-Doping Agency, as in force under the World Anti-Doping Code on 1 January 2021.

***Act*** means the *Defence Act 1903.*

***Poisons Standard*** mean the *Poisons Standard June 2021,* as in force on\
1 June 2021.

***World Anti-Doping Code*** has the same meaning as in the *Australian Sports Anti-Doping Authority Act 2006.*

###### 6 Prohibited substances

The following substances are prohibited substances for the purpose of Part VIIIA of the Act:

a substance in class S0, S1, S2, S4, S6, S7, S8 or S9 of the World Anti-Doping Code International Standard Prohibited List 2021;

a substance listed in Schedule 4, 8 or 9 of the 2021 Poisons Standard;

Alkylamines with stimulant properties;

Allylisopropylacetylurea;

1,3-Dimethylamylamine (DMAA);

1,5-Dimethylhexylamine (DMHA);

1,4-Dimethylpentylamine (DMPA);

Gamma Butyrolactone (GBL);

Isopropyl Nitrite;

Phenpromethamine;

1,1,1-Trichloroethane.

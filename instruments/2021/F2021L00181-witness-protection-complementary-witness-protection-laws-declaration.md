# Witness Protection (Complementary Witness Protection Laws) Declaration 2021

Witness Protection (Complementary Witness Protection Laws) Declaration 2021 (LIN 20/269)

I, PETER DUTTON, Minister for Home Affairs, make the following declaration.

Dated 23 February 2021

Peter Dutton

Minister of Home Affairs

Contents

**Part 1**— **Preliminary**

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Witness Protection (Complementary Witness Protection Laws) Declaration 2021*.

This instrument may be cited as LIN 20/269.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 3AA of the *Witness Protection Act 1994*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Complementary Witness Protection Laws

For the purposes of the *Witness Protection Act 1994*, each law mentioned in the following table is a ***complementary witness protection law***.

| **Item** | **Law** |
|---|---|
| 1 | *Witness Protection Act 1995* (NSW) |
| 2 | *Witness Protection Act 1991* (Vic) |
| 3 | *Witness Protection Act 2000* (Qld) |
| 4 | *Witness Protection (Western Australia) Act 1996* (WA) |
| 5 | *Witness Protection Act 1996* (SA) |
| 6 | *Witness Protection Act 2000* (Tas) |
| 7 | *Witness Protection Act 1996* (ACT) |
| 8 | *Witness Protection (Northern Territory) Act 2002* (NT) |

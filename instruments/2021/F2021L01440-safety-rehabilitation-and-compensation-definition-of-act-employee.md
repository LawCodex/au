# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of ACT Employee – COVID-19 Emergency Response Volunteers) Declaration 2021

I, MICHAELIA CASH, Minister for Industrial Relations, make the following declaration.

Dated 11 October 2021

MICHAELIA CASH

Minister for Industrial Relations

Contents

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Definition of ACT Employee* – *COVID-19 Emergency Response Volunteers) Declaration 2021*.

###### 2 Commencement

###### This declaration commences on 12 August 2021.

###### 3 Authority

This declaration is made under subsection 5(15) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

**4 Definitions**

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988.*

***ACT*** means the Australian Capital Territory.

***ACT Chief Health Officer*** means a person appointed by the Minister under s 7(1) of the *Public Health Act 1997* (ACT)*.*

**5 Declaration**

I declare that persons identified by the ACT Chief Health Officer in writing to be COVID-19 Response Volunteers who, without receiving any remuneration (other than payment of expenses incurred), and

at the request or direction, or for the benefit, of the ACT;

in accordance with a requirement made by or under an ACT enactment; or

at the request or direction, or for the benefit, of an authority or body established by an ACT enactment

assists in the delivery of ACT COVID emergency response services under the control or direction of an ACT Government officer, are taken to be an employee of the ACT for the purposes of the Act, when rendering such assistance.

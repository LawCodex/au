###### 1.03 Authority

These Rules are made under the *Family Law Act 1975*.

###### 1.04 Court may make orders or dispense with these Rules

(1) The court may, in the interests of justice, dispense with compliance, or full compliance, with any of these Rules at any time.

(2) If, in a proceeding, the court gives a direction or makes an order that is inconsistent with any of these Rules, the direction or order of the court prevails in that proceeding.

### Part 2—Application of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Family Law) Rules 2021 and the Family Court Rules 2021 (WA)

###### 2.01 Application of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Family Law) Rules 2021

The *Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Family Law) Rules 2021* (other than rules 1.01 to 1.03) apply to a court exercising jurisdiction under the *Family Law Act 1975* as if the *Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Family Law) Rules 2021* were provisions of these Rules.

Note: The reference to a court exercising jurisdiction under the *Family Law Act 1975* in this rule does not include a reference to the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (see subsection 123(1A) of the *Family Law Act 1975*).

###### 2.02 Application of the Family Court Rules 2021 (WA)

(1) The *Family Court Rules 2021* (WA), as in force at the start of 1 September 2021, apply to the Family Court of Western Australia and the Magistrates Court of Western Australia in exercising jurisdiction under the *Family Law Act 1975*.

(2) For the purpose of the exercise of jurisdiction under the *Family Law Act 1975* by the Family Court of Western Australia or the Magistrates Court of Western Australia, a provision of the *Family Court Rules 2021* (WA), as applied by subrule (1) of this rule, prevails over a provision of these Rules to the extent of any inconsistency.

# AusCheck (Major National Event—FIFA Women’s World Cup Australia & New Zealand 2023) Declaration 2021

LIN 21/038

AusCheck (Major National Event—FIFA Women’s World Cup Australia & New Zealand 2023) Declaration 2021

I, Karen Andrews, Minister for Home Affairs, declare the Fédération Internationale de Football Association Women’s World Cup Australia & New Zealand 2023 to be a major national event under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*.

This instrument commences the day after registration.

Dated 18 June 2021

Karen Andrews

Minister for Home Affairs

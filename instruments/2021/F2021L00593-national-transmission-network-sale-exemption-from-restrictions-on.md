###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 18(6) of the *National Transmission Network Sale Act 1998*.

###### 4 Definitions

Note: The following expression used in this instrument is defined in the Act:

(a) asset.

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *National Transmission Network Sale Act 1998*.

###### 5 Exemption

For the purposes of subsection 18(5) of the Act, each of the following assets are exempted from the operation of section 18 of the Act:

(a) Folio Identifier: 102/1268824 being Lot 102 in Deposited Plan 1268824, in the Local Government Area of Liverpool, Parish of St Luke and County of Cumberland; and

(b) Folio Identifier: 103/1268824 being Lot 103 in Deposited Plan 1268824, in the Local Government Area of Liverpool, Parish of St Luke and County of Cumberland;

being land situated at Wonga Road, Prestons, New South Wales that was part of the site identified as site number 2060 (Liverpool) in Part 1 of Schedule A to the declaration made under subsections 9(1) and (2) of the Act dated 27 April 1999 and published in the Gazette on 29 April 1999.

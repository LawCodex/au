# Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits (2021-2022) Determination 2021

**Superannuation (PSS)**

**Maximum Benefits (2021-2022)**

**Determination 2021**

I, ABBEY BROWNE, Senior Manager, Knowledge and Technical Advice, and Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation, pursuant to rules 5.6.5 and 5.7.7 of the PSS Rules, in the Schedule to the Deed in force under section 4 of the *Superannuation Act 1990,* DETERMINE as follows:

Dated 8 June 2021

A BROWNE

Delegate of Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation

[image]

**1 Name of Determination**

This Determination is the *Superannuation (PSS) Maximum Benefits*

*(2021-2022) Determination 2021*.

**2 Commencement**

This determination commences on 1 July 2021.

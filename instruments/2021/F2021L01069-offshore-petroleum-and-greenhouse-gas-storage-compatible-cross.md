###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 24A of the *Offshore Petroleum and Greenhouse Gas Storage Act 2006*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Commonwealth Act*** means the *Offshore Petroleum and Greenhouse Gas Storage Act 2006*.

###### 5 Laws declared to be compatible cross‑boundary laws

For the purposes of subsection 24A(1) of the Commonwealth Act, the *Offshore Petroleum and Greenhouse Gas Storage Act 2010* (Vic) is declared to be a ***compatible cross-boundary law*** for the purposes of the Commonwealth Act.

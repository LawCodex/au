###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under paragraph (o) of the definition of ‘designated program Act’ in subsection 16AB(3) of the Act.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Child Support (Registration and Collection) Act 1988*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 6 Designated program Acts

For the purposes of paragraph (o) of the definition of ‘designated program Act’ in subsection 16AB(3) of the Act, the following Acts are specified:

the *Australian Prudential Regulation Authority Act 1998*; and

the *Taxation Administration Act 1953*.

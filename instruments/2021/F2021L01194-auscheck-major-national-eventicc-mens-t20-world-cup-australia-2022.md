# AusCheck (Major National Event—ICC Men’s T20 World Cup Australia 2022) Declaration 2021

LIN 21/036

AusCheck (Major National Event—ICC Men’s T20 World Cup Australia 2022) Declaration 2021

I, Karen Andrews, Minister for Home Affairs, declare the International Cricket Council Men’s T20 World Cup Australia 2022 to be a major national event under subsection 5A(1) of the *AusCheck Act 2007*.

This instrument commences the day after registration.

Dated 25 August 2021

Karen Andrews

Minister for Home Affairs

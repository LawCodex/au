# Australian Federal Police (Approval of Screening Devices) Instrument 2021

I, Peter Dutton, Minister for Home Affairs, make this instrument under section 40R of the *Australian Federal Police Act 1979* (the ***Act***).

Dated 3 March 2021

Peter Dutton

Minister for Home Affairs

###### 1 Name

This instrument is the *Australian Federal Police (Approval of Screening Devices) Instrument 2021*.

###### 2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after it is registered.

###### 3 Revocation

*Australian Federal Police Act 1979 - Section 40R - Approval of Screening Device (09/06/2011)* is revoked.

###### 4 Approved screening devices

For subsection 40R(1) of the Act, each of the following devices is an approved screening device:

(a) the Lion Alcolmeter SD-400 breath screening device;

(b) the Alcolizer LE breath screening device;

(c) a device that is exhaled into and has been approved for the purpose of indicating whether alcohol is present in the blood of a person:

(i) under the law of a state or territory in which the breath screen is conducted; or

(ii) in an external territory or a foreign country under the law of a state or territory.

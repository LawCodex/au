###### 5 Terrorist organisation—Lashkar‑e‑Tayyiba

(1) For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of ***terrorist organisation*** in subsection 102.1(1) of the *Criminal Code*, the organisation known as Lashkar‑e‑Tayyiba is specified.

(2) Lashkar‑e‑Tayyiba is also known by the following names:

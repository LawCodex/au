###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 204A(3) of the Act.

###### 4 Definition

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency Act 2011*.

###### 5 Words or expressions with the same or similar meaning to university

The following words or expressions have the same or similar meaning as the word university for the purposes of subparagraph 204A(1)(a)(ii) of the Act:

(a) Universities;

(b) Uni, but not where those letters are used as part of a longer word, which is not university (such as community or junior); and

(c) A word in a foreign language that has the same meaning as the word university.

Note: Examples of words in foreign languages that have the same meaning as the word university include:

(a) Universita (Italian);

(b) Universität (German);

(c) Universidad (Spanish);

(d) Universite (French);

(e) Universiteit (Dutch);

(f) Universitet (Danish); and

(g) Universidade (Portuguese).

# Migration (Priority consideration for certain Visitor (Class FA) visa applications) Instrument (LIN 21/074) 2021

LIN 21/074

Migration (Priority consideration for certain Visitor (Class FA) visa applications) Instrument (LIN 21/074) 2021

I, Sandra Jeffery, delegate of the Minister, make this instrument under subregulation 2.12M(7) of the *Migration Regulations 1994* (the Regulations)*.*

Dated 22 November 2021

Sandra Jeffery

Assistant Secretary

Immigration Programs Division

Department of Home Affairs

1 Name

This instrument is the Migration (Priority consideration for certain Visitor (Class FA) visa applications) Instrument (*LIN 21/074*) 2021.

2 Commencement

This instrument commences on the day after registration.

3 Priority consideration for certain Visitor visa applications

For subregulation 2.12M(2) of the Regulations, an item in the following table specifies requirements for a request for priority consideration of a Visitor (Class FA) visa application for the provision of the Regulations mentioned in the item.

| Item | Provision of Regulations | Matter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | paragraph 2.12M(2)(a) | Visa: Subclass 600 (Visitor) visa in the Tourist stream or in the Business Visitor stream |
| 2 | subparagraph 2.12M(2)(b)(i) | Passport:<br>(a) issued by the People’s Republic of China indicating that the applicant is a national of the Peoples Republic of China; or<br>(b) issued by the Republic of India indicating that the applicant is a national of the Republic of India; or<br>(c) issued by the United Arab Emirates indicating that the applicant is a national of the United Arab Emirates |
| 3 | subparagraph 2.12M(2)(b)(ii) | Requirements: the applicant must be located in the country that issued the passport when the visa application and request for priority consideration for the application are made. |
| 4 | subparagraph 2.12M(2)(c)(i) | Form: 1419 (Internet) |
| 5 | subparagraph 2.12M(2)(c)(ii) | Way of making application: As an internet application through ImmiAccount |

4 Repeal

*Migration (IMMI 17/052: Priority Consideration of Certain Visa Applications) Instrument 2017* (F2017L00836) is repealed.

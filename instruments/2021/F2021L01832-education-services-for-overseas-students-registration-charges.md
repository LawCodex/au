###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Education Services for Overseas Students (Registration Charges) Act 1997*.

###### 4 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Education Services for Overseas Students (Registration Charges) Act 1997*.

***total enrolments***, for a provider for a year, has the same meaning as in the *Education Services for Overseas Students (TPS Levies) Act 2012*.

###### 6 CRICOS annual registration charge

For the purposes of paragraph 5(4)(b) of the Act, the amount of CRICOS annual registration charge imposed on a provider for a year is the sum of the following components:

(a) $440;

(b) $5 multiplied by the total enrolments for the provider for the previous year;

(c) if the provider is an approved school provider—$116;

(d) if the provider is an approved school provider and had at least one overseas student enrolled in the previous year for a course for which the provider was registered for that year—$695.

Note: These amounts are indexed under section 9.

###### 7 Schools initial registration charge

For the purposes of paragraph 6(4)(a) of the Act, the amount of schools initial registration charge imposed on an application is $2,690.

Note: This amount is indexed under section 9.

###### 8 Schools renewal registration charge

For the purposes of paragraph 7(4)(a) of the Act, the amount of schools renewal registration charge imposed on an application is $1,080.

Note: This amount is indexed under section 9.

###### 10 Exemption from charges imposed from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2022

For the purposes of subsections 5(9), 6(8) and 7(8) of the Act, the class of providers made up of all providers is prescribed for the following charges imposed for, or during, the calendar year beginning 1 January 2022:

(a) CRICOS annual registration charge;

(b) schools initial registration charge;

(c) schools renewal registration charge.

###### 11 Transitional provision

Despite the repeal of the *Education Services for Overseas Students (Registration Charges) Regulations 2011* by this instrument, those Regulations continue to apply in relation to a charge that became due for payment before the commencement of this section, as if that repeal had not happened.

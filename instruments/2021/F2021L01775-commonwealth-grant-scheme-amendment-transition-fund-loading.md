###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under section 238‑10 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003*.

###### 4 Schedules

The instrument specified in the Schedule to this instrument is amended as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule, and any other item in the Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.

###### 5 Application

The amendments made by this instrument apply to working out grants payable under Division 33 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003* for the years 2021, 2022 and 2023.

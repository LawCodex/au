# Intelligence Services Regulations 2021

I, General the Honourable David Hurley AC DSC (Retd), Governor‑General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, make the following regulations.

Dated 09 December 2021

David Hurley

Governor‑General

By His Excellency’s Command

Marise Payne

Minister for Foreign Affairs

Contents

This instrument is the *Intelligence Services Regulations 2021*.

###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under the *Intelligence Services Act 2001*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Intelligence Services Act 2001*.

***Home Affairs Department*** means the Department administered by the Minister administering the *Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018*.

###### 5 Cooperation with prescribed agencies

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 13A(1)(c) of the Act, the Home Affairs Department is prescribed.

(2) Subsection (1) has effect only in relation to ASD.

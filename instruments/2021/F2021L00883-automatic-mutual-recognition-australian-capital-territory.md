## 4 Simplified outline of this instrument

[image]

**5 Definitions**

In this instrument:

[image]

*Automatic Mutual Recognition (Australian Capital Territory) (Notification 1*

*Requirement—Architect) Determination 2021*

***Act*** means the *Mutual Recognition Act 1992* of the Commonwealth.

***covered activity*** means an activity mentioned in the *Architects Act 2004* of the Australian Capital Territory authorised to be carried on under a registration under that Act.

***local registration authority*** means the Australian Capital Territory Architects Board established under the *Architects Act 2004* of the Australian Capital Territory.

## 6 Notification requirement

For subsection 42J(4) of the Act, a person intending to carry on a covered activity in the Australian Capital Territory must notify the local registration authority before the person begins to carry on the activity.

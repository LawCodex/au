###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Biosecurity Act 2015*.

###### 5 Biosecurity activity zone—Mickleham (Victoria)

For the purposes of subsection 395(1) of the Act, the specified area in subsection (2), that is in Australian Territory, is determined to be a biosecurity activity zone.

The specified area is the area that:

is known as the Post Entry Quarantine Facility at Mickleham (the ***Facility***) on the commencement of this instrument; and

is on the land (the ***specified land***) known as 135 Donnybrook Road, Mickleham, Victoria on the commencement of this instrument; and

is bounded by the line starting at the point described in subparagraph (i) and running sequentially as described in the following subparagraphs:

(i) the point near the intersection of Donnybrook Road and Polaris Road, Mickleham, Victoria, on the north-east corner of a fence (the ***external perimeter fence***) that is a maximum-security fence on the outer perimeter of the Facility;

(ii) south along the external perimeter fence (including the security gates or other access points) of the Facility to the south-east corner of that fence near the boundary with the Protected Grassland on the specified land;

(iii) west along the external perimeter fence of the Facility to the western boundary of the specified land;

(iv) north along the western boundary of the specified land to the intersection of that boundary with the northern boundary of the specified land near Donnybrook Road, Mickleham, Victoria;

(v) east along the northern boundary of the specified land to the point along that boundary directly above the starting point;

(vi) south from that point mentioned in subparagraph (v) to the starting point; and

excludes the area on the specified land that is outside the area specified in paragraph (c).

Note 1: The specified land is set out in folio identifier 2/LP202576J at Mickleham, City of Hume, County of Bourke, Victoria.

Note 2: See section 399 of the *Biosecurity Act 2015* for the powers that may be exercised by a biosecurity officer in, or in relation to goods or premises in, a biosecurity activity zone.

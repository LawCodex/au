###### 3 Authority

This instrument is made under subsection 42S(5) of the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999*.

###### 4 Definitions

In this instrument:

***Act*** means the *Social Security (Administration) Act 1999.*

***dependent child*** has the meaning given by section 5 of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

###### 5 Schedules

Each instrument that is specified in the Schedule to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule.

###### 6 Specification of classes of persons

(1) For paragraph 42S (4) (b) of the Act, the following classes of persons are specified:

(a) persons with a dependent child;

(b) persons who have significant family or caring responsibilities;

(c) persons who have an illness, impairment or condition that requires treatment, and who cannot afford the treatment after meeting their essential expenses;

(d) persons who have a cognitive, neurological, psychiatric or psychological impairment or mental illness that has been established by medical evidence;

(e) persons who do not have access to safe, secure and adequate housing, or are using emergency accommodation or a refuge.

(2) Without limiting paragraph (1) (e), a person is taken not to have access to safe, secure, and adequate housing if:

(a) the housing to which the person has access:

(i) damages, or is likely to damage, the person’s health; or

(ii) threatens or is likely to threaten the person’s safety; or

(iii) does not provide the person with access to a reasonable level of personal amenities or the economic and social support that housing normally affords; or

(b) in the circumstances, the adequacy, safety, security or affordability of the housing to which the person has access is adversely affected or may be adversely affected; or

(c) the person does not have a right to remain, or a reasonable expectation of being able to remain, in the housing to which the person has access.

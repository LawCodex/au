# AD/B747/392 Amdt 2 - Fuselage Upper Lobe Doubler

**AIRWORTHINESS DIRECTIVE**

On the effective date specified below, and for the reasons set out in the background section, the CASA delegate whose signature appears below revokes Airworthiness Directive (AD) AD/B747/392 Amdt 1 and issues the following AD under subregulation 39.001(1) of CASR 1998. The AD requires that the action set out in the requirement section (being action that the delegate considers necessary to correct the unsafe condition) be taken in relation to the aircraft or aeronautical product mentioned in the applicability section: (a) in the circumstances mentioned in the requirement section; and (b) in accordance with the instructions set out in the requirement section; and (c) at the time mentioned in the compliance section.

| Background: | The FAA received reports of cracks in the radius detail of the fuselage upper lobe doublers. Such cracking, unless detected and corrected, could result in significant degradation of the fuselage structure and reduce its ability to carry flight loads from the vertical stabiliser, which could adversely affect the controllability of the aircraft.<br>This amendment is to allow the use of Revision 1 of Boeing Service Bulletin<br>747-53A2651 as an Alternative Method of Compliance (AMOC) and to allow FAA form 8100-9 approved repairs, without the need for a CASA Exclusion. There is some change to the requirements and compliance and these are specified in Paragraph 1E of Boeing Service Bulletin 747-53A2651, Rev 1, dated 2 September 2010.<br>Amendment 2 is to correct some wording in Requirement 1. There is no change to the intent of the requirements or compliance.<br>Amendment 1 of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 20 May 2011.<br>The original issue of this Airworthiness Directive became effective on 2 July 2009. |

# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Class of Employees Outside Australia) Notice 2011 (No. 1)

**Notice No. 1 of 2011**

I, CHRIS EVANS, Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Jobs and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under paragraph 6(1)(i) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 9th December 2011

CHRIS EVANS

Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Jobs and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation *(Class of Employees Outside Australia)* Notice 2011 (No. 1).

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of paragraph 6(1)(i) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*, I declare that:

persons engaged under subsection 19(1) of the *Australian Civilian Corps Act 2011*

are a class of employees to which this paragraph applies.

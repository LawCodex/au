# Superannuation (PSS) Temporary Employee Declaration 2011 (No. 1)

Superannuation (PSS) Temporary Employee Declaration 2011 (No. 1)1

I, Penelope Ying Yen Wong, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make this Declaration under section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990*.

Dated 20th April 2011

Penelope Ying Yen Wong

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (PSS) Temporary Employee Declaration 2011 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration commences immediately after the commencement of Part 3 of the *Australian Civilian Corps Act 2011.*

3 Temporary employees

The definition of “temporary employee” in section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1990* applies to a person who is engaged as an Australian Civilian Corps employee for employment outside Australia only, if the person was a member of the Public Sector Superannuation Scheme immediately before becoming so engaged.

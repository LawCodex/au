# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 - section 269A - Instrument Adopting and Revoking Recovery Plans (NSW, Qld, SA, VIC and WA) (05/04/2011)

Commonwealth of Australia

## Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**Section 269A**

Instrument Adopting and Revoking Recovery Plans

I, CAROLYN WEISS CAMERON, Assistant Secretary, Approvals and Wildlife Division, delegate for the Minister for Sustainability, Environment, Water, Population and Communities:

(a) pursuant to subsection 33(3) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* and subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999*, hereby revoke the adoption of the following State recovery plans:

\- Bickerton, D. (1999). Recovery plan for *Caladenia behrii* (Pink-lipped Spider-orchid). Department for Environment and Heritage, South Australia;

\- Bickerton, D. (2003). Recovery plan for *Arachnorchis macroclavia* (Large-club Spider-orchid). Department for Environment and Heritage, South Australia;

\- Bickerton, D. (2003). Recovery plan for *Arachnorchis woolcockiorum* (Woolcock’s Spider-orchid*)*. Department for Environment and Heritage, South Australia;

\- Bickerton, D. and Robertson, R. (2000). Halbury Greenhood (*Pterostylis* ‘Halbury’) recovery plan*.* Department for Environment and Heritage, South Australia;

\- Hollis, G.J. (1997). Recovery plan for the Baw Baw Frog (*Philoria frosti*). Report to Endangered Species Program, Environment Australia, Canberra;

\- Nolan, B. and Johnson, P. (2001). Recovery plan for the Proserpine Rock-wallaby *Petrogale persephone* 2000-2004. Report to Environment Australia, Canberra. Queensland Parks and Wildlife Service, Brisbane;

\- Robertson, M.A. and Bickerton, D. (2000). White Beauty Spider Orchid (*Caladenia argocalla)* recovery plan*.* Department for Environment and Heritage, South Australia; and

\- Watson, A.J. and Halley, M. (2000). Eastern Barred Bandicoot *Perameles gunnii* (mainland subspecies) recovery plan. Department of Natural Resources and Environment, Victoria

(b) under subsection 269A(7) of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999,* hereby adopt as recovery plans for the listed threatened species specified below, the respective plans made by New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Victoria and Western Australia (or agencies of those States) specified opposite those listed threatened species:

# Tariff Concession Order 1131123

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 13.09.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4810.19.90 | PAPER AND PAPERBOARD, coated with kaolin (china clay) OR other<br>inorganic substances, having a width NOT less than 15 cm and a<br>length NOT less than 36 cm, being EITHER of the following:<br>(a) coated both sides with equal coating;<br>(b) having ALL the following:<br>(i) virgin fibre;<br>(ii) brightness NOT less than 87% AND complying with<br>International Organisation for Standardization<br>ISO 2470-1;<br>(iii) maximum roughness of 1.3 microns AND complying<br>with International Organisation for Standardization<br>ISO 8791-4, print-surf method<br>Op. 13.09.11 - TC 1131123 | 50 |
|---|---|---|

# Tariff Concession Order 1119245

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 15.06.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4802.57.90 | PRINTING PAPER, uncoated, in sheets one side exceeding 36 cm<br>and the other side exceeding 15 cm, having ALL the following:<br>(a) weight NOT less than 42 gsm and NOT greater than 47 gsm;<br>(b) containing less than 10% mechanical pulp;<br>(c) opacity NOT less than 85%;<br>(d) CIE whiteness NOT less than 100%<br>Op. 15.06.11 - TC 1119245 | 50 |

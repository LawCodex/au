# Higher Education Provider Approval No. 3 of 2011

***NOTICE OF APPROVAL***

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***Higher Education Support Act 2003***

**Higher Education Provider Approval No. 3 of 2011**

**Approval under section 16-25, decision under paragraph 16-50(1)(a), and notice of approval under paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of the** ***Higher Education Support Act 2003*** **(HESA).**

I, David Hazlehurst, a delegate of the Minister for Tertiary Education for the purposes of sections 16-25 and 16-50 of the *Higher Education Support Act 2003* (HESA):

(a) approve the application of the Technical and Further Education Commission as a higher education provider in accordance with section 16-25 of HESA; and

(b) note that this approval decides the application of the Technical and Further Education Commission as required by paragraph 16-50(1)(a) of HESA; and

(c) hereby give the Technical and Further Education Commission written notice of my approval as required by paragraph 16-50(1)(b) of HESA.

In accordance with paragraph 16-55(2) of HESA this notice of approval is taken to have commenced on the first business day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.

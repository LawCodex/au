# Tariff Concession Order 1045652

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 08.10.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8302.41.00 | MOUNTS, ANTENNA, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) vertical mast;<br>(b) tongue and tube assembly;<br>(c) end plates;<br>(d) mounting hardware;<br>(e) rated wind classification N4 (W50) OR greater, complying<br>with Australian Standard 4055 - 2006 (AS 4055 - 2006),<br>for a 65 cm satellite dish<br>Op. 08.10.10 - TC 1045652 | 50 |

# Personal Property Securities (Priority of Statutory Interests) Instrument 2011

Personal Property Securities (Priority of Statutory Interests) Instrument 20111

*Personal Property Securities Act 2009*

I, ROBERT McCLELLAND, Attorney‑General, make this Instrument under subsection 73 (4) of the *Personal Property Securities Act 2009*.

Dated 8 December 2011

ROBERT McCLELLAND

Attorney‑General

###### 1 Name of Instrument

This Instrument is the *Personal Property Securities (Priority of Statutory Interests) Instrument 2011*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Instrument commences on 30 January 2012.

###### 3 Definitions

In this Instrument:

***Act*** means the *Personal Property Securities Act 2009*.

###### 4 Priority between security interests and declared statutory interests

For subsection 73 (4) of the Act, subsection 73 (2) of the Act applies to:

(a) a lien that arises under subsection 443F (1) of the *Corporations Act 2001*; and

(b) a lien that arises under subsection 189AC (2) of the *Bankruptcy Act 1966*.

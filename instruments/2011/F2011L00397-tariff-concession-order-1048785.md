# Tariff Concession Order 1048785

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 02.11.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 3824.90.90 | BINDERS, REFRACTORY, having ALL of the following:<br>(a) phenolic resin content NOT less 20% but NOT greater than<br>70%;<br>(b) triethylene glycol content NOT less than 20% but NOT<br>greater than 70%;<br>(c) glycerin content NOT greater than 20%;<br>(d) phenol content NOT greater than 15%<br>Op. 02.11.10 - TC 1048785 | 50 |

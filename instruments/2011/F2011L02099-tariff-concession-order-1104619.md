# Tariff Concession Order 1104619

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 02.02.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 9504.90.90 | MULTI-GAME TABLES, with OR without playing pieces, including a<br>soccer/football game and NOT less than 2 games of ANY of the<br>following:<br>(a) table tennis;<br>(b) pool/billiards;<br>(c) hockey;<br>(d) shuffleboard;<br>(e) chess;<br>(f) checkers;<br>(g) backgammon;<br>(h) ring toss;<br>(i) tic tac toe<br>Op. 02.02.11 - TC 1104619 | 50 |

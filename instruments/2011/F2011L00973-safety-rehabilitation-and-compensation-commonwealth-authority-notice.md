# Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Notice 2011 (1)

**Notice No. 1 of 2011**

I, CHRIS EVANS, Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Jobs and Workplace Relations, make this Notice under subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988*.

Dated 23 May 2011

CHRIS EVANS

Minister for Tertiary Education, Skills, Jobs and Workplace Relations

###### 1 Name of Notice

This Notice is the Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation (Commonwealth Authority) Notice 2011 (1).

###### 2 Commencement

This Notice commences on 1 July 2011.

###### 3 Declaration

For the purposes of subsection 4(1) of the *Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988* (the Act) in relation to the definition of ***Commonwealth authority,*** I declare that under paragraph (c) of that definition that NBN Co Limited (ACN 136 533 741) is a body corporate to which the Act applies.

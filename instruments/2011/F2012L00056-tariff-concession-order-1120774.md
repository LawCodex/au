# Tariff Concession Order 1120774

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Wendy Knowles, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 24.06.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 4202.92.90 | BAGS OR SACKS, outer surface non-woven plastic material,<br>including ALL of the following:<br>(a) sealed seams;<br>(b) roll down top;<br>(c) release buckles;<br>(d) length NOT greater than 75 cm;<br>(e) weight NOT greater than 100 g<br>Op. 24.06.11 - TC 1120774 | 50 |

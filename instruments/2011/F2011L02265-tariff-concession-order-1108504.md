# Tariff Concession Order 1108504

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 08.03.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

THE TABLE

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 6902.90.00 | BRICKS AND/OR SHAPES, REFRACTORY, BLAST FURNACE, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) carbon or graphite content NOT less than 95%;<br>(b) bulk density NOT less than 1 500 kg/m3;<br>(c) apparent porosity NOT less than 9% and NOT greater than<br>30%;<br>(d) cold crushing strength NOT less than 18 N/mm2 and NOT<br>greater than 60 N/mm2;<br>(e) modulus of rupture NOT less than 5 N/mm2 and NOT greater<br>than 15 N/mm2<br>Op. 08.03.11 - TC 1108504 | 50 |

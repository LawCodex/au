# Tariff Concession Revocation Order 55/2012 - Tariff Concession Order 1120752

**TARIFF CONCESSION REVOCATION ORDER**\
Under Section 269SD(3) of the Customs Act 1901, I Michael Perry a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer\
(a) revoke Tariff Concession Order Number 1100393 made on 28.03.11, in respect of the goods described in TABLE A below. This Revocation has effect from 04.01.11; and\
(b) make in its place the Tariff Concession Orders described in TABLE B. The goods specified in Column 1 of TABLE B are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of TABLE B applies. The Order shall have effect from the date notified in paragraph (a) above as the date of effect for the Revocation.

TABLE A

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 3816.00.00 | HOT REPAIR MIXTURE, REFRACTORY, sintered magnesia based, having<br>ALL of the following:<br>(a) magnesium oxide (MgO) content NOT less than 65%;<br>(b) calcium oxide (CaO) content NOT less than 15%;<br>(c) ferrous oxide (Fe2O3) content NOT less than 3%<br><br>Op. 04.01.11 - TC 1100393 | 50<br>N/A |

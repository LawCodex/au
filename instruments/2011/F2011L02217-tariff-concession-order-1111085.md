# Tariff Concession Order 1111085

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, David Mulcair, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 31.03.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| 8536.50.93 | SWITCHES, being ANY of the following:<br>(a) rotary, NOT less than 5 V and NOT greater than 25 V;<br>(b) ignition, 2 OR 3 OR 4 position;<br>(c) rocker, illuminated OR non-illuminated, having BOTH of the<br>following:<br>(i) NOT less than 10 V and NOT greater than 28 V;<br>(ii) NOT less than 5 A and NOT greater than 35 A,<br>(d) push button, having BOTH of the following:<br>(i) NOT less than 5.5 V and NOT greater than 36.5 V;<br>(ii) NOT less than 4.5 A and NOT greater than 50 A,<br>(e) toggle, having BOTH of the following:<br>(i) NOT less than 10 V and NOT greater than 28 V;<br>(ii) NOT less than 0.5 A and NOT greater than 25.5 A,<br>(f) rotary, push AND/OR pull, NOT less than 5 V and NOT<br>greater than 25 V<br>Op. 31.03.11 - TC 1111085 | 50 |

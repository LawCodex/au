# Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2011-2012) Declaration 2011

Superannuation (CSS)

Productivity Contribution

(2011-2012) Declaration 2011

I, STEVE WHITTON, Director, Australian Public Sector Payments and Entitlements, Position No. 44, and Delegate of the Australian Reward Investment Alliance, make the following Declaration under section 110D of the *Superannuation Act 1976.*

Dated 29 June 2011

S WHITTON\
Delegate of Australian Reward Investment Alliance

[image]

1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (CSS) Productivity Contribution (2011-2012) Declaration 2011*.

2 Commencement

This Declaration shall take effect from and including 1 July 2011.

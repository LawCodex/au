# Superannuation (CSS) Temporary Employee Approval 2011 (No. 1)

Superannuation (CSS) Temporary Employee Approval 2011 (No.1)1

I, Penelope Ying Yen Wong, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make this Approval under subsection 3(1) of the *Superannuation Act 1976*.

Dated 20th April 2011

Penelope Ying Yen Wong

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

1 Name of Approval

This Approval is the *Superannuation (CSS) Temporary Employee Approval 2011 (No. 1)*.

2 Commencement

This Approval commences immediately after the commencement of Part 3 of the *Australian Civilian Corps Act 2011*.

3 Temporary employees

A person who is engaged, otherwise than in a permanent capacity, as an Australian Civilian Corps employee for employment outside Australia only, for employment on a full-time basis is included in a class of persons for the purposes of the definition of “temporary employee” in section 3 of the *Superannuation Act 1976* if the person was an eligible employee immediately before becoming so employed.

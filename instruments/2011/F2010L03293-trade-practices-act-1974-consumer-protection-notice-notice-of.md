# Trade Practices Act 1974 - Consumer Protection Notice - Notice of Permanent Ban

COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA

*Trade Practices Act 1974*

NOTICE OF PERMANENT BAN

IN pursuance of section 65C (7) of the *Trade Practices Act 1974*, I, LIONEL FROST BOWEN, Attorney-General of the Commonwealth of Australia hereby impose a permanent ban on the goods specified below.

Particulars of Goods: Glucomannan in tablet form.

Dated this 16th day of December 1986.

LIONEL BOWEN

Attorney-General

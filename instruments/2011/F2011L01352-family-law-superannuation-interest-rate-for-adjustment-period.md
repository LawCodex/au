Dated 19 June 2011

PETER COLIN MARTIN

Australian Government Actuary

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Family Law (Superannuation) (Interest Rate for Adjustment Period) Determination 2011*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on 1 July 2011.

###### 3 Definition

In this Determination:

***Regulations*** means the *Family Law (Superannuation) Regulations 2001*.

# Tariff Concession Order 1049168

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 04.11.10 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8479.89.90 | BRAKE PAD MANUFACTURING MACHINES, programmable logic controlled,<br>incorporating ALL of the following:<br>(a) pressing stations;<br>(b) maximum pressing force NOT less than 1 800 kN;<br>(c) hopper storage capacity NOT less than 100 L;<br>(d) maximum moulding temperature NOT less than 45 degrees<br>Celsius;<br>(e) maximum mould filling height NOT less than 20 mm;<br>(f) tooling cleaners;<br>(g) release agent sprayers;<br>(h) weighers<br>Op. 04.11.10 - TC 1049168 | 50 |

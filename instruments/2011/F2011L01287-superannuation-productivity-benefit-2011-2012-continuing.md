# Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2011-2012 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2011

Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2011-2012 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 20111

I, Penelope YING YEN WONG, Minister for Finance and Deregulation, make the following Declaration under section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*.

Dated 21st June 2011

Penelope YING YEN WONG

Minister for Finance and Deregulation

###### 1 Name of Declaration

This Declaration is the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) (2011-2012 Continuing Contributions) Declaration 2011.*

###### 2 Commencement

This Declaration commences on 1 July 2011.

###### 3 Substitution of amounts

For section 3D of the *Superannuation (Productivity Benefit) Act 1988*, the Table in the Schedule to that Act has effect, for the financial year commencing on 1 July 2011, as if the amounts specified in the following Table were substituted for the amounts specified in the Table in that Schedule (whether sums of money or percentages):

**Table**

| Pay period rate of salary | Pay period contribution |
|---|---|
| Less than $150.33 | $13.53 |
| $150.33 or more but less than $3370.77 | The amount that is 9% of the employee’s pay period rate of salary |
| $3370.77 or more | $303.37 |

# Trade Practices Act 1974 - Consumer Protection Notice No. 2 of 2004 - Permanent Ban on Goods

**COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA**

***TRADE PRACTICES ACT 1974***

Consumer Protection Notice No. 2 of 2004

**PERMANENT BAN ON GOODS**

I, Ross Cameron, Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer, pursuant to section 65C(7) of the *Trade Practices Act 1974* **IMPOSE** a permanent ban on goods of a kind specified below.

Particulars of goods:

Any mini-cup jelly confectionery product containing the ingredient "konjac" (also known as glucomannan, conjac, konnyaku, konjonac, taro powder and yam flour), having a height or width of less than or equal to 45mm.

Dated this 13th day of April 2004

ROSS CAMERON

Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasurer

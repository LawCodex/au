# Personal Property Securities (Migration Time and Registration Commencement Time) Determination

Personal Property Securities (Migration Time and Registration Commencement Time) Determination 1

*Personal Property Securities Act 2009*

I, ROBERT MCCLELLAND, Attorney-General, make this Determination under section 306 of the *Personal Property Securities Act 2009*.

Dated 21 November 2011

ROBERT MCCLELLAND

Attorney-General

###### 1 Name of Determination

This Determination is the *Personal Property Securities (Migration Time and Registration Commencement Time) Determination* .

###### 2 Commencement

This Determination commences on 21 November 2011.

###### 3 Interpretation

In this Determination:

***Act*** means the *Personal Property Securities Act 2009*.

###### 4 Migration time

For paragraph 306 (1) (b) of the Act, the migration time is the start of 21 November 2011.

###### 5 Registration commencement time

For paragraph 306 (2) (b) of the Act, the registration commencement time is the start of 30 January 2012.

# Tariff Concession Order 1100575

**TARIFF CONCESSION ORDER**\
Under Section 269P of the Customs Act 1901, I, Paul Mercer, a delegate of the Chief Executive Officer declare that the goods specified in Column 1 of THE TABLE are goods to which the item in Part III of Schedule 4 to the Customs Tariff Act 1995 specified in Column 2 of THE TABLE applies. This Order shall have effect from 05.01.11 and continue in force until revoked under sections 269SC or 269SD of the Act, or the date, if any, specified in Column 2.

| COLUMN 1<br>Description of Goods including the<br>Customs Tariff Classification | COLUMN 2<br>Schedule 4 Item Number<br>Last date of effect |
|---|---|

| 8426.99.00 | CRANES, mobile, collapsible, remote controlled, having ALL of<br>the following:<br>(a) support column permanently mounted on nine axle road<br>trailers;<br>(b) road trailer width NOT greater than 3.1 m;<br>(c) multi-section boom articulated in NOT less than three<br>axes;<br>(d) NOT less than five hydraulic extension sections on<br>main boom;<br>(e) hydraulic horizontal outreach NOT exceeding 60 m without<br>manual jib extension<br>Op. 05.01.11 - TC 1100575 | 50 |

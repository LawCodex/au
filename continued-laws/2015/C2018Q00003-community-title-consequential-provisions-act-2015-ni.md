An Act consequential upon the making of the *Community Title Act 2015*, and for related purposes.

[Assented to 28 May 2015]

**BE IT ENACTED** by the Legislative Assembly of Norfolk Island as follows

Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Community Title (Consequential Provisions) Act 2015.*

**2.** Commencement

This Act commences on the day of publication of notice of assent in the Gazette.

**3.** Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in the Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule.

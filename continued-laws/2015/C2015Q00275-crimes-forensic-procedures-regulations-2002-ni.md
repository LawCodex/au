# Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2002 (NI)

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

## CRIMES (FORENSIC PROCEDURES) REGULATIONS 2002

### Regulations No. 2 of 2002

\________

I, Anthony John Messner, acting in accordance with the advice of the Executive Council of Norfolk Island, make the following regulations under the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Act 2002.*

Dated 11 April 2002.

A.J. Messner

Administrator

Chief Minister and Minister for Intergovernment Relations

\________

##### Short title

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2002.*

##### Commencement

**2.** These Regulations shall commence operation on the day on which notification of their being made is published in the Gazette.

##### Interpretation

**3.** In these Regulations —

“*the Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000*” means the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000* of the Australian Capital Territory, as at 9 April 2002.

##### Application of the Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000

**4.** The provisions of the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000* apply, by force of these Regulations, in Norfolk Island as a law of Norfolk Island, subject to the modifications prescribed in Schedule 1.

##### Application to offences

**5.** To remove any doubt, these Regulations are intended to apply to offences committed before or after the commencement of these Regulations.

### SCHEDULE ONE

(Regulation 4)

In the application in Norfolk Island of the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000* –

(a) a reference in those Regulations to the Australian Capital Territory or Territory shall be read as a reference to Norfolk Island;

(b) a reference in regulation 3 of those Regulations to “the police force or police service of a State” shall be read to include the Police Force of Norfolk Island; and

(c) a reference in those Regulations to “these Regulations” shall be read as referring to the *Crimes (Forensic Procedures) Regulations 2000* in their application to Norfolk Island.

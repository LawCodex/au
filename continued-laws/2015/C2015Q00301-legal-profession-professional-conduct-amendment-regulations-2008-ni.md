# Legal Profession (Professional Conduct) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 (NI)

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

## LEGAL PROFESSION (professional conduct) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

### Regulations No. 7 of 2008

[image]

I, Owen Edward John Walsh, Acting Administrator of Norfolk Island, acting in accordance with the advice of the Executive Council of Norfolk Island, make the following Regulations under Section 46 of the *Legal Profession Act 1993.*

Dated 26 June 2008.

O.E.J. Walsh

Acting Administrator

By His Honour’s Command

S.V. Jack

Minister for the Environment, Education and Social Welfare

Acting at the request of and for and on behalf of Minister for Tourism and Health

\________

##### Short title

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the *Legal Profession (Professional Conduct) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.*

**Commencement**

**2.** These Regulations are effective from the date their making is notified in the Gazette.

**Revocation of Regulation 5**

**3.** Regulation 5 of the *Legal Profession (Professional Conduct) Regulations 2008* is revoked.

Notified Gazette No. 29, 27 June 2008.

Commenced on gazettal (27 June 2008).

This instrument amends Regulations No. 6 of 2008.

Printed on the authority of the Administrator.

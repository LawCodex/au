**NORFOLK ISLAND**

**Retail Price Index Act 1983**

\_______________________________________________________________________

Relating to the Retail Price Index.

##### Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Retail Price Index Act 1983*.

##### Retail price index

**2. (1)** The Minister shall ensure that a retail price index, designed by a statistician qualified and experienced in prices statistics, is compiled for each period ending on the last day of March, June, September and December.

**(2)** The Minister shall publish in the Gazette the retail price index number for a period referred to in subsection 2(1) as soon as practicable after it is compiled.

##### Benefit adjustment factor

**3. (1)** The Minister shall ensure that a benefit adjustment factor, designed by a statistician qualified and experienced in price statistics, is compiled for the period of 6 months ending on 31 December 1993 and each subsequent period of 6 months.

**(2)** The benefit adjustment factor is a factor, expressed to 3 decimal places, for use in adjusting benefits under the *Social Services Act 1980* to compensate for changes in the level of retail prices of items relevant to the normal purchasing patterns of persons receiving those benefits.

**(3)** The Minister shall publish the benefit adjustment factor in the Gazette as soon as practicable after the end of the period to which it refers.

**(4)** Where a benefit adjustment factor is 1.000 or less, no adjustment of benefits referred to in subsection 3(2) shall be made.

**(5)** Notwithstanding section 29 of the *Social Services Act 1980*, but subject to subsection 3(4) of this section, the rates of benefit payable under that Act shall, after publication of a benefit adjustment factor under subsection 3(3), be adjusted by multiplying the current pension rates by the benefit adjustment factor.

**(6)** Where a new rate of benefit referred to in subsection 3(5) is not a multiple of 10 cents per fortnight, the amount of the benefit that is to be paid under subsection 3(5) is —

(a) where the rate of benefit exceeds by 5 cents or more the next lower amount that is a multiple of 10 cents - the next higher amount per fortnight that is a multiple of 10 cents; or

(b) in any other case - the lower multiple of 10 cents.

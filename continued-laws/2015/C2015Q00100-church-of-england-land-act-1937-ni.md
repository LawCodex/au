**Church of England Land Act 1937**

\_______________________________________________________________________

An Act to vest in the Commonwealth certain Lands in Norfolk Island granted as a site for a Church of England, and for other purposes

##### Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Church of England Land Act* 1937.

##### Definitions

**1A.** In this Act, unless the contrary intention appears 

“member” means a member of the Trust other than the Archbishop;

“the Archbishop” means the Archbishop of Sydney or the person for the time being holding the office of Archbishop of the Diocese in which the Church or Churches in which religious services according to the doctrines, rites and ceremonies of the Church of England are performed in Norfolk Island are for the time being placed.

“the Trust” means the Trust constituted by section 1B of this Act.

##### Constitution of Trust

**1B. (1)** Notwithstanding the repeal of section 1B of the *Church of England Land Act 1937-1942* effected by section 4 of the *Church of England Land Act 1970*, the body corporate established by section 1B of the *Church of England Land Act 1937-1942* under the name Church of England Property Trust 

(a) is preserved and continues in existence as a body corporate with perpetual succession under and subject to the provisions of this Act under that name but so that the corporate identity of the body corporate shall not be affected;

(b) shall have a seal;

(c) is capable of suing and being sued; and

(d) shall be constituted as provided in the next succeeding subsection.

**(2)** The Trust consists of 

(a) The Archbishop;

(b) one member, being a male person who 

(i) is, when appointed, a person ordinarily resident in Norfolk Island; and

(ii) is a lineal descendant of a person who was an original settler in Norfolk Island, being a settler generally referred to as a Pitcairner,

appointed for the purpose of this paragraph;

(c) one member, being a person who is, when appointed, a person ordinarily resident in Norfolk Island, appointed for the purpose of this paragraph; and

(d) one other member.

**(3)** The members of the Trust shall be appointed by the Archbishop.

**(4)** A member may resign his office by writing under his hand delivered to the Archbishop.

**(5)** A resignation under the last preceding subsection does not have effect until accepted by the Archbishop.

**(6)** Where a member appointed for the purpose of paragraph (b) or (c) of subsection (2) of this section is absent from Norfolk Island for a continuous period of more than six months, the Archbishop may, by writing under his hand delivered to the member, remove the member from office.

**(7)** The Archbishop may, by writing under his hand delivered to a member, remove the member from office if the Archbishop is of the opinion that the member is unfit to remain a member by reason of misconduct or negligence in the discharge of his duties as a member or because of his incapacity.

**(8)** Where the Archbishop appoints a person to be a member, accepts the resignation of a member or removes a member from office, the Archbishop shall forthwith notify the Administrator accordingly.

**(9)** Where the office of a member becomes vacant, the Archbishop shall, as soon as practicable after the office becomes vacant, appoint a person to be a member in place of that first-mentioned member.

**(10)** The exercise of a power or the performance of a function by the Trust is not invalidated 

(a) by reason only of there being a vacancy or vacancies in the membership of the Trust; or

(b) by reason only of a defect or irregularity in connection with the appointment of a member.

##### Proceedings of Trust

**1C. (1)** Except when otherwise directed by the Archbishop, a meeting of the Trust is not required to decide a question before the Trust, and the members may separately consider, and make their respective decisions on, the question.

**(2)** The decision of a majority of the members shall be taken to be the decision of the Trust.

**(3)** If the members are equally divided on a question, the decision of the Archbishop shall be taken to be the decision of the Trust.

**(4)** In this section, ‘member’ includes the Archbishop.

##### Vesting of lands in Commonwealth

**2.** The land described in the First and Fifth Schedules to this Act is hereby vested in the Commonwealth and freed and discharged from all trusts and encumbrances.

##### Land to vest for the use of Church of England

**3. (1)** The land described in the Second Schedule to this Act is hereby vested in the Trust for an estate in fee simple upon the trusts set out in this Act.

**(2)** The Trust shall not, without the consent of the Governor-General, alienate the whole or any part of the land described in the Second Schedule to this Act.

**(3)** The land described in the Third and Fourth Schedules to this Act is hereby vested in the Archbishop for an estate in fee simple upon the trusts set out in this Act.

##### Trusts upon which lands are held

**4.** The trusts upon which the lands referred to in the last preceding section shall be held shall be upon trust generally for the use and benefit of the Church of England in Norfolk Island for such purposes as may be approved by the Archbishop in accordance with the Acts and Rules made for the order and good government of the Church of England from time to time in force in Norfolk Island, and more particularly 

(a) as to the land described in the Third Schedule to this Act, upon trust 

(i) to repair, and maintain in good repair, the chapel erected on the land and known as the Chapel of St. Barnabas;

(ii) to indemnify and keep indemnified the Melanesian Mission Trust Board of Auckland in the Dominion of New Zealand against all liability for the repair and maintenance of the chapel;

(iii) to permit the removal from the chapel by the Bishop of Melanesia of such ornaments, fixtures or other articles in the chapel as are memorials of the late Bishop Patteson and other missionaries or are bequests specifically made for the use, benefit or purposes of the Melanesian Mission;

(iv) to insure, and keep insured, with a reputable insurance company, the chapel and its furnishings against damage or destruction by fire, storm and tempest and to apply any insurance moneys received for and in respect of the damage or destruction of the chapel towards its repair and reconstruction;

(v) to make any contributions required or permitted to be made by the National Security (War Damage to Property) Regulations in respect of the chapel and its furnishings, and to apply any moneys received for and in respect of any war damage to or destruction of the chapel or its furnishings by war damage towards the repair, reconstruction and replacement of the chapel and its furnishings;

(vi) to erect any building on the land:

Provided that no such building shall be erected unless a plan showing the location and type of the building has first been submitted to the Commonwealth Minister, and the Commonwealth Minister has approved of the plan; and

(vii) to keep and maintain the land, including the cemetery on the land and all paths and fences made or erected on the land, in a good, cleanly and orderly condition:

Provided that nothing in the foregoing provisions of this section shall be deemed to authorise the alienation in whole or in part of the land described in the Third Schedule; and

(b) as to the land described in the Fourth Schedule 

(i) to lease or sell the whole or any portion of the said land as the Archbishop, in his discretion, from time to time determines;

(ii) to pay into a fund, for investment, the proceeds derived from any sale, or the compensation moneys received in respect of any resumption by the Commonwealth Government, of the land or any part of it; and

(iii) to apply the income derived from any lease of the whole or any part of the land and from the investment of the moneys of the fund referred to in the last preceding sub-paragraph, in the first place, towards the maintenance and repair of the chapel and of the cemetery mentioned in paragraph (a) of this section, and, in the second place, towards the stipend of the person holding, for the time being, the office of Chaplain of Norfolk Island during such period as he conducts religious services in the chapel and has a duty imposed upon him to supervise the maintenance of the chapel in good repair.

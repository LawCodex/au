##### Citation

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the *Pharmacy Regulations*.

##### Definition

**2.** In these regulations, “the Act” means the *Pharmacy Act 1956*.

##### Record of prescriptions

**6.** The particulars to be recorded by a registered pharmaceutical chemist, in accordance with subsection 14(3) of the Act are as follows :

Date or dates medicine or drug supplied

Number of prescription

Copy of prescription and instructions on the prescription

Name of practitioner

Name or initials of person dispensing prescription.

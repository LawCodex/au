# Sellheim Grave Act 1934 (NI)

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

## SELLHEIM GRAVE ACT 1934

[Consolidated as at 1 September 1987

on the authority of the Administrator

and in accordance with

the *Enactments Reprinting Act 1980*]

\________

### TABLE OF PROVISIONS

1\. Short title

2\. Commencement

3\. Maintenance of grave

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

[image]

**Sellheim Grave Act 1934**

\_______________________________________________________________________

An Act relating to the upkeep of the grave of Major-General Victor Conradsdorf Morisset Sellheim, C.B., C.M.G.

##### Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Sellheim Grave Act 1934*.

##### Commencement

**2.** This Act shall commence and take effect on the day of its notification in the *Commonwealth of Australia Gazette*.

##### Maintenance of grave

**3.** The Administration shall care for, maintain and upkeep the grave at Kingston, Norfolk Island of Major-General Victor Conradsdorf Morisset Sellheim, C.B., C.M.G.

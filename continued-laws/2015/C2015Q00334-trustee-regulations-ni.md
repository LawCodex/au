# Trustee Regulations (NI)

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

**Trustee Regulations**

**Regulations No. 5 of 1980**

\_______________________________________________________________________

Being Regulations, made by the Administrator, acting with the advice of the Executive Council, under the *Trustee Act 1931.*

##### Short title

**1**. These Regulations may be cited as the *Trustee Regulations.*

##### Modification of Trustee Act

**2.** The Trustee Act is modified by omitting from section 43(6)(b) of the *Trustee Act 1925* of the State of New South Wales the words “twenty-one years” and substituting “18 years”.

Commenced 19 November 1980.

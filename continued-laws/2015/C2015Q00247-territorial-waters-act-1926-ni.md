**Territorial Waters Act 1926**

\_______________________________________________________________________

An Act relating to territorial waters

##### Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Territorial Waters Act 1926*.

##### Interpretation

**2. (1)** Subject to subsection 2(2), “territorial waters” means that part of the sea adjacent to the Territory of Norfolk Island that is within 12 nautical miles of a territorial sea baseline for the Territory as determined by Proclamation under section 7 of the Commonwealth *Seas and Submerged Lands Act 1973*.

**(2)** In the absence of a Proclamation referred to in subsection 2(1), “territorial waters” means that part of the sea adjacent to the Territory of Norfolk Island that is within 12 nautical miles of the coastline of the Territory at low-water.

**(3)** In this section “low-water” means lowest astronomical tide.

##### Application of laws of Norfolk Island to territorial waters

**3.** The application of any laws in force from time to time in Norfolk Island shall extend to territorial waters.

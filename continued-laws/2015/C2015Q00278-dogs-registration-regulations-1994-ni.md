##### 1. Short title

These Regulations may be cited as the *Dogs Registration Regulations 1994*.

**3. Designation of prescribed place**

For the purposes of subsection 13(3) of the Act the Chief Executive Officer may by notice in the Gazette declare a place or places to be a prescribed place.

##### 4. Prescribed offences and penalties

For the purposes of section 17A of the Act, the prescribed penalty in Schedule 1 in respect of an infringement described in column 2 of an item in the Schedule, being an infringement of a provision of the Act specified in column 1 of the item, is the penalty specified in column 3 of the item.

**5. Non‑registration of prohibited dogs**

The Registrar must not register a dog the importation or possession of which is prohibited or restricted except that any dog the importation or possession of which has been authorised under any other enactment may be registered but the registration shall be conditional upon the same conditions as those imposed upon its importation.

**6. Infringement notices**

**(1)** For the purposes of Part 5 of the Act, the administrative charge is 1 fee unit.

**(2)** For the purposes of subsection 17A(2) of the Act Form 1 in Schedule 2 is prescribed.

**(3)** For the purposes of subsection 17A(3) of the Act, Form 2 in Schedule 2 is prescribed.

**(4)** For the purposes of Section 17B, of the Act, Form 3 in Schedule 2 is prescribed.

**(5)** For the purposes of section 17F of the Act, Form 4 in Schedule 2 is prescribed.

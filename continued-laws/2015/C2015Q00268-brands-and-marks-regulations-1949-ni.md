##### Brands and Marks Regulations

##### Citation

**These regulations may be cited as the** ***Brands and Marks Regulations.***

##### Forms

**2.** Any reference in these regulations to a form shall be read as a reference to a form in Schedule 1 to these regulations.

##### Application for registration

**3. (1)** Application for the registration of a brand or mark shall be in accordance with Form 1.

**(2)** Application by the legal personal representative of a deceased proprietor for the transfer of a brand or mark shall be in accordance with Form 2.

##### Application for transfer

**4.** Application for the transfer of a brand or mark shall be in accordance with Form 3.

##### Certificate of registration

**5.** Upon the registration of any brand or mark or the transfer of any brand or mark, a certificate of registration in accordance with Form 4 shall be sent to the proprietor thereof.

##### Brands to comply with certain conditions

**6.** A brand shall not be registered unless it complies with the following conditions 

(a) where the brand, wholly or in part, consists of a letter or letters, conjoined letters or a numeral or numerals, or any combination of letters, conjoined letters and numerals, each of such letters, conjoined letters or numerals shall not be less than one and a half inches nor more than three inches in height measured in their normal position and shall not be less than half an inch apart from any letter, conjoined letters or numeral forming part of that brand.

(b) where the brand, wholly or in part, consists of a sign or character, such sign or character shall not be less than one and a half inches nor more than three inches in its greatest width or length, and shall not be less than half an inch apart from any letter, conjoined letters, numeral, sign or character forming part of that brand.

##### Cancellation of registration

**7.** Registration of a brand or mark may be cancelled by the Registrar upon the production of a certificate of the death of the proprietor thereof or other satisfactory evidence showing that such death occurred not less than six months previously if 

(a) an application under subregulation (2) of regulation 3 of these regulations has not been received by the Registrar; and

(b) notice of such intended cancellation has been posted to the legal personal representative of the deceased proprietor at his last known address and objection to such cancellation has not been received within two months from the date of posting of such notice.

##### Order of brands of cattle and horses

**9.** Any cattle and horses not already branded shall be branded in the following order and position 

Cattle 

1st brand  on the left rump.

2nd brand – on the right rump.

Horses 

1st brand  on the left shoulder.

2nd brand – on the right shoulder.

**Branding and marking of sheep**

**10. (1)** A pitch, tar or paint brand shall be on the rump of the sheep.

**(2)** An ear‑mark shall be made or marked with an ear‑plier, the cutters of which shall be of the size and shape described in Schedule 2 to these regulations.

##### (3) The ear‑mark shall in every case be marked on the left ear of male sheep and the right ear of female sheep.

**Penalties**

**11.** Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of these regulations or makes any false statement or declaration in any form presented to the Registrar shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable, upon conviction, to a fine not exceeding five pounds.

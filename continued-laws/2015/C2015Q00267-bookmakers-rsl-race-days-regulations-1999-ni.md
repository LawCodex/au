# Bookmakers (RSL Race Days) Regulations 1999 (NI)

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

## BOOKMAKERS (RSL RACE DAYS) REGULATIONS 1999

### Regulations No. 9 of 1999

I, A.J. Messner, Administrator of Norfolk Island, acting in accordance with the advice of the Executive Council of Norfolk Island, make the following Regulations under the *Bookmakers and Betting Exchange Act 1998.*

Dated 30 December 1999.

A.J. Messner

Administrator

By His Honour’s Command

Minister for Health

\________

##### Short title

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the *Bookmakers (RSL Race Days) Regulations 1999*.

##### Exemption from operation of Act – RSL Race Days

**2.** For the purposes of paragraph 51(1)(k) of the *Bookmakers and Betting Exchange Act 1998*, a person taking a bet from a natural person situated on Norfolk Island on a horse race constituting part of a race day held by the Norfolk Island RSL Sub Branch in the vicinity of the Norfolk Island Golf Club on or about 1 January or Easter Day in any year is exempt from all provisions of that Act if that person —

(a) is approved in writing by the executive member for the purposes of this Regulation; and

(b) complies with any conditions of that approval.

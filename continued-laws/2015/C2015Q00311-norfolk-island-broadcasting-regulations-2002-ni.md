##### Short title

**1**. These Regulations may be cited as the *Norfolk Island Broadcasting Regulations 2002*.

##### Commencement

**2**. These Regulations shall commence on the day on which notification of their being made is published in the Gazette.

##### Interpretation

**3.** In these Regulations “the Act” means the *Norfolk Island Broadcasting Act 2001*.

##### Prescribed manner and form of application

**4.** For the purposes of subsection 13(1) of the Act, an applicant for a licence shall apply for a licence as prescribed by Form 1 in Schedule 1.

##### Form of licence

**5.** For the purpose of sections 10, 11 and 13 of the *Norfolk Island Broadcasting Act 2001*, the prescribed form of licence to be issued by the Minister is that in Form 2 of Schedule 1.

**Prescribed fee**

**6.** The fees prescribed for the purposes of section 13(4) of the *Norfolk Island Broadcasting Act 2001* are set out in Schedule 2.

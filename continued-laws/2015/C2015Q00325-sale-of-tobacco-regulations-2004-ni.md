## Sale of Tobacco Regulations 2004

### Regulations No. 11 of 2004

##### Short title

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as *the Sale of Tobacco Regulations 2004.*

##### Reference to Sale of Tobacco Act

**2.** In these regulations reference to “the Act” is reference to the *Sale of Tobacco Act* 2004.

##### Signs on vending machines

**4.** For the purpose of subsection 9(2) of the Act a notice must be not less than 210 millimetres wide and 297 millimetres high (A4 size) with the words —

(a) “persons under 18 years must not operate this machine”; and

(b) “penalties apply” and “*Sale of Tobacco Act 2004”*,

printed in black on a white background in plain block letters of a height not less than 4 centimetres.

##### Obscuring advertising

**5.** For the purpose of subsection 15(1) of the Act —

(a) a tobacco advertisement may be obscured by —

(i) painting; or

(ii) covering

with an opaque substance or material that effectively hides the advertisement; and

(b) the specified period is 36 hours.

##### Content of signs in smoke free area

**6.** For the purposes of subsection 18(1) of the Act a sign must be not less than 210 millimetres wide and 297 millimetres high (A4 size) and must contain —

(a) a “no smoking” symbol in the form of a circle with a diagonal line printed in red over a depiction of a cigarette and smoke printed in black; and

(b) the words —

“no smoking” or “smoking prohibited” —

printed in black on a white background in plain block letters of a height not less than 3 centimetres ;and

(c) “penalties apply” and “*Sale of Tobacco Act 2004*” —

printed in black on a white background in plain block letters of a height not less than 1 centimetre.

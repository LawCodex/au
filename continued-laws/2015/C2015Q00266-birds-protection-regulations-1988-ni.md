###### Short title

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the *Birds Protection Regulations 1988*.

##### Interpretation

**2.** In these Regulations, “Act” means the *Birds Protection Act 1913*.

##### Protected birds

**3.** For the purposes of the definition of “protected bird” in section 2 of the Act, each species and sub-species of bird is a prescribed species, other than —

(a) a species or sub-species specified in the Schedule;

(b) a species or sub-species of birds in danger of extinction specified in Schedule 2 to the *Migratory Birds Act 1980*; or

(c) a species or sub-species of migratory birds specified in Schedule 3 to the *Migratory Birds Act 1980*.

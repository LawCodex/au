# Fugitive Offenders (Jurisdiction) Act 1923 (NI)

NORFOLK ISLAND

## FUGITIVE OFFENDERS (JURISDICTION) ACT 1923

[Consolidated as at 31 March 2002

on the authority of the Administrator

and in accordance with

the *Enactments Reprinting Act 1980*]

\________

### TABLE OF PROVISIONS

1\. Short title

2\. Court of Petty Sessions

[image]

**NORFOLK ISLAND**

**Fugitive Offenders (Jurisdiction) Act 1923**

\_______________________________________________________________________

An Act to confer on the Magistrate’s Court, jurisdiction under the Fugitive Offenders Act 1881 of the United Kingdom

##### Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Fugitive Offenders (Jurisdiction) Act 1923.*

##### Court of Petty Sessions to have jurisdiction

**2.** The jurisdiction under Part 1, of the *Fugitive Offenders Act 1881* of the United Kingdom (44 and 45, Vic C. 69) to hear a case and commit a fugitive to prison to await his return shall be exercised in Norfolk Island by the Court of Petty Sessions.

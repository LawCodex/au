# Drought Relief Payment Act 1994

**Drought Relief Payment Act 1994**

**No. 125 of 1994**

**An Act to amend the** ***Farm Household Support Act 1992*****, the**\
***Health Insurance Act 1973*****, the** ***Income Tax Assessment Act***\
***1936*** **and the** ***Social Security Act 1991*****, and for related**\
**purposes**

[*Assented to 18 October 1994*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

**Short title**

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Drought Relief Payment Act 1994.*

**Commencement**

**2.** This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

**Amendments**

**3.** The Acts specified in the Schedule are amended in accordance with the applicable items in the Schedule, and the other items in the Schedule have effect according to their terms.

(1) This item applies if, after 21 September 1994 and before the commencement of this item:

(a) a person purported to lodge a claim for drought relief payment; and

(b) assuming that the claim had been lodged immediately after the commencement of this item, it would have complied with sections 15 and 16 of the *Farm Household Support Act 1992.*

(2) The claim has effect, for the purposes of the *Farm Household Support Act 1992*, as if:

(a) it had been made under Part 3 of that Act immediately after the commencement of this item; and

(b) it had complied with sections 15 and 16 of that Act.

**81. Transitional—set-off of overpayments of farm household support**

(1) If:

(a) an amount purporting to be an amount of farm household support payable under the *Farm Household Support Act 1992* was paid to a person after 21 September 1994 and before the commencement of this item; and

(b) because of section 9A or 49A of that Act, the amount so paid was not payable to the person; and

(c) the amount so paid is recoverable as a debt due to the Commonwealth under section 56 of the *Farm Household Support Act 1992*;

the amount so paid may be deducted from one or more instalments of drought relief payment payable to the person under that Act.

(2) If a deduction is made from an instalment of drought relief payment, the instalment is taken to have been paid in full to the person.

**82. Transitional—set-off of pre-commencement “drought relief payment”**

(1) This item applies if, after 21 September 1994 and before the commencement of this item, a person was paid an amount described as “drought relief payment” out of money appropriated under an Appropriation Act.

**SCHEDULE—**continued

(2) The amount is to be deducted from one or more instalments of drought relief payment payable to the person under the *Farm Household Support Act 1992.*

(3) If a deduction is made from an instalment of drought relief payment, the instalment is taken to have been paid in full to the person.

Skills Australia Amendment (Australian Workforce and Productivity Agency) Act 2012

No. 69, 2012

An Act to amend the *Skills Australia Act 2008*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 27 June 2012*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Skills Australia Amendment (Australian Workforce and Productivity Agency) Act 2012*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on 1 July 2012.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

**42 Termination of appointment of members**

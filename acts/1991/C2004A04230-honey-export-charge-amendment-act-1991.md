# Honey Export Charge Amendment Act 1991

**Honey Export Charge Amendment Act 1991**

**No. 155 of 1991**

**An Act to amend the** ***Honey Export Charge Act 1973***

[*Assented to 21 October 1991*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

**Short title etc.**

**1.(1)** This Act may be cited as the *Honey Export Charge Amendment Act 1991.*

**(2)** In this Act, **"Principal Act"** means the *Honey Export Charge Act 1973*1*.*

**Commencement**

**2.** This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

**Rates of levy**

**3.** Section 7 of the Principal Act is amended by omitting from paragraph (1)(b) "0.50 cent" and substituting "0.75 cent".

**NOTE**

1\. No. 183, 1973, as amended. For previous amendments, see No. 146, 1980; No. 103, 1985; No. 30, 1988; No. 17, 1990; and No. 26, 1991.

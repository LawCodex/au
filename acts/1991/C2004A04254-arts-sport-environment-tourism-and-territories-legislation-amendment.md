# Arts, Sport, Environment, Tourism and Territories Legislation Amendment Act (No. 2) 1991

**Arts, Sport, Environment, Tourism and Territories Legislation Amendment Act**

**(No. 2) 1991**

**No. 179 of 1991**

**An Act to amend legislation relating to the arts, sport, the environment, tourism and Territories**

[*Assented to 25 November 1991*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

**Short title**

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Arts, Sport, Environment, Tourism and Territories Legislation Amendment Act (No. 2) 1991.*

**Commencement**

**2.** This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

**Amendments of Acts**

**3.(1)** Acts specified in the Schedule are amended as set out in the Schedule.

**(2)** A person who:

(a) immediately before the commencement of this Act, was the holder of a public office within the meaning of the *Remuneration Tribunal Act 1973*; and

(b) upon that commencement, has in respect of that office such recreation leave entitlements as are determined by the Remuneration Tribunal;

retains, in respect of that office, such rights (if any) in relation to leave of absence for recreation as had accrued to the person immediately before that commencement.

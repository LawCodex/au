Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Superannuation Guarantee Charge Act 1992

**1 Sections 5 and 6**

Omit “a quarter”, substitute “a QE day”.

2 Application of amendments

Despite the amendments of sections 5 and 6 of the *Superannuation Guarantee Charge Act 1992* made by this Schedule, those sections continue to apply on and after 1 July 2026 in relation to a quarter ending before that day, as if the amendments had not been made.

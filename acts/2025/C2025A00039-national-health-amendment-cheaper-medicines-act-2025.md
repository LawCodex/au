## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health Act 1953

**1 Subsection 84(1) (definition of general patient charge amount)**

Omit “$30.00”, substitute “$25.00”.

**2 Subparagraph 87(2)(e)(i)**

Omit “(less any allowable discount)”.

**3 Subparagraph 87(2)(e)(ii)**

Omit “lowest amount chargeable under subparagraph (i)”, substitute “general patient charge amount”.

**4 Subsection 87(2) (note 3)**

Repeal the note.

**5 Subsection 99(2C) (note)**

Omit “(less any allowable discount)”.

**6 Subsection 99G(1A)**

Omit “1 January 2025”, substitute “1 January 2026”.

**7 Section 99GC**

Repeal the section.

**8 Section 99GE**

Omit “99GC or”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Radiation Protection and Nuclear Safety Act 1998

**1 After subsection 10(1)**

Insert:

(1A) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a naval nuclear propulsion plant related to use in a conventionally‑armed, nuclear‑powered submarine.

**2 Subsection 10(2)**

Omit “mentioned in subsection (1)”, substitute “to which subsection (1) applies”.

##### Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

**3 Section 37J**

Before “The Minister”, insert “(1)”.

**4 At the end of section 37J**

Add:

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a naval nuclear propulsion plant related to use in a conventionally‑armed, nuclear‑powered submarine.

**5 Section 140A**

Before “The Minister”, insert “(1)”.

**6 At the end of section 140A**

Add:

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a naval nuclear propulsion plant related to use in a conventionally‑armed, nuclear‑powered submarine.

**7 Section 146M**

Before “The Minister”, insert “(1)”.

**8 At the end of section 146M**

Add:

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a naval nuclear propulsion plant related to use in a conventionally‑armed, nuclear‑powered submarine.

**9 After subsection 305(2)**

Insert:

(2A) Subparagraph (2)(d)(ii) does not apply to a naval nuclear propulsion plant related to use in a conventionally‑armed, nuclear‑powered submarine.

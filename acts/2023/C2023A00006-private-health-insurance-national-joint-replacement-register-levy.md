## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Private Health Insurance (National Joint Replacement Register Levy) Act 2009

**1 Subsection 5(1) (definition of joint replacement prosthesis)**

Repeal the definition.

**2 Subsection 5(1)**

Insert:

***joint replacement device*** means a medical device (within the meaning of the *Private Health Insurance Act 2007*) that:

(a) is listed in the Private Health Insurance (Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products) Rules; and

(b) is used in joint replacement.

***Private Health Insurance (Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products) Rules*** has the same meaning as in the *Private Health Insurance Act 2007*.

**3 Subsection 5(1) (definition of Private Health Insurance (Prostheses) Rules)**

Repeal the definition.

**4 Subsection 5(1) (definition of supplementary national joint replacement register levy day)**

Omit “section 6”, substitute “section 8A”.

**5 Subsection 5(2)**

Omit “joint replacement prosthesis if”, substitute “joint replacement device if”.

**6 Paragraphs 5(2)(a) and (b)**

Omit “prosthesis is currently listed in the Private Health Insurance (Prostheses) Rules”, substitute “device is currently listed in the Private Health Insurance (Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products) Rules”.

**7 Paragraph 5(2)(b)**

Omit “that prosthesis”, substitute “that device”.

**8 Subsection 5(3)**

Omit “prostheses are taken, or are taken not, to be joint replacement prostheses for the purposes of the definition of ***joint replacement prosthesis***”, substitute “medical devices (within the meaning of the *Private Health Insurance Act 2007*) are taken, or are taken not, to be joint replacement devices for the purposes of the definition of ***joint replacement device***”.

**9 Subsection 6(1)**

Omit “prosthesis”, substitute “device”.

**10 Paragraphs 7(2)(a) and (b)**

Omit “prostheses”, substitute “devices”.

**11 Paragraph 7(2)(c)**

Omit “prosthesis”, substitute “device”.

**12 Subsection 7(3)**

Omit “prosthesis”, substitute “device”.

**13 Section 7A**

Omit “prosthesis” (wherever occurring), substitute “device”.

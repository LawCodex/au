###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Family Law Act 1975

**1 Section 10P**

Omit “(Division 2)”, substitute “(Division 1)”.

##### Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia Act 2021

**2 Subsections 169(5) and 170(5)**

Omit “Judge of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 2)”, substitute “Judge of the Federal Court”.

**3 Subsection 257(1)**

Omit “Judge of the Court”, substitute “Judge of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 1)”.

**4 At the end of Chapter 4**

Add:

### Part 10—Miscellaneous

###### 277A Immunity for Judges of Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 2)

A Judge of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 2) has the same protection and immunity as a Judge of the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 1).

5 Application of amendments

The amendments made by this Schedule apply in relation to functions performed on or after the day this Schedule commences.

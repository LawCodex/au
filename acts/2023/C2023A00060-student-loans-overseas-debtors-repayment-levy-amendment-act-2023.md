[*Assented to 21 August 2023*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *Student Loans (Overseas Debtors Repayment Levy) Amendment Act 2023*.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Student Loans (Overseas Debtors Repayment Levy) Act 2015

**1 Section 3 (paragraph (b) of the definition of overseas debtors repayment levy)**

Omit “*Trade Support Loans Act 2014*”, substitute “*Australian Apprenticeship Support Loans Act 2014*”.

**2 Section 5**

Omit “*Trade Support Loans Act 2014*”, substitute “*Australian Apprenticeship Support Loans Act 2014*”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Passports Act 2005

**1 Subsection 6(1)**

Insert:

***Document Verification Service*** has the same meaning as ***DVS*** has in the *Identity Verification Services Act 2023*.

***Face Verification Service*** has the same meaning as ***FVS*** has in the *Identity Verification Services Act 2023*.

**2 Section 46**

Before “On request,”, insert “(1)”.

**3 After paragraph 46(d)**

Insert:

(da) participating in:

(i) the Document Verification Service; or

(ii) the Face Verification Service; or

(iii) any other service specified, or of a kind specified, in the Minister’s determination;

to share or match information relating to the identity of a person;

**4 At the end of section 46**

Add:

(2) To avoid doubt, information may be provided to the service referred to in paragraph (1)(da) before being disclosed to the person participating in the service.

5 Application of amendments

The amendments of section 46 of the *Australian Passports Act 2005* made by this Schedule apply in relation to any personal information disclosed after the commencement of this item (whether the information was obtained before or after that commencement).

**6 At the end of Division 1 of Part 5**

Add:

###### 46A Minister may arrange for computer programs to disclose personal information for the purposes of certain identity verification services

(1) The Minister may arrange for the use, under the Minister’s control, of computer programs in disclosing personal information under paragraph 46(1)(da) to a person participating in:

(a) the Document Verification Service; or

(b) the Face Verification Service.

(2) For the purposes of this Act or any legislative instrument made under this Act, the Minister is taken to have disclosed personal information in accordance with paragraph 46(1)(da) if the disclosure was made by the operation of a computer program under an arrangement made under subsection (1) of this section.

7 Application of amendments

Section 46A of the *Australian Passports Act 2005*, as inserted by this Schedule, applies in relation to any personal information disclosed after the commencement of this item (whether the information was obtained before or after that commencement).

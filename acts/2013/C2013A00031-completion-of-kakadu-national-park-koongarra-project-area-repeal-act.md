Completion of Kakadu National Park (Koongarra Project Area Repeal) Act 2013

No. 31, 2013

An Act to repeal the *Koongarra Project Area Act 1981*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 30 March 2013*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Completion of Kakadu National Park (Koongarra Project Area Repeal) Act 2013*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day after this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Repeal

##### Koongarra Project Area Act 1981

**1 The whole of the Act**

Repeal the Act.

Vocational Education and Training Funding Amendment Act 2002

No. 102, 2002

An Act to amend the *Vocational Education and Training Funding Act 1992*, and for other purposes

[*Assented to 10 November 2002*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Vocational Education and Training Funding Amendment Act 2002*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment of the Vocational Education and Training Funding Act 1992

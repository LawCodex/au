###### 51A PBR ceases if renewal fees are not paid

(1) PBR in a plant variety ceases if:

(a) the prescribed fee for the renewal of the PBR (the ***renewal fee***) is not paid within the prescribed period (the ***payment period***); and

(b) in the prescribed period (the ***extended payment period***) beginning immediately after the end of the payment period, the renewal fee and any prescribed additional fee for payment in the extended payment period are not paid.

(2) The PBR is taken to have ceased immediately after the end of the payment period.

(3) The Registrar must enter particulars of the cessation of the PBR into the Register.

(4) The PBR is taken not to be infringed by anything done after the cessation.

(5) During the extended payment period, unless the renewal fee and any prescribed additional fee have been paid, no action may be begun in relation to an infringement of the PBR that occurs during the extended payment period.

Note 1: If the renewal fee and any additional fees are paid during the extended payment period, subsection (5) does not prevent action being brought (after those fees are paid) in relation to an infringement of the PBR that occurs at any time during the extended payment period.

Note 2: If the renewal fee and any additional fees are not paid during the extended payment period, the PBR cannot be infringed during the extended payment period (as the PBR is taken to have ceased at the end of the original payment period—see subsection (2)).

**19 Section 52**

Repeal the section, substitute:

###### 52 Surrender of PBR

(1) The holder of PBR in a plant variety may, at any time, by written notice to the Registrar, offer to surrender the PBR.

(2) The notice must be in the approved form.

(3) The Registrar must give public notice of the offer.

(4) After hearing all interested persons who notify the Registrar in accordance with the regulations of their wish to be heard, the Registrar may accept the offer and revoke the PBR.

(5) If the Registrar revokes the PBR, the Registrar must enter details of the revocation in the Register.

(6) If relevant proceedings in relation to a PBR are pending, the Registrar must not accept an offer to surrender the PBR without either the leave of the court or the consent of the parties to the proceedings.

(7) If a licence under subsection 19(3) is in force in relation to a PBR, the Registrar must not accept an offer to surrender the PBR.

Note: A decision under this section to revoke, or refuse to revoke, PBR is reviewable by the ART under section 77.

#### Division 2—Transitional provisions

47 Transitional provision—NEDE reporting years

(1) If this Part commences on 1 July 2026, the period of 18 months beginning on 1 July 2025 is taken to be a NEDE reporting year for the purposes of Division 2A of Part 7 of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992* (and the calendar year beginning on 1 January 2026 is otherwise taken not to be a NEDE reporting year for the purposes of that Division).

(2) If this Part commences after 1 July 2026, the period of 18 months beginning on 1 July 2026 is taken to be a NEDE reporting year for the purposes of Division 2A of Part 7 of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992* (and the calendar year beginning on 1 January 2027 is otherwise taken not to be a NEDE reporting year for the purposes of that Division).

(3) For the purposes of applying Division 2A of Part 7 of the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992* on and after the commencement of this Part, treat a financial year ending before the start of the 18‑month period mentioned in subitem (1) or (2) (as the case requires) as a NEDE reporting year.

Note: Subitem (3) means that, for example, an amount of eligible drama expenditure incurred during the financial year ending immediately before the start of the 18‑month NEDE reporting year may be able to be carried forward to the 18‑month NEDE reporting year. A shortfall amount from that financial year may also need to be made up in the 18‑month NEDE reporting year.

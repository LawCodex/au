## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Sunsetting

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**1 Section 34JF**

Omit “7 March 2027”, substitute “the third anniversary of the commencement of the *Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Amendment Act (No. 2) 2026*”.

##### Intelligence Services Act 2001

**2 Paragraph 29(1)(ce)**

Omit “7 September 2023”, substitute “the second anniversary of the commencement of the *Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Amendment Act (No. 2) 2026*”.

### Part 2—Extending adult questioning warrants to additional heads of security

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**3 Section 34A (definition of adult questioning matter)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***adult questioning matter*** means a matter that relates to:

(a) the protection of, and of the people of, the Commonwealth and the several States and Territories from any of the following:

(i) espionage;

(ii) sabotage;

(iii) politically motivated violence;

(iv) promotion of communal violence;

(v) attacks on Australia’s defence system;

(vi) acts of foreign interference;

whether directed from, or committed within, Australia or not; or

(b) the protection of Australia’s territorial and border integrity from serious threats.

### Part 3—Expanding the disqualifying matters for appointment as a prescribed authority

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**4 At the end of subsection 34AD(2)**

Add:

; or (h) a member of the Defence Force; or

(i) an APS employee; or

(j) an Agency Head (within the meaning of the *Public Service Act 1999*); or

(k) a member of the Parliament of the Commonwealth or a State or the Legislative Assembly of a Territory; or

(l) a person employed under the *Members of Parliament (Staff) Act 1984*; or

(m) a Parliamentary Service employee (within the meaning of the *Parliamentary Service Act 1999*); or

(n) the Director of Public Prosecutions or a person performing a similar function appointed under a law of a State or Territory; or

(o) the Solicitor‑General of the Commonwealth or of a State or Territory; or

(p) an examiner (within the meaning of the *Australian Crime Commission Act 2002*).

### Part 4—Termination of appointment of prescribed authorities

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**5 Subsection 34AD(9)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

Termination of appointment

(9) The Attorney‑General may terminate the appointment of a prescribed authority:

(a) for misbehaviour; or

(b) if the prescribed authority is unable to perform the duties of a prescribed authority because of physical or mental incapacity.

(9A) The Attorney‑General must terminate the appointment of a prescribed authority:

(a) if the prescribed authority becomes bankrupt; or

(b) if the prescribed authority engages in paid or unpaid work, or has an interest, pecuniary or otherwise, that, in the Attorney‑General’s opinion, conflicts or could conflict with the proper performance of the prescribed authority’s duties; or

(c) if the prescribed authority has ceased to be eligible for appointment under subsection (1); or

(d) if the prescribed authority fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with subsection (6), (7) or (8).

### Part 5—Providing reports to the Attorney‑General

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**6 After paragraph 34HA(1)(a)**

Insert:

(aa) details of any action taken in relation to the warrant that:

(i) did not comply with guidelines given by the Minister under section 8A; or

(ii) contravened a written statement of procedures in force under section 34AF; or

(iii) contravened the warrant; or

(iv) contravened a condition or restriction in the warrant; or

(v) contravened a direction given by a prescribed authority in connection with the warrant; and

### Part 6—Prescribed authority for post‑charge questioning etc.

##### Australian Security Intelligence Organisation Act 1979

**7 Paragraph 34BD(1)(b)**

Omit “subsection (2)”, substitute “subsections (2) and (3A)”.

**8 After subsection 34BD(3)**

Insert:

Additional requirements for post‑charge questioning etc.

(3A) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), if:

(a) the warrant is a post‑charge questioning warrant; or

(b) the subject of the warrant has been charged with a related offence and that charge is still to be resolved; or

(c) such a charge is imminent;

the warrant authorises the Organisation to question the subject only before a prescribed authority to whom paragraph 34AD(1)(a) applies.

**9 Subsection 34BE(4)**

Omit “A questioning warrant”, substitute “Subject to subsection (4A), a questioning warrant”.

**10 After subsection 34BE(4)**

Insert:

(4A) For the purposes of subsection (4), if:

(a) the warrant is a post‑charge questioning warrant; or

(b) the subject of the warrant has been charged with a related offence and that charge is still to be resolved; or

(c) such a charge is imminent;

the warrant requests the subject to produce the record or thing, or class of record or thing, only before a prescribed authority to whom paragraph 34AD(1)(a) applies.

**11 Paragraph 34DE(1)(e)**

Omit “the prescribed authority”, substitute “a prescribed authority”.

## Schedule 2—Application and transitional provisions

1 Adult questioning matters

The amendments made by Part 2 of Schedule 1 apply in relation to a questioning warrant that is issued on or after the commencement of this item.

2 Appointment of prescribed authorities

The amendments made by Parts 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 apply in relation to an appointment of a prescribed authority that is made on or after the commencement of this item.

3 Providing reports to the Attorney‑General

The amendments made by Part 5 of Schedule 1 apply in relation to a questioning warrant that is issued on or after the commencement of this item.

4 Prescribed authority for post‑charge questioning etc.

The amendments made by Part 6 of Schedule 1 apply in relation to a questioning warrant that is issued on or after the commencement of this item.

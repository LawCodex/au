###### 195A Persons who must provide information and documents for the assessment of biosecurity risk

(1) This section applies in relation to a person (a ***traveller***) who:

(a) intends to enter, or enters, Australian territory on an incoming aircraft or vessel; and

(b) was, is, or will be:

(i) a passenger on an incoming aircraft or vessel; or

(ii) a member of the crew of an incoming aircraft or vessel; or

(iii) the person in charge of an incoming aircraft or vessel; or

(iv) a person included in a prescribed class of persons.

Information and documents must be provided in accordance with the regulations

(2) A traveller must provide prescribed information and prescribed documents in relation to themselves, and any other person that the traveller was, is, or will be in charge of on the relevant flight or voyage, in accordance with the regulations.

Note 1: The obligation in this subsection applies whether or not the traveller is in Australian territory at the relevant time (see subsection (8)).

Note 2: A person may commit an offence or contravene a civil penalty provision if the person provides false or misleading information or documents (see sections 137.1 and 137.2 of the *Criminal Code* and sections 532 and 533 of this Act).

(3) Information or a document prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) must be information or a document for any of the following purposes:

(a) assessing the level of biosecurity risk associated with a traveller or any goods that a traveller has with them;

(b) the future profiling, or future assessment, of biosecurity risks in relation to travellers.

Information and documents must be updated to remain accurate and complete

(4) If information or a document provided by a person for the purposes of subsection (2) is not, or is no longer, accurate or complete, the person must provide any information and documents required to correct or to complete the information or document in accordance with the regulations.

Note 1: The obligation in this subsection applies whether or not the person is in Australian territory at the relevant time (see subsection (8)).

Note 2: A person may commit an offence or contravene a civil penalty provision if the person provides false or misleading information or documents (see sections 137.1 and 137.2 of the *Criminal Code* and sections 532 and 533 of this Act).

Exceptions

(5) Despite subsections (2) and (4), a person is not required to provide information or documents under those subsections in prescribed circumstances.

Information and documents may be provided on behalf of the traveller

(6) In addition to subsection (2), a person may provide information and documents on behalf of a traveller for the purposes of subsection (2) or (4) in prescribed circumstances.

Note: A person may commit an offence or contravene a civil penalty provision if the person provides false or misleading information or documents (see sections 137.1 and 137.2 of the *Criminal Code* and sections 532 and 533 of this Act).

Civil penalty provision

(7) A person who is required to provide information or a document under subsection (2) or (4) must comply with the requirement.

Civil penalty: 120 penalty units.

Application to persons within and outside Australian territory

(8) Subsection (7) applies whether or not the person is in Australian territory when the person is required to provide the information or documents.

Note: This section is not subject to the privilege against self‑incrimination (see section 635).

Matters that may be prescribed for the provision of prescribed information and documents

(9) For the purposes of subsection (2) or (4), the regulations may do any or all of the following:

(a) prescribe the time or times at, or by which, any or all prescribed information or documents must be provided;

(b) prescribe the manner or manners in which any or all prescribed information or documents must be provided at, or by, any or all of those times;

(c) prescribe a time or manner by reference to a person to whom information or documents must be provided, a place, an event, or another circumstance;

(d) make provision for the scanning of one or more kinds of documents;

(e) make provision for the collection and retention of personal information provided, or personal information included in documents provided, for the purposes of subsection (2) or (4).

Example 1: The regulations may prescribe for the purposes of subsection (2) that a traveller is required to provide prescribed information in an electronic or written manner by the time the traveller enters Australia.

Example 2: The regulations may prescribe for the purposes of subsection (4) that the person must provide prescribed information by answering questions orally on request by a biosecurity officer or an Immigration and Border Protection worker (within the meaning of the *Australian Border Force Act 2015*) at a first point of entry.

Notice of proposed refusal to approve arrangement

Notice of proposed approval subject to conditions

Omit “**proposed variation, suspension or revocation**”, substitute “**certain proposed decisions relating to approval, variation, suspension or revocation**”.

**6 Before section 435A**

Insert:

###### 435AA Notice of proposed refusal of approval or approval subject to conditions

(1) For the purposes of subsections 406(4) and (5), including those subsections as they apply because of subsection 412(3), the relevant Director:

(a) must give the applicant for approval of a proposed arrangement a notice before refusing to approve the arrangement; and

(b) may give the applicant for approval of a proposed arrangement a notice before approving the arrangement subject to one or more conditions.

Contents of notice

(2) A notice under subsection (1) must:

(a) be in writing; and

(b) state the thing covered by subsection (1) that the relevant Director is considering doing; and

(c) state the reasons for the relevant Director giving the notice; and

(d) subject to subsection (3), invite the applicant to give the relevant Director, within 30 days after the day the notice is given to the applicant, a written submission in relation to the notice; and

(e) include a statement setting out the applicant’s right to seek review of a decision of the relevant Director to do a thing covered by subsection (1).

(3) A notice under subsection (1) is not required to include the invitation referred to in paragraph (2)(d) in relation to a thing covered by subsection (1) if the relevant Director is satisfied that the grounds for doing that thing are serious and urgent.

7 Application provision—new and pending applications for approval

The amendments of section 406 of the *Biosecurity Act 2015* made by this Schedule apply in relation to the following:

(a) an application for approval of a proposed arrangement or varied arrangement made on or after the commencement of this item;

(b) an application for approval of a proposed arrangement or varied arrangement made before the commencement of this item if, before that commencement:

(i) the relevant Director had not notified the applicant that the relevant Director is considering refusing to approve the arrangement or approving the arrangement subject to one or more conditions; and

(ii) the relevant Director had not made a decision on the application.

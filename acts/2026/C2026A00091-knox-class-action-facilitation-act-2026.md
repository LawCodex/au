## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013

**1 After subsection 11(2)**

Insert:

(2A) A settlement payment under the Settlement Distribution Scheme approved in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (Robodebt settlement)* [2026] FCA 1029 does not constitute compensation for the purposes of this Act.

##### Social Security Act 1991

**2 After paragraph 8(8)(jd)**

Insert:

(je) a settlement payment under the Settlement Distribution Scheme approved in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (Robodebt settlement)* [2026] FCA 1029;

**3 Subsection 17(2)**

Omit “subsection (2B)”, substitute “subsections (2B) and (2D)”.

**4 After subsection 17(2C)**

Insert:

(2D) A settlement payment under the Settlement Distribution Scheme approved in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (Robodebt settlement)* [2026] FCA 1029 does not constitute compensation for the purposes of this Act.

##### Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986

**5 After paragraph 5H(8)(mc)**

Insert:

(md) a settlement payment under the Settlement Distribution Scheme approved in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (Robodebt settlement)* [2026] FCA 1029;

**6 Subsection 5NB(2)**

Omit “or (6A)”, substitute “, (6A) or (6C)”.

**7 After subsection 5NB(6B)**

Insert:

(6C) A settlement payment under the Settlement Distribution Scheme approved in *Prygodicz v Commonwealth of Australia (Robodebt settlement)* [2026] FCA 1029 does not constitute ***compensation*** for the purposes of this Act.

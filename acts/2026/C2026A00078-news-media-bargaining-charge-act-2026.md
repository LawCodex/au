[*Assented to 26 August 2026*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *News Media Bargaining Charge Act 2026*.

###### 3 Imposition of charge

(1) Charge payable in accordance with section 13 of the *News Media Bargaining (Administration) Act 2026* is imposed.

(2) The rate of charge is 2.75%.

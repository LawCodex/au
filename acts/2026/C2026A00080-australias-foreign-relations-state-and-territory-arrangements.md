##### Subdivision C—When arrangements entered into without the Minister’s approval can be given effect

###### 32A Determination that arrangement can be given effect

(1) If section 30, 31 or 32 applies to a foreign arrangement, the Minister may determine, by written notice given to the core State/Territory entity, that section 30, 31 or 32 (whichever is applicable) does not apply to the arrangement.

(2) Before making a determination under subsection (1), the Minister must be satisfied that the arrangement:

(a) would not adversely affect, or would be unlikely to adversely affect, Australia’s national interest; and

(b) would not be, or would be unlikely to be, inconsistent with Australia’s foreign policy.

(3) If the Minister makes a determination under subsection (1):

(a) section 30, 31 or 32 (whichever is applicable) is taken to have never applied in relation to the arrangement; and

(b) the Minister is taken to have given approval under subsection 24(2) in relation to the arrangement; and

(c) the Minister’s approval under subsection 24(2) is taken to be in force in relation to the arrangement from the time immediately before the entity entered the arrangement.

(4) A determination under subsection (1) is not a legislative instrument.

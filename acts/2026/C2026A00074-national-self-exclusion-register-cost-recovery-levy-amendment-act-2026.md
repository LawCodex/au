## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Self‑exclusion Register (Cost Recovery Levy) Act 2019

**1 Section 6 (definition of cost)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***cost***:

(a) in relation to the ACMA, means an amount that, in accordance with accrual‑based accounting principles, is treated as a cost of the ACMA; or

(b) in relation to the Department, means an amount that, in accordance with accrual‑based accounting principles, is treated as a cost of the Department.

**2 At the end of subsection 10(1)**

Add:

; (h) any costs incurred, during the levy period, by the ACMA or the Department in relation to promotion of the Register;

(j) any other costs incurred by the ACMA or the Department, during the levy period, in relation to the operation of the Register;

(k) any costs incurred by the ACMA or the Department, during a previous levy period, in relation to the operation of the Register that have not been counted for the purposes of this subsection.

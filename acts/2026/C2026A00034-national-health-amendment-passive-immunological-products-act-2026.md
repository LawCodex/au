## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Health Act 1953

**1 Subsection 4(1) (definition of vaccine)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***vaccine*** means a preparation, including a vaccine or immunising agent, that confers protection to persons through active or passive immunity against disease.

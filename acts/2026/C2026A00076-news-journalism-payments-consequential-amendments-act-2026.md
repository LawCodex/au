[*Assented to 26 August 2026*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *News Journalism Payments (Consequential Amendments) Act 2026*.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Competition and Consumer Act 2010

**1 Section 52A**

Insert:

***Communications Secretary*** means the Secretary of the Department administered by the Minister administering the *News Journalism Payments Act 2026.*

**2 At the end of Division 3 of Part IVBA**

Add:

(2) The notification must:

(a) specify the day on which the event occurred; and

(b) be given as soon as practicable after the event; and

(c) if the notification is required to include details of a registered news business corporation—include details of the corporation’s point of contact (if any) set up for the purpose of section 52Z; and

(d) if the notification is required to include details of a news business—include details of each of the news sources that comprise the news business.

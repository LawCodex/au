## Schedule 1—Amendment

##### Passenger Movement Charge Act 1978

**1 Section 6**

Omit all the words after “Australia is”, substitute “$70”.

2 Application provision

The amendment made by item 1 of this Schedule applies in relation to the departure of a person from Australia on or after 1 July 2024, unless:

(a) the person departs using a ticket or an equivalent authority; and

(b) the ticket or authority was sold or issued before 1 July 2024.

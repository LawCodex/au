###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *National Cancer Screening Register Amendment Act 2024*.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Cancer Screening Register Act 2016

**1 Section 4 (at the end of the definition of designated cancer)**

Add:

; (c) lung cancer.

**2 At the end of section 10**

Add:

(3) The register may include information relating to individuals in connection with screening associated with lung cancer.

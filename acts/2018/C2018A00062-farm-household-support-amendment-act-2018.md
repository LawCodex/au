## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Farm Household Support Act 2014

**1 Paragraph 3(a)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**2 Paragraph 4(a)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**3 Subsection 5(1) (definition of 3 years or less)**

Repeal the definition.

**4 Subsection 5(1)**

Insert:

***4 years or less*** has the meaning given by subsection 6(3).

**5 Section 6 (heading)**

Omit “**3 years or less**”, substitute “**4 years or less**”.

**6 Subsection 6(3) (heading)**

Omit “***3 years or less***”, substitute “***4 years or less***”.

**7 Subsection 6(3)**

Omit “***3 years or less***”, substitute “***4 years or less***”.

**8 Subsection 6(3)**

Omit “1095 days”, substitute “1,460 days”.

**9 Section 7**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**10 Paragraph 8(h)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**11 Paragraph 9(j)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**12 Subsections 96(1) and (2)**

Omit “3 years”, substitute “4 years”.

**13 Application of amendments**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply in relation to the following:

(a) a person who made a claim for farm household allowance at any time before the day this Schedule commences (whether or not the claim had been determined before that day);

(b) a person who makes a claim for farm household allowance on or after the day this Schedule commences.

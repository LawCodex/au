Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998

**1 Paragraph 2(2)(c) of Schedule 7**

Omit “purchaser; or”, substitute “purchaser.”.

**2 Paragraph 2(2)(d) of Schedule 7**

Repeal the paragraph.

##### Primary Industries (Excise) Levies Act 1999

**3 Subparagraph 2(2)(b)(ii) of Schedule 14**

Omit “Australia; or”, substitute “Australia.”.

**4 Paragraph 2(2)(c) of Schedule 14**

Repeal the paragraph.

## Schedule 1—ASIC fees

##### National Consumer Credit Protection (Fees) Act 2009

**1 After section 8**

Insert:

###### 8A Different fees for matters relating to person liable to pay fee

Without limiting sections 5 and 6, the regulations may prescribe, in relation to a chargeable matter, different fees having regard to any matter relating to the person by whom the fee for the matter is payable.

**2 Section 10**

Omit “and 8”, substitute “, 8 and 8A”.

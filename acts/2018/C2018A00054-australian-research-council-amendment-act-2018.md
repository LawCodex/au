## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Research Council Act 2001

**1 At the end of subsection 48(2)**

Add:

; (q) the financial year starting on 1 July 2020;

(r) the financial year starting on 1 July 2021.

**2 Paragraphs 49(r), (s) and (t)**

Repeal the paragraphs, substitute:

(r) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2017—$758,055,000; and

(s) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2018—$759,925,000; and

(t) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2019—$771,932,000; and

(u) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2020—$771,932,000; and

(v) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2021—$771,932,000.

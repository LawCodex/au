## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Primary Industries Research and Development Act 1989

**1 Subsection 4(1) (at the end of the definition of marketing activities)**

Add “or an activity incidental to such marketing, advertising or promotion”.

**2 After paragraph 11(ea)**

Insert:

(eb) if a person makes a gift, grant, bequest or devise to the Corporation for the purpose of the Corporation carrying out marketing activities for the benefit of the primary industry or class of primary industries in respect of which the Corporation was established—to use the gift, grant, bequest or devise to carry out those marketing activities; and

**3 Paragraph 13(1)(b)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(b) marketing activities as mentioned in paragraph 11(ea) or (eb).

**4 Paragraph 14(1)(b)**

Repeal the paragraph, substitute:

(b) marketing activities as mentioned in paragraph 11(ea) or (eb).

**5 Subparagraph 28(a)(ia)**

Repeal the subparagraph, substitute:

(ia) the marketing activities that it coordinated or funded, wholly or partly, during the period; and

**6 At the end of section 28**

Add:

Note: For subparagraph (a)(ia), an R&D Corporation is permitted to carry out marketing activities as mentioned in paragraphs 11(ea) and (eb).

**7 After subsection 33(1)**

Insert:

(1A) Subsection (1) does not apply to an R&D Corporation’s money to the extent that it includes a gift, grant, bequest or devise of a kind mentioned in paragraph 11(eb).

Note: Under that paragraph, the R&D Corporation must use the gift, grant, bequest or devise to carry out marketing activities.

**8 At the end of section 33A**

Add:

Note: An R&D Corporation must use certain gifts, grants, bequests or devises to carry out marketing activities: see paragraph 11(eb).

**9 Application provisions**

(1) The amendments of sections 13 and 14 of the *Primary Industries Research and Development Act 1989* made by this Schedule apply in relation to agreements entered into on or after the commencement of this item.

(2) The amendments of section 28 of the *Primary Industries Research and Development Act 1989* made by this Schedule apply in relation to annual reports prepared on or after the commencement of this item.

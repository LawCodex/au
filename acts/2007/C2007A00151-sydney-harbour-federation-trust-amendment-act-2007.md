# Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Amendment Act 2007

No. 151, 2007

An Act to amend the *Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Act 2001*, and for related purposes

Contents

[image]

Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Amendment Act 2007

No. 151, 2007

An Act to amend the *Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Act 2001*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 24 September 2007*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Amendment Act 2007*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment of the Sydney Harbour Federation Trust Act 2001

**1 Paragraph 64(1)(b)**

Omit “10 years from the commencement of this Act”, substitute “19 September 2033”.

**2 At the end of paragraph 64(1)(b)**

Add:

Note: This Act commenced on 20 September 2001.

**3 Subsection 66(1)**

Omit “10 years from the commencement of this Act”, substitute “19 September 2033”.

**4 At the end of subsection 66(1)**

Add:

Note: This Act commenced on 20 September 2001.

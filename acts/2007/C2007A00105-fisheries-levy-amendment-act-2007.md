Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Fisheries Levy Act 1984

**1 Subsection 3(1) (at the end of the definition of plan of management)**

Add “or section 15A of the *Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984*”.

**2 Subsection 3(2)**

After “the fishing capacity”, insert “or a part of the fishing capacity”.

**3 Section 5**

After “imposed on”, insert “the following”.

**4 Paragraph 5(d)**

Omit “or (3)”, substitute “, (3) or (4A)”.

**5 After paragraph 5(e)**

Insert:

(ea) the making under subsection 21(1A) of the *Torres Strait Fisheries Act 1984* of an entry in a licence granted under subsection 19(4A) of that Act, being an entry included in a class of entries prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph;

**6 Paragraph 5(f)**

Omit “or (3)”, substitute “, (3) or (4A)”.

**7 Paragraph 5(fa)**

Omit “and”.

**8 Paragraph 5(g)**

Omit “or (3)”, substitute “, (3) or (4A)”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Subsection 3(1) (paragraph (e) of the definition of petroleum activity)**

Omit “*Petroleum (Submerged Lands) Act 1967*”, substitute “*Offshore Petroleum Act 2006*”.

**2 Schedule 4 (item 22A, the description of goods in column 2)**

Omit “Eastern Greater Sunrise area, within the meaning of the *Petroleum (Submerged Lands) Act 1967*”, substitute “Eastern Greater Sunrise offshore area, within the meaning of the *Offshore Petroleum Act 2006*”.

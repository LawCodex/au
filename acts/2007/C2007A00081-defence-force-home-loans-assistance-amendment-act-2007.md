Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Amendment Act 2007

No. 81, 2007

An Act to amend the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Act 1990*

[*Assented to 21 June 2007*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Amendment Act 2007*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment of the Defence Force (Home Loans Assistance) Act 1990

**1 Section 3 (definition of finishing day)**

Omit “31 December 2007”, substitute “30 June 2008”.

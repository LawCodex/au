[*Assented to 25 September 2007*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Veterans’ Entitlements Amendment (Disability, War Widow and War Widower Pensions) Act 2007*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on 20 March 2008.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

**11 Application and transitional provisions**

Application

(1) The amendments of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* made by this Act apply on and after the commencement of this Act.

Transitional—adjustment day that is 20 March 2008

(2) Subsection 198(5DA) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* (as amended by this Act) operates in relation to the adjustment day that is 20 March 2008 as if the general rate on the day before the adjustment day were $338.94 per fortnight.

(3) Subsection 198(5E) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* operates in relation to the adjustment day that is 20 March 2008 as if:

(a) the general rate on that day were that rate as affected by subsection 198(5DA) of that Act (as amended by this Act); and

(b) for the above general rate specified in paragraph (a) of the definition of ***above general rate*** in subsection 198(1) of that Act, the result of step 4 of the method statement in subsection 198(5E) of that Act were increased by $15.00.

Note: Paragraph (a) of the definition of ***above general rate*** in subsection 198(1) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* deals with the rate set by subsection 22(4) of that Act.

(4) Subsection 198(8A) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* (as amended by this Act) operates in relation to the adjustment day that is 20 March 2008 as if the rate in paragraph 30(1)(b) on the day before the adjustment day were $35 per fortnight.

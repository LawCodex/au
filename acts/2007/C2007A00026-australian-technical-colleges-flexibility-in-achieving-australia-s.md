No. 26, 2007

An Act to amend the *Australian Technical Colleges (Flexibility in Achieving Australia’s Skills Needs) Act 2005*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 15 March 2007*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Australian Technical Colleges (Flexibility in Achieving Australia’s Skills Needs) Amendment Act (No. 1) 2007*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

# Customs Tariff Amendment (Tobacco Content) Act 2008

No. 37, 2008

An Act to amend the *Customs Tariff Act 1995*, and for related purposes

Customs Tariff Amendment (Tobacco Content) Act 2008

No. 37, 2008

An Act to amend the *Customs Tariff Act 1995*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 24 June 2008*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment (Tobacco Content) Act 2008*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Subsection 3(1)**

Insert:

***tobacco content*** includes any thing (including moisture) added to the tobacco leaf during manufacturing or processing.

**2 Application**

The amendment made by item 1 applies in relation to:

(a) goods imported into Australia on or after 1 November 1999; and

(b) goods imported into Australia before 1 November 1999, where the time for working out the rate of import duty on the goods had not occurred before 1 November 1999.

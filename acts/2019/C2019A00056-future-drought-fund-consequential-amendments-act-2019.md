### Part 2—Transitional provisions

41 Transitional—reporting obligations of the Future Fund Board of Guardians

Despite the repeal of sections 113 and 114 (reporting obligations) of the *Nation‑building Funds Act 2008* by this Schedule, those sections continue to apply, after the commencement of this item, in relation to:

(a) the performance of the Future Fund Board of Guardians’ functions; and

(b) the operation of the Board;

in respect of the repealed Building Australia Fund, as if the repeal of those sections had not happened.

42 Transitional—reports

Despite the amendments of section 81 of the *Future Fund Act 2006* made by this Schedule, that section continues to apply, in relation to the annual report for the period in which this item commences, as if those amendments had not been made.

43 Transitional rules

(1) The Minister may, by legislative instrument, make rules prescribing matters of a transitional nature (including prescribing any saving or application provisions) relating to the amendments or repeals made by this Schedule.

(2) However, to avoid doubt, the rules may not do the following:

(a) create an offence or civil penalty;

(b) provide powers of:

(i) arrest or detention; or

(ii) entry, search or seizure;

(c) impose a tax;

(d) set an amount to be appropriated from the Consolidated Revenue Fund under an appropriation in this Act;

(e) directly amend the text of this Act.

This Act is the *Counter‑Terrorism (Temporary Exclusion Orders) (Consequential Amendments) Act 2019*.

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Independent National Security Legislation Monitor Act 2010

**1 Section 4 (at the end of the definition of counter‑terrorism and national security legislation)**

Add:

; (g) Part 2 of the *Counter‑Terrorism (Temporary Exclusion Orders) Act 2019* and any other provision of that Act as far as it relates to that Part.

##### Intelligence Services Act 2001

**2 After paragraph 29(1)(cb)**

Insert:

and (cc) to review, by the end of the period of 3 years beginning on the day the *Counter‑Terrorism (Temporary Exclusion Orders) Act 2019* commenced, the operation, effectiveness and implications of that Act; and

(cd) to monitor and review the exercise of powers under the *Counter‑Terrorism (Temporary Exclusion Orders) Act 2019* by the Minister administering that Act; and

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Customs Act 1901

**1 Subsection 206(2A) (heading)**

Repeal the heading, substitute:

Prohibited psychoactive substances, tobacco products and prohibited serious drug alternatives

**2 Paragraph 206(2A)(b)**

After “prohibited psychoactive substance”, insert “, tobacco products”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976

**1 Part 4 of Schedule 1 (before the item relating to Bauhinia Downs)**

Insert:

AMMAROO

All that land in the Northern Territory comprising the whole of Northern Territory Portion 7364 delineated on Survey Plan S2014/016 lodged with the Surveyor‑General, Darwin, and containing an area of approximately 3,105 hectares.

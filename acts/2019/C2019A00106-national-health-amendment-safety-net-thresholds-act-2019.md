## Schedule 1—Safety net thresholds

##### National Health Act 1953

**1 Section 99F (definition of concessional beneficiary safety net)**

Omit “60”, substitute “48”.

**2 Section 99F (definition of general patient safety net)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***general patient safety net*** means the amount of $1,486.80.

**3 After subsection 99G(1)**

Insert:

(1A) Despite subsection (1), the general patient safety net is not to be indexed on 1 January 2020.

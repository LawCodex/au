**Vocational Education and Training Funding Laws Amendment Act 1996**

No. 73, 1996

An Act to amend certain laws relating to vocational education and training, and for related purposes

[Assented to 5 December 1996]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

**1 Short title**

This Act may be cited as the Vocational Education and Training Funding Laws Amendment Act 1996.

**2 Commencement**

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

**3 Schedule(s)**

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

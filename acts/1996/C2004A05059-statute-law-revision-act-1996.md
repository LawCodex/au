Contents

An Act to make various amendments of the statute law of the Commonwealth, to repeal certain Acts, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 25 October 1996*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Statute Law Revision Act 1996*.

###### 2 Commencement

(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), this Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

(2) Each item in Schedule 2 commences or is taken to have commenced (as the case requires) at the time specified in the note at the end of the item.

(3) Each item in Schedule 3 is taken to have commenced when the Act containing the provision amended by the item received the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

(1) Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

(2) The repeal of an Act by this section does not affect the operation of any amendment of another Act made by the repealed Act.

###### 4 Definition

In this Act:

***Corporations Law*** means the Corporations Law set out in section 82 of the *Corporations Act 1989.*

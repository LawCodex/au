### Part 2—Application and transitional provisions

5 Definitions

In this Part:

***commencement*** means the day this item commences.

***new law*** means the *Defence Home Ownership Assistance Scheme Act 2008*, as amended by Part 1 of this Schedule.

***old law*** means the *Defence Home Ownership Assistance Scheme Act 2008*, as in force immediately before the commencement of this item.

6 Application provision—application for subsidy certificate

Subject to item 7, sections 17 and 18 of the new law apply in relation to an application for a subsidy certificate that:

(a) is made on or after commencement; or

(b) was pending immediately before commencement.

7 Transitional provision—application for subsidy certificate

(1) This item applies if:

(a) before commencement, the Secretary refused to give a subsidy certificate to an applicant under subsection 16(3) of the old law only because the application for the subsidy certificate (the ***old application***) was not made within the 2‑year period mentioned in paragraph 17(2)(a) or subsection 17(3) of the old law; and

(b) on or after commencement, the applicant makes an application (the ***new application***) for a subsidy certificate under the new law.

(2) In deciding the new application, the Secretary must disregard the old application for the purpose of being satisfied of the matter mentioned in paragraph 17(1)(c) of the new law.

8 Application provision—accrued subsidy period

Subsection 47(1) of the new law applies in relation to the calculation of a person’s accrued subsidy period at a time that occurs on or after commencement if:

(a) on or after commencement, the person is given a subsidy certificate; or

(b) immediately before commencement, the person holds a subsidy certificate that is in force; or

(c) both of the following apply:

(i) subsidy becomes payable to the person under the old law before commencement;

(ii) immediately before commencement, subsidy had not stopped being payable to the person under the old law.

## Schedule 2—Australian Defence Force Superannuation

The Department must pay contributions to ADF Super for the benefit of certain ADF Super members. Contributions may also be made by employers of ADF Super members who are former long‑term ADF members, and by ADF Super members who are self‑employed former long‑term ADF members, in accordance with the Trust Deed.

**2 Section 4**

Insert:

***employer*** has the same meaning as in the SIS Act.

***former long‑term ADF member***: a person is a ***former long‑term ADF member*** if:

(a) for a continuous period of at least 12 months, the person was any of the following:

(i) a member of the Permanent Forces;

(ii) a continuous full‑time Reservist; and

(b) at a particular time (whether before, on or after the commencement of this definition), the person ceased to be any of the following:

(i) a member of the Permanent Forces;

(ii) a continuous full‑time Reservist; and

(d) at all times since that time, the person:

(i) has continued to be an ADF Super member; and

(ii) has not been either a member of the Permanent Forces or a continuous full‑time Reservist.

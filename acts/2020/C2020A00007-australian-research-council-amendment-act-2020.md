###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *Australian Research Council Amendment Act 2020*.

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Research Council Act 2001

**1 At the end of subsection 48(2)**

Add:

; (s) the financial year starting on 1 July 2022.

**2 Paragraphs 49(t) to (v)**

Omit “$771,932,000”, substitute “$786,212,000”.

**3 At the end of section 49**

Add:

; and (w) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2022—$786,212,000.

[*Assented to 12 November 2015*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Social Services Legislation Amendment (Cost of Living Concession) Act 2015*.

###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Social Security Act 1991

**1 After paragraph 8(8)(zab)**

Insert:

(zac) a payment, known as the Cost of Living Concession, made by the Government of South Australia;

##### Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986

**2 After paragraph 5H(8)(zeb)**

Insert:

(zec) a payment, known as the Cost of Living Concession, made by the Government of South Australia;

**3 Application provision**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply in relation to payments made before, on or after the commencement of this item.

No. 72, 2015

An Act to amend the law relating to taxation, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 25 June 2015*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Labor 2013‑14 Budget Savings (Measures No. 1) Act 2015*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day after this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Clean Energy (Income Tax Rates Amendments) Act 2011

**1 Subsection 2(1) (table item 3)**

Repeal the item.

**2 Part 2 of Schedule 1**

Repeal the Part.

## Schedule 2—Clean Energy (Tax Laws Amendments) Act 2011

**1 Subsection 2(1) (table item 3)**

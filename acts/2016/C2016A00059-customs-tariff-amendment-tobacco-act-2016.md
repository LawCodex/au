## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Subsection 19AB(5)**

Omit “or 1 September 2016”, substitute “, 1 September 2016, 1 September 2017, 1 September 2018, 1 September 2019 or 1 September 2020”.

**2 Paragraph 19AB(6)(a)**

Omit “or 1 September 2016”, substitute “, 1 September 2016, 1 September 2017, 1 September 2018, 1 September 2019 or 1 September 2020”.

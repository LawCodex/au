## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Passenger Movement Charge Act 1978

**1 Section 6**

Omit “$55”, substitute “$60”.

**2 Application of amendments**

The amendment made by item 1 applies in relation to the departure of a person from Australia on or after the commencement of this Act, unless:

(a) the person departs using a ticket or equivalent authority; and

(b) the ticket or authority was sold or issued before that commencement.

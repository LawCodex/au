###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Cessation of visa labels

### Part 1—Repeal of Act

##### Migration (Visa Evidence) Charge Act 2012

**1 The whole of the Act**

Repeal the Act.

### Part 2—Amendments

##### Migration Act 1958

**2 Subsection 5(1)**

Repeal the following definitions:

(a) definition of ***visa evidence charge***;

(b) definition of ***visa evidence charge limit***.

**3 Subsection 33(10)**

Omit “AE,”.

**4 Subsections 34(3) and 35(4)**

Omit “, AE”.

**5 Subdivision AE of Division 3 of Part 2**

Repeal the Subdivision.

**6 Subsection 78(3)**

Omit “, AE”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Passenger Movement Charge Act 1978

**1 Section 3 (definition of Australia)**

Repeal the definition.

**2 Paragraph 4(3)(a)**

After “departs from”, insert “Norfolk Island or”.

**3 Paragraph 4(3)(b)**

Omit “Indian Ocean”.

**4 Paragraph 4(4)(a)**

After “other than”, insert “Norfolk Island or”.

**5 Paragraph 4(4)(b)**

After “Joint Petroleum Development Area, for”, insert “Norfolk Island or”.

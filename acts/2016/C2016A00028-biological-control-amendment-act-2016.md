## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Biological Control Act 1984

**1 Subsection 2(1) (definition of kind)**

After “organisms”, insert “, viruses or sub‑viral agents”.

**2 Subsection 2(1) (at the end of the definition of organism)**

Add:

; or (c) a virus or a sub‑viral agent.

**3 Subsection 2(1) (definition of prescribed live organisms)**

Repeal the definition.

**4 Subsection 2(1)**

Insert:

***prescribed organisms*** means organisms that are:

(a) live organisms; or

(b) viruses or sub‑viral agents;

but does not include live vaccines or resistant cultivars.

**5 Section 3**

Omit “live”, substitute “prescribed”.

**6 Subsections 21(1), 22(1) and 26(1)**

Omit “live”.

**7 Paragraphs 30(1)(b), 31(1)(a) and 34(2)(a)**

Omit “live”.

**8 Subsection 37(3)**

Omit “live”.

**9 Declarations of relevant State laws**

If:

(a) a declaration was made under section 9 of the *Biological Control Act 1984* before the commencement of this item; and

(b) the declaration was in effect immediately before that commencement;

the declaration continues in effect (and may be dealt with), after that commencement, despite the amendments made by this Schedule.

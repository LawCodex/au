## Schedule 1—Main amendments

### Part 1—Amendments commencing day after Royal Assent

##### Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991

**1 Subsection 4(1) (definition of appropriate government agency)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***appropriate government agency*** means:

(a) the relevant Department of State of the Commonwealth, a State, a Territory or New Zealand administered by a Minister who is a member of the Forum on Food Regulation; or

(b) any other body that has an officer on the Food Regulation Standing Committee; or

(c) any other body or officer of the Commonwealth, a State, a Territory or New Zealand that the Authority considers has a particular interest in the relevant matter.

**2 Subsection 4(1) (definition of Council)**

Repeal the definition.

**3 Subsection 4(1)**

Insert:

***Forum on Food Regulation*** means the Australia and New Zealand Food Regulation Ministerial Council established by the Food Regulation Agreement.

**4 Subsection 4(1)**

Repeal the following definitions:

(a) definition of ***Gene Technology Regulator***;

(b) definition of ***GMO***;

(c) definition of ***GM product***;

(d) definition of ***New Zealand lead Minister on the Council***.

**5 Subsection 4(1)**

Insert:

***New Zealand lead Minister on the Forum on Food Regulation*** means the Minister of the government of New Zealand who is:

(a) a member of the Forum on Food Regulation; and

(b) nominated by the government of New Zealand to be the New Zealand lead Minister on the Forum on Food Regulation.

**134 Application**

(1) The amendment made by item 78 of this Schedule applies to a notice given to the Authority on or after the day this item commences, even if the draft standard or draft variation to which the notice relates was prepared before that day.

(2) The amendment made by item 106 of this Schedule applies to a notice given to the Authority on or after the day this item commences, even if the standard or variation to which the notice relates was assessed before that day.

(3) The amendment made by item 127 of this Schedule applies to an appointment made on or after the day this item commences, even if the period of 2 years mentioned in subsection 128(3) of the *Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991* began before that day.

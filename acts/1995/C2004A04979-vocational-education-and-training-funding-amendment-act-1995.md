**Vocational Education and Training Funding Amendment Act 1995**

**No. 129 of 1995**

An Act to amend the Vocational Education and Training Funding Act 1992

[Assented to 14 November 1995]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

Short title

**1.** This Act may be cited as the *Vocational Education and Training Funding Amendment Act 1995*.

Commencement

**2.** This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

Amendments

**3.** The Vocational Education and Training Funding Act 1992 is amended as set out in the Schedule.

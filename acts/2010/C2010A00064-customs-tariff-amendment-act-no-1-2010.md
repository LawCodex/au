Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Schedule 4 (item 53C, the description of goods in column 2)**

After “1 January 2005”, insert “and on or before 31 December 2009,”.

**2 Schedule 4 (item 61, at the end of the description of goods in column 2)**

Add “, and entered for home consumption on or before 30 June 2010”.

**3 Schedule 4 (item 73, the description of goods in column 2)**

Omit “30 June 2017”, substitute “30 June 2011”.

# Excise Tariff Amendment (Tobacco) Act 2010

No. 76, 2010

An Act to amend the *Excise Tariff Act 1921*

Contents

[image]

Excise Tariff Amendment (Tobacco) Act 2010

No. 76, 2010

An Act to amend the *Excise Tariff Act 1921*

[*Assented to 28 June 2010*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Excise Tariff Amendment (Tobacco) Act 2010*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act is taken to have commenced on 30 April 2010.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Excise Tariff Act 1921

**1 Schedule (cell at table subitem 5.1, column headed “Rate of Duty”)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

$0.32775 per stick

**2 Schedule (cell at table subitem 5.5, column headed “Rate of Duty”)**

Repeal the cell, substitute:

$409.71 per kilogram of tobacco content

An Act to amend the *Interstate Road Transport Charge Act 1985*, and for related purposes

Interstate Road Transport Charge Amendment Act 2010

No. 57, 2010

An Act to amend the *Interstate Road Transport Charge Act 1985*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 23 June 2010*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Interstate Road Transport Charge Amendment Act 2010*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Interstate Road Transport Charge Act 1985

**1 Subsection 5(6)**

Repeal the subsection.

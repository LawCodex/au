Migration Legislation Amendment (Migration Advice Industry) Act 2003

No. 3, 2003

An Act to amend the *Migration Act 1958*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 24 February 2003*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Migration Legislation Amendment (Migration Advice Industry) Act 2003*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Migration Act 1958

**1 Division 7 of Part 3**

Repeal the Division.

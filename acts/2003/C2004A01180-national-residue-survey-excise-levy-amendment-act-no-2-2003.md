An Act to amend the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998*, and for related purposes

No. 95, 2003

An Act to amend the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 14 October 2003*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Amendment Act (No. 2) 2003*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998

**1 Subclause 4(1) of Schedule 7**

Omit “0.25 cents per kilogram”, substitute “0.3 cents per kilogram”.

**2 Subclause 4(1) of Schedule 7**

Omit “(not exceeding 0.3 cents per kilogram)”, substitute “(not exceeding 0.6 cents per kilogram)”.

**3 Application of amendments**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply to honey sold after the commencement of this item.

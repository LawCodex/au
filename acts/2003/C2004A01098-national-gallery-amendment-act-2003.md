# National Gallery Amendment Act 2003

No. 27, 2003

An Act to amend the *National Gallery Act 1975*, and for related purposes

Contents

[image]

National Gallery Amendment Act 2003

No. 27, 2003

An Act to amend the *National Gallery Act 1975*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 11 April 2003*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *National Gallery Amendment Act 2003*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day after it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—National Gallery Act 1975

**1 Subsection 9(4)**

Omit “gift or destruction unless the council”, substitute “destruction unless the Council”.

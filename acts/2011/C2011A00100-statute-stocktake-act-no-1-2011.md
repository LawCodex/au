# Statute Stocktake Act (No. 1) 2011

No. 100, 2011

An Act to make various amendments of the statute law of the Commonwealth, to repeal certain Acts and provisions of Acts, and for related purposes

Contents

[image]

Statute Stocktake Act (No. 1) 2011

No. 100, 2011

An Act to make various amendments of the statute law of the Commonwealth, to repeal certain Acts and provisions of Acts, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 15 September 2011*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Statute Stocktake Act (No. 1) 2011*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day after this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

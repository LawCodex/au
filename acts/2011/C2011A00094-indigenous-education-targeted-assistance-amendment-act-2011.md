Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Amendment Act 2011

No. 94, 2011

An Act to amend the law relating to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander education, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 8 September 2011*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Amendment Act 2011*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

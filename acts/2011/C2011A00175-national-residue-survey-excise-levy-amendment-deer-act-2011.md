An Act to amend the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998*, and for related purposes

An Act to amend the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 5 December 2011*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Amendment (Deer) Act 2011*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day after this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### National Residue Survey (Excise) Levy Act 1998

**1 Paragraphs 3(1)(a) and (b) of Schedule 11**

Omit “4 cents”, substitute “10.5 cents”.

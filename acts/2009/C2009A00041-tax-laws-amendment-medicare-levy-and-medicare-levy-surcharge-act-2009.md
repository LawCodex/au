Tax Laws Amendment (Medicare Levy and Medicare Levy Surcharge) Act 2009

No. 41, 2009

An Act to amend the law relating to taxation, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 23 June 2009*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Tax Laws Amendment (Medicare Levy and Medicare Levy Surcharge) Act 2009*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Medicare levy and Medicare levy surcharge income thresholds

##### A New Tax System (Medicare Levy Surcharge—Fringe Benefits) Act 1999

**1 Paragraphs 15(1)(c) and 16(2)(c)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

##### Medicare Levy Act 1986

**2 Subsection 3(1) (paragraph (b) of the definition of phase‑in limit)**

Omit “$26,967”, substitute “$29,763”.

**3 Subsection 3(1) (paragraph (c) of the definition of phase‑in limit)**

Omit “$20,363”, substitute “$20,934”.

**4 Subsection 3(1) (paragraph (b) of the definition of threshold amount)**

Omit “$22,922”, substitute “$25,299”.

**5 Subsection 3(1) (paragraph (c) of the definition of threshold amount)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

**6 Subsection 8(5) (definition of family income threshold)**

Omit “$29,207”, substitute “$30,025”.

**7 Subsection 8(5) (definition of family income threshold)**

Omit “$2,682”, substitute “$2,757”.

**8 Subsections 8(6) and (7)**

Omit “$29,207”, substitute “$30,025”.

**9 Paragraph 8D(3)(c)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

**10 Subparagraph 8D(4)(a)(ii)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

**11 Paragraph 8G(2)(c)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

**12 Subparagraph 8G(3)(a)(ii)**

Omit “$17,309”, substitute “$17,794”.

**13 Application of amendments**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply to assessments for the 2008‑09 year of income and later years of income.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Social Security (Administration) Act 1999

**1 Subsection 202(1)**

Omit “or the Family Homelessness Prevention and Early Intervention Pilot”, substitute “, the Family Homelessness Prevention and Early Intervention Pilot or the Digital Switch‑over Household Assistance Program”.

**2 After paragraph 202(2)(da)**

Insert:

(db) for the purposes of the Digital Switch‑over Household Assistance Program; or

**3 Subclause 1(1) of Schedule 1**

Insert:

***Digital Switch‑over Household Assistance Program*** means the program that:

(a) is administered by the Commonwealth; and

(b) involves the provision and/or installation of any or all of the following:

(i) set‑top boxes;

(ii) cables;

(iii) antennas;

for the purposes of enabling the members of certain households to view television programs transmitted in digital mode (within the meaning of Schedule 4 to the *Broadcasting Services Act 1992*).

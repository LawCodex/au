###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Incorporating Customs Tariff Proposal (No. 1) 2009

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Schedule 4 (after item 41G)**

Insert:

| 41H | Goods, as prescribed by by‑law, that are imported into Australia, and entered for home consumption, on or after 1 January 2009, being goods that are for use in the testing, quality control, manufacturing evaluation or engineering development of:<br>(a) motor vehicles designed or engineered, or in the process of being designed or engineered, in Australia by motor vehicle producers registered under the Automotive Competitiveness and Investment Scheme, set out in the *ACIS Administration Act 1999*; or<br>(b) components for inclusion in such motor vehicles | Free |

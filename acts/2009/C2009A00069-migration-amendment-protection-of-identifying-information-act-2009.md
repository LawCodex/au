## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Migration Act 1958

**1 Section 336A (definition of disclose)**

Omit “provided under section 40, 46, 166, 170, 175, 188, 192 or 261AA”, substitute “referred to in paragraph (a) of the definition of ***identifying information*** in this section”.

**2 Section 336A (paragraph (a) of the definition of identifying information)**

Omit “provided under section 40, 46, 166, 170, 175, 188, 192 or 261AA”, substitute “obtained by the Department for one or more of the purposes referred to in subsection 5A(3)”.

**3 At the end of subsection 336FA(1)**

Add:

; and (f) the information is only disclosed to the extent necessary in order to obtain that help.

**4 Paragraph 336FB(1)(c)**

Omit “and (e)”, substitute “, (e) and (f)”.

**5 Subsection 336FB(3)**

Omit “and (e)”, substitute “, (e) and (f)”.

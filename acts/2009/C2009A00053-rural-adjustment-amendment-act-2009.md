# Rural Adjustment Amendment Act 2009

No. 53, 2009

An Act to amend the *Rural Adjustment Act 1992*, and for related purposes

Contents

[image]

Rural Adjustment Amendment Act 2009

No. 53, 2009

An Act to amend the *Rural Adjustment Act 1992*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 25 June 2009*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Rural Adjustment Amendment Act 2009*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment

##### Rural Adjustment Act 1992

**1 Subsection 7(2)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(2) A person may be appointed as a member for a maximum of 3 periods.

**Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Amendment Act 2001**

**No. 154, 2001**

An Act to amend the *Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Act 2000*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 1 October 2001*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Amendment Act 2001*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment of the Indigenous Education (Targeted Assistance) Act 2000

**1 Subsection 14(2)**

Omit “$126,066,000”, substitute “$128,605,000”.

**2 Subsection 14(3)**

Omit “$128,438,000”, substitute “$132,942,000”.

**3 Subsection 14(4)**

Omit “$129,588,000”, substitute “$134,005,000”.

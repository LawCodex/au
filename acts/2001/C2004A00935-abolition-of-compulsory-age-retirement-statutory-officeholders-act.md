**Abolition of Compulsory Age Retirement (Statutory Officeholders) Act 2001**

**No. 159, 2001**

An Act to abolish compulsory age retirement for certain statutory officeholders, and for other purposes

[*Assented to 1 October 2001*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Abolition of Compulsory Age Retirement (Statutory Officeholders) Act 2001*.

###### 2 Commencement

(1) Subject to this section, this Act commences on the 28th day after the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

(2) If section 4 of the *Administrative Review Tribunal (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2001* has commenced before the 28th day after the day on which this Act receives the Royal Assent, items 6, 7 and 8 of Schedule 1 to this Act do not commence at all.

(3) If section 59 of the *Snowy Hydro Corporatisation Act 1997* has commenced before the 28th day after the day on which this Act receives the Royal Assent, item 86 of Schedule 1 to this Act does not commence at all.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Subject to section 2, each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

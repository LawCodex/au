# Customs Tariff Amendment Act (No. 5) 2001

Act No. 120 of 2001 as amended

This compilation was prepared on 26 March 2007

**[This Act was amended by Act No. 38 of 2002; No. 8 of 2007]**

**Amendments from Act No. 38 of 2002**

[Schedule 3 (item 1) amended item 27 of Schedule 1\
Schedule 3 (item 2) amended item 290 of Schedule 1

Schedule 3 (items 1 and 2) commenced immediately after 1 January 2002]

**Amendment from Act No. 8 of 2007**

[Schedule 2 (item 6) repealed and substituted item 135 of Schedule 1

Schedule 2 (item 6) commenced immediately before 1 January 2002]

Prepared by the Office of Legislative Drafting and Publishing,\
Attorney‑General’s Department, Canberra

Contents

An Act to amend the *Customs Tariff Act 1995*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 24 September 2001*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment Act (No. 5) 2001*.

###### 2 Commencement

(1) Subject to subsection (2), this Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

(2) Schedule 1 to this Act commences on 1 January 2002.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Subject to section 2, each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other items in a schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

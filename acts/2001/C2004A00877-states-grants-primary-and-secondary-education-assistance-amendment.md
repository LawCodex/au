**States Grants (Primary and Secondary Education Assistance) Amendment Act 2001**

**No. 110, 2001**

An Act to amend the *States Grants (Primary and Secondary Education Assistance) Act 2000*

[*Assented to 17 September 2001*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *States Grants (Primary and Secondary Education Assistance) Amendment Act 2001*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

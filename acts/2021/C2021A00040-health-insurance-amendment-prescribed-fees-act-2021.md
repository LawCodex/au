## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Health Insurance Act 1973

**1 Subsection 3DB(3)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

(3) An application under subsection (1) or (2) must be in writing.

**2 Subsection 3E(2)**

Omit “and on payment of the prescribed fee”.

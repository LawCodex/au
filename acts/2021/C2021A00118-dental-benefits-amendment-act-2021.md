## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Dental Benefits Act 2008

**1 Section 22 (paragraph beginning “A person qualifies”)**

Omit “he or she is aged between 2 and 18 years”, substitute “the person is aged under 18 years”.

**2 Subsection 23(1)**

Repeal the subsection, substitute:

Persons to whom this section applies

(1) This section applies to a person, in relation to a calendar year, if the person is aged under 18 years on 1 January in the calendar year.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Age Discrimination Act 2004

**1 Schedule 2 (table item 9A, column headed “Provision(s)”)**

Omit “sections 22‑15 and 22‑25”, substitute “sections 22‑5, 22‑15, 22‑25 and 22‑30”.

##### Private Health Insurance Act 2007

**2 Subsection 22‑5(2) (heading)**

Omit “*Dependent child‑only*”, substitute “*Dependent person‑only*”.

**3 Subsection 22‑5(2)**

Omit “\*dependent children”, substitute “\*dependent persons”.

**4 Subsection 22‑5(3)**

Omit “\*dependent children”, substitute “\*dependent persons”.

**5 Paragraph 22‑5(4)(c)**

Omit “\*dependent child”, substitute “\*dependent person”.

**6 Subsection 22‑15(5) (note 2)**

Omit “dependent children”, substitute “dependent persons”.

**7 Paragraph 22‑25(1)(b)**

Omit “\*dependent child”, substitute “\*dependent person”.

**8 Subsection 22‑25(2)**

Omit “\*dependent child”, substitute “\*dependent person”.

**9 Paragraph 22‑30(1)(b)**

Omit “\*dependent child”, substitute “\*dependent person”.

**10 Subsection 63‑5(4)**

Omit “\*dependent child non‑student”, substitute “\*dependent non‑student or \*dependent person with a disability”.

**11 Subsection 63‑5(4)**

After “\*dependent children”, insert “or \*dependent students”.

**12 Subsection 63‑5(4)**

Omit “dependent child non‑student” (last occurring), substitute “dependent non‑student or dependent person with a disability”.

**13 Subsection 63‑5(5)**

Repeal the subsection.

**14 Paragraph 333‑20(1)(a)**

Omit “or section”, substitute “, section or Schedule”.

**15 Subsection 333‑20(1) (table, heading to column headed “Chapter/Part/section”)**

Repeal the heading, substitute:

Chapter/Part/section/Schedule

**16 Subsection 333‑20(1) (table item 3, column headed “Chapter/Part/section”)**

Omit “and section 188‑1”, substitute “, section 188‑1 and Schedule 1”.

**17 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (definition of adult)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***adult***:

(a) when used outside Part 2‑3—means a person who is not a \*dependent person; or

(b) when used in Part 2‑3—means a person who is not:

(i) a \*dependent child; or

(ii) a \*dependent non‑student; or

(iii) a \*dependent student.

**18 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (definition of dependent child)**

Repeal the definition, substitute:

***dependent child*** means a person who:

(a) is aged under 18; and

(b) does not have a partner.

**19 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (definition of dependent child non‑student)**

Repeal the definition.

**20 Clause 1 of Schedule 1**

Insert:

***dependent non‑student*** means a person who:

(a) is aged between 18 and 31 (inclusive); and

(b) is not receiving full‑time education at a school, college or university; and

(c) is a dependent non‑student under the \*rules of the private health insurer that insures the person; and

(d) does not have a partner.

***dependent person*** means:

(a) a \*dependent child; or

(b) a \*dependent non‑student; or

(c) a \*dependent person with a disability; or

(d) a \*dependent student.

***dependent person with a disability*** means a person:

(a) who is aged 18 or over; and

(b) who is:

(i) a person with a disability within the meaning of the expression ***person with a disability*** as defined by the Private Health Insurance (Complying Product) Rules; or

(ii) a person with a disability within the meaning of the expression ***person with a disability*** as defined by the \*rules of the private health insurer that insures the person.

To avoid doubt, a ***dependent person with a disability*** may have a partner.

***dependent student*** means a person who:

(a) is aged between 18 and 31 (inclusive); and

(b) is receiving full‑time education at a school, college or university; and

(c) is a dependent student under the \*rules of the private health insurer that insures the person; and

(d) does not have a partner.

**21 Clause 1 of Schedule 1 (definition of holder)**

Omit “\*dependent child”, substitute “\*dependent person”.

22 Application

The amendment of paragraph 22‑30(1)(b) of the *Private Health Insurance Act 2007* made by this Schedule applies in relation to the following days:

(a) 1 April 2021;

(b) each later day.

##### Private Health Insurance (Prudential Supervision) Act 2015

**23 Subsection 4(1) (definition of dependent child)**

Repeal the definition.

**24 Subsection 4(1)**

Insert:

***dependent person*** has the same meaning as in the *Private Health Insurance Act 2007*.

**25 Subsection 15(4)**

Omit “dependent children”, substitute “dependent persons”.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

### Part 1—Main amendments

##### Industry Research and Development Act 1986

**1 Subsection 4(1) (definition of Board)**

Omit “Innovation and Science Australia”, substitute “Industry Innovation and Science Australia”.

**2 Section 6**

Repeal the section, substitute:

###### 6 Establishment of Industry Innovation and Science Australia

The body known immediately before the commencement of the *Industry Research and Development Amendment (Industry Innovation and Science Australia) Act 2021* as Innovation and Science Australia is continued in existence with the new name Industry Innovation and Science Australia.

Note: Subsection 25B(1) of the *Acts Interpretation Act 1901* provides that a body whose name is altered by an Act continues in existence under the new name so that its identity is not affected.

### Part 2—Consequential amendments

##### Income Tax Assessment Act 1997

**3 The whole of the Act**

The *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* is amended as follows:

(a) by omitting “\*Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(b) by omitting “Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(c) by omitting “**Innovation and Science Australia**” (wherever occurring) and substituting “**Industry Innovation and Science Australia**”;

(d) by omitting “*Innovation and Science Australia*” (wherever occurring) and substituting “*Industry Innovation and Science Australia*”;

(e) by omitting “***Innovation and Science Australia***” (wherever occurring) and substituting “***Industry Innovation and Science Australia***”.

##### Pooled Development Funds Act 1992

**4 Subsection 4(1) (definition of Board)**

Omit “Innovation and Science Australia”, substitute “Industry Innovation and Science Australia”.

##### Taxation Administration Act 1953

**5 The whole of the Act**

The *Taxation Administration Act 1953* is amended as follows:

(a) by omitting “\*Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(b) by omitting “\*Innovation and Science Australia’s” (wherever occurring) and substituting “\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia’s”;

(c) by omitting “Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(d) by omitting “**\*Innovation and Science Australia**” (wherever occurring) and substituting “**\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia**”;

(e) by omitting “**Innovation and Science Australia**” (wherever occurring) and substituting “**Industry Innovation and Science Australia**”.

##### Venture Capital Act 2002

**6 The whole of the Act**

The *Venture Capital Act 2002* is amended as follows:

(a) by omitting “\*Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(b) by omitting “\*Innovation and Science Australia’s” (wherever occurring) and substituting “\*Industry Innovation and Science Australia’s”;

(c) by omitting “Innovation and Science Australia” (wherever occurring) and substituting “Industry Innovation and Science Australia”;

(d) by omitting “Innovation and Science Australia’s” (wherever occurring) and substituting “Industry Innovation and Science Australia’s”;

(e) by omitting “**Innovation and Science Australia**” (wherever occurring) and substituting “**Industry Innovation and Science Australia**”;

(f) by omitting “***Innovation and Science Australia***” (wherever occurring) and substituting “***Industry Innovation and Science Australia***”.

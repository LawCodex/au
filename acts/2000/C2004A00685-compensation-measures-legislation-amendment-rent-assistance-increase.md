**Compensation Measures Legislation Amendment (Rent Assistance Increase) Act 2000**

**No. 93, 2000**

An Act to amend the *A New Tax System (Compensation Measures Legislation Amendment) Act 1999*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 30 June 2000*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Compensation Measures Legislation Amendment (Rent Assistance Increase) Act 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences, or is taken to have commenced, immediately before the commencement of Schedule 1 to the *A New Tax System (Compensation Measures Legislation Amendment) Act 1999*.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### A New Tax System (Compensation Measures Legislation Amendment) Act 1999

**1 Item 135 of Schedule 1**

Omit “7%”, substitute “10%”.

**2 Item 136 of Schedule 1**

Omit “7%”, substitute “10%”.

Note: The reference to 7% is inserted in paragraph 1206GAA(2)(a) of the *Social Security Act 1991* by item 136.

**3 Item 6 of Schedule 2**

Omit “7%”, substitute “10%”.

**4 Item 42 of Schedule 2**

Omit “7%”, substitute “10%”.

Note: The reference to 7% is inserted in paragraph 198GA(2)(a) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986* by item 42.

**5 Item 1 of Schedule 4**

Omit “**7%**”, substitute “**10%**”.

No. 118, 2000

Contents

[image: Commonwealth Coat of Arms of Australia]

**Retirement Assistance for Farmers Scheme Extension Act 2000**

**No. 118, 2000**

An Act to amend the *Social Security Act 1991* and the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*

[*Assented to 7 September 2000*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Retirement Assistance for Farmers Scheme Extension Act 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

**Customs Tariff Amendment Act (No. 2) 2000**

**No. 55, 2000**

An Act to amend the *Customs Tariff Act 1995*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 30 May 2000*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment Act (No. 2) 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

(1) Sections 1, 2 and 3 commence on the day on which this Act receives the Royal Assent.

(2) Schedule 1 is taken to have commenced on 22 July 1999.

(3) Schedule 2 is taken to have commenced on 3 September 1999.

(4) Schedule 3 is taken to have commenced on 1 October 1999.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Subject to section 2, each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendment of the Customs Tariff Act 1995 having effect from 22 July 1999

**1 After note 2 to Chapter 57 of Schedule 3**

Insert:

###### Additional Note.

1.‑ For the purposes of this Chapter, the expression “floor coverings” includes all mats, rugs, and similar goods.

**Customs Tariff Amendment (Product Stewardship for Waste Oil) Act 2000**

**No. 103, 2000**

An Act to amend the *Customs Tariff Act 1995,* and for related purposes

[*Assented to 6 July 2000*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment (Product Stewardship for Waste Oil) Act 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

(1) Subject to subsection (2), this Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

(2) Schedule 1 commences, or is taken to have commenced, on the commencement of section 9 of the *Product Stewardship (Oil) Act 2000*.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Subject to section 2, each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Customs Tariff Act 1995

**1 Subsection 19(1)**

Omit all the words after “Table,”, substitute “the rates of duty set out in the rate column of the subheading in Schedule 3, or the item in Schedule 4, in column 1 opposite that excise item are increased by the same amount on and from that day.”.

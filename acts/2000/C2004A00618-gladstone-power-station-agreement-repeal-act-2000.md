**Gladstone Power Station Agreement (Repeal) Act 2000**

**No. 26, 2000**

An Act to repeal the *Gladstone Power Station Agreement Act 1970*

[*Assented to 3 April 2000*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Gladstone Power Station Agreement (Repeal) Act 2000*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Gladstone Power Station Agreement Act 1970

**1 The whole of the Act**

Repeal the Act.

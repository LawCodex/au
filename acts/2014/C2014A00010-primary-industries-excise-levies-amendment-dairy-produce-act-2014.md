Primary Industries (Excise) Levies Amendment (Dairy Produce) Act 2014

No. 10, 2014

An Act to amend the *Primary Industries (Excise) Levies Act 1999*, and for related purposes

[*Assented to 18 March 2014*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Primary Industries (Excise) Levies Amendment (Dairy Produce) Act 2014*.

###### 2 Commencement

This Act commences on the day this Act receives the Royal Assent.

###### 3 Schedule(s)

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Primary Industries (Excise) Levies Act 1999

**1 Clause 11 of Schedule 6 (table item 6)**

Omit “0.058”, substitute “0.145”.

**2 Clause 12 of Schedule 6 (table item 6)**

Omit “0.13850”, substitute “0.34625”.

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment (Carbon Tax Repeal) Act 2014*.

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

### Part 4—Application of amendments

**38 Application**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply in relation to:

(a) goods imported into Australia at or after the commencement of this item; and

(b) goods imported into Australia before that commencement, where the time for working out the rate of import duty on the goods has not occurred before that commencement.

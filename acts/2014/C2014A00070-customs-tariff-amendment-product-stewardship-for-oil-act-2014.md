[*Assented to 30 June 2014*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Customs Tariff Amendment (Product Stewardship for Oil) Act 2014*.

(1) Each provision of this Act specified in column 1 of the table commences, or is taken to have commenced, in accordance with column 2 of the table. Any other statement in column 2 has effect according to its terms.

Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

**104 Application of amendments**

The amendments made by this Schedule apply in relation to:

(a) goods imported into Australia on or after the day that this Schedule commences; and

(b) goods imported into Australia before the day this Schedule commences, where the time for working out the rate of import duty on the goods had not occurred before that day.

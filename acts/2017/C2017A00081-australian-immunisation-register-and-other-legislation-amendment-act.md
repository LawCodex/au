###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Australian Immunisation Register amendments

##### Australian Immunisation Register Act 2015

**1 Paragraph 9(b)**

Omit “about each of those vaccinations”, substitute “provided by a recognised vaccination provider about each relevant vaccination”.

**2 Paragraph 9(c)**

After “general practitioner”, insert “, a paediatrician, a public health physician, an infectious diseases physician or a clinical immunologist”.

**3 Subparagraph 9(d)(iii)**

After “general practitioner”, insert “, paediatrician, public health physician, infectious diseases physician or clinical immunologist”.

**4 Application provisions**

(1) The amendment made by item 1 applies in relation to relevant vaccinations that take place on or after the commencement of that item.

(2) The amendments made by items 2 and 3 apply in relation to assessments made on or after the commencement of those items.

## Schedule 2—Family assistance amendments

##### A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999

**1 Paragraphs 6(3)(a) and (b)**

After “general practitioner”, insert “, a paediatrician, a public health physician, an infectious diseases physician or a clinical immunologist”.

**2 Application provision**

The amendment made by this Schedule applies in relation to certifications given on or after the commencement of this item.

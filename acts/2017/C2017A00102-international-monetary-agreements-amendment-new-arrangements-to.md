## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### International Monetary Agreements Act 1947

**1 Section 3 (paragraph (c) of the definition of New Arrangements to Borrow)**

Omit “2011.”, substitute “2011;”.

(d) Decision No. 16079‑(16/99), dated 4 November 2016.

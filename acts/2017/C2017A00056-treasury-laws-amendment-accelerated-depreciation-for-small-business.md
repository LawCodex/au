Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

**3 Paragraph 328‑180(3)(a) (note)**

**5 Subsection 328‑250(1) (note)**

**6 Subsection 328‑250(4) (note)**

**7 Subsection 328‑253(4) (note)**

###### 328‑180 Increased access to accelerated depreciation from 12 May 2015 to 30 June 2018

**10 Subparagraphs 328‑180(4)(b)(i) and (ii)**

Omit “on or before 30 June 2017”, substitute “on or before 30 June 2018”.

**11 Paragraph 328‑180(5)(b)**

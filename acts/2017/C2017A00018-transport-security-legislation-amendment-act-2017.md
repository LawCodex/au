## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Aviation Transport Security Act 2004

**1 Paragraphs 35(2)(f), 36(2)(f) and 36A(2)(f)**

After “for entry to”, insert “, or that are in,”.

**2 Subsection 41(1)**

Omit all the words after “made under”, substitute:

section 44:

(a) in preparation for boarding an aircraft; or

(b) in order to enter or remain in an area or zone within a security controlled airport.

**3 Subparagraph 41A(1)(b)(ii)**

After “enter”, insert “or remain in”.

**4 Subsection 42(1)**

Omit all the words after “made under”, substitute:

section 44:

(a) in preparation for being taken on board an aircraft; or

(b) in order to be taken into or remain in an area or zone within a security controlled airport.

**5 Subsection 43(1)**

Omit all the words after “made under”, substitute:

section 44:

(a) in preparation for being taken on board an aircraft; or

(b) in order to enter or remain in an area or zone within a security controlled airport.

**6 Subsection 44(3)**

After “(2)(aa)”, insert “, (2)(i)”.

**7 Subsection 127(2)**

Omit “who holds, or is acting in, an Executive Level 2, or equivalent, position”.

##### Maritime Transport and Offshore Facilities Security Act 2003

**8 Subsection 202(2)**

Omit “who holds, or is acting in, an Executive Level 2, or equivalent, position”.

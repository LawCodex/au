## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Disability Services Act 1986

**1 Section 17**

Insert:

***pension age*** has the meaning given by subsection 23(5A), (5B), (5C) or (5D) of the *Social Security Act 1991*.

**2 Paragraph 18(a)**

Omit “65 years of age”, substitute “pension age”.

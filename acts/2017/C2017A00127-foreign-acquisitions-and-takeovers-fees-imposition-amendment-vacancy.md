## Schedule 1—Amendments

#### Division 1—Imposition of fees as taxes

**4 Before section 6**

Insert:

#### Division 2—Fees under Part 6 of the Foreign Acquisitions Act

**5 Subsection 9(1)**

**6 Section 11 (heading)**

Repeal the heading, substitute:

###### 11 Regulations may lower fees specified in this Division

**7 Subsections 11(1) and (2)**

Omit “Part”, substitute “Division”.

**8 Subsection 12(1)**

Omit “Part” (wherever occurring), substitute “Division”.

**9 Subsection 12(8) (definition of base amount)**

**10 Subsection 12(8) (paragraphs (a), (b) and (d) of the definition of base financial year)**

**11 At the end of Part 2**

Add:

#### Division 3—Fees under Part 6A of the Foreign Acquisitions Act

###### 12A Vacancy fees

The amount of the vacancy fee a foreign person is liable to pay in relation to a dwelling on residential land is worked out in accordance with the following table.

Note 1: Part 6A of the Foreign Acquisitions Act sets out the liability of a foreign person for a vacancy fee.

Note 2: An order mentioned in item 6 of the table could specify that a foreign person may acquire interests in Australian land only to an extent specified in that order: see item 3 of the table in subsection 67(3) of the Foreign Acquisitions Act.

Note 3: Generally, regulations may lower the amount of a fee specified in Division 2 of this Part: see section 11. Fees specified in that Division are subject to indexation: see section 12.

Note 4: A residential land (near‑new dwelling interests) certificate is an exemption certificate under section 43A of the Foreign Acquisitions Regulation.

Note 5: A residential land (other than established dwellings) certificate is an exemption certificate under section 43B of the Foreign Acquisitions Regulation.

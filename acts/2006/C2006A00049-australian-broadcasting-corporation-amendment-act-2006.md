Each Act that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Broadcasting Corporation Act 1983

**1 At the end of paragraph 12(1)(a)**

Add “and”.

**2 Paragraph 12(1)(b)**

Repeal the paragraph.

**3 Subsection 12(4)**

Omit “or staff‑elected Director”.

**4 Subsection 12(4A)**

Repeal the subsection.

**5 Sections 13A and 13B**

Repeal the sections.

**6 Subsection 17(1A)**

Repeal the subsection.

**7 At the end of subparagraph 18(2)(a)(ii)**

Add “or”.

**8 Paragraph 18(2)(aa)**

Repeal the paragraph.

**9 Subsection 18(3)**

Repeal the subsection.

**10 Subsection 21(7)**

Repeal the subsection.

**11 Cessation of staff‑elected Director and deputy**

(1) Any person who is the staff‑elected Director immediately before the commencement of item 2 of this Schedule ceases, by force of this subitem, to be a member of the Board on the commencement of that item.

(2) Any person who is the deputy of the staff‑elected Director immediately before the commencement of item 5 of this Schedule ceases, by force of this subitem, to be the deputy of the staff‑elected Director on the commencement of that item.

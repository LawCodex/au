###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

Note: The provisions of the *Family Law Regulations 1984* amended by this Act, and any other provisions of those Regulations, may be amended or repealed by regulations made under section 125 of the *Family Law Act 1975* (see subsection 13(5) of the *Legislation Act 2003*).

## Schedule 1—Amendments relating to self‑employment programs

##### Family Law Regulations 1984

**1 After paragraph 12A(e)**

Insert:

(ea) a payment under the program known as:

(i) the Self‑Employment Assistance program; or

(ii) if the Self‑Employment Assistance program is known by another name—that other name;

**2 At the end of regulation 12A**

Add:

Note: For the purposes of subparagraph (ea)(ii), if the name of the Self‑Employment Assistance program changes, notice must be given of that change (see subsection 23(25) of the *Social Security Act 1991*).

##### Social Security Act 1991

**3 Paragraph 8(8)(t)**

Omit “the scheme known as the New Enterprise Incentive Scheme”, substitute “a self‑employment program”.

**4 Subsection 23(1) (definition of NEIS payment)**

Repeal the definition.

**5 Subsection 23(1)**

Insert:

***self‑employment program*** means:

(a) the scheme known as the New Enterprise Incentive Scheme; or

(b) the program known as:

(i) the Self‑Employment Assistance program; or

(ii) if the Self‑Employment Assistance program is known by another name—that other name.

Note: For the purposes of subparagraph (b)(ii), if the name of the Self‑Employment Assistance program changes, the Employment Secretary must give notice of that change (see subsection (25)).

**6 At the end of section 23**

Add:

Self‑Employment Assistance program

(25) If the program known as the Self‑Employment Assistance program becomes known by another name, the Employment Secretary must, by notifiable instrument, give notice of the change in name.

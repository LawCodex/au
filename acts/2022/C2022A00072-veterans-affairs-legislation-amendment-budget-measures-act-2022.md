###### 3 Schedules

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments to increase rate of certain pensions

##### Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986

**1 Subsection 24(4)**

Omit “$919.40”, substitute “$1,595.66”.

2 Application of amendment

The amendment made by this Schedule applies in relation to working out, in respect of days occurring on or after 1 January 2023:

(a) the rate at which pension is payable under subsection 24(4) of the *Veterans’ Entitlements Act 1986*; or

(b) the rate of any other pension, or the amount of any other payment, that is worked out by reference to that subsection under a law of the Commonwealth.

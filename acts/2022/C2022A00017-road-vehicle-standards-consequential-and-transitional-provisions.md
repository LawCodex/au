This Act is the *Road Vehicle Standards (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Act 2022*.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Road Vehicle Standards (Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Act 2018

**1 Subsection 2(1) (table item 6)**

Omit “12 months”, substitute “24 months”.

[*Assented to 1 April 2022*]

The Parliament of Australia enacts:

###### 1 Short title

This Act is the *Australian Research Council Amendment Act 2022*.

Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this Act has effect according to its terms.

## Schedule 1—Amendments

##### Australian Research Council Act 2001

**1 At the end of subsection 48(2)**

Add:

; (u) the financial year starting on 1 July 2024.

**2 Paragraph 49(v)**

Omit “$804,411,000”, substitute “$815,271,000”.

**3 Paragraph 49(w)**

Omit “$804,442,000”, substitute “$815,288,000”.

**4 Paragraph 49(x)**

Omit “$801,410,000”, substitute “$812,207,000”.

**5 At the end of section 49**

Add:

; (y) for the financial year starting on 1 July 2024—$811,169,000.
